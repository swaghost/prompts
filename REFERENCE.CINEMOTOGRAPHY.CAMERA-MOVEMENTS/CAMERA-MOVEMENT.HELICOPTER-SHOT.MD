# Helicopter Shot

## Shot Group

Aerial

## Description of Resulting Shot

A broad aerial camera movement from a helicopter perspective, revealing large-scale geography and subject position within the environment.

## Usage

Used for establishing scale, pursuit, travel, isolation, landscape, and production grandeur.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** helicopter aerial shot.

**Movement:** travel smoothly through open air with a wide environmental perspective.

**Speed:** steady aerial motion with controlled banking.

**Framing:** keep the landscape and primary subject legible at scale.

**End:** settle on the destination or final spatial relationship.
