# Low Tracking Shot

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement at ground or below-waist height alongside the subject's movement path, matching the subject, footsteps or wheels while keeping the low detail readable as the ground plane moves through frame.

## Usage

Used for emphasizing footsteps, showing ground-level detail, creating dramatic low perspective, or following vehicles/animals. Common in action sequences, horror moments, or when low angle creates specific dramatic effect.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** low tracking shot.

**Movement:** move at ground or below-waist height alongside the subject's movement path.

**Speed:** match the subject, footsteps or wheels.

**Framing:** keep the low detail readable while the ground plane moves through frame.

**End:** finish with the low perspective clearly maintained.
