# Orbit Counter-Clockwise Variant

## Shot Group

Orbit

## Description of Resulting Shot

A counter-clockwise camera orbit around the subject, documented as an alternate filename variant of the counter-clockwise orbit movement.

## Usage

Used for dynamic perspective changes, dimensional reveals, and relationship shifts around a stable subject.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** orbit counter-clockwise.

**Movement:** circle smoothly around the subject in a counter-clockwise direction.

**Speed:** steady, controlled, and continuous.

**Framing:** maintain subject scale and readable facial orientation while the background rotates.

**End:** finish at the intended counter-clockwise angle without reversing direction.
