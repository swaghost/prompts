# Camera Rise To Face

## Shot Group

Pedestal/Crane

## Description of Resulting Shot

The camera rises smoothly from a lower body or environmental view until the subject's face becomes the dominant focal point.

## Usage

Used for introductions, revelation, hero emphasis, and rising emotional attention.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** camera rise to face.

**Movement:** move vertically upward toward the subject's face.

**Speed:** slow, smooth, deliberate rise.

**Framing:** transition from the lower body or setting to a clear face-level composition.

**End:** settle at eye level with the face sharp and fully readable.
