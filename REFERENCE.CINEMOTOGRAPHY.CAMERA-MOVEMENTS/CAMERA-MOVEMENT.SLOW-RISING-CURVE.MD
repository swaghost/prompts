# Slow Rising Curve

## Shot Group

Crane/Arc

## Description of Resulting Shot

The camera rises gradually while following a smooth curved path around or past the subject.

## Usage

Used for revelation, elevation, transformation, landscape expansion, and graceful transitions.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** slow rising curve.

**Movement:** ascend while following a smooth curved path.

**Speed:** slow, continuous, and controlled.

**Framing:** keep the subject or destination readable as height and angle change.

**End:** finish at the elevated reveal with a stable horizon.
