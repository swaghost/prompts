# Aggressive Dolly In

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A forceful physical camera move rapidly forward toward the subject, closing distance with a strong acceleration and a decisive finish.

## Usage

Used for threat, urgency, confrontation, shock, or sudden psychological intensity.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** aggressive dolly in.

**Movement:** drive the camera physically forward toward the main subject.

**Speed:** fast acceleration with controlled deceleration.

**Framing:** preserve the subject's position and eye-line while the composition tightens.

**End:** finish in an intense close framing without collision or digital zoom.
