# Vehicle Tracking Variant

## Shot Group

Tracking/Vehicle

## Description of Resulting Shot

The camera tracks a moving vehicle from a matching vehicle or parallel travel path, preserving speed and directional relationship.

## Usage

Used for driving scenes, pursuit, travel, product motion, and environmental speed.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** vehicle tracking shot.

**Movement:** travel alongside or behind the moving vehicle.

**Speed:** match the vehicle with smooth stabilized motion.

**Framing:** preserve vehicle scale, travel direction, and readable surroundings.

**End:** finish with the vehicle continuing naturally through the frame.
