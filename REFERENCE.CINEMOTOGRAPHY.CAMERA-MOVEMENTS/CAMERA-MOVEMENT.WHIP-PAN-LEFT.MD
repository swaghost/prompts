# Whip Pan Left

## Shot Group

Pan/Tilt

## Description of Resulting Shot

A rapid horizontal rotation from the starting direction toward a new target on the left with fast snap motion and brief motion blur during rotation, landing on a sharp final frame.

## Usage

Used for creating energy, transitioning between subjects quickly, showing reactions, or building tension. Common in action sequences, comedic timing, and fast-paced storytelling where instant reframing creates impact.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** whip pan left.

**Movement:** rotate rapidly from the starting direction toward a new target on the left.

**Speed:** fast snap with brief motion blur during the rotation.

**Framing:** begin on one readable composition and land on a second readable target.

**End:** settle into a sharp final frame.
