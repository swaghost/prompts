# Pass Through Objects

## Shot Group

Pass-Through/Transition

## Description of Resulting Shot

The camera travels through foreground objects or environmental layers to reveal a new space or subject beyond them.

## Usage

Used for immersive transitions, impossible camera paths, discovery, and movement through layered environments.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** pass through objects.

**Movement:** travel smoothly through the selected foreground objects or opening.

**Speed:** controlled continuous movement.

**Framing:** preserve spatial continuity and make the passage feel physically motivated.

**End:** emerge into a clear final composition without clipping or object duplication.
