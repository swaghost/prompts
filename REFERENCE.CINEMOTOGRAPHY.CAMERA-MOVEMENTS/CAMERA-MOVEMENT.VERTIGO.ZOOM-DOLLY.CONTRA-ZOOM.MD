# Vertigo / Dolly-Zoom Contra-Zoom

## Shot Group

Dolly/Optical Zoom

## Description of Resulting Shot

The camera physically dollies forward or backward while the lens zooms in the opposite direction, keeping the subject's apparent size nearly constant while the background perspective visibly warps.

## Usage

Used for disorientation, realization, dread, vertigo, psychological rupture, and sudden shifts in perceived reality.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** dolly-zoom, also called a vertigo or contra-zoom.

**Movement:** dolly toward the subject while zooming out, or dolly away while zooming in at a matched rate.

**Speed:** smooth and precisely coordinated.

**Framing:** keep the subject's apparent size stable while the background expands or compresses dramatically.

**End:** hold the warped perspective briefly, then settle without a hard cut.
