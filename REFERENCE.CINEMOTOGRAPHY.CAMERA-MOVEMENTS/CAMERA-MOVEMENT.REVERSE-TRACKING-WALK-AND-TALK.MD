# Reverse Tracking Walk-and-Talk

## Shot Group

Reverse Tracking/Dialogue

## Description of Resulting Shot

The camera travels backward in front of walking subjects, maintaining their faces and conversation while matching their pace.

## Usage

Used for dialogue, relationship development, exposition, and natural movement through a location.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** reverse tracking walk-and-talk.

**Movement:** travel backward smoothly while facing the walking subjects.

**Speed:** match the walking pace with stabilized motion.

**Framing:** keep faces readable, preserve conversational eye-lines, and maintain comfortable headroom.

**End:** finish while continuing the subjects' direction of travel.
