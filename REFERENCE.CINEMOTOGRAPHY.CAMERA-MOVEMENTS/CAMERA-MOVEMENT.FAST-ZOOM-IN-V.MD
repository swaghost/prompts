# Fast Zoom In Vertical Variant

## Shot Group

Zoom

## Description of Resulting Shot

A fast optical zoom inward designed for a vertical composition, bringing a subject or detail forward without physical camera travel.

## Usage

Used for quick attention shifts, social-video emphasis, discovery, and energetic transitions.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** fast vertical zoom in.

**Movement:** zoom optically inward while remaining physically stationary.

**Speed:** fast but smoother than a crash zoom.

**Framing:** keep the vertical subject aligned and avoid cropping important features.

**End:** finish on a tight readable composition.
