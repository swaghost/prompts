# Crash Zoom In Vertical Variant

## Shot Group

Zoom

## Description of Resulting Shot

A rapid vertical-format crash zoom inward that compresses distance optically and lands abruptly on the subject or detail.

## Usage

Used for shock, realization, comic emphasis, alarm, and short-form vertical video punctuation.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** vertical crash zoom in.

**Movement:** zoom rapidly inward without physically moving the camera.

**Speed:** abrupt and forceful.

**Framing:** preserve the vertical composition while the focal subject expands quickly.

**End:** stop decisively on the intended detail without digital smearing.
