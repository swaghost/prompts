# Fast Tracking Shot

## Shot Group

Tracking

## Description of Resulting Shot

A high-speed camera movement that travels with or alongside a rapidly moving subject while preserving directional continuity.

## Usage

Used for pursuit, action, escape, athletic movement, and urgency.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** fast tracking shot.

**Movement:** track rapidly with the subject along the action path.

**Speed:** high speed with controlled stabilization and motivated motion blur.

**Framing:** keep the subject readable while the environment streaks naturally.

**End:** finish aligned with the subject's movement direction.
