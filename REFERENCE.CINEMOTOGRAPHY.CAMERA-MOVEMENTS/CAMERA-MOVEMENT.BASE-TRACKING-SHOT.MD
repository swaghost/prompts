# Base Tracking Shot

## Shot Group

Tracking

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera travels smoothly with a moving subject, maintaining a consistent relative distance, height, and screen position.

## Usage

Used for natural movement, pursuit, walk-and-talk scenes, and stable cinematic continuity.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** base tracking shot.

**Movement:** travel with the subject along the subject's path.

**Speed:** match the subject smoothly.

**Framing:** maintain consistent subject scale and direction of travel.

**End:** finish while keeping the subject readable and centered in the movement path.
