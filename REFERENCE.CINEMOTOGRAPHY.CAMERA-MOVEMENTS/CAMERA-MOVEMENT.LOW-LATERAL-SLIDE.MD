# Low Lateral Slide

## Shot Group

Slider/Low Angle

## Description of Resulting Shot

A low camera glides laterally across the scene while maintaining a near-ground perspective and a stable horizon.

## Usage

Used for tension, product detail, grounded movement, architecture, and stylized low-angle reveals.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** low lateral slide.

**Movement:** glide sideways close to the ground.

**Speed:** smooth and measured.

**Framing:** preserve the low perspective and keep the subject readable as the background shifts.

**End:** settle on the subject or final environmental detail.
