# Tracking Shot (Base Tracking Shot)

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement through the scene with the main subject, matching their pace while keeping them consistently readable as the environment moves around them.

## Usage

Used for following action, maintaining engagement with moving subjects, creating immersive movement, or showing journey. Common in walking dialogue scenes, action sequences, or any moment following a character through space.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** tracking shot.

**Movement:** move through the scene with the main subject.

**Speed:** match the subject's pace.

**Framing:** keep the subject consistently readable while the environment moves around them.

**End:** maintain a clear moving composition.
