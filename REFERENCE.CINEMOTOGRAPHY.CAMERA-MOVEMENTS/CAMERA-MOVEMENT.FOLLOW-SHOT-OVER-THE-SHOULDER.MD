# Follow-Shot / Over-the-Shoulder

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement behind the subject along their route at shoulder height, matching their pace while keeping the back, shoulder or head as the foreground guide with the route ahead staying readable.

## Usage

Used for subjective perspective, showing character's journey from behind, following investigations or discoveries, or creating audience identification with character. Common in suspense sequences, exploration moments, or intimate following.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** follow shot from behind.

**Movement:** move behind the subject along their route at shoulder height.

**Speed:** match the subject's pace.

**Framing:** keep the back, shoulder or head as the foreground guide while the route ahead stays readable.

**End:** continue following with the subject leading the frame.
