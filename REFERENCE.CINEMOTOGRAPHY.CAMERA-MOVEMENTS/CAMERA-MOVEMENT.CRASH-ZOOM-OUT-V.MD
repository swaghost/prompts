# Crash Zoom Out Vertical Variant

## Shot Group

Zoom

## Description of Resulting Shot

A rapid vertical-format crash zoom outward that suddenly reveals the subject's surrounding space.

## Usage

Used for surprise reveals, isolation, scale changes, comedy, or sudden context.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** vertical crash zoom out.

**Movement:** zoom rapidly outward without physically moving the camera.

**Speed:** abrupt and forceful.

**Framing:** preserve the vertical composition while the environment expands around the subject.

**End:** stop decisively on a readable wider composition.
