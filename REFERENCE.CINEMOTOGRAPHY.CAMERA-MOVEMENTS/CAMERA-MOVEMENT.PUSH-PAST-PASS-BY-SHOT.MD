# Push-Past / Pass-By Shot

## Shot Group

Tracking/Reveal

## Description of Resulting Shot

The camera moves past a subject or foreground obstruction, using the passing object to reveal a new angle, subject, or environment.

## Usage

Used for reveals, transitions, spatial continuity, and energetic movement through crowded environments.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** push-past pass-by shot.

**Movement:** travel past the foreground subject or obstruction along a continuous path.

**Speed:** smooth with purposeful forward momentum.

**Framing:** let the passing object briefly occlude the view, then reveal the destination subject.

**End:** settle on the newly revealed composition.
