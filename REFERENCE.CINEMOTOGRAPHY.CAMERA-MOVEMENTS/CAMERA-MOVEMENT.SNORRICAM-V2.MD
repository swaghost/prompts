# Snorricam Version 2

## Shot Group

Body-Mounted/Subjective

## Description of Resulting Shot

An alternate Snorricam setup keeps the camera rigidly attached to the subject while the background moves independently around the fixed subject framing.

## Usage

Used for disorientation, panic, intoxication, psychological pressure, and subjective instability.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** body-mounted Snorricam, version 2.

**Movement:** keep the camera fixed to the subject's body while the environment moves around them.

**Speed:** match the subject's movement with controlled instability.

**Framing:** keep the subject centered and relatively stable while the background shifts.

**End:** preserve the subjective locked relationship without random frame tearing.
