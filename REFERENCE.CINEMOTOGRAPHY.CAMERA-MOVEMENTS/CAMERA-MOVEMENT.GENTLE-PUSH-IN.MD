# Gentle Push In

## Shot Group

Dolly/Emotional Push

## Description of Resulting Shot

A subtle physical camera movement forward that gradually increases intimacy without creating a noticeable jolt.

## Usage

Used for realization, tenderness, quiet tension, confession, and emotional attention.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** gentle push in.

**Movement:** move slowly forward toward the subject.

**Speed:** nearly imperceptible, smooth, and steady.

**Framing:** preserve eye-line, lighting, and facial identity as the composition tightens.

**End:** finish slightly closer with the emotional detail still natural.
