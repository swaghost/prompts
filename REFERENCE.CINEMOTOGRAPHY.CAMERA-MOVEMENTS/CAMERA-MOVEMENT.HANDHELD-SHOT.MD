# Handheld Shot

## Shot Group

Human (Attached) Camera

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera held at human operator height with natural body movement creating responsive and organic motion, keeping the subject readable while the frame has subtle sway and micro-adjustments.

## Usage

Used for documentary realism, immediate energy, human-scale intimacy, or raw authenticity. Common in documentaries, run-and-gun scenes, intimate moments, or any sequence requiring naturalistic human perspective and energy.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** handheld shot.

**Movement:** hold the camera at human operator height with natural body movement.

**Speed:** responsive and organic.

**Framing:** keep the subject readable while the frame has subtle sway and micro-adjustments.

**End:** finish with a natural handheld composition.
