# First-Person View

## Shot Group

POV/Immersive

## Description of Resulting Shot

The camera represents the subject's direct visual perspective, with movement, height, and gaze aligned to the subject's experience.

## Usage

Used for immersion, discovery, danger, subjective memory, and direct audience identification.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** first-person view.

**Movement:** move from the subject's visual perspective with natural head and body motion.

**Speed:** match the subject's pace and emotional state.

**Framing:** show what the subject would realistically see, including natural gaze shifts.

**End:** settle on the subject's intended visual target.
