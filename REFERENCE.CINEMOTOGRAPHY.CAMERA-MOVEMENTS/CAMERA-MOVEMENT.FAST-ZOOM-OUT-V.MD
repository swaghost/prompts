# Fast Zoom Out Vertical Variant

## Shot Group

Zoom

## Description of Resulting Shot

A fast optical zoom outward designed for a vertical composition, expanding the visible setting around the subject.

## Usage

Used for reveal, context, isolation, and energetic short-form transitions.
