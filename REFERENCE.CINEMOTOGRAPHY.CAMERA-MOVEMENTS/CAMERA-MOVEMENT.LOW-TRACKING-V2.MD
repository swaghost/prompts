# Low Tracking Shot Variant

## Shot Group

Tracking/Low Angle

## Description of Resulting Shot

An alternate low tracking movement that follows the subject near ground level with a distinct path or timing from the base low-tracking reference.

## Usage

Used for pursuit, power, speed, footsteps, vehicles, and heightened physical presence.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** low tracking shot, alternate version.

**Movement:** track smoothly near ground level with the subject.

**Speed:** match the subject with controlled forward or lateral acceleration.

**Framing:** preserve the low-angle relationship and readable subject silhouette.

**End:** finish aligned with the subject's path.
