# Slow Lateral Drift

## Shot Group

Slider/Atmospheric

## Description of Resulting Shot

A nearly floating lateral camera movement that shifts slowly across the scene with minimal visible acceleration.

## Usage

Used for quiet unease, atmosphere, contemplation, dream states, and subtle spatial discovery.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** slow lateral drift.

**Movement:** drift sideways through the scene with a smooth floating path.

**Speed:** very slow and nearly constant.

**Framing:** preserve the calm composition while the spatial relationship gradually changes.

**End:** settle without a visible jolt or direction reversal.
