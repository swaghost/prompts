# Smooth Quarter-Circle

## Shot Group

Arc/Orbit

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera travels along a smooth quarter-circle path around the subject, changing perspective by approximately 90 degrees.

## Usage

Used for elegant reveals, character introductions, dimensional product shots, and controlled perspective shifts.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** smooth quarter-circle arc.

**Movement:** travel around the subject along a precise quarter-circle path.

**Speed:** smooth and measured.

**Framing:** maintain distance and subject scale while the angle changes through 90 degrees.

**End:** finish at the new three-quarter or side angle.
