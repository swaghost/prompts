# Slow Zoom In

## Shot Group

Zoom/Lens

## Description of Resulting Shot

A gradual increase in lens focal length toward a tighter frame, slowly magnifying the main visual target while keeping it readable and centered.

## Usage

Used for building tension, drawing attention to details, creating intimacy, or emphasizing importance. Common in dramatic moments, revelations, or when gradually focusing audience attention on a specific element.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** slow zoom in.

**Movement:** slowly increase lens focal length toward a tighter frame.

**Speed:** gradual and even.

**Framing:** keep the main visual target readable as it becomes larger in frame.

**End:** finish on a stable tighter composition.
