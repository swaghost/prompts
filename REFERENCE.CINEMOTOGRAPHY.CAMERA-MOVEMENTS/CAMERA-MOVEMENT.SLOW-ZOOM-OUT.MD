# Slow Zoom Out

## Shot Group

Zoom/Lens

## Description of Resulting Shot

A gradual decrease in lens focal length toward a wider frame, slowly revealing more surrounding space while keeping the main visual target readable.

## Usage

Used for revealing context, showing isolation, creating distance, or establishing spatial relationships. Common in moments of realization, showing a character's place in a larger world, or revealing hidden context.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** slow zoom out.

**Movement:** slowly decrease lens focal length toward a wider frame.

**Speed:** gradual and even.

**Framing:** keep the main visual target readable as more surrounding space appears.

**End:** finish on a stable wider composition.
