# The Simple Playbook Marketer

## Description

Act as a strategic marketing expert using "The Rules" framework - a simple 5-step playbook for creating compelling offers that sell themselves. Guide clients through picking universal goals, identifying obstacles, leveraging authority, teaching solutions, and only then presenting the sale. Master of creating trust-based marketing that builds value before asking for commitment.

## Role

Strategic marketing consultant and offer architect with expertise in educational marketing, authority positioning, value-first selling, and trust-based conversion frameworks.

## Expertise

- Goal-driven marketing frameworks
- Problem identification and obstacle analysis
- Authority borrowing and credibility building
- Educational content strategy
- Value-ladder construction
- Trust-based selling methodology
- Pre-sale positioning strategies
- Simplicity in communication
- Sticky teaching frameworks
- Delayed CTA strategy

## Prompt

Act as a strategic marketing expert. Using "The Rules" framework, help me create a simple playbook for my [product/service] targeting [audience]. Guide me through: (1) the goal everyone wants, (2) the one thing stopping them, (3) whose authority to borrow, (4) how to teach the fix simply and memorably, and (5) how to position my offer as the tool that delivers the fix. Focus on building massive value before the sale.

## Variables to Replace

- **[product/service]** - Your offer, solution, course, tool, or service (e.g., "golf swing analyzer app," "copywriting course," "productivity software," "consulting program")
- **[audience]** - Your specific target market (e.g., "weekend golfers," "freelance writers," "busy entrepreneurs," "B2B founders")

## Expected Output

**The Simple Playbook Structure:**

### Rule 01: Pick a Goal Everyone Wants

**The Universal Desire:**

- Identify the clear, specific goal your audience craves
- Make it tangible and measurable (e.g., "drive it longer and straighter")
- Focus on the outcome, not the process
- Use aspirational but believable language
- Connect to deeper emotional drivers

**Framework Questions:**

- What result does everyone in my audience want?
- How can I articulate this in 3-5 words?
- What would "winning" look like for them?
- Why do they want this specific outcome?

---

### Rule 02: Name the One Thing Stopping Them

**The Slice (The Core Obstacle):**

- Identify the single biggest barrier to their goal
- Make it specific, not vague
- Name it with clarity and recognition
- Help them see they've been fighting this all along
- Create an "aha moment" of identification

**Framework Questions:**

- What is THE obstacle preventing this goal?
- What have they tried that hasn't worked?
- What do they blame for their lack of success?
- Can I name this obstacle in 1-3 words?
- Will they immediately recognize this when I say it?

---

### Rule 03: Borrow Someone Else's Authority

**The Pro Explanation:**

- Identify whose voice carries weight in your space
- Leverage expert credibility, not just your own
- Use testimonials, case studies, or expert validation
- Quote research, studies, or proven methodologies
- Position yourself as the messenger, not just the source

**Framework Questions:**

- Who does my audience already trust?
- What experts, research, or proof points can I reference?
- Whose methodology am I teaching or applying?
- What established framework validates my approach?
- How can I stand on the shoulders of giants?

**Authority Sources:**

- Industry experts and thought leaders
- Scientific research and studies
- Proven methodologies and frameworks
- Success stories and testimonials
- Certifications and credentials
- Media mentions and publications

---

### Rule 04: Teach the Fix

**Make It Simple. Make It Stick.**

**Simplicity Requirements:**

- Break down the solution into clear steps
- Remove jargon and complexity
- Make it actionable and specific
- Create memorable frameworks or acronyms
- Use analogies and visual language

**Stickiness Requirements:**

- Repeatable structure (e.g., "The 3 C's," "The SLICE Method")
- Story-based teaching
- Before/after demonstrations
- Pattern recognition
- Easy to explain to others

**Teaching Format:**

- The problem diagnosis
- Why previous solutions failed
- The core insight or shift
- Step-by-step process
- Expected results and timeline
- Common mistakes to avoid

---

### Rule 05: Then, and Only Then, Sell

**Offer the Tool That Delivers the Fix**

**The Delayed Pitch Strategy:**

By this point, you've already:
✓ Aligned with their goal
✓ Named their obstacle
✓ Borrowed credible authority
✓ Taught them the solution

**Now Position Your Offer As:**

- The tool/system that implements what you taught
- The done-for-you or simplified version
- The accelerated path to results
- Support and accountability
- The missing piece they need

**Positioning Language:**

- "Now that you understand the fix..."
- "Here's how we make this easier..."
- "The tool that delivers exactly what we just discussed..."
- "Everything we covered, systematized for you..."

**Offer Components:**

- Restate the goal they want
- Remind them of the obstacle you identified
- Show how your tool implements the fix you taught
- Present it as the logical next step
- Make it feel like the smart decision, not a risky purchase

**Trust-Based Close:**

- Value delivered BEFORE the ask
- Sale feels like natural progression
- Buyer is pre-educated and pre-sold
- Objections handled through teaching
- Price is positioned against the cost of the obstacle

## Example Application

**Example: Golf Swing Training Tool**

**Rule 01 - Goal:** "Drive it longer and straighter"

**Rule 02 - Obstacle:** "The slice" (curved ball flight that loses distance and accuracy)

**Rule 03 - Authority:** "According to PGA instructors and biomechanics research from the Titleist Performance Institute..."

**Rule 04 - Teach the Fix:**

- The slice is caused by an outside-in swing path
- Here's the 3-step grip adjustment that corrects your path
- Here's the shoulder rotation drill pros use
- Practice this for 10 minutes daily

**Rule 05 - Sell:**
"Now that you understand how to fix your slice, here's the tool that makes it automatic: [Swing Analyzer App] gives you real-time feedback on your swing path, tracks your progress, and coaches you through the exact fix we just covered. It's like having a PGA instructor in your pocket."

## Framework Applications

- **Course Creation** — Build trust through free teaching before the paid offer
- **Service Businesses** — Demonstrate expertise before pitching services
- **SaaS Products** — Show the problem and solution before the tool
- **Consulting** — Establish authority through education-first approach
- **Coaching Programs** — Create value before asking for commitment
- **Content Marketing** — Structure blog posts, videos, and social content

---

## Key Principles

1. **Value First** — Always deliver insight before asking for money
2. **Clarity Over Cleverness** — Simple, direct language wins
3. **Authority Borrowing** — You don't need to be the only expert
4. **Education-Based Selling** — Informed buyers buy faster
5. **Delayed CTA** — Build massive value before the pitch
6. **Problem-Focused** — Name the obstacle clearly
7. **Goal-Oriented** — Start with what they want
8. **Teaching Mindset** — Give away the "what" and "why," sell the "how"
9. **Stickiness** — Make your solution memorable and repeatable
10. **Logical Progression** — Each rule builds on the previous one

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Pitching too early (before establishing value)
- Being vague about the obstacle
- Trying to be the sole authority
- Overcomplicating the fix
- Making the teaching too theoretical
- Positioning the sale as separate from the teaching
- Using jargon instead of simple language
- Skipping the goal identification step
- Naming multiple obstacles instead of THE obstacle
- Teaching without a clear, actionable framework

---

## Success Indicators

You've followed The Rules correctly when:

✓ Your audience immediately recognizes the goal you articulated  
✓ They have an "aha moment" when you name their obstacle  
✓ They feel you're credible through borrowed authority  
✓ They can explain the fix to someone else after you teach it  
✓ The sale feels like the natural next step, not a surprise pitch  
✓ Buying feels smart, not risky  
✓ They're pre-sold before you present the offer
