# The Website Copy System

## Description

Act as a complete website copywriting specialist. Write all the copy for a [business type] website, including the homepage headline, About section, Services section, testimonial suggestions, FAQ section, and footer CTA. Make every word earn its place.

## Role

Complete website copywriting specialist with expertise in full-site copywriting, cohesive messaging, conversion optimization across all pages, and strategic content architecture for complete websites.

## Expertise

- Full-website copywriting and content strategy
- Homepage conversion copywriting
- About page storytelling
- Service/product page copywriting
- Testimonial optimization and social proof
- FAQ strategy and objection handling
- Footer CTA optimization
- Brand voice consistency
- Message hierarchy across pages
- Content architecture and flow

## Prompt

Act as a complete website copywriting specialist. Write all the copy for my [business type] website: the homepage headline, About section, Services section, testimonial suggestions, FAQ section, and footer CTA. Make every word earn its place.

## Variables to Replace

- **[business type]** - Your specific business type with as much detail as possible (e.g., "boutique branding agency for wellness startups," "freelance web designer for small businesses," "life coaching practice for corporate professionals," "e-commerce store selling sustainable home goods")

## Expected Output

**Complete Website Copy Package:**

1. **Homepage Headline System**

   **Primary Headline:**
   - Main value proposition headline
   - 3-5 variations for testing
   - Attention-grabbing and benefit-focused
   - Clear, concise, compelling

   **Subheadline:**
   - Expands on main promise
   - Qualifies audience or clarifies offer
   - Creates curiosity or urgency
   - 2-3 variations

   **Supporting Homepage Copy:**
   - Hero section CTA copy
   - Above-fold description (1-2 sentences)
   - Section headlines for homepage
   - Problem/solution copy
   - Social proof integration
   - Final CTA section copy

2. **About Section Copy**

   **Structure:**
   - About headline that connects with readers
   - Opening paragraph that builds trust
   - Origin story or journey (condensed):
     - Where you started
     - Why you do what you do
     - What makes you different
   - Credentials woven naturally
   - Personal element for connection
   - Values or philosophy
   - Transition to working together
   - About section CTA

   **Tone Guidelines:**
   - Authentic and conversational
   - Professional but approachable
   - About them, not just you
   - Builds trust and connection

3. **Services Section Copy**

   **Services Overview:**
   - Services section main headline
   - Overview paragraph

   **Individual Service Descriptions:**
   For each service (typically 3-5 services):
   - Service name/headline
   - Who it's for
   - What's included (bullet points)
   - Outcome or transformation
   - Process overview (optional)
   - Starting price or investment level
   - Service-specific CTA

   **Format:**
   - Benefit-driven, not feature-dumping
   - Clear and scannable
   - Addresses "what's in it for me"
   - Differentiates each service

4. **Testimonial Suggestions**

   **Testimonial Strategy:**
   - Number of testimonials needed
   - Where to place them on site
   - What each should highlight:
     - Results-focused testimonials
     - Process/experience testimonials
     - Transformation testimonials

   **Testimonial Framework to Request:**
   - Before state (problem they had)
   - What they tried before
   - Why they chose you
   - What the experience was like
   - Results achieved
   - Recommendation to others

   **Testimonial Placement:**
   - Homepage social proof
   - Service page validation
   - About page trust-building
   - Dedicated testimonials page

   **Format Guidance:**
   - Name, photo, title/business
   - Specific results or metrics when possible
   - 2-3 sentences (short) or 1 paragraph (detailed)
   - Video testimonial opportunities

5. **FAQ Section Copy**

   **Strategic FAQ Questions:**
   Address objections and concerns:
   - Pricing and payment questions
   - Process and timeline questions
   - Qualification or fit questions
   - Results and guarantee questions
   - Logistics and practical questions

   **FAQ Structure:**
   - 8-12 questions typically
   - Organized by category if many
   - Answers: clear, concise, reassuring
   - Include CTA at end of FAQ section

   **Sample FAQ Categories:**
   - About the service/product
   - How it works
   - Pricing and payment
   - Results and timeline
   - Getting started

   **Tone:**
   - Helpful and reassuring
   - Anticipates concerns
   - Builds confidence
   - Reduces friction to buying

6. **Footer CTA Copy**

   **Final Call-to-Action:**
   - Compelling headline for footer CTA
   - Supporting copy (1-2 sentences)
   - Primary CTA button text
   - Secondary action if applicable
   - Value reminder or urgency

   **Purpose:**
   - Last chance to convert visitors
   - Remind of core value
   - Make next step obvious
   - Multiple contact options

   **Footer Elements:**
   - CTA section at top of footer
   - Navigation menu
   - Contact information
   - Social proof badge/summary
   - Copyright and legal links
   - Social media links

7. **Supporting Copy Elements**

   **Navigation Menu Copy:**
   - Clear, actionable menu items
   - Benefit-focused where possible

   **Meta Copy:**
   - Page titles (SEO)
   - Meta descriptions
   - Social sharing copy

   **Microcopy:**
   - Form labels and placeholders
   - Button copy throughout site
   - Error messages
   - Success messages
   - Tooltip text

   **Email/Newsletter Signup:**
   - Headline for signup form
   - Benefit of subscribing
   - Privacy assurance
   - Button text

8. **Brand Voice & Consistency Guidelines**

   **Voice Characteristics:**
   - Tone (professional, casual, authoritative, friendly)
   - Language style (simple, technical, creative)
   - Perspective (we, I, you)
   - Vocabulary (industry terms, accessible language)

   **Consistency Elements:**
   - Key phrases to repeat
   - Words to avoid
   - Message hierarchy
   - How to talk about services
   - How to address customer pain points

## Best Practices

**For Best Results:**

- Be specific about your business type and niche
- Define your ideal customer clearly
- List your main services or products
- Share your unique differentiator
- Note your brand personality (formal, casual, edgy, etc.)
- Provide price points or positioning (budget, premium, luxury)
- Share existing testimonials or common client feedback
- Note main objections or FAQs you encounter
- Specify conversion goals (bookings, sales, inquiries)
- Mention competitors or sites you admire

**Example Enhanced Prompt:**
"Act as a complete website copywriting specialist. Write all the copy for my boutique branding agency website specializing in wellness startups with $10K-$25K budgets: the homepage headline (main value prop: 'transform from scrappy startup to premium brand'), About section (founded by former wellness brand CMO), Services section (3 services: brand strategy $8K, full brand identity $15K, brand refresh $5K), testimonial suggestions (have 6 clients with great results), FAQ section (common question: 'Why not just use Canva?'), and footer CTA. Make every word earn its place. Brand voice: sophisticated but approachable, wellness-fluent but not woo-woo."

## Website Copy Architecture

**Information Hierarchy:**

- Homepage: Overview and primary conversion
- About: Trust and connection building
- Services/Products: Detailed value propositions
- Testimonials: Social proof and validation
- FAQ: Objection handling and reassurance
- Contact/Footer: Final conversion opportunities

**Message Consistency:**

- Core message repeated throughout
- Same value proposition in different formats
- Benefit hierarchy maintained
- Brand voice consistency
- Strategic repetition of key points

**Conversion Funnel Copy:**

- Awareness: Homepage attracts and intrigues
- Interest: Services/products create desire
- Consideration: About and testimonials build trust
- Decision: FAQ removes doubts
- Action: CTAs throughout guide to conversion

## Copy Quality Standards

**Every Word Earns Its Place:**

- No fluff or filler
- Active voice preferred
- Specific over generic
- Benefits before features
- Reader-focused (you, not we)
- Clear and concise
- Action-oriented
- Emotionally resonant

**Avoid:**

- Jargon without explanation
- Long-winded sentences
- Passive voice
- Clichés and buzzwords
- Vague promises
- Feature dumping
- Industry speak customers don't use
- Unnecessary words

## Output Format

Expect comprehensive website copy package including:

- Complete homepage copy (headline, subheadline, hero CTA, supporting sections)
- Full About section copy
- All Services section copy (overview + individual services)
- Testimonial strategy and request framework
- Complete FAQ section (8-12 Q&As)
- Footer CTA copy
- Navigation and microcopy suggestions
- Brand voice guidelines
- Meta copy (titles, descriptions)
- Implementation notes and hierarchy
- A/B testing suggestions for key elements
