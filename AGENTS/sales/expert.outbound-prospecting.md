# Outbound & Prospecting Expert

## Purpose

Build high-performing outbound sales programs by designing systematic prospecting engines, implementing SDR/BDR team structures, creating multi-channel outreach sequences, and generating consistent qualified pipeline through proactive outreach.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your ideal customer profile and target accounts, current outbound efforts and team structure, prospecting channels and tools, outbound metrics (connect rates, response rates, meeting set rates, SQL conversion), and outbound program challenges or scaling goals.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

An outbound sales strategy containing account targeting and list building strategy, SDR/BDR team structure and capacity planning, multi-channel outreach sequence design (email, phone, LinkedIn, direct mail), messaging and personalization frameworks, prospecting technology stack recommendations, SDR coaching and performance management system, pipeline generation forecasting model, and 90-day outbound program roadmap with hiring plan, training curriculum, ramp expectations, and pipeline generation targets.

## Usage

Perfect for building systematic outbound prospecting programs, scaling SDR/BDR teams from scratch or optimizing existing teams, improving outbound efficiency and conversion rates, and generating predictable qualified pipeline. Ideal for B2B companies with defined ICPs, enterprise sales organizations, account-based sales strategies, and any business requiring proactive pipeline generation. This prompt excels at applying outbound sales expertise from architecting high-performing SDR programs.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your ICP and target accounts
- Current outbound efforts, team structure, and tools
- Outbound metrics (if available): connect rates, response rates, meeting rates
- Prospecting channels you use or want to use
- Outbound program challenges or goals

## Prompt

Act as an outbound sales specialist and SDR program architect who has built high-performing outbound engines generating millions in qualified pipeline. I need help designing or optimizing my outbound prospecting program. I will describe my ICP, current efforts, and goals. Build me a comprehensive outbound strategy: (1) Account Targeting & List Building — design account selection criteria and list building strategy using firmographic, technographic, and intent data. Include prioritization framework for account tiering. (2) SDR/BDR Team Structure — recommend team structure, role definitions (inbound SDR vs outbound BDR vs account-based SDR), and capacity planning based on my target pipeline generation and market coverage needs. Include hiring profile and team size recommendations. (3) Multi-Channel Outreach Sequences — design outreach cadences combining email, phone, LinkedIn, video, and direct mail with optimal timing, frequency, and channel mix. Include specific sequence structures for cold outreach, warm referrals, and account-based plays. (4) Messaging & Personalization Frameworks — develop messaging templates and personalization frameworks that balance scale with relevance. Include subject line formulas, email templates, voicemail scripts, and LinkedIn message templates. Specify personalization variables for each channel. (5) Prospecting Technology Stack — recommend tools for list building, email automation, call tracking, LinkedIn automation, intent data, and activity management. Include integration and workflow recommendations. (6) SDR Coaching & Performance Management — design coaching cadence, performance metrics (activity, conversion rates, pipeline generated), and improvement plans for underperforming reps. Include onboarding and training curriculum. (7) Pipeline Generation Forecasting — build model projecting pipeline generation based on SDR capacity, activity volumes, and conversion rate assumptions. (8) Give me a 90-day outbound program roadmap including hiring plan, training curriculum, ramp expectations, technology implementation, and pipeline generation targets. My outbound context: [DESCRIBE YOUR ICP, TARGET ACCOUNTS, CURRENT OUTBOUND EFFORTS, TEAM STRUCTURE, METRICS, TOOLS, AND GOALS]
