# Sales Team Building Expert

## Purpose

Build and scale high-performing sales organizations by implementing systematic hiring processes, designing effective onboarding and training programs, creating coaching and performance management systems, and establishing sales cultures that drive consistent execution and team success.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your current sales team size and structure, hiring needs and growth plans, onboarding program, training curriculum, coaching approach, performance management systems, and sales team development challenges or scaling objectives.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A sales team building framework containing hiring profile and interview process design including ideal rep characteristics, structured interview questions, and evaluation scorecards, onboarding program with 30-60-90 day ramp plan and certification checkpoints, training curriculum covering product, methodology, process, and soft skills, coaching system with one-on-one cadence, call review process, and skill development frameworks, performance management approach including metrics, improvement plans, and difficult conversation guides, sales culture and team building recommendations, and 90-day talent roadmap with hiring timeline, training rollout, coaching implementation, and team development milestones.

## Usage

Perfect for scaling sales teams from small groups to substantial organizations, improving sales hiring quality and reducing mis-hires, building systematic onboarding that accelerates ramp time, and implementing coaching that develops consistent high performance. Ideal for rapidly growing sales organizations, first-time sales leaders building teams, companies struggling with sales turnover or underperformance, and any organization hiring multiple sales professionals. This prompt excels at applying VP of Sales expertise having hired, trained, and managed 50+ quota-carrying professionals.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Current sales team size, structure, and roles
- Hiring needs and growth plans
- Description of onboarding and training programs (if any)
- Current coaching and performance management approach
- Sales team development challenges or goals

## Prompt

Act as a VP of Sales and sales talent expert who has hired, trained, and managed 50+ quota-carrying sales professionals at scaling companies. I need help building or optimizing my sales team development systems. I will describe my team, hiring needs, and challenges. Build me a comprehensive sales team building strategy: (1) Hiring Profile & Interview Process — design ideal sales rep profile including must-have characteristics, experience requirements, and cultural fit criteria. Create structured interview process with specific questions assessing selling skills, coachability, resilience, and role fit. Include evaluation scorecard and decision framework. (2) Onboarding Program — design 30-60-90 day onboarding plan with clear milestones, learning objectives, and certification checkpoints at each phase. Include product training, sales methodology training, process training, and tool training. Specify shadowing requirements, mock demos, and ramp activities. (3) Training Curriculum — develop ongoing training program covering product knowledge, sales methodology reinforcement, competitive positioning, objection handling, and soft skills development. Include delivery method recommendations (instructor-led, self-paced, peer learning). (4) Coaching System — design manager coaching approach including one-on-one cadence, call review process, deal coaching frameworks, and skill development plans. Include coaching conversation guides and skill assessment rubrics. (5) Performance Management — define performance metrics (activity, pipeline, results), performance review cadence, and improvement plan process for underperforming reps. Include frameworks for difficult conversations and when to exit low performers. (6) Sales Culture & Team Building — recommend team building activities, motivational systems, career development paths, and culture-building practices that drive engagement and retention. (7) Give me a 90-day talent roadmap with hiring timeline, training program rollout, coaching implementation schedule, and team development milestones. Include recommended hiring sequence and team scaling approach. My sales team context: [DESCRIBE YOUR CURRENT TEAM SIZE/STRUCTURE, ROLES, HIRING NEEDS, ONBOARDING/TRAINING PROGRAMS, COACHING APPROACH, PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT, AND CHALLENGES]
