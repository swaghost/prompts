# Forecasting & RevOps Expert

## Purpose

Build revenue operations infrastructure and forecasting systems by implementing CRM architecture, designing pipeline management processes, creating data-driven forecasting models, and establishing sales analytics that drive predictable revenue achievement.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your CRM system and current configuration, sales process and pipeline stages, sales team structure and quotas, current forecasting approach and accuracy, key sales metrics and reporting, and RevOps challenges or optimization goals.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A revenue operations framework containing CRM architecture optimization including pipeline management, stage definitions, and data hygiene protocols, forecast methodology design with multiple forecast categories (commit, best case, pipeline) and accuracy tracking, sales metrics dashboard defining KPIs across activity, pipeline, and results dimensions, pipeline management best practices including deal review processes and inspection cadence, territory planning and quota allocation methodology, sales compensation plan analysis and recommendations, and 90-day RevOps roadmap prioritizing system implementations, process changes, and reporting buildout.

## Usage

Perfect for building revenue operations infrastructure from scratch, optimizing existing RevOps systems and processes, improving forecast accuracy and pipeline visibility, and implementing data-driven sales management. Ideal for scaling sales organizations, companies implementing or optimizing Salesforce/HubSpot, leadership teams requiring better pipeline visibility, and any sales operation needing systematic revenue predictability. This prompt excels at applying Revenue Operations expertise building forecasting and CRM systems.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your CRM system and current setup
- Sales process and pipeline stage definitions
- Sales team structure, quotas, and territories
- Current forecasting approach and accuracy levels
- Key sales metrics you track or want to track
- RevOps challenges or optimization goals

## Prompt

Act as a Revenue Operations director and sales analytics expert who has built forecasting and CRM systems that drive predictable revenue achievement. I need help designing or optimizing my RevOps infrastructure and forecasting. I will describe my systems, processes, and challenges. Build me a comprehensive RevOps strategy: (1) CRM Architecture Optimization — audit my current CRM setup and recommend improvements to pipeline management, stage definitions, required fields, validation rules, and automation. Include data hygiene protocols and governance policies. (2) Forecast Methodology Design — design forecasting process with multiple categories (Commit, Best Case, Pipeline) and clear criteria for each. Include forecast call structure, manager override protocols, and accuracy tracking methodology. Define what makes opportunities forecastable at each stage. (3) Sales Metrics Dashboard — identify the KPIs I should track across three dimensions: Activity Metrics (calls, meetings, emails), Pipeline Metrics (pipeline generation, pipeline velocity, stage conversion, deal age), and Results Metrics (bookings, win rate, ACV, quota attainment). Design reporting cadence and visualization approach. (4) Pipeline Management Best Practices — design deal review process and inspection cadence ensuring pipeline quality. Include deal qualification criteria, stage progression requirements, and red flags indicating at-risk deals. (5) Territory Planning & Quota Allocation — recommend territory design methodology and quota allocation approach balancing top-down company goals with bottom-up capacity planning. Include account assignment strategy. (6) Sales Compensation Plan Analysis — analyze current comp plan (if exists) or recommend comp structure that incentivizes desired behaviors. Include OTE mix, accelerators, SPIFs, and payment timing. (7) Give me a 90-day RevOps roadmap prioritizing CRM optimizations, process implementations, reporting buildout, and team training. Include quick wins and strategic initiatives. My RevOps context: [DESCRIBE YOUR CRM SYSTEM, SALES PROCESS, PIPELINE STAGES, TEAM STRUCTURE, QUOTAS, CURRENT FORECASTING APPROACH, KEY METRICS, AND CHALLENGES]
