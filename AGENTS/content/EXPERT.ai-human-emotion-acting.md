# Expert Guide: AI Human Emotion & Acting Performance

## Overview

This guide documents advanced techniques for generating ultra-realistic emotional acting performances using AI video generation. The focus is on micro-expressions, physiological responses, and nuanced emotional transitions that create believable human performances.

## Core Principles

### 1. Micro-Expression Control

**Extreme Muscle Control:**

- Facial muscles should "relax" within 0.1 seconds, leaving no trace
- Transitions must be imperceptible yet emotionally impactful
- Focus on millimeter-level detail (e.g., "1mm curve at the corner of the mouth")

**Eye Acting:**

- Pupil contraction and dilation convey emotional states
- Brightness at the bottom of eyes can rise by 0.5 degrees
- Iris focus can blur for 0.3 seconds during emotional breakdown
- Eyelash trembling frequency (e.g., once per second) indicates vulnerability
- Upper eyelid movement covering 35% of iris shows defensive introspection

**Lip Details:**

- Uncontrolled twitching of upper lip indicates suppressed emotion
- Pursing lips is instinctive reaction to suppress crying or grievance
- Lower teeth gently rubbing against lower lip creates 0.5mm indentation
- Corners of mouth tightening downward shows fighting back tears

### 2. Physiological Responses

**Flushing:**

- Faint redness near tear gland of lower eyelid is peak manifestation of grievance
- Physiological responses must be subtle but detectable

**Breathing:**

- Natural human breathing rise and fall
- Short and unsteady breathing during emotional distress
- Deep breaths with trembling eyelids during internal struggle

**Trembling:**

- Hand shakes causing gun barrel to wobble gently
- Adam's apple swallowing indicates suppressed speech
- Chin trembling shows emotional vulnerability
- Arm trembling faintly shows through held objects

### 3. Gaze and Eye Movement

**Directional Shifts:**

- Gaze hesitatingly drifts before forcing itself back to lock on target
- Eyes suddenly tremble with pupils slightly contracting
- Defocusing and distraction indicate inner defense line has taken hit
- Lowered gaze with upper eyelid covering iris shows defensiveness

**Emotional States Through Eyes:**

- Cold and restrained gaze with slightly clenched jaw
- Hint of pain rising in the eyes
- Eyes becoming slightly glistening with tears
- Eyes half-closed leaving just a slit pushes vulnerability to maximum

### 4. Emotional Arc Structure

**Phase 1: Hidden Restraint (Suppressed Secret Joy)**

- Smiling muscles rapidly relax within 0.1 seconds
- Smile completely withdrawn, leaving only 1mm curve
- Shows character cannot or does not want others to notice emotion
- Pupils contract, brightness at bottom of eyes rises 0.5 degrees
- "Eye lighting effect" - there is light, but it is restrained

**Phase 2: Instinctive Breakdown (Instant Surge of Grievance)**

- Physiological flushing near tear glands
- Iris focus blurs for 0.3 seconds
- Gaze loses focus - brief flash of panic and helplessness
- Uncontrolled upper lip twitching followed by pursing
- Classic instinctive human reaction to suppress crying

**Phase 3: Self-Healing Detachment (Sinking into Deep Thought)**

- Gaze lowers, upper eyelid covers 35% of iris
- Most typical "defensive" and "introspective" lowering of head
- Eyelashes tremble once per second
- Eyes half-closed, pushing vulnerability to absolute maximum
- Lower teeth gently rub against lower lip (0.5mm indentation)

## Key Principles for AI Acting Direction

### Restraint Over Exaggeration

- Emotions should be subtle and barely detectable
- Millimeter-level precision in facial movements
- 0.1-0.3 second timing for muscle transitions

### Physiological Authenticity

- Include natural involuntary responses (trembling, flushing, swallowing)
- Breathing patterns reflect emotional state
- Micro-movements show internal struggle

### Layered Emotional States

- Characters can have conflicting emotions simultaneously
- Secret emotions hidden from some while revealed to camera
- Internal struggle manifested through competing physical reactions

### Camera and Framing

- Extreme close-ups capture micro-expressions
- Shallow depth of field isolates subject
- Handheld feel adds realism
- Focus entirely on facial details

### Timing Precision

- Specify exact second ranges for emotional transitions
- Note duration of specific micro-expressions (0.1s, 0.3s)
- Build emotional arcs across 10-15 second segments

## Application Guidelines

### When to Use These Techniques

- Character-driven emotional scenes

- Pupil dilation/contraction

- Breathing patterns
- Involuntary trembling or flushing
- Object interactions (if present)
- Environmental details (wind, lighting)

## Measurement Precision Guide

**Facial Movements:**

- 1mm curve = barely perceptible smile
- 0.5mm indentation = gentle lip bite
- 0.1 seconds = imperceptible muscle transition
- 0.3 seconds = brief focus blur
- 0.5 degrees = subtle eye brightness increase
- 35% iris coverage = introspective lowered gaze

**Frequencies:**

- Once per second eyelash trembling = extreme vulnerability
- Continuous trembling = ongoing emotional distress
