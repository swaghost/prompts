## Comment Psychology Framework

### Why People Comment

**Primary Comment Motivations:**

1. **Agreement/Disagreement:** Strong opinion triggered
2. **Personal Experience:** "This happened to me too"
3. **Question/Curiosity:** Seeking clarification
4. **Correction:** "Actually, that's not quite right..."
5. **Contribution:** Adding to conversation
6. **Social Connection:** Building relationship
7. **Visibility:** Hoping for creator reply/engagement
8. **Validation:** Seeking affirmation

**Emotional States That Drive Comments:**

- Strong agreement ("YES! Exactly!")
- Frustration ("Finally someone said it!")
- Disagreement ("I actually think...")
- Curiosity ("But what about...?")
- Excitement ("This is amazing!")
- Relief ("I thought I was the only one")
- Inspiration ("I'm going to try this")

## Comment-Driving Strategies

### Strategy 1: The Direct Question Hook

**Technique:** Ask specific, polarizing, or thought-provoking question

**Question Types:**

**A. This or That:**

- "Coffee or tea for productivity?"
- "Morning workout or evening workout?"
- "Save money or invest it?"

**B. Ranking Challenge:**

- "Rate this [1-10]"
- "Which is worse: [X] or [Y]?"
- "Best to worst: [list of options]"

**C. Personal Experience:**

- "What's your biggest [topic] mistake?"
- "When did you realize [insight]?"
- "Drop a 🔥 if you've experienced this"

**D. Prediction/Guess:**

- "Guess what happened next..."
- "How much do you think this cost?"
- "What would you do in this situation?"

**E. Opinion Poll:**

- "Hot take: [controversial opinion]. Agree or disagree?"
- "Unpopular opinion: [statement]. Change my mind."
- "Is [X] overrated? Let's debate."

**Implementation:**

- Place question at END of caption (after value delivered)
- Use engaging question format (not boring)
- Make it easy to answer (not essay required)
- Prime answer examples in comments yourself

---

### Strategy 2: The Controversial Angle

**Technique:** Take contrarian or polarizing stance that triggers reaction

**Controversy Frameworks:**

**A. Challenge Popular Belief:**

- "Everyone says [X], but here's why that's wrong..."
- "Unpopular opinion: [X] is actually bad for you"
- "Stop doing [popular thing]. Here's what works instead"

**B. "Am I Wrong?" Format:**

- "[Controversial statement]. Tell me I'm wrong."
- "I think [unpopular opinion]. Roast me in comments."
- "Hot take that'll get me cancelled: [statement]"

**C. Tier List/Ranking:**

- "Ranking [popular things] from best to worst"
- "[Thing] is S-tier, [other thing] is D-tier. Fight me."
- "Controversial tier list of [category]"

**D. "I Don't Get The Hype":**

- "Why is everyone obsessed with [popular thing]?"
- "I tried [trend] for [time] and here's my honest opinion..."
- "[Popular thing] is overrated. Here's why"

**Controversy Guardrails:**

- Relevant to niche (not just clickbait)
- Defensible position (not baseless)
- Invites debate (not offense)
- Adds value (not just rage bait)

---

### Strategy 3: The Fill-in-the-Blank

**Technique:** Create interactive sentence completion

**Formats:**

**A. Finish the Sentence:**

- "My biggest [topic] fail was \_\_\_\_"
- "I wish someone told me \_\_\_\_ before starting"
- "The moment I realized [topic] changed my life: \_\_\_\_"

**B. One-Word Answer:**

- "Describe [topic] in ONE word 👇"
- "Your biggest [topic] struggle in one word"
- "First word that comes to mind when you see [X]:"

**C. Emoji Response:**

- "React with emoji: How does [topic] make you feel?"
- "🔥 if you agree, 💀 if you disagree"
- "Which emoji represents your [situation]?"

**D. Caption This:**

- Share image/video and "Caption this 👇"
- "Wrong answers only: What's happening here?"
- "Give this [thing] a funny name"

**Why It Works:**

- Low effort to respond (quick answer)
- Fun/playful interaction
- No wrong answer (low pressure)
- Creates community conversation thread

---

### Strategy 4: The Vulnerable Share

**Technique:** Share personal struggle/failure to invite reciprocal sharing

**Vulnerability Frameworks:**

**A. Confession Format:**

- "I'll go first: My embarrassing [topic] moment was..."
- "Something I'm not proud of: [admission]"
- "I used to think [wrong belief]. Then [realization]..."

**B. "You're Not Alone" Format:**

- "If you've ever felt [relatable struggle], you're not alone"
- "Raise your hand if you've [common mistake] 🙋"
- "Please tell me I'm not the only one who [relatable thing]"

**C. Failure Story:**

- "I failed at [X] before I succeeded. Here's what went wrong..."
- "The mistake that cost me [consequence]"
- "My rock bottom moment with [topic]"

**D. Growth Journey:**

- "Where I started vs. where I am now with [topic]"
- "What I wish I could tell myself 1 year ago"
- "The one thing that changed everything for me"

**Why It Works:**

- Creates emotional safety
- Invites reciprocal vulnerability
- Builds authentic connection
- "Me too!" comments flood in

---

### Strategy 5: The Call-to-Action (CTA)

**Technique:** Explicitly tell audience what to comment

**CTA Frameworks:**

**A. Instructional CTA:**

- "Comment [X] and I'll send you [resource]"
- "Type [word] if you want the full guide"
- "Reply with [emoji] and I'll share the link"

**B. Value Exchange CTA:**

- "Comment your biggest question and I'll answer"
- "Share your [situation] and I'll give you personalized advice"
- "Tell me your [challenge] and I'll recommend [solution]"

**C. Community Building CTA:**

- "Tag someone who needs to see this"
- "Who else struggles with this? 👇"
- "Let's support each other - share your experience"

**D. Engagement Prompt CTA:**

- "Double-tap if you agree, comment if you disagree"
- "Save this + comment SAVED so I know to make more"
- "Comment [X] if you want Part 2"

**E. Algorithmic CTA:**

- "Comment your favorite tip from this list"
- "Which number resonated most? (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5?)"
- "Reply with the timestamp that hit different"

**CTA Placement:**

- At END of caption (after value delivered)
- In text overlay on video (visual reminder)
- Verbal CTA in video/audio
- Pin comment with CTA reminder

## Content Structure for Comments

### Optimal Comment-Driving Structure

**1. Hook (First 1-3 seconds/sentences)**

- Grab attention
- Create curiosity or strong emotion

**2. Value Delivery (Main Content)**

- Deliver promised value
- Build trust and credibility
- Create "this is valuable" feeling

**3. Relatable Element (Mid-Content)**

- "You might be thinking..."
- "I used to struggle with this too"
- "Sound familiar?"

**4. Engagement Trigger (Near End)**

- Controversial statement
- Thought-provoking question
- Personal experience invitation

**5. Direct CTA (Final Statement)**

- Explicit instruction to comment
- Easy response format
- Reason to engage

## Testing & Optimization

### Comment Strategy Testing

**Test Variables:**

1. CTA placement (beginning vs. end)
2. Question type (open vs. this-or-that)
3. Controversy level (safe vs. polarizing)
4. CTA specificity (vague vs. exact instruction)
5. Value-first vs. engagement-first approach

**Metrics to Track:**

- Comment count (quantity)
- Comment depth (quality/length)
- Reply rate (how many you respond to)
- Comment-to-view ratio
- Conversation threads (multi-reply chains)

**A/B Test Example:**

- Post A: "What do you think about [topic]?" (generic)
- Post B: "Hot take: [opinion]. Agree or disagree?" (specific)
- Compare comment volume and quality

## Quick Comment-Driving Tweaks

### Tweak 1: End with Specific Question

**Before:** "Hope this helps!"
**After:** "Which of these 5 tips will you try first? Drop the number below 👇"
**Why:** Specific answer = easier response = more comments

### Tweak 2: Add Controversial Statement

**Before:** "Here are 5 productivity tips"
**After:** "Unpopular opinion: Waking up at 5am is overrated. Here's what actually works..."
**Why:** Controversy = strong opinions = comments

### Tweak 3: Invite Personal Experience

**Before:** "This strategy works well"
**After:** "This strategy changed my [result]. What's YOUR biggest [topic] struggle? Let's solve it together"
**Why:** Personalization = shares personal stories = comments

### Tweak 4: Create Fill-in-Blank

**Before:** "Productivity is important"
**After:** "Finish the sentence: My productivity goes to zero when \_\_\_\_"
**Why:** Completion format = fun + easy = comments

### Tweak 5: Use Comment Bait CTA

**Before:** No CTA at end
**After:** "Comment SEND and I'll DM you the full template"
**Why:** Exchange value = incentive = comments

## Common Comment-Killing Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No Clear CTA

❌ Ending with "Thanks for watching!"
✅ "Which tip surprised you most? Let me know below!"

### Mistake 2: Too Generic Question

❌ "What do you think?"
✅ "Hot take: [specific opinion]. Agree or am I wrong?"

### Mistake 3: No Engagement Trigger

❌ Pure value delivery with no invitation to discuss
✅ Value + relatable element + discussion prompt

### Mistake 4: Never Replying to Comments

❌ Post and ghost (kills future engagement)
✅ Reply to comments (especially first hour)

### Mistake 5: Asking Yes/No Questions

❌ "Do you agree?" (limits response)
✅ "What's your experience with this?" (opens conversation)
