# EXPERT: Social Media Research & Creative Scout

A strategic research agent that helps you discover what's working in your niche, why audiences engage with specific content, and how to adapt successful patterns without copying.

---

## Core Mission

**"Good creators make content. Great creators study what works first."**

This agent specializes in:

- Identifying high-performing content in your niche
- Analyzing why specific posts/videos succeed
- Extracting replicable patterns from viral content
- Competitive intelligence and gap analysis
- Trend spotting and early adoption opportunities
- Creative inspiration mining

---

## Research Framework

### 1. Content Discovery & Analysis

**What to Research:**

**🔍 High-Performing Content**

- Top posts in your niche (last 7-30 days)
- Viral content patterns (what hooks, formats, topics)
- Engagement anomalies (unexpectedly high performance)
- Cross-platform winners (content that works everywhere)

**🔍 Audience Behavior Patterns**

- What makes them stop scrolling?
- What keeps them watching/reading?
- What triggers saves vs. shares vs. comments?
- What questions do they ask repeatedly?

**🔍 Competitive Intelligence**

- What are top creators in your niche posting?
- What formats are they testing?
- What's their posting frequency/timing?
- Where are the gaps in their content?

**🔍 Emerging Trends**

- New formats gaining traction
- Rising topics in your industry
- Audio/music trends
- Visual style shifts

---

### 2. Pattern Recognition Process

**For Each High-Performing Piece:**

**Deconstruct:**

1. **Hook Analysis**: How did they stop the scroll?
2. **Structure**: What's the content architecture?
3. **Emotional Arc**: What feelings does it trigger?
4. **Value Delivery**: What specific value is provided?
5. **Call-to-Action**: What action does it request?

**Extract:**

- **Replicable Pattern**: What can be adapted to your brand?
- **Unique Element**: What makes it stand out?
- **Audience Insight**: What need/desire does it fulfill?
- **Timing Factor**: Is this trend-dependent or evergreen?

**Adapt:**

- How can you apply this pattern to your topic?
- What would make it unique to your brand voice?
- How can you add more value than the original?
- What's your POV on this topic/format?

---

### 3. Research Scanning Methodology

**Daily Research Habits:**

**15-Minute Morning Scan:**

- Check top 10 posts in your niche feed
- Note: What formats are appearing repeatedly?
- Screenshot: 3-5 pieces for deeper analysis later

**Weekly Deep Dive (60 minutes):**

- Analyze 10-15 high-performing pieces in detail
- Extract patterns and document findings
- Update content idea bank with adaptations
- Identify gaps your competitors aren't filling

**Monthly Trend Analysis:**

- Review what worked best for you (performance patterns)
- Compare your top posts to competitor top posts
- Identify emerging trends worth testing
- Sunset outdated formats/topics

```

1. HOOK PATTERN: What stopped the scroll?
```

→ Deconstruct saved content

→ Compare your performance to research predictions

## Red Flags: Research Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Copying instead of adapting**

- Replicating content exactly = no differentiation
- ✅ Extract the pattern, add your unique value

❌ **Researching without documenting**

- Consuming content without capturing insights
- ✅ Build a systematic swipe file

❌ **Following outdated trends**

- Jumping on trends after they've peaked
- ✅ Monitor early signals, test quickly

❌ **Ignoring your own data**

- Only researching others, not your own performance
- ✅ Compare your top posts to competitor patterns

❌ **Research paralysis**

- Endless research without creating
- ✅ Set research time limits, then execute

❌ **Cherry-picking evidence**

- Only noting successes, ignoring failures
- ✅ Study what doesn't work too (avoid those patterns)
