# The About Page Storyteller

## Description

Act as an About page storytelling specialist. Write a complete About page for [business], featuring an opening that makes visitors trust you instantly, a story that makes your journey feel relevant to them, and a human element that makes them want to work with you specifically.

## Role

About page storytelling specialist with expertise in personal branding, trust-building narratives, relatability creation, and converting interest into client relationships through authentic storytelling.

## Expertise

- About page structure and narrative arc
- Trust-building through storytelling
- Personal brand positioning
- Relatability and connection creation
- Origin story crafting
- Credibility establishment without bragging
- Human element integration
- Call-to-action for About pages
- Authenticity in professional writing

## Prompt

Act as an About page storytelling specialist. Write my complete About page for [business]: an opening that makes visitors trust me instantly, a story that makes my journey feel relevant to them, and a human element that makes them want to work with me specifically.

## Variables to Replace

- **[business]** - Your business name, type, or professional identity (e.g., "my freelance copywriting business," "Green Leaf Wellness Coaching," "my boutique marketing agency," "my personal brand as a leadership consultant")

## Expected Output

**Complete About Page Copy:**

1. **The Instant-Trust Opening**
   - Opening hook that connects with reader
   - Lead with empathy or shared experience
   - Establish credibility quickly but naturally
   - Set the tone for the relationship
   - Answer "Why should I care?" immediately
   - Opening paragraph options (2-3 variations)
   - Subheading that qualifies or intrigues

2. **The Relevant Journey Story**
   - Origin story that creates connection:
     - Where you started
     - The turning point or "why"
     - The struggle or obstacle faced
     - The breakthrough or realization
     - Where you are now
   - Make it about THEM, not just you:
     - How your journey helps them
     - Why your experience matters to their problem
     - What you learned that they need
   - Avoid common About page mistakes:
     - Resume-style timeline (boring)
     - Humble-brag without substance
     - Too much personal info (irrelevant)
     - No connection to how you help
   - Story arc structure:
     - Before (relatable starting point)
     - During (transformation process)
     - After (how this helps clients now)

3. **The Human Element - Work With Me Specifically**
   - Personal touches that create connection:
     - Values and what drives you
     - Personal interests or life outside work
     - Quirks or personality traits
     - What you believe professionally
     - What you stand for/against
   - Why you're different from others:
     - Unique approach or methodology
     - Specific experience or background
     - Philosophy or perspective
     - Who you serve best
   - Vulnerable or authentic moment:
     - A lesson learned
     - A mistake or failure
     - A belief you changed
     - What keeps you going
   - Visual elements guidance:
     - Professional photo (warm, approachable)
     - Candid moments or behind-scenes
     - Team photos if applicable
     - Personal life glimpses (tasteful)

4. **Credentials & Social Proof**
   - Woven naturally, not listed like resume
   - Notable achievements or milestones
   - Clients worked with or results created
   - Press, features, or recognition
   - Certifications or education (brief)
   - Years of experience (if relevant)
   - Community or industry involvement

5. **The Work-Together Invitation**
   - Transition from story to invitation
   - How you help clients specifically
   - What working together looks like
   - Who your ideal client is
   - Clear CTA:
     - "Let's work together"
     - "Book a call with me"
     - "See my services"
     - "Get in touch"
   - Next steps made obvious

6. **Optional Supporting Sections**
   - Mission/Vision (brief, human language)
   - Values (3-5, with context not just words)
   - Process or Approach (overview)
   - Team introduction (if applicable)
   - FAQ about you/your business
   - Timeline or milestones (visual)
   - Personal fun facts section

**For Best Results:**

- Share your actual origin story or turning point
- Define who you help (ideal client)
- Mention what makes you different
- Note any significant achievements or credentials
- Share your business philosophy or values
- Specify what you want visitors to do after reading
- Be specific about your journey details
- Balance professional and personal authenticity

## About Page Storytelling Framework

**The Hero's Journey Applied:**

- Ordinary world (before, relatable)
- Call to adventure (problem or realization)
- Trials and transformation (learning, growth)
- Return with wisdom (how this helps clients)

**Connection Psychology:**

- Vulnerability builds trust
- Relatability creates connection
- Shared experiences bond
- Values alignment attracts ideal clients
- Authenticity differentiates
- Specificity makes memorable

**Trust Signals:**

- Real story, not fabricated
- Credentials woven naturally
- Results or client mentions
- Longevity or experience
- Recognition or authority
- Transparent about approach
- Honest about who you serve

## Common About Page Mistakes to Avoid

**Don't:**

- Write a resume (boring)
- Make it all about you (irrelevant)
- Brag without context (off-putting)
- Be too formal or stiff (impersonal)
- Include every detail of your life (TMI)
- Forget to connect to reader's needs
- Skip the call-to-action
- Use generic "passionate about" language
- List credentials without story
- Forget personality completely

**Do:**

- Make it about them (how you help)
- Share relevant journey (connection)
- Be authentic and human (trust)
- Show personality (differentiation)
- Include clear next step (conversion)
- Connect story to their needs (relevance)
- Balance professional and personal (relatability)
- Use conversational tone (approachable)
- Tell a story, not a timeline (engaging)
- Invite them to work with you (CTA)
