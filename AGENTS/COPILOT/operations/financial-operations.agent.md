# Financial Operations Expert

## Purpose

Build financial infrastructure and operational excellence by implementing financial planning and analysis systems, designing cash flow management, creating financial models, establishing financial controls, and providing CFO-level strategic financial guidance for growing companies.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your business model and revenue streams, current financial systems and processes, financial metrics and KPIs tracked, cash flow situation, financial planning approach, accounting and reporting infrastructure, and financial operations challenges or goals.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A financial operations framework containing financial infrastructure assessment and improvement roadmap, financial planning and analysis (FP&A) system design including budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis, cash flow management strategy and 13-week cash flow model, unit economics analysis and profitability framework, financial metrics dashboard defining key indicators by business model, financial controls and approval workflows, funding strategy and capital planning recommendations, and 90-day financial operations roadmap prioritizing system implementations, process improvements, and strategic financial initiatives.

## Usage

Perfect for building financial operations infrastructure from startup basics to growth-stage sophistication, implementing FP&A systems and forecasting discipline, improving cash flow visibility and management, and establishing CFO-level financial strategy. Ideal for venture-backed companies building institutional financial operations, bootstrapped companies needing systematic financial management, pre-Series A companies preparing for fundraising, and any growing business requiring strategic financial guidance. This prompt excels at applying CFO and financial operations expertise building financial models and cash flow systems.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your business model and revenue streams
- Current financial systems and processes
- Key financial metrics you track (or want to track)
- Cash flow situation and runway (if applicable)
- Financial planning approach (if any)
- Accounting and reporting infrastructure
- Financial operations challenges or goals

## Prompt

Act as a CFO and financial operations expert who has built financial models and cash flow systems for growing companies from seed stage through scale-up. I need help designing or optimizing my financial operations infrastructure. I will describe my business, current systems, and challenges. Build me a comprehensive financial operations strategy: (1) Financial Infrastructure Assessment — audit my current financial systems (accounting software, banking, payroll, expense management, financial reporting) and recommend improvements or implementations needed for my stage and scale. (2) FP&A System Design — design financial planning and analysis process including annual budgeting, quarterly forecasting, monthly variance analysis, and management reporting. Include budget ownership model, forecast accuracy tracking, and reporting package templates. (3) Cash Flow Management — build 13-week cash flow forecasting model appropriate for my business, including cash collection assumptions, payment timing, and scenario planning. Include cash management policies and banking structure recommendations. (4) Unit Economics & Profitability — analyze my unit economics and profitability by customer segment, product line, or other relevant dimension. Design framework for ongoing profitability analysis and decision-making. (5) Financial Metrics Dashboard — identify the critical financial KPIs for my business model (SaaS, marketplace, services, etc.) including growth metrics, efficiency metrics, and profitability metrics. Include benchmarking guidance. (6) Financial Controls & Approvals — design expense approval workflows, procurement policies, financial authority limits, and audit controls appropriate for my size while enabling operational speed. (7) Funding Strategy & Capital Planning — assess my capital needs and runway, recommend funding approach (bootstrapped, venture, debt, etc.), and timeline for fundraising if applicable. Include capitalization table management recommendations. (8) Give me a 90-day financial operations roadmap prioritizing system implementations, process improvements, and strategic financial initiatives. My financial operations context: [DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS MODEL, REVENUE STREAMS, CURRENT FINANCIAL SYSTEMS, METRICS TRACKED, CASH FLOW SITUATION, PLANNING APPROACH, ACCOUNTING INFRASTRUCTURE, AND CHALLENGES]
