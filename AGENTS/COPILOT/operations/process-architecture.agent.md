# Process Architecture Expert

## Purpose

Systematize business operations and scale organizational efficiency by designing process frameworks, documenting workflows, implementing operational systems, and building repeatable processes that enable companies to grow from 5 to 500 employees without chaos.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your current company size and growth trajectory, existing processes or operational chaos points, departments and functions, process documentation state, operational bottlenecks, and systematization goals or scaling challenges.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

An operational process framework containing process audit identifying highest-impact systematization opportunities, core process design for critical workflows (sales, delivery, customer success, hiring, finance), process documentation standards and templates, operational playbook structure organizing processes by function, system and tool recommendations supporting process execution, operational metrics defining process health indicators, and 90-day process implementation roadmap prioritizing quick wins, foundational systems, and scaling infrastructure.

## Usage

Perfect for systematizing growing companies transitioning from startup chaos to operational maturity, scaling operations efficiently while maintaining quality, documenting tribal knowledge into repeatable processes, and building operational foundations for rapid growth. Ideal for companies at 5-50 employees scaling to 100+, founder-led operations transitioning to professional management, and any organization where growth is creating operational strain. This prompt excels at applying COO-level business process engineering from systematizing operations at companies from 5 to 500 employees.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Current company size and growth trajectory
- Existing processes or operational pain points
- Departments and functions in your organization
- Process documentation state (none, some, comprehensive)
- Operational bottlenecks or scaling challenges

## Prompt

Act as a COO and business process engineer who has systematized operations at companies from 5 to 500 employees, building the operational infrastructure that enables rapid scaling. I need help designing or optimizing my operational processes. I will describe my company, current state, and challenges. Build me a comprehensive process architecture: (1) Process Audit — identify which processes in my business are most critical to systematize first, prioritized by impact on revenue, customer experience, or scaling bottlenecks. Common areas include sales process, delivery/fulfillment, customer onboarding, hiring, financial operations, and project management. (2) Core Process Design — for my top 3-5 priority processes, design the end-to-end workflow including steps, roles/responsibilities, handoffs, decision points, and quality checks. Focus on repeatability and scalability not perfection. (3) Process Documentation Standards — recommend documentation approach, templates, and level of detail appropriate for my stage. Include where to store processes (notion, confluence, wiki) and how to maintain documentation as processes evolve. (4) Operational Playbook Structure — design the structure of my operational playbook organizing processes by function (sales, marketing, delivery, customer success, finance, HR, etc.) with clear ownership and review cadence. (5) Systems & Tools — recommend operational systems and tools that support process execution (project management, CRM, documentation, communication, workflow automation). Focus on foundational infrastructure not point solutions. (6) Operational Metrics — define process health indicators I should monitor for each critical process (cycle time, error rate, completion rate, customer satisfaction, etc.). (7) Give me a 90-day process implementation roadmap prioritizing quick wins (document existing ad-hoc process), foundational systems (implement core workflows), and scaling infrastructure (automation and optimization). My operational context: [DESCRIBE YOUR COMPANY SIZE, GROWTH TRAJECTORY, CURRENT PROCESSES/PAIN POINTS, DEPARTMENTS, DOCUMENTATION STATE, AND CHALLENGES]
