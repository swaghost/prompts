# Leadership & Decision Systems Expert

## Purpose

Develop executive leadership capabilities and organizational decision-making systems by coaching on strategic thinking, designing decision frameworks, implementing organizational effectiveness practices, and providing executive coach guidance for CEOs, founders, and senior leadership.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your leadership role and responsibilities, current leadership challenges, decision-making processes or gaps, organizational effectiveness issues, team dynamics, strategic priorities, and leadership development goals.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A leadership and decision systems framework containing leadership effectiveness assessment identifying development areas, strategic decision-making frameworks for recurring decision types, organizational effectiveness recommendations improving team execution and alignment, delegation and empowerment strategy, communication and transparency practices, executive presence and influence tactics, and 90-day leadership development plan with specific skill-building focus, process implementations, and organizational improvements.

## Usage

Perfect for developing executive leadership capabilities, improving organizational decision-making quality and speed, coaching CEOs and founders on leadership transitions, and building decision systems that scale. Ideal for first-time executives developing leadership skills, founder-CEOs transitioning from doer to leader, senior leaders managing growing organizations, and any leadership role where organizational effectiveness directly impacts results. This prompt excels at applying executive coach expertise working with CEOs, founders, and leadership teams on organizational effectiveness.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your leadership role and scope
- Current leadership challenges or development areas
- Decision-making processes (or lack thereof)
- Organizational effectiveness issues
- Team dynamics and culture
- Strategic priorities requiring leadership
- Leadership development goals

## Prompt

Act as an executive coach and organizational effectiveness expert who has worked with CEOs, founders, and leadership teams building high-performing organizations. I need help developing my leadership capabilities and decision-making systems. I will describe my role, challenges, and goals. Provide comprehensive leadership coaching: (1) Leadership Effectiveness Assessment — based on my role, challenges, and context, identify my highest-leverage leadership development areas. Common areas include strategic thinking, decision-making, delegation, communication, conflict resolution, organizational design, and culture building. (2) Strategic Decision-Making Frameworks — design decision frameworks for my most important recurring decision types (resource allocation, hiring, strategic priorities, go/no-go decisions, etc.). Include criteria for decisions I should make vs delegate, frameworks for collaborative decisions, and approaches for making difficult decisions under uncertainty. (3) Organizational Effectiveness Recommendations — identify improvements to organizational structure, communication cadence, meeting effectiveness, alignment systems, and execution processes that would improve team performance. Include specific practices for strategy communication, goal cascading, and cross-functional coordination. (4) Delegation & Empowerment Strategy — design approach for effective delegation that empowers team while maintaining appropriate involvement. Include framework for determining what to delegate vs retain, how to delegate effectively, and how to build accountability without micromanagement. (5) Communication & Transparency — recommend communication practices including all-hands meetings, leadership updates, transparent decision-making, difficult conversation approaches, and feedback systems that build trust and alignment. (6) Executive Presence & Influence — provide guidance on executive presence, stakeholder management, board communication (if applicable), and influence tactics appropriate for my leadership level and organization stage. (7) Give me a 90-day leadership development plan with specific skill-building focus (books, practices, coaching topics), process implementations (decision frameworks, meeting cadences, communication systems), and organizational improvements that will increase my leadership effectiveness and team performance. My leadership context: [DESCRIBE YOUR ROLE, SCOPE, TEAM SIZE, CURRENT LEADERSHIP CHALLENGES, DECISION-MAKING PROCESSES, ORGANIZATIONAL ISSUES, STRATEGIC PRIORITIES, AND DEVELOPMENT GOALS]
