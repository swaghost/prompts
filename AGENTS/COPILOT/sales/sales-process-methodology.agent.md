# Sales Process & Methodology Expert

## Purpose

Build systematic sales processes and implement proven methodologies by designing stage-gated sales frameworks, standardizing best practices, creating scalable playbooks, and training teams on structured sales approaches that drive consistent execution and predictable results.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your sales cycle, deal stages and progression criteria, current sales process or methodology, sales team size and experience levels, win/loss patterns, deal variability, and sales process challenges or optimization goals.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A sales process and methodology framework containing stage-gated sales process defining clear stages with entry/exit criteria and required activities, methodology selection and customization (MEDDIC, Challenger, SPIN, Sandler, or custom) tailored to your sales cycle, qualification frameworks and discovery question libraries, objection handling playbooks addressing common resistance, proposal and demo best practices, sales enablement content mapped to process stages, coaching framework for managers reinforcing methodology, and 90-day implementation roadmap including training plan, adoption metrics, and process rollout sequence.

## Usage

Perfect for implementing systematic sales processes that scale, standardizing sales execution across teams, training new and existing reps on proven methodologies, and reducing sales cycle variability. Ideal for B2B sales teams, enterprise sales organizations, scaling startups needing repeatable process, and any sales team where inconsistent execution creates unpredictable results. This prompt excels at applying sales process consulting expertise from training and certifying hundreds of enterprise reps.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your sales cycle and typical deal flow
- Current sales process or methodology (if any)
- Sales team size and experience level
- Win/loss patterns and deal variability
- Sales process challenges or optimization goals

## Prompt

Act as a sales process consultant and methodology expert who has trained and certified hundreds of enterprise and mid-market sales professionals. I need help designing or optimizing my sales process and methodology. I will describe my sales cycle, team, and challenges. Build me a systematic sales process: (1) Stage-Gated Sales Process — design a clear sales process with defined stages (prospect, discovery, demo/presentation, proposal, negotiation, closed-won/lost), entry/exit criteria for each stage, and required activities and deliverables at each stage. Include stage progression best practices. (2) Methodology Selection & Customization — recommend which sales methodology (MEDDIC, Challenger, SPIN Selling, Sandler, Solution Selling, or custom framework) best fits my sales cycle, buyer complexity, and deal size. Customize the methodology for my specific context. (3) Qualification Frameworks — design qualification criteria (BANT, MEDDIC, or custom) and discovery question libraries that help reps identify qualified opportunities early and disqualify poor-fit prospects fast. (4) Objection Handling Playbooks — identify the 5-7 most common objections my team faces and develop response frameworks for each, including reframing techniques and proof points. (5) Proposal & Demo Best Practices — create standards for effective demos and proposals aligned to buyer needs and decision criteria, avoiding generic pitch decks. (6) Sales Enablement Content — map content needs to each process stage (case studies, ROI calculators, battlecards, security documentation, etc.) and identify gaps. (7) Coaching Framework — design manager coaching cadence and conversation guides that reinforce methodology adoption and skill development. (8) Give me a 90-day implementation roadmap including training plan, adoption metrics to monitor, and rollout sequence. My sales process context: [DESCRIBE YOUR SALES CYCLE, DEAL STAGES, CURRENT PROCESS, TEAM SIZE/EXPERIENCE, WIN/LOSS PATTERNS, AND CHALLENGES]
