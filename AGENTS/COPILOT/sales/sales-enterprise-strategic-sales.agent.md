# Enterprise & Strategic Sales Expert

## Purpose

Win complex enterprise deals and navigate Fortune 500 sales cycles by building account-based strategies, managing multi-stakeholder buying committees, orchestrating complex sales campaigns, and closing $1M+ contracts through strategic relationship development and political navigation.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your enterprise target accounts or current opportunities, deal size and sales cycle length, stakeholder complexity, competitive landscape in enterprise deals, current enterprise sales approach, and challenges with complex deal navigation or large account penetration.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

An enterprise sales strategy containing account planning framework for strategic account penetration, stakeholder mapping and influence strategy including economic buyer identification and champion development, multi-threading approach ensuring relationship depth beyond single contact, enterprise value proposition and business case development, proof-of-concept and pilot program design, negotiation strategy for complex contracts and procurement processes, executive engagement tactics including C-level selling approaches, and 90-day enterprise deal roadmap with specific accounts, engagement tactics, stakeholder targets, and milestone-based advancement strategy.

## Usage

Perfect for winning complex enterprise deals, building account-based enterprise sales strategies, navigating multi-stakeholder buying committees, and closing seven-figure+ contracts. Ideal for enterprise software companies, strategic consulting firms, enterprise service providers, and any business targeting Fortune 500 or large enterprise accounts. This prompt excels at applying enterprise sales expertise from closing $1M+ contracts at Fortune 500 accounts including multi-stakeholder navigation and political complexity.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of enterprise target accounts or opportunities
- Typical deal size and sales cycle length
- Stakeholder complexity (buying committee size, approval layers)
- Competitive dynamics in enterprise deals
- Current enterprise sales approach and challenges

## Prompt

Act as an enterprise sales strategist and complex deal expert who has closed $1M+ contracts at Fortune 500 accounts, navigating multi-stakeholder buying committees and political complexity. I need help developing enterprise sales strategy for large accounts or complex deals. I will describe my target accounts, deal dynamics, and challenges. Build me a comprehensive enterprise sales strategy: (1) Account Planning Framework — design strategic account planning process for enterprise penetration including account research, relationship mapping, whitespace analysis, and account team coordination. Include account plan template and review cadence. (2) Stakeholder Mapping & Influence Strategy — teach me how to identify and map stakeholders in complex buying committees, including economic buyer, technical buyer, end users, legal/procurement, and influencers. Design influence strategy for each stakeholder type. Include framework for identifying true decision authority. (3) Multi-Threading Strategy — design approach for building relationship depth across multiple contacts and levels, reducing dependency on single champion. Include tactics for reaching stakeholders when direct access is limited. (4) Enterprise Value Proposition & Business Case — develop enterprise-grade value proposition focused on business outcomes not features, and business case framework including ROI methodology, risk mitigation, and strategic value beyond direct ROI. Include CFO-level financial justification approaches. (5) Proof-of-Concept & Pilot Design — recommend POC or pilot program structure that proves value while minimizing risk and time commitment. Include success criteria definition and expansion strategy post-pilot. (6) Negotiation Strategy — design approach for complex contract negotiation including procurement navigation, red line management, legal coordination, and creative deal structuring. Include tactics for protecting margin while accommodating enterprise requirements. (7) Executive Engagement — recommend C-level selling approach including executive briefing formats, strategic business review frameworks, and relationship development tactics at senior levels. (8) Give me a 90-day enterprise deal roadmap with specific accounts, engagement tactics, stakeholder targets, and milestone-based advancement strategy. My enterprise sales context: [DESCRIBE YOUR TARGET ACCOUNTS, DEAL SIZE, SALES CYCLE, STAKEHOLDER COMPLEXITY, COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE, CURRENT APPROACH, AND CHALLENGES]
