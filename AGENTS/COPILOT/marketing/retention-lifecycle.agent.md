# Retention & Lifecycle Marketing Expert

## Purpose

Build systematic customer retention and lifecycle marketing programs by reducing churn, increasing customer lifetime value, implementing data-driven engagement and nurture systems, and creating expansion and advocacy programs that maximize revenue from existing customers.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your customer lifecycle stages, current retention metrics (churn rate, retention curves, NRR/GRR), customer engagement data, expansion or upsell opportunities, known churn drivers or at-risk indicators, and lifecycle marketing efforts or gaps.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A retention and lifecycle strategy containing churn reduction roadmap identifying at-risk indicators and intervention tactics, customer onboarding optimization improving time-to-value and activation rates, engagement and nurture programs mapped to lifecycle stages, expansion and upsell strategy with targeting criteria and campaign design, customer advocacy and referral program framework, retention metrics dashboard defining KPIs and monitoring systems, and 90-day lifecycle marketing roadmap prioritizing initiatives by impact on LTV and churn reduction.

## Usage

Perfect for building systematic retention programs that reduce churn, optimizing customer lifecycle marketing, increasing expansion revenue and LTV, and creating data-driven engagement systems. Ideal for subscription businesses, SaaS companies, membership programs, B2B service businesses, and any recurring revenue model where retention drives economics. This prompt excels at applying customer lifecycle expertise specializing in churn reduction and LTV expansion.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your customer lifecycle stages
- Current retention metrics (churn rate, cohort retention, NRR)
- Customer engagement data or behavior insights
- Known churn drivers or expansion opportunities
- Lifecycle marketing goals or problem areas

## Prompt

Act as a customer lifecycle marketing expert and retention strategist who specializes in reducing churn and maximizing customer lifetime value. I need help designing or optimizing my retention and lifecycle programs. I will describe my customer lifecycle, retention metrics, and challenges. Build me a comprehensive retention strategy: (1) Churn Reduction Roadmap — analyze my churn patterns and identify at-risk indicators (usage drops, support tickets, billing issues, engagement declines). Design intervention tactics for each churn driver, including predictive outreach, win-back campaigns, and reactive save programs. (2) Onboarding Optimization — design customer onboarding experience that accelerates time-to-value, improves activation rates, and reduces early-stage churn. Include specific milestones, messaging, and success metrics. (3) Engagement & Nurture Programs — map lifecycle stages and design engagement programs for each (onboarding, adoption, expansion-ready, at-risk, dormant). Include email nurture sequences, in-app messaging, and success team touchpoints. (4) Expansion & Upsell Strategy — identify expansion opportunities (feature adoption, usage thresholds, team growth) and design targeting criteria, campaign messaging, and sales handoff for upgrade opportunities. (5) Customer Advocacy & Referral Program — design systems for converting satisfied customers into advocates, including review generation, referral programs, case study development, and community building. (6) Retention Metrics Dashboard — define the KPIs I should monitor (cohort retention curves, NRR/GRR, engagement scores, health scores, expansion rates) and cadence for review. (7) Give me a 90-day lifecycle marketing roadmap prioritizing initiatives by impact on LTV, churn reduction, and expansion revenue. My retention and lifecycle context: [DESCRIBE YOUR CUSTOMER LIFECYCLE, RETENTION METRICS, CHURN DRIVERS, ENGAGEMENT DATA, AND GOALS]
