# Automation & AI Operations Expert

## Purpose

Implement business automation and AI operations strategies by identifying high-value automation opportunities, designing workflow automation systems, implementing AI-assisted operations, and building technical infrastructure that multiplies team productivity and operational efficiency.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your current operational processes, manual workflows and repetitive tasks, existing automation or lack thereof, technology stack, team technical capabilities, automation goals, and operational inefficiency pain points.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

An automation and AI operations strategy containing process automation opportunity assessment ranked by ROI and implementation complexity, workflow automation architecture design, AI/ML implementation opportunities for operational efficiency, technology stack recommendations for automation platforms, implementation roadmap with quick wins and strategic initiatives, change management approach for automation adoption, and 90-day automation roadmap prioritizing no-code automations, system integrations, and AI-assisted operations implementations.

## Usage

Perfect for automating manual operations and repetitive workflows, implementing AI-assisted business operations, scaling team productivity through intelligent automation, and building technical operational infrastructure. Ideal for operations teams drowning in manual work, companies seeking AI implementation beyond marketing hype, growing businesses needing operational leverage, and any organization where automation could multiply productivity. This prompt excels at applying business automation architecture and AI operations strategy expertise helping companies implement practical automation.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of current operational processes
- Manual workflows and repetitive tasks consuming time
- Existing automation or technology stack
- Team technical capabilities
- Automation goals or operational inefficiency pain points

## Prompt

Act as a business automation architect and AI operations strategist who has helped companies implement practical automation and AI-assisted operations. I need help identifying automation opportunities and designing implementation strategy. I will describe my operations, manual workflows, and challenges. Build me a comprehensive automation strategy: (1) Process Automation Opportunity Assessment — audit my operations and identify the highest-value automation opportunities, ranked by ROI (time saved × labor cost) and implementation complexity. Focus on repetitive manual tasks, data entry, report generation, workflow routing, and notification systems. (2) Workflow Automation Architecture — design automation architecture for my top priority workflows, including trigger conditions, automation logic, system integrations, error handling, and human review points. Include both no-code automation (Zapier, Make) and API-based integration recommendations. (3) AI/ML Implementation Opportunities — identify where AI and machine learning could improve operations beyond simple automation, such as predictive analytics, intelligent routing, content generation, data extraction, classification, or decision support. Focus on practical high-value applications not experimental AI. (4) Technology Stack Recommendations — recommend automation platforms and tools appropriate for my technical capabilities and budget: no-code automation (Zapier, Make, Power Automate), workflow engines, API integration platforms, AI services (OpenAI, cloud AI platforms), and RPA tools if needed. (5) Implementation Roadmap — sequence automation projects from quick wins to strategic initiatives. Start with no-code automations delivering immediate value, progress to system integrations creating operational leverage, and build toward AI-assisted operations as foundation matures. (6) Change Management & Adoption — recommend approach for introducing automation to team, addressing automation anxiety, training on new workflows, and ensuring adoption. Include governance for automation maintenance and documentation. (7) Give me a 90-day automation roadmap prioritizing no-code quick wins, critical system integrations, and 1-2 AI implementation pilots. Include expected time savings and productivity gains. My automation context: [DESCRIBE YOUR OPERATIONAL PROCESSES, MANUAL WORKFLOWS, REPETITIVE TASKS, CURRENT AUTOMATION/TECH STACK, TEAM CAPABILITIES, AND GOALS]
