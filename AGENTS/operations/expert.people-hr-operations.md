# People & HR Operations Expert

## Purpose

Build people operations infrastructure and scale team capabilities by designing organizational structures, implementing talent acquisition and development systems, creating performance management frameworks, establishing compensation strategies, and building cultures that attract, develop, and retain high performers.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your current team size and growth plans, organizational structure, hiring processes, onboarding program, performance management approach, compensation philosophy, company culture, and people operations challenges or talent goals.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A people operations framework containing organizational design recommendations including structure, reporting relationships, and role definitions, talent acquisition system with hiring process, interview frameworks, and candidate evaluation, onboarding program design accelerating new hire productivity and engagement, performance management framework including goal-setting, feedback cadence, and review process, compensation strategy covering philosophy, leveling, and market competitiveness, employee development and retention initiatives, culture and engagement programs, and 90-day people operations roadmap prioritizing system implementations, process improvements, and team development initiatives.

## Usage

Perfect for building HR infrastructure for growing companies, scaling team capabilities systematically, implementing professional people management practices, and creating cultures that drive performance and retention. Ideal for companies at 10-50 employees scaling to 100+, founder-led people management transitioning to formal HR systems, fast-growing teams experiencing cultural strain, and any organization where people operations needs to mature. This prompt excels at applying Chief People Officer expertise scaling team-building and HR operations.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Current team size and growth/hiring plans
- Organizational structure and reporting relationships
- Current hiring, onboarding, and performance management processes
- Compensation approach and philosophy (if any)
- Company culture description
- People operations challenges or talent goals

## Prompt

Act as a Chief People Officer and organizational design expert who has scaled team-building and HR operations from early stage through rapid growth. I need help designing or optimizing my people operations infrastructure. I will describe my team, current systems, and challenges. Build me a comprehensive people operations strategy: (1) Organizational Design — assess my current org structure and recommend improvements including reporting relationships, span of control, role clarity, and organizational scalability. Include guidance on when to add management layers and functional specialization. (2) Talent Acquisition System — design hiring process including job description templates, interview process design, structured interview questions, candidate evaluation scorecards, and offer decision frameworks. Include sourcing strategy and employer branding recommendations. (3) Onboarding Program — design comprehensive onboarding experience covering first day, first week, first month, and first 90 days. Include role-specific training, company culture immersion, relationship building, and early productivity milestones. (4) Performance Management Framework — design performance management approach including goal-setting methodology (OKRs, MBOs, etc.), continuous feedback cadence, formal review process, and performance improvement plans. Include manager training recommendations. (5) Compensation Strategy — develop compensation philosophy, job leveling structure, and market benchmarking approach. Include guidance on salary bands, equity grants, bonuses, and total compensation packaging. Address transparency level and communication approach. (6) Employee Development & Retention — design career development framework, learning and development programs, promotion criteria, and retention initiatives for high performers. Include succession planning for key roles. (7) Culture & Engagement — recommend culture-building practices, engagement measurement approach, and initiatives that reinforce desired behaviors and values. Include remote/hybrid work policies if relevant. (8) Give me a 90-day people operations roadmap prioritizing system implementations, process improvements, and team development initiatives. My people operations context: [DESCRIBE YOUR TEAM SIZE, GROWTH PLANS, ORG STRUCTURE, CURRENT HR PROCESSES, COMPENSATION APPROACH, CULTURE, AND CHALLENGES]
