# Scaling Infrastructure Expert

## Purpose

Guide companies through critical growth inflection points by designing scaling strategies, anticipating operational bottlenecks, implementing infrastructure for next stage, and providing COO-level strategic guidance navigating transitions from $0-$1M, $1M-$10M, and beyond.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your current revenue stage and growth trajectory, operational infrastructure and systems, team size and structure, known scaling challenges or bottlenecks, next growth milestone targets, and strategic priorities for scaling.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A scaling infrastructure strategy containing stage-specific operational assessment identifying what works at current stage but will break at next stage, scaling bottleneck analysis predicting constraints before they limit growth, infrastructure roadmap implementing foundational systems for next stage, organizational scaling recommendations including team structure evolution and leadership additions, process maturity recommendations appropriate for stage, and 90-day scaling roadmap prioritizing infrastructure investments, process implementations, and organizational changes required to reach next milestone without operational breakdown.

## Usage

Perfect for navigating critical growth inflection points, building operational infrastructure ahead of growth needs, anticipating and preventing scaling bottlenecks, and implementing stage-appropriate systems. Ideal for companies preparing to scale from $1M to $10M ARR, startups post-Series A entering rapid growth, founder-led companies requiring operational maturity, and any organization where growth is straining existing infrastructure. This prompt excels at applying COO and scaling strategy expertise guiding companies through critical inflection points from $0 to $1M to $10M and beyond.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Current revenue stage and growth rate
- Current operational infrastructure and systems
- Team size and organizational structure
- Known scaling challenges or bottlenecks
- Next growth milestone or target (e.g., $5M ARR, 100 employees)
- Strategic priorities for scaling

## Prompt

Act as a COO and scaling strategist who has guided companies through the critical inflection points from $0-$1M, $1M-$10M, and beyond. I need help building the operational infrastructure for my next growth stage. I will describe my current state, growth trajectory, and next milestone. Build me a comprehensive scaling strategy: (1) Stage-Specific Operational Assessment — based on my current stage, identify what's working now but will break at my next stage. Include processes, systems, team structure, and leadership that need evolution. Common inflection points: $1M (founders doing everything), $5M (needs functional leadership), $10M (needs professional management), $25M+ (needs executive team and institutional processes). (2) Scaling Bottleneck Analysis — predict the operational constraints that will limit my growth if not addressed proactively. Include capacity bottlenecks (team bandwidth, infrastructure limits), capability bottlenecks (missing skills or leadership), and process bottlenecks (manual workflows, lack of systems). (3) Infrastructure Roadmap — design the foundational systems I need for my next stage, including CRM/sales systems, financial systems, project management, documentation/knowledge management, communication infrastructure, and automation platforms. Prioritize by growth-enabling impact. (4) Organizational Scaling Recommendations — recommend how my team structure and leadership needs to evolve. Include: when to add functional leadership (VP Sales, VP Marketing, CFO, CPO), when to add management layers, role specialization vs generalist balance, and hiring sequence aligned to growth milestones. (5) Process Maturity Recommendations — identify which processes need what level of sophistication at my next stage (ad-hoc → documented → measured → optimized → automated). Avoid over-engineering early or under-investing late. (6) Give me a 90-day scaling roadmap prioritizing infrastructure investments, process implementations, and organizational changes I need to complete before my growth outpaces my operational capacity. Include quick wins that relieve immediate pressure and strategic initiatives preparing for next stage. My scaling context: [DESCRIBE YOUR CURRENT REVENUE STAGE, GROWTH RATE, OPERATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE, TEAM SIZE/STRUCTURE, KNOWN BOTTLENECKS, NEXT MILESTONE TARGET, AND STRATEGIC PRIORITIES]
