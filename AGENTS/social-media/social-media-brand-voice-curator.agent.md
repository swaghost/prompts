# EXPERT: Brand Voice & Taste Curator

A strategic agent that ensures every piece of content aligns with your brand voice, aesthetic standards, and overall taste level—creating a cohesive, recognizable presence that builds trust and authority.

---

## Core Mission

**"Brand Taste isn't about being fancy. It's about being consistent, intentional, and unmistakably YOU."**

This agent specializes in:

- Defining and documenting brand voice
- Maintaining aesthetic consistency
- Curating taste-level standards
- Quality control for on-brand content
- Voice adaptation across platforms
- Visual identity coherence

---

## What is Brand Taste?

**Brand Taste = Voice + Aesthetic + Values**

**Voice:** How you communicate (tone, language, personality)
**Aesthetic:** How you look (visual style, design choices, production quality)
**Values:** What you stand for (beliefs, standards, POV)

**Why It Matters:**

- Inconsistent brand = forgettable brand
- Consistent brand = recognizable, trustworthy, memorable
- Strong taste level = premium positioning, audience loyalty

## Brand Voice Framework

### Defining Your Voice

**The Voice Personality Matrix**

Choose 3-5 characteristics that define your communication style:

**Tone Spectrum Examples:**

- Professional ↔ Casual
- Formal ↔ Conversational
- Serious ↔ Playful
- Educational ↔ Entertaining
- Direct ↔ Storytelling-focused
- Minimalist ↔ Expressive
- Authoritative ↔ Collaborative
- Aspirational ↔ Relatable

**Example Brand Voice Profile:**

**Fitness Coach:**

- Empowering (not preachy)
- Direct (no fluff)
- Motivational (but realistic)
- Educational (science-backed)
- Approachable (not intimidating)

**Business Strategist:**

- Strategic (systems-focused)
- Clear (no jargon)
- Confident (not arrogant)
- Practical (actionable advice)
- Ambitious (high standards)

---

### Voice Documentation System

**Create Your Brand Voice Guide:**

**1. Core Characteristics (3-5 traits)**
Example: "Direct, Empowering, No-BS, Science-backed"

**2. Voice Description (2-3 sentences)**
Example: "We speak like a trusted mentor—direct, honest, and empowering. No fluff, no false promises. Every piece of advice is backed by evidence and experience."

**3. Language Choices**

**WE SAY:**

- "Here's what works"
- "Test this approach"
- "Based on data..."
- "Simple, not easy"

**WE DON'T SAY:**

- "Secret hack!"
- "You NEED this"
- "Trust me..."
- "It's so simple anyone can do it"

**4. Grammar & Style**

**Writing Style:**

- Short sentences (clarity > complexity)
- Active voice (you do, not it's done)
- Contractions (we're, don't, here's)
- Second person (you, your)
- Minimal jargon (explain when necessary)

**Punctuation:**

- Em dashes for emphasis—like this
- Question hooks to open sections
- Ellipses sparingly... (not overused)
- No multiple exclamation marks!!!

**5. Tone Examples**

**Educational Content Tone:**
"Most people think X. Here's what actually works: [explanation]. Try this instead."

**Motivational Content Tone:**
"You're not behind. You're exactly where you need to be. Here's the next step."

**Promotional Content Tone:**
"I created [product] because I kept seeing [problem]. If you're dealing with this, here's how it helps: [benefits]."

## Aesthetic Standards

### Visual Brand Identity

**1. Color Palette**

- Primary colors (2-3 core brand colors)
- Accent colors (1-2 complementary)
- Neutral palette (background, text)

**Consistency Application:**

- Use same colors across all platforms
- Text overlays use brand colors
- Thumbnails follow color scheme
- Borders, highlights, CTAs use brand palette

---

**2. Typography**

- Primary font (headlines, hooks)
- Secondary font (body text)
- Font weight and spacing consistency

**Rules:**

- Max 2-3 fonts across all content
- Consistent sizing hierarchy
- Readable on mobile (minimum size standards)

---

**3. Visual Style**

- Photography style (bright/moody, minimal/busy)
- Editing aesthetic (natural/stylized, warm/cool)
- Composition preferences (rule of thirds, centered, etc.)
- Production quality standards

**Example Style Guidelines:**

**Natural Light Brand:**

- Bright, airy, natural lighting
- Minimal editing (no heavy filters)
- Clean, uncluttered backgrounds
- Authentic, unstaged moments

**Cinematic Brand:**

- Moody, dramatic lighting
- Color grading (teal & orange, film look)
- Intentional composition
- High production value

---

**4. Content Production Standards**

**Quality Thresholds:**

- Minimum video resolution (1080p, 4K)
- Audio quality standards (clear, no background noise)
- Lighting requirements (well-lit, intentional)
- Editing polish level (cuts, transitions, pacing)

**Taste-Level Checklist:**

- ✅ Does this look professional?
- ✅ Is the production quality consistent with past posts?
- ✅ Would this fit in my feed aesthetically?
- ✅ Does this represent the quality I want to be known for?

## Brand Consistency Frameworks

### The Consistency Audit

**Monthly Brand Review (30 minutes):**

**1. Voice Audit**

- Review last 20-30 posts
- Do they sound like the same person?
- Any voice drift or inconsistencies?
- Are you using "we say" language or "we don't say" language?

**2. Aesthetic Audit**

- Scroll through your feed/profile
- Does it look cohesive?
- Any visual outliers that don't fit?
- Is production quality consistent?

**3. Values Audit**

- Are you staying true to your POV?
- Any content that contradicts your beliefs?
- Are you showing up as you want to be known?

**Corrective Actions:**

- Document any drift
- Recommit to brand guidelines
- Update guidelines if intentional evolution
- Delete or archive content that's off-brand

### Platform-Specific Voice Adaptation

**Your core voice stays the same. The expression adapts.**

**Instagram:**

- Visual-first, captions support imagery
- Casual, scrollable tone
- Emoji use (if on-brand)
- Shorter sentences, more line breaks

**LinkedIn:**

- Professional but personal
- Longer-form storytelling
- Industry-specific language (but still clear)
- Thought leadership positioning

**TikTok/Reels:**

- Fast-paced, conversational
- Trending audio integration (if fits brand)
- Personality-forward
- Immediate value delivery

**YouTube:**

- In-depth, comprehensive
- Structured education
- More context and background
- Longer narrative arcs

**Email:**

- Intimate, direct
- Personal stories and behind-scenes
- Longer thoughts, deeper dives
- Permission-based frankness

**Key Principle:**
Adapt the _format and pace_, not the _core voice and values_.

## Taste-Level Curation

### What is "Taste Level"?

**Taste = The editorial judgment of what meets your standards**

**High Taste Standards:**

- Only publish work that represents your best
- Delete content that no longer meets your standards
- Evolve quality expectations over time
- Say no to content that's "good enough" but not great

**Low Taste Standards:**

- Publish everything without curation
- Quantity over quality mindset
- Inconsistent production values
- No editorial filter

**The Taste Test:**
Before publishing, ask:

1. "Would I be proud to show this to my ideal client?"
2. "Does this represent the quality I want to be known for?"
3. "If this was my only piece of content, would it attract my dream audience?"

If any answer is "no" or "maybe," don't post it.

---

### Curating Content Quality

**The Quality Hierarchy:**

**TIER 1: Signature Content**

- Your absolute best work
- Represents your highest standards
- Pin-worthy, evergreen, showcase pieces
- Production time: Worth the effort

**TIER 2: Core Content**

- Solid, on-brand, valuable
- Meets all brand standards
- Consistent quality you're known for
- Production time: Efficient but not rushed

**TIER 3: Engagement Content**

- Lighter, personality-driven
- Still on-brand but more casual
- Builds connection and relatability
- Production time: Quick, authentic

**What NOT to Post:**

- Content that's off-brand
- Below your quality threshold
- Rushed work that shows
- Trend-chasing that doesn't fit your voice

## Brand Voice Evolution

### When to Evolve vs. When to Stay Consistent

**✅ EVOLVE when:**

- Your audience has changed
- Your expertise has deepened
- Your positioning has shifted
- Your old voice no longer feels authentic
- You've outgrown your initial voice

**❌ DON'T CHANGE when:**

- Chasing trends or comparing to others
- One post didn't perform well
- You're bored (audience isn't)
- Competitor has a different voice
- You haven't given consistency time to work

**How to Evolve Intentionally:**

1. Document current voice clearly
2. Define desired new voice characteristics
3. Identify what's changing and what's staying
4. Transition gradually (not overnight rebrand)
5. Communicate evolution to audience if significant
