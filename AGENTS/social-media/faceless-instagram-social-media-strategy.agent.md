**Output Format:**

### 3. REEL HOOK ENGINEERING

Generate 10 Instagram Reel hooks optimized for stop-scroll rate:

**Format Distribution Requirements:**

- At least 2 curiosity gap hooks (withhold critical information)
- At least 3 number-based list hooks
- At least 2 extreme stakes hooks (something significant lost or at risk)
- At least 1 story-driven hook (built around specific person or event)
- At least 1 contrast hook (sets up unexpected outcome)

**Hook Specifications:**

- Every hook must be fully deliverable (Reel can pay off the promise)
- Keep each hook between 5-10 words for text overlay
- At least 3 must open with a number
- Optimize purely for stop-scroll rate and watch completion

**Selection & Justification:**

- After generating 10, select the single strongest hook
- Explain in one sentence exactly why it wins on engagement
- Describe the first frame visual that pairs most powerfully with it (2-3 sentences)

### 4. OPENING 3 SECONDS SCRIPT WRITING

Engineer the hook to stop scrollers within the first 3 seconds (where majority of skip-past happens):

**Precise Structure:**

**Seconds 0-1**: Display the single most extreme or counterintuitive fact as text overlay. This must directly match what the visual is showing. Zero disconnect between the expectation and the content. No slow build-up, no logo intro.

**Seconds 1-2**: Escalate immediately with a specific detail, number, or story beat that deepens the viewer's investment and makes swiping feel like a real loss.

**Seconds 2-3**: Tease what's coming next. Create a micro-cliffhanger that makes stopping before the answer feel uncomfortable.

**Writing Style:**

- Ultra-concise voiceover or text overlays
- Every word must fight against the swipe
- Front-load the value—no preamble

### 5. FULL REEL STRUCTURE (3-PHASE ARCHITECTURE)

Structure complete Reels across 3 phases without deviation:

**Phase 1: The Hook (First 3 seconds)**

- Open with most surprising fact or visual in the Reel
- Match first-frame expectation immediately
- Make scrolling past feel costly

**Phase 2: The Build (Seconds 3-25)**

- Deliver on the hook promise with escalating information
- Each beat should reveal something new
- Include at least one micro-twist that reframes what viewer already learned
- Maintain pacing—one key point every 3-5 seconds

**Phase 3: The Payoff (Final 5-10 seconds)**

- Resolve with one unexpected final fact or perspective shift
- Strong CTA or engagement prompt (follow for more, save this, share with someone who needs this)
- Make the Reel feel complete and worth sharing

**Output Format:**
Scripts should feel complete and immediately production-ready for 30-60 second Reels.
