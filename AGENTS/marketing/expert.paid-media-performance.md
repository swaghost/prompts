# Paid Media & Performance Marketing Expert

## Purpose

Design and optimize high-performance paid media programs by building multi-channel acquisition strategies, implementing advanced measurement and attribution, managing substantial advertising budgets efficiently, and driving profitable customer acquisition at scale.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your target market and customer acquisition goals, current paid media efforts and channels, advertising budget, key performance metrics (CAC, LTV, ROAS, conversion rates), attribution challenges, and paid marketing objectives or problem areas.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A performance marketing strategy containing channel mix recommendations optimized for your funnel and CAC targets, campaign structure and audience segmentation strategy, creative testing framework and ad messaging recommendations, landing page optimization priorities, attribution model design or improvement recommendations, budget allocation strategy across channels and funnel stages, and 90-day paid media roadmap with specific campaigns, budget distribution, expected performance metrics, testing roadmap, and scaling criteria for successful campaigns.

## Usage

Perfect for building scalable paid acquisition programs, optimizing underperforming paid channels, managing substantial advertising budgets efficiently, and improving attribution and measurement. Ideal for B2B SaaS companies, e-commerce businesses, high-LTV service businesses, performance marketing agencies, and any organization investing significantly in paid customer acquisition. This prompt excels at applying enterprise-level performance marketing expertise managing $10M+ annual spend.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your target customers and acquisition goals
- Current paid media efforts, channels, and budget
- Key metrics (CAC, LTV, ROAS, conversion rates by channel)
- Attribution challenges or measurement gaps
- Paid marketing objectives or problem areas

## Prompt

Act as a performance marketing strategist who has managed $10M+ in annual paid media spend and built scalable acquisition engines. I need help designing or optimizing my paid media program. I will describe my market, current efforts, metrics, and goals. Build me a comprehensive performance marketing strategy: (1) Channel Mix Recommendations — identify the optimal channel mix for my funnel and CAC targets, including paid search, paid social, display/programmatic, retargeting, and emerging channels. Prioritize by expected efficiency at my scale. (2) Campaign Structure & Audience Segmentation — design campaign architecture and audience targeting strategy for each channel, including cold audience prospecting and warm audience nurturing. (3) Creative Testing Framework — recommend ad formats, messaging angles, and creative testing methodology to systematically improve performance. Include specific ad messaging recommendations for my value proposition. (4) Landing Page Optimization — identify conversion rate optimization priorities for landing pages and post-click experience. (5) Attribution Model Design — recommend attribution approach (first-touch, last-touch, multi-touch, etc.) appropriate for my sales cycle and measurement capabilities. Include recommendations for closing attribution gaps. (6) Budget Allocation Strategy — recommend budget distribution across channels, campaigns, and funnel stages, with rules for shifting budget based on performance. (7) Give me a 90-day paid media roadmap with specific campaigns, budget distribution, expected performance metrics (CAC, ROAS, conversion rates), testing roadmap, and scaling criteria. My paid marketing context: [DESCRIBE YOUR MARKET, CURRENT PAID EFFORTS, BUDGET, KEY METRICS, ATTRIBUTION CHALLENGES, AND GOALS]
