## Product Consistency Best Practices

### Critical Consistency Elements

**Always Maintain Exact**:

- Product shape and silhouette
- Colors and color blocking
- Materials and textures
- Logo placement and size
- Design details and accents
- Proportions and dimensions
- Branding elements

### The Consistency Instruction

**Add to Every Prompt**:

```
Use the exact [product] from the provided reference images without changing its
shape, colors, materials, [key elements], branding, proportions, or detailing. Maintain
perfect product consistency across all designs.
```

### Verification Checklist

Before finalizing any asset:

- [ ] Product matches reference exactly
- [ ] Colors are accurate (not AI-altered)
- [ ] Logo and branding correct
- [ ] Proportions maintained
- [ ] Materials rendered accurately
- [ ] All design details present

## Campaign Cohesion Strategies

### Visual Cohesion

1. **Color Palette**: Extract 3-5 colors from product, use consistently
2. **Typography**: Choose 2-3 fonts, use hierarchically across campaign
3. **Photography Style**: Maintain consistent lighting, composition approach
4. **Background Treatment**: Similar background styles (white, gradients, etc.)
5. **Graphic Elements**: Repeat geometric shapes, patterns, or motifs

### Messaging Cohesion

1. **Brand Voice**: Maintain consistent tone (bold, refined, playful, etc.)
2. **Taglines**: Core message repeated or referenced across touchpoints
3. **Value Props**: Consistent benefit messaging (comfort, performance, style)
4. **Storytelling**: Narrative arc from brand identity to lifestyle integration

### Platform Adaptation (While Maintaining Cohesion)

- **Instagram Feed**: Polished, aspirational version
- **Instagram Stories**: Behind-the-scenes, authentic version
- **TikTok**: Trend-integrated, fast-paced version
- **Pinterest**: Inspirational, evergreen version
- **LinkedIn**: Professional, innovation-focused version

## Common Brand Campaign Challenges

### Challenge 1: Product Inconsistency Across Assets

**Problem**: AI changes product details between different prompts  
**Solution**:

- Always add consistency instruction at end of every prompt
- Use same reference images for all assets
- Verify each asset against original product reference
- Be very specific about product details in prompts

### Challenge 2: Wrong Brand Tier Aesthetic

**Problem**: Assets look too cheap or too expensive for brand  
**Solution**:

- Reference comparable brands at correct tier
- Adjust material language (matte vs glossy, minimal vs bold)
- Review brand tier framework and adjust aesthetic references
- Use tier-appropriate photography style

### Challenge 3: Campaign Lacks Cohesion

**Problem**: Assets feel disconnected, not part of same campaign  
**Solution**:

- Extract color palette from product, use consistently
- Maintain same photography lighting style
- Repeat messaging themes across touchpoints
- Use similar background treatments and composition styles

### Challenge 4: Social Media Performance vs Brand Standards

**Problem**: Balancing algorithm performance with brand sophistication  
**Solution**:

- Create platform-specific variations while maintaining brand core
- Use trending formats but with brand-appropriate execution
- Balance: Feed for brand image, Stories/Reels for engagement
- Test and iterate based on performance data

### Challenge 5: Product Lost in Lifestyle Content

**Problem**: Model campaign or handheld doesn't emphasize product enough  
**Solution**:

- Specify product emphasis in prompts
- Use dynamic angles that showcase product
- Shallow depth of field (product sharp, background soft)
- Position product prominently in frame

## Expert Workflow: Creating a Complete Campaign

### Step 1: Brand Brief Analysis

**Questions to Answer**:

- What brand tier? (Luxury/Premium/Mass)
- What are core brand values? (List 3-5)
- Who is target demographic? (Age, lifestyle, platforms)
- What comparable brands? (Aspirational benchmarks)
- What's the hero product? (Exact details)

### Step 2: Visual Identity Foundation

**Actions**:

- Extract product color palette (3-5 colors)
- Define typography approach (2-3 fonts, hierarchy)
- Establish photography style (lighting, composition)
- Create Brand Identity Board first (campaign foundation)

### Step 3: Product-Focused Assets

**Create in Order**:

1. Signature Product Showcase (hero product shot)
2. Hero Advertisement Poster (with messaging)
3. Verify product consistency between these assets

### Step 4: Lifestyle & Connection

**Create Next**:

1. Handheld Product Campaign (human connection)
2. Model Campaign (aspirational lifestyle)
3. Check product visibility and emphasis

### Step 5: Digital Experience

**Develop**:

1. Mobile App UI (mobile commerce)
2. Website Design (e-commerce hub)
3. Ensure brand cohesion with product assets

### Step 6: Physical Experience

**Complete With**:

1. Packaging Design (unboxing touchpoint)
2. Verify all brand elements present
3. Check cohesion with overall campaign

### Step 7: Platform Optimization

**Adapt Assets**:

- Instagram Feed: 4:5 versions of key assets
- Instagram Stories/TikTok: 9:16 vertical versions
- Pinterest: 2:3 vertical aspirational content
- Website: 16:9 hero banners
- Ads: Platform-specific sizes

### Step 8: Campaign Review

**Final Checks**:

- [ ] Product consistency across all assets
- [ ] Visual cohesion (colors, typography, style)
- [ ] Messaging consistency
- [ ] Platform-optimized formats
- [ ] Brand tier appropriate aesthetics
- [ ] Social media best practices integrated

## Expert Philosophy

**Brand First, Platform Second**: Always honor brand values and positioning, then adapt for platform effectiveness. A luxury brand should never sacrifice sophistication for virality, but can use trending formats executed with brand-appropriate sophistication.

**Consistency is Credibility**: Product consistency across campaign assets builds trust and professionalism. Variations in product representation damage brand credibility.

**Cohesion Creates Recognition**: Visual and messaging cohesion across touchpoints creates strong brand recognition and recall. Random varied content confuses audience.

**Storytelling Over Selling**: Best campaigns tell complete story from brand values to lifestyle integration. Product is hero, but story creates emotional connection.
