# Full Stack Growth Audit Expert

## Purpose

Conduct comprehensive growth audits across the entire marketing and growth funnel by leveraging CMO-level strategic thinking and 20+ years of B2B scaling experience to identify bottlenecks, optimization opportunities, and systematic growth levers.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your current growth systems, marketing operations, funnel metrics, team structure, go-to-market strategy, and any known bottlenecks or underperforming areas across acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, and referral.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A full-stack growth audit report identifying critical bottlenecks limiting growth velocity, quick-win optimizations requiring minimal resources but delivering immediate impact, strategic initiatives requiring investment but offering transformational results, organizational gaps in team structure or capability, technology stack assessment revealing tool redundancies or missing infrastructure, and a prioritized 90-day growth roadmap with specific metrics targets and resource requirements for each initiative.

## Usage

Perfect for growth audits across entire marketing operations, strategic planning for scaling companies, board presentation preparation for growth reviews, and identifying systematic growth improvements. Ideal for B2B SaaS companies, agencies evaluating their own marketing, venture-backed startups preparing for scale, and any organization seeking CMO-level strategic assessment. This prompt excels at providing world-class marketing leadership perspective without full-time executive hiring costs.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Overview of your current growth/marketing systems
- Key funnel metrics (traffic, conversion rates, CAC, LTV, etc.)
- Context about your business model and target market
- Known challenges or areas of concern

## Prompt

Act as a world-class Chief Marketing Officer and growth strategist with 20+ years of experience scaling B2B companies. I need you to conduct a full-stack growth audit of my business. I will describe my current growth systems, funnel metrics, team structure, and challenges. Analyze across these dimensions: (1) Critical Bottlenecks — identify the 3-5 highest-impact constraints limiting growth velocity right now, ranked by impact magnitude. (2) Quick Wins — identify optimizations I could implement in the next 30 days with minimal resources that would deliver measurable improvement. (3) Strategic Initiatives — identify 2-3 transformational projects requiring significant investment but offering step-function growth improvements. (4) Organizational Gaps — assess my team structure and capabilities, identifying missing roles or skill gaps limiting execution. (5) Technology Stack — evaluate whether I have the right tools, unnecessary redundancies, or missing infrastructure. (6) Give me a prioritized 90-day growth roadmap with specific initiatives, expected impact, resource requirements, and success metrics for each. My business and growth context: [DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS, FUNNEL METRICS, TEAM, SYSTEMS, AND CHALLENGES]
