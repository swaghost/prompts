# Demand Generation Expert

## Purpose

Build systematic pipeline generation engines for B2B SaaS and high-growth companies by designing multi-channel demand strategies, optimizing conversion funnels, implementing lead scoring and nurture systems, and creating predictable revenue pipelines.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your target market, ideal customer profile, current demand generation efforts, lead volume and quality metrics, sales cycle length, conversion rates at each funnel stage, and pipeline generation goals or challenges.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A demand generation architecture containing channel strategy recommendations prioritized by pipeline efficiency for your ICP, conversion funnel optimization identifying specific friction points and optimization tactics at each stage, lead scoring model defining qualification criteria and routing logic, nurture campaign framework with content mapping to buyer journey stages, demand capture strategy for high-intent prospects, and 90-day demand generation roadmap with pipeline targets, channel mix, budget allocation recommendations, and success metrics at each funnel stage.

## Usage

Perfect for building systematic pipeline generation programs, optimizing underperforming demand generation, scaling lead volume while maintaining quality, and creating predictable revenue engines. Ideal for B2B SaaS companies, high-growth startups, PLG companies adding sales-led motion, and any business requiring consistent qualified pipeline. This prompt excels at designing demand systems based on proven high-growth SaaS playbooks.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your ICP and target market
- Current pipeline generation efforts and channels
- Key funnel metrics (MQLs, SQLs, conversion rates, CAC)
- Sales cycle length and deal size
- Pipeline generation goals

## Prompt

Act as a demand generation director who has built pipeline engines at both high-growth SaaS companies. I need help designing or optimizing my demand generation program. I will describe my target market, current efforts, funnel metrics, and goals. Build me a systematic demand generation strategy: (1) Channel Strategy — recommend the 3-5 highest-leverage channels for reaching my ICP, prioritized by pipeline efficiency not just lead volume. Include channel-specific tactics. (2) Conversion Funnel Optimization — analyze my funnel stages and identify specific friction points and optimization tactics at each stage (awareness → engagement → MQL → SQL → opportunity → closed-won). (3) Lead Scoring Model — design a qualification framework defining what makes a lead sales-ready, including demographic, firmographic, and behavioral criteria. Include routing logic to sales. (4) Nurture Campaign Framework — map content and messaging to buyer journey stages, identifying gaps in current nurture programs and recommending campaign sequences. (5) Demand Capture Strategy — design high-intent conversion paths for prospects already searching for solutions (bottom-funnel content, comparison pages, demo optimization). (6) Give me a 90-day demand generation roadmap with pipeline targets, channel mix, budget allocation recommendations, and success metrics at each stage. My demand generation context: [DESCRIBE YOUR ICP, CURRENT EFFORTS, FUNNEL METRICS, SALES PROCESS, AND GOALS]
