## Template 1: Minimalist Scandinavian Residential Complex

### Core Description

A minimalist Scandinavian **residential complex** composed of several small, modern houses with **steep gabled roofs**. The buildings are clad in **large-format ceramic tiles** in warm **brownish-red** and **muted terracotta tones**, arranged in clean, precise grid patterns that emphasize materiality and craftsmanship.

### Material Details

**Primary Materials:**

- Large-format ceramic tiles (brownish-red and muted terracotta tones)
- Clean, precise grid patterns
- Emphasis on materiality and craftsmanship

**Wood Elements:**

- **Natural light wood** is used for **doors**, soffits, and recessed entry areas
- Provides a soft contrast to the earthy ceramic surfaces

### Architectural Elements

**Roofing:**

- Steep gabled roofs
- Lightly covered with fresh snow
- Accentuates the roof geometry
- Reinforces the winter setting

**Design Philosophy:**

- Clean, precise grid patterns
- Minimalist aesthetic
- Material-focused design
- Scandinavian sensibility

### Environmental Setting

**Atmosphere:**

- Winter setting
- Fresh snow on roofs
- Clean, crisp environment
- Emphasis on architecture and materials

### Key Directive

**No people are present**, keeping the focus entirely on the architecture and environment.

## Template 2: Framed Landscape Architecture Shot

### Camera & Composition

**Shot Type:** A wide, **eye-level landscape shot** framed naturally by tall pine trees on both sides.

**Framing Elements:**

- Trees are inward from the left and right edges of the frame
- Forming an **organic canopy that visually cradles** the **house in the center**

### Foreground Details

**Terrain:**

- The **foreground slopes upward** with rocky terrain and low vegetation
- Leading the viewer's eye toward the house

### Composition Structure

**Visual Hierarchy:**

- The **composition uses strong horizontal lines** from the architecture
- Contrasted with the vertical and curved lines of the trees
- Creates visual tension and balance

**Purpose:**

- Natural framing directs focus to central subject
- Architectural lines provide stability
- Organic tree forms add movement and life
- Eye is guided naturally from foreground to subject
