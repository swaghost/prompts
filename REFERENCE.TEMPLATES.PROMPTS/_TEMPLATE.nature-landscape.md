## Botanical Accuracy Standards

### Species Selection Principles

**Geographic Accuracy:**

- Use species native to or naturalized in the region
- Avoid mixing incompatible climate zones
- Research authentic plant communities
- Consider elevation and microclimate

**Ecological Relationships:**

- Plants that naturally grow together
- Appropriate understory for canopy type
- Realistic ground cover for forest type
- Natural competition and spacing

**Visual Authenticity:**

- Correct growth habits
- Appropriate seasonal appearance
- Natural plant sizes and proportions
- Realistic foliage density

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Mixing tropical and temperate species  
❌ Perfect garden-like arrangement in wild settings  
❌ Unrealistic plant sizes or proportions  
❌ Wrong seasonal characteristics  
❌ Impossible species combinations  
❌ Unnatural spacing or patterns

✓ Regionally appropriate species  
✓ Natural, organic plant distribution  
✓ Realistic growth patterns  
✓ Season-appropriate appearance  
✓ Ecologically sound combinations  
✓ Natural spacing and competition

## Nature Setting Customization

### Seasonal Variations

**Mediterranean:**

- Spring: Wildflowers, fresh green growth
- Summer: Dry, golden grasses, fruit on trees
- Autumn: Olive harvest, muted colors
- Winter: Green but dormant, occasional rain

**Highland:**

- Spring: Fresh green growth, streams full
- Summer: Heather in bloom, lush vegetation
- Autumn: Golden bracken, berry colors
- Winter: Snow possible, stark beauty

**Boreal:**

- Spring: Fresh needles, birch leaves emerging
- Summer: Full green, long daylight
- Autumn: Golden birch, mushrooms, berries
- Winter: Snow-covered, evergreens visible

**Tropical:**

- Dry season: Less dense canopy, more light
- Wet season: Lush growth, full canopy
- Flowering periods: Species-dependent
- Year-round green: Consistent appearance
