# Enlivening Scenes with People

Prompts for adding realistic, natural human presence to architectural and environmental scenes.

---

## Core Principle: Authentic Human Presence

**Key Directive:** Preserve the original composition, perspective, subject placement, relative spacing, proportions, timing, and camera angle exactly as provided.

**Quality Standard:** Ultra-realistic human behavior, lifelike body language, natural interaction dynamics, high-end cinematic visualization quality, and professional photorealistic rendering.

## People Addition Guidelines

### Natural Integration Principles

**Spatial Awareness:**

- People must fit naturally into the environment
- Appropriate scale for distance
- Realistic placement within architecture
- Natural use of space

**Activity Appropriateness:**

- Actions must match the environment
- Clothing appropriate to setting and weather
- Activities believable for location
- Natural reason for presence

**Social Dynamics:**

- Group sizes appropriate to space
- Natural spacing between individuals
- Believable interaction patterns
- Authentic social behaviors

### Realism Checklist

**Physical Accuracy:**

- [ ] Correct human proportions
- [ ] Accurate anatomy (especially hands)
- [ ] Natural body positions
- [ ] Realistic clothing fit and drape
- [ ] Appropriate shadows and lighting

**Behavioral Authenticity:**

- [ ] Natural body language
- [ ] Believable actions
- [ ] Appropriate timing
- [ ] Realistic interaction with environment
- [ ] Genuine facial expressions

**Environmental Integration:**

- [ ] Proper scale within scene
- [ ] Correct lighting on figures
- [ ] Appropriate shadows cast
- [ ] Natural reflections if applicable
- [ ] Realistic depth placement

**Motion Quality:**

- [ ] Smooth, natural movement
- [ ] Believable poses mid-action
- [ ] Realistic weight distribution
- [ ] Natural gesture timing
- [ ] Authentic momentum

### Natural Diversity

**Include Variety In:**

- Age ranges appropriate to setting
- Body types and sizes
- Ethnicities reflecting location

**Avoid:**

- Stock photo diversity (too perfect)
- Unrealistic demographic distribution
- Tokenism or forced inclusion
- Stereotypical representations

**Children:**

- Playing, running, exploring
- With supervision if young
- Natural kid behavior

- Working, socializing, commuting
- Various activity levels
- Natural adult postures

**Seniors:**

- Walking, sitting, observing
- Appropriate mobility

## Clothing & Appearance

### Weather Appropriateness

**Match Clothing to Conditions:**

- Clear summer: Light clothing, short sleeves
- Rain: Raincoats, umbrellas (optional)
- Cold/fog: Layers, jackets, appropriate coverage
- Storm: Protected clothing, weather-appropriate gear

### Style Consistency

**Clothing Should:**

- Match the setting formality
- Reflect local culture
- Show natural wear and fit
- Include appropriate accessories
- Display realistic materials and textures

### Common Mistakes

❌ Perfect, unwrinkled clothing  
❌ Mismatched weather/clothing  
❌ Overly coordinated groups  
❌ Fashion shoot aesthetics  
❌ Unrealistic accessories

✓ Natural wear and wrinkles  
✓ Weather-appropriate attire  
✓ Individual style variety  
✓ Candid, realistic appearance  
✓ Functional accessories

## Usage Guidelines

### When to Add People

**Enhances the Scene:**

- Shows scale and human connection
- Demonstrates space usage
- Creates relatable context
- Adds life and energy
- Provides narrative element

**Scene Types:**

- Architectural visualization
- Public space design
- Urban planning
- Real estate marketing
- Environmental documentation

### When NOT to Add People

**Preserve Architectural Focus:**

- Pure architectural photography
- Material and detail emphasis
- Minimalist aesthetic intent
- Technical documentation
- When explicitly stated "no people"
