## Advanced Weather Techniques

### Transitional Weather

**Dawn Storm:**

```
Storm conditions with subtle warm light breaking through clouds on horizon,
mixing dramatic thunderclouds with soft golden undertones. Heavy atmosphere
with layered lighting showing storm receding or approaching.
```

**Clearing Fog:**

```
Dense fog beginning to lift, revealing partial details while maintaining
mystery. Wisps clearing in patches, sun breaking through in places, creating
dramatic shafts of light through remaining mist.
```

**Rain Ending:**

```
Light rain with breaks in clouds, wet surfaces reflecting emerging sunlight,
rainbow possibility, fresh clean atmosphere with mixed dark and light sky.
```

### Extreme Weather (Use Carefully)

**Heavy Storm:**

- Near-black clouds
- Heavy rain visible
- Strong wind effects
- Dramatic lighting
- High drama

**Thick Fog:**

- Visibility under 50 feet
- Nearly white-out conditions
- Extreme atmospheric density
- Maximum mystery

**Downpour:**

- Heavy rainfall
- Strong precipitation visible
- Significant wind
- Wet everything
- Active weather
