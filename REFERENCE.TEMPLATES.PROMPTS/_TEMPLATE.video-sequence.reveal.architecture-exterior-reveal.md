### Storyboard Notes

**Panel 1 ([TIME]):** Establish the exterior site, camera position, initial building state, lighting, and negative space.

**Panel 2 ([TIME]):** Introduce the first reveal trigger or effect and show the initial movement toward the building.

**Panel 3 ([TIME]):** Show the first exterior elements arriving, transforming, assembling, or being uncovered.

**Panel 4 ([TIME]):** Show the central reveal action, including the effect path and the unchanged portions of the architecture.

**Panel 5 ([TIME]):** Show the reveal advancing across the facade, roof, site, or landscape with clear spatial continuity.

**Panel 6 ([TIME]):** Show the near-complete structure and camera positioning for the final hero moment.

**Panel 7 ([TIME]):** Show final exterior details, lighting, landscaping, material finish, or effect resolution.

**Panel 8 ([TIME]):** Show the completed exterior hero frame and any final camera hold or move.
