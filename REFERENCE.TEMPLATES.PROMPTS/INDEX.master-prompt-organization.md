## 🔑 Key Concepts

### Identity Preservation

Critical for maintaining recognizable faces across multiple generated images. Always include:

- Reference image protocol statement
- List of features to preserve
- Warnings against beautification/genericization

### Scale Manipulation

For surreal composite images. Key elements:

- Explicit height descriptions (e.g., "15-20 stories tall")
- Viewing angle specification
- Negative space composition
- Hyper-realistic aesthetic to sell the illusion

### Progressive Detail Hierarchy

Structure all prompts from general → specific:

1. Category/Style
2. Main composition
3. Detailed elements
4. Technical qualities
5. Style keywords
6. Critical avoidances

### Negative Prompting

Always explicitly state what NOT to include:

- Style violations (CGI, plastic skin, beauty filters)
- Content violations (text, logos, unwanted people)
- Quality issues (blurry details, garbled text, cropped elements)

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with a template** rather than blank page - faster and more consistent results
2. **Be specific with technical specs** - exact aspect ratios, resolutions, camera equipment
3. **Use film stock references** (Kodak Portra 400, etc.) for authentic analog aesthetics
4. **Layer your lighting descriptions** - direction + quality + color + effects
5. **Include negative prompts** - as important as positive descriptions
6. **For consistency work** - always use explicit identity preservation protocols
7. **Emphasize natural imperfections** for photorealistic results (pores, uneven posture, etc.)
8. **Progressive transformation** - describe animation sequences phase by phase with timing
