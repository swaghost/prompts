# Fast Zoom Out

## Shot Group

Zoom/Lens

## Description of Resulting Shot

A quick decrease in lens focal length away from the main visual target with decisive zoom motion while keeping the target readable as surrounding space appears.

## Usage

Used for sudden context reveals, comedic beats, showing surprise reactions, or rapid spatial reorientation. Common in moments where immediate context or environment becomes important to the story.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** fast zoom out.

**Movement:** quickly decrease lens focal length away from the main visual target.

**Speed:** quick decisive zoom.

**Framing:** keep the target readable as the surrounding space appears.

**End:** finish on a stable wider composition.
