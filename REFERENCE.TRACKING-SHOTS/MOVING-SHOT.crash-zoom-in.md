# Crash Zoom In

## Shot Group

Zoom/Lens

## Description of Resulting Shot

A very fast and punchy lens snap rapidly toward the main visual target, landing on a bold tighter composition with aggressive motion.

## Usage

Used for maximum impact moments, horror scares, comedic exaggeration, or intense dramatic beats. Common in genre films (horror, comedy, action) where extreme emphasis creates stylistic punch.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** crash zoom in.

**Movement:** snap the lens rapidly toward the main visual target.

**Speed:** very fast and punchy.

**Framing:** keep the target readable through the sudden scale change.

**End:** land on a bold tighter composition.
