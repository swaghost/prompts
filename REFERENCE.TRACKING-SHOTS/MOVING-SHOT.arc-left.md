# Arc Left

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement on a shallow curved path around the main subject toward the left side with smooth measured curve, keeping distance and height consistent while the angle changes.

## Usage

Used for revealing new angles gracefully, showing dimensional form, creating elegant movement around subjects, or smooth perspective shifts. Common in product reveals, character introductions, or beauty shots.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** arc left.

**Movement:** move on a shallow curved path around the main subject toward the left side.

**Speed:** smooth measured curve.

**Framing:** keep distance, height and subject readability consistent while the angle changes.

**End:** finish from a new left-side angle.
