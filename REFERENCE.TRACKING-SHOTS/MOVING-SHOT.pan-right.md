# Pan Right

## Shot Group

Pan/Tilt

## Description of Resulting Shot

A horizontal camera rotation from left to right from one fixed point, smoothly revealing new space entering from the right side of the frame while keeping the horizon level.

## Usage

Used for revealing environments, following action moving right, showing spatial relationships, or creating a sense of discovery. Common in landscape reveals, architecture showcases, and following subject movement across frame.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** pan right.

**Movement:** rotate the camera horizontally from left to right from one fixed point.

**Speed:** smooth constant rotation.

**Framing:** keep the horizon level while new space enters from the right side of the frame.

**End:** settle on a clear final composition.
