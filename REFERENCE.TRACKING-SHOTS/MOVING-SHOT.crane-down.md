# Crane Down

## Shot Group

Drone/Crane

## Description of Resulting Shot

A smooth vertical camera movement downward through open space with slow controlled descent, keeping the subject or location readable as the camera descends to finish with lower subject or destination clearly visible.

## Usage

Used for dramatic descents, revealing ground-level subjects, creating anticipation, or establishing approach. Common in opening sequences, arrival moments, or when descending from wide to intimate scale.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** crane down.

**Movement:** travel smoothly downward through open space.

**Speed:** slow controlled vertical descent.

**Framing:** keep the subject or location readable as the camera descends.

**End:** finish with the lower subject or destination clearly visible.
