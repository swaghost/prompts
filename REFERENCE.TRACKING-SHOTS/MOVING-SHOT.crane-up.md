# Crane Up

## Shot Group

Drone/Crane

## Description of Resulting Shot

A smooth vertical camera movement upward through open space with slow controlled lift, keeping the subject or location readable as the camera rises to finish with higher scale clearly visible.

## Usage

Used for revealing scale, creating grandeur, showing spatial context from above, or majestic reveals. Common in establishing shots, finale moments, or sequences requiring elevated perspective to show scope.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** crane up.

**Movement:** travel smoothly upward through open space.

**Speed:** slow controlled vertical lift.

**Framing:** keep the subject or location readable as the camera rises.

**End:** finish with the higher scale clearly visible.
