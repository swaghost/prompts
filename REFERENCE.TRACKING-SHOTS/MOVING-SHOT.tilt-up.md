# Tilt Up

## Shot Group

Pan/Tilt

## Description of Resulting Shot

A vertical camera rotation upward from one fixed point, smoothly traveling upward while keeping the vertical subject or architecture centered.

## Usage

Used for revealing height, showing scale, following vertical action, or creating a sense of grandeur. Common in architecture reveals, character power moments, and establishing tall structures or spaces.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** tilt up.

**Movement:** rotate the camera upward from one fixed point.

**Speed:** smooth constant tilt.

**Framing:** keep the vertical subject or architecture centered as the frame travels upward.

**End:** land on the upper target.
