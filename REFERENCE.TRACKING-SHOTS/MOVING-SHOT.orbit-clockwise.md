# Orbit Clockwise

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement circling clockwise around the main subject at a consistent radius with smooth controlled orbit, keeping the subject centered while the background rotates around them.

## Usage

Used for complete subject reveals, showing all angles, creating dynamic beauty shots, or building dramatic emphasis. Common in hero moments, product showcases, character power reveals, or establishing spatial relationships.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** clockwise orbit.

**Movement:** circle clockwise around the main subject at a consistent radius.

**Speed:** smooth controlled orbit.

**Framing:** keep the subject centered while the background rotates around them.

**End:** complete the intended arc or full circle with stable framing.
