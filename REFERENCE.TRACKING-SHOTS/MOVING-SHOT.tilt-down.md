# Tilt Down

## Shot Group

Pan/Tilt

## Description of Resulting Shot

A vertical camera rotation downward from one fixed point, smoothly traveling downward while keeping the vertical subject or architecture centered.

## Usage

Used for revealing what's below, following descending action, creating ominous mood, or establishing ground-level details. Common in establishing shots moving from sky to ground, or revealing lower story elements.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** tilt down.

**Movement:** rotate the camera downward from one fixed point.

**Speed:** smooth constant tilt.

**Framing:** keep the vertical subject or architecture centered as the frame travels downward.

**End:** land on the lower target.
