# Body-Mounted-Cam / Snorricam

## Shot Group

Human (Attached) Camera

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera fixed relative to the subject's torso or face while the subject moves, matching body motion and keeping the subject close, centered and facing the camera as the background moves around them.

## Usage

Used for subjective intensity, disorientation effects, showing character's mental state, or creating unique perspective. Common in psychological sequences, drug/dream sequences, or moments emphasizing character's disconnection from environment.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** body-mounted Snorricam.

**Movement:** keep the camera fixed relative to the subject's torso or face while the subject moves.

**Speed:** match the subject's body motion.

**Framing:** keep the subject close, centered and facing the camera as the background moves around them.

**End:** finish with the subject still locked in frame.
