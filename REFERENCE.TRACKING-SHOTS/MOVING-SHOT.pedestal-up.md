# Pedestal Up

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A vertical camera movement upward in a straight line with smooth constant lift, keeping the lens level and pointed in the same direction during the vertical move.

## Usage

Used for following vertical action, revealing height, adjusting framing vertically, or following subjects standing up. Common in interview adjustments, vertical reveals, or maintaining framing on moving subjects.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** pedestal up.

**Movement:** move the entire camera vertically upward in a straight line.

**Speed:** smooth constant lift.

**Framing:** keep the lens level and pointed in the same direction during the vertical move.

**End:** finish with the higher framing clearly readable.
