# Infinite Zoom

## Shot Group

Zoom/Lens

## Description of Resulting Shot

A continuous zoom inward toward the exact center target with smooth accelerating motion, keeping the circular target centered as it expands until the next visual world fills the frame.

## Usage

Used for dreamlike transitions, psychedelic sequences, infinite regression effects, or surreal storytelling. Common in experimental films, music videos, or sequences exploring nested realities or consciousness.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** infinite zoom.

**Movement:** zoom continuously inward toward the exact center target.

**Speed:** smooth accelerating zoom.

**Framing:** keep the circular target centered as it expands.

**End:** finish when the next visual world fills the frame.
