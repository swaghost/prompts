# Dolly Out

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A physical camera movement backward in a straight line away from the main subject with smooth controlled retreat, revealing more environment while maintaining consistent lens direction and camera height.

## Usage

Used for revealing context, creating distance or isolation, showing environmental relationships, or transitioning to wider perspective. Common in ending sequences, isolation moments, or revealing hidden environmental elements.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** dolly out.

**Movement:** move the camera physically backward in a straight line away from the main subject.

**Speed:** smooth controlled retreat.

**Framing:** keep lens direction and camera height consistent while more environment enters frame.

**End:** finish in a wider composition.
