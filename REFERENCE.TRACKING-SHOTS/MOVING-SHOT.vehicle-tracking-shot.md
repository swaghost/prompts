# Vehicle Tracking Shot

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement with the vehicle along its route, matching the vehicle's pace while keeping it stable in frame as the road or environment moves past.

## Usage

Used for car chases, travel sequences, showing vehicle movement, or maintaining focus on vehicle occupants. Common in action films, road movies, or any sequence following vehicle-based action or dialogue.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** vehicle tracking shot.

**Movement:** move with the vehicle along its route.

**Speed:** match the vehicle's pace.

**Framing:** keep the vehicle stable in frame while the road or environment moves past.

**End:** maintain a clear moving vehicle composition.
