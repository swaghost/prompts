# Time-Lapse

## Shot Group

Specials (not any of the others)

## Description of Resulting Shot

A locked-camera position holding the same composition and horizon while time moves rapidly forward with fast time compression, showing visible passage of time through the frame.

## Usage

Used for showing time passage, environmental changes, long-duration processes compressed, or creating temporal perspective. Common in documentaries, montages, sunrise/sunset sequences, or showing construction, decay, or natural processes.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** locked-camera time-lapse.

**Movement:** hold one fixed camera position while time moves rapidly forward.

**Speed:** fast time compression with a stable camera.

**Framing:** keep the same composition and horizon as motion passes through the frame.

**End:** finish from the same camera angle with visible passage of time.
