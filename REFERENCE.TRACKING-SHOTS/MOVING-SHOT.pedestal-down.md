# Pedestal Down

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A vertical camera movement downward in a straight line with smooth constant descent, keeping the lens level and pointed in the same direction during the vertical move.

## Usage

Used for following vertical action downward, revealing lower elements, adjusting framing, or following subjects sitting down. Common in framing adjustments, ground-level reveals, or maintaining framing on descending subjects.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** pedestal down.

**Movement:** move the entire camera vertically downward in a straight line.

**Speed:** smooth constant descent.

**Framing:** keep the lens level and pointed in the same direction during the vertical move.

**End:** finish with the lower framing clearly readable.
