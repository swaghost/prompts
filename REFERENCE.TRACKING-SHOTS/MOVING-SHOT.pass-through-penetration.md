# Pass-Through / Penetration

## Shot Group

Specials (not any of the others)

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement forward toward a visible object, surface or barrier, continuing through it with smooth centered glide to arrive inside the revealed space beyond.

## Usage

Used for impossible camera moves, magical transitions, VFX sequences, or surreal storytelling. Common in fantasy films, dream sequences, architectural visualizations, or moments requiring physics-defying camera movement through solid objects.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** pass-through movement.

**Movement:** move forward toward a visible object, surface or barrier and continue into the space beyond.

**Speed:** smooth centered glide.

**Framing:** keep the opening or surface centered as the transition point.

**End:** arrive inside the revealed space beyond.
