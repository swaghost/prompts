# Broad Lighting

## Description

Broad lighting illuminates the side of the face turned toward the camera, creating even, open illumination with the lit side dominating the composition. This technique makes the face appear wider and more evenly lit, with shadows falling on the far side away from camera.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys openness, confidence, and approachability. Creates welcoming, positive atmosphere.

**Psychological Purpose:** Makes subjects feel open and accessible. Associated with friendly, outgoing personalities. Ideal for portraying warmth and extroversion.

**Physical Purpose:** Widens face visually, useful for narrower face shapes. Creates even, bright illumination ideal for commercial work, social media portraits, and approachable professional headshots. Reveals facial features openly.
