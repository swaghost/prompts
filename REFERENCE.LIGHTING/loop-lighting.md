# Loop Lighting

## Description

Loop lighting positions the light source slightly above eye level and 30-45 degrees to the side, creating a small shadow of the nose that "loops" down toward the corner of the mouth (but doesn't touch it). This versatile technique provides dimension and flattering shadows while remaining universally appealing.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys approachability, warmth, and pleasant engagement. Creates balanced mood without extreme drama.

**Psychological Purpose:** Makes subjects feel confident and naturally attractive. Associated with professional portraits, corporate headshots, and accessible commercial content. Feels polished but not overly stylized.

**Physical Purpose:** Creates subtle dimension and flattering shadows for most face shapes. Ideal for professional headshots, portrait sessions, commercial photography, and general-purpose portraiture. Works well for diverse subjects and situations.
