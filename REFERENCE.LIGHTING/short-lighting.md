# Short Lighting

## Description

Short lighting illuminates the side of the face turned away from the camera, leaving the side closest to the camera in relative shadow. This technique narrows the apparent width of the face by placing the shadow side nearest the viewer, creating slimming effect and emphasizing facial contours.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys mystery, intrigue, and subtle drama. Creates contemplative, introspective mood.

**Psychological Purpose:** Suggests thoughtfulness and depth of character. Makes subjects appear more serious or mysterious. Associated with character-driven portraiture and editorial work.

**Physical Purpose:** Slims face visually, emphasizes cheekbones and jawline, creates dramatic dimensionality. Ideal for portrait subjects who want slimming effect, editorial fashion, and creating sculptural facial definition.
