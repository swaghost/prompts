# Window Lighting

## Description

Window lighting uses natural or simulated light coming through a window frame, creating directional soft light with natural falloff. This creates beautiful gradation from highlight to shadow with organic quality, often with visible window frame patterns or soft wrap-around illumination.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys contemplation, intimacy, melancholy, or peaceful solitude. Creates pensive, introspective mood.

**Psychological Purpose:** Associated with moments of reflection, isolation, longing, or quiet beauty. Makes subjects feel vulnerable and authentic. Used for emotional character moments and intimate storytelling.

**Physical Purpose:** Creates beautiful soft directional light with natural gradation. Ideal for portraits, interior scenes, intimate moments, and creating organic lighting aesthetic. Provides flattering illumination with motivated source.
