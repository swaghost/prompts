# Front Lighting

## Description

Front lighting positions the light source directly in front of the subject, facing them head-on. This creates even, flat illumination across the entire face and body with minimal shadows, revealing all details clearly. The light reduces texture and depth, creating a clean, uniform appearance.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys clarity, openness, and visibility. Creates approachable, friendly, and inviting mood without mystery or drama.

**Psychological Purpose:** Makes subjects feel safe and clearly seen. Associated with commercial beauty, corporate headshots, and accessible content. Eliminates hiding or concealment.

**Physical Purpose:** Eliminates facial shadows and texture, ideal for beauty shots, product photography, passport photos, and clean commercial work. Minimizes skin imperfections and creates even complexion appearance.
