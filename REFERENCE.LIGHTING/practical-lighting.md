# Practical Lighting

## Description

Practical lighting uses visible light sources within the scene itself (lamps, candles, neon signs, screens, etc.) that serve both as illumination and set dressing. These sources create motivated, realistic lighting that appears organic to the environment while adding atmosphere and authenticity.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys intimacy, realism, comfort, and environmental authenticity. Creates lived-in, genuine atmosphere.

**Psychological Purpose:** Makes environments feel real and inhabited. Associated with naturalistic storytelling, intimate moments, and grounded narratives. Helps viewers suspend disbelief through visible light sources.

**Physical Purpose:** Creates motivated lighting that feels organic to the scene, adds production design elements, creates warm ambient glow. Ideal for interior scenes, cozy moments, realistic environments, and naturalistic cinematography. Justifies lighting choices visually.
