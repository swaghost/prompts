A good tip is to keep the jersey, number, color palette, and national branding consistent. GPT Image 2.0 usually performs better when the visual identity is very clear instead of mixing too many team styles at once.

You can also swap the country and color palette easily. For example:

"Japan → Brazil / yellow, green, blue
Japan → Germany / white, black, red
Japan → France / navy, white, red
Japan → Argentina / sky blue, white, gold
Japan → Turkey / red, white, deep burgundy"
