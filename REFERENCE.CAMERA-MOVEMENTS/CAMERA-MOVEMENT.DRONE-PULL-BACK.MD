# Drone Pull Back

## Shot Group

Drone/Crane

## Description of Resulting Shot

A smooth aerial camera movement flying backward away from the subject or destination with controlled aerial retreat, keeping the subject readable as more landscape appears to finish on a wider aerial composition.

## Usage

Used for revealing aerial context, showing isolation from above, creating departing perspective, or grand finale reveals. Common in ending sequences, showing scale of locations, or establishing geographical context.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** drone pull back.

**Movement:** fly smoothly backward away from the subject or destination.

**Speed:** controlled aerial retreat.

**Framing:** keep the subject readable as more landscape appears.

**End:** finish on a wider aerial composition.
