# Crash Zoom Out

## Shot Group

Zoom/Lens

## Description of Resulting Shot

A very fast and punchy lens snap rapidly away from the main visual target, landing on a bold wider composition with aggressive motion revealing context.

## Usage

Used for shock reveals, comedic reactions, sudden context shifts, or stylistic punctuation. Common in moments where immediate environmental context creates surprise or humor.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** crash zoom out.

**Movement:** snap the lens rapidly away from the main visual target.

**Speed:** very fast and punchy.

**Framing:** keep the target readable as the surrounding space appears.

**End:** land on a bold wider composition.
