Across cinematography guides, filmmakers use a consistent set of linguistic patterns:

A. Directional Verbs
pan left, tilt up, push in, pull out, crane down, track right

B. Emotional Verbs
reveal, isolate, connect, abandon, heighten, build tension

C. Motivational Framing
motivated movement — justified by character or story

unmotivated movement — stylistic or expressive, not tied to action

D. Speed Qualifiers
slow pan, rapid whip pan, slow push, snap zoom

E. Psychological Labels
intimacy move, power tilt, disorientation angle, tension build

F. Spatial Labels
establishing sweep, geography reveal, context pull-out
