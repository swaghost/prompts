# Helicopter

## Shot Group

Drone/Crane

## Description of Resulting Shot

A high-altitude aerial camera movement along a broad gradual flight path with steady controlled motion, keeping the landscape or distant moving subject readable at wide scale.

## Usage

Used for epic establishing shots, showing vast geography, following vehicles over long distances, or cinematic scope. Common in action films, travel documentaries, or sequences requiring maximum aerial scale and production value.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** helicopter-style aerial shot.

**Movement:** move from high altitude along a broad gradual flight path.

**Speed:** steady controlled aerial motion.

**Framing:** keep the landscape or distant moving subject readable at wide scale.

**End:** finish on a stable high-altitude composition.
