# Side Tracking Shot

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement parallel beside the subject along their direction of travel, matching their motion while keeping them in side profile or three-quarter profile at a stable distance.

## Usage

Used for showing subject and environment equally, creating lateral movement energy, revealing parallel action, or maintaining neutral perspective. Common in walking scenes, vehicle shots, or moments emphasizing journey alongside environment.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** side tracking shot.

**Movement:** move parallel beside the subject along their direction of travel.

**Speed:** match the subject's motion.

**Framing:** keep the subject in side profile or three-quarter profile at a stable distance.

**End:** continue the parallel movement with clear horizontal motion.
