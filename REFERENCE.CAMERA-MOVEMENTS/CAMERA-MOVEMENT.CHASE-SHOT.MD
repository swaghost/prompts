# Chase Shot

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A camera movement following a moving subject quickly along the action route with fast, reactive and physically close positioning, keeping the subject visible while allowing energetic reframing.

## Usage

Used for intense action sequences, pursuit moments, creating urgency and energy, or visceral following. Common in action films, chase sequences, or moments requiring maximum kinetic energy and audience immersion.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** chase shot.

**Movement:** follow a moving subject quickly along the action route.

**Speed:** fast, reactive and physically close.

**Framing:** keep the subject visible while allowing energetic reframing.

**End:** stay connected to the subject in motion.
