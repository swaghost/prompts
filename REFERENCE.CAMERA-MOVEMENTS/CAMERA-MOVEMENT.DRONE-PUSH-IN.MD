# Drone Push In

## Shot Group

Drone/Crane

## Description of Resulting Shot

A smooth aerial camera movement flying forward through open space toward the subject or destination with controlled aerial glide, keeping the route and destination readable as the camera approaches to arrive at a closer aerial composition.

## Usage

Used for cinematic aerial approaches, establishing locations, creating epic scale, or smooth transitions from wide to medium aerial view. Common in travel content, establishing shots, or premium production value moments.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** drone push in.

**Movement:** fly smoothly forward through open space toward the subject or destination.

**Speed:** controlled aerial glide.

**Framing:** keep the route and destination readable as the camera approaches.

**End:** arrive at a closer aerial composition.
