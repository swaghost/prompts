# Dolly In

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A physical camera movement forward in a straight line toward the main subject with smooth controlled push, closing distance while maintaining consistent camera height and lens direction.

## Usage

Used for building intimacy, increasing tension, drawing audience into the scene, or emphasizing importance. Common in dramatic approaches, character focus moments, or when physical camera movement creates more impact than zoom.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** dolly in.

**Movement:** move the camera physically forward in a straight line toward the main subject.

**Speed:** smooth controlled push.

**Framing:** keep camera height, lens direction and subject position consistent while distance closes.

**End:** finish in a tighter composition.
