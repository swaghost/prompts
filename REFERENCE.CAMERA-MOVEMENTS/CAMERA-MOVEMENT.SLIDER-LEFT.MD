# Slider Left

## Shot Group

Dolly/Track

## Description of Resulting Shot

A small lateral camera slide to the left with slow controlled constant motion, creating subtle parallax shifts between foreground, subject and background layers.

## Usage

Used for adding production value to static shots, creating depth through parallax, subtle reveals, or elegant movement in tight spaces. Common in interviews, product shots, or moments requiring refined cinematic motion.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** slider left.

**Movement:** slide the camera a small distance to the left.

**Speed:** slow controlled constant motion.

**Framing:** keep foreground, subject and background layers readable as parallax shifts.

**End:** finish on a refined composition with the new left-side angle visible.
