# Fast Zoom In

## Shot Group

Zoom/Lens

## Description of Resulting Shot

A quick increase in lens focal length toward the main visual target with decisive zoom motion while keeping the target centered or clearly readable.

## Usage

Used for sudden emphasis, shock moments, rapid focus shifts, or action punctuation. Common in reaction shots, sudden revelations, or moments requiring immediate audience attention on a specific detail.

## Video Prompt

**Camera:** fast zoom in.

**Movement:** quickly increase lens focal length toward the main visual target.

**Speed:** quick decisive zoom.

**Framing:** keep the target centered or clearly readable during the scale change.

**End:** finish on a stable tighter composition.
