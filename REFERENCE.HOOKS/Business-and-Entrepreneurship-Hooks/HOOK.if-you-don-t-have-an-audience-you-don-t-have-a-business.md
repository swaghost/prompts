# If you don't have an audience, you don't have a business

## Hook Group

Business & Entrepreneurship Hooks

## Desired Viewer Preception

The viewer sees you as customer-first and commercially sharp, someone who knows attention and demand matter more than hype.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally creates relevance and self-awareness, intellectually clarifies customer focus, psychologically reduces wasted effort, and physically encourages close reading and immediate evaluation.

## Psychology

Attention economy framing.

## Example

MrBeast built a brand empire before selling chocolate.

## Why it works

This works because it gives the audience a simpler, more actionable lens for growth. Instead of chasing every possible buyer, it invites them to focus on the people most likely to value what they offer. That creates clarity, lowers wasted effort, and feels like a strategic breakthrough. Attention economy framing. reinforces the idea that discipline and focus are more powerful than scattershot effort.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

It can fail if the audience sees it as a crude sales pitch rather than a strategic insight. If the positioning advice is too broad, the hook loses sharpness. It may also feel obvious to experienced operators. Over-indexing on one tactic can make the content feel one-dimensional.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

It can backfire by making the audience feel they are being marketed to rather than taught. If the message becomes too transactional, it can reduce trust. Some viewers may also feel the advice is too simplistic for complex markets.
