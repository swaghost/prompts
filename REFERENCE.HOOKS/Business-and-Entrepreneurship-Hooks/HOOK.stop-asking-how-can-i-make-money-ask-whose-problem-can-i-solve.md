# Stop asking "How can I make money?" Ask "Whose problem can I solve?"

## Hook Group

Business & Entrepreneurship Hooks

## Desired Viewer Preception

The viewer sees you as financially grounded and operationally wise, someone who understands real business health is more than surface-level income.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally creates caution and clarity, intellectually activates financial realism, psychologically increases trust through groundedness, and physically produces a strong pause and deeper focus.

## Psychology

Service > selfish framing.

## Example

Fiverr sellers solved “cheap logos” demand → billion-dollar brand.

## Why it works

This works because it cuts through romanticized business thinking and replaces it with reality. The audience feels a sense of relief when someone says what they secretly know but avoid admitting. It builds authority by sounding like a practical truth, not a slogan. Service > selfish framing. helps because people trust messages that feel honest and grounded.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

It can fail if it sounds too cynical or overly negative. Some audiences will feel judged rather than informed. The hook may also feel too broad if it does not distinguish between different business stages. If the advice is overly simplistic, it can sound preachy.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

It can backfire by making your content feel overly cynical or pessimistic. Some viewers may feel you are discouraging ambition rather than helping them think clearly. If the message is too hard-nosed, it can reduce emotional connection.
