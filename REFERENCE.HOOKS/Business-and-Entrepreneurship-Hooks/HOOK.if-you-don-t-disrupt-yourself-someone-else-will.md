# If you don't disrupt yourself, someone else will

## Hook Group

Business & Entrepreneurship Hooks

## Desired Viewer Preception

The viewer sees you as future-focused and adaptive, someone who understands change, reinvention, and market timing.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally creates anticipation and excitement, intellectually sharpens strategic awareness, psychologically creates future orientation, and physically produces a strong urge to continue reading.

## Psychology

Disruption bias.

## Example

Blockbuster laughed at Netflix—now it’s gone.

## Why it works

This works because it reframes innovation as adaptation rather than novelty. The audience likes messages that feel intelligent and current without sounding flashy. It makes your perspective look sharp and future-aware. Disruption bias. reinforces the urge to stay ahead of the curve.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

It can fail if it sounds too abstract or too familiar. The audience may feel the line is clever but not useful. If the hook is delivered without enough specificity, it becomes easy to dismiss. Overuse can make it feel like a recycled slogan.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

It can backfire by sounding too absolute or too polished for the audience to trust. If the hook feels like a slogan rather than a real insight, it may create skepticism. Overusing the same framing can make your content feel predictable.
