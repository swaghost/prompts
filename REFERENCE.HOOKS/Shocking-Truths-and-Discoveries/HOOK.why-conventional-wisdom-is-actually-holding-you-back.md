# Why [conventional wisdom] is actually holding you back

## Hook Group

Shocking Truths & Discoveries

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as someone who questions received wisdom and identifies when it limits rather than helps. They see you as critical thinker who evaluates advice by results not authority. You appear as someone whose wisdom-questioning liberates audiences from invisible constraints.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers curiosity about what accepted wisdom actually harms them. Intellectually activates critical evaluation of conventional advice. Psychologically creates permission to abandon wisdom that doesn't serve them. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "holding you back" promises identification of hidden obstacles in disguise of helpful advice.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because conventional wisdom feels safe but if it's limiting, following it explains stuck progress - audiences want liberation. This hook leverages credibility through contrarianism where questioning accepted wisdom requires compelling evidence. The "holding you back" framing transforms advice into obstacle. By targeting conventional wisdom, you ensure broad relevance to audiences following common guidance. This satisfies desire for breakthrough through removal of unrecognized constraints.

## Example

Why "follow your passion" is actually holding you back: it assumes passion exists before skill development, making people quit pursuits before competence creates enjoyment - better advice: build skill in valuable areas, passion often emerges from mastery and impact, not discovery of pre-existing love.

## Why it works

This hook works because it explains why following seemingly good advice hasn't produced expected results. The holding-back framing reframes advice from helpful to harmful. By questioning conventional wisdom, you provide permission to abandon constraining guidance. The hook satisfies desire to understand why progress is stuck despite following "right" advice. This combination of stuck-progress explanation, advice reframing, abandonment permission, and constraint identification creates liberated audiences.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when conventional wisdom actually serves audiences well and your contrarianism is baseless. If you constantly oppose conventional wisdom, you seem reflexively contrarian. The hook also fails if you strawman conventional wisdom - attacking oversimplified version not how it's actually practiced. If you don't provide alternative to wisdom you critique, you create vacuum without replacement. Too much wisdom-questioning can make audiences distrust all guidance including yours.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who reject all conventional wisdom reflexively, losing valuable accumulated knowledge. You may attract people who prefer feeling special through contrarianism over following effective practices. The constant wisdom-critique can reduce appreciation for advice that works for many despite limitations. If your critique is wrong and conventional wisdom actually works, you've directed audiences away from effective approaches. This approach can also position you as perpetual outsider unable to appreciate mainstream wisdom. The liberation emphasis may reduce healthy respect for accumulated wisdom that usually serves well.
