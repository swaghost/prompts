# The pattern I keep seeing in [group] that's concerning

## Hook Group

Shocking Truths & Discoveries

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as observant pattern-recognizer raising important concerns. They see you as willing to name worrying trends others overlook or avoid discussing. You appear as someone whose concern motivates warning rather than judgment.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers concern about pattern they might be part of or affected by. Intellectually activates pattern validation through their own observations. Psychologically creates urgency to understand and potentially interrupt concerning trend. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "concerning pattern" signals systematic problem requiring attention.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because repeated patterns suggest systematic issue not isolated incidents - understanding pattern helps avoid or address it. This hook leverages the power of trend identification where audiences want early warning of problems before they're widespread. The "keep seeing" framing demonstrates repeated observation validating pattern's existence. By expressing concern rather than judgment, you position as worried ally not critic. This creates protective motivation to understand and address pattern.

## Example

The pattern I keep seeing in solopreneurs that's concerning: optimizing every business process except rest and recovery, leading to 18-24 month burnout cycles where they rebuild from scratch after crashing - treating businesses as sustainable but themselves as expendable resources.

## Why it works

This hook works because it provides early-warning system for patterns audiences might not recognize in themselves yet. The concern framing demonstrates you care about group welfare not just criticism. By identifying systematic pattern, you help audiences interrupt it before suffering consequences. The hook satisfies desire for pattern awareness that enables preemptive action. This combination of early warning, welfare concern, interruption opportunity, and preemptive value creates protectively engaged audiences.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when your pattern is cherry-picked observations not actual systematic trend. If you constantly see concerning patterns everywhere, you seem alarmist or negative. The hook also fails if concern masks judgment - supposedly worried but actually critical. If pattern you identify is actually healthy adaptive behavior you misunderstand, concern seems paternalistic. Too many concerning-pattern revelations can make audiences feel perpetually anxious about trends.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating anxious audiences constantly watching for concerning patterns, fostering hypervigilance. You may attract people who bond over identifying problems rather than building solutions. The constant concern emphasis can make your community feel negative and problem-focused. If your concerning patterns are misidentified, you've created unnecessary anxiety. This approach can also alienate the group you express concern about, even when concern is genuine. The trend-focus may reduce appreciation for individual agency where people can avoid patterns others fall into.
