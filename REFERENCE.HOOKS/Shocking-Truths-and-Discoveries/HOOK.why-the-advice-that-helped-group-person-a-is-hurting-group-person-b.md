# Why the advice that helped [group/person A] is hurting [group/person B]

## Hook Group

Shocking Truths & Discoveries

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as someone understanding context-dependent advice applicability. They see you as analytical about when guidance helps versus harms different groups. You appear as someone whose conditional-advice awareness prevents harmful misapplication of context-specific guidance.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers relief about permission to ignore advice that doesn't fit. Intellectually activates conditional thinking about guidance. Psychologically creates discernment about advice applicability. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "advice helped one hurts another" promises understanding when to follow versus ignore guidance based on context.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because if same advice helps some and harms others, context determines applicability - universal prescriptions miss individual situations. This hook leverages appeal of nuance where recognizing conditional effectiveness demonstrates wisdom. The "helping-hurting" framing creates stark contrast. By revealing context-dependence, you enable discernment. This satisfies desire for understanding that advice effectiveness varies by situation not absolute quality.

## Example

Why the advice "hustle 24/7" that helped ambitious twenty-somethings is hurting parents with young children: single focused people benefit from intensity; parents need sustainable balance preventing burnout and family neglect. Context determines appropriateness - life stage, responsibilities, resources all affect what works. Advice isn't universally good or bad; fit matters. Misapplied intensity creates damage not results.

## Why it works

This hook works because it reveals conditional advice applicability enabling appropriate guidance selection. The helped-hurting framing creates contrast awareness. By explaining context-dependence, you enable discernment. The hook satisfies desire for conditional understanding. This combination of context awareness, conditional thinking, appropriate selection, and discernment enablement creates wisely discerning audiences.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when you suggest no advice applies broadly or when you use context-dependence to dismiss valuable guidance too easily. If you constantly reveal helped-hurting patterns, you foster distrust of all advice. The hook also fails if you validate avoiding challenging but beneficial guidance claiming it "doesn't fit." If contrast you draw is exaggerated, claim misleads. Too many context-dependent revelations can create excessive advice-rejection.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who reflexively reject advice claiming different context rather than trying adaptations. You may attract people using context-difference to avoid uncomfortable but valuable guidance. The constant conditional focus can reduce appreciation for broadly applicable principles. If you encourage excessive advice-dismissal, audiences miss valuable guidance. This approach can also foster belief everyone's situation is so unique nothing general applies. The context emphasis may reduce understanding that while context matters, most advice can be adapted for different situations rather than being binary helpful-harmful, and dismissing too quickly prevents learning from guidance requiring interpretation.
