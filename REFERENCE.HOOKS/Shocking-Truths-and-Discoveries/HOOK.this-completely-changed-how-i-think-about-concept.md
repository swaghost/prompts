# This completely changed how I think about [concept]

## Hook Group

Shocking Truths & Discoveries

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as someone who had significant mental model shift about important concept. They see you as willing to update thinking based on new understanding. You appear as someone whose cognitive transformation offers valuable reframing.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers curiosity about what insight changed your thinking. Intellectually activates interest in mental model transformation. Psychologically creates openness to reconsidering own concept understanding. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "completely changed" promises dramatic cognitive shift worth understanding.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because complete thinking changes signal powerful insights - if understanding transformed your thinking, it might transform theirs too. This hook leverages power of transformation narratives where dramatic change creates intrigue. The "completely changed" framing emphasizes significance. By focusing on thinking not just behavior, you address fundamental mental models. This satisfies desire for insights that shift perspective not just actions.

## Example

This completely changed how I think about failure: stopped seeing it as indicator of ability ("I failed because I'm not good enough") and started seeing it as feedback about approach ("this specific method didn't work in this context") - shifted from identity-level interpretation to strategy-level interpretation, making failure informative rather than devastating.

## Why it works

This hook works because it provides reframing that transforms concept from limiting to empowering. The complete-change framing demonstrates significance. By sharing mental model shift, you enable audiences to adopt new perspective. The hook satisfies desire for cognitive shifts that unlock new possibilities. This combination of empowering transformation, significance demonstration, perspective adoption, and possibility unlocking creates enlightened audiences.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when your thinking change is modest refinement not complete transformation as claimed. If you constantly have complete thinking changes, you seem unstable or constantly wrong rather than thoughtfully evolving. The hook also fails if your new thinking is actually less accurate or useful than previous understanding. If you present simple reframing as dramatic revelation, you overstate. Too many thinking-change revelations can make audiences feel perpetually wrong in their current understanding.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who constantly revise thinking rather than developing stable frameworks. You may attract people who enjoy perspective-shifting more than applying perspectives. The constant transformation emphasis can reduce confidence in current understanding. If your thinking change leads you wrong direction, you've modeled problematic cognitive shift. This approach can also devalue stable consistent thinking by implying constant revision is superior. The dramatic framing may reduce appreciation for incremental understanding evolution versus sudden transformation.
