# Why [traditional approach] works for [group A] but fails for [group B]

## Hook Group

Shocking Truths & Discoveries

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as someone understanding context-dependent effectiveness. They see you as analytical about approach-fit variability. You appear as someone whose conditional-effectiveness insight prevents inappropriate approach generalization.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers recognition that one-size-fits-all rarely works. Intellectually activates context-dependent thinking. Psychologically creates appreciation for fit assessment. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "works for one fails for other" promises context awareness enabling appropriate approach selection.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because if approaches work conditionally, context assessment matters - understanding fit prevents misapplication. This hook leverages appeal of nuance where recognizing context-dependence demonstrates sophistication. The "works-for-but-fails-for" framing creates conditional insight. By explaining differential effectiveness, you enable fit assessment. This satisfies desire for understanding when approaches apply versus don't.

## Example

Why traditional job application works for new grads but fails for experienced professionals: entry-level hiring evaluates potential through credentials; senior hiring evaluates proven results through networks - different stages require different strategies. Applying fresh-grad playbook at senior level signals not understanding hiring dynamics. Context determines approach appropriateness; universal strategies miss fit.

## Why it works

This hook works because it reveals context-dependent effectiveness enabling appropriate approach selection. The works-fails framing creates conditional awareness. By explaining differential fit, you prevent misapplication. The hook satisfies desire for conditional understanding. This combination of context awareness, fit assessment, conditional effectiveness, and appropriate selection creates contextually applying audiences.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when you create false dichotomies where both groups can benefit from approach or when you oversimplify nuanced effectiveness into clean categories. If you constantly reveal works-for-fails-for patterns, you seem to see binary effectiveness everywhere. The hook also fails if you use conditional claims to dismiss valuable approaches. If groups you contrast aren't actually differentially responsive, claim misleads. Too many context-dependent revelations can create excessive qualification paralysis.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who endlessly assess fit rather than trying approaches to learn experientially. You may attract people using context-dependence to avoid committing to any approach. The constant conditional focus can reduce appreciation for broadly applicable principles. If your effectiveness claim is wrong, you've misdirected approach selection. This approach can also foster belief everything is so context-dependent nothing generalizes. The fit emphasis may reduce understanding that while context matters, many approaches work broadly with context-appropriate adaptation, not binary works-or-fails.
