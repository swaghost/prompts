# Here's why [approach A] beats [approach B] every time

## Hook Group

Shocking Truths & Discoveries

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as someone who has compared approaches and can definitively declare superiority. They see you as experienced with both to make authoritative comparisons. You appear as someone who saves audiences from inferior approach by sharing conclusive insights.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers relief about choosing right approach. Intellectually activates interest in comparison logic and evidence. Psychologically creates confidence that clear winner exists for their decision. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "every time" suggests universal superiority worth understanding.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because definitive superiority claims ("every time") cut through decision paralysis - audiences want confident guidance. This hook leverages authority through comparison experience where you've tested both approaches. The absolute framing ("every time") creates certainty in domains where audiences feel uncertain. By explaining WHY one beats the other, you provide transferable understanding. This satisfies desire for clear answers in confusing comparison landscapes.

## Example

Here's why async communication beats real-time meetings every time: async allows deep thinking between responses, creates automatic documentation, respects different peak productivity hours, and eliminates performative presence - meetings optimize for appearance of collaboration over actual collaborative output.

## Why it works

This hook works because it provides decisive guidance ending deliberation paralysis. The comparison format helps audiences understand trade-offs through contrast. By claiming universal superiority, you take responsibility for recommendation confidence. The hook satisfies desire for authoritative guidance in complex choices. This combination of decision clarity, trade-off illumination, recommendation ownership, and confident guidance creates grateful decisive audiences.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when you ignore contexts where approach B legitimately excels - universal claims are rarely accurate. If you constantly declare absolute winners, audiences realize oversimplified framing. The hook also fails if you strawman approach B rather than engaging its real strengths. If your comparison favors approach you profit from, objectivity seems compromised. Too many "every time" claims reduce credibility through obvious exaggeration.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who seek simple absolutes rather than understanding contextual trade-offs. You may attract people who want permission to dismiss alternatives rather than thoughtful comparison. The universal framing can reduce nuanced thinking where "it depends" is more accurate than "every time." If audiences use your favored approach in contexts where alternative excels, your recommendation backfires. This approach can also alienate practitioners of approach B you declare universally inferior. The certainty may reduce your credibility among sophisticated audiences who recognize complexity.
