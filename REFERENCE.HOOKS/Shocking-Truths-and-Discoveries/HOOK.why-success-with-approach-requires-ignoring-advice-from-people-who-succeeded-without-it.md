# Why success with [approach] requires ignoring advice from people who succeeded without it

## Hook Group

Shocking Truths & Discoveries

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as someone understanding advice-validity contexts and when guidance misleads. They see you as discerning about whose input applies. You appear as someone whose advice-filtering wisdom enables recognizing when successful people's guidance misapplies to different approaches.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers permission to ignore certain successful people's advice. Intellectually activates context-dependent wisdom thinking. Psychologically creates confidence in approach-specific strategies. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "ignore advice from succeeded-without-it people" promises permission to filter guidance based on approach-relevance.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because if advice validity depends on approach-match, filtering guidance by context matters - success via different paths provides mismatched wisdom. This hook leverages appeal of discernment where recognizing irrelevant advice demonstrates sophistication. The "succeeded without it" framing creates non-applicability awareness. By validating advice-filtering, you enable focused execution. This satisfies desire for understanding when to ignore successful people's guidance.

## Example

Why success with paid ads requires ignoring advice from people who succeeded via organic content: organic creators say "just make great content consistently" - their path built through different mechanism. Ad success needs copywriting, offer design, funnel optimization; organic success needs long-term audience building. Both work; tactics differ completely. Applying organic advice to paid approach wastes money. Different paths require different wisdom.

## Why it works

This hook works because it validates advice-filtering based on approach-match enabling focused execution. The succeeded-without framing creates non-applicability clarity. By emphasizing path differences, you enable discernment. The hook satisfies desire for ignoring mismatched guidance. This combination of filtering validation, context-distinction, path-difference emphasis, and permission-granting creates appropriately selective audiences.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when you use approach-difference to dismiss all external input or when you create false dichotomies between similar approaches. If you constantly validate ignoring advice, you foster closed-mindedness. The hook also fails if you use succeeded-without thinking to avoid challenging feedback. If approaches are more similar than different, distinction is artificial. Too much advice-filtering emphasis can reduce valuable cross-pollination from adjacent successful approaches.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who dismiss all advice from people using different tactics. You may attract people who seek permission to ignore challenging feedback. The constant approach-difference focus can reduce appreciation for universal principles transcending specific tactics. If approaches share more than they differ, you've encouraged ignoring relevant wisdom. This approach can also foster belief your specific path is unique requiring only same-path advice. The filtering emphasis may reduce understanding that while tactics differ, most success principles transfer, and over-filtering limits learning from adjacent approaches' lessons.
