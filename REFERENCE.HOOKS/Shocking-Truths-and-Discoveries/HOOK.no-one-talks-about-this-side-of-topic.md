# No one talks about this side of [topic]

## Hook Group

Shocking Truths & Discoveries

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewers should perceive the creator as observant and willing to discuss neglected aspects of topics that mainstream discourse systematically ignores, positioning them as filling important information gaps others leave open. The creator appears to have deeper understanding or broader experience than typical sources, noticing and valuing dimensions that get overlooked in conventional narratives, making them valuable for accessing complete rather than partial understanding.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Intellectually: Viewers should anticipate learning about overlooked dimensions, underexplored consequences, or hidden aspects that complete their understanding of topics they thought they knew. Emotionally: Curiosity about what's been missing from their knowledge combined with possible frustration or validation about gaps in mainstream discussion. Psychologically: Activation of information-seeking behavior to fill identified gap, desire to access insider knowledge others lack, and potential relief if the neglected aspect explains confusing personal experiences. Physically: Forward-leaning attention focused on absorbing the missing piece of the puzzle.

## Psychology

Secrecy effect → curiosity grows when something feels hidden. The phrase "no one talks about" creates perception of deliberate or accidental omission, triggering curiosity about why the information is absent from typical discussions and what's being missed. This positions the content as rare or exclusive information that viewers cannot easily access elsewhere, increasing perceived value. The implication of collective silence suggests either taboo (too uncomfortable to discuss) or oversight (everyone misses it), both creating urgency to learn what's being suppressed or overlooked.

## Example

No one talks about this side of freelancing.

## Why it works

This hook works because it identifies gaps in mainstream narratives while avoiding the antagonistic framing of "everyone is wrong" approaches. By suggesting an aspect is simply "not talked about" rather than actively misrepresented, it creates curiosity without triggering defensiveness. The hook resonates particularly strongly with people who have direct experience with the topic and have noticed the same omission, creating validation and bonding through shared recognition of narrative gaps. It positions the creator as courageous or observant enough to address what others avoid, building trust through apparent completeness of coverage. The format also creates value through complementing rather than contradicting existing knowledge—viewers don't have to abandon what they already know but rather add missing dimension to their understanding. This makes the content feel additive rather than corrective, creating more positive reception. Additionally, the hook works well for attracting insiders who want validation of their experiences and outsiders who want complete information before committing.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

The hook fails if the "neglected" aspect is actually widely discussed within communities that engage seriously with the topic, making the creator appear uninformed or disconnected from relevant conversations. If the overlooked dimension proves to be minor, obvious, or not particularly important, viewers feel the dramatic framing oversold the significance. The hook also fails when multiple creators are using identical framing for the same topic, creating crowded space of people all claiming to discuss what "no one" discusses. If the creator cannot explain WHY this aspect goes undiscussed (taboo, complexity, marketing interests), the premise feels weak. Additionally, if the content becomes a complaint session about the overlooked aspect without constructive framing or solutions, it feels like venting rather than valuable information sharing. The hook may also fail if viewers believe the aspect IS discussed and disagree with the premise entirely, creating friction from the opening.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This hook can inadvertently offend or alienate other creators who HAVE discussed the topic, creating unnecessary conflict or perception that creator doesn't research or acknowledge others' work. It may create expectation for consistently novel angles that becomes unsustainable, pressuring creator to manufacture "neglected" aspects that aren't actually that important. The format can attract viewers who feel victimized or overlooked, potentially shifting community toward grievance rather than growth mindset. If the "side" being discussed is genuinely taboo or uncomfortable, addressing it may attract negative attention from people who prefer that aspect remain unexamined. The hook may also create pressure to justify why "no one" discusses this, leading to conspiracy theorizing or unfair characterization of others' motivations for omission. Regular use can position creator as perpetually identifying problems or gaps rather than celebrating what works, creating exhaustion. Finally, if the neglected aspect is something gatekeepers deliberately don't discuss to protect beginners, the creator may face criticism for "spoiling" or discouraging people unnecessarily.
