## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because if high percentage of success comes from showing up, persistence beats brilliance - attrition creates opportunity for consistent. ## Example

Staying in game while others leave automatically advances relative position. Success isn't beating everybody; it's outlasting quitters. Showing up when hard beats creating when inspired. Persistence wins through attrition not superiority.

## Why it works

This hook works because it quantifies persistence advantage validating consistency through attrition dynamics. The percentage-showing-up-when-quit framing creates specific encouragement. By emphasizing relative positioning, you enable persistence motivation. The hook satisfies desire for achievable success paths. This combination of quantification, persistence validation, attrition awareness, and accessibility emphasis creates consistently showing audiences.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when you dismiss quality's importance or when you overstate percentage without evidence. If you constantly emphasize showing up over excellence, you romanticize persistence without skill development. The hook also fails if you suggest longevity alone guarantees success. If percentage is your estimate not validated, specificity is false precision. Too many showing-up messages can reduce appreciation for strategic quality improvement.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who persist in wrong directions believing consistency alone sufficient. You may attract people using showing-up philosophy to avoid improving quality. The constant persistence emphasis can reduce appreciation for skill development enabling higher-quality contribution. If you overstate showing-up importance, audiences may underinvest in capability building. This approach can also foster belief consistency beats competence. The attrition emphasis may reduce understanding that while showing up matters significantly, sustainable success usually requires both consistent showing plus continuous quality improvement.
