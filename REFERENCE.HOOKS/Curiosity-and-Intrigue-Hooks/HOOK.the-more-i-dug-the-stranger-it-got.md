# The more I dug, the stranger it got

## Hook Group

Curiosity & Intrigue Hooks

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as depth-investigator uncovering escalating-strangeness. They see you as rabbit-hole-explorer. You appear as someone whose stranger-it-got emphasis demonstrates you discover increasing-weirdness enabling audience to follow progressive-revelation journey.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers escalating-curiosity. Intellectually activates escalation-intrigue thinking. Psychologically creates need for completion. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "stranger it got" promises revealing that investigation uncovered progressively-bizarre discoveries requiring explanation.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because creates escalation intrigue - if got stranger with depth, investigation-journey contains building-revelations. This hook leverages appeal of progressive-discovery where each layer reveals more-unusual elements. The more-I-dug framing creates journey-narrative. By emphasizing escalating-strangeness, you enable suspense-building. This satisfies desire for adventure-story through deepening-investigation revealing increasing-oddity.

## Example

The more I dug into this niche, the stranger it got. Surface looked normal. First layer revealed unusual patterns. Deeper layer showed bizarre behavior. Each level increased weirdness. Progressive investigation uncovered escalating-oddity. Depth correlated with strangeness. Journey became increasingly surprising.

## Why it works

This hook works because it builds through escalation-framing teaching progressive-revelation. The stranger-it-got creates suspense-accumulation. By showing normal-to-unusual-to-bizarre progression, you demonstrate concrete escalation. The hook satisfies desire for journey-story. This combination of building-teaching, suspense-accumulation, escalation-demonstration, and story-provision creates audiences who follow completely understanding investigation-depth often reveals progressively-unusual discoveries creating compelling narrative.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when escalation is manufactured or when strangeness doesn't actually increase. If you constantly claim escalating-weirdness but progression is flat, you lose credibility. The hook also fails if you use strange-claiming to sensationalize ordinary findings. If "stranger" aspects are forced or insignificant, framing feels manipulative. Too much escalation-emphasis can reduce trust and create belief you exaggerate for drama.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who expect constant escalation becoming dissatisfied with steady information. You may attract sensation-seekers who use strange-excuse to dismiss normal-but-valuable findings. The constant escalating-intrigue emphasis can reduce appreciation for how most valuable insights come at consistent revelation-level. If only valuing escalation never accepting steady-value, followers become drama-dependent. This approach can also foster sensationalism. The progressive emphasis may reduce understanding that while some investigations reveal escalating-strangeness, most valuable research produces steady-insight without drama, and excessive escalation-seeking often produces sensationalism replacing substantive learning.
