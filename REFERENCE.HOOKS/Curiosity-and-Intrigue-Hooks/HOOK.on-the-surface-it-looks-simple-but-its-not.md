This hook fails when you overstate complexity or when simple things are genuinely simple. If you constantly claim hidden-complexity but depth is minimal, you lose credibility. Too much depth-emphasis can reduce appreciation for how genuine-simplicity has value and create belief everything requires complex-analysis.
