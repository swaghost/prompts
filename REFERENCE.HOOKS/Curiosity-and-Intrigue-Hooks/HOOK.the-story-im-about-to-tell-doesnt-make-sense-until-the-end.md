# The story I'm about to tell doesn't make sense—until the end

## Hook Group

Curiosity & Intrigue Hooks

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as narrative-architect building to satisfying-resolution. They see you as storytelling-master. You appear as someone whose until-the-end emphasis demonstrates you structure compelling-narratives enabling audience to trust confusing-journey leads to clarity-payoff.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers commitment to story-completion. Intellectually activates open-loop-to-closure thinking. Psychologically creates need for resolution. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "until the end" promises revealing that confusing-elements resolve into satisfying-coherent conclusion.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because creates open loop leading to closure - if doesn't make sense until end, completion provides resolution-satisfaction. This hook leverages appeal of narrative-resolution where confusion creates completion-need. The until-the-end framing creates commitment. By promising eventual-sense-making, you enable patience-with-confusion. This satisfies desire for resolution through trusting confusing-journey reaches clarifying-destination.

## Example

The story I'm about to tell about my first client doesn't make sense—until the end. Started with rejection. Led to better opportunity. Failure enabled success. Confusing setback revealed blessing. Nonsensical progression reached clear conclusion. Journey clarified at destination.

## Why it works

This hook works because it commits through promise-of-resolution framing teaching trust-the-process. The doesn't-make-sense creates intrigue-with-promise. By showing rejection-to-opportunity progression, you demonstrate concrete confusion-to-clarity. The hook satisfies desire for narrative-payoff. This combination of commitment-teaching, intrigue-promise, clarity-demonstration, and payoff-satisfaction creates audiences who stay engaged understanding confusing-narratives often lead to satisfying-resolutions rewarding patience.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when resolution disappoints or when you create artificial confusion. If you constantly promise sense-at-end but conclusions are weak, you lose credibility. The hook also fails if you use confusion-claiming to justify rambling-narratives. If story genuinely doesn't have satisfying resolution, framing sets false-expectations. Too much confusion-emphasis can reduce clarity and create belief obfuscation equals depth.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who tolerate unnecessary-confusion accepting poor-storytelling claiming patience. You may attract people who use until-the-end-excuse to justify unclear-communication. The constant open-loop emphasis can reduce appreciation for how clear-communication shouldn't require confusion-tolerance. If only creating confusing-narratives never communicating clearly, followers develop acceptance of poor-clarity. This approach can also foster tolerance-of-obfuscation. The resolution emphasis may reduce understanding that while some stories build through confusion, most effective communication provides clarity throughout, and excessive confusion-reliance often produces tolerance-of-poor-communication replacing expectation of clear-explanation.
