# What you assume is the answer… isn't

## Hook Group

Curiosity & Intrigue Hooks

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as assumption-challenger exposing false-beliefs. They see you as conventional-wisdom-disruptor. You appear as someone whose assume-isn't emphasis demonstrates you reveal truth-behind-assumptions enabling audience to question default-beliefs through belief-disruption creating paradigm-shifts.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers curiosity about wrong-assumptions. Intellectually activates belief-disruption thinking. Psychologically creates assumption-questioning mindset. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "isn't the answer" promises revealing that confident-default-belief is incorrect creating need-to-know what-truth-actually-is.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because creates belief disruption - if assume-answer isn't, confident-belief proves wrong making correction urgent. This hook leverages appeal of assumption-challenging where questioning-defaults reveals hidden-truth. The assume-isn't framing creates cognitive-dissonance. By suggesting wrong-default-belief, you enable paradigm-shift. This satisfies desire for accurate-understanding through correcting false-confident-assumptions revealing actual-truth that contradicts-intuition.

## Example

What you assume is the answer to low engagement… isn't. Most think: post-more-frequently. Actual-answer: post-more-consistently-same-time. Frequency-assumption proves wrong. Timing-consistency matters more. Default-belief misleads. Counter-intuitive-truth revealed. Assumption-correction shifts approach.

## Why it works

This hook works because it corrects through assumption-challenging framing teaching belief-questioning value. The assume-isn't creates urgent-correction-need. By showing consistency beating frequency, you demonstrate concrete false-default-belief. The hook satisfies desire for accurate-knowledge. This combination of correction-teaching, urgency-creation, belief-demonstration, and accuracy-provision creates audiences who question assumptions understanding confident-default-beliefs often prove wrong requiring correction revealing counter-intuitive-actual-truths.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when common-assumption is actually correct or when you manufacture false-default-beliefs. If you constantly claim assume-wrong but conventional-wisdom proves right, credibility suffers. The hook also fails if you use disruption-claiming to appear contrarian without valid-counter-evidence. If assumption-challenging lacks substance, framing feels empty-contrarianism. Too much belief-disruption can reduce trust in valuable-conventional-wisdom and create belief all-defaults are wrong.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who distrust all assumptions including valid-reasonable-defaults developing chronic-skepticism. You may attract contrarians who use assume-wrong-excuse to reject all-conventional-wisdom claiming everything-common is false. The constant belief-disruption emphasis can reduce appreciation for how many-default-assumptions prove correct through collective-wisdom. If only questioning-assumptions never trusting reasonable-defaults, followers develop decision-paralysis. This approach can also foster pathological-skepticism. The disruption emphasis may reduce understanding that while some assumptions deserve-questioning, most default-beliefs work reasonably-well, and excessive assumption-challenging often produces pathological-skepticism replacing balanced-belief-evaluation.
