# These 3 tiny habits rebuilt my momentum

## Hook Group

Motivational & Inspirational Hooks

## Psychology

Small wins effect (micro-actions feel doable and compound). This leverages the psychological principle that small, manageable changes are far less intimidating than massive overhauls, making it easier for people to take the first step. The promise of "rebuilding momentum" addresses a common pain point of feeling stuck or burnt out.

## Example

These 3 five-minute rituals rebuilt my focus after burnout.

## Why it works

The small wins effect works because it reduces the psychological friction of starting. When faced with a major life change, people often feel overwhelmed and paralyzed by the magnitude of what's required. By breaking down transformation into "tiny habits," the hook removes the excuse of "I don't have time" or "It's too hard." The specific number "3" makes the commitment feel finite and manageable, while "rebuilt my momentum" suggests a complete transformation from a relatable starting point (being stuck). The word "tiny" is deliberately chosen to lower resistance, while "rebuilt" implies that momentum was once there and can be regained, offering hope that change is possible. This combination makes the reader think "If these small things worked for them, I can definitely do this too."
