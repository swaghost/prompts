# What I wish I knew before [milestone]

## Hook Group

Relatable Hooks

## Psychology

Pre-emptive wisdom.

## Example

What I wish I knew before quitting my job to freelance.

## Why it works

Pre-emptive wisdom hooks work by sharing hindsight knowledge that would have changed the creator's approach if known earlier, providing viewers the advantage of learning without personally experiencing the mistakes. The phrase "I wish I knew" carries emotional weight—it suggests real cost or pain from not having this information earlier, making the wisdom feel valuable because it was earned through difficult experience. The structure promises to compress years of learning into actionable insights, giving viewers a head start by revealing what's genuinely important before a major transition or milestone. This is particularly valuable for people approaching similar milestones who want to avoid preventable mistakes. The example about quitting a job to freelance addresses a major career transition many people contemplate but fear due to uncertainty. The wisdom might include: save more runway than you think, establish client relationships before quitting, expect irregular income and emotional challenges, understand the administrative burden of running your own business, or recognize that freedom comes with its own stresses. These insights help people make informed decisions about whether to pursue freelancing and how to prepare properly if they do. The hook works because it provides strategic advantage through pattern recognition—the creator has already walked the path and can warn about pitfalls or highlight what truly matters versus what seems important but isn't. It positions the creator as a guide who wants to smooth the path for others, building trust through generosity with hard-won knowledge.
