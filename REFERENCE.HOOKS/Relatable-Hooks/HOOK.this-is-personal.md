# This is personal

## Hook Group

Relatable Hooks

## Psychology

Targeted frustration.

## Example

This is personal—Wi-Fi that says "connected" but isn't working.

## Why it works

Personal-grievance hooks work by treating impersonal annoyances as deliberate attacks worthy of taking personally, creating humor through exaggerated emotional investment in technical problems. The phrase "this is personal" suggests ongoing feud or vendetta, applying interpersonal-conflict language to situations that have no malicious intent or agency. This personification creates humor through treating neutral circumstances as if they're deliberately targeting you, making frustration feel more valid through framing it as response to attack rather than random bad luck. The format also creates engagement through shared recognition of frustrations that feel targeted despite being impersonal. The structure works particularly well for technical problems or coincidental bad timing that create disproportionate frustration. The example about Wi-Fi showing "connected" while not actually providing internet access captures specific modern frustration: your device says it's connected to Wi-Fi, suggesting everything should work, yet you have no actual internet access. This creates unique frustration because the problem is invisible and difficult to troubleshoot—the connection appears fine but functionally isn't. You're stuck between connection and no-connection states, unable to proceed with online tasks but unsure what's wrong. The false positive of "connected" status makes it feel like deliberate deception worthy of personal offense. The hook works because it validates disproportionate frustration with technical problems by framing them as personal attacks, giving permission to have strong emotional reactions to impersonal difficulties. It builds community through recognition that certain frustrations feel uniquely targeted despite being common technical issues, creating bonding through collective treatment of neutral problems as personal enemies.
