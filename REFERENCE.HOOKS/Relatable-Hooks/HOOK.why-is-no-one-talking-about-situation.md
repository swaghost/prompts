# Why is no one talking about [situation]

## Hook Group

Relatable Hooks

## Psychology

Silent problem spotlight.

## Example

Why is no one talking about how exhausting being "on" at work is?

## Why it works

Silence-breaking hooks work by calling attention to common experiences that aren't discussed publicly, creating validation through acknowledgment of shared but unspoken struggles. The question "why is no one talking about" serves multiple purposes: it identifies the gap between private experience and public conversation, suggests the silence is notable or even problematic, and invites discussion to fill that gap. The structure implies conspiracy of silence or cultural taboo around the topic, making discussion feel brave or important. The format also validates that if you're experiencing something nobody discusses, the silence itself is the problem rather than your experience being invalid. The example about exhaustion from being "on" at work addresses real but rarely acknowledged phenomenon: the emotional and cognitive labor of maintaining professional persona, managing others' perception of you, performing competence and enthusiasm, and staying "on brand" throughout work hours. This performance is draining yet discussing it risks seeming unprofessional or uncommitted. The quotation marks around "on" acknowledge that this is performance distinct from authentic self-expression, highlighting the disconnect between professional mask and actual state. The hook works because it transforms private frustrations into public conversations, creating relief through discovery that unspoken struggles are widely shared. It builds community by breaking silence around difficult topics, positioning the creator as willing to discuss what others avoid. The question format invites engagement as viewers offer theories about why the topic isn't discussed or share their own experiences, building collective conversation around previously isolated struggles.
