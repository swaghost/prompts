# My ex broke up with me because I was too focused on [thing]

## Hook Group

Relatable Hooks

## Psychology

Personal drama relatability (mixes vulnerability + ambition tension).

## Example

My ex broke up with me because I was too focused on building my business.

## Why it works

Personal-drama hooks work by sharing intimate relationship struggles that create emotional connection while exploring the tension between different life priorities. Breakup stories are inherently engaging because relationships matter deeply to most people, making the stakes feel high and the vulnerability real. The structure "my ex broke up with me because" creates immediate narrative tension and curiosity about what behavior or priority was significant enough to end a relationship. By attributing the breakup to focus on something ambitious like building a business, the hook creates complex emotions: sympathy for the personal loss, respect for the dedication to a goal, and curiosity about whether the sacrifice was worth it. This ambiguity—was this a tragedy or a necessary sacrifice for success?—creates engagement as viewers form their own judgments and want to hear the resolution. The hook also resonates with anyone who has struggled with work-life balance, especially entrepreneurs who pour enormous time and energy into building something while relationships suffer. The example perfectly captures the entrepreneur's dilemma: building a business requires intense focus and sacrifice, which can strain or break relationships with people who don't share or support that vision. The hook works because it humanizes the pursuit of ambitious goals by showing their real costs, not just the potential rewards typically highlighted in entrepreneur content. It creates relatability through honest admission that success often requires difficult tradeoffs, and builds engagement through curiosity about whether those tradeoffs ultimately led to success that justified the sacrifice.
