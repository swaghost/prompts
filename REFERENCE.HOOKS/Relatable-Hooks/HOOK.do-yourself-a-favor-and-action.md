# Do yourself a favor and [action]

## Hook Group

Relatable Hooks

## Psychology

Self-care directive.

## Example

Do yourself a favor and stop checking if they viewed your story.

## Why it works

Self-care-directive hooks work by framing advice as gift to yourself rather than obligation or criticism, creating motivation through self-kindness rather than guilt. The phrase "do yourself a favor" positions following the advice as act of self-care and self-respect, making it feel like treating yourself well rather than following rules. This framing creates positive motivation because the benefit is positioned as accruing to you directly rather than meeting external standards. The imperative format is directive but comes from place of care rather than judgment, making it feel like friend looking out for you rather than authority figure demanding compliance. The structure works particularly well for stopping behaviors that cause you harm or stress, positioning cessation as favor to yourself. The example about stopping checking story views addresses specific social media anxiety behavior: obsessively checking who viewed your story (especially whether specific person you care about viewed it), which creates anxiety cycle—checking provides temporary information but reinforces the importance of that information, leading to more checking. The behavior is both understandable (curiosity about who's interested in your content) and harmful (creates unnecessary anxiety, perceived rejection when certain people don't view, waste of mental energy). Stopping this checking is genuinely a favor to yourself through reducing anxiety. The hook works because it reframes stopping behaviors as self-gift rather than deprivation, making change feel positive rather than restrictive. It positions self-care as active kindness to yourself rather than avoiding harm, creating motivation through self-compassion. The directive format creates clear actionable advice while the "favor" framing makes following it feel like choice that benefits you rather than obligation you should fulfill.
