# Nobody prepares you for [situation]

## Hook Group

Relatable Hooks

## Psychology

Unspoken truth hook.

## Example

Nobody prepares you for how draining small talk is at networking events.

## Why it works

Preparation-gap hooks work by identifying experiences that catch people unprepared because they're systematically excluded from advice, education, or preparation. The phrase "nobody prepares you for" creates the sense that important information is missing from standard guidance, leaving people to discover difficult realities through experience rather than forewarning. This builds community among people who encountered the same surprising challenge while creating value by filling the preparation gap for those who haven't faced it yet. The structure also validates difficulty—if nobody prepared you for it, struggling isn't your failure but a gap in collective preparation. The format positions challenges as predictable and worth warning about rather than unique personal struggles. The example about networking small talk being draining is particularly effective because networking is typically framed as opportunity and taught as skill (elevator pitch, business card exchange, follow-up) without acknowledging the emotional and social exhaustion of sustained small talk with strangers while performing professional competence and likability. The cognitive load of tracking conversations, remembering names, presenting well, and maintaining energy across dozens of brief interactions is genuinely depleting yet rarely discussed. People often feel alone in finding networking exhausting because it's framed as positive opportunity rather than acknowledged as emotionally demanding performance. The hook works because it validates hidden difficulties that people discover through experience rather than preparation, creating relief that struggling with "opportunities" is normal. It positions the creator as honest about complete reality rather than selling simplified narratives, building trust through inclusion of uncomfortable truths others omit.
