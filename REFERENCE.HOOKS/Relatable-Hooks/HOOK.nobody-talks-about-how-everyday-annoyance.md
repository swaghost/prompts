# Nobody talks about how [everyday annoyance]

## Hook Group

Relatable Hooks

## Psychology

Overlooked truths engage.

## Example

Nobody talks about how waiting for replies is the worst.

## Why it works

Overlooked-truth hooks work by naming frustrations that are common but rarely explicitly discussed, creating validation and relief when someone finally articulates what everyone experiences silently. The phrase "nobody talks about" identifies a gap between private experience and public conversation, suggesting this annoyance is widely felt but somehow doesn't make it into typical discourse. This creates curiosity about whether the experience is truly widespread or just the creator's perception, driving engagement as viewers confirm whether they've noticed this too. The hook also positions the creator as willing to discuss what others avoid or overlook, building trust through willingness to name uncomfortable truths. The format validates that if something bothers you but nobody discusses it, you're not oversensitive—it's genuinely annoying and the silence around it is the aberration. The example about waiting for replies captures modern communication anxiety that's intensely frustrating but difficult to discuss without sounding needy or impatient. The waiting period after sending a message (especially important ones like job applications, date planning, or business inquiries) creates suspension where you can't move forward until you receive response, leading to constant phone checking, second-guessing what you sent, and anxiety about whether/when response will come. The "worst" framing validates that this waiting genuinely feels terrible despite being objectively minor. The hook works because it validates minor frustrations by bringing them into explicit discussion, creating permission to acknowledge that small annoyances genuinely affect mood and wellbeing. It builds community through recognition that silent frustrations are actually shared, creating bonding through mutual validation of experiences everyone has but nobody discusses.
