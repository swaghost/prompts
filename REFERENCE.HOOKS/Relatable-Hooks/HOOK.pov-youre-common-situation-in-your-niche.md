# POV: You're [common situation in your niche]

## Hook Group

Relatable Hooks

## Psychology

Mirroring (people feel understood when their struggles are reflected).

## Example

POV: You're trying to grow your business but every post flops.

## Why it works

POV (point of view) hooks work by directly mirroring the viewer's current experience, creating instant recognition and connection. The phrase "POV: You're" invites the audience to step into a specific scenario that reflects their reality, making them feel seen and understood. This mirroring effect is psychologically powerful because it validates the viewer's experience—they're not alone in their struggle. When people see their exact situation described, they experience a moment of recognition that triggers engagement: "That's exactly me right now." The hook leverages the TikTok/Instagram convention of POV content, which has trained audiences to expect relatable, immersive scenarios. By focusing on a "common situation in your niche," the creator demonstrates understanding of their audience's specific context rather than generic problems, building trust and authority. The example about business posts flopping is particularly effective because it captures a universal frustration for entrepreneurs and content creators—the discouragement of putting effort into content that gets no traction. This vulnerability creates immediate empathy. The hook works because it doesn't just describe a problem abstractly but places the viewer directly inside the experience, making the content feel personally relevant rather than theoretical. This emotional resonance dramatically increases the likelihood they'll keep watching to see the resolution or guidance offered.
