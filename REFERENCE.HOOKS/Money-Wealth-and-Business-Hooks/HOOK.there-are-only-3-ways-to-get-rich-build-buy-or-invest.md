# There are only 3 ways to get rich: build, buy, or invest

## Hook Group

Money, Wealth & Business Hooks

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as wealth-framework teacher simplifying path-options. They see you as strategy-clarifier. You appear as someone whose three-ways simplification demonstrates you understand wealth-building follows limited pathways enabling audience to choose strategic direction.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers clarity through simplification. Intellectually activates framework-thinking. Psychologically creates strategic decision-empowerment. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "only 3 ways" promises revealing complete wealth-path framework.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because creates framework-simplification - if only three paths exist, choosing direction becomes manageable. This hook leverages appeal of clarity where limited options enable decision. The only-3 framing creates completeness. By providing build-buy-invest framework, you enable strategic selection. This satisfies desire for wealth-roadmap through path-clarification.

## Example

Build business = create value. Buy business = acquire value. Invest capital = multiply value. Three paths, same destination. Creation, acquisition, multiplication. Different approaches, common outcome. Framework clarifies options enabling strategic choice.

## Why it works

This hook works because it simplifies through framework-provision teaching path-clarity. The only-3 creates digestibility. By defining build-buy-invest clearly, you demonstrate complete options. The hook satisfies desire for strategic direction. This combination of simplification-teaching, digestibility-creation, option-demonstration, and direction-provision creates audiences who choose wealth-paths strategically understanding limited options enable focused action.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when you oversimplify wealth-building or when you ignore execution-complexity within each path. If you constantly emphasize only-3, you may understate how each path requires extensive skills. The hook also fails if you use framework-claiming to suggest wealth-building is simple. If execution within paths is extremely complex, simple framing misleads. Too much simplification can reduce appreciation for how each path requires years mastering and create belief choosing path alone builds wealth.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who believe choosing a path is sufficient without understanding execution-mastery. You may attract people who use framework-excuse to feel oriented without developing capabilities. The constant only-3 emphasis can reduce appreciation for how execution within paths requires extensive learning. If only choosing direction without building skills, followers make symbolic choices without real progress. This approach can also foster oversimplification. The framework emphasis may reduce understanding that while path-selection matters, most wealth-building requires both strategic direction AND years mastering execution within chosen path, and simple choice often produces false confidence without capability development.
