# Every expert I know agrees on this one principle.

## Hook Group

Authority & Expertise Hooks

## Desired Viewer Perception

The viewer should perceive this as consensus wisdom among top practitioners—not one person's opinion but universal agreement among experts, suggesting fundamental truth that transcends individual styles or approaches.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Viewers should feel:

- Confidence in universally agreed-upon wisdom
- Trust in consensus-based knowledge
- Curiosity about what experts universally agree on
- Validation that fundamental truths exist and are knowable
- Relief at finding non-controversial, broadly accepted guidance
- Appreciation for distilled collective wisdom

## Psychology

Consensus among experts carries special weight because it suggests the principle isn't opinion, preference, or style—it's fundamental truth that everyone who has achieved mastery recognizes. This taps into social proof at the expert level, not just crowd wisdom but validated practitioner agreement. It also addresses our exhaustion with contradictory advice—when experts agree despite different approaches, backgrounds, and styles, it suggests universal truth. The phrase "every expert" is absolute, creating powerful certainty in a world of conflicting information. It positions the creator as having access to multiple expert perspectives, suggesting broader insight than individual practitioners.

## Example

"Every expert I know agrees on this one principle: systems compound, motivation fluctuates—build systems."

## Why it works

This hook leverages expert consensus to create maximum credibility and certainty. "Every expert" is a bold, absolute claim that creates strong anticipation—what could possibly be so fundamental that all experts agree? It positions the principle as non-negotiable, foundational truth rather than subjective opinion or situational advice. The creator's access to multiple experts suggests they're well-connected and have synthesized broad wisdom. It also creates safety—if every expert agrees, following this advice feels like a guaranteed good decision. The format cuts through noise and conflicting advice by identifying universal agreement, providing clarity viewers desperately want.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

The hook fails when the principle shared is too obvious, too general, or something anyone would know without expert validation. It loses credibility if viewers can name respected experts who would disagree with the principle, undermining the "every expert" claim. If the creator can't demonstrate access to multiple experts or if the experts aren't credible in the field, the consensus claim feels fabricated. The hook also underperforms when the principle is too abstract to be actionable or when its universality makes it feel toothless—so broadly acceptable it doesn't provide specific guidance. When there's actually significant expert disagreement on the topic, the claim appears either ignorant or dishonest.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This hook can stifle innovation and contrarian thinking by suggesting expert consensus is always right, when breakthrough insights often come from challenging established wisdom. It may create false certainty about complex topics where nuance and context matter more than universal principles. The absolute "every expert" claim invites scrutiny and counterexamples, potentially leading to debates that distract from the actual advice. If the consensus principle later proves less universal or effective than claimed, it damages the creator's judgment and research credibility. The hook can also make the creator appear risk-averse or unoriginal, just echoing what everyone else says rather than offering unique insights.
