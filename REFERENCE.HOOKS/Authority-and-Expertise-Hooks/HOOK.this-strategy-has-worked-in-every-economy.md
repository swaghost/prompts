# This strategy has worked in every economy.

## Hook Group

Authority & Expertise Hooks

## Desired Viewer Perception

The viewer should perceive this as recession-proof, crisis-tested advice that transcends economic cycles and market conditions. They should see the creator as someone offering stability and reliability regardless of external circumstances.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Viewers should feel:

- Security knowing the advice works in both good times and bad
- Relief from economic anxiety and uncertainty
- Confidence in a recession-proof approach
- Trust in the universal applicability
- Motivation to implement something reliable
- Freedom from timing concerns or market conditions

## Psychology

Economic uncertainty creates deep anxiety because our survival and security depend on financial stability. A strategy that works "in every economy" addresses fundamental fears about downturns, recessions, and market volatility. This taps into our desire for control and predictability in an unpredictable world. The claim suggests the advice is based on fundamental human behavior or universal principles rather than temporary market conditions, making it feel more valuable and timeless. It also implies the speaker has either lived through multiple economic cycles or studied them extensively.

## Example

"This strategy has worked in every economy: build skills that solve problems people have regardless of GDP."

## Why it works

This hook provides psychological safety by removing timing risk and economic anxiety from the equation. The universal applicability makes the advice feel more valuable because it's not dependent on specific market conditions or perfect timing. It positions the strategy as fundamental rather than tactical, suggesting it works because it's based on unchanging human needs or behaviors. The claim also implies extensive testing across different contexts, which builds credibility. For viewers worried about economic conditions or uncertain times, this offers relief and a sense of control.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

The hook fails if the strategy shared is too general, obvious, or something everyone already does. It loses credibility when viewers can immediately think of economic conditions where the strategy wouldn't work, undermining the "every economy" claim. If the advice requires significant capital or resources that aren't available during recessions, it contradicts the premise. The hook also underperforms when it's not clear the speaker has actually experienced or studied multiple economic cycles, making the claim feel aspirational rather than evidential. Vague strategies without specific implementation details will disappoint.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This hook can create skepticism because absolute claims like "every economy" invite scrutiny and counterexamples. It may attract desperate viewers in difficult economic times who are looking for guarantees, leading to disappointment when they discover success still requires effort and adaptation. The hook can make the creator seem out of touch if the strategy isn't realistically accessible during recessions or doesn't account for individual circumstances. If the strategy later fails during a specific economic condition, it can severely damage credibility and lead to accusations of false advertising or oversimplification of complex economic realities.
