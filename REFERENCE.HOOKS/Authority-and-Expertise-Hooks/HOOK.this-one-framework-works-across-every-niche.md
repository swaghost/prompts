# This one framework works across every niche

## Hook Group

Authority & Expertise Hooks

## Desired Viewer Preception

Viewer perceives you as someone who has distilled universal principles that transcend specific industries. They see you as a systems thinker who understands fundamental patterns. You appear as someone whose framework knowledge provides transferable value regardless of niche, establishing you as a meta-level expert.

## Desired Viewer Emotional/Intellectual/Psychological/Physical Elicitations or Response

Emotionally triggers relief that success principles are learnable and applicable. Intellectually activates pattern-recognition thinking across domains. Psychologically creates confidence that your expertise transfers to their situation. Physically manifests as stopping scroll because "works across every niche" promises universal applicability eliminating niche-specific barriers.

## Psychology

The mind interrupts to pay attention because universality signals fundamental truth rather than surface tactics - if it works everywhere, it must be deeply valid. This hook leverages appeal of transferable knowledge where cross-niche validation demonstrates principle-level understanding. The "across every niche" framing creates confidence through breadth. By positioning framework as universal, you establish meta-expertise. This satisfies desire for foundational knowledge that applies regardless of specific context.

## Example

This one framework works across every niche: hook formula works whether you're a coach, agency, or creator - problem-agitate-solve structure transcends industry. Coaches use it for client pain, agencies for business challenges, creators for audience problems. Framework is niche-agnostic; only application details change. Universal principles beat niche-specific tactics. Core psychology remains constant across contexts.

## Why it works

This hook works because it positions your expertise at the principle level rather than tactical level, demonstrating deep understanding. The cross-niche framing creates trust through universality - if it works in multiple domains, the underlying truth must be solid. By offering a framework that transfers, you enable audience to apply your knowledge regardless of their specific niche. The hook satisfies desire for fundamental knowledge rather than surface-level tricks. This combination of universal applicability, principle-level positioning, transferable value, and fundamental truth creates audiences who trust your expertise transcends their specific context.

## Pitfalls - How it can fail

This hook fails when your framework is actually niche-specific dressed up as universal or when you oversimplify complex domain-specific nuances. If you constantly claim universal applicability, you seem to ignore important contextual differences that matter. The hook also fails if you use universality claims to avoid developing deep niche expertise. If framework doesn't actually transfer well, claim damages credibility. Too many "works everywhere" assertions can reduce appreciation for specialized knowledge and domain-specific adaptations.

## Unintended Consequences - How it can backfire

This can backfire by creating audiences who apply frameworks mechanically without adapting to context, leading to poor results. You may attract people who seek magic formulas rather than understanding underlying principles requiring thoughtful application. The constant universality focus can reduce appreciation for niche-specific expertise and domain knowledge that actually matters for execution. If your framework fails in certain contexts, you've oversold its applicability. This approach can also foster belief that surface-level principles alone drive success. The universal emphasis may reduce understanding that while some frameworks do transfer well, most expertise requires both universal principles AND context-specific adaptation for optimal results.
