**Usage Tips:**

1. **Stack Triggers:** Combine 2-3 psychological mechanisms for more powerful hooks (e.g., Loss Aversion + Specificity)
2. **Match to Audience State:** Use curiosity for cold audiences, authority for warm, conversion for hot
3. **Test Combinations:** Try pairing contrarian framing with social proof, or identity recognition with transformation
4. **Rotate Mechanisms:** Don't over-rely on one trigger; varied psychology keeps content fresh
