# Last week I was doing **_ when _** happened

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Story hook + tension curve. This hook uses narrative structure to create immediate engagement, with the setup creating curiosity about what unexpected event or realization occurred.

## Example

Last week I was on a client call when this mindset shift clicked.

## Why it works

Narrative hooks tap into our brain's natural preference for stories over facts. By starting with a temporal anchor ("Last week"), the hook immediately grounds the story in recent, relatable time, making it feel current and relevant rather than distant anecdote. The structure "I was doing X when Y happened" creates inherent tension and anticipation—something unexpected is coming. This two-part structure mimics natural storytelling where setup leads to payoff, and our brains are wired to want resolution once a story begins. The recency also suggests the insight is fresh and hasn't been over-processed into abstraction, which makes it feel more authentic. The example is particularly effective because it describes a common professional scenario (client call) being interrupted or enhanced by an unexpected insight. The phrase "mindset shift clicked" promises a clear aha moment that transformed understanding, which is highly appealing to people seeking breakthroughs in their own thinking. This hook works because it promises both practical context and transformative insight delivered through an engaging story structure rather than dry instruction.
