# I was surprised by [method]—it works

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Tech-assisted leverage. This hook often highlights methods involving tools or technology that exceeded expectations, suggesting smart use of technology can amplify results.

## Example

Using AI outlines sped up my writing.

## Why it works

Technology-adoption hooks work by addressing skepticism or uncertainty about new tools while providing social proof of effectiveness. The phrase "I was surprised" acknowledges that adoption wasn't automatic—the creator had doubts, which validates readers' own hesitation. The simple declaration "it works" is powerful because it's definitive and results-focused, cutting through hype and nuance to deliver a clear verdict. This is particularly valuable in technology adoption where there's often confusion about whether new tools are genuinely useful or just trendy. The example about AI-assisted writing is timely and addresses a common concern: will AI help or hinder creative work? By specifying "outlines," the example shows sophisticated use—AI for structure, human for content—which demonstrates thoughtful integration rather than replacement. The verb "sped up" quantifies a practical benefit (efficiency) that matters to busy professionals. The hook works because it provides the social proof needed to overcome adoption hesitation while showing respectful, augmentative use of technology. It suggests that skeptics should reconsider based on practical results rather than hype, which is more persuasive than uncritical enthusiasm. It positions technology as leverage rather than solution or threat.
