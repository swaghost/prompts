# How to get ___ fast

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Speed promise reduces perceived effort and increases urgency. By emphasizing speed, this hook addresses one of the biggest barriers to action: the belief that achieving the goal will take too long or require too much sustained effort.

## Example

How to get your site indexed on Google fast.

## Why it works

The word "fast" is psychologically powerful because it directly addresses time anxiety and impatience. In a world where people are overwhelmed with competing priorities, the promise of speed makes the goal feel more achievable and reduces the psychological burden of commitment. This hook works because it reframes the goal from a long-term project to a quick win, which is far less intimidating. The simple "How to" structure provides procedural clarity—readers know they'll get actionable steps, not just theory. The combination of a desirable outcome with a fast timeline creates a compelling urgency: "I could have this result soon, so why wait?" This is particularly effective for technical or complex-sounding goals (like Google indexing) because it demystifies the process and suggests there's a shortcut or insider method that makes it easier than expected.
