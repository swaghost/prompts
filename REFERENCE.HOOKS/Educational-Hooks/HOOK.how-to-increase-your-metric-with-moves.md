# How to increase your [metric] with [#] moves

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Countable, doable steps. This hook provides both a specific goal (metric improvement) and a defined number of actions required, making the path to success feel structured and achievable.

## Example

How to increase sales with 3 emails.

## Why it works

Countable-steps hooks work by removing ambiguity from both goal and method. The specific metric makes the outcome measurable and verifiable, while the specific number of moves makes the required effort clear and bounded. This answers the two key questions: "what will I achieve?" and "what will it take?" The low number (typically 3-5 moves) makes the process feel manageable rather than overwhelming, reducing resistance to starting. Each "move" implies a discrete action rather than vague advice, suggesting the guidance will be concrete and actionable. The example about increasing sales with "3 emails" is particularly effective because it's extremely specific and modest—not "build a comprehensive email nurture sequence" but literally three emails. This specificity makes the advice feel immediately implementable. The emails might be: 1) Re-engagement to past leads, 2) Clear value proposition to current prospects, 3) Limited-time offer to fence-sitters. The constraint of three emails forces focus on highest-impact messages rather than email overload. The hook works because it transforms potentially overwhelming goals (increase sales) into simple, countable actions that feel within reach. The numeric specificity also creates memorability—people can remember and visualize "3 moves" far better than general advice to "improve your email marketing."
