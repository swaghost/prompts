# The truth about [myth]

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Myth-busting novelty. This hook promises to reveal reality behind a common misconception or oversimplification, creating curiosity about what's actually true versus what's widely believed.

## Example

The truth about "followers = sales."

## Why it works

Truth-revelation hooks work by challenging conventional wisdom or popular beliefs. The word "truth" implies that current understanding is incomplete or incorrect, creating cognitive dissonance that demands resolution. It positions the creator as someone willing to correct misconceptions rather than perpetuating comfortable myths. The structure works particularly well when the myth is something many people have heard or believe, making the correction widely relevant. The hook creates curiosity about what the real story is if the common belief is wrong. The example about "followers = sales" is particularly effective because it challenges a pervasive assumption in social media marketing—that audience size directly correlates with revenue. The truth is more nuanced: engagement quality, audience fit, trust, offer relevance, and conversion strategy matter far more than follower count. Many creators with small engaged audiences earn more than those with large disengaged ones. By exposing this myth, the hook provides relief to creators discouraged by slow growth while also offering strategic redirection toward metrics that actually matter. The hook works because it disrupts flawed mental models that might be guiding bad decisions, then promises to replace them with accurate understanding that leads to better strategy. It positions the creator as someone who sees through marketing myths to understand what actually drives results, which builds authority and trust.
