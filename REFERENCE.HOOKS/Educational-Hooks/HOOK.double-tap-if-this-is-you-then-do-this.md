# Double-tap if this is you—then do this

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Micro-commitment primes action. This hook uses a social media interaction (double-tap/like) to create small initial commitment, then immediately follows with a concrete action to take.

## Example

Double-tap if focus is hard—then try website blockers.

## Why it works

Micro-commitment hooks work by leveraging the psychological principle of consistency—once someone publicly acknowledges a problem or identity (by double-tapping), they're more likely to take the suggested action to remain consistent with that acknowledgment. The phrase "if this is you" creates a self-identification moment that's easy and low-stakes (just tap twice), but that small act creates psychological investment. The structure "double-tap...then do this" creates a two-step progression from passive agreement to active implementation. The social media engagement (double-tap) serves as a bridge between scrolling and acting—it's still within the platform's native behavior but creates enough activation energy to make the next step (taking real action) more likely. The example is effective because it identifies a widely relatable struggle (focus difficulty) and offers a specific, simple solution (website blockers) that can be implemented within minutes. The hook works particularly well on social platforms where engagement mechanisms are built in. It transforms passive content consumption into active participation by creating a progression: 1) see relatable problem, 2) publicly acknowledge it (tap), 3) take recommended action. The micro-commitment of engagement makes the leap to real action feel smaller and more natural.
