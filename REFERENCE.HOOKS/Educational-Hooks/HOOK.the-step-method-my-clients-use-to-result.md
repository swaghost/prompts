# The [#]-step method my clients use to [result]

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Social proof + process. This hook combines third-party validation (clients use it) with structural clarity (numbered steps), suggesting a proven, systematic approach to achieving results.

## Example

The 5-step method clients use to hit 6-figures.

## Why it works

Client-validated method hooks work by providing double credibility: the method is systematic (numbered steps suggest repeatable process) and proven (clients achieve results with it). The phrase "my clients use" provides social proof—this isn't just what the creator recommends but what actual people implement successfully. This suggests field-testing and validates that the method is practical, not just theoretical. The word "method" implies a systematic approach rather than random tactics, suggesting comprehensive coverage of what's needed. The specific number of steps makes the process feel tangible and manageable—readers can conceptualize working through 5 steps more easily than vague advice to "improve your business." The focus on results rather than activities is crucial—not "to improve marketing" but "to hit 6-figures," which is a concrete, measurable, desirable outcome. The example is particularly effective because hitting six figures ($100k+ revenue) is a meaningful milestone for many businesses—it represents legitimate business viability and often the point where entrepreneurship becomes financially sustainable. The hook works because it addresses the credibility gap that often exists with business advice—instead of claims or theory, it offers evidence that this method produces actual results for actual people. It positions the creator as a guide who helps clients achieve outcomes rather than just teaches concepts, which builds trust in the practical effectiveness of the advice.
