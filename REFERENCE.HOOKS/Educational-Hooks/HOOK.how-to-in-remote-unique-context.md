# How to **_ in _** (remote/unique context)

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Context-fit advice feels tailored. This hook acknowledges that standard advice may not apply in specific contexts, offering adapted guidance that respects unique constraints or circumstances.

## Example

How to stay productive in a remote team.

## Why it works

Context-specific hooks work because they acknowledge that generic advice often fails when circumstances vary. By explicitly naming the context (remote, small team, limited budget, etc.), the hook signals that the advice has been adapted for these specific constraints rather than assuming universal applicability. This makes the guidance feel more relevant and trustworthy—the creator understands the specific challenges of this context. The hook particularly resonates with people who have tried standard advice and found it doesn't work in their situation, creating frustration and self-doubt. Context-specific guidance validates that their struggles aren't personal failures but contextual mismatches. The example about remote team productivity is effective because remote work has fundamentally different dynamics than office work (different communication patterns, visibility challenges, boundary issues, async work, etc.), yet much productivity advice assumes office environments. Remote-specific guidance might emphasize different variables like communication clarity, documentation, async workflows, and explicit boundaries. The hook works because it targets people who feel existing advice doesn't apply to them, offering hope that their situation isn't impossible but just requires adapted approaches. It positions the creator as understanding nuance and real-world variation rather than pushing universal solutions.
