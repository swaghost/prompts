# I spent X years in [niche]—here are the real secrets

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Time-earned authority. This hook establishes credibility through duration of experience, suggesting the insights come from deep immersion rather than superficial knowledge or theory.

## Example

I spent 10 years in SaaS—here are the pricing secrets.

## Why it works

Time-based authority hooks work because they signal expertise through sustained commitment. The specific number of years (especially when substantial—5, 10, 15+) suggests the creator has seen patterns across cycles, encountered edge cases, and refined understanding beyond beginner insights. The word "spent" implies investment and dedication, not just passive time passing. The phrase "real secrets" is powerful because it suggests insider knowledge that's not publicly available or widely understood—information you can only gain from years inside the industry. "Real" implies there are fake or surface-level secrets circulating, and these are the genuine insights. "Secrets" suggests valuable information that insiders know but don't typically share with outsiders, creating exclusivity and perceived value. The example about SaaS pricing is particularly effective because pricing strategy is notoriously opaque—companies don't publicly share their pricing psychology, testing results, or margin strategies. This makes insider knowledge genuinely valuable and difficult to obtain elsewhere. The hook works because it combines earned authority with promise of insider information, creating both credibility and curiosity.
