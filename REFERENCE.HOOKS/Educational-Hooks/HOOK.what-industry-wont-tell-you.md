# What the ___ industry won't tell you about ___

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Curiosity via secrecy/insider knowledge. This hook positions the creator as someone with inside information that established players don't want you to know, creating an "us vs. them" dynamic that builds trust and intrigue.

## Example

What the skincare industry won't tell you about "miracle" serums.

## Why it works

This hook leverages the powerful psychological appeal of forbidden or hidden knowledge. By suggesting that an entire industry is withholding information, it creates conspiracy-level curiosity while positioning the creator as a truth-teller who's willing to break ranks. This builds immediate credibility and trust—if industries hide information for profit, then someone revealing that information must be acting in the reader's best interest. The hook works particularly well because it acknowledges the reader's suspicion that they might be getting ripped off or misled, which is a common feeling when dealing with complex products or services. The phrase "won't tell you" is stronger than "doesn't tell you" because it implies deliberate concealment rather than mere omission, increasing the sense of revelation. The example is especially effective because the skincare industry is notorious for marketing hype around "miracle" ingredients, making the promise of insider truth highly appealing. This hook works across any industry where consumers feel information asymmetry or where marketing claims seem too good to be true.
