# How to turn [money] into [more money]

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Opportunity framing + specificity. This hook presents financial growth as achievable through specific methods, with concrete numbers making the promise feel real and measurable rather than vague or theoretical.

## Example

How to turn $100 into $600 with smart flips.

## Why it works

Money multiplication hooks work because they promise tangible, measurable return on investment with a clear starting point. The specificity of both numbers (starting amount and end result) makes the promise concrete and verifiable, which increases credibility. The ratio matters too—a 6x return is impressive but still believable, whereas promising to turn $100 into $10,000 would trigger skepticism. The hook works particularly well for people who want to grow wealth but feel limited by their starting capital. By showing that significant returns are possible from modest investments, it removes the excuse of "I don't have enough money to invest" and reframes wealth-building as requiring strategy more than capital. The phrase "smart flips" in the example suggests there's a methodology involving buying undervalued items and reselling them at profit, which feels accessible and skill-based rather than luck-dependent. This hook works because it combines the universal appeal of financial gain with specific, achievable numbers that make the promise feel like a realistic project rather than a fantasy. The clarity of the math creates trust and makes success measurable.
