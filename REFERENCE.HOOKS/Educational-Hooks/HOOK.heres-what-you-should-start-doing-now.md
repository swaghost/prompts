# Here's what you should start doing now

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Urgency + next step. This hook creates immediate call to action by suggesting the optimal time to start is right now, not later when circumstances are better.

## Example

Start building an email list now—here's why.

## Why it works

Urgency hooks work by collapsing the gap between learning and doing. The phrase "start doing now" removes permission to delay or prepare more—the message is that enough is known to begin acting immediately. This addresses the common trap of perpetual preparation without execution. The word "now" creates temporal urgency that counteracts procrastination and the belief that "someday when I'm ready" is a better time to start. The phrase "what you should" provides directional guidance from someone with authority or experience, reducing decision paralysis about what action to take. The follow-up "here's why" promises justification for the urgency, which is important—readers are more likely to act when they understand the reasoning, not just receive orders. The example about email list building is particularly effective because it's advice many creators have heard but postponed, thinking they'll start when they have a bigger audience or better lead magnet. The urgency addresses a real cost: every day without list-building loses potential subscribers who could be nurtured into customers. Starting now—even imperfectly—means beginning to build an owned asset rather than depending entirely on rented platforms. The hook works because it challenges the comfortable delay of "I'll start when..." and argues that imperfect action now beats perfect action later. It positions starting as the variable that matters most, not perfect conditions or complete preparation.
