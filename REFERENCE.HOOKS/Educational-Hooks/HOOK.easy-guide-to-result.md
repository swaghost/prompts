# Easy guide to [result]

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

"Easy" lowers barrier. This hook explicitly promises that the path to the result won't be difficult or complex, removing the fear of difficulty as a barrier to starting.

## Example

Easy guide to landing your first 1k followers.

## Why it works

Simplicity-promise hooks work by removing the intimidation factor that prevents people from starting. Many people avoid pursuing goals because they assume the process will be difficult, complex, or require expertise they don't have. The word "easy" directly contradicts that assumption and promises accessibility. While "easy" could trigger skepticism if overused, when applied honestly to processes that genuinely can be simplified, it provides reassurance that starts are within reach. The word "guide" suggests step-by-step instruction rather than just principles or theory, promising clear direction that reduces decision-making burden. The combination of "easy" and "guide" positions the content as beginner-friendly and supportive rather than expert-level or challenging. The example about reaching 1,000 followers is particularly effective because it targets a specific, meaningful milestone for creators (1k followers is often when platforms unlock features or when growth begins to compound). The specific number makes the goal concrete and the "first 1k" acknowledges this is for beginners, not advanced growth tactics. The hook works because it makes an intimidating goal (building an audience) feel achievable by promising that the path is simpler than feared. It removes the excuse of "it's too hard" and positions audience growth as a learnable process rather than mysterious talent.
