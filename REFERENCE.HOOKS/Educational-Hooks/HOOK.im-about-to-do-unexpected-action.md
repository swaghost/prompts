# I'm about to do [unexpected action]—here's why

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Drama + rationale = intrigue. This hook creates curiosity by announcing an unconventional or surprising decision, then promising to explain the reasoning behind it.

## Example

I'm about to delete 80% of my apps—here's why.

## Why it works

Announcement-of-intent hooks work because they create narrative tension—the creator is about to do something unusual, and readers want to understand the motivation. The phrase "I'm about to" suggests imminent action, which creates urgency and implies the decision is well-considered rather than impulsive. The unexpected nature of the action (deleting most apps, quitting a job, eliminating a service) contradicts normal behavior, which creates cognitive dissonance: "Why would someone do that?" The promise of explanation ("here's why") ensures readers they'll understand the logic, not just witness the drama. This structure is particularly effective because it invites readers into the decision-making process of someone who has thought deeply about a problem they also face. The example about deleting 80% of apps speaks to digital overwhelm and decision fatigue, common pain points in modern life. The extreme percentage (80%) makes the action dramatic enough to be attention-grabbing while the promise of reasoning suggests there's valuable insight about what's truly necessary versus what's noise. This hook works because it models unconventional thinking and invites readers to reconsider their own assumptions.
