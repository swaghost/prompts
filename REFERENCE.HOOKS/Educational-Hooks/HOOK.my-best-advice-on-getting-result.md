# My best advice on getting [result]

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

"Best of" curation = value density. This hook promises distilled wisdom by positioning the advice as the creator's most important insight on the topic, suggesting years of experience compressed into key learnings.

## Example

My best advice on staying consistent with workouts.

## Why it works

"Best advice" hooks work because they promise curation and prioritization. In a world overflowing with tips and tactics, readers value guidance on what matters most. By explicitly labeling this as their "best" advice, the creator signals they've tested many approaches and are sharing only the most impactful. This creates expectation of high value-to-word ratio—you're getting the insight that makes the biggest difference, not a comprehensive but diluted overview. The first-person framing ("my best advice") adds credibility through experience—this isn't generic wisdom but hard-won personal insight. It also creates intimacy, as if the creator is sharing their secret weapon. The example about workout consistency is particularly effective because consistency is the most common failure point in fitness, not lack of knowledge about exercises. By promising advice specifically on the sustainability challenge rather than the technical aspects, the hook addresses the real bottleneck. This demonstrates understanding of what people actually struggle with versus what they think they need help with. The hook works because it promises essential wisdom rather than comprehensive information, respecting the reader's time while maximizing value.
