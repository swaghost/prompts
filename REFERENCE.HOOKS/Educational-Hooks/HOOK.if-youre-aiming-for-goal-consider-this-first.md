# If you're aiming for [goal], consider this first

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Pre-commitment guidance feels protective. This hook positions itself as essential consideration before pursuing a goal, suggesting there's important context or preparation that should precede action.

## Example

If you're aiming for lean gains, consider protein timing.

## Why it works

Pre-condition hooks work because they intercept people at a crucial decision point—before they've committed fully to a path. The phrase "consider this first" suggests that starting without this information would be premature or inefficient, creating mild anxiety about missing a crucial step. This positions the content as preventive wisdom that could save time, money, or effort later. The word "consider" is softer than "do" or "must," which respects reader agency while still suggesting importance. The hook works particularly well when the "thing to consider first" addresses a common mistake or oversight. The example about protein timing for lean gains is effective because many people focus on total protein intake without realizing that distribution throughout the day significantly impacts muscle protein synthesis. By identifying this variable as something to "consider first," the hook suggests it's a foundational principle that affects everything else. This creates value by helping readers prioritize correctly rather than discovering later that they've been working inefficiently. The hook succeeds because it positions the creator as a protective advisor who helps readers avoid common pitfalls before they stumble into them.
