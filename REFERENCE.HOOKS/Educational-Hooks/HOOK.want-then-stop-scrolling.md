# Want ___? Then stop scrolling.

## Hook Group

Educational Hooks

## Psychology

Pattern interrupt + direct call to action. This hook disrupts automatic scrolling behavior and forces a conscious decision moment, creating heightened attention and self-awareness about the reader's current action versus their stated goals.

## Example

Want more energy at work? Then stop scrolling—do this 5-minute reset.

## Why it works

This hook is exceptionally effective because it calls out the exact behavior the reader is currently engaged in (scrolling), creating an immediate moment of self-awareness and mild cognitive dissonance. The structure creates a stark contrast between what the reader wants (the stated goal) and what they're currently doing (mindlessly scrolling), which subtly challenges them to align their actions with their intentions. The command "stop scrolling" serves as a pattern interrupt that breaks the hypnotic flow state of social media consumption, making the reader more receptive to the message. By framing continued engagement as a conscious choice rather than passive consumption, it increases commitment—readers who continue past this hook are implicitly agreeing that they DO want the promised outcome and are willing to take action. The example adds a concrete, immediate action ("5-minute reset") that feels achievable right now, which increases the likelihood of follow-through. This is particularly powerful for productivity and self-improvement content where the target audience is often procrastinating through social media.
