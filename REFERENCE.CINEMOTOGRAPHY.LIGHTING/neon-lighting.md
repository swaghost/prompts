# Neon Lighting

## Description

Neon lighting uses bright, saturated colored light sources (typically pink, blue, purple, cyan, or red) to create vibrant, contemporary aesthetic associated with urban nightlife, cyberpunk, and modern commercial aesthetics. Creates striking color contrasts and bold visual identity with glowing, electric quality.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys energy, modernity, urban excitement, and contemporary coolness. Creates vibrant, youthful, edgy atmosphere.

**Psychological Purpose:** Associated with nightlife, technology, youth culture, and urban environments. Makes content feel current, trendy, and visually exciting. Used for fashion, music, lifestyle content targeting younger demographics.

**Physical Purpose:** Creates bold color contrasts, adds immediate visual impact and contemporary style. Ideal for music videos, fashion campaigns, social media content, urban portraits, and creating Instagram-ready aesthetic. Defines modern commercial photography style.
