# Rembrandt Lighting

## Description

Rembrandt lighting creates a distinctive triangle of light on the shadowed cheek, positioned at approximately 45 degrees to the subject. Named after the Dutch master painter, this technique creates dramatic chiaroscuro effect with one side of the face well-lit and the other mostly in shadow, except for the characteristic triangle under the eye.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys depth, sophistication, contemplation, and artistic gravitas. Creates moody, thoughtful atmosphere.

**Psychological Purpose:** Suggests wisdom, complexity, and introspection. Associated with portraits of thinkers, artists, and characters with depth. Creates sense of timeless classical beauty and intellectual presence.

**Physical Purpose:** Creates dramatic dimensionality and artistic quality. Ideal for portrait photography, character studies, editorial work, and creating painterly aesthetic. Emphasizes facial contours while maintaining recognizable features.
