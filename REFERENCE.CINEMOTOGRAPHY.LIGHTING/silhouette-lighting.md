# Silhouette Lighting

## Description

Silhouette lighting places the subject between the camera and a bright background light source, rendering the subject as a dark shape without visible detail against the bright background. This creates striking, graphic compositions emphasizing form and outline over detail.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys mystery, anonymity, drama, and universal human experience. Creates iconic, memorable imagery.

**Psychological Purpose:** Suggests everyman/everywoman representation, mystery, or hidden identity. Makes subjects universal and archetypal. Associated with poetic storytelling and symbolic representation.

**Physical Purpose:** Creates bold graphic compositions, emphasizes shape and posture, eliminates facial recognition. Ideal for artistic portraits, dramatic reveals, title sequences, and creating instantly recognizable iconic imagery. Works beautifully in backlit situations.
