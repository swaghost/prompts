# Side Lighting

## Description

Side lighting places the light source 90 degrees to the side of the subject, illuminating one half of the face and body while leaving the other half in shadow. This creates dramatic contrast, strong definition, and pronounced texture. The split between light and shadow emphasizes three-dimensional form.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys drama, conflict, duality, and intensity. Creates mood of contemplation, struggle, or internal division.

**Psychological Purpose:** Suggests complexity, hidden depth, and moral ambiguity. Often used for characters with secrets or internal conflict. Associated with film noir and psychological drama.

**Physical Purpose:** Emphasizes facial structure, texture, and dimensionality. Ideal for dramatic portraits, character studies, fashion editorial, and creating strong visual impact. Reveals skin texture and contours.
