# Split Lighting

## Description

Split lighting divides the face precisely down the middle with light on one side and shadow on the other, creating a stark 50/50 division. This dramatic technique creates strong contrast and emphasizes symmetry while revealing duality in the subject's character or mood.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys duality, internal conflict, mystery, and dramatic tension. Creates mood of psychological complexity or moral ambiguity.

**Psychological Purpose:** Suggests hidden nature, two sides to a personality, or internal struggle. Often used for villains, complex characters, or moments of decision. Associated with psychological thriller aesthetics.

**Physical Purpose:** Creates maximum drama and contrast, emphasizes facial symmetry and structure. Ideal for editorial portraits, album covers, dramatic character shots, and creating bold visual statements.
