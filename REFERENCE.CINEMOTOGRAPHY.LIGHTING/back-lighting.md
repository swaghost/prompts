# Back Lighting

## Description

Back lighting positions the light source behind the subject, creating a glowing rim or halo effect around the edges while keeping the front in relative shadow. This separates the subject from the background dramatically and creates ethereal, luminous quality. Hair and edges catch beautiful rim light.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys ethereal beauty, mystery, romance, and transcendence. Creates dreamy, otherworldly, or spiritual atmosphere.

**Psychological Purpose:** Suggests hope, transformation, revelation, or divine presence. Often used for angelic characters, romantic moments, or epiphany scenes. Creates separation and elevation.

**Physical Purpose:** Separates subject from background, creates depth, highlights hair and silhouette beautifully. Ideal for golden hour portraits, music videos, romantic scenes, and creating visual separation. Adds production value instantly.
