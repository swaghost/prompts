# Kicker Lighting

## Description

Kicker lighting uses a secondary light placed behind and to the side of the subject at a low angle, creating a bright accent on the side of the face, jaw, or cheekbone. This adds dimension and separation without fully outlining the subject like rim light, creating subtle sculptural definition.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys sophistication, style, and polished production quality. Creates refined, intentional aesthetic.

**Psychological Purpose:** Adds subtle emphasis and visual interest without overwhelming the primary mood. Associated with professional cinematography and high-end portraiture. Shows attention to detail.

**Physical Purpose:** Adds facial dimension, separates subject from background subtly, emphasizes cheekbone and jaw structure. Ideal for professional portraits, commercial work, narrative cinematography, and adding depth without excessive drama.
