# Butterfly Lighting

## Description

Butterfly lighting positions the light source directly above and in front of the subject, creating a small butterfly-shaped shadow under the nose. This classic Hollywood glamour technique creates symmetrical illumination with emphasis on cheekbones while casting subtle shadows that define facial structure.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys elegance, glamour, beauty, and classic sophistication. Creates timeless, refined aesthetic.

**Psychological Purpose:** Associated with classic Hollywood beauty, confidence, and polished perfection. Makes subjects feel elevated and idealized. Used for aspirational beauty and fashion content.

**Physical Purpose:** Emphasizes cheekbones, defines facial structure beautifully, and creates flattering symmetrical shadows. Ideal for beauty portraits, fashion photography, headshots, and creating classic glamorous aesthetic. Minimizes under-eye shadows.
