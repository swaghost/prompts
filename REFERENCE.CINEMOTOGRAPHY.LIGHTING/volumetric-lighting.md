# Volumetric Lighting

## Description

Volumetric lighting creates visible light beams cutting through atmosphere, revealing the "shape" of light itself through fog, haze, smoke, or dust particles. This creates dramatic god rays, shaft of light effects, and tangible atmospheric quality where light becomes a visual element rather than just illumination.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys drama, spirituality, mystery, and cinematic grandeur. Creates epic, transcendent, or ominous atmosphere depending on context.

**Psychological Purpose:** Associated with revelation, divine presence, industrial environments, or thriller suspense. Makes spaces feel volumetric and tangible. Used for moments of significance, discovery, or atmospheric storytelling.

**Physical Purpose:** Reveals three-dimensional space, adds depth and atmosphere, creates dramatic visual interest. Ideal for cinematic scenes, atmospheric environments, concert photography, warehouse/industrial settings, and creating immediate production value and mood.
