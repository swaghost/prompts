# Edge Lighting

## Description

Edge lighting (also called rim lighting) places light sources behind or to the sides of the subject to create bright, defined outlines along the edges of the form. This separates the subject from the background dramatically while creating striking visual definition and emphasizing silhouette and contours.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys energy, drama, excitement, and visual impact. Creates dynamic, powerful presence.

**Psychological Purpose:** Makes subjects stand out and command attention. Associated with power, confidence, and dramatic entrance moments. Used for heroes, performers, and impactful character reveals.

**Physical Purpose:** Separates subject from background, creates depth, emphasizes body shape and silhouette. Ideal for fitness photography, action shots, music videos, dramatic portraits, and creating immediate visual separation with production value.
