# Natural Light

## Description

Natural light refers to sunlight coming through windows, doors, or outdoor settings without artificial modification. It creates soft, organic illumination with subtle color temperature shifts throughout the day, offering authentic and warm visual quality. The light wraps around the subject naturally with minimal harsh shadows.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys authenticity, warmth, peace, and naturalism. Creates intimate, genuine moments that feel unforced and real.

**Psychological Purpose:** Evokes comfort, honesty, and relatability. Makes subjects appear approachable and trustworthy. Associated with documentary realism and everyday life.

**Physical Purpose:** Provides soft, flattering illumination ideal for portraits, lifestyle content, and natural beauty shots. Best for creating organic, unmanipulated aesthetics in fashion, editorial, and social media content.
