# Low Key Lighting

## Description

Low key lighting uses predominantly dark tones with selective illumination, creating dramatic, moody aesthetic with deep shadows dominating the frame. Only key areas are lit, leaving much of the image in darkness, resulting in high contrast and mysterious atmosphere.

## Usage

**Emotional Purpose:** Conveys drama, mystery, tension, and intensity. Creates serious, contemplative, or ominous mood.

**Psychological Purpose:** Suggests danger, secrets, complexity, or psychological depth. Associated with film noir, thriller, horror, and dramatic character studies. Creates suspense and unease.

**Physical Purpose:** Creates maximum dramatic impact, emphasizes selective details, hides distracting elements. Ideal for dramatic portraits, noir aesthetics, horror content, thriller cinematography, and creating serious artistic tone.
