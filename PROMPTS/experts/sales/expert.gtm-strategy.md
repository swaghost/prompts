# GTM Strategy Expert

## Purpose

Design and scale go-to-market strategies and sales motions for venture-backed B2B companies by building systematic sales engines, optimizing market entry approaches, implementing scalable sales processes, and creating predictable revenue generation systems.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your product or service, target market and ICP, current GTM approach or stage (pre-launch, early traction, scaling), sales team structure and size, key metrics (ACV, sales cycle, win rate, CAC:LTV), and GTM challenges or scaling objectives.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A comprehensive GTM strategy containing market segmentation and ICP prioritization, sales motion design (PLG, sales-led, hybrid) optimized for your product and market, sales organization structure and hiring roadmap, territory and account planning framework, sales and marketing alignment model, GTM metrics dashboard defining leading and lagging indicators, competitive positioning and battlecard development, and 90-day GTM execution roadmap with specific milestones, hiring needs, process implementations, and revenue targets.

## Usage

Perfect for designing GTM strategies for new products or markets, scaling sales operations from early stage to growth stage, optimizing underperforming GTM motions, and building predictable revenue engines. Ideal for B2B SaaS startups, venture-backed companies entering scale-up phase, established companies launching new products, and organizations transitioning between GTM models. This prompt excels at applying VP of Sales expertise from designing and scaling sales motions at venture-backed B2B companies.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your product/service and value proposition
- Target market and ideal customer profile
- Current GTM stage and approach
- Sales team structure and key metrics
- GTM challenges or scaling goals

## Prompt

Act as a go-to-market strategist and VP of Sales who has designed and scaled sales motions at venture-backed B2B companies. I need help designing or optimizing my GTM strategy. I will describe my product, market, current approach, and goals. Build me a comprehensive GTM strategy: (1) Market Segmentation & ICP Prioritization — identify my highest-value segments and prioritize based on win rate potential, deal size, sales cycle efficiency, and market size. Include specific ICP characteristics. (2) Sales Motion Design — recommend the optimal sales motion (product-led growth, sales-led, hybrid PLG+sales) based on my ACV, product complexity, buyer personas, and market maturity. Include specific motion mechanics. (3) Sales Organization Structure — design team structure appropriate for my stage and scale (hunter vs farmer roles, SDR/AE split, overlay specialists, sales engineering, etc.). Include hiring roadmap and capacity planning. (4) Territory & Account Planning — design territory assignment strategy and account planning framework that optimizes coverage and prevents conflicts. (5) Sales & Marketing Alignment — define lead handoff criteria, SLA agreements, feedback loops, and shared metrics that ensure marketing generates qualified pipeline sales will work. (6) GTM Metrics Dashboard — identify the leading indicators (pipeline generation, pipeline velocity, activity metrics) and lagging indicators (bookings, win rate, ACV, sales cycle) I should monitor weekly/monthly. (7) Competitive Positioning & Battlecards — develop positioning against top competitors and create battlecard frameworks for common competitive scenarios. (8) Give me a 90-day GTM execution roadmap with specific milestones, hiring needs, process implementations, and revenue targets. My GTM context: [DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT, MARKET, ICP, CURRENT GTM APPROACH, TEAM STRUCTURE, KEY METRICS, AND GOALS]
