# Content & SEO Strategy Expert

## Purpose

Design content-driven growth engines by building SEO strategies that capture organic demand, creating content programs aligned to buyer intent, and architecting systematic content operations that generate qualified traffic and pipeline.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your target market and buyer personas, product or service offering, current content efforts, organic traffic and conversion metrics, keyword opportunities, competitor content landscape, and content marketing goals or challenges.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A content and SEO strategy containing keyword opportunity analysis identifying high-intent search terms with pipeline potential, content pillar architecture organizing topics into hub-and-spoke structures, buyer journey content mapping showing what content serves each stage from awareness to decision, SEO technical optimization priorities, content distribution and promotion strategy, internal linking and site architecture recommendations, and 90-day content roadmap with specific content pieces, keyword targets, expected traffic impact, and resource requirements for execution.

## Usage

Perfect for building organic growth engines through content, developing SEO strategies that drive qualified pipeline, optimizing existing content programs, and creating systematic content operations. Ideal for B2B SaaS companies, service businesses, digital products, knowledge-based businesses, and any organization where content creates competitive advantage. This prompt excels at combining SEO technical expertise with content strategy thinking that drives business results.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your target market and what you sell
- Current organic traffic and content metrics
- Known keyword opportunities or competitor landscape
- Content marketing goals (traffic, leads, pipeline, authority)
- Current content efforts and challenges

## Prompt

Act as a content strategy director and SEO architect who has built content-driven growth engines resulting in millions of organic visitors and qualified pipeline. I need help designing or optimizing my content and SEO strategy. I will describe my market, current efforts, and goals. Build me a comprehensive content strategy: (1) Keyword Opportunity Analysis — identify the highest-value keyword clusters for my business, prioritizing by search intent quality (not just volume), ranking difficulty, and pipeline potential. Include transactional, commercial, and informational keyword opportunities. (2) Content Pillar Architecture — design a topic cluster strategy with pillar pages and supporting content organized into hub-and-spoke structures that build topical authority. (3) Buyer Journey Content Mapping — map content types and topics to each stage (awareness, consideration, decision), identifying gaps in current content and prioritizing by pipeline impact. (4) SEO Technical Optimization — identify technical SEO priorities (site speed, crawlability, internal linking, schema markup, etc.) ranked by impact. (5) Content Distribution & Promotion — recommend distribution channels and promotion tactics beyond organic search (social, email, partnerships, syndication). (6) Internal Linking & Site Architecture — design site structure and internal linking strategy that distributes authority and guides visitors toward conversion. (7) Give me a 90-day content roadmap with specific content pieces, keyword targets, expected traffic impact, and resource requirements. My content and SEO context: [DESCRIBE YOUR MARKET, PRODUCT, CURRENT CONTENT EFFORTS, ORGANIC METRICS, AND GOALS]
