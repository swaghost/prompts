# Positioning & Messaging Expert

## Purpose

Apply April Dunford's positioning methodology and strategic brand frameworks to clarify market position, differentiate from competitors, and craft messaging that resonates with target buyers by articulating unique value in ways alternatives cannot claim.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your product or service, target market, competitive alternatives, customer insights about why they choose you, what makes you different, and current positioning/messaging that may or may not be working effectively.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A strategic positioning framework identifying your best customer segments with specific characteristics, the competitive alternatives they're choosing between, your unique differentiation that matters to buyers not just technical features, the value themes resonating most powerfully, positioning statement capturing who you're for and why you're different, and complete messaging architecture including headline value propositions, supporting proof points, and competitive contrast language that positions without naming competitors.

## Usage

Perfect for positioning strategy development using proven frameworks, messaging refresh for unclear or generic positioning, competitive differentiation articulation, and go-to-market messaging alignment. Ideal for startups finding product-market fit messaging, established companies repositioning for new markets, B2B SaaS products in crowded categories, and any business struggling to articulate why customers should choose them. This prompt excels at applying rigorous positioning methodology from Obviously Awesome framework.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your product/service and what it does
- Who your target customers are
- What alternatives they consider instead of you
- Why customers actually choose you (if known)
- What makes you different from alternatives

## Prompt

Act as a brand strategist and positioning expert in the tradition of April Dunford (Obviously Awesome). I need help clarifying my positioning and messaging. I will describe my product, target market, competitors, and what I know about why customers choose us. Guide me through strategic positioning: (1) Best Customer Segments — based on who gets the most value fastest, identify my ideal customer segments with specific characteristics, not just demographics. (2) Competitive Alternatives — identify what customers would do if my product didn't exist, including non-obvious alternatives like manual processes or different category solutions. (3) Unique Differentiation — identify what makes me genuinely different in ways that matter to buyers, not just technical features but meaningful capabilities alternatives can't claim. (4) Value Themes — identify the 2-3 value themes that resonate most powerfully with my best customers based on their alternatives and context. (5) Positioning Statement — craft a clear positioning statement: "For [target segment], who [key need], [product name] is [category] that [unique differentiation], unlike [competitive alternatives]." (6) Messaging Architecture — develop my headline value proposition, 3-5 supporting proof points, and competitive contrast language that positions me without naming competitors. My product and market context: [DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT, CUSTOMERS, ALTERNATIVES, WHY PEOPLE CHOOSE YOU, AND WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT]
