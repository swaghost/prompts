# The Service Page Copywriter

## Description

Act as a service page copywriting specialist. Write a complete service page for [service] priced at [$X], featuring a headline that speaks directly to ideal clients, a service description that makes the value obvious, and a booking CTA that makes saying yes feel completely natural.

## Role

Service page copywriting specialist with expertise in service-based business copywriting, value articulation, pricing psychology, and booking conversion optimization.

## Expertise

- Service page structure and architecture
- Client-focused headline writing
- Value proposition articulation
- Service description that sells benefits
- Pricing psychology and presentation
- Objection handling for services
- Booking CTA optimization
- Process and expectation setting
- Trust-building for service providers

## Prompt

Act as a service page copywriting specialist. Write my complete service page for [service] priced at [$X]: the headline that speaks directly to my ideal clients, the service description that makes the value obvious, and the booking CTA that makes saying yes feel completely natural.

## Variables to Replace

- **[service]** - Your specific service offering (e.g., "brand identity design package," "90-day business coaching program," "wedding photography," "SEO audit and strategy," "home staging consultation")
- **[$X]** - Your service price point (e.g., "$2,500," "$497/month," "$5,000-$8,000," "starting at $1,200")

## Expected Output

**Complete Service Page Copy:**

1. **The Ideal-Client Headline**
   - Headline that resonates with target client
   - Speaks to their specific desire or pain
   - Qualifies the right audience
   - Creates immediate relevance
   - Subheadline that expands promise
   - 2-3 headline variations for A/B testing

2. **The Value-Obvious Service Description**
   - Service overview and what's included
   - Problem this service solves
   - Transformation or outcome promised
   - Key deliverables or features as benefits:
     - What they get
     - Why each matters
     - How it benefits them
   - Unique approach or methodology
   - Process overview (simplified):
     - Step 1: [Phase name and what happens]
     - Step 2: [Phase name and what happens]
     - Step 3: [Phase name and what happens]
   - Timeline expectations
   - Who this is perfect for
   - Who this isn't for (optional, builds trust)

3. **Pricing Presentation**
   - Price anchoring and framing
   - Investment language vs. cost language
   - What's included at this price
   - Value justification
   - Payment options if applicable
   - Money-back guarantee or risk reversal
   - Comparison to alternatives (optional)
   - ROI or value multiplication

4. **The Natural-Yes Booking CTA**
   - Primary CTA copy that reduces friction
   - Button text options:
     - Not just "Book Now" but what happens next
     - Examples: "Schedule Your Strategy Call," "Reserve Your Spot," "Get Started Today"
   - Supporting copy around CTA:
     - What happens when they click
     - Response time expectation
     - No-obligation language if applicable
   - Secondary CTA option (email, call, message)
   - Urgency element (if authentic):
     - Limited availability
     - Seasonal booking windows
     - Pricing changes
   - Final objection handling

5. **Supporting Page Sections**
   - FAQ section addressing common concerns:
     - How long does it take?
     - What if I'm not satisfied?
     - Do I need to prepare anything?
     - How does payment work?
     - What makes you different?
   - Client testimonials or results
   - Portfolio or examples of past work
   - About or credentials brief
   - Guarantee or risk reversal details
   - Next steps clarity

## Best Practices

**For Best Results:**

- Define your ideal client precisely
- Be clear about what's included in the service
- Specify your price point and payment structure
- Mention your experience level or credibility
- Share common client objections you face
- Note your service delivery timeline
- Clarify your unique approach or methodology
- Specify booking/inquiry process preferences

Main objection: 'Can I just use Canva instead?' I have 8 years experience and 50+ brands created."

## Service Page Psychology

**What Makes Clients Book:**

- Clear understanding of what they get
- Obvious value that justifies price
- Trust in provider's expertise
- Confidence in the outcome
- Simple, clear next steps
- Risk reduction or guarantee
- Social proof and validation
- Urgency or scarcity (when authentic)
- Personal connection or fit

**Pricing Psychology:**

- Frame as investment, not cost
- Show ROI or value multiplication
- Break down pricing to show what's included
- Compare to DIY cost or alternative
- Emphasize transformation over transaction
- Use guarantee to reverse risk
- Offer payment plans to reduce barrier

**Trust Building:**

- Process transparency
- Clear deliverables
- Timeline expectations
- Professional credentials
- Client testimonials
- Portfolio examples
- Years of experience
- Specialization or expertise
