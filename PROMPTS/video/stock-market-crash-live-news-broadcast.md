# Stock Market Crash: Live Business News Broadcast

**Format:** Live breaking news broadcast, multiple segments  
**Type:** Financial news coverage, real-time market crash reporting  
**Duration:** Approximately 60-90 seconds continuous broadcast  
**Subject:** Female journalist reporting on sudden stock market crash  
**Style:** Authentic broadcast journalism, live news realism

---

## CONCEPT

Live business news broadcast capturing sudden stock market crash during active trading hours at 11:20 AM. Professional female journalist (early 30s) reports from outside stock exchange building, positioned in front of glass-walled trading floor where anxious traders visible through windows gesturing frantically. Digital ticker boards flash red declining numbers behind reporter, reflecting on glass surfaces. **Authentic broadcast realism** — genuine tension, composed but visibly stressed reporter, traders ignoring camera focused on crisis, real-time number changes driving reactions, natural interruptions from passersby checking phones. Multi-camera setup: steady tripod-mounted main shot with secondary handheld cutaway camera, occasional quick zooms to plunging ticker numbers. Five distinct live segments: (1) opening explanation of sudden index drop and market panic, (2) update cutting to trading floor screens showing plunging graphs, (3) eyewitness moment with trader stepping outside commenting on chaos, (4) situation change with fresh ticker plunge and reporter reacting live, (5) closing sign-off noting continued market monitoring into afternoon. **Live audio environment:** muffled trading floor shouting through glass, city traffic ambient, notification pings from nearby phones checking market, reporter's controlled professional voice, no music. Bright daylight conditions, glass reflections of red digital numbers, financial district atmosphere, breaking news urgency, professional broadcast journalism standards.

## NEGATIVE PROMPT

**Avoid:**

### Performance/Acting Errors:

- Overacting reporter (hysterical, melodramatic, unbelievable)
- Smiling/inappropriate emotion (situation serious, not entertaining)
- Traders looking at camera (breaking fourth wall, performing, inauthentic)
- Staged reactions (not genuine, scripted, fake)
- Poor acting (amateur, unconvincing, wooden)

### Technical Errors:

- Shaky main camera (should be tripod-stable, professional)
- Poor audio (muffled reporter, inaudible, distorted)
- Wrong microphone (no logo, handheld mic absent, incorrect gear)
- Bad lighting (too dark, overexposed, unprofessional)
- Low video quality (grainy, poor resolution, amateur)
- No earpiece (reporter missing IFB, unprofessional)

### Content Errors:

- **Music soundtrack** (must be diegetic sound only, no score)
- Static ticker boards (numbers must change, not frozen)
- Green/positive numbers (must be red/declining, crisis indicator)
- Empty trading floor (must be populated, active, chaotic)
- Cartoon graphics (professional broadcast graphics only)
- Incorrect time of day (must be 11:20 AM, bright daylight, midday)

### Broadcast Realism Errors:

- Wrong network aesthetic (not soap opera, not film, broadcast news style)
- Sensational tabloid style (professional journalism required, not trash TV)
- Comedy tone (situation serious, not funny, not parody)
- Found footage/horror style (professional broadcast, not genre film)
- Overly cinematic (not feature film, news broadcast aesthetic)

### Environmental Errors:

- Wrong location (not stock exchange, not financial district)
- Night time (must be 11:20 AM, bright daylight)
- Empty streets (financial district busy, people present)
- No glass walls (trading floor must be visible, transparency)
- Wrong architecture (modern financial building, not old/wrong style)

### Reporter Errors:

- Wrong attire (casual clothes, not business formal, unprofessional)
- No microphone (reporter must have handheld mic with logo)
- Wrong age (too young, not credible, not experienced)
- Male reporter (specified female journalist, respect casting)
- Calm/unbothered (must be visibly tense, concerned, invested)

### Trader Errors:

- Traders waving at camera (must ignore camera, focused on work)
- Calm traders (must be anxious, gesturing, stressed)
- Celebrating (wrong emotion, crisis not party)
- Too few traders (trading floor must be populated, busy)

### Authenticity Errors:

- Scripted feel (must seem spontaneous, live, real-time)
- Polished/perfect delivery (reporter human, occasional stumbles realistic)
- No interruptions (natural pauses, environmental interference authentic)
- Numbers not changing (must update in real-time, dynamic)
