# Harbour Island Miniature Diorama Formation — 15-Second Build

## Description

15-second cinematic miniature-diorama formation video showing an enormous harbour town rapidly assembling across an entire island surrounded by bright blue sea. Premium handcrafted architectural diorama aesthetic—luxury collector model quality with highly detailed practical film miniature appearance. The island foundation, harbour town, piers, lighthouse, hillside neighbourhoods, and coastal details assemble with precise, satisfying movements before coming alive with subtle animation.

## Usage

Create expansive, cinematic miniature world construction content perfect for social media, promotional videos, architectural visualization, or satisfying transformation sequences. Emphasizes premium craftsmanship, wide-scale island composition, and rhythmic assembly animation. Uses **@reference_image** to ensure final island matches exact layout, coastline, architecture, and appearance.

## Prerequisites

- **Reference Image Required**: @reference_image showing completed harbour island
- **Match Elements**: Island layout, coastline, harbour structure, architecture, lighthouse, roof colours, bridges, canals, piers, terrain, overall appearance
- **Format**: 1:1 square format (Instagram/social media optimized)
- **Duration**: Exactly 15 seconds

## Visual Style

### Premium Miniature Aesthetic

- **Quality**: Handcrafted architectural diorama, luxury collector model
- **Craftsmanship**: Highly detailed practical film miniature quality
- **Materials**: Realistic painted wood and stone, precise handcrafted textures
- **NOT**: Cheap toy, glossy plastic, low-quality craft project, rough handmade

### Scale & Composition

- **Island**: Enormous, expansive, wide-scale (not tiny tabletop)
- **Environment**: Large harbour town across entire island
- **Camera**: Wide-angle compositions maintaining island scale
- **Depth**: Layered foreground, midground, background throughout

### Lighting & Color

- **Daylight**: Bright, clear, cinematic coastal daylight
- **Water**: Sparkling bright blue sea with clean white waves
- **Architecture**: Red, blue, and white rooftops
- **Materials**: Wooden piers, stone seawalls, polished craftsmanship
- **Atmosphere**: Charming but realistic, premium coastal charm

### Depth of Field

- **Effect**: Subtle miniature scale depth of field
- **Purpose**: Enhances handcrafted diorama feeling
- **Avoid**: Extreme shallow DOF making it look like tiny toy
- **Balance**: Maintain environmental scale while preserving miniature charm

## Camera Specifications

### Wide-Angle Priority

Continuously communicate the scale of the island through:

- High aerial opening
- Smooth descending push toward harbour
- Medium-wide waterfront tracking
- Gentle lighthouse orbit
- Grand aerial pullback for final reveal

### Camera Movements

1. **Opening**: High aerial wide establishing shot
2. **Descent**: Smooth push down toward main harbour
3. **Harbour Level**: Medium-wide tracking along waterfront
4. **Lighthouse**: Gentle orbit around tower
5. **Final Reveal**: Grand aerial pullback to complete island view

### Composition Rules

- **Layered Shots**: Foreground, midground, background in frame
- **Multiple Elements**: Harbour + hillside + lighthouse + bay + sea visible together
- **Scale Preservation**: Wide framing showing island as expansive
- ✅ Use seamless cinematic transitions (2-3 during 15 seconds)
- ❌ Avoid long static shots
- ❌ Avoid repetitive straight-line motion
- ❌ Avoid extreme close-ups making it look like tiny toy

### Pacing

- **Speed**: Fast, energetic, focused on satisfying formation
- **Rhythm**: Precise assembly with clean mechanical feel
- **Energy**: Exciting construction progression
- **Flow**: Smooth transitions between scenes/camera angles

## Negative Prompt

### Scale & Environment Issues

```
small island, tiny enclosed tabletop scene, cramped harbour, only a few
houses, limited environment scale, narrow composition, empty harbour,
confined space, miniature appearing too small, tabletop toy look
```

### Quality & Material Issues

```
cheap toy appearance, glossy plastic materials, low-quality craft project,
rough handmade construction, amateur craftsmanship, poor quality miniature,
shiny plastic, unrealistic materials, cartoon style
```

### Lighting & Atmosphere Issues

```
dark cloudy weather, muddy water, dull colors, poor lighting, overcast sky,
grey tones, desaturated, dim lighting, night scene, foggy atmosphere
```

### Human & Character Issues

```
oversized humans, human characters as the main subject, full-size people
walking through the miniature, prominent human figures, people in frame,
disproportionate humans, giant people
```

### Camera & Composition Issues

```
excessive macro shots, extreme shallow depth of field, only close-up views,
repetitive linear camera motion, long static shots, no camera movement,
boring composition, single angle only, frozen ending
```

### Design Consistency Issues

```
chaotic building placement, incorrect island layout, design that does not
match @reference_image, wrong harbour configuration, different coastline,
mismatched architecture, altered lighthouse position, wrong roof colors
```

### Text & Branding Issues

```
text overlays, captions, subtitles, logos, branding, watermark, UI elements,
labels, date stamps, social media handles
```
