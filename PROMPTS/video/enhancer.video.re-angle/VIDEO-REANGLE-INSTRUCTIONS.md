# Video Re-Angle Instructions

## Overview

Use the reference video as the performance source. Preserve the subject's body movement, timing, facial expression, dialogue, lip sync, action, environment, and overall choreography exactly as in the original. Change only the camera perspective.

## Camera Replacement Guidelines

Replace the original camera with [YOUR NEW CAMERA ANGLE].

This new camera should remain physically consistent with its described position and movement throughout the entire shot.

The framing should clearly establish this as a completely new viewpoint rather than a variation of the original camera. The camera must never drift back toward the original framing, transition into the original viewpoint, or imitate the original composition.

## Subject Awareness Rules

If the subject is looking toward another camera or point of interest in the original video, preserve that original eyeline. The subject should not acknowledge or look toward this new camera unless explicitly intended. This camera is purely observational.

## Technical Consistency Requirements

Maintain realistic perspective, lens behaviour, parallax, motion blur, camera inertia, and depth consistent with the chosen camera placement.

Preserve every aspect of the original performance, animation, pacing, timing, and interaction exactly as in the reference video.

Do not modify the action, choreography, dialogue, or environment in any way. Only the camera perspective changes.

## Usage Notes

- This is an enhancer for video content that changes camera angle while preserving all performance aspects
- The key principle: Everything stays the same except the camera viewpoint
- Subject eyelines and interactions remain locked to the original video
- Camera position must be physically and optically consistent throughout
- No drift toward original framing or composition allowed
