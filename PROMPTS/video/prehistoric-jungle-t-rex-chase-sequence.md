# Prehistoric Jungle T-Rex Chase Sequence

**Format:** 10 seconds, cinematic action sequence  
**Type:** Dramatic chase scene with dynamic camera movements  
**Subject:** Young woman fleeing from pursuing Tyrannosaurus rex  
**Style:** Photorealistic cinematic, Jurassic Park aesthetic

---

## CONCEPT

10-second cinematic realistic video capturing terrifying prehistoric predator chase through dense misty jungle environment. Terrified young woman with short blonde bob wavy hair and bangs, wearing short magenta-pink dress, barefoot, desperately running through foggy tropical forest. Massive photorealistic Tyrannosaurus rex with detailed scaly gray skin and open roaring jaws aggressively pursues from behind and side. Dense prehistoric jungle setting: tall palm trees, thick green foliage, ferns, muddy dirt path, atmospheric volumetric fog. Dynamic camera progression: tracking shot following woman's desperate sprint, over-the-shoulder perspective from T-Rex viewpoint, close-up of muddy bare feet splashing in wet mud, concluding with extreme close-up of panicked tearful screaming face. Cinematic lighting (diffused through fog, dramatic shadows), high photorealistic detail, dramatic motion blur on fast movement, authentic Jurassic Park visual style. Intense survival horror atmosphere, primal fear, life-or-death chase energy.
