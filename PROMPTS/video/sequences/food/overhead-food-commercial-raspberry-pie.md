# Overhead Food Commercial - Raspberry Pie Preparation

## Description

Create an ultra-realistic cinematic food commercial in 16:9 format, 10 seconds duration, with a static overhead camera locked at exactly 90 degrees. The camera never moves, never rotates, never zooms. This prompt delivers a complete cooking sequence from ingredients to final plated dessert with premium studio lighting, soft reflections, high-end food photography standards, realistic materials, natural motion blur, physically accurate food simulation, and seamless continuity—ideal for premium food advertising, cooking show intros, recipe visualization, and luxury culinary brand marketing.
