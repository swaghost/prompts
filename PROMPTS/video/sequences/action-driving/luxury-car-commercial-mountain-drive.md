# Luxury Car Commercial - Mountain Drive

## Description

Create a cinematic luxury car commercial following a precise story in exact order. A stylish man in a tailored black suit walks toward a matte black Mercedes-AMG coupe parked in front of a modern mountain villa, enters the vehicle, and drives through dramatic mountain scenery. Entire story unfolds in 10 seconds with smooth continuity, premium color grading, shallow depth of field, natural motion blur, and golden hour atmosphere.

## Overview

This prompt generates an ultra-realistic luxury automotive commercial that showcases premium lifestyle, sophisticated design, and driving performance through a carefully choreographed 10-shot sequence. The narrative follows classic luxury car advertising structure: arrival → engagement → power → departure → performance → conclusion, all executed with high-end cinematography and seamless transitions.

## Technical Notes

### Shot Duration and Pacing

**1 Second Per Shot Strategy:**

- **Establishing (Shot 1)**: Wide context, set scene
- **Engagement (Shots 2-3)**: Human connection, entry
- **Power (Shot 4)**: Detail, mechanical excellence
- **Departure (Shot 5)**: Beginning of journey
- **Performance (Shots 6-8)**: Dynamic driving, capability
- **Scale (Shot 9)**: Environment, epic scope
- **Hero (Shot 10)**: Final impact, lasting impression

**Transition Techniques:**

- Match on action (movement continuity)
- Cut on motion blur (smooth, invisible cuts)
- Graphic match (similar compositions)
- Momentum match (maintaining energy flow)

### Camera Equipment and Settings

**Wide Shots (Shots 1, 5, 9):**

- Lens: 24-35mm full-frame equivalent
- Aperture: f/4-f/5.6 (moderate depth)
- Shutter: 1/50s (180° shutter angle at 24fps)
- Movement: Smooth slider, dolly, or crane

**Medium Shots (Shots 2, 8):**

- Lens: 50-85mm
- Aperture: f/2.8-f/4 (shallow depth)
- Shutter: 1/50s
- Movement: Tracking rig, gimbal stabilization

**Close-Ups (Shots 3, 4):**

- Lens: 85-100mm, macro for Shot 4
- Aperture: f/1.4-f/2.8 (very shallow)
- Shutter: 1/50s
- Movement: Static or minimal

**Tracking Shots (Shots 6, 7, 10):**

- Camera: Pursuit vehicle rig, Russian arm, or drone
- Lens: 35-85mm depending on distance
- Aperture: f/2.8-f/5.6
- Shutter: 1/50s (natural motion blur)
- Stabilization: Gyro-stabilized head

**Aerial (Shot 9):**

- Camera: Cinema drone (Inspire 3, Alta, etc.)
- Lens: 35-50mm equivalent
- Aperture: f/4-f/5.6
- Movement: Smooth GPS-controlled flight path

### Golden Hour Lighting Specifications

**Time of Day:**

- 1-2 hours before sunset (depending on season and latitude)
- Sun angle: 10-20° above horizon
- Color temperature: 3500-4500K (warm, golden)
- Quality: Directional but soft, long shadows

**Lighting Characteristics:**

- Warm golden glow on surfaces
- Long shadows creating depth and dimension
- Contrast moderate (not harsh)
- Highlights rich and saturated
- Shadows still retain detail (not crushed)

**Additional Lighting (if needed):**

- Reflectors to fill shadows on car
