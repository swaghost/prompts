# Luxury Interior Wave Assembly Sequence

## Description

A premium architectural visualization sequence showcasing an empty space transforming into a fully furnished luxury interior through progressive wave-like assembly from left to right. Each element materializes in logical construction order—flooring, wall panels, cabinetry, furniture, lighting, plants, and decorative objects—creating a smooth, choreographed reveal that emphasizes the design's sophistication and spatial flow. The sequence mimics a cinematic tracking shot moving through the space as elements seamlessly appear.

## Prerequisites

- Empty interior space with defined architecture (walls, ceiling, floor boundaries)
- Luxury or high-end interior design aesthetic
- Consistent directional flow (left to right) for element assembly
- Clear spatial depth allowing for wave progression
- Premium materials: hardwood flooring, quality cabinetry, designer furniture
- Sophisticated lighting design with multiple fixture types
- Curated decorative elements and greenery
