# Cinematic FPV Drone - Milad Tower Spiral Ascent

## Description

Create an ultra-smooth cinematic FPV drone shot featuring a single continuous unbroken take with hyper-professional stabilization. The sequence captures a dramatic spiral ascent around an iconic tower (Milad Tower, Tehran) with progressive lens compression, strong directional motion blur, and photorealistic hyperreal detail. No cuts, no morphing, no camera shake or jitter—pure technical perfection with energetic relentless pacing and professional sound design.

## Shot Overview

**Type:** Single continuous unbroken take, FPV drone cinematography  
**Movement:** Street level launch → fast powerful spiral ascent → arrival at antenna spire  
**Duration:** Constant high speed throughout, energetic and relentless  
**Style:** Photorealistic, hyperreal detail, no warped architecture  
**Technical:** Hyper-smooth stabilization, zero camera shake, progressive lens compression
