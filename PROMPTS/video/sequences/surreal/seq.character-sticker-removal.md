# Character Sticker Removal

## Description

A creative video effect where a character is physically peeled away from the scene like a 2D sticker, revealing the background underneath, then held up for display.

## Prerequisites

- Scene with character to be removed
- Background scene to reveal

## Video Prompt

First, show a wide establishing shot with a realistic photographic texture.

Then, a slender hand enters from the right side of the frame, pinching the upper-right corner of the character from the right side of the frame wearing a suit with an emoji face, as if it were a 2D sticker. from head to toe, the entire character, The whole character is lifted by the fingertips like a piece of paper and slowly peeled away diagonally from the top-right to the bottom-left.

After the character is completely removed, only the background remains. The hand then holds the emoji-faced character wearing a suit sticker up toward the camera for display. The sticker matches the original character's size and shape, gently wobbling as it is held. The scene ends on a frozen frame.

**Sound design:** realistic scrapbook-like handling sounds, paper tearing sounds, and ambient white noise.

## Usage

Perfect for creative transitions, social media content, advertising reveals, magical removal effects, comedic sketches, and artistic video effects. Ideal for character removal narratives, background reveals, playful transformation sequences, and creative editing demonstrations with physical sticker peeling metaphor.
