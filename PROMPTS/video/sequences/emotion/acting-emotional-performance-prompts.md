## Prompt 1: Gun Scene - Internal Struggle

**Duration:** 12 seconds  
**Theme:** Character pointing gun at camera, experiencing internal conflict about pulling trigger

### Full Prompt

Realistic style, cinematic soft lighting and shadow, natural colors. Handheld shooting with natural human breathing rise and fall, conveying a hand-held feel. The framing uses an extreme close-up with a shallow depth of field, heavily blurring the foreground to focus entirely on the character's facial micro-expressions. No BGM, no subtitles.

**0-2 seconds:** The frame remains completely identical to reference image. The gun barrel points steadily at the camera; the gaze is cold and restrained, the jaw slightly clenched, accompanied by subtle breathing and strands of hair blowing in the wind.

**2-4 seconds:** The eyes suddenly tremble, the pupils slightly contract, and the brow furrows faintly before instantly relaxing; the corner of the lip twitches imperceptibly, and the hand holding the gun shakes minutely, causing the barrel to wobble gently as inner struggle begins to surface.

**4-6 seconds:** The gaze hesitatingly drifts toward the bottom right before forcing itself back to lock onto the camera, a hint of pain rising in the eyes; the lips part slightly as if to speak but stop, ultimately closing silently as the Adam's apple gently swallows.

**6-8 seconds:** The brow slightly furrows but immediately relaxes again, the rims of the eyes becoming slightly glistening; the gun barrel dips slightly but remains directed at the camera, the chin trembles slightly, and the breathing becomes short and unsteady.

**8-10 seconds:** Suddenly closing both eyes, taking a deep breath as the eyelids tremble uncontrollably; upon opening them again, tears are clearly visible, the expression vulnerable and struggling, the gun barrel is leveled once more, and the trembling of the arm faintly shows through the gun body.

**10-12 seconds:** The corners of the mouth tighten downward, fighting back tears that are about to spill over, as the gun barrel begins to lower slowly, inch by inch.

## Prompt 2: Scarf Scene - Hidden Emotions

**Duration:** 11.5 seconds  
**Theme:** Character suppressing secret joy while experiencing vulnerability and internal conflict

### Full Prompt

Realistic style, cinematic soft lighting and shadow, natural colors. Handheld shooting with natural human breathing rise and fall, conveying a hand-held feel. The framing uses an extreme close-up with a shallow depth of field, heavily blurring the foreground to focus entirely on the character's facial micro-expressions. No BGM, no subtitles.

**0-5 seconds: Hidden Restraint (Suppressed Secret Joy)**

Extreme Muscle Control: The smiling muscles rapidly "relax" within 0.1 seconds, leaving no trace.

Emotion Conveyance: The smile is completely withdrawn, leaving only a 1mm curve at the corner of the mouth. This extremely restrained treatment shows that the character cannot, or does not want, others around to notice her emotion, leaving only a hint of secret joy for "the person opposite her" that only the two of them can understand.

Eye Lighting Effect: The pupils contract and the brightness at the bottom of the eyes rises by 0.5 degrees. This is an extremely delicate piece of "eye acting"—there is light, but it is restrained.

**5s - 7s: Instinctive Breakdown (Instant Surge of Grievance)**

Physiological Flushing: The faint redness near the tear gland of the lower eyelid at the 5th second is the peak physiological manifestation of a sense of grievance.

Defocusing and Distraction: The iris focus blurs for 0.3 seconds, and the gaze loses focus, indicating that the inner defense line has taken a hit at this moment, resulting in a brief flash of panic and helplessness.

Lip Details: The uncontrolled twitching of the upper lip followed by pursing the lips is a classic instinctive human reaction when attempting to suppress crying or grievance.

**7s - 11.5s: Self-Healing Detachment (Sinking into Deep Thought)**

Lowered Gaze: The gaze returns to the scarf, and the upper eyelid moves down to cover 35% of the iris. This is the most typical "defensive" and "introspective" lowering of the head.

Frequency Tremble: The eyelashes tremble once per second, with the eyes half-closed leaving just a slit, pushing the character's vulnerability to the absolute maximum.

The Final Defiance: The lower teeth gently rub against the lower lip, pressing out a 0.5mm indentation. This "lip-biting" action represents final emotional resistance.

## Key Elements for Custom Prompts

### Universal Opening Format

```
Realistic style, cinematic soft lighting and shadow, natural colors. Handheld shooting with natural human breathing rise and fall, conveying a hand-held feel. The framing uses an extreme close-up with a shallow depth of field, heavily blurring the foreground to focus entirely on the character's facial micro-expressions. No BGM, no subtitles.
```

### Timing Structure

Break down performance into 2-second segments with precise descriptions:

- Start with static or baseline emotional state
- Build emotional intensity gradually
- Include specific micro-expressions at exact timestamps
- End with resolution or peak emotional moment

### Micro-Expression Elements to Include

**Eyes:**

- Pupil dilation/contraction
- Eye trembling
- Glistening or tears
- Focus blur duration
- Eyelid position and movement
- Eyelash trembling frequency
- Brightness changes in eye bottom

**Mouth/Lips:**

- Corner curves (specify millimeters)
- Lip twitching
- Lip pursing
- Lip biting with indentation depth
- Mouth opening/closing
- Corner tightening

**Face:**

- Brow furrowing and relaxing
- Jaw clenching
- Chin trembling
- Physiological flushing locations
- Muscle tension and relaxation timing

**Body/Breathing:**

- Breathing patterns (subtle, short, unsteady, deep)
- Adam's apple movement
- Hand/arm trembling
- Natural breathing rise and fall

**Gaze Direction:**

- Where eyes look and when
- Directional shifts (specific: "bottom right")
- Lock-on moments vs. drifting
- Focus vs. defocus

### Restrictions

**Always Include:**

- No BGM
- No subtitles
- Natural colors
- Realistic style
- Cinematic soft lighting and shadow

**Camera Style:**

- Handheld shooting
- Natural human breathing rise and fall
- Extreme close-up
- Shallow depth of field
- Heavily blur foreground
