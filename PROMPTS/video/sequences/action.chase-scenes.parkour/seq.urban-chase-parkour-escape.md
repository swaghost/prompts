## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**

- Make running/jumping unrealistic (superhuman speed/height)
- Ignore physics (floating over barrier)
- Over-dramatize (wire-work style parkour)
- Perfect the environment (too clean, too dry)
- Stabilize camera too much (loses handheld realism)
- Add impossible camera angles (drone-like movements)
- Make vehicle near-miss too close (unrealistic)
- Ignore consequences (no exhaustion, no effort)
- Clean up the rain (must persist throughout)

✅ **Do:**

- Show realistic running mechanics
- Demonstrate effort (climbing barrier takes work)
- Maintain wet environment throughout
- Use natural camera shake (human operator)
- Show breathing and exertion
- Make vehicle brake at safe realistic distance
- Use handrail on stairs (realistic safety behavior)
- Keep all movements grounded in physics
