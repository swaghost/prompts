## POSSIBLE MISCATEGORIZATION

## Description of Resulting Video

A cinematic hyperlapse aerial time-lapse with locked-off fixed camera position, never moving or cutting, maintaining one unbroken take from first frame to last. The camera remains completely stationary in elevated aerial position looking down on the abandoned two-story tropical villa, with the same exact framing throughout showing cracked dirty concrete deck, empty rectangular pool with blue mosaic tiles, round empty jacuzzi beside it, stained concrete house walls with graffiti, broken windows, white columns, pink debris on the ground floor, bare concrete columns, no railings on upper balcony, and ocean/jungle visible beyond in background. The dramatic sky transformation provides all visual dynamism—time-lapse clouds in constant fast motion racing across frame, shadows sweeping dramatically over villa and deck as sun position shifts, and natural lighting transitioning through accelerated daylight hours. White clouds glide rapidly across blue sky while the abandoned architecture below remains frozen testament to human absence, creating powerful contrast between celestial perpetual motion and earthly static decay.
