## Description of Resulting Video

A cinematic time-lapse from locked-off fixed aerial camera position directly overhead, maintaining one unbroken bird's-eye take with camera absolutely never moving throughout entire duration. The same exact framing persists from first frame to last—empty rectangular swimming pool with dark blue mosaic tiles beneath filth, round empty jacuzzi adjacent, severely deteriorated concrete deck with cracks and staining, debris scattered throughout, and lush tropical vegetation encroaching from edges with palm fronds visible. The dramatic visual transformation comes entirely from accelerated sky overhead—time-lapse clouds racing rapidly across blue sky in constant fast motion visible in any reflective surfaces or at frame edges, creating sweeping shadow patterns across pool deck, concrete, and vegetation below as sun position shifts dramatically throughout compressed daylight hours. The overhead perspective emphasizes geometric shadow movement—rectangular pool shape receiving changing shadow patterns, circular jacuzzi casting evolving shadows, vegetation shadows sweeping across concrete surfaces, and the interplay between static architectural decay and dynamic celestial cycles creating powerful temporal narrative.
