## Description of Resulting Video

A cinematic linear camera motion time-lapse like slider hyperlapse showing smooth horizontal camera movement across an abandoned luxury tropical mansion during golden hour transition, with the sky above continuously transforming through accelerated cloud movement while white clouds glide steadily across blue sky from harsh midday to warm golden late afternoon. The camera executes smooth linear horizontal slider motion throughout the entire video, traveling laterally across the property while maintaining consistent framing of the deteriorated two-story curved villa and swimming pool. The sky transformation provides dramatic time-passage visual through fast time-lapse cloud motion, with shadows sweeping dramatically across the villa and deck below as sun position changes. The mansion remains the constant architectural subject—dirty stained concrete facade with graffiti on upper floor, crumbling white columns, broken windows, pink stained walls, and the abandoned rectangular pool with blue mosaic tiles visible throughout. Ground level view captured from abandoned property perspective shows the pool and mansion consistently as camera glides horizontally, maintaining architectural storytelling while sky creates dynamic time-lapse background. The blue sky fills upper frame with white clouds in constant smooth fast motion suggesting hours compressed into seconds, shadows shift naturally across surfaces as light angle changes, and the overall atmosphere transitions from harsh bright midday through softer warm golden late afternoon lighting. The entire composition balances static abandoned architecture in foreground/midground with dynamic accelerated sky movement above, creating cinematic contrast between frozen-in-time human structures and ever-changing natural elements.
