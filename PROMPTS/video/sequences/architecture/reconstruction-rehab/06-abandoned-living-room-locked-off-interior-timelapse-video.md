## Description of Resulting Video

A cinematic locked-off fixed camera time-lapse maintaining one unbroken stationary take from slightly elevated frontal interior perspective, with camera absolutely never moving throughout entire duration. The same exact framing persists from first frame to last—spacious abandoned modernist living room with massive broken floor-to-ceiling windows, lush tropical vegetation visible through all openings, dirty pink concrete or terrazzo tile flooring with debris, white/light walls with moisture stains and decay, and open-plan spatial flow. The dramatic visual transformation comes entirely from natural lighting evolution as harsh sunlight streaming through broken windows shifts position dramatically over accelerated daylight hours, creating sweeping shadow patterns across pink flooring, moving volumetric light shafts through the interior space, changing wall illumination that reveals or conceals decay details, and transforming the relationship between bright exterior and dim interior throughout the compressed time period. The interplay between static abandoned domestic architecture and dynamic natural light cycles creates powerful contemplative narrative about time's passage in frozen spaces.
