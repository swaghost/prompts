# Netflix Documentary Interview – Cinematic Portrait to 15-Second Opening

## Description

A sophisticated two-step AI video workflow creating a premium streaming documentary interview opening sequence with cinematic production value, professional lighting, and realistic behind-the-scenes energy. The process begins with generating a polished interview portrait using face reference for identity preservation—subject seated in vintage leather armchair against textured navy-blue wall with diagonal orange color streak and warm practical lamp, styled in refined monochromatic beige outfit with gold accessories, lit with premium cinematic key/rim/edge lighting creating soft highlight rolloff and teal-and-orange color grading. This static portrait then becomes the single visual reference for a 15-second video sequence featuring five distinct camera angles: empty chair opener with slow zoom-in as subject enters and sits, main seated interview angle with uncertain "which camera should I look at?" question, tight top-down portrait with throat-clear gesture, behind-the-scenes camera monitor shot showing production equipment, and over-the-shoulder reverse angle revealing interviewer and studio environment with crew. The video captures authentic documentary interview preparation moments—subject's initial uncertainty transitioning to relaxed confidence, interviewer's calm professional presence announcing "episode one," and subject's shy sweet laugh responding "alright… let's do this." Features strict dialogue assignment preventing voice swapping, accurate lip synchronization, spatial continuity maintaining consistent screen direction and studio geography, realistic body language avoiding exaggerated reactions, and premium streaming aesthetic with subtle film grain, cinematic bokeh, and sophisticated lighting. Perfect for personal documentaries, interview series openings, creator origin stories, brand founder narratives, and any content requiring polished professional interview aesthetic with authentic human moments.

## Pro Tips

### Identity Preservation

**Best Face Reference:**

- Clear frontal portrait, well-lit
- Neutral expression (natural smile acceptable)
- Face fills 40-60% of frame
- Sharp focus on facial features
- Even lighting (no harsh shadows)
- High resolution source

**If Identity Drifts in Video:**

- Step 1 portrait must be clear with strong identity match first
- Regenerate Step 1 if identity not matching before proceeding to Step 2
- Step 2 video inherits identity from Step 1 portrait
- Cannot fix identity drift in video if Step 1 portrait unclear

### Outfit Customization Success

**Keep These Elements:**

- Monochromatic or coordinated color scheme
- Fitted but tasteful (not too tight or baggy)
- Gender-neutral, minimalist aesthetic
- Gold or metallic accessories for accent
- High-quality fabric descriptions
- Professional/elevated feel

**Good Alternative Outfits:**

- Black turtleneck + black trousers + silver jewelry
- Charcoal blazer + white shirt + gold watch
- Navy button-up + navy chinos + gold cufflinks
- Burgundy knit sweater + dark jeans + gold rings
- Forest green shirt + beige pants + bronze necklace

### Dialogue Customization

**Keep These Qualities:**

- Brief conversational lines (3-8 words)
- Natural speech patterns
- Appropriate to documentary interview context
- Clear speaker assignments
- Realistic emotional tone

**Good Custom Examples:**

- Subject: "Am I in the right spot?"
- Interviewer: "[Name], take one, whenever you're ready."
- Subject: "Okay, here we go." + nervous laugh

**Remember BOTH Places:**

- Update in STRICT DIALOGUE ASSIGNMENT section
- Update in timed sequence at exact timestamp
- Match both or video won't sync properly

### Camera Monitor Shot Success

**Key Elements:**

- Monitor/display is PRIMARY FOCUS (sharp and clear)
- Subject visible on monitor wearing exact outfit from Step 1
- Real subject behind monitor softly blurred (shallow DOF)
- Both monitor-subject and real-subject match in identity and pose
- Some camera body, softbox, or light stand visible
- Professional BTS aesthetic (not cluttered)

**Common Issues:**

- If monitor not sharp: Emphasize "camera monitor is primary focus, sharp and crisp"
- If real subject not visible behind: Emphasize "real subject also visible behind monitor, softly blurred"
- If identity doesn't match on screen: Check Step 1 portrait identity clarity

### Reverse Angle Environment

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION:**

- Forward angles (subject's POV): Polished interview set (navy wall, orange stripe, lamp)
- Reverse angle (interviewer's POV): Real production room (cameras, crew, equipment)

**If Reverse Shows Wrong Background:**

- Emphasize "interviewer must NOT appear against polished hero background"
- Strengthen language: "reverse angle reveals REAL PRODUCTION ROOM behind set"
- List specific elements: cameras, operator, stands, softbox, crew, cables, cases
- Specify "rear side or edge of constructed backdrop visible"

### Performance Direction

**Subject Uncertainty (Early):**

- Subtle, not exaggerated
- Soft glances left/right (not jerky head swivels)
- Slight hesitation (not frozen or stiff)
- Friendly unsure tone (not panicked)
- Natural body language

**Throat-Clear Gesture:**

- Hand rises near mouth briefly
- Polite preparation gesture
- NOT dramatic cough
- NOT looking upward
- Maintains forward eyeline toward interviewer

**Final Laugh:**

- Shy, sweet, bashful
- Short and warm
- Natural smile progression
- Soft body language (shoulders relax)
- Charming modest reaction
- NOT loud, theatrical, or exaggerated

### Video Editing Add-Ons

**On-Screen Text (Add in Editor):**

- Subject name and title/description
- "Episode One" or episode designation
- Appears ~0-3 seconds during empty chair opener
- White sans-serif font (Helvetica, Arial, Gotham)
- Lower third or center placement
- Fade in/out or simple cut

**Sound Design Enhancement:**

- Subtle room tone throughout (already in generation)
- Can add very quiet ambient studio sound
- Slight reverb on dialogue (optional, keeps intimacy)
- Gentle fade-in at start, fade-out at end
- NO music (documentary realism)

**Color Grading Refinement:**

- Generated video has teal-orange grading built-in
- Can fine-tune in editor if needed
- Lift shadows slightly if too dark
- Warm highlights slightly more amber
- Keep skin tones natural (don't over-grade)
