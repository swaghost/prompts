# House Construction Timelapse - Aerial

## Description

A 10-second static aerial timelapse showing custom home construction from empty lot to completed house with BBC documentary style. Features locked camera position, consistent midday lighting, and blurred construction crew throughout.

## Prerequisites

None - generates from scratch

## Video Prompt

**Duration:** 10 seconds. Single continuous shot. No cuts. No dissolves. No transitions.

**Style:** BBC documentary style timelapse — hushed reverence, patient observation, nothing staged, subjects unaware of camera.

**CAMERA:** Static aerial drone-height hold, locked off, positioned directly above and slightly forward of the build site at a high overhead angle, roughly 45 degrees down-angle looking toward the lot. Camera does not pan, does not tilt, does not drift, does not zoom. Perfectly still for the entire 10 seconds, as if mounted and locked in place. Center-weighted framing — the house footprint remains dead center of frame at all times, never drifting off-center.

**LIGHTING:** Consistent bright midday daylight throughout the entire shot. Sun position fixed directly overhead, shadows short and constant in length and direction from first frame to last frame. No sunrise, no sunset, no color temperature shift, no shadow movement — this is timelapse footage compressed from many consecutive midday work sessions, not a single day cycle, so the light never moves.

**SUBJECT:** A custom home construction site, viewed from directly above at drone height, evolving continuously within this single static frame across the following unbroken progression. A construction crew is visibly present and working throughout every stage — always rendered as fast, blurred, indistinct human motion, never resolving into individual faces or clear figures, consistent with real speed-up timelapse footage of moving people.

### Timeline

**0-2s — RAW LOT:** Empty staked rectangular lot, string lines marking the foundation outline, bare earth. Blurred crew figures moving quickly around the stakes, surveying and marking.

**2-4s — FOUNDATION:** Concrete foundation and slab poured and cured in place, rebar caps visible at the perimeter. Blurred crew streaking across the slab pushing wheelbarrows and running concrete finishing tools.

**6-8s — ENVELOPE:** Roof sheathing and black felt cover the trusses, exterior walls sheathed in plywood, window openings framed, scaffolding appears along the sides. Blurred crew moving rapidly along the scaffold and roofline.

**8-10s — COMPLETE HOME:** Finished siding, stone accents, driveway paved, landscaping installed, roof shingled, the completed custom home fully resolved directly below camera. Blurred crew finishing final landscaping and cleanup, then fading out of frame as the shot settles on the completed, empty, finished home.

All transformation happens as continuous, physically plausible growth within the same locked frame — no jump cuts, no fades, no flash transitions between stages. Materials and structures evolve smoothly as if compressed real time. Crew motion is fast and blurred throughout but never chaotic — always readable as construction work. Camera position, angle, and framing remain absolutely identical from first frame to last frame. Lighting never changes.

No avatar. No dialogue. No text overlays. No music.

## Usage

Ideal for construction company marketing, custom home builder portfolios, real estate development showcases, BBC-style documentary sequences, timelapses demonstrations, and construction process visualizations. Perfect for showcasing building progression with locked aerial camera, documentary-style realism, and compressed construction timelines with consistent lighting and worker activity.
