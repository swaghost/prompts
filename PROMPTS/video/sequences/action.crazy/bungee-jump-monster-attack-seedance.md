## SCENE BREAKDOWN

### 0-4 SECONDS - FINAL SAFETY CHECK

The video begins on the bungee jump platform shown in @image1.

The **main character** from @image2 stands near the centre of the platform wearing the exact same grey T-shirt, black shorts, white socks, white trainers, climbing helmet and safety harness shown in his reference image.

The two **bungee operators** from @image3 and @image4 stand close beside him.

They are professional employees of the bungee jump company and calmly perform the **final safety inspection** before the jump.

- One operator firmly checks the main waist section and attachment points of the harness with both hands.
- The second operator checks the bungee connection, attachment hardware and safety connection at the front of the harness.
- Their movements must look natural, practiced and professional rather than theatrical.
- Show realistic physical interaction between their hands, the harness straps, buckles and cord.

The main character looks slightly nervous but composed.

The drone slowly drifts sideways and backwards during this inspection, subtly revealing the enormous river valley and terrifying height behind them.

**Do not rush this section.**

---

### 4-8 SECONDS - APPROACHING THE EDGE

Once the operators finish their checks, they release the harness and step safely backward.

The main character turns toward the edge of the platform.

He slowly and carefully walks forward in several small controlled steps.

His movements should communicate natural apprehension.

The bungee cord trails correctly from his harness and remains physically consistent.

The drone smoothly moves around toward a slightly front three-quarter position outside the platform, effectively moving backward over open space while keeping the man framed.

This movement should gradually reveal the immense vertical drop directly behind and beneath him.

The two operators remain visible farther back on the platform but become less dominant in the frame.

The main character reaches the very edge.

His toes approach the edge of the platform.

He briefly looks downward toward the distant river.

---

### 8-10 SECONDS - FINAL BREATH

The man pauses at the edge.

He takes **one clearly visible deep breath**.

His chest rises.

He exhales.

He then deliberately extends both arms outward beside him, preparing to jump.

There should be a brief moment of stillness and anticipation.

The drone continues drifting gently backward and slightly downward, maintaining a clear cinematic view of the man silhouetted against the huge river valley below.

---

### 10-15 SECONDS - THE JUMP AND FALL

The main character decisively pushes himself forward from the platform and jumps.

His body leaves the platform naturally under gravity.

Immediately after he jumps, the drone accelerates downward and forward to follow him.

**This must remain the exact same uninterrupted camera shot.**

The camera follows alongside and slightly above him as he falls rapidly toward the river.

- His body should respond realistically to air resistance and gravity.
- His arms and legs move naturally during freefall.
- His clothing reacts to the rushing air.
- The bungee cord extends progressively behind him in a physically believable manner.

The bridge rapidly rises into the background as the man falls deeper into the valley.

The drone should dynamically descend beside him, creating a dramatic cinematic tracking shot while maintaining believable drone flight.

The river grows rapidly larger beneath him.

Maintain a strong sense of extreme height, speed and scale.

**There must be no slow motion. The fall occurs in real time.**

---

### 15-18 SECONDS - MONSTER ATTACK

As the man reaches dangerously close to the surface of the river, something enormous moves beneath the water.

At the last possible moment, the **giant prehistoric marine monster** from @image5 suddenly erupts vertically from the river directly beneath him.

The creature must be **absolutely enormous**, dramatically larger than the human, with the scale clearly matching the gigantic proportions established in its character reference.

Its massive head violently breaks through the surface. A huge volume of river water explodes upward around its skull. Its enormous mouth is already opened extremely wide.

Clearly preserve the exact oversized jaw, tooth structure, head shape, skin colour, scars and anatomical design from @image5.

**The scale difference should be shocking:** the man appears tiny compared with the creature's enormous open mouth.

The monster continues its upward momentum and catches the falling man in one enormous bite, closing its jaws around him and taking him out of view.

Keep the impact intense and sudden but cinematic rather than excessively graphic. Do not show exposed organs, dismemberment or prolonged gore.

The bungee cord reacts naturally to the violent movement and remains physically connected according to the preceding action.

Immediately after the bite, gravity pulls the enormous creature downward. The monster falls back into the river with tremendous mass.

Its giant body produces an enormous realistic splash and powerful displacement of water. Huge sheets of water, spray and foam erupt outward.

The drone instinctively banks upward and backward slightly to avoid the spray while continuing to track the exact location where the monster disappears beneath the surface.

---

### 18-20 SECONDS - AFTERMATH

The monster is now completely submerged.

The drone hovers above and slightly forward of the impact location, looking down toward the disturbed river surface.

Large expanding waves, bubbles, foam and turbulent water remain where the monster submerged. The enormous disturbance gradually begins settling.

For approximately two seconds, there is no sign of the man or monster.

Then, very subtly, a **small faint patch of reddish colour** begins diffusing through the disturbed water at the centre of the impact area.

Keep this restrained and realistic: a subtle diluted red tint mixing slowly into the river rather than exaggerated gore.

The implication is clear that the man has been killed by the creature.

End the video while the drone continues hovering over the slowly expanding ripples and faint red discoloration. Do not reveal the monster again.

**The splash** must correspond to the immense scale of the creature. Water droplets, mist, foam, waves and displacement should react naturally.
