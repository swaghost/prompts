# Miniature Construction Gourmet Hot Dog

## Description

A 10-second cinematic sequence showing tiny construction workers and machinery building a gourmet hot dog step by step in a premium café setting. Features Pixar-level miniature realism and continuous smooth animation.

## Prerequisites

- Storyboard reference image

## Storyboard Prompt

Create a 6-panel storyboard showing the miniature construction of the gourmet hot dog:

**Panel 1 (0:00-0:02):** Wide shot of cozy premium café interior with warm lighting, empty construction platform centered on wooden table, tiny construction workers in orange safety vests arriving with blueprints, miniature excavators and cranes positioning themselves, construction site setup phase, camera slow push forward, shallow depth of field with café background.

**Panel 2 (0:02-0:04):** Medium shot showing construction crane lowering bottom bun into position, miniature workers guiding with ropes, then crane lifting grilled sausage to place into bun, scaffolding rising around the construction, dynamic camera orbiting around construction site, workers actively coordinating.

**Panel 3 (0:04-0:06):** Close-up of tiny crews rapidly installing toppings, workers climbing ladders, cranes repositioning ingredients, then large ketchup and mustard bottles applying perfect zigzag sauce streams like industrial dispensers, miniature workers coordinating from elevated platforms, macro food photography style with construction activity.

**Panel 4 (0:06-0:08):** Medium shot of miniature engineers inspecting completed hot dog with inspection lights, workers celebrating, construction equipment beginning organized departure, forklifts and cranes leaving in convoy, scaffolding being dismantled, dust settling, café atmosphere becoming calm.

**Panel 5 (0:08-0:09):** Cinematic hero orbit shot around the perfectly completed gourmet hot dog, tiny workers standing proudly beside their finished masterpiece, warm café lighting creating luxurious reflections on hot dog surface, all construction equipment cleared, premium food photography aesthetic.

**Panel 6 (0:09-0:10):** Ultra-detailed close-up of finished gourmet hot dog with beautiful sauce patterns, perfect assembly, warm golden lighting, shallow depth of field blurring café background, tiny workers visible in background celebrating, final product hero shot, Pixar-level miniature realism.

Style: Photorealistic, cinematic food commercial, Pixar-quality miniature details, macro food photography, warm café lighting, 16:9 format, each panel showing clear construction progression and miniature worker activity with continuous narrative flow.

## Video Prompt

**Reference:** Follow the storyboard exactly. Create one continuous 10-second cinematic shot with seamless transitions. No jump cuts, no teleporting objects, no sudden scene changes. Every construction step must naturally evolve into the next. The miniature workers and construction machinery remain active throughout the entire sequence.

A cozy premium café interior with warm cinematic lighting and shallow depth of field. A giant gourmet hot dog sits centered on a wooden table. Tiny construction workers wearing orange safety vests, miniature excavators, cranes, forklifts, scaffolding and lifting equipment build the hot dog step by step.

00:00-00:01 — Construction Site
The scene begins with an empty construction platform where the hot dog will be built. Miniature workers arrive, unfold blueprints, excavators level the work area, cranes rotate into position, forklifts transport materials. Camera slowly pushes forward.

00:01-00:02 — Bun Installation
A large construction crane carefully lowers the bottom bun into position while workers guide it with ropes. Excavators compact crumbs, forklifts deliver additional ingredients. Camera performs a smooth orbit around the construction site.

00:02-00:03 — Sausage Placement
A crane lifts the grilled sausage and slowly lowers it perfectly into the bun. Workers secure positioning while scaffolding rises around the sausage. Dynamic camera movement with slight speed ramp during the lowering sequence.

00:03-00:04 — Precision Assembly
Tiny crews rapidly install toppings. Workers climb ladders, cranes reposition ingredients, machinery continuously transports supplies. The construction site feels alive with synchronized movement.

00:04-00:05 — Sauce Application
Large ketchup and mustard bottles act like industrial dispensing systems. Perfect zigzag streams are applied while miniature workers coordinate from elevated platforms. Macro close-ups alternate with cinematic crane movements.

00:05-00:06 — Final Finishing
Workers inspect every section. Tiny brushes clean crumbs. Cranes remove temporary supports. Forklifts clear equipment while finishing touches are completed. Smooth speed ramp emphasizes precision.

00:06-00:07 — Quality Inspection
Miniature engineers examine the completed hot dog. Inspection lights sweep across the surface. Workers celebrate while machinery slowly powers down. Camera circles around the hot dog with elegant motion.

00:07-00:08 — Cleanup
Construction equipment leaves the site in an organized convoy. Workers dismantle scaffolding and remove barriers. Dust settles naturally. The café atmosphere becomes calm.

00:08-00:10 — Hero Reveal
The camera performs a beautiful cinematic hero orbit around the perfectly completed gourmet hot dog. Tiny workers stand proudly beside their finished masterpiece while warm café lighting creates luxurious reflections. End on an ultra-detailed close-up of the finished hot dog with subtle depth of field.
