Transformation Logic (Critical Instruction) Every time the user selects a healthy product in the interface, the woman's appearance MUST immediately and visibly change. This visual transformation is the primary focus of the video. The transformation must happen smoothly over 0.5-1 second using an ultra-realistic morph animation with no cuts, no flashes, and no sudden jumps. Each selected product produces a new visible stage of transformation while preserving the woman's identity. ### First Selection (00:02-00:04) The first healthy product is selected. A very thin elegant luminous connection line smoothly grows from the selected product to the woman's abdomen and digestive area. Immediately after the selection, the woman's appearance visibly changes: _ waist becomes noticeably slimmer _ stomach becomes flatter _ body volume visibly decreases _ face becomes slightly slimmer _ cheeks become less puffy _ posture becomes straighter _ shoulders relax _ skin tone becomes healthier The audience must clearly notice that the woman has already become healthier after the first product. --- ### Second Selection (00:05-00:07) The second product is selected. A new elegant connection line links the product with the woman's face and skin. Immediately after the click, her appearance changes again. Compared to the previous stage: _ face becomes significantly slimmer _ jawline becomes more defined _ skin becomes clearer and smoother _ eyes become brighter _ lips gain healthier color _ hair becomes shinier _ neck becomes slimmer _ overall body loses additional weight The transformation should be clearly stronger than the previous one. --- ### Third Selection (00:07-00:09) The third healthy product is selected. A connection line links to the metabolism and entire body. Immediately after the selection, the final transformation occurs. Compared to the previous stage: _ body becomes visibly slim and athletic _ waist becomes well defined _ arms become slimmer _ shoulders become more elegant _ neck becomes longer and slimmer _ face becomes attractive while preserving the original identity _ skin glows with a healthy complexion _ posture becomes confident _ she smiles naturally _ she now looks energetic, healthy and fit The final woman must clearly be the same person, but transformed into the healthiest and most attractive version of herself. The transition between all three stages must be obvious enough that the viewer instantly understands: Product Selected → Immediate Visible Change in Appearance. The transformation is progressive: Stage 1 → noticeably healthier Stage 2 → visibly slimmer and more attractive Stage 3 → fully transformed into a slim, healthy, smiling version of the same woman. This progressive visual transformation is the main storytelling element of the entire video and should receive the highest animation priority.
