### Lighting Specifications

**Key Light: Race Monitors (Warm Red/Orange)**

- **Source**: Glowing race monitors (practical)
- **Color**: Warm red to orange (2200-2800K)
- **Quality**: Soft diffuse glow
- **Position**: Front (from monitor direction)
- **Intensity**: Medium-high (dominant light source)
- **Effect**: Warm atmospheric pit wall look

**Fill Light: Ambient Pit Wall**

- **Source**: Pit wall ambient, overhead garage lights
- **Color**: Neutral to slightly warm (3200-4000K)
- **Quality**: Soft diffuse
- **Position**: Above and around
- **Intensity**: Low-medium (lifts shadows)
- **Effect**: Reduces harsh contrast

**Rim/Back Light: Garage Depth**

- **Source**: Background pit wall lights, other monitors
- **Color**: Neutral to cool (4000-5000K)
- **Quality**: Soft
- **Position**: Behind and to side
- **Intensity**: Low (separation)
- **Effect**: Depth, separates from background

**Accent Lights: Monitor Glows**

- **Source**: Multiple monitors, screens, displays
- **Color**: Various (red, orange, white, blue data screens)
- **Quality**: Point sources, bokeh circles
- **Position**: Background
- **Intensity**: Medium (atmospheric)
- **Effect**: Depth, F1 tech aesthetic

### Depth of Field Strategy

**Shallow DoF (f/1.8-f/2.8):**

- Portrait close-ups
- Extreme eye close-up
- Orbit shot
- Creates luxury cinematic aesthetic
- Background monitors become bokeh

**Moderate DoF (f/2.8-f/4.0):**

- Over-shoulder shots
- Reaction shots
- Broadcast handheld
- Monitors readable, subject sharp
- Maintains F1 environment context

**Bokeh Quality:**

- Circular smooth bokeh (premium lens simulation)
- Monitor lights create warm bokeh circles
- Depth and dimension
- Cinematic film aesthetic

### Audio Mixing Guidelines

**Balance:**

- Ambient garage: 30% (foundation)
- Engine sound: 25% (F1 signature)
- Radio/team: 15% (authenticity)
- Music (if used): 20% (emotion)
- Details (clicks, footsteps, alerts): 10% (realism)

**EQ:**

- Engine: Boost highs 8-12kHz (F1 scream), boost lows 80-150Hz (rumble)
- Radio: Boost mids 1-3kHz (radio voice quality), roll off lows and highs
- Ambient: Gentle low-end 60-200Hz (space), minimal highs
