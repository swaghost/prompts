# Superhero House Lifting – Cinematic Real Estate Fantasy Film (15s, 9:16 Vertical)

## Description

A premium cinematic AI video prompt featuring a superhero-style character who flies into a Brazilian neighborhood, lifts an entire full-scale realistic house from its foundation, carries it through the air to a spectacular mountaintop location, and places it overlooking a breathtaking sunset panorama. The 15-second vertical video combines magical realism with photorealistic VFX, maintaining the house at full architectural scale throughout (never miniature or toy-sized) while showing visible superhuman effort during lifting and flight. Opens with dramatic flying arrival, includes Brazilian Portuguese dialogue ("Então você quer uma casa com vista? Deixa comigo."), showcases golden hour cinematography across neighborhood-to-mountain transition, and concludes with emotional interior shot revealing the panoramic sunset view through the window. Shot in premium ARRI Alexa 35 aesthetic with 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural motion blur, warm sunset color grading, realistic dust/debris effects, atmospheric haze, and high-end VFX quality suitable for luxury real estate advertising with superhero narrative twist.

- **Duration**: 15 seconds (divided into 5 distinct shot sequences)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 vertical (portrait orientation for mobile/social media)

## Shot-by-Shot Breakdown (15 Seconds)

### Shot 1: Superhero Arrival (0:00–0:03 | 3 seconds)

**Action:**

- Character flies into frame from above/side, descending elegantly toward the house
- Powerful but controlled arrival, not crash landing
- Character lands or hovers beside the house in the neighborhood
- Looks directly at camera and delivers line: "Então você quer uma casa com vista? Deixa comigo." (Translation: "So you want a house with a view? Leave it to me.")

**Camera:**

- Starts with slightly low-angle tracking shot following character's descent
- Dynamic movement tracking character from sky to ground level
- Reveals neighborhood context (neighboring houses, sidewalks, trees, parked cars)
- Frame composition shows both character and full house in same shot
- Camera settles as character lands/hovers

**Environment:**

- Quiet contemporary Brazilian residential neighborhood
- Modern houses visible in background
- Sidewalks, landscaping, parked cars establish realistic setting
- Golden hour lighting (warm late afternoon sun)
- Long shadows, warm color temperature

**Character Performance:**

- Confident, charismatic demeanor
- Direct eye contact with camera (breaking fourth wall)
- Natural Brazilian Portuguese delivery (authentic accent)
- Slight smile, reassuring expression
- Body language: controlled power, elegance

**VFX Requirements:**

- Realistic flight physics (not cartoon hover)
- Wind effects on clothing and nearby foliage during descent
- Subtle dust/debris as character lands
- Atmospheric haze from golden hour lighting
- Natural motion blur during descent

**Lighting:**

- Golden hour warm tones (orange, amber, golden yellow)
- Directional sunlight from low angle (late afternoon)
- Rim lighting on character from sun
- Soft shadows appropriate to time of day
- Natural lens flare or sun glow

### Shot 2: Positioning and Lift Preparation (0:03–0:06 | 3 seconds)

**Action:**

- Character moves to side/front corner of house foundation
- Positions body beside the house, showing scale comparison (person vs. full-size house)
- Lowers body into lifting stance: bent knees, braced core
- Places both hands under the edge of foundation/base
- Begins initial lift with visible effort

**Camera:**

- Widens slightly to clearly show full house and character together
- Medium-wide shot emphasizing scale (house is MASSIVE compared to person)
- Slight camera rise as character begins lifting
- Stable framing to show physics clearly

**House Scale Emphasis:**

- House must appear full-size, realistic architectural scale
- Two-story or single-story modern Brazilian home (clearly not miniature)

**VFX Requirements:**

**Sound Design Implications (Visual Cues):**

## Pro Tips

### Character Consistency Across 15 Seconds

- **Reference Image Critical**: Upload high-quality, well-lit photo of person. Face must be clearly visible.
- **Multiple Segment Challenge**: If generating in segments, use SAME reference image for each generation to maintain consistency.
- **Character Description Reinforcement**: In each segment prompt, describe character explicitly (e.g., "Brazilian man with short dark hair, athletic build, wearing casual blue shirt").
- **Face Lock Features**: Platforms like Seedance, Kling AI, or Runway allow face/character reference uploads - use this feature for every segment.

### Maintaining House Scale Realism

- **Explicitly State "Full-Size"**: Use phrases like "full-scale realistic house," "architectural scale," "two-story modern home" to prevent AI making it miniature.
- **Scale References**: Mention neighboring houses, parked cars, trees for size comparison context.
- **Emphasize Weight**: Describe house as "heavy," "massive," showing "visible effort" to lift. This prevents weightless cartoon physics.
- **Negative Prompt Critical**: Include "miniature house, toy house, dollhouse scale, lightweight" in negative prompt for every segment.

### Cinematic Quality Execution

- **ARRI Alexa Look**: Request "ARRI Alexa 35" or "ARRI Alexa LF" aesthetic for premium cinematic rendering.
- **Lens Specification**: "35mm lens" creates natural perspective. Can also try "50mm" for more compression or "24mm" for wider shots.
- **Shallow DOF**: Request f/2.8 or f/4 equivalent depth of field for subject separation and cinematic bokeh.
- **Color Grading Keywords**: "Warm golden hour grading," "sunset color palette," "orange and amber tones" guide AI toward desired look.

### Golden Hour Lighting Mastery

- **Time Specification**: "Late afternoon," "golden hour," "one hour before sunset" sets specific lighting quality.
- **Sun Position**: Describe "low sun angle," "directional warm light," "long shadows" for authentic golden hour characteristics.
- **Color Temperature**: Mention "warm color temperature," "orange sunlight," "amber glow" for golden hour warmth.
- **Atmospheric Effects**: "Lens flare," "atmospheric haze," "volumetric lighting" enhance golden hour atmosphere.

### VFX Realism Best Practices

- **Ground Interaction**: Describe realistic ground cracking, concrete breaking, dust rising during lift for believable physics.
- **Flight Physics**: Request "realistic flight physics," "forward momentum," "natural wind effects" to avoid hovering cartoon style.
- **Landing Impact**: Specify "heavy landing," "ground impact," "settling," "contact shadows" for realistic house placement.
- **Debris and Dust**: "Subtle dust," "natural debris," "atmospheric particles" add realism without overdoing effects.

### Brazilian Setting Authenticity

- **Architecture Style**: "Contemporary Brazilian residential architecture," "modern Brazilian home design" for authentic look.
- **Neighborhood Details**: Mention "Brazilian neighborhood," "tropical trees," "local vegetation" for location authenticity.
- **Language Accuracy**: Ensure Brazilian Portuguese dialogue, not European Portuguese. "Então você quer uma casa com vista? Deixa comigo" is natural Brazilian phrasing.

### Multi-Segment Production Workflow

1. **Pre-Production**: Storyboard 5 segments, plan exact transitions, select character reference image
2. **Generate Segments**: Create each 3-second segment separately for better quality control

4. **Editing Assembly**: Import all segments into editing software (Premiere, DaVinci, Final Cut)
5. **Transition Smoothing**: Add subtle dissolves or cuts at natural action points between segments
6. **Color Grading**: Apply unified golden hour color grade across all segments for consistency
7. **Sound Design**: Add dialogue, wind sounds, impact effects, atmospheric ambient audio
8. **Music**: Consider cinematic score building to emotional peak at final window view
9. **Final Polish**: Add motion blur, lens flare, atmospheric effects as needed in post

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Problem: Character Face Changes Between Segments**

- **Cause**: Each segment generation creates new character interpretation
- **Solution**: Use same reference image for ALL segments. Describe character explicitly in each prompt. Consider using platform's character lock feature.

**Problem: House Appears Miniature or Toy-Like**

- **Cause**: AI defaults to easier small-scale physics
- **Solution**: Repeatedly emphasize "full-size," "realistic architectural scale," "two-story house." Add "miniature, toy, dollhouse" to negative prompt.

**Problem: Cartoon Physics (Weightless House)**

- **Cause**: AI creating fantastical unrealistic motion
- **Solution**: Emphasize "realistic physics," "visible effort," "heavy weight," "cinematic VFX quality." Reference Marvel/DC superhero VFX style.

**Problem: Wrong Time of Day or Lighting**

- **Cause**: AI not maintaining golden hour consistently
- **Solution**: Specify "golden hour" in EVERY segment prompt. Describe sun position, warm color temperature, long shadows.

**Problem: Jerky Transitions Between Segments**

- **Cause**: Separately generated segments don't flow smoothly
- **Solution**: In editing, use motion blur, subtle cross-dissolves (0.5-1 second), or match-cut at action peaks. Color grade uniformly.

**Problem: Dialogue Not Matching Lip Movement**

- **Cause**: AI lip sync still imperfect
- **Solution**: Generate without expecting perfect sync, add dialogue audio in post-production. Use visual cues (looking at camera) to show speaking.

**Problem: Location Doesn't Look Brazilian**

- **Cause**: Generic neighborhood or mountain rendering
- **Solution**: Specify "Brazilian contemporary residential," mention tropical vegetation, warm climate architecture cues.

### Advanced Techniques

**Hybrid VFX Workflow:**

1. Generate base footage with AI
2. Export to After Effects or Nuke
3. Enhance VFX: add more realistic dust, ground cracking, flight trail
4. Composite AI-generated character onto higher-quality background plates
5. Result: Premium VFX quality exceeding AI alone

**Real Location Plates:**

1. Film real Brazilian neighborhood during golden hour (background plate)
2. Film real mountaintop location with sunset view
3. Generate character and house-lifting action with AI
4. Composite AI elements onto real locations for ultimate realism

**3D House Integration:**

1. Model house in Blender/Cinema 4D
2. Render house separately with camera matching AI-generated character motion
3. Composite 3D house with AI character for perfect scale control
4. Ensures house never appears miniature or unrealistic

**Motion Capture Reference:**

- Film actor performing lifting/flying motions against green screen
- Use as reference for AI generation or motion guide
- Results in more realistic human body mechanics during superhuman action
