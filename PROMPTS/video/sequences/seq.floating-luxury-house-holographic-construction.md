# Floating Luxury House Holographic Construction

## Description

A 10-second ultra-realistic architectural CGI commercial showing a floating luxury house on a rock transforming through holographic construction phases from concept to completed building with premium Apple-inspired UI.

## Prerequisites

- Uploaded reference image (floating luxury house on floating rock)

## Storyboard Prompt

Create a 6-panel storyboard showing the holographic construction transformation of the floating luxury house:

**Panel 1 (0:00-1:00 - Concept):** Wide shot of completed luxury house floating on rock against pure black background, elegant golden holographic particles beginning to appear around the building, thin blueprint lines and architectural sketches materializing, house starting to become semi-transparent, camera slow push beginning, centered composition.

**Panel 2 (1:00-2:00 - Planning):** The house transformed into complete architectural blueprint hologram, floating floor plans and elevation drawings surrounding the rock, thin animated construction lines connecting projections, clean minimal CAD-style visualization, camera continuing slow push and beginning subtle orbit.

**Panel 3 (2:00-4:00 - Modeling & Structure):** Glowing golden wireframe model of the house with structural framework visible, steel columns and concrete beams in holographic form, elegant construction projections with small light pulses, transparent UI panels showing progress, camera orbiting approximately 15 degrees.

**Panel 4 (5:00-7:00 - Construction):** House rapidly assembling from holographic guides into realistic architecture, floors, walls, windows, and facade panels building with construction light particles, mid-construction state showing both complete and assembling sections, dynamic construction visualization, camera continuing orbital push-in.

**Panel 5 (7:00-8:50 - Finishing):** Nearly completed house with landscape elements, railings, plants appearing through elegant projection effects, interior lights illuminating room by room, final holographic indicators confirming completion, minimal UI elements still present, camera performing smooth cinematic orbit.

**Panel 6 (8:50-10:00 - Final):** Completed luxury house with all holograms dissolved, floating perfectly centered on rock, warm interior lighting glowing through windows, subtle rotation of house, clean black background with soft floating particles, hero shot with premium architectural commercial aesthetic, closest camera position.

Style: Ultra-realistic architectural CGI, premium Apple-inspired design, glassmorphism UI, minimalist black background, golden holographic elements, volumetric lighting, cinematic composition, 16:9 format, each panel showing clear transformation stage and camera progression.

## Video Prompt

Ultra-realistic cinematic CGI, premium architectural commercial, 10 seconds, 16:9. Use the uploaded floating luxury house on the floating rock as the locked reference. Keep the camera smooth and cinematic throughout. The house always remains centered, floating on the rock against a completely black minimalist background. No ground, no horizon, no environment, no people. Premium warm interior lighting. Modern luxury visualization with elegant holographic architectural projections, clean minimal UI, thin glowing lines, glassmorphism panels, volumetric light, subtle particles, physically accurate materials, ray-traced reflections.

**0.0-1.0 sec — CONCEPT**
Camera slowly pushes toward the floating house. Elegant golden holographic particles appear around the house. Thin blueprint lines, architectural sketches and glowing concept diagrams materialize around the building with soft fade and scale animation. The finished house begins fading into a transparent holographic concept while remaining perfectly centered.

**1.0-2.0 sec — PLANNING**
The finished building smoothly dissolves into a complete architectural blueprint. Floating floor plans, dimensions, elevation drawings, structural grids and CAD-style projections appear around the rock. Thin animated construction lines connect every projection. Everything remains extremely clean and minimal.

**2.0-3.0 sec — 3D MODELING**
Blueprint transforms into a glowing golden wireframe model. Every floor, wall, balcony and roof volume is visualized with elegant holographic geometry. Transparent UI panels display simplified modeling progress. Camera slowly orbits about 15 degrees.

**3.0-4.0 sec — STRUCTURAL DESIGN**
Structural framework animates into place. Steel columns, concrete beams, slabs and support elements assemble automatically with precise magnetic motion. Elegant construction projections continuously update around the building. Small light pulses travel through structural lines.

**4.0-5.5 sec — MATERIAL SELECTION**
Wireframe gradually receives realistic materials. Glass, black concrete, stone cladding, metal panels, warm wood and lighting appear sequentially. Floating material sample cards emerge beside the house with beautiful liquid glass UI animation. Thin connector lines indicate where each material is applied.

**5.5-7.0 sec — CONSTRUCTION**
Hyper-clean cinematic construction sequence. Floors, walls, windows, balconies, facade panels and roof assemble automatically from holographic guides into fully realistic architecture. Tiny construction light particles follow every assembling component. No workers, No heavy machinery. Pure premium visualization.

**7.0-8.5 sec — FINISHING DETAILS**
Landscape elements, railings, lighting fixtures, plants and facade details appear through elegant projection effects. Interior lights illuminate one room after another. Minimal holographic indicators confirm completion of every stage. Camera performs a slow cinematic orbit while continuing the push-in.

**8.5-10.0 sec — FINAL HOUSE**
All holograms dissolve away. The completed luxury modern house remains floating on the rock in the center of the frame. Warm interior lighting glows through the windows. Camera slowly pushes closer while the house performs an almost imperceptible slow rotation. Final hero shot with premium architectural commercial aesthetics, clean black background, luxurious lighting, subtle floating particles, high-end CGI, ultra photorealistic, elegant, minimal, sophisticated. No text overlays, no watermark, no logo.
