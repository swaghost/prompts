# Flooded Ceiling — Water Burst Hook

**VIRAL HOOK SEQUENCE / SEEDANCE 2.0**

---

## Platform Chain

**Two Video Captures** → **Seedance 2.0** (via Renoise.ai, 1080p, 5 seconds, 9:16)

---

## Concept

Viral social media hook where water violently bursts through ceiling and crashes onto subject. Two-frame technique: start dry, end soaked. Static camera, hyper-realistic water simulation with authentic surprised reaction. Ceiling damage persists throughout, water floods and stays on floor.

**Style:** Hyper-realistic water physics, photorealistic  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)  
**Duration:** 5 seconds

---

## Workflow

**Step 1:** Film two videos

- Video 1: You standing dry (exact position)
- Video 2: You standing wet (same exact position)

**Step 2:** Take a screenshot from each video

- Screenshot 1: Dry frame
- Screenshot 2: Wet frame

**Step 3:** Open Renoise AI

**Step 4:** Select Video → Seedance 2.0

**Step 5:** Upload your screenshots

- First frame: dry screenshot
- Second frame: wet screenshot

**Step 6:** Paste the prompt (see below)

**Step 7:** Use these settings

- ✔️ 9:16
- ✔️ 1080p
- ✔️ 5 seconds
- ✔️ Generate Audio

**Step 8:** Click Generate and wait a couple of minutes

**Step 9:** Download your video and enjoy the result! ✨

## VIDEO PROMPT

Static front-facing shot of a woman. Out of nowhere, an enormous wall of water violently bursts through the ceiling and crashes directly onto her, completely soaking her within a split second. The final frame must inherit the ceiling damage from the first frame. The hole is a permanent part of the environment and must still exist in the end frame. Don't make the ceiling hole disappear. Water keeps pouring down, quickly flooding the floor and staying on the floor. Hyper-realistic water simulation, natural physics, authentic surprised reaction, single continuous shot, no camera movement, no music, photorealistic, 4K.

## Two-Frame Technique

**Frame 1 (Start):**

- Subject dry
- Standing in specific position
- Normal ceiling (or with hole ready to burst)

**Frame 2 (End):**

- Subject completely wet/soaked
- Same exact position
- Ceiling damage visible
- Water on floor
- Water still pouring

**AI Generates:**

- The burst transition
- Water physics
- Ceiling breaking/damage
- Flooding action
- Surprised reaction
- Continuous water pour

## Pro Tips

**Filming:**

- Mark your exact standing position

- Maintain same framing
- Film from tripod (no shake)

**Screenshots:**

- Capture at similar moment in stance
- Ensure position matches exactly
- Good lighting in both
- Clear subject visibility
- 9:16 aspect ratio
