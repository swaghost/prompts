# Battleship Yamato Final Attack Sequence

**Format:** 15 seconds, 1:1 square aspect ratio  
**Type:** WWII naval battle action sequence  
**Subject:** Battleship Yamato under carrier-based aircraft attack  
**Style:** High-quality live-action war movie footage, realistic naval warfare

---

## CONCEPT

15-second cinematic sequence depicting the final moments of battleship Yamato under massive wave-like general attack by US Navy carrier-based aircraft. Six precisely timed camera sequences show accumulating damage from bombs, torpedoes, and proximity explosions across the 263-meter super-dreadnought. Sequence builds from wide overhead establishing shots → dive bomb attack → sea-surface torpedo run → sustained general assault → culminates in single fatal massive explosion originating from hull center at 12.2 seconds. Bright, clear war footage with vivid color separation. Sound effects only, no music.
