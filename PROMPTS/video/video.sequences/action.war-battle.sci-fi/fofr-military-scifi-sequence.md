# FOFR - Military Sci-Fi Action Sequence

**Format:** 30-45 seconds, cinematic action/adventure trailer  
**Type:** Military sci-fi mission briefing sequence  
**Character Reference:** Woman in image (soldier/protagonist)  
**Style:** Arrival meets Black Hawk Down, powerful cinematic action

---

## CONCEPT

30-45 second cinematic military sci-fi sequence establishing mission to mysterious phenomenon in jungle. Opens with misty jungle flyover and soldier protagonist in chopper discussing "foh-fur country" and "latent space." Rapid cuts through: helicopter landing with team deployment → urgent mission briefing → convoy racing through rugged jungle → arrival at towering obsidian monolith reaching into sky → title reveal "FOFR". Action/adventure sci-fi soundtrack with deep reverberating bass throughout. Powerful, mysterious, urgent tone combining military realism with otherworldly sci-fi elements.

## SIX-SCENE SEQUENCE

### SCENE 1: Establishing Flyover + Chopper Interior

**Duration:** ~6-8 seconds

**Visual:**

**Establishing Shot (0-3s):**

- **Flyover shot of misty jungle**
- **Early morning** (dawn light breaking through mist)
- Dense tropical jungle canopy
- Fog/mist rolling through valleys
- Aerial perspective (drone or helicopter POV)
- Mysterious, primordial atmosphere
- Mountains or hills visible through mist
- Vast, untouched wilderness

**Camera:**

- Smooth forward-moving aerial shot
- Gliding over jungle canopy
- Cinematic crane/drone movement
- Establishes scale and remoteness

**Chopper Interior (3-8s):**

- Cut or transition to **interior of military helicopter**
- **Soldier (woman from image) visible in army uniform**
- Inside helicopter cabin
- Other soldiers visible (team members)
- Vibration from rotors (camera slight shake)
- Window shows jungle passing outside
- Military equipment visible

**Lighting:**

- Early morning light (soft, diffused)
- Interior helicopter lighting (practical)
- Misty atmospheric haze outside

**Dialogue:**

- **"We're entering foh-fur country now. This is latent space, anything could happen."**

**Audio:**

- Helicopter rotors (loud, rhythmic thump)

- Wind
- Radio chatter (subtle background)
- Action/adventure score begins (building)
- **Deep reverberating bass** starts
