A 15-second, 9-shot cinematic product commercial showing a deep crimson red snapback cap receiving a white lion crown embroidery stitch by stitch in an industrial workshop, then transitioning into a dark studio floating hero reveal.

## Video Prompt

Create a 15-second cinematic embroidery process video: red structured snapback cap with white lion crown embroidery built stitch by stitch from bare fabric to complete design, ending with floating cap hero reveal.

**Duration:** 15 seconds. 9 shots.

**Style:** Shot on RED Komodo 6K cinema camera, 100mm macro lens, f/2.8 aperture, shallow depth of field throughout. Warm fluorescent industrial workshop lighting for first 10 seconds. Dark dramatic studio lighting for final 5 seconds. Color palette: deep crimson red and pure white only throughout entire video. Zero other colors anywhere. Matte red cotton fabric with natural weave grain texture. Natural film grain. Zero plastic sheen. Zero synthetic shine. Zero CGI feel. Photorealistic commercial documentary grade.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Critical: At 0 seconds the cap fabric must be completely bare red with zero embroidery. The white lion builds gradually from 0% to 100% only. Never show the complete lion before the final stitching shots. Deep crimson red cap color and pure white embroidery are locked from first frame to last.
