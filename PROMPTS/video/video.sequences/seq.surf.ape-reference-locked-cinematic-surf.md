## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 15-second, 9:16 photorealistic cinematic surf video of a reference-locked ape riding a powerful ocean wave. The single handheld shot progresses from a drop-in through a bottom turn, cutback, tube section, and aerial finish with realistic surf physics.

## Usage

Perfect for character-led action-sports reels, AI video demonstrations, and cinematic social content that needs a specific subject and surfboard preserved from supplied references. Use when Image 1 defines the surfer and Image 2 defines the board, then generate the sequence as one continuous ride.

## Video Prompt

**Duration:** 15 seconds. 9:16 vertical, one continuous shot.

**Style:** Photoreal cinematic surf action. Golden hour with warm low side-light on the wave face, deep amber and cyan palette, rim light on fur and spray, rich contrast, rolled-off highlights, and fine film grain. ARRI Alexa Mini LF with vintage anamorphic lens character and a 2.39:1 letterbox feel within 9:16. No HDR glow and no oversaturation.

**Scene Setup/Context:**

The surfer is the APE from Image 1. Face, fur, anatomy, and proportions must match Image 1 exactly throughout; identity never drifts. He surfs bare-chested in boardshorts only, with no accessories. The surfboard is from Image 2. Its shape, color, graphics, and fin setup must match Image 2 exactly and remain consistent in every frame.

A large, powerful, perfectly shaped real ocean wave provides a clean open face, defined curl, and enough wall for the complete maneuver sequence. Water is translucent blue-green with white foam, fine spray, and active surface texture. Never show artificial, pool-like, or CGI-clean water.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:04 - DROP-IN
The ape paddles hard into the wave, pops up smoothly, and drops down the steep face. The board rail bites into the water as he sets his line at the bottom, crouching low with clear weight and momentum.

0:04-0:08 - BOTTOM TURN + CUTBACK
He drives a powerful bottom turn up the face, then executes a sharp cutback. He leans hard into the heel edge; the fins release briefly and spray fans wide before he reconnects with the curl and redirects down the line.

0:08-0:12 - TUBE SECTION
The wave throws over ahead of him. He crouches deeper and pulls into a short barrel, visibly framed inside the curl by the pitching lip, then exits cleanly as the section rolls through.

0:12-0:15 - AERIAL FINISH
From the final closeout, he launches an aerial with the board fully clearing the lip. He rotates slightly, lands cleanly back on the wave face with a spray of white water, and rides out of frame.

**Camera:**

One continuous fluid handheld shot, alive rather than stabilized. Begin wide enough to capture the full wave and drop-in, then tighten dynamically through the maneuvers, ending on a tight tracking shot for the aerial. Let the camera breathe naturally, with occasional saltwater spray on the lens and slight motion blur during the fastest moments. Never use a locked-off camera.

**Lighting:**

Golden-hour low sun side-lights the wave. Warm amber highlights meet cyan-blue water; fur and spray catch a natural rim light.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**

Real surf mechanics throughout: accurate weight transfer, rail-to-rail transitions, believable spray, fin release and re-engagement, and a surfboard that behaves like a real board on a real wave. No floating motion or impossible rotations.

**Audio:**

Natural ocean sound only: deep wave roar, rushing white water, fin hum, board slap on water, wind, and spray. No music and no dialogue.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**

Photorealistic real ocean water, stable ape identity, stable board identity, 24 fps cinematic motion, and natural film grain. No human face or body; no wrong board; no calm water, pool, or artificial wave; no perfect CGI water; no extra characters; no text, logos, or watermarks beyond the graphics already present on Image 2's board.
