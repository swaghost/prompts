# Hyper-Construction FPV - Modern Building Assembly

## Description

A 10-second ultra-dynamic continuous FPV construction sequence in which one identical modern building assembles around an aggressive moving camera. Blue glass panels, white facade modules, steel beams, and concrete slabs fly into their future positions with realistic weight and precise architectural alignment, ending on a completed tower reveal. The sequence is photorealistic, 14mm FPV, vertically framed, and contains no cuts.

## Usage

Ideal for architectural visualization, real-estate development marketing, construction technology presentations, architecture-firm showreels, and premium social-media campaigns. Use it when a finished building must be revealed through a fast, physically grounded construction journey with an immersive first-person camera.

## Engines/Models

Suitable for Seedance, Kling, Runway, Veo, Sora, or comparable AI video generators that support a storyboard image and continuous camera motion. Attach the required storyboard as a visual reference before generating the video.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- **Dependency 1 - Required storyboard reference:** A single 10-panel storyboard showing the same modern building and the camera path from the initial construction frame to the completed exterior reveal.
- **Dependency 2 - Optional architecture reference:** A clear image, render, or model reference of the finished building to lock its facade geometry, glazing, corner radius, proportions, and material palette.
- **Dependency 3 - Optional empty-state reference:** A matching exterior-corner view showing the exposed structural frame before assembly begins.
- All reference images must share the same building design, camera orientation, facade proportions, blue-glass/white-facade palette, and exterior corner location.

## Storyboard Requirement

The storyboard is required for this sequence because the rapid FPV camera path and synchronized multi-directional assembly cannot be reliably inferred from text alone. Generate or attach one storyboard image containing ten numbered panels in chronological order. Each panel must show the same building corner, preserve the architectural geometry already established, and make the camera's position, height, direction, and visible assembly boundary obvious.

### Required Storyboard Description

**Panel 1 (0:00-0:01):** ZIPPER ACTIVATION. Extreme 14mm FPV view racing 20 cm above rough construction ground toward an exposed structural frame. Blue glass panels, white facade modules, steel beams, and concrete slabs hover around the building in their future assembly zones. Camera moves forward, with the unfinished structure ahead and the completed direction indicated behind the camera.

**Panel 2 (0:01-0:02):** VERTICAL ZIPPER. FPV camera reaches the exterior corner and pitches sharply upward. Glass panel flies horizontally from the left, white facade module flies from the right, concrete slab rises from below, and steel beam rotates down from above. All four components converge exactly at camera height and lock into place.

**Panel 3 (0:02-0:03):** FOUR-WAY ATTACK. Camera continues its vertical ascent through four converging streams. Massive blue glass panels attack from the left, curved white facade sections arrive from the right, floor slabs rise from below, and beams descend from above. Glass misses the lens by centimetres while the assembly boundary remains visible.

**Panel 4 (0:03-0:04):** ZIPPER WAVE. Camera banks slightly outward while climbing, revealing the moving boundary between unfinished structure below and finished facade above. Multiple panels, beams, and slabs converge in synchronized waves, locking mechanically into place immediately behind the camera.

**Panel 5 (0:04-0:05):** CLOSING CORRIDOR. FPV camera dives through an unfinished floor and races horizontally through an open structural corridor. Glass panels slide into left openings, white facade modules close right openings, and concrete slabs rise from below while beams descend from above. Each gap closes milliseconds after the camera passes.

**Panel 6 (0:05-0:06):** 90-DEGREE PIVOT. Camera exits at the opposite corner and performs a violent 90-degree bank back upward. Construction waves follow the camera. A huge concrete slab rotates beside the lens, blue glass overtakes one side, and white facade modules snap into position as the camera rolls through the remaining gap.

**Panel 7 (0:06-0:07):** DOUBLE ZIPPER. Camera rockets vertically between two visible exterior edges. Synchronized left and right facade assembly waves climb beside it; panels close toward the centre while slabs and beams lock between the streams. A rapid SNAP-SNAP-SNAP rhythm shows each completed connection.

**Panel 8 (0:07-0:08.5):** FACADE STORM. Camera accelerates into a tight upward corkscrew around the curved corner. Hundreds of blue glass panels and white facade modules converge at camera height from both sides. Brackets and beams align between them, and each floor becomes finished immediately after the camera passes.

**Panel 9 (0:08.5-0:09.5):** ROOF ESCAPE. Camera enters the final opening beneath the roof and races through the last unfinished gap. Two facade panels close from left and right, a beam rotates overhead, and massive black rooftop structures descend into position from above. The opening contracts around the lens.

**Panel 10 (0:09.5-0:10):** SLAM AND HERO REVEAL. Camera bursts into open blue sky as the final black rooftop component locks into position and the last facade sections snap shut. FPV performs an extreme speed-ramp backward and upward, revealing the completed modern tower in full against the blue sky.

**Storyboard Style:** Photorealistic architectural visualization, 10 clearly numbered panels, consistent modern tower geometry, curved corner, blue glass, white facade modules, exposed concrete and steel, realistic heavy construction physics, 14mm FPV perspective, sharp daylight, vertical 9:16 composition. The panels are a camera-and-assembly map, not separate shots to be edited together.

## Camera

- Continuous first-person FPV shot with no cuts, transitions, or hidden edits.
- Extreme 14mm wide-angle perspective with aggressive acceleration, vertical ascent, bank, roll, corkscrew, and final backward-upward reveal.
- The camera remains the assembly reference point; construction must happen immediately around and behind it.
- Maintain coherent spatial continuity and the same building corner, lens character, and vertical framing throughout.

## Lighting and Materials

- Bright natural daylight with a clear blue sky and consistent exposure.
- Realistic blue glass reflections, white facade finish, exposed concrete, structural steel, brackets, seams, and contact shadows.
- Construction components have visible mass, inertia, impact, friction, and mechanically believable connections.
