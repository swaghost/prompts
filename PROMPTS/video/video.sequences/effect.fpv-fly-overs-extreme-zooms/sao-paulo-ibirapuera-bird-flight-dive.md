# São Paulo — Ibirapuera Bird-Flight Dive

## Description

A 20-second cinematic first-person flight through Parque do Ibirapuera in São Paulo. The camera behaves like a bird in flight: it dives from high above the park, runs between tree trunks, skims the lake, passes through a precisely two-second frozen underwater moment, then climbs and banks around a stone obelisk while the city skyline sweeps behind it.

This is one continuous take with constant forward motion from the first frame to the last. The only frozen subject is one carp during the underwater section.

## Technical Specifications

- **Duration:** 20 seconds
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Shot:** One continuous take, no cuts or shot changes
- **POV:** First-person bird in flight
- **Camera:** Organic flight motion with subtle rhythmic bob, never gimbal-smooth
- **Underwater moment:** Exactly 2 seconds, from 10s to 12s
- **Ending:** Still moving on a wide banking curve around the obelisk
- **Audio:** Diegetic sound only, no soundtrack music

## Environment

Parque do Ibirapuera, São Paulo, in low late-afternoon sun: a wide green park ringed by grey city towers, dense flat-crowned trees, open lawns, a large lake with tall fountain jets, green underwater light, and a tall pale stone obelisk on a circular plaza.

## Master Video Prompt

```text
Create a 20-second cinematic 16:9 first-person bird-flight sequence through Parque do Ibirapuera in São Paulo. One continuous unbroken take, no cuts, no shot changes, no transitions, no fades, and no camera resets. The camera has constant forward momentum from the first frame to the last.

The entire sequence is a first-person view of a bird in flight. Use a subtle rhythmic bob synchronized to wing beats, alive and organic, never gimbal-smooth. The bird itself must never be visible in frame. The bob dissolves into a weightless glide only during the exactly two-second underwater moment. The camera never stops, hovers, pauses, or floats still.

ENVIRONMENT
Parque do Ibirapuera, São Paulo, in low late-afternoon sun: a wide green park ringed by grey city towers, dense flat-crowned trees, open lawns, a large lake with a row of tall fountain jets, green underwater light, and a tall pale stone obelisk with a circular plaza at its base.

TIMELINE AND CAMERA PATH

PHASE 1 — 0–3 SECONDS: HIGH PARK DIVE
Open high above the large urban park. Grey city towers ring the green park on every side. The camera pitches forward and dives toward the treetops, accelerating continuously all the way down.

PHASE 2 — 3–4 SECONDS: CANOPY PUNCH-THROUGH
The camera punches through the canopy. Dense green leaves fill the entire frame for a moment. Preserve high-speed forward movement and natural impact with the foliage.

PHASE 3 — 4–7.5 SECONDS: GROUND RUN
The camera levels out just above the ground and runs fast between tree trunks. Roots and grass rush beneath the lens. Shafts of low sun strobe naturally through the trunks. Distant park visitors and joggers appear only as blurred figures; faces are never sharp.

PHASE 4 — 7.5–9.5 SECONDS: LAKE SKIM
The camera bursts from the treeline onto the lake shore and skims the water surface straight toward a row of tall fountain jets. Spray flicks across the lens. Keep the forward trajectory direct and fast.

PHASE 5 — 9.5–10 SECONDS: WATER ENTRY
The camera pierces the water surface. Foam and bubbles fill the frame as the viewpoint passes below the lake.

PHASE 6 — 10–12 SECONDS: EXACTLY TWO SECONDS UNDERWATER
For exactly two seconds, show one large carp suspended completely motionless mid-swim. Food pellets and bubbles are frozen in the green water column, and shafts of light hang still. The carp is razor-sharp and completely motionless. Nothing else is slowed or frozen. The camera continues moving forward past the fish in a shallow arc and never stops.

PHASE 7 — 12–13 SECONDS: SURFACE BURST
The camera bursts back out through the surface in an explosion of water and climbs hard. Motion immediately returns to normal. Restore the sound of the world with the burst through the surface.

PHASE 8 — 13–20 SECONDS: OBELISK BANKING REVEAL
The camera climbs toward a tall pale stone obelisk and curves around it on one wide continuous banking arc. Keep the obelisk on the LEFT side of frame while the city skyline sweeps behind it. Leave the curve on its tangent and remain in motion as the take ends. Do not end on a static frame.

POV AND MOTION
The flight must feel organic and physically bird-like: subtle rhythmic bob on the dive and ground run, natural forward momentum, realistic acceleration, organic banking, and no gimbal stabilization. Heavy motion blur appears on the dive and ground run, then vanishes completely during the two underwater seconds. The camera never rotates in place, spins on its own axis, makes a 180-degree or 360-degree turn, or orbits without forward travel.

LIGHTING AND STYLE
Use low warm late-afternoon sun, long shadows raking across the grass, sunlight strobing between trunks, silver glare on the lake, deep green filtered light underwater, and warm light on the obelisk with the city in haze behind it. Use cinematic natural physics, crisp detail on the frozen carp and obelisk stone, natural colour, subtle film grain, and vivid greens against a grey-hazed city.

AUDIO
Use only diegetic sound. Wind and altitude during the dive; birds and rustling leaves as the camera enters the trees; grass and nearby footsteps during the ground run; the lake opening wide with the hiss of the fountain jets; sound compressing into a deep muffled underwater hush for exactly two seconds; sound snapping back with the burst through the surface; wind rising during the climb; and the distant hum of the city taking over around the obelisk. No soundtrack music.
```

## Subject and Continuity Locks

- Exactly one large carp is frozen underwater.
- The carp remains completely motionless and razor-sharp.
- Pellets and bubbles remain frozen only during the two underwater seconds.
- Distant visitors and joggers remain blurred with no sharp faces.
- No part of the bird is visible at any point.
- Forward motion continues through every phase, including underwater.
- The underwater section lasts exactly two seconds, from 10s to 12s.
- The obelisk remains on the left during the final banking curve.
- The final frame remains in motion on the curve's tangent.
