# Ultra Realistic Miniature Mumbai Local Train Build Sequence

## Concept Title

**ASMR Miniature Mumbai Local Train Build (15-Second Edition)**

## Core Brief

Create a 15-second ultra-realistic cinematic macro video in vertical 9:16 format.

## Concept

A skilled miniature artist builds a highly detailed Mumbai local train model and railway track by hand. Everything must look physically handcrafted, realistic and satisfying—not CGI.

## 15-Second Video — Six Shot Segments

**SHOT 1 — 0:00–0:03 — Railway Track Foundation**  
Extreme macro close-up of human hands carefully placing shiny steel railway tracks onto small grey concrete sleepers using precision tweezers. Warm workshop lighting, wooden workbench, tiny dust particles, very shallow depth of field.

**SHOT 2 — 0:03–0:05 — Ballast Application**  
Quick seamless close-up: a miniature metal scoop pours realistic grey ballast stones between the sleepers. A soft brush spreads the gravel neatly around the rails.

**SHOT 3 — 0:05–0:07 — Soldering & Infrastructure**  
Macro soldering shot: a tiny soldering iron touches the rail joint, producing one brief orange spark and a delicate curl of smoke. Immediately cut to hands installing a miniature signal pole and overhead electric wires.

**SHOT 4 — 0:07–0:10 — Train Assembly**  
Hands assemble the same silver-and-maroon Mumbai local train: place the body onto its motorized wheel chassis, connect the tiny copper motor, then carefully install the rooftop pantograph. Maintain the exact same train design throughout.

**SHOT 5 — 0:10–0:12 — Train Placement & Activation**  
The completed miniature Mumbai local is gently placed on the finished track. The pantograph makes contact with the overhead wire. Destination display reads "CHURCHGATE." Its warm-white headlights switch on dramatically.

**SHOT 6 — 0:12–0:15 — Station Environment & Movement**  
Seamless transformation into a complete miniature Mumbai railway station environment. The train moves smoothly toward and past the camera, realistic wheel rotation and slight suspension vibration. Tiny passengers wait on the platform, signals change from red to green, station structures and footbridge visible in the background. Finish with the train travelling forward along the track.

## Camera & Look

Fast premium macro editing, precise match cuts, 85mm macro lens, shallow depth of field, realistic miniature scale, warm cinematic workshop lighting transitioning into natural morning station light, accurate metal reflections, realistic hands and tools, crisp 4K detail.

## Audio Profile

No dialogue, no voice-over, no music. Only satisfying ASMR sounds—tweezers clicking, gravel pouring, brush strokes, soft solder spark, metal assembly clicks, electric activation hum, train motor, wheel-on-track rhythm and a subtle Mumbai local horn at the final reveal.

## Strict Continuity Requirements

⛔ **CRITICAL:** The same track, same hands, same silver-and-maroon train and same proportions in every shot. No object morphing, no extra fingers, no floating tools, no changing train design, no incorrect wheels, no text distortion, no sudden scale changes, no flicker, no jump cuts in actions, no CGI or plastic appearance.

## Key Elements

- **Train Design:** Silver-and-maroon Mumbai local (consistent throughout)
- **Destination Display:** "CHURCHGATE"
- **Location:** Wooden workshop transitioning to miniature Mumbai station
- **Scale:** Realistic miniature proportions maintained
- **Lighting:** Warm workshop → natural morning station light
- **Movement:** Realistic wheel rotation, suspension vibration

## Format

**9:16 vertical video** for YouTube Shorts/Reels/TikTok.
