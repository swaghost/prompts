# Ultra Realistic Miniature World Construction Sequence

## Topic

[WRITE CAR NAME / FUEL STATION NAME HERE]

## Style

Ultra realistic miniature world, tiny professional workers, same location and same object continuity in every scene. Every next scene must start exactly from the previous scene's final frame. Macro lens, cinematic camera movement, realistic textures, 4K ultra HD, photorealistic quality.

## Scene 1 (0-10 seconds) – Base Construction

Show the complete starting process from zero level. Tiny workers build the foundation and main structure using realistic tools and machines. Show raw materials, metal parts, frame construction, welding, assembling, and detailed close-up shots. The object should slowly take shape. End with a clear unfinished but recognizable structure.

## Scene 2 (10-20 seconds) – Detailed Finishing

Continue exactly from the last frame of Scene 1. Show workers adding all important details, mechanical parts, design elements, lights, paint, decorations, and premium finishing. Show realistic craftsmanship, polishing, installation, and final preparation. End with a nearly completed masterpiece.

## Scene 3 (20-30 seconds) – Final Luxury Reveal

Continue exactly from the previous frame. Show the fully completed final product in a cinematic showcase. Slow camera slide and 360-degree rotation showing every detail. Add realistic lighting, reflections, shadows, premium presentation, and a satisfying final reveal like a luxury advertisement.

## Camera

Macro close-ups, slow motion, smooth tracking shots, cinematic angles, shallow depth of field.

## Negative Prompt

cartoon, low quality, blurry, fake plastic look, unrealistic proportions, broken parts, missing details, distorted object, bad lighting, text, watermark.

## Aspect Ratio

9:16 vertical video for Shorts/Reels.

## Usage Notes

- Ensure continuity between scenes - each scene must start from the exact final frame of the previous scene
- Focus on realistic miniature construction details and tiny worker activity
- Maintain photorealistic quality throughout all three scenes
- Ideal for automotive brands, fuel stations, product reveals, or luxury item showcases
- 30-second total duration optimized for social media short-form video
