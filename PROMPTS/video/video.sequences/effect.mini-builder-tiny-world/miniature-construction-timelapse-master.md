# Miniature Construction Timelapse - Master Prompt

## Variable

Replace `[CONSTRUCTION NAME]` with the exact building, place, structure, or object to construct.

## Video Prompt

```text
Create an ultra-realistic cinematic miniature construction timelapse of [CONSTRUCTION NAME], showing the entire structure being built from an empty plot of raw ground to its fully completed final form.

FORMAT AND CONTINUITY

Create a 10-15 second vertical 9:16 video. Use one continuous, fast but smooth construction timelapse with no sudden cuts between stages. The miniature scale remains consistent throughout: workers, vehicles, cranes, materials, and [CONSTRUCTION NAME] are physically connected to one detailed miniature world. Construction progression must stay unmistakably clear: empty land -> foundation -> base structure -> multiple levels -> top level -> exterior finishing -> completed masterpiece.

CONSTRUCTION PROGRESSION

1. Start with an empty piece of land at foundation level. Tiny workers arrive, survey, mark out, and prepare the raw ground.
2. Tiny excavators, cranes, dump trucks, and concrete mixers dig and move soil.
3. Workers build the foundation, underground structures, and base columns.
4. Steel reinforcement, concrete footings, and structural frames are installed.
5. Workers build walls, floors, pillars, and the major structural elements.
6. Cranes continuously lift heavy materials to the higher levels.
7. The building or structure grows upward floor by floor from ground level to the top.
8. Workers install the roof, glass, doors, windows, exterior panels, roads, and surrounding infrastructure.
9. Include believable construction lights, machinery movement, dust, concrete pouring, welding flashes, and material transportation.
10. Complete landscaping, roads, lighting, and final detailing.
11. End with a spectacular completed [CONSTRUCTION NAME], beautifully illuminated like a real miniature architectural model.

WORKER ACTIVITY

Keep dozens of tiny, realistically scaled workers visible throughout. They perform distinct, believable actions: carrying materials, operating machinery, welding, installing steel, pouring concrete, guiding crane loads, fitting panels, and working on different floors. Vehicles move along sensible paths, materials have weight, and every construction action follows the evolving state of the build.

CAMERA AND LOOK

Use a cinematic elevated three-quarter perspective, slightly top-down, with framing that remains recognizably consistent throughout the timelapse. Use slow, smooth camera drift with a few brief macro detail passes, then naturally return to the master view. Photorealistic miniature diorama, incredibly detailed tiny humans and machinery, realistic material texture, realistic shadows, cinematic depth of field, natural atmospheric lighting, subtle dust particles, and accurate scale and proportions.

LIGHTING

Create a satisfying day-to-night progression: clear daylight during early construction, warm sunset as the structure reaches its final form, and realistic miniature construction lights at night. Light direction, shadows, weather, and atmosphere change gradually and naturally with the timelapse.

AUDIO AND EXCLUSIONS

No dialogue, narration, subtitles, text overlays, logos, or watermark. Use optional natural miniature construction ambience only: distant machinery, light metal contact, vehicle movement, concrete work, and environmental atmosphere. No unrealistic object transformations, teleporting materials, floating workers, sudden stage jumps, distorted vehicles, or disconnected miniature worlds.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Duration:** 10-15 seconds
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical
- **Camera:** Elevated three-quarter miniature-diorama perspective with restrained smooth motion
- **Structure:** One continuous timelapse progression from empty land to final reveal
- **Style:** Photorealistic miniature construction, natural depth of field, realistic materials and scale
