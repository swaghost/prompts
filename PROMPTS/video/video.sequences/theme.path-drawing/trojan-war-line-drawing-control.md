# Trojan War - Line Drawing Control (Continuous Camera Path)

Transform uploaded images into cinematic AI video using line-drawing path control. The camera follows a drawn path through the scene in one continuous shot.

## Platform

- **AI Video**: deevid.ai/app
- **Model**: Master V4.5 (Seedance 2.5)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9
- **Duration**: 15s

## Instructions

**Step 1:** Download or generate your image of the Trojan War scene.

**Step 2:** Draw the camera path directly on the image:

- Use a bright contrasting color line
- Mark the start point clearly
- Draw the continuous path the camera should follow
- Add direction arrows along the path

**Step 3:** Upload the marked image and use the prompt below.

Rear-follow framing never loses horse; it dominates frame and exceeds buildings. Sunbeams through dust; victory conceals danger.

Hidden Greek soldiers, dusty, sweaty, covered in wood shavings, worn damp armour, tired but steady, grip weapons and control breath. Final push to eyes in darkness with distant celebration.

## Key Technical Requirements

- **Camera Movement**: ONE continuous shot, no cuts
- **Path Following**: Camera strictly follows the drawn line
- **Scale Consistency**: The Trojan Horse must remain 50 metres tall throughout
- **Rear Perspective**: Camera always behind the horse, never front-facing
- **Fog Transition**: Long, gradual fog bank crossing between fleet and shore
- **Single Horse**: Only ONE horse structure in the entire shot

## Tips

1. **Line Color**: Use bright green, cyan, or magenta that contrasts with the image
2. **Path Clarity**: Make the line continuous with no breaks
3. **Start/End Markers**: Clearly mark where the camera begins and ends
4. **Smooth Curves**: Avoid sharp angles; use flowing paths
5. **Atmospheric Elements**: The prompt emphasizes mist, dust, and weather
