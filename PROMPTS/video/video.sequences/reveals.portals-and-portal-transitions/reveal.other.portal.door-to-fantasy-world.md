## Concept

Create viral door portal transition videos where opening a door reveals a completely different world or environment. The technique combines real phone footage of someone walking through a door with AI-generated continuation showing a magical portal to fantasy landscapes, stunning vistas, or surreal environments. The transition appears as one continuous seamless shot.

**Perfect for:** Viral social media content, fantasy storytelling, creative transitions, magical realism, TikTok/Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, imaginative world-building, "follow me" invitation videos

## Seven-Step Workflow

### **Step 1: Record Your Video**

**Setup:**
- Set phone/camera on tripod or completely stable surface
- Frame the shot showing you and the door
- Camera must remain **completely still** (critical for seamless transition)

**Action:**
1. Stand in front of a closed door
2. Walk toward the door naturally
3. Open the door (push or pull)
4. Begin walking through the doorway
5. Continue for 2-3 seconds past the threshold

**Pro Tips:**
- Keep steady walking pace
- Natural door-opening motion (not too fast)
- Camera stability is critical (use tripod, not handheld)
- Good lighting helps AI understand the scene
- Film more than you need (easier to trim later)

---

### **Step 2: Capture the Transition Frame**

**Find the Perfect Frame:**
1. Review your recorded video
2. Pause at the **exact moment the door is fully open**
3. Your body should be partially through the doorway
4. Door should be swung wide (not mid-swing)

**Capture Screenshot:**
- Take a high-quality screenshot of this exact frame
- This becomes the first reference image for AI generation
- Frame should show: open doorway, your position, room interior

**What Makes a Good Transition Frame:**
- Door fully open (stable position)
- You're stepping through or about to step through
- Clear view through the doorway
- Good lighting on both sides of door
- No motion blur

### **Multiple Doorways Sequence**

Create a series of portal transitions through multiple doors:

1. Generate first portal (door 1 → fantasy world A)
2. Capture frame in fantasy world A with another door
3. Generate second portal (door 2 → fantasy world B)
4. Edit all sequences together for multi-portal journey

**Result:** Epic journey through multiple magical worlds

### **Return Portal**

Create a loop by returning to starting room:

1. Generate outbound portal (room → fantasy world)
2. Generate return portal (fantasy world → room) using reverse logic
3. Edit together for complete journey and return

**Result:** "There and back again" narrative structure

### **Different Person Through Portal**

Person entering doesn't match person exiting:

1. Film Person A walking toward and through door

## Pro Tips

### **Recording the Original Footage**
- **Tripod is essential** - handheld footage creates misalignment
- Film in good lighting (helps AI understand scene)
- Walk naturally toward and through door (not too fast)
- Open door smoothly (avoid jerky motion)
- Continue walking 2-3 seconds past threshold
- Film multiple takes for options

### **Choosing the Screenshot Frame**
- Door should be **fully open** (stable position)
- Your body partially through doorway (mid-transition)
- Clear view through the door opening
- No motion blur on this frame
- Good exposure on both sides of door
- This frame is **critical** - take time to find perfect one

### **Face Reference Image**
- Use clear, well-lit photo of face
- Frontal or 3/4 view works best
- Good resolution (not blurry)
- Neutral expression or slight smile
- This ensures face consistency in AI generation

### **Environment Description**
- Be **very specific** about portal environment
- Include: landscape type, structures, sky, lighting, atmosphere
- Add unique details (animals, architecture, vegetation)
- More detail = more specific AI output
- Use descriptive, visual language

### **Camera Movement**
- "Smooth steadicam movement" = professional look
- Shoulder height is natural and immersive
- "Past her shoulder" = reveals environment gradually
- Continuing motion prevents abrupt stop

### **Consistency Instructions**
- Always include face/clothing consistency statements
- "Throughout the whole video" = no morphing
- Reference images maintain identity and outfit
- Critical for seamless real-to-AI transition

### **Editing the Final Video**
- Find exact matching frame in both clips
- No duplicate frames at transition point

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Transition looks jarring or jumpy
- **Solution:** Ensure screenshot frame exactly matches where you cut original footage, verify no duplicate or missing frames at transition point, align clips perfectly on timeline

**Issue:** Face changes or looks different after portal
- **Solution:** Upload clear face reference image as second reference, emphasize "face stays identical to the second reference image" in prompt, use high-quality face photo

**Issue:** Clothing changes during portal transition
- **Solution:** Emphasize "clothing and hairstyle stay identical to the first reference image" in prompt, ensure starting frame screenshot is clear and well-lit

**Issue:** Camera movement looks unnatural in AI portion
- **Solution:** Add "smooth steadicam movement" to prompt, specify exact camera behavior (following behind, shoulder height, etc.), reduce complexity of camera description

**Issue:** Portal environment doesn't match description
- **Solution:** Use more specific, detailed description, reference specific styles or aesthetics, add more visual details about architecture/landscape/atmosphere

**Issue:** Character doesn't make gesture
- **Solution:** Be very explicit about gesture timing and description, place gesture instruction early in sequence, describe exact hand/arm movement

**Issue:** Original footage too shaky for clean transition
- **Solution:** Re-film using tripod or very stable surface, enable phone's video stabilization if available, use video stabilization in editing software as last resort

**Issue:** Lighting doesn't match between real and AI footage
- **Solution:** Film original in similar lighting to portal environment, describe lighting style in prompt ("warm cinematic lighting"), color grade both clips in editing to match
