# Elevator Portal Workflow

**Platform:** Higgsfield.ai (Nano Banana + Seedance 2.0)  
**Duration:** 15 seconds  
**Technique:** Single elevator photo becomes portal to multiple worlds  
**Effect:** Doors open to reveal different worlds each time

---

## Overview

**The Concept:** Take one elevator photo and use one prompt. Every time the doors open, a new world appears.

This workflow uses Nano Banana (or ChatGPT) to create the perfect elevator interior shot with you inside, then Seedance 2.0 to animate the doors opening to reveal different worlds. The person stays consistent throughout while the world beyond the doors changes.

**Platform:** [Higgsfield AI — AI-native creative suite](https://higgsfield.ai)

---

## Complete Workflow

### Step 1: Open Higgsfield.ai

Select **Nano Banana** (or ChatGPT Image).

---

### Step 2: Upload Your Elevator Photo

Upload your original photo that shows you (or your subject).

---

### Step 3: Paste the Image Prompt

**Purpose:** Generate you inside a luxury elevator with perfect framing and composition.

### Complete Image Prompt

```
Use your photo as the subject. Keep your same real face and identity. Create an ultra-realistic full-body photo of you inside a modern luxury elevator, standing on the left side, leaning against the wall with both hands in pockets and one leg crossed over the other, looking toward the side. The elevator should have dark wood panels, brushed metal doors, warm vertical lights, steel handrails, and a marble floor. Professional camera look, cinematic lighting, sharp details, realistic reflections, natural shadows, 16:9 landscape.

Do not change the face. No extra fingers, no distortions, no AI look.
```

## The Video Prompt — Seedance 2.0

**Duration:** 15 seconds continuous shot  
**Camera:** Does not cut on its own

### Complete Video Prompt

```
One continuous 15 second shot, the camera does not cut on its own. A person leans against the side wall of a brushed steel elevator, hands in their pockets, doors closed, exactly as in the start frame. A soft elevator chime. The doors slide open and reveal WORLD ONE, e.g. the pyramids of Giza behind a turquoise ocean at sunset. Light from the world floods the car. The person turns their head and looks out, staying against the wall. The doors slide closed and the car returns to normal. A second chime. The doors slide open again and reveal WORLD TWO, e.g. a green meadow with a giant castle floating in the sky. The person pushes off the wall and walks out through the doorway into the world at a calm walking pace, and the camera follows behind them in one smooth move, keeping their full figure centered. The person, their outfit and their face stay 100% consistent with the start frame. Sound effects only, no music.
```

## Key Success Factors

### 1. Face Consistency

**Critical:**

- Original photo must be clear and well-lit
- Face should be fully visible
- Image prompt must emphasize "Keep your same real face and identity"
- Video prompt must specify "100% consistent with the start frame"

---

### 2. Elevator Composition

**Optimal Setup:**

- Person on left side of frame
- Leaning against wall
- Looking toward side (where doors will open)
- Full body visible
- Room for doors to open on right side

**Why Left Side?**

- Allows doors to open and reveal world on right
- Person can look right toward opening
- Camera can follow as person walks right into world

---

### 3. World Descriptions

**Be Specific:**

- ❌ "a beautiful landscape"
- ✅ "the pyramids of Giza behind a turquoise ocean at sunset"

**Include:**

- Main landmark/feature
- Environment type
- Lighting/time of day
- Atmospheric details
- Scale indicators

---

### 4. Pacing

**World One (5 seconds):**

- Chime → doors open → light floods → person looks → doors close
- Person stays in elevator (just observes)
- Teaser for what's possible

**World Two (8 seconds):**

- Chime → doors open → person walks out → camera follows
- This is the payoff
- Smooth transition from elevator to world

---

## Pro Tips

### Lighting Integration

**World Light Flooding Elevator:**

- Sunset world → warm orange/golden light
- Ocean world → blue/turquoise light
- Night city → neon/colorful light
- Space → cool blue/purple light
- Fire world → intense orange/red light

### Camera Follow Technique

**Smooth Following Shot:**

- Camera locked at start (person waiting)
- Remains locked during first portal (observation)
- Begins smooth follow as person walks out
- Maintains full figure in frame
- Natural walking pace (not rushed)

### World Contrast

**Make Them Different:**

- World 1: Observe from safety (majestic/grand)
- World 2: Worth entering (inviting/magical)
- Consider color contrast between worlds
- Vary time of day (sunset, then midday)
- Different scales (pyramids distant, meadow walkable)

## Troubleshooting

### Face Changes Between Image and Video

**Problem:** Person's face changes in video generation  
**Solution:**

- Emphasize in video prompt: "The person, their outfit and their face stay 100% consistent with the start frame"
- Use higher quality source image
- Ensure face is well-lit and clear in generated image

### Doors Don't Open Properly

**Problem:** Doors blur or don't reveal world clearly  
**Solution:**

- Specify in prompt: "The doors slide open smoothly and fully"
- Add: "The world beyond is clearly visible through the open doorway"
- Describe the light flooding in to emphasize the opening

### Person Doesn't Walk Out

**Problem:** Person stays in elevator for World 2  
**Solution:**

- Be explicit: "The person pushes off the wall and walks out"
- Add direction: "walks forward through the doorway into the world"
- Specify pacing: "at a calm walking pace"

### Camera Doesn't Follow

**Problem:** Camera stays locked when person exits  
**Solution:**

- Specify: "the camera follows behind them in one smooth move"
- Add: "keeping their full figure centered as they walk"
- Clarify: "camera moves with them into the world"
