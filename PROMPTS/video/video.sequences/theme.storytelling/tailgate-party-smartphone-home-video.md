# Tailgate Party Smartphone Home Video (Seedance 2.5)

## Description

Super casual real smartphone home video footage of a lively tailgate party in a stadium parking lot before a football game. Natural mobile phone camera aesthetic with authentic handheld shake, rapidfire montage with constant quick jump cuts every 1-2 seconds like scrolling through phone memories. Features unpolished authentic phone recording of friends grilling, cheering, and hanging out near cars with pure raw home video feel—no cinematic polish or heavy effects. The sequence uses a provided reference photo as the strict visual reference for the main woman character, maintaining her exact appearance with zero deviation while generating a mixed group of friends around her.
