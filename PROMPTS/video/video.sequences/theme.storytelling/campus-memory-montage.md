# Campus Memory Montage – College Friends Smartphone Video

## Description

Authentic smartphone home-video montage of college friends enjoying a casual sunny day on campus. Rapid jump-cut style (1-2 second clips) capturing spontaneous moments: walking together, laughing, taking selfies, studying outdoors, sharing snacks, toasting drinks, and relaxing on the grass. Raw, unpolished, genuine phone footage that feels like scrolling through personal memories from a college day.

## Usage

Create nostalgic, relatable college lifestyle content with authentic smartphone aesthetic. Perfect for social media storytelling, university marketing, friendship montages, or lifestyle vlogs. Uses strict identity lock for main subject (@reference) while maintaining a diverse friend group throughout the 15-second sequence.

## Subject/Actor

### Main Woman (@reference)

- **Identity Lock**: STRICT — @reference is the absolute visual authority
- **Preservation**: Exact face, hairstyle, hair color, skin tone, age, body shape, clothing, overall appearance
- **Consistency**: Zero deviation throughout entire video
- **Never**: Replace, reinterpret, beautify, morph, or alter identity

### Supporting Characters

- Diverse mixed group of college friends (same age range)
- Natural everyday campus outfits
- Each friend visually distinct
- No duplicated faces, bodies, clothing, or accessories
- All friends remain consistent and recognizable throughout

## Environment

### Campus Setting

- **Time**: Bright, sunny college day between classes
- **Locations**: Open grass lawns, tree-lined walkways, campus benches, outdoor study tables
- **Props**: Bicycles, backpacks, notebooks, takeaway drinks, snacks, coffee cups
- **Background**: Modern university buildings
- **Atmosphere**: Active but relaxed — students casually walking, talking, studying, taking photos, eating

## Video Style

### Authentic Smartphone Aesthetic

- **Quality**: Super-casual home-video footage
- **Camera Movement**: Natural mobile phone recording with genuine handheld shake
- **Framing**: Imperfect, spontaneous composition
- **Focus**: Realistic autofocus changes
- **Exposure**: Subtle automatic adjustment
- **Motion**: Normal frame-rate (no slow motion)

### Editing Style

- **Montage Type**: Rapid memory-style with frequent jump cuts
- **Cut Frequency**: Every 1-2 seconds (abrupt transitions)
- **Feeling**: Like casually scrolling through phone memories
- **Aesthetic**: Spontaneous, unplanned, slightly messy, personally recorded by a friend

### What This Is NOT

- ❌ No cinematic polish
- ❌ No professional stabilization
- ❌ No dramatic lighting
- ❌ No commercial composition
- ❌ No beauty filters
- ❌ No stylized color grading

## 15-Second Shot Sequence

### 0-2.5 Seconds — Campus Arrival

**Scene**: Walking across sunny campus lawn with friends

- Shaky handheld rapid cuts of @reference and friends laughing naturally
- Quick close-up of her smiling toward phone camera
- Abrupt flashes: sneakers walking across pavement, backpacks swinging, friends playfully bumping shoulders
- Heading toward class together

### 2.5-5 Seconds — Friends and Selfies

**Scene**: Group selfie and walking

- @reference pushing sunglasses onto top of head while smiling
- Friend raises phone for casual group selfie
- Group squeezes into frame, laughs, makes playful expressions
- Brief loss of framing as phone moves naturally
- Cut to friends walking beside university building, talking over one another

### 5-7.5 Seconds — Outdoor Study Break

**Scene**: Campus table study session

- Fast handheld footage of @reference sitting with friends around outdoor table
- Scattered props: open laptops, notebooks, pens, takeaway coffee cups, snacks, backpacks
- She chats animatedly, laughs, reacts naturally to friend's comment
- Realistic sunlight creates mild lens reflections and small auto-exposure changes

### 7.5-10 Seconds — Campus Fun

**Scene**: Playful moments on grass

- Quick close-up: @reference gives camera big warm smile and playful wave
- Abrupt jump cut to group sitting on grass
- Tossing notebook between them, sharing snacks, laughing
- One friend casually photographs group while others make spontaneous poses
- Camera framing remains imperfect and handheld

### 10-12.5 Seconds — Lunch Between Classes

**Scene**: Casual outdoor dining

- Abrupt edit: everyone gathered near shaded campus bench or outdoor café table
- Friends casually pass chips, sandwiches, cold drinks
- Playful toast with cups and cans raised
- Talking and laughing over one another
- Natural hand contact, realistic object handling, believable group interaction

### 12.5-15 Seconds — Final Campus Memory

**Scene**: Relaxed ending on grass

- @reference relaxed on grass among friends, softly smiling while looking across campus
- She briefly turns toward phone camera, smiles naturally
- Video ends with gentle accidental phone sway toward trees and university buildings
- As though friend recording simply stopped filming

## Camera and Motion

### Technical Specs

- **Format**: Vertical 9:16 smartphone framing
- **Movement**: Authentic handheld phone movement with slight shake
- **Composition**: Occasional imperfect framing
- **Motion**: Normal frame-rate with realistic actions (walking, laughing, sitting, waving, eating)
- **Editing**: Rapid jump cuts every 1-2 seconds

### What to AVOID

- ❌ No slow motion
- ❌ No speed ramps
- ❌ No cinematic camera movements
- ❌ No drone shots
- ❌ No artificial stabilization
- ❌ No exaggerated motion blur

## Lighting

### Natural Daylight

- **Primary**: Natural warm daytime sunlight
- **Shadows**: Realistic outdoor shadows beneath people, benches, trees, buildings
- **Exposure**: Subtle smartphone auto-exposure changes (sunlight ↔ shade transitions)
- **Skin**: Natural texture with no smoothing or beauty enhancement

## Audio

### Authentic Campus Ambience ONLY

- Natural overlapping laughter
- Casual conversation
- Footsteps
- Light wind through trees
- Distant student voices
- Bicycle sounds
- Birds chirping
- Backpack movement
- Subtle city or campus background noise

### What to EXCLUDE

- ❌ No background music
- ❌ No narration
- ❌ No artificial cinematic sound effects
