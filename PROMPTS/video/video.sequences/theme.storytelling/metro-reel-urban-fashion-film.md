# Metro Reel – Urban Cinema × AI Fashion Film

## Description

A 15-second cinematic urban fashion film created entirely with AI - no camera, no crew, just AI generation. Shot in dark cinematic editorial style with a model navigating through various metro/subway environments including glass corridors, escalators, benches, platforms, and trains. The sequence follows documentary realism with handheld-style cinematography, capturing every shot from different angles while maintaining character consistency throughout. Features teal and orange color palette with natural lighting, film grain, and a focus on authentic urban atmosphere. Generated using Higgsfield AI for images, Seedance 2.5 for video animation, GPT Image 2 for character consistency, and Nano Banana Pro for reference-based generation.

## Usage

Perfect for fashion brand lookbooks, streetwear campaigns, urban editorial content, AI filmmaking demonstrations, fashion film portfolios, social media fashion content, documentary-style brand videos, subway/metro aesthetic content, cinematic fashion reels, and showcasing AI video generation capabilities for commercial fashion applications.

## 13-Shot Storyboard Breakdown

### SHOT 1 (0-1.5s) — SILHOUETTE

Pure silhouette. Woman walking LEFT TO RIGHT past large backlit frosted glass wall. Camera static side profile. Only rim light. Deep teal. Reflective floor. Hair and wide trousers move with stride. **Expression**: absorbed, in her own world.

### SHOT 2 (1.5-3s) — ESCALATOR FROM BOTTOM

Camera at BOTTOM looking UP. She rides DOWN toward camera. Looks at phone then lifts eyes with curiosity. Fluorescent lights streak behind. Teal tunnel walls. Hair falls forward. **Expression**: distracted, glances up.

### SHOT 3 (3-5s) — ESCALATOR FACE-ON

Camera at TOP shooting DOWN. She faces camera front-on, arms on railings. Wide angle symmetrical. Teal tunnel stretches dramatically below. **Expression**: calm, open, slight attitude.

### SHOT 4 (5-6.5s) — ESCALATOR BACK

Camera BEHIND her full body back shot. Faces away, rides into dark tunnel. Auburn hair cascades down, moves gently. Camera slowly pushes forward. Hair cascades down. Tunnel swallows frame. **Expression**: mysterious, unknown.

### SHOT 5 (6.5-8.5s) — MIRROR REFLECTION

Curved convex security mirror near turnstile. Camera over right shoulder. Her reflection visible — she stares, tilts head, studies herself. Green exit signs glow in background. **Expression**: self-aware, quiet recognition.

### SHOT 6 (8-10s) — BENCH SITTING

Camera LOW front-on. Sits legs apart, forearms on knees, hands clasped. Stares at ground then looks up at camera. Dark metal mesh wall behind. Single overhead light pools on her. **Expression**: pensive, tired but strong.

### SHOT 7 (10-12s) — PLATFORM PHONE

Camera SIDE ANGLE. Orange train rushes LEFT TO RIGHT in full motion blur. Wind hits — hair, shirt move, trousers billow. She stays completely still. **Expression**: unbothered, untouchable.

### SHOT 8 (12-13.5s) — LOW ANGLE PLATFORM

Camera LOW ANGLE looking UP. She holds phone, slowly raises eyes into camera. Hair slightly windswept. Shallow DOF, orange bokeh behind her. **Expression**: direct, quietly intense.

### SHOT 9 (13.5-15s) — DOOR FRAME HERO

Dead centre in open orange train doors. Arms loose. Extremely slow push in. Zero expression. Total presence. Orange frame surrounds her like a portrait. **Expression**: power, stillness.

### SHOT 10 (15-17s) — INSIDE TRAIN

Camera handheld LOW across from her. Sits on teal bench, leans on window. Tunnel lights strobe across face as train moves. Touches necklace absently. **Expression**: introspective, somewhere else mentally.

### SHOT 11 (17-19.5s) — PLATFORM TWIRL

Camera WIDE low. Train pulls away RIGHT TO LEFT. She turns — hair fans out in wide arc. 40% slow motion. Trousers and shirt swirl. **Expression**: free, caught mid-movement.

### SHOT 12 (19.5-21s) — FACE CLOSEUP

Camera MACRO. Eyes, nose, necklace fill entire frame. Looks LEFT then stares into lens. Teal catchlight in eyes. Real skin texture. One slow blink. **Expression**: raw, alive, intense.

### SHOT 13 (21-23s) — EXIT WALK

Camera FLOOR LEVEL looking up. She steps forward toward camera with authority. Orange train alongside. Walks toward lens, fills frame. FADES TO BLACK. **Expression**: purposeful, final, closing statement.

## Pro Tips

### Maximum Impact

- **Character Consistency is Everything**: The character sheet with 4 angles is your foundation. Spend time getting this right - same lighting, same styling, clean white background. This becomes your reference for all 13 shots.
- **Storyboard as Visual Script**: The 13-panel storyboard isn't optional - it's your shot-by-shot visual guide that Seedance uses to generate the sequence. Each panel should clearly show camera angle, character position, and environment.
- **Documentary Feel Over Perfection**: The handheld style and natural movements create authenticity. Don't aim for perfectly smooth camera work - slight sway and organic movement make it feel real.
- **Teal + Orange Contrast**: This complementary color palette is the visual signature. Teal for shadows/tunnels, orange for trains/highlights. The contrast creates the cinematic mood.

### Professional Quality

- **Every Shot Different Angle**: This is what makes it cinematic rather than static. Low angles, overhead, behind, side, macro close-up, wide - varying perspectives keep viewers engaged across 15 seconds.
- **Character Alive Throughout**: She's not posing - she's living in the space. Looking at phone, glancing up, touching necklace, turning naturally. Micro-movements and expressions tell the story.
- **Real Fabric Physics**: Hair blowing when train passes, trouser fabric draping naturally, shirt moving with turns - these details separate AI-generated from obviously fake.
- **Film Grain as Texture**: ISO 3200 grain adds organic texture that helps blend any AI artifacts. It also adds to the documentary/film aesthetic rather than clean digital look.

### Technical Execution

- **Seedance 2.5 Workflow**: Attach character sheet first, then storyboard reference, then input the complete prompt with all 13 shots detailed. Seedance will generate as continuous sequence.
- **Identity Lock for Consistency**: Use @image1 or similar reference system in platforms that support it (Nano Banana Pro) to lock the exact face/features across different environmental shots.
- **Shallow DOF Strategic**: Not every shot needs f1.8 bokeh. Use it strategically in shots 8, 9, 12 for emotional emphasis. Wider shots (1, 7, 11) can have deeper focus to show environment.
- **Lighting Continuity**: Metro lighting is specific - fluorescent overhead strips (teal cast), train interior lights (warm white), platform lights (pools of light with dark surrounds). Maintain this logic.

### Platform-Specific Tips

- **For Seedance 2.5**: The prompt structure matters. Format as "SHOT X — duration · TITLE" followed by camera angle, action, expression. This gives clear parsing for AI generation.
- **For Higgsfield AI**: When generating character sheet, emphasize "same girl, same outfit, same lighting, clean white background" multiple times. Use "2×2 grid format" explicitly.
- **For GPT Image 2**: Provide extremely detailed character description including specific clothing items, accessories, tattoos, hair style, skin tone. The more detail, the better consistency.
- **For Runway/Pika (Individual Shots)**: If generating shots separately, use the character sheet image as reference for each shot. Edit together in Adobe Premiere or DaVinci Resolve with tight cuts matching storyboard timing.

### Storytelling Enhancement

- **Narrative Arc**: The shot sequence tells a journey - entering metro (silhouette) → navigating (escalators/mirror/bench) → waiting (platform) → traveling (train interior) → leaving (exit walk). This structure gives purpose.
- **Expression Evolution**: Her emotional state progresses - absorbed/distracted → self-aware → pensive → unbothered → powerful → introspective → free → raw → purposeful. Each shot has internal logic.
- **Visual Motifs**: Recurring elements tie it together - headphones (she's in her own world), phone (modern life), necklace touch (self-soothing gesture), direct-to-camera moments (connection with viewer).
- **No Dialogue Needed**: The visuals, expressions, and ambient metro sounds tell the complete story. This is pure visual cinema.

### Color Grading Details

- **Teal Shadows**: Push blue-green into shadow areas, particularly in tunnel walls, bench areas, train interiors. This creates the underground atmosphere.
- **Orange Highlights**: Warm highlights from train lights, platform lighting, any bright sources. This creates the complementary contrast.
- **Crushed Blacks**: Deep, rich blacks without detail loss. Metro environments have genuine darkness - embrace it rather than lifting shadows.
- **Film Grain**: Apply in post if AI generation doesn't include it naturally. Use ISO 3200-6400 equivalent grain texture, focused in shadow and midtone areas.

### Fashion Editorial Strategy

- **Outfit as Character**: The white tank, striped wide-leg trousers, tied shirt, chunky sneakers create a specific look - comfortable urban style that's elevated but relatable. This isn't high fashion runway - it's wearable street style.
- **Accessories Tell Story**: Headphones (music lover), necklace (personal style), tattoos (individuality), bracelet (attention to detail). Each element adds character depth.
- **Movement Shows Garment**: Wide-leg trousers flowing, loose hair moving, tied shirt responding to turns - the garments are active participants in the shots, not just worn.
- **No Beauty Filter Philosophy**: "Real skin texture, real fabric movement, documentary realism" - this positions the fashion as authentic and attainable rather than unreal perfection.

### Production Value Without Production

- **Zero Physical Shoot Costs**: No location fees, no model fees, no crew, no equipment rental, no permits for filming in metro. AI generation removes all physical production costs.
- **Infinite Iterations**: Don't like shot 7? Regenerate just that shot with adjusted prompt. Can't do that with real production without expensive reshoot.
- **Impossible Shots Made Possible**: Some camera angles (floor level, tight escalator shots, train door framing) would be difficult/dangerous/prohibited in real metro filming. AI makes them easy.
- **Speed to Delivery**: From concept to finished 15-second film in hours instead of weeks of production planning, shooting, and editing.

### Social Media Optimization

- **15-Second Format**: Perfect for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts. The platform-optimized duration ensures full views.
- **Hook Within 3 Seconds**: Shot 1 (silhouette) and Shot 2 (escalator from below) are visually striking enough to stop scrolling immediately.
- **Vertical Format Option**: The storyboard can be adapted for 9:16 vertical by adjusting framing while keeping the 13-shot structure and narrative flow.
- **No Music Dependency**: The ambient metro sounds are part of the aesthetic, but if platforms mute, the visual storytelling still works completely.

### AI Generation Considerations

- **Face Consistency Challenge**: This is the hardest part. The character sheet helps massively, but be prepared to regenerate shots where the face drifts from the reference.
- **Hand/Finger Detail**: AI often struggles with hands. In shots where hands are prominent (shot 6 on knees, shot 8 holding phone), may need multiple generations to get natural-looking fingers.
- **Motion Blur Authenticity**: Shot 7 with train motion blur is tricky - AI needs to blur the train dramatically while keeping her sharp. May require specific motion blur language in prompt.
- **Fabric Physics**: Wide-leg trousers and loose hair moving naturally is complex physics. Emphasize "natural fabric movement" and "hair responds to movement" explicitly in prompts.
