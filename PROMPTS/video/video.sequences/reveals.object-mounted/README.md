## Shared Workflow

1. Take one screenshot of the exact product.
2. Open Higgsfield Video and select Seedance 2.5 Reference.
3. Upload the product screenshot and paste the complete matching prompt.
4. Generate and rerun as needed.
5. Keep the product reference authoritative: no altered shape, typography, color, materials, or proportions.

## Shared Reveal Grammar

- 10-second continuous shot with no cuts or fades.
- Fast, aggressive, percussive movement rather than slow motion.
- Camera remains object-mounted and never becomes a free-floating camera.
- Build begins from a blank or unassembled product state.
- Camera follows the literal geometry of the construction: stitches, lettering, print contours, seams, hems, or hardware.
- Finished product matches the uploaded reference exactly before the handoff or wear shot.
- Hard flat daylight, neutral grade, crisp material texture, diegetic sound only, and no music.
