# Orbital Camera Rotation

## Description

A simple technique for creating slow, smooth orbital camera movement around a frozen subject with perfectly centered rotation and consistent lighting.

## Prerequisites

- Subject image or video frame

## Video Prompt

Slow, smooth orbital camera movement around subject from left to right, perfectly centered rotation, no camera shake, no zoom, stationary subject, subject does not move at all - is frozen in time, consistent lighting throughout rotation.

## Usage

Perfect for product photography, fashion portraits, character showcases, 3D model presentations, and any content requiring smooth 360-degree rotation reveal. Ideal for e-commerce product displays, character turnarounds, portfolio presentations, and social media content featuring centered subjects with professional rotating camera movement.
