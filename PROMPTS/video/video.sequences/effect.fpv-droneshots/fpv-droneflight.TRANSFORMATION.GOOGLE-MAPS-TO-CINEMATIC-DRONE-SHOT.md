# FPV Google Earth Route Drone Flight

## Classification

**Product Category:** location-environment

**Reveal Effect:** cinematic-drone-route

**Reveal Mechanism:** FPV drone trajectory guided by an uploaded Google Earth screenshot with a drawn route line

**Sequence Type:** reference-match moving shot

**Filename:** `seq.location-environment.fpv-drone-route.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A cinematic single-shot FPV drone flight generated from a Google Earth reference image, following a user-drawn route line exactly while preserving the real geography, landmarks, terrain, and spatial relationships. The final video feels like a realistic golden-hour drone run with smooth banked turns, natural altitude changes, speed, and physically grounded movement.

## Usage

Perfect for travel content, real estate location reveals, tourism promos, destination previews, event route visualizations, cinematic map-to-video concepts, and social media reels built from Google Earth planning references.

Ideal for turning a static aerial or 3D map screenshot into a dynamic FPV flight path, previsualizing a drone route before production, creating cinematic flythroughs of recognizable locations, and generating geography-faithful video prompts from annotated reference images.

## Engines/Models

Use with image-to-video or video generation models that support reference images, path-following instructions, camera movement control, and cinematic FPV motion. The uploaded Google Earth screenshot is the geographic reference, and the drawn route line is an instruction guide only.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Dependency #1 - Google Earth screenshot showing the full intended flight area.
- Dependency #2 - A clearly drawn visible line on the screenshot marking the exact FPV drone trajectory.
- Dependency #3 - A defined route direction, such as `[LEFT -> RIGHT]`, showing where the drone starts and ends.

## Video Prompt

Create a cinematic video sequence from the uploaded Google Earth reference image, using the drawn line only as the FPV drone trajectory.

**Duration:** 8-12 seconds. Single uninterrupted shot.

**Style:** Golden hour, photorealistic, cinematic, 4K, realistic drone optics, natural motion blur, accurate depth, believable speed, smooth FPV movement, detailed terrain, realistic lighting, realistic shadows, and physically grounded camera movement.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Begin at the exact start of the drawn route, using the reference image as the geographic source. The line indicates the drone's flight path only and must not appear in the final video. Preserve the real terrain, buildings, beaches, roads, landmarks, coastline, vegetation, shadows, and spatial relationships shown in the reference.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:02
The drone launches into a single continuous FPV shot from the route's start point, following the direction `[LEFT -> RIGHT]`. Move forward with realistic drone physics, natural acceleration, subtle camera vibration, and a strong sense of speed while staying faithful to the geography of the reference image.

0:02-0:06
As the flight progresses, follow every curve and directional change in the drawn trajectory. Use smooth banked turns, realistic roll angles, gentle yaw corrections, and natural altitude shifts that respond to the terrain, structures, slopes, water, streets, and visible landmarks along the path.

0:06-0:10
Maintain a cinematic low-to-mid altitude FPV perspective unless the route requires a natural climb or descent. Pass over and around the visible geographic features in the correct order, keeping the motion physically believable and avoiding impossible turns, sudden teleportation, or abrupt camera cuts.

0:10-0:12
End exactly at the final point of the drawn route, still in one uninterrupted shot. The final frame should clearly reveal the destination area, surrounding landmarks, terrain, and spatial context from the reference image.

**Camera:**
Single continuous FPV drone camera. Follow the route line exactly in the direction `[LEFT -> RIGHT]` with realistic acceleration, smooth banked turns, natural roll, subtle yaw corrections, low-to-mid altitude movement, and terrain-responsive elevation changes.

**Lighting:**
Golden-hour cinematic lighting with realistic shadows, natural highlights, accurate reflections where water or glass appears, and lighting direction preserved from the reference geography whenever visible.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
The drawn line is only a path guide and must not appear in the final video. Preserve the real terrain, buildings, beaches, roads, landmarks, coastline, vegetation, shadows, and spatial relationships shown in the uploaded Google Earth reference. The flight must feel physically possible, with no teleporting, no abrupt cuts, no impossible turns, and no generic aerial replacement footage.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
Photorealistic 4K cinematic output with stable motion, realistic drone physics, natural motion blur, accurate depth, detailed terrain, clean geography matching, no route-line artifacts, no distorted landmarks, and no visible annotation marks.

Do not show the drawn line in the final video. Do not change the route direction. Do not invent a new location, replace buildings, alter major landmarks, ignore the terrain, or cut away from the single continuous FPV flight. Do not use generic aerial footage; the video must follow the exact trajectory and geography shown in the uploaded reference.
