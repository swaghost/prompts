# AI Map Diorama-to-Drone Orbit

## Classification

**Product Category:** location-environment

**Reveal Effect:** map-diorama-drone-orbit

**Reveal Mechanism:** a realistic geographic map generates a miniature 3D city diorama at the correct location, then the uploaded frame is animated with stabilized clockwise drone orbit, depth, parallax, and environmental motion

**Sequence Type:** reference-match reveal

**Filename:** `reveal.location-environment.map-diorama-drone-orbit.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A two-stage AI map animation workflow that first creates a square photorealistic 3D geographic map diorama, then converts that frame into a 5-second cinematic drone orbit video. The monument remains the focal point while the camera adds true 3D depth, parallax, golden-hour lighting, atmosphere, and subtle environmental motion.

## Usage

Perfect for travel content, tourism promos, city explainers, geography videos, destination reveals, landmark showcases, educational map animations, real estate area context, and short-form cinematic social content.

Ideal for turning a country, city, or landmark into a premium miniature map diorama, then animating it as a realistic drone-cinema reveal with orbital motion, parallax, atmospheric depth, and polished film-grade color.
