# Champion Sweatshirt - A Single Thread Writes the Script Logo

## Classification

**Product Category:** Apparel / branded sweatshirt

**Reveal Effect:** Self-writing embroidery

**Reveal Mechanism:** Navy and red embroidery threads

**Sequence Type:** Self-build reference-match reveal

## Description

A 7-second locked-off commercial product sequence in which embroidery writes a supplied Champion-style logo across heather-grey fleece.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2 for Step A; Seedance 2.0 for Step B; Picsart Flow workflow.

## Prerequisites

Attach the logo to reproduce as Image 1. Feed the finished Step A still into Seedance as both reference and target.

## Step A - Image (GPT Image 2, uses Image 1 logo)

```text
Image 1 is the source logo: reproduce this exact mark unchanged, with the same shapes, proportions, colours and script letterforms. Create a photorealistic extreme close-up product photograph of the chest of a heather-grey heavyweight cotton sweatshirt, shot straight on, fabric filling the frame, edge of a ribbed collar visible at top left. Resolve the loopback fleece with fine vertical wales, marled grey and white fibres, soft rumpling and one gentle fold below.

Embroidered across the chest is the Image 1 logo in dense satin stitch: a bold navy elliptical C-form at the left with a red half-disc in its counter, and flowing navy script to the right on one baseline. Raised stitching has tight directional sheen, crisp outlines and fine solid connecting strokes; the fleece puckers slightly around it while bare grey cloth shows through every counter and gap. Macro e-commerce photography, soft diffused upper-left light, embroidery tack-sharp, fabric softening at edges.

Constraints: reproduce Image 1 exactly, no additional text, labels, captions, watermark, other logos, hands, people, hangers, props, or background beyond the fabric.
```

## Step B - Video (Seedance 2.0)

```text
Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts. The camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame: same framing, angle, distance and lens, with no pan, push, zoom, or reframing. Show an extreme macro straight-on view of heather-grey fleece, ribbed collar edge at top left and one gentle fold below.

Open on bare fleece with no embroidery. A single navy thread descends and stitches the mark without being touched. It fills the bold elliptical C-form first, then a red thread fills the half-disc in the counter. The navy thread travels right and writes the connected script on one baseline, including ascenders, the long tail below the line, and final upward flick, leaving grey fleece visible through all counters and gaps.

Both threads pull taut and settle flat. The cloth relaxes and the finished mark holds identical to the reference for the last second. Audio: thread pulling through heavy fleece and a faint airy rustle of cotton. Negative prompt: no cuts, shot changes, camera movement, zoom, pan, reframing, editing, needles, sewing machine, knitting, print, patch, music, soundtrack, library audio, additional text, watermark, hands, tools, people, or anything except fabric and floating threads.
```
