## Shared Workflow

1. Generate the finished Step A hero still in GPT Image 2.
2. Check logo spelling, mark geometry, proportions, and product details at close range.
3. Feed the Step A still into Seedance 2.0 as both the reference and target.
4. Paste the matching Step B self-build prompt.
5. Keep the camera locked off and preserve the final-frame match.
6. Use diegetic material sounds only; do not add music.

## Shared Self-Build Formula

- **Step A:** Nail the finished hero still with accurate product and logo details.
- **Step B:** Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position.
- **Opening state:** Empty, bare, or blank surface with the finished piece absent.
- **Build:** Product-specific materials assemble themselves without hands, tools, machinery, or people.
- **Payoff:** The final frame matches the Step A reference exactly and holds for the last second.

## Important Usage Note

The examples recreate real trademarks for demonstrations and portfolio pitches. Do not sell or publish brand-logo work without the relevant permission. For client outreach, replace the demonstration brand with the prospect's own product and authorized logo.
