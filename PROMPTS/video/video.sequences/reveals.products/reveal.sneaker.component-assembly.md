# Dad-Shoe Sneaker - The Shoe Assembles Sole-Up

## Classification

**Product Category:** Footwear / running sneaker

**Reveal Effect:** Sole-up component assembly

**Reveal Mechanism:** Outsole, foam, mesh, suede panels, laces, and stitched letter

**Sequence Type:** Self-build reference-match reveal

## Description

A 7-second locked-off commercial footwear sequence in which a grey suede and mesh dad sneaker assembles itself from outsole to stitched upper.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2 for Step A; Seedance 2.0 for Step B; Picsart Flow workflow.

## Prerequisites

Feed the finished Step A still into Seedance as both reference and target.
