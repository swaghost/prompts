# Heinz Ketchup Bottle - Molten Glass Forms, Ketchup Pours, Label Wraps

## Classification

**Product Category:** Beverage / packaged condiment

**Reveal Effect:** Molten formation and label wrap

**Reveal Mechanism:** Molten glass, liquid ketchup, cap, and self-wrapping paper label

**Sequence Type:** Self-build reference-match reveal

## Description

A 7-second locked-off commercial product sequence in which a glass ketchup bottle forms from molten glass, fills with ketchup, caps itself, and wraps itself in its label.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2 for Step A; Seedance 2.0 for Step B; Picsart Flow workflow.

## Prerequisites

Attach the source bottle and label as Image 1. Feed the finished Step A still into Seedance as both reference and target.
