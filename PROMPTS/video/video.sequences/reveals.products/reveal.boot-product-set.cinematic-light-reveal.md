# Dr. Martens 1460 - Hero, Catalogue Sheet, and Reveal Film

## Classification

**Product Category:** Footwear / boot product set

**Reveal Effect:** Cinematic light-and-haze reveal

**Reveal Mechanism:** Sequential hard light, rim light, haze, and product movement

**Sequence Type:** Catalogue-to-film reference reveal

## Description

A three-prompt product set: a finished hero still, a seven-panel catalogue board, and a dark 7-second cinematic reveal film. The board and film use the hero still as their product reference.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2 for the hero and catalogue sheet; Seedance 2.0 for the cinematic reveal; Picsart Flow workflow.

## Prerequisites

Generate the hero still first. Use it as the visual reference for both the catalogue sheet and the cinematic reveal film.

## C - Cinematic Reveal Film (Seedance 2.0, uses hero reference)

```text
Use the attached hero image as the product reference. Preserve the exact deep-oxblood Dr. Martens 1460 throughout: same silhouette, waxed leather, eight brass eyelets, yellow welt stitching, heel loop and air-cushioned sole. Laces are threaded through all eight eyelets, tied in a symmetrical bow and tucked short in every shot.

Create a 7-second high-contrast product film in a deep black void with no floor, walls or visible surfaces. Thin haze drifts through darkness only where hard light reveals it. Open in macro on the brass eyelets. A hard light rakes from the right; each eyelet ignites in sequence, waxed leather lifts into deep red, black laces remain dense, and smoke crosses the beam as the camera drifts slowly up the column.

Cut to the full boot in lateral profile floating level in darkness, lit by a hard rim along the top edge. Backlight fans through haze. A key sweeps from left across the flank, bringing out oxblood leather and igniting the yellow welt stitching as a bright line; the boot holds while haze rolls outward. Cut to a low three-quarter front angle close on the toe. The boot tips toward the lens and stops hard, tied bow snapping taut, collar compressing, and a highlight racing along the grooved sole as it settles.

Camera is locked off in each shot except the opening drift. Product centred with generous black space. Look: high-contrast product film, pure black background, zero ambient light, no props, no text. Audio: no music, low room tone, deep leather creak on settle, low tonal swell on each cut. Duration: 7 seconds.
```
