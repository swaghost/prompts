# Guinness Sign - Stout Pours In, the Head Forms the Letters

## Classification

**Product Category:** Beverage signage / pub fascia

**Reveal Effect:** Liquid formation and foam lettering

**Reveal Mechanism:** Stout, descending bubbles, creamy head, and dimensional sign forms

**Sequence Type:** Self-build reference-match reveal

## Description

A 7-second locked-off commercial sign sequence in which a pub fascia fills with stout, bubbles settle, and dimensional Guinness-style lettering forms from the creamy head.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2 for Step A; Seedance 2.0 for Step B; Picsart Flow workflow.

## Prerequisites

Feed the finished Step A still into Seedance as both reference and target.

## Step A - Image (GPT Image 2)

```text
Create a photorealistic architectural photograph of an old Dublin corner pub at dusk, viewed from the pavement opposite looking up at the facade. Show glossy black timber shopfront, warm amber windows, wet cobbles and deep blue evening sky. Mounted across the shopfront is a dimensional deep-black sign with a solid-gold Guinness harp at left, cream-white "GUINNESS" in wide bold capitals, gold lines "EXTRA STOUT" and "BREWED IN DUBLIN SINCE 1759", and a lower cream line "DRAUGHT  •  LIVE MUSIC  •  SNUG AT THE BACK". All elements stand proud with side returns, gilded edges and shadows; lettering is perfectly legible. Add wet-cobble reflections and a lamp post for scale. Constraints: exact four lines, no other text, signage, watermark or people.
```

## Step B - Video (Seedance 2.0)

```text
Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts. Camera never moves: same framing, angle, distance and lens, no pan, push, zoom or reframing. Wide low three-quarter view from the wet pavement, with the long fascia, black shopfront, warm windows, stone facade, blue dusk sky, street lamp, terrace and cobble reflections all fixed. Only the fascia face changes.

Open on a bare black fascia. Dark stout pours into the board and settles into a glossy black field. A dense curtain of pale bubbles descends through the dark while the liquid clears from bottom upward. Creamy head rises and forms "GUINNESS" left to right. Gold then draws the harp, forepillar, soundboard and strings, followed by "EXTRA STOUT" and "BREWED IN DUBLIN SINCE 1759". The bottom cream line and gold bullet dots form last; a thin gold pinstripe closes around the board. All letters thicken into dimensional forms, catch warm light and cast shadows. Hold the exact reference for the final second.

Audio: liquid surging and settling, fizzing bubbles, quiet wet street, faint muffled pub voices. Negative prompt: no cuts, shot changes, camera movement, zoom, pan, reframing, editing, close-up, flat frontal view, bubbles rising upward, glasses, pints, music, soundtrack, library audio, additional text, watermark, people, hands or tools.
```
