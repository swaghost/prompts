# Stone Island Patch - Threads Stitch the Compass Badge

## Classification

**Product Category:** Apparel / branded patch

**Reveal Effect:** Self-stitching embroidery

**Reveal Mechanism:** Floating golden-yellow and forest-green threads

**Sequence Type:** Self-build reference-match reveal

## Description

A 7-second locked-off commercial product sequence in which an embroidered compass badge stitches itself onto an olive technical jacket sleeve.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2 for Step A; Seedance 2.0 for Step B; Picsart Flow workflow.

## Prerequisites

Use a clean source-logo reference if needed. Feed the finished Step A still into Seedance as both reference and target.

## Step A - Image (GPT Image 2)

```text
Create a photorealistic extreme close-up product photograph of the upper left sleeve of an olive-green technical nylon jacket, shot straight on, the sleeve filling the entire frame at a slight angle, the coated nylon showing a soft directional sheen and one gentle crease running below.

Fastened to the sleeve is a horizontal rectangular patch of dark navy woven fabric, wider than it is tall, lying flat across the sleeve with its long edges running horizontally. It stands clearly on top of the nylon rather than being part of it, its edges finished and its corners slightly rounded.

Embroidered at the centre of that rectangular patch is a circular compass emblem, filling most of the patch's height. At its centre is a four-pointed compass rose: one long point up, one down, one left and one right, each point a slender elongated diamond. Every point is split lengthways down its own axis, one half in golden yellow and the other half in dark forest green, so the star reads as alternating light and dark facets around its centre.

Around the star runs a golden yellow embroidered ring. Set into that ring in dark forest green capitals is the wordmark "STONE ISLAND", appearing twice: once curving along the upper half of the ring reading left to right, and once curving along the lower half rotated 180 degrees, so the two readings sit head to head around the circle. The letters follow the curve of the ring exactly. The embroidery sits slightly raised above the navy cloth with a visible satin-stitch sheen.

The patch is held on by two small dark buttons, one at the left end of the rectangle and one at the right end, sitting level with the centre of the circle, each passing through a reinforced eyelet in the patch and through a short fabric tab beneath. The nylon puckers very slightly around each button.

Macro product photography for a fashion e-commerce listing. Soft diffused studio light from the upper left, the patch casting its own crisp shadow on the sleeve, shallow depth of field.

Constraints: exact spelling "STONE ISLAND" on both halves of the ring, no other text, no captions, no watermark, no other logos, no hands, no people, no hangers, no props, no background beyond the sleeve.
```

## Step B - Video (Seedance 2.0)

```text
Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video. The camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves: same framing, angle, distance and lens from first frame to last. No pans, pushes, zooms, or reframing.

Extreme macro close-up of the olive-green jacket sleeve filling the entire frame, the same viewpoint, angle and distance as the reference image, the patch running horizontally across the middle with coated fabric and soft folds visible above and below. The dark navy rectangular patch is already fastened from the first frame and never moves; its position, size, finished edge, two black buttons and yellow thread loops remain unchanged. Only the embroidery changes.

The shot opens with the patch face completely bare, plain dark navy weave with no embroidery. A thick bundle of golden yellow and dark forest green threads hangs from above and stitches the emblem by itself. Strands lower onto the cloth, sink through it and rise back out, drawing dense satin stitch; several threads remain taut while the loose bundle sways above.

The four star points draw outward one at a time: up, down, left, right. Yellow and green strands fill the two halves of each point. The threads then close the small inner yellow ring, followed by the large outer yellow ring. Finally, green strands lay the capitals "STONE ISLAND" along the upper half and then the rotated lower half until both readings sit head to head.

All remaining strands pull taut and lift out of frame. The raised glossy stitching settles against the navy weave, the cloth puckers fractionally, and the finished emblem holds identical to the reference for the last second.

Audio: many threads drawing tight through dense woven cloth, a soft fibrous whisper as strands move through air, faint airy rustle of coated fabric. Negative prompt: no cuts, no shot changes, no camera movement, no zoom, no pan, no reframing, no editing, no removing or re-attaching patch, no moving buttons, no needles, no sewing machine, no music, no soundtrack, no library audio, no additional text, no watermark, no hands, no tools, no people, nothing except sleeve, patch and floating threads.
```
