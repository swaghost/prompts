# Michelin Sign - The Figure Inflates Ring by Ring

## Classification

**Product Category:** Service signage / tyre service

**Reveal Effect:** Pneumatic ring inflation

**Reveal Mechanism:** Self-inflating rounded white segments

**Sequence Type:** Self-build reference-match reveal

## Description

A 7-second locked-off commercial sign sequence in which a white rounded-ring Michelin-style figure inflates on a workshop wall and the service copy seats into place.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2 for Step A; Seedance 2.0 for Step B; Picsart Flow workflow.

## Prerequisites

Feed the finished Step A still into Seedance as both reference and target.

## Step A - Image (GPT Image 2)

```text
Create a photorealistic architectural photograph of a tyre service workshop on an industrial street, viewed from the forecourt at a low three-quarter angle, roller shutters below, overcast flat daylight and wet concrete apron. Mounted on the blue wall is a large three-dimensional sign. At its left is the Michelin man, a standing figure built from stacked white rounded tyre rings, thick at limbs, narrow at waist, with a smooth round head and friendly face, one arm raised in a wave. To the right, thick blue capitals read "MICHELIN". Beneath are smaller blue lines "TYRE SERVICE CENTRE" and "FITTING  BALANCING  ALIGNMENT". Along the lower edge is a narrow yellow line reading "NO APPOINTMENT NEEDED  •  ALL SIZES IN STOCK".

Every element is a deep moulded form proud of the blue cladding, with side returns and clean edges casting soft individual shadows. Lettering is sharp, correctly kerned and legible. Include cladding seams, drainpipe and a wheelie bin far below for scale. Constraints: exact spelling of all four lines, no other text, signage, watermark, people or cars.
```

## Step B - Video (Seedance 2.0)

```text
Single continuous locked-off 7-second shot, no cuts, one camera position. Camera stays exactly in place with identical framing, angle, distance and lens: no pan, push, zoom or reframing. Keep the workshop in low three-quarter view with roller shutters and wet apron visible.

Open on the bare blue wall. The figure inflates itself with nothing touching it: white rounded segments swell from nothing at the foot, then stack upward like soft cushions. Legs fill first, body widens and pinches at the waist, arms puff from shoulders, the raised arm lifts into a wave, and the smooth round head inflates last as its face settles. Segments are glossy white, without tread or pattern, softly grey in the creases.

Blue capitals push forward word by word, followed by the two smaller lines. The narrow yellow band slides along the bottom and locks flush. Finished sign holds identical to reference for the last second. Audio: compressed air, soft-rubber squeaks, seating clicks, quiet industrial ambience. Negative prompt: no cuts, shot changes, camera movement, zoom, pan, reframing, editing, close-up, flat frontal view, tyres, wheels, tread, rubber texture, black rubber, deflated shapes, music, soundtrack, library audio, additional text, watermark, people, hands, tools or air compressors.
```
