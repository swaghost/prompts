# Shell Pylon - The Pecten Shell Grows Rib by Rib

## Classification

**Product Category:** Fuel signage / petrol-station pylon

**Reveal Effect:** Rib-growth formation

**Reveal Mechanism:** Red and gold shell ribs unfurling from a common base

**Sequence Type:** Self-build reference-match reveal

## Description

A 7-second locked-off commercial pylon sequence in which a red-and-gold scallop shell grows rib by rib on a roadside sign before its dimensional copy locks into place.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2 for Step A; Seedance 2.0 for Step B; Picsart Flow workflow.

## Prerequisites

Feed the finished Step A still into Seedance as both reference and target.

## Step A - Image (GPT Image 2)

```text
Create a photorealistic photograph of a petrol station forecourt pylon on a tall white steel post beside a rural road, viewed from the tarmac looking upward, bright hard midday sun, pale sky and flat dry landscape. The pylon carries a Shell pecten emblem: scallop shape narrowing to a point, radiating ribs fanning from the base, alternating deep red and golden yellow, red outline and rounded scalloped top. Beneath it are red capitals "SHELL", then "V-POWER FUELS" and "OPEN 24 HOURS", and a bottom band "SHOP  •  CAR WASH  •  AIR AND WATER  •  EV CHARGING". Dimensional moulded forms cast hard shadows on the white panel. Show post seams, bolt plates and a distant bollard for scale. Constraints: exact four lines, no other text, signage, watermark, people or cars.
```

## Step B - Video (Seedance 2.0)

```text
Single continuous locked-off 7-second shot, no cuts. Camera stays in the same low upward viewpoint with identical framing, angle, distance and lens; no pan, push, zoom or reframing. Keep the steel post and pale sky visible.

Open on a blank white panel. A small red rib rises at the shell base, then rib after rib unfurls outward in a widening fan, alternating red and gold. Each rib thickens forward with side returns; the top scallops into rounded lobes, the red outline closes, and the red wordmark and two smaller lines push forward word by word. The bottom band slides in and locks flush. Each element casts its hard midday shadow. Hold the exact reference for the final second.

Audio: smooth ceramic growth, seating clicks, open wind and distant traffic. Negative prompt: no cuts, shot changes, camera movement, zoom, pan, reframing, editing, close-up, flat frontal view, music, soundtrack, library audio, additional text, watermark, people, cars, hands or tools.
```
