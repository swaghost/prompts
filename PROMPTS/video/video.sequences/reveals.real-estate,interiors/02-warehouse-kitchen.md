## Concept

A luxury interior photograph that animates from bare to fully styled — the empty warehouse kitchen assembles itself piece by piece in choreographed sequence: fire ignites, island lowers, stools slide in, table arrives with chairs, pendants descend and light, shelves mount with vessels. No hands, everything moves autonomously. Final frame reads as a still, high-end listing photo.

1. Open [Picsart Flow](https://picsart.com/flow) → new canvas

## IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2)

Photorealistic interior photograph for a luxury private-residence listing.

**Scene:** the kitchen and dining end of a converted brick warehouse apartment. A wall of tall steel-framed factory glazing with narrow black glazing bars fills the far wall and looks onto a lit city street at blue hour. The left wall is original bare red brick with visible mortar. The floor is polished poured concrete with a soft sheen. The ceiling is exposed riveted steel trusses with a plain solid boarded soffit between them, a continuous unbroken surface. A blackened steel wood stove with a straight flue rising to the soffit stands against the brick, lit.

**Furnishings:** a long kitchen island in honed black soapstone with a blackened steel base running across the centre of the room, four low-backed stools in tan saddle leather tucked along it, a wall of flat-fronted smoked oak cabinetry behind the island, two narrow blackened-steel open shelves on the brick carrying rows of ceramic vessels and stacked plates, a long solid walnut dining table set at right angles to the island with six spindle-back chairs in dark ash, three ribbed glass pendants hanging low in a row over the island, a slim brass reading lamp on the far end of the island, a large hand-knotted flatweave runner in charcoal and rust under the dining table, a tall potted rubber plant in a matte black planter beside the glazing, a stack of thick chopping boards leaning at the end of the island, a wide stoneware bowl of pears and a glass carafe on the dining table, two framed black and white photographs in slim black frames on the brick above the shelves.

**Camera:** eye level, straight on, 24mm equivalent, verticals perfectly parallel, framed to include the full truss height and the far glazing.

**Light:** cool blue dusk from the glazing as the key, warm pendant and stove light as secondary, deep soft shadows across the brick, no direct sun. Real material texture — brick grain and mortar, concrete pitting, soapstone honing marks, leather creasing, wood end grain, faint dust suspended in the air.

**Constraints:** no people, no pets, no text, no captions, no watermark, no logos, no signage, no openings in the ceiling, no skylight, no HDR halos, no oversaturation, no golden-hour orange grade, no fisheye, no wide-angle distortion, no tilted verticals.

## ANIMATION PROMPT (Omni Flash, video node)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

Photorealistic. Match the reference image's materials, colour and lighting exactly.

### Absolute Rules

**Reference rule, absolute:** the attached image is the complete and final state of this room. Every object that appears is an object visible in that image, arriving at exactly the position, size, material and colour it has there. Nothing else ever enters the frame. No added furniture, no added decor, no substituted or alternative versions of any piece, no duplicates.

**Camera rule, absolute:** the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance, focal length and lens from the first frame to the last. No pans, no tilts, no pushes, no orbits, no reframing.

**Architecture rule, absolute:** the bare red brick wall with its visible mortar, the wall of steel-framed factory glazing with its narrow black bars and the lit city street at blue hour beyond, the polished poured concrete floor, the riveted steel trusses and the solid boarded soffit between them, the smoked oak cabinetry wall, and the blackened steel wood stove with its straight flue rising to the soffit are rigid and fixed. Their positions, proportions and perspective are identical in every frame. Only the fire, the island, the seating, the table, the pendants and lamps, the textiles, vessels, plant and small objects change.

### Animation Sequence

The shot opens on this same room stripped completely bare: bare concrete floor, bare brick, the cabinetry wall empty-fronted and still, the stove cold and dark, the shelves absent, nothing standing on the floor and nothing hanging above it. Everything that follows happens inside this one unmoving frame, assembling itself with nothing and no one touching it, each piece arriving directly at its final place in the reference and stopping there.

**0.0s - 1.2s — FIRE**

Flames rise inside the stove and settle into a steady burn, warm light spreading up the flue and across the brick beside it.

**1.2s - 2.4s — THE ISLAND**

The long soapstone island lowers onto its blackened steel base across the centre of the room and seats onto the concrete, and the four tan leather stools slide in and tuck themselves along it one after another.

**2.4s - 4.0s — THE DINING END**

The walnut table lowers into place at right angles to the island, the six spindle-back chairs arriving around it one by one, and the charcoal and rust flatweave runner unrolls itself flat beneath the table as they settle.

**4.0s - 5.2s — LIGHT**

The three ribbed glass pendants descend on their cords in a row over the island and ignite one after another, their warm pools spreading across the soapstone, and the slim brass reading lamp sets itself down at the far end of the island and switches on.

**5.2s - 6.4s — DRESSING**

The two blackened-steel shelves fix themselves to the brick and their ceramic vessels and stacked plates settle into rows along them. The two framed black and white photographs rise and hang themselves on the brick above. The rubber plant lowers into its matte black planter beside the glazing, the chopping boards lean themselves against the end of the island, and the stoneware bowl of pears and the glass carafe place themselves on the table.

**6.4s - 7.0s — FINAL HOLD**

All assembly motion stops. The room now matches the reference image exactly, object for object. The grade settles into its final cool blue dusk from the glazing with warm pendant and stove light against it. The final frame reads as a still, high-end interior photograph.
