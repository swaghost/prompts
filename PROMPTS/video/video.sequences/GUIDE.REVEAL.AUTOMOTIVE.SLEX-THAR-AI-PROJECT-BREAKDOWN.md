## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A complete AI car-content workflow for turning two clean phone reference shots of a Mahindra Thar-style SUV in an underground parking garage into multi-angle stills, stone/lava concept frames, cinematic camera moves, and a locked-off Hollywood-style mechanical assembly video.

## Usage

Perfect for automotive content creators, car VFX breakdowns, AI commercial tests, social media vehicle edits, and cinematic before/after workflow documentation.

Ideal for building a repeatable pipeline from simple car references into a full angle set, material redesign stills, environment swaps, short camera-motion clips, and a final first-frame/last-frame assembly sequence.

Tools used in the source guide: Nano Banana 2 for image generation/editing, Kling 3.0 for motion from stills, Seedance 2.0 for first-frame/last-frame assembly, and After Effects for beat-timed motion, color, glow, and grain.

Create a complete AI automotive content workflow for a Thar-style SUV, starting from two clean phone reference shots and ending in cinematic motion and a mechanical assembly reveal.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Start with two clean phone shots of the same SUV: front and rear references in the same underground parking location with mixed natural and industrial light. The car identity, proportions, geometry, trim, stance, and location details should stay consistent unless a specific material or environment transformation step says otherwise.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

Step 01 - Starting Reference Shots
Use two clean phone shots of the car as the base: one front reference and one rear reference. Both are in the same underground parking location with consistent natural light mix.

Step 02 - Generating Multiple Angles
Image model: Nano Banana 2.

Step 07 - Turning the Stills Into Motion, Tilt Down
Video model: Kling 3.0.
Prompt: "Camera starts on a high top-down angle and descends smoothly and vertically until the car becomes the main focus. No rotation, no orbit, clean top-to-bottom move only. Realistic reflections on the wet floor."

Step 08 - Empty Space to Finished SUV
Video model: Seedance 2.0.
Use the supplied first frame and final frame as strict references. The camera remains completely locked with identical framing, preserving the exact perspective and composition from start to finish. An empty parking space transforms into the finished SUV through a premium Hollywood-style mechanical assembly sequence. The transformation is clean, cinematic, and physically believable, resembling a high-end automotive commercial or blockbuster VFX shot. The vehicle assembles using large pre-built modules only. No tiny individual parts, no random debris, and no chaotic construction.

Stage 1: a complete rolling chassis with all four wheels already attached materializes in the exact final position, settling naturally onto the ground.

Stage 2: the complete front mechanical assembly, including the engine bay and structural frame, locks onto the chassis as one rigid module with powerful mechanical impacts.

Stage 3: the entire body shell descends smoothly from above and aligns perfectly with the chassis before locking into place with heavy metallic precision.

Stage 4: the hood, four doors, roof panel, front bumper, rear bumper, and fenders fly in individually, rotating naturally through the air before snapping perfectly into position with satisfying mechanical force.

Stage 5: the windshield, side windows, mirrors, headlights, taillights, grille, and exterior trim install themselves with clean, precise motion.

Stage 6: a cinematic wave of dust, scratches, and dried mud rapidly spreads across the entire vehicle until it perfectly matches the supplied final frame.

Every module follows realistic momentum, rigid-body physics, and believable mechanical movement. Every impact produces subtle vibrations, metallic clunks, dust bursts, tiny sparks, and realistic weight. Maintain the original parking garage environment, industrial fluorescent lighting, realistic reflections, soft shadows, atmospheric dust, and photorealistic materials throughout the shot.

Step 09 - After Effects, Keying Motion to the Beat
Every move gets its own hand-drawn value graph instead of a default ease so cuts land on the beat. Push-ins get a steep S-curve so the last few frames snap instead of drifting. Orbit and tilt moves get a slower rise-and-settle so they do not feel robotic. Color, glow, and grain are added on top once timing is locked.

**Camera:**
Use vertical 9:16 anamorphic framing. For still-to-motion clips, use clean physical camera moves only: fast cinematic forward push-in, high top-down tilt/descend, or locked-off assembly. In the Seedance assembly shot, the camera remains completely locked with identical framing from first frame to last. No camera movement, no teleporting, no reframing.

**Lighting:**
Underground garage industrial fluorescent lighting for reference and assembly scenes. Cool cinematic grade for garage stills. Lava-floor and lava-cave variants add warm rim light from glowing lava while keeping the car geometry intact.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
Image steps include 9-angle generation, individual angle extraction, stone material swap, lava floor addition, and lava cave environment swap. Video steps include Kling camera movement clips and a Seedance module-based mechanical assembly from empty parking space to finished SUV.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
Keep the car identical across reference, multi-angle, and motion steps unless the specific prompt changes only material or environment. Use ultra sharp 4K stills, photorealistic automotive materials, realistic reflections, stable geometry, and premium cinematic VFX.

Negative Prompt: Avoid morphing, melting, liquid-metal effects, floating random parts, duplicated wheels, changing chassis geometry, warped body panels, incorrect proportions, environment changes during locked assembly, camera movement during locked assembly, flickering, unstable geometry, low-detail artifacts, chaotic construction, tiny random parts, and unrealistic mechanical movement.
