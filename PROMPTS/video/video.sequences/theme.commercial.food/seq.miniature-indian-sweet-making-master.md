```text

The central subject is one enormous, realistic finished {SWEET NAME}, gigantic beside dozens of tiny Indian chefs only a few centimetres tall. Adapt every ingredient, cooking step, and shaping technique to the actual traditional recipe of {SWEET NAME}; do not use random or incorrect ingredients or methods.

FORMAT AND CONTINUITY

Vertical 9:16, 10-15 seconds, one continuous fast but smooth miniature cooking progression. Keep the same warm wooden-and-stainless-steel Indian sweet kitchen, tiny chef character design, white chef coats, white chef hats, utensils, props, lighting, and photoreal miniature scale throughout. {SWEET NAME} remains the central subject from ingredient stage through final reveal. The massive food, workers, tools, and kitchen must look physically connected to one miniature world.

SCENE 1 - INGREDIENT PREPARATION

Tiny Indian chefs in clean matching white chef uniforms and white chef hats unload and prepare the traditional ingredients required for {SWEET NAME}. Show miniature wooden bowls, sacks, utensils, trays, and accurately oversized raw ingredients. Ingredients tower above the workers while remaining recognizable and authentic to the recipe.

SCENE 2 - MAIN PREPARATION

Multiple chefs work simultaneously with miniature cooking equipment, following the real method for {SWEET NAME}. Depending on the authentic recipe, show precise actions such as grinding, mixing, kneading, roasting, simmering, frying, stirring, or preparing syrup. Each group handles a distinct task with believable hand gestures, tool use, ingredient quantities, and food physics.

SCENE 3 - COOKING

The recipe mixture cooks in a gigantic traditional Indian cooking vessel appropriate to {SWEET NAME}. Tiny chefs use huge spoons and wooden paddles to stir it carefully. Show realistic steam, heat, bubbling where appropriate, ingredient movement, and gradual transformation into the correct texture and consistency. Never show an incorrect texture or cooking method for the selected sweet.

SCENE 4 - SHAPING AND FINISHING

Tiny chefs transfer the prepared sweet onto an enormous tray or work surface. They roll, cut, shape, press, fill, soak, garnish, or decorate it according to the traditional form of {SWEET NAME}. The sweet becomes exceptionally detailed, appetizing, glossy or textured as appropriate, with realistic crumbs, syrup, nuts, or other authentic finishing elements.

SCENE 5 - FINAL REVEAL

The miniature chefs gather around the enormous finished {SWEET NAME}. The camera performs a slow cinematic push-in for a spectacular macro close-up. Show accurate food texture, tiny fingerprints, crumbs, steam when appropriate, and rich Indian sweet-shop details.

SCENE 6 - CELEBRATION

After {SWEET NAME} is fully finished, the tiny chefs celebrate naturally: they clap, dance, high-five, and raise tiny plates holding the finished sweet. Keep their movement believable and joyful, without blocking the hero food close-up.

CAMERA AND VISUAL STYLE

Hyper-realistic photoreal miniature world, premium Indian mithai shop aesthetic, warm wooden kitchen, realistic stainless-steel utensils, rich Indian colours, cinematic macro photography, shallow depth of field, soft warm lighting, subtle bokeh, detailed textures, realistic shadows, natural focus transitions, and physically believable food movement. Use cinematic low angles to establish the chefs' tiny scale, overhead views during preparation, close-up food shots, and slow push-ins. Maintain smooth camera movement and consistent miniature proportions.

QUALITY AND EXCLUSIONS

Ultra-detailed, photorealistic, cinematic 8K look, realistic food physics, natural chef movement, correct anatomy, and consistent clothing. No dialogue, narration, subtitles, text overlays, logos, watermark, distorted faces, extra limbs, deformed hands, cartoon styling, unrealistic transformations, incorrect recipe steps, unrelated ingredients, floating food, or sudden cuts between stages.
```

- **Jalebi:** Fermented flour batter is piped in spirals into hot oil, fried crisp, then immersed in saffron-cardamom syrup.
- **Ladoo:** The correct base is roasted or cooked, combined with its traditional binder, rolled into spheres, and finished with the appropriate garnish.
