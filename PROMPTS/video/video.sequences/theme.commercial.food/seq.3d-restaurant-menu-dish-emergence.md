# 3D Restaurant Menu - Dish Emergence Commercial

## Description

A premium 10-second cinematic food commercial created from one restaurant menu image. The original menu remains the visual source of truth while 3-4 clearly identifiable dishes physically emerge from their exact printed positions and become realistic dimensional plated food. The camera moves elegantly across the menu and ends on a polished restaurant-menu hero composition.

## Usage

Use this for restaurant advertising, menu launches, food-delivery campaigns, hospitality marketing, social-media food content, and premium culinary brand presentations. It is designed for menus that clearly show dish names or recognizable food imagery and works best when the menu typography, logo, colours, prices, and layout need to remain exact.

## Engines/Models

The source prompt was created for Seedance through Apetelligence. It is also suitable for comparable image-to-video generators that accept an uploaded menu reference and support smooth camera movement, food physics, and consistent text preservation.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- **Dependency 1 - Required menu reference:** One high-resolution restaurant menu image showing the complete layout, branding, dish names, prices, typography, colours, and decorative elements.
- **Dependency 2 - Optional restaurant reference:** A supporting image of the restaurant's visual identity or dining environment.
- The menu must clearly identify at least 3 dishes. If a dish name or visual identity is unclear, do not select or invent it.
- Preserve the menu's original aspect ratio, with 16:9 or 9:16 selected according to the intended platform.

## Menu Fidelity Rules

- Treat the uploaded menu as the sole source of truth.
- Preserve the restaurant name, logo, branding, menu layout, colours, typography, dish names, prices, decorative elements, and overall visual identity exactly.
- Do not redesign, rewrite, replace, invent, duplicate, or remove menu text.
- Select only 3-4 dishes that can be confidently understood from the menu image.
- Keep the menu intact beneath and around each emerging dish.
- Do not hallucinate ingredients or turn one dish into another.

## Effect Design

### Flat Menu Element to Plated Dish

Each selected dish physically emerges from its exact printed position on the menu and transitions from a flat printed representation into a fully dimensional plated dish. The transformation must feel physical, elegant, appetizing, and believable rather than magical or explosive.

### Dish-Specific Effects

- **Hot dishes:** Realistic rising steam, subtle heat distortion, glossy sauce movement, and a freshly served appearance where appropriate.
- **Fried or crispy dishes:** Visible crisp texture, restrained oil sheen, and delicate crumb or garnish movement.
- **Cheese-based dishes:** Realistic cheese stretch or gentle melting only when the selected dish supports it.
- **Cold drinks or desserts:** Condensation, chilled surfaces, ice movement, or creamy texture. Never add hot steam to cold items.
- **Grilled or roasted dishes:** Subtle sizzling, realistic charred texture, and gentle wisps of heat or smoke only where physically appropriate.

Do not exaggerate any effect. Every dish must retain realistic restaurant proportions and remain correctly plated.
