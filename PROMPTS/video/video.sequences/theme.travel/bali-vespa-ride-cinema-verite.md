# Bali Vespa Ride — Cinéma Vérité Tracking Shot

## Concept Title

**Gritty Handheld Vespa Journey Through Central Bali Highlands**

## Style & Approach

Gritty ciné vérité, handheld tracking shot, 35mm natural film grain, humid tropical daylight, real documentary energy, subtle camera shake and operator breathing, central Bali highlands, terraced rice paddies and tea plantation rows on all sides.

## Main Subject

Shot locked on a Greek man in his mid-twenties with olive sun-tanned skin, dark curly hair, short stubble, muscular athletic build, wearing an open faded sage linen shirt over a bare chest, sand board shorts, leather sandals, thin gold chain. He rides a vintage cream-and-chrome Vespa scooter at a relaxed cruising pace, one hand on the throttle, shirt and hair moving with the wind. Rattan basket on the rear rack, open-face helmet hanging from the mirror.

## 20-Second Timeline

**0-2s: [Wide tracking]**  
He rolls out through a village stone split gate onto the open road. A woman in a sarong sets a palm-leaf offering on the wall and glances up as he passes. A rooster darts across the asphalt behind him.

**2-4s: [Tracking]**  
The road opens onto terraced rice paddies. A farmer in a conical hat straightens knee-deep in the flooded field and raises a hand. A line of white egrets lifts off the water beside the road and climbs across frame.

**4-6s: [Dynamic tracking]**  
He accelerates along the ridge between tea and coffee rows. Two women walking the shoulder with woven baskets on their heads turn to watch him go by. Wet leaves flick past the front wheel.

**6-8s: [Side tracking]**  
A pack of ducks is herded across the road ahead by an elderly farmer with a bamboo stick. He slows, drops one sandal to the tarmac, and steers around them. Dragonflies hover over the irrigation ditch.

**8-10s: [Low-angle tracking]**  
He passes a roadside warung — bananas and snake fruit hanging from the beam. Kids in school uniforms wave hard and run a few steps alongside the Vespa, laughing.

**10-12s: [Tracking]**  
A water buffalo is led across a mud track in front of him on a rope. He brakes gently, rolls wide around it. Steam drifts off the paddies, volcano silhouette soft in the haze behind.

**12-14s: [Continuous tracking]**  
He rides into a bamboo tunnel, dappled sunlight strobing hard across his face and chest. A troop of macaques sits along the moss-covered wall; one drops down onto the road behind him.

**14-16s: [Wide tracking]**  
He comes out over the open terraces. A small procession in white and yellow walks the far edge of the road, women carrying tall fruit offerings on their heads. He eases off the throttle and nods as he passes.

**16-18s: [Tracking]**  
He rides under arching bamboo penjor poles, incense smoke drifting across the road from a small stone shrine. A village dog trots off the asphalt. Egrets cross overhead in a loose line.

**18-20s: [Side tracking pulling wide]**  
He opens the throttle along the ridge, shirt flapping open, palm shadows striping the road in fast bands. Rice terraces step away below him as the camera falls back and he rides small into the frame.
