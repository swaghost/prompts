# She Went Alone - Himalayan Travel Editorial

## Concept

A 15-second vertical travel-fashion reel following a solo traveller through a Himalayan morning. The visual arc moves from mountain atmosphere to motion, stillness, camp, and a quiet waterfall ending.

## Character Lock

Use the same adult woman in every shot: early twenties, soft East Asian features, very fair warm porcelain skin with visible natural texture and pores, long dark-brown hair in one loose thick braid with flyaways, soft almond-shaped dark-brown eyes, small nose, gently rounded jaw, light tinted lip and faint mascara.

Wardrobe remains unchanged: bright pink baseball cap worn forward, white fitted cropped zip-up athletic jacket, light-grey loose wide-leg sweatpants with drawstring waist, clean white sneakers, and a sage-green hiking backpack. Her body language is candid and unposed; she never performs a fashion pose.

## Character Sheet Prompt

```text
Ultra-realistic outdoor travel photograph, Canon EOS R5, 85mm lens, f/2.8, bright natural Himalayan daylight. Full-body portrait of one adult woman in her early twenties with soft East Asian features, very fair warm porcelain skin with natural texture and visible pores, long dark-brown hair in one loose thick braid with a few flyaways, soft almond-shaped dark-brown eyes, small nose, gently rounded jaw, light tinted lip and faint mascara. She wears a bright pink baseball cap forward, white fitted cropped zip-up athletic jacket, light-grey loose wide-leg sweatpants with drawstring waist, clean white sneakers, and a sage-green hiking backpack on both shoulders. She stands naturally on a mountain trail, one hand loosely touching her braid, looking slightly upward and away from the camera. Lush green Himalayan hills, distant snow-capped peaks, blue sky with natural clouds. Full body, candid rather than posed. Photorealistic, realistic fabric texture, real skin, natural anatomy, 8K detail, no beauty smoothing, no plastic skin, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## Six-Panel Storyboard

Use the character sheet as the identity and wardrobe reference. Generate a 2 by 3 grid of vertical 9:16 panels with thin dark borders, consistent photographic realism, natural film grain, and a golden-hour-to-sunrise palette.

1. **0:00-0:02 - Prayer Flags:** Extreme close-up of colourful Himalayan prayer flags snapping in cold wind above a mist-filled valley and snow-capped peaks. Soft gold-pink sunrise; no person.
2. **0:02-0:05 - Running Free:** Side/back tracking view as she runs across a high alpine meadow. Her backpack bounces, braid streams behind, and she laughs naturally; wildflowers, blue sky, and snow peaks behind her.
3. **0:05-0:07 - The View:** Directly behind her on a rocky ridge, arms slightly open, looking across layered mountain ranges and mist in the valleys. Wind moves her jacket and braid.
4. **0:07-0:09 - Trail Walk:** Candid medium-wide side shot of her walking a rocky Spiti Valley trail in warm light, one hand adjusting the pink cap, relaxed small smile, jagged peaks behind.
5. **0:09-0:11 - Camp Morning:** View from inside a small tent; sleeping bag and tent frame in foreground. Outside, she faces away and completes a gentle morning stretch near a still mountain lake reflecting a pink dawn sky.
6. **0:11-0:15 - Waterfall:** Back view of her seated on a boulder at a powerful waterfall flowing into a turquoise pool. She raises one hand into the mist, then rests it. End without captions or burned-in text.

Preserve the same adult woman, face, skin tone, loose dark-brown braid, pink baseball cap, white cropped zip-up athletic jacket, light-grey wide-leg sweatpants, white sneakers, and sage-green hiking backpack in every shot. Canon EOS R5 documentary look, 4K, 24fps, natural film grain, authentic skin and fabric texture, real-time speed only, handheld camera with restrained natural sway. No slow motion, drone footage, stabilization, beauty filter, plastic skin, identity drift, wardrobe changes, text, captions, logos, or watermark.

```text
0:00-0:02 - PRAYER FLAGS: Extreme close-up of colourful Himalayan prayer flags snapping in strong cold wind. Snow-covered peaks behind, thick white morning mist lying in the valley, deep blue high-altitude sky and a soft gold-pink sunrise. Camera tilts slowly down to reveal the vast misty valley. No person.

0:02-0:05 - RUNNING FREE: The same woman runs naturally across a high-altitude Himalayan green meadow with wildflowers, snow-capped peaks, and bright blue sky. Full-body side/back tracking shot. 0:05-0:07 - THE VIEW: Directly behind her on a rocky Himalayan ridge. She opens her arms slightly and takes one long, visible breath while looking over endless layered mountain ranges glowing orange-pink; mist hangs in the valleys. Camera holds low and mostly still. 0:07-0:09 - TRAIL WALK: Medium-wide candid side shot. She walks a dramatic rocky Spiti Valley trail in golden-amber light, adjusts her pink cap once, and continues at a relaxed pace with a small natural smile. The camera walks alongside with gentle hand-held sway; jagged peaks remain legible behind her.

0:09-0:11 - CAMP MORNING: Camera is inside a small camping tent looking outward. Sleeping bag and tent frame remain in the foreground. Outside at a high-altitude lake, she faces away from camera and makes one slow complete morning stretch, raising both arms overhead, holding briefly, then dropping them naturally. 0:11-0:15 - WATERFALL: A powerful Himalayan waterfall falls into a turquoise-green pool. The woman sits on a boulder with her back to camera; she slowly lifts her right hand once to let the mist touch her palm, then relaxes her shoulders. Keep realistic water physics, wet rock detail, and continuous mist. Finish on the waterfall and her still silhouette, with only waterfall ambience and wind.
```

## Technical Notes

- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical
- **Camera:** Canon EOS R5 documentary treatment; handheld throughout
- **Lighting:** Cold early-morning blue shifting to gold-pink and dawn warmth
- **Sound:** Wind, fabric, footfalls, distant natural ambience, waterfall; no music required
- **Reference workflow:** Generate the character sheet first, then the six-panel storyboard, then attach both to the video generation prompt.
