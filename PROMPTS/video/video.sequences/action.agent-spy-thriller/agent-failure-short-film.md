# Agent Failure — 30-Second Short Film

**Genre:** Spy/Tech Thriller Short  
**Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9 horizontal  
**Structure:** 15 shots, hard cuts, 2 seconds each  
**Style:** Handheld character-follow, 35mm film aesthetic  
**Platform:** Video generation (Seedance or similar)

---

## Concept

A late-night office worker battles with a failing AI agent dashboard, eventually finding success on a backup machine. In desperation and triumph, he grabs the solution, crashes through the building's security, hotwires a car, and escapes into the blue-hour dusk — all without a single word of dialogue.

---

---

## Global Style Guidelines

### Visual Aesthetic

- **Film Look:** 35mm film aesthetic with soft film grain
- **Color Grade:** Muted green-teal grade throughout
- **Contrast:** Low-key contrast
- **Physics:** Realistic physics and natural human motion
- **No exaggerated poses** — every gesture weighted and believable

### Lighting Philosophy

**FACE LIGHTING RULE:** In every close-up with the face near the lens:

- Strong **NEGATIVE FILL** on the shadow side
- Single-source modeling only (monitor, window, elevator light, streetlamp, dash glow)
- Off side falling into deep clean black
- Hard cheekbone contrast
- No ambient lift

### Camera Movement

**Glued to the character — reactive handheld follow-cam with realistic operator movement:**

- Tracks his gestures
- Whips when he whips
- Hustles when he runs
- Breathes when he stills
- Micro-shake, focus hunting
- Never stabilized, never static
- **It moves BECAUSE he moves**

## Shot-by-Shot Breakdown

### [0–2s] CUT 1 — TYPING IN THE DARK

**Setup:** Handheld mid behind his shoulder  
**Action:** The only lit desk on the empty floor, he types fast, dashboard glowing, silhouette carved against the bright window. A red error banner blinks; his fingers stop dead.

---

### [2–4s] CUT 2 — SCRIBBLE

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld top-down over the desk, monitor glow the only light on the page  
**Action:** He slashes notes across a printout — arrows, crossed-out lines — camera jittering with each stroke, whipping up as his eyes dart to the screen.

---

### [4–6s] CUT 3 — THE SCREEN SAYS NO

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld push-in past his profile onto the monitor  
**Action:** Progress bar crawling, then a red ERROR stamping over the blurred chat, red flare reflecting in his eyes. Camera jolts on the ping.

---

### [6–8s] CUT 4 — PAPER CHECK

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld frontal mid, window light rimming his shoulders  
**Action:** He grips the curling printout in both hands, half his face cold blue from the screen, half in shadow, jaw grinding, loose tie swinging.

---

### [8–10s] CUT 5 — PAPERS FLY

**Setup:** Hard cut: he sweeps both arms and hurls the stack — camera whips with the arm, tilts up  
**Action:** Sheets tumbling as bright silhouettes against the teal glass, gliding down between the empty desks. His breathing echoes.

---

### [10–12s] CUT 6 — THE THROW

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld wide from the side, full silhouette against the wall of glass  
**Action:** He rips the monitor off the desk, cable tearing, and hurls it across the office — camera whip-pans with its arc to the crash of plastic skidding on carpet, then whips back: he stands chest-heaving in the wreck.

---

### [12–14s] CUT 7 — THE IDEA

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld push-in to a tight close-up, teal rim on one side, negative-fill black on the other  
**Action:** His breathing slows, eyes de-focus — then snap sharp with the thought, a small inhale. He crouches and rises with one crumpled printout.

---

### [14–16s] CUT 8 — CROSSING THE FLOOR

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld follow behind his shoulder  
**Action:** He strides between the empty desks to a neighboring workstation, camera hustling past chairs, his silhouette cutting across the glowing windows. He drops into the mesh chair; it rolls and creaks.

---

### [16–18s] CUT 9 — SECOND MACHINE

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld top-down  
**Action:** He flattens the crumpled sheet by the keyboard, taps a key — the dark monitor blinks awake, cold blue flooding the page and his hands — and he types in fast deliberate bursts, camera bobbing with the rhythm.

---

### [18–20s] CUT 10 — GREEN

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld close on his face, screen glow the single source, negative fill crushing the far side  
**Action:** The progress bar completes and the dashboard flashes a soft green banner — one bright chime across the silent floor, the light on his face turning from blue to green. His tired eyes widen, then crease into the first real smile.

---

### [20–22s] CUT 11 — GRAB AND GO

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld mid  
**Action:** He snatches the printout, folds it into his shirt pocket, grabs a dark jacket off a chair back and strides off fast — camera whips with the spin and chases; the green dashboard keeps glowing behind him in the dark.

---

### [22–24s] CUT 12 — ELEVATOR

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld swing as he jabs the call button in the dim corridor; ding — steel doors open, cold light spilling  
**Action:** Quick beat inside: tight close-up, single top-light raking one side of his face, negative-fill black on the other, a grin cracking as the doors slide shut on his eyes.

---

### [24–26s] CUT 13 — THE WINDOW

**Setup:** Hard cut: blue-hour street, handheld tight at the sedan's driver door  
**Action:** One sharp jacket-wrapped elbow strike — the side window crazes and collapses in a shower of glittering cubes under the sodium lamp. Camera jolts with the impact; a half-second alarm chirp dies. He reaches in and pops the lock.

---

### [26–28s] CUT 14 — HOTWIRE

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld over his shoulder into the dark footwell  
**Action:** Hands wrench the panel loose and work in shadow — a white-blue SPARK snaps, lighting his clenched jaw for a single frame — the engine coughs, dies, then catches and roars; dash lights flood on, warm glow sculpting one side of his face against negative-fill black, glass cubes glittering on his shoulder.

---

### [28–30s] CUT 15 — GONE

**Setup:** Hard cut: handheld wide from the pavement  
**Action:** The sedan lurches out with a tire chirp and rolls off fast, taillights smearing red into the teal dusk, the dark office tower rising behind with one faint green screen-glow in a window. Camera drifts a step after it. Freeze as the taillights shrink.
