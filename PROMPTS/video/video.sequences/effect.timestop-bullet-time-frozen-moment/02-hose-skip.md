## Concept

Near-frozen time jump with garden hose. Slim young man with coppery red hair mid-jump above lawn, holding mint green garden hose with water jetting from open end. 180-degree orbit from front to side. Handheld with near-frozen drift.

**Style:** Youth fashion editorial, cool-toned suburban

## IMAGE PROMPT

Ultra high definition hyper-realistic photograph, 4K clarity. Medium shot from a low angle, the subject filling roughly two thirds of the frame height. A slim young man with chin-length coppery red hair blown upward, wearing slim silver wraparound sunglasses, a beaded necklace and an oversized marigold yellow t-shirt soaked with dark water splatter, loose grey-blue nylon shorts layered over navy trousers and tan suede hiking sneakers, frozen at the top of a jump roughly one metre above a green lawn, knees tucked to his chest. He is holding a pale mint green garden hose. His raised hand grips the hose at shoulder height on his right, where the hose runs upward and out of the top of the frame toward an unseen tap. His lowered hand, held down near his hip on the left, grips the free cut open end of the hose, and a broad fan of water is jetting directly out of that open end. Between his two hands the slack length of hose sweeps in a wide loose curve down past and beneath his tucked feet, a hanging arc of hose rather than a closed circle. Surrounding him, the water thrown from the open hose end breaks into at least two hundred individual droplets, beads, ropes and fine mist suspended mid-air, densest immediately at the pipe mouth and spreading out to the left and downward, the nearest droplets within twenty centimetres of the lens showing internal refraction and the inverted hedge reflected inside them. Environment is a suburban garden, a dense flat-topped dark green hedge running across the background about four metres behind him, mown lawn below, an even pale blue-grey overcast sky above. Soft directionless daylight, cool tone.

SKIN AND MATERIAL DETAIL: visible skin pores, fine pale arm hair, freckles across the nose and cheekbones, real subsurface scattering in the earlobes and fingertips, water beading on the forearms with individual specular highlights, wet cotton clinging with visible weave and darkened saturation lines, individual strands separating in the airborne hair, hose showing soft rubber sheen and a visibly hollow open bore at the held end.

CAPTURE: shot on a medium format digital camera, 50mm lens from just below waist height roughly four metres from the subject, f/6.3, shutter 1/8000, subject and near droplets in crisp focus, hedge falling gently soft.

FINISH: absolutely clean digital output. Zero grain. Zero noise. Zero film texture. No halation, no bloom, no vignette, no colour fade, no retro grade. Clean cool colour, true skin tone. Tack sharp. Native 4K resolution, commercial retouch finish. Real photograph of a real person, not a 3D render, not CGI, not illustration, not painting. No motion blur on any element. No text, logos, graphics on clothing or watermarks anywhere in frame.

## VIDEO PROMPT

NEAR-FROZEN TIME - SEEDANCE 2.5 - 1080p - 8s - ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT - NO CUTS

FRAME ONE: the video opens on the attached image exactly as it is - identical framing, identical pose, identical light, identical colour. Hold that frame completely still for the first 0.3 seconds, then let motion begin from it. The first frame of the video and the attached image must be indistinguishable.

TIME STATE: time is running at roughly one twentieth of normal speed. Everything moves, but barely - a slow rhythmic drift, dreamlike and continuous, never speeding up. No element completes its action within the 8 seconds. He never lands.

SUBJECT MOTION: the red-haired jumper hangs at the top of his jump and sinks only a few centimetres across the whole shot, knees staying tucked. His hair drifts slowly, strand by strand. His grip hands stay closed on the hose, fingers flexing minutely. The wet fabric of his t-shirt ripples slowly against his chest.

PROP MOTION: the hose slack sways gently, the hanging curve below his feet undulating a few centimetres like a slow rope in water, always staying a single length with one free open end in his lowered hand. Water continues to leave the open pipe mouth in extreme slow motion, the fan advancing only slightly outward over the 8 seconds. Individual droplets creep along their arcs, rotating and wobbling as separate beads, some elongating faintly.

CAMERA MAP - slow 180 degree orbit, front to side:

0.0-2.0s - Camera holds low at the original frontal angle looking slightly up, then pushes gently in, passing through the nearest droplets.

2.0-6.0s - Continuous smooth 180 degree arc to the right, rising to his eye level, travelling from directly in front around to full profile. The hose curve rotates through perspective revealing its true slack and the open end in his hand.

6.0-8.0s - Camera holds the side angle and eases back into a wider frame carrying the full hose length and water fan, decelerating into a soft settle.

CAMERA FEEL: a subtle handheld layer rides on top of the whole move - gentle micro-drift, a faint breathing bob, tiny weight shifts as if a real operator is walking the arc. Very low amplitude. Organic and alive, never shaky, never jittery, never a whip or a snap.

LIGHT: unchanged throughout. Clean daylight, shadows staying where they are, refractive highlights inside each droplet shifting only as the camera moves.

SOUND: heavily slowed diegetic bed - a deep pitched-down water hiss stretched almost to a drone, faint low garden air. No splash, no landing. Cool crystalline high shimmer. Slow rising tone from 6.0s settling at 8.0s.

VIBE: cool-toned fashion campaign suspended in dream-time. Anamorphic 1080p, shallow depth of field, clean digital finish, no grain.

QUALITY: no morphing, no warping, no melting, no glitching, no flicker. Face and sunglasses stay the same at every distance. Limbs and fingers stay correctly counted. The hose stays one continuous length with one free open end - it must never close into a ring, join to itself, duplicate, snake or re-route, and the water must keep leaving that open end and nowhere else. Droplets do not multiply, disappear or accelerate. Motion stays slow and even at all times, with no sudden acceleration and no snap back to normal speed. No new people enter frame. Every frame survives a freeze-frame.
