# Driveway Wipeout — Frozen Time

**SCENE 03 / SUBURBAN ABSURDISM / NEAR-FROZEN DRIFT**

## Concept

Suburban driveway wipeout with tennis equipment explosion. Athletic woman falling backwards surrounded by four tennis rackets, dozen yellow tennis balls, and pink/blue streamers. Wide reveal orbit. Handheld near-frozen drift through absurd moment.

**Style:** Suburban absurdism, bright midday  
**Camera:** Wide reveal orbit, handheld  
**Time:** Near-frozen drift (1/20th speed)

---

## Workflow

1. Generate the still with IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2 or Seedream 5.0 Pro)

3. Run VIDEO PROMPT exactly as written
4. Export at full resolution, trim to strongest stretch

## IMAGE PROMPT

Ultra high definition hyper-realistic photograph, 4K clarity. An athletic woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a fitted black sleeveless sports dress and bright red low-top sneakers, frozen mid-fall backwards across a pale concrete suburban driveway, body twisted at forty-five degrees with one shoulder dropping to within thirty centimetres of the ground, one leg kicked high, both arms flung upward, face caught between shock and laughter. Surrounding her, four tennis rackets tumbling at different rotations, at least a dozen yellow tennis balls at completely different heights and distances showing felt nap and seam stitching, and long ribbons of pink and pale blue streamer frozen in wide continuous curling loops through the air around her body. Environment is a bright suburban front yard, a white timber picket fence about six metres behind her showing real paint grain, a white clapboard two-storey house with a grey shingled roof and dark window frames beyond it, a trimmed hedge to the left. Hard clean midday sunlight from above, crisp short shadows on the concrete.

SKIN AND MATERIAL DETAIL: visible skin pores, fine arm hair, real subsurface scattering in the ears and fingers, faint sweat sheen on the collarbone and forehead with individual speculars, muscle tension visible in the raised arm and neck, technical sportswear fabric showing knit structure and stretch, sneaker canvas showing weave and stitch.

CAPTURE: shot on a medium format digital camera, 28mm lens from a low angle roughly two metres from the subject, f/8, shutter 1/8000, everything from the near ribbons to the house in crisp focus.

FINISH: absolutely clean digital output. Zero grain. Zero noise. Zero film texture. No halation, no bloom, no vignette, no colour fade, no retro grade. Clean saturated daylight colour. Tack sharp edge to edge. Native 4K resolution, commercial retouch finish. Real photograph of a real person, not a 3D render, not CGI, not illustration, not painting. No motion blur on any element. No text, logos, brand marks or watermarks anywhere in frame.

## VIDEO PROMPT

NEAR-FROZEN TIME - SEEDANCE 2.5 - 1080p - 8s - ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT - NO CUTS

FRAME ONE: the video opens on the attached image exactly as it is - identical framing, identical pose, identical light, identical colour. Hold that frame completely still for the first 0.3 seconds, then let motion begin from it. The first frame of the video and the attached image must be indistinguishable.

TIME STATE: time is running at roughly one twentieth of normal speed. Everything moves, but barely - a slow rhythmic drift, dreamlike and continuous, never speeding up. No element completes its action within the 8 seconds. She never reaches the ground, no ball ever bounces.

SUBJECT MOTION: the woman continues her backward fall in extreme slow motion, her dropping shoulder sinking only a few centimetres over the whole shot. Her raised leg drifts fractionally, her flung-out fingers spread slightly wider, her expression holds between shock and laughter with the smallest shift. Loose hair strands drift. She never lands.

PROP MOTION: the four tennis rackets each rotate slowly on their own separate axes, ten to fifteen degrees across the shot, drifting a few centimetres. The yellow tennis balls creep along their arcs at their own individual speeds, each spinning gently, the count staying constant. The pink and blue ribbon streamers undulate slowly along their whole length like ribbons in still water, keeping their loops.

CAMERA MAP - slow wide orbit revealing depth:

0.0-2.0s - Camera holds the original low wide angle, then glides right at a steady distance, one racket passing across the top of frame.

2.0-5.5s - Continuous wide smooth arc from front to right side, rising slowly to standing height, staying back rather than closing in. Parallax between the drifting debris field and the fixed picket fence and house behind.

5.5-8.0s - Camera cranes up into a slightly high three-quarter angle looking down across the whole field, house and fence still in frame, decelerating into a soft settle.

CAMERA FEEL: a subtle handheld layer rides on top of the whole move - gentle micro-drift, a faint breathing bob, tiny weight shifts as if a real operator is walking the arc. Very low amplitude. Organic and alive, never shaky, never jittery, never a whip or a snap.

LIGHT: unchanged throughout. Hard clean overhead sunlight, shadows staying where they are, highlights on the ribbons and balls shifting only as the camera moves.

SOUND: heavily slowed diegetic bed - a deep pitched-down suburban air-tone, a long stretched low swell. No clatter, no bounce, no impact. Slow rising tone from 5.5s settling at 8.0s.

VIBE: high-gloss suburban absurdism suspended in dream-time. Anamorphic 1080p, shallow depth of field, clean saturated daylight colour, clean digital finish, no grain.

QUALITY: no morphing, no warping, no melting, no glitching, no flicker. Face stays the same face at every distance. Limbs and fingers stay correctly counted, both red sneakers stay on. Ribbons stay continuous unbroken strips and do not writhe violently, thicken or re-route. Ball and racket counts stay constant - nothing multiplies, disappears or transforms. Motion stays slow and even at all times, with no sudden acceleration and no snap back to normal speed. The house, fence and hedge stay effectively still. No new people enter frame. Every frame survives a freeze-frame.
