# Overhead Fall — Frozen Time

**SCENE 04 / VERTIGO EDITORIAL / SLOW MOTION FALL**

---

## Platform Chain

**GPT Image 2 or Seedream 5.0 Pro** → **Seedance 2.5** (1080p, 8 seconds)

---

## Concept

Extreme overhead vertigo shot. Man falling through open air 15 metres above city street, shot from high above looking straight down. Boot sole enormous in foreground, loose objects suspended between him and street below. Descending vertical sweep following the fall. Dizzying scale and depth.

**Style:** Vertigo editorial, extreme depth  
**Camera:** Descending vertical sweep, falling with subject  
**Time:** Slow motion fall (1/20th speed)

---

## Workflow

1. Generate the still with IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2 or Seedream 5.0 Pro)
2. Load as START FRAME in Seedance 2.5 (not as reference)
3. Run VIDEO PROMPT exactly as written
4. Export at full resolution, trim to strongest stretch

**CRITICAL:** Load the image as the start frame, NOT as a reference. Frame one must be your still.

Ultra high definition hyper-realistic photograph, 4K clarity, shot from a high overhead angle looking straight down. ## VIDEO PROMPT

SLOW-MOTION FALL - SEEDANCE 2.5 - 1080p - 8s - ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT - NO CUTS

FRAME ONE: the video opens on the attached image exactly as it is - identical framing, identical overhead angle, identical pose, identical light, identical colour. Hold that frame completely still for the first 0.3 seconds, then let the fall begin from it. The first frame of the video and the attached image must be indistinguishable.

TIME STATE: extreme slow motion, roughly one twentieth of normal speed. Everything in the frame is genuinely falling - the man and every loose object are descending together toward the street far below - but at a dreamlike crawl. The fall is continuous and never speeds up. He does not reach the ground within the 8 seconds; the street stays far beneath him at the end.

SUBJECT MOTION: the man falls slowly and steadily downward through open air, descending in a smooth even glide across the whole shot with no jerk and no acceleration. As he falls he rotates gently on his own axis, perhaps fifteen degrees over the 8 seconds, his back tipping a little further and his bent knees drifting slowly. His raised hand stays reaching up toward the camera, fingers flexing minutely and spreading a fraction wider. His open-mouthed shout holds throughout. His white t-shirt ripples slowly upward against the rising air, the hem lifting and settling, and his jeans flutter faintly at the ankles. The lifted boot stays kicked toward the lens.

PROP MOTION: the phone, loose coins, sheets of paper, keys and second pair of sunglasses all fall with him, each descending at its own slightly different speed and tumbling slowly on its own separate axis. The heavier items - phone, keys, coins - sink slightly faster and pull gradually below him. The papers fall slowest, drifting and turning as they sink, tilting from flat to edge-on. The whole loose cluster stays in the air column between him and the road, spreading apart slowly as it descends.

CAMERA MAP - slow descending sweep, falling with him:

0.0-2.0s - Camera holds the original steep overhead angle directly above him, then begins to descend, falling at close to his own rate so he stays roughly centred in frame. A drifting coin and a turning page pass close to the lens.

2.0-5.5s - Camera continues descending with him and arcs slowly around his body, the angle tilting from straight-down toward a raking diagonal. He holds centre while the street grid, parked cars and trees far below rotate and slide underneath, the ground creeping gradually closer and larger.

5.5-8.0s - Camera drops below his feet and swings up to look back past the boot sole toward the sky, still descending alongside him, decelerating into a soft hold on the final frame.

CAMERA FEEL: every camera movement is slow and weighted, matching the pace of the fall - no fast moves anywhere in the shot. A subtle handheld layer rides on top of it, gentle micro-drift, a faint breathing bob, tiny weight shifts as if a real operator is falling alongside him. Very low amplitude. Organic and alive, never shaky, never jittery, never a whip or a snap.

LIGHT: unchanged throughout. Hard afternoon sun from above, the shadows on the road far below staying in place, speculars on metal, glass and the sunglass lenses shifting only as the camera and the falling objects move.

SOUND: heavily slowed diegetic bed - a deep pitched-down wind rush stretched into a hollow drone, faint stretched traffic far below, a low sub pulse under the descent. No shout, no impact, no landing. Slow rising tone from 5.5s settling at 8.0s.

VIBE: vertigo-inducing editorial realism in dream-time, extreme depth, dizzying scale, the ground always waiting. Anamorphic 1080p, deep focus holding both the man and the street, real sun flare, clean digital finish, no grain.

QUALITY: no morphing, no warping, no melting, no glitching, no flicker. Face and outstretched hand stay the same at every distance, five fingers only, no extra fingers appearing as the hand flexes. Limbs stay correctly counted and both boots stay on. Cars, vans and pedestrians on the street below stay effectively still. The falling objects stay the same objects throughout - nothing multiplies, disappears, teleports or transforms into something else. The descent stays smooth and even at all times, with no sudden plummet, no acceleration and no snap back to normal speed. He never lands, never flips upright and never leaves frame. No new people or vehicles enter frame. Every frame survives a freeze-frame.
