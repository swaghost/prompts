# Bullet Time Jump Effect Guide

## Overview

**Effect Name**: Bullet Time Jump  
**Tagline**: Freeze yourself mid air and orbit the moment.  
**Platform**: Higgsfield Seedance 2.0  
**Creator**: Learn with Kayo

Create a dramatic frozen-in-air effect where the camera orbits around you while you're suspended mid-jump and the entire world is frozen in time.

---

## Step 01: Your Footage

_The frame everything hangs on_

### Filming Instructions

1. **Film yourself jumping**
   - Shoot a few takes so you have one where both feet are clearly off the ground
   - Ensure good framing and clear peak of jump

2. **Screenshot the peak**
   - Open the video in your gallery
   - Screenshot the exact peak of the jump (both feet clearly off ground)

3. **⚠️ CRITICAL: Remember the frame**
   - Remember **exactly where that frame sits** in your video
   - You will cut your video on it later
   - **It has to be the same one**

---

## Step 02: Seedance 2.0 Setup

_Set it up properly_

### Platform Instructions

1. Go to **higgsfield.ai** and open **Create Video**
2. Select **Seedance 2.0** as the model
3. Upload your screenshot as the reference image
4. Paste the prompt from Step 03

### Settings

- **Duration**: 4 seconds
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16
- **Resolution**: 1080p or 4K

---

## Step 03: The Prompt

_Paste this in_

### Complete Prompt Text

```
SCENE CONTEXT
Time is frozen on the street in the attached first frame. The camera
orbits the airborne man while the world holds still.

CAMERA
One continuous 4-second orbit. The camera circles the man to the right
at constant 1.1 m height and constant 4 m distance, covering a full 90
degrees at one steady rate of 22.5 degrees per second, the same speed
in the first frame as in the last. He stays centered throughout. Wide
63 degree FOV, held, no drift. Focus locked on his torso.

ACTION
The camera move is the only motion in the frame. The man hangs in the
air with his joints, limbs, head and hands holding the exact pose of
the first frame. Buildings, vehicles, screens, sky and shadows all hold
that same instant as solid geometry. Parallax between near and far
objects is the only thing that changes as the viewpoint travels.

PHYSICS
A steady 25 km/h wind crosses from camera-right to camera-left and acts
on his clothing only. The open jacket panels lift and ripple away from
his torso within a 12 cm range, two to three soft cycles across the
four seconds. Trouser fabric ripples at the calves within a 3 cm range.
The cap stays firmly seated.

AUDIO
Near silence, one sustained low sub-bass tone and a steady low airflow
hiss.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The camera completes a full 90 degrees of orbit across the four seconds.
His joints, limbs and head hold completely still. Only his clothing
moves.
His feet stay the same distance above the crosswalk in every frame.
Vehicles, pedestrians, screens and sky hold one single instant
throughout.
```

### Prompt Breakdown

**Scene Context**

- Time frozen on the street
- Camera orbits airborne subject
- World holds completely still

**Camera Specifications**

- Duration: 4-second continuous orbit
- Direction: Circles subject to the right
- Height: Constant 1.1 m
- Distance: Constant 4 m from subject
- Arc: Full 90 degrees
- Speed: Steady 22.5 degrees per second (constant throughout)
- FOV: Wide 63 degrees
- Focus: Locked on torso
- Subject stays centered throughout

**Action Rules**

- Camera move is **only motion** in frame
- Subject hangs in air frozen
- Joints, limbs, head, hands: Exact pose from first frame
- Buildings, vehicles, screens, sky, shadows: All hold as solid geometry
- Only parallax changes as viewpoint travels

**Physics (Clothing Movement)**

- Wind: Steady 25 km/h from camera-right to camera-left
- Acts on clothing only
- Jacket panels: Lift and ripple 12 cm range, 2-3 soft cycles
- Trouser fabric: Ripples at calves, 3 cm range
- Cap: Stays firmly seated

**Audio**

- Near silence
- One sustained low sub-bass tone
- Steady low airflow hiss

**Positive Locks (Critical Constraints)**

- ✓ Camera completes full 90 degrees across 4 seconds
- ✓ Joints, limbs, head hold completely still
- ✓ Only clothing moves
- ✓ Feet stay same distance above ground in every frame
- ✓ Vehicles, pedestrians, screens, sky hold one single instant

## Step 04: The Edit

_Where it becomes an effect_

### Editing Workflow

1. **Open both videos** in any editing app
   - Your original jump video
   - The Higgsfield generated video

2. **Cut your original clip**
   - Cut right where you screenshotted
   - The Higgsfield clip starts right after this cut point

3. **Duplicate and reverse**
   - Duplicate the Higgsfield clip
   - Reverse the copy

4. **Let original continue**
   - Let your original video run on from the cut
   - You land normally after the frozen moment

### Final Timeline Structure

```
[Original jump → takeoff] → [Higgsfield orbit] → [Higgsfield orbit REVERSED] → [Original jump → landing]
```

### Sell It Further

**Optional enhancements** (worth playing with once the cut is working):

- Speed curves across transitions
- Touch of motion blur across transitions
- These push the effect a long way

## Troubleshooting

### The image will not upload

**Problem**: Upload fails or is rejected  
**Cause**: Brand signage and recognizable faces inside your photo  
**Solution**: Blur them or paint them out, then try again

### The camera refuses to orbit

**Problem**: Camera doesn't move or orbits incorrectly  
**Cause**: Too much freeze language buries the move  
**Solution**: Keep the camera paragraph short and near the top, and the orbit comes back

### Your body drifts mid shot

**Problem**: Subject position shifts during the orbit  
**Cause**: AI generation variance  
**Solution**: Generate four or five times and keep the steadiest one. Any drift doubles once you reverse the clip, so it is worth being fussy here.

## Key Success Factors

### Critical Elements

1. **Exact Frame Matching**
   - Screenshot and cut point must be identical frame
   - This is what makes the effect seamless

2. **Clean Jump**
   - Both feet clearly off ground
   - Good peak of jump captured

3. **Camera Paragraph Position**
   - Keep camera specifications early in prompt
   - Don't bury under too much freeze language

4. **Generation Quality**
   - Generate multiple times
   - Select steadiest version (no drift)
   - Remember drift doubles when reversed

5. **Edit Precision**
   - Cut exactly on the screenshotted frame
   - Smooth transitions with speed curves and motion blur
