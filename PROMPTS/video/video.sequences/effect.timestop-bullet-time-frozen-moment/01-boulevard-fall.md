## VIDEO PROMPT

ULTRA SLOW MOTION FREE FALL - SEEDANCE 2.5 - 1080p - 8s - ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT - NO CUTS

FIRST FRAME - MANDATORY: the uploaded image IS the first frame of this video. Frame one must be the uploaded image, pixel for pixel - same framing, same crop, same pose, same wardrobe, same cobblestones, same buildings, same lamp post, same light, same colour grade. The video begins from that exact image and from nothing else. Do not re-compose it, do not re-frame it, do not re-interpret it, do not generate a new opening shot. Hold the uploaded image still for the first 0.3 seconds, then let the fall begin from it.

TIME STATE: extreme slow motion, roughly one twentieth of normal speed. Gravity is completely real - only time is slowed. Everything moves with full natural physics, nothing is frozen and nothing hangs unnaturally in the air, but it all unfolds at a dreamlike crawl. Motion is continuous and even from start to finish, never speeding up, never snapping to normal speed.

THE FALL - THE CORE ACTION: from 0.3s the woman and every one of her belongings are in genuine free fall, dropping toward the cobblestones under real gravity, all of it in ultra slow motion. The downward pull is the driving force of the entire shot and it never stops. She travels a real and visible distance closer to the stones across the 8 seconds. The ground is always beneath her and always getting nearer. At 8.0s she is still falling, closer to the cobbles than she was at the start but not yet landed.

HER MOTION: the woman in the oversized camel wool overcoat and wide trousers falls backwards in ultra slow motion, her whole body dropping steadily toward the street, her buckled knee giving further by degrees as she goes down. Her raised heel drifts, her raised arm lowers a few centimetres and her spread fingers curl slightly, her flung-out arm swings back very slowly for balance. Her mouth stays open in the yelp, the expression evolving faintly rather than resetting. Her dark hair lifts and streams upward against the rising air as she drops. The heavy coat billows slowly, the hem and loose belt ends lifting and undulating with real wool weight as she falls through them.

HER BELONGINGS - ALL FALLING WITH HER: the upside-down handbag free-falls alongside her, turning slowly as it drops, maybe fifteen degrees across the shot, its handle swinging lazily. The spilled lipstick, compact, glass bottle, keys, folded card and coins are all falling too, every one of them dropping toward the cobblestones in ultra slow motion, each tumbling gently on its own axis. The heavier items - keys, coins, the glass bottle - pull downward slightly faster and sink below the lighter ones, so the spilling column stretches out and separates as the whole cluster descends. Nothing hovers, nothing hangs still, nothing lands.

CAMERA - CONTINUOUS ORBIT AROUND HER, SHOT HANDHELD: the camera travels through space around her without stopping for the entire 8 seconds. It is a real orbit - the camera physically circles her body, changing angle and revealing new sides of the scene the whole way, descending gently with her as she falls. It never parks, never holds a fixed position, never simply shakes in place.

0.3-2.5s - Camera begins close on her right side at low chest height and walks forward and around toward her front, the background buildings sliding steadily behind her as the angle changes.

2.5-5.5s - Camera keeps circling leftward past her front and on toward her far side, dropping with her as she falls, the handbag and the descending column passing through frame as the perspective on them opens up.

5.5-8.0s - Camera continues around to a front three-quarter angle, still travelling and still descending, pushing gradually closer as it goes, and decelerates only in the last half second into the final frame.

CAMERA FEEL: the entire orbit is shot handheld by an operator walking the circle with a heavy camera on the shoulder. The path is smooth and continuous; the texture is rough. Real footsteps put a visible bounce in the frame, the horizon tips and corrects, the operator drifts slightly wide then reframes, and there are small jolts and micro-corrections all the way around. Harsh, physical, documentary-rough - but always still orbiting, always still moving around her. The camera moves at full real-world speed while everything inside the frame falls at one twentieth speed. She and the falling objects never leave frame and never become unreadable.

LIGHT: unchanged throughout. Warm low afternoon sun raking from the right, the street's own shadows staying where they are, speculars on the leather, the bag and the heel shifting as objects turn and the camera travels.

SOUND: heavily slowed diegetic bed - a deep pitched-down street rumble, a long stretched low tone under the fall, the faintest slowed wind rising across the coat as she drops. No impact, no clatter, no dialogue. A slow rising tone from 5.5s settling at 8.0s.

VIBE: golden-hour luxury street campaign filmed like a news crew circling inside it - travelling camera, liquid time, everything falling. Anamorphic 1080p, shallow depth of field, real sun flare, clean digital finish, no grain.

QUALITY: no morphing, no warping, no melting, no glitching, no flicker. Her face stays the same face throughout and never re-renders, even during the roughest handheld bounce. Limbs and fingers stay correctly counted and anatomically stable while they move, both shoes stay on. Every belonging stays the same object - nothing multiplies, disappears, teleports or transforms mid-fall. The fall stays slow and even at all times, with no sudden plummet, no acceleration, no flailing and no snap back to normal speed. She never lands, never rights herself and never leaves frame. Distant pedestrians, bicycles and awnings move at the same ultra slow rate. No new people or vehicles enter frame. Every frame survives a freeze-frame.
