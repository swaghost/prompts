# Paper Flight — Frozen Time

**SCENE 05 / PASTEL ABSURDISM / NEAR-FROZEN DRIFT**

## Platform Chain

**GPT Image 2 or Seedream 5.0 Pro** → **Seedance 2.5** (1080p, 8 seconds)

## Concept

Horizontal flying man with paper scatter. Man in his forties with black-framed glasses frozen fully airborne and perfectly horizontal above concrete forecourt, surrounded by forty individual sheets of white typed paper mid-scatter. Flat pale pink stucco wall background. Lateral orbit travelling front-left to right. Near-frozen drift through surreal office moment.

**Style:** Pastel absurdism, clean architecture  
**Camera:** Lateral orbit, front-left to right, handheld  
**Time:** Near-frozen drift (1/20th speed)

---

## Workflow

1. Generate the still with IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2 or Seedream 5.0 Pro)
2. Load as START FRAME in Seedance 2.5 (not as reference)
3. Run VIDEO PROMPT exactly as written
4. Export at full resolution, trim to strongest stretch

**CRITICAL:** Load the image as the start frame, NOT as a reference. Frame one must be your still.

## IMAGE PROMPT

Ultra high definition hyper-realistic photograph, 4K clarity. Medium shot, camera close to the subject, his body filling most of the frame width. A man in his forties with short dark hair and heavy black-framed glasses, frozen fully airborne and perfectly horizontal about one metre above a bare concrete forecourt, body stretched flat like a swimmer mid-lunge, mouth open in an expression of startled surprise, wearing a black and mustard horizontally striped fine-knit sweater, black and white checked slim trousers and polished black leather shoes, one arm thrown forward with fingers splayed and one leg kicked up behind him. Immediately around his outstretched hand, at least forty individual sheets of white typed paper frozen mid-scatter in a tight wide arc, each page at a different height, curl, rotation and distance, several within thirty centimetres of the lens showing the paper fibre texture and printed line spacing, others a metre or two back. Environment is a flat pale pink stucco wall filling the frame behind him, its surface showing real trowel texture and fine grit, with a narrow strip of sunlit street corner visible at the right edge, a terracotta brown building with an arched window, a parked white pickup truck, a yellow steel bollard. Hard afternoon sunlight raking from the right, long crisp shadows across the concrete.

SKIN AND MATERIAL DETAIL: visible skin pores, stubble shadow along the jaw, fine lines around the open mouth and eyes, real subsurface scattering in the ear rim, faint reflection and shadow cast by the glasses frame onto the cheek, knit sweater showing individual yarn loops and stripe edge irregularity, trouser check fabric showing weave.

CAPTURE: shot on a medium format digital camera, 40mm lens at chest height roughly three metres from the subject, f/7.1, shutter 1/8000, natural perspective, subject and nearest pages in crisp focus with the street corner falling gently soft.

FINISH: absolutely clean digital output. Zero grain. Zero noise. Zero film texture. No halation, no bloom, no vignette, no faded pastel grade, no blown highlights, no retro colour treatment. Colours clean and true, pink wall reading as a real painted surface. Tack sharp. Native 4K resolution, commercial retouch finish. Real photograph of a real person, not a 3D render, not CGI, not illustration, not painting. No motion blur on any element. No text, logos, signage or watermarks anywhere in frame.

## VIDEO PROMPT

NEAR-FROZEN TIME - SEEDANCE 2.5 - 1080p - 8s - ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT - NO CUTS

FRAME ONE: the video opens on the attached image exactly as it is - identical framing, identical pose, identical light, identical colour. Hold that frame completely still for the first 0.3 seconds, then let motion begin from it. The first frame of the video and the attached image must be indistinguishable.

TIME STATE: time is running at roughly one twentieth of normal speed. Everything moves, but barely - a slow rhythmic drift, dreamlike and continuous, never speeding up. No element completes its action within the 8 seconds. He never lands and never drops.

SUBJECT MOTION: the flying man drifts forward a few centimetres across the whole shot, staying horizontal. His splayed fingers flex minutely, his kicked-up leg drifts fractionally, his startled expression holds. The knit of his striped sweater ripples slowly along the ribs and his checked trouser fabric flutters faintly at the ankle.

PROP MOTION: the scattered white pages each rotate slowly on their own separate axes and drift outward at their own individual speeds, some tilting flat to the lens and some turning edge-on, corners curling very slightly. They stay a loose constant-count field ahead of his hand and never flutter fast, never flock together and never blow away.

CAMERA MAP - lateral orbit, front-left travelling to the right:

0.0-2.0s - Camera holds the original waist-height angle on his front-left, then tracks laterally right at a steady speed, threading between the two nearest pages.

2.0-5.5s - Continuous lateral travel becoming a slow arc around him, rising slightly. Maximum parallax: near pages move across the flat pink wall behind, the wall sliding steadily and separating in depth from the paper field, the sunlit street corner opening at the edge.

5.5-8.0s - Camera continues right and eases back into a wider frame holding the man, the full paper arc and the wall together, decelerating into a soft settle.

CAMERA FEEL: a subtle handheld layer rides on top of the whole move - gentle micro-drift, a faint breathing bob, tiny weight shifts as if a real operator is walking the track. Very low amplitude. Organic and alive, never shaky, never jittery, never a whip or a snap.

LIGHT: unchanged throughout. Hard sunlight raking across the scene, shadows on the concrete staying where they are, the pink wall staying evenly lit.

SOUND: heavily slowed diegetic bed - a deep pitched-down air tone, the faintest stretched paper rustle almost below hearing, distant slowed street. No impact. Single soft rising tone from 5.5s resolving at 8.0s.

VIBE: award-show advertising photography suspended in dream-time. Anamorphic 1080p, shallow depth of field, clean true colour, clean digital finish, no grain.

QUALITY: no morphing, no warping, no melting, no glitching, no flicker. Face and glasses stay the same at every distance. Limbs and fingers stay correctly counted. Page count stays constant - pages do not multiply, disappear, tear, bend sharply or turn into other objects, and the printed text on them never becomes legible or re-renders. The pink wall stays a flat even plane with no rippling or texture crawl. Motion stays slow and even at all times, with no sudden acceleration and no snap back to normal speed. No new people or vehicles enter frame. Every frame survives a freeze-frame.
