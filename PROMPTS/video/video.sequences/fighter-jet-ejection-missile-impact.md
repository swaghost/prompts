# Fighter Jet Ejection — Missile Impact

**Format:** 5-8 seconds, extreme slow-motion  
**Type:** Cinematic military action sequence  
**Aesthetic:** Hollywood blockbuster, photorealistic destruction  
**Style:** 35mm cinema, volumetric effects, dramatic slow-motion

---

## CONCEPT

Wide-angle pull-out camera captures pilot ejecting from fighter jet cockpit in extreme slow-motion. One second after ejection, missile slams into jet creating massive cinematic explosion with volumetric fire, smoke trails, and metal debris.

---

## VISUAL STYLE

### Cinematography:

- **Wide-angle lens** (captures full action scope)
- **Pull-out camera movement** (reveals scale, creates depth)
- **Extreme slow-motion** (every detail visible)
- **35mm Hollywood action movie style**
- Professional cinema camera look
- Dramatic composition

### Visual Quality:

- **Photorealistic** (believable military hardware)
- **Volumetric fire** (three-dimensional flames)
- **Volumetric smoke trails** (realistic atmospheric effects)
- High-detail metal debris
- Realistic physics at slow speed
- Premium VFX quality

---

## SHOT SEQUENCE

### Beat 1: 0-3 seconds

**EJECTION SEQUENCE**

**Pilot Ejection:**

- Pilot ejects from cockpit
- Ejection seat fires upward
- **Burst of sparks** from ejection mechanism
- **Canopy flies off** (explosive bolts)
- Canopy tumbling through air
- Pilot and seat separating from aircraft

**Visual Details:**

- Ejection sparks bright and visible
- Canopy glass catching light as it spins
- Pilot's body rigid in ejection seat
- G-force effects visible (suit movement, helmet)
- Smoke trail from ejection rocket
- Aircraft continuing forward momentum

**Camera Action:**

- **Wide-angle framing** (captures jet, pilot, incoming threat)
- **Pull-out movement** begins (camera moving back/away)
- Reveals scale of action
- Creates cinematic depth
- Extreme slow-motion throughout

---

### Beat 2: 3 seconds (The Impact Moment)

**ONE SECOND AFTER EJECTION**

**Missile Strike:**

- Missile **slams directly into the jet**
- Impact at cockpit or fuselage
- Initial explosion flash
- Missile penetrates aircraft structure

**Critical Timing:**

- Pilot has cleared immediate blast zone
- Still close enough for dramatic proximity
- Creates tension and relief simultaneously

---

### Beat 3: 3-8 seconds

**MASSIVE CINEMATIC EXPLOSION**

**Explosion Characteristics:**

- **Massive scale** (engulfs entire aircraft)
- **Volumetric fire** (three-dimensional flames expanding)
- **Fireball** expanding in slow-motion
- Orange/yellow core with darker edges
- Realistic combustion physics

**Metal Debris:**

- Aircraft **tearing apart**
- Large sections of fuselage breaking off
- Wings separating
- Engine components tumbling
- Cockpit frame disintegrating
- Debris flying in all directions
- Spinning, tumbling metal pieces
- Reflective surfaces catching light

**Volumetric Effects:**

- **Volumetric smoke trails** from debris
- Thick black smoke billowing
- Fuel vapor clouds
- Atmospheric depth
- Light rays through smoke
- Particle effects (sparks, embers)

**Visual Progression:**

- Initial bright flash
- Fireball expansion
- Debris field spreading
- Smoke clouds forming
- Atmospheric distortion from heat

**Camera Continued:**

- Pull-out movement continues
- Reveals full scale of destruction
- Pilot visible in foreground or side (safe distance)
- Explosion dominates frame
- Wide composition showing scope
