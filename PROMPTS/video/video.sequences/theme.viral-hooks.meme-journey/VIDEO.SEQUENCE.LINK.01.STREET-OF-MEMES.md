## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 30-second vertical raw smartphone-style one-take where the protagonist films themself walking down one suburban street and passes seven meme scenarios in sequence. The camera never cuts; every meme appears naturally as a physical event on the same side of the street.

Ideal for replacing the meme references with a custom set while preserving the core structure: one person, one phone camera, one neighborhood route, one continuous walk, and a final spoken punchline.

## Video Prompt

Create raw amateur iPhone footage filmed as one single continuous unbroken 30-second take.

**Duration:** 30 seconds.

**Style:** Vertical 9:16 raw iPhone selfie footage, natural daylight, handheld step bounce, minor autofocus shifts, minor exposure shifts, real suburban neighborhood audio, no cinematic stabilization, no polished commercial look.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
The phone is the camera and stays behind the lens from first frame to last. The protagonist from @image1 films a selfie video while moving through one American suburban neighborhood street with detached houses, sidewalks, lawns, porches, driveways, parked cars, and a small residential village feeling.

Everything happens on the protagonist's own side of the street. The protagonist starts lightly jogging, slows into walking, and physically passes each meme situation one after another. No cuts, hidden cuts, jump transitions, scene changes, teleporting, sudden appearances, resets, or separate shots.

**Camera:**
The same phone records everything. When the camera changes direction, it is because the protagonist physically turns the phone while continuing to move. When facing forward, the protagonist is out of frame. When facing the protagonist, it is selfie mode. Situations grow in frame because the protagonist walks toward them and leave frame because the protagonist walks past.

**Lighting:**
Natural daylight on a suburban street, with raw smartphone exposure behavior and no cinematic relighting.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
All meme references appear as live-action neighborhood moments, not pasted overlays. Keep the physical order exactly: protagonist jogging, burning house and Disaster Girl, Old Man porch, Distracted Boyfriend couple, car with Side-Eye child, clothes rack with white-and-gold dress and Roll Safe, final selfie, Okay Man.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
Maintain one route, one neighborhood, one continuous recording, one protagonist identity, natural pedestrian movement, raw live neighborhood audio, the car engine starter, boyfriend whistle, and Okay Man's final line.

Negative Prompt: no cuts, hidden cuts, teleporting, sudden appearances, separate scenes, resets, cinematic stabilization, polished commercial look, meme overlays, wrong side of street, duplicate meme characters, dress changing to black and blue, phone visible in frame, protagonist holding extra objects, characters appearing without being approached, or final Okay Man line before the word "internet".
