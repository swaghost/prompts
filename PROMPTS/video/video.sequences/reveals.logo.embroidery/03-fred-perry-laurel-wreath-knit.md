## Concept

A macro product shot of embroidered laurel wreath logo on black merino knit polo that animates itself stitching from nothing — single gold thread works both branches symmetrically from base upward, filling leaves on the return path. No hands, autonomous embroidery.

## IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2)

Create a photorealistic extreme close-up product photograph of the chest of a black fine-gauge merino knit polo shirt, shot straight on, the fabric filling the entire frame, the edge of a twin-tipped collar in white and black just visible along the top edge.

The knit is clearly resolved — fine even rows of wool stitches running vertically, the yarn matte and slightly hairy, individual fibres catching the light, the cloth soft with a gentle fold running below.

Embroidered directly into the knit on the left chest is the Fred Perry laurel wreath: two slender curved branches rising from a single point at the bottom and sweeping upward and outward in a mirrored arc, open at the top, each branch carrying a row of small pointed leaves along its outer side, the leaves smallest near the base and largest toward the top of the arc.

The wreath is worked in dense gold-yellow satin stitch, each leaf a solid raised teardrop with its stitches running along its length so the thread catches the light down the leaf, the twin stems laid as fine unbroken cords, the outline crisp, the wool puckering very slightly all around the embroidery where the thread pulls the knit in.

Macro product photography for a fashion e-commerce listing. Soft diffused studio light from the upper left, the gold thread glowing warm against the matte black wool, the raised stitching casting its own fine shadow, shallow depth of field, the embroidery tack-sharp and the knit softening away at the edges of frame.

**Constraints:** no text, no words, no wordmark, no labels, no captions, no watermark, no other logos, no hands, no people, no hangers, no props, no background beyond the fabric.

## ANIMATION PROMPT (Omni Flash, video node)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

The shot opens on the bare black merino knit with no embroidery on it at all, nothing else in frame but cloth. A single gold thread descends out of the air from the top of the frame, its loose end hanging and swaying, and touches down on the knit.

From that first contact the thread stitches the entire emblem by itself, in one continuous unbroken path, working strictly from the base of the wreath upward. It sinks into the wool and rises back out over and over, laying dense satin stitch as it travels, the knit puckering slightly around each pass, the finished stitching left solid behind it while the remaining length of thread still floats loose in the air above.

It lays the joined base first, then runs the left stem upward as a single fine unbroken cord all the way to its tip, then travels back down the outside of that stem filling each leaf in turn as a solid raised teardrop, smallest at the base and largest toward the top. It then returns to the base and repeats the same path on the right side, running the right stem up to its tip and filling its row of leaves on the way back down, so the two branches finish as exact mirrors of each other.

The single thread completes the whole emblem, matching exactly the shape, position and proportions shown, then pulls taut and settles flat. The camera pushes in slowly on the finished raised stitching. Macro depth of field throughout, soft studio light, the fabric breathing slightly.
