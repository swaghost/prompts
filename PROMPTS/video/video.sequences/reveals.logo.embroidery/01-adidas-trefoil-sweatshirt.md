## Concept

A macro product shot of embroidered logo on heather grey fleece that animates itself stitching from nothing — thread descends, works the emblem autonomously, pulls taut. No hands, no tools, just the thread doing the work by itself.

## IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2)

Create a photorealistic extreme close-up product photograph of the chest of a heather grey heavy loopback cotton sweatshirt, shot straight on, the fabric filling the entire frame, a section of the ribbed neckband just visible along the top edge.

The loopback face is clearly resolved — a dense soft pile of grey and white fibres, the yarn slightly heathered so darker and lighter strands read separately, the cloth thick and heavy with a soft fold running below.

Embroidered directly into the pile on the centre chest is the adidas Trefoil: three rounded leaf shapes fanning outward from a common point, one pointing straight up in the centre and one angling down to each side, the whole mark broadly triangular, with three narrow horizontal gaps cutting straight across all three leaves at even intervals where the bare grey fleece shows through unstitched. Directly beneath the trefoil, the wordmark "adidas" in small lowercase rounded letters on a single line.

Everything is worked in dense white satin stitch, the stitches laid tightly side by side so the thread reads as a solid raised surface sitting proud of the pile, the surrounding fibres pushed outward and standing up slightly around the edge of every stitched form, the outline crisp against the soft fleece.

Macro product photography for a fashion e-commerce listing. Soft diffused studio light from the upper left, the raised stitching casting its own fine shadow into the pile, shallow depth of field, the embroidery tack-sharp and the fleece softening away at the edges of frame.

**Constraints:** exact spelling of the wordmark, no other text, no captions, no watermark, no other logos, no hands, no people, no hangers, no props, no background beyond the fabric.

## ANIMATION PROMPT (Omni Flash, video node)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

### Absolute Rules

**Camera rule, absolute:** the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance, focal length and lens from first frame to last. No pans, no tilts, no pushes, no zooms, no reframing, no change of magnification.

**Framing rule, absolute:** the framing matches the reference image exactly — an extreme close-up of the centre chest of the sweatshirt, shot straight on, with a section of the ribbed neckband visible along the top edge of frame and a soft fold in the fleece running below. Those garment details are present in the very first frame and hold the same position in frame throughout.

**Target rule, absolute:** the final frame must match the reference image exactly — same framing, same distance, same angle, same trefoil, same wordmark, same spelling, same position, same size, same proportions.

### Animation Sequence

The shot opens on this same framing with the heather grey fleece bare, no embroidery on it at all, the ribbed neckband visible along the top edge and the fold running below. A single white thread descends out of the air from the top of the frame, its loose end hanging and swaying, and touches down on the pile.

From that first contact the thread stitches the entire emblem by itself, in one continuous unbroken path, working strictly from the top of the mark downward. It sinks deep into the fleece and rises back out over and over, laying dense satin stitch as it travels, the soft fibres parting ahead of each pass and standing up around the finished stitching, the solid raised thread building behind the loose floating length.

It builds the upright centre leaf first, filling it from its rounded tip down toward the common point, then swings out and fills the left leaf from its outer tip inward, then the right leaf the same way, laying each leaf in three separate stitched bands and skipping cleanly over the three narrow horizontal gaps so the bare grey fleece is left showing through between them.

The thread completes the trefoil, then drops straight below and stitches the wordmark "adidas" in lowercase, letter by letter, the letters connected in a single unbroken path.

The thread completes the whole emblem, matching exactly the shape, spelling, position and proportions shown, then pulls taut and settles flat. The finished embroidery holds, identical to the reference, for the last second of the video. Macro depth of field throughout, soft studio light, the fabric breathing very slightly while holding its position in frame.
