## Video Prompt

**Duration:** 15 seconds. Single continuous narrative sequence.

**Style:** Ultra-cinematic automotive commercial, documentary-style handheld cinematography, dramatic nighttime lighting, premium car advertisement, moody atmospheric aesthetic, realistic motion, practical light sources, shallow depth of field, film grain, high contrast night photography.

**Scene Setup:**
A nighttime gas station with dramatic overhead lighting creating pools of light against deep darkness. A Nissan GT-R and its driver (@img1) in an intimate refueling stop that transitions into high-speed highway driving. The sequence feels like a premium automotive documentary with handheld energy and cinematic composition.

**Timeline:**

**1.2-2.4s — Gas Station Arrival**
The GT-R rolls into the gas station's tight pool and cone of light, only the suspension dipping and settling smoothly as it stops squared perfectly to the pump. The car sits motionless under dramatic overhead lighting with deep shadows. Surrounding darkness creates high contrast. Cinematic wide shot establishing the location.

**2.4-3.6s — Approach to Pump**
@img1 walks around the car toward the pump at a relaxed pace, natural stride, arms swinging naturally. Handheld camera follows with subtle movement. Practical gas station fluorescent lighting. He moves with casual confidence through the frame.

**3.6-4.8s — Fuel Nozzle Lift**
He lifts the fuel nozzle off its cradle, hose sagging slightly with its own weight. His hand holds it steady. Close-up shot showing the mechanical interaction. Realistic weight and physics. Ambient gas station sounds.

**4.8-6.3s — Fueling and Return**
The nozzle seals into the fuel port with a small mechanical give, his hand holding it steady and firm. Cut to: He returns to the driver's door, swings it open with smooth motion, settles into the seat in two natural movements. Interior warm cabin lighting visible. Door closes.

**6.3-7.8s — Departure**
The GT-R pulls away from the pump, accelerating smoothly and progressively. The car passes behind a parked truck before disappearing into the dark beyond the station's light. Taillights fade into darkness. Wide cinematic shot showing the exit. Smooth camera pan following the car.

**7.8-9.0s — Highway Transition**
Now on the highway. A shaky low rear three-quarter view shows the GT-R holding a hard pace, weaving slightly with small steering corrections. Handheld camera energy. Road texture visible. Nighttime highway atmosphere with minimal ambient light.

**9.0-10.2s — Driver Focus**
Quick handheld close-up on @img1's focused face as parallel light bars from overhead highway lights sweep across it in quick succession. His expression shows concentration and intensity. Dramatic lighting patterns. Shallow depth of field. Face partially lit by dashboard glow and passing lights.

**10.2-11.4s — Steering Detail**
Extreme close-up on the steering wheel's chrome GT-R badge. No hands in frame, just the badge catching a brief glint of light. Metallic reflection. Premium detail shot emphasizing the brand and luxury.

**11.4-12.6s — Footwell Action**
Low footwell shot. His foot presses firmly into the pedal, sole compressing visibly against the pad. Realistic pressure and motion. Interior lighting from dashboard. Emphasis on control and power application.

**12.6-13.8s — Trailing View**
A trailing dashcam view catches the car passing beneath a glowing highway exit sign with traffic ahead. Green exit sign illuminated overhead. Other vehicles' taillights visible in distance. Following camera perspective showing forward motion.

**13.8-15.0s — Highway Hero Shot**
Wider elevated view as the GT-R curves through the roadway with red taillights glowing, banking through the curve into the darker stretch ahead. The car disappears into the night. Cinematic ending composition showing the journey continuing. Atmospheric highway lighting. Moody final frame.

**Camera:**
Handheld documentary-style throughout with natural shake and energy. Mix of wide establishing shots, medium following shots, extreme close-ups for details, and tracking shots. Dynamic camera movement matching the intensity of each moment. No stabilized tripod shots—everything has organic handheld motion.

**Lighting:**
Practical light sources only: gas station overhead fluorescents creating dramatic pools of light, dashboard glow, highway overhead lights creating passing streaks, exit sign illumination, headlights from other vehicles, GT-R's taillights. High contrast nighttime cinematography. Deep shadows. Dramatic light and dark interplay.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
Ultra-realistic automotive cinematography, 8K, HDR, high contrast night photography, film grain texture, shallow depth of field on close-ups, realistic motion blur during movement, physically accurate vehicle suspension and motion, authentic handheld camera shake, cinematic color grading with cool blues and warm practical lights, no artificial lighting, premium car commercial quality, photorealistic rendering.
