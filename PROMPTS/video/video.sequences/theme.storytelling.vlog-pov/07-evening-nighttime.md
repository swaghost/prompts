# POV Evening & Nighttime

**POV LIFESTYLE / EVENING ROUTINE CONTENT**

---

## Platform

First-person POV lifestyle content platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video or still images  
**Style:** Cozy, relaxing, wind-down evening moments

---

## Concept

Winding down, cozy evening routines, nightlife from first-person perspective—capturing the intimate feeling of ending the day, relaxing at home, or experiencing nighttime adventures through the viewer's eyes.

---

## Typical Shots

- Cozy on couch with blanket (feet visible, holding book/remote)
- View from bed preparing to sleep (nightstand, lamp, book)
- Holding wine glass while watching sunset from balcony
- Lighting candles around bathtub
- Reading in bed (hands holding book, warm lamp light)
- City lights view from apartment window at night
- Cooking dinner in kitchen (evening meal prep)
- Getting ready for night out (makeup, outfit selection)
- Unwinding with tea/wine on couch

## Sequence Building

Create evening wind-down routine:

1. **Sunset Moment** (balcony with wine, watching sky) - 4 seconds
2. **Dinner** (cooking or plating meal) - 4 seconds
3. **Clean Up** (tidying kitchen, lighting candles) - 3 seconds
4. **Skincare** (bathroom routine) - 5 seconds
5. **Cozy Up** (getting into pajamas, blankets) - 3 seconds
6. **Reading** (settling with book and tea) - 4 seconds
7. **Sleep Prep** (turning off lights, bed ready) - 2 seconds

Total: 25-second evening routine
