# POV Social & Dining

**POV LIFESTYLE / DINING & SOCIAL CONTENT**

---

## Platform

First-person POV lifestyle content platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video or still images  
**Style:** Social, culinary, experiential dining moments

---

## Concept

Meals, restaurants, social gatherings from the diner's perspective—capturing the intimate experience of dining out, sharing food, and social moments through first-person POV. Shows what you see across the table, what you're eating, and who you're with.

---

## Typical Shots

- View across restaurant table at friends/date
- Hands holding fork/chopsticks with food
- Looking down at beautifully plated meal
- Holding cocktail/wine glass with restaurant background
- Sharing food (hands reaching across table)
- View from outdoor dining patio
- Cheers/toasting moment (glasses meeting)
- Hands cutting into dish
- Food being delivered to table

---

## Sequence Building

Create dinner date experience:

1. **Arrival** (being seated, menu in hand) - 3 seconds
2. **Drinks** (ordering/receiving cocktails) - 4 seconds
3. **Appetizers** (sharing plates arriving) - 4 seconds
4. **Main Course** (beautiful plate delivered) - 5 seconds
5. **Eating** (hands with utensils, taking bites) - 5 seconds
6. **Toasting** (glasses meeting, celebration) - 2 seconds
7. **Dessert** (sweet ending) - 2 seconds

Total: 25-second dining experience

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Poorly lit food (too dark)** → ✅ Well-lit dishes  
❌ **Messy table/clutter** → ✅ Intentional composition  
❌ **Unflattering food angles** → ✅ Appetizing presentation  
❌ **No social context** → ✅ Dining companions visible  
❌ **Shaky hand-held shots** → ✅ Steady stable footage
