# POV Workspace & Productivity

**POV LIFESTYLE / HOME OFFICE & PRODUCTIVE CONTENT**

---

## Platform

First-person POV lifestyle content platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video or still images  
**Style:** Organized, productive, aspirational work-from-home moments

---

## Concept

Working from home, desk setups, creative work sessions from first-person perspective. Capturing the aesthetic and productive feeling of a well-organized workspace through the creator's eyes—showing what they see while working, creating, and being productive.

---

## Typical Shots

- View looking down at organized desk with laptop and coffee
- Hands typing on laptop keyboard
- View from desk toward window/plants
- Hands writing in planner/journal
- Taking notes, checking phone while working
- Aesthetic desk organization (stationery, plants, tech)
- Hands using iPad/tablet with Apple Pencil
- Morning desk setup routine

## Sequence Building

Create productive work session story:

1. **Setup** (sitting down, opening laptop) - 3 seconds
2. **Coffee** (placing cup on coaster) - 2 seconds
3. **Planning** (opening planner, writing goals) - 5 seconds
4. **Working** (typing on laptop) - 8 seconds
5. **Note-Taking** (writing in notebook) - 4 seconds
6. **Pause** (sipping coffee, thinking) - 3 seconds

Total: 25-second productive session
