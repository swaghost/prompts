## SCENE 1: THE VOYAGE MAP

**00:00–00:03 | Extreme Cinematic Macro**

### Visual Elements:

- Cold dawn light travels across monumental **sun-bleached limestone relief** of the Mediterranean Sea
- Carved coastlines reveal islands, reefs, mountains, fortress walls, ancient ports, distant island kingdom
- Thin line of **dark crimson mineral pigment** slowly travels through carved sea route toward final island
- Sea salt crystals glisten inside grooves
- Route reaches destination and becomes completely still

### Title Card:

- Carved directly into limestone
- Monumental classical serif typography

## SCENE 2: RELICS OF WAR AND SEA

**00:03–00:06 | Rough Charcoal Linen Surface**

### Visual Elements:

- Cut to rough charcoal linen
- Objects slowly slide into frame:
  - Broken bronze spearhead
  - Battered shield fragment
  - Weathered olive-wood oar
  - Bronze helmet piece
  - Dark ship timber
  - Salt-covered coin
- Objects interlock into **original circular seafaring royal seal**
- Behind: two tiny bronze sailing ships cross paths once over strip of black volcanic glass

### Title Card:

- No emblem
- No additional text

## SCENE 3: THE RETURNING KING

**00:06–00:10 | Polished Black Volcanic Glass**

- Sea salt slowly retreats from surface
- Reveals reflection of main character from **@Image1** standing alone on rocky shoreline

### Character Details:

- Weathered bronze armor
- Rough linen
- Heavy wool cloak moving subtly in ocean wind
- Expression: exhausted, determined, controlled
- Distant ship barely visible through mist
- Storm clouds cover horizon

- Warm amber firelight touches one side of silhouette

- Hold briefly

## SCENE 4: THE ODYSSEY REVEALED

**00:10–00:15 | Vast Miniature Dark-Blue Sea**

### Opening:

- Credit fades naturally
- Camera slowly pulls backward from volcanic glass
- Reflection becomes vast miniature dark-blue sea surrounding ancient island kingdom
- Small sailing ship pushes through rough water toward island
- Rain sweeps across sea
- Lightning reveals towering cliffs
- Something enormous moves beneath surface
- Main character remains visible as distant silhouette on shoreline

### Transformation Moment:

- Weathered bronze coin falls through sea spray
- Strikes black glass

Pale limestone and aged-bronze letterforms rise slowly around glowing horizon.
