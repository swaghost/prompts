## SHOT BREAKDOWN

### SHOT 1 (0:00–0:05) — BOUNCE PODS

**Camera:** Wide broadcast shot, 35mm, locked-off on a high tower camera, full course visible left to right.

**Action:** Buzzer sounds. She sprints off the timber launch deck at far LEFT and leaps onto the first inflatable pod, which compresses and rebounds under her weight. She hops to the second, then the third — the third pod tips hard to the side, she pinwheels both arms and drops her hips to recover, then jumps onto the fourth pod and lands crouched at its right edge. Crowd rises behind her.

**SFX:** Buzzer, crowd roar, air-pod thump and rebound, wet footfalls, water slap.

---

### SHOT 2 (0:05–0:10) — THE SWEEPER

**Camera:** Hard cut. Stabilized tracking shot moving LEFT → RIGHT alongside the floating causeway, 50mm, chest height, matching her pace.

**Action:** She jumps from the last pod onto the white causeway and sprints forward. The long padded sweeper arm rotates in from the RIGHT at knee height. She reads it, drops flat onto her front and slides through the water film under the arm as it passes over her back. She pushes up immediately, water running off her forearms, and runs to the base of the wet ramp. The arm keeps rotating behind her.

**SFX:** Motor hum, padded arm whoosh, body sliding on wet vinyl, breathing, crowd surge.

---

### SHOT 3 (0:10–0:15) — WET RAMP AND FALL

**Camera:** Hard cut. Front three-quarter low angle from the water, 85mm handheld with slight shake, ramp filling frame, finish bell visible at upper right.

**Action:** She runs up the curved red ramp face, water sheeting down against her. She catches a hanging rope with both hands and hauls herself higher, feet driving. Near the lip her right foot slips on the wet surface, her left hand tears off the rope, and she falls backward off the ramp into the pool. Large realistic splash.

Hard cut to a water-level close-up: she surfaces directly below the ramp, completely soaked, pushes wet hair back from her face, looks up at the finish platform, then laughs and slaps the water once with her palm. Crowd erupts. Water keeps sheeting down the ramp behind her.

**THIS IS THE SIGNATURE SHOT.**

**SFX:** Rope creak, wet friction, hand slipping, heavy body splash, water surface close-up, crowd cheer swelling, distant air horn.
