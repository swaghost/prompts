## CONCEPT

15-second raw vertical iPhone footage-style influencer real estate tour of a modern luxury clifftop villa. Female influencer (mid-20s, Dragon Ball fan energy) shows distinctive yellow and white rounded villa with 360° ocean views through authentic handheld phone camera. Five quick-cut segments: exterior with yellow quirky car → open-plan living space → circular garden terrace → round meditation room → clifftop edge finale.
