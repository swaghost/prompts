# Living Fashion Poster

## Description

A luxury fashion editorial where the subject remains completely frozen like a photograph while the world moves around them. Only hair and clothing move naturally in the wind, creating a striking contrast between stillness and motion.

## Prerequisites

- Subject image (person in fashion pose)
- Background environment (urban street scene)

## Video Prompt

Living fashion editorial poster. The man appears completely frozen in time, like a still photograph inside a moving world. His pose, face, eyes, expression, body, clothing, headphones and sunglasses remain perfectly motionless throughout the entire shot. Only his long blonde hair and trench coat moves naturally in the wind, with elegant flowing strands and realistic soft motion and his hand on the top left makes a rotating move extremely slowly. People smoothly pass through the background street, a person on an electric scooter glides, and a cyclist rides by across the scene. The city remains alive and moving while the man stays perfectly still. Typography, white frame and graphic elements remain stable, sharp and fully readable throughout all scene. Locked camera, premium fashion campaign aesthetic, realistic motion, cinematic urban atmosphere, strong contrast between the frozen subject and the moving city around him, luxury living poster effect.

## Usage

Perfect for fashion brand campaigns, urban lifestyle marketing, editorial fashion content, magazine cover concepts, luxury apparel advertisements, and artistic fashion photography. Ideal for creating striking contrast between frozen models and dynamic environments, living poster effects, and premium fashion editorial narratives with cinematic urban backdrops.
