# Michelin Sign Pneumatic Inflation Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** service-signage

**Reveal Effect:** pneumatic-inflation

**Reveal Mechanism:** the wall sign inflates segment by segment, then molded lettering pushes out from the wall and locks into place

**Sequence Type:** self-build reference-match reveal

**Filename:** `reveal.service-signage.pneumatic-inflation.michelin-sign.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 7-second locked-off architectural sign reveal where a bare blue workshop wall builds a three-dimensional tyre-service sign by self-inflating the white figure and pushing molded blue and yellow lettering outward from the wall. The final frame must match the Step A architectural reference exactly.

## Usage

Perfect for service-signage mockups, automotive workshop ads, storefront logo reveals, local business pitch videos, and tactile brand-sign animation demos.

Ideal for showing signage forming physically on a real facade while the camera remains locked, the environment stays unchanged, and the final dimensional sign lands exactly on the hero still.

## Source / Target Image Prompt

Create a photorealistic architectural photograph of a tyre service workshop on an industrial street, shot from the forecourt looking at the front of the building from a low three-quarter angle, roller shutter doors below, overcast flat daylight, wet concrete apron.

Mounted on the blue wall above the shutters is a large three-dimensional sign. At its left the Michelin man: a standing figure built entirely from stacked white rounded tyre rings, thickest at the legs and arms, narrowing at the waist, a smooth round head with a wide friendly face on top, one arm raised in a wave, the whole figure bright white with soft grey shading between the rings.

To the right of the figure, in thick blue capitals, the wordmark "MICHELIN". Beneath it on two lines in smaller blue capitals: "TYRE SERVICE CENTRE" above and "FITTING BALANCING ALIGNMENT" below. Along the lower edge, a narrow band of the smallest yellow capitals reading "NO APPOINTMENT NEEDED - ALL SIZES IN STOCK".

Every element is a deep moulded form standing well proud of the wall, with visible side returns and clean edges, casting soft individual shadows on the blue cladding. All lettering sharp, correctly kerned, perfectly legible from the street.

Realistic industrial architecture photography, visible cladding seams and drainpipe, a wheelie bin far below giving scale.

Constraints: exact spelling of all four lines, no other text, no other signage, no watermark, no people, no cars.

## Video Prompt

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

**Duration:** 7 seconds. Finished sign holds for the last second.

**Style:** Photorealistic industrial architecture video, pneumatic self-build signage, overcast daylight, wet concrete texture, molded dimensional letters, restrained premium local-business reveal.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Camera rule, absolute: the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance and lens from first frame to last. No pans, no pushes, no zooms, no reframing.

Framing rule, absolute: the workshop is seen from the forecourt at a low three-quarter angle, the same viewpoint as the reference image, the roller shutters and wet concrete apron both visible in frame.

Target rule, absolute: the final frame must match the reference image exactly - same figure, same segment count and proportions, same position, same size, same letterforms, all four lines of copy present and identical.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:01
The shot opens on this same view of the workshop with the blue wall completely bare, nothing mounted on it at all.

0:01-0:04
The figure inflates itself into being against the wall, everything moving on its own with nothing and no one touching it: a smooth white balloon-like segment swells outward from nothing at the foot of the sign position, rounding into a plump ring, then another swells above it, then another, each one puffing up in turn and settling snugly against the one below, the stack growing upward like a column of soft inflated cushions. The legs fill first as thick rounded segments, then the body swells wider and pinches in at the waist, then the arms puff outward from the shoulders with the raised one lifting into its wave as it fills, and finally the smooth round head inflates last and its face settles onto it.

0:04-0:05
Every segment is a smooth glossy white inflated form with no texture, no tread and no pattern on its surface, softly shaded grey in the creases where one segment presses against the next.

0:05-0:06
The blue capitals of the wordmark then push forward out of the wall word by word to the right, followed by the two smaller lines one above the other, and finally the narrow yellow band slides along the bottom edge and locks flush.

0:06-0:07
The finished sign holds, identical to the reference, for the last second of the video.
