## Source / Target Image Prompt

Create a photorealistic e-commerce product photograph of a single grey suede and mesh running sneaker in a classic dad-shoe silhouette, shot in exact side profile from the lateral side, standing on a plain seamless light grey studio backdrop, the shoe filling most of the frame.

The upper is built from clearly separated overlapping panels: soft grey pigskin suede overlays at the toe, midfoot and heel, each with a visible stitched edge and a soft nap, over a fine grey mesh base that shows its weave between them. Flat grey laces run through five eyelet panels. A dark grey suede heel counter wraps the back.

The midsole is thick cream-white foam with a visible density line running through it and a soft grey rubber outsole beneath with a waffle tread. Fixed across the midfoot panel is a large capital letter "N" in light grey suede with a stitched outline, sitting proud of the panels beneath it.

Macro-sharp product photography, soft large-source studio light from the upper left, a soft contact shadow under the outsole, the suede nap and mesh weave both clearly resolved, shallow depth of field.

Constraints: exact single letter "N" only, no other text, no words, no labels, no captions, no watermark, no other logos, no second shoe, no box, no hands, no people, no props.
