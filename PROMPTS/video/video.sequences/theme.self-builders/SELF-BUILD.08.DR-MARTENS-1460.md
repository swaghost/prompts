## Video Prompt

C - CINEMATIC REVEAL FILM (Seedance 2.0, uses hero as reference)

Use the attached image as the product reference. Preserve this exact boot throughout: the same silhouette, the same waxed oxblood leather, the same eight brass eyelets, the same yellow welt stitching, the same heel loop and air-cushioned sole. The laces are threaded through all eight eyelets and tied in a symmetrical bow at the top, the ends tucked short - the boot is fully laced and fastened in every shot.

**Duration:** 7 seconds.

**Style:** High-contrast product film, pure black background, zero ambient light, thin haze, hard specular lighting, deep oxblood leather glow, premium footwear reveal, no props, no text.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Deep black void with no floor, no walls and no visible surfaces. Thin haze drifts slowly through the space, invisible in the darkness and revealed only where a beam of light cuts through it. The light falls off sharply, so everything beyond the lit volume goes fully black.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:02
The shot opens in macro on the row of brass eyelets, filling the frame. A hard light rakes across from the right at a shallow angle: each brass ring ignites into a hard specular ring in turn as the beam passes, the waxed leather between them lifting into a deep red glow while the black laces stay dense and swallow the light. Smoke drifts through the beam as the camera moves slowly up the column, eyelet after eyelet passing through frame.

0:02-0:05
Cut to the full boot in lateral profile, floating level in the darkness, lit only by a hard rim of light along its top edge with the body of the boot in near silhouette. The backlight fans through the haze into visible shafts behind it. A key light sweeps in fast from the left and travels across the flank, tearing a bright path through the smoke as it comes, the waxed leather blooming into deep oxblood as it passes and the yellow welt stitching igniting into a bright line running the whole length of the sole join, then the light settles low and the boot holds still as the disturbed haze rolls slowly outward.

0:05-0:07
Cut to a low three-quarter angle from the front, close on the toe. The boot tips forward toward the lens and stops hard, the tied bow snapping taut and the collar compressing, a bright highlight racing along the grooved edge of the black sole as it settles, a thin curl of smoke passing through the beam beside it.

**Camera:**
Camera locked off in each shot except the opening drift. Product centred, generous black space around it. Use the opening macro camera drift up the eyelet column, then locked-off full lateral profile, then locked-off low front three-quarter close-up.

**Lighting:**
Hard light rakes across from the right in the macro opening, a hard rim light defines the full profile, backlight fans through haze, and a fast key light sweep reveals the oxblood leather and yellow welt stitching. Zero ambient light beyond the lit volume.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
Preserve the exact boot throughout: silhouette, waxed oxblood full-grain leather, eight brass eyelets, black waxed cotton laces, symmetrical bow, yellow welt stitching, heel loop, black air-cushioned rubber sole, grooved edge, and lightly scalloped sidewall. Thin haze and smoke reveal light beams only.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
High-contrast product film, pure black background, zero ambient light, no props, no text, stable boot identity, sharp leather grain, brass eyelets, yellow welt stitching, and sole edge. Product remains centred with generous black space.

Audio: no music, low room tone, a deep leather creak as the boot settles, a low tonal swell on each cut.

Negative Prompt: no product redesign, no missing eyelets, no extra eyelets, no changed laces, no missing yellow welt stitching, no visible floor, no walls, no props, no text, no watermark, no logo changes, no distorted boot silhouette, no floating debris except haze, no ambient fill light, no generic boot replacement.
