# Stone Island Patch Self-Stitch Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apparel

**Reveal Effect:** self-stitching

**Reveal Mechanism:** floating golden yellow and dark forest green threads stitch the compass emblem into a fixed navy sleeve patch

**Sequence Type:** self-build reference-match reveal

**Filename:** `reveal.apparel.self-stitching.stone-island-patch.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 7-second locked-off macro product video where a bare navy sleeve patch stitches its own compass emblem from floating threads. The final frame must match the Step A hero still exactly, including the patch, buttons, jacket fabric, embroidery colors, emblem geometry, and wordmark placement.

## Usage

Perfect for fashion brand mockups, embroidery demos, apparel-logo reveals, product pitch videos, and high-end social media creative for clothing brands.

Ideal for showing a brand mark physically forming from its own material while keeping the camera fixed, the product surface tactile, and the final frame target-matched to a polished hero still.

## Source / Target Image Prompt

Create a photorealistic extreme close-up product photograph of the upper left sleeve of an olive-green technical nylon jacket, shot straight on, the sleeve filling the entire frame at a slight angle, the coated nylon showing a soft directional sheen and one gentle crease running below.

Fastened to the sleeve is a horizontal rectangular patch of dark navy woven fabric, wider than it is tall, lying flat across the sleeve with its long edges running horizontally. It stands clearly on top of the nylon rather than being part of it, its edges finished and its corners slightly rounded.

Embroidered at the centre of that rectangular patch is a circular compass emblem, filling most of the patch's height. At its centre is a four-pointed compass rose: one long point up, one down, one left and one right, each point a slender elongated diamond. Every point is split lengthways down its own axis, one half in golden yellow and the other half in dark forest green, so the star reads as alternating light and dark facets around its centre.

Around the star runs a golden yellow embroidered ring. Set into that ring in dark forest green capitals is the wordmark "STONE ISLAND", appearing twice: once curving along the upper half of the ring reading left to right, and once curving along the lower half rotated 180 degrees, so the two readings sit head to head around the circle. The letters follow the curve of the ring exactly. The embroidery sits slightly raised above the navy cloth with a visible satin-stitch sheen.

The patch is held on by two small dark buttons, one at the left end of the rectangle and one at the right end, sitting level with the centre of the circle, each passing through a reinforced eyelet in the patch and through a short fabric tab beneath. The nylon puckers very slightly around each button.

Macro product photography for a fashion e-commerce listing. Soft diffused studio light from the upper left, the patch casting its own crisp shadow on the sleeve, shallow depth of field.

Constraints: exact spelling "STONE ISLAND" on both halves of the ring, no other text, no captions, no watermark, no other logos, no hands, no people, no hangers, no props, no background beyond the sleeve.

## Video Prompt

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

**Duration:** 7 seconds. Final finished emblem holds for the last second.

**Style:** Photorealistic macro product video, fashion e-commerce detail, raised satin-stitch embroidery, coated nylon texture, soft diffused studio light, shallow depth of field, premium restrained brand-film finish.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Camera rule, absolute: the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance and lens from first frame to last. No pans, no pushes, no zooms, no reframing.

Framing rule, absolute: an extreme macro close-up of the olive-green jacket sleeve filling the entire frame, the same viewpoint, angle and distance as the reference image, the patch running horizontally across the middle of the frame with the coated fabric and its soft folds visible above and below.

Everything that stays fixed for the whole shot: the dark navy rectangular patch is already fastened to the sleeve from the very first frame and never moves - its position, its size, its finished edge, its two black buttons at the left and right ends and the yellow thread loops holding them are all present, seated and unchanged from start to finish. Only the embroidery inside the patch changes.

The emblem being stitched, described in full: a circular compass, centred on the patch. A four-pointed star at the middle, one long point up, one down, one left and one right, each a slender elongated diamond split lengthways down its own axis into a golden yellow half and a dark forest green half. A smaller golden yellow circle rings the centre of the star, and a larger golden yellow circle rings the whole emblem. Between those two rings sits the wordmark "STONE ISLAND" in dark green capitals, appearing twice - once curving along the upper half reading left to right, once curving along the lower half rotated 180 degrees, the two readings head to head around the circle.

Target rule, absolute: the final frame must match the reference image exactly - same emblem, same star, same two rings, same wordmark twice, same colours, same position and size within the patch.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:01
The shot opens on that same sleeve with the navy patch already buttoned in place but its face completely bare, plain dark navy weave with no embroidery on it at all.

0:01-0:03
A thick bundle of loose threads hangs down into frame from above, golden yellow and dark forest green mixed together, dozens of long strands drifting and swaying slowly in the air over the patch, their cut ends catching the light. They stitch the emblem into the patch themselves, with nothing and no one touching them: strand after strand lowers onto the navy cloth, sinks through it and rises back out on the other side of its path, the working length pulling taut and singing as it draws through, then slackening again, so that at every moment several threads are stretched tight down into the patch while the rest of the bundle sways loose above. Each pass lays another row of dense satin stitch, the finished stitching left solid and slightly raised behind it.

0:03-0:05
The four points of the star draw outward from the centre one at a time, up first, then down, then left, then right, yellow strands filling one half of each point and green strands the other, the two colours working side by side. The threads then gather and travel around the centre, the small inner yellow ring running itself closed, followed by the large outer yellow ring circling the whole emblem.

0:05-0:06
Finally the green strands lay the capitals into the band between the rings, curving along the upper half first and then along the lower half in the opposite direction, until both readings sit complete head to head.

0:06-0:07
As the last stitches land, every remaining loose strand pulls taut at once and lifts away out of the top of frame, the finished satin stitch settling raised and glossy against the navy weave, the cloth puckering a fraction where the thread pulls it in. The patch relaxes flat against the sleeve and the finished emblem holds, identical to the reference, for the last second of the video.
