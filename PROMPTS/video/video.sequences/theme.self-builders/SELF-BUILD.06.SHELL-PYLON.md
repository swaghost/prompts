# Shell Pylon Rib-Growth Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** fuel-signage

**Reveal Effect:** rib-growth

**Reveal Mechanism:** the pecten emblem grows rib by rib from a blank pylon panel, then the wordmark and service lines push forward from the panel

**Sequence Type:** self-build reference-match reveal

**Filename:** `reveal.fuel-signage.rib-growth.shell-pylon.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 7-second locked-off roadside sign reveal where a blank white pylon panel grows the shell emblem from a base point, rib by rib, followed by raised wordmark and service text. The final frame must match the Step A pylon reference exactly.

## Usage

Perfect for fuel-station signage mockups, roadside brand reveals, service pylon concepts, local business pitches, and physical logo-growth demonstrations.

Ideal for showing a recognizable sign forming from its own geometric structure while maintaining hard midday shadows, low camera angle, and a precise final target frame.

## Source / Target Image Prompt

Create a photorealistic photograph of a petrol station forecourt pylon sign on a tall white steel post beside a rural road, shot from the tarmac looking up at it from below, bright hard midday sun, clear pale sky, flat dry landscape behind, the edge of the canopy at the frame's edge.

The pylon carries the Shell pecten emblem: a scallop shell shape, wider at the top and narrowing to a point at the bottom, its face divided into radiating ribs that fan outward from the base, the ribs alternating deep red and golden yellow, the whole shell outlined in red, the top edge scalloped into rounded lobes.

Beneath the emblem, in thick red capitals, the wordmark "SHELL". Below it on two lines in smaller red capitals: "V-POWER FUELS" above and "OPEN 24 HOURS" below. Along the bottom edge of the panel, a narrow band of the smallest red capitals reading "SHOP - CAR WASH - AIR AND WATER - EV CHARGING".

Every element stands proud of the white panel as a deep moulded form with visible side returns and clean edges, casting hard individual shadows in the midday sun. All lettering sharp, correctly kerned, perfectly legible from the road.

Realistic roadside commercial photography, visible steel post seams and bolt plates, a bollard far below giving a sense of scale.

Constraints: exact spelling of all four lines, no other text, no other signage, no watermark, no people, no cars.

## Video Prompt

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

**Duration:** 7 seconds. Finished pylon sign holds for the last second.

**Style:** Photorealistic roadside commercial video, low-angle pylon view, hard midday sunlight, raised molded sign components, clean rib-growth logo reveal, dry rural forecourt atmosphere.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Camera rule, absolute: the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance and lens from first frame to last. No pans, no pushes, no zooms, no reframing.

Framing rule, absolute: the pylon is seen from the forecourt tarmac looking up at it from below, the same low viewpoint as the reference image, the steel post and pale sky both visible in frame.

Target rule, absolute: the final frame must match the reference image exactly - same emblem, same rib count and rib colours, same position, same size, same letterforms, all four lines of copy present and identical.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:01
The shot opens on this same view of the pylon with the white panel completely blank, nothing on it at all.

0:01-0:04
The shell grows itself out of the panel with nothing and no one touching it: a single small red rib rises at the base point and pushes upward, then rib after rib unfurls outward from that same base in a widening fan, alternating red and gold, each one thickening forward off the panel with its own side returns as it seats, the shell spreading wider and its top edge scalloping into rounded lobes as the last ribs lock in, then the red outline runs itself around the whole grown shape and closes.

0:04-0:06
The capitals of the wordmark then push forward out of the panel beneath it word by word, followed by the two smaller lines one above the other, and finally the narrow band along the bottom edge slides in and locks flush. Each element casts its hard midday shadow as it seats.

0:06-0:07
The finished sign holds, identical to the reference, for the last second of the video.
