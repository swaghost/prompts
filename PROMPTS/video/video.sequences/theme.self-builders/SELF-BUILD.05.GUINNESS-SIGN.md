## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 7-second locked-off evening pub sign reveal where a bare black fascia board fills itself with stout, bubbles settle, cream forms the main wordmark, and gold sign elements rise into place. The final dimensional sign must match the Step A architectural pub reference exactly.

## Usage

Perfect for pub signage mockups, beverage brand reveals, hospitality promos, storefront sign films, local business pitches, and atmospheric product-signage animation.

Ideal for showing a venue sign forming from its own material language while preserving the building, wet street, dusk lighting, and final sign composition.

## Source / Target Image Prompt

Create a photorealistic architectural photograph of an old Dublin corner pub at dusk, shot from the pavement opposite looking up at the facade, glossy black-painted timber shopfront below, warm amber light in the small windows, wet cobbles, deep blue evening sky.

Mounted across the top of the shopfront is a large three-dimensional sign standing proud of the timber. Its field is deep black gloss. At its centre the Guinness harp emblem in solid gold: a classic Irish harp seen from the side, a curved forepillar on the left, a straight soundboard on the right, and a row of vertical strings of decreasing length between them.

To the right of the harp, in thick cream-white letters, the wordmark "GUINNESS" in wide bold capitals. Beneath it on two lines in smaller gold capitals: "EXTRA STOUT" above and "BREWED IN DUBLIN SINCE 1759" below. Along the lower edge, a narrow band of the smallest cream capitals reading "DRAUGHT - LIVE MUSIC - SNUG AT THE BACK".

Every element is a deep box letter standing well proud of the field, with visible side returns, gilded edges and a trim cap, casting individual shadows. All lettering sharp, correctly kerned, perfectly legible from across the street.

Realistic evening street photography, reflections on wet cobbles, a lamp post giving scale.

Constraints: exact spelling of all four lines, no other text, no other signage, no watermark, no people.

## Video Prompt

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

**Duration:** 7 seconds. Finished sign holds for the last second.

**Style:** Photorealistic evening street photography, glossy black pub fascia, liquid stout animation, creamy dimensional lettering, gold sign details, warm pub-window reflections, wet cobbles, premium restrained beverage-sign reveal.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Camera rule, absolute: the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance and lens from first frame to last. No pans, no pushes, no zooms, no reframing.

Framing rule, absolute: the pub corner is seen from the wet pavement at street level in a wide three-quarter view from the left, the same viewpoint as the reference image. The long black fascia board runs diagonally across the upper half of the frame, the black timber shopfront with its small-paned windows and warm amber interior light below it, pale rough stone and sash windows above lit by uplighters, a deep blue dusk sky with cloud at the upper right, a lit Victorian street lamp at the right, a terrace of brick houses receding down the wet street behind it, and reflections across the wet cobbles in the foreground.

Everything that stays fixed for the whole shot: the building, the shopfront, the windows and their interior light, the stone above, the uplighters, the sky, the street lamp, the terrace and the wet cobbles are rigid and unchanged from the first frame to the last. Only the face of the fascia board changes.

Target rule, absolute: the final frame must match the reference image exactly - same gold harp at the left, "GUINNESS" in large cream-white serif capitals, "EXTRA STOUT" in gold capitals beneath it, "BREWED IN DUBLIN SINCE 1759" in gold capitals below that, and "DRAUGHT - LIVE MUSIC - SNUG AT THE BACK" along the bottom in cream capitals with gold bullet dots, all in the same positions, sizes and letterforms, with the thin gold pinstripe border around the board.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:01
The shot opens on this same view of the pub with the fascia board mounted but completely empty, a bare black board with nothing on it at all.

0:01-0:03
Everything fills itself with nothing and no one touching it: dark stout pours into the board from the top and rises to fill the whole field, surging and churning, then the cascade begins - a dense curtain of tiny pale bubbles tumbling downward through the dark while the liquid settles beneath it, the churn clearing from the bottom upward until the field stands deep still black and glossy.

0:03-0:05
A thick creamy head then rises up out of that black and gathers along the centre of the board, and as it firms it becomes the wordmark: the tall cream-white serif capitals of "GUINNESS" forming one after another from left to right, each settling solid. Then gold rises out of the black beneath: the harp draws itself upward at the left, its forepillar first, then the soundboard, then the row of strings one by one; then "EXTRA STOUT" gathers in gold beneath the wordmark; then "BREWED IN DUBLIN SINCE 1759" below it.

0:05-0:06
The bottom line forms last, its cream capitals settling in with the small gold bullet dots between them, and finally the thin gold pinstripe runs itself around the whole board and closes.

0:06-0:07
Every element then thickens forward off the black field into a dimensional letter with its own side returns, catching the warm light from the shopfront below and dropping its shadow onto the board, and the finished sign holds, identical to the reference, for the last second of the video. All movement is slow, liquid and continuous, never sudden.
