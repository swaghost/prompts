# Become a Green Energy Hero with DeeVid AI

## Overview

Turn yourself into a cinematic Green Energy Hero with a full 30-second transformation, rescue, monster battle, and final energy attack using DeeVid AI.

**Workflow:** Upload your photo → Choose a video model → Add the timeline prompt → Generate the final 30s video.

## Step 1: Upload Your Photo

Open the DeeVid AI Video Generator:
https://deevid.ai/app/video
Upload a clear photo of yourself.
For better character consistency, use a photo with:

- Clear facial features
- Good lighting
- Front or 3/4 face angle
- Upper body visible
- Minimal face obstruction
- Natural expression
  The uploaded image will be used as the main identity reference throughout the video.
  The goal is to keep the same face and overall appearance while transforming the person from an ordinary civilian into a cinematic green-energy superhero.

## Step 2: Choose Your Video Model

Choose either:

- **Master V4.5** — powered by Seedance 2.5
- **Quality V4.7** — powered by Wan 3
  Use the uploaded photo as the main visual reference and keep the same:
- Face
- Hairstyle
- Age
- Body proportions
- Overall identity
  The transformed superhero must still clearly look like the person in the uploaded photo.

## Step 3: Generate the 30-Second Green Energy Hero Video

Paste the following prompt into DeeVid AI.

### Timeline Prompt

```text
Create a 30-second, photorealistic live-action superhero disaster film.
The story must clearly follow:
ordinary person caught in disaster → discovers power → transforms → saves a civilian → defeats small monsters → faces the Boss → explosive finish.
The overall look should feel like a high-budget live-action superhero movie, not anime or game CG.
The scene takes place on a damaged modern city street with burning cars, smoke, debris and partially collapsed buildings. Keep the crowd sparse, with only a few civilians.
There are 2–3 original underground goblin-like creatures causing chaos, followed later by one much larger Boss monster.
#### 0–4s | Immediate Hook
Start with no introduction.
An out-of-control burning car rolls in from the side of the road and flies past the camera at high speed.
The main character is still in an ordinary civilian state, wearing a casual jacket. The explosion throws them to the ground.
A few civilians run away while small monsters overturn street objects, damage vehicles and chase people.
The hero looks up and sees the city completely out of control.
#### 4–8s | Power Awakens
A much larger Boss emerges through the smoke.
The hero quickly stands and instinctively moves in front of one or two civilians.
They look down as the green ring on their hand begins glowing.
Green energy spreads from the ring across the hand, arm and body.
Clearly show:
ordinary person → notices the ring → decides to use the power.
#### 8–13s | Transformation
The hero raises the ring hand.
Green energy bursts out and wraps around the body, forming a cinematic energy-based combat suit.
Keep the original face clearly recognizable.
Include a short facial close-up so viewers understand this is the same person from the uploaded photo.
After transforming, the hero lifts slightly above the ground with fire and smoke behind them.
#### 13–19s | Hero Rescue
A civilian is trapped beside the road.
A large broken building structure or falling utility pole collapses directly toward them.
The hero immediately creates a huge translucent green energy shield.
Maintain this exact spatial relationship:
falling object → green shield → protected civilian
The structure crashes into the shield.
Show strong green energy ripples, sparks, debris and impact, but the shield remains intact.
The civilian is clearly safe behind it.
The audience must immediately understand:
the hero used the shield to save the civilian.
The hero then quickly knocks back 1–2 nearby small monsters using simple green energy punches or shockwaves.
#### 19–25s | Boss Fight
The giant Boss charges through the smoke.
Begin a cinematic confrontation with aerial dodges, monster lunges, close-range energy strikes and environmental destruction.
Always maintain:
hero → attack direction → Boss
The hero must never become the target of their own energy attack.
The hero extends the ring hand.
Green energy gathers at least half a meter in front of the body and forms a large floating energy cannon.
It is not a physical gun held against the chest.
Maintain:
hero → clear distance → floating energy cannon → Boss
The cannon always points toward the Boss.
#### 25–30s | Final Attack
The Boss roars and charges directly toward the hero.
The hero stops retreating and hovers in midair.
The ring emits its strongest green light while the floating energy cannon finishes charging.
Show a brief close-up of the hero’s determined face.
The cannon fires a massive green energy beam away from the hero’s body.
The beam travels straight into the Boss’s chest, creating a powerful shockwave.
The Boss is blasted backward into the ruins, causing a huge explosion, debris and smoke.
End with the hero hovering above the burning street while the defeated Boss lies in the distance, remaining small monsters flee, and a few rescued civilians look up at the hero.
```
