### Consistency Strategy

1. **Product Lock:** All prompts reference identical bike specifications
   - Glossy corsa red with white diagonal stripes
   - Matte black carbon winglets
   - DUCATI branding placement
   - Öhlins gold, Brembo silver, black wheels with red pinstripe

2. **Wardrobe Lock:** Rider outfit consistent across all scenes
   - Black and red racing leathers with white diagonal accents
   - Matching bike colorway exactly
   - Helmet with black and red graphics

3. **Model Lock:** Single professional male rider/model
   - Athletic build
   - Short groomed hair
   - Confident/intense expression

4. **Scene Variety:** 7 distinct scenarios covering full campaign range
   - Hero arrival video (cinematic action)
   - Studio product shot (press-kit style)
   - Portrait (lifestyle)
   - Dynamic action (cornering, burnout, wheelie)
   - Atmospheric editorial (night rain scene)

### Lighting & Color Grading Patterns

- **Studio shots:** Dramatic low-key lighting, pure black backgrounds
- **Night scenes:** Cool blue-teal tones with warm accent lights
- **Action shots:** Motion blur backgrounds, sharp subject focus, cinematic color grading
- **Tunnel scenes:** Teal-grey or warm beige tones with streaking light sources

## Key Techniques

### Motion Capture in Stills

- Panning technique (sharp subject, blurred background)
- Motion blur conveys speed and direction
- Fast shutter freezes action mid-stunt

### Atmospheric Drama

- Weather effects (rain, smoke, mist)
- Practical lighting (street lamps, headlights, tunnel lights)
- Rim lighting for silhouette definition

### Studio Precision

- Low-key lighting for drama
- Product placement and angle optimization
- Reflections on glossy surfaces controlled

### Cinematic Color

- Cool/warm contrast (blue-teal vs amber/warm glow)
- Moody, undersaturated tones
- High-end automotive campaign aesthetic
