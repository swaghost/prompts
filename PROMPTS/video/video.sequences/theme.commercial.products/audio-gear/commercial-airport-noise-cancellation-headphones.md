# Airport Noise-Cancellation Headphones Commercial - 15-Second Cinematic

## Description

An ultra-realistic cinematic 15-second commercial set inside a premium international airport at night. Uses eleven deliberately directed shots with HARD CUTS only at the specified timestamps, featuring real-time playback with NO slow motion. This commercial showcases noise-cancellation headphones through a narrative of a sleeping traveler experiencing airport chaos, discovering his headphones, activating noise cancellation, and finding peace—all while a couple argues and eventually reconciles in the background.

## DETAILED TIMELINE (0.0-15.05 seconds)

### 0.0-3.2s — LOUD AIRPORT CHAOS

**Shot**: Wide front-facing shot, 35mm

**Action**: Main character already asleep in foreground. Exact headphones rest clearly beside his right hand, carry-on beside his feet. Five metres behind, girlfriend pulls suitcase while boyfriend follows and shouts. He never touches her.

**Audio**: Extremely loud overlapping shouting, announcements, suitcase wheels, passing travellers, crying baby, aircraft hum and rain. Keep sleeping man, headphones and couple inaudible in one composition.

**Soundtrack**: Chaotic, overwhelming ambient airport noise

---

### 3.2-5.0s — HE WAKES UP

**Shot**: Medium front three-quarter close-up

**Action**: Shouting wakes him. He opens his eyes, looks toward the couple and shows realistic irritation. Rubs his eyes, notices chaos. Face briefly tilts one temple while noise continues at full volume.

**Audio**: Continue loud chaos

---

### 5.0-6.5s — HEADPHONES DISCOVERED

**Shot**: Close product shot

**Action**: Exact headphones rest beside his hand. His hand enters frame, fingers them correctly and lifts them. Earcups rotate only within realistic range. Couple keeps shouting in the softly blurred background.

**Audio**: Shouting continues but slightly reduced as focus shifts to foreground

---

### 6.5-8.2s — NOISE CANCELLATION ACTIVATES

**Shot**: Medium side-profile product shot

**Action**: He expands the headband naturally and places the headphones correctly over both ears. Digital Crown position visible on the RIGHT ear. As the EXACT FRAME both cushions seal, all sound cuts instantly. One brief pressure-drop effect, then absolute silence.

**Audio**:

- Up to seal: Full chaos
- Exact moment of seal: One brief pressure-drop sound
- Immediately after: Absolute silence (no voices, slap sound, announcements, airport ambience, aircraft, rain, music or effects of any kind)

**Critical**: The sound cut must be instant and complete

---

### 8.2-11.3s — SILENT ARGUMENT AND SLAP

**Shot**: Over-the-shoulder close-up

**Action**: Main character's face seated man, one earcup visible in foreground. Couple keeps arguing visibly beside the windows, but nothing is heard. Girlfriend slaps, turns, shouts silently and delivers one clear open-handed slap. Show realistic movement, shocked expression.

**Audio**: COMPLETE SILENCE (no slap sound, no voices, no environmental noise)

**Visuals**:

- Boyfriend recoils slightly
- Looks shocked
- Then remains silent
- Arguing hand gestures
- No verbal escalation
- Airport employees look toward them
- Soundtrack remains completely silent

---

### 11.3-13.2s — PEACE RESTORED

**Shot**: Tight front three-quarter close-up

**Action**: Main character watches for a moment, gives a subtle satisfied smile and reclines. He closes his eyes while the exact headphones remain prominently visible and gleaming on his head.

**Audio**: Continued absolute silence

**Visuals**: Peaceful expression, satisfaction with noise-cancellation performance

---

### 13.2-15.05 — FINAL COMMERCIAL FRAME

**Shot**: Wide symmetrical airport shot

**Action**: Main character sleeps peacefully in foreground wearing the headphones. Couple continues an energetic but silent argument beside luggage. Aircraft taxis outside. Gentle backward zoom reveals negative space around him for text.

**Audio**: Continued absolute silence

**Visuals**:

- Peaceful sleeping figure with headphones
- Chaotic couple arguing silently in background
- Airport activity continuing
- Product prominence maintained
- Clean composition for end title card
