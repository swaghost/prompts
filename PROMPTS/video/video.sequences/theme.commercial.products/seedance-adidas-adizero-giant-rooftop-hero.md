# Seedance 2.5 - adidas Adizero EVO SL Giant Rooftop Hero

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 5-second vertical giant-scale shoe commercial in which a colossal braided runner sits on a skyscraper while one exact adidas Adizero EVO SL hangs over the rooftop edge toward a street-level camera. The depth map controls the scene geometry, while the shoe remains the sharp product anchor through a final forward swing.

## Usage

Perfect for athletic footwear launches, scale-driven sports advertising, urban performance campaigns, and dramatic social video. Ideal when a supplied depth map contains placeholder head or architectural volumes that must be selectively overridden by product and athlete references. This sequence emphasizes product fidelity, extreme perspective, and realistic weight transfer.

## Engines/Models

- **Video model:** Seedance 2.5
- **Interface:** CapCut Web or compatible Seedance workflow
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Frame rate:** 30fps
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- **@DEPTH:** Uploaded depth-map video; sole geometry reference except the stated hair override
- **@PRODUCT:** Exact adidas Adizero EVO SL shoe reference image
- **@ATHLETE:** Athlete identity, hairstyle, wardrobe, and body reference
- **@ENV:** Modern city-rooftop environment reference or description
