## Concept

## PRODUCT PHOTO PROMPT (GPT Image 2, Node 1)

E-commerce product photograph of a The North Face Nuptse down jacket in high-visibility acid yellow with ultraviolet purple and black blocking, photographed as an invisible-mannequin ghost shot: the jacket holds a filled three-dimensional body shape with no person inside, hollow at the collar and cuffs.

Glossy ripstop nylon shell with a fine visible grid weave and a wet-looking sheen that throws sharp specular highlights along every curve. Horizontal quilted baffle channels running across the chest, back and sleeves, each baffle heavily inflated and rounded so the surface swells and separates the light, the stitch lines pulling the shell in tight between them.

Colour blocking: the body is acid yellow, the upper chest and both sleeves are ultraviolet purple, and the lowest three baffles at the hem are black, each block meeting at a baffle seam so the colours change cleanly on the stitch line.

A high stand collar in black rises around the neck and holds its shape, filled and rounded like the body baffles. A full-length front zip in exposed black plastic teeth with a chunky pull, a concealed hem drawcord, elasticated black cuffs, and two zipped hand pockets set low with black tape trim. A small reflective silver box logo on the upper left chest and a matching reflective strip across the upper back.

Shot straight-on, centred, on a pure white seamless background. Lit by a large soft key light from the upper left and a broad fill from the right, so the glossy shell reads as strong specular bands along the crest of every baffle with deep shadow in the seams between. Gentle contact shadow beneath the hem. Baffle structure, colour block transitions, the zip teeth and the nylon grid weave sharp throughout.

## COMMERCIAL PROMPT (Omni Flash, Node 3)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

Use the attached image as the product reference. Preserve this exact garment throughout: the same glossy acid yellow, ultraviolet purple and black blocking, the same horizontal inflated baffles, the same high black stand collar, the same exposed black zip and reflective logo. The garment holds a filled three-dimensional body shape with no person inside, hollow at the collar and cuffs, the collar opening a pocket of pure black. The front zip is fully closed in every shot.

Deep black void with no floor, no walls and no visible surfaces. Thin haze drifts slowly through the space, invisible in the darkness and revealed only where a beam of light cuts through it. The light falls off sharply, so everything beyond the lit volume goes fully black.

### Shot 1

The shot opens on empty darkness. The jacket descends into frame from above, falling slowly and smoothly as if through water, the sleeves lifting and trailing during the drop, the hem flaring and settling as it slows. It eases to a stop floating upright in the centre, the baffles compressing softly and the hem swinging once as the motion runs out. It is lit from behind so it stands almost entirely in silhouette with a hard rim of light tracing the shoulders, the top edge of the stand collar and the crest of every baffle, the backlight fanning through the haze into wide visible shafts, the disturbed smoke rolling outward around it.

### Shot 2

Cut to macro across the chest baffles, the frame filled by horizontal inflated channels where the purple block meets the yellow. A hard key light sweeps slowly from the left: a hard specular band climbs the curve of each baffle in turn, races along its crest and drops away into the stitch line below, the acid yellow blowing out bright while the ultraviolet purple burns deep and saturated beside it. Smoke drifts through the beam as the camera moves right following the sweep, the colour change passing through frame on a single stitch line.

### Shot 3

Cut to a three-quarter angle from the front left, close on the stand collar and shoulder. A hard light swings across from the right, cutting a bright path through the smoke, and the whole shell blooms: the glossy nylon ignites into hard specular streaks along every baffle, the reflective logo flares hard and white, the black zip teeth catch a bright line down the centre front, and the black of the collar opening stays absolute against the glare.

Camera locked off in each shot except the macro drift. Product centred, generous black space around it.

### Look & Audio

**Look:** high-contrast product film, pure black background, zero ambient light, no props, no text.

**Audio:** no music, low room tone, a soft nylon rustle as the jacket settles, a low tonal swell on each cut.
