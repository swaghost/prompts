# Seedance 2.5 - SK-II GenOptics CC Primer Car-Roof Hero

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 5-second vertical product commercial in which an oversized SK-II GenOptics CC Primer rides on the roof of a decorated white Volkswagen Beetle along a bright Miami beachfront road. A depth map controls the exact camera geometry while the product, car, ribbons, ocean, and daylight remain photorealistic and commercially sharp.

## Usage

Perfect for skincare product launches, luxury beauty advertising, automotive-lifestyle campaigns, and short-form social product heroes. Ideal when a product must be composited into a moving environment while preserving a supplied depth-driven camera path. This sequence excels at creating scale, parallax, and a clean final product hold in 9:16.

## Engines/Models

- **Video model:** Seedance 2.5
- **Interface:** CapCut Web or compatible Seedance workflow
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Frame rate:** 30fps
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- **@DEPTH:** Uploaded depth-map video; sole geometry reference
- **@PRODUCT:** Exact SK-II GenOptics CC Primer product reference image
- **@CAR:** Clean white Volkswagen Beetle reference
- **@ENV:** Miami beachfront road and ocean environment reference or description

## Video Prompt

```text
[SEEDANCE 2.5 - DEPTH-DRIVEN PRODUCT HERO | 5s | 9:16 | 30fps]

REFERENCE CONTROL (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
@DEPTH is the uploaded depth map video. Use @DEPTH as the single source of truth for the entire shot: camera movement, camera height, focal length, framing, subject scale, subject screen position, entry timing, spatial layering, parallax, and depth falloff. Every frame's geometry must match @DEPTH 1:1. Do not reinterpret, retime, smooth, or improvise the camera path. @DEPTH controls geometry only, not lighting, color, or time of day. Final render is bright daylight.

@PRODUCT is the uploaded product reference image. Reproduce the product exactly as shown: identical shape, proportions, cap geometry, surface finish, color, label layout, and logo placement. Pixel-faithful identity. Do not redesign, restyle, simplify, re-letter, or reinterpret packaging. Do not generate the product from description; the reference image overrides text interpretation. Map @PRODUCT onto the large upright cylindrical volume on the car roof in @DEPTH, scaled hero-size, standing perfectly vertical with its front face angled toward camera, lit by hard daylight with a crisp specular edge.

LOCK REFERENCES
@CAR is a clean white Volkswagen Beetle in side-three-quarter profile, front pointing LEFT of frame, with glossy factory-white paint, chrome trim, chrome bumpers, and dark tinted glass. Wedding-car decoration consists only of two wide satin red ribbon bands running in a V from the centre of the hood outward to both front corners, plus one red satin bow at the centre of the hood. Ribbons have a matte-satin sheen and loose ends flutter in the breeze. No other decoration and no writing on the car.

@ENV is a Miami beachfront road in bright midday daylight: a wide sun-bleached asphalt coastal road parallel to the shore; immediately beyond it a low sea wall, open sandy beach, turquoise ocean with white surf, tall coconut palms with swaying fronds and long diagonal shadows across the asphalt, clear blue sky, light sea haze, ground heat shimmer, and distant pastel art-deco buildings soft and out of focus. The vertical pole element in @DEPTH maps to a coconut palm trunk. Empty road: no crowds and no traffic.

SHOT
Low camera near road level. The decorated white Beetle carries the oversized product on its roof and glides along the Miami beachfront while the camera tracks it into a centred hero frame.

BEATS (timestamps reset to zero)
0.00-0.60 - Empty sunlit asphalt fills the lower frame, coconut palms on the right, beach and turquoise ocean beyond. Subject is still outside the left edge. Strong foreground road texture and heat shimmer.
0.60-1.50 - The car with the oversized product on its roof enters from frame left, very close to lens. Massive scale, product dominating the left third. Strong parallax: road streaks fast, palms drift slowly.
1.50-2.70 - Camera widens and eases back. Full silhouette resolves: Beetle in side-three-quarter view, red hood ribbons clearly readable, product standing tall and centred on the roof. Sunlight rakes across white paint and glossy packaging.
2.70-4.00 - Slow push back in. Subject settles slightly left of centre. Product front face rotates fully toward camera and locks. Ribbon ends flutter in the breeze.
4.00-5.00 - Final hero hold. Car and product lock in frame with gentle forward drift only. Palm fronds sway, ocean glitters behind, and sun flare grazes the top-right corner. Product is razor sharp and fully readable.
```
