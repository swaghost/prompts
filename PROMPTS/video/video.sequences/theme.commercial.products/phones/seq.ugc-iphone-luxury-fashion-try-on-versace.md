# Video - UGC iPhone Luxury Fashion Try-On (Versace Dress Haul)

## Description of Resulting Video

Ultra-realistic handheld iPhone UGC video in luxury bedroom setting with soft natural daylight. A woman sits on a hotel bed with legs casually crossed holding a white iPhone with natural handheld movement and slight shake. She's wearing a fitted white tank top and white see-through lace mesh leggings while sharing her newest Versace dress purchase in a friendly, relatable, confident manner. The video progresses through three natural scenes: (1) introduction while holding phone (0-4s), (2) showing the dress on hanger with close-up details of luminous satin fabric, Medusa shoulder hardware, and designer tag (4-8s), and (3) after natural hand-swipe transition revealing the woman now wearing the complete Versace dress, turning 360 degrees to show all angles, lifting the slit, and finishing with happy smile and confident pose (8-15s).
