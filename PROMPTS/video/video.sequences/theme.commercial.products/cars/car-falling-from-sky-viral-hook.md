# Car Falling From Sky — Viral Hook

**Purpose:** Dramatic viral-reel hook with car drop impact and creator reveal  
**Platform:** ChatGPT Image 2 (character sheet) → Seedance 2.5 (video)  
**Format:** 1080p, 10 seconds  
**Style:** Cinematic viral social media content with realistic physics  
**Workflow:** Two-stage (character sheet generation → video generation)

---

## Workflow Overview

**Step 1:** Generate character reference sheet using ChatGPT Image 2  
**Step 2:** Generate video using Seedance 2.5 with character sheet + car image references

This is a two-stage pipeline for identity-locked video generation with dramatic product/scene integration.

---

## STEP 1 — Character Sheet Generation (ChatGPT Image 2)

Upload your selfie image and any reference outfit, then use this prompt:

### Character Sheet Prompt

```
Character reference sheet, wearing the reference outfit, multiple views: front, side, back, front close-up, top-down view. Subject: [your subject description].

Realistic photographic style, not illustrated or 3D rendered.

Clean light gray background. Views arranged in a grid layout like a professional character design sheet. Additional detail swatches at the bottom. Labels for each view.

Ultra detailed, photorealistic, cinematic quality, 35mm film grain, natural lighting.
```

**Output:** Character sheet with multiple angles for identity consistency in video generation.

---

## STEP 2 — Video Generation (Seedance 2.5)

Upload character sheet + car image into Seedance 2.5 video model (1080p, 10 seconds).

---

## Reference Locks

### @carimage — Exact Car + Location Reference

Preserve the same **black Mercedes G-Class–style vehicle**, its proportions, body design, wheels, color, exterior details and overall appearance.

Preserve the same **European city street location**, including the architecture, cobblestone street, sidewalks, buildings, lighting direction and overall environmental layout.

**Do not relocate the scene or redesign the car.**

### @charactersheet — Exact Character Identity Reference

Preserve his face, facial structure, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions, **red sweater, red baseball cap** and overall appearance consistently throughout the shot.

**Do not change his identity, clothing or physical appearance.**

### Reference Purpose

The references define the car, location and character only. Maintain realistic physical integration between the car and the environment.

```

The camera tilts down smoothly, tracking the car as it plummets, the car growing larger in frame as it approaches. The car slams down hard onto the ground with violent impact - suspension compresses deeply, bounces once, dust bursts outward from under the tires. Slight camera shake on impact. The camera follows his movement with a subtle dynamic push-in, maintaining him and the car clearly in frame.

As he settles onto the bonnet, the camera smoothly zooms in toward him for a strong close-up. He looks directly into the camera with an excited, confident expression and says, "Using Seedance 2.5." His delivery is punchy and enthusiastic, like a creator revealing the key trick behind the shot. Natural facial expressions, realistic body movement, believable weight and impact, seamless interaction with the car, cinematic timing, energetic viral-reel pacing.
```

## Sequence Breakdown

### Phase 1: Car Drop (0-5 seconds)

1. **Camera starts:** Tilted up at open sky
2. **Car appears:** Small and distant, falling fast toward ground
3. **Camera movement:** Tilts down smoothly, tracking the car as it plummets
4. **Car grows:** Larger in frame as it approaches
5. **Impact:** Car slams down hard onto ground
   - Suspension compresses deeply
   - Bounces once
   - Dust bursts outward from under tires
   - Slight camera shake on impact
6. **Camera locks:** Finishes tilt-down, locks into final framing as car settles perfectly still

### Phase 2: Creator Entrance (5-8 seconds)

1. **Character enters:** From right side with energetic, excited body language
2. **Runs toward car:** Enthusiastically says, "Here's how to make this viral hook in just a single generation!"
3. **Jumps onto bonnet:** One fluid, playful movement, lands naturally sitting confidently
4. **Camera follows:** Subtle dynamic push-in, maintaining character and car clearly in frame

### Phase 3: Close-Up Reveal (8-10 seconds)

1. **Camera zooms:** Smoothly zooms in toward character for strong close-up
2. **Direct address:** Looks directly into camera with excited, confident expression
3. **Punchline:** "Using Seedance 2.5."
4. **Delivery:** Punchy and enthusiastic, like a creator revealing the key trick
