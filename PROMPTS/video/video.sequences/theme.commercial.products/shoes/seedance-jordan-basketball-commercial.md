# Seedance 2.5 — 20-Second Jordan Basketball Shoe Commercial

**Complete Production Guide**

A premium cinematic basketball shoe commercial featuring an athletic character performing dynamic action sequences in a dark arena setting. 20 seconds, 15 scenes, rapid-fire editing with smash cuts and flash frames building from atmospheric anticipation to explosive power.

## Technical Specifications

- **Model:** Seedance 2.5 (via Syntx platform)
- **Duration:** 20 seconds
- **Scenes:** 15 distinct beats with transitions
- **Format:** Anamorphic 2.39:1 cinematic aspect ratio
- **Genre:** Premium product commercial
- **Workflow:** Character Generation → Character with Product → Video Generation

## The Concept

**One-liner:** A premium Jordan basketball shoe commercial that builds from quiet anticipation (spotlight snapping on empty court) through explosive athletic action (crossovers, drives, elevation) to hero product beauty shot (translucent sole glowing under spotlight) ending with confident walk-away into light.

**Why This Works:**

- Proven commercial structure: anticipation → action → product beauty → resolution
- Single location (basketball arena) = consistent environment
- One character = no casting drift across scenes
- Hero product (Jordan Luka 3 shoes with translucent blue midsole) = clear focal point
- Edit rhythm builds intensity: slow → explosive → percussive → quiet powerful exit

**Rule:** Premium sports commercial editing means hard cuts on beats, flash frames between scenes, slow motion at peak moments, and rhythmic pacing that feels like a heartbeat accelerating then resolving.

## The 3-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Character Generation (Neutral Base)

Generate clean casting photo with neutral studio lighting and seamless background. This becomes the identity anchor.

### Step 2: Character with Product/Wardrobe

Same character now in full basketball gear with Jordan shoes, in arena setting with dramatic lighting. This becomes the visual reference for video.

### Step 3: Video Generation (Multi-Scene Sequence)

20-second video prompt referencing character image, breaking down into 15 timed scenes with specific camera positions, movements, and transitions.

## Key Prompt Strategies

**Character Consistency:**

- "Exact character from the reference image" + detailed description = locks identity
- Repeat all wardrobe details = prevents costume drift
- "Identical in every frame" stated explicitly

**Lighting Consistency:**

- "Overhead arena spotlights" repeated throughout
- "Dramatic top-down key light" = direction locked
- "Cool rim lighting from behind" = secondary light specified
- "Atmospheric haze catching light beams" = depth element

**Camera Specificity:**

- Every scene states: camera position, height, angle, lens
- Movement verbs: tracking, pushing, dollying, arc, whip-pan
- "Smooth, intentional, motivated" = professional operation

**Edit Rhythm Language:**

- SMASH CUT, WHIP PAN, FLASH FRAME = transition types specified
- Timecodes in parentheses = hard beat boundaries
- "Building intensity from slow to explosive to quiet" = arc stated

**Physics & Realism:**

- "Real human biomechanics, natural weight transfer, realistic momentum"
- "Proper joint articulation, authentic athletic body mechanics"
- Compression, absorption, force, gravity = physics language

**Product Hero Moments:**

- Scene 4: Heel glow (translucent material backlit)
- Scene 9: Grip macro (outsole under pressure)
- Scene 13: Rotating beauty (the money shot, slow motion, maximum glow)

## Reusable Commercial Template

This structure works for any product hero commercial:

**Structure:**

1. **Anticipation (0-5s):** Environment intro, product tease, build tension
2. **Action (5-12s):** Dynamic movement, product in use, explosive energy
3. **Beauty (12-15s):** Hero product shot, slow motion, premium showcase
4. **Flash Cuts (15-17s):** Rapid percussive details, rhythm accelerates
5. **Resolution (17-20s):** Confident exit, fade to black, mood lands

**Customize by changing:**

- Character demographics and build
- Product (shoes → watch, sunglasses, tech device, etc.)
- Environment (arena → desert, studio, city street, etc.)
- Action (basketball → running, driving, climbing, etc.)
- Product feature (translucent sole → carbon fiber, LED, texture, etc.)

**Keep constant:**

- 3-step workflow (base → styled → video)
- Edit rhythm (slow → explosive → beauty → percussive → quiet)
- Lighting consistency language
- Physics/biomechanics realism
- Camera position specificity
