# The Black-Void Ad - Nike Air Max 1 Sneaker

**CLIENT 2B · PRODUCT COMMERCIALS / 3-STAGE CHAIN**

---

## Platform Chain

**GPT Image 2** (product photo) → **GPT Image 2** (catalogue sheet) → **Omni Flash** (commercial)

Chained in Picsart Flow

---

## Concept

Cinematic sneaker film from one photo. Three-node chain builds a black-void commercial where the shoe floats in darkness, backlight burns through the Air window, sweeping key reveals leather and suede contrast, dramatic close-ups against pure black.

---

## Workflow

1. Open [Picsart Flow](https://picsart.com/flow) → new canvas
2. **Node 1:** Image → GPT Image 2 → product photo prompt → generate
3. **Node 2:** Image → GPT Image 2 → attach Node 1 output → catalogue sheet prompt → generate
4. **Node 3:** Video → Omni Flash → attach Node 2 output → commercial prompt → generate
5. Run each node 2–3×, keep the best

## CATALOGUE SHEET PROMPT (GPT Image 2, Node 2)

Using the attached photograph as the product reference, build an e-commerce catalogue sheet for this Nike Air Max 1 sneaker in bone white and burnt ochre. Preserve the exact shoe in every panel: the same silhouette, the same bone leather panels and ochre suede mudguard and heel counter, the same stitched ochre swoosh, the same lacing and tongue, the same bone midsole with its transparent heel Air window, and the same gum outsole.

Lay out seven panels on a white sheet with thin white gutters between them, no borders, no text, no labels. Top row of three: outer lateral profile, inner medial profile, and a three-quarter view from the front left. Middle row of three: straight-on front, straight-on back showing the ochre heel counter and the Air window, and a top-down view looking into the collar and lacing. Bottom row, one wide panel: the shoe tilted up to show the full gum rubber waffle outsole.

The swoosh appears only on the outer and inner side panels and nowhere else. The transparent Air window stays clear and unobstructed in every panel where the heel is visible. Every panel on the same pure white background with the same soft even studio lighting and the same scale relationship, so the panels read as one consistent product set. Leather grain, suede nap and stitching sharp throughout.

quality: high

## COMMERCIAL PROMPT (Omni Flash, Node 3)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

Use the attached image as the product reference. Preserve this exact sneaker throughout: the same silhouette, the same bone leather and ochre suede, the same stitched swoosh, the same midsole and transparent heel Air window. The laces are neatly tied in a symmetrical double bow, the loops holding their shape and the ends tucked short — the shoe is fully laced and fastened in every shot.

Deep black void with no floor, no walls and no visible surfaces. Thin haze drifts slowly through the space, invisible in the darkness and revealed only where a beam of light cuts through it. The light falls off sharply, so everything beyond the lit volume goes fully black.

### Opening Macro

The shot opens in macro on the transparent Air window at the heel, filling the frame. A hard light rises behind the shoe and burns through the window from the far side, the clear unit lighting up and glowing through while the ochre suede around it stays dense and opaque and drinks the light, the internal curve of the chamber reading as a bright lens. Smoke drifts across the beam behind the window as the camera moves slowly left across the surface.

### Wide Lateral Profile

Cut to the full shoe in lateral profile, floating level in the darkness, lit only by a hard rim of light along its top edge with the body of the shoe in near silhouette. The backlight fans through the haze into visible shafts behind it. A key light sweeps in fast from the right and travels across the flank, tearing a bright path through the smoke as it comes, the leather panels lifting into soft even sheen as it passes while the suede swoosh stays matte and dark against them, and the Air window flaring bright for an instant, then the light settles low and the shoe holds still as the disturbed haze rolls slowly outward.

### Close Toe Box

Cut to a low three-quarter angle from the front, close on the toe box. The shoe tips forward toward the lens and stops hard, the tied bow snapping taut and the collar compressing, a bright highlight racing back along the stitched edge of the swoosh as it settles, a thin curl of smoke passing through the beam beside it.

Camera locked off in each shot except the opening drift. Product centred, generous black space around it.

### Look & Audio

**Look:** high-contrast product film, pure black background, zero ambient light, no props, no text.

**Audio:** no music, low room tone, a soft leather creak as the shoe settles, a low tonal swell on each cut.
