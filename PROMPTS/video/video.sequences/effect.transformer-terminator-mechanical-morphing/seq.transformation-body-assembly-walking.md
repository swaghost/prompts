# Ultra-Realistic Body Assembly Transformation - Video Prompt Guide

## Overview

Ultra-realistic cinematic transformation video featuring a woman assembling herself into existence from the ground up, part by part, while continuously walking forward. Raw handheld footage aesthetic with natural lighting, ASMR sound design, and extreme anatomical detail progression.

## Core Concept

A stunning curvy Levi model with warm sun-kissed light-brown tanned skin materializes from nothing - building upward from feet to full body while maintaining continuous forward walking motion. The transformation happens mechanically/robotically in sequential layers, with each body part forming through quick progressive builds before the next part forms above it.

**Key Principle:** At NO point does a full body or full robot exist at the start - ONLY feet exist initially, walking on pavement with NOTHING above them (empty air, no legs, no torso, no body). Each new body part builds ONE AT A TIME from the bottom up while she keeps walking.

## Visual Specifications

### Camera & Technical Settings

- **Frame Rate:** 60fps at NATURAL real-life speed
- **Camera Style:** Real raw handheld footage with constant visible handheld shake
- **Motion:** Natural sway and micro-jitter the ENTIRE time
- **Movement:** NO slow motion, NO speed ramps, everything moving at real everyday tempo
- **Lighting:** Natural true-to-life color grade, soft muted overcast street tones (NOT warm golden, NOT oversaturated)
- **Sound Design:** Immersive ASMR natural sound only - footsteps on pavement as a normal walking rhythm, faint mechanical clicks and servo whirs as each part forms
- **Quality:** Ultra-realistic RAW skin with visible pores, peach fuzz, faint freckles, natural warm light-brown tone
- **Color:** Soft muted overcast color grade, balanced exposure, subtle sensor grain, smooth 60fps
- **Look:** Realistic optical depth of field, NO CGI-cartoon look, no oversaturation, no warm golden tones

### Shot Composition & Camera Movement

**Primary Shot: 0.0-1.5s - EXTREME LOW GROUND-LEVEL CLOSE-UP**
- ONLY her TWO FEET exist, walking mid-stride at normal pace on pavement
- Completely EMPTY AIR above the ankles - no legs, no body
- Feet form on the move, mechanical robotic feet with servos
- Black heels striding forward in form, still striding forward
- Nothing exists above them

**Progressive Build Sequence (timing in seconds):**

1. **1.5-3.0s — ANKLES AND CALVES**
   - Build upward from walking feet, completely in warm light-brown tanned skin
   - Mechanized robotic limb extrude upward, muscle-building
   - Real human calves, legs still walking
   - Above the knees: empty air

2. **3.0-4.5s — KNEES AND THIGHS**
   - Build upward, robotic frame extends up
   - Muscle and movement, high-cut hem of black ruched mandarin dress with high high slit
   - Above the hips: nothing yet, she keeps walking

3. **4.5-6.0s — HIPS, BELLY AND LOWER TORSO**
   - Build upward, mechanical torso rising up
   - Fabric of black dress forming, out-waist detail
   - Above the waist: empty

4. **6.0-7.5s — UPPER TORSO, CHEST AND ARMS**
   - Build upward, robotic frame up
   - Breasts and muscles, black dress forming over chest and shoulders
   - Hands in grip: Rolex and rings, sweatshirt neckline and straps
   - Above shoulders: empty air, still striding

5. **7.5-9.0s — NECK AND LOWER FACE**
   - Build upward from shoulders
   - Mechanical neck frame rises, chin and cat-eye forming
   - She is now FULLY assembled and human
   - Above the head: high dark ponytail still growing out

6. **9.0-10.0s — FULL HEAD AND HAIR**
   - Skull structure rises, sheaths into complete face
   - Gold hoops and sunglasses appearing, pushed up on her head
   - Long dark hair growing out into a sleek high ponytail
   - She is now FULLY assembled and exact human, matching @image

7. **10.0-15.0s — FINAL WALK**
   - Camera cranes OUT to full low-angle body shot
   - Walking at same natural pace toward and past camera
   - Fully formed, calm and unbothered
   - Chin lifted, long dark ponytail swaying
   - Black handbag in hand, pedestrians on both sides turning to watch

## Technical Execution Guidelines

### Camera Operation

**Movement Rules:**
- Camera cranes UPWARD in sync with the build
- Tracking back with her stride
- Always tracking her continuous natural-paced forward walk
- 0-10s: then out 10-15s
- NO slow motion
- NO speed ramping
- Constant visible shake

**Framing Progression:**
- Start: EXTREME LOW GROUND-LEVEL (only feet visible)
- Progress: Camera rises with body build
- End: Low-angle full body shot as she walks past

### Build Mechanics

**Assembly Rules:**
- Mechanical/robotic extrude first
- Then immediately flesh/skin layers
- Quick layers building each part
- NO floating frame
- NO pre-existing robot skeleton
- Each part forms through quick layers
- Looking into the FINAL real human form BEFORE next part forms above it

**Critical Constraints:**
- At every moment ONLY the already-built parts exist
- Everything above current build point = empty air, no full robot ever visible
- NO external parts flying in
- Each part building on the last through mechanical-skin-fabric layers
- All while she walks continuously at natural real-life speed
