# Elite Urban Running Commercial - 12-Shot Vertical Sequence

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 20-second vertical elite-running commercial filmed across rain-soaked urban concrete. Twelve fast, story-driven shots move from pre-run focus through ground-level sprint detail and urban endurance to a final exhausted recovery beat. The visual language is premium sportswear advertising: raw, kinetic, tactile, and never over-produced.

## Usage

Use for performance-running campaigns, sportswear launches, athlete reels, footwear or apparel commercials, and urban-endurance storytelling. The sequence is designed for a single lead runner and one consistent rain-soaked city route, with fast edits that retain believable biomechanics and spatial continuity.

## Engines/Models

Seedance 2.5, Veo, Kling, Runway, Sora, or an equivalent video generator with reference-image support and editable shot timing.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- **Dependency 1 - Runner reference:** A clear head-to-toe reference of the lead runner, including footwear, apparel, hairstyle, and accessories.
- **Dependency 2 - Product reference (optional):** Exact footwear or apparel reference if a branded or specific product must remain consistent.
- **Dependency 3 - Location reference:** A rain-soaked urban route with wet concrete, underpasses, walls, road markings, and practical city lighting.
- Keep the runner, wardrobe, footwear, rain intensity, route materials, and cool overcast grade consistent across all shots.

## Camera and Cinematography

- **Lenses:** 24mm for ground-level and POV energy; 35mm for route context; 50mm for running profile and footwear; 85mm for pressure, breath, and compression.
- **Motion:** Handheld pursuit, side tracking, backward tracking, body-mounted POV, and motivated whip pans.
- **Focus:** Allow brief autofocus hunting and lens breathing; focus must recover on the runner or footwear before each shot resolves.
- **Edit Rhythm:** Flash cuts and whip pans connect shots. Use motion blur only where the camera or subject is moving quickly.

## Lighting and Materials

- Cool overcast daylight with hard-to-soft reflections from wet concrete and rain.
- Rain remains present throughout; no sudden weather shift.
- Preserve tactile wet fabric, skin pores, water on hair, shoe texture, concrete grit, and water displacement.
- Keep the grade neutral and low contrast with restrained halation; no neon, HDR gloss, or stylized color washes.
