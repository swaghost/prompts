# Born to Fight - Hayabusa x Marvel Studios Captain America Edition

A 12-shot, 15-second Seedance 2.5 boxing-sports product campaign built around one East Asian female boxer and one exact pair of Hayabusa x Marvel Studios Captain America limited-edition gloves.

## Product Lock

- Navy blue premium leather
- Raised silver metallic five-pointed star on the knuckle panel
- Geometric white and deep crimson red leather sections
- White and silver metallic wrist strap
- Red-and-white circular Avengers A badge
- Marvel Studios Powered by Hayabusa wrist branding
- Every seam, stitch, panel, thread, and surface grain remains accurate

## Character Lock

- Adult East Asian female boxer, approximately 5'10", lean and muscular
- Warm honey skin, visible pores and veins
- Long thick braids
- White sports bra and white high-waisted biker shorts
- Deep crimson red high-top boxing boots with white laces
- Same face, age, hair, outfit, body proportions, and gloves throughout

## Technical Direction

- **Platform:** Seedance 2.5
- **Format:** 15 seconds, 8K cinematic
- **Camera:** Anamorphic, macro detail, static or controlled movement as specified
- **Lighting:** Dark boxing gym, single overhead spotlight, volumetric beams, chalk and dust
- **Palette:** Navy, deep crimson red, silver, white, black gym shadows
