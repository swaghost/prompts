# Bench Press Anatomy and Technique Visualization

## Description

A premium 10-second fitness-anatomy CGI sequence using a fixed matte-white faceless male mannequin to visualize incorrect and corrected bench-press technique. The video isolates excessive elbow flare and shoulder stress in RED, then shows the corrected arm path and muscle activation in CYAN-BLUE across a controlled repetition, finishing with a clean anatomical hero shot.

## Usage

Ideal for sports-science education, strength-training instruction, fitness-app demonstrations, coaching content, exercise-form analysis, and premium gym or athletic-brand campaigns. Use it when the goal is to make technique errors, joint stress, muscle activation, and corrective movement visually understandable without showing a real person's face.
