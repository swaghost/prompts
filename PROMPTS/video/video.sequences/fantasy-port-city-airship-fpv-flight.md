# Fantasy Port City Airship FPV Flight

**Format:** 60-90 seconds, ultra-high-quality animation  
**Type:** First-person FPV flight sequence  
**Setting:** Spectacular white and gold fantasy port city  
**Style:** Overwhelming speed, cinematic drone racing, smooth camera work

---

## CONCEPT

60-90 second super high-quality first-person perspective flight sequence through massive fantasy port city dominated by white marble and gold accents. Camera operates as FPV drone-style from single-seater flight ship piloted by woman (pilot not visible in frame). Route begins at sea surface level, accelerates through blue harbor weaving between ships/piers/port buildings, rockets toward enormous cathedral rushing along main tower, explodes upward into sky with dramatic low-angle view, transitions to magnificent aerial overview of entire cityscape/port/mountains, performs clockwise orbiting slew around central pagoda/tower showcasing white palaces/blue ocean/bridges/harbors, then decelerates while flying along right side of city past canal roads/plazas/piers/gold buildings/glass-roofed structures and harbor streets. Emphasizes overwhelming speed, smooth motion blur, realistic atmospheric effects (air, water reflections, sunlight, volumetric clouds), grand cinematic scale, ultra-high brightness, three-dimensional depth. **Critical: No UI elements** (no red lines, arrows, annotations, or visible characters in finished video).

## NEGATIVE PROMPT

**Avoid:**

### UI/HUD Elements (Critical):

- Red lines, arrows, annotations
- Visible characters (pilot shown)
- Text overlays, labels
- HUD displays, meters, indicators
- Targeting reticles, waypoints

### Visual Errors:

- Low quality rendering (muddy textures)
- Jagged edges (aliasing)
- Pop-in (objects appearing suddenly)
- Clipping (camera through geometry)
- Flickering (lighting/shadow issues)

### Motion Errors:

- Jerky camera (unsmooth)
- Excessive motion blur (unrealistic)
- Jittery flight path (not fluid)
- Unnatural physics (impossible moves breaking immersion)

### Style Errors:

- Dark, gloomy (must be bright)
- Muted colors (must be vibrant)
- Flat clouds (must be volumetric)
- No reflections (water must reflect)
- Modern city elements (cars, neon, contemporary)

### Technical Errors:

- Low resolution (pixelation)
- Compression artifacts
- Low frame rate (choppy)
- Poor lighting (flat, unnatural)
