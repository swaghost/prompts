## Video Prompt

Create a seamless cinematic keyframe-to-keyframe video sequence through an alpine retreat and mountain landscape.

**Duration:** Generate as three connected motion segments between Frame 1 -> Frame 2, Frame 2 -> Frame 3, and Frame 3 -> Frame 4.

**Style:** Ultra-photorealistic cinematic architectural and landscape motion, late golden-hour light, stabilized camera movement, realistic parallax, subtle breeze, refined material detail, natural high dynamic range, no morphing.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
The sequence begins inside an elegant minimalist retreat with a seated woman, sculptural cream lounge chair, warm-brown matte walls, organic grass and wildflowers in the floor, floor lamp, furniture, plants, and a large circular opening framing a Dolomite-style alpine landscape. It then physically travels through the circular opening into the outdoor landscape and continues as a drone-like flight toward the mountain summit.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

Frame 1 -> Frame 2
Create a seamless cinematic camera movement between the two exact keyframes. Begin from the composition of Frame 1 and slowly move the camera laterally and slightly forward, creating a subtle orbital movement around the seated woman while maintaining her natural position in the armchair. Preserve her body, face, clothing, chair, furniture, lamp, plants, and every interior element. Use realistic parallax between foreground furniture, vegetation, the woman, the circular opening, and distant mountain. The floor lamp remains illuminated. Small indirect ambient lights softly illuminate the room. Grass, flowers, and plants move gently in a natural breeze. End precisely at the perspective and composition of Frame 2.

Frame 2 -> Frame 3
Begin exactly from Frame 2. The camera slowly moves forward toward the large circular opening, then smoothly accelerates and physically passes through it into the outdoor landscape. The camera must actually travel through the opening; do not dissolve, fade, morph, or teleport. As the camera crosses the opening, the circular wall and interior elements pass the frame edges with strong parallax while the mountain landscape grows larger. Once outside, transition naturally into smooth cinematic drone-like forward flight, descending slightly toward the meadow before stabilizing above the terrain. Grass, wildflowers, leaves, and branches move gently and organically in wind. Preserve the same mountain silhouette, rock formations, vegetation, lake, terrain, sunlight direction, golden-hour atmosphere, and visual style. End precisely at Frame 3.

Frame 3 -> Frame 4
Begin exactly from the wide landscape composition of Frame 3 and continue the same cinematic drone flight. Move rapidly but smoothly forward across the alpine meadow toward the mountain, gradually gaining altitude as the peak approaches. Use stable, controlled, physically believable high-end FPV or cinematic drone movement. Create strong progressive parallax: foreground grass, flowers, and rocks pass beneath first, followed by trees and terrain, while the mountain grows larger. Gently increase altitude and angle the camera upward so the summit becomes the focal point. Vegetation moves organically but subtly. Preserve the same mountain silhouette, peaks, rock structure, snow patterns, proportions, lighting, and atmosphere. Gradually reduce speed near the summit and end very close to the upper peak, with the summit centered and clean open sky above it, matching Frame 4 exactly.

**Camera:**
Physical camera movement only. Use lateral movement, subtle orbit, forward push, real passage through the circular portal opening, drone-like forward flight, gradual climb, and controlled deceleration. No cuts inside a segment, no fades, no dissolves, no teleportation, no sudden acceleration, and no object transformations.

**Lighting:**
Late golden-hour natural light with warm sunset illumination from the left, soft amber floor-lamp glow inside, realistic interior/exterior exposure balance, atmospheric haze around distant mountains, and consistent sunlight direction across all frames.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
Use true 3D depth and parallax. Keep the woman, interior architecture, circular opening, furniture, plants, landscape, lake, mountain silhouette, rock formations, snow, vegetation, and terrain visually consistent with the keyframes.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
Photorealistic 16:9 cinematic output with stable perspective, realistic material detail, strong natural depth, consistent keyframe matching, subtle vegetation motion, no warped circular architecture, no mountain changes, no newly appearing objects, no landscape transformation, and no text, logos, or watermarks.
