# ASMR Orb World Injection: Birth of the Obsidian Realm

## Concept Title

**ASMR Orb World Injection: Birth of the Obsidian Realm**

## Scene Description

A pristine, empty **crystal orb** sits on an ornate volcanic-stone stand. The environment is dark, quiet, and atmospheric — soft ASMR ambience with faint room-tone. A sleek syringe enters frame, filled with a glowing molten serum. As the needle pierces the orb's surface, the interior transforms into a miniature volcanic world: bubbling lava rivers, erupting micro-volcanoes, glowing magma veins, and shimmering obsidian shards forming a dramatic landscape. The orb becomes a living, breathing molten planet.

## Injection Liquid

- **Color:** Deep molten orange with glowing red cores and subtle gold flecks
- **Particles:** Micro-ember fragments, drifting ash specks, slow-moving magma globules
- **Viscosity:** Thick, syrup-like lava serum; slow-flowing, heavy, glossy, high-density fluid
