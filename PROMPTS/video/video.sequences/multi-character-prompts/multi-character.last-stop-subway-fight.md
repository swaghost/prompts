# LAST STOP — Subway Fight Sequence

**MULTI-CHARACTER ACTION / TRAIN FIGHT CHOREOGRAPHY**

---

## Platform Chain

**Image References (@image 1, @image 2, @image 3)** → **Video Generation Platform** (Seedance 2.5 or similar multi-reference platform)

---

## Concept

A 45-second hyper-realistic Hollywood-style train fight sequence where a lone woman takes down five goons in close-quarters combat inside a moving subway car. Uses character consistency across multiple attackers, practical stunt choreography, and confined-space fight dynamics with dialogue throughout.

---

## Workflow

1. Prepare three reference images:
   - @image 1: Main female character (consistent face, outfit)
   - @image 2: Subway train interior (location/environment)
   - @image 3: Five goons (all distinct faces and builds)
2. Upload all three references to platform
3. Use shot-by-shot breakdown or master prompt
4. Generate 45-second sequence with dialogue
5. Maintain character consistency throughout all shots

---

## Story Overview

Late-night moving train. @image 1 sits on one side bench. @image 3 (five goons) already seated opposite. Silent tension escalates to verbal warning from leader. @image 1 remains calm, stands first, and systematically takes down each goon using the train's narrow aisle, seats, window reflections, and body leverage. Train continues moving, doors never open. @image 1 remains standing inside as final victor.
