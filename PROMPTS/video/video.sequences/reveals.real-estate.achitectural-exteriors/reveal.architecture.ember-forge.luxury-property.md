# Luxury Property Ember Forge Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** ember-forged architecture
**Reveal Mechanism:** heat lines trace and forge the structure before cooling into final materials
**Sequence Type:** reference-match exterior transformation

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second dusk reveal where controlled embers trace the final building, forge its shell, and cool into a photorealistic reference-matched property.

## Usage

For dramatic luxury launches, architecture campaigns, premium construction brands, and dusk property reels.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with particle-path control, heat-to-material conversion, and stable final geometry.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site dusk plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: dusk site, ember outline, forged shell, cooled materials, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Ember Forge

Embers follow architectural edges rather than forming a random firestorm. Heat creates geometry in order; cooling changes material appearance without reshaping the building.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous dramatic luxury property reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty lot at dusk from the same camera direction as @reference.
1.0-3.0s: Restrained streams of glowing embers and heat lines trace the exact footprint, walls, roof, windows, and facade edges in the air.
3.0-6.5s: The traced structure forges into stable architectural geometry from foundation upward. Walls and roof become solid while glowing at their edges.
6.5-8.4s: The glow cools surface by surface into the exact concrete, stone, timber, metal, and glass of @reference.
7.5-9.2s: Landscaping forms and exterior lights activate naturally in the dusk scene.
9.2-10.0s: End on the complete photorealistic property with a restrained hero hold or glide.

Realistic heat shimmer, falling sparks, cooling surfaces, and dusk reflections. No people, text, logos, cuts, uncontrolled fire, burning landscape, explosions, smoke obstruction, molten geometry, random embers, architecture drift, or residual glowing seams.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Ember paths trace exact reference geometry.
- [ ] Structure forms in logical foundation-to-roof order.
- [ ] Fire remains controlled and does not burn the site.
- [ ] Cooling preserves geometry and yields correct materials.
- [ ] Landscape and lighting match `@reference`.
- [ ] No glowing seams remain in the hero frame.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
