# Luxury Property Blueprint Sheet Spiral-Fold Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** floating drawing-sheet assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** architectural sheets spiral inward, fold into a paper model, and convert into final architecture
**Sequence Type:** reference-match drawing-to-reality reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second reveal where plans and floor-plan sheets spiral over an empty site, fold into the referenced property, and become full-scale photoreal architecture.

## Usage

For architecture firms, planning presentations, design-process storytelling, and luxury development campaigns.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with paper simulation, controlled folding, scale continuity, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional plan-sheet graphics derived from the design.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: floating sheets, spiral, folded model, material conversion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Blueprint Sheet Spiral Fold

Sheets remain readable as architectural drawings, spiral without chaos, and fold into connected planes. The paper model matches final geometry before conversion.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic architectural reveal.

0.0-1.2s: Start with an empty site and architectural drawings and floor-plan sheets floating above it.
1.2-3.5s: The papers spiral inward in an elegant organized motion, preserving visible plan linework and avoiding collisions.
3.5-6.2s: Sheets fold and layer into the rough but accurate three-dimensional shape of the property: walls, roof, terraces, openings, and site planes.
6.2-8.7s: The paper model expands and transforms into real materials, windows, landscaping, pool, and lighting, matching @reference without geometry change.
8.7-10.0s: End on the completed photorealistic property.

No people, text overlays, logos, cuts, random loose papers, unreadable clutter, torn sheets, paper airplanes, disconnected folds, visible final paper, abrupt scale jump, or architecture drift.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Sheets read as architectural drawings.
- [ ] Spiral remains organized and collision-safe.
- [ ] Folded model forms one connected property.
- [ ] Model matches reference silhouette before conversion.
- [ ] Paper and linework disappear completely.
- [ ] Final architecture and landscape match `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is continuous and 10 seconds long.
