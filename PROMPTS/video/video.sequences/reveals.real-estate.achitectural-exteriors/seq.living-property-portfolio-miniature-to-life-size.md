# The Living Property Portfolio - Miniature to Life-Size Real-Estate Reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A two-part cinematic real-estate video workflow. Part 1 presents a leather-bound property portfolio in a premium 3D animated vertical format, where each page unfolds into a photoreal miniature property. Part 2 begins from a captured frame of the full miniature villa and continuously transforms it into a full-size luxury home, ending with a rooftop hero reveal and optional broker logo card.

## Usage

Perfect for real-estate brokers, property developers, luxury villa campaigns, rental portfolios, architectural visualization, and vertical social-media property marketing. Use it when a listing needs to move from brochure detail and pricing into an emotionally persuasive, life-size property experience. The modular property pages can be customized for local listings, including Raipur, Chhattisgarh.

## Engines/Models

Suitable for image-to-video and cinematic 3D video systems such as Seedance, Kling, Runway, Veo, Sora, Higgsfield, or comparable tools. Part 1 may be generated as a 3D animated vertical video; Part 2 requires the screenshot captured from Part 1 as the starting reference image.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Dependency 1 - Property listing data: property name, price, area, BHK, amenities, rating, and description.
- Dependency 2 - Optional local branding: broker or builder name, tagline, office address, mobile number, RERA number, logo, and Raipur skyline reference.
- Dependency 3 - Part 1 output: the generated portfolio video containing the miniature villa reveal.
- Dependency 4 - Screenshot handoff: capture the frame where the full miniature villa with pool, rooftop terrace, and glowing interiors is clearly visible over the open book.
- Dependency 5 - Part 2 reference image: use the captured frame as the starting image for the miniature-to-life-size transformation.
- Dependency 6 - Editing software: assemble the selected Part 1 opening and Part 2 continuation in a video editor.

## Workflow Overview

1. Generate Part 1 using the complete portfolio prompt below.
2. Review the result and identify the strongest frame where the full miniature villa is visible over the book.
3. Take a screenshot of that exact frame. This is the handoff frame and the first frame for Part 2.
4. Generate Part 2 using the screenshot as the uploaded image reference.
5. In editing software, cut Part 1 immediately before or at the screenshot handoff point and place Part 2 directly after it.
6. Check that the cut preserves the same villa geometry, pool, rooftop terrace, lighting direction, and camera axis.

## Part 1 - The Living Property Portfolio

### Format

Cinematic 3D animated vertical video (9:16), Octane Render quality, shallow depth of field, luxury architectural lighting.

### Core Concept

A rich, leather-bound property portfolio rests on a polished marble or dark walnut table, beside a pair of reading glasses and a fountain pen. Pages turn in a slow, weighty flip - leather-textured, with a soft embossed crest on the cover. As each page settles, a 3D miniature pop-up of the property (house exterior, or a specific room) rises up from the paper like fine architectural paper-engineering, fully rendered and photoreal, with tiny working details - miniature lights turning on in windows, tiny landscaping, a small driveway with a car - while the surrounding page holds the property's name/title, price, sqft, amenities, and rating in refined serif typography.

### Visual Style

**Lighting:** warm gold desk-lamp key light mixed with a cool blue "dusk" rim light on the model - gives it a premium real-estate-brochure feel.

**Paper texture:** cream/ivory linen-finish paper with a thin gold-foil border and a subtle architectural blueprint watermark faintly visible under the text.

**Typography:** elegant serif headers, fine-print amenity icons (bed, bath, sqft, parking) etched in thin gold linework.

**Background:** blurred bokeh of a city skyline at dusk (can be Raipur skyline for local branding) or a soft-focus garden/interior, giving an aspirational feel.

**Color palette:** deep navy/charcoal + gold + warm amber - shifts slightly cooler/moodier for "premium villa" listings and warmer/brighter for "affordable flats," subtly signaling price tier.

**Camera:** slow dolly-in with a gentle orbit around the miniature model, like an architect admiring a scale model.

### Page Mechanics (per property)

Page turns with a heavier, leather-bound flip (visible paper grain, slower fall, soft embossed shadow).

Page lands flat - a fold-crease pop-up mechanism unfolds from the spine like architectural pop-up paper art.

A 3D miniature model rises and unfolds panel by panel (walls lifting, roof folding into place) then solidifies into a fully rendered, photoreal miniature - tiny lights glowing in windows, subtle steam/mist for realism, a miniature tree swaying.

Surrounding page text: Property Name/Title (bold serif), one-line description, 5-star "Location Rating," Price in bold gold/navy ribbon banner, sqft + BHK + amenities in a clean icon row.

Camera slowly orbits the miniature (like a drone flyaround at tiny scale) before the next page turn begins.

### Sequence of Properties

Customize the property details and miniature architecture per broker's listings.

**2BHK COMPACT FLAT - ₹ 12,000/month**

Pop-up: A miniature apartment building rises panel-by-panel, one floor's window glowing warmly, a tiny balcony with a plant.

**3BHK FAMILY APARTMENT - ₹ 22,000/month**

Pop-up: A slightly larger miniature building unfolds, complete with a tiny rooftop garden and a small parking bay with a miniature car.

**LUXURY VILLA (Standalone) - ₹ 85 Lakh (Sale)**

Pop-up: A detailed miniature villa exterior rises with a tiny swimming pool that has a subtle rippling-water shader, landscaped hedges, and a driveway gate.

**COMMERCIAL SHOP SPACE - ₹ 35,000/month**

Pop-up: A miniature storefront pops up with a glowing signboard (blank/customizable), glass shutter reflecting soft light.

**PENTHOUSE SUITE - ₹ 1.2 Cr (Sale)**

Pop-up: A miniature high-rise top-floor unit rises with a glass balcony overlooking a tiny city skyline, subtle warm interior glow visible through window panels.

**PLOT / LAND PARCEL - ₹ 45 Lakh**

Pop-up: A miniature terrain block rises with a paper-fold boundary fence unfolding, small survey-marker flags popping up at corners.

**GATED COMMUNITY ROW HOUSE - ₹ 65 Lakh**

Pop-up: A row of miniature connected houses unfolds like an accordion, tiny street lamps flickering on along a shared pathway.

**STUDIO / 1BHK RENTAL - ₹ 8,000/month**

Pop-up: A cozy miniature single-room unit rises, tiny curtains fluttering, a small reading lamp glowing in the window.

### Final Page - Schedule a Visit

The last page turn reveals a formal, gold-embossed closing page like a broker's signature/certificate page:

- Broker/Builder Name & Tagline: e.g. "Your Trusted Address in Raipur."
- Address: [Office address, Raipur, Chhattisgarh]
- Mobile Number: [Contact number]
- RERA Number (if applicable): small fine-print line for credibility
- Banner: subtle glowing ribbon stamp - "Site Visit Available" or "Limited Units Left"

The book gently closes, an embossed crest or builder logo glowing faintly on the leather cover before the frame fades to black.

### Camera Movement

Slow, weighty dolly mimicking a serious buyer flipping through - gliding downward/inward with each page, never jerking. Subtle rack-focus pulls between the printed detail text and the rising miniature model. Lighting shifts from warm-amber (affordable/rental listings) to cooler navy-gold dusk tones (luxury/premium listings) as the portfolio progresses - visually signaling the property tier without needing extra text.

## Screenshot Handoff Frame

Take the screenshot when the full home is visible over the open book. The preferred frame shows the complete miniature pop-up villa, including its pool, rooftop terrace, glowing interiors, leather-bound book, and enough of the blurred city skyline to preserve the lighting and spatial reference.

Use this frame as the first image reference for Part 2. Do not select a frame where the villa is still unfolding, where the page is turning, or where the home is partially hidden by typography or foreground objects.

## Part 2 - Miniature to Life-Size Transformation

### Master Prompt

```text
Style: Photorealistic, cinematic, shallow depth of field transitioning to deep focus, warm golden-hour-to-dusk lighting, luxury real estate commercial aesthetic, seamless miniature-to-life-size transformation, smooth motion, no jump cuts - each scene flows into the next via continuous camera movement or match-cut zoom.

Scene 1 (0:00-0:02) - "The Reveal"
Start exactly on the uploaded frame: a miniature pop-up book villa with pool, rooftop terrace, and glowing interiors sitting on a leather-bound book, blurred city skyline behind, fountain pen resting in foreground. Camera holds static for a beat, then begins a slow, deliberate push-in toward the pool and living area of the tiny model. Golden lamp light flickers warmly at frame edge.

Scene 2 (0:02-0:04) - "The Zoom Begins"
Camera continues pushing in aggressively toward the miniature pool deck. As it approaches, the paper textures, gold foil trim, and miniature proportions dissolve/morph - edges soften, scale distorts - the dollhouse begins expanding into full-size architecture. Motion blur increases. The book pages and desk fall out of frame naturally as the "world" grows around the camera.

Scene 3 (0:04-0:06) - "Full Scale Arrival"
Transformation completes: camera is now at true human eye-level, standing at the edge of a real infinity pool at dusk, water rippling, loungers now full-size, glass-walled living room glowing warm light to the left. Camera glides forward low over the pool surface, reflections of the illuminated home shimmering on the water.

Scene 4 (0:06-0:08) - "Interior Glide"
Camera rises slightly and glides through the open glass sliding doors into the sunken living room - real leather sofas, warm pendant lighting, floor-to-ceiling windows framing the now-massive city skyline outside. Smooth lateral tracking shot past the furniture toward the staircase leading to the rooftop terrace.

Scene 5 (0:08-0:10) - "Rooftop Finale"
Camera ascends the stairs in one continuous motion and emerges onto the rooftop terrace with loungers and skyline view, now bathed in blue-hour light with city lights twinkling below. Final wide pull-back reveals the entire real-size luxury home silhouetted against the skyline, ending on a slow crane-up shot. Fade to soft glow / logo card.
```

### Camera and Continuity Requirements

- Begin Part 2 on the uploaded screenshot with the villa composition, camera axis, and lighting direction preserved.
- Hold static briefly before the push-in so the handoff frame reads clearly.
- Keep the pool and living area as the visual target throughout the zoom.
- Use a continuous push-in and match-cut scale transition; do not jump to a new viewpoint.
- Transition from shallow depth of field to deep focus as the world reaches life size.
- Preserve the villa's geometry, pool placement, rooftop terrace, glowing interiors, and skyline relationship.
- Finish with a clean wide view suitable for a logo card or broker end slate.

### Negative Prompt

```text
jump cuts, disconnected shots, camera teleportation, changing villa design, changing pool shape, missing rooftop terrace, inconsistent interiors, warped architecture, melting geometry, unstable scale, duplicated furniture, floating objects, broken glass, impossible reflections, unreadable text, random signage, extra buildings, extra pools, people, crowds, visible hands, flicker, jitter, frame skipping, excessive motion blur, low resolution, cartoon style, illustration, game-engine look, logo, watermark, subtitles, horizontal format
```

## Editing Assembly

1. Import the Part 1 portfolio video into the timeline.
2. Find the moment where the complete miniature villa is fully visible over the book.
3. Capture that frame and use the screenshot as the Part 2 image reference.
4. Trim Part 1 so the final visible frame is the selected handoff frame, or place the cut immediately after it if the editor preserves the same frame on both clips.
5. Place the generated Part 2 clip directly after the handoff frame.
6. Use a straight cut or a very short match-cut transition only if it does not interrupt the continuous push-in.
7. Match exposure, color temperature, and crop between clips. Keep the final output vertical 9:16.
8. Add the broker or builder logo card only after the rooftop finale has completed and the camera has settled.
