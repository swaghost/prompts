# Apartment Development Glass-Cube Growth and Release Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-development exterior
**Reveal Effect:** glass-cube contained growth and full-site release
**Reveal Mechanism:** a development grows to completion within a site-sized transparent cube before the cube dissolves and the contained geometry expands to its final footprint
**Sequence Type:** reference-match architectural scale-transition reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where a compact development grows inside a transparent cube, the enclosure elegantly cracks and dissolves, and the complete design expands coherently across its full site as entries and landscaping spread into place.

## Usage

For master-planned apartment launches, development overview films, and architectural concept presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with transparent refraction, synchronized geometric scaling, fracture dissolution, site registration, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment development as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty site and cube, contained growth, complete compact design, elegant cracking, synchronized site expansion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Glass-Cube Contained Growth and Release

The cube is an optical enclosure, not a source object: architecture grows inside it, then all developed geometry expands together around fixed anchors after the glass safely dissolves.
