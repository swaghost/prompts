# Luxury Property Luminous Scan-Grid Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** drone-view scanning grid
**Reveal Mechanism:** a luminous ground grid sweeps across the site and raises precise geometry behind its moving boundary
**Sequence Type:** reference-match scan construction

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second drone-view reveal where a scanning grid traverses an empty site, generates architecture and landscape, then the camera lowers into a final luxury hero view.

## Usage

For proptech, masterplan presentations, development campaigns, smart-city visuals, and premium architectural reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with scan-mask progression, drone camera control, and stable geometry.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned aerial empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: aerial empty site, active grid, geometry rise, material and landscape pass, lowered hero view.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Luminous Scan Grid

One scan front moves continuously across the ground. Completed areas remain stable behind it; the grid generates geometry before materials, vegetation, and water.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic scan-grid reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Start from a high drone-like view of an empty site matching the final context.
1.0-3.0s: A precise luminous architectural grid activates and sweeps steadily across the ground.
3.0-6.2s: Wherever the scan passes, exact foundation, walls, floors, openings, facade geometry, and roof rise in coherent order. Finished areas remain stable.
6.2-8.2s: Structure becomes the real materials shown in @reference; vegetation, paths, driveway, and water appear behind the same scan boundary.
8.2-10.0s: The grid disappears and the camera lowers smoothly into a restrained luxury exterior hero view of the completed property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, multiple scan fronts, random grid, uneven completion, floating geometry, holographic final building, camera reset, fast dive, architecture drift, or residual scan lines.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Aerial start and final view preserve spatial orientation.
- [ ] One continuous scan boundary crosses the site.
- [ ] Geometry precedes material and landscape.
- [ ] Completed areas remain stable behind the scan.
- [ ] Camera lowering is smooth and continuous.
- [ ] Grid disappears before final frame.
- [ ] Completed property matches `@reference` in exactly 10 seconds.
