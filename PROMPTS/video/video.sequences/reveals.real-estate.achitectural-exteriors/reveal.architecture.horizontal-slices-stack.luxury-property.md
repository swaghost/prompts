# Luxury Property Horizontal Slice Stack Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** horizontal architectural slice stacking
**Reveal Mechanism:** thin complete slices descend and combine from ground level to roof before seams vanish
**Sequence Type:** reference-match layered assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take assembly where horizontal slices stack into the referenced property, merge seamlessly, and receive final glazing, facade, and landscape.

## Usage

For modular construction, architecture presentations, development marketing, and satisfying layer-build reels.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rigid-layer alignment, collision control, and stable material conversion.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: hovering slices, ground layer, mid-stack, seam merge, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Horizontal Slice Stack

Each slice spans the correct building cross-section, remains horizontal and rigid, and lands on the completed layer below without overlap or missing volumes.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous architectural assembly.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty site and thin horizontal architectural slices hovering in ordered levels above the footprint.
1.0-6.5s: Slices descend one by one from ground level to roof, each containing the correct cross-section of walls, openings, balconies, and structural volume. Every layer lands flush and remains stable.
6.5-8.2s: After the final roof slice connects, seams disappear, windows become reflective, and facade finishes resolve into the exact materials of @reference.
8.2-9.2s: Landscaping, paths, driveway, pool, and exterior lights form last.
9.2-10.0s: Finish on the completed photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, tilted layers, wrong order, layer collision, gaps, overlaps, floating floors, visible final seams, missing openings, architecture drift, or unfinished landscaping.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Slices are horizontal, ordered, rigid, and reference-shaped.
- [ ] Stack progresses from ground to roof.
- [ ] Each layer lands flush without collision or gaps.
- [ ] Seams disappear without changing geometry.
- [ ] Glazing, facade, and landscape complete afterward.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence remains one continuous 10-second take.
