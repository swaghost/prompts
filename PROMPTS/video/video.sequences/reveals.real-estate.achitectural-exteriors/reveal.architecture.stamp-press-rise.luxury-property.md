# Luxury Property Architectural Stamp Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** footprint stamp and structural rise
**Reveal Mechanism:** a giant architectural stamp imprints the exact footprint and triggers construction upward
**Sequence Type:** reference-match exterior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second cinematic reveal where a giant hand presses a property-shaped stamp into an empty site and the complete referenced structure rises from the imprint.

## Usage

For development branding, architecture concepts, construction-company reels, and memorable property-launch hooks.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with hand-object interaction, contact physics, and architectural consistency.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final exterior target as `@reference`.
- Optional stamp-design plate matching the footprint.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty site, stamp arrival, imprint, building rise, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Architectural Stamp Press

The hand and stamp maintain correct scale and anatomy. The imprint equals the final footprint; architecture rises only after the stamp clears.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic property reveal.

0.0-1.2s: Start with an empty site matching @reference in camera direction and context.
1.2-3.0s: A giant realistic human hand enters holding a precise architectural stamp shaped like the property's footprint and presses it flat onto the land with believable weight.
3.0-3.8s: The hand lifts the stamp cleanly, leaving one crisp footprint imprint.
3.8-7.2s: Foundation, walls, floors, windows, facade, and roof rise from the imprint in logical order and exact reference geometry.
7.2-9.0s: Glass and materials resolve, then landscaping, pool, driveway, and exterior lights complete.
9.0-10.0s: The hand and stamp are gone; hold or gently glide over the fully photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people except the single construction hand, no text, logos, cuts, extra hands, malformed fingers, stamp clipping, wrong footprint, structure before stamp lift, rubber walls, random geometry, or unfinished final state.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] One anatomically plausible hand controls one correctly scaled stamp.
- [ ] Stamp contacts the ground and leaves the exact final footprint.
- [ ] Building begins only after the stamp lifts.
- [ ] Construction follows foundation-to-roof order.
- [ ] Hand and stamp fully exit before the hero frame.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence remains one continuous 10-second take.
