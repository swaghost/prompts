# Apartment Tower Printing-Beam Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-building exterior
**Reveal Effect:** vertical architectural printing
**Reveal Mechanism:** a printing beam deposits the podium and tower layer by layer before real materials resolve
**Sequence Type:** reference-match apartment construction

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take apartment build where a futuristic beam prints the podium, floors, walls, balconies, and roof upward into the exact reference tower.

## Usage

For construction technology, multifamily development marketing, modular systems, and investor presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with layer deposition, repeated-floor control, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: footprint beam, podium, lower tower, upper tower, material finish, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Apartment Printing Beam

The beam follows exact contours and deposits stable layers. Completed floors never melt or change; repeated architecture maintains one grid.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final apartment building exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic development reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Start with an empty development site from the final camera direction.
1.0-2.5s: A futuristic architectural printing beam traces and prints the podium and lobby footprint.
2.5-6.8s: The beam moves upward layer by layer, creating floor slabs, walls, cores, balconies, and the complete tower with the exact floor count and facade rhythm of @reference.
6.8-8.5s: Printed surfaces become real concrete, stone, glass, metal, and facade systems; rooftop structures and entrance details finish.
8.5-9.3s: Landscaping and site circulation complete.
9.3-10.0s: End on the photorealistic apartment development matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, melted extrusion, uneven print layers, changing floor count, floating balconies, wrong podium, beam wandering, geometry drift, unfinished facade, or residual printing glow.
```
