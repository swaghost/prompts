# Luxury Property Light-Trail Drawing Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** long-exposure light drawing
**Reveal Mechanism:** moving light trails trace architecture in space and solidify into final materials
**Sequence Type:** reference-match dusk reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second dusk reveal where luminous trails draw the complete referenced property in mid-air before becoming real architecture and landscape.

## Usage

For luxury launches, lighting brands, architectural visualization, and elegant nighttime property campaigns.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with trajectory control, stable line-to-solid conversion, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site dusk plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: dusk site, footprint traces, spatial outline, material solidification, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Long-Exposure Architecture Drawing

Trails follow exact edges in construction order. They remain controlled continuous paths and become solid geometry without flashes or random light scribbles.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic luxury transformation at dusk.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty site from the final camera direction.
1.0-3.0s: Bright moving light trails draw the exact footprint and ground-plane paths like refined long-exposure photography.
3.0-5.8s: Trails rise and trace walls, floors, roof, windows, pool, and landscape outlines in clear architectural order.
5.8-8.3s: The complete glowing drawing solidifies into the exact concrete, stone, timber, metal, glass, water, and planting of @reference.
8.3-9.2s: Exterior lights settle into their final practical illumination as drawing trails disappear.
9.2-10.0s: End on the fully photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, random light painting, tangled trails, multiple exposures, global flash, floating lines, incomplete geometry, residual glow, architecture drift, or neon final materials.
```

- [ ] Light paths trace exact reference edges.
- [ ] Footprint precedes vertical architecture.
- [ ] Drawing completes before solidification.
- [ ] Solid materials preserve traced geometry.
- [ ] All trails clear before final frame.
- [ ] Final property and lighting match `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
