# Luxury Property Shadow Silhouette Fill Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** shadow-silhouette rise and material fill
**Reveal Mechanism:** the final footprint shadow rises into a solid silhouette that fills with architecture and landscape
**Sequence Type:** reference-match exterior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where the future property's shadow rises from an empty site, gains volume, fills with real materials, and resolves into the exact reference exterior.

## Usage

For luxury real-estate launches, developer campaigns, architectural visualization, and concept-to-completion films.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video models such as Seedance 2/2.5, Veo, Kling, Runway, or Sora.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- `@reference`: required completed exterior target.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.
- Treat `@reference` as the final target, not the first frame.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional four-panel board: empty site and shadow, raised silhouette, material fill and landscape growth, final hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Shadow Silhouette Fill

The shadow must match the final footprint, rise as one coherent opaque volume, then receive materials locally without geometry morphing. Landscaping starts only after the building silhouette is stable.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second cinematic luxury property reveal in one continuous shot with no cuts.

0.0-1.2s: Start with an empty site under bright sunlight from the same camera direction and compatible perspective as @reference. No building is present.
1.2-3.2s: A dark architectural shadow appears on the ground, tracing the exact footprint and silhouette logic of the future property.
3.2-5.2s: The shadow rises vertically into a full three-dimensional architectural silhouette. Keep edges straight, proportions stable, and the footprint locked.
5.2-7.5s: Real materials fill the silhouette progressively: concrete, stone, timber, metal, and reflective glass, matching @reference exactly.
7.5-9.0s: Landscaping grows around the stable building, the driveway forms, and exterior lights activate locally.
9.0-10.0s: Finish on a subtle stabilized hero push or glide showing the completed photorealistic property matching @reference in architecture, proportions, materials, landscaping, camera direction, and atmosphere.

Use warm natural architectural light, realistic shadows and reflections, premium visualization quality, and continuous physically coherent motion. No people, text, logos, watermarks, cuts, fades, teleportation, liquid morphing, duplicate structures, geometry drift, or unfinished elements.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Empty site and final frame share camera direction and context.
- [ ] Shadow matches the exact final footprint.
- [ ] Silhouette rises without bending, stretching, or duplication.
- [ ] Materials preserve geometry and match `@reference`.
- [ ] Landscaping appears only after the structure stabilizes.
- [ ] Final property and hero frame match `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is exactly 10 seconds with no cuts, people, text, or logos.
