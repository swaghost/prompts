# Luxury Property Reverse-Time Reconstruction

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** reversed deconstruction
**Reveal Mechanism:** scattered construction and landscape materials fly backward along plausible paths and reconnect into a pristine property
**Sequence Type:** reference-match reverse assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second reverse-time build where scattered site materials retrace deconstruction paths and reconstruct the exact final property piece by piece.

## Usage

For construction brands, renovation campaigns, dramatic real-estate reveals, and satisfying reverse-motion content.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with reverse physics, rigid-body continuity, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional debris-site starting plate derived from the same composition.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: scattered materials, reverse lift, structural reconnection, finish restoration, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Reverse-Time Reconstruction

Every object retraces a plausible prior path into one final destination. Materials never multiply, vanish mid-flight, or pass through completed structure.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous reverse-time luxury reconstruction.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty disturbed site containing organized piles of stone, timber, glass, concrete, metal, and landscape material.
1.0-3.5s: Time reverses. Foundation and structural pieces lift and travel backward into exact positions with realistic weight and reverse dust behavior.
3.5-6.5s: Walls, floors, roof, facade panels, windows, and doors reconnect in logical reverse-deconstruction order. Pieces avoid collision and lock flush.
6.5-8.7s: Paths, driveway, pool, planting, soil, and trees restore themselves; dust and fragments return cleanly to their source materials.
8.7-10.0s: The property is pristine and photorealistic, matching @reference exactly. Finish with a restrained hero hold or glide.

No people, text, logos, cuts, forward-time debris, explosions, duplicated pieces, floating leftovers, impossible paths, collisions, ghost materials, architecture drift, or damage in the final frame.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Starting materials correspond to final components.
- [ ] Reverse paths are plausible and collision-safe.
- [ ] Structure reconnects before finishes and landscape.
- [ ] No material duplicates or disappears without destination.
- [ ] Dust and debris resolve completely.
- [ ] Final property is pristine and matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second reverse build.
