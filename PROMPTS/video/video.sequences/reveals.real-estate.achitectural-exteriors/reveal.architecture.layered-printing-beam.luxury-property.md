# Luxury Property Layered Printing-Beam Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** architectural 3D printing
**Reveal Mechanism:** a controlled beam follows the footprint and prints structure upward in sequential layers
**Sequence Type:** reference-match construction reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A futuristic 10-second one-take build where a precise printing beam constructs the referenced property from foundation to roof before materials and landscaping resolve photorealistically.

## Usage

For architecture-tech campaigns, development announcements, proptech media, and luxury construction visualization.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video models with stable geometry, path-following effects, and layer-by-layer construction control.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- `@reference`: required completed property target.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty site and outline, foundation print, vertical structure, material finish, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Layered Architectural Printing

The beam moves continuously along visible construction contours. Printed layers remain solid and accumulate without melting, extrusion wobble, or replacement cuts.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic architectural 3D-printing reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty site matching the final environment and camera direction. A precise glowing architectural base outline appears on the ground.
1.0-3.0s: A futuristic printing beam travels around the footprint and forms foundation and ground-level structure in thin stable layers.
3.0-6.5s: The beam rises progressively, printing walls, floors, balconies, columns, openings, and roof geometry from bottom to top. Every completed layer remains fixed.
6.5-8.3s: Printed surfaces convert into the exact concrete, stone, timber, glass, and metal of @reference. Windows and facade details seat in their correct locations.
8.3-9.2s: Landscaping, pathways, driveway, pool, and exterior lighting complete the site.
9.2-10.0s: Hold or use a subtle hero glide on the completed property matching @reference exactly.

Premium photorealistic architectural visualization, warm natural light, one continuous shot. No people, text, logos, cuts, hidden cuts, floating layers, melted extrusion, unstable walls, random printing paths, wrong floor count, geometry drift, or unfinished materials.
```
