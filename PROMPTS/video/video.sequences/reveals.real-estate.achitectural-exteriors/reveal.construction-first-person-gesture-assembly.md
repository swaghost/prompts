3.1-4.0s: CONCRETE SLAB. Right finger flicks ONE massive slab upward toward POV. Left OPEN PALM stops it close to camera. Wrist rotation levels it. Both hands push forward. Pinch aligns. Downward gesture -> slab SLAMS onto structure with dust and subtle camera recoil.

4.0-5.2s: STRUCTURE GROWS. Hands speed up. Columns and beams move ONE AFTER ANOTHER: FLICK -> RISE -> ROTATE -> ALIGN -> SNAP. Floors assemble progressively from bottom to top. Installed components never move again.

8.5-9.2s: COMPLETE EXTERIOR. Remaining facade parts move from storage, align, and mechanically lock into place. No morphing or duplicated parts. Storage areas visibly empty as the building becomes complete.

9.2-10.0s: FINAL ROOF. Only ONE massive black rooftop structure remains. Right index flick -> roof rapidly rises. Wrist rotation -> correct orientation. Both hands frame it. Precision pinch -> exact alignment. Finger snap -> roof drops into mounts with a massive SLAM. Dust settles. Hands lower. Reveal the fully completed modern blue-glass-and-white-facade building.

STRICT: exactly TWO black-gloved hands, five fingers each, correct anatomy, large clear gestures. Every component moves only after a gesture. All materials come from the visible site.
