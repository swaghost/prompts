# Luxury Property Water Ripple Layer Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** concentric water-ripple construction
**Reveal Mechanism:** successive ripples from one droplet generate ordered property layers
**Sequence Type:** reference-match liquid-trigger reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second reveal where one droplet creates concentric ripples and each wave constructs another layer of the exact luxury property and site.

## Usage

For resort, wellness, waterfront, pool, and serene luxury-property campaigns.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with fluid-wave propagation, radial reveal masks, and final-frame consistency.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional empty-site plate with a plausible central water pool.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: small pool, droplet, structural ripple, facade ripple, landscape ripple, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Concentric Ripple Construction

One droplet creates ordered outward waves. Each wave performs one construction layer; completed areas remain stable and water resolves into the final pool.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic property transformation.

0.0-1.2s: Begin with an empty site and one small calm pool of water at the exact center of the future footprint.
1.2-2.0s: A single droplet falls and creates a large circular ripple.
2.0-3.5s: Ripple 1 expands and forms foundation and primary structure.
3.5-5.2s: Ripple 2 creates walls, floors, roof, and major architectural volumes.
5.2-6.8s: Ripple 3 adds windows, facade materials, balconies, and details.
6.8-8.8s: Ripple 4 generates landscape, final pool, driveway, paths, and exterior lights.
8.8-10.0s: Water settles into the reference feature and the fully photorealistic property holds or receives a subtle hero glide.

No people, text, logos, cuts, multiple droplets, random wave origins, flooding, destructive waves, rebuilding completed stages, floating architecture, liquid final walls, geometry drift, or residual glow.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] One droplet creates one coherent ripple sequence.
- [ ] Each wave has a distinct ordered construction role.
- [ ] Completed stages remain stable.
- [ ] Water obeys radial propagation and settles naturally.
- [ ] Final water occupies only reference features.
- [ ] Property matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is a continuous 10-second reveal.
