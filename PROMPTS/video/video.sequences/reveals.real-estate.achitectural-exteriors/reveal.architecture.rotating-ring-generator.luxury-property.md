# Luxury Property Rotating Ring Generator Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** rising generative ring
**Reveal Mechanism:** a luminous ring rotates around the footprint and constructs every element below its upward path
**Sequence Type:** reference-match scan-build reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second reveal where a large glowing ring rises from ground to roof, generating the reference property and landscape below its moving plane.

## Usage

For technology-led development films, luxury architecture campaigns, and clean scan-style construction reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with controlled volumetric masks, stable progressive geometry, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final exterior target as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty footprint, ring at foundation, mid-height build, roof completion, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Rotating Ring Generator

The ring is one coherent horizontal construction boundary. Completed architecture remains fixed below it; nothing appears above it before contact.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic luxury property transformation.

0.0-1.0s: Start with an empty site matching the final environment and camera direction.
1.0-2.0s: A large refined glowing ring forms horizontally around the exact property footprint at ground level and begins rotating slowly.
2.0-6.8s: The rotating ring travels upward. Wherever its plane passes, architecture is generated in exact reference order: foundation, walls, floors, windows, balconies, facade finishes, and roof. Everything below the ring is complete and stable; everything above remains empty.
6.8-8.5s: As the ring clears the roof, landscaping, pool, driveway, paths, and exterior lights complete at ground level.
8.5-9.2s: The ring fades cleanly without changing the building.
9.2-10.0s: End with a smooth restrained hero glide on the photorealistic property matching @reference.

Ultra-realistic premium architecture, warm natural light, stable geometry, realistic materials. No people, text, logos, cuts, multiple rings, uneven scan boundary, unfinished strips, floating floors, geometry regeneration, teleportation, excessive neon, or residual glow.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Ring begins at the exact footprint and stays horizontal.
- [ ] Completed geometry remains stable below the ring.
- [ ] Nothing appears above the active construction plane.
- [ ] Build order runs foundation to roof.
- [ ] Landscape completes after the ring clears the structure.
- [ ] Ring disappears before the hero frame.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference` in one continuous 10-second shot.
