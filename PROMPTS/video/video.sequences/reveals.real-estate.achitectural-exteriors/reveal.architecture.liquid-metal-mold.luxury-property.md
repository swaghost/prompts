# Luxury Property Liquid-Metal Mold Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** liquid-metal mold casting
**Reveal Mechanism:** molten reflective metal fills a hollow architectural mold and cools into final materials
**Sequence Type:** reference-match liquid-to-solid reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second reveal where shimmering liquid metal fills the exact property mold from bottom to top, cools, and transforms into the completed photorealistic exterior.

## Usage

For premium construction campaigns, metal and material brands, architecture visualization, and dramatic property launches.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with viscous fluid simulation, mold containment, cooling, and final geometry lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional empty-site and mold-state plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty mold, initial pour, rising metal, cooling materials, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Liquid-Metal Mold Casting

Metal remains contained inside a hollow mold, rises continuously, and forms geometry through fill rather than random morphing. Cooling changes surface composition without changing shape.
