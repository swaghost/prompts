# Gravity Build — Architectural Reveal

## Description

A completed building assembles itself from an empty construction site in one continuous, cinematic FPV shot. Construction materials launch upward as gravity reverses, progressively forming the architecture, façade, and details from the uploaded reference image. In the final two seconds, gravity returns to normal and the remaining elements slam into place before the camera pulls back to reveal the completed building.

**Source:** [Gravity Build — Construction Reveal](https://gravity-build-animation.netlify.app/)

## Reference Image Requirement

Use the uploaded image as the **final architectural target only**. It is not the starting frame.

At 00:00, the building must not exist. Begin with an empty construction site or environment matching the reference image's location, surrounding context, camera perspective, and atmosphere. By 00:10, the completed building must match the reference exactly in architecture, proportions, silhouette, façade, colours, materials, windows, roof structure, footprint, and location.

Add the image as a **Reference Image**, not a Start Frame. In Google Flow, use the image as an Ingredient rather than a Frame.

## Technical Specifications

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Format:** Select the platform's supported cinematic format; preserve a wide architectural view
- **Camera:** 14mm extreme wide-angle FPV camera
- **Shot:** One continuous, uninterrupted FPV take
- **Motion:** Extreme speed, aggressive speed ramps, realistic construction physics
- **Style:** Photorealistic Hollywood construction blockbuster, IMAX scale
- **Lighting:** Dramatic volumetric sunlight
- **Effects:** Concrete dust, sparks, metallic reflections, atmospheric particles

## Master Video Prompt

```text
GRAVITY BUILD — 10-SECOND ONE-TAKE FPV CONSTRUCTION SEQUENCE

USE THE UPLOADED REFERENCE IMAGE AS THE FINAL ARCHITECTURAL TARGET ONLY.

The uploaded reference image shows the completed final building. It is NOT the starting state.

At 00:00, the building does not exist at all. Begin with an empty construction site or background matching the location, environment, camera perspective, and surrounding context of the uploaded reference image. There must be no building, no partially constructed building, no building silhouette, and no pre-existing structure at the beginning.

All construction materials are initially scattered throughout the empty site. The building is created entirely during the continuous shot as materials violently fly upward and progressively assemble.

By 00:10, construction is complete. The finished building must match the uploaded reference image exactly in architecture, proportions, silhouette, façade, colours, materials, windows, roof structure, footprint, and location.

ABSOLUTE CONTINUITY — ONE SINGLE UNBROKEN CAMERA TAKE

Capture the entire 10-second sequence as one continuous, uninterrupted FPV camera movement. No cuts, edits, hidden cuts, transitions, fades, camera resets, time jumps, or discontinuous motion. The camera physically travels through the entire construction process from the empty site to the completed building. Every stage flows directly into the next.

GLOBAL GRAVITY RULE

A single gravity reversal controls the entire sequence. From 00:00 to 00:08, gravity is reversed upward. All loose construction materials, steel, reinforcement, concrete, glass, façade pieces, dust, gravel, and debris move exclusively upward. From 00:08 to 00:10, gravity instantly returns to normal. All remaining suspended materials and debris move exclusively downward. The change in gravity must be visually unmistakable.

00:00–00:01 — EMPTY SITE / GRAVITY ACTIVATION

Begin on an empty construction site matching the reference image's environment and location. The completed building is completely absent. The camera races approximately 20 centimetres above rough ground. The ground violently vibrates. Small stones, bolts, gravel, dust, and loose metal fragments detach from the ground and fly straight upward past the lens. Excavators, cranes, and construction equipment remain grounded. The camera accelerates directly into the rising particles.

00:01–00:02 — GRAVITY REVERSES / MATERIAL LAUNCH

Gravity violently reverses upward. Massive steel beams, reinforcement cages, concrete components, and structural materials launch vertically from the empty site. The camera rockets upward through the ascending materials. Huge rotating objects narrowly miss the lens by inches. Dust, gravel, and metal fragments stream violently upward toward the sky. The building is still being constructed from nothing.

00:02–00:03 — ZERO-G CONSTRUCTION CHAOS

The camera banks aggressively between enormous ascending steel beams, concrete slabs, and reinforcement cages. Construction materials rotate, accelerate, and converge toward their future positions. Show extreme near-misses on both sides of the camera, strong parallax, realistic motion blur, and a massive sense of depth and speed. The camera dives toward the foundation area as materials continue rising from below.

00:03–00:04 — FOUNDATION LOCK

Foundation reinforcement cages and structural components fly upward from multiple directions. They precisely converge and snap into their correct positions, creating the first recognizable foundation of the building. Every impact ejects realistic concrete dust upward. The camera rapidly circles the newly forming foundation before pitching vertically upward. The architecture begins emerging from the empty site.

00:04–00:05 — COLUMNS RISE

The camera rockets vertically alongside the rapidly forming structure. Giant columns launch upward from below, passing within inches of the lens. Each column rotates into exact alignment and snaps into position. Additional structural elements rise continuously. The building rapidly grows upward around the camera, progressively following the architecture of the uploaded reference image.

00:05–00:06 — FLOOR SLAB ATTACK

The camera turns horizontal and blasts through the open structural frame. Massive concrete floor slabs launch upward from below. Each slab rotates approximately 90 degrees and locks into its exact position milliseconds after the camera passes. Columns and floors rapidly stack upward. The camera threads through closing structural gaps with extreme near-misses.

00:06–00:07 — ARCHITECTURAL STORM

The camera bursts outside into an extreme high-speed orbit around the rapidly forming building. Hundreds of steel beams, panels, cables, metal components, and construction debris spiral upward around the structure. Materials progressively attach themselves to the correct architectural locations. The building rapidly transforms from an exposed structural frame toward the exterior architecture shown in the reference image. The camera aggressively weaves through the ascending construction storm.

00:07–00:08 — FACADE STORM

The camera races inches from the exterior. Huge blue glass panels and white curved façade modules launch upward from street level. They overtake the camera, rotate into their exact orientations, and snap onto the building in a synchronized upward wave. Sparks, metallic particles, and concrete dust stream upward through the foreground. The building rapidly becomes visually identical to the uploaded reference image. At 00:08, the building is almost completely finished, with only final structural and rooftop elements remaining.

00:08–00:09 — GRAVITY RETURNS

The camera reaches the upper floors. Use an extreme speed ramp into near-total freeze. Remaining rooftop structures, façade components, beams, and construction elements hang suspended around the building. Gravity instantly returns to its normal downward direction. Every remaining suspended element falls violently toward its exact architectural position. The camera dives between the falling structures in extreme near-miss passes. Dust and loose particles visibly reverse from upward movement to downward movement.

00:09–00:10 — FINAL GRAVITY SLAM / REVEAL

The massive black rooftop structure and final façade elements slam simultaneously into their exact positions. A synchronized impact creates a circular shockwave of dust and particles expanding outward. Construction is now completely finished. The camera instantly rockets backward and upward in the same continuous shot, revealing the completed building. The final building must match the uploaded reference image exactly: same architecture, proportions, façade, materials, silhouette, and location. Dust and small debris now fall naturally downward. The camera rapidly decelerates into a monumental cinematic hero shot.
```

## Generation Workflow

### Paid Method: Morphix Pro

1. Open the Video section in [Morphix Pro](https://morphix.pro/?ref=tpb3fbuj).
2. Add the completed-building image as a **Reference Image**.
3. Paste the master prompt.
4. Choose Seedance 2 or Seedance 2.5, dimensions, duration, and resolution.
5. Generate the one-take construction reveal.

### Free Alternative: Google Flow

1. Open [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow).
2. Add the completed-building image using **Ingredients**, not Frames.
3. Paste the master prompt.
4. Choose VEO3 Lite or VEO3 Fast and the appropriate dimensions.
5. Generate the sequence.
