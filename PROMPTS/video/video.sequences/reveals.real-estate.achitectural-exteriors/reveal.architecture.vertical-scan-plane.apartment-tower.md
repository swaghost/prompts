# Apartment Tower Vertical Scan-Plane Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-building exterior
**Reveal Effect:** upward scan-plane construction
**Reveal Mechanism:** a thin horizontal plane rises and leaves the completed tower below its boundary
**Sequence Type:** reference-match scan reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second apartment-development reveal where one scan plane rises from ground to roof and progressively creates the exact podium, floors, balconies, glazing, and rooftop.

## Usage

For proptech campaigns, multifamily launches, digital-twin visuals, and urban architecture presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with progressive mask control, repeated-floor stability, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty lot, podium scan, mid-tower scan, roof completion, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Vertical Apartment Scan

One thin plane moves upward. Everything below is complete and stable; everything above remains empty until crossed.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final apartment building exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Start with an empty development site from the final camera direction.
1.0-2.0s: A thin glowing horizontal scan plane activates at ground level.
2.0-7.5s: The plane travels vertically upward. Everything below becomes the completed apartment building in exact order: podium, lobby, repeating floors, balconies, glass facade, roof, and rooftop features. Everything above remains empty.
7.5-9.0s: When the scan reaches the top, landscaping, entrance lights, driveway, and surrounding details activate.
9.0-10.0s: The scan disappears and the completed photorealistic tower matches @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, multiple scan planes, uneven boundary, unfinished strips, architecture above the scan, changing floor count, floating floors, excessive neon, residual grid, or facade drift.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] One horizontal scan begins at ground level.
- [ ] Completed geometry stays stable below it.
- [ ] Empty space remains above it until contact.
- [ ] Floor count and balcony rhythm match `@reference`.
- [ ] Landscape and entrance lights activate after roof completion.
- [ ] Scan disappears before final frame.
- [ ] Sequence is exactly 10 seconds without cuts.
