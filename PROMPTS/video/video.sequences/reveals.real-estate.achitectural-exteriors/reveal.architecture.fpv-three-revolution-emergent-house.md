# Emergent House Reveal - Three-Revolution FPV Orbit

## Classification

**Product Category:** Luxury residential architecture / real-estate exterior

**Reveal Effect:** Camera-traced golden wireframe construction and progressive materialization

**Reveal Mechanism:** Architecture, pool, deck, interiors, and landscaping generate immediately behind a continuously orbiting FPV drone

**Sequence Type:** Exact-reference final-frame reveal / three-revolution one-take build

**Source:** User-authored sequence specification

## Description of Resulting Video

A high-speed FPV drone is already orbiting an empty graded property at the first frame. It completes approximately three continuous revolutions while progressively drawing a luxury house into existence behind its flight path. The first revolution builds foundations, slabs, stone walls, and pool water. The second assembles glazing, roofs, overhangs, and pergola while climbing to roof height. The third widens and rises as the deck, furniture, lawn, olive trees, hedges, and warm interior lights appear below. The drone then decelerates without leaving the orbit and converges on the exact final camera position represented by `@image`.

## Usage

Use for luxury-property launches, architectural visualization, developer presentations, real-estate reels, design-concept reveals, before-to-finished transformations, and reference-matched construction sequences. This variant is designed specifically for a residence with a pool, deck, glazed facades, stone walls, pergola, and landscaped grounds, but those components can be replaced with elements visible in another final-frame reference.

## Engines And Models

Seedance, Veo, Kling, Runway, Sora, or another image-to-video model with strong final-frame reference adherence, continuous FPV motion, progressive geometry generation, and stable architectural consistency.

## Opening-State Lock

At `00:00`, the orbit is already underway. Show only:

- Bare graded plot
- Exact future house footprint marked on the ground
- Empty pool excavation or pool footprint as required by the reference geometry
- Existing hills, valley, horizon, and sky
- Permanent terrain that belongs to the final setting

No completed foundation, wall, roof, glass, pergola, deck, furniture, lawn, hedge, tree, finished pool water, or interior light may exist at the first frame.

The surrounding hills, valley, terrain, and sky already exist and remain unchanged for the entire shot.

## Absolute Orbit Rule

- The drone is orbiting from frame one.
- The drone never flies straight.
- The drone never approaches the property from a distance.
- The drone never stops, hovers, reverses, resets, or leaves the orbital path.
- Complete roughly three full revolutions during the 15-second take.
- Maintain strong continuous banking and forward-facing FPV orientation.
- The camera always faces the direction of travel rather than staring sideways at the house like a conventional gimbal orbit.
- Use physically continuous curvature, velocity, banking, inertia, climb, and deceleration.
- The final settle remains the terminal phase of the same orbit.

## Camera-Traced Generation Rule

Golden light trails and glowing wireframe construction appear only at the drone's current pass location and immediately behind its path.

Everything not yet reached by the moving camera remains bare ground. Never show future architecture ahead of the drone. Never generate a complete structure simultaneously across the site.

As each wireframe component forms behind the drone, it resolves into the exact physical material specified by `@image`. Previously completed components remain stable and never rebuild, move, warp, or disappear.

## Beat-by-Beat Timeline

### 00:00-00:05 - First Revolution: Ground And Stone

Begin mid-orbit at very low altitude and high speed, approximately pool-edge height, skimming just above the graded ground and emerging water surface. Use a tight orbit radius, aggressive FPV banking, wide-angle lens distortion, strong near-ground parallax, and physically believable inertia.

Directly behind the drone's sweep:

1. Golden light traces the exact building and pool footprints.
2. Foundation outlines rise as glowing structural wireframes.
3. Floor slabs extend into their precise final positions.
4. Travertine or reference-specific stone walls rise from the traced lines.
5. The pool structure completes only as the drone reaches it.
6. Turquoise water fills progressively behind the passing drone, with physically correct surface level, ripples, reflections, and no overflow.

Keep every unvisited section as empty graded ground. No roof, glazing, pergola, deck furniture, lawn, or mature landscaping yet.

### 00:05-00:10 - Second Revolution: Envelope And Roof

Continue the orbit without pause, cut, reset, or straight segment. Climb progressively to roof height while preserving the same rotational direction and forward-facing FPV orientation.

As the drone sweeps past each facade, generate directly behind it:

1. Window and door frames
2. Full-height glazing
3. Roof slabs
4. Cantilevered overhangs
5. Fascia and edge details
6. Wooden pergola columns, beams, and slats
7. Remaining upper facade materials

Use strong parallax between the close facade, pool, emerging roof edges, and permanent landscape. Completed lower work remains fully stable. The unvisited side stays incomplete until the drone reaches it.

### 00:10-00:13 - Third Revolution: Landscape And Occupancy

Continue into the third revolution. Widen the orbit radius and climb above roof height while maintaining curved travel, bank angle, forward-facing FPV orientation, and uninterrupted momentum.

Complete the exterior environment directly behind the camera path:

1. Decking extends into its exact final geometry.
2. Loungers and outdoor furniture appear in final positions.
3. Lawn spreads across the graded soil.
4. Olive trees grow or materialize at their exact reference locations.
5. Hedges fill in with stable height and density.
6. Remaining planting, paths, and exterior details resolve.
7. Warm interior lights switch on behind the glazing in a coherent room-by-room progression.

Do not change the hills, valley, sky, or terrain. Do not add landscaping absent from `@image`.

### 00:13-00:15 - Final Orbital Convergence

Remain on the same orbital path. Gradually reduce speed, bank angle, and orbit radius while adjusting altitude and lens framing toward the exact final reference camera.

Do not introduce a straight approach, hover, cut, time jump, or camera reset. The continuous curved path must naturally converge on the exact position, angle, roll, perspective, focal character, distance, and framing of `@image`.

By `00:15`, all golden wireframes and light trails have resolved or faded. Hold the finished property for the final frames as an exact photorealistic recreation of `@image`.

## Camera And Cinematography

- **Movement:** Three continuous orbits with no non-orbital segment
- **Orientation:** Forward-facing FPV throughout
- **Revolution 1:** Very low, tight, fast, pool-edge height
- **Revolution 2:** Tight-to-medium radius, climbing to roof height
- **Revolution 3:** Wider radius, climbing above roof height
- **Finale:** Curved deceleration and convergence into the reference camera
- **Banking:** Hard and continuous early, gradually reduced only in the final two seconds
- **Lens:** Wide-angle FPV character with controlled edge distortion
- **Parallax:** Strong near-ground and facade parallax
- **Motion:** Physically plausible inertia, no robotic spline movement
