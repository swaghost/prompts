# Apartment Tower Golden-Hour Light-Sweep Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-tower exterior
**Reveal Effect:** golden-hour light materialization
**Reveal Mechanism:** a warm architectural beam sweeps upward and creates the completed building directly behind its illumination front
**Sequence Type:** reference-match cinematic exterior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where a warm beam rises from an empty late-golden-hour site, forming podium, floors, balconies, windows, facade, and roof before interior and entrance lights activate as the sun lowers.

## Usage

For premium apartment launches, sunset property campaigns, architectural teasers, and sales presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with volumetric light, progressive geometry materialization, sun continuity, exposure control, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final golden-hour apartment exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate with matching horizon and sun direction.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty golden site, podium beam, rising floors, upper facade, roof and light activation, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Golden-Hour Upward Light Sweep

A narrow warm illumination front rises at constant speed; architecture becomes solid only where the beam has passed and remains stable as natural daylight gradually lowers.
