# Luxury Property Mosaic Tile Assembly

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** material mosaic self-assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** stone, glass, wood, and metal tiles lift and arrange into a fragmented building before merging smoothly
**Sequence Type:** reference-match component assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take assembly where thousands of material tiles form every exterior and site component, then merge into the exact photorealistic property.

## Usage

For material suppliers, architectural campaigns, mosaic-inspired branding, and satisfying luxury-property reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with many-object coordination, material consistency, and final geometry control.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: scattered tiles, lift, mosaic structure, seam merge, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Material Mosaic Assembly

Tiles retain stone, glass, wood, or metal identity and travel to matching final surfaces. The fragmented shell completes before seams merge.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic property assembly.

0.0-1.0s: Start with thousands of small stone, glass, wood, and metal tiles spread across an empty site.
1.0-3.0s: Tiles lift into the air in controlled groups organized by material and destination.
3.0-6.8s: They arrange like a giant mosaic into walls, roof, windows, pathways, pool edges, and landscape elements, matching exact reference geometry.
6.8-8.5s: The complete fragmented structure merges into smooth continuous realistic architecture while retaining correct materials and openings.
8.5-10.0s: Landscaping settles and the completed photorealistic property holds or receives a subtle hero glide.

No people, text, logos, cuts, chaotic tile storm, material cross-contamination, random colors, gaps, overlaps, floating tiles, visible final mosaic seams, duplicate surfaces, or architecture drift.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Starting tiles use only relevant final materials.
- [ ] Tiles move in controlled destination groups.
- [ ] Mosaic shell matches exact geometry before merging.
- [ ] Openings and edges remain clean.
- [ ] Seams vanish without shape changes.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
