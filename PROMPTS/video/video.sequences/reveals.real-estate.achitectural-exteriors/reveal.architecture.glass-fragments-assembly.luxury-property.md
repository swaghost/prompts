# Luxury Property Glass Fragment Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** floating glass-fragment assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** reflective shards converge into a transparent building shell before converting into final materials
**Sequence Type:** reference-match exterior transformation

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second luxury exterior reveal in which controlled glass fragments assemble the property's transparent form, then become the exact photorealistic reference design and landscape.

## Usage

For premium property marketing, architecture portfolios, glass-facade campaigns, and elegant transformation reels.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rigid-body particle control, glass rendering, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- `@reference`: required final exterior target.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate and facade-material references.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty site, hovering shards, transparent shell, material conversion, final hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Glass Fragment Assembly

Shards remain rigid, reflective, and collision-safe; each maps to a final plane. They lock into a transparent shell before real materials replace only the appropriate surfaces.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic luxury property transformation.

0.0-1.0s: Show an empty site with the same environment, framing, and camera direction as @reference.
1.0-3.0s: Hundreds of elegant glass fragments hover above the footprint, catching warm natural light with clean physically accurate reflections.
3.0-5.8s: The rigid shards move inward in controlled collision-safe paths and lock together into the rough transparent shape of the property: walls, rooflines, terraces, facade planes, and window zones.
5.8-8.3s: The transparent shell converts surface by surface into the exact stone, concrete, timber, metal, and polished glass shown in @reference. Geometry never changes during material conversion.
6.8-9.0s: Driveway, pathways, grass, trees, plants, pool or water features, and exterior lighting form in their exact reference positions.
9.0-10.0s: End with a restrained drone-style glide or gentle push-in on the completed photorealistic property matching @reference.

Ultra-realistic premium architectural visualization, elegant motion, warm natural light, realistic refraction and contact shadows. No people, text, logos, watermarks, cuts, shard explosions, dangerous chaotic motion, floating remnants, warped glass, random geometry, invented landscape, or architecture drift.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Reference controls final architecture, materials, landscape, and camera direction.
- [ ] Site begins empty.
- [ ] Shards remain rigid, collision-safe, and visually refined.
- [ ] Transparent shell matches the final silhouette before material conversion.
- [ ] Real materials replace surfaces without geometry drift.
- [ ] Final hero frame matches `@reference` exactly.
- [ ] Sequence is 10 seconds, one take, with no people, text, or logos.
