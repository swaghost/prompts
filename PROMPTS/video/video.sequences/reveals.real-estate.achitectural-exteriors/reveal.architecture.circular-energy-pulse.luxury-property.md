# Luxury Property Circular Energy Pulse Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** concentric construction pulses
**Reveal Mechanism:** successive circular pulses generate foundation, structure, facade, and site layers
**Sequence Type:** reference-match staged materialization

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second reveal where four controlled energy pulses expand from the site center and construct the property in distinct architectural stages.

## Usage

For technology-forward real estate, smart-home campaigns, developer launches, and rhythmic architecture reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with radial mask control, staged geometry, and final-reference adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final exterior target as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: glowing point, four pulse stages, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Circular Energy Pulse Build

Each pulse has one job and one visible radial front. Completed stages remain unchanged; no global flashes or repeated rebuilding.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic property transformation.

0.0-1.0s: Start with an empty site and one restrained glowing point at the center of the exact footprint.
1.0-2.5s: Pulse 1 expands across the land and creates the foundation and ground planes.
2.5-4.5s: Pulse 2 expands and builds walls, columns, floors, and roof structure.
4.5-6.5s: Pulse 3 adds windows, doors, balconies, facade panels, and final materials.
6.5-8.8s: Pulse 4 generates landscaping, pool, driveway, pathways, and exterior lighting.
8.8-10.0s: The glow dissipates and the completed photorealistic property holds or receives a subtle hero glide, matching @reference exactly.

No people, text, logos, cuts, shockwave destruction, explosions, multiple random origins, global flashes, rebuilding completed stages, floating geometry, excessive neon, architecture drift, or residual energy rings.
```
