# Luxury Property Sunrise Sweep Materialization

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** sunlight-driven materialization
**Reveal Mechanism:** the first sunlight beam sweeps across the land and reveals complete architecture beneath its moving edge
**Sequence Type:** reference-match environmental reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second pre-dawn reveal where a natural sunrise beam crosses an empty site and materializes the exact luxury property wherever it touches.

## Usage

For sunrise listings, wellness resorts, hospitality campaigns, solar or sustainability storytelling, and elegant property reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with light-mask progression, dawn exposure control, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned pre-dawn empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: dark empty site, first beam, partial reveal, landscape completion, sunrise hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Sunrise Sweep Materialization

One physically motivated sunlight front moves from the horizon across the site. Completed areas remain fixed and correctly illuminated; the effect is not a global brightness dissolve.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic luxury property reveal.

0.0-1.2s: Start before sunrise with the site almost completely dark and empty from the final camera direction.
1.2-2.2s: The first natural beam of sunlight crosses the horizon and touches one edge of the land.
2.2-7.8s: The sunlight front sweeps steadily across the site. Wherever direct light reaches, the finished property materializes in order: structure, windows, facade materials, trees, landscaping, pool, and local exterior lighting. Revealed areas remain stable.
7.8-9.0s: The beam clears the property and dawn ambient light rises naturally across the complete environment.
9.0-10.0s: End on the fully completed photorealistic property matching @reference in design, proportions, materials, and luxury atmosphere.

No people, text, logos, cuts, multiple suns, impossible light direction, global dissolve, strobe, abrupt exposure jump, architecture ahead of the beam, unstable revealed areas, residual glow, or geometry drift.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Site begins dark, empty, and aligned to the reference.
- [ ] One sunlight front moves from a plausible horizon direction.
- [ ] Property appears only where light has passed.
- [ ] Revealed geometry remains stable.
- [ ] Dawn exposure changes gradually and naturally.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is exactly 10 seconds without cuts.
