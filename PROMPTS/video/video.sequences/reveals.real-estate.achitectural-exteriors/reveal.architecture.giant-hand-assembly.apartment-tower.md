# Apartment Tower Giant-Hand Assembly

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-building exterior
**Reveal Effect:** giant-hand model assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** one realistic hand places podium, floor modules, balconies, facade, glazing, roof, and landscape like a premium model
**Sequence Type:** reference-match apartment assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where a giant hand builds the apartment tower module by module and the completed architectural model becomes photorealistic.

## Usage

For apartment launches, modular-building campaigns, architecture model storytelling, and development marketing.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with hand anatomy, object placement, repeated-floor consistency, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: hand and podium, lower floors, upper floors, facade/roof, landscape, crane-up hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Giant-Hand Apartment Assembly

One anatomically correct hand places rigid complete modules in structural order. Each module remains fixed after contact; the hand never obscures critical placement for long.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final apartment building exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic apartment-development reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty development site from the final camera direction.
1.0-2.5s: One giant realistic human hand enters and places the podium accurately on the footprint.

5.8-7.8s: The hand installs balconies, facade panels, windows, rooftop structures, and entrance canopy in exact positions.

9.0-10.0s: The hand exits and the camera ends with a gentle cinematic crane-up on the apartment tower matching @reference.

No people except the construction hand, no text, logos, cuts, extra hands, malformed fingers, hand clipping, dropped modules, wrong floor count, duplicate balconies, unstable floors, toy-like final building, or geometry drift.
```
