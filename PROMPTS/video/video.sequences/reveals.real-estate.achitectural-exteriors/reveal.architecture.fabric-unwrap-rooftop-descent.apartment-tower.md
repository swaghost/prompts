# Apartment Tower Rooftop Fabric Unwrap Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-tower exterior
**Reveal Effect:** fitted-fabric spiral unveiling
**Reveal Mechanism:** one continuous elegant fabric covering unwinds from rooftop to ground and exposes the completed property
**Sequence Type:** reference-match luxury exterior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where a complete apartment tower hidden beneath fitted fabric is uncovered by a continuous rooftop-to-ground spiral, exposing facade, podium, entrance, and landscaping in order.

## Usage

For luxury apartment launches, architectural unveilings, sales campaigns, and presentation openings.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with cloth simulation, surface collision, controlled tension, progressive occlusion reveal, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment-tower exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional fitted-cloth first frame aligned to the final silhouette.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: covered silhouette, rooftop release, upper spiral, mid-facade reveal, podium and landscape reveal, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Rooftop-to-Ground Fabric Unwrap

One unbroken cloth sheet remains under controlled tension as its release edge descends in a readable helix, never passing through the architecture or re-covering exposed areas.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the exact final apartment-tower exterior target. Create a 10-second one-take unveiling from one stable cinematic camera with no cuts.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with the complete tower and podium concealed beneath one elegant fitted fabric cover that accurately follows the final silhouette; the surrounding site remains visible.

8.8-9.4s: The entire sheet gathers neatly beyond frame at ground level; practical lights activate with the fabric fully gone.
9.4-10.0s: Hold the unobstructed completed property matching @reference exactly.

Preserve camera, architecture, floor count, facade, materials, site, and @reference fidelity. Cloth must maintain continuous topology, weight, tension, folds, contact, and occlusion with one descending reveal boundary. No people, text, logos, cuts, fabric tearing, extra sheets, clipping through balconies, upward reversal, re-covering, exposed unfinished areas, architecture deformation, camera drift, or fabric remaining in the final frame.
```
