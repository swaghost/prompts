# Luxury Property Ink-Line Drawing Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** ink-line architectural drawing
**Reveal Mechanism:** black lines draw the property in space, thicken into geometry, and receive final materials
**Sequence Type:** reference-match drawing-to-reality reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second elegant reveal where flowing black architectural lines draw the referenced property and landscape before becoming solid photoreal architecture.

## Usage

For architect portfolios, concept presentations, real-estate campaigns, and drawing-to-built-form storytelling.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with path drawing, stable line-to-geometry conversion, and final reference matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final property image as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty site.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty site, footprint drawing, spatial linework, solid geometry, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Ink Line to Architecture

Lines follow actual edges and construction order. They remain thin, coherent, and attached to geometry; thickening produces solid planes rather than smoke or liquid.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous elegant architectural transformation.

0.0-1.0s: Start with an empty site from the final camera direction.
1.0-3.0s: Black flowing ink lines draw the exact property footprint and landscape edges across the ground.
3.0-5.5s: Linework rises and continues through the facade, rooflines, windows, terraces, and structural edges in clear construction order.
5.5-7.2s: The line drawing thickens into stable solid geometry while retaining exact reference proportions and openings.
7.2-9.0s: Real stone, concrete, timber, metal, and glass spread across the completed geometry. Paths, plants, pool, and lighting resolve in their final positions.
9.0-10.0s: Finish with a subtle luxury drone-style glide on the photorealistic property matching @reference.

Premium architectural visualization, warm natural light, crisp controlled linework, no cuts. No people, text, logos, ink splashes, random calligraphy, liquid walls, tangled lines, invented geometry, residual black lines, camera jumps, or architecture drift.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Lines trace real reference geometry rather than decorative scribbles.
- [ ] Footprint precedes vertical and roof linework.
- [ ] Thickened lines become stable solid planes.
- [ ] Openings and silhouette remain reference-accurate.
- [ ] All ink disappears during material conversion.
- [ ] Landscape and pool match the reference.
- [ ] Final 10-second one-take hero frame is photorealistic.
