# Luxury Property Orbiting Materials Spiral Assembly

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** orbiting material spiral
**Reveal Mechanism:** stone, timber, glass, metal, landscape, and pool components spiral inward and install by category
**Sequence Type:** reference-match component assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take architectural assembly where material components orbit the footprint, spiral inward, and become the exact final luxury property.

## Usage

For material-led architecture campaigns, facade brands, real-estate reveals, and premium assembly visuals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with object trajectory, collision, occlusion, and final geometry control.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional material references for exact finishes.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty site, organized orbit, structural install, finishes and landscape, final glide.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Orbiting Material Spiral

Components remain recognizable and grouped by function. They install through short direct paths after orbiting; no random debris cloud or cross-category morphing.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic assembly.

0.0-1.0s: Begin on an empty site from the reference-compatible camera direction.
1.0-3.0s: Stone slabs, timber panels, glass sheets, metal frames, plants, and pool components hover in organized concentric orbits around the footprint.
3.0-6.8s: Components spiral inward by construction category: structure and walls first, roof and facade second, glazing and trim third. Each piece follows a collision-safe path and locks once into its exact final position.
6.8-8.8s: Driveway and paths settle, plants root into place, and pool sections assemble and fill. Material reflections and contact shadows resolve naturally.
8.8-10.0s: Finish with a cinematic hero glide on the complete property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, chaotic debris, collisions, floating remnants, wrong materials, cross-category morphing, duplicate panels, impossible intersections, architecture drift, or missing landscape.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Material categories remain visually distinct.
- [ ] Orbit remains organized and collision-safe.
- [ ] Installation follows structural-to-detail order.
- [ ] Every component locks once at correct scale.
- [ ] Landscape and water complete last.
- [ ] No orbiting pieces remain in the final frame.
- [ ] Final 10-second shot matches `@reference`.
