# Apartment Tower Floor-Plate Descent Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-building exterior
**Reveal Effect:** descending floor-plate stack
**Reveal Mechanism:** complete apartment levels descend from the sky and stack precisely above the podium
**Sequence Type:** reference-match modular tower assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second apartment reveal where the podium forms and complete floor plates descend sequentially before facade, windows, roof, and landscape complete.

## Usage

For modular apartment construction, multifamily marketing, engineering visualization, and development launches.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rigid-module placement, repeated-floor consistency, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty urban-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty site, podium, lower plates, full stack, facade/roof, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Apartment Floor-Plate Descent

Each complete level remains horizontal and rigid, descends only after the level below locks, and preserves exact floor count and balcony rhythm.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final apartment building exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic apartment assembly.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty urban lot while ordered apartment floor plates hover above the footprint.
1.0-2.3s: The podium forms and locks to the site.
2.3-6.5s: Residential floor plates descend one by one from lowest to highest, stacking precisely on the level below with correct core, column, and balcony alignment.
6.5-8.3s: Rooftop structures, balcony systems, facade panels, windows, and entrance details install.
8.3-9.2s: Landscaping, driveway, and exterior lights complete.
9.2-10.0s: End on the finished photorealistic apartment building matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, wrong floor count, tilted plates, crushed levels, collisions, gaps, floating floors, changing balcony rhythm, duplicate windows, or facade drift.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Podium forms before residential levels.
- [ ] Floor plates descend in exact bottom-to-top order.
- [ ] Floor count, cores, columns, and balconies remain aligned.
- [ ] Every level locks before the next descends.
- [ ] Facade, roof, entrance, and landscape complete last.
- [ ] Final tower matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
