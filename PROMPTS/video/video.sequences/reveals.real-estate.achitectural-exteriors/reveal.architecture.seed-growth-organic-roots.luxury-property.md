# Luxury Property Seed and Root Growth Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** futuristic seed germination
**Reveal Mechanism:** glowing roots map the footprint while stem-like columns and spreading floors grow into architecture
**Sequence Type:** reference-match organic construction

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second cinematic growth sequence where one futuristic seed generates roots, columns, floors, facade, landscape, and water before resolving into the final property.

## Usage

For sustainable design, biophilic developments, eco-resorts, luxury property campaigns, and growth-themed reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with root propagation, connected organic growth, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-lot plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: seed, crack and roots, columns, floors/facade, landscape, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Seed-Grown Architecture

One seed is the sole source. Roots follow the final footprint; columns grow from roots, floors from columns, and finishes from stable geometry.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second cinematic luxury property growth video in one continuous shot.

0.0-1.0s: Start with a single futuristic seed planted at the center of an empty lot matching the final camera direction.
1.0-2.5s: The seed cracks open and sends restrained glowing roots across the exact property footprint.
2.5-5.5s: Architectural columns grow upward like stems from the rooted foundation; floors spread outward and lock onto them in correct order.
5.5-7.5s: Walls, roof, window frames, facade panels, and glazing form from the connected growth system.
7.5-9.0s: Organic textures transform into realistic modern architecture while grass, trees, plants, paths, pool, and water features complete.
9.0-10.0s: End with the fully photorealistic property matching @reference exactly.

No people, text, logos, cuts, multiple seeds, disconnected growth, invasive vines, fantasy treehouse, unsupported floors, organic final facade, glowing final roots, geometry drift, or random vegetation.
```
