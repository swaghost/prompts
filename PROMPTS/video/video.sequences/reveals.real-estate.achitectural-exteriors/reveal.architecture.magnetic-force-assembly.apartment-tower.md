# Apartment Tower Magnetic-Force Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-tower exterior
**Reveal Effect:** magnetic structural assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** separated building and site components rotate, align, and lock bottom-up under a visible magnetic field
**Sequence Type:** reference-match modular architecture reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where slabs, columns, balconies, facade, windows, roof, entry, and landscape float separately before a magnetic force assembles the exact apartment tower from foundations upward.

## Usage

For apartment launches, modular-construction storytelling, engineering presentations, and development campaigns.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rigid-body dynamics, magnetic-field visualization, collision-free alignment, structural sequencing, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment-tower exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned clear-site starting plate.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Magnetic Bottom-Up Assembly

A restrained field pulls each rigid component along a readable path, rotates it to one correct orientation, and locks it only when supporting structure is ready.
