# Luxury Property Projection-to-Reality Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** projected architectural imagery
**Reveal Mechanism:** flat projections gain parallax, thickness, and physical material until becoming real architecture
**Sequence Type:** reference-match dimensional transformation

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second reveal where a blank white building mass receives projected materials and landscape imagery that progressively become physical and photoreal.

## Usage

For facade concepts, material presentations, architecture visualization, projection-mapping campaigns, and luxury developments.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with projection alignment, depth transition, and stable material conversion.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned white-massing plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional four-panel board: blank mass, flat projection, emerging depth, final property.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Projection Becomes Physical

Projected imagery remains registered to surfaces. Depth emerges continuously through parallax and material thickness; it does not jump from texture to object.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous architectural transformation.

0.0-1.2s: Start with a simple blank matte-white building mass matching the final footprint, silhouette, and camera direction.
1.2-3.8s: Architectural imagery is projected accurately onto its surfaces: stone, timber, windows, facade divisions, landscaping, and lighting.
3.8-7.2s: The flat projections gain realistic parallax and physical depth. Window recesses deepen, frames and panels become dimensional, material grain develops, and landscape projections rise into rooted vegetation.
7.2-9.0s: All projected content converts into real concrete, stone, timber, metal, reflective glass, ground surfaces, pool water, and exterior lighting without geometry drift.
9.0-10.0s: End with the fully photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, projector hardware, sliding texture maps, UV distortion, flat final surfaces, holographic glow, fake depth, geometry changes, duplicate facade, or projection residue.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] White mass matches final footprint and silhouette.
- [ ] Projections align to correct architectural surfaces.
- [ ] Depth develops gradually with coherent parallax.
- [ ] Materials become physically dimensional and realistic.
- [ ] Landscape projection becomes rooted real planting.
- [ ] No projected residue remains.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference` in 10 seconds without cuts.
