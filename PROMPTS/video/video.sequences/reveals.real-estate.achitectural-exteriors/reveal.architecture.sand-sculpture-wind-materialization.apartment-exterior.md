# Apartment Exterior Sand-Sculpture Wind Materialization Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-development exterior
**Reveal Effect:** sand sculpture to finished architecture
**Reveal Mechanism:** rising sand forms a detailed tower sculpture before a controlled upward wind converts it into permanent materials
**Sequence Type:** reference-match architectural materialization reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where sand rises from an empty urban site into a complete apartment sculpture, then an upward wind replaces it with the exact concrete, stone, glass, metal, and vegetation of the finished development.

## Usage

For apartment launches, placemaking films, architectural visualization, and premium property campaigns.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with granular simulation, directional wind, progressive material replacement, environmental interaction, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate with matching light and perspective.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty site, rising sand mass, detailed sculpture, upward conversion, landscape finish, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Sand-Sculpture Wind Materialization

Fine sand coherently accumulates into supported architectural detail; a narrow conversion front travels upward and leaves stable permanent materials behind without eroding completed geometry.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the exact final apartment exterior target. Create a 10-second one-take reveal from one stable cinematic camera with no cuts.

0.0-1.0s: Show the empty urban site aligned to the exact final footprint, perspective, and late-day environment.
1.0-4.2s: Fine sand rises in controlled streams and compacts from the ground upward into a detailed monochrome sculpture of the full podium, tower, balconies, roof, paths, and landscape zones matching @reference.
4.2-8.5s: A focused upward wind circles the base and climbs continuously. Directly behind its front, sand grains transform in place into reference-accurate concrete, stone, glass, metal, windows, rails, and planted vegetation; converted surfaces remain fixed.
8.5-9.4s: The final roof details solidify, loose grains clear naturally downwind, and entrance and landscape lights activate.
9.4-10.0s: Hold the complete photorealistic development matching @reference exactly.

Preserve camera, footprint, floor count, architecture, surroundings, and @reference fidelity. Sand must conserve volume, remain granular, build supported forms, and convert at one readable upward boundary with continuous shadows and occlusion. No people, text, logos, cuts, sandstorm obscuration, random dunes, collapsing sculpture, sideways conversion jumps, melted geometry, duplicated floors, drifting finished materials, camera movement, or unfinished final frame.
```
