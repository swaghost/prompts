# Apartment Tower Structural-Skeleton Upward Finish Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-tower exterior
**Reveal Effect:** upward architectural finishing wave
**Reveal Mechanism:** a continuous rising front adds enclosure and finishes to an existing raw structural skeleton
**Sequence Type:** reference-match construction-completion reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where an already formed grid of columns, beams, slabs, and exposed floors receives walls, glazing, balconies, facade, rooftop, and landscape through one upward finishing wave.

## Usage

For construction progress stories, apartment marketing, facade presentations, and architectural visualization.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with structural persistence, progressive surface generation, installation continuity, material maturation, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment-tower exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned raw-structure plate matching the final frame and floor count.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: raw skeleton, podium enclosure, lower floors, upper floors, roof and site finish, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Structural-Skeleton Upward Finish

One horizontal finishing front climbs floor by floor; the existing load-bearing frame never moves while enclosure components attach only to completed structural bays.
