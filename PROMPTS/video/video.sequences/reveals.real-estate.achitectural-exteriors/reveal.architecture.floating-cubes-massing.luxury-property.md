# Luxury Property Floating Cubes Massing Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** geometric cube massing assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** simple white cubes align into final building massing before merging into real surfaces
**Sequence Type:** reference-match geometric transformation

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second clean architectural reveal where floating white cubes build the correct property massing, merge seamlessly, and acquire the exact materials and landscape of the final reference.

## Usage

For concept-massing presentations, architecture studios, property marketing, and design-development reels.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rigid geometric motion, collision control, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property photo or render as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty site, floating cubes, massing alignment, surface merge, hero reveal.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Floating Cube Massing

Cubes translate and rotate as rigid blocks, settle without collision, and merge only after matching the final volumes. The merge removes seams but never alters silhouette.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous architectural transformation.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty site matching @reference in environment, camera direction, and perspective.
1.0-3.0s: Simple matte-white cubes hover above the footprint in a controlled architectural arrangement.
3.0-5.8s: Cubes slide and rotate one at a time into the correct property massing: podium, primary volumes, upper volumes, terraces, and roof. Each rigid cube locks cleanly without collisions or overlap.
5.8-7.4s: The aligned cubes merge into smooth continuous architectural surfaces while preserving the completed silhouette and openings.
7.4-9.0s: Surfaces become the exact stone, concrete, timber, glass, and metal shown in @reference; landscaping and pathways appear last.
9.0-10.0s: Finish with a subtle premium hero move on the photorealistic reference-matched property.

Clean premium motion, warm natural light, realistic shadows and materials. No people, text, logos, cuts, colorful game blocks, Tetris UI, random cubes, collisions, floating remnants, rubber geometry, scale drift, or visible cube seams in the final building.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Cubes are matte white and rigid, not colorful game pieces.
- [ ] Cube arrangement reproduces final massing accurately.
- [ ] Translation and rotation remain collision-safe.
- [ ] Seam removal preserves silhouette and openings.
- [ ] Materials and landscape match `@reference`.
- [ ] No cube seams or floating blocks remain.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
