# Apartment Tower Blueprint Rise Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-building exterior
**Reveal Effect:** blueprint-to-tower rise
**Reveal Mechanism:** ground-plan lines rise into a 3D tower while slabs, walls, balconies, and facade systems assemble
**Sequence Type:** reference-match apartment development reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second continuous apartment-development reveal where glowing plans become a full tower and receive realistic concrete, glass, metal, stone, landscape, and entrance lighting.

## Usage

For multifamily developments, preconstruction marketing, architecture presentations, investor reels, and urban property launches.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with repeated-floor consistency, vertical construction control, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty urban-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: footprint lines, podium rise, floor stacking, balconies/facade, site completion, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Blueprint Rise to Apartment Tower

Blueprint lines define exact podium, lobby, repeating floors, balconies, facade, and roof. Vertical repetition remains consistent with no changing floor count.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final apartment building exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic apartment-development reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Start with an empty urban site from the final camera direction.
1.0-2.5s: Glowing architectural blueprint lines draw the exact footprint, lobby, balcony rhythm, facade grid, and roofline across the ground.
2.5-5.8s: The blueprint rises vertically into a full 3D tower. Podium forms first; floor slabs stack upward; columns and walls connect; repeating residential levels remain consistent.
5.8-7.8s: Balconies extend, windows seat, and concrete, glass, metal, stone, and facade finishes fill progressively.
7.8-9.2s: Landscaping, entrance canopy, driveway, lobby light, and surrounding details complete.
9.2-10.0s: Finish on the photorealistic apartment building matching @reference exactly.

No people, text, logos, cuts, wrong floor count, changing balcony rhythm, stretched tower, floating slabs, blueprint residue, duplicate windows, facade drift, or invented urban context.
```
