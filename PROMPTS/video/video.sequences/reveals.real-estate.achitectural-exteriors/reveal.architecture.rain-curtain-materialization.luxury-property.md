# Luxury Property Rain Curtain Materialization

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** moving rain-curtain materialization
**Reveal Mechanism:** a controlled band of rainfall crosses the site and leaves completed architecture behind
**Sequence Type:** reference-match weather reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second continuous reveal where a rain curtain moves across an empty site, materializing architecture, finishes, landscape, and pool before the sky clears.

## Usage

For atmospheric property campaigns, resort marketing, weather-led reveals, and cinematic real-estate content.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with weather-mask progression, wet-surface realism, and final-frame consistency.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site storm plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty storm site, rain front, structure reveal, landscape reveal, clearing hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Rain Curtain Materialization

The rain front is one coherent moving boundary. Completed geometry remains behind it, with wet reflections and drainage resolving naturally as weather clears.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic property reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Start with an empty site beneath dramatic clouds from the final camera direction.
1.0-2.2s: A controlled vertical curtain of rain enters one side of the site.
2.2-6.8s: The rain front travels steadily across the land. Wherever rain falls, the exact property appears in order: structure first, then facade materials and windows.
5.5-8.3s: Behind the same boundary, landscaping, pool, paths, driveway, and exterior lights complete. Finished areas remain stable and wet with realistic reflections.
8.3-9.2s: Rain exits, runoff settles, clouds open, and the scene clears without changing architecture.
9.2-10.0s: End on the fully completed photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, global dissolve, random rain patches, flooding, indoor rain, architecture appearing ahead of the front, lightning damage, geometry drift, or persistent storm obstruction.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Rain forms one readable moving curtain.
- [ ] Architecture appears only where rain has passed.
- [ ] Structure precedes finishes and landscape.
- [ ] Wet surfaces, runoff, and reflections are realistic.
- [ ] Weather clears without altering the property.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
