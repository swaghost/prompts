# Luxury Property Mirror Fragment Assembly

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** reflective mirror-fragment assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** shards reflecting final property regions assemble into a mirrored shell before becoming real materials
**Sequence Type:** reference-match exterior transformation

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where mirrored shards each carry part of the final image, assemble the property's silhouette, and transform into reference-matched architecture and landscape.

## Usage

For luxury property launches, reflective facade campaigns, premium editorial reels, and cinematic real-estate reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with reflection consistency, rigid-fragment motion, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: hovering mirrors, reflected fragments, shell assembly, material conversion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Mirror Fragment Assembly

Every shard remains rigid and reflects a spatially correct part of the final scene. Pieces form one shell without collision, duplication, or kaleidoscopic distortion.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic property assembly.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty site matching the final camera direction.
1.0-3.0s: Reflective mirror fragments hover above the footprint, each showing a coherent portion of the final property.
3.0-6.2s: Shards move together along controlled paths and form the complete mirrored building silhouette, aligned to the exact walls, roof, terraces, and openings of @reference.
6.2-8.5s: Mirrored surfaces convert into real glass, stone, concrete, timber, pool water, and landscape while geometry remains fixed.
8.5-10.0s: Finish on the completed photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, broken-glass violence, chaotic shards, duplicate reflections, kaleidoscope effects, incorrect reflected regions, warped shell, floating remnants, material drift, or mirrored final surfaces where not present in the reference.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Each shard reflects a coherent reference region.
- [ ] Fragments remain rigid and collision-safe.
- [ ] Mirrored shell matches final silhouette and openings.
- [ ] Material conversion preserves geometry.
- [ ] Reflections resolve to reference-accurate surfaces.
- [ ] No fragments remain in the final frame.
- [ ] Final 10-second one-take matches `@reference`.
