# Luxury Property Bonsai Growth Scale Expansion

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** bonsai-like organic miniature growth
**Reveal Mechanism:** roots and branches become architectural systems as a miniature house grows continuously to full scale
**Sequence Type:** reference-match organic scale transformation

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second reveal where a bonsai-sized house grows from the lot center, translates organic forms into architecture, and expands to the exact full-scale property.

## Usage

For biophilic architecture, sustainable luxury, landscape-led developments, resort campaigns, and organic transformation reels.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with growth simulation, scale continuity, and material conversion.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final property as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: miniature sprout-house, root spread, branch architecture, scale expansion, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Bonsai Architecture Growth

Roots define the footprint and branches transform into structural members through continuous connected growth. Scale increases smoothly without a jump or duplicate building.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic luxury transformation.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty lot and one tiny bonsai-like miniature house emerging at the center.
1.0-3.2s: Fine roots spread beneath and across the exact final footprint while branch structures define walls, rooflines, terraces, and openings.
3.2-6.5s: The connected miniature grows continuously toward full scale. Roots become foundation and paths; branches become structural and facade geometry.
6.5-8.7s: Organic bark, leaf, and root textures convert into the exact concrete, glass, timber, stone, pool, and landscape materials of @reference.
8.7-10.0s: Finish with the complete full-scale photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, duplicate house, abrupt scale jump, uncontrolled vines, treehouse design, branches through windows, organic final walls, warped geometry, invented landscape, or remaining miniature.
```
