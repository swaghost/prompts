# Luxury Property Domino Chain-Reaction Build

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** architectural domino chain
**Reveal Mechanism:** falling slabs trigger sequential transformations into final building components
**Sequence Type:** reference-match chain-reaction assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second continuous reveal where architectural slabs fall like dominoes and each impact converts one site zone into the exact referenced property.

## Usage

For playful construction campaigns, social hooks, modular architecture, and satisfying real-estate reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rigid-body chain physics, impact-triggered masks, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: standing slabs, chain start, progressive build, final slab, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Domino Construction Chain

One falling slab triggers the next through contact. Each impact creates one stable corresponding property section; completed architecture never falls or rebuilds.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic property assembly.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty site and small architectural slabs standing like dominoes along a deliberate route through the footprint.
1.0-2.0s: The first slab falls and physically contacts the next, beginning one uninterrupted chain reaction.
2.0-7.8s: As each slab falls, it transforms at contact into the corresponding stable part of the real building: foundation, walls, floors, roof, windows, facade, pathways, pool, and landscape in logical order.
7.8-9.0s: The final slab lands, completing remaining details and exterior lights. All temporary domino pieces are gone.
9.0-10.0s: Hold or gently glide over the completed photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, skipped dominoes, non-contact triggers, random chain branches, falling completed walls, oversized impacts, duplicate slabs, incomplete sections, cartoon physics, or architecture drift.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Domino route maps logically through the site.
- [ ] Each slab triggers the next through visible contact.
- [ ] Each impact creates one stable reference component.
- [ ] Completed architecture never falls.
- [ ] Temporary slabs disappear through conversion.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is continuous and exactly 10 seconds.
