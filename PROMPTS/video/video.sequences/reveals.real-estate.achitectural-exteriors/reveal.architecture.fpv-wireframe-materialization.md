# Architecture Reveal - FPV Wireframe Materialization

## Classification

**Product Category:** Architecture / real-estate exterior

**Reveal Effect:** Camera-traced golden wireframe materialization

**Reveal Mechanism:** Golden light trails and glowing structural wireframes generate the building immediately behind the FPV camera path, then transform into the exact finished architecture.

**Sequence Type:** Reference-match reveal / continuous FPV architectural buildup

**Filename:** `reveal.architecture.fpv-wireframe-materialization.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take FPV drone sequence that dynamically draws an unfinished building into existence during a close orbit and spiral climb. Golden wireframes materialize behind the camera, then collapse into the exact photorealistic architecture from the uploaded destination frame.

## Usage

Perfect for architectural visualization, real-estate launches, developer presentations, landmark reveals, construction campaigns, and high-impact social media property films. Use it when the final building image is available and the reveal should feel like the camera is physically generating the architecture as it moves. This sequence is designed for close-proximity FPV energy rather than a distant establishing approach.

## Engines/Models

Seedance 2.5, Veo, Kling, Runway, Sora, or another image-to-video model that accepts a final architecture reference image. Best suited to an FPV-capable workflow with strong image-reference adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- **Dependency 1 - @image:** High-resolution final destination frame of the completed architecture
- **Dependency 2 - Optional camera/depth reference:** A depth map or camera-path reference matching the intended close FPV movement
- The final reference must clearly show the target building's form, footprint, silhouette, facade, materials, lighting, and surrounding area.
- Keep the reference at the intended delivery aspect ratio before generation.

## Camera and Cinematography

- **Opening:** Close ground-level FPV position, 1-2 metres above ground, rapid vertical rise and backward whip tilt
- **Orbit:** Tight-radius central-axis orbit with approximately 50-60 degrees of aggressive roll
- **Climb:** Fast close spiral ascent with continuous target contact
- **Peak:** Smooth transition to high-angle top-down view and brief hover
- **Finale:** Natural deceleration into the exact reference composition
- **Lens:** Wide-angle FPV/fisheye character with believable distortion and inertia
- **Continuity:** One physically possible unbroken take; no distant approach or teleportation

## Lighting

- Match the final reference's lighting and color grade during materialization.
- Use brilliant golden trails and wireframe lines only along the camera's recently passed path.
- Keep the unvisited side empty or faintly outlined.
- Reveal exact reference materials, glass, concrete, metal, surfaces, and colors at materialization.
- Retain only subtle golden residual energy during the final lock.
