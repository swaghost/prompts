# Apartment Building Tower-Crane Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-building exterior
**Reveal Effect:** tower-crane modular assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** a realistic crane lowers major building sections in structural order and they connect precisely
**Sequence Type:** reference-match construction assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second continuous construction reveal where a tower crane places podium, apartment modules, roof, balconies, and facade before windows and site details complete.

## Usage

For contractors, modular builders, multifamily developments, construction marketing, and engineering presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with crane rigging, load physics, modular alignment, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty construction-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: crane and empty site, podium lift, floor modules, facade/roof, site finish, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Tower-Crane Module Assembly

Loads hang from visible cables, remain level, sway minimally, and land only on completed supports. Crane placement follows realistic structural order.

## Video Prompt

```text

```
