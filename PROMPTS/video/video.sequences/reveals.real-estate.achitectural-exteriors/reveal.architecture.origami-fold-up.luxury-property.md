# Luxury Property Origami Fold-Up Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior

**Reveal Effect:** architectural origami fold-up

**Reveal Mechanism:** a flat floor-plan diagram becomes a hinged construction system whose walls, roof planes, glazing frames, and facade panels fold, slide, and lock into the final building geometry

**Sequence Type:** reference-match exterior reveal

**Filename:** `reveal.architecture.origami-fold-up.luxury-property.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second continuous architectural reveal beginning with an empty site and a flat origami-style floor plan. Building planes fold upward and lock together, landscaping emerges, and the stylized assembly resolves into a photorealistic luxury exterior matching `@reference`, followed by a restrained hero glide.

## Usage

Perfect for luxury real-estate launches, architecture-firm portfolios, developer campaigns, planning-to-reality presentations, and social property reveals. Ideal when the final exterior design already exists as a clean render or photograph and must remain the exact architectural target.

Suitable for Seedance 2/2.5, Veo, Kling, Runway, Sora, or comparable tools with strong reference adherence and controlled camera motion.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Architectural Origami Fold-Up

**Purpose:** Communicate the direct progression from architectural plan to constructed luxury property through a clear and satisfying design-led assembly.

**Trigger:** The floor-plan lines become physical hinge boundaries and construction guides.

**Visual Behaviour:** Flat wall footprints lift as rigid planes, roofs unfold across their true spans, frames slide into openings, facade modules align and lock, and the stylized shell resolves into final photoreal materials.

**Physical Rules:**

- Every wall rotates around a visible base hinge aligned to its corresponding floor-plan line.
- Panels remain rigid and planar while folding; they do not bend, stretch, inflate, melt, or grow organically.
- Opposing walls fold in a collision-safe sequence rather than intersecting.
- Roof panels unfold only after supporting walls reach their final positions.
- Window frames and facade sections travel along short, direct paths and stop flush with their openings.
- Each component completes one continuous motion and locks once without bouncing or drifting.
- Landscaping emerges only after the architectural shell is stable.
- Photoreal material conversion follows completed geometry and does not alter its shape.

**Effect Negative Controls:** No paper airplane shapes, decorative origami animals, crumpled paper, tearing, floating panels, rubber walls, random geometry, teleportation, explosive debris, magical particles, or final paper texture.
