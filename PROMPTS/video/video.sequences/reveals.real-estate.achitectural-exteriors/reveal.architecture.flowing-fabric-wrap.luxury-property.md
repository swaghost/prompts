# Luxury Property Flowing Fabric Wrap Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** fabric-wrapped architectural formation
**Reveal Mechanism:** premium fabric conforms to an invisible form, develops architecture beneath, then pulls away
**Sequence Type:** reference-match luxury unveiling

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second luxury reveal where a giant flowing fabric sheet wraps an invisible property form and peels away to expose the completed reference architecture.

## Usage

For premium property launches, fashion-adjacent real-estate campaigns, hospitality reveals, and elegant ceremonial visuals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with cloth simulation, occlusion continuity, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: floating sheet, drape, tight wrap, pull-away, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Fabric Wrap Formation

One continuous fabric sheet obeys gravity and tension. Architecture develops under contact regions; cloth never passes through solid geometry or duplicates.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic luxury reveal.

0.0-1.2s: Begin with an empty site and one large sheet of premium fabric floating above the footprint.
1.2-3.5s: The fabric descends and wraps tightly around an invisible building-shaped form, revealing accurate rooflines, terraces, and facade volume through tension and folds.
3.5-6.8s: Architecture develops beneath the cloth in exact reference geometry. Windows, materials, and structural edges become solid while the fabric remains outside them.
6.8-8.8s: The cloth pulls upward and away in one coherent motion, revealing finished architecture, pool, driveway, landscaping, and lighting.
8.8-10.0s: Fabric exits completely; finish on the photorealistic property matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, multiple fabrics, cloth clipping, cloth entering windows, impossible tension, unsupported fabric motion, incomplete architecture, residual cloth, geometry drift, or excessive wind.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] One continuous fabric sheet drives the reveal.
- [ ] Cloth shows believable weight, tension, folds, and edge behavior.
- [ ] Wrapped silhouette matches final architecture.
- [ ] Cloth never intersects completed geometry.
- [ ] Fabric fully exits before the hero frame.
- [ ] Final property and site match `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
