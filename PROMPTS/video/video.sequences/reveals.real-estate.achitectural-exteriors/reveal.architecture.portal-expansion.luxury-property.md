# Luxury Property Portal Expansion Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** expanding portal replacement
**Reveal Mechanism:** a portal reveals the completed reference world and expands until it replaces the empty site
**Sequence Type:** reference-match environmental reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where a glowing portal opens over an empty lot, already contains the completed property, and expands cleanly until the real scene fully replaces the site.

## Usage

For high-impact property launches, social hooks, hospitality campaigns, and before-to-after real-estate visuals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with mask expansion, occlusion continuity, and exact scene matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property and environment as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional four-panel board: empty site, small portal, expanding window, complete final scene.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Portal Scene Expansion

The portal acts as one continuous spatial aperture. Its interior shows the final scene at matching scale and perspective; expansion replaces only the area it crosses.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic reveal.

0.0-1.2s: Begin with an empty lot matching the final camera direction and environment.
1.2-2.5s: A refined glowing rectangular portal opens at the center of the property footprint. Through it, the completed luxury property from @reference is already visible at correct perspective and scale.
2.5-7.8s: The portal expands outward smoothly in all directions. Wherever its boundary passes, the empty site is replaced by the completed architecture, driveway, pool, lawn, landscaping, and lighting. Preserve perfect alignment across the boundary with no duplicate scene layers.
7.8-9.0s: The portal reaches and clears the frame edges; its glow fades without altering the revealed property.
9.0-10.0s: Hold or gently push toward the complete photorealistic exterior matching @reference.

Premium architectural realism, warm natural light, coherent perspective and reflections. No people, text, logos, cuts, floating doorway, second world misalignment, duplicate buildings, warped portal edge, scale mismatch, fantasy debris, excessive glow, or final portal residue.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Portal interior matches final perspective and scale from first appearance.
- [ ] Expansion boundary is continuous and spatially aligned.
- [ ] Revealed areas remain stable after the portal passes.
- [ ] No duplicate or misregistered building appears.
- [ ] Portal clears completely before the final frame.
- [ ] Final scene matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is continuous, 10 seconds, and free of people, text, and logos.
