# Luxury Property Water Sculpture Solidification Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** liquid-water architectural sculpture
**Reveal Mechanism:** water gathers into the final form and solidifies into architecture while excess water settles into site features
**Sequence Type:** reference-match liquid-to-solid reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take transformation where flowing water shapes the property and hardens into exact architectural materials, leaving remaining water in the reference pool or feature.

## Usage

For waterfront properties, resort marketing, pool campaigns, sustainable-design visuals, and fluid luxury reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with fluid simulation, stable liquid-to-solid conversion, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final exterior as `@reference`.
- Reference should clearly show any pool or water feature receiving the remaining water.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: water stream, gathering volume, liquid house, solidification, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Water Sculpture Solidification

Water obeys gravity and conservation of volume, remains connected, and forms a coherent temporary shell. Solidification follows the final geometry without splashing through walls.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous luxury exterior reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty site from the final camera direction and a stream of water entering across the land.
1.0-3.5s: Water gathers at the exact footprint and rises as a coherent liquid sculpture defining walls, rooflines, terraces, openings, and glazing zones.
3.5-6.5s: The complete water structure stabilizes with believable weight, refraction, surface tension, and downward flow.
6.5-8.5s: The liquid shell solidifies into the exact concrete, stone, timber, glass, and metal of @reference without changing shape. Landscaping and paths form around it.
8.0-9.2s: Remaining water drains and settles naturally into the final pool or water feature shown in @reference.
9.2-10.0s: Finish on the fully photorealistic reference-matched property.

No people, text, logos, cuts, flooding, impossible upward water without connected flow, floating droplets, gelatinous architecture, melting final materials, water inside rooms, missing pool, geometry drift, or wet artifacts on the final facade.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Water source and flow remain physically coherent.
- [ ] Liquid sculpture matches final silhouette and openings.
- [ ] Surface tension, gravity, and reflections look realistic.
- [ ] Solidification preserves exact geometry.
- [ ] Excess water settles only into reference water features.
- [ ] Final materials and landscape match `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is continuous and exactly 10 seconds.
