# Apartment Tower Facade Puzzle Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** apartment-tower exterior
**Reveal Effect:** facade puzzle assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** hundreds of material-specific facade pieces fly into and snap onto an existing structural tower
**Sequence Type:** reference-match architectural completion reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where an unfinished apartment structure receives its exact stone, glazing, rails, screens, windows, and decorative facade pieces before landscaping and lights complete the property.

## Usage

For apartment launches, facade-system presentations, architectural visualization, and real-estate development campaigns.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rigid-body trajectories, collision avoidance, precise facade registration, material continuity, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Final apartment-tower exterior as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned unfinished structural-tower plate from the same camera.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: raw structure, lower facade pieces, glazing and rails, upper screens and decor, landscaping and lights, final hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Facade Puzzle Assembly

Every piece travels visibly from off frame, maintains rigid scale and material identity, and snaps once into its unique matching location without crossing the structure or another piece.
