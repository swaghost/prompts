# Luxury Property Zipper Ground Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** ground zipper unveiling
**Reveal Mechanism:** one zipper opens the site and exposes the completed reference scene beneath its moving boundary
**Sequence Type:** reference-match exterior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take luxury reveal where a giant zipper travels across an empty landscape and unveils the completed property section by section.

## Usage

For high-impact listing launches, developer campaigns, social hooks, and playful premium real-estate reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with fabric/ground deformation, mask continuity, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-site plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional four-panel board: closed zipper, opening start, progressive reveal, complete hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Ground Zipper Reveal

One zipper follows a clean path. The opened edges peel aside with material thickness while the revealed property remains stable and aligned below.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic luxury property reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty landscape matching the final camera direction.
1.0-2.2s: A single giant refined zipper appears along the site's central reveal path.
2.2-7.8s: The zipper slider travels forward smoothly. Ground layers open cleanly to either side, and the completed luxury architecture, driveway, pool, lawn, and landscaping are revealed behind the moving boundary section by section. Revealed areas remain stable.
7.8-9.0s: The zipper clears the site and opened edges settle invisibly into the final terrain.
9.0-10.0s: Finish on the fully photorealistic property matching @reference exactly.

No people, text, logos, cuts, multiple zippers, torn terrain, exposed void, unstable revealed areas, duplicate building, scale drift, cartoon zipper, or residual seam.
```

## Exterior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] One zipper follows a clear continuous path.
- [ ] Opened edges show plausible thickness and motion.
- [ ] Property appears only behind the moving zipper.
- [ ] Revealed areas remain stable and aligned.
- [ ] Terrain seam disappears by completion.
- [ ] Final property matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is exactly 10 seconds without cuts.
