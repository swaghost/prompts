# Luxury Property Ceramic Shell Crack Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** luxury-property exterior
**Reveal Effect:** ceramic shell fracture and dust dissolve
**Reveal Mechanism:** a matte-white property-shaped shell cracks and sheds clean pieces to expose finished architecture beneath
**Sequence Type:** reference-match exterior unveiling

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second luxury reveal where a white ceramic shell fractures and dissolves away, progressively exposing the exact property, landscape, driveway, and pool.

## Usage

For gallery-like architecture launches, minimalist luxury campaigns, facade reveals, and sculptural real-estate films.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with controlled fracture, occlusion continuity, dust behavior, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Completed property as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned ceramic-shell first frame.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: intact shell, initial cracks, clean shedding, exposed landscape, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Ceramic Shell Fracture

The shell conforms to the final silhouette, cracks from coherent stress paths, and breaks outward without damaging the architecture beneath. Debris dissolves before the hero frame.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final exterior design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous cinematic luxury property reveal.

0.0-1.2s: Start with a smooth matte-white ceramic sculpture shaped roughly but accurately like the final property on the empty site.
1.2-3.2s: Fine controlled cracks spread across the shell along architectural edges.
3.2-7.2s: Ceramic pieces break away cleanly from top to bottom and dissolve into restrained fine dust, revealing fully detailed architecture directly underneath.
6.0-8.8s: As lower shell regions clear, landscaping, driveway, paths, and pool become visible in their exact final positions.
8.8-10.0s: All ceramic and dust disappear. End on the completed photorealistic property matching @reference in design, proportions, materials, and atmosphere.

No people, text, logos, cuts, violent explosion, dangerous flying shards, damage to architecture, random holes, shell regrowth, heavy dust obstruction, residual ceramic, geometry drift, or incomplete landscape.
```
