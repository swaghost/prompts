# Living Room Vertical Guide-Line Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** hanging guide-line construction

**Reveal Mechanism:** vertical ceiling guides register final object locations, grow precise volumes around them, and resolve into real materials

**Sequence Type:** reference-match technical interior assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

Thin vertical guides descend from the ceiling to define furniture and lighting coordinates; shapes form around them and become the exact photoreal living room.

## Usage

Use for technical design reveals, spatial-planning presentations, furniture systems, architectural campaigns, and measured assembly films.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with line registration, precise volume growth, vertical alignment, material conversion, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell frame.
- Exact ceiling-to-object guide coordinates derived from the final layout.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell, guide descent, footprint registration, volume formation, material conversion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Vertical Guide-Line Assembly

Each guide remains straight and attached to one final coordinate. Geometry forms concentrically around it, settles on a real support, and releases the guide only after completion.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with thin vertical hanging guide lines dropping from the ceiling. These lines define the locations of the sofa, rug, coffee table, chairs, shelving, and lighting. Shapes form around the guides, then real textures and materials appear until the photorealistic final living room matches @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second locked-camera reveal. 0.0-1.5s: thin guides descend to exact furniture and fixture coordinates in the empty shell. 1.5-4.8s: precise neutral volumes form around sofa, chair, table, rug, shelving, and light guides without drifting. 4.8-7.8s: volumes resolve into reference wood, fabric, stone, glass, metal, and lighting while staying supported. 7.8-9.0s: curtains, plants, artwork, and decor complete at registered locations; guides retract vertically. 9.0-10.0s: hold the final room.

Preserve camera, architecture, inventory, layout, proportions, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No angled or tangled guides, floating geometry, coordinate drift, guide residue, duplicates, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```
