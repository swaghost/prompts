# Living Room Woven-Threads Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** luxury tapestry weaving assembly

**Reveal Mechanism:** fabric threads, wood strips, and metal lines weave into final furniture and decor before fusing into seamless materials

**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior woven construction reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

Material-specific strands weave through an empty room to construct a complete living room like a three-dimensional tapestry, then resolve into stable photoreal furniture and finishes.

## Usage

Use for textile-led interiors, craftsmanship campaigns, furniture launches, material stories, and luxury design reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with path-controlled weaving, strand-to-surface conversion, stable geometry, textile physics, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-shell first frame.
- Assign fabric, timber, and metal strands only to their corresponding final materials.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty room, first strands, structural weave, furniture forms, seamless material fusion, hero hold.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Woven Material Assembly

Threads follow short deliberate paths, interlace only within final object boundaries, and fuse into continuous supported surfaces without loose strands remaining.
