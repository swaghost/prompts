# Living Room Mist-Clearing Materialization Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** atmospheric mist emergence

**Reveal Mechanism:** silhouettes resolve inside localized fog and gain real materials as the mist clears

**Sequence Type:** reference-match atmospheric interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A soft empty room filled with restrained mist gradually reveals furniture and decor silhouettes, then precise materials and color as the atmosphere clears.

## Usage

Use for calm luxury reveals, wellness interiors, premium property films, hospitality campaigns, and atmospheric design presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with volumetric fog, stable hidden geometry, controlled clearing, exposure continuity, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty shell with matching light direction.
- Local fog density that never obscures architectural alignment.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: misty shell, faint silhouettes, readable furniture, material emergence, fog exit, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Mist-Clearing Materialization

Fog acts only as an occluding atmosphere. Final geometry stays fixed beneath it, becomes visible progressively, and never morphs as opacity clears.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with a soft empty room filled with atmospheric mist. Through the fog, the shapes of furniture and decor gradually emerge. As the mist clears, realistic materials, colors, and textures become visible until the complete luxury living room matches @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second locked-camera reveal. 0.0-1.5s: show the aligned empty shell under soft localized mist. 1.5-4.5s: exact sofa, chair, rug, table, shelving, curtain, and lamp silhouettes become faintly readable at final coordinates. 4.5-7.8s: fog thins steadily while reference fabric, wood, stone, glass, metal, foliage, artwork, and decor become fully visible without geometry movement. 7.8-9.0s: remaining mist leaves the room naturally; shadows, reflections, and lighting settle. 9.0-10.0s: hold the clear final interior.

Preserve architecture, camera, inventory, layout, materials, colors, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No fog-generated objects, silhouette drift, morphing, permanent haze, exposure pumping, duplicates, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```
