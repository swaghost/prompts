# Living Room Dust-Condensation Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** fine-particle condensation construction

**Reveal Mechanism:** controlled dust gathers into solid architectural and furniture forms before final material textures appear

**Sequence Type:** reference-match particle-to-solid interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

Fine dust swirls through an empty room, condenses into flooring, tables, shelves, walls, and furniture, then receives final fabric, wood, glass, metal, and decor textures.

## Usage

Use for atmospheric luxury interiors, material-transformation films, furniture campaigns, property reveals, and particle-based visualization.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with controlled particle fields, density-to-solid conversion, stable geometry, clean air resolution, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell first frame.
- Exact particle destinations and support-first build order.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty dusty shell, particle streams, structural condensation, furniture forms, material finishing and clean air, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Dust Condensation Assembly

Particles move in bounded streams to exact final volumes, increase density into solid forms, and stop moving once converted; the final room contains no airborne residue.
