# Living Room Rug-First Foundation Build Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** rug-anchored composition growth

**Reveal Mechanism:** the final rug establishes the layout before seating, tables, lighting, curtains, shelving, plants, and decor build around it

**Sequence Type:** reference-match foundation-object interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

The rug appears first as the composition base, then the complete living room grows outward from its edges and center in a support-driven sequence.

## Usage

Use for rug campaigns, styling demonstrations, composition-led reveals, furniture marketing, and interior-design presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with textile placement, object hierarchy, support logic, stable layout, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell opening frame.
- Exact rug footprint and final furniture relationships.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell, rug placement, sofa and chairs, table and upper elements, decor completion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Rug-First Foundation Build

The rug resolves flat at its final footprint and acts as the fixed datum for every later object's distance, orientation, and support.

## Video Prompt

```text

The rug acts as the base for the rest of the design: sofa rises from one side, chairs form around it, coffee table appears in the center, and lighting, curtains, shelving, plants, and decor grow into place. End with the completed photorealistic room matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second locked-camera reveal. 0.0-1.6s: the final rug unrolls or resolves flat at its exact footprint. 1.6-4.4s: sofa rises at one side and chairs form around the rug with correct spacing and orientation. 4.4-6.2s: coffee and side tables appear at exact center and support points. 6.2-8.8s: shelving, lighting, curtains, plants, artwork, and decor complete in logical order. 8.8-10.0s: hold the final room.

Preserve camera, architecture, inventory, layout, scale, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No rug drift, furniture overlap, incorrect spacing, unsupported growth, duplicates, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```
