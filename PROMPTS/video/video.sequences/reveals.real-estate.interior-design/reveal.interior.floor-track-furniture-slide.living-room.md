# Living Room Floor-Track Furniture Slide Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** precision furniture choreography
**Reveal Mechanism:** glowing hidden floor tracks guide furnishings along short paths into exact final positions
**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior assembly reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where floor tracks glow and guide the rug, sofa, chairs, tables, shelves, and lamps into place before curtains, art, plants, and decor settle.

## Usage

For furniture brands, interior styling films, residential marketing, and satisfying layout reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with constrained path motion, rigid-body stopping, textile settling, object collision avoidance, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned finished shell without furnishings.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell and tracks, rug path, seating paths, tables and shelves, soft goods and decor, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Floor-Track Precision Slide

Each furnishing follows one short illuminated path, decelerates into a unique destination, and stays fixed while track light extinguishes beneath it.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the exact final living-room target. Create a 10-second one-take interior assembly from the exact final camera position with no cuts.

0.0-1.0s: Start with the completed architectural shell empty of furniture. Thin hidden floor tracks glow briefly, mapping short collision-free paths to final object positions.
1.0-2.6s: The rug glides flat along its track and stops beneath the seating zone without folding, stretching, or crossing walls.
2.6-5.8s: Sofa and chairs slide along separate tracks, maintaining orientation and spacing; each decelerates softly into the exact @reference position before the next path crosses nearby space.
5.8-7.6s: Coffee table, side tables, shelves, and floor lamps travel short precise routes and settle with realistic weight and contact shadows.
7.6-9.2s: Curtains drop vertically from installed rails, artwork mounts flush, and plants and decor descend gently into their exact supported locations. Track glows extinguish completely.
9.2-10.0s: Hold on the finished photorealistic room matching @reference exactly.

Preserve camera, shell, openings, furniture inventory, orientation, scale, layout, and @reference fidelity. Keep paths short and visible, motions sequential where collision zones overlap, textiles physically plausible, and all objects supported. No people, text, logos, cuts, long conveyor travel, collisions, spinning furniture, floor grooves, track residue, floating decor, objects passing through walls, camera drift, or unfinished final frame.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] The finished shell begins empty and remains unchanged.
- [ ] Every major furnishing follows one short visible track.
- [ ] Rug stays flat and enters before seating.
- [ ] Overlapping paths are sequenced to prevent collisions.
- [ ] Objects decelerate and settle at exact final positions.
- [ ] Curtains, art, plants, and decor use supported final motions.
- [ ] Track glow disappears completely before the final hold.
- [ ] No people, text, logos, cuts, collisions, or floor grooves appear.
- [ ] The 10-second final frame matches `@reference` exactly.
