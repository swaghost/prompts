# Living Room Clay Growth Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** sculpted clay growth and material wave
**Reveal Mechanism:** room shell and furnishings grow from one clay platform before a wave converts them into real materials
**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior transformation

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second continuous reveal where a flat clay platform grows the complete living room and a material wave converts it into the exact photoreal reference design.

## Usage

For interior concept films, furniture campaigns, clay-model visualization, and design-process storytelling.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with connected growth, stable room geometry, surface conversion, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned clay-platform start frame.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: platform, shell rise, furniture growth, material wave, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Clay Room Growth

All forms stay connected to the platform while growing. Architecture completes before loose furniture; the material wave changes surfaces only and preserves geometry.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous interior transformation.

0.0-1.0s: Start with a flat matte-clay platform representing the room from the final camera perspective.
1.0-3.5s: Walls, floor, ceiling edges, openings, and fixed built-ins rise from the clay in exact reference geometry.
3.5-6.5s: Sofa, chairs, coffee table, shelves, rug, lighting, and decor grow upward as complete connected clay forms in logical order.
6.5-8.8s: One transformation wave passes through the finished clay room, converting surfaces into the exact wood, fabric, glass, plaster, metal, artwork, and plant materials of @reference without changing shape.
8.8-10.0s: End on the fully photorealistic living room matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, disconnected growth, melted geometry, moving openings, furniture fused together, multiple material waves, clay residue, floating decor, or room-layout drift.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Camera and room geometry match the final reference.
- [ ] Shell and built-ins grow before loose furniture.
- [ ] All clay forms remain connected and correctly scaled.
- [ ] Furniture inventory and placement match `@reference`.
- [ ] Material wave preserves geometry.
- [ ] No clay remains in final frame.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
