# Living Room Ceiling-Descent Installation Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** sequential ceiling descent

**Reveal Mechanism:** room components lower vertically on invisible supports and lock into exact final positions

**Sequence Type:** reference-match overhead installation reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

Pendant lights, curtains, shelving, artwork, seating, tables, and decor descend in logical order from the ceiling and settle into a complete photorealistic living room.

## Usage

Use for installation films, furniture campaigns, interior presentations, and choreographed room reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with vertical path control, object rigidity, collision avoidance, support removal, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell opening frame.
- Clear vertical paths and exact final support positions.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell, pendants, curtains and shelves, seating, tables and decor, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Ceiling-Descent Installation

Each complete object descends on one straight vertical path, decelerates above its final support, makes contact, and remains fixed as the invisible support vanishes.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with an empty room. Pieces descend elegantly from the ceiling on invisible supports: pendant lights first, then curtains, shelving, artwork, sofas, tables, and decor. When they reach the correct position, the supports vanish and the room becomes fully photorealistic, matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second locked-camera reveal. 0.0-1.0s: empty shell. 1.0-2.5s: pendant fixtures descend from exact ceiling points. 2.5-4.5s: curtains lower beside correct windows while shelving and artwork descend flush to walls. 4.5-7.5s: sofa, chairs, rug, and tables lower in collision-free order and settle with contact shadows. 7.5-9.0s: plants and decor descend onto exact supports; invisible supports vanish only after contact. 9.0-10.0s: hold the final room.

Preserve camera, shell, inventory, layout, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No horizontal flight, collisions, dangling final objects, visible cables, duplicate pieces, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```
