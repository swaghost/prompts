# Living Room Bookshelf Unfold Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** bookshelf expansion and room unfold
**Reveal Mechanism:** one central bookshelf extends into built-ins and triggers sequential furniture, finish, lighting, and decor placement
**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second continuous interior concept reveal where one bookshelf in a blank shell unfolds into the exact completed living room shown in `@reference`.

## Usage

For interior-design presentations, built-in furniture campaigns, residential marketing, and concept-to-room transformations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with interior geometry lock, object assembly, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living-room image as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-shell plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: shell and shelf, shelf expansion, finishes, furniture, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Bookshelf-to-Room Unfold

Fixed architecture never moves. Shelf components extend along walls without clipping; furniture and loose objects arrive only after surfaces and built-ins stabilize.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous interior concept reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with the room as a simple blank shell from the exact final camera position, with one small bookshelf centered in the room.
1.0-3.5s: The bookshelf expands outward and unfolds into the complete shelving and built-in system shown in @reference, extending to walls through rigid aligned components.
3.5-5.5s: Flooring, wall paneling, and permanent finishes resolve while all walls, ceiling, openings, and camera geometry remain fixed.
5.5-8.5s: Sofa, chairs, tables, rug, curtains, lighting, and decor arrive in logical groups and settle with realistic contact, fabric, and weight.
8.5-10.0s: Practicals illuminate and the room becomes fully photorealistic, matching @reference exactly. End with a subtle interior hero push.

No people, text, logos, cuts, moving walls, changed openings, shelf clipping, floating furniture, duplicate objects, incorrect scale, rug through furniture, unstable decor, or room-geometry drift.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Shell, openings, camera, and perspective remain fixed.
- [ ] Bookshelf is the sole reveal trigger.
- [ ] Built-ins complete before furniture arrives.
- [ ] Furniture lands with correct scale and contact shadows.
- [ ] Textiles and decor settle naturally.
- [ ] Final room matches `@reference`.
- [ ] Sequence is one continuous 10-second shot.
