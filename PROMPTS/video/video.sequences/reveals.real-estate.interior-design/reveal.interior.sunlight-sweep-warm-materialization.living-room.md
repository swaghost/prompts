# Living Room Warm Sunlight Materialization Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** warm sunlight materialization
**Reveal Mechanism:** natural window light spreads through a dark empty room and forms finished surfaces and furnishings behind its advancing edge
**Sequence Type:** reference-match cinematic interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where warm window light crosses a dark empty room and creates flooring, walls, furniture, soft furnishings, lights, plants, and decor in a physically coherent progression.

## Usage

For residential campaigns, interior-design showcases, furniture launches, and warm lifestyle presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with sunlight transport, progressive materialization, shadow continuity, exposure adaptation, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned dark empty-room plate with the same window geometry.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: dark shell, first window beam, surface formation, furniture formation, soft goods and lights, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Warm Sunlight Sweep Materialization

One natural light boundary advances outward from the real window; completed materials remain solid behind it while illumination, shadows, and reflections obey the fixed source direction.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the exact final living-room target. Create a 10-second one-take reveal from the exact final camera position with no cuts.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with the aligned room dark and empty, preserving all final walls, windows, doors, ceiling geometry, and perspective.
1.0-3.2s: Warm natural light enters through the actual window and spreads across the floor. Behind its leading edge, final flooring, wall finishes, built-ins, and the rug materialize in place.
3.2-6.8s: The sunlight expands deeper and upward, forming the sofa, chairs, tables, shelves, and fixed lighting in exact @reference positions with correct contact shadows and material response.
6.8-8.8s: As light reaches the remaining room, cushions, curtains, artwork, plants, books, and decor resolve; textiles relax and foliage settles naturally.
8.8-9.4s: Practical lamps activate subtly while daylight exposure balances without changing the established sun direction.
9.4-10.0s: Hold on the complete warm photorealistic room matching @reference exactly.

Preserve camera, shell, window source, layout, furniture inventory, scale, sunlight direction, and @reference fidelity. Keep one continuous illumination front, stable completed geometry, plausible indirect light, shadows, reflections, and exposure. No people, text, logos, cuts, supernatural light sources, flicker, blown highlights, objects forming ahead of light, disappearing furnishings, duplicate decor, camera drift, or unfinished final frame.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Shadows, reflections, exposure, and practical lights stay coherent.
- [ ] Camera, layout, scale, and object inventory remain unchanged.
