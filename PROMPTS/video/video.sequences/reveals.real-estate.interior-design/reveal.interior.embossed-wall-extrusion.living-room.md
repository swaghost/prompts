# Living Room Embossed-Wall Extrusion Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** embossed architectural extrusion

**Reveal Mechanism:** final design components press outward from back and side walls, gain depth, detach only where appropriate, and settle on final supports

**Sequence Type:** reference-match wall-origin interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

Shelving, sofa, artwork, curtains, lighting, tables, and decor emerge as relief shapes pressed from the room walls, then become fully dimensional and photoreal.

## Usage

Use for wall-system concepts, sculptural interior films, furniture reveals, architecture campaigns, and embossed-to-real transformations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with surface extrusion, topology stability, support transitions, material conversion, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned blank shell with back and side walls visible.
- Exact wall origins and final support paths for each component.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: blank shell, first relief, shelving and art, seating extrusion, full materialization, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Embossed Wall Extrusion

Reliefs remain attached while gaining depth, then seating and tables transition along short paths to final floor contact; wall-mounted objects remain flush.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Create a 10-second transformation. Start with a blank room. Parts of the final design press outward from the back wall and side walls like embossed shapes: shelving first, then sofa, art, curtains, lighting, tables, and decor. The protrusions become fully real and settle into a finished living room matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Use one continuous locked-camera take. 0.0-1.0s: blank aligned room. 1.0-3.2s: shelving, wall panels, and artwork emboss outward at exact wall positions. 3.2-6.4s: sofa, chairs, and tables extrude as complete silhouettes, then move only the short distance required to meet their final floor supports. 6.4-8.6s: curtains, lighting, plants, and decor emerge; relief materials convert into reference finishes. 8.6-10.0s: wall surfaces close cleanly behind detached objects and the final room holds.

Preserve architecture, camera, inventory, layout, scale, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No wall damage, impossible stretching, long travel, object intersection, residual cavities, duplicates, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```
