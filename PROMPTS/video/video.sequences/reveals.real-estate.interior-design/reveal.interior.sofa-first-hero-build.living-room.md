# Living Room Sofa-First Hero Build Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** hero-sofa priority assembly

**Reveal Mechanism:** the central sofa forms first and anchors a support-driven sequence of rug, tables, chairs, curtains, lighting, shelving, and decor

**Sequence Type:** reference-match focal-object interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

An empty room gains its hero sofa first; every remaining design layer organizes around that focal object until the exact completed living room is achieved.

## Usage

Use for sofa launches, furniture campaigns, focal-point design films, property reveals, and composition-led interior presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with object-priority sequencing, support hierarchy, stable layout, textile behavior, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell first frame.
- Exact sofa design, footprint, and center relationship defined by the reference.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell, sofa formation, rug and table, chairs and curtains, shelves and decor, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Sofa-First Hero Build

The sofa fully resolves and locks before any dependent composition forms. Every later object installs relative to its stable final position.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with an empty room. The sofa forms first in the center as the hero object. Once the sofa is complete, the rug spreads beneath it, coffee table appears, chairs slide in, curtains drop, lighting activates, shelves assemble, and decor finishes the scene. End with the fully photorealistic living room matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second locked-camera reveal. 0.0-1.0s: empty shell. 1.0-3.2s: build the exact sofa from frame and upholstery to cushions and seams; lock it at final position. 3.2-5.2s: rug spreads beneath it and coffee table forms at the correct center. 5.2-7.2s: chairs move short distances into final positions and curtains descend. 7.2-9.0s: lighting, shelving, plants, artwork, and decor complete in place. 9.0-10.0s: hold the final room.

Preserve camera, architecture, inventory, layout, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No sofa redesign, center drift, furniture crossing the sofa, premature decor, collisions, duplicates, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```
