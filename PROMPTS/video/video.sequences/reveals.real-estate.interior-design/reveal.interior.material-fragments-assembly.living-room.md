# Living Room Material-Fragments Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** material-fragment object assembly
**Reveal Mechanism:** hundreds of wood, stone, glass, metal, and fabric fragments converge into complete furnishings and decor
**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior fabrication reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where material-specific fragments assemble the sofa, tables, rug, lights, curtains, and decor, then blend into seamless photoreal objects.

## Usage

For material brands, furniture campaigns, interior visualization, and residential marketing.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with material-aware particle motion, collision avoidance, object topology, seam blending, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned finished empty shell.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell and fragments, rug and sofa cores, table and seating assembly, lights and curtains, seamless refinement, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Material-Fragments Assembly

Each fragment retains its physical material and joins only its designated object, building supported forms from internal structure outward before connection seams blend away.

## Video Prompt

```text

Preserve camera, shell, object inventory, scale, layout, materials, and @reference fidelity. Maintain material identity, visible collision-free trajectories, inside-out structural assembly, one-time joins, gravity, and support continuity. No people, text, logos, cuts, chaotic debris, fragment collisions, wrong-material joins, intersecting objects, shredded textiles, duplicate furniture, floating decor, camera drift, or visible seams in the final frame.
```
