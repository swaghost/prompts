# Living Room Sunlight-Beam Drawing Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** sunlight line-drawing construction

**Reveal Mechanism:** window light traces final object outlines, raises them into 3D volumes, and converts them into real materials

**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior light-drawn reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

Sunlight acts as an architectural drawing instrument, tracing the final living-room layout across floor and walls before the linework rises into three-dimensional furniture and photoreal materials.

## Usage

Use for daylight-focused interiors, architectural concept reveals, design drawing transitions, property films, and premium visualization campaigns.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with coherent daylight direction, line tracking, 2D-to-3D extrusion, material conversion, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell first frame with visible windows and matching daylight direction.
- Exact final footprints for all traced objects.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty room, first sunlight trace, complete floor drawing, 3D extrusion, material conversion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Sunlight Beam Drawing

Light lines appear only where direct window beams reach, remain registered to final footprints, extrude cleanly into rigid volumes, and disappear as real surfaces replace them.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with an empty room. Sunlight beams entering through the windows act like drawing tools, tracing the outlines of the sofa, chairs, coffee table, rug, and decor on the floor and walls. The linework becomes 3D and then transforms into the completed realistic living room matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second reveal from the final camera. 0.0-1.2s: hold the empty shell with reference-matched daylight. 1.2-3.8s: narrow sunlight beams trace exact sofa, chair, table, rug, shelving, lighting, plant, artwork, and decor outlines at final positions. 3.8-6.6s: linework rises into stable 3D volumes without moving footprints. 6.6-8.6s: real wood, stone, glass, metal, fabric, foliage, and wall finishes replace the luminous drawings while light remains physically tied to the windows. 8.6-10.0s: all linework fades and the completed room holds.

Preserve architecture, camera, window positions, daylight direction, object inventory, layout, scale, and @reference fidelity. No laser effects, free-floating drawings, line drift, geometry ahead of tracing, object relocation, exposure clipping, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```
