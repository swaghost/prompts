# Living Room Circular-Ripple Materialization Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** expanding spatial ripple

**Reveal Mechanism:** one circular floor wave expands from the room center and materializes each final element as its edge passes

**Sequence Type:** reference-match radial interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A restrained circular ripple travels outward across an empty room, revealing finishes, furniture, textiles, lighting, and decor in exact radial order.

## Usage

Use for elegant property reveals, smart-home campaigns, interior presentations, and minimal energy-wave transformations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with radial propagation, contact-edge generation, stable completed geometry, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell opening frame.
- One defined ripple center and exact final object footprints.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty room, center pulse, inner furniture, outer furnishings, wall and light completion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Circular Spatial Ripple

One low-amplitude wave expands at constant speed. Final materials appear only where the edge passes and remain fully stable behind it.

```text

Preserve camera, shell, inventory, layout, scale, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No repeated pulses, water flood, shockwave damage, geometry ahead of the edge, moving completed objects, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```
