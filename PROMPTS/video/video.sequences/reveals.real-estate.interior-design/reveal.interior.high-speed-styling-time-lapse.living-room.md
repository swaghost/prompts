# Living Room High-Speed Styling Time-Lapse Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** logical high-speed styling time-lapse

**Reveal Mechanism:** finishes and furnishings install in conventional floor-to-detail order within one locked shot

**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior completion time-lapse

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A completely empty room is finished and styled rapidly in one camera-locked take, progressing from surfaces through primary furniture to lighting, plants, artwork, and small decor.

## Usage

Use for interior design before-and-after films, renovation marketing, styling demonstrations, property presentations, and furniture installations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with ordered installation, stable architecture, object continuity, realistic time-lapse motion, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Pixel-aligned empty-shell opening frame.
- Exact final inventory and logical styling order.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell, finishes, rug and sofa, remaining furniture, lighting and decor, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: High-Speed Styling Time-Lapse

Every element appears once in practical installation order, travels only a short path or resolves at its support, and remains fixed after placement.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Create a 10-second high-speed styling time-lapse. Start with a completely empty room in a locked camera shot. Flooring and wall finishes appear first, followed by the rug, sofa, chairs, coffee table, shelving, curtains, lighting, plants, artwork, and small decor in logical styling order. End with the fully photorealistic living room matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Use one continuous locked-camera take. 0.0-1.0s: hold the empty aligned room. 1.0-2.8s: flooring and wall finishes install with stable boundaries. 2.8-5.4s: rug settles, sofa forms, chairs and coffee table install at exact positions. 5.4-7.5s: shelving, curtains, and lighting complete with realistic supports. 7.5-9.0s: plants, artwork, cushions, books, and small decor settle in final locations. 9.0-10.0s: stop all movement and hold the final room.

Preserve camera, architecture, inventory, layout, scale, materials, light direction, and @reference fidelity. No workers, tools, debris, random order, object teleportation, duplicates, cuts, camera drift, or unfinished final frame.
```
