# Kitchen Giant-Hand Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** kitchen interior
**Reveal Effect:** giant-hand model-set installation
**Reveal Mechanism:** one realistic hand installs cabinetry, island, surfaces, fixtures, appliances, lighting, seating, and decor in construction order
**Sequence Type:** reference-match kitchen assembly

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second continuous kitchen transformation where a giant hand installs every major component like a premium architectural model until the room matches `@reference`.

## Usage

For kitchen designers, cabinet and appliance brands, renovation presentations, home builders, and satisfying installation reels.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with hand anatomy, object placement, cabinet alignment, immutable room geometry, and final-frame adherence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished kitchen as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-kitchen-shell plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional seven-panel board: shell, lower cabinets, upper cabinets/island, worktops, fixtures/appliances, lights/stools/decor, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Giant-Hand Kitchen Installation

One hand places rigid components in real installation order. Cabinets align to walls and each other; counters sit on completed bases; fixtures and appliances occupy exact openings.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final kitchen design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous kitchen transformation.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty kitchen shell from the exact final camera position. Preserve walls, ceiling, floor, windows, doors, utilities, and openings.
1.0-3.0s: One giant realistic human hand installs lower cabinets, then upper cabinets, aligned precisely to walls and reference dimensions.
3.0-4.5s: The hand installs the island and locks it to the exact final location and orientation.
4.5-6.5s: Countertops and backsplash seat cleanly; sink and faucet install into correct cutouts.
6.5-8.2s: Appliances slide into exact openings, followed by pendant lights and bar stools.
8.2-9.2s: Final decor is placed sparingly according to @reference and all materials become fully photorealistic.
9.2-10.0s: The hand exits and the completed kitchen holds as an exact reference match.

No other people, text, logos, cuts, extra hands, malformed fingers, moving walls, cabinet misalignment, floating island, unsupported countertop, wrong appliance size, duplicate fixtures, plumbing mismatch, hand clipping, or unfinished surfaces.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Room shell, openings, utilities, and camera remain fixed.
- [ ] One anatomically plausible hand performs all installation.
- [ ] Lower cabinets precede uppers, island, and countertops.
- [ ] Sink, faucet, appliances, lights, and stools fit exact reference positions.
- [ ] Cabinet seams, worktops, backsplash, and appliance clearances align.
- [ ] Hand exits before final frame.
- [ ] Final kitchen matches `@reference` in one continuous 10-second shot.
