# Living Room Floating-Pen Drawing Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** floating-pen spatial drawing

**Reveal Mechanism:** one pen traces final object outlines, which extrude into 3D forms and convert into real materials

**Sequence Type:** reference-match illustrated-to-real interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A single floating pen draws the living-room layout in smooth strokes; the registered lines rise into furniture and decor before becoming photoreal.

## Usage

Use for design-process films, sketch-to-reality campaigns, furniture concepts, architectural presentations, and creative interior reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with pen-tip tracking, continuous line drawing, 2D-to-3D extrusion, stable materials, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell frame.
- One pen and a defined collision-free drawing order.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell, rug trace, furniture outlines, 3D extrusion, material conversion and pen exit, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Floating Pen Drawing Construction

The pen creates one continuous registered line system. Geometry exists only after the tip traces it, rises from that exact line, and remains fixed as the pen moves on.

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] One pen is visible with no hand or operator.
- [ ] Pen tip remains connected to every active stroke.
- [ ] Lines match exact final object footprints.
- [ ] Geometry forms only after its outline completes.
- [ ] Extruded objects remain supported and stable.
- [ ] Pen and line residue leave before final hold.
- [ ] Camera, architecture, layout, and lighting remain stable.
- [ ] No people, text, logos, cuts, scribbles, or duplicates appear.
- [ ] The 10-second final frame matches `@reference` exactly.
