# Living Room Stamped-Pattern Depth Build Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** repeating material-stamp construction

**Reveal Mechanism:** flat patterns mark final material and object zones, gain controlled depth, and become complete furnishings and finishes

**Sequence Type:** reference-match pattern-to-reality interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

Stamped patterns representing wood grain, stone, upholstery, and decor spread across final zones, rise into exact objects, and resolve into a photorealistic living room.

## Usage

Use for surface-design campaigns, material brands, graphic-to-real transitions, interiors, and pattern-led product reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with planar pattern registration, relief extrusion, material identity, stable geometry, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty shell.
- Distinct pattern language assigned to each material family.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell, first stamps, complete pattern map, depth growth, real material fusion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Stamped Pattern Depth Build

Patterns remain fixed to exact final surfaces, repeat consistently, extrude only into assigned object volumes, and disappear as authentic materials replace them.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with an empty room. Repeating stamped design patterns appear across the floor and walls, suggesting wood grain, stone texture, upholstery, and decor placement. The patterns quickly gain depth and form into the real sofa, tables, rug, wall styling, and accessories. End with the fully photorealistic living room matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second locked-camera reveal. 0.0-1.0s: empty shell. 1.0-3.5s: material-specific stamps register across exact floor, wall, rug, sofa, table, shelf, and decor zones. 3.5-6.8s: patterns gain depth into stable final silhouettes, support-first and without sliding. 6.8-8.8s: stamped graphics fuse into real fabric, wood, stone, glass, metal, foliage, and wall finishes. 8.8-10.0s: all pattern residue disappears and the final room holds.

Preserve camera, architecture, inventory, layout, proportions, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No random stamps, text-like marks, pattern drift, wrong material assignment, repeated furniture, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```

- [ ] No stamp residue remains at final hold.
- [ ] Camera, architecture, layout, and lighting remain stable.
