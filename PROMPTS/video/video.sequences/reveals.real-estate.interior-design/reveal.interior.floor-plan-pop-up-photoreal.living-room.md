# Living Room Floor-Plan Pop-Up Photoreal Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** floor-plan pop-up transformation
**Reveal Mechanism:** a flat printed plan folds upward into dimensional architecture and furnishings before becoming photoreal
**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior construction reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where a flat floor plan opens like a premium pop-up book, raising walls, shelves, furniture, and decor before printed surfaces transform into the exact photoreal living room.

## Usage

For interior concepts, floor-plan presentations, property marketing, and design-development storytelling.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with paper-fold kinematics, hinge continuity, dimensional extrusion, material transition, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned floor-plan artwork corresponding to the final layout.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: flat plan, wall folds, furniture pop-up, complete paper room, material conversion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Floor-Plan Pop-Up to Photoreal

Printed elements rise along visible scored hinges in structural order; once fully dimensional, a grounded material front converts paper into real architecture and furnishings without changing geometry.

## Video Prompt

```text

Preserve camera, floor-plan registration, shell, openings, furniture inventory, layout, scale, and @reference fidelity. Use visible hinges, connected folds, stable supports, and one continuous material conversion with no geometry replacement. No people, text, logos, cuts, readable labels, loose pages, torn paper, impossible folds, floating furniture, scale jumps, duplicate decor, camera drift, or unfinished final frame.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] The flat plan aligns with final walls and furniture locations.
- [ ] Walls and built-ins rise before loose furnishings.
- [ ] Every pop-up form stays connected by plausible folds or tabs.
- [ ] Furniture reaches exact final scale and position.
- [ ] Material conversion preserves completed geometry.
- [ ] Paper seams disappear only after full transformation.
- [ ] Camera, architecture, layout, and lighting remain continuous.
- [ ] No people, text, logos, cuts, labels, loose pages, or floating forms appear.
- [ ] The 10-second final frame matches `@reference` exactly.
