# Living Room Magnetic Finishing-Pull Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** magnetic finishing-piece installation

**Reveal Mechanism:** loose cushions, textiles, books, lighting, artwork, and decor move from local suspension into exact final support points

**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior finishing reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

An already assembled but unfinished living room receives its final styling as loose finishing pieces are magnetically drawn into exact positions and the whole scene resolves to photoreal quality.

## Usage

Use for styling reveals, decor campaigns, furniture finishing films, interior merchandising, and final-stage property transformations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with magnetic path choreography, object identity retention, support-point contact, collision avoidance, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished room as `@reference`.
- Aligned rough-assembly opening state containing primary furniture but no finishing accessories.
- Exact final inventory and support points for every floating item.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: rough room, suspended pieces, first magnetic pull, textiles and art, final styling, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Magnetic Finishing Pull

Each finishing object follows one short controlled path to its own final support, rotates once into alignment, makes contact, and remains fixed.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with the main living room already roughly assembled in an unfinished version. Cushions, throws, books, vases, lamps, artwork, and decor float around the room. A magnetic pull draws each finishing piece into its exact final position, after which the entire room becomes fully photorealistic and matches @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second reveal from the exact final camera. 0.0-1.2s: show the stable rough room and all final finishing pieces floating separately near their destination zones. 1.2-4.5s: cushions, throws, books, and vases follow short collision-free magnetic paths and settle on exact supports. 4.5-7.2s: lamps and artwork align with tables, floors, and walls; cords, frames, and contact points resolve correctly. 7.2-8.8s: remaining decor locks in, materials, reflections, fabric folds, and shadows become fully realistic. 8.8-10.0s: hold the exact finished room.

Preserve architecture, primary furniture, camera, object inventory, layout, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No long flight paths, spinning chaos, collisions, duplicate decor, floating final objects, magnetic glow residue, camera movement, or unfinished final frame.
```
