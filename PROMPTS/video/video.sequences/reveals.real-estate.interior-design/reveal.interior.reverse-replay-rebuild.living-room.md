# Living Room Reverse-Replay Rebuild Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** reverse disappearance followed by forward reconstruction

**Reveal Mechanism:** the finished room rapidly disassembles backward to an empty shell, then the identical motion replays forward into the exact final state

**Sequence Type:** reference-match temporal reversal reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

The completed room appears briefly, reverses into an empty shell, then immediately reconstructs in the proper forward direction and ends on the same exact reference frame.

## Usage

Use for before-and-after loops, renovation campaigns, social hooks, temporal transformations, and satisfying reverse-build interiors.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with reversible object trajectories, temporal identity retention, fixed camera, clean direction change, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference` and opening/final frame.
- Aligned empty-shell midpoint state.
- One reversible path for every non-structural object.

## Storyboard Requirement

Recommended six-panel board: finished hold, reverse begins, empty midpoint, forward rebuild, detail completion, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Reverse-Replay Rebuild

Every object follows the same path backward and forward. The reversal occurs once at the empty midpoint with no cut, reset, alternate inventory, or trajectory mismatch.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with the completed living room for a brief moment, then instantly reverse into an empty room as everything flies backward into nothing. Immediately play the transformation forward properly, rebuilding the full living room from empty to finished in a satisfying sequence. End with the photorealistic room matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second locked-camera temporal reveal. 0.0-0.8s: hold the exact completed reference. 0.8-4.0s: furnishings, decor, curtains, lighting, shelves, tables, seating, and rug retrace controlled paths backward until only the architectural shell remains. 4.0-4.4s: hold the empty midpoint and reverse direction cleanly without a cut. 4.4-8.8s: replay those exact trajectories forward in reverse order, rebuilding every item at its original scale and coordinate. 8.8-10.0s: all movement stops and the exact completed room holds.

Preserve camera, architecture, inventory, object identity, path symmetry, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No one-way vanish, changed paths, time jump, hidden cut, debris, duplicates, camera reset, or final mismatch.
```
