# Living Room Circular Furniture Choreography Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** circular furniture choreography
**Reveal Mechanism:** furnishings orbit gently from the room center into exact final positions while materials and details refine
**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior arrangement reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where soft circular motion carries furniture from a central arrangement into final positions, then materials refine and finishing details appear.

## Usage

For furniture collections, interior styling showcases, property marketing, and choreographed room reveals.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with constrained orbital paths, collision avoidance, orientation control, textile dynamics, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned finished shell with a clear central staging area.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell and center staging, orbit begins, seating placement, tables and shelves, material refinement and decor, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Circular Furniture Choreography

Each object follows a shallow non-overlapping arc around one shared center, stays upright, faces its final orientation during deceleration, and stops without sliding again.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the exact final living-room target. Create a 10-second one-take choreographed reveal from the exact final camera position with no cuts.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with the finished architectural shell and the major furniture grouped compactly but separately around a clear central point, all at correct scale.
1.0-4.8s: A soft circular motion begins. Sofa and chairs follow broad shallow arcs outward along separate paths, rotating gently to face their final orientations and decelerating into exact @reference positions.
4.8-7.0s: Coffee table, side tables, rug, shelves, and lamps follow shorter coordinated arcs into remaining spaces without crossing settled furniture; the rug unfurls flat beneath the seating group.
7.0-8.8s: Material finish refines in place: wood grain, stone, glass, metal, fabric texture, cushion softness, reflections, and shadows reach final realism.
8.8-9.4s: Curtains, artwork, plants, books, and decor appear through short radial settling motions onto their exact supports.
9.4-10.0s: All motion stops completely; hold on the photorealistic room matching @reference exactly.

Preserve camera, shell, object inventory, scale, final layout, materials, and @reference fidelity. Keep motion gentle, grounded, collision-free, and continuous, with stable upright objects and realistic inertia. No people, text, logos, cuts, rapid spinning, full-room rotation, furniture collisions, orbiting after placement, floating objects, duplicate decor, camera movement, or unfinished final frame.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] All staged furniture begins separate and at correct scale.
- [ ] Each major object follows a shallow non-overlapping arc.
- [ ] Furniture stays upright and approaches its final orientation.
- [ ] Settled objects do not move again.
- [ ] Rug unfurls flat beneath the completed seating layout.
- [ ] Material refinement does not alter geometry or position.
- [ ] Camera, shell, layout, scale, and lighting remain continuous.
- [ ] No people, text, logos, cuts, collisions, or rapid spinning appears.
- [ ] The 10-second final frame matches `@reference` exactly.
