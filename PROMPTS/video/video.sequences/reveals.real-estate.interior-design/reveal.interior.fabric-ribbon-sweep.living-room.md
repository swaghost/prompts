# Living Room Fabric Ribbon Sweep Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** flowing fabric-ribbon construction
**Reveal Mechanism:** ribbons sweep through the shell and leave completed finishes, furniture, textiles, lighting, and decor behind
**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second premium interior reveal where elegant fabric ribbons flow through an empty room and build the exact finished living room along their paths.

## Usage

For textile brands, luxury interiors, furniture campaigns, residential marketing, and smooth material-led transformations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with cloth simulation, path-based reveal masks, object placement, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-room plate.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional five-panel board: empty shell, initial ribbon, finishes, furniture/textiles, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Fabric Ribbon Sweep

Ribbons remain continuous textiles with weight and folds. Finished elements appear only along their passing path and remain stable afterward.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and exact final visual match. Create a 10-second continuous luxury living room reveal.

0.0-1.0s: Begin with an empty room shell from the final camera position.
1.0-3.2s: Elegant ribbons of premium fabric enter and sweep around the space with smooth controlled cloth motion.
3.2-5.5s: Along their paths, finished flooring, wall textures, and fixed built-ins appear while architecture remains immovable.
5.5-8.5s: Further passes leave the exact sofa, chairs, tables, rug, curtains, lighting, artwork, plants, and decor shown in @reference. Every object settles with realistic contact and scale.
8.5-9.2s: The ribbons lift and disappear completely.
9.2-10.0s: End on the fully photorealistic room matching @reference.

No people, text, logos, cuts, ribbon clipping, tangled cloth, furniture wrapped in fabric, objects appearing away from the path, changed room geometry, duplicate furniture, floating decor, or residual ribbons.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Fixed shell, openings, and camera remain unchanged.
- [ ] Ribbons behave as coherent fabric.
- [ ] Finishes precede furniture and decor.
- [ ] Objects appear only behind ribbon paths.
- [ ] Furniture and textiles settle realistically.
- [ ] Ribbons exit completely.
- [ ] Final room matches `@reference` in one 10-second shot.
