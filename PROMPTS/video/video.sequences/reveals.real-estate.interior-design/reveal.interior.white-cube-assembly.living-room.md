# Living Room White-Cube Assembly Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** modular white-cube furniture assembly

**Reveal Mechanism:** simple cubes stack and reshape into exact furniture volumes before converting into final materials

**Sequence Type:** reference-match modular interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

White cubes organize across an empty room into sofa, tables, rug platform, shelving, lights, and decor, then fuse into the exact photoreal materials and layout of the reference.

## Usage

Use for modular-design films, furniture systems, block-based concept reveals, interior campaigns, and geometry-to-reality transitions.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rigid-body stacking, geometric fusion, stable supports, material conversion, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty room with enough space for staged cubes.
- Exact final object volumes and material assignments.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: scattered cubes, footprint sorting, furniture massing, complete white room, material conversion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: White-Cube Modular Assembly

Cubes follow short collision-free paths, stack from floor supports upward, fuse into clean final silhouettes, then convert into reference materials without residual seams.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Begin with simple white cubes scattered across the room. The cubes stack and reshape themselves into the forms of the sofa, tables, rug platform, shelves, lights, and decor. A material transformation turns the cube-built forms into a fully photorealistic living room matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second reveal from the final camera. 0.0-1.0s: show separate white cubes across the empty floor. 1.0-4.8s: cubes move along short paths and stack at exact final footprints, forming lower furniture masses before upper details. 4.8-6.8s: modules fuse into precise sofa, chair, table, shelving, lighting, rug, and decor silhouettes. 6.8-8.8s: white geometry converts into reference fabric, wood, stone, glass, metal, foliage, artwork, and lighting. 8.8-10.0s: seams disappear and the final room holds.

Preserve camera, architecture, inventory, scale, layout, materials, lighting, and @reference fidelity. No tumbling chaos, collisions, unsupported cubes, toy-block final appearance, morphing between object types, leftover seams, cuts, or unfinished final frame.
```

- [ ] Cube seams disappear completely.
- [ ] Final materials match the reference accurately.
- [ ] Camera, shell, layout, and lighting remain stable.
- [ ] No people, text, logos, cuts, toy look, or leftover cubes appear.
