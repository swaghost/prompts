# Living Room Glass-Sculpture Materialization Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** transparent glass sculpture materialization

**Reveal Mechanism:** clear full-scale furniture forms remain fixed while real materials spread through their exact volumes

**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior material conversion reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal begins with the completed living-room arrangement represented as transparent glass sculpture. Warm wood, fabric, stone, metal, foliage, and decor progressively fill those clear forms without changing their geometry, ending on the exact photorealistic interior shown by `@reference`.

## Usage

Use for luxury interior presentations, material-design showcases, furniture campaigns, real-estate reveals, and glass-to-reality visual transformations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with stable transparent geometry, bounded material propagation, realistic refraction, temporal consistency, and final-frame matching.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned transparent-sculpture opening frame generated from the reference.
- Preserve the exact final camera, shell, object inventory, layout, scale, materials, and lighting.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: transparent room, first material seeds, furniture fill, textile and wood completion, decor and lighting, final hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Glass Sculpture Materialization

Every clear object occupies its exact final volume from frame one. Real material propagates inside each boundary from supported contact points, replacing transparency without swelling, moving, duplicating, or leaking into adjacent objects.
