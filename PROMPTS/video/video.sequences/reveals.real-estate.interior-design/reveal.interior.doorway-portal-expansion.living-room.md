# Living Room Doorway Portal Expansion Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior
**Reveal Effect:** doorway portal expansion
**Reveal Mechanism:** a glowing rear doorway shows the completed room and expands outward until its spatial boundary replaces the entire empty interior
**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior portal reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second one-take reveal where a doorway portal at the back of an empty room displays the completed design beyond, then expands through the room until every surface and furnishing matches the final reference.

## Usage

For property reveals, interior-design launches, furniture campaigns, and cinematic room transformations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with planar portal compositing, perspective registration, progressive spatial replacement, boundary occlusion, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Optional aligned empty-room plate containing the same rear doorway.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty room and portal, completed view beyond, doorway widening, mid-room expansion, near-camera completion, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Doorway Portal Expansion

The portal is a single planar boundary registered to the rear doorway; it expands laterally, upward, and toward camera while leaving one stable completed reality behind it.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the exact final living-room target. Create a 10-second one-take portal reveal from the exact final camera position with no cuts.

0.0-1.0s: Begin in the aligned empty room. A restrained glowing portal opens precisely inside the rear doorway, showing the completed living room beyond from perfectly matched perspective.
1.0-3.2s: The portal expands beyond the doorframe laterally and upward. Behind its single advancing boundary, rear walls, finishes, shelves, curtains, artwork, and lighting become the exact completed @reference interior.
3.2-6.8s: The boundary moves smoothly toward camera through the room, replacing empty space with the sofa, chairs, rug, tables, lamps, plants, and decor at exact scale and final coordinates.
6.8-8.8s: The portal reaches the side walls, ceiling, floor, and foreground edges without bending straight architecture or displacing completed objects.
8.8-9.4s: The glowing perimeter exits beyond frame and fades completely; reflections, shadows, and practical lighting settle into one continuous room.
9.4-10.0s: Hold on the fully photorealistic living room matching @reference exactly.

Preserve camera, perspective, shell geometry, openings, object inventory, layout, scale, and @reference fidelity. Maintain one continuous registered portal plane, correct occlusion at its boundary, and stable completed geometry behind it. No people, text, logos, cuts, extra portals, infinite tunnel, perspective mismatch, warped walls, object duplication, partial furniture, boundary reversal, glow residue, camera movement, or unfinished final frame.
```
