# Living Room Circular Floor-Plate Lift Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** rotating floor-plate furniture lift

**Reveal Mechanism:** circular floor plates open, rotate, and elevate complete furniture into exact final positions

**Sequence Type:** reference-match mechanical interior reveal

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

Circular floor plates spin open and lift seating, tables, lamps, and decor into position before closing flush as textiles, plants, and wall styling complete the room.

## Usage

Use for mechanical luxury reveals, smart-home concepts, furniture campaigns, design installations, and cinematic property presentations.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with rotating platform mechanics, vertical lifting, stable furniture, floor restoration, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty shell with unobstructed floor.
- Exact plate centers beneath final furniture positions.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty floor, plate opening, primary lifts, secondary lifts, plate closure and styling, hero frame.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Circular Floor-Plate Lift

Each plate rotates only around its center, lifts one stable object vertically, stops at floor level, and closes flush without moving the installed object.
