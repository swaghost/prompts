# The Room That Furnishes Itself - Lakeside Bathing Room

**CLIENT 4A · REAL ESTATE & INTERIORS / SELF-ASSEMBLY ANIMATION**

## Concept

A luxury interior photograph that animates from bare to fully styled — the empty room assembles itself piece by piece in choreographed sequence: fire ignites, tub lowers and fills, towels and vessels place themselves, lights switch on. No hands, everything moves autonomously. Final frame reads as a still, high-end listing photo.

## IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2)

Photorealistic interior photograph for a luxury private-residence listing.

**Scene:** the bathing room of a private Nordic lakeside house. A full-height fixed window fills the far wall and looks onto a still grey lake and a low bank of dark spruce under flat silver overcast light. The walls are broad vertical cedar boards, dark and oil-finished. The floor is riven grey slate laid in large irregular slabs. A low bench platform in the same cedar runs along the left wall. The ceiling is plain solid cedar boarding, a continuous unbroken surface, with a single dark beam crossing it. A squat matte-black wood stove with a wide steel basket for stones stands against the left wall, lit.

**Furnishings:** a deep freestanding soaking tub in honed black granite set square in front of the window, filled and steaming, a low teak duckboard mat beside it, a slim blackened steel rail carrying two folded waffle-linen towels in oatmeal, a round teak stool holding a brass bowl and a wooden dipper, a heap of dark rounded granite stones in the stove basket, a tall bundle of dried birch branches standing in a matte black floor vessel beside the stove, three squat ceramic vessels in mottled grey on the cedar bench, two thick cream church candles in low iron holders on the bench, a slim recessed brass sconce beside the window casting a warm pool onto the cedar, a single large unframed photographic print of still water in soft grey leaning against the cedar wall above the bench, a folded charcoal linen robe hanging from a single black hook.

**Camera:** eye level, straight on, 28mm equivalent, verticals perfectly parallel, framed to include the full ceiling height and the far window.

**Light:** flat silver overcast daylight from the lake window as the key, warm firelight through the stove door and one warm sconce pool as secondary, soft low-contrast shadows, no direct sun. Real material texture — cedar grain and oil sheen, riven slate cleavage, honed granite, waffle linen weave, wet stone, steam hanging in the air.

**Constraints:** no people, no pets, no text, no captions, no watermark, no logos, no signage, no openings in the ceiling, no skylight, no HDR halos, no oversaturation, no golden-hour orange grade, no fisheye, no wide-angle distortion, no tilted verticals.

## ANIMATION PROMPT (Omni Flash, video node)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

Photorealistic. Match the reference image's materials, colour and lighting exactly.

### Absolute Rules

**Reference rule, absolute:** the attached image is the complete and final state of this room. Every object that appears is an object visible in that image, arriving at exactly the position, size, material and colour it has there. Nothing else ever enters the frame. No added furniture, no added decor, no substituted or alternative versions of any piece, no duplicates.

**Camera rule, absolute:** the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance, focal length and lens from the first frame to the last. No pans, no tilts, no pushes, no orbits, no reframing.

**Architecture rule, absolute:** the vertical cedar board walls, the cedar bench platform along the left wall, the boarded cedar ceiling and its single beam, the riven slate floor plane, the black wood stove with its stone basket, and the full-height window on the far wall with the lake and spruce beyond are rigid and fixed. Their positions, proportions and perspective are identical in every frame. Only the fire, the water, the steam, the tub, textiles, vessels, candles, the sconce, the birch bundle and the wall print change.

### Animation Sequence

The shot opens on this same room stripped completely bare: bare slate floor, bare cedar walls, the stove cold and dark with its basket empty, no tub, nothing standing on the floor, nothing on the bench and nothing hanging on any wall. Everything that follows happens inside this one unmoving frame, assembling itself with nothing and no one touching it, each piece arriving directly at its final place in the reference and stopping there.

**0.0s - 1.2s — FIRE AND STONE**

The dark granite stones drop into the steel basket one at a time and settle into a heap, then flames rise inside the stove and settle into a steady burn, their light spreading across the cedar boards and the near slate.

**1.2s - 2.4s — THE TUB**

The black granite tub lowers into place square in front of the window and seats onto the slate, then fills from empty to full with clear water that rises and settles flat and still, steam beginning to lift from the surface.

**2.4s - 4.0s — GROUNDING**

The teak duckboard mat lays itself down beside the tub. The steel towel rail fixes itself to the cedar wall and the two folded towels settle onto it one after the other. The round teak stool sets itself down and the brass bowl and wooden dipper place themselves on it.

**4.0s - 5.2s — LIGHT AND GREENERY**

The brass sconce sets itself into the cedar beside the window and ignites, its pool spreading across the boards. The two candles stand up in their iron holders on the bench and their wicks light one after the other. The bundle of dried birch branches rises into its black floor vessel beside the stove, the branches fanning open.

**5.2s - 6.4s — DRESSING**

The three ceramic vessels place themselves along the cedar bench. The large photographic print rises and leans itself against the cedar wall above the bench. The black hook fixes itself to the boards and the charcoal linen robe hangs itself from it and settles into its folds.

**6.4s - 7.0s — FINAL HOLD**

All assembly motion stops. The water surface goes glassy still and only the steam keeps drifting. The room now matches the reference image exactly, object for object. The grade settles into its final flat silver overcast daylight with firelight and one warm sconce pool. The final frame reads as a still, high-end interior photograph.

### Audio
