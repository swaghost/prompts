# Living Room Miniature Scale-Expansion Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** living-room interior

**Reveal Effect:** miniature room scale expansion

**Reveal Mechanism:** one tiny central room arrangement expands uniformly to full architectural scale while materials sharpen

**Sequence Type:** reference-match interior scale transformation

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A tiny simplified living room expands from the center of an empty shell until its furniture, textiles, shelving, curtains, and plants reach exact full scale and photoreal detail.

## Usage

Use for interior concept launches, miniature-to-life reveals, furniture campaigns, property marketing, and scale-driven design films.

## Engines/Models

Reference-aware video model with uniform scale control, stable object relationships, collision-free growth, material refinement, and final-frame lock.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Finished living room as `@reference`.
- Aligned empty-shell frame with clear central floor area.
- Exact final object inventory, proportions, positions, materials, and camera locked from `@reference`.

## Storyboard Requirement

Optional six-panel board: empty shell and miniature, early expansion, half scale, near full scale, material refinement, final hero.

## Effect

### Effect Name: Miniature Scale Expansion

The complete miniature arrangement scales as one coherent system from a fixed center, preserving relative spacing and proportions before each object settles into its exact final coordinate.

## Video Prompt

```text
Use @reference as the final living room design target and final visual match. Start with a tiny simplified living room in the center of the empty room. The furniture and decor begin inflating and expanding smoothly to full scale: sofa, rug, chairs, coffee table, curtains, shelving, and plants. Textures and materials sharpen into a realistic luxury interior matching @reference exactly. No people, no text, no logos, no cuts.

Create one continuous 10-second reveal from the exact final camera. 0.0-1.2s: hold the empty shell with the complete tiny room centered on its final layout origin. 1.2-5.8s: expand the miniature smoothly and uniformly; preserve every object's relative position, shape, and orientation while sofa, rug, chairs, table, curtains, shelving, and plants grow together. 5.8-8.4s: stop scaling at exact final dimensions and refine fabric, wood, stone, glass, metal, foliage, seams, edges, reflections, and shadows. 8.4-10.0s: settle all details and hold the completed room.

Preserve architecture, camera, inventory, layout, scale relationships, lighting, and final @reference match. No balloon deformation, uneven scaling, object collision, sliding, floating, duplication, room expansion, camera movement, or unfinished final frame.
```

## Interior Reveal Checklist

- [ ] Tiny room begins at the exact layout center.
- [ ] All objects scale together with stable proportions.
- [ ] Architecture and camera never scale or move.
- [ ] Objects finish at exact reference positions and sizes.
- [ ] Material detail sharpens only as scale approaches final.
- [ ] No collision, sliding, floating, or balloon deformation occurs.
- [ ] Lighting and shadows remain coherent.
- [ ] No people, text, logos, cuts, or duplicate objects appear.
- [ ] The 10-second final frame matches `@reference` exactly.
