**Platform:** Seedance 2.5

## Description

A passenger train leaves present-day New York, performs a superluminal leap, and arrives beside the Great Pyramids while they are still under construction. The first half preserves a rigid square-on train-window composition; after arrival, the cameraman follows his friend through the carriage and into a large, historically grounded construction site.
