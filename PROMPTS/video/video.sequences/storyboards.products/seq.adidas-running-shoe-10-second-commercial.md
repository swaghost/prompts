# Adidas Running Shoe - 10-Second Seedance Commercial

## Prerequisites

- The ten-panel Adidas running-shoe storyboard
- Original shoe reference image
- Seedance 2.0

## Global Direction

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Style:** Premium sportswear commercial, cinematic realism, luxury sneaker campaign
- **Color grade:** Cool neutral tones, deep blacks, crisp whites, slightly desaturated high contrast
- **Lighting:** Soft cinematic studio light mixed with natural daylight, dramatic rim light, subtle volumetric atmosphere
- **Lenses:** 35mm, 50mm, and 85mm cinema lenses
- **Depth of field:** Very shallow
- **Motion:** Slow elegant product moves mixed with energetic sports shots
- **Cadence:** Mostly 24fps; action moments feel like high-speed footage slowed down

## Music and Sound

Music begins with deep cinematic bass, soft atmospheric synth, and minimal piano, then builds into a modern electronic sports beat. Add a powerful cinematic hit near second 7 and end with deep impact and long reverb.

Sound design: shoebox opening, tissue-paper crinkle, soft shoe placement, fabric movement, lace tightening, water splash, running footsteps, wind pass, and final bass hit.

## Master Video Prompt

```text
Create a single uninterrupted 10-second premium sportswear commercial from the supplied ten-panel storyboard and black-and-white Adidas Cloudfoam running-shoe reference. Preserve the shoe's exact black mesh upper, white three-stripe placement, Cloudfoam sole, heel design, proportions, materials, and colorway in every shot.

0:00-1:00 - FRAME 01, OPENING: A premium matte-black Adidas shoebox is held with both hands against a dark studio background. Begin a slow cinematic push-in while the hands gently lift the lid. Soft side light creates clean highlights and tiny dust particles drift in the air. Camera: slow dolly forward, tiny handheld breathing, 85mm lens.

1:00-2:00 - FRAME 02, HERO REVEAL: As the lid opens fully, white tissue paper folds back naturally to reveal the black Adidas Cloudfoam shoe. Perform a graceful orbital move while dramatic rim light reveals the mesh texture and white stripes. Match-cut from the lid opening into the tissue-paper reveal.

2:00-3:00 - FRAME 03, STUDIO HERO: The shoe appears on a clean white seamless background. Rotate around it approximately 120 degrees while making a slight upward crane move. Soft reflections travel across the Cloudfoam sole and white stripes. Use slow product rotation and a restrained floating presentation.

3:00-4:00 - FRAME 04, TEXTURE DETAIL: A macro cinema lens glides slowly across the mesh upper. Resolve every fiber while light sweeps gently across the material. Pull focus gradually from mesh texture to stitching.

4:00-5:00 - FRAME 05, LOGO AND LACES: Slide diagonally across the white three stripes while rack-focusing from lace eyelets to logo. Laces move subtly in a gentle air current. Emphasize clean craftsmanship.

5:00-6:00 - FRAME 06, HEEL DETAIL: Begin with the heel tab heavily blurred, then pull into perfect focus on the Adidas branding. Circle slightly around the heel as a soft rim light creates a clean glowing edge.

6:00-7:00 - FRAME 07, FIRST STEP IMPACT: Increase pace. The shoe lands forcefully on a wet black surface in dramatic slow motion. Water erupts in physically realistic droplets. Track beside the shoe with slight rotation and restrained impact shake. Begin the powerful bass hit here.

7:00-8:00 - FRAME 08, LIFESTYLE: Speed-ramp into a model wearing the shoes while walking down modern concrete stairs. Follow from behind at ankle level, then slide smoothly to a front three-quarter angle. Keep the shoe dominant and the environment softly blurred.

8:00-9:00 - FRAME 09, RUNNING MOTION: The runner sprints through a modern city street. Track perfectly beside them. Keep the shoe razor sharp while the background streaks with realistic motion blur. Clothing reacts naturally to wind and sunlight creates dynamic highlights.

9:00-10:00 - FRAME 10, FINAL HERO: Dissolve into a dramatic studio. Both shoes rotate slowly together on a glossy black reflective surface under soft volumetric light. Finish with a subtle push toward the white stripes, one deep impact hit, settling logo reflection, long atmospheric reverb, synth fade, then fade to black.

Maintain cohesive lighting, product geometry, branding, material response, and color grading through match cuts and speed ramps. No product drift, incorrect logo, altered sole, color change, warped shoe, duplicate limbs, deformed feet, floating contact, fake water, abrupt jump cuts, inconsistent scale, text overlays, or watermark.
```
