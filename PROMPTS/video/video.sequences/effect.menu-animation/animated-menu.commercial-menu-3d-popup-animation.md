# Commercial Menu 3D Popup Animation

## Classification

**Product Category:** Food advertising / miniature menu / 3D popup animation

**Reveal Effect:** Menu items rise from a display board as realistic handcrafted miniature pizzas

**Sequence Type:** Cinematic menu-board scan / miniature food reveal / commercial animation

**Source:** [Vexmo Animation Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-1XY4AaTO1x5mlkO6EZ_-bqkMUsHjGzc/mobilebasic?urp=gmail_link)

## Description

A cinematic close-up video of a luxury miniature pizza-shop display board. As the camera slowly glides across the board, each listed item transforms into a realistic handcrafted miniature 3D popup emerging seamlessly from the surface.

The camera smoothly transitions focus from one menu item to the next while the food rises, assembles, and receives its finishing ingredients. The result should feel premium, tactile, appetizing, and suitable for a restaurant commercial.

## Technical Specifications

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape
- **Setting:** Warm golden trattoria interior at night
- **Camera:** Smooth glide across the menu board with focus transitions between items
- **Motion:** Slow-motion reveal, smooth camera movement, no jump cuts
- **Style:** Luxury miniature food advertising, handcrafted 3D popup realism

### Margherita — $7

## Shot Progression

Use the sequence as a continuous reveal:

1. **Opening:** Establish the luxury miniature pizza-shop menu board in the warm, dark trattoria setting.
2. **Menu scan:** Glide slowly across the board and bring the first item into focus.
3. **Margherita reveal:** Raise the pizza and settle the mozzarella and basil.
4. **Pepperoni reveal:** Transition focus and animate the pepperoni cups and oil.
5. **Truffle Mushroom reveal:** Shift focus as truffle falls over the melted cheese and mushrooms.
6. **Prosciutto Arugula reveal:** Finish with prosciutto, arugula, and olive oil.
7. **End frame:** Hold a polished commercial hero view of the completed miniature menu.

Keep every transition smooth and connected. Do not turn the sequence into separate jump-cut shots.
