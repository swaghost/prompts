# Luxury Coffee Bar Marble Menu Sequence

## Classification

**Product Category:** Specialty coffee advertising / miniature menu / beverage transformation

**Reveal Effect:** Engraved coffee icons on a marble board transform into miniature drinks

**Sequence Type:** Luxury menu-board showcase / macro food animation / continuous reveal

**Format:** 16:9 landscape video

## Concept

A cinematic close-up video of a luxury miniature coffee-bar display board. Each drink begins as a flat engraved icon or etching on a matte black honed marble surface. As the camera moves across the board, the icon becomes a physical miniature drink: the cup rises, liquid pours, foam forms, and toppings settle with realistic gravity and contact.

The board has refined specialty-coffee-boutique styling, with brushed copper inlay accents along the edges and a subtle reflective sheen that catches the warm light. Soft steam wisps drift faintly across the marble surface throughout the shot.

## Board and Setting

- Matte black honed marble display board
- Brushed copper inlay accents along the edges
- Refined specialty-coffee-boutique aesthetic
- Subtle reflective sheen catching warm light
- Soft steam wisps drifting across the board throughout the shot
- Luxury miniature coffee-bar presentation

## Transformation Mechanic

Each menu item starts as a flat engraved icon or etching on the marble. The icon then transforms into a miniature beverage through visible, physically connected actions:

- The cup rises from the marble surface.
- Liquid pours from above or forms inside the cup.
- Foam develops and settles naturally.
- Toppings fall, sprinkle, or drizzle according to their real physical behavior.
- Gravity, splash, clink, contact, and settling must look natural.
- No floating ingredients or unnatural motion.

## Menu Items and Reveals

### Espresso Shot — $4

A tiny espresso cup rises from the marble as a thin stream of dark, crema-topped espresso pours in from above. A fine layer of golden crema settles on top.

### Cappuccino — $5

A miniature cup rises as steamed milk pours in. A dollop of foam settles on top, followed last by a light dusting of cocoa powder.

### Caramel Macchiato — $6

A small glass cup rises as espresso layers beneath steamed milk. A thin ribbon of caramel then drizzles across the foam in a crosshatch pattern.

### Iced Vanilla Latte — $6

A tiny glass rises as ice cubes drop in first with a soft clink. Cold milk pours over espresso in a marbled swirl, and a few coffee beans settle on top.

- **Duration:** 10 seconds total
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape
- **Frame rate:** 24fps
- **Movement:** Smooth continuous camera travel across the board
- **Focus:** Transition focus from one active item to the next
- **Lens:** Macro food-photography simulation

- **Motion feel:** Approximately 0.75x speed during rises and pours
- **Continuity:** One unbroken shot, no jump cuts, no camera shake

Use the camera movement to reveal the items in order while maintaining a clear active-item focal plane. The inactive sections of the board may soften naturally, but the marble, copper inlay, and menu identity should remain coherent.

## Physics and Material Requirements

- Espresso must pour as a thin, dark stream and form a fine golden crema layer.
- Steamed milk must flow naturally into the cappuccino before the foam settles.
- Cocoa powder must fall after the foam and land lightly on its surface.
- Espresso and steamed milk in the macchiato must form visibly distinct layers.
- Caramel must drizzle in a clean, thin crosshatch pattern over the foam.
- Ice cubes must drop before the iced latte pour and produce a subtle clink.
- Cold milk must create a visible marbled swirl as it mixes with espresso.
- Coffee beans must settle onto the iced drink rather than float.
- Steam must remain soft, sparse, and atmospheric across the board.
- All liquids, powders, ice, foam, and toppings must obey gravity and believable surface contact.
