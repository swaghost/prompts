# Restaurant Menu 3D Dish Emergence Sequence

## Description

A 10-second premium restaurant food commercial built from an uploaded menu image. The original menu remains the source of truth while three or four clearly identifiable dishes physically emerge from their exact printed positions, transforming flat menu elements into realistic three-dimensional plated dishes.

The camera moves smoothly across the menu with elegant dolly movement, controlled focus transitions, natural parallax, and no jump cuts. Each dish receives a different food-specific action based on what it actually is.

**Source:** [Restaurant 3D Menu Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1No3rUjKvllbjYqkoN3JG0mG0ENbqiE8dh2ogzMEdR20/mobilebasic?urp=gmail_link)

## Prerequisites

- Upload one clear photograph or scan of the restaurant menu.
- Use the uploaded menu as the primary and only source of truth.
- Select only 3 or 4 dishes whose names and visual identity can be clearly understood.
- Do not invent, guess, duplicate, or add dishes that are not present.

## Technical Specifications

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Preferred format:** Vertical 9:16 image-to-video
- **Alternative format:** 16:9 landscape
- **Style:** Photorealistic premium food-commercial cinematography
- **Camera:** Smooth continuous dolly movement across the menu
- **Focus:** Controlled focus pulls between active dishes
- **Lens:** Macro food-photography detail
- **Depth of field:** Shallow, with the active dish in focus
- **Motion:** Seamless transitions, no jump cuts
- **Finish:** Luxury restaurant advertising aesthetic

## Sequence Structure

### 0–2.5 Seconds: First Dish

Establish the full menu and slowly push toward the first clearly selected dish. The flat printed menu element begins to emerge from its exact location and becomes a realistic plated dish. Apply only motion appropriate to the identified food.

### 2.5–5 Seconds: Second Dish

Transition smoothly across the menu toward the second selected dish. It rises naturally from its printed position and becomes fully dimensional. Maintain the menu underneath and around the emerging food.

### 5–7.5 Seconds: Third Dish

Move to the third dish with a controlled focus pull. Repeat the physical emergence using a different food-specific action that matches the dish's real properties.

### 7.5–10 Seconds: Fourth Dish or Hero Hold

Reveal a fourth dish only when it is confidently identifiable from the menu. Otherwise, give the strongest selected dish a more detailed hero moment, then pull back to reveal the intact restaurant menu and integrated dishes.

## Dish-Specific Motion Guide

Use only the motion appropriate to the identified dish:

- **Hot dishes:** Steam, subtle heat distortion, glossy sauce, sizzling or freshly served movement.
- **Fried or crispy dishes:** Crisp surface, restrained oil sheen, crumbs, or garnish movement.
- **Cheese dishes:** Gentle melt or cheese stretch only when naturally expected.
- **Cold drinks and desserts:** Condensation, chilled surfaces, ice movement, or creamy texture. No hot steam.
- **Grilled or roasted dishes:** Subtle sizzle, charred texture, and physically appropriate heat or smoke.

Do not add effects simply to make the sequence busier. The transformation must remain physical, elegant, and believable.

## Consistency Rules

- The uploaded menu is the source of truth.
- Preserve restaurant branding, restaurant name, logo, menu layout, colours, typography, dish names, prices, decorative elements, and overall design.
- Keep the original menu intact underneath and around the emerging dishes.
- Use only dishes clearly identified from the uploaded menu.
- Use the actual menu names and visual references.
- Do not add dishes that are not present.
- Do not duplicate dishes.
- Do not hallucinate ingredients inconsistent with the identified dish.
- Do not turn one dish into another.
- Do not create random plates or food in the background.
- Do not introduce unrelated props.
- Keep the food at realistic restaurant-serving proportions, not miniature scale.
