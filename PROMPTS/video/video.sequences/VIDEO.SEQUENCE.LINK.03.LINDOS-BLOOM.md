## Video Prompt

Create a 30-second, 16:9 romantic cinematic couple film with soft rhythmic cuts driven by rack-focus transitions and motion-blur passes.

**Duration:** 30 seconds, 16:9.

**Style:** 35mm analog film aesthetic matching @Image 3: fine grain, soft pastel grade, creamy halation, muted greens, warm skin tones, golden late-afternoon Aegean light, gentle handheld sway, shallow depth of field as the storytelling tool, romantic realism, one visual identity, no style mixing.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
@Image 1 defines both faces and relationship identity. The man has curly dark brown hair, light beard, black square-framed wayfarer sunglasses, thin silver chain, cream resort shirt with botanical embroidery, relaxed beige linen trousers, and tan espadrilles. The woman has a dark chin-length bob under a white patterned headscarf tied at the nape, white angular cat-eye sunglasses, small red hoop earrings, and a sleeveless denim mini dress locked by @Image 2. Both keep sunglasses on in every shot.

@Image 3 defines the white classic coupe, front trunk overflowing with wildflowers, tall meadow, and analog film texture. @Image 4 defines only the Lindos acropolis, white cube houses, and hazed hillside backdrop. Use it as scenery only; do not move the acropolis into the foreground.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:03
Open with a rack focus from foreground wildflowers to the white classic coupe in the meadow. End state: the car is sharp, flowers still partially blurred, and the golden-hour Aegean palette is established.

0:03-0:06
A motion-blur pass reveals the couple moving through the meadow toward the car. Preserve faces, sunglasses, wardrobe, and body proportions. End state: the couple is clear, with the flower-stuffed trunk visible as the destination.

0:06-0:09
Close on hands and flowers near the open front trunk. Stems shift naturally, petals brush lens edge, and the focus lands on one orange-gold bloom.

0:09-0:12
The couple spins or crosses around the coupe in a playful, private moment. Gentle handheld sway, shallow focus, and warm light keep the scene intimate. End state: their movement settles without losing identity.

0:12-0:15
Rack focus through a reflection or lens edge from his face to her smile. Keep both faces locked to @Image 1 and eyewear unchanged.

0:15-0:18
The flower leaves the trunk and becomes the emotional handoff. Focus follows the bloom rather than cutting abruptly. End state: her hand is almost caught or the bloom is almost placed.

0:18-0:21
The orange-gold bloom becomes part of her styling near the headscarf. Hold enough detail to confirm it remains consistent from this point through the finale.

0:21-0:24
The couple runs or moves left to right through the meadow near the coupe. Motion blur is rhythmic and romantic, never chaotic. The acropolis remains hazy scenery only.

0:24-0:27
They enter or pass the classic coupe. Use rack focus from foreground flower detail to car door, then to faces. Keep exactly one white coupe and one flower arrangement.

0:27-0:30
Final poetic wide: the valley road, the white coupe, the meadow, and the Lindos backdrop resolve through one last rack focus. End with the car and couple tiny but legible, golden Aegean light intact.

**Camera:**
Use human-scale camera language: rack focus as the editor, gentle handheld sway, soft rhythmic cuts, motion-blur transitions, no drone shots. Every shot has a clear end state and preserves the spatial relationship among couple, car, meadow, and Lindos backdrop.

**Lighting:**
Golden late-afternoon Aegean light, warm skin tones, muted green meadow, pastel highlights, creamy halation, and consistent sun direction.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
Rack focus is the main transition grammar: petals to coupe, blooms to running couple, reflections to smiles, and final wildflower to distant car. Keep @Image 4 as scenery only. Preserve the orange-gold bloom once placed at her headscarf.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
One consistent 35mm analog look, stable faces, no removed sunglasses, no second car, no other people, no style mixing, no CGI look, no warped acropolis, no foreground acropolis, no duplicated flower arrangement, no broken left-to-right flow, and no identity drift.

Audio direction: soft romantic natural ambience, delicate stem snap, trunk lid soft click, twin door thunks, felt piano entering on the first rack focus and lifting when the flower is given.
