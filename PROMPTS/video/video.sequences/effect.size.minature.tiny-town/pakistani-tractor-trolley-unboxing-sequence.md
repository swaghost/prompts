Capture realistic worker movements, welding details, polishing, installation, and final preparation. Show the Pakistani-style tractor trolley design with realistic textures, scratches, metal reflections, and farming equipment details. End with the trolley fully unloaded and positioned in the yard.

## Scene 3 (20-30 seconds) – Final Luxury Reveal

Continue exactly from the previous frame. Finish with a cinematic luxury reveal of the fully unboxed tractor trolley in the miniature farm yard. Show a slow camera slide, smooth 360-degree rotation, and detailed views of the front, sides, wheels, trolley bed, metal structure, and all finishing details. Add realistic sunlight, shadows, reflections, and a premium advertisement-style final reveal. Present it like a high-end agricultural equipment showcase.
