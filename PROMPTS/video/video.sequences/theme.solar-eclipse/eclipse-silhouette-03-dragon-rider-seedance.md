# Eclipse Silhouette: Dragon Rider - Seedance

**Platform:** Seedance (Higgsfield AI)  
**Technique:** First & Last Frame Workflow  
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Subject:** Armored rider on rearing dragon  
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard (wing mechanics + creature anatomy)

---

## Concept

A dragon rider enters from the right, lands at center, and the dragon rears—wings flaring wide, neck arched, front claws pawing air. This is the **wildest visual** of the three Eclipse Silhouette shots because you get wing mechanics plus creature anatomy, but it's also easier than the banner shot because there's no cloth physics to manage—just leather membranes and scaled muscle.

**Critical Challenge:** Wing structure must be anatomically coherent. If the wings look broken or the joints disappear, strengthen the "visible bone structure through stretched membrane" language.

- **Wings:** Massive, flared wide, visible bone structure through membrane
- **Anatomy:** Long serpentine neck, segmented spine plates, spiked tail
- **Muscle:** Layered scaled definition across chest/shoulders/haunches
- **Claws:** Front claws raised and spread, rear talons dug into ridge
- **Head:** Angled skyward mid-roar, jaws open showing teeth
- **Action:** Flying → Landing → Rearing

### Rider Design

- **Armor:** Segmented plate with pauldrons

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Wings look broken or joints are unclear  
**Solution:** Emphasize "visible bone structure through stretched wing membrane" and list the joints explicitly: "shoulder joint, elbow joint, wrist, finger joints"

**Issue:** Dragon anatomy looks blobby or undefined  
**Solution:** Strengthen "layered scaled muscle definition" and add "visible tendon lines down the rear legs"

**Issue:** Landing looks floaty or weightless  
**Solution:** Add "dust bursting outward," "talons digging in," "massive wing beats displacing air"

**Issue:** Dragon stops off-center  
**Solution:** Emphasize "lands at the exact centre of the eclipse" and confirm last frame is loaded correctly

**Issue:** Entrance not visible (starts centered)  
**Solution:** Strengthen "The crest is empty" and "enters from the right edge of frame"

**Issue:** Roar not visible  
**Solution:** Add more detail: "jaws opening wide, rows of teeth flashing as negative shapes against the corona"

## Pro Tips

### Wing Structure is Everything

Dragon wings must have **clear joint articulation**. If you can't see shoulder-elbow-wrist-fingers, it's not working. The bone framework is what makes the silhouette readable.

### Landing Weight Matters

The dragon should feel **massive**. Use "dust bursting," "talons digging in," "deep thumping bass" from wing beats. Weight = believability.

### Rear Timing

The rear should occupy the **last 2 seconds** of the shot. Don't rush it—let the front claws rise slowly, wings flare wide, and hold the pose.

### Camera Lock is Critical

State the camera lock at least three different ways at the start of the prompt. Seedance will drift without aggressive reinforcement.

### Audio Sells the Shot

The diegetic audio (massive wing beats, talons scraping rock, roar resonating) adds massive authenticity. The wing beat bass should be **felt**, not just heard.
