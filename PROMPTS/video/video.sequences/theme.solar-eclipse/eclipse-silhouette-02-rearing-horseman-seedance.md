# Eclipse Silhouette: Rearing Horseman - Seedance

**Platform:** Seedance (Higgsfield AI)  
**Technique:** First & Last Frame Workflow  
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Subject:** Armored rider on rearing warhorse  
**Difficulty:** Medium (muscle + wind interaction)

---

## Concept

An armored rider gallops along the eclipse ridgeline, stops at center, and the horse rears—front legs pawing air, mane and tail flowing in the wind. This is the **easiest visual hit** of the three Eclipse Silhouette shots: the horse muscle reads clean in silhouette, and the mane/tail flow is simpler than the banner cloth physics.

**Critical Challenge:** Keeping the horse silhouette anatomically readable. If the legs get messy or the muscle definition disappears, strengthen the "layered muscle definition under stretched skin" language.

### Horse Design

- **Type:** Heavy warhorse
- **Anatomy:** Arched neck, layered muscle, visible tendons
- **Mane:** Long, streaming with strand separation
- **Tail:** Long, flowing, coiling and rippling

- **Posture:** Head thrown back, mouth open mid-whinny

### Rider Design

- **Armor:** Segmented plate with pauldrons
- **Helm:** Crested design
- **Cloak:** Long, streaming behind, flows over hindquarters
- **Weapon:** Lance angled upward over shoulder
- **Pose:** Locked in saddle, one arm raised with reins

### Action Timeline

1. **0-3 seconds:** Gallops in from right edge, hooves pounding
2. **3-4 seconds:** Reaches center, begins rearing
3. **4-5 seconds:** Front legs pawing air, holds rearing position

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Horse anatomy looks undefined or blobby  
**Solution:** Strengthen "layered muscle definition under stretched skin" and add "visible tendon lines"

**Issue:** Mane/tail looks stiff or clumped  
**Solution:** Emphasize "long loose strands separating," "strand separation and weight," "catching air"

**Issue:** Horse stops off-center  
**Solution:** Emphasize "reach the exact centre of the eclipse" and confirm last frame is loaded correctly

**Issue:** Entrance not visible (starts centered)  
**Solution:** Strengthen "The crest is empty" and "enters from the right edge of frame"

**Issue:** Rear looks incomplete or front legs don't rise  
**Solution:** Add more detail: "front legs rising and pawing the air, hooves flashing up against the corona, head thrown back"

**Issue:** Rider bounces or slides in saddle  
**Solution:** Emphasize "locked in the saddle" and add "weight centered over the horse's balance point"

## Pro Tips

### Silhouette Readability

The horse's anatomy must be **immediately readable** in silhouette. Don't let it become a blob—the muscle definition is what makes this shot work.

### Gallop Rhythm

The gallop should have **visible rhythm**—hooves pound, mane streams, dust kicks up. This creates visual beats that lead into the rear.

### Rear Timing

The rear should occupy the **last 2 seconds** of the shot. Don't rush it—let the front legs rise slowly and hold the pose.

### Camera Lock is Critical

State the camera lock at least three different ways at the start of the prompt. Seedance will drift without aggressive reinforcement.

### Audio Sells the Shot

The diegetic audio (hoof thunder, armor creaking, horse snorting, leather flexing) adds massive authenticity.
