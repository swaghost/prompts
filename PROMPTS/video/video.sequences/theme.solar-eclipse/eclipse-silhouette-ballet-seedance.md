# Create a Cinematic Total Solar Eclipse Ballet Video with Seedance 2.5 in 1080p

## Overview

This workflow uses Seedance 2.5 in 1080p on Higgsfield AI to transform a single portrait into a cinematic fashion film featuring a graceful ballerina performing beneath a Total Solar Eclipse.

**Platform:** Higgsfield AI (https://higgsfield.ai)

**Output:** 16:9 aspect ratio, 10 seconds, 1080p cinematic video

If you're new to Higgsfield, you can try Seedance 2.5 in 1080p for a limited time without purchasing a subscription.

---

## Step 1 — Open Higgsfield AI

1. Go to https://higgsfield.ai
2. Select **Seedance 2.5 (1080p)**
3. Set the aspect ratio to **16:9**
4. Set the video length to **10 Seconds**

---

## Step 2 — Upload Your Reference Image

Upload one portrait image of your character.

**The uploaded image will be used only to preserve the person's identity.**

Seedance will automatically recreate the same:

- Face
- Hairstyle
- Freckles
- Skin tone
- Eye shape
- Smile
- Body proportions
- Overall identity

...while generating an entirely new cinematic scene.

1. **Reference Image Quality:** Use a high-resolution portrait with clear facial features

5. **Lighting Realism:** The solar corona provides dramatic backlighting while twilight provides natural front fill
