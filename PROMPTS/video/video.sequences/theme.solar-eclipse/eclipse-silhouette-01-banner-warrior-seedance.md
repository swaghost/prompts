# Eclipse Silhouette: Banner Warrior - Seedance

**Platform:** Seedance (Higgsfield AI)  
**Technique:** First & Last Frame Workflow  
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Subject:** Lone warrior with banner staff  
**Difficulty:** Hard (cloth physics intensive)

---

## Concept

A lone armored warrior walks along the eclipse ridgeline carrying a banner staff, plants it at center, and holds position as the banner flows continuously in the wind. This is the **hardest of the three** Eclipse Silhouette shots because the flag is the whole shot—roughly a third of the video prompt is spent on cloth physics alone.

**Critical Challenge:** Getting realistic fabric physics. If the banner comes back stiff, push further into the fluid language rather than adding wind.

---

## Step 1: Build the Last Frame Plate

### Image Prompt

```
A cinematic anamorphic still photograph, extreme wide shot on a heavy telephoto lens. A lone warrior stands in full silhouette on the crest of a bare dark ridgeline, dead centre of frame, rendered entirely as black shape with no facial detail and no colour readable — only contour. Segmented plate armour with layered pauldrons and a ridged crested helm, a heavy cloak hanging from the shoulders and lifting at the hem in the wind. Both hands gripping a long banner staff driven into the ground at his side, the staff rising vertically through the centre of the frame. A large heavy banner hangs from a crossbar near the top of the staff, catching wind and pulling sideways, the fabric rippling with visible weight and a ragged frayed trailing edge, its embroidered emblem readable only as dark negative shape against the light behind it. Behind and above the ridge, a total solar eclipse fills the upper two thirds of the frame — flat charcoal disc, searing thin amber-white corona, warm halo bleeding into a deep grey-violet sky. Dust and haze drift through faint volumetric beams. The ridge is a smooth unbroken dark curve across the lower third, top edge rimmed in amber. No vegetation, no structures. Shot on ARRI Alexa Mini LF, Panavision Ultra Vintage anamorphic 85mm at T2.3, Tiffen Black Pro-Mist 1/4, heavy telephoto compression, deep focus, strong negative space, warm amber against cold desaturated grey, Kodak Vision3 250D with fine grain, soft vignette and halation bloom on the corona edge. Fabric with real weave, real weight, real drape and wind load. Metal armour with worn edges and a hard specular rim where the backlight catches the silhouette. Photographic, not rendered.
```

**Key Elements:**

- Warrior dead center in pure silhouette
- Segmented plate armor with crested helm
- Heavy cloak hanging from shoulders
- Banner staff driven into ground, rising vertically
- Large heavy banner catching wind, pulling sideways
- Fabric rippling with ragged frayed trailing edge
- Emblem readable as dark negative shape only

## Step 2: Animate It

### Settings

- **First frame:** The empty eclipse plate (from base plate file)
- **Last frame:** The plate you just generated
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Multi-shot:** Off

### Video Prompt (5 seconds)

```
Extreme long-lens telephoto shot, locked-off tripod, no camera movement whatsoever — no zoom, no push-in, no pull-back, no pan, no tilt, no reframing. The frame never changes. A total solar eclipse fills the frame — a flat black disc ringed by a searing amber-white corona bleeding into a deep grey-violet sky — sitting low behind a bare dark ridgeline. The crest is empty. A lone warrior in pure black silhouette walks in from the right edge of frame along the ridgeline, crested helm, segmented plate armour, a long banner staff carried in one hand, his cloak dragging sideways in the wind, dust lifting from each footfall. The banner is thin worn cloth, soft and weightless, never stiff and never board-like — it undulates continuously in slow rolling waves down its length, the fabric rippling and folding over on itself, the frayed trailing edges curling and fluttering loosely, catching air pockets that travel from the staff outward to the tip. He walks steadily left until he reaches the exact centre of the eclipse, then stops and plants the staff butt into the ground, both hands settling on it, chin lifting. The banner keeps flowing the whole time, billowing and collapsing and billowing again, folds sliding across the corona behind it, the cloak rippling loosely against his legs. Dust drifts through the corona light, heat shimmer along the horizon. Only the subject and the cloth move; the camera stays completely still. Heavy telephoto compression flattening the eclipse against the ridge, deep focus, strong negative space, warm amber against cold desaturated grey, Kodak Vision3 250D with fine grain, soft vignette and halation bloom on the corona edge, 24fps 180 degree shutter, roughly five seconds. Audio: diegetic only — low steady wind across open ground, boots compressing dry earth, armour plates shifting with each stride, the staff thudding into the ground, soft cloth rippling and fluttering, grit skittering downslope, no music.
```

## Key Elements Breakdown

### Character Design

- **Armor:** Segmented plate with layered pauldrons
- **Helm:** Ridged crested design
- **Cloak:** Heavy, dragging sideways in wind
- **Pose:** Both hands gripping staff, chin lifted

### Banner Design

- **Staff:** Long, driven into ground, rises vertically
- **Banner:** Large, heavy, hanging from crossbar
- **Fabric:** Thin worn cloth, soft and weightless
- **Edge:** Ragged frayed trailing edge
- **Emblem:** Readable only as dark negative shape

### Action Timeline

1. **0-3 seconds:** Walks in from right edge
2. **3-4 seconds:** Reaches center, stops, plants staff
3. **4-5 seconds:** Holds position, banner continues flowing

### Cloth Physics Language (Critical)

**Use These (Fluid):**

- "Undulates continuously in slow rolling waves"
- "Rippling and folding over on itself"
- "Frayed trailing edges curling and fluttering loosely"
- "Catching air pockets that travel from staff to tip"
- "Billowing and collapsing and billowing again"
- "Folds sliding across the corona"

**Never Use (Stiff):**

- Taut
- Snaps
- Whips
- Pulled tight
- Board-like

## Advanced Techniques

### Cloth Physics Density

For more dramatic fabric movement:

```
...the banner catches a strong gust and flares wide, stretching nearly horizontal before folding back on itself, the entire length rippling in waves, edges curling inward...
```

For calmer, heavier fabric:

```
...the banner moves slowly under its own weight, thick folds cascading down from the crossbar, gentle undulations traveling through the weave, minimal flutter at the edges...
```

### Staff Planting Impact

Add dust burst when staff hits ground:

```
...then stops and drives the staff butt into the ground with force, a burst of dust erupting from the impact point and drifting outward...
```

### Resolution Beat Extension

Add small movements after landing:

```
...chin lifting, then he adjusts his grip slightly on the staff, weight shifting from one foot to the other, cloak settling around his legs...
```

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Banner looks stiff like cardboard  
**Solution:** Remove any "taut," "snap," or "whip" language. Add more fluid descriptors: "rolling waves," "folding over on itself," "curling edges"

**Issue:** Warrior stops off-center  
**Solution:** Emphasize "walks steadily left until he reaches the exact centre of the eclipse" and confirm last frame is loaded correctly

**Issue:** Banner stops moving after staff is planted  
**Solution:** Add "The banner keeps flowing the whole time, billowing and collapsing and billowing again"

**Issue:** Entrance not visible (starts centered)  
**Solution:** Strengthen "The crest is empty" and "walks in from the right edge of frame"

**Issue:** Cloth physics too extreme (flapping wildly)  
**Solution:** Remove wind references, focus on weight: "moves slowly under its own weight," "thick folds cascading"

## Pro Tips

### Fabric Weight Matters

Describe the banner as having "visible weight" and "real drape"—this grounds the physics in reality rather than cartoon movement.

### The 1/3 Rule

Roughly one-third of the video prompt is cloth physics. This ratio is deliberate—the fabric is the hero element of this shot.

### Camera Lock is Critical

State the camera lock at least three different ways at the start of the prompt. Seedance will drift without aggressive reinforcement.

### Audio Sells the Shot

The diegetic audio (boots, armor, staff thudding, cloth fluttering) adds massive authenticity even though you're generating video.
