# From Thread to Texture Knitwear Sequence

## Classification

**Product Category:** apparel

**Reveal Effect:** textile-process-reveal

**Reveal Mechanism:** yarn spinning, knit formation, denim distressing, patch stitching, flat-lay product reveal, and worn macro hero shot build one sweater texture story

**Sequence Type:** multi-shot product process sequence

**Filename:** `seq.apparel.textile-process-reveal.from-thread-to-texture.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 15-second multi-shot fashion commercial sequence that traces a black ribbed knit sweater with distressed denim patch pocket and silver button details from yarn and construction textures to a finished flat-lay and worn product hero shot.

## Usage

Perfect for knitwear campaigns, fashion construction reels, product texture storytelling, apparel commercials, textile process sequences, and Seedance multi-shot prompt scripting.

Ideal for transforming one product reference into a tactile manufacturing narrative where each shot has one clear motion: spin, weave, fray, stitch, reveal, and wear.

## Engines/Models

Designed as a Seedance multi-shot prompt script. Global target: 4K, 24fps, smooth camera motion, cinematic fashion-commercial style.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Dependency #1 - Uploaded reference image of a black ribbed knit sweater.
- Dependency #2 - Reference must include distressed denim patch pocket and cuff detail.
- Dependency #3 - Reference must show silver buttons, denim wash, fraying pattern, rib knit texture, and button placement clearly.

## Video Prompt

Create a Seedance multi-shot product texture sequence: Concept 1, "From Thread to Texture," a 15-second cut.

**Duration:** Approximately 15 seconds total: 2 + 2.5 + 2.5 + 3 + 2 + 3 seconds.

**Style:** Cinematic aesthetic, moody editorial lighting, shallow depth of field, soft film grain, high-end fashion commercial style, 4K, 24fps, smooth camera motion.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Reference image: uploaded black ribbed knit sweater with distressed denim patch pocket and cuff detail, silver buttons. This exact product must remain 100% consistent across every shot: same denim wash, same fraying pattern, same rib knit texture, same button placement. Do not alter color, stitching, or design in any shot.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

Shot 1 - Yarn Origin, 2s
Prompt: Extreme macro of dark charcoal wool yarn spinning on a wooden bobbin against black backdrop, soft top light, slow focus pull from blur to sharp.
Camera: Static macro, quick focus pull.

Shot 2 - Knit Formation, 2.5s
Prompt: Close-up of knitting needles interlocking wool yarn, forming ribbed knit texture matching reference sweater. Warm amber side light, shallow focus, hypnotic motion.
Camera: Slight dolly-in, macro.

Shot 3 - Denim Distress, 2.5s
Prompt: Macro of raw denim, exact wash of reference patch pocket, being hand-frayed with a seam ripper, threads lifting in slow motion. Cool key light, crisp texture.
Camera: Static, slow-motion look.

Shot 4 - Stitching the Patch, 3s
Prompt: Extreme macro of needle piercing denim and knit fabric, thread pulling taut, replicating reference sweater's pocket and cuff construction. Slow-motion, needle catches light glint.
Camera: Slow push-in, macro.

Shot 5 - The Reveal, 2s
Prompt: Wide shot revealing the complete finished sweater laid flat on wooden table, exactly matching uploaded reference: denim patch, cuff, buttons, ribbed knit. Soft natural window light, crane-up reveal.
Camera: Fast crane-up.

Shot 6 - Worn Final Shot, 3s
Prompt: Woman wears the exact reference sweater, unchanged denim patch and knit texture. Soft window light. Camera starts on her face, tilts down and racks focus to hold on denim patch pocket and buttons in macro. Cinematic, product is hero of frame.
Camera: Face to tilt-down rack focus to hold. Duration: 3s, ends on held frame.

**Camera:**
Use clear single-motion shots: static macro focus pull, slight macro dolly-in, static slow-motion texture, slow macro push-in, fast crane-up, then face-to-product tilt-down with rack focus. Each shot should have one readable action.

**Lighting:**
Moody editorial lighting overall. Soft top light for yarn, warm amber side light for knit formation, cool key light for denim distress, glinting macro stitch light, soft natural window light for reveal and worn product hero.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
Preserve the exact reference sweater identity throughout: black ribbed knit texture, distressed denim patch pocket and cuff detail, same denim wash, same fraying pattern, same button placement, same silver buttons, same stitching, and same design.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
4K, 24fps, shallow depth of field, soft film grain, high-end fashion commercial finish. If the model trims automatically, prioritize keeping Shot 4 and Shot 6 at full length since those carry the most visual payoff: stitching detail and final product hero shot.

Pacing Note: At this length, cut faster than the timings suggest feel. Seedance tends to render smoother if each shot has a clear single motion: spin, weave, fray, stitch, reveal, wear, rather than multiple actions per clip.

Negative Prompt: no altered sweater design, no changed denim wash, no changed fraying pattern, no changed rib knit texture, no changed button placement, no missing silver buttons, no mismatched pocket, no synthetic plastic fabric, no low-detail textile, no chaotic edits, no random extra garments, no distorted hands, no unreadable product detail.
