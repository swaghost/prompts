# Cupid - Angel and Bag Throw Sequence (Seedance 2.5)

## Overview

A 19.5-second ultra-realistic cinematic fantasy sequence in which a bored rooftop angel targets a woman walking below, only for her to throw her shoulder bag back at him. The woman remains calm and inward-looking throughout while the angel's confusion and disbelief become the final visual beat.

**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 30fps  
**Model:** Seedance 2.5  
**Style:** Photorealistic feature-film fantasy campaign, golden-hour European city light fading into dusk

## Style and Technical Notes

- Consistent golden-hour warm sidelight transitioning to cooler dusk tones
- Realistic wing and feather aerodynamics, including downstroke lift, upstroke drag reduction, independent feather movement, and continuous hover balancing
- Realistic bag-throw physics with proper arc, spin, impact, and momentum
- Realistic gold metal reflections on the bow and quiver
- Natural skin and fabric texture
- Soft lens flares, cinematic teal-and-amber color grade, cinematic shadows, HDR lighting
- Shallow depth of field isolating the woman in sharp foreground focus during the final shot while the angel remains a soft, readable silhouette
- Butter-smooth camera motion with no jarring cuts during the aerial shots
- ARRI Alexa 65 and Zeiss Master Prime lens feel
- Big-budget feature-film color science and high-end editorial fantasy-campaign aesthetic
- No face distortion, extra people, or visual glitches
- The angel's head and gaze stay locked downward on the woman throughout the aerial sequence and never turn toward camera
- The woman's expression stays calm, detached, and inward-looking: no smugness and no triumph, only quiet indifference
- The angel's confusion and disbelief remain readable even at a distance in the final background
- The large vertical gap between the two figures during the hover sequence is a key visual element emphasizing height and scale
