# POV Wellness & Self-Care

**POV LIFESTYLE / WELLNESS CONTENT**

---

## Platform

First-person POV lifestyle content platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video or still images  
**Style:** Calming, restorative, mindful wellness moments

---

## Concept

Exercise, spa moments, meditation, healthy living from first-person perspective—capturing the intimate and personal experience of self-care rituals, wellness routines, and healthy lifestyle choices through the viewer's eyes.

---

## Typical Shots

- Yoga mat view looking down at hands in poses
- View from bathtub (legs visible, book/wine/candles nearby)
- Hands applying skincare products
- Morning stretch view from bed
- Holding healthy smoothie bowl (view looking down)
- Running/walking trail view with feet visible
- Spa/massage table view looking down
- Meditation cushion perspective
- Hands preparing healthy meal

## Sequence Building

Create self-care evening routine:

1. **Bath Prep** (filling tub, adding salts) - 3 seconds
2. **Light Candles** (hands lighting multiple candles) - 3 seconds
3. **Get In** (stepping into tub) - 2 seconds
4. **Settle** (legs visible, bath tray setup) - 4 seconds
5. **Skincare** (face mask application) - 5 seconds
6. **Relax** (reading or sipping wine) - 5 seconds
7. **Wind Down** (candles still glowing) - 3 seconds

Total: 25-second self-care sequence
