# POV Morning Routines

**POV LIFESTYLE / MORNING CONTENT**

---

## Platform

First-person POV lifestyle content platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video or still images  
**Style:** Immersive, relatable, aspirational morning moments

---

## Concept

Start of the day from bed to breakfast to getting ready—capturing morning routines from the first-person perspective. The camera becomes the viewer's eyes, showing what they see as they wake up and begin their day.

---

## Typical Shots

- Waking up, view from bed looking at sunlit room
- Hands reaching for phone on nightstand
- View looking down at cozy slippers/bare feet on bedroom floor
- First-person view making coffee/tea in kitchen
- Hands holding warm mug with steam
- View from bathroom mirror (doing skincare/makeup)
- Selecting outfit from closet (hands touching fabrics)
- View looking out window with coffee in hand

---

## Camera Angles

**From Bed (Looking Forward):**

- View toward window/room
- Establishes bedroom environment
- Shows morning light quality

**Looking Down (Most Common):**

- View of hands reaching for items
- Looking at feet/slippers
- View into coffee cup
- Shows immediate actions

**Mirror View:**

- Bathroom/vanity mirror
- Skincare routine
- Getting ready moment
- Face partially visible

---

## Composition Tips

**Rule of Thirds:**

- Position hands/mug at intersection points
- Balance foreground (hands) and background (room)
- Create visual interest

**Depth Layers:**

- Foreground: Hands/items held
- Midground: Nightstand/counter
- Background: Room/window/blurred context

**Negative Space:**

- Clean uncluttered frames
- Focus on key morning moment
- Minimal distractions

---

## Sequence Building

Create story through connected morning moments:

1. **Wake up** (view from bed, hands reaching for phone) - 3 seconds
2. **Get up** (feet hitting floor in slippers) - 2 seconds
3. **Kitchen** (making coffee, hands pouring) - 5 seconds
4. **Enjoy** (holding mug, looking out window) - 4 seconds
5. **Bathroom** (skincare in mirror) - 5 seconds
6. **Closet** (selecting outfit) - 4 seconds
7. **Ready** (final look, ready to start day) - 2 seconds

Total: 25-second morning routine sequence

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Harsh overhead lighting** → ✅ Natural window light  
❌ **Cluttered nightstand** → ✅ Curated minimal items  
❌ **Unnatural arm angles** → ✅ Relaxed natural positioning  
❌ **Too fast movements** → ✅ Slow intentional actions  
❌ **Dark underexposed shots** → ✅ Well-lit bright morning scenes
