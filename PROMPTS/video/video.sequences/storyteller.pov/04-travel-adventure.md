# POV Travel & Adventure

**POV LIFESTYLE / TRAVEL CONTENT**

---

## Platform

First-person POV lifestyle content platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video or still images  
**Style:** Immersive, wanderlust, aspirational travel moments

---

## Concept

Exploring new places from the traveler's perspective—capturing the excitement, wonder, and intimate moments of travel through first-person POV. The camera becomes the viewer's eyes as they experience airports, destinations, hotel rooms, and adventures.

---

## Typical Shots

- View from airplane window seat (hands holding phone/book)
- Walking view of feet on different surfaces (beach sand, cobblestones, hotel floor)
- Hands holding passport and boarding pass
- View from hotel balcony/window
- Holding map or phone while walking in city
- Hands in frame pointing at landmarks
- View from driver's seat/passenger seat on road trip
- Checking into hotel (hands with key card)
- Packing/unpacking suitcase view

---

## Sequence Building

Create destination arrival story:

1. **Airport** (boarding pass in hand) - 3 seconds
2. **Flight** (window view with coffee) - 4 seconds
3. **Arrival** (walking through airport) - 3 seconds
4. **Transport** (taxi/car to hotel) - 3 seconds
5. **Hotel** (opening door, entering room) - 4 seconds
6. **First Look** (balcony view reveal) - 5 seconds
7. **Settling** (unpacking essentials) - 3 seconds

Total: 25-second arrival sequence

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Shaky walking footage** → ✅ Smooth stabilized movement  
❌ **Overcrowded tourist shots** → ✅ Authentic quieter moments  
❌ **Poor airplane window reflection** → ✅ Clean window, proper angle  
❌ **Boring static shots** → ✅ Movement and progression  
❌ **Missing location context** → ✅ Clear destination visible
