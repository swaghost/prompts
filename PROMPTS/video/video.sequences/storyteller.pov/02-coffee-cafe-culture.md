# POV Coffee & Cafe Culture

**POV LIFESTYLE / COFFEE SHOP CONTENT**

---

## Platform

First-person POV lifestyle content platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video or still images  
**Style:** Cozy, aesthetic, aspirational cafe moments

---

## Concept

Coffee shop visits, latte art appreciation, cozy cafe working sessions from first-person perspective. Capturing the intimate feeling of cafe culture—from holding a warm cup to sitting at a window seat—through the viewer's eyes.

---

## Typical Shots

- Hands holding coffee cup with latte art (view looking down at cup)
- Sitting at cafe table with coffee and pastry visible
- View across cafe table toward barista/cafe interior
- Hands typing on laptop with coffee nearby
- Close-up of hand bringing coffee cup to mouth (bottom of frame)
- View from cafe window seat looking outside
- Stirring coffee, adding sugar/cream
- Hands holding menu

## Coffee Types & Details

### Hot Coffees

- **Latte:** Heart, tulip, or rosetta latte art
- **Cappuccino:** Thick foam, dusted cocoa
- **Flat White:** Velvety microfoam
- **Americano:** Simple, clean, black
- **Cortado:** Small glass, layered

### Iced Coffees

- **Iced Latte:** Clear glass, milk swirl
- **Cold Brew:** Tall glass, ice cubes
- **Iced Americano:** Simple, refreshing
- **Shaken Espresso:** Layered, frothy

### Specialty Drinks

- **Matcha Latte:** Vibrant green
- **Chai Latte:** Warm spices
- **Hot Chocolate:** Whipped cream, marshmallows
- **Tea Service:** Elegant teapot, cup

## Camera Angles

**Looking Down (Most Common):**

- View into coffee cup (latte art visible)
- Looking at hands holding cup
- View of table spread (laptop, pastry, coffee)
- Shows immediate workspace

**Looking Across:**

- View across table toward cafe interior
- Shows barista counter background
- Establishes cafe environment
- Creates "being there" feeling

**Window View:**

- Looking out from window seat
- Cup in foreground, street outside
- Contrast indoor/outdoor
- Reflective peaceful mood

---

## Composition Tips

**Classic Coffee Shot:**

- Cup centered or rule-of-thirds
- Latte art clearly visible
- Pastry or prop at edge
- Shallow depth of field

**Work Setup Shot:**

- Laptop in upper frame
- Coffee in lower foreground
- Clean negative space
- Organized productive look

**Window Seat Shot:**

- Hands/cup in foreground
- Window view background (bokeh)
- Depth and atmosphere
- Storytelling layers

## Sequence Building

Create cafe visit story:

1. **Arrival** (walking in, menu in hand) - 3 seconds
2. **Order** (receiving coffee from barista) - 3 seconds
3. **Settle In** (sitting at table, unpacking laptop) - 4 seconds
4. **First Sip** (hands holding cup, steam) - 3 seconds
5. **Work/Read** (typing or reading with coffee nearby) - 8 seconds
6. **Pause** (looking out window, reflective) - 4 seconds

Total: 25-second cafe moment sequence
