# Tactical Getaway — Rooftop Sprint

**Format:** 6-10 seconds, cinematic action sequence  
**Type:** Thriller escape scene, tactical action  
**Aesthetic:** Modern action thriller, urgent getaway  
**Style:** 4K cinema, 24fps, dramatic dusk lighting, film grain

---

## CONCEPT

High-angle rooftop parking lot at dusk. Man in tactical gear bursts through metal doors and sprints at full speed toward waiting getaway car with spinning tires. Quick cuts between runner, tires, headlights. Tense, fast-paced escape atmosphere.

## CAMERA WORK

### Primary Shot:

- **Cinematic wide shot**
- **High-angle** (elevated rooftop view)
- Captures full scene (doors, runner, car, environment)
- Establishes geography

### Camera Movement:

- **Tracks smoothly alongside his movement**
- Parallels runner's sprint
- Professional tracking shot
- Smooth cinematic rig feel (dolly or gimbal)
- Maintains framing on subject
- Reveals environment

### Quick Cuts:

**Cut 1: Close-up of rushing feet**

- Boots hitting concrete rapidly
- Motion blur
- Speed emphasized
- Ground detail (skid marks, texture)

**Cut 2: Screeching tires**

- Tight shot on spinning rear tires
- Smoke and dust kicking up
- Tire tread visible
- Rubber on tarmac
- Screech emphasized

**Cut 3: Car's idling headlights**

- Front of vehicle
- Headlights bright in dusk
- Grille visible
- Intimidating front end
- Engine heat distortion possible
- Waiting, ready

**Editing Rhythm:**

- Fast cuts (1-2 seconds each)
- Builds tension
- Emphasizes urgency
- Action thriller pacing
- Intercutting between wide and close
