# Restaurant Menu-to-3D Food Animation

## Classification

**Product Category:** food

**Reveal Effect:** menu-pop-out

**Reveal Mechanism:** printed menu items and dish photos rise from the page as animated 3D food models with cinematic camera movement

**Sequence Type:** reference-match reveal

**Filename:** `reveal.food.menu-pop-out.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A cinematic 3D animated restaurant menu video where the camera glides across a static menu and featured dishes rise from the page in premium slow motion. Dish names, prices, branding, menu design, and food references remain accurate while the menu becomes a polished ready-to-use restaurant content piece.

## Usage

Perfect for restaurants, cafes, food trucks, digital menus, restaurant ads, food promotions, website hero videos, short-form social content, and branded menu showcases.

Ideal for transforming a static restaurant menu into a cinematic food experience, creating a repeatable service for local restaurants, producing social media demos, and generating multiple customized menu animations by swapping restaurant name, brand style, dishes, prices, colors, and menu design.

## Engines/Models

Originally intended for Google Flow-style video generation workflows, but suitable for any image-to-video or video generation model that supports menu references, food-image references, cinematic camera movement, 3D animation, text accuracy, and brand consistency.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Dependency #1 - Restaurant menu image, menu design, or menu reference.
- Dependency #2 - Dish names and prices to display accurately.
- Dependency #3 - Optional high-quality food images as references for each featured dish.
- Dependency #4 - Optional restaurant name, brand style, colors, logo, and visual identity guidelines.

## Video Prompt

Turn the provided restaurant menu into a cinematic 3D animated menu video using AI.

**Duration:** 8-15 seconds. Create a polished short-form sequence suitable for social media, digital menus, restaurant ads, food promotions, website hero videos, and ready-to-use restaurant content.

**Style:** Cinematic 3D food animation, premium restaurant advertising, smooth controlled camera motion, realistic food detail, appetizing lighting, high-end menu presentation, slow-motion food reveals, clean branding, realistic depth, polished commercial finish.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
Begin with the restaurant menu as the exact visual foundation. The viewer should first understand that this is a regular static menu, then experience it becoming a cinematic 3D food environment. Preserve the restaurant name, brand style, menu design, dish names, prices, colors, and layout unless the provided prompt asks for changes. No complicated animation workflow or manual 3D modeling should be implied in the result; the menu itself becomes the source of the animation.

Use the menu, dish names, dish prices, restaurant branding, and optional food references as the source of truth. Replace placeholder items with the restaurant's actual dishes and prices. Example items may include: Classic Beef Burger - $12, Truffle Mushroom Pizza - $18, Creamy Alfredo Pasta - $15, and Chocolate Lava Cake - $9. Adapt the concept to almost any restaurant menu.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:02
Opening shot: the camera starts above the restaurant menu. Show the static menu clearly, with the restaurant branding, dish names, prices, colors, and overall menu design visible. Establish the menu page as the base surface for the animation.

0:02-0:04
Cinematic camera movement begins. The camera smoothly glides across the menu, traveling through the layout as if the viewer is moving through the menu itself. Use a slow push-in toward the first featured dish area.

0:04-0:06
The first dish starts rising directly from the menu page. The printed or photographed dish element lifts upward, becomes dimensional, and forms into a realistic 3D food presentation above the page. The dish floats briefly in cinematic slow motion, with steam, texture, sauce shine, crumbs, garnish, or ingredient detail where appropriate.

0:06-0:08
The camera pans or glides toward the next menu item. Use the existing menu typography, price line, dish photo, ingredient graphic, or page divider as a transition element so the sequence feels connected rather than like separate shots.

0:08-0:11
The second featured dish pops off the menu in slow motion. Another dish rises, floats above the page, and becomes the focus. The movement should feel premium and dramatic, making the viewer feel like the food is coming directly out of the menu.

0:11-0:13
Move into a close-up of the food details. Highlight appetizing texture, fresh ingredients, realistic depth, warm reflections, sauce, crisp edges, melted cheese, steam, garnish, or dessert shine depending on the actual dish. Keep dish names and prices accurate and connected to the menu layout.

0:13-0:15
Final shot: the camera pulls back or glides to reveal the full menu coming together with the restaurant branding. Multiple dishes can hover subtly above their menu positions before settling into a cohesive final branded composition. The static menu has transformed into a cinematic food experience.

**Camera:**
Use smooth, controlled, cinematic camera movement. The camera can glide across the menu, perform a slow push-in toward featured dishes, pan from one menu item to another, and use slow motion for premium food reveals. The goal is to make the viewer feel like they are moving through the menu. Avoid static framing, abrupt movement, chaotic cuts, or fast unreadable camera changes.

**Lighting:**
Use appetizing restaurant-ad lighting with warm highlights, soft shadows, premium reflections, and enough contrast to make the food look dimensional and desirable. Match the restaurant's brand mood where possible. Keep the menu readable and the dish details clear.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
The menu page transforms into a 3D experience: static menu -> cinematic camera -> dishes come to life -> 3D menu animation -> restaurant content. Each dish should appear to pop directly off the page. The dish starts rising, food floats above the page, cinematic slow motion emphasizes the movement, and the dish becomes the focus. Animate dish names, prices, ingredient highlights, and menu lines only when it supports clarity. Use high-quality food references when available. Make each dish the visual focus for a moment.

**Quality/Technical Requirements:**
Generate a ready-to-use cinematic restaurant menu video with accurate dish names, accurate prices, consistent branding, smooth camera movement, controlled pacing, premium slow-motion food shots, readable typography, realistic 3D food detail, polished commercial lighting, and a cohesive final menu composition. Create short versions suitable for social media if needed.

Do not change dish names or prices. Do not ignore the restaurant branding. Do not make the camera movement chaotic. Do not leave the menu as a static page. Do not create disconnected food shots unrelated to the menu. Do not introduce random dishes that are not on the provided menu. Do not distort readable text, logo placement, or menu layout unless the prompt specifically asks for a redesigned version.
