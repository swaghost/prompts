# RoseAi Menu Book 3D Dish Reveal

## Classification

**Product Category:** Fine-dining menu advertising

**Reveal Effect:** Printed dish illustration transforms into a hovering photorealistic 3D dish

**Reveal Mechanism:** Page lands flat, printed illustration peels from the paper, inflates into full volume, remains page-locked, and dissolves during the next page turn

**Sequence Type:** Single-take vertical menu-book reveal

**Target Model:** Vosu.ai / Seedance 2.5 or equivalent reference-aware video model

**Source:** [RoseAi Menu Reveal Prompt Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10hJV4gQ2dzmpXjzQSJUvNb7XyCWENpiBqvxqYn07_Yc/mobilebasic?urp=gmail_link)

## Description of Resulting Video

A closed fine-dining menu book opens on a white marble table during one continuous 15-second shot. Each page initially contains only a flat printed dish illustration. After the page lands, the illustration peels upward, gains depth and realistic texture, and becomes a finished dish hovering close above its own page. The dish remains mechanically attached to that page as it turns, then dissolves into colored particles before the next page lands. Four dishes lead into a final branded particle reveal.

## Usage

Use for premium restaurant campaigns, vertical social advertisements, chef-menu launches, reservation promotions, seasonal menu reveals, and reusable restaurant content templates. Replace the sample dishes, prices, logo, palette, and dining-room design while preserving the physical emergence and page-lock rules.

- **Duration:** 15 seconds

- **Structure:** One continuous take with no cuts or scene changes

- **Camera:** Slightly elevated three-quarter view with continuous left-to-right drift and subtle push-in
- **Depth of Field:** Shallow, with the active page and hovering dish readable

- **Color Grade:** Ivory white, rose gold, warm golden brown, deep red, and fresh herb green

## Scene Setup

- White marble tabletop
- Crisp white linen runner
- Brushed-gold cutlery
- Crystal water glass in soft focus
- One red rose in a crystal bud vase
- Cream-and-walnut dining room rendered as restrained bokeh
- Slim hardcover menu book with matte ivory-cream cover
- Rose-gold foil logo and premium serif typography
- A pair of natural hands positioned near the book edge

Frame tightly enough that each hovering dish rests in the upper-middle of the vertical frame while the printed title and price remain fully visible below it.

## Governing Animation Rules

### Emergence Rule

The page must land flat before anything rises. At landing, the dish exists only as flat printed ink with no thickness, cast shadow, or duplicate object above it. Over approximately 0.4 seconds, the printed edges peel upward, detach, and inflate into a fully dimensional dish with realistic volume, surface texture, garnish, and plate geometry.

Once the 3D dish becomes solid, remove the underlying printed illustration completely. Never leave a duplicate printed dish beneath the hovering model.

### Hover-Height Rule

Keep the finished dish approximately 3-4 cm above its page. Maintain a small believable contact shadow and a grounded visual relationship. Never let the dish rise high into the frame or touch the paper.

### Page-Lock And Exit Rule

Treat each dish as rigidly anchored to its own page by an invisible support. When the page turns, the dish must swing through the same arc while maintaining its fixed distance from the paper. Dissolve it only during the final third of the page-turn arc, when it has moved away from the center.

### Camera Rule

Maintain one uninterrupted left-to-right camera drift with a subtle push-in for the full sequence. The camera must not stop, reverse, snap, jump, or cut between beats.

### Timing Rule

- Every page turn takes approximately 0.8 seconds with smooth easing.
- Every dish emergence takes approximately 0.4 seconds with smooth easing.
- Hovering dishes rotate at one constant, slow presentation speed.
- Keep all movement continuous and physically connected.

### Typography Rule

Keep dish title and price printed flat on the paper in uppercase premium serif type, spanning roughly 80% of the readable page width. Never let the dish, hands, steam, particles, shadow, or glow obscure the title or price.

### Plating Rule

Every dish must look ready to leave a fine-dining pass: spotless plate rim, controlled garnish, no drips, no stray ingredients, and nothing floating independently off the plate.

### Lighting And Particle Rule

Build each dish-specific glow during emergence and attach it to the dish during exit:

- Pasta: golden butter and parmesan-dust particles
- Pizza: golden crust with blush-pink highlights and ember particles
- Lasagna: deep red and amber with red-gold particles
- Drinks: blush rose with light crystalline particles

The glow may tint the page but must never wash out its typography.

## Master Video Prompt

```text
Create a single continuous 15-second, 9:16 vertical fine-dining commercial shot. Begin on a slim closed ivory-cream hardcover menu book resting on a white marble table with a crisp linen runner, brushed-gold cutlery, a soft-focus crystal glass, and one red rose in a crystal bud vase. Use soft daylight from tall windows, a blurred cream-and-walnut dining room, cinematic macro detail, shallow depth of field, rose-gold accents, and a premium commercial finish.

Keep the camera in a slightly elevated three-quarter angle and maintain one uninterrupted left-to-right drift with a subtle push-in for the entire video. Never stop, reverse, snap, or cut.

The cover opens in a smooth 0.8-second arc. Every page must land flat before its dish appears. At landing, the dish exists only as a flat two-dimensional printed illustration with no thickness, shadow, duplicate, or floating object. Over 0.4 seconds, the printed edges peel off the paper and inflate into a fully photorealistic 3D plated dish. Remove the flat illustration completely once the 3D dish is solid.

Keep each finished dish hovering only 3-4 cm above its own page with a subtle contact shadow. Keep its printed uppercase serif title and price fully readable below it. When the page turns, keep the dish rigidly page-locked at the same distance and swing it through the page arc. Dissolve it into dish-colored particles only during the final third of that arc.

Reveal four menu beats in order: Black Truffle Tagliatelle above TRUFFLE TAGLIATELLE - $34; Burrata and Prosciutto Pizza above BURRATA PIZZA - $26; Braised Short Rib Lasagna above SHORT RIB LASAGNA - $32; Rose Spritz with cold brew and citrus cooler behind it above ROSE SPRITZ - $14.

Use pristine fine-dining plating, spotless rims, realistic steam, controlled garnish, constant slow dish rotation, and physically believable page motion. Use golden-butter particles for pasta, golden embers with blush highlights for pizza, red-amber particles for lasagna, and blush-rose crystalline particles for drinks. Never wash out the menu text.

On the final beat only, release the drinks from the page and transform them into particles that form the rose logo, restaurant name, and reservation call to action above the book. Ease the camera to a stop and fade to ivory white during the final 0.3 seconds.
```

## Failure Checks

- [ ] The video is one uninterrupted 15-second take.
- [ ] The page lands before each emergence begins.
- [ ] No dish is visible through a turning page.
- [ ] No dish floats before its page has landed.
- [ ] The flat illustration disappears after the 3D dish solidifies.
- [ ] No printed duplicate remains under a hovering dish.
- [ ] Every dish remains 3-4 cm above its page.
- [ ] Each dish follows its page during the exit arc.
- [ ] Dissolution begins only in the final third of the page turn.
- [ ] Page title and price remain readable throughout.
- [ ] Plate rims remain spotless and garnish stays controlled.
- [ ] Camera drift remains left-to-right with no reversals or cuts.
- [ ] Only the final dish releases fully from its page anchor.
- [ ] The final particles resolve cleanly into the brand card.

## Hard Negatives

`No jump cuts, no scene changes, no camera reversal, no dish visible before page landing, no dish clipping through paper, no duplicate printed food beneath the 3D dish, no excessive hover height, no obscured text, no warped hands, no illegible price, no dirty plate rim, no random ingredients, no disconnected food shots, no floating particles before emergence, no unmotivated logo change.`

## Reuse Note

Preserve the six-beat mechanism when adapting the sequence. Replace the brand and dish content, but keep the order of operations identical: cover, page landing, flat illustration, 3D emergence, low hover, page-locked exit, particle dissolution, next page, final brand resolution.
