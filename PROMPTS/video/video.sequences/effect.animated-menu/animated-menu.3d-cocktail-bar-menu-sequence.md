# 3D Cocktail Bar Menu Animation

## Classification

**Product Category:** Cocktail-bar advertising / animated beverage menu

**Reveal Effect:** Miniature cocktail glass rise and ingredient assembly

**Reveal Mechanism:** Each labelled menu item becomes a physical miniature cocktail as its glass rises, liquid pours, ice and garnishes assemble, and the camera transfers focus to the next drink

**Sequence Type:** 3D animated menu / macro beverage showcase / continuous focus-transition sequence

**Filename:** `animated-menu.3d-cocktail-bar-menu-sequence.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A 10-second cinematic close-up of a luxury miniature cocktail-bar display board. Four cocktails rise and assemble sequentially under moody warm bar light as the camera smoothly transfers focus from an Old Fashioned to an Espresso Martini, Mojito, and Margarita.

## Usage

Perfect for cocktail-bar menus, hotel-lounge advertising, restaurant displays, social campaigns, digital signage, and premium mixology promotions. Use when four signature drinks need distinct, physically believable miniature reveals within one continuous landscape composition.

## Engines / Models

The source labels this concept `Vexmo Animation`. Use with a video model that supports cinematic macro rendering, realistic liquid and garnish physics, controlled focus transitions, readable menu labels, and a continuous 10-second 16:9 sequence.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- None - generates from text.
- Optional: provide a menu-board reference when exact bar branding, typography, materials, or layout must be preserved.

## Production-Ready Video Prompt

```text
Create a cinematic close-up video of a luxury miniature cocktail-bar display board. The display is a refined dark bar surface with subtle brass detailing, restrained menu typography, and four clearly separated cocktail positions. Preserve one coherent board, one consistent miniature scale, and one continuous spatial layout throughout the shot.

The camera smoothly transitions focus between four menu items in strict order. As each item becomes active, its glass rises cleanly from its corresponding position on the board and the cocktail assembles through realistic liquid, ice, carbonation, foam, garnish, gravity, contact, and settling behavior. Keep the active drink tack-sharp while the other menu positions fall into natural shallow optical blur.

0.0-2.5 seconds - OLD FASHIONED - $9: Begin with a macro establishing view of the luxury miniature cocktail display. Focus settles on the Old Fashioned label. A tiny heavy-bottomed rocks glass rises vertically from the board. One clear spherical ice piece is already seated naturally inside or settles first with a restrained glass clink. Transparent amber whiskey pours over the single ice sphere, flowing around it with realistic refraction and a controlled liquid level. A fresh orange peel curls and twists into place above the surface, then rests naturally against the glass rim. Preserve a clean amber color, realistic glass thickness, condensation restraint, and premium reflections.

2.5-5.0 seconds - ESPRESSO MARTINI - $10: Use a smooth lateral camera glide and optical rack focus to transfer attention to the second label without a cut. A miniature coupe glass rises from the board. Dark espresso-martini liquid spirals and swirls into the coupe with believable viscosity and momentum. A thin, even crema-like foam layer settles across the surface. Exactly three coffee beans drop softly onto the foam one after another, make gentle contact, and remain arranged on top without sinking, bouncing away, duplicating, or floating above the drink.

5.0-7.5 seconds - MOJITO - $8: Continue the same unbroken camera move and focus transition. A small highball glass rises from its menu position. Fresh mint leaves and lime pieces tumble naturally into the glass, followed by crushed ice settling with crisp granular contact. Clear soda rises and fizzes through the crushed ice with fine carbonation bubbles. Keep the mint fresh green, lime recognizable, liquid transparent, ice physically contained, and fizz localized inside the glass. Nothing floats outside the drink.

7.5-10.0 seconds - MARGARITA - $9: Glide and rack focus to the final menu item. A tiny stemmed margarita glass with a clean salt rim rises from the board. Pale green margarita liquid pours into the glass with realistic flow, subtle translucency, and controlled surface movement. A single lime wheel slides into place on the rim and settles securely. Finish with a brief polished hero hold on the completed Margarita while the other three finished cocktails remain coherently present in the softly blurred background.

CAMERA: One continuous unbroken macro product-photography shot. Smooth lateral dolly or slider movement across the menu board with controlled rack-focus transitions between drinks. No cuts, jumps, whip pans, handheld shake, orbit, sudden zoom, or reframing. Use an 85-100mm macro lens character, shallow depth of field, restrained focus breathing, and stable cinematic motion.

LIGHTING: Moody warm luxury-bar lighting with soft amber key light, subtle brass highlights, controlled reflections on glass, gentle warm practical bokeh in the distant background, deep but readable shadows, and realistic specular highlights on liquids, ice, foam, salt, citrus, and coffee beans. Keep all four reveals in one consistent lighting environment.

AUDIO: No music, narration, or dialogue. Use subtle synchronized cocktail ASMR only: glass rise and contact, restrained ice clink, liquid pour, citrus twist, soft swirl, coffee beans landing on foam, crushed ice settling, soda fizz, salt-rim contact, and lime wheel placement. Keep the sound premium, close, crisp, and proportionate to the miniature scale.

VISUAL QUALITY: Photorealistic premium 3D macro animation, luxury beverage-advertising finish, realistic glass thickness and refraction, physically accurate liquid behavior, natural garnish textures, detailed ice, stable menu typography, subtle film grain, clean color separation, and polished commercial color grading.

TECHNICAL: Exactly 10 seconds, 16:9 landscape, 24fps cinematic motion, macro product-photography detail, shallow depth of field, smooth continuous focus transitions, no cuts, and no camera shake.

NEGATIVE: changed cocktail names, changed prices, misspelled labels, unreadable text, duplicated drinks, missing drinks, wrong reveal order, incorrect glassware, multiple ice spheres in the Old Fashioned, missing orange peel, excessive foam, fewer or more than three coffee beans, coffee beans sinking, missing mint, missing lime, hot steam on cold cocktails, oversized garnish, missing salt rim, duplicate lime wheel, floating ingredients, liquid outside the glass, impossible pouring, warped glass, broken stems, overflowing drinks, unstable scale, redesigning the menu between shots, camera jumps, abrupt focus changes, jump cuts, excessive glow, neon color cast, cartoon rendering, low-poly objects, blur on the active drink, watermark, subtitles, added logos.
```

## Continuity and Physics Rules

- Keep one display board, bar environment, light direction, camera path, and miniature scale throughout.
- Preserve cocktail names, prices, reveal order, and corresponding glass types.
- Every glass rises from its own menu position without teleporting or morphing.
- Liquids remain inside their glasses and interact naturally with ice and glass surfaces.
- Garnishes enter through visible motion, make contact, and settle under gravity.
- Use exactly one ice sphere for the Old Fashioned and exactly three coffee beans for the Espresso Martini.
- Keep cold cocktails free of steam.
- Keep carbonation inside the Mojito and foam limited to the Espresso Martini surface.
- Keep the Margarita salt rim intact before and after the pour.
- Leave completed drinks stable as focus moves to the next item.
