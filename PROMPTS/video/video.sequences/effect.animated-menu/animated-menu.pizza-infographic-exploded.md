Icon stroke weight must exactly match connector line thickness. Archival, museum-catalog premium aesthetic. Background: warm cinematic kitchen ambiance, soft depth of field, realistic lighting, natural textures. Ingredients must float vertically above the pizza in a physically believable exploded composition. Final pizza centered at bottom. Top Title Block (fixed, centered): Arabic dish title on top, English below, identical spacing and size across all images. Ingredient Labels (PIZZA ONLY): Auto-detect visible pizza ingredients only. No sauces, no unrelated toppings, no optional items unless clearly shown, no meat unless visible. Arabic names ONLY. One ingredient per label. Short, direct names. No adjectives. No duplication. If not clearly visible, do not label. Each ingredient must have: one small circular point near it, one thin straight connector line (no curves, no intersections), and one same-sized circular line-style icon beside the name (no fill, no text inside icon). Even left-right distribution, symmetrical balance, no overlap, consistent vertical stacking rhythm, safe margins. ABSOLUTE: Do not change layout, colors, or icon style. Do not add descriptions, emojis, hashtags, AI mentions, or analysis. Output only the final formatted on-image text. Aspect ratio 4:5.
