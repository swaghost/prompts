# Pattern Interruption: Domino's Delivery Chase & Interface Crash

**Social Media Pattern Interruption Campaign** — Domino's delivery scooter speeds across asphalt intersection and clears the Instagram interface safely, while pursuing police car crashes through it with dramatic impact.

**Campaign Type:** Food Delivery / Quick Service
**Physical Interaction:** Scooter crosses cleanly, police car smashes through interface creating cracks and debris
**Brand Message:** "Delivered before you finished scrolling." — delivery speed, playful rebellion

---

## Campaign Overview

### Concept

A Domino's delivery scooter races across a realistic asphalt city intersection, crosses over the Instagram interface strip cleanly and continues into the lower half of frame. A police car chases close behind with heavier, aggressive motion. The scooter clears the strip safely, but the police car strikes the interface and smashes through it as if it's a physical layer built into the road — creating sharp impact with cracking, fragments, debris, and dust. The scooter briefly stops, rider looks back for one quick glance, then instantly accelerates away and exits.

### Viral Hook

**The "wait, did that just crash through my feed?" moment.** The Instagram interface becomes a physical barrier on the road. The nimble Domino's scooter escapes cleanly while the pursuing police car crashes through the UI, creating a playful chase narrative that makes the brand hero. The unexpected crash physics combined with the "look back" beat creates memorable storytelling.

### Brand Application

- **Domino's:** Fast delivery, beats expectations, playful brand personality
- **Other Delivery Brands:** Any food delivery, quick service, courier services
- **Related Categories:** Fast food, delivery apps, speed-focused services, brands with playful/rebellious tone

- Realistic asphalt city intersection fills entire frame

## Advanced Prompt Engineering Tips

### Enhancing Chase Narrative

**Build story beats:**

- "The Domino's scooter accelerates from top, crosses interface cleanly, continues into lower half"
- "Police car chases close behind with heavier aggressive motion"
- "Scooter clears safely, but police car strikes and smashes through"
- "Scooter reaches lower area, briefly stops, rider turns head back for quick glance, then instantly accelerates away"

**Emphasize contrast:**

- "Scooter: nimble, fast, confident — Police car: heavier, aggressive, can't stop"
- "Scooter clears cleanly — Police car crashes through"
- "Brand hero wins, obstacle fails"

### Strengthening Crash Physics

**Interface as physical barrier:**

- "The police car strikes the interface and smashes through as if it's a physical layer built into the road"
- "Interface behaves like rigid material embedded in asphalt"
- "Sharp impact with cracking, fragments, debris scatter, dust burst"

**Control damage level:**

- "Create sharp impact while keeping remaining layout recognizable"
- "Interface damaged but still readable"
- "Fragmentation visible but overall structure maintained"

**Prevent total destruction:**

- "Don't completely obliterate interface — it should still be identifiable"
- "Cracked and broken but layout recognizable"
- "Damage tells story without losing readability"

### Nailing the "Look Back" Beat

**Critical storytelling moment:**

- "The scooter reaches the lower area, briefly stops"
- "The rider turns his head back for one quick glance"
- "Then instantly accelerates away and exits"

**Timing emphasis:**

- "Brief stop — pause beat for dramatic effect"
- "Quick glance — moment of acknowledgment"
- "Instant acceleration — throttle burst, confident escape"

**Creates emotional connection:**

- Rider checks crash (human moment)
- Confirms escape success (victory moment)
- Continues delivery (brand commitment moment)

### Protecting Interface Legibility

**Even damaged, must be readable:**

- "Keep interface fixed and readable with no flicker, regeneration, text wrapping, or spelling changes"
- "Preserve exactly: [list all text] even if cracked or fragmented"
- "Interface can be damaged but text should remain legible"
- "Cracks go through interface, not through individual letters"

### Camera Stability

**Absolutely critical:**

- "Keep the camera locked in a perfect top-down aerial view"
- "Camera must remain completely stationary — no shake, zoom, pan, or tilt even during crash"
- "Maintain exact 90-degree overhead perspective throughout chase and impact"

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue 1: Both Vehicles Damage Interface

**Problem:** Scooter also cracks or damages interface instead of crossing cleanly.

**Solutions:**

- Clarify differential: "The scooter clears the strip safely, but the police car strikes it and smashes through"
- Strengthen: "Scooter crosses cleanly with NO damage — only police car crashes"
- Add: "Nimble scooter jumps/crosses lightly — heavy police car can't avoid impact"
- Emphasize weight difference: "Light scooter vs. heavy police car"

### Issue 2: Interface Completely Destroyed

**Problem:** Crash obliterates interface to point it's unrecognizable.

**Solutions:**

- Control damage: "Create sharp impact with cracking, interface fragments, small debris and brief dust burst while keeping the remaining layout recognizable"
- Add: "Interface damaged but still readable and identifiable"
- Specify: "Cracks and breaks but overall structure maintained"
- Reference: "Cracked phone screen effect — broken but you can still see content"

### Issue 3: No Clear "Look Back" Moment

**Problem:** Rider doesn't stop or look back, missing key storytelling beat.

**Solutions:**

- Break into steps: "The scooter reaches the lower area, briefly stops, the rider turns his head back for one quick glance, then instantly accelerates away and exits"
- Emphasize timing: "Brief stop (pause beat), quick glance (acknowledge crash), instant acceleration (escape)"
- Add: "This look-back moment is critical for story — rider checks crash, confirms escape, then continues"
- Specify camera visibility: "Turn head back toward crash site, visible from overhead angle"

### Issue 4: Police Car Not Visible or Too Dominant

**Problem:** Either police car isn't shown, or it dominates the first frame too much.

**Solutions:**

- Balance first frame: "A police car may be barely hinted at farther behind near the top edge, but it must not dominate the frame"
- Specify focus: "Hero is Domino's scooter — police car is supporting chase element"
- For barely visible: "Suggestion of police car farther back, or first visible during animation"
- For too dominant: "Police car much smaller/farther back than scooter in first frame"

### Issue 5: Chase Sequence Timing Off

**Problem:** Chase feels too fast, too slow, or beats don't land properly.

**Solutions:**

- Specify duration zones: "Scooter acceleration (1s) → interface crossing (1s) → police crash (1s) → look back beat (1s) → escape (1s) = ~5 seconds total"
- Add pacing notes: "Fast action but clear story beats, not rushed or dragging"
- Emphasize key moments: "Impact and look-back are tentpole moments — give them time to register"
- Reference: "Action-comedy pacing — fast but with comedy timing beats"

### Issue 6: Sound Effects Missing or Wrong

**Problem:** Audio doesn't match action or key sounds absent.

**Solutions:**

- List required sounds: "Realistic scooter motion, police siren, tire scrub, impact smash, debris scatter, quick throttle burst"
- Specify timing: "Police siren throughout, impact smash at crash moment, throttle burst at escape acceleration"
- Add layers: "Background urban ambiance + vehicle sounds + impact effects"
- Reference: "Action movie chase scene audio, not silent or minimal"
