# Ancient Construction - Hidden Observer Sequence

## Classification

**Product Category:** Fictional historical thriller

**Sequence Type:** Single-take hidden-camera sequence

**Duration:** 10 seconds

**Format:** 9:16 vertical

## Description

A fictional, found-footage-style glimpse of an unfinished ancient Egyptian monument. A frightened observer hides in nearby brush and documents silent workers and colossal humanoid labourers moving immense stone blocks. This is speculative fantasy, not a reconstruction of historical events.
