# Walking Camera — Japanese Neighborhood Handheld DV

**Purpose:** Ultra-realistic handheld walk-and-talk in authentic Japanese residential setting  
**Platform:** Seedance 2.0  
**Format:** Not specified (standard video)  
**Duration:** ~30 seconds  
**Camera Style:** Early-2000s DV camcorder, friend-shot, accidental aesthetic  
**Key Feature:** Heavy handheld shake, imperfect framing, authentic casual feel

---

## Core Concept

One original character, fully locked throughout the entire sequence. The character walks beside their friend (the cameraman who is never visible) through a quiet residential Japanese neighborhood. The camera work must look like an authentic friend casually grabbed an early-2000s DV camcorder and started filming without any planning or setup.

**Critical:** Use attached reference photo as the identity lock for the character and preserve every facial feature consistently across the clip. He wears the clothes from the reference. No facial changes, no outfit changes, no identity changes, no redraw.

---

## Location — Authentic Japanese Residential

A quiet residential neighborhood in Japan. Classic narrow streets lined with compact houses, small local cafés, bicycles parked outside homes, vending machines appearing occasionally, overhead utility cables, neatly trimmed plants, subtle Japanese street signs. Clean streets and everyday pedestrians going about their morning.

**No tourist attractions, no temples, no crowded shopping districts.** It should feel like an authentic lived-in Japanese neighborhood.

---

## Camera Style — Early-2000s DV Handheld

The camera is the key. It must look like a friend casually grabbed an early-2000s DV camcorder and started filming without any planning or setup.

**Camera characteristics:**

- Heavy handheld shake
- Constant recomposing
- Imperfect framing
- Hesitant autofocus hunting and breathing
- Occasional slight overcorrections
- Faded colors, low contrast, washed-out image
- Authentic early-2000s digital compression
- Chroma noise
- Soft detail
- Slight motion blur
- Imperfect exposure changes

**Absolutely no stabilization, no cinematic camera moves, and no modern color grading.**

The recording should feel accidental, spontaneous, and completely authentic.

---

## Camera Movement and Framing

The cameraman is walking beside his friend, holding the camcorder naturally at chest height. The cameraman is never visible.

His friend walks nearby and to the left of the camera while both continue forward along the street. The framing constantly shifts: the cameraman reacts casually by turning the camera toward his friend while walking, occasionally letting him drift toward the edge of the frame before correcting.

Local pedestrians casually pass by in both directions without acknowledging the camera. A few cozy neighborhood cafés appear naturally in the background as they continue walking.

---

## Character Performance and Dialogue

The friend speaks casually while looking ahead most of the time, only occasionally glancing toward the camera naturally.

**Dialogue (You, surprised tone):**  
"Seriously! This prompt style actually generates the most realistic videos I've ever seen! The camera shake, the raw lighting... it doesn't feel like AI at all!"

**Brief natural pause as they continue walking. The camera slightly loses focus before recovering.**

**Cameraman (off-camera):**  
"Wait... really?!"

**The friend smiles slightly, still walking at the same pace.**

**Dialogue (You, surprised tone):**  
"Yeah, man! It just looks like a normal camera clip!"

**The conversation feels completely spontaneous, with natural pacing, tiny hesitations, overlapping footsteps, and subtle breathing. Nothing sounds scripted or performed.**
