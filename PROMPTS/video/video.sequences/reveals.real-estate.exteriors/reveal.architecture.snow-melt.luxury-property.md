# Real Estate Snow Reveal

**Format:** 10 seconds, cinematic transformation  
**Type:** Property reveal through snow melt  
**Aesthetic:** Magical winter-to-summer architectural unveiling  
**Style:** Ultra-realistic premium architectural visualization

---

## CONCEPT

Start with entire luxury property completely buried under deep snow, then reveal the finished architecture through elegant snow melt transformation. The property progressively emerges from winter landscape to showcase final photorealistic exterior design.

---

## REFERENCE IMAGE SYSTEM

**@Image1 = Final exterior design target**

- Use uploaded image as final visual match
- Camera direction and framing match reference
- Design, proportions, materials match reference exactly
- Final frame should be photorealistic match to uploaded exterior

---

## VISUAL SEQUENCE

### Opening: 0-2 seconds

**Complete Snow Burial**

- Entire site buried under deep snow
- Smooth frozen landscape
- Subtle hints of hidden structure beneath
- Same camera direction and framing as final exterior
- Cold wind blowing across scene
- Fine snow particles drifting naturally

---

### Middle: 2-8 seconds

**Progressive Snow Melt Reveal**

**Reveal Order (clean, elegant progression):**

1. **First:** Rooflines and upper forms emerge
2. **Then:** Large glass facade appears
3. **Next:** Walls and architectural details
4. **Following:** Terraces and outdoor structures
5. **Then:** Driveway and pathways
6. **Next:** Pool area
7. **Finally:** Landscaping, grass, trees, plants

**Melt Quality:**

- Rapid but elegant melt
- Clean, visually satisfying
- Magical feeling, as if winter layer uncovering finished architecture
- Natural progression from top to bottom
- Each element fully revealed before next appears

---

### Finale: 8-10 seconds

**Completed Property Showcase**

- Entire property and site fully revealed
- Photorealistic match to @Image1 reference
- Design, proportions, materials exact
- Luxury atmosphere preserved
- **Camera move:** Subtle cinematic drone-style glide or gentle push-in
- Hold on completed exterior to showcase finished property

---

## FULL GENERATION PROMPT

Use @Image1 as the final exterior design target and final visual match. Create a 10-second cinematic luxury property transformation video. Start with the entire site completely buried under deep snow, viewed from the same camera direction and framing as the final exterior. The snow should form a smooth frozen landscape with only subtle hints of the hidden structure beneath. Cold wind blows across the scene, sending fine snow particles drifting naturally. Then the snow begins to melt and recede rapidly in a clean, visually satisfying way. As the snow disappears, the luxury property is progressively revealed underneath: first the rooflines and upper forms, then the large glass facade, walls, terraces, driveway, pool, pathways, and surrounding landscaping. Grass, trees, plants, and exterior details become fully visible as the melt continues. The reveal should feel elegant and magical, as if the winter layer is uncovering the finished architecture. By the end, the entire property and site are fully photorealistic and match @reference in design, proportions, materials, and luxury atmosphere. In the final seconds, hold on the completed exterior with a subtle cinematic drone-style glide or gentle push-in to showcase the finished property. Ultra-realistic, premium architectural visualization, natural lighting, clean motion, no people, no text, no logos, no cuts.
