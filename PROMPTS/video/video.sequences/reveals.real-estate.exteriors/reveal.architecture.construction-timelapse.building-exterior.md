# Real Estate Architectural Reveal — Building Construction Time-Lapse

**Model:** Seedance 2.5 (image-to-video)  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds  
**Input:** One straight-on photo of your building, full frame, daylight  
**Motion setting:** MEDIUM (high melts the roofline)

---

## Overview

Cinematic architectural time-lapse showing a building constructing itself from nothing on its own site. One continuous unbroken shot, no cuts. The building materializes from foundation to finished facade as time passes from cool blue dawn to warm golden hour.

---

## Master Video Prompt — Copy Exactly

```
Cinematic architectural time-lapse. Photoreal. Vertical 9:16. 15 seconds. One continuous unbroken shot, no cuts.

The building in the reference image constructs itself from nothing on its own site.

Open on empty ground at cool blue dawn, with a glowing golden outline tracing the building's footprint on the bare earth. The foundation pours and sets. Walls rise up out of the slab course by course. Stone and masonry skin travels up the facade. Timber roof trusses swing in from above and lock into place along the ridge. Glazing drops into every opening and seals. The finished facade materialises with all its cornices, columns, roof detailing and chimneys complete. Landscaping unfurls outward across the grounds, hedges, planting and pathways filling in around the approach.

The camera holds a slow, steady, level position, drifting back and rising almost imperceptibly as the building grows to fill the frame. Perfect symmetry, corrected verticals.

Time passes as it builds. The sky moves from cool blue dawn to warm golden hour, clouds drifting, shadows swinging across the ground. At the end, warm interior lights glow on behind every window one floor at a time, and the low sun flares behind the roofline.

Long lens compression, shallow depth on the foreground. Rich natural colour, deep shadow detail, subtle film grain. Photoreal architectural commercial cinematography.
```

## Key Techniques

### Construction Logic

The building assembles in architecturally correct order:

1. Foundation (ground level)
2. Walls (vertical rise, course by course)
3. Roof structure (from above, locks in place)
4. Facade details (glazing, ornament, finishes)
5. Landscaping (unfurls outward from building)
6. Lighting (interior lights, one floor at a time)

### Time-Lapse Realism

- **One continuous shot:** No cuts, no jumps
- **Sky movement:** Dawn → golden hour transition
- **Shadow play:** Shadows swing across ground as sun moves
- **Clouds:** Drifting naturally
- **Construction speed:** Accelerated but believable

### Cinematic Quality

- **Symmetry:** Perfect architectural symmetry maintained
- **Verticals:** Corrected (no perspective distortion)
- **Compression:** Long lens flattens depth
- **Grain:** Subtle film texture
- **Photoreal:** Not CGI-looking, authentic architectural cinematography

## Motion Setting Critical Note

⚠️ **MEDIUM motion only**

- **MEDIUM:** Building construction progresses smoothly, roof stays intact
- **HIGH:** ❌ Melts the roofline, causes distortion, ruins architecture

Always use MEDIUM motion for architectural subjects.

## Best Practices

### Input Photo Guidelines

✅ **Good inputs:**

- Straight-on architectural photography
- Full building visible in frame
- Centered, symmetrical composition
- Clear daylight (any time of day)
- Sharp focus on building
- Minimal foreground obstruction

❌ **Avoid:**

- Extreme angles (low or high)
- Partial building crops
- Heavy shadows obscuring facade
- Cluttered foreground
- Motion blur or soft focus

### Generation Workflow

1. **Upload:** Straight-on building photo
2. **Select:** Seedance 2.5, image-to-video mode
3. **Settings:** 9:16 vertical, 15s duration, MEDIUM motion
4. **Paste:** Complete prompt exactly as written
5. **Generate:** 4 variants
6. **Select:** Keep best 1 (usually smoothest construction progression)

### What to Look For

- Smooth foundation → walls → roof → details progression
- No sudden jumps or morphing
- Roof stays intact (no melting)
- Symmetry maintained throughout
- Sky transition dawn → golden hour
- Interior lights come on naturally at end

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Roof melts or distorts  
**Solution:** Use MEDIUM motion, not HIGH

**Problem:** Building morphs unnaturally  
**Solution:** Ensure input photo is straight-on, well-lit, sharp focus

**Problem:** Landscaping doesn't appear  
**Solution:** Include ground/foreground in input photo

**Problem:** Interior lights don't glow at end  
**Solution:** Regenerate variants, select one where lights activate naturally

**Problem:** Sky doesn't transition properly  
**Solution:** Input photo should have visible sky above building
