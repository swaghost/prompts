# Real Estate Mini-House Expansion

**Format:** 8-12 seconds, single continuous shot, no cuts  
**Type:** Property reveal through miniature-to-full-size transformation  
**Aesthetic:** Playful scale magic, satisfying expansion  
**Style:** Realistic physics with seamless scale transformation

---

## CONCEPT

Single continuous shot showing a hand holding a miniature modern house model, throwing it into an empty lot, and watching it expand seamlessly into the full-size real building. Camera remains locked and front-facing throughout entire transformation.

---

## REFERENCE IMAGE SYSTEM

**@Image1 = Final property design target**

- Miniature model matches this design
- Full-size building matches this design exactly
- Design, proportions, materials consistent throughout transformation
- Same windows, balcony, lighting, architectural details

---

## SHOT STRUCTURE

### Single Continuous Shot Elements:

1. **Hand holding miniature model** (in front of camera)
2. **Gentle throw into empty lot** (model travels through air)
3. **Model lands on ground** (clean landing)
4. **Immediate expansion begins** (seamless scale change)
5. **Full-size building revealed** (completed transformation)

**Critical:** Entire transformation remains fully visible from start to finish in one seamless shot.

---

## VISUAL SEQUENCE

### Beat 1: 0-2 seconds

**Miniature Model Introduction**

- Hand enters frame holding miniature modern house model
- Model clearly visible and detailed
- Position: in front of camera, centered
- Empty lot visible in background
- Model design matches @Image1 exactly (at miniature scale)

**Miniature Model Details:**

- Architectural accuracy
- Visible windows
- Balcony details
- Materials recognizable even at small scale
- Professional architectural model quality

---

### Beat 2: 2-4 seconds

**The Throw**

- Hand gently throws model into empty lot
- Model travels through air in realistic arc
- Camera stays locked and front-facing (no following movement)
- Model visible throughout flight
- Anticipation builds

**Physics:**

- Natural throwing motion
- Realistic arc trajectory
- Appropriate speed (gentle, not aggressive)
- Rotation possible but controlled
- Gravity affecting flight

---

### Beat 3: 4-5 seconds

**Landing**

- Model lands cleanly on ground in empty lot
- Touches down at correct position for final building
- No bouncing or tumbling (clean stable landing)
- Brief moment of stillness before expansion
- Camera maintains front-facing locked position

---

### Beat 4: 5-9 seconds

**Seamless Expansion Transformation**

- Model **immediately** begins expanding after landing
- Smooth continuous growth from miniature to full-size
- Scale increases naturally and progressively
- Design remains identical throughout (proportions maintained)
- Materials become more detailed as size increases
- Windows, balcony, doors, facade all expand proportionally
- Lighting becomes more defined
- Texture detail increases with scale

**Expansion Characteristics:**

- Smooth (not jerky or sudden)
- Continuous (steady growth rate)
- Center-aligned (grows outward from landing point)
- Proportional (all elements scale together)
- Visually satisfying progression
- Fully visible throughout (nothing obscured)

---

### Beat 5: 9-12 seconds

**Completed Full-Size Building**

- Transformation completes at full architectural scale
- Building now matches @Image1 at real size
- Exact same design as miniature (perfect consistency)
- Photorealistic materials
- All architectural details visible
- Lighting natural and realistic
- Building settled and stable on lot

**Optional:** Subtle final camera adjustment (slight push-in or angle shift) to showcase completed property
