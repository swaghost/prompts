# Stock Market Crash Live Report

**Format:** 60-90 seconds, live business news broadcast  
**Type:** Breaking financial news, market crash coverage  
**Aesthetic:** Professional broadcast journalism, real-time crisis reporting  
**Style:** Steady main camera + handheld cutaway, live studio-quality production

---

## CONCEPT

Live business news report covering a sudden stock market crash during active trading hours. Female journalist delivers controlled but tense field report outside stock exchange building as digital ticker boards flash plunging red numbers. Tripod-mounted main camera with secondary handheld cutaway captures trading floor chaos visible through glass partition. Trader eyewitness provides brief comment. Reporter reacts live to fresh market plunge. Real-time number changes drive narrative urgency. Closing sign-off notes continued monitoring into afternoon trading.

- Traders in background reacting

### SEGMENT 3: Eyewitness Moment

**A trader stepping outside briefly comments on the chaos**

- **Trader exits building** (brief break from floor)

- Returns inside quickly

### SEGMENT 4: Situation Change

**Ticker shows fresh plunge, reporter reacts live to the number**

- **Critical real-time moment**
- Ticker board updates with worse numbers

- "And we're seeing another drop right now..."
- Camera may zoom to ticker showing new lows
- Genuine unscripted reaction
- Emphasizes live urgency

Duration: ~10-15 seconds

---

### Reporter Opening:

_"Markets are in freefall this morning. Behind me, the [exchange name] is watching the [index name] plunge more than [X]% in just the last hour—this represents a loss of [amount] in market value. Traders inside are calling this one of the steepest drops we've seen in [timeframe]..."_

### Reporter Update Lead-In:

_"You can see the trading floor behind me—those screens showing the technical charts painting a picture of steep, sustained selling..."_

### Trader Eyewitness:

_"It's absolute chaos in there. Every sector is bleeding red. We haven't seen panic selling like this in years. Phones are ringing off the hook."_

### Reporter Live Reaction to Fresh Plunge:

_"And we're seeing—[glances at ticker]—another significant drop just now, the [index] falling below [number] for the first time since [date]. This is... this is accelerating."_

### Reporter Closing:

_"We'll be monitoring these markets closely throughout the trading day and into the closing bell. The question now is whether we'll see any recovery this afternoon, or if this selloff will continue. For [Network Name], I'm [Reporter Name], live at the [Exchange Name]."_
