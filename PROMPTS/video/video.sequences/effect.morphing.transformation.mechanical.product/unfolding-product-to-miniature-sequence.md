# Unfolding Product-to-Miniature Commercial Sequence

## Classification

**Product Category:** Product transformation / miniature world / commercial animation

**Reveal Effect:** An uploaded subject unfolds into a fully functional miniature version of itself

**Sequence Type:** Button-activated mechanical transformation / product reveal / miniature commercial

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 video

## Concept

A clean product-photography setup becomes a satisfying mechanical transformation sequence. A human finger enters from the right and presses a hidden button integrated into the uploaded subject. The subject then unfolds with precision-engineered movements, revealing a complete miniature version of itself.

The transformation must preserve the original subject's colours, branding, materials, proportions, and design language. The finished miniature includes realistic architectural or functional details, tiny accessories, illuminated interiors, decorative elements, miniature people, subtle environmental animation, and small steam effects.

## Sequence Beats

1. **Opening setup:** Place the uploaded subject on a clean white tabletop near a window. Establish the original product clearly with a static, slightly elevated front angle.
2. **Activation:** A human finger enters from the right side and presses the hidden button integrated into the subject.
3. **Mechanical release:** Panels split apart, hidden gears rotate, and internal mechanisms activate with precise physical timing.
4. **Structural unfolding:** Sections slide outward, components unfold, architectural elements emerge, windows deploy, and signage rises.
5. **Automatic assembly:** Intricate miniature details, accessories, decorative elements, and illuminated interiors assemble into place.
6. **Miniature reveal:** The subject becomes a fully functional miniature version of itself, with miniature people and subtle environmental animation providing scale and life.
7. **Commercial hold:** Hold on the completed miniature as a premium product hero shot. Include realistic reflections, material detail, small steam effects, and a satisfying completed state.

## Camera and Composition

- Static camera throughout the transformation.
- Slightly elevated front angle.
- Vertical 9:16 composition.
- Macro-lens product photography.
- Shallow depth of field with the subject and transformation mechanism as the focal plane.
- Clean white tabletop and soft natural window light.
- Blurred potted plants and wooden windowsill in the background.
- Realistic shadows and cinematic reflections.
- Keep the full subject visible and avoid cropping during the transformation.

## Mechanical and Continuity Requirements

- Preserve the uploaded subject's exact colours, branding, materials, proportions, and design language.
- Keep the same subject identity from the first frame through the final miniature reveal.
- Make every transformation step mechanically legible and physically connected.
- Panels, gears, rails, hinges, windows, signage, and architectural elements must move with believable engineered motion.
- Components must not float, teleport, duplicate, or disappear without a visible mechanical cause.
- Keep the camera static and the background stable.
- Ensure the final miniature remains recognizable as a scaled, unfolded version of the original subject.
