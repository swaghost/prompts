# Hyperreal Influencer Fashion - Coastal Balcony Selfie

**Sequence Type:** Influencer Fashion / Unposed Phone Editorial
**Platform:** Seedance 2.5 through Arcads
**Duration:** 9 seconds
**Opening Frame:** Use the matching coastal balcony still as the exact first frame
**Format:** Horizontal 3:2 opening image; adapt output to the target platform without changing the composition logic

## Description

A sun-washed influencer-fashion selfie evolves through natural gestures, tactile skin and hair detail, a second speaker entering the frame, and a final vintage-camera flash that washes the image to pure white. The clip should feel like an authentic phone recording rather than a polished commercial.

## Prerequisites

- Character sheet with frontal, profile, and rear views
- Exact opening balcony still
- Separate wardrobe, prop, and location references assigned by role
- Seedance 2.5 image-to-video generation

- `@Image 6`: blue-and-white striped linen shirt wardrobe reference

```text
SEEDANCE 2.5 - 9 SECONDS - HORIZONTAL 3:2 - SINGLE CONTINUOUS SHOT - WITH AUDIO

FRAME ONE: use @Image 1 exactly as the opening frame. Preserve the woman's face, hair, layered gold necklaces, white strapless top, green railing, white pillar, pastel cliffside houses, banana leaves, hazy sea, sun-washed warm grade, glossy natural skin, fine grain, and compact-camera softness. CHARACTER LOCK - THE WOMAN: preserve the exact face, bone structure, brows, hooded hazel eyes, full glossy lips, sun-streaked centre-parted long hair, gold layered necklaces, white strapless top, gold rings, slim bangle, and natural skin. Real pores, natural sheen, faint freckles, fine facial hairs, and subtle asymmetry remain visible. No smoothing or beauty filter.

CHARACTER LOCK - GEORGE: preserve the exact platinum-blond buzz fade, olive-tan skin, thick dark brows, brown eyes, natural lips, faint stubble, and face from the George references. He wears the blue-and-white vertical-stripe linen shirt from @Image 6, open at the chest with sleeves rolled, tucked into white trousers. His right hand holds the vintage silver-and-black Canon SLR from @Image 5 against his chest, fingers naturally wrapped around the lens and body; the slim strap runs over his shoulder. He has no sunglasses.

LOOK: natural sun-washed warm daylight, hazy blooming highlights over the sea, saturated Mediterranean greens, glossy real skin, lifted blacks, fine vintage compact-camera grain. No cinematic LUT, no artificial HDR, no beauty smoothing, no plastic skin.

CAMERA: front-facing smartphone selfie held at arm's length as in @Image 1. Natural handheld micro-shake from a held arm, breathing corrections, small framing adjustments, continuous phone autofocus that occasionally breathes, and auto-exposure reacting naturally. No stabilizer smoothness, no gimbal movement, no digital zoom.

MOMENT 1 - 0.0-3.8s - OPEN ON START FRAME / HAIR TUCK / LINE 1

Open pixel-identical to @Image 1. Within the first half-second, the woman's free hand lifts from the railing and sweeps loose strands away from her face and behind her ear; two or three fine strands remain loose and continue moving in the breeze. She speaks in natural American English with a relaxed creator tone, not an announcer voice: "No one is telling you how to make AI characters look this real." Emphasize "NO ONE" with a slight brow lift and tiny head shake, and "THIS real" with a small chin dip and direct look into the lens. Between emphasized words, her free hand makes small loose talking gestures: open-palm turn, soft point toward the lens on "you," then settles near her collarbone. Her eye-line stays mostly on the lens with natural blinks and tiny drifts to the preview screen below it.

The breeze moves hair ends, banana leaves, and bougainvillea. Small gentle waves and sea glitter remain visible. Two or three distant seagulls pass as tiny specks. Around 2.5s, a small butterfly drifts briefly through the soft-focus background near the flowers, never crossing her face. Her close phone-mic voice is clear, with faint breeze, leaf rustle, and distant gull calls.

MOMENT 2 - 3.8-4.5s - THE SKIN / PUSH-IN / FOCUS HUNT

Without a cut, she says "The skin," while the phone arm physically moves 15-20 cm closer, tightening to a close-up of cheek, mouth, and eyes. The movement is slightly uneven and arm-driven, not a digital zoom. At the same time, the fingertips of her free hand glide lightly across her cheekbone and down the cheek in one effortless pass. Autofocus hunts for 0.5-1 second, the skin briefly softens, then sharpens onto visible pores, the same natural sun sheen, fine peach fuzz, faint freckles, and slight sun flush. Auto-exposure nudges naturally as the face fills the frame. Her voice becomes slightly louder and warmer as the microphone nears her mouth; a small breath is audible.

MOMENT 3 - 4.5-5.1s - HAIR / LATERAL REFRAME / GUST

She says "hair," and the phone angles slightly toward screen-right so the hair over that shoulder fills more of the frame against hazy water. She turns her head slightly; her free hand lifts a section of hair and lets it fall in an easy demonstration gesture. A stronger breeze lifts and separates individual strands and flyaways, backlit against the sea, while banana leaves and bougainvillea move more noticeably. Autofocus hunts again for 0.5-1 second, then locks on the moving strands. Auto-exposure shifts briefly and recovers. Her voice returns toward normal volume as the phone moves out.

MOMENT 4 - 5.1-6.4s - THE WAY THEY TALK AND MOVE / RETURN TO BASE

The phone eases back to the opening arm's-length framing. On "the way they talk," the free hand makes relaxed talking gestures in rhythm: a small open-hand roll near her chin, natural mouth shapes, glossy lips, and exact lip-sync. On "and move," she demonstrates casually with a small shoulder roll, side sway, weight shift from one hip to the other, and a free-hand sweep down and outward. The frame sways with her body and a hint of motion blur appears only on the fast hand pass. Necklaces swing subtly and rings catch light. She finishes with a small smile and the energy drops for a beat.

MOMENT 5 - 6.4-6.9s - SILENCE / BIRD CUE

She holds the lens with a small closed-mouth smile and natural blink. A small bird flies in from the upper right, veers away, and lands on the white pillar or wall behind her at screen-left next to the green railing. It settles and turns its head toward the left edge, subtly drawing attention there. No speech: only breeze, distant gulls, and a tiny wing flutter.

MOMENT 6 - 6.9-7.9s - GEORGE ENTERS / BUT I WILL

At the exact moment the bird turns its head, George walks into frame from screen-left at a normal relaxed pace, first his striped shoulder and forearm, then his face, settling at the woman's right shoulder, screen-left of her. He does not pop in or appear from nowhere. He leans slightly toward the phone as people do when joining a selfie. He looks into the lens with a friendly playful half-smile and says with a laid-back slight Californian accent: "But I will." Emphasize "I" with a small chin dip and brow lift. His shirt, Canon, strap, face, and skin catch the same sun. The woman turns her head slightly toward him and gives a knowing smile without speaking. Handheld micro-shake continues; autofocus holds both faces without a dramatic rack.

MOMENT 7 - 7.9-9.0s - CANON RAISE / FLASH / PURE WHITE

George raises the Canon in one smooth arc to his right eye, his left hand cradling the lens in a two-hand SLR grip, lens aimed directly into the phone lens. As it approaches, phone autofocus briefly reacquires on the lens. At 8.4s, a mechanical shutter clack sounds and the flash fires directly into the phone camera. From 8.4-8.7s, hard white flash bloom expands from the centre, highlights clip, and the full frame blows out to 100% pure white. From 8.7-9.0s, hold on a completely white frame with no residual image, vignette, or bounce-back. The final frame is pure white. Ambient breeze and gulls continue faintly underneath; no audio dropout.

GLOBAL CONTINUITY: same woman and George, same faces, hair, jewelry, wardrobe, prop, voices, sun-washed look, skin texture, and atmosphere. Realistic weight and timing, spontaneous gestures, natural pauses, precise lip-sync, no morphing hands or faces. No extra people beyond the woman and George, no phone visible, no third hand, no text, captions, logos, or watermarks. No black ending; finish on pure white.
```

## Effects and Audio

- Handheld selfie micro-shake throughout

- Bird landing cues George's natural entrance

- No music; use close phone-mic voice, breeze, leaves, gulls, wing flutter, shutter clack, and flash ambience
