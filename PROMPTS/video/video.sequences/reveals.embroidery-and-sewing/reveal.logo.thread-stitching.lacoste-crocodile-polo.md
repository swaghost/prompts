# The Self-Stitching Logo - Lacoste Crocodile Polo

**CLIENT 1B · FASHION BRANDS / LOGO EMBROIDERY REVEAL**

## Platform Chain

**GPT Image 2** → **Omni Flash** (chained in Picsart Flow)

## Concept

A macro product shot of embroidered crocodile logo on white pique polo that animates itself stitching from nothing — green thread works the body from snout to tail, then red fills the mouth and white adds teeth. Autonomous embroidery with no visible tools.

---

## Workflow

1. Open [Picsart Flow](https://picsart.com/flow) → new canvas
2. Image node → GPT Image 2 → paste IMAGE prompt → generate (reference frame)
3. Attach video node → Omni Flash → image feeds automatically → paste ANIMATION prompt → generate
4. Run each node 2–3×, keep the best

## IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2)

Create a photorealistic extreme close-up product photograph of the chest of a white cotton pique polo shirt, shot straight on, the fabric filling the entire frame, the placket and one buttonhole just visible at the left edge.

The pique weave is clearly resolved — the small raised waffle cells of the knit, individual cotton fibres catching the light, the cloth soft and slightly rumpled with a gentle fold running below.

Embroidered directly into the weave on the left chest is the Lacoste crocodile: a small crocodile in side profile facing right, its jaws wide open at the front showing a row of pointed white teeth and a red open mouth, a ridged spine running the length of its arched back with a row of raised triangular scales along the top, four short splayed legs beneath it and a long tail tapering to a point at the rear and kicking upward.

The body is worked in dense bright green satin stitch, the stitches laid tightly side by side across the form so the thread reads as a solid raised surface with a clear directional sheen, the outline crisp, the pique puckering very slightly all around the embroidery where the thread pulls the weave in. The mouth is filled in red, the teeth in white, a tiny white eye stitched into the head.

Macro product photography for a fashion e-commerce listing. Soft diffused studio light from the upper left, the raised stitching casting its own fine shadow across the white cotton, shallow depth of field, the embroidery tack-sharp and the fabric softening away at the edges of frame.

**Constraints:** no text, no words, no wordmark, no labels, no captions, no watermark, no other logos, no hands, no people, no hangers, no props, no background beyond the fabric.

## ANIMATION PROMPT (Omni Flash, video node)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

The shot opens on the bare white pique fabric with no embroidery on it at all, nothing else in frame but cloth. A single green thread descends out of the air from the top of the frame, its loose end hanging and swaying, and touches down on the weave.

From that first contact the thread stitches the entire emblem by itself, in one continuous unbroken path, working strictly from the snout at the front to the tail at the rear. It sinks into the pique and rises back out over and over, laying dense satin stitch as it travels, the fabric puckering slightly around each pass, the finished stitching left solid behind it while the remaining length of thread still floats loose in the air above.

It builds the open jaws first at the front, then works back through the head, then along the arched spine laying the row of raised triangular scales one after another, then down the body, then out along the tail to its point, and finally back to fill the four short legs beneath.

As the green path finishes and settles, a red thread drops in and fills the open mouth, then a white thread lays the row of pointed teeth one by one and finishes with a single tiny stitch for the eye.

The threads complete the whole emblem, matching exactly the shape, position and proportions shown, then pull taut and settle flat. The camera pushes in slowly on the finished raised stitching. Macro depth of field throughout, soft studio light, the fabric breathing slightly.
