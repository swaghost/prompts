# Red Cap Embroidery 03 - Needle Macro

**Duration:** 2s-4s | **Transition:** Hard cut

## Prompt

```text
Extreme macro close-up of a single steel embroidery needle tip positioned exactly 2mm above completely bare deep crimson red cotton fabric. Zero embroidery and zero design are visible, only plain cotton weave texture. White thread is loaded through the needle eye. The machine powers on; the needle descends in one sharp motion and pierces the bare fabric. The first single white stitch appears. Camera locked completely static with zero camera movement. RED Komodo 6K, 100mm macro lens, f/2.8, natural film grain. Sound: one sharp loud needle strike.
```

## Continuity
