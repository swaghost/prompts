# Red Cap, White Lion and One Perfect Stitch

Nine-shot, 15-second Seedance 2.5/Kling product-commercial sequence. A deep crimson red structured snapback cap receives a white lion crown embroidery stitch by stitch, then floats into a dark studio hero reveal.

## Shared Locks

- RED Komodo 6K, 100mm macro, f/2.8
- Deep crimson red cap and pure white thread only
- Matte red cotton weave, natural film grain, no plastic sheen
- Bare fabric at 0s; lion builds from 0% to 100%
- Shots 1-6 hard-cut industrial process; shots 7-9 smooth reveal
- Zero text, watermark, color drift, machine glitch, or disconnected threads
