# Red Cap Embroidery - Global Style and Camera Rules

## Style

Fast hard cuts between shots 1-6. No slow fades or dissolves. Sharp energetic pace. Smooth slow movement in shots 7-9. Warm fluorescent industrial overhead lighting for the first 10 seconds and dark dramatic studio lighting for the final 5 seconds, with zero flicker or color-temperature drift within either half. Deep crimson red and pure white only. Matte cotton fabric and raised white thread remain visible. Natural film grain throughout.

## Camera

RED Komodo 6K cinema camera, 100mm macro lens, f/2.8 throughout. Shots 1-6 use locked or physically motivated workshop movement, beginning wide and moving to extreme needle macro, then gradually pulling back as the lion builds. Shots 7-9 use a physical tracking move for the floating cap, then static hero rotation and final push. Real lens breathing and natural machine vibration are allowed. No digital zoom or artificial stabilization.

## Negative Prompt

```text
complete lion at start, lion appearing suddenly, plastic cap texture, synthetic shine, blurry stitching, distorted lion shape, extra lion limbs, disconnected threads, background people except gloved hands in shots 2 and 7, text overlay, watermark, CGI, neon lighting, blue tones, green tones, gold thread, cap color change, machine duplication, warped fabric, needle duplication, thread warping, color bleeding, jump cuts within a shot, camera teleporting, jerky floating motion, dead still frames, identity drift on cap or lion shape
```

## Hard Rules

- At 0 seconds: bare red fabric and zero embroidery.
- Lion builds from 0% to 100% only.
- Cap remains deep crimson red every frame.
- Lion shape remains identical once visible.
- Color palette is red and white only.
- Zero text and zero watermark.
- People appear only as gloved hands in shots 2 and 7.
- Shots 1-6 use hard cuts; shots 7-9 use smooth movement.
- Every camera move is physical; no camera teleport.
- Fabric remains matte cotton with no plastic texture.
