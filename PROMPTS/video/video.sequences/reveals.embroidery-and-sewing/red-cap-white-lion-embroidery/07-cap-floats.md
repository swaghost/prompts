# Red Cap Embroidery 07 - Cap Floats

**Duration:** 10s-12s | **Transition:** Smooth studio reveal

## Prompt

```text
Background shifts from the warm industrial workshop to a deep dark dramatic studio black. One gloved hand enters and confidently removes the finished deep crimson red cap from the aluminum hoop. The hand releases the cap from below and it begins floating upward slowly in dark space. A dramatic warm spotlight from the left hits the white lion crown embroidery; every raised stitch catches the light sharply and 3D thread texture is visible. Cap floats upward with confident gentle motion. Sound: deep cinematic bass rise and soft fabric whoosh.
```
