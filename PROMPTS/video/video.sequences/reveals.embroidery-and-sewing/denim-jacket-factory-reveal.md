# Denim Trucker Jacket Factory Reveal

**Format:** 16:9, 30 seconds, one continuous handheld take, no cuts

**Type:** Product reveal through manufacturing process  
**Aesthetic:** Raw industrial realism, unglamorous working environment  
**Film Stock:** Kodak Vision3 500T grade, 35mm T2.8

---

## CINEMATOGRAPHY

### Camera

- 35mm at T2.8, handheld with natural sway
- Constant micro-jitter, never a smooth glide
- Focus breathing throughout
- Close and low positioning, moving with the production line

### Lighting

- One hard key from dirty steel-frame windows screen-left
- Raking low across the machines
- Fill is concrete bounce three stops down
- Right third of frame falls to true black
- A third of frame in shadow, darkest areas reach true black
- No lifted blacks, no brightness lift, no bloom, no glow, no HDR

### Color Palette

- Raw indigo against ochre wood
- Oxidised copper against cold steel grey
- Bone-white thread against blue-black denim
- Desaturated, green shadows, amber highlights
- Fine 35mm grain
- Indigo lint visible in the air

---

## CHARACTERS

### Man (Early 60s)

- Grey stubble
- Sun-damaged brown skin
- Scar through left eyebrow
- Indigo dust under nails
- Cracked thumb visible in close-up
- Faces stable throughout, no deformation

### Woman (Mid 50s)

- Oiled bun
- Deep crow's feet

- Heat flush on cheekbones and neck
- Faces stable throughout, no deformation

## PRODUCT

### Denim Trucker Jacket Specifications

- Raw selvedge Type III trucker jacket
- 13.5oz right-hand twill
- Unwashed rope-dyed indigo with white core at every cut edge
- Brass shank buttons
- Copper bar tacks
- Orange-gold topstitch
- Chainstitched hem with roping
- **Identical in every beat** (no variation)

---

## SETTING

**Location:** First floor of old industrial building

- Bare concrete floors
- Tube lights switched off (natural light only)
- Two rows of single-needle lockstitch machines facing each other
- Chainstitch hemmer at head of line
- Pneumatic bar-tacker
- Rolling garment rail
- Oscillating fan

---

## SHOT SEQUENCE

### Beat 1: 0-8 seconds

**Extreme Macro Needle Work**

- **Framing:** Extreme macro 20cm from the needle
- **Action:** Needle drops through two layers of raw indigo nine times per second
- **Detail:** Each puncture dents the twill and lifts puff of white lint
- **Thread:** Orange thread pulls tight
- **Movement:** Panel creeps 3cm per second under presser foot
- **Hand:** Man's cracked thumb rides seam allowance, steers it straight
- **Camera:** Drifts left along the seam

**Sound:**

- Flat wooden thud of needle through heavy denim
- Rising servo whine
- Tick of take-up lever
- Presser foot slapping down

---

### Beat 2: 8-17 seconds

**Production Line Reveal**

- **Camera:** Pulls back two metres to chest height, arcing right
- **Reveal:** Eleven operators at eleven machines, panels stacking down the line
- **Action:** Woman at third machine snaps thread with hard sideways pull
- **Movement:** Drops yoke into bin without looking, drags next panel across in one motion

**Sound:**

- Eleven machines out of phase
- Thread snips
- Trolley wheels on concrete
- Fan sweeping past mic
- Voice calling number down the line

---

### Beat 3: 17-24 seconds

**Chainstitch & Bar-Tack**

- **Camera:** Drops and pushes in low on chainstitch hemmer
- **Action:** Jacket hem feeds through, chain laying down looping on underside
- **Detail:** Fabric curling into visible roping as it exits
- **Next:** Man drops half-built jacket onto bar-tacker, hits pedal
- **Bar-tack:** Fires dense block of stitches into pocket corner and stops dead

**Sound:**

- Chainstitcher pitched higher than lockstitches
- Air hiss
- Pneumatic clunk
- Beat of silence after

---

### Beat 4: 24-30 seconds

**Final Reveal & Hold**

- **Action:** Man lifts jacket by shoulders, shakes once
- **Detail:** Sleeves fall, lint flies off into window light
- **Placement:** Hooks jacket on rolling rail
- **Camera:** Settles and holds static on jacket hanging still
- **Light:** Shaft of light crossing diagonally
- **Hold:** Two seconds

**Sound:**

- Fabric snap
- Hanger scraping metal
- Machines continuing behind
- Fan ambient
