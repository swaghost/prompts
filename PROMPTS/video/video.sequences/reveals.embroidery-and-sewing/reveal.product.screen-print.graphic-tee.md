# Object-Mounted Product Film - Graphic Tee Screen Print

**Object:** Boxy oversized off-white heavy-cotton tee with black stacked typography "NEED MONEY FOR PORSCHE", small subtext "URGENTLY" and "911", and a grayscale Porsche 911 rear three-quarter graphic.

**Actions:** Black typography prints first, grayscale Porsche artwork prints in a second registered pass, a heat press cures the ink, then the tee is handed off and pulled over a model's torso.
