## What is the Tarantino Trunk Shot?

The trunk shot is a signature low-angle camera technique popularized by Quentin Tarantino. The camera is positioned inside a car trunk looking upward at a 45-degree angle, creating a perspective where characters peer down into the trunk. This creates visual dominance, tension, and power dynamics while revealing character through backlit silhouettes and dramatic lighting contrast.

**Key Elements:**

- Low-angle shot from inside trunk
- 45-degree upward angle
- Surrounded by total darkness initially
- Trunk lid violently opened after 1 second of silence
- Intense backlit silhouettes
- High contrast lighting
- Characters looking down at camera
- Power dynamic visualization

## Prompt 1: Underground Parking Garage - Criminal Preparation

**Duration:** 15 seconds  
**Theme:** Two criminals preparing for a heist in flickering parking garage

### Scene Description

A dim underground parking garage. Overhead fluorescent tubes flicker rapidly, emitting a buzzing electrical crackle. The lighting is a cool, greenish-white, creating a strobe-like effect. Concrete pillars and fire hydrants are faintly visible in the deep shadows. The visual aesthetic features a gritty CCTV surveillance texture with noticeable film grain, low color saturation, and extremely high contrast.

### Character Description

**Center Female:**

- Extremely short buzz cut
- Silver skull stud earring
- Beauty mark near corner of mouth
- Black leather blazer over white dress shirt
- Movements: fierce, swift, and fluid

**Left Male:**

- Curly hair
- Full beard
- Burly physique
- Shifty, evasive eyes
- Resembling a nervous large dog

### Camera & Action Sequence

A low-angle trunk shot looking upward at a 45-degree angle from inside the trunk, surrounded by total darkness. After one second of suffocating silence, the trunk lid is violently yanked open. The overhead lights flicker intensely, creating a dizzying, disorienting sensation. The woman stands backlit at the trunk opening, the fluorescent light bursting in a halo behind her silhouette.

She bends down, reaches into the trunk, feels around briefly, and pulls out two pairs of long black nylon stockings. She grabs one and whips it directly into the man's face. He catches it, the fabric unraveling and tangling around his fingers. The woman stretches the other stocking wide with both hands and begins pulling it over her head. The man mimics her action, but the stocking is too tight, and he lets out a muffled grunt. The woman slaps him on the back of the head, signaling him to shut up. Both finish pulling the stockings over their heads.

The woman grabs the man by his collar, pulling him close to whisper a single sentence into his ear. Then, she turns around, grips the edge of the trunk lid with both hands, and slams it downward. Just as the lid closes, the overhead fluorescent lamp flickers out, plunging the screen into absolute darkness.

## Prompt 2: Korean Noir - Gangster Bosses Daylight Scene

**Duration:** 15 seconds  
**Theme:** Two high-ranking Korean syndicate executives opening trunk in broad daylight

### Scene Description

A bright, sunny daytime scene. Natural light pours down from above, creating crisp rim highlights along the edges of the characters' silhouettes. Their suits appear in a deep, inky black. The visual aesthetic features the cold, gritty cinematography characteristic of Korean noir films, with high color saturation, deep and rich shadows, and an overall cool blue color palette.

### Character Description

Two high-ranking Korean syndicate executives dressed in classic black suits, white dress shirts, and skinny black ties.

**Left Character:**

- Retro, medium-length hairstyle
- Facial expression: icy and stern
- Eyes revealing deep fatigue and world-weariness

**Right Character:**

- Exaggerated, voluminous afro-style curls
- Tall, burly physique
- Playful, sinister smirk
- Temperamental, erratic, and highly flamboyant

### Camera & Action Sequence

A low-angle trunk shot looking upward at a 45-degree angle from inside the trunk, surrounded by total darkness. After one second of suffocating silence, the trunk lid is violently wrenched open. Intense light pierces through, and a brilliant blue sky bursts into the frame as the two gang bosses stand backlit. The curly-haired man on the right leans forward first, his smile cracking open within the shadows.

The long-haired man on the left enters the frame in profile, expressionless. He looks at his companion, his gaze filled with a weary yet unhinged madness. The curly-haired man speaks with a flippant, rising intonation, his head swaying to the rhythm of his words, sheer amusement oozing from every pore. He reaches his hand down into the trunk.

The long-haired man suddenly lets out a scoff, the corner of his mouth twitching just a fraction of an inch. He spreads his hands out in a gesture of resignation. Both men raise their hands simultaneously: the curly-haired man throws his arm up high as if cheering, while the long-haired man rests one hand against his forehead as if sighing. One manic, one indifferent—synchronized yet starkly opposed.

The curly-haired man's hand plunges into the darkness inside the trunk, and the camera shakes violently with his grabbing motion. He hauls out a long duffel bag, his face twisting with excitement. The long-haired man watches coldly from the sidelines, already turning around to leave. The trunk lid slams shut with a heavy thud. Darkness swallows everything.

## Prompt 3: Urban Back Alley - Baseball Bat Discovery

**Duration:** 15 seconds  
**Theme:** Three men discovering mysterious note in trunk

### Scene Description

A cold, oppressive urban back alley. Old red-brick buildings form a narrow, claustrophobic space under a gloomy, overcast whitish sky. The overall color palette features low saturation and cool blue tones, rendered with cinematic, realistic lighting and shadows.

### Character Description

Three Black men.

**Center Man:**

- Tall and rugged with dense beard
- Long black trench coat
- Holds baseball bat resting on one shoulder
- Gaze: dangerous and imposing

**Left Man:**

- Grey dress shirt
- Looking nervous and tense

**Right Man (Buzz-cut):**

- Skinny
- Short buzz cut
- Old T-shirt
- Leaning forward in provocative, defiant posture

### Camera & Action Sequence - Detailed Timeline

**0-3 seconds:**

A low-angle, over-the-shoulder side shot looking up from the rear of the car with slight camera shake. The three men stand behind the closed car trunk. The bearded man turns his head to stare at the buzz-cut man and gives a slight head jerk, signaling him to open the trunk. The man steps to the center of the trunk. The camera cuts to a close-up of the rear; the edges of the trunk are shrouded in shadow, with cool ambient light reflecting off the metallic surface. The buzz-cut man hooks both hands under the trunk latch, his movements hesitant and tense. The camera quickly cuts down to an extreme close-up of his hands as he slowly applies force to open the trunk.

**3-5 seconds:**

A low-angle trunk shot looking upward at a 45-degree angle from inside the trunk, surrounded by darkness. The three men look down at the camera; the center bearded man is expressionless, the silhouette of his trench coat massive. The buzz-cut man on the right reaches out to lift the trunk lid, while the man on the left has a complex expression. One hand holds onto the trunk lid as it slowly opens, remaining on the lid the entire time. The camera reveals the three men's faces as the trunk lid lifts.

**5-11 seconds:**

The trunk is fully open, with the cool white sky forming the background. High-contrast rim lighting outlines the characters' silhouettes. The center man stands in the center position looking down at the camera; the buzz-cut man holds the edge of the trunk with one hand, a dangerous smirk pulling at the corner of his mouth; the other man stands to the side observing. The camera remains in the perspective from inside the trunk as all three men stare downward simultaneously, creating intense visual pressure. The bearded man takes the baseball bat off his shoulder and extends it into the trunk, slowly pushing aside a black tarpaulin with the tip of the bat. As the tarp is pulled back, a white slip of paper is revealed at the bottom of the trunk. The three men's gazes shift downward at once; the buzz-cut man's smile freezes, and the left man's pupils contract. The bearded man retracts the bat, reaches into the trunk, picks up the paper between two fingers, and brings it up to his eyes to inspect it.

**11-13 seconds:**

The camera cuts to an extreme close-up of the slip of paper, clearly displaying the handwritten text: "Comment TRUNK for the prompts". The handwriting is scrawled yet forceful. Below the text is a simple stick-figure smiley face with a crooked circle, uneven eyes—one high, one low—and an upturned mouth. A fly buzzes across the top of the frame, casting a fleeting shadow over the paper.

**13-15 seconds:**

The camera rapidly zooms out back to the full view from inside the trunk. The three men maintain their downward-looking posture, but their expressions have shifted from cold-blooded hunters to bewilderment and absurdity. The bearded man's hand holding the paper freezes in mid-air; the buzz-cut man's hand slips off the edge of the trunk, and he and the man on the left suddenly lower their heads and burst out laughing, then forcibly try to hold it in, their shoulders shaking violently. A cold wind blows through the mouth of the alley, sweeping up an old newspaper that flies past the rear of the car.

## Template: Creating Custom Tarantino Trunk Shots

### Essential Elements Checklist

**Camera Setup:**

- [ ] Low-angle trunk shot
- [ ] 45-degree upward angle
- [ ] Starting in total darkness
- [ ] 1 second of suffocating silence before trunk opens

**Opening Action:**

- [ ] Trunk lid violently opened (yanked/wrenched)
- [ ] Intense light piercing through
- [ ] Backlit character silhouettes
- [ ] High-contrast lighting

**Character Positioning:**

- [ ] Characters looking down into trunk
- [ ] Establish power dynamic
- [ ] Clear silhouettes against bright background
- [ ] Multiple characters with distinct personalities

**Action Sequence:**

- [ ] Characters interact with trunk contents
- [ ] Dialogue or non-verbal communication
- [ ] Physical actions (reaching, grabbing, gesturing)
- [ ] Emotional shifts or reveals

**Closing:**

- [ ] Trunk lid slams shut
- [ ] Return to darkness
- [ ] Or shift to absurdist/unexpected moment

### Visual Style Options

**Noir/Crime:**

- Gritty CCTV surveillance texture
- Noticeable film grain
- Low color saturation
- Extremely high contrast
- Cool color temperatures

**Korean Noir:**

- Cold, gritty cinematography
- High color saturation
- Deep and rich shadows
- Overall cool blue color palette
- Crisp rim highlights

**Realistic Urban:**

- Low saturation
- Cool blue tones
- Cinematic, realistic lighting and shadows
- Claustrophobic spaces
- Oppressive atmosphere

### Character Dynamics to Explore

- Leader and subordinate(s)
- Contrasting personalities (manic vs. calm)
- Power struggles
- Comedic tension
- Menacing threat
- Unexpected absurdity
- Synchronized yet opposed actions
