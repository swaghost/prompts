## Concept

Cinematic ad reveal where helicopter tears crimson satin cover off giant roadside billboard. Design poster with legible text in GPT Image 2, mock it up at real-world scale on billboard, then animate dramatic unveiling with helicopter pulling satin sheet from top to bottom. Matched before/after technique keeps camera, light, city locked while only cover moves.

**Style:** Documentary aerial photography, cinematic reveal  
**Format:** Vertical 3:4 poster, aerial drone perspective  
**Duration:** Continuous reveal, one single pull

## STEP 4: HELICOPTER REVEAL PROMPT (SEEDANCE 2.0)

**ATTACH:** the satin-covered billboard as the start frame

A helicopter lifts a crimson satin cover off a roadside billboard above a tropical city interchange. The helicopter hovers above the billboard with a taut cable running down to the top edge of the cloth. The cable stays attached to the cloth at all times. The helicopter climbs steadily, drawing the cable tight, and the satin sheet peels off the billboard face from the top downward in one single continuous pull. The cloth billows out into a wide sail as it comes free, catching the wind, twisting and rippling as it rises, then whips upward and leaves the frame at the top, trailing after the helicopter.

Revealed underneath: the billboard face, flat and rigid on its steel frame, electric blue with a white and lime running shoe and large white lettering, completely still and unmoving throughout. The printed face never bends, never ripples and never shifts.

The whole scene is alive throughout. Cars, buses and scooters drive continuously along every lane of the interchange, entering and leaving frame at a steady pace, never stopping and never idling. Palm fronds sway in the wind along the roadsides. The tree canopy moves. White clouds drift across the sky. Birds cross the frame once. The helicopter rotors spin at full speed the entire time.

Held constant: the billboard structure, the steel lattice, the roads, the palms, the city and horizon, the blue sky, the sun angle from upper left, the colour and grain. The cover comes off once and does not return.

Camera: locked off at drone height, straight on to the billboard, holding the same wide framing throughout with only a faint float.

Audio: helicopter rotors overhead, heavy satin snapping and cracking in the wind, wind rush, traffic noise from below.

## The Core Trick

**Matched before/after logic:**

1. Generate the "after" (poster mounted at scale)
2. Generate the "before" (same shot with artwork covered)
3. Seedance animates the transition between them

Camera, light, city, and color stay locked — only the cover moves. This makes it feel like real footage instead of a cut.

---

## Style Notes

**DO:**

- Get poster text perfect first (regenerate until crisp)
- List every text string explicitly
- Use "no text other than strings specified"
- Make cloth physics heavy and realistic
- Keep billboard face rigid throughout
- Show continuous traffic and movement
- Lock camera (faint float only)
- Run reveal multiple times for best take

**DON'T:**

- Let the printed face wobble or ripple
- Have cover return after removal
- Stop traffic or freeze background
- Move camera busily
- Use HDR or overly processed look
- Let text get garbled
- Add extra logos or text

---

## Cloth Physics Details

Critical for cinematic realism:

- "Real cloth weight"
- "Billows into a wide sail"
- "Catching the wind"
- "Twisting and rippling"
- "Heavy satin snapping and cracking"
- "Draws taut against cable"

The heavier the fabric reads, the more cinematic the reveal.

## Pro Tips

**Poster Design:**

- GPT Image 2 is best for large legible text
- Regenerate until every letter is crisp
- Lock the poster before moving to mock-ups

**Matched Frames:**

- Use "same photograph, one change" in prompt
- Keep tower, light, sun angle, crop identical
- Only the cloth/cover should differ
- Change traffic/pedestrians for natural feel

**Animation:**

- Run reveal multiple times
- Keep take where cover pulls cleanly
- Printed face must stay dead still
- Moving background sells the illusion

**Presentation:**

- Show finished animation as hero
- Add "references" strip with stills
- Include prompt visible alongside
- Frame like real campaign case study
