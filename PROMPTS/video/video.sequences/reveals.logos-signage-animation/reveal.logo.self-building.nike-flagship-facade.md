## Concept

An architectural facade sign photographed from the pavement that animates itself extruding from the concrete — brushed metal swoosh and letters push forward out of the wall autonomously, cast individual shadows, seat with mechanical clicks. No workers, no tools, just the sign emerging from stone.

## IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2)

Create a photorealistic architectural photograph of a raw board-formed concrete building on a corner in a dense city, shot from the pavement opposite looking up at the upper facade above a tall dark glass shopfront, hard low sun from the right, sharp shadows raking across the concrete, deep blue sky.

Mounted on the concrete is a large three-dimensional sign standing well proud of the wall. At its centre the Nike swoosh: a single thick curved shape that starts as a fine point at the lower left, sweeps down and along in a shallow curve, then rises steeply to the right and broadens into a wide flat wing that ends in a sharp point high on the right, one continuous solid form worked in brushed white metal.

To the right of the swoosh, in thick white capitals on two lines: "JUST DO IT" above and "FLAGSHIP STORE" below. Along the lower edge, a narrow band of the smallest white capitals reading "RUNNING • TRAINING • MEMBER PICKUP INSIDE".

Every element is a deep box form standing well proud of the wall, machined from brushed metal with visible side returns, clean sharp edges and a trim cap along each edge, casting individual hard shadows onto the concrete. All lettering sharp, correctly kerned, perfectly legible from across the street.

Realistic urban architecture photography, visible board-form timber grain in the concrete, tie holes in a regular grid, a service door far below giving a sense of the sign's scale.

**Constraints:** exact spelling of all three lines, no other text, no other signage, no watermark, no people, no cars.

## ANIMATION PROMPT (Omni Flash, video node)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

### Absolute Rules

**Camera rule, absolute:** the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance and lens from first frame to last. No pans, no pushes, no zooms, no reframing.

**Framing rule, absolute:** the building is seen from the pavement opposite, looking up at the corner facade at an angle from below, the same low three-quarter viewpoint as the reference image, the dark glass shopfront and a slice of blue sky both visible in frame.

**Target rule, absolute:** the final frame must match the reference image exactly — same camera angle, same framing, same swoosh, same position on the wall, same size, same letterforms, all three lines of copy present and identical.

### Animation Sequence

The shot opens on this same view of the building with the concrete facade completely bare, nothing mounted on it at all. The sign extrudes itself out of the wall, everything moving on its own with nothing and no one touching it: the swoosh pushes forward out of the concrete as a single continuous form, growing from its fine point at the lower left and travelling along the curved path to the right, the brushed metal emerging smoothly from the stone and extending outward on invisible brackets, the form separating from the wall and leaving a sharp shadow behind it as it comes.

It completes the swoosh, then the letterforms extrude beside it, starting with "JUST DO IT" on the first line, the capitals pushing out one after another left to right, then "FLAGSHIP STORE" on the second line below, then the smallest line "RUNNING • TRAINING • MEMBER PICKUP INSIDE" along the bottom edge, each letter and word emerging as a deep box form and casting its own hard shadow.

As the last letter seats into place with a solid mechanical click, the whole sign settles and holds. The finished installation matches the reference exactly, all elements proud of the wall, all shadows sharp, all lettering legible, the concrete facade now carrying the complete three-dimensional sign for the last second of the video.
