## Platform Chain

**GPT Image 2** (poster design + banner mock-ups) → **Seedance 2.0** (banner drop animation via OpenArt)

---

## Concept

Giant fabric banner unrolls down twelve-storey tower facade. Design poster with legible text in GPT Image 2, mock it up as hanging banner and as rolled bundle, then animate dramatic drop from roofline to street. Matched before/after technique where banner starts rolled at roofline and falls open to reveal artwork as it descends.

**Style:** Editorial architectural photography, street-level perspective  
**Format:** Vertical 3:4 poster, wide angle street view  
**Duration:** Continuous fall, roofline to street

## STEP 4: BANNER DROP PROMPT (SEEDANCE 2.0)

**ATTACH:** the rolled-banner image as the start frame

A rolled fabric banner drops open down the face of a tower in a tropical city. The banner starts rolled and strapped along the rail at the roofline, a tight bundle against the blue sky, the facade below bare and unbroken to street level. The straps give way, the bundle loosens, and the leading edge tips free of the roofline.

The cloth unspools downward in one single continuous fall from roofline to street, the roll thinning above as the sheet lengthens. The printed artwork becomes visible only where the cloth has already left the roll, so the cream field, the blue stripes, the lettering and the sneaker each come into view as that part of the sheet passes, never before. The falling edge stays level with a slight bow in the centre. The edge reaches the lower storeys, the cloth draws taut against its lashings, one slow ripple travels up the sheet and dies out, and the banner settles flat with the lettering fully readable.

The whole scene is alive throughout. Palm fronds sway and rustle in the wind on both sides of the street. Leaves on the shrubs below move constantly. Cars drive along the road at a steady pace, entering and leaving frame, never stopping and never idling. Pedestrians walk along the pavement at a natural pace from the first frame to the last. White clouds drift slowly across the sky. A bird crosses the upper frame once.

Held constant: the tower, the facade, the windows and balconies, the palms, the sky, the sun angle from upper left, the shadow direction, the colour and grain. The banner falls once and stays down.

Camera: a slow steady rise from street level, tilting up as the cloth descends, keeping the full height of the tower in frame. Wide-angle perspective held at the same width throughout, one direction only.

Audio: straps snapping, heavy cloth unspooling, wind through palm fronds, passing traffic, distant street life.

## The Core Trick

**Matched before/after logic:**

1. Generate the "after" (banner hanging fully)
2. Generate the "before" (banner rolled at roofline)
3. Match exactly: same tower, light, crop
4. Seedance animates the transition

Only the cloth changes — tower, light, sun angle stay locked. This sells the illusion of real footage.

---

## Progressive Reveal Detail

**Critical specification:**

"The printed artwork becomes visible only where the cloth has already left the roll, so the cream field, the blue stripes, the lettering and the sneaker each come into view as that part of the sheet passes, never before."

This creates a natural progressive reveal as fabric unspools, making the animation feel physically accurate.

---

## Style Notes

**DO:**

- Get poster text perfect first
- Match tower, light, crop exactly
- Show real cloth weight and physics
- Keep facade/tower rigid throughout
- Show continuous traffic and movement
- Camera rises slowly with fall
- One ripple travels up at end
- Run drop multiple times for best take

**DON'T:**

- Show artwork on rolled bundle
- Let banner fall multiple times
- Stop traffic or freeze background
- Make tower/facade wobble
- Rush the camera movement
- Skip the settling/ripple at end
- Use HDR or overly processed look

---

## Pro Tips

**Three Stills Required:**

1. Poster design (clean artwork)
2. Banner hanging (the "after")
3. Banner rolled (the "before" start frame)

The hanging and rolled frames must match exactly for the illusion to work.

**Progressive Artwork Reveal:**

Emphasize that artwork only appears where cloth has already left the roll — this prevents the AI from showing the full design too early.

**Scene Alive:**

Traffic, pedestrians, palms, clouds all moving continuously. The contrast between moving world and rigid banner makes it feel like real footage.

**Presentation:**

Frame like a real out-of-home campaign reveal. Show the animation large, include reference stills, display the prompt, add "Comment AI" card at end.
