# Fluted Glass 3D Signage — Logo Reveal Animation

**GOOGLE FLOW / OMNI FLASH / VFX OVERLAY REVEAL**

---

## Platform

**Google Flow** (labs.google/fx/tools/flow or flow.google)  
**Model:** Omni Flash  
**Duration:** 10 seconds

---

## Concept

VFX reveal animation for 3D lettering on textured glass background (fluted/ribbed panels). Letters extrude/pop out from flat surface into full 3D depth with shadows forming on textured ribbed surface. Camera locked with gentle zoom-in. Perfect for premium signage on architectural glass, ribbed acrylic panels, fluted backgrounds, textured surface installations.

**Material:** 3D raised letters on fluted/ribbed glass  
**Reveal Style:** Extrude/pop-out with textured surface shadow interaction  
**Camera:** Slight zoom-in (gentle push-in)  
**Challenge:** Shadows must interact with ribbed/fluted texture  
**Example:** "altr ego" black 3D lettering on green ribbed glass background

## Style Guidelines

**DO:**

- Specify "fluted glass" or "ribbed panel"
- Emphasize "textured surface"
- Include "soft drop shadows forming on the textured surface"
- Describe background color and texture
- Note texture direction (vertical ribs, horizontal channels)

**DON'T:**

- Skip textured surface description
- Forget shadow detail on texture
- Treat as flat smooth background
- Ignore texture-shadow interaction

---

## Textured Surface Shadow Detail

**Critical Language:**
"With soft drop shadows forming on the textured surface"

**Why It Matters:**

- Shadows on flat surface ≠ shadows on ribbed texture
- Texture creates dimensional shadow pattern
- Essential for photorealistic depth
- Distinguishes from smooth background reveals

**Enhanced Description:**

- "Shadows falling into the vertical ribs of the fluted glass"
- "Drop shadows interacting with the grooved texture pattern"
- "Dimensional shadows defined by the ribbed surface"

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Shadows look flat, don't interact with texture**

- Solution: Explicitly state "forming on the textured surface" and "interacting with ribbed pattern"

**Problem: Texture disappears or background changes**

- Solution: Strengthen "fluted glass panel" description, ensure reference shows clear texture

**Problem: Letters don't look 3D enough**

- Solution: Emphasize "full 3D raised depth" and "extruding outward from surface"

**Problem: Multiple text elements appear simultaneously**

- Solution: Split timeline clearly: primary [0:02-0:04], secondary [0:04-0:06]
