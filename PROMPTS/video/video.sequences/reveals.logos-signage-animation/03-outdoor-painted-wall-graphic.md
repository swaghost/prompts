# Outdoor Street Wall Graphic — Logo Reveal Animation

**GOOGLE FLOW / OMNI FLASH / VFX OVERLAY REVEAL**

---

## Platform

**Google Flow** (labs.google/fx/tools/flow or flow.google)  
**Model:** Omni Flash  
**Duration:** 10 seconds

---

## Concept

VFX reveal animation for painted outdoor wall graphics and typography. Text and icons extrude/pop out from flat painted surface, building line by line with natural outdoor lighting. Camera locked with gentle zoom-in. Perfect for street murals, exterior signage, painted wall graphics, directional wayfinding, outdoor brand installations.

**Material:** Painted wall graphics (hand-painted or vinyl)  
**Reveal Style:** Painted extrusion (flat to slightly raised)  
**Camera:** Slight zoom-in (gentle push-in)  
**Setting:** Outdoor street walls, natural lighting  
**Example:** "HAVE YOU HAD COFFEE YET?" white painted text with directional arrow on sage green wall

## Customization Guide

**Replace These Variables:**

- **[COLOR]** — Paint color (white, black, bright colors)
- **[SURFACE COLOR]** — Wall background color/texture
- **[PRIMARY TEXT]** — Main message or question
- **[GRAPHIC ELEMENT]** — Icons, arrows, decorative elements
- **[SECONDARY TEXT]** — Brand name, location info, tagline

## Technical Requirements

**Input Image:**

- Outdoor wall clearly visible
- Good natural lighting (avoid harsh shadows covering text)
- Sharp focus on painted graphics
- Environmental context (sidewalk, street edge, etc.)
- Clear contrast between paint and wall color

**Wall Characteristics:**

- Clean painted surface
- Text and graphics clearly defined
- Good paint coverage visible
- Outdoor weathered aesthetic acceptable

**Lighting:**

- Natural daylight preferred
- Even illumination across wall
- Avoid extreme shadows or blown highlights

---

## Style Guidelines

**DO:**

- Specify "painted wall graphics"
- Use "line by line from top to bottom"
- Include multiple elements in sequence
- Describe wall color/texture
- Keep outdoor aesthetic natural
- Specify exact positions for multiple elements

**DON'T:**

- Skip environmental context
- Forget to sequence multiple elements
- Over-complicate camera movement
- Ignore outdoor lighting characteristics

## Multi-Element Sequencing

**For Graphics with Multiple Components:**

**Option 1 - Grouped Reveal:**

```
[0:02-0:04] Main text appears
[0:04-0:05] Icon/arrow appears
[0:05-0:06] Brand name appears
```

**Option 2 - Continuous Flow:**

```
[0:02-0:06] All elements appear sequentially, main text first (lines 1-3), then directional arrow, then brand name below, building smoothly from top to bottom of composition.
```

## Pro Tips

**Outdoor Authenticity:**
Emphasize outdoor setting:

- "painted on exterior street wall"
- "outdoor wall graphic with natural daylight"
- "street-facing wall panel"

**Paint Material Detail:**
Specify paint characteristics:

- "white painted text with clean edges"
- "hand-painted appearance with slight texture"
- "smooth painted finish"

**Environmental Context:**
Include surrounding details:

- "with sidewalk visible at base"
- "street wall with urban context"
- "building exterior surface"

**Directional Graphics:**
For arrows and wayfinding:

- Specify direction (left, right, down, up)
- Position relative to text (above, below, beside)
- Style (simple, bold, decorative)

**Brand Integration:**
For secondary brand text:

- Smaller than main message
- Positioned below or to side
- Completes narrative (answers "where?" after "what?")

---

## Outdoor Lighting Considerations

**Natural Daylight:**

- Maintains authentic outdoor feel
- Soft shadows acceptable
- Avoid specifying time of day unless critical

**Shadow Management:**

- "Natural outdoor lighting with soft shadows"
- Don't let shadows obscure key text in reference image
- Shadows add authenticity but must not hide content

---

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Elements appear out of sequence**

- Solution: Clearly specify "[0:02-0:04] main text, [0:04-0:06] icon and brand name"

**Problem: Paint looks too glossy or 3D**

- Solution: Use "painted wall graphics" language, emphasize "flat painted surface"

**Problem: Outdoor context lost**

- Solution: Mention "exterior wall," "outdoor," "street" in description

**Problem: Multiple elements animate simultaneously when you want sequence**

- Solution: Split timeline clearly with specific elements per time block
