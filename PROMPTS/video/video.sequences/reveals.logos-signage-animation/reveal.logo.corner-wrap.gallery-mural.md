# Gallery Corner Mural — Logo Reveal Animation

**GOOGLE FLOW / OMNI FLASH / VFX OVERLAY REVEAL**

---

## Platform

**Google Flow** (labs.google/fx/tools/flow or flow.google)  
**Model:** Omni Flash  
**Duration:** 10 seconds

---

## Concept

VFX reveal animation for wrapped typography around 90-degree corner wall. Text extrudes/pops out from flat surface, wrapping smoothly around architectural corner with subtle shadows forming. Camera locked with gentle zoom-in. Perfect for corner murals, wrapped wall graphics, architectural typography, gallery installations.

**Material:** Painted or vinyl wall graphics wrapping corner  
**Reveal Style:** Extrude/pop-out with corner geometry wrapping  
**Camera:** Slight zoom-in (gentle push-in)  
**Challenge:** Typography wraps around 90-degree corner smoothly  
**Example:** "PEOPLE MAKE THIS PLACE" dark red text on white gallery corner

## Pro Tips

**Corner Clarity:**
Make corner geometry explicit:

- "wrapping around the 90-degree interior corner"
- "text continuing across the vertical architectural edge"
- "following the corner from left wall plane to right wall plane"

**Gallery Aesthetic:**
For museum/gallery installations:

- Specify "gallery white wall"
- Use "architectural typography"
- Emphasize "clean minimal aesthetic"

**Paint vs. Vinyl:**
Specify material if critical:

- "painted directly on corner surfaces"
- "vinyl lettering applied around corner"
- Material affects slight depth/shadow quality

**Complex Phrases:**
For 5+ words, consider splitting:

- Upper half: 0:02-0:04
- Lower half: 0:04-0:06
- Or stagger word-by-word for longer reveals

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Text appears flat, doesn't wrap corner**

- Solution: Emphasize "wrapping around the wall corner" and "wrapping smoothly around the vertical edge"

**Problem: Corner geometry lost**

- Solution: Add "90-degree corner" specification, ensure reference image shows clear corner

**Problem: Shadows missing on corner**

- Solution: Specify "soft subtle shadows forming" on both wall planes

**Problem: Text position shifts on corner**

- Solution: Strengthen anchor lock, add "in its exact original place wrapping around corner"
