# Red Acrylic 3D Letters — Logo Reveal Animation

**GOOGLE FLOW / OMNI FLASH / VFX OVERLAY REVEAL**

---

## Platform

**Google Flow** (labs.google/fx/tools/flow or flow.google)  
**Model:** Omni Flash  
**Duration:** 10 seconds

---

## Concept

VFX reveal animation for raised 3D acrylic lettering on textured wall. Letters extrude/pop out from flat against surface into full 3D raised depth, building left to right with drop shadows forming as they rise. Camera locked with subtle zoom-in. Perfect for glossy dimensional signage, raised acrylic letters, mounted 3D wordmarks.

**Material:** Raised acrylic 3D letters  
**Reveal Style:** Extrude/pop-out from flat to 3D depth  
**Camera:** Slight zoom-in (gentle push-in)  
**Example:** "SHOW TIME" red 3D script lettering on off-white textured wall

## Customization Guide

**Replace These Variables:**

- **[COLOR]** — Letter color (red, black, gold, white, etc.)
- **[TEXT]** — Full text content of logo
- **[SURFACE COLOR/TEXTURE]** — Background surface description
- **[FIRST TEXT]** — Primary/top wordmark
- **[SECOND TEXT]** — Secondary/bottom wordmark (if applicable)
- **[FULL TEXT]** — Complete logo text for final state

**Single-Line Logo:**
If logo is single line, combine timing:

```
[0:02-0:05] The [COLOR] "[TEXT]" wordmark extrudes/pops out from flat against the wall into its full 3D raised depth, letters building into place from left to right, with a soft drop shadow forming as it extrudes outward.
```

## Key Elements

**Reveal Style:**

- Extrude/pop-out animation
- Flat to full 3D raised depth
- Letters build into place
- Drop shadows form during extrusion

**Direction:**

- Left to right letter building
- Can customize to: top-to-bottom, center-out, right-to-left

**Shadow Detail:**

- "Soft drop shadow forming as it extrudes outward"
- Critical for dimensional realism
- Shadows anchor letters to surface

**Camera Movement:**

- Slight zoom-in (gentle push-in)
- Minimal and subtle
- Adds cinematic quality without distraction

## Timeline Breakdown

**0:00-0:02 (Empty State):**

- Surface shown without letters
- Camera movement begins
- Establishes location

**0:02-0:04 (Primary Reveal):**

- First/main wordmark extrudes
- Letters build left to right
- Shadows form

**0:04-0:06 (Secondary Reveal):**

- Second wordmark/tagline extrudes
- Same style as primary
- Positioned below or adjacent

**0:06-0:10 (Hold Final Frame):**

- Logo fully extruded and complete
- Matches reference image exactly
- Camera continues gentle zoom
- Professional polish

## Technical Requirements

**Input Image:**

- High resolution (1080p minimum, 4K preferred)
- Sharp focus on letters
- Well-lit, even lighting
- Clear contrast between letters and background
- Straight-on or slight angle shot

**Logo Characteristics:**

- 3D raised letters clearly visible
- Shadows present in reference image
- Good depth definition
- Clean letter edges

## Style Guidelines

**DO:**

- Specify exact colors
- Describe surface texture
- Use "extrudes/pops out" language
- Include "drop shadow forming" detail
- Specify direction (left to right)
- Keep camera movement minimal

**DON'T:**

- Skip the anchor lock phrase
- Forget negative prompt
- Use aggressive camera movement
- Change timeline structure
- Skip shadow detail

## Common Variations

**Color Variations:**

- Gold/brass metallic on dark walls
- Black matte on white surfaces
- White glossy on colored walls
- Chrome/silver on dark backgrounds

**Direction Variations:**

- **Left to Right:** Western reading order, natural
- **Top to Bottom:** Stacked text, vertical compositions
- **Center Out:** Radial reveal, symmetrical logos
- **Right to Left:** Artistic choice, unexpected

**Shadow Variations:**

- "Soft drop shadow" — gentle, diffused
- "Hard drop shadow" — crisp, defined
- "Shadows deepening" — emphasis on depth
- "Subtle shadows" — minimal depth effect

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Letters morph instead of extruding**

- Solution: Use precise language "extrudes/pops out from flat...into its full 3D raised depth"

**Problem: Shadows missing or incorrect**

- Solution: Explicitly state "with soft drop shadow forming as it extrudes outward"

**Problem: Background changes**

- Solution: Ensure anchor lock phrase present, strengthen negative prompt

**Problem: Camera moves too much**

- Solution: Emphasize "gentle and minimal movement" in camera description
