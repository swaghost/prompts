# The Sign That Builds Itself - Coca-Cola Neon Rooftop

**CLIENT 3A · SIGNAGE & STOREFRONTS / SELF-ASSEMBLY ANIMATION**

## Platform Chain

**GPT Image 2** → **Omni Flash** (chained in Picsart Flow)

## Concept

A rooftop neon sign photographed from the wet street below that animates itself building from nothing — glass tubes grow, bend into script, mount themselves, then fire with gas and glow. Autonomous sign construction in rain with no visible workers.

---

## Workflow

1. Open [Picsart Flow](https://picsart.com/flow) → new canvas
2. Image node → GPT Image 2 → paste IMAGE prompt → generate (reference frame)
3. Attach video node → Omni Flash → image feeds automatically → paste ANIMATION prompt → generate
4. Run each node 2–3×, keep the best

## IMAGE PROMPT (GPT Image 2)

Create a photorealistic night photograph of a large rooftop advertising sign on a black steel lattice frame above a city intersection, shot from the wet street below at an angle looking up past the corner of a stone building, light rain falling, the road below glossy with reflections, deep blue-black sky.

The sign face is a deep red panel. Mounted on it, formed entirely from glass neon tubing, the word "Coca-Cola" in flowing connected white script, one continuous cursive line, the tall capital C of each word sweeping up and trailing a long curling tail beneath the letters that follow, the whole wordmark filling the width of the panel and glowing bright white.

Beneath the wordmark, in white neon capitals on one line: "ICE COLD", and below it in smaller white neon capitals "DELICIOUS AND REFRESHING". Along the lower edge, a narrow band of the smallest white neon capitals reading "SINCE 1886 • SOLD EVERYWHERE".

Every element is a run of real glass tubing standing well proud of the red panel on its own standoff brackets, visible tube bends, visible electrodes at the end of each run, thin dark support wires crossing behind, the glow spilling warm red and white light onto the panel and onto the steel frame around it.

Cinematic night advertising photography, rain streaks lit by the sign, reflected colour on the wet rooftop and on the street far below, all lettering sharp and perfectly legible.

**Constraints:** exact spelling of all four lines, no other text, no other signage, no watermark, no people.

## ANIMATION PROMPT (Omni Flash, video node)

**Duration:** 7 seconds

Single continuous locked-off shot, 7 seconds, no cuts, one camera position for the entire video.

### Absolute Rules

**Camera rule, absolute:** the camera stays exactly where it is in the opening frame and never moves. Same framing, angle, distance and lens from first frame to last. No pans, no pushes, no zooms, no reframing.

**Target rule, absolute:** the final frame must match the reference image exactly — same script, same position on the panel, same size, same letterforms, same glow, all four lines of copy present and identical.

### Animation Sequence

The shot opens on the bare deep red panel on its steel frame against the wet night sky with nothing mounted on it at all, rain falling through the frame. The sign draws itself in glass, everything moving on its own with nothing and no one touching it: a single dark glass tube grows out from the left edge of the panel and bends itself along the path of the script, working left to right, forming the capital C and its trailing tail first, then running through the lowercase letters one after another, the tube extending smoothly ahead while staying fixed behind, the shape building as a continuous dark outline floating proud of the panel on its brackets.

It completes the flowing "Coca-Cola" wordmark as a single unbroken cursive path, then drops below and forms "ICE COLD" in straight capitals, then "DELICIOUS AND REFRESHING" below it, then the smallest line "SINCE 1886 • SOLD EVERYWHERE" along the bottom edge, each line working left to right.

As the last tube segment seats into place, the gas fires — the entire sign ignites at once, all the tubing flooding with bright white light and the glow spilling warm across the red panel, onto the steel frame and down onto the wet street below, rain streaks now lit and visible falling through the frame. The finished sign holds, identical to the reference, glowing steady in the rain for the last second of the video.

### Audio

no cuts, no shot changes, no camera movement, no zoom, no pan, no reframing, no editing, no screens, no displays, no LED panels, no pixels, no music, no soundtrack, no library audio, no additional text, no watermark, no people, no hands, no tools, no ladders.
