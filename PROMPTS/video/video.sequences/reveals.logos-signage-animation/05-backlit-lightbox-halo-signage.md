# Backlit Lightbox Signage — Logo Reveal Animation

**GOOGLE FLOW / OMNI FLASH / VFX OVERLAY REVEAL**

---

## Platform

**Google Flow** (labs.google/fx/tools/flow or flow.google)  
**Model:** Omni Flash  
**Duration:** 10 seconds

---

## Concept

VFX reveal animation for backlit signage with halo lighting effect. Raised lettering flickers on line by line with bright backlighting powering on simultaneously, creating luminous halo glow around letters. Camera locked with gentle zoom-in. Perfect for lightbox signs, halo-lit letters, LED backlighting, illuminated signage, neon-style electric signs.

**Material:** Raised letters with backlighting (halo effect)  
**Reveal Style:** Flicker-on (electrical power-on, not extrusion)  
**Camera:** Slight zoom-in (gentle push-in)  
**Lighting Effect:** Bright white or colored halo glow  
**Example:** "finally, good coffee" black raised lettering with bright white halo backlighting

## Key Elements

**Reveal Style (Different from Extrusion):**

- Flicker-on (electrical power-on effect)
- NOT extrusion or pop-out
- Lighting animation, not dimensional reveal
- Electrical/neon aesthetic

**Backlighting Animation:**

- Powers on simultaneously with letters
- Creates halo glow around edges
- Bright luminous effect
- Steady glow after initial flicker

**Flicker Detail:**

- Each line flickers once or twice
- Brief unstable moment before steady state
- Natural electrical startup behavior
- Not continuous flickering (settles quickly)

**Halo Glow:**

- White backlighting most common
- Glow surrounds letter edges
- Luminous halo effect
- Creates depth through light, not dimension

## Timeline Breakdown

**0:00-0:02 (Empty State):**

- Wall panel with no lighting
- Sign location visible but dark/empty
- Camera begins gentle zoom

**0:02-0:06 (Flicker-On Sequence):**

- Letters flicker on line by line
- Top to bottom progression
- Backlighting powers on simultaneously
- Each line: flicker once or twice → steady glow

- Describe halo glow color

- ✓ Halo glow

**NOT This Type (3D Lettering):**

## Pro Tips

**Electrical Authenticity:**
Emphasize electrical startup:

- "Like a neon sign powering on for the first time"
- "Electrical startup sequence"
- "LED backlighting coming online"

**Flicker Control:**
Balance flicker for natural feel:

- Too much: "flickering repeatedly" (feels broken)
- Too little: "instantly on" (loses electrical character)
- Just right: "flickering once or twice before settling"

**Glow Quality:**
Describe glow characteristics:

- "Soft halo glow" — diffused, gentle
- "Bright halo glow" — intense, prominent
- "Even halo glow" — consistent around all letters

**Line-by-Line Pacing:**
For multi-line signs, specify:

- "Top line first, then middle, then bottom"
- "Line by line from top to bottom"
- "Each phrase in sequence"

**Indoor vs. Outdoor:**
Context affects appearance:

- Indoor: "Interior cafe sign"
- Outdoor: "Exterior storefront signage"
- Affects ambient lighting interpretation

## Multi-Line Sequencing

**For 3-Line Signs:**

```
[0:02-0:03] Top line flickers on, settles to steady glow
[0:03-0:04] Middle line flickers on, settles to steady glow
[0:04-0:05] Bottom line flickers on, settles to steady glow
[0:05-0:06] Brief moment with all lines fully lit
[0:06-0:10] Steady glow hold, all lines illuminated
```

**For 4+ Line Signs:**
Tighten timing:

```
[0:02-0:06] All lines flicker on sequentially top to bottom, each line flickering briefly before settling, completing the full sign illumination.
```

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Letters extrude instead of flicker**

- Solution: Remove extrusion language, use only "flickers on" and "backlighting powering on"

**Problem: Continuous flickering (doesn't settle)**

- Solution: Add "before settling into a steady glow" — emphasizes stable final state

**Problem: Halo glow missing or too subtle**

- Solution: Emphasize "bright [color] backlighting" and "halo glow around letters"

**Problem: All lines light simultaneously instead of sequentially**

- Solution: Explicitly state "line by line from top to bottom"
