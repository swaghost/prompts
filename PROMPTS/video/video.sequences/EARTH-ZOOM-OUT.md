# Locked-Location Earth Zoom-Out

## Classification

**Product Category:** location-environment

**Reveal Effect:** earth-zoom-out

**Reveal Mechanism:** continuous camera pullback from the uploaded reference location through local, regional, national, continental, planetary, and orbital scale

**Sequence Type:** reference-match moving shot

**Filename:** `seq.location-environment.earth-zoom-out.md`

## Description of Resulting Video or Video Sequence

A single continuous ultra-realistic Earth zoom-out shot that begins from the exact uploaded image location and pulls upward into outer space. The starting location remains locked to the center throughout the entire movement, expanding from local detail to Indonesia, Southeast Asia, Earth, and a final orbital planet-scale view.

## Usage

Perfect for global context reveals, documentary openings, travel videos, geography explainers, destination intros, event-location reveals, real estate area context, and cinematic social media sequences that need a dramatic scale shift from one real place to the planet.

Ideal for transforming a single location reference image into a seamless Earth zoom-out, emphasizing place, scale, interconnectedness, or the relationship between a local scene and its wider geographic context.

## Engines/Models

Use with image-to-video or video generation models that support reference-image preservation, continuous zoom movement, location locking, realistic geographic expansion, satellite-style detail, and planet-scale camera transitions. The uploaded image is the source of truth for the opening frame and fixed geographic anchor.

## Prerequisites / Dependencies

- Dependency #1 - Uploaded image of the exact starting location.
- Dependency #2 - The starting image should contain enough visible environmental detail to lock land, buildings, roads, vegetation, terrain, colors, lighting, atmosphere, and spatial arrangement.
- Dependency #3 - Geographic context should correspond to Indonesia if the Indonesia-centered version of this prompt is used.

## Video Prompt

Use the uploaded image as the sole visual reference and exact starting frame.

Create a single continuous, ultra-realistic Earth zoom-out shot beginning from the exact location shown in the uploaded image.

**Duration:** 8-12 seconds. Single uninterrupted zoom-out shot.

**Style:** Photorealistic, cinematic, physically believable scale, seamless camera motion, realistic satellite imagery, high detail, realistic Earth curvature, atmosphere, clouds, sunlight, oceans, and landmass detail.

**Scene Setup/Context:**
The uploaded image is the absolute source of truth for the opening frame. Preserve the exact land, buildings, roads, vegetation, terrain, colors, lighting, atmosphere, and spatial arrangement visible in the image. Do not redesign, regenerate, replace, move, or reinterpret the starting environment. The geographic location represented by the uploaded image is a fixed locked anchor point for the entire shot.

**Timeline/Shot Breakdown:**

0:00-0:02
Start from the exact camera position, perspective, and composition of the uploaded image. Hold the location for a brief moment so the viewer recognizes the opening frame as the uploaded reference. The exact starting location remains perfectly centered.

0:02-0:04
The camera smoothly and rapidly pulls straight upward and backward, expanding from the uploaded location into the surrounding area. Preserve the starting environment as it recedes naturally, with no redesign, no replacement, and no sudden camera repositioning.

0:04-0:06
Continue the same uninterrupted pullback from the surrounding area into the wider Indonesian landscape. Reveal realistic Indonesian terrain, islands, coastlines, roads, vegetation, and geographic proportions while continuously tracking the same starting point at the center of the frame.

0:06-0:08
Expand from Indonesia to Southeast Asia, keeping Indonesia and the exact original geographic point centered. Avoid allowing the camera to drift toward open ocean. Do not invent landmasses, distort coastlines, or alter real geographic proportions.

0:08-0:10
The camera reaches extreme altitude and naturally reveals the curvature of Earth and the atmosphere. Transition from detailed landscape and satellite-scale geography into a realistic orbital view while maintaining the same locked geographic center throughout the movement.

0:10-0:12
Finish with a spectacular planet-scale view of Earth from space, with Indonesia clearly visible and positioned exactly at the center of the frame. Show realistic Earth curvature, atmosphere, clouds, sunlight, oceans, and landmass detail.

**Camera:**
Earth zoom out. Start from the exact camera position, perspective, and composition of the uploaded image. Smoothly and rapidly pull straight upward and backward, continuously expanding the field of view: uploaded location -> surrounding area -> wider Indonesian landscape -> Indonesia -> Southeast Asia -> Earth -> outer space. Everything must happen in one uninterrupted take. No cuts, no transitions, no teleportation, no sudden camera repositioning, no random camera rotation, and no camera shake.

**Lighting:**
Preserve the opening frame's lighting, colors, atmosphere, and environmental mood as the scale expands. At high altitude, transition naturally into realistic sunlight, atmospheric scattering, cloud detail, ocean reflections, and physically believable orbital lighting.

**Visual Effects/Technical Details:**
The exact starting location from the uploaded image must remain perfectly centered throughout the entire zoom-out. As the camera rises higher, continuously track the same geographic point so the viewer never loses the original location. As the scale expands, reveal the real surrounding geography naturally. Preserve realistic Indonesian terrain, islands, coastlines, and geographical proportions. Use a smooth, rapid expanding zoom-out with cinematic acceleration. The movement should feel extremely fluid and continuous, as if a single camera is traveling from ground level all the way into orbit.
