# Seedance 2.5 - Transformer Robot-to-Car Sequence

**Platform:** Seedance 2.5 Video Model  
**Duration:** 24 seconds  
**Style:** Ultra-photorealistic cinematic live-action VFX  
**Creator:** @chalkleyvisuals (Instagram)

---

## Overview

Create a 24-second ultra-photorealistic cinematic live-action video in one continuous uninterrupted shot featuring a humanoid robot falling from the sky, landing with massive impact, mechanically transforming into a black Audi, and then driving away with a human character.

---

## Reference Images/Videos Required

- **@image1** = exact humanoid robot design
- **@image2** = exact final car appearance, placement and composition after transformation
- **@video1** = exact action and camera reference for seconds 10-24

---

## CORE OBJECT RULE

**CRITICAL:** The robot in @image1 and the black Audi in @image2 / @video1 are the same single physical object.

**Before transformation:**

- The robot exists
- The car does NOT exist

**During transformation:**

- The robot physically becomes the car

**After transformation:**

- The car exists
- The robot no longer exists

**Never show:**

- ❌ The black Audi before the transformation
- ❌ The robot and the car together
- ❌ A second car

---

## PLACEMENT RULE

**Critical Positioning:**

The robot must land in the same area where the Audi is parked in @video1 and shown in @image2.

The landing point must feel visually correct relative to the later car position.

**Do NOT:**

- ❌ Let the robot land near the camera in the foreground
- ❌ Let it land far away from the future car position

**Must:**

- ✅ Robot must land at or extremely close to the exact future Audi footprint
- ✅ Later transformation and continuity must feel natural

---

## DETAILED TIMELINE

### 0-1.5 SECONDS: EXTREMELY FAST DESCENT

**Camera:**

- Begins facing upward toward the sky
- Reacts quickly and tilts downward fast to follow the robot

**Robot:**

- The exact robot from @image1 is already falling downward at extremely high speed
- **Do NOT** make the fall slow, graceful or floaty
- Robot should drop from high overhead to near ground level in roughly one second
- It is a huge multi-ton machine falling feet-first under gravity with enormous downward velocity

**Visual Effects:**

- Realistic motion blur
- Rushing air
- Subtle vibration of mechanical panels

**Prohibitions:**

- ❌ No wings
- ❌ No jets
- ❌ No thrusters
- ❌ No flying

---

### 1.5-2.6 SECONDS: HARD IMPACT AND VISIBLE HUMAN REACTION

**Landing Impact:**

The robot slams down upright on both feet in the exact area where the Audi will later be parked in @video1 and shown in @image2.

**The landing is extremely heavy and impactful.**

**Show:**

- Sharp knee and ankle compression
- Hydraulic suspension movement
- Body and armour vibration
- Strong weight transfer
- Small mechanical rebound
- Brief realistic camera jolt

**Environmental Effects:**

- Impact kicks up a powerful but believable burst of dust and grit around the robot's feet
- Displaced air creates a short pressure wave

**VERY IMPORTANT: HUMAN REACTION**

The man must visibly react to the landing.

His reaction should feel natural and physically believable for a huge impact.

**Show a clear but grounded reaction such as:**

- A noticeable flinch
- A quick turn or jerk of the head toward the robot
- A brief step, stagger or weight shift
- Arms and shoulders reacting instinctively
- Clothing visibly blown by the pressure wave

**Clothing Movement:**
His shirt, jacket or trouser fabric should move from the force of the impact.

Lightweight environmental elements such as dust, leaves or small plants can also react.

**Do NOT:**

- ❌ Make the man ignore the landing
- ❌ Make the reaction comical or exaggerated

**Must:**

- ✅ He must react clearly, but remain believable
- ✅ Still transition naturally into the later action from @video1

**Prohibitions:**

- ❌ No explosion
- ❌ No crater
- ❌ No excessive destruction

---

### 2.6-4 SECONDS: SETTLE AND CAMERA ALIGNMENT

**Robot:**

- Stabilises after impact

**Environment:**

- Dust spreads outward and begins clearing

**Man:**

- Settles naturally after his reaction
- Remains consistent with the later action

**Camera:**

- Finishes tracking downward
- Settles into the exact viewpoint required by @image2 and the beginning of @video1

**Positioning:**

- Robot stands exactly where the future Audi will be parked, or extremely close to that exact parked position
- The Audi must still not be visible anywhere

---

### 4-9.5 SECONDS: FAST MECHANICAL TRANSFORMATION

**Core Transformation:**

The robot mechanically transforms directly in place into the exact black Audi shown in @image2.

**Transformation Requirements:**

- Must be fast, complex and physically articulated
- Multiple parts transform simultaneously (overlapping movement)
- Energetic and visually exciting
- Robot remains in the same physical location throughout
- There is only one object transforming

**DO NOT USE:**

- ❌ Morphing
- ❌ Dissolves
- ❌ Magic
- ❌ Liquid metal
- ❌ Smoke transitions
- ❌ Instant replacement

**SHOW: Interconnected mechanical parts rapidly folding, rotating, sliding and locking:**

**Head & Upper Body:**

- Head retracts
- Chest splits
- Audi grille rotates into position
- Headlights reposition

**Arms & Torso:**

- Shoulders collapse
- Arms and hands fold into body panels
- Torso compresses into vehicle chassis

**Back & Roof:**

- Back unfolds into roof
- Glass forms windscreen and windows

**Lower Body:**

- Hips and legs fold beneath the chassis
- Wheels rotate into their four final positions
- Suspension extends

**Rear Section:**

- Rear panels and bumpers lock into place
- Remaining robot mechanics retract underneath the finished bodywork

---

### 9.5-10 SECONDS: FINAL MATCH

**Transformation Completion:**

The transformation finishes as the exact black Audi shown in @image2.

**Match exactly:**

- ✅ Exact car model
- ✅ Exact black paint
- ✅ Exact wheels
- ✅ Exact orientation
- ✅ Exact position
- ✅ Exact scale
- ✅ Exact perspective
- ✅ Exact ride height
- ✅ Exact relationship to the man and environment

**Final Details:**

- Final panels lock into place
- Suspension settles

**Critical Timing:**

- At 10 seconds, the scene must align seamlessly with the beginning of the action from @video1

---

### 10-24 SECONDS: FOLLOW @VIDEO1 EXACTLY

**Reference Authority:**

From 10 seconds onward, @video1 becomes the absolute reference.

Reproduce the remaining 14 seconds exactly as shown in @video1.

**Match the man's exact:**

- Walking path
- Walking speed
- Step timing
- Body movement
- Arm movement
- Hand movement
- Head movement
- Interaction with the Audi
- Door opening
- Getting into the vehicle
- Door closing

**Match the Audi's exact:**

- Movement timing
- Wheel rotation
- Steering
- Acceleration
- Trajectory
- Speed
- Drive-away direction
