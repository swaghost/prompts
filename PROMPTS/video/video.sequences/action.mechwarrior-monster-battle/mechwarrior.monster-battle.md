# Live-Action Mecha vs. Monster Battle with DeeVid AI

## Overview

This workflow uses DeeVid AI to create a cinematic 30-second live-action mecha battle from a single character reference image.
The key is to first lock the pilot + cockpit design, then use a detailed timeline prompt to control the battle rhythm, camera shots, character reactions, and final attack.

## Step 1: Create Your Mecha Pilot Reference

Open DeeVid AI’s Image Generator:
https://deevid.ai/app/image
Select GPT Image 2.
Generate the main reference image for your video.
For this workflow, the image should clearly establish:

- An adult female pilot
- Clear and recognizable facial features
- Black fitted pilot suit
- Massive futuristic mecha cockpit
- Transparent cockpit canopy
- Both hands holding control sticks
- Heavy industrial mechanical structure
- Photorealistic live-action style
- Cinematic lighting
- Clear view of both the pilot and cockpit
  The most important goal is to establish a strong and consistent visual identity before generating the video.
  Try to make the pilot’s face clearly visible, since the final video will frequently cut between cockpit reactions and exterior mecha combat.

## Step 2: Open the DeeVid AI Video Generator

Go to DeeVid AI’s Video Generator:
https://deevid.ai/app/video
Choose one of the following models:

- Master V4.5 — powered by Seedance 2.5
- Quality V4.7 — powered by Wan 3
  Upload the mecha pilot image created in Step 1 as your main visual reference.
  Use this image as the primary reference for the female pilot and mecha cockpit, keeping the same:
- Facial features
- Face shape
- Hairstyle
- Age and overall identity
- Black pilot suit
- Cockpit design
- Overall cinematic visual style
  The goal is to keep the pilot and cockpit recognizable throughout the entire battle while allowing the video model to expand the single image into a large-scale mecha combat sequence.

## Step 3: Generate the 30-Second Mecha Battle

Paste the following prompt into DeeVid AI.

### Timeline Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the only reference for the female pilot and cockpit. Strictly preserve the same adult woman’s face, hairstyle, age, black pilot suit, identity, and cockpit design.
Generate a 30s, 16:9, 4K, photorealistic live-action cinematic mecha battle. A massive heavy combat mecha, controlled by the woman from its chest cockpit, fights an original gigantic alien monster with distorted limbs, brutal speed, realistic biological texture, and overwhelming scale.
High-budget sci-fi movie look, realistic metal, explosions, smoke, sparks, debris, hydraulic movement, destruction, physical weight and impact. Fast pacing with brief slow-motion impact shots. Frequently cut between cockpit close-ups, pilot facial reactions, exterior combat, low angles and tracking shots. No anime, cartoon or game UI.
#### 0–3s | Strong Hook
Start immediately inside the cockpit. The mecha violently shakes as warning lights flash. A giant alien suddenly lunges forward and slams its claws into the armor beside the cockpit, tilting the entire machine.
Close-up of the pilot being thrown slightly by the impact; her expression instantly changes from shock to focus.
Cut outside: the monster charges through smoke while the mecha slides backward, crushing the ground before stabilizing.
#### 3–8s | First Exchange
The pilot pushes both control sticks forward.
The mecha throws a massive punch into the charging monster, producing sparks, debris and a shockwave.
Continue rapid close combat:
mecha punches the monster’s shoulder → monster claws the chest armor → mecha blocks with its forearm → sparks tear across the armor → mecha delivers a heavy knee strike.
Quickly intercut the pilot operating the controls, her focused face, mechanical hydraulics and exterior combat to show the connection between pilot movement and mecha action.
#### 8–14s | Monster Takes Control
The monster suddenly accelerates and crashes into the mecha, knocking it into battlefield ruins.
Low-angle shot: the monster pins the mecha down and attacks violently while the mecha blocks with both arms.
Brief cockpit close-up: the pilot breathes rapidly but remains focused.
The mecha forces the monster away, delivers a brutal headbutt, rolls into a half-kneeling position and punches the monster in the abdomen. The monster slides away, then immediately charges back on all fours.
Emphasize heavy physical struggle and realistic weight.
#### 14–20s | Heavy Counterattack
The monster leaps and smashes both claws downward. The mecha crosses its arms to block and the ground cracks beneath them.
The mecha grabs one of the monster’s limbs and throws it sideways. The monster rolls, turns and bites toward the mecha’s shoulder. The mecha answers with a heavy punch to its head, but the monster sweeps the mecha backward with its tail or rear limb.
Brief slow motion: the mecha lands a powerful punch on the monster’s head or chest. Shockwave, sparks, dust and biological debris explode through the air, then immediately return to full speed.
#### 20–24s | Berserk Phase
The injured monster becomes enraged. Glowing biological patterns spread across its body as it roars and attacks faster.
It leaps onto the mecha and lands two heavy strikes. One side of the armor is torn open, exposing electrical sparks.
Cut to the pilot. Her hair is slightly messy, her expression changes from tension to determination. She sharply pulls the controls and prepares a finishing attack.
#### 24–27s | Final Charge
The damaged mecha rises from a half-kneeling position.
Its chest core opens and begins charging a massive energy weapon. Electricity, sparks, steam and intense red-orange energy surround the machine.
The monster roars and charges directly toward it.
Brief pilot close-up: she stares forward without retreating.
Cut outside to a heroic shot of the mecha facing the incoming monster.
#### 27–30s | Finisher
The monster charges at full speed with claws extended and jaws open.
At the final moment, the mecha fires an extremely powerful concentrated energy cannon from its chest or arms.
The beam pierces directly through the monster’s torso, erupting from the opposite side in a massive flash, shockwave, smoke and debris.
Brief slow-motion impact.
End with the damaged mecha standing on the devastated battlefield as the monster collapses behind it, surrounded by drifting smoke, fire and sparks.
```

## Final Workflow

1. **DeeVid AI Image Generator** - Create and lock the female pilot and mecha cockpit.
2. **DeeVid AI Video Generator** - Upload the reference image.
3. **WAN 3.0** - Add the 30-second timeline prompt.
4. **Final result** - A cinematic live-action Mecha vs. Alien Monster battle combining cockpit performance, large-scale combat, close-range fighting, and a final energy-cannon attack.
