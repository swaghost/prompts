## Overview

The room starts empty (IMAGE 1), bright cyan neon construction lines appear in mid-air drawing the future design like Apple Vision Pro holograms, then real materials progressively materialize to reveal the finished luxury space (IMAGE 2).

- **IMAGE 1**: Empty room (completely static, serves as the blank canvas)

- **Progressive Construction**: Lines draw wireframes → materials materialize → finished interior
- **IMAGE 2**: Completed luxury interior (final target state)
- **No People**: Pure digital magical transformation
- **Slow Camera Push**: Smooth forward movement throughout
- **Apple Vision Pro Aesthetic**: Futuristic, premium, high-tech visualization

## Holographic Blueprint System

### Blueprint Visual Characteristics

**Color Palette:**

- **Primary**: Bright Cyan (#00D9FF) - Main blueprint lines
- **Highlights**: Electric Blue (#00FFFF) - Brighter accents
- **Shadows**: Deep Blue (#0088AA) - Depth/dimension
- **Glow Core**: White (#FFFFFF) - Center of brightest lines

**Line Properties:**

- **Width**: 2-4 pixels for most lines, 5-6px for major structural lines
- **Style**: Clean vector appearance, perfectly straight or smoothly curved
- **Opacity**: 70-90%, allowing see-through effect
- **Anti-aliasing**: Smooth edges, no pixelation
- **Consistency**: Even thickness throughout (no hand-drawn variation)

**Glow Effect:**

- **Inner glow**: 2-3px white core
- **Outer glow**: 8-12px cyan blur
- **Intensity**: Brighter at intersections and corners
- **Pulsing**: Optional subtle 0.5-1 second pulse cycle (80-100% opacity)

### Blueprint Drawing Animation

**Drawing Sequence (4 seconds total):**

1. **Major Structure (0.5s)** - Room perimeter, wall divisions, ceiling
2. **Cabinet Framework (1.0s)** - Upper and lower cabinet outlines
3. **Island Structure (0.8s)** - Central island base and counter
4. **Shelving Systems (0.7s)** - Floating shelves, storage areas
5. **Lighting Fixtures (0.6s)** - Pendant light outlines
6. **Appliances (0.5s)** - Refrigerator, oven, dishwasher outlines
7. **Details (0.4s)** - Handles, faucets, small elements

**Drawing Style:**

- Lines "draw" smoothly from start point to end point
- Speed: ~500-800 pixels per second
- Corners: Brief pause (0.1s) before direction change
- Connections: Lines connect at corners with perfect joints
- No gaps: All wireframe connections are complete

### Blueprint Spatial Logic

**3D Perspective:**

- Lines follow proper perspective (vanishing points)
- Depth is clearly visible
- Closer lines appear thicker/brighter
- Distant lines appear thinner/dimmer
- Proper foreshortening

**Architectural Accuracy:**

- Cabinets: Proper depth (24" base, 12" upper)
- Island: Realistic size (3-4ft wide, 7-8ft long)
- Heights: Base cabinets 36", uppers 30-42" above counter
- Spacing: Logical gaps and alignments
- Structural logic: Follows real construction rules

### Blueprint to Material Transition

**Transition Methods:**

**Method 1: Progressive Solidification**

- Blueprint line becomes solid edge
- Material "fills" from wireframe
- Line fades as material replaces it
- Takes 0.5-1.0 second per element

**Method 2: Material Growth**

- Material appears at one end of wireframe
- Grows/spreads along blueprint outline
- Blueprint fades as material progresses
- Takes 0.8-1.2 seconds per element

**Method 3: Simultaneous Replace**

- Multiple blueprint sections materialize at once
- Creates cascade effect through room
- Blueprint fades in groups
- Takes 1.0-1.5 seconds per group

**Transition Timing:**

- Blueprint should stay 0.5-1.0 seconds ahead of materialization
- Never have blueprint disappear before material appears
- Smooth crossfade (0.3-0.5s) between states
- Final blueprint traces fade at 95% material completion

## Materialization Sequence

### Material Types & Appearance

**Wood (Cabinets, Shelves):**

- Grain pattern flows in (directional)
- Color deepens from light to final tone
- Surface texture becomes visible
- Gloss/matte finish emerges last
- Takes 0.8-1.2 seconds per cabinet

**Marble (Island Countertop):**

- Base white color appears first
- Veining spreads like liquid ink
- Depth and translucency develop
- Polished shine emerges
- Takes 1.0-1.5 seconds

**Metal (Appliances, Hardware):**

- Reflective surface builds from dull to polished
- Chrome/steel color appears
- Reflections strengthen progressively
- Highlights and speculars emerge last
- Takes 0.5-1.0 seconds per appliance

**Glass (Pendant Lights):**

- Transparency fades in
- Reflections appear
- Thickness/dimension becomes visible
- Light transmission develops
- Takes 0.6-1.0 seconds per fixture

**Tile (Backsplash):**

- Individual tiles appear row by row
- Grout lines define last
- Gloss finish develops
- Takes 0.8-1.2 seconds total (bottom to top)

### Materialization Order Strategy

**Option 1: Foundation First (Recommended)**

- Lower cabinets → Upper cabinets → Island → Details
- Feels natural, like real construction
- Grounded and logical progression

**Option 2: Center Out**

- Island first → Cabinets outward → Details
- Dramatic, focuses on centerpiece
- Works well for hero island designs

**Option 3: Left to Right**

- Progressive sweep across room
- Clean, organized feel
- Works for wider compositions

**Option 4: Cascade**

- Multiple elements simultaneously
- Faster, more dynamic
- Higher complexity

### Progressive Detail Emergence

**Stage 1: Basic Forms (60% Complete)**

- Large shapes and volumes
- Basic material colors
- No fine detail yet
- Low reflectivity

## 30 Pro Tips & Best Practices

### Blueprint Effect Tips

**1. Blueprint Color Matters**  
Bright cyan (#00FFFF) has highest visibility against neutral walls. Avoid dark blues (hard to see).

Specify "floating in mid-air" or "3D wireframe". Flat 2D blueprints aren't impressive.

**4. Drawing Speed Sweet Spot**  
Blueprint should draw over 3-5 seconds. Faster = rushed, slower = boring.

**5. Apple Vision Pro Reference**  
Mentioning "Apple Vision Pro aesthetic" gives AI clear style target for holographic look.
