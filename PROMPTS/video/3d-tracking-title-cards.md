# How to Get 3D Tracking Title Cards

## Overview

Create AR-style 3D tracking text directly on your phone—no VFX software required. Perfect for product showcases, tech demos, and creative content with professional-looking holographic text overlays that track to objects in 3D space.

**Models Used:**
- **Nano Banana Pro** (3D text graphic generation)
- **Kling 3.0 Omni Edit** (Animation and motion tracking)

**Platform:** Higgsfield.ai

**Visual Concept:** 3D holographic text labels anchored to physical objects (like a camera, phone, or product) with specs, features, or callouts that track perfectly in 3D space as you move the object.

---

## Introduction

This is how you can create 3D tracking title cards directly on your phone without any VFX software.

All you need is Higgsfield AI and a simple video of you holding an object.

**The workflow is simple:**
1. Generate the 3D text graphic
2. Animate the appearance
3. Track the graphics onto your video

---

## Step 1 — Film Your Shot

First film a video of yourself holding an object such as a banana, phone, camera, or any product.

Then **screenshot the first frame of the video**. This screenshot will be used to generate the 3D text graphic.

### What to Film

**Objects that work well:**
- **Tech products:** Cameras, phones, laptops, headphones
- **Food/beverages:** Banana, coffee cup, protein bar, energy drink
- **Beauty/fashion:** Perfume bottle, makeup product, sunglasses
- **Sports gear:** Sneaker, water bottle, fitness band
- **Any branded product:** Watch, gaming controller, tool

### Recording Tips

**Camera setup:**
- Stable camera (tripod or stable surface recommended)
- Good lighting (soft, even lighting works best)
- Clean background (not too cluttered)
- Object clearly visible and well-framed

**Hand positioning:**
- Hold object steady but naturally
- Position where text overlays will fit
- Leave space around object for graphics
- Consider object orientation for text placement

**Movement (if tracking):**
- Slow, smooth movements
- Rotate or tilt object gradually
- Keep object in frame throughout
- Avoid quick jerks or shakes

**Duration:**
- Static shot: 3-5 seconds
- Moving shot: 5-10 seconds
- Gives enough footage for effect

## Step 3 — Animate or Track the Graphics

Go to **Higgsfield.ai → Create → Videos → Kling 3.0 Omni Edit**

Upload your full video, the plain screenshot, and the generated image.

**You now have two options depending on the effect you want.**

---

### Option 1 — VFX Animation (Appearance Effect)

Use this when you want the text to smoothly appear and settle into place.

**When to use:**
- Static or minimal camera movement
- Want dramatic "reveal" effect
- Product introduction sequences
- Opening shots of videos

---

#### Exact Prompt (Option 1: VFX Animation)

```
Smoothly transition from the first image to the second image.

The holographic text and UI graphics should animate outward from the surface they are attached to, as if being projected from it.

The graphics should expand out in 3D space and settle into their final locked position.

The overlay must feel physically anchored and properly integrated into the scene.

Maintain accurate perspective, depth, and scale throughout the transition.

All original elements must remain stable with no warping, distortion, or geometry changes.

No camera movement. No background alterations.

Clean AR-style emergence with subtle depth and natural stabilization.
```

---

#### Prompt Breakdown (Option 1)

**Animation behavior:**
- **Smoothly transition** from plain image to text overlay image
- Graphics **animate outward from surface** they're attached to
- **As if being projected** from the object itself
- **Expand out in 3D space** and settle into final position

**Physical integration:**
- Must feel **physically anchored** to object
- Properly integrated into the scene
- Not just screen-overlaid

**Technical requirements:**
- Maintain **accurate perspective, depth, and scale**
- All original elements remain **stable**
- **No warping, distortion, or geometry changes**
- **No camera movement**
- **No background alterations**

**Visual style:**
- Clean AR-style emergence
- Subtle depth
- Natural stabilization

---

### Option 2 — Locked Tracking Effect (Motion Tracking)

Use this when you want the text to track perfectly to a moving object.

**When to use:**
- Object moves during video
- Hand rotates or tilts object
- Camera moves around object
- Dynamic product showcases
- Want professional motion tracking

---

#### Exact Prompt (Option 2: Locked Tracking)

```
Apply precise 3D motion tracking to the existing text and UI graphics.

The text and callout elements must:
Stay perfectly locked to the object
Be anchored to a consistent surface point
Move naturally with the hand and object
Maintain correct 3D perspective and depth
React accurately to rotation and tilt
Preserve proper scale relative to distance from camera

Tracking must feel rigid and physically attached to the object — not screen-space tracked.

No drifting
No sliding
No jitter

Clean, professional AR-style overlay.
```

---

#### Prompt Breakdown (Option 2)

**Primary objective:**
- Apply precise **3D motion tracking** to text and UI graphics

**Tracking requirements:**
- **Stay perfectly locked** to the object
- **Anchored to consistent surface point** (doesn't slide around)
- **Move naturally** with the hand and object
- **Maintain correct 3D perspective and depth**
- **React accurately to rotation and tilt**
- **Preserve proper scale** relative to camera distance

**Critical distinction:**
- Must feel **rigid and physically attached** to object
- **NOT screen-space tracked** (would feel flat and wrong)

**Quality standards:**
- **No drifting** (graphics stay exactly where they should)
- **No sliding** (don't slip around object)
- **No jitter** (smooth, stable tracking)

**Final result:**
- Clean, professional AR-style overlay
- Indistinguishable from real AR app

## Complete Workflow Summary

### Pre-Production
1. Choose your object/product
2. Plan text content (title + 3-5 feature lines)
3. Gather reference images from pack
4. Set up lighting and background

### Production (Recording)
1. Position camera with stable mount
2. Hold object in frame
3. Record 5-10 seconds
4. Keep movement smooth if moving
5. Screenshot first frame

### Generation (Nano Banana Pro)
1. Upload screenshot + reference image
2. Customize template prompt with your text
3. Generate 3D text graphic
4. Review and regenerate if needed

### Animation/Tracking (Kling 3.0 Omni Edit)
1. Upload full video + plain screenshot + generated image
2. Choose Option 1 (animation) or Option 2 (tracking)
3. Use appropriate prompt
4. Generate animated/tracked video
5. Download result

### Post-Production (Optional)
1. Import to CapCut or editor
2. Add sound effects (whoosh, tech sounds)
3. Color grade if needed
4. Add background music
5. Export final video

## Choosing Between Option 1 and Option 2

### Use Option 1 (VFX Animation) When:
- ✅ Object is mostly static
- ✅ Want dramatic reveal/appearance effect
- ✅ Introduction or opening shot
- ✅ Minimal camera movement
- ✅ Hero product shot aesthetic
- ✅ Emphasizing the "tech reveal" moment

### Use Option 2 (Locked Tracking) When:
- ✅ Object moves during video
- ✅ Hand rotates or tilts product
- ✅ Camera moves around object
- ✅ Want realistic AR app feel
- ✅ Dynamic product demonstration
- ✅ Showing product from multiple angles

### Can You Use Both?
Yes! Combine them:
1. Use Option 1 for text to appear
2. Export that result
3. Use Option 2 to track the graphics through movement

## Tips for Best Results

### Filming Tips
- **Stable camera:** Tripod or stable surface crucial for clean tracking
- **Good lighting:** Soft, even lighting prevents harsh shadows
- **Clean background:** Less visual clutter = better AI performance
- **Object visibility:** Keep entire object clearly in frame
- **Smooth movement:** If moving object, do so slowly and smoothly
- **Framing:** Leave space around object for text overlays

### Screenshot Tips
- **First frame is critical:** This is what AI uses to generate text
- **High quality:** Use highest resolution available
- **Clear object:** Object should be sharp and well-lit
- **Good framing:** Entire object visible with space around it

### Text Content Tips
- **Concise:** 2-5 words per line maximum
- **Scannable:** Users should understand at a glance
- **Hierarchy:** Title first, then key specs
- **Relevant:** Choose most compelling features
- **Readable:** Avoid technical jargon unless appropriate

### Generation Tips
- **Reference images are key:** They teach style to AI
- **Be specific:** Clear object name in prompt
- **Exact text:** Specify exactly what text should say
- **Regenerate if needed:** Don't settle for poor first result
- **Check positioning:** Text should feel naturally placed

### Tracking Tips
- **Shorter clips:** 5-10 seconds easier to track than 30+
- **Object in frame:** Must be visible entire duration
- **Smooth movement:** Jerky movement = bad tracking
- **Good contrast:** Object should stand out from background

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Text doesn't appear where expected
**Solution:**
- Ensure object is clearly visible in screenshot
- Try different reference image from pack
- Be more specific in prompt about object type
- Regenerate with emphasis on "anchored to [specific part]"

### Problem: Text is hard to read
**Solution:**
- Use shorter, more concise text
- Reduce number of feature lines
- Ensure reference image shows clear text style
- Regenerate requesting "bold, clear text"

### Problem: Tracking drifts or slides
**Solution:**
- Use shorter video clip (5 seconds vs 10)
- Ensure object stays clearly visible
- Avoid quick movements
- Re-emphasize "no drifting, no sliding" in prompt
- Try regenerating

### Problem: Graphics look flat, not 3D
**Solution:**
- Use proper reference image showing 3D depth
- Emphasize "3D perspective" in generation prompt
- For tracking, stress "rigid 3D tracking, not screen-space"
- Request "maintain correct depth and scale"

### Problem: Animation looks unnatural
**Solution:**
- Use Option 1 prompt exactly as written
- Emphasize "animate outward from surface"
- Request "physically anchored" appearance
- Try regenerating with "subtle, natural animation"

### Problem: Text overlaps object badly
**Solution:**
- In Nano Banana, request text positioned around object
- Specify "do not obscure main product"
- Regenerate with better positioning instructions
- Edit in CapCut to mask if needed
