## CONCEPT

Immersive 12.8-second first-person POV paragliding flight through vast prehistoric canyon filled with massive dinosaurs. **Strict continuous POV throughout** — never breaks to third-person, maintains uninterrupted first-person perspective from paraglider pilot's eyes. **Ultra-wide 14mm action lens** with locked forward flight captures hands gripping worn paraglider toggles, dark flying boots kicking in air, and prehistoric predators crossing flight path. Journey progresses through five distinct moments: (1) canyon lip launch with hard drop toward jungle canopy, (2) diving between treetop crowns past towering long-neck herbivore's swaying neck, (3) threading narrow river corridor as pterosaur flock erupts across path, (4) blasting into muddy clearing with collapsing canopy and dragging boot soles, (5) ground-level stumble with charging Spinosaurus bursting from river mist with jaws wide until teeth fill frame. **Live-action feature film realism** with crisp action-camera sharpness, ancient atmospheric haze, naturalistic film print emulation color. Emotional arc builds from reckless speed into unstoppable hunted panic. Prehistoric canyon setting: drops from open sky into fern-choked treetops, fast river corridor, muddy predator clearing under harsh daylight haze. **Uninterrupted plunge maintained throughout** — no camera breaks, no resets, continuous locked POV. Sound design: violent wind rush, harness snaps, deep animal bellows, pterosaur wingbeats, river thunder, fabric strain, mud scraping, ground-shaking footfalls, guttural roars, snapping jaws.
