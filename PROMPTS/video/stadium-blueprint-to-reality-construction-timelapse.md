# Stadium Blueprint to Reality: Construction Timelapse

**Format:** 6 seconds, overhead locked camera, single continuous shot  
**Type:** Cinematic construction timelapse transformation  
**Subject:** Stadium evolving from architectural blueprint to finished photorealistic structure  
**Style:** Blueprint-to-reality reveal with smooth construction progression

---

## CONCEPT

6-second cinematic construction timelapse documenting complete transformation of stadium from flat architectural blueprint to fully realized photorealistic structure. Sequence begins with exact stadium blueprint from reference image (top-down architectural drawing on dark blue drafting grid with white technical linework, labeled gates/seating/tunnels/VIP areas/field markings), progresses through glowing animation, rising 3D wireframe formation, rapid construction phases (concrete foundations, steel frameworks, crane assembly, seating installation, pitch development), and concludes with completed photorealistic stadium matching final reference image. **Camera remains perfectly overhead, centered, and locked to stadium layout throughout entire video** — no angle changes, no rotation, only subtle final push-in. Smooth continuous timelapse with no cuts or scene changes, realistic construction progression following actual building logic, cinematic daylight evolution, high-detail construction animation. Audio features construction ambience only (distant machinery, metal assembly sounds, subtle crowd fading in) with no music.
