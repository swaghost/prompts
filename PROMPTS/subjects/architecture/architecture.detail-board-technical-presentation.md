## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL:**
Transform the uploaded interior into a professional architectural detail board, preserving the original design while creating comprehensive technical documentation with multiple views, dimensions, and specifications.

**Subject:**
Complete architectural detail board layout showing: primary exploded axonometric view (center), supporting elevation views, section cuts, dimension annotations, material call-outs, assembly sequence indicators, and construction details. Preserve the design intent and spatial relationships from the original interior photograph.

**Setting/Environment:**
Clean professional presentation board with white or light neutral background. Multi-panel layout with clear hierarchy: large central exploded view surrounded by supporting technical drawings, all coordinated and aligned on professional grid system.

**Visual Characteristics:**

- **Exploded View (Central):** Isometric or axonometric drawing showing all components separated along assembly axis with connection lines, revealing internal construction and assembly sequence
- **Elevation Views:** Front, side, and relevant orthographic projections showing exterior appearance with dimensions
- **Section Cuts:** Cut-away views revealing internal construction, material layers, and structural details
- **Dimensions:** Precise measurements with extension lines, dimension lines, and clear numerical annotations
- **Material Specifications:** Call-out boxes identifying materials: wood species, metal types, glass specifications, finishes, hardware
- **Construction Details:** Close-up details of critical connections, joints, fasteners, and assembly methods
- **Annotation Style:** Clean sans-serif typography, leader lines, numbered call-outs, professional technical drawing conventions

**Technical Drawing Elements:**

- Clean thin line work for object outlines
- Thicker profile lines for cut edges
- Dashed lines for hidden elements
- Center lines and construction lines (light gray)
- Dimension strings with arrows or ticks
- Material hatching patterns (optional, subtle)
- Assembly sequence numbers
- North arrow and scale notation

**Layout Structure:**
Professional board composition with primary exploded view occupying central 50-60% of space, supporting elevations and sections arranged in logical grid around perimeter, detail call-outs positioned clearly, title block in corner with project information.

**Typography Treatment:**
Clean professional architectural font (sans-serif recommended), varied text sizes for hierarchy (title, view labels, dimensions, notes), consistent spacing and alignment, professional technical drawing standards.

**Composition:**
Balanced multi-panel layout with clear visual hierarchy, generous white space, aligned elements on grid system, professional margins, coordinated views showing comprehensive documentation.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Contemporary architectural technical drawing aesthetic. Professional construction documentation quality. Clean minimal presentation with focus on clarity and technical accuracy. Similar to high-end architectural detail boards and shop drawings.

**Technical Specifications:**
High-resolution technical illustration, precise dimensional accuracy, professional CAD drawing aesthetic, clean line work, coordinated multi-view projection, architectural drawing standards compliance, professional presentation board quality.

**Color Palette:**
Primarily monochrome: black lines on white background, with subtle color accents for material differentiation (optional: light wood tones, metal grays, glass blues - all muted and professional). Clean minimal color use focused on clarity.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Professional, precise, technical, clear, comprehensive, construction-ready, detail-oriented, architectural excellence, documentation quality.

**Quality Keywords:**
Architectural detail board, exploded view, technical drawing, construction documentation, professional presentation, dimensioned drawings, material specifications, assembly sequence, multi-view projection, high detail, technical precision, clean illustration.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Photorealistic rendering, decorative flourishes, cluttered layout, inconsistent line weights, poor typography, missing dimensions, unclear annotations, perspective distortion in technical views, colorful backgrounds, artistic interpretation, incomplete documentation, messy presentation.
