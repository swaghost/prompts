## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL:**
Transform the uploaded building photograph into a physical architectural presentation model, preserving the exact massing and proportions while applying model-making materials and professional studio presentation.

**Subject:**
Architectural building model constructed from MDF base, foam board walls, paper details, and card stock elements. Preserve exact façade proportions, window placements, roofline, massing, and architectural features from the original building. Model should appear professionally crafted with clean edges and precise construction.

**Setting/Environment:**
Clean studio environment with neutral backdrop (white, light gray, or soft beige). Model placed on MDF or acrylic base platform. Professional photography setup with soft diffused lighting. Minimal distractions, focus entirely on the architectural model.

**Visual Characteristics:**

- Neutral color palette (whites, grays, natural wood tones, beiges)
- MDF base platform (natural wood or painted neutral)
- Foam board for wall surfaces (matte finish)
- Paper or card stock for window details and texture
- Clean cut edges and precise construction
- Subtle material texture visible (foam board grain, paper fibers)
- No color except natural material tones
- Simplified but accurate architectural details
- Professional craftsmanship quality

**Material Treatment:**
Apply realistic foam board texture to wall surfaces, paper grain to detail elements, smooth MDF finish to base, and matte presentation quality throughout. All materials should appear tactile and authentic to architectural model-making practice.

**Composition:**
Centered model with clean background, professional studio setup, appropriate viewing angle showing building's best features, and clear spatial context with base platform visible.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Professional architectural presentation model photography aesthetic. Contemporary architecture school or professional practice quality. Clean, minimal, focused on architectural form and massing.

**Technical Specifications:**
High-resolution studio photography, accurate scale representation, precise material rendering, soft diffused lighting, shallow depth of field, professional color grading, clean presentation.

**Color Palette:**
Neutral tones only: white, off-white, light gray, medium gray, natural MDF wood tone, beige, cream. No bright colors unless minimally present in original architecture as accent details.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Professional, clean, minimal, architectural, precise, contemporary, studio quality, presentation-ready, academic excellence.

**Quality Keywords:**
Architectural model photography, foam board aesthetic, professional presentation, studio lighting, material texture, precise construction, clean minimalism, high detail, neutral palette, physical model realism.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Colorful finishes, realistic building materials (brick, concrete texture), outdoor setting, harsh shadows, distorted proportions, messy construction, bright colors, low quality craftsmanship, cluttered background, incorrect scale, digital rendering look.
