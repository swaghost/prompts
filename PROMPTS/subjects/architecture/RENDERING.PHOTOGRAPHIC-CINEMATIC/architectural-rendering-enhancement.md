## General Enhancement Tips

### Composition Preservation

**Critical phrases:**

- "Preserving the original composition exactly"
- "Maintain exact layout and viewpoint"
- "Preserve building form and design"
- "Keep camera angle and framing identical"

### Quality Levels

**Standard Enhancement:**

- "Photorealistic"
- "4K quality"
- "Professional architectural photography"

**Premium Enhancement:**

- "Ultra-photorealistic"
- "Magazine-quality" or "Architectural Digest level"
- "Professional architecture photography (cite photographer)"
- "Award-winning architectural visualization"

### Lighting Descriptions

**Time of Day:**

- Golden hour (warm, long shadows)
- Midday (bright, short shadows)
- Overcast (soft, even, no harsh shadows)
- Dusk/twilight (blue sky, interior glow)
- Night (dramatic, interior-lit)

**Quality:**

- Soft (diffused, gentle shadows)
- Dramatic (strong directional, high contrast)
- Even (commercial, low contrast)
- Natural (realistic sun position and quality)

### Material Realism

**Always specify:**

- Material types (wood, stone, concrete, metal, glass)
- Finish quality (polished, matte, textured)
- Weathering/age (pristine vs. lived-in)
- Reflection properties
- Texture detail level
