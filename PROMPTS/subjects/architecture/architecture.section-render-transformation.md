## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL:**
Transform the uploaded black-and-white technical section into a fully rendered architectural section, preserving the exact original structure, floor levels, wall positions, and spatial relationships.

**Subject:**
Complete architectural section cut-away showing multiple floor levels, interior spaces, structural elements, roof structure, and foundation. Preserve exact floor heights, wall thicknesses, ceiling heights, window positions, and stair configurations from the original technical drawing.

**Setting/Environment:**
Section cut-away view showing interior spaces furnished with appropriate furniture, exterior context with landscaping and vegetation, sky visible through roof cut, ground plane with natural terrain or urban context, and complete vertical spatial relationships.

**Visual Characteristics:**

- Realistic building materials: concrete, wood, brick, glass, metal
- Interior furniture scaled appropriately in rooms
- Vegetation and trees at ground level and visible through windows
- Natural sky gradient where section cuts through open air
- Material textures visible (wood grain, concrete finish, flooring)
- Soft natural lighting entering through windows
- Interior ambient lighting in rooms
- Realistic shadows on floors and walls
- Section cut edges highlighted or slightly darker
- Clear differentiation between cut elements and visible-beyond elements

**Interior Details:**
Include appropriately scaled furniture in rooms: beds, sofas, tables, kitchen appliances, bathroom fixtures, lighting fixtures, artwork, and human figures (optional) for scale reference.

**Exterior Context:**
Show ground plane with grass, pavement, or landscaping. Include trees, shrubs, or urban context elements visible beside the building. Natural sky gradient where section cuts through open areas.

**Material Treatment:**
Apply realistic textures: hardwood flooring, carpet, tile, concrete walls, painted drywall, exposed brick, glass transparency, metal fixtures, and natural wood finishes. All materials should be architecturally accurate.

**Composition:**
Centered section view with clean margins, professional presentation layout, clear vertical spatial hierarchy, and balanced composition showing complete building context from foundation to roof.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Photorealistic architectural rendering with technical precision. Contemporary visualization style blending technical documentation with realistic material representation. Professional presentation quality for client review and design communication.

**Technical Specifications:**
High-resolution architectural rendering, accurate scale representation, precise dimensional preservation, realistic material rendering, natural lighting simulation, soft ambient shadows, professional presentation quality.

**Color Palette:**
Natural material colors: warm wood tones, neutral concrete gray, white painted walls, natural stone, green vegetation, blue sky gradient, realistic interior colors (varied but harmonious).

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Professional, clear, inviting, technically accurate yet visually appealing, warm natural lighting, lived-in interior spaces, architectural clarity with human context.

**Quality Keywords:**
Architectural section rendering, photorealistic visualization, professional presentation, accurate scale, realistic materials, natural lighting, interior context, vegetation details, technical precision, high detail rendering.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Distorted proportions, inaccurate floor heights, perspective distortion, cluttered composition, unrealistic furniture scale, harsh shadows, poor material quality, low resolution, messy presentation, extra floors or walls not in original, cartoon style, flat colors.
