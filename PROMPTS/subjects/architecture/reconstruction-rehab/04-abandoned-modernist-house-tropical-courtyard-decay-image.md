# Abandoned Modernist House - Tropical Courtyard Decay

## Description of Resulting Image

A photorealistic photograph capturing an abandoned modernist tropical house from an elevated three-quarter view perspective, showcasing dramatic architectural bones beneath advanced decay and nature's relentless reclamation. The composition reveals a distinctive multi-level structure featuring exposed concrete construction with geometric modernist design principles—clean lines now compromised by weathering, horizontal planes interrupted by vegetation, and formerly pristine white surfaces marred by tropical climate assault. The central courtyard area displays an empty rectangular swimming pool surrounded by deteriorating white concrete deck, its distinctive blue interior tiles visible beneath years of accumulated filth and standing water. Lush tropical vegetation dominates the scene—massive trees towering over the structure, dense foliage cascading from upper levels, vines crawling across railings and walls, and ground-level jungle encroachment suggesting years of abandonment. The modernist architecture features characteristic elements: horizontal strip windows now broken, cantilevered concrete slabs showing structural integrity beneath cosmetic decay, open-air courtyard design inviting nature inside, and clean geometric volumes gradually being consumed by organic forms. Harsh tropical sunlight creates strong shadows defining architectural planes while emphasizing the textural contrast between smooth concrete surfaces and chaotic vegetation patterns. The elevated perspective reveals the full extent of property abandonment—roof areas covered in fallen debris, interior spaces visible through broken windows filled with darkness, concrete surfaces stained with water damage and biological growth, yet the fundamental architectural quality remains evident beneath the tropical assault.
