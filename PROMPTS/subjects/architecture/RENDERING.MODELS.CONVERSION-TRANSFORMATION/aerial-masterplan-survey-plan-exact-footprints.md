## Best Practices

**Survey Accuracy:**

- Maintain exact building footprint positions and dimensions
- Preserve all internal road layouts precisely
- Keep parking configurations exactly as surveyed
- Position sports courts at exact surveyed locations
- Respect plot boundaries and setbacks
- Maintain tree line positions and green street locations
- Preserve all surveyed infrastructure elements

**Aesthetic Enhancement (Without Compromising Accuracy):**

- Upgrade materials while maintaining footprints
- Add realistic textures to existing elements
- Enhance landscaping within surveyed areas
- Apply premium finishes to roads and paving
- Use clean, mature trees at surveyed positions
- Create lush appearance within existing green spaces

**Lighting & Atmosphere:**

- Use soft, natural daylight
- Avoid dramatic sun rays or light beams
- Ensure even illumination across site
- Create professional, refined appearance
- Maintain clarity and visibility of all elements

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

- Moving building positions even slightly
- Altering road curves or widths
- Changing parking space configurations
- Relocating sports courts or amenities
- Moving tree positions from survey
- Distorting plot boundaries
- Adding elements not shown on survey
- Using dramatic or stylized lighting
- Compromising survey accuracy for aesthetics
