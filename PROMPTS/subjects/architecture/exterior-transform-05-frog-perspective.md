# Architectural Transform: Frog Perspective - Dramatic Ground-Level

**Style**: Dramatic ground-level wide-angle architectural photography

---

## PROMPT

```
Lower the camera to a dramatic ground-level frog perspective using a wide-angle lens. Place the car or landscaping elements prominently in the foreground while making the architecture rise powerfully behind them. Preserve the exact geometry, façade materials, windows, landscaping, and proportions of the original property. Use realistic lens perspective, strong depth, cinematic composition, natural lighting, and photorealistic architectural photography.
```

---

## KEY ELEMENTS

- Dramatic ground-level frog perspective
- Wide-angle lens
- Car or landscaping elements prominent in foreground
- Architecture rising powerfully behind
- Strong depth
- Cinematic composition
- Natural lighting

---

## PRESERVATION REQUIREMENTS

**Must Remain Exact**:

- Geometry
- Façade materials
- Windows
- Landscaping
- Proportions

---

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

- **Photography Style**: Photorealistic architectural photography
- **Camera Position**: Dramatic ground-level (frog perspective)
- **Lens**: Wide-angle
- **Composition**: Foreground elements prominent, architecture rising behind
- **Perspective**: Realistic lens perspective
- **Depth**: Strong depth
- **Composition**: Cinematic
- **Lighting**: Natural

---

## USAGE NOTES

Use this prompt to create dramatic, imposing architectural photography with strong perspective and depth. The low camera angle creates a powerful, monumental feel while maintaining the property's design integrity. Perfect for hero shots, dramatic portfolio images, and creating visual impact in marketing materials.
