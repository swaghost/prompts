# Architectural Detail Close-Ups: 6 Alternative Camera Angles

## Overview

Create 6 alternative shots of architectural details of the same building while keeping the exact same building design, proportions, placement, weather conditions, time of day, color palette, materials, and all other details unchanged.

**Only need alternative compositions with different camera angles.**

## Shot Format

**1024 × 1538** (Vertical/Portrait Orientation)

## Camera Requirements

All shots should feel as if they were captured on a **RED Komodo X with Zeiss lenses**, using a professional cinematic camera setup.

## Shot Type

**The shots must be close-ups** of architectural details.

---

## Six Alternative Camera Angles

### Shot 1: Extreme Close-Up Detail

- **Focus:** Tight frame on a single architectural element (e.g., corner junction, material transition, texture detail)
- **Angle:** Straight-on or slight angle to emphasize material quality
- **Composition:** Fill the frame with intricate detail, minimal negative space

### Shot 2: Oblique Low Angle

- **Focus:** Looking up at architectural detail from ground level
- **Angle:** 45-degree upward angle capturing underside or overhang details
- **Composition:** Dynamic perspective with strong diagonal lines

### Shot 3: High Angle Detail

- **Focus:** Looking down at architectural element from elevated position
- **Angle:** 30-45 degree downward angle
- **Composition:** Bird's-eye perspective showing detail and shadow play

### Shot 4: Side Profile Close-Up

- **Focus:** Architectural detail captured from direct side profile
- **Angle:** Perpendicular 90-degree side view
- **Composition:** Clean silhouette emphasizing depth and layering

### Shot 5: Corner Detail with Depth

- **Focus:** Corner or edge detail with shallow depth of field
- **Angle:** 45-degree angle showing two surfaces meeting
- **Composition:** Leading lines drawing eye through the architectural junction

### Shot 6: Macro Abstract Detail

- **Focus:** Ultra-tight macro shot of material texture, pattern, or connection detail
- **Angle:** Intimate straight-on or slightly angled view
- **Composition:** Abstract composition where architectural detail becomes almost sculptural

---

## Consistency Requirements

### Must Remain Identical Across All 6 Shots:

- Building design and architecture style
- Proportions and scale
- Exact placement and location
- Weather conditions (clouds, sky, atmosphere)
- Time of day and lighting quality
- Color palette and color grading
- Materials and finishes
- Surface textures
- Surrounding context (if visible)
- Atmospheric conditions
- All other architectural details

### What Changes:

- **Camera angle only**
- **Framing and composition**
- **Focus point on different architectural details**
