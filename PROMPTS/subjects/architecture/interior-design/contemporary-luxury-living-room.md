## Room Elements & Materials

### Architectural Features

**Ceiling Treatment:**

- Smooth flat ceiling or floating planes
- Height: 11-14 feet
- Integrated LED lighting in recessed channels
- Linear slot diffusers for HVAC (concealed)
- Possible coffered sections with modern details
- Backlit coves for ambient lighting
- Matte white or dark painted finish
- Acoustic panels integrated seamlessly

**Wall Systems:**

- Large format porcelain slabs (wall cladding)
- Natural stone: marble, travertine, limestone
- Wood veneer panels in book-matched patterns
- Leather or fabric upholstered wall panels
- Fluted or ribbed wall treatments
- High-gloss lacquer panels
- Metallic accent panels (brass, bronze, steel)
- Floor-to-ceiling applications
- Shadow gaps instead of baseboards

**Flooring:**

- Large format porcelain (48x48 or 36x72 inches)
- Natural stone with book-matched veining
- Wide plank engineered wood (10-12 inch width)
- Colors: white, gray, black, rich browns
- High-gloss or honed matte finishes
- Minimal or invisible grout lines
- Radiant floor heating throughout

**Windows & Doors:**

- Floor-to-ceiling glass walls
- Minimal frame systems (frameless when possible)
- Automated shading systems integrated
- Pivot doors or hidden pocket doors
- Premium hardware in custom finishes
- Acoustic glazing for sound control
- Smart glass options (privacy on demand)

### Lighting Design

**Integrated Lighting:**

- Linear LED systems in ceiling coves
- Recessed trimless downlights
- Wall grazing for texture emphasis
- Toe-kick lighting at base of walls
- Color-tunable systems (2700-5000K)
- Fully dimmable and programmable
- Lighting control system integrated

**Statement Fixtures:**

- Sculptural chandelier or pendant
- Designer piece (Italian, Scandinavian, custom)
- Materials: glass, metal, crystal, organic forms
- Positioned as art piece
- Scale appropriate: 36-60 inches depending on room
- Suspended from ceiling at calculated height

**Accent Lighting:**

- Adjustable track systems (architectural grade)
- Picture lights for artwork
- Linear LED in shelving and built-ins
- Backlit onyx, alabaster, or translucent panels
- Floor uplights for plants or features

**Natural Light:**

- Maximized through large glass expanses
- Automated roller shades or drapery
- Views to landscape or cityscape
- Controlled with building management system
