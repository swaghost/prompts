# SketchUp to Realistic Render

## Description

Convert a SketchUp living room model into a realistic render in a modern interior style. This prompt transforms basic 3D models into photorealistic visualizations with proper materials, lighting, and styling, perfect for client presentations and design validation.

## Usage

Perfect for architects, interior designers, 3D visualization artists, and design students who have created basic SketchUp models and need professional-quality photorealistic renders. Ideal for client presentations, design approvals, marketing materials, and portfolio development without requiring advanced rendering software skills.

## Prerequisites

- SketchUp model file or screenshot
- Desired interior style (modern specified, but adaptable)
- Room function and context
- Preferred color palette
- Lighting conditions (time of day, natural/artificial)
- Level of furnishing detail desired

## Image Prompt

Convert this SketchUp living room model into a photorealistic render with a modern interior style.

**[ATTACH OR DESCRIBE SKETCHUP MODEL]**

**Rendering Requirements:**

**Materials & Textures:**

- Replace basic SketchUp materials with realistic textures
- Flooring: [HARDWOOD/TILE/CARPET with natural grain and variation]
- Walls: [SMOOTH PAINT/TEXTURED PLASTER with subtle imperfections]
- Furniture upholstery: [LINEN/LEATHER/VELVET with realistic fabric texture]
- Wood furniture: [NATURAL GRAIN, appropriate finish - matte/satin]
- Metal elements: [BRUSHED/MATTE finishes on hardware and accents]
- Glass: [REALISTIC REFLECTIONS and transparency]
- Stone/countertops: [NATURAL VEINING if applicable]

**Lighting:**

- Natural light: [SOFT SUNLIGHT from windows, time of day - MORNING/AFTERNOON/EVENING]
- Artificial light: [WARM AMBIENT from ceiling fixtures, table/floor lamps ON]
- Lighting quality: Realistic shadows, bounce light, ambient occlusion
- Overall mood: [BRIGHT AND AIRY / WARM AND COZY / DRAMATIC]
- Color temperature: [WARM 2700-3000K for inviting atmosphere]

**Modern Interior Style:**

- Clean lines and minimal ornamentation
- Neutral color palette with [WHITES, GRAYS, NATURAL WOOD TONES]
- Contemporary furniture silhouettes
- Uncluttered aesthetic
- Quality materials and finishes
- Sophisticated simplicity

**Styling & Details:**

- Add realistic décor: [THROW PILLOWS, ARTWORK, PLANTS, BOOKS, ACCESSORIES]
- Style coffee table and surfaces
- Include architectural details (baseboards, crown molding if present)
- Add window treatments if appropriate
- Natural elements (plants, flowers, organic objects)
- Layer textures for warmth

**Camera & Composition:**

- Eye-level perspective (5-5.5 feet height)
- Balanced composition showing room's best features
- Natural field of view (35-50mm equivalent)
- Avoid extreme wide-angle distortion
- Show depth and spatial relationships

**Realism Factors:**

- Realistic proportions maintained from model
- Natural imperfections (not too perfect)
- Depth of field for photographic quality
- Subtle wear and lived-in quality
- Proper material reflectivity
- Accurate physics of light and shadow

Generate a photorealistic interior rendering that looks like a professional architectural photography image. The final result should be indistinguishable from a real photograph of a beautifully designed space.

## Negative Prompt

Unrealistic CGI appearance, overly perfect and sterile, harsh lighting, no shadows, flat materials, plastic-looking textures, no depth, over-saturated colors, artificial atmosphere, cluttered or chaotic, poor proportions, visible 3D artifacts, low resolution, blurry details

## Output Specifications

- **Quality:** Photorealistic, high resolution
- **Lighting:** Natural and dimensional
- **Materials:** Realistic textures with variation
- **Styling:** Professionally decorated
- **Composition:** Architectural photography quality
- **Atmosphere:** Inviting and livable

## Rendering Enhancement Guidelines

**From SketchUp to Photorealism:**

**Material Upgrades:**

**Before (SketchUp):**

- Flat color fills
- Basic texture mapping
- No material variation
- Simple reflections
- Generic appearance

**After (Photorealistic):**

- Detailed texture maps
- Natural color variation
- Surface imperfections
- Realistic reflectivity
- Specific material properties

**Lighting Transformation:**

**SketchUp Basic:**

- Default ambient light
- Minimal shadows
- Flat appearance
- No atmosphere
- Technical visualization

**Photorealistic Render:**

- Natural light from windows
- Layered artificial lighting
- Soft, realistic shadows
- Volumetric lighting effects
- Atmospheric depth
- Time-of-day specific quality

**Styling Additions:**

**Minimal SketchUp Model:**

- Basic furniture placement
- No accessories
- Empty surfaces
- Sterile appearance
- Placeholder items

**Fully Styled Render:**

- Throw pillows and blankets
- Books and magazines
- Artwork on walls
- Plants and flowers
- Table lamps and fixtures
- Decorative objects
- Window treatments
- Rugs and textiles
- Personal touches

**Technical Rendering Factors:**

**Global Illumination:**

- Indirect light bounces
- Color bleeding between surfaces
- Realistic ambient light
- Soft shadow fill

**Depth & Atmosphere:**

- Subtle depth of field
- Atmospheric perspective
- Volumetric effects (dust particles in sunlight)
- Lens effects (subtle vignette, chromatic aberration)

**Material Properties:**

- Bump and normal maps for texture depth
- Specular highlights appropriate to material
- Fresnel reflections (angle-dependent)
- Subsurface scattering for fabrics
- Anisotropic reflections for wood grain

**Post-Processing:**

- Color grading for mood
- Exposure and contrast balance
- Subtle lens effects
- Sharpening for clarity
- Noise reduction if needed

## Camera Angles to Consider

**Eye-Level Perspective:**

- Most natural and relatable
- Shows room as you'd experience it
- 5-5.5 feet camera height
- Best for client presentations

**Wide Shot:**

- Shows entire room layout
- Captures spatial relationships
- Demonstrates flow and proportion
- Overview understanding

**Vignette/Detail Shots:**

- Close-up of styled coffee table
- Seating area detail
- Fireplace or focal point
- Captures texture and material quality
- Supports main render

**Multiple Angles:**

- Provide 2-3 different views
- Show different aspects of design
- Corner perspectives
- View toward focal wall
