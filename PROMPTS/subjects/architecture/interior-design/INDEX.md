# Interior Design Style Prompts - Index

A comprehensive collection of professional interior design prompts for generating full architectural documentation including elevations, plans, and sectional drawings.

## Overview

Each prompt in this collection provides detailed specifications for creating professional architectural drawings and photorealistic renders of complete interior spaces. All prompts follow the format of generating:

- Front, Back, Left, Right elevations
- Plan View (top-down)
- Sections A-A / B-B
- Accurate dimensions and annotations
- Material schedules
- Lighting schedules
- Furniture specifications
- CAD-style professional presentations

## How to Use These Prompts

### For Architectural Drawings:

Each prompt includes a detailed formula for generating professional architectural documentation. Use the provided specifications to create:

1. **Elevation Views** - Front, Back, Left, Right with dimensions
2. **Plan View** - Top-down layout with furniture placement
3. **Sections** - A-A and B-B cuts showing vertical details
4. **Schedules** - Materials, finishes, lighting, furniture
5. **Annotations** - CAD-style professional notes

### For Photorealistic Renders:

Each prompt also includes rendering parameters for creating ultra-realistic 8K visualizations with appropriate:

- Lighting conditions
- Material textures
- Camera settings
- Atmospheric qualities
- Photography style

### Customization:

All prompts are structured to allow easy customization:

- Adjust room dimensions
- Modify color palettes
- Change specific furniture pieces
- Alter material specifications
- Add or remove architectural features

## Technical Specifications

**Drawing Standards:**

- Scale: 1/4" = 1'-0" for plans and elevations
- Scale: 1/2" = 1'-0" for detailed sections
- All dimensions in feet and inches
- CAD-style professional presentation
- Material callouts and specifications

**Rendering Standards:**

- Resolution: 8K minimum
- Professional interior photography aesthetic
- Appropriate lighting for each style
- Material texture emphasis
- Architectural photography composition

## Style Selection Guide

**Choose Based on:**

### Budget Level:

- **Budget-Friendly:** Scandinavian, Modern Minimalist, Modern Farmhouse
- **Mid-Range:** Transitional, Coastal, Bohemian
- **High-End:** Contemporary Luxury, Classical, Art Deco

### Lifestyle:

- **Family Living:** Modern Farmhouse, Transitional, Coastal
- **Entertaining:** Classical, Art Deco, Transitional
- **Relaxation:** Scandinavian, Japandi, English Country
- **Creative/Artistic:** Bohemian, Mid-Century Modern, Industrial

### Space Size:

- **Small Spaces:** Scandinavian, Modern Minimalist, Japandi
- **Medium Spaces:** Most styles adapt well
- **Large Spaces:** Classical, Contemporary Luxury, Industrial Loft

### Maintenance Level:

- **Low Maintenance:** Modern Minimalist, Contemporary, Scandinavian
- **Moderate:** Most transitional and modern styles
- **High Maintenance:** English Country, Classical, Bohemian
