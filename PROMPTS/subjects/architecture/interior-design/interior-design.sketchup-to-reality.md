# SketchUp to Realistic Interior Photo & Video

## Description

Transform SketchUp reference images into photorealistic interior renderings with ultra HD resolution and cinematic quality. This prompt converts basic 3D layouts into DSLR-quality photos without altering geometry or layout, while dramatically enhancing material realism, lighting, reflections, and atmospheric details. Includes optional video animation prompt for dynamic camera movement and human interaction.

## Usage

Perfect for converting architectural sketches into client presentations, portfolio pieces, or marketing materials. Maintains exact spatial relationships and design intent while adding photorealistic textures, lighting, and lived-in atmosphere with human presence.

## Prerequisites

- SketchUp reference image or basic 3D layout
- Clear understanding of desired materials and finishes
- Optional: Human subject description for populated scenes
- Video generation capability (for animated version)

---

## Image Prompt

Generate this Sketchup reference image into a realistic photo, similar to a DSLR camera shot with ultra HD resolution, without changing the layout, geometry, or material textures from the reference image. Enhance the material textures to achieve a highly realistic appearance. The wall and floor finishes are large-format beige porcelain tiles with a satin texture and thin grout lines. On the right side, include a rectangular mirror with integrated LED lighting along the edge, emitting a soft glow. Below it, place a wooden vanity with natural grain patterns and a white vessel sink. The fixtures, including the faucet and door handle, should be black with a subtle satin sheen. In the background, include a fluted glass door with a thin black frame. On the ceiling, add round recessed spotlights with warm lighting, creating soft halos. Add a woman sitting on the toilet, wearing a white bathrobe and combing her hair. Use advanced PBR materials, realistic reflections on the mirror and glass, 8K resolution, photorealistic quality, cinematic lighting, architectural photography style, sharp focus, and high dynamic range.

## Best Practices

**For Optimal Results:**

1. **Preserve Layout Integrity**
   - Never alter geometry or spatial layout from reference
   - Maintain exact proportions and scale
   - Keep furniture and fixture positions identical
   - Respect original design intent

2. **Enhance Material Realism**
   - Specify exact finish types (satin, matte, glossy)
   - Include texture details (grain, pattern, sheen)
   - Describe grout or joinery details
   - Note reflective vs diffuse surfaces

3. **Lighting Strategy**
   - Specify light source types and positions
   - Describe light color temperature
   - Note special effects (halos, soft glow, edge lighting)
   - Balance ambient, task, and accent lighting

4. **Human Presence**
   - Add people for scale and livability
   - Keep actions natural and relevant to space
   - Use appropriate attire for context
   - Position subjects to enhance composition

5. **Technical Excellence**
   - Request 8K resolution for maximum detail
   - Specify PBR materials for accuracy
   - Include cinematic lighting approach
   - Use architectural photography framing
   - Maintain sharp focus throughout

**Video-Specific Tips:**

- Keep camera movements slow and smooth
- Match movement speed to mood (contemplative, energetic)
- Coordinate camera and subject motion
- End with a clear focal point or moment
- Keep duration short (5-15 seconds optimal)
- Ensure consistent lighting throughout movement
- Consider natural entry/exit points for looping

## Workflow Integration

**Standard Process:**

1. **Create SketchUp layout** with accurate dimensions and basic materials
2. **Generate static photorealistic render** using image prompt with specific material callouts
3. **Review composition and lighting** - iterate if needed
4. **Add video animation** using video prompt for dynamic presentation
5. **Deliver both formats** to client (static hero image + animated walkthrough)

**Iterative Refinement:**

- Start with basic material specifications
- Generate initial render to check layout preservation
- Refine material descriptions for specific finishes
- Adjust lighting descriptions for desired mood
- Add or modify human elements for context
- Create video version once static image is approved
