# Modern TV Unit Design

## Description

Design a modern TV unit for a living room wall with storage, display shelves and materials that suit a contemporary aesthetic. This prompt creates custom media center solutions that combine functionality, storage, and visual appeal while serving as a focal point in the space.

## Usage

Perfect for interior designers, furniture designers, custom cabinet makers, and homeowners planning media rooms or living room renovations. Ideal for visualizing built-in units, floating consoles, entertainment walls, and integrated storage solutions before construction or purchase.

## Prerequisites

- Wall dimensions (width × height)
- TV size and mounting preference (wall-mount or stand)
- Storage needs (AV equipment, media, decorative items)
- Room style and existing furniture
- Cable management requirements
- Budget range

## Negative Prompt

Outdated style, bulky proportions, visible cable mess, cheap materials, poorly balanced composition, insufficient storage, awkward dimensions, unrealistic rendering, cluttered appearance, poor lighting, mismatched with room style

## Design Considerations

**Sizing Guidelines:**

**TV to Wall Unit Proportions:**

- Unit width: 80-120% of TV width minimum
- Or extend to full wall width for integrated look
- Leave 6-12" breathing room around TV on floating units
- Full-wall units create dramatic focal point

**Vertical Balance:**

- Low console: 18-24" height, creates horizontal emphasis
- Mid-height: 24-48" height, balanced proportions
- Full-height: Floor to ceiling, maximum storage
- Floating units: Mount 6-12" off floor for modern look

**Depth Requirements:**

- AV equipment: 16-20" deep (receivers, game consoles)
- Display shelves: 10-14" deep
- Shallow drawers: 12-14" deep
- Deep storage: 16-18" deep

**Functional Features:**

**Cable Management:**

- Vertical channels behind unit
- Pass-through holes with grommets
- Rear access panels
- Power strip mounting
- HDMI and power routing

**AV Equipment Storage:**

- Ventilated cabinets (vented backs/bottoms)
- Pull-out shelves for accessibility
- Adjustable interior shelving
- IR extender compatibility
- Heat dissipation consideration

**Display Integration:**

- Decorative object display areas
- Plant ledges or niches
- Artwork integration space
- Asymmetric open/closed balance
- Backlit display areas

**Material Combinations:**

**Modern Combinations:**

- Walnut veneer + matte black metal frames
- White lacquer + brass accents
- Concrete + natural oak
- Dark stained wood + glass shelves
- Two-tone (dark base + light top)

**Contemporary Finishes:**

- Matte lacquer (fingerprint-resistant)
- Wood veneer with clear coat
- Textured laminate
- Metal powder-coat
- Stone or concrete composite

**Hardware Options:**

- Integrated linear pulls (recessed)
- Push-to-open (handleless)
- Slim metal pulls (brushed brass, matte black)
- Leather pulls for warmth
- No hardware on floating panels

## Style Variations

**Minimalist Modern:**

- Clean horizontal lines
- Handleless cabinets
- Monochromatic palette
- Hidden storage dominant
- Floating design preferred

**Scandinavian:**

- Light wood tones (birch, ash, oak)
- Simple lines, functional
- Mix open and closed storage
- Minimal ornamentation
- Legs or floating

**Industrial Modern:**

- Metal frames and accents
- Reclaimed or dark wood
- Open shelving prominent
- Visible hardware
- Mix of materials

**Contemporary Luxury:**

- High-gloss lacquer finishes
- Integrated LED lighting
- Premium materials (stone tops, wood veneers)
- Sophisticated hardware
- Built-in soundbar integration

**Mid-Century Modern:**

- Tapered legs or platform base
- Walnut or teak veneer
- Sliding doors or tambour
- Asymmetric arrangement
- Vintage-inspired pulls

## Technical Details

**Mounting & Installation:**

- Wall anchors for floating units (find studs)
- Leveling system for floor units
- Adjustable feet for uneven floors
- Wall-mounted TV bracket rated for weight
- Professional installation recommended for built-ins

**Lighting Integration:**

- LED strip under floating units (floor wash)
- Interior cabinet LED strips
- Backlit display niches
- Soft white 3000K recommended
- Dimmable controls
