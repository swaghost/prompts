# Client Consultation Assistant

## Description

A comprehensive client questionnaire generator for new interior design projects. This prompt creates a complete intake form covering lifestyle, budget, design preferences, family needs, storage requirements, timeline, and project expectations. Essential for understanding clients and gathering all necessary information before beginning design work.

## Usage

Use this prompt to generate a professional client questionnaire that you can send to new clients before your first consultation. The form ensures you gather all essential information to create tailored design solutions that meet client needs and expectations.

## Questionnaire Sections

A complete client questionnaire includes:

- **About You**: Contact info, household members
- **Lifestyle**: Daily routines, entertaining, work from home
- **Budget**: Total investment, flexibility, priorities
- **Design Preferences**: Styles, colours, inspiration references
- **Family Needs**: Children, pets, accessibility requirements
- **Storage Requirements**: Current pain points, organizational needs
- **Timeline**: Project start, completion goals, flexibility
- **Project Expectations**: Communication preferences, decision-making process

## Output Format

**SECTION 1: ABOUT YOU**

- Name:
- Email:

**SECTION 2: LIFESTYLE**

**SECTION 3: BUDGET**

- What is your total budget for this project?
- Is this budget flexible?

**SECTION 4: DESIGN PREFERENCES**

- Describe your ideal style in 3 words:

- Colours you dislike:

**SECTION 5: FAMILY NEEDS**

- Do you have children? Ages?
- Do you have pets? Types?
- Any accessibility requirements?

- Must-have functional requirements?

**SECTION 6: STORAGE REQUIREMENTS**

- What are your biggest storage challenges?
- What items need dedicated storage? (Shoes / Books / Toys / Coats / etc.)
- Do you prefer hidden storage or display?

**SECTION 7: TIMELINE**

- When would you like to start?
- Ideal completion date:

**SECTION 8: PROJECT EXPECTATIONS**

- What prompted this project?

**SECTION 9: EXISTING ELEMENTS**

- What furniture/items do you want to keep?
- What will you replace?
- Any sentimental pieces to work around?

**SECTION 10: ADDITIONAL NOTES**

- Anything else we should know?

## Question Types

**Open-Ended Questions:**

- "Describe your ideal morning routine in this space"
- "What frustrates you most about your current layout?"
- "If budget were unlimited, what would you add?"

**Multiple Choice:**

- Formality level: Casual / Semi-formal / Formal
- Pattern preference: Lots / Minimal / None
- Furniture style: New/matching / Eclectic mix / Vintage/antique

**Scale Questions:**

- Rate clutter tolerance: 1 (minimalist) to 5 (maximalist)
- Colour boldness: 1 (neutrals only) to 5 (vibrant colours)
- Trend vs timeless: 1 (classic forever) to 5 (cutting-edge trends)

## Best Practices

- Keep language conversational and friendly
- Explain why you're asking each question
- Make it easy to fill out (checkboxes, dropdowns when possible)
- Request inspiration images for visual reference
- Include space for "anything else" at the end
- Specify expected completion time (e.g., "15-20 minutes")
- Offer to discuss answers in follow-up call

## Use Cases

- Pre-consultation client intake
- Project scope definition
- Budget and timeline alignment
- Design direction setting
- Identifying potential challenges early
- Setting client expectations
- Creating personalized proposals
- Documenting client preferences for reference throughout project

## Delivery Formats

Send questionnaire as:

- **Google Form**: Easy to fill, automatic organization
- **PDF**: Professional, printable, email-friendly
- **Email**: Simple copy-paste into reply
- **Typeform**: Interactive, engaging interface
- **Word Doc**: Editable, flexible

## Follow-Up Strategy

After receiving responses:

1. Review thoroughly before consultation
2. Note any red flags or clarifications needed
3. Prepare mood boards based on preferences
4. Draft preliminary scope and budget
5. Schedule in-depth consultation call
6. Reference questionnaire throughout project
