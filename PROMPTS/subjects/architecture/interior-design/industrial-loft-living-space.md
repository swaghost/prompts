## Room Elements & Materials

### Architectural Features

**Ceiling Treatment:**

- Exposed structure: steel I-beams or wood joists
- Ceiling height: 12-16 feet (loft standard)
- Exposed ductwork, HVAC, and plumbing
- Painted black, dark gray, or left raw
- Concrete deck or wood plank ceiling visible
- Industrial pendant lights suspended on chains or pipes
- Optional mezzanine or loft level with metal railing

**Wall Systems:**

- Exposed brick (red clay or painted white)
- Original mortar joints visible
- Concrete block or poured concrete walls
- Large steel-framed factory windows
- Combination: brick + white painted drywall
- Accent walls in weathered wood planks
- Metal corrugated panels
- Chalkboard paint sections

**Flooring:**

- Polished or sealed concrete (most common)
- Gray with slight texture and variation
- Possible acid stain or dye coloring
- Expansion joints visible
- Alternatively: wide plank reclaimed wood
- Distressed hardwood with saw marks
- Dark to medium brown stain
- OR: sealed painted concrete in charcoal

**Windows:**

- Oversized steel-frame factory windows
- Black metal frames with divided lites
- Grid pattern (6-12 panes per window)
- Arched tops possible
- Operable casement or fixed
- Minimal to no window treatments
- Height: floor to near ceiling

### Lighting Design

**Industrial Pendants:**

- Metal cage or dome shades
- Factory-style barn lights
- Edison bulbs visible (60-100W equivalent LED)
- Finishes: matte black, brushed steel, aged brass
- Suspended on chains, rods, or cloth cord
- Varied heights: 24-48 inches above surfaces
- Clustered over dining or kitchen areas

**Track Lighting:**

- Black or raw metal track systems
- Adjustable spotlight heads
- Mounted on exposed beams or ceiling
- Directional accent lighting for artwork or features

**Floor Lamps:**

- Tripod legs in wood or metal
- Industrial cage shades
- Adjustable arm designs
- Height: 60-72 inches
- Positioned beside seating

**Edison Bulb Fixtures:**

- Bare bulb chandeliers
- Multiple bulbs on black cords
- Vintage-style filament bulbs
- Warm 2200-2700K color temperature
- Statement piece over dining or coffee table

**Task Lighting:**

- Clamp-on desk lamps
- Architect's adjustable arm lamps
- Metal construction in black or silver
- Positioned on shelving or side tables

### Furniture Layout & Details

**Seating:**

- Leather sofa in brown, cognac, or black
- Distressed or aged appearance
- Nailhead trim details
- Low back or Chesterfield style
- Length: 84-96 inches
- Deep seat: 40-44 inches
- Exposed wood or metal legs

**Accent Chairs:**

- Vintage industrial metal chairs
- Leather sling chairs
- Welded steel frames
- Distressed leather or canvas
- Mix-and-match styles acceptable
- Positioned at angles

**Coffee Table:**

- Reclaimed wood top (thick planks 2-4")
- Metal base: steel I-beam, pipe legs, or iron frame
- Distressed finish with saw marks
- Size: 48-60 inches length x 24-30 width
- Industrial casters optional
- Lower shelf for storage

**Dining Table (if applicable):**

- Long plank wood top
- Metal trestle or pipe leg base
- Seats 6-8 people
- Length: 72-96 inches
- Width: 36-42 inches
- Mix of seating: metal chairs, bench, vintage stools

**Media Console/Storage:**

- Reclaimed wood with metal frame
- Industrial cart or locker-style cabinet
- Open shelving with pipe brackets
- Length: 60-72 inches
- Wheels or casters
- Vintage factory cart aesthetic

**Shelving:**

- Pipe and wood plank shelving
- Wall-mounted or floor-standing
- Black iron pipe frame
- Thick wood planks (12-16 inch depth)
- 4-5 shelf heights
- Display books, plants, industrial objects

**Bench/Ottoman:**

- Upholstered in leather or canvas
- Metal frame or hairpin legs
- Tufted top
- Size: 48 inches length
- Positioned at coffee table or entryway

### Materials & Finishes

**Primary Materials:**

- Reclaimed wood: weathered barn wood, pallet wood
- Steel and iron: raw, powder-coated black, rusted patina
- Concrete: smooth troweled or exposed aggregate
- Brick: red clay or painted white/gray
- Glass: clear, frosted, or industrial wire glass

**Upholstery:**

- Full-grain leather (brown, cognac, black)
- Distressed and aged appearance
- Canvas or duck cloth in gray or tan
- Denim or heavy cotton
- Minimal patterns, solid colors preferred

**Metal Finishes:**

- Matte black powder coat
- Raw steel with clear coat
- Aged brass or bronze
- Galvanized steel
- Copper (for accents)
- Intentional patina and wear

**Window Treatments:**

- Often none to maximize light
- If used: simple roller shades in gray or black
- Canvas or burlap curtains
- Mounted on iron pipe rods
- OR exterior roll-down security shutters (authentic loft detail)

**Area Rug:**

- Distressed vintage Persian or Oriental
- Faded colors (gray, rust, navy, tan)
- OR cowhide in brown or black
- OR jute/sisal natural fiber
- OR no rug, bare concrete floor
- Size: 8x10 or 9x12 feet if used

### Architectural Drawing Requirements

**Elevation Views (Front, Back, Left, Right):**

- Exposed beam and joist locations with dimensions

- Ceiling height measurements
- Exposed ductwork routing and sizes

**Plan View (Top-Down):**

- Overall loft dimensions (often 1200-2000 sq ft open)
- Furniture placement with clearances

- Structural column locations (if applicable)
- Window locations and sizes
- Door locations (often minimal)
- Lighting layout with fixture types
- Electrical outlets (often exposed conduit)
- Mezzanine or loft level outline
- Area rug placement

**Sections A-A / B-B:**

- Ceiling heights (12-16 feet typical)
- Exposed joist depths and spacing
- Mezzanine level height (if applicable)
- Window sill and head heights
- Furniture profile heights
- Suspended lighting drop lengths
- Concrete floor thickness
- Exposed ductwork clearances
- Structural beam sizes

**General Notes:**

- All dimensions in feet and inches
- Material specifications: steel beam sizes, brick type
- Reclaimed wood sources and finishes
- Metal finishes and coating types
- Lighting fixture specifications (manufacturer, style)
- Furniture specifications with dimensions
- Concrete floor finish details (sealer type, sheen)
- Window specifications (steel frame, glass type)
- Exposed mechanical systems to remain visible
- CAD-style professional drafting
- Scale: 1/4" = 1'-0" for plans, 1/2" = 1'-0" for details

- Adaptive reuse aesthetic
- City loft lifestyle
- Warehouse conversion character
- Efficient use of space
