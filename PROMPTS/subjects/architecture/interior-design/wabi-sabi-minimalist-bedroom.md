# Wabi-Sabi Minimalist Bedroom Photography

## Description

Create ultra-realistic editorial-style photography of a wabi-sabi minimalist bedroom featuring textured plaster walls, dark oak flooring, floating wooden bed platform, and soft ivory linen bedding. This prompt delivers warm earthy tones, Japanese minimalism aesthetics, cinematic natural light with moody shadows, raw materials, and hyper-detailed 8K quality shot on Hasselblad H6D with 50mm lens—capturing architectural photography perfection in 4:5 aspect ratio with raw style treatment.

## Photography Overview

**Style:** Wabi-sabi minimalist  
**Room Type:** Bedroom interior  
**Aesthetic:** Japanese minimalism, warm earthy tones  
**Camera:** Hasselblad H6D, 50mm lens  
**Quality:** Ultra realistic, editorial style, hyper detailed, 8K  
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 (vertical portrait orientation)  
**Treatment:** Raw style (--style raw)
