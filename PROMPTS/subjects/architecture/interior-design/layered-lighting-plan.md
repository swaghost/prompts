# Layered Lighting Plan

## Description

Create a layered lighting plan for a living room including ambient, task, accent and decorative lighting with fixture suggestions and placement ideas. This comprehensive approach ensures proper illumination for all activities while creating depth, mood, and visual interest.

## Usage

Perfect for interior designers, lighting designers, electricians, and homeowners planning lighting renovations. Ideal for new construction, remodels, lighting upgrades, and creating sophisticated, functional lighting schemes that enhance both aesthetics and usability.

## Prerequisites

- Room dimensions and ceiling height
- Architectural features to highlight
- Primary room functions and activities
- Natural light sources and quality
- Existing electrical locations (for renovations)
- Style preferences and budget

## Lighting Design Principles

**The Four Layers:**

**Ambient Lighting (Foundation):**

- 20 lumens per square foot for living spaces
- 50-75 lumens per square foot for task-intensive rooms
- Evenly distributed throughout space
- Primary source: recessed, surface-mount, or suspended fixtures
- Should be dimmable for flexibility

**Task Lighting (Functional):**

- 50-75 lumens per square foot for task area
- Positioned to avoid shadows on work surface
- Adjustable and directional when possible
- Examples: reading lamps, under-cabinet, desk lamps
- Higher color rendering index (CRI 90+) for accuracy

**Accent Lighting (Drama):**

- 3:1 ratio to ambient light for highlighting
- Narrow beam angles (15-45 degrees) for focus
- Creates depth and visual interest
- Examples: picture lights, uplights, wall washers
- Low wattage but strategic placement

**Decorative Lighting (Aesthetic):**

- Statement pieces that enhance design style
- May provide little functional light
- Contributes to room's character and ambiance
- Examples: chandeliers, sculptural pendants, artistic lamps
- Selected for visual impact and style

**Room-Specific Guidelines:**

**Living Rooms:**

- Central ambient source (chandelier or recessed grid)
- Table lamps flanking seating areas
- Floor lamp near reading chair
- Picture lights or track for artwork
- LED strips for millwork or media center

**Bedrooms:**

- Soft ambient overhead (flush-mount or pendant)
- Bedside lamps or wall sconces (each side)
- Closet lighting (LED strip or fixture)
- Accent for artwork or architectural features
- All on dimmers for mood control

**Kitchens:**

- Recessed grid for general ambient
- Under-cabinet LED for task on counters
- Pendant over island for task + decorative
- Interior cabinet lighting for display
- 50-75 lumens per square foot overall

**Bathrooms:**

- Central ambient overhead
- Vanity side lights or vertical sconces (avoid top-only)
- Shower/tub lighting (wet-rated)
- Night light for safety
- High CRI (90+) for accurate color

**Dining Rooms:**

- Statement chandelier or pendant as focal point
- 30-36 inches above table surface
- Dimmer essential for ambiance
- Accent lights for sideboard or artwork
- Wall sconces for additional ambient layer

## Technical Specifications

**Fixture Heights:**

- Chandelier over table: 30-36" above surface
- Pendant over counter: 30-36" above surface
- Wall sconces: 60-66" to center (flanking mirror: 60-70")
- Recessed spacing: 1× ceiling height apart
- Picture lights: 6-12" above frame top

**Color Temperature Guide:**

- 2700K: Warm, cozy, traditional spaces
- 3000K: Warm white, versatile for most rooms
- 3500K: Neutral, contemporary spaces
- 4000K: Cool white, task-focused areas
- 5000K+: Daylight, workspaces, garages

**Control Systems:**

- Separate switches for each lighting layer
- Dimmers on all ambient and decorative fixtures
- Smart controls for scenes and scheduling
- Motion sensors for closets/bathrooms
- Timers for security and energy savings
