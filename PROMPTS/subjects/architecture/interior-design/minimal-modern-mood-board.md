# Minimal Modern Mood Board

## Description

Generate a mood board in minimal, modern design style with a neutral color palette. This prompt creates cohesive visual collections that communicate design direction, establish aesthetic guidelines, and inspire interior design projects with clean, contemporary sophistication.

## Usage

Perfect for interior designers presenting concepts to clients, design teams establishing project direction, homeowners planning renovations, and design bloggers creating visual content. Ideal for setting design intent, gaining approval before detailed work, and ensuring all stakeholders share the same aesthetic vision.

## Prerequisites

- Design style direction (minimal modern established)
- Project type (room or full home)
- Specific room functions if applicable
- Color palette preferences within neutral range
- Inspirational references (optional)
- Budget considerations

## Image Prompt

Create a minimal modern mood board with a neutral color palette for a [PROJECT TYPE - e.g., living room, full apartment, bedroom].

**Design Aesthetic:** Minimal modern - clean lines, uncluttered, functional, sophisticated, timeless

**Color Palette:**

- **Primary Neutrals:** [WHITES - warm white, pure white, off-white]
- **Secondary Neutrals:** [GRAYS - light gray, medium gray, charcoal]
- **Accent Neutrals:** [WARM TONES - beige, taupe, sand, warm gray]
- **Grounding Tones:** [BLACK, DEEP CHARCOAL, DARK BROWN]
- **Natural Elements:** [WOOD TONES - light oak, walnut, natural finish]

**Mood Board Elements to Include:**

**Materials & Textures (6-8 samples):**

- Light wood flooring or furniture sample
- Smooth plaster or painted wall texture
- Linen or cotton textile swatch
- Matte metal finish (black or brushed brass)
- Natural stone or concrete
- Glass (clear or frosted)
- Wool or bouclé upholstery

**Furniture Pieces (4-6 images):**

- Clean-lined sofa in neutral fabric
- Minimalist coffee table (wood, marble, or metal)
- Modern accent chair with simple silhouette
- Streamlined shelving or storage unit
- Simple side table or pedestal
- Bed frame with low profile (if bedroom)

**Décor & Styling (4-6 elements):**

- Abstract art or minimalist photography
- Sculptural vase or ceramic object
- Greenery (architectural plants like fiddle leaf fig, monstera)
- Minimal table lamp or floor lamp
- Textured throw or pillow in neutral
- Simple decorative object (book stack, bowl)

**Color Swatches:**

- 5-7 color chips showing the complete palette
- Labeled with specific tones
- Arranged from light to dark

**Typography & Text:**

- "Minimal Modern" title in clean sans-serif font
- Brief descriptive words: "Serene, Uncluttered, Sophisticated, Timeless"
- [PROJECT NAME] if applicable

**Layout:** Grid or collage arrangement with balanced composition, consistent spacing, professional aesthetic, clear visual hierarchy.

## Negative Prompt

Cluttered composition, busy patterns, bright colors, ornate details, mismatched styles, poor image quality, chaotic layout, inconsistent aesthetic, dated elements, overly trendy items, conflicting design directions

## Output Specifications

- **Format:** Professional mood board composition
- **Layout:** Balanced grid or curated collage
- **Image Quality:** High resolution, professional photography
- **Cohesion:** All elements work together harmoniously
- **Clarity:** Clear visual communication of design intent

## Mood Board Design Guidelines

**Minimal Modern Characteristics:**

**Form & Function:**

- Form follows function philosophy
- No unnecessary ornamentation
- Clean, geometric shapes
- Emphasis on negative space
- Quality over quantity
- Intentional simplicity

**Material Palette:**

- Natural materials (wood, stone, linen, cotton)
- Matte finishes preferred over shiny
- Tactile textures over visual patterns
- Mixed materials with restraint
- Sustainable and timeless choices

**Furniture Style:**

- Low profiles and sleek silhouettes
- Exposed legs creating visual lightness
- Neutral upholstery in natural fibers
- Wood tones (light oak, walnut, natural)
- Metal accents (matte black, brass, brushed nickel)
- Modular and flexible pieces

**Color Theory:**

- Monochromatic base with subtle variation
- Layered neutrals creating depth
- Warm and cool neutrals balanced
- 80% neutrals, 20% accent (wood tones, black)
- Minimal use of pure white and black for contrast

**Texture Strategy:**

- Vary textures to prevent monotony
- Smooth + rough (plaster + linen)
- Hard + soft (wood + wool)
- Matte + subtle sheen (paint + silk)
- Natural + manufactured (stone + metal)

**Composition Tips:**

**Layout Options:**

**Grid Layout:**

- Evenly spaced elements in rows/columns
- Clean, organized, professional
- Works well for multiple small images
- Creates sense of order and structure

**Collage Layout:**

- Artfully overlapped images
- More dynamic and creative
- Shows layering and relationships
- Organic yet intentional arrangement

**Asymmetric Layout:**

- Varied image sizes
- Creates visual interest
- Maintains balance through color and weight
- Modern and sophisticated

**Board Structure:**

- Title and project name at top or center
- Color palette along one edge
- Mix of large hero images and smaller details
- Balance of materials, furniture, and décor
- Breathing room between elements
- Cohesive flow when eye moves across board

**Element Selection:**

**Do Include:**

- 2-3 hero images that capture the essence
- Mix of wide shots and detail close-ups
- Variety of scales (room views + objects)
- Tactile material swatches when possible
- Inspirational lifestyle images
- Natural elements and organic materials

**Avoid:**

- Overly busy or cluttered images
- Conflicting design styles
- Low-quality or pixelated photos
- Too many competing focal points
- Dated or trendy elements
- Logos or branding (unless intentional)

## Project Applications

**By Space Type:**

**Living Rooms:**

- Focus on seating, coffee table, lighting
- Emphasize comfort with sophistication
- Include artwork and styling elements
- Show scale with room view

**Bedrooms:**

- Prioritize bedding, textiles, softness
- Calming color emphasis
- Simple furniture silhouettes
- Ambient lighting examples

**Kitchens:**

- Clean cabinetry, minimal hardware
- Stone or solid surface materials
- Integrated appliances aesthetic
- Simple fixture finishes

**Bathrooms:**

- Spa-like serenity
- Natural stone, clean tile
- Floating vanities, simple fixtures
- Soft towels and minimal accessories

**Full Home:**

- Cohesive thread across all spaces
- Slightly varied execution per room
- Consistent color and material palette
- Multiple lifestyle images
