# PROMPT 09: Strategic Renovation

**Purpose:** Renovation Strategist  
**Workflow Position:** Alternative Starting Point (Existing Space Redesign)

---

## Goal

Redesign existing room to improve functionality, storage, lighting, and visual cohesion. Preserve structural walls, openings, and essential utilities. Propose a realistic renovation with buildable solutions, accurate scale, and a clear before-and-after transformation.

---

## Full Prompt

```
Redesign this existing (ROOM) to improve functionality, storage, lighting and visual
cohesion. Preserve structural walls, openings and essential utilities. Propose a
realistic (STYLE) renovation with buildable solutions, accurate scale and a clear
before-and-after transformation.
```

---

## Variables

- **ROOM:** kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living room, dining room, office, laundry room, etc.
- **STYLE:** modern, contemporary, traditional, transitional, Scandinavian, industrial, farmhouse, etc.

---

## Analysis Phase

### Assess Current State:

- Existing layout inefficiencies
- Storage inadequacies
- Lighting problems (insufficient, poorly placed)
- Visual cohesion issues (mixed styles, dated finishes)
- Circulation problems (awkward traffic flow)
- Functional limitations (poor workspace, inadequate counter space)
- Dated elements (old fixtures, worn finishes)
- Structural constraints (cannot be changed)

### Identify Fixed Elements:

- Structural/load-bearing walls
- Window locations and sizes
- Door openings and swing directions
- Plumbing locations (kitchens, bathrooms - expensive to move)
- Electrical panels
- HVAC systems and ductwork
- Building envelope (exterior walls)

---

## Preservation Requirements

### Must Preserve:

- **Structural walls** (cannot be removed without engineering)
- **Load-bearing elements** (columns, beams)
- **Window and door openings** (often structural)
- **Plumbing risers and drains** (very expensive to relocate)
- **Essential utilities** (gas lines, water mains, electric service)
- **Building code requirements** (egress, ventilation, safety)
- **Existing ceiling heights** (if structural)

### Can Modify:

- Non-structural walls (partition walls)
- Finishes and surfaces (paint, tile, flooring)
- Furniture and fixtures
- Lighting systems (add/relocate fixtures)
- Storage solutions (cabinets, built-ins)
- Color and materials
- Decorative elements

---

## Improvement Areas

### Functionality:

- Better space utilization
- Improved traffic flow
- Activity zone optimization
- Ergonomic improvements
- Multi-functional spaces
- Flexible use areas

### Storage:

- Built-in solutions
- Custom cabinetry
- Hidden storage (under stairs, in walls)
- Vertical space utilization
- Organization systems
- Appropriate sizing for actual needs

### Lighting:

- Layered lighting plan (ambient, task, accent)
- Natural light maximization
- Task lighting addition
- Ambient improvements
- Accent highlighting
- Modern fixtures

### Visual Cohesion:

- Unified color palette
- Material consistency
- Style coherence
- Design language throughout
- Proportional balance
- Aesthetic upgrade

---

## Buildable Solutions

### Realistic Construction:

- Standard materials available at building suppliers
- Conventional building methods (framing, drywall, tile)
- Code-compliant solutions
- Reasonable budget considerations
- Practical timelines (weeks/months, not years)
- Accessible products (not rare imports)

### Accurate Scale:

- Real-world dimensions
- Proper clearances (code-compliant)
- Standard product sizes (appliances, fixtures)
- Realistic proportions
- Functional measurements (counters at proper height)
- Achievable details (not fantasy construction)

## Room-Specific Renovation Priorities

### Kitchens:

- **Critical:** Plumbing locations usually stay
- **Focus:** Cabinet layout, storage, countertop space, lighting
- **Common upgrades:** New cabinets, countertops, backsplash, appliances, lighting

### Bathrooms:

- **Critical:** Plumbing is very expensive to move
- **Focus:** Layout within constraints, storage, lighting, ventilation
- **Common upgrades:** Vanity, tile, fixtures, lighting, mirror

### Bedrooms:

- **Critical:** Maintain egress (window) requirements
- **Focus:** Built-in storage, lighting layers, closet organization
- **Common upgrades:** Paint, flooring, lighting, closet system, built-ins

### Living Rooms:

- **Critical:** Few fixed constraints (most flexible)
- **Focus:** Layout, flow, focal points, lighting
- **Common upgrades:** Paint, flooring, built-ins, lighting, fireplace update

### Home Offices:

- **Critical:** Electrical capacity for equipment
- **Focus:** Workspace layout, storage, lighting, acoustics
- **Common upgrades:** Built-in desk, shelving, lighting, soundproofing

## Pro Tips

### Work Within Constraints:

- Moving plumbing = expensive (often 30-50% of budget)
- Removing structural walls requires engineering
- Window/door changes often need permits
- Make the most of what you have

### Prioritize Improvements:

1. Functionality first (does it work better?)
2. Storage second (solves everyday problems)
3. Lighting third (transforms the space)
4. Aesthetics fourth (makes it beautiful)

### Before/After Clarity:

- Same camera angle for both views
- Clearly show what changed and what stayed
- Highlight improvements visually
- Make transformation obvious

### Realistic Expectations:

- Budget-conscious material selections
- Standard product sizes and availability
- Code-compliant solutions
- Achievable within reasonable timeline

### Common Renovation Mistakes to Avoid:

- **Over-customization:** Use standard sizes when possible
- **Trend-chasing:** Choose timeless over trendy
- **Poor lighting planning:** Most common regret
- **Inadequate storage:** Plan for actual needs
- **Ignoring constraints:** Can't always do what you want

---

## Budget Considerations

### Renovation Budget Ranges (General)

**Cosmetic (surface changes only):**

- Paint, fixtures, hardware
- Minimal construction
- 1-3 weeks

**Moderate (some structural changes):**

- New cabinets, counters, flooring
- Updated fixtures and lighting
- Some layout changes
- 4-8 weeks

**Extensive (major renovation):**

- Plumbing/electrical relocation
- Structural changes (with engineering)
- Custom everything
- 8-16+ weeks
