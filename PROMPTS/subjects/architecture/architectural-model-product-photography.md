# Architectural Model Product Photography

## Description

Create hyper-realistic professional product photographs of 1:50 scale architectural models with front elevation views. Models crafted from premium basswood and matte charcoal cardstock, centrally placed on thick white museum-grade plinths atop professional studio tables, captured with editorial architecture photography excellence.

## Overview

This prompt generates ultra-high-quality product photography of architectural scale models that emphasizes craftsmanship, materiality, and professional presentation. The aesthetic combines museum-quality display standards with commercial product photography techniques, creating images suitable for portfolios, publications, and exhibitions.

## Best Practices

### Pre-Photography Preparation

1. **Clean model thoroughly** - remove dust, fingerprints, glue residue
2. **Inspect for defects** - repair any damage or loose components
3. **Position carefully** - ensure perfect alignment on plinth
4. **Level plinth and table** - prevent perspective distortion
5. **Check lighting setup** - test for even illumination and soft shadows

### Camera Setup

1. **Use tripod** for absolute stability and sharpness
2. **Level camera** perfectly horizontal and perpendicular to model
3. **Focus precisely** on mid-façade point, check corners for sharpness
4. **Shoot RAW format** for maximum post-processing flexibility
5. **Bracket exposures** if needed for optimal exposure

### Lighting Refinement

1. **Position soft-box** directly overhead and slightly forward
2. **Check for reflections** on any glossy surfaces (should be none)
3. **Verify shadow softness** - adjust diffusion if too harsh
4. **Maintain color accuracy** - use grey card for white balance
5. **Test exposure** - preserve detail in both basswood and charcoal

### Composition Guidelines

1. **Center model** in frame with balanced negative space
2. **Keep horizon parallel** - no tilting or skewing
3. **Frame to include plinth** fully with slight margin
4. **Maintain context** - show enough table/background for grounding
5. **Avoid cropping critical elements** - entire model visible

### Post-Processing Standards

1. **Minimal adjustment** - preserve authentic materials and colors
2. **Correct white balance** - ensure plinth reads as pure white
3. **Subtle contrast enhancement** - maintain soft lighting character
4. **Sharpen appropriately** - enhance detail without artifacts
5. **Clean background** - remove any dust spots or distractions

## Technical Notes

### Camera Equipment Specifications

**Nikon D850 Characteristics:**

- 45.7 megapixel full-frame sensor
- Exceptional dynamic range (14.8 EV)
- ISO 64-25,600 (expandable to 102,400)
- 7fps continuous shooting
- 4K UHD video capability
- Professional-grade build quality

**85mm Macro Lens Benefits:**

- Working distance: 1-2 meters from model
- Minimal perspective distortion at this distance
- Sharp corner-to-corner performance at f/5.6
- True macro capability for detail shots
- Professional architectural documentation standard

**Alternative Equipment:**

- **Canon EOS R5**: 45MP, comparable quality
- **Sony A7R IV**: 61MP, excellent resolution
- **Hasselblad H6D**: Medium format, ultimate quality
- **Lenses**: 50mm macro, 100mm macro, tilt-shift for perspective control

### Lighting Equipment Guide

**Soft-Box Specifications:**

- Size: 36" x 48" or larger
- Diffusion: Double-diffused fabric
- Strobe Power: 500-1000 watt-seconds
- Position: Centered overhead, 45° angle
- Distance: 1-2 meters above model

**Additional Lighting Options:**

- **Fill Cards**: White foam core for bounce fill
- **Rim Light**: Subtle edge definition from behind
- **Background Light**: Separate control of cove brightness
- **Reflector**: Silver or white to lift shadows if needed

**Light Modifiers:**

- Soft grids to control spill
- Diffusion fabrics for extra softness
- Scrims to reduce intensity without changing quality
- Polarizing filters to eliminate reflections

### Depth of Field Calculations

**f/5.6 at 85mm Lens:**

- Subject distance: ~1.5 meters
- Depth of field: ~400-500mm (sufficient for model depth)
- Circle of confusion: 0.03mm (Nikon D850 standard)
- Front to back sharpness: Entire model façade in focus
- Background blur: Minimal, controlled infinity cove remains soft

**Alternative Aperture Choices:**

- **f/8**: Increased depth, slightly reduced sharpness
- **f/11**: Maximum depth, potential diffraction
- **f/4**: Shallow depth, selective focus possible
- **f/5.6**: Optimal balance (recommended)

### Color Accuracy and White Balance

**Material Color Targets:**

- Basswood: 5000-5500K color temperature for natural appearance
- White Plinth: Pure white (255, 255, 255 RGB)
- Charcoal Cardstock: Neutral grey-black (no color cast)
- Background: Neutral light grey (no warm/cool shift)

**White Balance Methods:**

- Grey card reference shot under same lighting
- Custom white balance in-camera
- RAW file adjustment in post-production
- Maintain consistency across multiple shots

### Post-Production Workflow

**RAW Processing (Lightroom/Capture One):**

1. Import RAW files, verify exposure and focus
2. Set white balance using plinth or grey card reference
3. Adjust exposure for optimal tonal range
4. Fine-tune highlights/shadows to preserve detail
5. Apply subtle clarity for material texture enhancement
6. Correct lens distortion and chromatic aberration
7. Export to TIFF or high-quality JPG (8K resolution)

**Retouching (Photoshop):**

1. Clean background: remove dust spots or distractions
2. Perfect plinth white: ensure pure white without clipping
3. Enhance sharpness: Smart Sharpen or High Pass filter
4. Correct minor defects: heal small imperfections if needed
5. Final color grade: subtle adjustments for consistency
6. Save master file: layered PSD for future adjustments
7. Export final: 8K JPG (quality 10-12) or TIFF for print
