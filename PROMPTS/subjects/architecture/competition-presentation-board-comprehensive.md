# Competition Presentation Board - Comprehensive Architectural Documentation

Transform the uploaded architectural render into a high-end international competition presentation board while preserving the exact building design, geometry, proportions, façade composition, terraces, landscaping, and architectural identity of the original project.

Create a single ultra-professional vertical architectural poster (9:16 ratio) with a refined and cohesive graphic language inspired by world-class architecture competition boards, studio presentations, and award-winning archviz publications.

---

## Layout Composition

- Hero perspective render positioned in the top-left quadrant as the dominant visual element.
- Large clean white/ivory background with subtle paper texture.
- Strong grid-based composition with perfect alignment and hierarchy.
- Carefully balanced negative space.
- Editorial architecture magazine quality.
- Consistent margins and spacing throughout.
- Rich visual storytelling from concept to technical resolution.

---

## Board Content Generation

Generate realistic architectural documentation derived from the building design:

### Top Section

- Large hero render of the project.
- Project title and subtitle.
- Short concept statement.
- Design narrative block.

### Middle Section

- Highly detailed master plan / site plan.
- Ground floor plan.
- Typical floor plan.
- Roof plan.
- Urban integration diagram.
- Landscape strategy diagram.
- Circulation diagrams.
- Functional zoning diagrams.
- Environmental performance diagrams.
- Solar analysis diagrams.
- Wind flow diagrams.
- Program distribution diagrams.

### Technical Section

- Multiple architectural sections.
- Longitudinal section.
- Cross section.
- Façade studies.
- Elevations.
- Structural concept diagrams.
- Construction assembly diagrams.
- Exploded axonometric.
- Program stacking diagrams.
- Vertical circulation studies.

### Visualization Section

- Secondary perspective renders.
- Detail zooms.
- Material studies.
- Terrace and landscape close-ups.
- Human-scale experience views.
- Architectural detail callouts.
