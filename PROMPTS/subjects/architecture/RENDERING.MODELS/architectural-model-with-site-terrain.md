# Architectural Model with Site Terrain Product Photography

## Description

Create hyper-realistic professional product photographs of 1:50 scale architectural models featuring meticulously handcrafted site terrain from coarse-textured terracotta-red pigmented plaster showing raw granular contour surfaces. Models centrally placed on thick white museum-grade plinths, captured with Nikon D850 at 85mm macro, f/5.6 for complete sharpness, with soft overhead lighting and seamless light-grey infinity cove backgrounds.

## Overview

This prompt generates museum-quality product photography of architectural scale models that emphasizes both building design and site topography. The terracotta terrain provides striking contrast against light-colored building models, while professional studio photography technique ensures editorial excellence suitable for portfolios, exhibitions, and publications.

## Technical Notes

### Terracotta Plaster Recipe

**Material Composition:**

- Plaster of Paris or Hydrocal base
- Terracotta/rust pigment (iron oxide powder)
- Coarse aggregate (sand, pumice, or vermiculite)
- Water to mixing ratio per manufacturer specs

**Mixing Technique:**

- Add pigment to dry plaster (2-5% by weight)
- Mix aggregate into pigmented plaster (10-20% by weight)
- Add water and mix to desired consistency
- Pour or sculpt onto site base
- Allow to cure fully before photography

**Achieving Granular Texture:**

- Coarse sand (1-2mm) creates visible granular surface
- Pumice or vermiculite adds irregular texture
- Mix just until combined (overmixing smooths texture)
- Allow natural settling and drying texture
- Do not smooth or finish surface

### Topographical Modeling Techniques

**Contour Line Method:**

- Laser-cut foam board layers stacked by elevation
- Each layer represents contour interval (e.g., 1-meter at 1:50 = 20mm)
- Plaster over foam to create smooth transitions
- Sand and sculpt to refine contours
- Apply final terracotta plaster layer

**Sculpted Method:**

- Build up base form with rigid insulation foam
- Carve and shape topography by hand or CNC
- Apply plaster coating in layers
- Sculpt final surface while plaster is workable
- Preserve texture as plaster cures

**Hybrid Digital-Analog:**

- 3D model terrain digitally from site survey data
- CNC mill terrain from foam or plaster block
- Refine by hand for natural appearance
- Apply terracotta plaster finish coat
- Integrate model building into milled terrain

### Camera and Lens Specifications

**Nikon D850 Settings:**

- Resolution: 8256 x 5504 pixels (45.7MP)
- ISO: 100-200 (base ISO for maximum quality)
- White Balance: 5500K (daylight) or custom for studio lights
- File Format: NEF (RAW) 14-bit uncompressed
- Color Space: Adobe RGB (wider gamut than sRGB)

**85mm Macro Lens Recommendations:**

- Nikon AF-S 85mm f/3.5G ED VR Micro
- Canon EF 100mm f/2.8L Macro IS USM (equivalent)
- Sigma 105mm f/2.8 EX DG OS HSM Macro
- Working Distance: 1-1.5 meters from model
- f/5.6: Sweet spot for corner-to-corner sharpness

**Depth of Field at f/5.6:**

- Subject distance: ~1.5 meters
- Depth of field: ~300-500mm (sufficient for model depth)
- Hyperfocal strategy: Focus 1/3 into model for maximum sharpness
- Circle of confusion: 0.03mm (full-frame standard)

### Lighting Equipment Specifications

**Soft-Box Configuration:**

- Size: 36" x 48" or 48" x 72" (larger for even coverage)
- Diffusion: Double-diffused (inner baffle + outer fabric)
- Strobe: 500-1000 watt-seconds (studio strobe or speedlight)
- Position: Centered overhead, 45-60° angle from horizontal
- Distance: 1-2 meters above model

**Additional Light Sources (Optional):**

- **Fill Card**: White foam core reflector for shadow lift
- **Background Light**: Separate light for infinity cove control
- **Rim Light**: Subtle edge definition from behind (minimal)

**Light Meter Use:**

- Incident metering at model position
- Target: f/5.6 at ISO 100, 1/125-1/250 second
- Even exposure across model (no hot spots)
- Consistent readings across terrain and building

### Post-Production Workflow

**RAW Processing (Lightroom/Capture One):**

1. Import RAW files, verify sharpness and exposure
2. Set white balance (neutral, plinth should be pure white)
3. Adjust exposure for optimal tonal range
4. Fine-tune highlights/shadows (preserve terrain texture detail)
5. Apply lens correction (distortion and vignetting)
6. Slight clarity boost for terrain texture enhancement
7. Export to TIFF or high-quality JPG (8K resolution)

**Retouching (Photoshop):**

1. Clean background: remove dust spots on infinity cove
2. Perfect plinth white: ensure pure white without color cast
3. Clone out any defects in terrain or model (if needed)
4. Enhance sharpness: Unsharp Mask or Smart Sharpen filter
5. Verify color accuracy: Terracotta and white tones true
6. Final export: 8K JPG (quality 10-12) for portfolio/publication
