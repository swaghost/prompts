# Conceptual Architectural Photography - Contemporary Chapel in Landscape

## Description

Create a conceptual architectural photograph of a small contemporary chapel featuring a single rectangular volume in board-formed concrete with narrow vertical slot windows, set in a golden wheat field during late afternoon light. This prompt delivers painterly atmospheric quality with a single tree in foreground, no people, shot on medium format film with hyper-realistic 8K quality in 4:5 aspect ratio—capturing minimalist sacred architecture in rural landscape with artistic sophistication.

## Photography Overview

**Subject:** Small contemporary chapel  
**Architectural Style:** Minimalist modern, sacred architecture  
**Setting:** Golden wheat field, rural landscape  
**Time:** Late afternoon light (golden hour)  
**Style:** Conceptual architectural photography, painterly quality  
**Film:** Medium format film aesthetic  
**Quality:** Hyper-realistic, 8K  
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 (vertical portrait orientation)  
**Treatment:** --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 6
