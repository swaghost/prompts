# Site Analysis & Design Diagrams - Architectural Presentation Board

Create a 4K ultra-detailed vertical architectural presentation board (aspect ratio 9:16) titled:
**"SITE ANALYSIS & DESIGN DIAGRAMS – FROM CONTEXT TO ARCHITECTURAL FORM"**

Transform the provided building model into a complete analytical diagram sheet, maintaining exact consistency in style, layout, diagram language, and visual hierarchy.

---

## 🧩 GENERAL STYLE

- Clean white background
- Minimal, high-end architectural competition board aesthetic
- Soft shadows under all 3D/isometric elements
- Precise thin linework (light grey + black)
- Controlled pastel color accents only when needed
- Consistent scale across all diagrams
- Grid-based layout with perfect alignment and spacing
- Modern sans-serif typography (thin, clean, minimal)
- No noise, no clutter, extremely sharp edges
- Professional infographic + architectural hybrid style

---

## 📐 LAYOUT STRUCTURE (VERY IMPORTANT)

Organize the board into clearly separated modules, following this exact hierarchy:

### TOP ROW

1. **01. Site Context**
2. **02. Environmental Forces**
3. **03. Concept Diagram**

### SECOND ROW

1. **04. Program Zoning**
2. **05. Circulation & Movement**
3. **06. Landscape & Water Strategy**

### THIRD ROW

1. **07. Massing & Void Relationship**
2. **08. Environmental Performance**
3. **09. Structural Logic**

### BOTTOM ROW

- **Form Generation** (5-step sequence)
- **10. Final Composition Diagram**
- **11. Final Architectural Form** (hero render)

Include a small Design Intent paragraph + icons at the bottom.

---

## 🏗️ DIAGRAM RULES (APPLY TO ANY BUILDING)

### 01. SITE CONTEXT

- Simplified isometric model
- Fade surrounding context (roads, landscape, water)
- Highlight site boundary + access points

### 02. ENVIRONMENTAL FORCES

Overlay:

- Sun path arc (yellow/orange)
- Wind arrows (blue)
- Noise direction (red dashed)
- View corridors (green)

### 03. CONCEPT DIAGRAM

Abstract the building into smooth simplified volumes:

- Show transformation logic
- Add keywords:
  - Flow
  - Carve
  - Connect
  - Harmony
- Include minimal icons

### 04. PROGRAM ZONING

Apply soft color overlays:

- Main function
- Secondary/support
- Public zones
- Service zones

### 05. CIRCULATION & MOVEMENT

Overlay:

- Primary circulation (bold red line)
- Secondary (thin orange dashed)
- Pedestrian paths
- Service routes
- Vertical cores (points)

### 06. LANDSCAPE & WATER STRATEGY

Highlight:

- Green areas
- Hardscape
- Water elements
- Buffers

### 07. MASSING & VOID

- Show solid vs void clearly
- Courtyards + openings
- Porosity arrows

### 08. ENVIRONMENTAL PERFORMANCE

Use gradient overlays:

- High / medium sun exposure
- Shaded areas
- Ventilation paths
- Cooling airflow

### 09. STRUCTURAL LOGIC

- Structural grid overlay
- Core locations
- Load-bearing zones
- Spine direction

---

## 🔄 FORM GENERATION (KEY MOVES)

Create 5 small sequential diagrams:

1. **Divide** base mass
2. **Carve** voids
3. **Connect** volumes
4. **Elevate/shift**
5. **Smooth** final form

Minimal arrows between steps.

---

## 10. FINAL COMPOSITION DIAGRAM

Overlay combined systems:

- Circulation
- Landscape
- Axes
- Key nodes

---

## 11. FINAL ARCHITECTURAL FORM

- Clean, detailed isometric render
- Slight realism but still diagrammatic
- Soft shadows
- Context lightly included

---

## 🎨 VISUAL LANGUAGE

**Use:**

- Light greys for base models
- Soft pastel accents (blue, green, orange, red)

**No:**

- Harsh colors
- Heavy textures
- Maintain diagram clarity over realism

---

## 📊 TYPOGRAPHY

- All caps section titles
- Small clean annotations
- Minimal text
- Consistent spacing
- Subtle labels + legends

---

## ⚙️ OUTPUT QUALITY

- 4K resolution (minimum 3840px height)
- Ultra sharp lines
- No distortion
- No pixelation
- Balanced whitespace
- Perfect alignment

---

## 🧠 KEY BEHAVIOR (IMPORTANT)

- Adapt ALL diagrams intelligently to ANY building geometry
- Do NOT distort original building form
- Keep proportions accurate
- Maintain consistency across all diagram scales
- Ensure each diagram reads clearly on its own
