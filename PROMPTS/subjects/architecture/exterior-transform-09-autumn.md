# Architectural Transform: Autumn - Fall Foliage Scene

**Style**: Cinematic autumn architectural photography

---

## PROMPT

```
Transform the reference scene into a cinematic autumn environment. Replace the foliage with warm brown, amber, copper, and muted orange tones. Add subtle fallen leaves across the driveway and landscaping, light mist in the background, slightly damp textures, and soft diffused lighting. Preserve the exact house geometry, façade materials, windows, landscaping structure, car, and composition. Photorealistic autumn photography, natural color grading, refined and sophisticated mood.
```
