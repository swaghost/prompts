# Eye-Level Perspective View – Interior Kitchen Photography

## Description

Photorealistic human-eye-level perspective view from inside the kitchen. Uses compressed telephoto perspective (70-100mm focal length) with shallow depth of field for elegant architectural photography. Preserves all design elements, layout, materials, and styling from the input image while presenting the space from a natural standing viewpoint within the kitchen itself.

## Usage

Use with an existing kitchen/interior design image as input. The AI will maintain all design decisions, proportions, island configuration, appliances, accessories, materials, and colors from the reference image, but present it as a professional architectural photograph taken from inside the space with sophisticated depth of field and lighting.

## Prerequisites

- **Input Image Required**: Must provide a reference image of the kitchen design
- **Design Locked**: All elements from input image are preserved exactly as shown
- **Viewpoint Change Only**: Only the camera position and focal length are modified
- **Cabinet Style**: Must have smooth white handleless cabinet fronts

## Subject/Scene

- **Space**: Kitchen interior with island, cabinetry, appliances
- **Camera Position**: Inside the kitchen, natural human standing height
- **Viewpoint**: First-person perspective looking across the kitchen
- **Focus**: Main kitchen cabinetry and island (sharp)
- **Depth**: Foreground and distant background softly blurred

## Design Preservation Rules

### Kitchen Elements (Exact Preservation)

- Smooth white handleless cabinet fronts
- Island configuration and position
- Stools, appliances, exact positions
- All windows, lighting fixtures
- Accessories, plants, decorative elements
- Exact arrangement of all cabinetry and fixtures

### Materials (From Input Image)

- All colors preserved exactly
- All materials preserved exactly
- All textures preserved exactly
- All finishes preserved exactly

## Photographic Effects

### Depth of Field

- **Focal Point**: Main kitchen cabinetry and island (clearly sharp)
- **Foreground**: Softly blurred but understandable
- **Background**: Softly blurred but detail preserved enough to read layout
- **Style**: Elegant compressed perspective with architectural clarity

### Lighting

- **Primary**: Warm natural daylight
- **Accent**: Subtle artificial lighting
- **Under-Cabinet**: LED lighting only under kitchen cabinets
- **Quality**: Premium architectural photography lighting

## Technical Specifications

### Camera Settings

- **Focal Length**: 70-100mm (compressed telephoto perspective)
- **Aperture**: f/2.8 - f/4 (shallow depth of field)
- **Height**: Human eye level (approximately 5'6" - 5'10" / 165-178cm standing)
- **Position**: Inside the kitchen looking across the space
- **Format**: Full-frame 35mm equivalent or medium format

### Depth of Field Strategy

- **Sharp Zone**: Main kitchen cabinetry and island
- **Soft Foreground**: Elements closer to camera gently blurred
- **Soft Background**: Distant areas maintain readability but with elegant blur
- **Purpose**: Direct viewer attention while maintaining spatial understanding

### Composition

- **Perspective**: Natural human viewpoint, compressed by telephoto lens
- **Framing**: Show full kitchen layout from interior vantage point
- **Focus Area**: Kitchen cabinetry and island as primary subject
- **Balance**: Maintain architectural clarity despite depth of field effects

### Lighting Design

- **Natural Light**: Warm daylight as primary source
- **Under-Cabinet**: LED strip lighting (subtle, warm)
- **Artificial Accent**: Subtle supplementary lighting for depth
- **Quality**: Soft, architectural, premium editorial photography style
- **Color Temperature**: Warm, inviting, natural

### Rendering Style

- **Quality**: Premium architectural photography
- **Detail Level**: High detail in focus areas, elegant blur elsewhere
- **Style**: Professional interior design editorial photography
- **Realism**: True-to-life materials, accurate lighting, natural perspective

### Design Constraints

- ✅ **Preserve**: Layout, proportions, all elements, materials, colors, textures, finishes
- ✅ **Required**: Smooth white handleless cabinet fronts
- ✅ **Maintain**: Exact positions of island, stools, appliances, accessories, plants
- ✅ **Lighting**: Warm natural daylight, LED under-cabinet only
- ❌ **Do Not Add**: Extra elements, furniture, accessories not in input
- ❌ **Do Not Change**: Layout, colors, materials, cabinetry arrangement, fixtures
- ❌ **Avoid**: Top-down view, dollhouse view, axonometric view, floor plan visible
