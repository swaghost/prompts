## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL:**
Transform the uploaded black-and-white floor plan into a fully rendered colored presentation, preserving the exact original layout and dimensions.

**Subject:**
Complete architectural floor plan with all rooms, walls, doors, and openings exactly matching the original technical drawing. Maintain precise spatial relationships, wall thicknesses, and dimensional accuracy.

**Setting/Environment:**
Top-down orthographic view of interior floor plan with modern furniture placement, showing living spaces, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, and all functional areas clearly defined with appropriate furnishings.

**Visual Characteristics:**

- Natural wood flooring throughout (oak, maple, or walnut tones)
- Modern furniture pieces in appropriate scales
- Soft shadows cast by furniture and walls
- Material textures visible (wood grain, fabric, etc.)
- Wall thickness clearly defined
- Door swings and openings accurate
- Color-coded room functions (optional subtle tinting)
- Clean white or light gray walls
- Realistic material finishes on all surfaces

**Furniture & Details:**
Include appropriately scaled modern furniture: sofas, chairs, beds, tables, kitchen appliances, bathroom fixtures, rugs, and decorative elements. All furniture should be proportionally accurate and positioned logically.

**Composition:**
Centered floor plan with clean margins, professional presentation layout, and clear spatial hierarchy. Maintain technical precision while adding realistic visual context.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Photorealistic architectural rendering with modern interior design aesthetic. Professional presentation quality suitable for client presentations, marketing materials, and architectural portfolios.

**Technical Specifications:**
High-resolution architectural rendering, accurate scale representation, precise dimensional preservation, realistic material rendering, soft ambient shadows, clean professional presentation.

**Color Palette:**
Natural wood tones (warm browns, honey, walnut), soft neutral walls (whites, light grays, beiges), modern furniture colors (grays, blues, earth tones), subtle accent colors for visual interest.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Professional, clean, inviting, modern, accessible, warm, technically accurate yet visually appealing.

**Quality Keywords:**
Architectural rendering, photorealistic floor plan, professional presentation, accurate scale, realistic materials, soft shadows, modern interior design, clean visualization, high detail, precise layout.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Distorted proportions, inaccurate layout, perspective distortion, cluttered composition, unrealistic furniture scale, harsh shadows, poor material quality, low resolution, messy presentation, extra rooms or walls not in original.
