# Bathroom Architectural Presentation Board

## Description

Transform a bathroom interior render into a premium architectural presentation board while maintaining the original bathroom design, layout, finishes, lighting, and viewpoint with no redesign. Creates comprehensive technical documentation including a material palette, bathroom fixture schedule, floor plan excerpt, interior elevations, exploded detail diagrams, lighting notes, section details, dimensions, and finish callouts using a minimal Swiss grid with off-white background, warm neutral tones, sharp alignment, and luxury editorial presentation quality.

## Usage

Use this prompt specifically for bathroom projects when you need to create client-ready presentation boards with complete technical specifications. Perfect for bathroom renovations, new construction, hospitality projects, and portfolio documentation of bathroom designs.

## What This Preserves

- **Original bathroom design**: No redesign, keep all aesthetic choices
- **Layout**: Exact fixture and furniture placement
- **Finishes**: All tile, stone, surfaces as shown
- **Lighting**: Every fixture and ambient quality
- **Viewpoint**: Camera angle and perspective
- **Styling**: Towels, accessories, décor exactly as rendered

## What This Adds

- Material palette (tile, stone, hardware swatches)
- Bathroom fixture schedule (vanity, toilet, shower, tub specs)
- Floor plan excerpt with dimensions
- Interior elevations (wall sections showing tile patterns)
- Exploded detail diagrams (vanity construction, shower assembly)
- Lighting notes (fixture placement, lumens, Kelvin)
- Section details (waterproofing, transitions, thresholds)
- Dimensions and clearances
- Finish callouts and specifications

## Bathroom-Specific Elements

**Fixture Schedule**:

- Vanity (dimensions, material, sink type, faucet finish)
- Toilet (model, height, water efficiency)
- Shower/tub (size, material, glass enclosure, fixtures)
- Mirrors (size, frame, lighting)
- Hardware (towel bars, toilet paper holder, robe hooks)

**Material Palette**:

- Floor tile (size, material, finish, grout colour)
- Wall tile (field tile, accent tile, border)
- Countertop (material, edge profile, finish)
- Cabinetry finish (paint, stain, hardware)
- Plumbing fixture finish (chrome, brushed nickel, matte black)
- Stone or solid surface materials

**Floor Plan Details**:

- Fixture locations with centerlines
- Door swing clearances
- ADA/accessibility compliance if applicable
- Electrical and plumbing rough-in locations
- Tile layout start points

**Interior Elevations**:

- Each wall shown flat
- Tile patterns and layouts
- Mirror and medicine cabinet locations
- Lighting fixture heights
- Towel bar and accessory placement
- Height dimensions from floor

**Exploded Details**:

- Vanity assembly (cabinet, countertop, sink, plumbing)
- Shower/tub surround construction
- Niche details (shower shelf, storage)
- Tile edge transitions
- Waterproofing layers

**Section Details**:

- Shower curb/threshold detail
- Tile-to-floor transition
- Vanity countertop edge
- Cove base or baseboard detail
- Waterproofing membrane locations

## Bathroom-Specific Dimensions

**Critical Clearances**:

- Toilet: 30" width minimum, 21" front clearance
- Vanity: 30-36" width per sink, 21" depth typical
- Shower: 36" × 36" minimum, 48" × 36" comfortable
- Tub: 60" × 30" standard, 72" × 36" luxury
- Door swing: 32" clear opening minimum

**Fixture Heights**:

- Vanity countertop: 32-36" (34" standard)
- Mirror bottom edge: 3-4" above countertop
- Sconce lights: 60-70" to center (36-40" apart)
- Towel bar: 48" above floor
- Toilet paper holder: 26" above floor
- Shower controls: 38-48" above floor
- Showerhead: 72-78" above floor
