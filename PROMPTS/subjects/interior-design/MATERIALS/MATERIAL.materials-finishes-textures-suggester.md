# Materials, Finishes & Textures Suggester

## Description

Suggest the best materials, finishes and textures that suit the style, budget and functionality of your design. This prompt provides detailed material recommendations with visual representations, helping you select appropriate surfaces, fabrics, and finishes for cohesive interior design.

## Usage

Perfect for interior designers, architects, contractors, and homeowners selecting materials for renovations or new construction. Ideal for creating material boards, comparing options, ensuring style consistency, and balancing aesthetics with durability and budget requirements.

## Prerequisites

- Design style and aesthetic goals
- Budget range (budget-friendly, mid-range, luxury)
- Functional requirements (high-traffic, moisture-resistant, pet-friendly)
- Room type and usage patterns
- Climate considerations (optional)
- Maintenance preferences

## Image Prompt

Create a comprehensive material palette for a [ROOM TYPE] in [DESIGN STYLE] style with a [BUDGET LEVEL] budget.

**Design Style:** [MODERN, TRADITIONAL, RUSTIC, CONTEMPORARY, etc.]

**Functional Requirements:**

- Traffic level: [LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH]
- Moisture exposure: [DRY/MODERATE/HIGH]
- Special needs: [PET-FRIENDLY, CHILD-SAFE, ALLERGY-CONSCIOUS]

**Material Categories to Include:**

**Flooring:**

- Primary recommendation with finish type
- Durability rating and maintenance needs
- Alternative options

**Wall Treatments:**

- Paint colors and finishes (matte, satin, semi-gloss)
- Wallpaper or wall covering options
- Accent wall materials (wood, tile, stone)

**Countertops & Surfaces:**

- Material type and finish
- Edge profile recommendations
- Backsplash coordination

**Cabinetry & Millwork:**

- Wood species or material
- Stain or paint finish
- Hardware finish (brushed nickel, matte black, brass)

**Textiles & Soft Goods:**

- Upholstery fabric type and weight
- Window treatment materials
- Rug material and pile type

**Accent Materials:**

- Metal finishes for fixtures and hardware
- Glass types (clear, frosted, textured)
- Natural materials (stone, wood, rattan)

Generate a material board showing samples of each recommended material with texture details, color coordination, and finish specifications. Include close-up details showing texture and quality.

## Negative Prompt

Mismatched materials, clashing textures, low-quality finishes, unrealistic combinations, poor lighting on samples, blurry textures, inconsistent style, cheap appearance, dated materials, impractical selections for intended use

## Output Specifications

- **Layout:** Grid or mood board arrangement
- **Detail Level:** Clear texture and pattern visibility
- **Lighting:** Natural, accurate color representation
- **Labeling:** Material names and finish types
- **Cohesion:** Unified palette across all selections

## Material Selection Guidelines

**By Design Style:**

**Modern/Contemporary:**

- Smooth, clean-lined materials
- Glass, metal, engineered stone
- Low-pile or flat-weave textiles
- Matte or satin finishes
- Monochromatic or bold accent colors

**Traditional:**

- Rich wood tones and natural materials
- Marble, granite, hardwood
- Upholstered fabrics with texture
- Polished or glazed finishes
- Classic color palettes

**Rustic/Farmhouse:**

- Reclaimed or distressed wood
- Natural stone, brick, concrete
- Linen, cotton, jute textiles
- Matte and weathered finishes
- Warm, earthy color palette

**Scandinavian:**

- Light woods (birch, ash, pine)
- White or light stone
- Natural wool, linen, cotton
- Matte finishes
- Neutral with black accents

**Industrial:**

- Exposed brick, concrete, metal
- Steel, iron, weathered wood
- Leather, canvas, denim
- Raw, unfinished looks
- Dark neutrals with metal accents

**By Budget Level:**

**Budget-Friendly:**

- Laminate flooring, luxury vinyl
- Painted cabinets, laminate counters
- Basic tile, standard paint grades
- Synthetic textiles, budget hardware

**Mid-Range:**

- Engineered hardwood, quality tile
- Semi-custom cabinets, quartz counters
- Designer paint, quality wallpapers
- Natural/synthetic blends, mid-tier fixtures

**Luxury:**

- Solid hardwood, natural stone
- Custom cabinetry, marble/granite counters
- Premium paints, luxury wallcoverings
- Natural fibers, designer fixtures

**By Functionality:**

**High-Traffic Areas:**

- Durable flooring (tile, luxury vinyl, hardwood)
- Washable paints (semi-gloss, satin)
- Performance fabrics (stain-resistant)
- Sealed or protected surfaces

**Moisture-Prone Areas:**

- Porcelain or ceramic tile
- Water-resistant paints
- Moisture-resistant materials
- Sealed stone or engineered products

## Application Areas

- Material selection for renovations
- Design concept development
- Client presentation boards
- Contractor specifications
- Budget planning and estimates
- Design continuity across spaces
- Finish schedules for construction
- E-design material recommendations
