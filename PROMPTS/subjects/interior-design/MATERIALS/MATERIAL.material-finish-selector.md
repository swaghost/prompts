# Material & Finish Selector

## Description

A comprehensive material and finish recommendation tool for interior design projects. This prompt suggests the best materials and finishes for flooring, wall treatments, countertops, cabinetry, fabrics, and metals based on your chosen style. Includes durability assessments, maintenance requirements, and practical considerations for real-world use.

## Usage

Specify your interior style and room type to receive curated material and finish recommendations that are aesthetically cohesive and functionally appropriate. Use this when selecting finishes for renovation projects, new construction, or design concept development.

## Style Applications

Works with any interior design style:

- Modern Minimalist, Industrial, Contemporary
- Scandinavian, Japandi, Mid-Century Modern
- Transitional, Traditional, Classic
- Bohemian, Coastal, Rustic, Farmhouse
- Art Deco, Maximalist, Eclectic

## Material Categories

- **Flooring**: Hardwood, tile, stone, luxury vinyl, concrete, carpet
- **Wall Finishes**: Paint, wallpaper, paneling, tile, textured treatments
- **Countertops**: Natural stone, quartz, solid surface, laminate, wood
- **Cabinetry**: Wood species, paint finishes, stains, hardware
- **Fabrics**: Upholstery, drapery, rugs, cushions
- **Metals**: Hardware, fixtures, lighting, decorative accents

## ChatGPT Prompt

```
Suggest the best materials and finishes for a [Style] interior. Include flooring, wall finishes, countertops, cabinetry, fabrics, metals, durability notes, and maintenance level.

INCLUDE:
• Flooring options with specific material recommendations
• Wall finish treatments and paint types
• Countertop materials for kitchens and bathrooms
• Cabinetry finishes (wood species, paint, hardware)
• Fabric selections for upholstery and textiles
• Metal finishes for fixtures and hardware
• Durability ratings for each material
• Maintenance requirements and care instructions

Make recommendations cohesive to the style while being practical and long-lasting.
```

## Output Format

The AI will provide:

1. **Flooring**: 2-3 options with specific products/materials
2. **Wall Finishes**: Paint types, wallpaper, or treatments
3. **Countertops**: Material recommendations by function
4. **Cabinetry**: Wood species, finishes, hardware styles
5. **Fabrics**: Upholstery, window treatments, area rugs
6. **Metals**: Fixture finishes and hardware colours
7. **Durability Assessment**: High/medium/low traffic ratings
8. **Maintenance Guide**: Cleaning and care requirements

## Durability Considerations

- **High Traffic Areas**: Durable, scratch-resistant, easy-clean materials
- **Moisture-Prone Spaces**: Water-resistant, mold-proof finishes
- **Pet-Friendly**: Stain-resistant, claw-proof surfaces
- **Family Homes**: Wipeable, forgiving, low-maintenance choices
- **Low-Traffic Areas**: Delicate or premium materials acceptable

## Maintenance Levels

- **Low Maintenance**: Wipe-clean surfaces, sealed materials
- **Medium Maintenance**: Periodic sealing, gentle cleaning
- **High Maintenance**: Regular polishing, professional care, delicate handling

## Best Practices

- Specify the room type for function-appropriate materials
- Mention lifestyle factors (pets, children, entertaining)
- Note budget constraints (luxury vs. mid-range vs. budget)
- Include any existing materials to coordinate with
- Request samples or product names for sourcing
- Ask about eco-friendly or sustainable alternatives if desired
