## Creative Variations

### Different Design Styles

**Minimalist Modern:**
Replace olive-green sofa with white leather modular, vintage walnut with glass/chrome table, Persian rug with solid cream wool, abundant plants with single sculptural fig tree, eclectic decor with minimal art.

**Industrial Loft:**
Replace off-white walls with exposed brick, wooden floor with polished concrete, corduroy sofa with leather chesterfield, floating shelf with metal pipe shelving, add Edison bulbs.

**Coastal Contemporary:**
Replace olive-green with white linen sectional, mustard throw with blue/white striped, Persian rug with natural jute, walnut table with driftwood table, emphasize beach house vibe.

**Maximalist Bohemian:**
Enhance plants to jungle levels, add layered rugs, macramé wall hangings, dozens of eclectic frames in gallery wall, floor cushions, poufs, hanging chair, vibrant textiles.
