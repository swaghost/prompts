## Room Elements & Materials

### Architectural Features

**Ceiling Treatment:**

- White painted ceiling or exposed wood beams
- Ceiling height: 9-11 feet
- Beams in white-washed or natural weathered wood
- Optional tongue and groove wood planks between beams
- Painted white for brightness
- Ceiling fans with woven blades
- Simple flat plaster finish alternative

**Wall Systems:**

- White or off-white painted walls
- Textured plaster or stucco finish
- Possible shiplap or beadboard accent walls
- Painted in white or soft coastal blue
- Wainscoting at 36-48 inches height (white)
- Arched doorways and niches (Mediterranean influence)
- Large windows and glass doors

**Flooring:**

- Wide plank pine or oak flooring
- White-washed, pickled, or natural light finish
- Weathered appearance acceptable

**Windows & Doors:**
