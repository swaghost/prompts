## Room Elements & Materials

### Architectural Features

**Ceiling Treatment:**

- Exposed wood beams (reclaimed or new with aged finish)
- Beam sizes: 6x8 or 8x10 inches
- Spacing: 4-6 feet on center
- Stained in natural, weathered gray, or dark walnut
- White painted tongue and groove planks between beams
- OR white painted flat ceiling
- Ceiling height: 10-12 feet (vaulted optional)
- Simple fans with wood or metal blades

**Wall Systems:**

- Shiplap accent walls (iconic farmhouse element)
- Horizontal wood boards 6-8 inches wide
- Painted white or natural weathered finish
- Vertical board and batten accent walls
- Standard painted drywall on remaining walls (white or soft gray)
- Wainscoting with board and batten at 36-48 inches
- Exposed brick (painted white or natural)
- Simple baseboards: 4-6 inches

**Flooring:**

- Wide plank hardwood (6-10 inch width)
- Reclaimed barn wood or distressed new wood
- Stain: medium to dark brown, weathered gray, or natural
- Visible knots and imperfections add character
- Matte finish (low or no gloss)
- Consistent throughout open concept space

**Doors & Windows:**

- Barn doors on sliding hardware (black metal)
- Large black-framed windows (modern farmhouse signature)
- Steel frame or painted wood in matte black
- Multiple panes or large single panes
- White painted interior trim (4-6 inches)
- Tall doors (8 feet height)
