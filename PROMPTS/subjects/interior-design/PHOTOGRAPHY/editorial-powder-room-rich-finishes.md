# Editorial Powder Room Photography - Rich Finishes & Intimate Luxury

## Description

Create hyper-realistic editorial photography of a small powder room featuring rich luxurious finishes: deep green plaster walls, brass wall sconces, single carved stone basin, oversized antique mirror, and marble floor in herringbone pattern. Shot on Phase One medium format with 50mm lens, this prompt delivers soft warm light, intimate close framing, and ultra-detailed 8K quality in 4:5 aspect ratio with raw style treatment—capturing boutique luxury bathroom design with editorial sophistication.

## Photography Overview

**Style:** Editorial luxury interior photography  
**Room Type:** Small powder room (half bath)  
**Aesthetic:** Rich finishes, boutique luxury, intimate elegance  
**Camera:** Phase One medium format, 50mm lens  
**Quality:** Hyper-realistic, 8K, ultra detailed  
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 (vertical portrait orientation)  
**Lighting:** Soft warm light  
**Framing:** Intimate close-framing  
**Treatment:** Raw style (--style raw --v 6)
