## Style Description

**Colonial** design reflects the architectural and decorative traditions of early American settlers, characterized by symmetry, craftsmanship, and timeless elegance. This style features rich wood tones, classic proportions, traditional patterns, and refined details. Colonial interiors evoke heritage, stability, and understated sophistication through quality materials, proper proportions, and historical references. The aesthetic balances formality with livability, creating spaces that feel established, dignified, and enduring. This style appeals to those who value tradition, craftsmanship, and a sense of historical continuity in their living spaces.
