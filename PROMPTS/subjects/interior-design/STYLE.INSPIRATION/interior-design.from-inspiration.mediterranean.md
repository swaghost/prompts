## Style Description

**Mediterranean** design captures the sun-drenched beauty of coastal regions around the Mediterranean Sea, characterized by warm earth tones, textured plaster, and rustic elegance. This style features terracotta tiles, wrought iron, arched doorways, and azure blue accents. Mediterranean interiors evoke warmth, relaxation, and la dolce vita—spaces that feel like villas overlooking the sea. The aesthetic celebrates indoor-outdoor living, natural materials, and the laid-back luxury of Mediterranean culture. This style appeals to those seeking warmth, casual sophistication, and the sun-soaked charm of Italian, Spanish, and Greek coastal living.
