## Style Description

**Regency** design reflects the elegant sophistication of early 19th-century British style, characterized by Greek and Roman influences, refined details, and gracious proportions. This style features gilt accents, striped fabrics, Grecian motifs, and delicate furniture with saber legs. Regency interiors evoke refinement, social grace, and literary elegance—spaces that feel like settings from Jane Austen novels. The aesthetic balances formality with comfort, classicism with lightness. This style appeals to Anglophiles, period drama enthusiasts, and those who appreciate the restrained elegance of Georgian and Regency-era England.
