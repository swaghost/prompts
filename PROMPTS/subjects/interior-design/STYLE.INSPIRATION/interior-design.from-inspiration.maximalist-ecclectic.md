## Style Description

**Maximalist/Eclectic** design celebrates abundance, personality, and fearless mixing of styles, periods, and patterns. This style features bold colors, layered textures, collected treasures, and "more is more" philosophy. Maximalist interiors evoke joy, creativity, and confident self-expression—spaces that tell rich stories through curated collections and unexpected combinations. The aesthetic rejects minimalism in favor of visual richness, personality, and exuberant decoration. This style appeals to collectors, artists, and bold personalities who view their homes as galleries of personal expression and lived experiences.
