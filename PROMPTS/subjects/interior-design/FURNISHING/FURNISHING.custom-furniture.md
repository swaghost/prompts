# PROMPT 06: Custom Furniture Design

**Purpose:** Custom Furniture Designer  
**Workflow Position:** Phase 3 - Custom Elements

---

## Goal

Design truly functional furniture. Integrate storage, lighting, niches, and cable management without compromising the environment.

---

## Full Prompt

```
Design a custom (TV UNIT, CABINET OR WARDROBE) for this wall. Use the full available
dimensions while respecting outlets, openings and circulation. Include closed storage,
display niches, integrated warm LED lighting and concealed cables. Match a (STYLE)
aesthetic. Preserve the room, camera angle and proportions. Photorealistic joinery
details.
```

---

## Variables

- **TV UNIT, CABINET OR WARDROBE:** TV media unit, storage cabinet, wardrobe closet, bookshelf, kitchen cabinetry, bathroom vanity, desk with storage, entertainment center, etc.
- **STYLE:** modern, Scandinavian, minimalist, traditional, industrial, mid-century, contemporary, etc.

---

## Furniture Type Options

### TV Unit/Media Console:

- Screen accommodation (specific size)
- Media equipment storage (receivers, game consoles)
- Cable management system
- Display areas for décor
- Integrated lighting (behind TV, shelves)

### Cabinet/Storage:

- Adjustable shelving
- Closed and open storage mix
- Drawer systems
- Specialized compartments
- Hardware integration

### Wardrobe/Closet:

- Hanging space (short/long garments)
- Drawer systems
- Shelf organization
- Accessories storage (ties, belts, jewelry)
- Mirror integration

### Bookshelf/Library:

- Adjustable shelf heights
- Mix of open and closed storage
- Display niches for objects
- Integrated lighting for shelves
- Ladder access (if tall)

---

## Design Requirements

### Dimensional Planning:

- Use full available wall dimensions
- Respect outlet locations
- Account for openings (doors, windows)
- Maintain circulation clearances
- Consider ceiling height
- Measure depth constraints

### Functional Elements:

#### Closed Storage:

- Doors (hinged, sliding, lift-up)
- Drawers (sizes vary by use)
- Hidden compartments
- Soft-close mechanisms
- Internal organization (dividers, inserts)

#### Display Niches:

- Open shelving
- Glass-front cabinets
- Backlit sections
- Adjustable shelves
- Varied depths for visual interest

#### Integrated Lighting:

- Warm LED strips (2700K-3000K)
- Under-shelf lighting
- Interior cabinet lighting
- Toe-kick lighting
- Concealed fixtures

#### Cable Management:

- Concealed routing channels
- Grommet holes
- Rear access panels
- Organized power distribution
- Clean, invisible appearance

---

## Style Matching

### Preservation:

- Room design and style
- Camera angle and perspective
- Room proportions
- Existing architecture
- Overall aesthetic

### Style Adaptation:

- Match materials to room (wood species, finishes)
- Coordinate finishes (matte, gloss, texture)
- Align design language (modern, traditional lines)
- Maintain visual harmony
- Appropriate hardware selection

---

## Quality Standards

### Photorealistic Joinery:

- Visible wood grain direction (follows construction logic)
- Realistic edge banding (on plywood edges)
- Accurate hardware placement (handles, hinges)
- Proper reveals and gaps (consistent spacing)
- Material transitions (seamless connections)
- Construction details (visible or concealed)
- Authentic craftsmanship appearance

## Pro Tips

### Measure Twice, Design Once:

- Verify all dimensions carefully
- Check for obstructions (outlets, vents, switches)
- Consider door/drawer clearances
- Account for baseboard and trim depths

### Think About Access:

- How will it be installed? (built in place or modular)
- Can large components fit through doorways?
- Is there access to the back for future cable changes?
- Can shelves be adjusted later?

### Future-Proofing:

- Cable management for devices that don't exist yet
- Adjustable shelving for changing needs
- Modular design for easy updates
- Removable backs for access

### Material Selection:

- Match or complement existing materials
- Consider durability for high-use areas
- Choose appropriate finish (matte for fingerprints)
- Quality hardware makes a difference
