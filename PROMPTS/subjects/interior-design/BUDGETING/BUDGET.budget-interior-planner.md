# Budget Interior Planner

## Description

A comprehensive budget planning tool that designs beautiful interiors within specific financial constraints. This prompt prioritizes where to invest for maximum impact, suggests affordable alternatives, provides detailed cost estimates, and maximizes style without exceeding budget. Perfect for cost-conscious projects that still demand great design.

## Usage

Specify your room type and total budget, then receive a complete design plan with itemized costs, priority recommendations, affordable alternatives, and savings strategies. Use this for realistic project planning, client budgeting, or DIY renovations with limited funds.

## Budget Ranges

- **Luxury**: $50,000+ (high-end materials, custom elements)
- **Mid-Range**: $15,000-$50,000 (quality materials, some custom pieces)
- **Budget-Friendly**: $5,000-$15,000 (smart selections, DIY elements)
- **Tight Budget**: Under $5,000 (thrifted, IKEA-hacked, creative solutions)

## Budget Allocation

Typical percentage breakdown:

- **Furniture**: 35-40%
- **Materials & Finishes**: 20-25%
- **Lighting**: 10-15%
- **Décor & Accessories**: 10-12%
- **Labor & Installation**: 12-15%
- **Contingency**: 3-5%

## ChatGPT Prompt

```
Design a beautiful [Room Type] with a total budget of [Amount]. Prioritize where to invest, suggest affordable alternatives, estimate costs, and maximize style without crossing the budget.

INCLUDE:
• Complete budget breakdown by category
• Detailed cost estimates for each element
• Priority list: where to splurge vs. save
• Affordable alternatives for expensive items
• Money-saving tips and strategies
• Itemized shopping list with estimated prices
• Total budget summary showing 100% allocation

Make the design beautiful and achievable within the specified budget.
```

## Example Usage

**Living Room - $15,000 Budget:**

```
Design a beautiful living room with a total budget of $15,000. Prioritize where to invest, suggest affordable alternatives, estimate costs, and maximize style without crossing the budget. The room is 16x14 feet and needs seating for 5, storage, and lighting.
```

**Bedroom Makeover - $5,000 Budget:**

```
Design a beautiful bedroom makeover with a total budget of $5,000. Prioritize where to invest, suggest affordable alternatives, estimate costs, and maximize style without crossing the budget. Keep the existing bed frame but update everything else.
```

## Output Format

The AI will provide:

**1. Budget Summary Table**
| Category | Amount | % |
|----------|--------|---|
| Furniture | $X | X% |
| Materials | $X | X% |
| Lighting | $X | X% |
| Décor | $X | X% |
| Labor | $X | X% |
| **TOTAL** | **$X** | **100%\*\* |

**2. Invest in Key Pieces** (High-Impact Items)

- Statement furniture worth splurging on
- Quality items that last
- Elements that define the space

**3. Cost Breakdown** (Itemized List)

- Every item with estimated price
- Multiple options at different price points
- Links or store suggestions

**4. Affordable Alternatives**

- Budget-friendly swaps for expensive items
- Where to save without sacrificing style
- DIY opportunities

**5. Money-Saving Tips**

- Shop sales and secondhand
- Paint instead of replace
- IKEA hacks and budget finds
- Phased approach if needed

**6. Priority Order**

- Phase 1: Essentials (must-haves)
- Phase 2: Nice-to-haves
- Phase 3: Finishing touches

## Where to Splurge

High-impact investments:

- **Sofa**: Used daily, affects comfort and aesthetic
- **Mattress**: Health and sleep quality
- **Lighting**: Transforms ambiance
- **Flooring**: Visible, high-wear surface
- **Statement Pieces**: Focal points that define the space

## Where to Save

Budget-friendly choices:

- **Accessories**: Thrift stores, HomeGoods, Amazon
- **Side Tables**: IKEA, Facebook Marketplace
- **Artwork**: Printables, DIY, budget frames
- **Pillows & Throws**: Online retailers, sales
- **Storage**: Affordable systems, secondhand

## Cost Estimation Tips

- Include sales tax in calculations
- Add 10-15% for delivery and assembly
- Budget 3-5% contingency for surprises
- Research actual prices from target retailers
- Account for labor if not DIY

## Best Practices

- Be realistic about total budget (include tax, delivery, labor)
- Specify what you already own and can keep
- Mention if you can DIY or need everything installed
- Note any non-negotiable elements (e.g., "must have real hardwood")
- Ask for phased approach if budget is tight
- Request specific store suggestions (IKEA, Wayfair, West Elm, etc.)
