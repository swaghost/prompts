# PROMPT 05: Harmonious Color Palette

**Purpose:** Color Palette Generator  
**Workflow Position:** Phase 2 - Design Development

---

## Goal

Find the right palette without guessing. Use light, materials, and proportion to reach a balanced and applicable combination.

---

## Full Prompt

```
Generate a harmonious five-color palette for this (ROOM), inspired by (STYLE OR MOOD).
Consider the existing materials, natural light and room size. Define 60% dominant, 30%
secondary and 10% accent colors. Apply the palette realistically while preserving all
architecture and objects. Include the five HEX codes.
```

---

## Variables

- **ROOM:** bedroom, kitchen, living room, bathroom, dining room, office, etc.
- **STYLE OR MOOD:** coastal, warm minimalist, serene spa, energetic modern, earthy natural, moody dramatic, fresh Scandinavian, industrial chic, bohemian eclectic, traditional elegant, etc.

---

## The 60-30-10 Color Rule

### 60% Dominant Color:

- **Application:** Walls, large surfaces
- **Function:** Sets overall mood
- **Character:** Usually neutral or soft
- **Role:** Creates visual foundation

### 30% Secondary Color:

- **Application:** Furniture, major elements
- **Function:** Supports dominant color
- **Character:** Adds depth and interest
- **Role:** Complements dominant

### 10% Accent Color:

- **Application:** Accessories, small details
- **Function:** Creates visual pop
- **Character:** Draws the eye
- **Role:** Bold or contrasting

---

## Considerations

### Existing Materials:

- Wood tones present
- Stone or tile colors
- Fixed elements (cabinets, counters)
- Flooring color
- Existing furniture

### Natural Light:

- **Light direction:** North (cool, even), South (warm, bright), East (morning light), West (afternoon light)
- **Light quality:** Warm or cool based on direction
- **Amount of natural light:** Abundant, moderate, limited
- **Time of day considerations:** How light changes throughout day
- **Seasonal light changes:** Summer vs. winter sun angle

### Room Size:

- **Small rooms:** Lighter, expansive colors open up space
- **Large rooms:** Can handle darker, richer tones
- **Ceiling height:** Affects color impact and perception
- **Proportions:** Influence how colors are perceived

---

## Application Guidelines

### Preserve:

- All architecture
- Structural elements
- Objects and furniture placement
- Camera angle
- Proportions

### Apply Realistically:

- Color on appropriate surfaces
- Material-appropriate application
- Natural color transitions
- Light interaction with colors
- Color consistency throughout

### Deliverable:

- Five HEX codes (#RRGGBB format)
- Color names/descriptions
- Distribution percentages (60/30/10)
- Application recommendations
- Material considerations

---

## Pro Tips

### Color Testing:

- Run this prompt multiple times with different moods
- Compare palettes side-by-side
- Consider how colors appear in different lighting

### Distribution Flexibility:

- The 60-30-10 rule is a guideline, not rigid law
- Can adjust to 70-20-10 or 50-30-20 as needed
- Multiple accent colors can share the 10%

### Working with Existing Elements:

- Let fixed materials (floors, counters) inform your palette
- Pull colors from existing wood tones or stone
- Don't fight what you can't change - work with it

### Light Impact:

- Colors appear different in natural vs. artificial light
- North-facing rooms make colors cooler
- South-facing rooms make colors warmer
- Test samples in actual lighting conditions
