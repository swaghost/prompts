# Street Style Fashion - Men's Fashion

## Description

Contemporary street style fashion photography capturing authentic urban style in real-world environments. Features candid or semi-posed shots with natural light, urban backgrounds, and contemporary menswear aesthetics. Perfect for streetwear brands, lifestyle campaigns, fashion blogs, and modern editorial content showcasing everyday style with editorial polish.

## Technical Specifications

**Camera Setup:**

- Camera: Professional mirrorless or DSLR
- Lens: 35mm f/1.4, 50mm f/1.8, or 85mm f/1.8
- Aperture: f/2.0 - f/4.0 (moderate DOF)
- ISO: 200-800 (daylight conditions)
- Shutter Speed: 1/250 - 1/1000 (freeze movement)

**Composition:**

- Full-length or three-quarter body shots
- Environmental context important
- Urban architecture as backdrop
- Vertical or square format
- Subject off-center using rule of thirds
- Breathing room in frame

**Lighting:**

- Natural daylight primary
- Overcast soft light ideal
- Open shade or urban canyon lighting
- Golden hour warm glow
- Avoid harsh midday sun
- Authentic street lighting

## Lighting Scenarios

- Soft even lighting

- True color rendering

**Golden Hour:**

- Warm directional sunlight

## Photography Approach

### Documentary + Editorial

**Candid Moments:**

- Subject in motion (walking)
- Natural interactions
- Environmental engagement
- Spontaneous captures
- Real moments

**Directed Candid:**

- Subject aware but natural
- Directed movement or positioning
- Authentic feeling maintained
- Professional polish added
- Best of both approaches

**Full Editorial:**

- Intentional posing
- Precise positioning
- Fashion-forward composition
- High-end magazine quality
- Still retains street authenticity

### Capture Techniques

**Continuous Shooting:**

- Burst mode for walking shots
- Capture natural stride
- Select best frame from sequence
- Natural movement frozen

**Anticipation:**

- Pre-focus and wait
- Capture decisive moment
- Background element timing
- Perfect stride or expression

**Framing:**

### Detail Shots

**Close-Ups:**

- Sneakers and styling details
- Accessory focus (watch, bag, jewelry)
- Texture and material emphasis
- Complementary to full-body
- Complete visual story
