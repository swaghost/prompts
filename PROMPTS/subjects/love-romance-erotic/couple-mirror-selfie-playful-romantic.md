The man carries the woman over his right shoulder in a joyful, spontaneous pose while taking the selfie with his smartphone—capturing their reflection in a full-length mirror. He wears a premium cream-white textured shirt with loose white linen trousers, while she wears an elegant sleeveless black midi dress with glossy black heels. ## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Man's Face Doesn't Match Reference

**Solution**:

- Increase character reference weight (MidJourney: `--cw 100`)

- Explicitly list features to preserve: "Preserve his exact nose shape, eye shape, jawline from reference"
- Add restriction: "No beautification, no AI smoothing, no stylization"

### Issue: Mirror Reflection Looks Wrong

**Solution**:

- Specify "accurate mirror physics, proper reflection angles"
- Mention "true mirror selfie perspective, not direct camera view"
- Add "couple appears in mirror reflection, not directly facing camera"
- Request "realistic mirror depth and spatial relationships"

### Issue: Carrying Pose Looks Awkward or Impossible

**Solution**:

- Describe weight distribution: "He supports her weight naturally with his left arm"
- Specify body positioning: "She drapes over his shoulder gracefully, head tilted naturally"
- Add "realistic human anatomy, believable pose mechanics"

### Issue: Lighting Too Harsh or Too Dark

**Solution**:

- Specify "soft natural window light, diffused through sheer curtains"
- Add "bright but not overexposed, gentle shadows"
- Mention "subtle HDR, balanced exposure across room"
- Request "high-key lighting, airy and bright atmosphere"

### Issue: Extra Fingers or Distorted Hands

**Solution**:

- Emphasize "realistic hand anatomy, proper finger count"

### Issue: Skin Looks Too Smooth or Plastic

- Mention "subtle skin imperfections, realistic complexion"

- Request "shallow depth of field, background softly blurred"
