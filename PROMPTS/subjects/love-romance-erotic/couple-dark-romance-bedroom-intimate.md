## Platform-Specific Tips

### ChatGPT / DALL-E

**Optimal Workflow**:

1. **Upload Reference Photos**: Upload his face photo and optionally her face photo for identity preservation
2. **Detailed Prompt**: Use complete prompt with all specifications
3. **Iterative Refinement**: Request adjustments to lighting, positioning, or expressions
4. **Specific Corrections**: "Adjust the hand positioning to be more natural" or "Soften the lighting on her face"

**Strengths**: Excellent fabric rendering (satin sheen), realistic skin texture, accurate hand anatomy, responsive to detailed instructions

**Tips**: Emphasize "realistic hands and fingers" and "natural anatomy" to avoid common AI errors

### MidJourney

**Optimal Settings**:

```
[Your detailed prompt] --ar 2:3 --style raw --stylize 400 --cref [URL to face reference] --cw 100
```

**Parameter Guidance**:

- `--ar 2:3`: Vertical portrait format (use `--ar 4:5` for Instagram, `--ar 16:9` for landscape)
- `--style raw`: Maximum photorealism, less artistic interpretation
- `--stylize 400`: Balanced aesthetic refinement (300-600 range recommended)
- `--cref [URL]`: Character reference for identity preservation
- `--cw 100`: Full character reference weight (0-100, higher = stronger likeness)

**Prompting Tips**:

- Add "editorial photography, Vogue, Harper's Bazaar style" for premium aesthetic
- Include "shot on Sony A1, 85mm f/1.4" for authentic photographic look
- Use "32K, hyperrealistic" for maximum detail
- Avoid overly complex sentences; MidJourney prefers descriptive phrase lists

### Adobe Firefly

**Optimal Approach**:

- Use "Photographic" style preset
- Select "Cinematic" lighting option
- Adjust warmth slider toward warm amber tones
- Use "Depth of Field: Shallow" setting
- Enable "High Detail" for skin and fabric textures

**Strengths**: Excellent with intimate lighting scenarios, strong fabric physics, good facial expression control

### Flux Photography Preset

**Settings**:

- Select "Photography" preset
- Guidance Scale: 7-10 (higher for strict adherence to prompt)
- Steps: 30-50 (more steps = more refined details)
- Emphasize "photorealistic, 8K, natural lighting"

**Tips**: Flux is excellent for moody cinematic photography with strong lighting control

### Stable Diffusion XL

**Recommended Checkpoints**:

- Realistic Vision XL
- DreamShaper XL
- Juggernaut XL

**Settings**:

- Sampling Method: DPM++ 2M Karras or Euler A
- Sampling Steps: 30-40

## Workflow Tips

### For Identity Preservation

1. **Collect Reference Photos**: Gather 3-5 well-lit photos of each person's face (front view, 3/4 view, profile)
2. **Upload References**: Use platform-specific identity features (DALL-E: direct upload, MidJourney: `--cref`)
3. **Emphasize Distinctive Features**: Explicitly mention unique characteristics (his stubble and jawline, her pink hair and tattoo)
4. **Iterate if Needed**: Request facial adjustments until likeness is accurate

### For Cinematic Lighting

1. **Specify Light Sources**: "Warm amber bedside lamps" rather than just "warm lighting"
2. **Describe Light Quality**: "Soft, diffused" vs. "harsh, direct"
3. **Mention Shadows**: "Soft cinematic shadows" creates depth
4. **Request HDR**: Ensures balanced exposure across highlights and shadows

### For Fabric Realism

1. **Name Specific Fabrics**: "Satin" has different rendering than "silk" or "cotton"
2. **Describe Fabric Behavior**: "Natural draping, realistic folds, subtle sheen"
3. **Mention Lighting Interaction**: "Luxurious highlights on satin fabric"
4. **Avoid Plastic Look**: Emphasize "natural, realistic fabric textures"

### For Intimate Poses

1. **Describe Body Positioning Clearly**: "He sits behind her, arm around waist"
2. **Specify Spatial Relationships**: "Leaning toward her neck" establishes proximity
3. **Mention Expressions**: "Calm possessive expression" vs. "intense stare" changes mood
4. **Balance Passion with Elegance**: "Passionate without explicit" maintains sophistication

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Hands Look Distorted

**Solution**: Add "realistic hands and fingers, proper hand anatomy, natural finger positioning" and in MidJourney use a hands LoRA or reference image

### Issue: Satin Looks Plastic or Fake

**Solution**: Specify "natural fabric draping, subtle sheen, realistic fabric folds" and mention specific lighting interaction with fabric

### Issue: Too Explicit or Inappropriate

**Solution**: Emphasize "elegant, sophisticated, tasteful, editorial fashion photography" and "passionate without being explicit"

### Issue: Lighting Too Bright or Flat

**Solution**: Specify "moody, low-key lighting, soft shadows, rich contrast, cinematic darkness" and mention specific warm amber practical lights

### Issue: Background Too Distracting

**Solution**: Request "shallow depth of field, soft bokeh background, blurred ambient details, focus on couple"

### Issue: Faces Don't Match References

**Solution**: Increase character reference weight (MidJourney `--cw 100`) or upload clearer reference photos with consistent lighting

### Issue: Composition Feels Off

**Solution**: Be specific about framing: "low-angle close-up, waist-up framing, vertical 2:3 composition"
