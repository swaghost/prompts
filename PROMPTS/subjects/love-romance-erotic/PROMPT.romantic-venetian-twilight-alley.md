## Venetian Architecture Specifics

**Distinctive Elements**:

- **Curved Stone Walls**: Organic, flowing (not rigid geometric)
- **Graceful Archways**: Rounded, elegant proportions
- **Centuries-Old**: Byzantine, Gothic, Renaissance influences
- **Hidden Canals**: Narrow alleyways near water
- **Hanging Lanterns**: Traditional Venetian street lighting

**Venice vs. Other European Cities**:

- Stone walls **curve** (unique to Venice's organic growth)
- Graceful, flowing architecture (not harsh angles)
- Proximity to canals and water
- Softer, more romantic architectural style
- Maritime city character

## Twilight Magic

- **Fading Light**: Between day and night, magical transition
- **Luminous Background**: Glowing, not dark
- **Twilight Shimmer**: Reflective quality on surfaces
- **Warm Lantern Glow**: Artificial light blending with natural
- **After Sunset**: Blue hour approaching
