# Architecture AI Prompt Pack - 15 Ready-to-Use Prompts

For architecture students and young architects. Replace bracketed fields with project-specific information.

## Prompt Formula

`PROJECT + CONTEXT + ARCHITECTURE + MATERIAL + LIGHTING + CAMERA + VISUAL STYLE`

## 01 - Residential Exterior

```text
Create a photorealistic architectural visualization of a contemporary [Indian residential house] located in [location]. Architecture should respond to the local climate and context, with [architectural features such as courtyards, deep overhangs, screens, verandahs or shaded openings]. Use [materials] as the primary material palette. Add realistic landscaping with [plants / trees / hardscape]. Lighting: [morning / afternoon / golden hour / overcast]. Camera: eye-level architectural photography, 35mm lens, balanced composition and realistic proportions. Style: high-end architectural photography, photorealistic materials, natural textures, realistic shadows and professional architectural visualization. Avoid fantasy architecture and unrealistic proportions.
```

## Five-Layer Formula

1. **Building:** What are you designing?
2. **Context:** Where is it, including location, climate, and surroundings?
3. **Material:** What is it made from?
4. **Light:** What should the atmosphere feel like?
5. **Style:** How should it look and be photographed?

Quick formula: `[BUILDING] + [LOCATION / CLIMATE] + [ARCHITECTURAL STYLE] + [MATERIALS] + [LIGHTING] + [CAMERA] + [VISUAL QUALITY]`.
