## CINEMATOGRAPHY

### Format:

- **1:1 square format** (1080×1080, 2160×2160, or higher)
- Social media optimized (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok compatible)
- Maintains cinematic quality despite square constraint

### Camera Approach:

**Prioritize Wide-Angle Compositions:**

- **Continuously communicate island scale** (not cramped)
- Multiple elements in frame simultaneously (harbor, hillside, lighthouse, sea visible together)
- Expansive geography (entire island comprehensible)
- Layered depth (foreground, midground, background)

**Camera Movements (Smooth Cinematic Transitions):**

**Scene 1:**

- High aerial opening (maximum altitude)
- Slow descent (gradual approach)
- Push toward main harbour (subtle dolly forward)

**Scene 2:**

- Wide forward-moving aerial (continued descent and approach)
- Gradual altitude decrease (closer but not too close)

**Scene 3:**

- Seamless transition to harbour-level (smooth cut or blend)
- Medium-wide waterfront tracking (lateral movement along docks)
- Layered composition maintained

**Scene 4:**

- Transition toward lighthouse (move to landmark)
- Gentle orbit around lighthouse (circular tracking, 90-180°)
- Begin pulling away (preparing for finale)

**Scene 5:**

- Grand aerial pullback (dramatic altitude gain and retreat)
- Complete island-wide hero shot (final reveal)

**Total Transitions:**

- **Two or three seamless cinematic transitions** during 15 seconds
- Smooth blending between scenes (no jarring cuts)
- Maintains flow and energy

**Avoid:**

- **Long static shots** (keep camera moving, energy maintained)
- **Repetitive straight-line camera motion** (vary movement types)
- **Extreme close-ups** (make environment appear tiny cheap toy)
- Cramped framing (always show scale context)

**Pacing:**

- **Fast, energetic** (construction happens rapidly)
- **Focused on satisfying formation process** (assembly is the star)
- Rhythmic (musical quality to construction)
- Dynamic (never boring, always progressing)
