## Description of Resulting Video

A cinematic vertical hyperlapse with smooth linear camera motion ascending the abandoned courtyard stairwell like a motorized slider traveling upward, maintaining consistent framing throughout while the sky above remains in constant fast time-lapse motion with clouds racing rapidly across frame. The camera executes smooth vertical upward movement throughout entire duration, traveling from lower stairwell levels toward the courtyard opening above where dramatic sky transformation provides temporal drama—white clouds gliding steadily across blue sky from harsh midday through warm golden late afternoon lighting transition. As camera glides upward, it reveals systematic architectural decay at each level: cracked concrete stairs with debris and biological growth, white/light walls with moisture stains and peeling paint spreading black mold, aggressive tropical vegetation colonization with vines and foliage at every level, broken or missing railings creating compositional voids, and the vertical relationship between stairwell interior and open courtyard above becoming progressively clearer. The interplay between smooth upward camera motion (revealing spatial progression) and accelerated sky time-lapse overhead (suggesting hours compressed) creates powerful dual temporal narrative—physical vertical journey through abandoned architecture combined with celestial time passage, both emphasizing the structure's prolonged exposure to tropical elements and complete abandonment.
