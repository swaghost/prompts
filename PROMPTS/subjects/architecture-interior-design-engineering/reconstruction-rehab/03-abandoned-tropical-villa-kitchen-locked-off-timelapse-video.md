## POSSIBLE MISCATEGORIZATION

## Description of Resulting Video

A cinematic locked-off fixed camera time-lapse maintaining one unbroken stationary take from an elevated interior perspective looking down into the abandoned kitchen space, with camera absolutely never moving throughout entire duration. The same exact framing persists from first frame to last—stainless steel countertop with filthy greasy pans, embedded corroded appliances, pink grease-stained walls, pink concrete floor with scattered gray tiles, white tile breakfast bar, and broken windows revealing lush tropical vegetation beyond. The dramatic visual interest comes entirely from natural lighting transformation as sunlight streaming through windows shifts position dramatically over accelerated hours, creating sweeping shadow patterns across interior surfaces while intensity and color temperature evolve throughout the day. Light shafts move visibly across countertops, walls, and floor as sun angle changes, emphasizing decay details through dynamic illumination while the abandoned kitchen itself remains frozen testament to interrupted domestic life. Sky visible through broken windows shows accelerated cloud movement overhead, contributing to atmospheric temporal passage while maintaining the powerful contrast between static human abandonment and nature's perpetual motion.
