## Description of Resulting Video

A cinematic time-lapse with locked-off fixed camera position maintaining one unbroken elevated take from three-quarter perspective, camera absolutely never moving throughout entire duration. The same exact framing persists from first frame to last—abandoned multi-level modernist house with exposed concrete construction, central courtyard featuring empty rectangular swimming pool with blue tiles, deteriorating white concrete deck, and lush tropical vegetation dominating throughout (massive trees, dense foliage cascading, vines crawling). The dramatic visual transformation comes entirely from accelerated sky above—time-lapse clouds racing rapidly across blue sky in constant fast motion, creating sweeping shadow patterns across modernist architecture and courtyard below as sun position shifts throughout compressed daylight hours. The interplay between static geometric human-made structures and dynamic celestial motion creates powerful narrative: abandoned modernist architecture frozen in tropical decay while nature's cycles continue perpetually overhead, shadows sweeping across concrete planes and vegetation, light quality evolving, and clouds suggesting hours of passage compressed into seconds.
