# Checklists & Quality Control - Architectural Text Prompts

## Overview

AI text prompts for generating comprehensive pre-delivery checklists, quality control protocols, and review procedures ensuring project completeness and compliance. Use ChatGPT or other AI text tools to create systematic verification processes, catch omissions, and ensure quality standards.

**Part of:** [Architectural Professional Text Tools Collection](architectural-professional-text.md)

---

## Use Cases

- **Pre-Delivery Review** - Final verification before handover
- **Quality Assurance** - Systematic quality verification
- **Code Compliance Verification** - Ensure regulatory compliance
- **Accessibility Review** - ADA/accessibility compliance check
- **Construction Milestone Checks** - Inspection at key phases
- **Drawing Set QC** - Coordination and completeness verification
- **Specification Review** - Check specs for completeness and coordination
- **Submittal Review** - Systematic review of contractor submittals
- **Punch List Organization** - Structured deficiency tracking
- **Commissioning Checklists** - System testing and verification

## Basic Prompt Templates

### Pre-Delivery Checklist

**Prompt:**

```
My project is a residential building with a leisure area on the ground floor and an apartment on the first floor.

Create a comprehensive pre-delivery checklist covering:
- Accessibility compliance (ramps, door widths, bathroom clearances)
- Safety requirements (railings, fire safety, emergency egress)
- Applicable NBR standards compliance (or local standards)
- Architectural quality and finish verification
- MEP systems completion and testing
- Landscape and site work completion
- Documentation and as-built drawing requirements
- Warranty and maintenance manual requirements
- Final cleaning and touch-up items
- Client orientation and handover process
```

**When to Use:**

- Project nearing completion
- Before final inspection
- Prior to client handover
- Certificate of occupancy application
- Final payment release
- Warranty period start

**Customization:**

- Replace project type with your building
- Add specific features or systems
- Change code references to local standards
- Include special requirements
- Add client-specific items

### Accessibility Compliance Checklist

**Prompt:**

```
Create accessibility compliance checklist for [project] ensuring compliance with [ADA/ICC A117.1/local accessibility code].

Verify:

SITE:
- Accessible route from parking to entrance
- Accessible parking spaces (number, size, signage, access aisle)
- Passenger loading zones
- Curb ramps (slope, width, detectable warnings)
- Ramps (slope, landings, handrails)
- Walks and paths (width, surface, running/cross slope)
- Exterior stairs (if applicable - handrails, nosings)

BUILDING ENTRANCE:
- Accessible entrance (number required, identification)
- Doors (width, maneuvering clearance, threshold, opening force)
- Hardware (height, operable with one hand, 5lb force)
- Vestibules (maneuvering space)
- Ramps or lifts if level changes

INTERIOR:
- Accessible routes (width, surface, slope, turning space)
- Doors (all requirements throughout)
- Corridors (width, turning spaces, protruding objects)
- Elevators (car size, controls, signage, communication)
- Platform lifts (if applicable)
- Stairs (handrails both sides, extensions, nosings, tactile warnings)

ACCESSIBLE TOILET ROOMS:
- Number and location
- Turning space (60" diameter or T-turn)
- Stall size and configuration (60" wide min, 59" deep for floor-mounted, 56" deep for wall-mounted)
- Toilet (height 17"-19" to top of seat, centerline 16"-18" from side wall)
- Grab bars (side and rear walls, heights, lengths, mounting)
- Lavatory (knee and toe clearance, rim height, faucet reach, insulated pipes)
- Accessories (heights, reach ranges, operation)
- Door swing and maneuvering clearance
- Signage (ISA symbol, tactile, Braille)

DRINKING FOUNTAINS:
- Number (one high, one low if more than one)
- Spout height (36" max for accessible)
- Knee clearance
- Reach to controls

SIGNAGE:
- Room identification (tactile, Braille, mounting height, contrast)
- Directional and informational (visual contrast, finish)
- Exit signs (illuminated, accessible means of egress)

REACH RANGES:
- Forward reach (48" max unobstructed)
- Side reach (48" max for unobstructed)
- Obstructed reach requirements

CONTROLS AND OPERATING MECHANISMS:
- Light switches (48" max height)
- Thermostats (48" max height)
- Electrical outlets (15" min height)
- Operable with one hand, no tight grasping

KITCHENS (if applicable):
- Work surface heights
- Knee and toe clearances
- Appliance controls and reach
- Sink depth

Include:
- Requirements for each space type
- Specific dimensional requirements
- Common violations to avoid
- Inspection and verification procedures
```

- Fire protection plans (sprinkler layout if applicable)

- Plumbing fixtures match finish schedule

### Passive House Checklist

**Prompt:**

```
Create Passive House certification checklist for [project]:

DESIGN VERIFICATION:
- PHPP (Passive House Planning Package) modeling complete
- Space heating demand ≤15 kWh/(m²·yr)
- Space cooling demand ≤15 kWh/(m²·yr)
- Primary energy demand ≤120 kWh/(m²·yr) (or 60 for Passive House Plus/Premium)
- Airtightness ≤0.6 ACH50
- Overheating <10% hours over 25°C

ENVELOPE REQUIREMENTS:
- U-values meet Passive House requirements (climate-specific)
- Thermal bridge-free design (Ψ ≤0.01 W/mK)
- Window U-values ≤0.8 W/m²K
- Window g-values appropriate for climate
- Window installation per certified details
- Air barrier continuity (no gaps or holes)
- Insulation continuity (no thermal bridges)

MECHANICAL SYSTEMS:
- Heat recovery ventilation (≥75% efficiency)
- Space heating system (if needed, minimal)
- Space cooling system (if needed, minimal)
- Domestic hot water (efficient system)
- Renewable energy (for Plus/Premium)

DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED:
- PHPP model (verified)
- Thermal bridge calculations
- Window certifications
- Ventilation unit certification
- Construction details (thermal bridge-free)
- Quality assurance plan

TESTING:
- Blower door test (preliminary and final)
- Air leakage locations identified and sealed
- Ventilation system commissioning
- Thermal imaging (recommended)

VERIFICATION:
- Third-party certification application
- Site inspections by certifier
- Testing results
- Final certification
```

## Professional Use Guidelines

### Appropriate Applications

✅ **Good for:**

- Creating checklist templates
- Identifying common items to verify
- Organizing review processes
- Training junior staff
- Establishing QC protocols
- Standardizing procedures

✅ **With Professional Customization:**

- All checklists need project-specific customization
- Add items unique to your project
- Remove items that don't apply
- Adjust for local codes and standards
- Adapt to firm standards and procedures
- Verify with team and consultants

---

### Best Practices

**Always:**

- Customize AI checklists for specific project
- Add project-specific critical items
- Verify code and standard references
- Use as minimum baseline, add your expertise
- Review with full project team
- Update as project progresses
- Document deviations and reasons
- Keep records of completed checklists

**Never:**

- Use generic checklist without customization
- Rely solely on AI for quality control
- Skip items that seem unimportant
- Ignore professional judgment
- Rush through verification process

---

### Risk Management

1. **Professional Responsibility** - QC is professional obligation
2. **Customize for Project** - Generic lists miss specifics
3. **Multiple Reviews** - Different eyes catch different things
4. **Document Everything** - Keep records of reviews and findings
5. **Follow Through** - Ensure identified issues are resolved
6. **Don't Skip Steps** - Shortcuts lead to problems
7. **Involve Team** - Consultants, contractors, specialists
8. **Code Compliance** - Verify against adopted codes
