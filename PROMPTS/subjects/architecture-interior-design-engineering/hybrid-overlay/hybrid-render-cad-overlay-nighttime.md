# Hybrid Render + CAD Overlay – Nighttime Architecture Version

## Description

A cinematic nighttime architectural visualization style combining photorealistic rendering with technical drafting overlays, emphasizing dramatic lighting contrasts between warm interior glow and cool exterior darkness. Features modern architecture with controlled mood lighting including fireplaces, wall sconces, LED strips, diffused ambient light, and soft exterior reflections. Architectural annotations remain visible but subtle using white or soft-blue linework, glowing annotation effects, and clean schematic overlays that complement the nighttime atmosphere. Two annotation modes available: hand-drawn with soft chalk-like lines and warm sketch strokes, or CAD-precise with luminous vector lines and crisp technical symbols. Creates atmospheric architectural presentations that balance dramatic lighting mood with technical documentation clarity for evening and nighttime scenarios.

## Nighttime Lighting Specifications

### Time of Day Options

**Blue Hour / Twilight (Preferred):**

- Time: 15-30 minutes after sunset
- Exterior: Deep blue sky with residual ambient light
- Interior: Warm artificial lights turned on
- Balance: Beautiful contrast between cool blue exterior and warm interior
- Sky: Gradient from dark blue at top to lighter blue-purple at horizon
- Best for: Residential exteriors, restaurants, hospitality

**Full Night:**

- Time: 1-2 hours after sunset, complete darkness
- Exterior: Near-black sky with stars (if visible)
- Interior: Full artificial lighting reliance
- Balance: Maximum contrast between dark exterior and warm interior
- Atmosphere: Dramatic, moody, intimate
- Best for: Urban settings, bars, nightclubs, theatrical spaces

**Dusk (Early Evening):**

- Time: Just at sunset, still some daylight remaining
- Exterior: Orange-purple sunset colors transitioning to blue
- Interior: Lights beginning to activate but still ambient daylight present
- Balance: Transitional lighting with both natural and artificial
- Atmosphere: Warm, inviting, golden hour extending into evening
- Best for: Outdoor dining, terraces, residential transitions

### Interior Warm Lighting (2200K-2700K)

**Fireplace Lighting:**

- Warm orange-yellow flickering glow (1800K-2000K)
- Dancing light patterns on nearby surfaces
- Cozy intimate atmosphere
- Realistic fire behavior (not static)
- Soft shadows with warm edges

**Wall Sconces:**

- Warm diffused uplight or downlight

- Gentle shadows enhancing texture

**LED Strip Lighting:**

- Linear continuous warm glow

- Subtle indirect illumination
- Modern architectural integration
- Even consistent light distribution

**Pendant Lights:**

- Warm downward cast light over tables or counters
- Visible bulbs or diffused shades

- Focused task lighting with ambient spill
- Contemporary fixtures with warm edison bulbs or diffused globes

**Table & Floor Lamps:**

- Soft ambient pools of warm light
- Fabric or paper shades diffusing light
- Intimate scale lighting
- Warm inviting glow zones
- Reading or task light quality

**Ambient Diffused Light:**

- Overall soft warm illumination filling space
- No visible source (integrated or hidden)
- Creates comfortable welcoming atmosphere
- Soft shadows, minimal contrast
- Warm color cast throughout interior

### Exterior Cool Lighting (4000K-6500K)

**Moonlight (Natural):**

**Landscape Uplighting:**

- Grazing lights on facade textures

- Complementing interior warm glow

### Lighting Contrast & Balance

**Warm-Cool Contrast:**

- Interior warm (2500K) vs exterior cool (5000K+)
- Creates visual interest and depth
- Emphasizes indoor-outdoor relationship
- Cozy interior invitation against cool night
- Cinematic Hollywood lighting aesthetic

**Controlled Contrast Ratio:**

- Interior brightness: Moderate (not blown out)
- Exterior darkness: Visible detail (not pure black)
- Balanced exposure showing both interior and exterior
- Windows showing interior warmth without overexposure
- Exterior still has form definition and texture visibility

**Soft Reflections:**

- Interior light reflecting on exterior glass
- Warm glow reflecting on wet pavement or water
- Subtle material reflections (polished floors, metal, glass)
- Adds depth and realism
- Romantic atmospheric quality

**Shadow Quality:**

- Soft warm shadows inside from multiple light sources
- Cool subtle shadows outside from ambient light
- No harsh black shadows (retain detail)
- Layered shadow complexity from multiple sources
- Ambient occlusion visible in corners and recesses

## Nighttime Annotation Overlay Specifications

### Annotation Visibility Strategies for Dark Backgrounds

**Light-Colored Linework:**

- **White** (#FFFFFF) — Maximum visibility on dark backgrounds
- **Soft Blue** (#8DB4E2, #A4C8E1) — Cool technical feel complementing nighttime
- **Warm Beige/Cream** (#E8DCC4, #F5EFE7) — Softer contrast, vintage blueprint feel
- **Light Gray** (#CCCCCC, #D3D3D3) — Subtle technical overlay

**Subtle Glow Effect (Optional):**

- Very slight outer glow on annotation lines (1-2px)
- Enhances visibility without being distracting
- Mimics subtle illumination effect
- Particularly effective for CAD-precise mode
- Creates "luminous overlay" aesthetic

**Opacity & Transparency:**

- 75-90% opacity for primary annotations
- Slightly higher opacity than daytime overlays for visibility
- Still maintains some transparency for integration
- Balance between visibility and subtlety

### Annotation Elements for Nighttime

**Lighting Diagrams (Priority for Nighttime):**

- Light source locations marked with symbols
- Illumination direction arrows
- Light type labels: "Fireplace," "LED strip," "Pendant light"
- Lumens or wattage specifications
- Color temperature notes (2700K warm, 5000K cool)

**Ceiling Height Markers:**

- Standard dimension arrows (white or soft blue)
- Format: "H= 3.0m" or "Ceiling: 10'-0""
- Visible against dark ceiling or sky areas

**Material Callouts:**

- Emphasis on reflective materials: "Polished concrete floor," "Glass facade"
- Light-reflecting surface notes
- Material finish specifications affecting lighting

**Window & Opening Dimensions:**

- Glazing dimensions showing interior-exterior connection
- Frame specifications
- Transparency and light transmission notes

**Section Indicators:**

- Section cut lines showing spatial layers
- Particularly useful for showing lighting layers
- Interior-exterior relationship diagrams

**Atmospheric Notes:**

- "Warm interior glow 2700K"
- "Cool exterior ambient 5000K"
- "Fireplace ambient lighting"
- "Soft reflections on polished surfaces"

### Overlay Layout for Nighttime

**Strategic Placement:**

- Over dark sky or exterior: Lighting diagrams, legends, section cuts
- Over dark foreground: Material callouts, dimension lines
- Margins: Extensive notes and annotations
- Minimal overlay on illuminated interior zones (preserve warm glow)

**Annotation Density:**

- 25-40% coverage (less than daytime to preserve atmosphere)
- Concentrated in darker areas to maintain lighting drama
- Avoid obscuring light sources and glowing elements
- Preserve cinematic mood while adding technical info
