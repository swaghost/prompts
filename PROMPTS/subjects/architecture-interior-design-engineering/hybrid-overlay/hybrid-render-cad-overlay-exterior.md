# Hybrid Render + CAD Overlay – Exterior Architecture Version

## Description

A sophisticated exterior architectural visualization style combining photorealistic 3D rendering with technical drafting overlays for modern building exteriors. Features dynamic curved geometry, timber-and-glass construction, realistic outdoor materials, natural daylight, and environmental integration with integrated architectural annotations including structural notes, elevation markers, dimension lines, arrows, material callouts, and schematic diagrams. The technical overlay enhances understanding of the building's form, materials, and spatial relationships while preserving the beauty of the photorealistic rendering. Two annotation modes available: hand-drawn sketch aesthetic with organic character, or CAD-precise technical drafting with engineering accuracy. Ideal for showcasing building exteriors with both visual appeal and technical clarity.

## Usage

Perfect for architectural competition boards, exterior design presentations, building permit visualizations, real estate development marketing, architectural firm portfolios, design review submissions, facade studies with technical documentation, urban planning presentations, architectural student exterior projects, construction documentation visualization, contractor coordination drawings, building envelope design presentations, material specification showcases, structural system diagrams, sustainability feature callouts, zoning review graphics, community engagement presentations, architectural book illustrations, exhibition boards, and any exterior architectural visualization requiring both aesthetic impact and technical information.

- **AI Image Platform**: Midjourney v6.1/v7 (--style raw), DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion XL with architecture models, Leonardo AI PhotoReal
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 landscape (standard), 3:2 or 4:3 (presentation boards), 4:5 vertical (portfolio/social)
- **Style Mode**: Photorealistic exterior architectural rendering + technical annotation overlay
- **Annotation Choice**: Hand-drawn sketch mode OR CAD-precise mode
- **Scene Type**: Building exterior with surrounding environment/context

## Exterior Architecture Specifications

### Building Form & Geometry

**Dynamic Curved Geometry:**

- Organic flowing rooflines (curved, undulating, sculptural)
- Cantilevered sections and dramatic overhangs
- Asymmetric compositions with visual interest
- Geometric complexity balanced with clean lines
- Contemporary interpretation of natural forms

**Volumetric Composition:**

- Primary volume with secondary supporting elements
- Clear hierarchy of masses (larger to smaller)
- Interesting positive and negative space relationships
- Integration of solid and void (windows, openings, terraces)
- Dynamic silhouette against sky

**Architectural Features:**

- Large glazing expanses (floor-to-ceiling windows)
- Exposed structural elements (timber beams, steel columns)
- Outdoor terraces, decks, or balconies
- Green roofs or planted areas
- Cantilevered volumes or projections
- Sculptural staircases or ramps

### Material Palette (Timber + Glass Focus)

**Timber Elements (Primary Material):**

- **Type**: Exposed structural timber beams, cross-laminated timber (CLT), glulam columns, timber cladding
- **Species**: Douglas fir, cedar, oak, larch, or pine
- **Finish**: Natural weathered gray, oiled wood with visible grain, clear sealed, or charred (shou sugi ban)
- **Application**: Structural beams, roof structure, facade cladding, pergolas, decking
- **Details**: Visible joints and connections, end grain, natural wood texture
- **Weathering**: Realistic weathering patterns if exposed (silver-gray patina)

**Glass Facades (Primary Material):**

- **Type**: Large format glazing, floor-to-ceiling curtain walls, frameless glass systems
- **Characteristics**: High transparency, subtle reflections of sky and environment, minimal frames
- **Framing**: Slim black steel, aluminum, or timber frames (minimal visual impact)
- **Details**: Mullions and transoms visible, realistic glass reflections and refractions
- **Function**: Views, natural light, visual connection indoor-outdoor

**Concrete (Supporting Material):**

- Exposed cast-in-place concrete walls or foundations
- Board-formed texture or smooth troweled finish
- Natural gray tones or pigmented concrete
- Foundation walls, retaining walls, structural walls
- Realistic formwork patterns and construction details

**Metal Cladding (Supporting Material):**

- Corten steel (rust patina), zinc, copper, or aluminum panels
- Standing seam metal roofing
- Industrial aesthetic or refined modern finish
- Weathering and patina realistic
- Seams, fasteners, and panel joints visible

**Natural Stone (Supporting Material):**

- Local stone for walls, bases, or accents
- Dry-stacked or mortared stone masonry
- Natural color variation and texture
- Integration with landscape

**Green Roofs / Living Walls:**

- Vegetation on roofs or facades
- Native grasses, sedums, or planted gardens
- Realistic plant growth and natural appearance
- Integration with sustainable design narrative

### Exterior Lighting (Natural Daylight)

**Daytime Natural Light (Primary Scenario):**

- **Quality**: Soft to moderate natural daylight
- **Sun Position**: Side lighting or three-quarter lighting (not directly overhead)
- **Time of Day**: Mid-morning to mid-afternoon (10am-3pm ideal)
- **Sky Condition**: Clear sky with some clouds, or overcast for even light
- **Color Temperature**: 5000K-6500K (neutral daylight to slightly cool)
- **Shadows**: Defined shadows with soft edges showing form and depth
- **Environmental Reflections**: Sky reflecting in glass, surroundings visible in windows

**Shadow Quality:**

- Shadows cast by building onto ground and itself
- Soft shadow gradients (not harsh black shadows)
- Ambient occlusion in recesses and under overhangs
- Realistic shadow density based on sun angle
- Shadows reveal building form and geometry

**Material Interaction with Light:**

- Timber: Warm tones in sunlight, visible grain texture
- Glass: Reflections of sky, trees, clouds; semi-transparent showing interior
- Concrete: Cool tones, subtle texture visible in light
- Metal: Reflective highlights, material-specific sheen
- Stone: Natural color variation enhanced by light and shadow

### Environmental Context & Landscaping

**Site Context:**

- Natural landscape setting (preferred): forest edge, meadow, hillside, waterfront
- Urban context (alternative): streetscape, plaza, neighboring buildings
- Appropriate scale relationship between building and site
- Clear ground plane showing topography

**Landscape Elements:**

- Native vegetation: trees, shrubs, grasses
- Hardscape: pathways, patios, retaining walls, steps
- Natural terrain: slopes, berms, natural grade changes
- Water features (if applicable): ponds, streams, rain gardens

**Sky & Atmosphere:**

- Realistic sky (not generic blue gradient)
- Cloud formations appropriate to lighting scenario
- Atmospheric depth showing distance
- Sky color consistent with time of day and weather

**Foreground, Middle Ground, Background:**

- Foreground: Immediate site elements (plants, ground cover, paths)
- Middle Ground: The building itself (primary focus)
- Background: Distant landscape, trees, sky, horizon

## Exterior Annotation Overlay Specifications

### Annotation Elements for Exteriors

**Elevation Markers:**

- Ground level: ±0.00m or 0'-0"
- Floor levels: +3.50m, +7.00m (metric) or corresponding imperial
- Roof peak elevation
- Terrace and deck levels
- Foundation depth (if visible in section)

**Dimension Lines:**

- Building width and length
- Height from grade to roof peak
- Cantilever dimensions (overhang distances)
- Window and door opening sizes
- Spacing between structural elements

**Material Callouts:**

- "Timber CLT beams, Douglas fir, natural oil finish"
- "Floor-to-ceiling glazing, low-E glass"
- "Board-formed concrete wall"
- "Corten steel cladding, weathered finish"
- "Green roof, native sedum planting"

**Structural Notes:**

- Beam span dimensions
- Column locations and grid references
- Cantilever support systems
- Foundation type notes
- Roof structure descriptions

**Section Indicators:**

- Section cut lines showing where building is sliced
- Section reference symbols (A-A, B-B, etc.)
- Direction arrows for section views
- Small section diagrams in margins

**Site Context Annotations:**

- North arrow indicating orientation
- Property lines or site boundaries
- Landscape element labels (existing trees, new plantings)
- Driveway or path dimensions
- Setback dimensions

**Sustainable Features Callouts:**

- Solar panel locations
- Rainwater collection systems
- Passive ventilation strategies
- Thermal mass indicators
- Daylighting strategies

### Overlay Layout for Exteriors

**Annotation Distribution:**

- Margins: Dimension lines, elevation markers, notes
- Over sky: Section diagrams, material legends, detail callouts
- Over landscape: Site context annotations, north arrow
- Directly on building: Material pointers, structural callouts
- Balance: 30-50% annotation coverage, 50-70% clean render visible

**Layer Hierarchy:**

- Primary annotations: Building dimensions, major material callouts
- Secondary annotations: Detail notes, smaller dimensions
- Tertiary annotations: Reference symbols, legends

### Mode 1: Hand-Drawn Exterior Annotations (Details)

**Sketch Line Quality:**

- Organic slightly uneven lines (not perfectly straight)
- Pencil or charcoal sketch appearance
- Varying line weights showing pressure variation
- Loose gestural quality for dimension lines
- Expressive designer sketching feel

**Handwritten Notes:**

- Architect's lettering style or organic handwriting
- Slightly irregular letter sizing and spacing
- Underlines or circles for emphasis
- Natural human imperfections
- Still legible and clear

**Exterior-Specific Sketch Elements:**

- Quick tree or landscape gesture sketches
- Loose section cut lines
- Freehand north arrow
- Organic dimension arrows
- Sketchy cloud or sky texture overlays (subtle)

**Overall Feel:**

- Concept design development aesthetic
- Working drawing energy
- Designer's notebook overlay
- Artistic and approachable

### Mode 2: CAD-Precise Exterior Annotations (Details)

**Technical Line Quality:**

- Perfectly straight vector lines
- Uniform stroke weight throughout
- Crisp clean edges (no fuzziness)
- Geometric precision for all elements
- Professional drafting appearance

**Technical Typography:**

- Sans-serif architectural fonts (Arial, Helvetica, DIN)
- All-caps for labels, mixed case for notes
- Consistent sizing and spacing
- Justified alignment to dimension lines
- Maximum legibility

**Exterior-Specific Technical Elements:**

- Standard architectural elevation markers (+3.50m format)
- CAD-style dimension arrows and extension lines
- Technical north arrow symbol
- Standard section cut symbols
- Grid reference notations
- Material hatch patterns in small diagrams

**Overall Feel:**

- Construction documentation quality
- Engineering precision
- Professional consultant deliverable
- Corporate presentation standard
