# Hybrid Render + CAD Overlay – Interior with People Version

## Description

A sophisticated architectural visualization style combining photorealistic interior rendering with technical drafting overlays, featuring human presence to show scale and lifestyle. Modern lounge, café, or studio spaces with concrete walls, warm wood elements, contemporary furniture, soft natural lighting, and people interacting naturally—sitting, walking, conversing—seamlessly integrated with realistic lighting and shadows. Architectural annotations including ceiling height markers, lighting diagrams, material notes, furniture layout lines, and sectional sketches overlay the scene while preserving both the spatial beauty and the human narrative. Two annotation modes available: hand-drawn sketch aesthetic with expressive designer-style character, or CAD-precise technical drafting with structured layout diagrams. Ideal for showing both architectural design intent and lived spatial experience with technical documentation.

## Usage

Perfect for hospitality design presentations, coworking space concepts, café and restaurant design pitches, retail interior proposals, corporate office visualizations, residential living space studies, community space planning, architectural lifestyle marketing, interior design portfolio work, real estate staging concepts, workspace design documentation, student lounge designs, library reading room proposals, gallery and exhibition space planning, boutique hotel interiors, wellness center designs, fitness studio concepts, collaborative workspace layouts, educational facility interiors, mixed-use development interior zones, architectural human-scale studies, and any interior visualization requiring both lifestyle narrative and technical architectural clarity.

- **Style Mode**: Photorealistic interior rendering + people + technical annotation overlay
- **Annotation Choice**: Hand-drawn sketch mode OR CAD-precise mode

## Interior with People Specifications

### Human Figure Integration

**Number of People:**

- Small intimate space: 2-4 people
- Medium space (café, lounge): 4-6 people
- Large space (coworking, restaurant): 6-10 people
- Density: Not overcrowded, comfortable spacing showing circulation

**Human Scale & Proportions:**

- **Accurate Height**: Adults 1.65m-1.85m (5'5"-6'1") average
- **Realistic Proportions**: Natural body proportions, no elongation or compression
- **Proper Scale**: People sized correctly relative to furniture and architecture
- **Diversity**: Mixed gender, age (adults primarily), ethnicity representation
- **Clothing**: Contemporary casual or business casual appropriate to space type

**Natural Interactions & Activities:**

**Seated Activities:**

- Sitting on chairs, sofas, benches with natural posture
- Leaning forward in conversation
- Relaxed lounging with one leg crossed
- Working on laptops at tables
- Reading books or looking at phones
- Drinking coffee or eating (café settings)

**Standing Activities:**

- Walking through space naturally
- Standing in conversation groups (2-3 people)
- Leaning against walls or counters
- Browsing shelves or looking at displays
- Waiting at service counters
- Transitioning between zones

**Conversation & Social Interaction:**

- Two people facing each other talking
- Small groups in circular arrangements
- One person speaking, others listening naturally
- Casual body language (not stiff or posed)
- Natural gestures (hands moving, leaning in)
- Eye contact between conversing people

**Solo Activities:**

- Individual working quietly on laptop
- Person reading alone in corner
- Someone looking out window contemplatively
- Solo coffee drinking or waiting
- Checking phone while standing

**Lighting & Shadow on People:**

- Natural shadows cast by people onto floor and furniture
- Soft lighting on faces and clothing
- Realistic fabric textures and folds
- Hair showing natural light interaction
- No overly dramatic lighting (keep natural)

## Annotation Overlay Specifications (Interior with People)

### Interior-Specific Annotation Elements

**Ceiling Height Markers:**

- Floor-to-ceiling dimension arrows
- Format: "H= 3.6m" or "Ceiling Ht: 12'-0""
- Positioned vertically along walls
- Shows spatial volume and openness

**Furniture Layout Lines:**

- Light guide lines showing furniture placement grid
- Circulation path indicators
- Zone delineation lines (lounge area, work area, etc.)
- Furniture spacing dimensions

**Lighting Diagrams:**

- Pendant light location markers
- Window light direction arrows
- Artificial light coverage zones
- "Natural light" or "Ambient lighting" callouts

**Material Notes:**

- "Polished concrete floor"
- "Oak wood table, oiled finish"
- "Upholstered linen seating"
- "Board-formed concrete wall"
- Material pointers with leader lines

**Sectional Sketches:**

- Small section cuts in margins showing ceiling/wall/floor relationships
- Furniture profile diagrams
- Simplified spatial section overlays

**Human Scale References:**

- Optional dimension showing human height (1.75m / 5'9")
- Scale figures in margin for reference
- "Human scale" note emphasizing comfortable proportions

**Activity Zone Labels:**

- "Quiet focus area"
- "Collaborative zone"
- "Circulation path"
- "Service counter"
- Zone boundary lines (subtle)

### Overlay Placement Considerations

**Avoid Obscuring People:**

- Annotations around people, not across faces
- Keep people clearly visible and recognizable
- Annotations in margins, over ceiling, over floor
- Minimal overlay directly on human figures

**Annotation Distribution:**

- Upper third: Ceiling height markers, lighting diagrams
- Margins: Material notes, furniture dimensions
- Lower third: Floor material callouts, furniture layout lines
- Over background: Section diagrams, legends

**Balance with Human Narrative:**

- 25-40% annotation coverage (less than pure architectural renders)
- Preserve lifestyle feel and human story
- Technical overlay informs but doesn't dominate
- Clean breathable layout

## Pro Tips

### Human Integration Excellence

**Natural People Prompting:**

- Specify activities explicitly: "people sitting at tables talking," "person walking with coffee"
- Request "natural casual interactions" and "realistic body language"
- Include "people seamlessly integrated with realistic lighting and shadows"
- Avoid "posing" or "portrait" keywords—emphasize "lifestyle" and "natural moments"

**Scale & Proportion:**

- Mention "accurate human proportions and scale relative to furniture"
- Include "realistic human heights" in prompt
- Specify "people appropriately sized" to avoid giant or tiny figures
- Reference "human-scale furniture and architecture"

**Diversity & Representation:**

- Request "diverse group of people" for inclusive representation
- Specify "mixed gender and age" for realistic demographics
- Include "contemporary casual clothing" appropriate to space type
- Avoid specific celebrity names or recognizable faces

### Balancing People & Architecture

**Composition Strategy:**

- People should be naturally distributed, not all clustered in one area
- Some people in foreground (larger), some in middle/background (smaller) for depth
- Leave circulation paths clear to show spatial flow
- Show both occupied and empty spaces for breathing room

**Lighting People & Space Together:**

- Request "soft natural lighting on both people and interior"
- Include "realistic shadows from people onto floor"
- Specify "people naturally lit from window light"
- Avoid theatrical or dramatic lighting that feels staged

### Annotation Overlay with People

**Preserve Human Narrative:**

- Specify "annotations around people, not obscuring faces"
- Request "25-35% annotation coverage" (less than pure architectural renders)
- Include "preserve lifestyle feel while adding technical information"
- Mention "human story remains primary focus"

**Strategic Placement:**

- Annotations in upper third (ceiling area), lower third (floor), and margins
- Avoid center where people typically are positioned
- Use background areas (walls, ceiling) for larger diagrams
- Keep annotations subtle and balanced

### Platform-Specific Strategies

**For Midjourney with People:**

- Midjourney v6+ excellent at natural human figures in architectural spaces
- Specify number of people: "4 people sitting and conversing"
- Include specific activities: "person working on laptop," "two people talking"
- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic people, not stylized
- May need multiple generations to get natural interactions right

**For DALL-E 3:**

- DALL-E generally good at people in scenes
- Be very specific about people positions and activities
- Include "photorealistic human figures" explicitly
- May produce slightly more formal poses—request "casual relaxed atmosphere"

**For Stable Diffusion:**

- Use models trained on people in realistic settings (Realistic Vision)
- Very detailed human description needed
- Strong negative prompt for human artifacts (distorted limbs, wrong proportions)
- Consider separate generation + compositing for maximum control

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Problem: People Look Stiff or Mannequin-Like**

- **Solution**: Request "natural relaxed poses," "casual body language," "authentic interactions," avoid "posing" keyword

**Problem: Wrong Scale (People Too Big or Too Small)**

- **Solution**: Specify "accurate human scale," "realistic proportions," "people sized correctly relative to furniture"

**Problem: Awkward or Distorted Limbs**

- **Solution**: Use Midjourney or DALL-E (better at human anatomy), include "realistic human proportions" in prompt, strong negative: "no distorted limbs"

**Problem: Annotations Obscuring People**

- **Solution**: Specify "annotations around people," "people clearly visible," "overlay does not obscure faces"

**Problem: People Look Cloned or Identical**

- **Solution**: Request "diverse group," "people with different appearances," negative: "no cloned people"

**Problem: Space Feels Overcrowded**

- **Solution**: Reduce number of people, specify "comfortable spacing," "not crowded," show empty circulation areas
