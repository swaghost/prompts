## Common Requests & Solutions

### Too Dark
"Increase overall exposure by one stop while maintaining shadow detail"

### Too Bright/Washed Out
"Reduce highlights, increase contrast slightly, maintain material color saturation"

### Flat/No Dimension
"Add atmospheric depth, increase shadow definition, enhance material texture"

### Unrealistic Materials
"Increase material texture detail, add appropriate reflections, subtle color variation"

### Wrong Time of Day
"Adjust lighting to [golden hour/midday/overcast], maintain composition"

### Needs More Drama
"Increase shadow depth, add directional lighting, enhance contrast and atmosphere"

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Composition Changed:** Building altered from original  
❌ **Over-Processed:** Too much contrast, saturation, sharpening  
❌ **Unrealistic Materials:** Plastic-looking, wrong reflections  
❌ **Wrong Lighting:** Time of day inconsistent with shadows  
❌ **Lost Detail:** Over-smoothing or blur  
❌ **Artificial Look:** Obviously computer-generated  
❌ **Inconsistent Style:** Mixed aesthetics confusing message  

✅ **Instead:** Preserve composition, subtle enhancements, realistic materials, consistent lighting, maintain detail, photographic realism, cohesive professional style
