# Architectural Transform: Rain - Moody Weather Scene

**Style**: Cinematic moody rainy day architectural photography

---

## PROMPT

```
Transform the reference scene into a realistic rainy day. Add visible natural rain, an overcast sky, soft diffused lighting, wet vegetation, water droplets on surfaces, and subtle reflections across the driveway and car. Preserve the original architecture, façade materials, landscaping, geometry, proportions, and composition. Keep the warm interior lights visible through the windows, creating contrast with the cool exterior. Cinematic, moody, photorealistic luxury real-estate photography.
```

---

- Wet vegetation
- Water droplets on surfaces
- Subtle reflections (driveway, car)

- Contrast between warm interior and cool exterior
- Cinematic moody atmosphere
