## Technical Specifications

### Window Visibility Balance

**Reflection vs. Transparency:**

The key challenge is balancing glass reflections with interior visibility.

**Daytime:**

- More reflection (60% reflection / 40% transparency)
- Sky and surroundings visible in glass
- Interior visible but not dominant
- Soft interior lighting present

**Golden Hour:**

- Balanced (50% reflection / 50% transparency)
- Warm sky reflections
- Interior warmly lit and visible
- Beautiful balanced quality

**Dusk:**

- Less reflection (30% reflection / 70% transparency)
- Twilight sky in glass
- Interior lighting prominent
- Dramatic contrast building

**Night:**

- Minimal reflection (10% reflection / 90% interior)
- Dark sky barely reflects
- Interior brilliantly visible
- Maximum drama and glow

**Keywords:**

- "Balance of reflection and transparency"
- "Realistic window reflections balanced with interior visibility"
- "Glazing showing both reflection and interior appropriately"
- "Controlled reflections with visible interior"

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Too Much Reflection:** Can't see interior at all  
❌ **No Reflection:** Glass looks like open holes  
❌ **Wrong Time Coordination:** Bright interior with bright exterior  
❌ **Fake Interiors:** Obvious CGI or unrealistic spaces  
❌ **Inconsistent Lighting:** Interior lighting doesn't match exterior time  
❌ **Overlit Interiors:** Too bright, washed out  
❌ **Underlit Interiors:** Too dark, can't see details  
❌ **Lost Composition:** Interior views distract from architecture

✅ **Instead:** Balanced reflections, realistic glass performance, coordinated lighting, photorealistic interiors, appropriate interior brightness, consistent time of day, composition enhanced not lost
