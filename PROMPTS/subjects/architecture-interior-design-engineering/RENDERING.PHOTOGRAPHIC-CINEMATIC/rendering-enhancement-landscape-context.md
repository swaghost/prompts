## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final result:

- [ ] Original composition exactly preserved
- [ ] Comprehensive landscape added appropriately
- [ ] Hardscape elements realistic and integrated
- [ ] Sky and atmosphere compelling
- [ ] Context surroundings appropriate
- [ ] Depth clearly established (foreground/mid/background)
- [ ] Lighting consistent throughout scene
- [ ] Seasonal character consistent if specified
- [ ] Scale of elements correct (trees, people, cars)
- [ ] Environmental story coherent
- [ ] Building remains focal point
- [ ] 4K photorealistic quality maintained

---

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Overgrown Landscape:** Vegetation overwhelming architecture  
❌ **Scale Issues:** Trees, people, cars wrong size  
❌ **No Depth:** Flat appearance, missing layering  
❌ **Inconsistent Season:** Mixed seasonal signals  
❌ **Wrong Context:** Urban building in rural setting  
❌ **Too Busy:** Too many elements competing  
❌ **Fake Sky:** Unrealistic or poorly integrated sky  
❌ **Lost Building:** Architecture not prominent enough

✅ **Instead:** Balanced landscape, correct scale, clear depth layers, consistent season, appropriate context, composed not cluttered, realistic sky, architecture still emphasized

## Iteration Strategy

"Add more mature trees, enhance foreground planting, improve sky interest"

### Third Pass: Activity & Life

### Fourth Pass: Depth & Atmosphere
