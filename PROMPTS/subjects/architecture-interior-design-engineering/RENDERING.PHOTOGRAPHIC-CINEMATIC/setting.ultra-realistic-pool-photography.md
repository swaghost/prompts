## Best Practices

### Pre-Production Planning

1. **Scout location and time** - assess natural lighting conditions
2. **Clean pool thoroughly** - remove debris, skim surface, vacuum
3. **Balance water chemistry** - ensure perfect clarity
4. **Stage furniture and accessories** - arrange for composition
5. **Test camera position** - verify water level perspective works

### Camera and Settings

1. **Use tripod or stabilization** - critical for sharpness at water level
2. **Select wide aperture** - f/2.8 to f/4 for shallow depth of field
3. **Focus precisely on pool edge** - use single-point autofocus
4. **Shoot RAW format** - maximum post-processing flexibility
5. **Bracket exposures** - capture full dynamic range for HDR if needed

### Composition Guidelines

1. **Position pool edge as leading line** - guide viewer through frame
2. **Balance foreground and background** - don't overcrowd composition
3. **Maintain level horizon** - critical for water photography
4. **Include context** - villa, landscape, furniture in background
5. **Leave breathing room** - don't fill entire frame

### Lighting Optimization

1. **Prefer overcast or soft light** - avoid harsh shadows on water
2. **Golden hour alternative** - warm, inviting atmosphere
3. **Avoid midday sun** - creates harsh contrast and glare
4. **Use reflectors if needed** - subtle fill light
5. **Capture sky reflections** - interesting clouds add visual interest

### Post-Processing Excellence

1. **Adjust white balance** - natural, accurate color temperature
2. **Enhance clarity selectively** - sharpen foreground, maintain soft background
3. **Correct lens distortion** - ensure straight lines remain straight
4. **Fine-tune color** - rich but realistic saturation
5. **Preserve highlights in water** - avoid blown-out reflections

## Technical Notes

### Camera Equipment Recommendations

**Camera Body:**

- Full-frame sensor (superior low-light and depth of field control)
- High resolution (24MP minimum, 45MP+ ideal for 8K output)
- Weather sealing (protection from splashing)
- Examples: Nikon D850, Canon EOS R5, Sony A7R IV

**Lens Selection:**

- **50mm f/1.4-f/1.8**: Natural perspective, excellent bokeh
- **85mm f/1.4-f/1.8**: Flattering compression, shallow DOF
- **35mm f/1.4-f/1.8**: Wider view, environmental context
- Prime lenses preferred for optical quality and wide apertures

**Accessories:**

- Sturdy tripod with adjustable legs (for water-level positioning)
- Waterproof camera housing or splash protection
- Remote shutter release or timer (avoid camera shake)
- Polarizing filter (reduce glare, enhance sky)
- ND filter (if shooting in bright conditions with wide aperture)

### Depth of Field Calculations

**f/2.8 at 50mm Lens:**

- Subject distance: 3-5 meters
- Depth of field: ~0.5-1 meter sharp zone
- Foreground pool edge in focus
- Background villa and forest softly blurred
- Bokeh: Smooth, circular highlights

**f/4 at 85mm Lens:**

- Subject distance: 5-8 meters
- Depth of field: ~0.8-1.5 meters
- Slightly more sharpness extending into mid-ground
- Still significant background blur
- Good balance for architecture visibility

### Water Photography Techniques

**Achieving Crystal Clarity:**

- Proper filtration and chemical balance
- Skim surface immediately before shooting
- Vacuum pool floor to remove sediment
- Allow water to settle after disturbance
- Shoot in shade if algae or cloudiness present

**Capturing Realistic Ripples:**

- Gentle wind creates natural movement
- Assistant can create subtle disturbance
- Wait for natural settling patterns
- Avoid perfectly still water (looks artificial)
- Capture motion at 1/125-1/250 second shutter speed

**Reflection Management:**

- Sky and surroundings should be visually interesting
- Polarizing filter can reduce or enhance reflections
- Angle and wind affect reflection quality
- Overcast sky provides softer, more even reflections
- Blue sky adds vibrant color to water

### Rendering Software Workflow (CGI Alternative)

**3D Rendering Software:**

- **V-Ray**: Excellent water and reflection rendering
- **Corona Renderer**: User-friendly, realistic atmosphere
- **Lumion**: Real-time rendering with good water effects
- **Unreal Engine**: Cutting-edge real-time ray tracing
- **Octane Render**: Fast GPU rendering, photorealistic output

**Water Simulation:**

- Procedural ripple displacement maps
- Physics-based water surface animation
- Subsurface scattering for depth
- Caustics for light refraction on pool floor
- Realistic IOR (Index of Refraction) for water: 1.33

**Camera Settings in 3D:**

- Physical camera with depth of field enabled
- f/2.8-f/4 aperture simulation
- Focus distance on foreground pool edge
- Bokeh shape: Circular (quality lens simulation)
- Lens shift or perspective correction if needed

**Lighting Setup:**

- HDRI sky dome for global illumination
- Overcast sky HDRI or diffused sun
- Color temperature: 5500-6500K
- Exposure value (EV): 13-15 for daylight
- V-Ray sun or Corona sun for directional light
