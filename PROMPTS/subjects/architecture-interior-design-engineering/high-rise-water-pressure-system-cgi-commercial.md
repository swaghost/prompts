## CONCEPT

Dynamic 10-second premium high-end CGI architectural/engineering commercial showcasing modern high-rise residential tower water supply infrastructure. Features photorealistic architectural cutaway revealing internal plumbing systems, pressure-zoning principles, booster pump station, and dedicated upper-zone water supply. Core narrative: weak water pressure at top floor → reveal underground booster pump station with two separate supply zones → pumps activate → strong stable water flow reaches upper zone → powerful stable water pressure. Clean infinite matte-black background throughout. Highly detailed photorealistic 3D objects only (real engineering components, no neon graphics, no UI/HUD overlays).
