# Architectural Transform: Real to White Clay Render Animation

**Style**: Premium white clay architectural render with cinematic particle transformation animation

---

## OVERVIEW

Transform a real architectural image into a white clay volumetric render, then create a cinematic particle transformation animation using Seedance 2.0 or similar video generation tools.

---

## STEP 1: IMAGE PREPARATION

### Original Image Requirements

For best results, use an image with:

- **Good resolution**
- **Good lighting**
- **Organized framing**
- **Minimal obstructions** blocking the architecture
- **Correct aspect ratio** for final video (e.g., 9:16 for Stories/Reels)

The better the original image quality, the better the transformation result.

---

## STEP 2: TRANSFORM TO WHITE CLAY RENDER

### PROMPT: Clay Render Transformation

```
Transform this image into a premium white clay architectural render while strictly preserving the original architecture, geometry, furniture placement, proportions, composition and camera angle.

Convert every visible material into a refined monochromatic white finish with a subtle white concrete texture. Keep all walls, ceilings, floors, furniture, decorative objects, vegetation, landscape and architectural details in the same positions.

Maintain the original depth, perspective and spatial organization. Use soft natural lighting, subtle shadows, realistic ambient occlusion and delicate variations in white to preserve the visibility of every form and surface.

The final result must look like a sophisticated architectural volumetric study or high-end clay render, with clean white materials, realistic depth and premium architectural visualization quality.

No structural changes, no new objects, no removed elements, no distorted furniture, no changes to the camera position, no text and no people.

High-end ArchViz, white concrete finish, monochromatic clay render, soft global illumination, realistic shadows, minimal aesthetic, ultra-detailed, photorealistic, 9:16 vertical composition.
```

---

## STEP 3: VERIFY CONSISTENCY

After generation, you should have:

**INITIAL IMAGE**: The real, fully finished image

**FINAL IMAGE**: The same scene transformed into white clay render

Both images must have:

- ✓ Same framing
- ✓ Same architecture
- ✓ Same furniture
- ✓ Same proportions
- ✓ Same format
- ✓ Little to no geometry changes

If the AI changes the scene too much, regenerate emphasizing that it must preserve the entire original structure.

---

## STEP 4: SEEDANCE 2.0 CONFIGURATION

### Video Generation Setup

**INITIAL IMAGE**: Real image

**FINAL IMAGE**: White clay render image

**FORMAT**: 9:16

**CAMERA MOVEMENT**: Smooth and stabilized

The initial image will be the first frame of the video, while the white image will be used as reference for the final frame.

---

## STEP 5: ANIMATION PROMPT

### PROMPT: Seedance 2.0 Particle Transformation

```
Create a premium cinematic transformation from the realistic architectural scene in the initial image into the clean white clay architectural render shown in the final image.

Strictly preserve the original architecture, geometry, furniture placement, landscape, proportions and spatial composition throughout the entire sequence.

The transformation begins subtly along the exposed edges, corners, rooflines, walls, furniture, vegetation and architectural details. The original materials slowly disintegrate into billions of ultra-fine particles and are immediately reconstructed as smooth monochromatic white surfaces with a subtle white concrete finish.

The particle transformation progresses naturally across the entire scene like controlled procedural erosion and reconstruction. Every surface gradually loses its original colors and materials while becoming part of the final white clay render.

Use only microscopic dust particles and delicate fragments. Avoid large debris, broken structures or violent destruction.

The particles must behave with realistic wind dynamics, gentle turbulence, natural depth and physically accurate motion. Fine volumetric particle trails interact with the existing sunlight, producing subtle atmospheric scattering, realistic highlights and soft motion blur.

The camera performs a very slow and smooth cinematic orbit to the left while maintaining the same composition and keeping the main architectural subject perfectly centered. The movement must feel like a stabilized professional drone or controlled cinematic camera.

The transformation must be gradual, elegant and continuous, with no sudden cuts, no flickering, no visual jumps and no changes to the geometry.

By the end of the video, the scene must perfectly match the final reference image: a clean monochromatic white clay architectural visualization with a subtle white concrete finish, realistic shadows, ambient occlusion and refined depth.

High-end Houdini-style procedural particle simulation, ultra-high particle count, seamless material transformation, realistic global illumination, volumetric lighting, physically based animation, premium architectural visualization, luxury CGI, cinematic depth of field, HDR, ultra-detailed and photorealistic.

No explosions, no fire, no dark smoke, no violent destruction, no collapsing structures, no large debris, no people, no text, no additional objects, no architectural deformation and no camera shake.
```

---

## STEP 6: TROUBLESHOOTING

### If Transformation is Too Fast

Add to prompt:

```
Slow and progressive transformation throughout the entire video. Do not complete the transition too early.
```

### If Camera Movement is Too Extreme

Add to prompt:

```
Extremely subtle camera movement. Minimal left orbit. Preserve the original framing.
```

## QUICK WORKFLOW SUMMARY

1. Choose a real architectural image
2. Send image to AI image generation tool
3. Use clay render prompt to transform entire scene to white volumetric render
4. Preserve exact same framing, architecture, and furniture
5. Open Seedance 2.0 or similar video generation tool
6. Set real image as initial image
7. Set white image as final image
8. Select 9:16 format
9. Paste animation prompt
10. Generate video and verify transformation consistency

---

## KEY SUCCESS FACTORS

### Consistency is Critical

The secret to this effect is consistency between initial and final images.

**The AI should NOT**:

- Redesign the environment
- Change geometry
- Alter framing
- Add or remove elements

**The AI should ONLY**:

- Replace materials
- Replace colors
- Replace textures
- Convert to monochromatic white finish

The more similar the two images are in terms of geometry and framing, the cleaner and more professional the Seedance 2.0 transition will be.
