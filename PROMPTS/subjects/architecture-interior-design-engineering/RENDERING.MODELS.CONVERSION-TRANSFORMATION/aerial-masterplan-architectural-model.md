## Best Practices

**Model Translation:**

- Preserve exact spatial organization from model
- Maintain building-to-building relationships
- Keep circulation patterns intact
- Respect scale and proportions
- Preserve special features (temples, courts, tiles)
- Maintain site organization logic

**Material Enhancement:**

- Upgrade from model materials to photorealistic
- Apply premium finishes while preserving forms
- Use realistic textures appropriate to building types
- Ensure material consistency across similar elements
- Balance modern and traditional materials if applicable

**Landscape Integration:**

- Add natural greenery respecting model's open spaces
- Place clean, mature trees in logical locations
- Create lush appearance without obscuring architecture
- Integrate landscape with architectural elements
- Maintain views and sight lines from model

**Lighting Strategy:**

- Use soft, natural daylight
- Avoid dramatic shadows or ray effects
- Ensure even illumination across site
- Create professional, refined atmosphere
- Highlight architectural features subtly
- Maintain clarity of all elements

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

- Distorting building proportions from model
- Moving elements from their model positions
- Changing circulation or road patterns
- Adding dramatic lighting effects
- Using weathered or distressed materials
- Obscuring important features with vegetation
- Losing special elements (temples, courts)
- Altering tile patterns or paving designs
- Creating unrealistic or fantasy appearance

## Conversion Guidelines

**From Physical Model:**

- Enhance material realism while preserving forms
- Add appropriate scale elements (people, cars if needed)
- Translate model textures to realistic materials
- Maintain lighting direction from model photography

**From Digital/3D Model:**

- Preserve exact geometry and spatial organization
- Enhance material quality and realism
- Add environmental context if absent in model
- Maintain design intent and aesthetic

**From Massing Model:**

- Add architectural detail appropriate to scale
- Apply realistic materials to simple forms
- Integrate contextual landscape elements
- Maintain volume relationships and proportions

## Output Specifications

**Resolution:** High resolution suitable for presentations (4K+ recommended)  
**Format:** PNG for precision, JPEG for large file distribution  
**Color Space:** sRGB for digital presentation  
**Aspect Ratio:** Match model proportions or 16:9 for standard presentations  
**Detail Level:** Clear visibility of all major elements and features
