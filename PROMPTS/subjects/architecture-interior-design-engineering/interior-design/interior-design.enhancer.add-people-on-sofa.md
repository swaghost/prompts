# Interior Design Enhancer - Add People on Sofa

## Description

Populate an existing interior with people seated naturally on the sofa and engaged in believable conversation. The enhancement adds lifestyle context and human scale while preserving the room, furnishings, camera, and lighting.

## Best For

- Lifestyle marketing
- Hospitality and residential visualization
- Human scale demonstration
- Social interaction and emotional context

## Prerequisites

- One clear interior image containing a visible sofa
- Enough unobstructed sofa space and frame area for seated figures
- Lighting and perspective readable enough to integrate people convincingly

## Original Prompt

```text
In this image add people who are talking sit on the sofa
```

## Production-Ready Prompt

```text
Add a small group of people seated naturally on the existing sofa and engaged in a relaxed conversation. Preserve the exact architecture, room dimensions, camera position, perspective, sofa design and position, all furniture, decor, materials, lighting fixtures, windows, doors, and object placement.

Seat each person with realistic body weight, cushion compression, posture, anatomy, eye lines, and conversational spacing. Use subtle varied gestures and attentive expressions so the interaction feels candid rather than posed. Match the room's existing light direction, color temperature, shadows, reflections, depth of field, and photographic style. Keep circulation clear and do not obscure important interior-design features.
```

## Variations

- `Add one person reading by the window.`
- `Add a family having dinner at the table.`
- `Add someone working at the desk.`
- `Add children playing naturally on the floor.`

## Preservation Rules

- Preserve architecture, camera, furnishings, decor, materials, and lighting.
- Add only the people and the minimal cushion or fabric response caused by their weight.
- Keep figures correctly scaled to the sofa and room.
- Maintain plausible seating, occlusion, contact shadows, eye lines, and conversational interaction.

## Negative Prompt

```text
changed room, moved sofa, redesigned furniture, added furniture, removed decor, wrong scale people, floating people, standing on sofa, bodies intersecting sofa, no cushion compression, duplicate people, duplicate faces, extra limbs, missing limbs, malformed hands, extra fingers, broken joints, impossible posture, mismatched eye lines, everyone looking at camera, staged pose, blocked circulation, obscured architecture, mismatched light, missing contact shadows, text, logo, watermark
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Architecture, sofa, furniture, decor, camera, and lighting remain unchanged.
- [ ] People are correctly scaled and seated with believable body weight.
- [ ] Cushions compress and clothing folds naturally at contact points.
- [ ] Anatomy, hands, feet, posture, eye lines, and occlusions are plausible.
- [ ] Conversation reads naturally through varied expressions and subtle gestures.
- [ ] No duplicate figure, floating body, blocked circulation, text, logo, or watermark appears.
