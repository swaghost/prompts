# Interior Design Enhancer - AD Magazine Photography

## Description

Transform an uploaded interior image into a premium architecture-and-design editorial photograph while preserving the room itself. The enhancement targets polished styling, professional lighting, disciplined composition, realistic materials, and an award-caliber luxury magazine finish.

## Best For

- Client presentations
- Interior-design portfolios
- Marketing materials
- Premium real-estate listings

## Prerequisites

- One clear, high-resolution interior image
- Architecture, furniture, materials, and major styling elements visible enough to preserve

## Original Prompt

```text
Show me the AD magazine photography version of this image.
```

## Production-Ready Prompt

```text
Transform the uploaded interior image into a premium architecture-and-design magazine photograph. Preserve the exact room architecture, dimensions, camera position, perspective, furniture, object placement, materials, finishes, windows, doors, lighting fixtures, and design intent. Do not redesign, add, remove, relocate, or resize anything.

Enhance only the photographic presentation: refined editorial composition, balanced exposure, professional interior lighting, natural window-light response, controlled highlights, open but realistic shadows, accurate white balance, lifelike material texture, clean vertical lines, subtle depth, restrained styling polish, and sophisticated color grading. Keep the result photorealistic, elegant, calm, and publication-ready, like a professionally photographed luxury interior feature.
```

## Preservation Rules

- Keep the exact architecture, layout, furniture, decor, materials, and viewpoint.
- Treat this as a photography enhancement, not a redesign or virtual staging task.
- Preserve realistic scale, perspective, reflections, shadows, and material behavior.
- Do not invent brands, products, rooms, openings, or architectural details.

## Negative Prompt

```text
redesigned room, changed layout, moved furniture, replacement furniture, added decor, removed objects, changed architecture, new windows, new doors, altered dimensions, warped verticals, impossible perspective, fake materials, plastic texture, excessive HDR, oversaturation, crushed shadows, blown highlights, heavy bloom, artificial CGI look, text, logo, watermark
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Room geometry and camera perspective match the source.
- [ ] Furniture, objects, materials, fixtures, doors, and windows remain unchanged.
- [ ] Lighting is polished but physically plausible.
- [ ] Vertical lines, reflections, shadows, and material textures look photographic.
- [ ] The result reads as editorial interior photography rather than a redesign.
- [ ] No invented objects, text, logo, or watermark appears.
