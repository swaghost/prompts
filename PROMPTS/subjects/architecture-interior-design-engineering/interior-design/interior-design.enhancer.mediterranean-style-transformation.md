# Interior Design Enhancer - Mediterranean Style Transformation

## Description

Restyle an existing interior with a warm Mediterranean design language built from linen, wood, terracotta, stone, plaster, and other natural materials while preserving the source architecture and spatial relationships.

## Best For

- Style exploration
- Client mood boards
- Regional design adaptations
- Vacation-property styling

## Prerequisites

- One clear interior reference image
- Room architecture and furniture layout visible enough to preserve

## Original Prompt

```text
Restyle this interior in a Mediterranean style with warm natural materials and linen textures.
```

## Production-Ready Prompt

```text
Restyle the uploaded interior in a refined Mediterranean design language using warm natural materials and tactile linen textures. Preserve the exact architecture, room dimensions, floor plan, camera position, perspective, windows, doors, circulation, and major furniture placement.

Apply warm limewash or mineral-plaster walls where appropriate, natural oak or aged timber, terracotta accents, pale limestone or travertine, woven fibers, relaxed linen upholstery and curtains, handmade ceramic details, and a restrained sun-washed palette. Keep the styling elegant, organic, and contemporary rather than themed or rustic. Use warm natural daylight, realistic material texture, believable wear, and a calm coastal-European atmosphere.
```

## Preservation Rules

- Preserve structural geometry, openings, circulation, scale, and camera perspective.
- Keep major furniture positions unless a change is strictly required to express the style.
- Replace finishes and soft furnishings coherently rather than layering unrelated decor over the source.
- Maintain realistic construction, material transitions, shadows, and proportions.

## Negative Prompt

```text
changed floor plan, moved walls, new windows, new doors, altered ceiling height, distorted perspective, overcrowded decor, theme-park Mediterranean, excessive blue and white, fake rustic props, random arches, unrelated Moroccan styling, plastic materials, glossy synthetic linen, oversaturation, cartoon, CGI look, text, logo, watermark
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Architecture, dimensions, circulation, and viewpoint match the source.
- [ ] Mediterranean materials form one coherent restrained palette.
- [ ] Linen, plaster, timber, terracotta, and stone look tactile and realistic.
- [ ] Styling feels contemporary and regionally informed rather than theatrical.
- [ ] Furniture placement and room functionality remain believable.
- [ ] No invented structure, distorted geometry, text, logo, or watermark appears.
