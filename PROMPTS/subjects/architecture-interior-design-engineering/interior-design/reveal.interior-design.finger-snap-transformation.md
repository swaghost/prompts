# Interior Design Snap Transformation

## Description

A 12-second luxury commercial showing a character snapping fingers twice to instantly transform an empty room through two different interior design styles. Features kinetic physical assembly of furniture and smooth backward camera dolly.

## Prerequisites

- Reference Image 1: First interior design style
- Reference Image 2: Empty modern living room
- Reference Image 3: Second interior design style
- Character image (JOHN)

## Video Prompt

**Duration:** 12 seconds. Single continuous shot. No cuts. No crossfades. No dissolves.

**Style:** Luxury commercial style — smooth, aspirational, confident, never oversold, just states the result.

**CHARACTER:** JOHN — male, strong build, natural authority, commanding presence, professional casual clothing. Standing centered in the room throughout. Completely silent for the entire shot — no dialogue, mouth does not move except for a satisfied smile in the final beat.

**STARTING ENVIRONMENT:** Matches Reference Image 2 exactly — the empty modern living room with soaring vaulted ceiling, warm walnut wood paneling on the left, floor-to-ceiling glass on the right showing trees outside, travertine stone floor and steps, recessed skylight above. Room is completely empty of furniture at the start. JOHN stands centered in the middle of this empty room, facing camera.

**CAMERA:** Continuous slow dolly backward starting close on JOHN at medium-wide framing, pulling back steadily and smoothly for the full 12 seconds, gradually revealing more of the room's height and width as it retreats. Camera comes to a complete stop and holds still in the final beat, right as JOHN crosses his arms. No pan, no orbit — straight backward pull only.

### Timeline

**0-4s — FIRST SNAP AND BUILD:** JOHN snaps his fingers once, confident and sharp. The instant the snap fires, every piece of furniture and decor rapidly materializes and assembles into place all at once — the cognac leather sofa builds itself from the floor up, the walnut credenzas snap together, the Eames lounge chair and ottoman assemble, artwork appears on the walls, the arc floor lamp extends into position, potted plants grow into place, the wood coffee table and books settle in. All of this happens in under a second — fast, kinetic, energetic construction, not a slow build — like the room is assembling itself at high speed. No flash, no light burst, no dissolve — purely physical materialization of solid objects snapping into their final positions. The result matches Reference Image 1 exactly. JOHN reacts with mild pleased surprise, looking around at the space.

**4-8s — THE PAUSE GESTURE:** JOHN turns back to face camera directly. He raises one hand and gives a clear "one moment" gesture — index finger up, holding briefly, as if telling the audience to wait for something better. Confident, playful energy. No dialogue, no words — purely physical gesture. Camera continues its slow backward pull through this beat.

**8-12s — SECOND SNAP AND FINAL BUILD:** JOHN snaps his fingers a second time, same sharp confident motion. Instantly, the first design's furniture rapidly disassembles and the second design's elements build themselves into place with the same fast, kinetic energy — the curved boucle sofa assembles, the wood-slat fireplace feature wall constructs itself panel by panel at high speed, the round walnut coffee table and black accents settle in, the jute rug unrolls into place, the olive sofa builds in the foreground. All in under a second — fast, physical, no flash, no dissolve. The result matches Reference Image 3 exactly. Camera dolly reaches its final resting position and holds completely still. JOHN looks around at the finished space with visible satisfaction, then turns to camera and crosses his arms with confident, successful body language — chin slightly up, relaxed but commanding stance. Hold on this final composed frame.

**Technical Requirements:**

Transformations occur only at the exact instant of each finger snap, rendered as rapid physical construction of solid objects snapping and assembling into place — never a light flash, never a gradual dissolve, never a soft morph. Room geometry, ceiling height, windows, and architectural bones remain structurally identical and consistent across all three states — only furniture, decor, and finishes change. JOHN's likeness, proportions, and position remain consistent and undistorted throughout the entire shot. No text overlays, no music. No other people in frame.

Clear and stable structural details, no distortion or warping of architectural lines, natural proportions, consistency from frame to frame. Widescreen aspect ratio, shallow depth of field, film grain. 4K HD, rich details, cinematic quality, no blur, no ghosting, no flickering, stable and smooth picture. No music.

Ideal for demonstrating instant design changes with snap-finger magic, multiple style options, and cinematic interior reveals with professional character presentation.
