# Existing Wall to Custom Built-In Furniture

## Conversion

- **Source type:** Existing interior wall reference
- **Target type:** Photorealistic custom built-in furniture design

## Description

Design a TV unit, cabinet, wardrobe, bookshelf, vanity, pantry, bar, or related built-in that uses the available wall while respecting outlets, openings, circulation, and room proportions.

## Usage

Use for millwork studies, joinery proposals, storage planning, renovation presentations, client options, and fabrication concept development.

## Prerequisites

- One clear wall and room reference
- Known wall dimensions when available
- Selected `(TV UNIT, CABINET OR WARDROBE)` type
- Selected `(STYLE)`
- Existing outlets, openings, skirting, switches, vents, and circulation identified

## Preservation Rules

- Preserve the room architecture, camera angle, openings, outlets, furniture context, and proportions.
- Do not cover required ventilation, access, circulation, windows, or doors.
- Keep joinery buildable with realistic panels, reveals, hardware, supports, and material thickness.

## Complete Prompt

```text
Design a custom (TV UNIT, CABINET OR WARDROBE) for this wall. Use the full available dimensions while respecting outlets, openings and circulation. Include closed storage, display niches, integrated warm LED lighting and concealed cables. Match a (STYLE) aesthetic. Preserve the room, camera angle and proportions. Photorealistic joinery details.
```

## Variables and Features

- **Furniture types:** TV unit, media console, floor-to-ceiling TV wall, sideboard, display cabinet, bookshelf, wardrobe, entry storage, office built-ins, pantry, vanity, bar cabinet, mudroom storage
- **Styles:** Scandinavian minimalist, mid-century modern, industrial, contemporary luxury, traditional classic, Japanese zen, Art Deco, coastal, farmhouse, transitional
- **Features:** Closed storage, display niches, 2700-3000K LED strips, concealed cable channels, ventilation, soft-close hardware, realistic full-wall dimensions

## Example

Design a custom TV unit for this wall. Use the full available dimensions while respecting outlets, openings and circulation. ## Negative Prompt

```text
changed room geometry, covered doorway, blocked window, hidden outlet without relocation plan, blocked ventilation, obstructed circulation, impossible cantilever, unsupported shelf, unrealistic panel thickness, missing cable management, distorted joinery, floating cabinet, changed camera, oversized unit, undersized unit, generic furniture, low detail, watermark, logo
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Furniture type and style variables are replaced.
- [ ] Wall dimensions and fixed constraints are respected.
- [ ] Doors, windows, outlets, vents, and circulation remain functional.
- [ ] Storage, niches, lighting, cable management, and ventilation are resolved.
- [ ] Joinery proportions and supports are buildable.
- [ ] Camera and room context remain unchanged.
