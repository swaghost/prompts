## Style Description

**Baroque** design originated in 17th-century Europe, epitomizing grandeur, drama, and elaborate ornamentation. This style is characterized by rich details, curved forms, gilded accents, ornate carvings, and opulent textiles. Baroque interiors evoke power, theatricality, and emotional intensity through layered decoration, dramatic lighting, and sumptuous materials. The aesthetic creates an immersive sensory experience—spaces feel palatial, extravagant, and awe-inspiring. This style is ideal for those who embrace maximalism, historical references, and unapologetic luxury. Baroque spaces tell stories of wealth, artistry, and cultural sophistication.
