## Style Description

**Sci-Fi Brutalism** merges the raw concrete aesthetics of brutalist architecture with futuristic, dystopian science fiction atmospheres. This style features exposed concrete, harsh geometries, industrial materials, dramatic lighting, and cyberpunk undertones. Sci-Fi Brutalist interiors evoke power, alienation, and stark beauty—spaces that feel like settings from Blade Runner or dystopian space stations. The aesthetic celebrates monumentality, uncompromising honesty of materials, and the beauty found in harsh, unforgiving environments. This style appeals to those drawn to dystopian futures, industrial aesthetics, and the intersection of brutalism and science fiction.
