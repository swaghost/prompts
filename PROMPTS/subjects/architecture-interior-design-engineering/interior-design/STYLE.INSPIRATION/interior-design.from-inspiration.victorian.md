## Style Description

**Victorian** design embodies the elaborate, ornate aesthetics of the Victorian era (1837-1901), characterized by rich patterns, heavy drapery, and decorative excess. This style features dark woods, floral wallpapers, tufted furniture, ornamental details, and layered textiles. Victorian interiors evoke history, romance, and genteel prosperity—spaces that feel like period homes from the height of the British Empire. The aesthetic celebrates craftsmanship, ornamentation, and the accumulation of beautiful objects. This style appeals to history enthusiasts, lovers of period dramas, and those who appreciate the elaborate beauty of 19th-century design.
