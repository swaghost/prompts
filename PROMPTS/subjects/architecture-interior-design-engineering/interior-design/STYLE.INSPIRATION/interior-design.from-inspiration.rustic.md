## Style Description

**Rustic** design celebrates the raw beauty of natural materials and handcrafted elements, characterized by reclaimed wood, stone, and organic textures. This style features exposed beams, natural finishes, handmade furniture, and connection to nature. Rustic interiors evoke warmth, authenticity, and grounded simplicity—spaces that feel like mountain cabins or countryside retreats. The aesthetic values imperfection, patina, and the honest beauty of natural materials. This style appeals to nature lovers, outdoor enthusiasts, and those seeking refuge from modern life in spaces that feel organic, genuine, and timeless.
