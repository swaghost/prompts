## Style Description

**Art Deco** embodies the glamour and optimism of the 1920s-1940s, characterized by bold geometric shapes, luxurious materials, and symmetrical elegance. This style celebrates modernity, sophistication, and opulence through streamlined forms, metallic finishes, and rich jewel tones. Art Deco interiors evoke drama, luxury, and confidence—perfect for creating spaces that feel theatrical, refined, and timeless. The aesthetic blends industrial precision with handcrafted artistry, resulting in interiors that are both functional and aspirational. This style appeals to those seeking grandeur, symmetry, and a sense of vintage Hollywood glamour.
