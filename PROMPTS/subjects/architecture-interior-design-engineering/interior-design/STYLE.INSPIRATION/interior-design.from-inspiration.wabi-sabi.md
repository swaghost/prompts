## Style Description

**Wabi-Sabi** embraces the Japanese philosophy of finding beauty in imperfection, impermanence, and incompleteness. This style features weathered materials, organic forms, muted earth tones, handmade objects, and asymmetrical compositions. Wabi-Sabi interiors evoke serenity, acceptance, and profound simplicity—spaces that celebrate the beauty of age, wear, and natural cycles. The aesthetic rejects perfectionism in favor of authentic, lived-in beauty. This style appeals to philosophers, minimalists with soul, and those seeking spiritual depth and authentic beauty beyond surface perfection.
