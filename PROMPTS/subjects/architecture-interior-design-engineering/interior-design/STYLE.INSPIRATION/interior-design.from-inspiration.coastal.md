## Style Description

**Coastal** design captures the breezy, relaxed essence of seaside living, characterized by light color palettes, natural textures, and airy spaces. This style embraces weathered woods, soft linens, nautical accents, and ocean-inspired hues of blue, white, and sandy neutrals. Coastal interiors evoke calm, freshness, and casual elegance—perfect for creating spaces that feel like a perpetual vacation. The aesthetic celebrates natural light, organic materials, and connection to nature. This style appeals to those seeking tranquility, simplicity, and a laid-back atmosphere that brings the serenity of the shore indoors.
