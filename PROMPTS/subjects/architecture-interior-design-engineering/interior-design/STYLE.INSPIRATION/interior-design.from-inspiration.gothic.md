## Style Description

**Gothic** design draws from medieval architecture and Victorian Gothic revival, characterized by dramatic arches, ornate details, and dark romanticism. This style features pointed arches, stained glass, velvet drapes, wrought iron, and cathedral-like grandeur. Gothic interiors evoke mystery, drama, and haunting beauty—spaces that feel like castles, cathedrals, or the settings of Gothic novels. The aesthetic embraces darkness, history, and theatrical atmosphere. This style appeals to romantics, history lovers, and those drawn to the beautiful darkness of medieval aesthetics and Victorian Gothic literature.
