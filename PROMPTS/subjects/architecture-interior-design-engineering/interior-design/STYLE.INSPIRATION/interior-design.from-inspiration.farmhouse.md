## Style Description

**Farmhouse** design celebrates rural simplicity, practical beauty, and nostalgic comfort through weathered woods, vintage finds, and homespun textures. This style features shiplap walls, apron sinks, open shelving, and cozy textiles in neutral palettes. Farmhouse interiors evoke warmth, hospitality, and down-to-earth charm—spaces that feel like grandmother's country kitchen reimagined for modern life. The aesthetic values function, durability, and lived-in comfort over perfection. This style appeals to those seeking wholesome simplicity, family-centered living, and a connection to agricultural heritage and simpler times.
