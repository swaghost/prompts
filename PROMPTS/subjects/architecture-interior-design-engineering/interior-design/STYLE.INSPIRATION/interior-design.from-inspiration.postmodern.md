## Style Description

**Postmodern** design playfully subverts modernist principles through irony, eclecticism, and bold experimentation. This style features unexpected color combinations, Memphis Milano influences, geometric patterns, and deliberate rule-breaking. Postmodern interiors evoke wit, creativity, and intellectual playfulness—spaces that challenge conventions and celebrate individuality. The aesthetic embraces contradiction, historical references remixed with contemporary irreverence, and design as communication. This style appeals to intellectuals, artists, and those who appreciate design that makes statements, provokes thought, and refuses to take itself too seriously.
