## Style Description

**Bohemian (Boho)** design celebrates free-spirited creativity, global influences, and artistic expression. This style embraces layered textures, mixed patterns, natural materials, and collected treasures from travels and vintage finds. Boho interiors evoke warmth, individuality, and relaxed comfort through earthy color palettes, handcrafted elements, and an eclectic mix of cultural references. The aesthetic rejects rigid rules in favor of personal storytelling and organic flow. This style appeals to creative souls who value authenticity, self-expression, and a lived-in, welcoming atmosphere. Boho spaces feel like personal sanctuaries filled with meaning and memories.
