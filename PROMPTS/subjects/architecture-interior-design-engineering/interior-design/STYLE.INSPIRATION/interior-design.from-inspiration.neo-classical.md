**Neo-Classical** design revives the grandeur of ancient Greek and Roman architecture with refined elegance and symmetry. This style features columns, pediments, classical moldings, marble, and balanced proportions. Neo-Classical interiors evoke timeless beauty, cultural sophistication, and architectural harmony—spaces that feel like refined temples of taste and learning. The aesthetic celebrates order, proportion, and the enduring principles of classical beauty. This style appeals to traditionalists, classicists, and those who find perfection in the architectural principles established by ancient civilizations.
