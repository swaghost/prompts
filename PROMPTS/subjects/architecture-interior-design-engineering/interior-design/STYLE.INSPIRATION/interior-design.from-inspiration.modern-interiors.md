## Style Description

**Modern** design emphasizes clean lines, open spaces, and functional simplicity rooted in early-to-mid 20th century modernism. This style features minimal ornamentation, neutral palettes, quality materials, and emphasis on form following function. Modern interiors evoke clarity, sophistication, and purposeful living—spaces that feel uncluttered, organized, and effortlessly elegant. The aesthetic celebrates honest materials, geometric forms, and the beauty of simplicity. This style appeals to those seeking order, clarity, and spaces that support focused, contemporary living without unnecessary distraction.
