# Abandoned Place - Degradation & Decay Transformation

## Description

Introduce heavy realistic degradation across the entire scene, including strong dirt accumulation, stains, cracks, material damage, creating a neglected and deteriorated environment while maintaining the original structure. This prompt transforms pristine architectural or interior scenes into abandoned, weathered spaces showing years of neglect—featuring authentic decay patterns, environmental damage, material aging, and atmospheric deterioration—ideal for film/TV production design, video games, post-apocalyptic concepts, urban exploration aesthetics, and dramatic storytelling through environmental transformation.

## Transformation Overview

**Effect:** Pristine → Abandoned/neglected state  
**Degradation Level:** Heavy realistic decay  
**Structure:** Original architecture maintained  
**Elements:** Dirt, stains, cracks, damage, deterioration  
**Purpose:** Abandoned aesthetic, decay visualization, storytelling
