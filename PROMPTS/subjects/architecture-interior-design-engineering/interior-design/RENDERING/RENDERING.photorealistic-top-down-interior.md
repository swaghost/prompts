## Core Elements

### Photorealistic Rendering Quality

**Lighting:**

- Soft natural daylight streaming through windows
- Realistic shadows cast by furniture and architectural elements
- Ambient occlusion in corners and under furniture
- Subtle rim lighting on edges
- Light bounce and global illumination
- Natural color temperature (5500-6500K daylight)

**Materials & Textures:**

- High-resolution surface textures
- Realistic material properties (wood grain, fabric weave, metal polish)
- Bump/displacement mapping for depth
- Accurate reflectivity (glossy floors, glass surfaces)
- Subsurface scattering (fabrics, lampshades)
- True-to-life color and finish

**Details:**

- Individual objects clearly rendered
- No AI artifacts or distortions
- Sharp where needed, soft where appropriate
- Photographic depth of field (optional slight blur in distance)
- Realistic scale and proportions

## Best Practices

### ✅ Do:

1. **Reference 2026 trends** — Keep materials and styles current
2. **Use cohesive color palette** — 3-5 colors max throughout
3. **Include greenery** — Plants add life and biophilic design
4. **Layer textures** — Multiple tactile surfaces create depth
5. **Show realistic proportions** — Furniture scaled correctly to rooms
6. **Emphasize natural light** — Soft daylight creates realism
7. **Style intentionally** — Every object placed with purpose
8. **Mix materials** — Wood + metal + stone + fabric creates richness

---

### ❌ Don't:

1. **Overcrowd spaces** — Less is more in 2026 aesthetic
2. **Use outdated trends** — No grey-on-grey, no industrial overload
3. **Forget lighting** — Dark, murky renders look unprofessional
4. **Mix too many styles** — Cohesive aesthetic is key
5. **Use harsh colors** — Sophisticated palettes are muted and harmonious
6. **Neglect scale** — Oversized furniture in small rooms looks unrealistic
7. **Skip the details** — Books, plants, art make spaces feel lived-in
8. **Use flat lighting** — Realistic shadows create depth and realism

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Renders look too dark

**Solution:**

- Emphasize "soft natural daylight" in prompt
- Add "bright, well-lit, airy atmosphere"
- Specify "large windows allowing abundant light"
- Upscale and adjust brightness in post if needed

---

### Issue: Colors look off or garish

**Solution:**

- Specify "cohesive, sophisticated color palette"
- List exact colors: "warm white, sage green, terracotta, natural oak"
- Add "muted, harmonious tones"
- Avoid mentioning bright or saturated colors

---

### Issue: Furniture looks too small/large

**Solution:**

- Mention "accurate proportions and realistic scale"
- Provide room dimensions in prompt
- Specify furniture types and typical sizes
- Reference "standard residential furniture scale"

---

### Issue: Style looks outdated

**Solution:**

- Explicitly mention "2026 interior design trends"
- List current trends: "warm minimalism, biophilic design, organic shapes"
- Avoid dated terms: "industrial loft", "grey on grey", "subway tile"
- Reference current design magazines/Instagram for inspiration

---

### Issue: Too sterile or staged

**Solution:**

- Add "lived-in, editorial styling"
- Include "books, plants, decorative objects"
- Mention "warm, inviting atmosphere"
- Avoid "showroom" or "perfectly styled"
