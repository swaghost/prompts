# SketchUp Model or Sketch to Photorealistic Interior Render

## Conversion

- **Source type:** SketchUp model, screenshot, sketch, or basic 3D render
- **Target type:** Photorealistic architectural interior render

## Description

Convert a schematic interior representation into a high-realism architectural image while preserving geometry, proportions, furniture positions, and camera viewpoint.

## Usage

Use for design presentations, SketchUp enhancement, portfolio renders, client visualization, material studies, and previsualization before production rendering.

## Prerequisites

- SketchUp file, high-resolution screenshot, sketch, or basic 3D render
- Clear camera composition and spatial geometry
- Defined furniture layout
- Existing or specified material indications

## Preservation Rules

- Preserve exact geometry, dimensions, openings, furniture positions, camera angle, crop, and perspective.
- Improve only materials, textures, lighting, architectural details, reflections, shadows, and realism.
- Do not redesign the room or add unsupported architecture.

## Complete Prompt

```text
Convert this SketchUp model or sketch into a photorealistic interior render. Preserve geometry, proportions, furniture positions and camera angle. Apply realistic materials, natural daylight, warm indirect lighting and refined details. Architectural photography, high realism.
```

## Rendering Enhancements

- Replace basic materials with correctly scaled PBR wood, stone, fabric, and metal textures.
- Add natural window light, realistic falloff, warm indirect bounce, and soft shadows.
- Resolve baseboards, trim, door frames, switch plates, joints, and transitions where supported.
- Use subtle photographic depth of field and a natural 3000-4000K color response.
- Preserve realistic furniture and architectural scale.

## Negative Prompt

```text
redesigned architecture, moved wall, changed opening, changed furniture position, changed camera, changed crop, changed perspective, distorted geometry, altered proportions, invented window, invented door, floating furniture, wrong scale, stretched texture, fake reflection, excessive depth of field, oversaturation, illustration, cartoon, AI-looking render, watermark, logo
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Source geometry and camera remain unchanged.
- [ ] Furniture positions and scale match the source.
- [ ] Materials use realistic scale, texture, and reflection.
- [ ] Daylight and warm indirect light have coherent direction and falloff.
- [ ] Architectural details are refined without unsupported additions.
- [ ] Final image reads as professional architectural photography.
