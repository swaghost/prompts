# 3D Architectural Floor Plan Render - Modern Luxury Single-Floor House

## Description

Create an ultra-detailed 3D architectural floor plan render of a modern luxury single-floor house with isometric top-down view and realistic interior visualization. This prompt produces cinematic ambient lighting, soft glowing LED accents, premium contemporary furniture, and photorealistic materials with depth and perspective—delivering professional architectural visualization in Unreal Engine style with 8K ultra HD quality and elegant modern home design.

## Render Overview

**View Type:** Isometric top-down  
**Style:** Modern luxury residential
