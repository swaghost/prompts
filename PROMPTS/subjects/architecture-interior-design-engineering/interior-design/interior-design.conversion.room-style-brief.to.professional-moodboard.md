# Room and Style Brief to Professional Moodboard

## Conversion

- **Source type:** Room type and design-style brief
- **Target type:** Editorial interior-design moodboard

## Description

Create a cohesive presentation board combining palette, materials, textures, furniture silhouettes, lighting references, and decor into one premium design story.

## Usage

Use for client discovery, concept presentations, design pitches, material direction, procurement alignment, portfolio pages, and early-stage visual communication.

## Prerequisites

- Selected `(ROOM)` and `(STYLE)`
- Optional reference images, product references, and verified material choices
- Any required palette, budget, lifestyle, or brand direction

## Presentation Rules

- Use an off-white, cream, or soft-grey neutral background rather than pure white.
- Maintain generous margins and a coherent grid or balanced free-flow composition.
- Use one hero image at roughly 30-40%, supporting images at 15-20%, and detail images at 10-15%.
- Keep all materials and objects consistent with one design story.

## Complete Prompt

```text
Create a professional moodboard for a (ROOM) in (STYLE). Include a cohesive color palette, material samples, textures, furniture silhouettes, lighting references and décor details. Organize it with clear hierarchy, generous spacing and editorial composition. Neutral background, realistic samples, premium presentation.
```

## Required Components

1. Five to seven color swatches with HEX codes
2. Wood, stone, fabric, and metal samples
3. Close-up texture references
4. Key furniture silhouettes
5. Pendant, floor, wall, or natural-light references
6. Artwork, plants, textiles, and accessories
7. Room and style title
8. Clear hierarchy and generous white space

## Example

```text
Create a professional moodboard for a master bedroom in mid-century modern. Include a cohesive color palette, material samples, textures, furniture silhouettes, lighting references and décor details. Organize it with clear hierarchy, generous spacing and editorial composition. Neutral background, realistic samples, premium presentation.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
incoherent styles, unrelated materials, random furniture, pure white background, crowded layout, no hierarchy, tiny unreadable samples, missing palette, missing materials, repeated images, flat texture, unrealistic samples, excessive text, misspelled title, cheap collage, watermark, logo
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Room and style variables are replaced.
- [ ] Palette, materials, furniture, lighting, and decor tell one story.
- [ ] Five to seven colors include HEX codes.
- [ ] Samples are realistic and materially legible.
- [ ] Layout has a hero, support, details, alignment, and generous spacing.
- [ ] Background is neutral but not pure white.
