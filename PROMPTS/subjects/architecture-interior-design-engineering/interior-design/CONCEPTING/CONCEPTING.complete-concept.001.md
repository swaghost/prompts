# PROMPT 01: Complete Interior Design Concept

**Purpose:** Concept Creator  
**Workflow Position:** Phase 1 - Initial Concept

---

## Goal

Create the complete environment concept. Define style, palette, materials, lighting, furniture, and atmosphere in a single cohesive visual direction.

---

## Full Prompt

```
Create a complete interior design concept for this (ROOM TYPE), based on (STYLE).
Preserve the original architecture and proportions. Define a cohesive color palette,
materials, furniture, lighting, décor and overall mood. Prioritize functionality,
circulation and realistic scale. Photorealistic architectural visualization, natural
light, refined details.
```

---

## Variables

- **ROOM TYPE:** living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, dining room, office, nursery, etc.
- **STYLE:** modern, minimalist, Scandinavian, industrial, mid-century, contemporary, traditional, coastal, bohemian, farmhouse, etc.

---

## Key Elements

### Define:

- Style (modern, minimalist, Scandinavian, industrial, etc.)
- Color palette (cohesive throughout)
- Materials (wood, stone, fabric, metal)
- Furniture selection and placement
- Lighting approach (natural and artificial)
- Décor and accessories
- Overall atmosphere and mood

### Preserve:

- Original architecture
- Room proportions
- Structural elements
- Window and door placement

### Prioritize:

- Functionality for intended use
- Circulation and flow
- Realistic scale and proportions
- Natural light integration

### Quality Standards:

- Photorealistic architectural visualization
- Natural light rendering
- Refined details throughout
