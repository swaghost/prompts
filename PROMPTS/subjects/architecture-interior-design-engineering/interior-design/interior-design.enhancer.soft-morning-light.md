# Interior Design Enhancer - Soft Morning Light

## Description

Change only the lighting and atmosphere of an interior photograph to gentle morning daylight while preserving every architectural, furnishing, material, styling, and camera detail.

## Best For

- Time-of-day variations
- Mood adjustments
- Natural-lighting studies
- Peaceful atmospheric visualization

## Prerequisites

- One clear interior photograph or render
- Visible windows or another plausible daylight entry direction preferred

## Original Prompt

```text
Change the lighting in this photo to soft morning light.
```

## Production-Ready Prompt

```text
Change only the lighting in the uploaded interior image to soft morning light. Preserve the exact architecture, room layout, camera position, perspective, furniture, decor, materials, colors, windows, doors, fixtures, and object placement.

Introduce gentle early-morning daylight from the physically plausible existing window direction, with soft warm-neutral illumination, delicate long-edged shadows, controlled highlight rolloff, mild ambient fill, natural reflections, and a peaceful serene atmosphere. Keep practical fixtures consistent with the intended morning scene. Maintain realistic exposure and material color; do not apply a uniform orange filter or change the room design.
```

## Variations

- `Change the lighting to dramatic sunset light.`
- `Change the lighting to blue-hour twilight.`
- `Change the lighting to soft overcast daylight.`
- `Change the lighting to golden afternoon light.`

## Preservation Rules

- Change only illumination, exposure response, shadows, reflections, and atmosphere.
- Preserve all geometry, objects, materials, colors, and camera properties.
- Keep daylight direction consistent with actual openings.
- Avoid relighting that implies new or impossible light sources.

## Negative Prompt

```text
redesigned room, moved furniture, changed decor, changed materials, changed wall color, new window, new light fixture, impossible light direction, multiple suns, uniform orange tint, heavy yellow cast, blown windows, crushed shadows, harsh midday light, dramatic sunset unless requested, fake god rays, excessive bloom, overexposure, underexposure, text, logo, watermark
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Architecture, furniture, objects, materials, and viewpoint remain unchanged.
- [ ] Morning light enters from a physically plausible direction.
- [ ] Shadows, reflections, highlights, and ambient fill agree with that direction.
- [ ] Color remains natural without a uniform orange or yellow filter.
- [ ] The room feels softly illuminated, calm, and realistically exposed.
- [ ] No new object, fixture, opening, text, logo, or watermark appears.
