## Room Elements & Materials

### Architectural Features

**Ceiling Treatment:**

- Flat smooth ceiling at 10-12 feet height
- Concealed perimeter lighting in recessed cove
- Flush-mounted HVAC vents
- Clean plaster finish in matte white
- Optional floating ceiling plane for depth
- Minimal or no crown molding
- Integrated linear LED lighting channels

**Wall Systems:**

- Smooth painted drywall in pure white or light gray
- Feature wall options:
  - Vertical wood slat panels
  - Large format porcelain slabs
  - Textured concrete panels
  - Floor-to-ceiling glass
- Recessed baseboards (shadow gap detail)
- No traditional moldings
- Clean corner transitions

**Flooring:**

- Large format porcelain tile (24x48 or 36x36)
- Polished concrete with smooth finish
- Wide plank engineered oak (8-10" width)
- Pale gray, white oak, or light beige tones
- Minimal or no grout lines visible
- Radiant floor heating integration
- Seamless transitions between spaces

**Windows & Doors:**

- Floor-to-ceiling glass panels
- Minimal frames in black or bronze
- Pocket doors or concealed hinges
- Large sliding glass doors to exterior
- No window trim or casing
- Clean reveals at openings

### Lighting Design

**Ambient Lighting:**

- Recessed LED downlights (4-6" aperture)
- Spacing: 4-6 feet on center grid
- Trimless plaster-in fixtures
- 3000K warm white color temperature
- Dimmable systems throughout
- Concealed cove lighting at perimeter

**Accent Lighting:**

- Track lighting in black or white
- Picture lights for artwork
- Floor uplights for plants
- Linear LED strips in shelving
- Under-cabinet lighting in built-ins

**Feature Fixtures:**

- Single statement pendant (optional)
- Sculptural floor lamp with arc design
- Minimal table lamps with simple shades
- Integrated lighting in furniture pieces
- All fixtures in matte black, white, or brushed metal

### Furniture Layout & Details

- Neutral upholstery (white, gray, beige linen or leather)

- Depth: 40-44 inches

- Low rectangular or round design
- Materials: glass, marble, or wood

- Size: 48-60 inches length

**Side Tables:**

- Nesting tables or single accent table

- Materials: metal, wood, or stone

- Small footprint (18-24 inch diameter/width)

**Media Console:**

- Wall-mounted floating unit (preferred)

- Length: 72-96 inches
- Depth: 16-20 inches
- Handleless drawers with push-to-open
- Cable management concealed
- White lacquer, wood veneer, or matte gray finish

**Shelving/Display:**

- Floating shelves (12-16 inch depth)
- Wall-mounted modular systems
- Glass or wood materials
- Minimal brackets or concealed support
- Asymmetric or geometric arrangements

**Seating Accent:**

- Single lounge chair with ottoman

- Leather or performance fabric
