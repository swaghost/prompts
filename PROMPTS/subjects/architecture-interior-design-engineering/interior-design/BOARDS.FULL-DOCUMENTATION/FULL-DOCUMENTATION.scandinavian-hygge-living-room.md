**Aesthetic:** Light, airy, cozy, functional simplicity with warmth  
**Color Palette:** White, soft gray, natural wood, black accents, muted pastels

## Room Elements & Materials

### Architectural Features

**Ceiling Treatment:**

- Simple flat white ceiling at 9-10 feet height
- Matte white paint finish
- Minimal recessed lighting
- Possible exposed white-painted wood beams
- Simple crown molding or none
- Clean and unadorned
- Pendant light mounting points

**Wall Systems:**

- Painted drywall in pure white or warm white
- Wood accent walls:
  - Vertical tongue and groove boards
  - Light wood species (pine, birch, ash)
  - Natural or white-washed finish
  - Half-wall height or full wall feature
- Minimal baseboards (3-4 inches)
- Simple door and window casings
- No ornate moldings

**Flooring:**

- Wide plank white oak or ash
- Light natural finish or white wash
- Matte or low-sheen polyurethane
- Width: 6-8 inches
- Consistent throughout open spaces
- Visible wood grain and knots (character)
- Pale blonde to light honey tones

**Windows:**

- Large windows for maximum natural light
- White painted wood frames
- Simple divided lites or large panes
- Floor-to-ceiling when possible
- Multiple windows for cross-ventilation
- Window seats built into deep sills

### Lighting Design

**Pendant Lights:**

- Simple sculptural forms
- Materials: white metal, natural wood, paper
- Globe pendants in white or opal glass
- Cone or bell-shaped shades
- Suspended over dining table or seating
- Height: 30-36 inches above surfaces
- Single pendant or cluster of 2-3

**Floor Lamps:**

- Tripod legs in natural wood (ash, oak)
- White or natural linen drum shade
- Arc lamp designs
- Height: 60-68 inches
- Positioned beside sofa for reading
- Black metal accents acceptable

**Table Lamps:**

- Ceramic or wood bases in white or natural
- Simple geometric or organic forms
- White or natural fabric shades
- Height: 20-26 inches
- On side tables or console

**Candles:**

- White pillar candles
- Grouped on trays or in lanterns
- Essential for hygge atmosphere
- Various heights and sizes
- Natural beeswax preferred

**Natural Light:**

- Primary light source
- Sheer curtains to diffuse
- Mirrors to reflect and amplify
- Light colored surfaces to bounce light

### Furniture Layout & Details

**Sofa:**

- Clean-lined Scandinavian design
- Low or mid-height back
- Wooden legs in natural finish
- Upholstery: light gray, oatmeal, soft white linen
- Length: 84-90 inches
- Seat height: 17-18 inches
- Removable slip covers common
- Loose cushion backs for comfort

**Lounge Chairs:**

- Iconic Danish or Swedish designs
- Wood frames in oak, ash, or beech
- Natural or soap-finished wood
- Upholstered seat and back
- Organic curved forms
- Armchair with ottoman combination
- Positioned for conversation and light

**Coffee Table:**

- Solid wood construction
- Round, oval, or rectangular with rounded corners
- Natural wood finish
- Height: 16-18 inches
- Size: 36-48 inch diameter or equivalent
- Simple joinery visible
- Lower shelf optional

**Side Tables:**

- Small wood tables or stools
- Nesting tables in white or wood
- Round or organic shapes
- Height: 20-24 inches
- Minimal footprint

**Console/Sideboard:**

- Low horizontal piece
- Light wood with simple hardware
- OR painted white with wood top
- Length: 48-60 inches
- Height: 30-32 inches
- Open and closed storage combination
- Display area for plants and objects

**Shelving:**

- Wall-mounted wood shelves
- String-style modular shelving system
- Floating shelves in white or light wood
- Depth: 10-12 inches
- Asymmetric arrangements
- Books organized by color or size

**Seating Bench/Daybed:**

- Built-in window seat with storage
- Simple wood bench with cushions
- Nordic daybed with textiles
- Multipurpose functionality
