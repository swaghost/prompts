# PROMPT 07: Professional Moodboard

**Purpose:** Moodboard Creator  
**Workflow Position:** Phase 3 - Presentation

---

## Goal

Transform references into visual direction. Organize colors, materials, textures, furniture, and lighting into a coherent narrative.

---

## Full Prompt

```
Create a professional moodboard for a (ROOM) in (STYLE). Include a cohesive color
palette, material samples, textures, furniture silhouettes, lighting references and
décor details. Organize it with clear hierarchy, generous spacing and editorial
composition. Neutral background, realistic samples, premium presentation.
```

---

## Variables

- **ROOM:** living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, dining room, office, nursery, etc.
- **STYLE:** modern, Scandinavian, industrial, bohemian, minimalist, traditional, coastal, mid-century, farmhouse, contemporary, etc.

---

## Moodboard Components

### 1. Color Palette:

- 5-7 color swatches
- HEX codes included
- Proportional representation (60/30/10 rule)
- Color names clearly labeled
- Clean circular or square samples

### 2. Material Samples:

- Wood species (actual grain visible)
- Stone/tile samples (texture and veining)
- Fabric swatches (weave pattern)
- Metal finishes (brushed, polished)
- Glass types (clear, frosted, tinted)
- Realistic, true-to-life textures

### 3. Textures:

- Close-up surface details
- Tactile quality visible
- Varied scales (macro to micro)
- Natural and synthetic
- Pattern examples (if applicable)

### 4. Furniture Silhouettes:

- Key pieces illustrated
- Scale indicators (if helpful)
- Style consistency across selections
- Manufacturer/designer references (optional)
- Multiple views if needed (front, side)

### 5. Lighting References:

- Fixture images (pendants, sconces, lamps)
- Lighting effects (ambient, task, accent)
- Atmosphere examples (mood shots)
- Day/night comparisons
- Light quality indicators

### 6. Décor Details:

- Accessories and styling elements
- Art references or examples
- Plants/greenery
- Textiles (pillows, throws, rugs)
- Small accent objects

---

## Layout & Composition

### Hierarchy:

- **Largest:** Hero images, main inspiration photos
- **Medium:** Material samples, furniture illustrations
- **Small:** Color swatches, detail shots
- Clear visual flow (eye travels logically)
- Purposeful arrangement (not random)

### Spacing:

- Generous white space between elements
- Clean margins around perimeter
- Breathing room (not crowded)
- Not cluttered or overwhelming
- Professional layout grid

### Editorial Composition:

- Magazine-quality arrangement
- Visual balance (asymmetric but harmonious)
- Intentional placement of each element
- Asymmetric but balanced
- Curated selection (quality over quantity)

---

## Presentation Standards

### Background:

- Neutral (white, off-white, light gray)
- No distracting patterns or textures
- Clean and minimal
- Allows elements to shine
- Professional canvas

### Sample Quality:

- Realistic material representation
- High-resolution images (no pixelation)
- Accurate color reproduction
- True-to-life textures
- Professional photography quality

### Premium Presentation:

- Polished, refined appearance
- Designer-grade quality
- Client-ready (no rough drafts)
- Print or digital appropriate
- Cohesive visual language throughout

## Pro Tips

### Less is More:

- Don't include everything - curate carefully
- 8-12 well-chosen elements better than 25 random ones
- Quality and cohesion over quantity

### Tell a Story:

- Elements should relate to each other
- Create a narrative flow
- Each element supports the overall concept
- Visual harmony throughout

### Real vs. Generic:

- Use actual product images when possible
- Real material photos, not stock textures
- Specific furniture pieces, not generic silhouettes
- Authentic references create clarity

### Organization:

- Group related items together
- Create visual relationships between elements
- Use alignment to create structure
- Balance heavy and light visual weight

### Format Considerations:

- **Digital:** RGB color mode, 72-300 DPI, screen-optimized
- **Print:** CMYK color mode, 300 DPI minimum, proper bleeds
- Consider final output when creating

---

## Moodboard Variations

### Concept Moodboard:

- More inspirational, abstract
- Focus on mood and feeling
- Lifestyle images, textures, colors
- Less specific product selections

### Specification Moodboard:

- More detailed and specific
- Exact products with model numbers
- Precise material samples
- Budget and sourcing information

### Style Moodboard:

- Pure aesthetic direction
- Visual style references
- Color and texture focus
- Less about specific products
