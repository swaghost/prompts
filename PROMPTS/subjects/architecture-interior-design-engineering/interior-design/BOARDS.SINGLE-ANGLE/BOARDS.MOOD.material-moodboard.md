# Material Moodboard - Interior Design Material Presentation

## Description

Create a high-end interior design material moodboard using only the materials present in the 3D scene. Arrange the samples in an artistic layered composition similar to luxury architectural boards, with realistic textures, shadows, and soft studio lighting. This prompt produces sophisticated physical material boards with premium presentation quality—ideal for client presentations, design development, material specifications, and professional interior design communication.

## Moodboard Overview

**Purpose:** Material palette presentation  
**Source:** Materials from existing 3D scene  
**Style:** Luxury architectural board aesthetic  
**Presentation:** Physical material samples, artistic arrangement  
**Application:** Interior design, client presentations, material specifications
