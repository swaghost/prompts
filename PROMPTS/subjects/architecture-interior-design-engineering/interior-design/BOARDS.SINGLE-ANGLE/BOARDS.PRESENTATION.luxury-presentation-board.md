# Luxury Presentation Board - Interior Render to Editorial Board

## Description

Transform an existing interior render into a luxury editorial presentation board with precise Swiss-style layout. This prompt preserves all original design elements (furniture, materials, styling, lighting, camera perspective) while creating a complete professional presentation board with material palette, furniture specifications, joinery details, interior elevations, floor plans, lighting callouts, dimensions, and product references. Perfect for client-ready presentations, portfolios, and competitions.

## Usage

Use this when you have a 3D render or interior design image that you want to convert into a comprehensive presentation board that looks like it came from a high-end design firm. The AI will maintain your exact design while building editorial-quality documentation around it.

## What This Preserves

- **Exact furniture**: Keep every piece as shown
- **Materials & finishes**: Preserve all surface selections
- **Styling**: Maintain décor, accessories, artwork
- **Lighting**: Keep all fixtures and ambiance
- **Camera perspective**: Exact viewpoint and angle
- **Design intent**: Overall aesthetic and mood

## What This Adds

- Material palette with swatches
- Furniture specification sheet
- Joinery notes and details
- Interior elevations (wall views)
- Floor plan excerpt with annotations
- Detail sections with callouts
- Lighting schedule
- Dimensions and measurements
- Product references
- Swiss grid layout (off-white paper, soft beige accents, refined typography, precise spacing)

## Board Aesthetic

- **Layout**: Clean Swiss grid, generous white space
- **Typography**: Refined, precise, professional
- **Colours**: Off-white background, soft beige and grey palette
- **Spacing**: Organized, aligned, breathing room
- **Quality**: Editorial, highly detailed, suitable for Behance/Pinterest

## Board Components

**Hero Image**: Your original render (largest element, focal point)

**Material Palette**:

- Flooring sample
- Wall finish swatch
- Wood tone
- Fabric texture
- Metal finish
- Stone/counter sample
- All with labels and specifications

**Furniture Specifications**:

- Each piece labeled with dimensions
- Manufacturer or style reference
- Material and finish notes
- Custom vs. standard notation

**Interior Elevations**:

- Flat wall views showing millwork, shelving, art placement
- Height callouts
- Material notes

**Floor Plan Excerpt**:

- Annotated layout showing furniture placement
- Circulation paths
- Dimensions
- Room labels

**Detail Sections**:

- Joinery details (how cabinets meet walls, shelf brackets, etc.)
- Material transitions
- Construction callouts

**Lighting Schedule**:

- Fixture list
- Placement diagram
- Lumens/wattage
- Kelvin temperature

**Dimensions & Callouts**:

- Key measurements throughout
- Arrows and leader lines
- Neat annotations

**Product References**:

- Similar items for sourcing
- Paint colours
- Material suppliers

## Layout Principles

**Swiss Grid System**:

- Columns and rows for alignment
- Consistent margins
- Visual hierarchy through scale
- Negative space as design element

**Typography**:

- Sans-serif for clean look (Helvetica, Futura, similar)
- Size hierarchy (titles, headings, body, callouts)
- All caps for labels
- Proper kerning and spacing

**Colour Palette**:

- Off-white background (#F2EFE9, #FAF8F3)
- Soft beige accents (#DDD8CC, #C9C4BA)
- Warm greys for text (#6B6B6B, #4A4A4A)
- Black for emphasis (sparingly)

## Quality Standards

- High resolution (print-ready detail)
- Crisp text (readable at all sizes)
- Professional line weights
- Accurate scale relationships
- Consistent styling throughout
- No spelling errors or sloppy alignment

## Output Format

- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 (vertical) - ideal for Pinterest, print, presentations
- **Style**: Raw photographic quality with no artistic filters
- **Version**: Midjourney v6.1 for maximum control

## Best Practices

- Start with a high-quality, well-lit render
- Ensure your original render has good composition
- The hero image should be large enough to see details
- All original design elements will be preserved exactly
- Additional documentation built around (not replacing) your render
- Result is print-ready, client-ready, competition-ready
