# Existing Room to Complete Interior Design Concept

## Conversion

- **Source type:** Existing room image or architectural interior reference
- **Target type:** Complete photorealistic interior design concept

## Description

Develop a complete room concept in a selected design style while preserving the source architecture and proportions. The output coordinates palette, materials, furniture, lighting, decor, mood, circulation, and realistic scale.

## Usage

Use for early design development, client concept presentations, real-estate staging, room makeovers, style exploration, and portfolio visualization.

## Prerequisites

- One clear room image or architectural reference
- A selected `(ROOM TYPE)`
- A selected `(STYLE)`
- Any known functional, occupancy, storage, or budget requirements

## Preservation Rules

- Preserve original walls, openings, ceiling height, room proportions, and structural features.
- Keep scale, circulation, and furniture placement physically plausible.
- Do not invent structural changes unless explicitly requested.

## Complete Prompt

```text
Create a complete interior design concept for this (ROOM TYPE), based on (STYLE). Preserve the original architecture and proportions. Define a cohesive color palette, materials, furniture, lighting, décor and overall mood. Prioritize functionality, circulation and realistic scale.

Photorealistic architectural visualization, natural light, refined details.
```

## Variables

### `(ROOM TYPE)`

Living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, home office, dining room, entryway, walk-in closet, nursery, home theater, library, master suite, guest bedroom, powder room, laundry room, mudroom, sunroom, basement lounge, attic conversion, or studio apartment.

### `(STYLE)`

Scandinavian minimalist, mid-century modern, industrial loft, contemporary luxury, traditional classic, coastal, bohemian eclectic, Japanese zen, Art Deco, farmhouse rustic, Mediterranean villa, urban modern, French provincial, transitional, Hollywood Regency, tropical resort, Nordic hygge, Italian Renaissance, Moroccan, or English cottage.

## Example

```text
Create a complete interior design concept for this master bedroom, based on Scandinavian minimalist. Preserve the original architecture and proportions. Define a cohesive color palette, materials, furniture, lighting, décor and overall mood. Prioritize functionality, circulation and realistic scale.

Photorealistic architectural visualization, natural light, refined details.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
changed architecture, moved walls, invented windows, changed openings, distorted room proportions, oversized furniture, undersized furniture, blocked circulation, floating objects, impossible construction, incoherent materials, flat lighting, oversaturated colors, fake plants, generic stock-photo styling, low detail, illustration, cartoon, watermark, logo
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Room type and style variables are replaced.
- [ ] Existing architecture and proportions remain unchanged.
- [ ] Furniture scale and circulation are realistic.
- [ ] Color, materials, furniture, lighting, decor, and mood form one concept.
- [ ] Natural light and material response are physically coherent.
- [ ] Final image reads as professional architectural visualization.
