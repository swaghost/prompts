## Description of Resulting Video

A 7-second ultra-photorealistic construction time-lapse video documenting the complete build sequence of a modern skyscraper from ground-level foundations to finished tower, maintaining consistent camera position and perspective throughout the entire animation while preserving strict construction realism. The sequence follows completely realistic construction sequencing, beginning at ground level with foundation work and structural elements, then progressing strictly upward as one continuous structure with scaffolding support systems visible throughout active construction zones. The animation adheres exactly to real-world skyscraper construction methodology—never animating or lifting pre-built structures into place, but instead showing organic growth from bottom to top with middle-of-construction states clearly depicting lower completed floors while upper floors remain under active construction with visible scaffolding, cranes, and support systems. The sequence concludes with removal of construction equipment, installation of architectural details and landscaping, and final tower completion. The entire video maintains dark moody atmosphere with cloudy weather, naturally muted colors, and cinematic gloomy ambiance matching overcast conditions, creating a dramatic yet photorealistic architectural construction documentary aesthetic.

## Video Prompt

**CRITICAL:**
Create a 7-second ultra-photorealistic construction time-lapse of the skyscraper, with the same camera position, angle, and focal length throughout the entire animation, preserving the exact same focal length, framing, and perspective as the source image.

**Subject:**
Modern skyscraper construction from ground-level foundations to completed tower, following completely realistic construction sequencing with visible scaffolding, support systems, and organic bottom-to-top growth.

**Setting/Environment:**
Urban construction site with surrounding cityscape visible for context. Dark overcast atmosphere with cloudy weather, naturally muted colors, and moody cinematic conditions throughout entire sequence.

**Visual Characteristics:**

**Construction Sequencing Requirements (CRITICAL):**

- Construction MUST follow completely realistic sequencing
- Animation MUST resemble beginning ground-level foundations and progressing strictly from the bottom upward as one continuous structure
- NEVER animate or lift structures—growth must be organic from bottom to top
- NEVER generate isolated floors or sections
- Every stage MUST include appropriate scaffolding support during active construction
- Sequence MUST be exactly like a real skyscraper under construction

**Detailed Construction Phases:**

**Phase 1: Foundation & Ground Level (0-1 second):**

- Site preparation and excavation
- Foundation work at ground level
- Initial structural elements emerging
- Construction equipment active (cranes, machinery)
- Ground-level concrete and steel framework beginning

**Phase 2: Structural Growth - Lower Floors (1-3 seconds):**

- Steel framework rising floor by floor from bottom
- Continuous upward progression
- Scaffolding surrounding active construction zones
- Cranes positioned at construction level
- Lower floors begin structural completion while upper floors under active construction
- Visible construction activity at top edge of structure

**Phase 3: Mid-Construction State (3-4 seconds):**

- Show middle of the top under active construction
- Lower floors should appear finished or nearly finished
- Upper floors clearly under construction with visible scaffolding
- Construction equipment and support systems visible at height
- Realistic work-in-progress appearance
- Never show completed floors magically appearing

**Phase 4: Exterior Completion (4-5 seconds):**

- Glass façade installation progressing upward
- Scaffolding gradually removing from lower sections as completion progresses
- Building envelope closing from bottom to top
- Top floors completing last
- Crane removal as upper floors finish

**Phase 5: Final Details (5-6 seconds):**

- Architectural details and landscaping installation
- Ground-level finishing work
- Site cleanup and construction equipment removal
- Final exterior polish

**Phase 6: Completed Structure (6-7 seconds):**

- Fully completed skyscraper
- All scaffolding and construction equipment removed
- Landscaping complete
- Final tower standing complete in urban environment
