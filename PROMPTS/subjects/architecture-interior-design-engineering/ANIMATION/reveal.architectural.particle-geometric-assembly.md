# Architectural Reveal - Particle Geometric Assembly

## Classification

Architectural animation; modular particle assembly; technical visualization; contemporary residential exterior; photorealistic construction reveal.

## Description of Resulting Video

A 12-15 second precision reveal follows thousands of metallic and glass cubes, triangles, and hexagons as they organize from an abstract field into a foundation, walls, transparent windows, and roof. Pieces lock together like an engineered system, then fuse and lose their seams to reveal a complete photorealistic contemporary house.

## Usage

Use for modern architecture practices, engineering and technology brands, modular systems, facade launches, parametric design showcases, and concise technical social films.

## Engines and Models

- Sora or Google Veo: preferred for long-range geometry and camera consistency.
- Runway Gen-3/Gen-4: suitable for staged assembly with a matched final reference.
- Kling: useful for rigid-body motion and high-detail contemporary exteriors.
- Pika: use for short modular lock-in inserts when a full-duration generation is unavailable.

## Prerequisites and Reference Lock

- Supply a final exterior reference with unambiguous footprint, elevations, window grid, and roofline.
- Assign cubes to structural mass, triangles to transitions and roof planes, and hexagons to facade/detail zones.
- Lock the building origin, finished floor level, horizon, lens, and orbit radius.
- Keep each installed piece fixed; later phases may fuse seams but cannot relocate geometry.
- Match metallic and glass particle mass to the final opaque and transparent components.

## Transformation Physics and Continuity

Shapes behave as rigid bodies guided by a visible but restrained architectural grid. They translate and rotate along clean paths, decelerate near assigned coordinates, and lock once with no bounce or interpenetration. Lower structural pieces install before upper loads. Transparent shapes collect only in window openings; opaque particles never contaminate glazing. Puzzle seams shrink through controlled fusion after the building envelope is complete. No piece multiplies or vanishes without becoming material, and the footprint, openings, and roofline remain invariant.

## Beat-by-Beat Timeline (five phases with times adjusted to variant duration)

1. **0.0-2.4s - Abstract field:** Metallic and glass cubes, triangles, and hexagons hover around a faint technical grid.
2. **2.4-4.8s - Foundation logic:** Base pieces align to survey lines and lock into the exact footprint and structural datum.
3. **4.8-8.6s - Envelope assembly:** Walls rise in courses; transparent particles reserve and fill window openings; upper pieces define the roofline.
4. **8.6-11.8s - Precision fusion:** Smaller particles complete frames and details while metallic modules become concrete, cladding, and structure and seams disappear.
5. **11.8-14.0s - Modern reveal:** The orbit settles on a complete photorealistic contemporary house under crisp dramatic light.

```text
Generate one continuous 14-second technical architectural reveal in an abstract graphite space. Thousands of small geometric shapes, specifically cubes, triangles, and hexagons, float in a controlled particle field; alternate brushed metallic silver, matte graphite, white ceramic, and optically clear glass materials. A stabilized camera makes a restrained clockwise 25-degree orbit on a 35 mm lens while maintaining the building center and level horizon. At 2.4 seconds a subtle survey grid activates and particles organize with precise engineered motion. Metallic cubes rotate into alignment and lock into the foundation pattern at the base. Structural modules then rise in logical courses to form columns and walls. Triangles bridge corners, resolve angled transitions, and assemble the roof planes. Transparent cubes and hexagons move exclusively into window openings and fuse into continuous architectural glazing. Show every shape following a deliberate path, easing into position, locking once, and remaining fixed. By 8.6 seconds the foundation, walls, windows, and roof are recognizable and faithful to the final reference. Smaller particles complete mullions, reveals, joints, soffits, railings, and facade details. The assembled puzzle then fuses: seams tighten and disappear while particle materials convert into realistic concrete, steel, stone cladding, coated aluminum frames, and clear glass without changing geometry. End with a complete photorealistic contemporary house, exact proportions, crisp edges, realistic structural logic, dramatic directional light, subtle reflections, premium architectural render quality, and no loose geometric particles. Hold the final exterior from 11.8 to 14 seconds with no cut.
```

## Camera and Lighting

Use a measured orbit rather than a dramatic spin so the foundation-to-roof order remains readable. Start with cool rim light that separates each shape. Introduce a strong architectural key and clean skylight as surfaces fuse, with controlled specular highlights on metal and glass and deep but legible shadows.

## Sound Design

Layer low electronic ambience with precise ticks, magnetic snaps, glass pings, and damped mechanical locks. Increase rhythmic density as walls rise, then use a brief sub-bass seal when seams fuse. Finish with a clean tonal resolve and restrained exterior ambience.
