# Construction Assembly Floating Room Flythrough

## Description

A 10-second ultra-photorealistic architectural CGI sequence showing a floating room being constructed from foundation to fully furnished interior, with a cinematic camera flythrough ending in an aerial hero shot.

## Prerequisites

- Uploaded storyboard image showing the floating room design
- Reference image of the target room architecture and design

## Storyboard Prompt

Create a 6-panel storyboard illustrating the floating room construction sequence:

**Panel 1 (0:00-0:02):** Wide shot showing concrete foundation and structural columns assembling rapidly from below in mid-air, floating against neutral background, early construction phase with raw materials appearing, camera positioned for clear view of foundation assembly.

**Panel 2 (0:02-0:04):** Medium shot of walls, wooden framing, and ceiling constructing themselves in sequence, visible electrical wiring, plumbing, and HVAC systems connecting, insulation and drywall appearing, construction progression clearly visible, camera angle showing multiple construction layers.

**Panel 3 (0:04-0:06):** Interior view showing wood flooring installing, wall panels appearing, lighting fixtures mounting, built-in furniture assembling, room becoming fully furnished with modern interior, warm lighting beginning to turn on, transformation from construction to finished space.

**Panel 4 (0:06-0:07):** Camera positioned outside the open side of the floating room, beginning smooth flythrough movement toward the interior, showing the furnished bedroom with shelves, stone wall accent, plants, complete interior visible, inviting composition.

**Panel 5 (0:07-0:08):** Interior flythrough shot showcasing bed, shelving details, stone wall texture, plants, designer lighting, premium furniture in macro-quality detail, camera gliding through the space, warm atmospheric lighting, shallow depth of field.

**Panel 6 (0:08-0:10):** Wide exterior aerial shot showing camera exiting through panoramic window, revealing cinematic fog, dense forests, mountains, and winding river below, completed floating room in context of dramatic landscape, hero ending composition with environmental atmosphere.

Style: Ultra-photorealistic architectural CGI, Unreal Engine quality, cinematic composition, 16:9 format, each panel clearly showing camera position and construction phase progression, warm interior lighting contrasting with cool exterior atmosphere.
