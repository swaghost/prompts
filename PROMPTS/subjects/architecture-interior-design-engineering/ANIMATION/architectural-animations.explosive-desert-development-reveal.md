## Description

A 10-second ultra-dynamic photorealistic architectural CGI reveal in which a luxury residential complex constructs itself across a bright desert through four timed ground-impulse events. Each pulse produces a concentrated flash, circular pressure wave, dust displacement, and physically grounded structural assembly, progressing from foundation to tower, landscape infrastructure, and final aerial hero reveal.

## Effect Design

### Ground-Impulse Construction Pulse

Each major construction stage begins with a physically grounded ground impulse. A concentrated flash erupts from the active construction boundary, followed by a circular pressure wave, radial dust displacement, and realistic particles. The pulse is a transition trigger for construction activity, not a destructive explosion.

### Four Pulse Structure

1. **First pulse:** Foundation and first structural elements.
2. **Second pulse:** Floors four through six.
3. **Third pulse:** Floors seven through eight.
4. **Fourth and strongest pulse:** Upper floors, facade completion, and final structural lock.

The pulse intensity may escalate, but the building must remain structurally intact. Flash, shockwave, dust, and assembly must resolve quickly enough for the next floors to build visibly.

### Construction Boundary Effect

The active construction boundary moves upward with the camera. Below or behind the boundary, the building is complete; ahead or above it, the structure remains unfinished. This moving boundary prevents distant independent assembly and makes the construction feel causally connected to the camera's path.

### Site-Development Wave

After the tower is complete, a final ground impulse expands outward from the foundation. Roads, paths, pools, water features, gardens, vegetation, and infrastructure assemble in organized radial and geometric zones. The site-development wave must preserve the tower and desert context while revealing the completed master plan.

## Camera and Cinematography

- Begin with a rapid push from a wide desert establishing shot.
- Rise and orbit around the active tower during structural assembly.
- Use extreme speed ramps at each flash and shockwave, with only brief readable slow-motion at impact.
- Pull backward and upward after the tower completes to reveal the expanding site-development wave.
- Finish with a high aerial pull-back showing the entire master plan.
- Maintain spatial continuity, realistic parallax, and stable building proportions.
