# 3x3 Product Storyboard - Vertical 9:16 Format

## Description

Create one final vertical 9:16 image as a clean 3×3 commercial product storyboard grid with 9 equal panels, using an attached photo as the strict product anchor. Keep the exact same product identity, shape, proportions, materials, colors, logo, packaging and key design details. Do not redesign the product. Build a premium advertising shoot around it with varied angles, compositions, and lighting across 9 distinct panels.

## Usage

Perfect for commercial product photography, advertising campaigns, social media stories (Instagram/TikTok vertical format), product launches, brand presentations, and portfolio showcases. Ideal for demonstrating product versatility, creating premium campaign visuals, and storytelling through sequential imagery.

## Prerequisites

- Product reference photo (exact product to match)
- Product category and main features
- Target audience and campaign goals
- Brand aesthetic and guidelines
- Desired photography style (studio vs lifestyle)
- Campaign narrative or theme

## Image Prompt

Main product: [PRODUCT NAME/CATEGORY - e.g., A BAG]

**[ATTACH PRODUCT REFERENCE PHOTO]**

Create one final vertical 9:16 image as a clean 3×3 commercial product storyboard grid with 9 equal panels.

**Product Consistency (CRITICAL - Must Match Reference Exactly):**

- **Identity:** Exact same product from reference photo
- **Shape & Proportions:** Identical dimensions and silhouette
- **Materials:** Same textures and material types
- **Colors:** Exact color matching
- **Logo/Branding:** Same placement and design
- **Packaging:** Identical if shown
- **Key Design Details:** All details must match reference
- **DO NOT REDESIGN THE PRODUCT**

**9-Panel Storyboard Layout:**

Build a premium advertising shoot around the product. Each panel must feel different in angle, distance, light or composition.

**Panel 1: Wide Hero Scene**

- Establishing shot with product in lifestyle or studio context
- Full environment visible
- Product prominent but within larger scene
- Sets the campaign mood and tone

**Panel 2: Clear Front Product Shot**

- Direct, clean product presentation
- Full product visible from front angle
- Professional studio or lifestyle setting
- Clear view of key features

**Panel 3: Three-Quarter Product Angle**

- Angled view showing depth and dimension
- Product as hero with context
- Shows multiple sides of product
- Clean, commercial aesthetic

**Panel 4: Macro Detail of Texture/Material/Logo**

- Extreme close-up of product detail
- Material texture emphasis
- Logo, stitching, or craftsmanship focus
- Shallow depth of field for premium feel

**Panel 5: Product in Hand or Natural Use**

- Product being held, worn, or used
- Human interaction (hands, body)
- Natural, lifestyle context
- Usage demonstration

**Panel 6: Product with Packaging**

- Product alongside or with packaging
- Unboxing moment or presentation
- Brand identity visible
- Premium presentation aesthetic

**Panel 7: Atmospheric Light-and-Shadow Frame**

- Dramatic lighting on product
- Strong shadows or highlights
- Mood and atmosphere emphasis
- Artistic composition with product

**Panel 8: Close-Up Detail with Shallow Depth of Field**

- Tight crop on specific product feature
- Bokeh background for luxury feel
- Texture and craftsmanship focus
- Premium photography technique

**Panel 9: Final Campaign Hero Shot**

- Strongest, most impactful composition
- Product as star of the frame
- Memorable, campaign-defining image
- Aspirational and premium aesthetic

**Technical Requirements:**

**Format:**

- Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio (Instagram Story / TikTok format)
- 3×3 grid of 9 equal panels
- Thin, clean borders between panels
- No text, no captions, no watermark

**Photography Quality:**

- Premium commercial photography
- Realistic studio or lifestyle lighting
- Sharp focus with intentional depth of field control
- Real materials and textures
- Natural shadows and highlights

**Consistency:**

- Product remains identical across all 9 panels
- Cohesive color grading throughout
- Unified photographic style
- Campaign-level polish and cohesion

**Variety:**

- Each panel different in angle, distance, light, or composition
- Mix of wide, medium, close-up, and macro shots
- Varied lighting scenarios
- Different contexts (studio, lifestyle, detail)
- Diverse compositional approaches

Generate the complete 9:16 vertical storyboard with 9 equal panels, maintaining perfect product consistency while creating diverse, premium commercial imagery throughout.

## Negative Prompt

Product redesigned, inconsistent product appearance, different colors or materials, modified logo, altered proportions, cheap photography, poor lighting, artificial looking, cluttered composition, text overlays, captions, watermarks, thick borders, unequal panels, low quality, blurry focus, unrealistic materials, harsh lighting, repetitive angles, grouped by type, amateur photography

- **Format:** Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio

- **Borders:** Thin, clean separation between panels

- **Product Consistency:** Exact match to reference
- **Variety:** Each panel unique in execution

## Panel-by-Panel Breakdown

**Panel 1 - Wide Hero Scene:**

- Purpose: Establish context and mood
- Framing: Wide shot, product in environment
- Lighting: Overall scene illumination
- Product size: Visible but within larger frame

**Panel 2 - Clear Front Product Shot:**

- Purpose: Primary product presentation
- Framing: Full product, straight-on view
- Lighting: Even, professional lighting
- Product size: Large, clear, identifiable

**Panel 3 - Three-Quarter Product Angle:**

- Purpose: Show product dimension
- Framing: 45-degree angle or similar
- Lighting: Dimensional, showing form
- Product size: Hero product with context

**Panel 4 - Macro Detail:**

- Purpose: Showcase craftsmanship/quality
- Framing: Extreme close-up
- Lighting: Focused, highlighting texture
- Product size: Detail fills frame

**Panel 5 - Product in Hand/Use:**

- Purpose: Demonstrate functionality and scale
- Framing: Product with human element
- Lighting: Natural or lifestyle lighting
- Product size: In context with hand/body

**Panel 6 - Product with Packaging:**

- Purpose: Show brand presentation
- Framing: Product + packaging together
- Lighting: Clean commercial lighting
- Product size: Both product and package visible

**Panel 7 - Atmospheric Light-and-Shadow:**

- Purpose: Create mood and drama
- Framing: Artistic composition
- Lighting: Strong contrast, dramatic shadows
- Product size: Silhouette or partially lit

**Panel 8 - Close-Up with Shallow DOF:**

- Purpose: Premium detail showcase
- Framing: Tight crop on feature
- Lighting: Soft, focused light
- Product size: Detail with bokeh background

**Panel 9 - Final Campaign Hero Shot:**

- Purpose: Strongest brand image
- Framing: Most impactful composition
- Lighting: Perfect, aspirational lighting
- Product size: Dominant, premium presentation

## Photography Style Guidelines

**Premium Commercial Aesthetic:**

- Professional lighting setup
- Studio-quality or high-end lifestyle
- Intentional composition
- Editorial polish
- Brand-appropriate sophistication

**Studio Lighting Options:**

- Softbox for even illumination
- Key light with fill for dimension
- Backlighting for separation
- Rim lighting for drama
- Clean white or colored backgrounds

**Lifestyle Lighting Options:**

- Natural window light
- Golden hour outdoor light
- Interior ambient with accents
- Realistic environmental lighting
- Authentic settings and contexts

**Depth of Field Variety:**

- Deep DOF for full product clarity (panels 2, 3, 6)
- Shallow DOF for premium feel (panels 4, 8)
- Variable DOF for artistic effect (panels 1, 7, 9)
