# Commercial Product Storyboard Grid - 9 Panel Vertical

## Prompt Description

Create a clean vertical 9:16 commercial product storyboard presented as a 3x3 grid with 9 equal panels. Each panel tells a different part of the product story while maintaining the exact same product identity throughout. No redesign—build a premium advertising shoot around the product with varied angles, contexts, and hero moments. Designed for professional commercial campaigns with realistic studio/lifestyle lighting and sharp focus.

---

## Complete Prompt Template

**Main product: [A BAG]**

**Create one final vertical 9:16 image as a clean 3x3 commercial product storyboard grid with 9 equal panels, using the attached image as the strict reference anchor. Keep the exact same product identity, shape, proportions, materials, colors, logo, packaging and key design details. Do not redesign the product. Build a premium advertising shoot around it.**

**Panel 1**: Wide hero scene  
**Panel 2**: Clean full-shot product view  
**Panel 3**: Three-quarter product angle  
**Panel 4**: Macro detail of texture/material/logo  
**Panel 5**: Product in hand or in natural use  
**Panel 6**: Product with atmosphere/lifestyle shadow frame  
**Panel 8**: Close-up detail with shallow depth of field  
**Panel 9**: Final campaign hero shot

**Each panel must feel different in angle, distance, light or composition. Premium commercial photography, realistic studio or lifestyle lighting, sharp focus, real materials, natural shadows, no text.**

## Structured Breakdown

### OVERALL SPECIFICATIONS

**Format**: 9:16 vertical aspect ratio  
**Grid Layout**: 3x3 grid with 9 equal panels  
**Presentation**: Clean commercial storyboard aesthetic  
**Quality**: Premium commercial photography standard

### PRODUCT CONSISTENCY (ALL 9 PANELS)

**Critical Requirements**:

- Exact same product identity
- Same shape and proportions
- Same materials and textures
- Same colors throughout
- Same logo and branding elements
- Same packaging (if applicable)
- Same key design details

**Do NOT Redesign**: Build shoot around existing product, don't alter the product itself

**Product Type Examples**:

- Bags (handbags, backpacks, clutches, totes)
- Accessories (wallets, shoes, jewelry)
- Electronics (headphones, devices, gadgets)
- Cosmetics (bottles, compacts, palettes)
- Food/Beverage (packaging, bottles, containers)

### PANEL-BY-PANEL STORYBOARD LAYOUT

**Panel 1 (Top-Left): Wide Hero Scene**

- Establishing shot showing product in environment
- Context and atmosphere
- Product visible but scene sets the mood
- Lifestyle or studio setting

**Panel 2 (Top-Center): Clean Full-Shot Product View**

- Product photographed in full, clearly visible
- Clean presentation, minimal distractions
- Straight-on or slight angle
- Studio or neutral background
- Product as hero of frame

**Panel 3 (Top-Right): Three-Quarter Product Angle**

- Product shot at 45-degree angle (three-quarter view)
- Shows depth and dimension
- Reveals multiple sides of product
- Professional product photography angle

**Panel 4 (Middle-Left): Macro Detail of Texture/Material/Logo**

- Extreme close-up of product details
- Texture of materials (leather grain, fabric weave, metal finish)
- Logo or branding detail
- Craftsmanship showcase
- Sharp focus on detail

**Panel 5 (Middle-Center): Product in Hand or Natural Use**

- Product being held, worn, or used
- Human interaction (hands visible)
- Natural use case demonstration
- Lifestyle context
- Realistic scale reference

**Panel 6 (Middle-Right): Product with Atmosphere/Lifestyle Shadow Frame**

- Product styled with atmospheric lighting
- Interesting shadows or silhouettes
- Lifestyle framing elements
- Environmental context
- Mood and ambiance

**Panel 7 (Bottom-Left): [Flexible Composition]**

- Additional angle or context shot
- Complement to other panels
- Could be: overhead view, side profile, alternative styling

**Panel 8 (Bottom-Center): Close-Up Detail with Shallow Depth of Field**

- Close detail shot
- Shallow depth of field (blurred background)
- Focus on key product feature
- Bokeh effect
- Artistic and premium aesthetic

**Panel 9 (Bottom-Right): Final Campaign Hero Shot**

- Strongest final statement image
- Product presented at its best
- Campaign-worthy presentation
- Memorable closing visual
- Commercial advertising quality

### VISUAL VARIETY REQUIREMENTS

**Each Panel Must Differ In**:

- Camera angle and perspective
- Distance from product (wide, medium, close, macro)
- Lighting setup or quality
- Composition and framing
- Context or styling

**Maintain Cohesion Through**:

- Consistent product identity
- Unified color palette
- Similar lighting mood (even if varied)
- Professional commercial aesthetic

### LIGHTING & PHOTOGRAPHY STANDARDS

**Lighting Types**:

- Realistic studio lighting (softbox, key light, fill light)
- Natural lifestyle lighting (window light, outdoor)
- Dramatic commercial lighting (spotlights, rim lighting)
- Soft diffused light for texture detail

**Photography Quality**:

- Sharp focus where intended
- Proper exposure (not overblown or underexposed)
- Realistic shadows (natural shadow behavior)
- Real materials (authentic texture rendering)
- Professional color grading

**Technical Standards**:

- No artificial or fake look
- Photorealistic product rendering
- Natural depth of field
- Accurate material representation

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Product Design Changes Between Panels

**Solution**: Emphasize "Use attached image as strict reference anchor. Do not redesign product. Keep exact same identity, shape, proportions, colors, logo, materials throughout all 9 panels."

### Issue: Panels Look Too Similar or Repetitive

**Solution**: Specify "Each panel must feel different in angle, distance, light, or composition. Vary wide shots, medium, close-ups, macro details, and atmospheric frames."

### Issue: Low-Quality or Unrealistic Product Rendering

**Solution**: Request "Premium commercial photography quality, realistic studio lighting, sharp focus, real materials, authentic textures, professional color grading."

### Issue: Text or Watermarks Appear

**Solution**: Explicitly state "No text, no captions, no watermark, clean commercial presentation"

### Issue: Grid Layout Not Equal or Misaligned

**Solution**: Specify "3x3 grid with 9 perfectly equal panels, clean layout, consistent spacing"

### Issue: Product Not Visible in Some Panels

**Solution**: Clarify "Product must be visible and recognizable in all 9 panels, though at different scales and contexts"

## Workflow Tips

1. **Prepare Product Reference**: High-quality photo showing product clearly from best angle
2. **Define Product Type**: Specify category (bag, shoe, bottle, etc.) for context
3. **Choose Lighting Mood**: Decide on studio, lifestyle, or dramatic lighting approach
4. **List Panel Requirements**: Customize panel descriptions for your specific product
5. **Generate Storyboard Grid**: Create complete 9-panel grid in one generation
6. **Review Product Consistency**: Verify same product identity across all panels
7. **Check Visual Variety**: Ensure each panel offers different perspective/context
8. **Refine Specific Panels**: Request adjustments while maintaining overall cohesion

## Technical Considerations

**Aspect Ratio & Resolution**:

- Format: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920px or 2160x3840px)

- Grid: 3 columns x 3 rows, equal sizing

**File Output**:

- PNG for maximum quality
- JPG for web/social media
- sRGB color space for digital display

**Grid Construction**:

- Equal panel dimensions
- Thin or no borders between panels
- Aligned rows and columns
