# Premium Product Collage - 9 Panel Vertical Grid (Female Model)

## Prompt Description

Create a premium 9:16 vertical 3x3 collage showcasing a product with a female model across 9 equal panels. Each panel features the same model, product design, packaging, logo, colors, materials, and proportions. The model appears exactly 4 times (top-center, middle-left, middle-right, bottom-center) while product-focused shots fill remaining panels. Designed for high-end commercial product advertising with photorealistic editorial quality.

---

## Complete Prompt

**Using the attached product image as the strict reference, create one final vertical 9:16 premium 3x3 collage with 9 equal panels, thin borders, no text, no watermark. Keep the exact same product design, packaging, logo, label, colors, materials, proportions and applicator. The product must appear in all 9 panels. The same female model must appear exactly 4 times only: top-center, middle-left, middle-right, bottom-center.**

**Panel layout: 1-product laying flat or elegant hands/brand, 2 - model face close-up / grip, product soft on fabric / silk / glass, 4 - model holding product near face, 5 - macro detail of logo / texture / packaging, 6 - product application close-up, 7 - reflective still life with mirror / glass, 8-model in an unusual angle, interacting with the product (viewed strictly from above/ below / close-up), 9-close-up hero shot with hands and product. Keep one cohesive luxury beauty campaign mood: soft premium light, controlled palette based on the product colors, glossy textures, realistic skin, realistic hands, sharp product detail, photorealistic editorial quality.**

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Model Appears Inconsistent Across Panels

**Solution**: Specify "Use exact same female model with identical appearance, same face, same skin tone, same styling in panels 2, 4, 6, and 8"

### Issue: Product Design Changes Between Panels

**Solution**: Emphasize "Keep exact same product design, logo, colors, proportions, packaging in all 9 panels without variation"

### Issue: Grid Layout Not Equal or Aligned

**Solution**: Request "3x3 grid with 9 perfectly equal panels, aligned rows and columns, consistent spacing"

### Issue: Text or Watermarks Appear

**Solution**: Explicitly state "No text, no captions, no watermark, no interface elements"

### Issue: Wrong Aspect Ratio or Borders Too Thick

**Solution**: Specify "9:16 vertical format with thin subtle borders between panels"

### Issue: Panel Content Doesn't Match Description

**Solution**: List panel-by-panel requirements in numbered format for clarity

## Workflow Tips

1. **Prepare Product Reference**: High-quality product photo from multiple angles
2. **Define Color Palette**: Extract primary colors from product for cohesive grid
3. **Choose Model Style**: Specify demographic, styling, and mood
4. **Generate Initial Grid**: Create first version with all specifications
5. **Refine Individual Panels**: Request adjustments to specific panels if needed
6. **Check Consistency**: Verify model appears exactly 4 times, product in all panels
7. **Final Quality Check**: Ensure photorealistic quality, sharp details, proper borders

- Alignment: Precise grid alignment, no overlap
