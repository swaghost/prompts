# Commercial Doodle Product Ads - Creative Advertising

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** ChatGPT with DALL-E (primary), MidJourney, other AI image generators supporting image uploads

**Technical Specifications:** 4:5 vertical format (Instagram-optimized), ultra-premium minimalist composition, hyper-realistic product photography combined with hand-drawn black-ink illustration

---

## Prompt Description

A creative advertising technique combining hyper-realistic product photography with elegant black-and-white hand-drawn doodle illustrations. The uploaded product image becomes the hero element while tiny illustrated characters physically interact with it - climbing, riding, building on, or exploring the product as if it were part of their miniature world. The doodle creates a clever visual metaphor where the product naturally functions as a parachute, vehicle, mountain, boat, playground, or other element within the illustrated story. Premium minimalist aesthetic with generous negative space, warm neutral background, and sophisticated editorial quality perfect for social media advertising campaigns.

---

## Complete Prompt

Use the uploaded product or food image as the exact hero reference.

Create an ultra-premium minimalist 4:5 advertising poster combining the real, hyper-realistic uploaded object with an elegant black-and-white hand-drawn doodle illustration.

**Creative Concept**

First analyze the uploaded object's shape, silhouette, orientation, function, texture, and distinctive features. Then invent ONE clever visual metaphor specifically inspired by it.

Imagine: "What could this object become inside a tiny illustrated world?"

It could naturally function as a parachute, vehicle, mountain, boat, building, flying machine, playground, landscape, tool, planet, bridge, etc.—but do not force these examples. Create an original idea that best fits the specific object.

**Most Important Rule**

The doodle must physically interact with the real object, not simply decorate the space around it.

Tiny hand-drawn characters may climb, ride, hang from, fly with, build on, push, pull, sail, explore, or otherwise use the object.

REAL OBJECT + DOODLE = ONE COMPLETE VISUAL IDEA.

Make the interaction clever, instantly understandable, physically believable, witty, and sophisticated.

**Product Accuracy**

Keep the uploaded object photorealistic and recognizable. Preserve its original shape, proportions, colors, materials, textures, branding, packaging, and important details.

For food, preserve authentic texture and ingredients while making it extremely fresh and appetizing.

**Visual Style**

- Hyper-realistic premium product/food photography
- Thin, refined black-ink hand-drawn illustration
- Tiny elegant illustrated characters
- Warm white / pale neutral background
- Generous negative space
- Soft premium studio lighting
- Subtle realistic shadows
- Minimal editorial composition
- One hero object
- One strong doodle concept
- Intelligent, poetic humor
- Luxury advertising campaign aesthetic

Typography is optional and extremely minimal. If no brand information is provided, do not invent a brand.

**Avoid:** random decorative doodles, childish cartoons, thick outlines, clutter, multiple concepts, excessive characters, busy backgrounds, distorted products, fake ingredients, inaccurate branding, unrealistic interactions, excessive typography, cheap poster aesthetics.

Aspect ratio: 4:5 vertical.

The final image should feel like world-class commercial photography merged seamlessly with sophisticated editorial illustration, where the uploaded object becomes an essential structural part of the doodle concept.
