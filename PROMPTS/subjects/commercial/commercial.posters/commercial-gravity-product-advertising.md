# Commercial Gravity Product Advertising - Dramatic Visual Pull

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** ChatGPT with DALL-E (primary), MidJourney, other AI image generators supporting product reference images

**Technical Specifications:** 4:5 vertical format (Instagram-optimized), premium commercial advertising aesthetic, dramatic gravitational vortex effect, photorealistic product with surreal environment

---

## Prompt Description

A dramatic product advertising technique where the hero product appears to have gravitational force, pulling product-relevant objects, particles, light, energy, and typography into curved trajectories around it. The product floats at the visual center as a powerful gravitational force, creating a realistic spatial vortex with warped perspective, radial motion trails, and bent environmental elements. The product remains perfectly sharp, undistorted, and dominant while the surrounding environment curves dramatically around it. Campaign headline uses gravity, orbit, pull, force, or attraction metaphors specifically based on the product's main benefit. Premium global advertising art direction combining surreal visual drama with sophisticated minimalism.

---

## Complete Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 **PRODUCT HAS GRAVITY advertising poster** for **[PRODUCT/BRAND]** on a clean editorial background. Place the authentic photorealistic product floating at the visual center as a powerful gravitational force, pulling product-relevant objects, particles, light, energy and typography into dramatic curved trajectories around it. Build a realistic spatial vortex with warped perspective, radial motion trails and bent environmental elements, while keeping the hero product perfectly sharp, undistorted and dominant. Create a **short, bold campaign headline specifically based on the product's main benefit, expressed through a gravity, orbit, pull, force, mass or attraction metaphor; never use a fixed generic gravity headline**. Surrounding objects must directly relate to the product's real use case and visually reinforce its benefit. Use controlled brand-color accents, cinematic commercial lighting, realistic physics, strong depth, generous negative space and premium global advertising art direction; surreal yet believable, sophisticated, minimal, high-impact, no irrelevant objects, no clutter.

## Platform-Specific Tips

**ChatGPT with DALL-E:**

- Upload clear product reference image first
- Replace [PRODUCT/BRAND] with specific product name
- Request 4:5 aspect ratio explicitly
- Specify main product benefit if needed
- Ask for variations with different object arrangements
- Can refine headline separately if needed

**MidJourney:**

- Use uploaded product as image reference
- Add `--ar 4:5` for vertical format
- Emphasize "gravitational pull" and "curved trajectories"
- May need `--style raw` for photorealistic product
- Consider `--stylize 500-700` for dramatic effect

---

## Tips for Better Results

**Product Image Quality:**

- Use high-resolution reference images
- Clear product branding visible
- Good lighting and focus
- Front or 3/4 angle works best
- Avoid extremely low-resolution sources

**Object Relevance:**

- Ensure surrounding objects relate directly to product use
- Charger = devices, cables, charging symbols
- Headphones = sound waves, music elements
- Sneakers = movement, performance indicators
- No random unrelated objects

**Product Clarity:**

- Keep hero product perfectly sharp
- Gravity distortion affects environment, not product
- Product remains recognizable and undistorted
- Branding and details preserved

**Composition Balance:**

- If too cluttered, request more negative space
- Ask to reduce surrounding elements by 30-40%
- Maintain dramatic effect without chaos
- Balance surreal drama with sophistication

**Brand Verification:**

- Manually verify logos and branding
- Check product specifications accuracy
- Verify technical claims if present
- Review typography before publishing
