# The Rise — Mint Series

**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / BLACK VOID**

**GPT Image 2** or **Seedream 5.0 Pro** (4:5 vertical)

## Concept

Explosive upward drive from deep crouch in pure black void. Male dancer exploding upward, feet still touching floor but heels lifting, body stretching vertically into mint veils that elongate and fade toward top of frame. Single long exposure with no flash. Sneakers at bottom of frame, zero blur, solid beneath dissolving body.

**Style:** Black void, continuous motion smear, seafoam mint  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 35mm lens, low looking up  
**Technique:** Single long exposure, no flash (continuous smear, no hard edges)

---

**Sneaker product reference** - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

## PROMPT

Use the attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe.

**SHOT:** Professional athletic footwear campaign photograph. A lean young male dancer explodes upward out of a deep crouch in a black void, both feet still touching the dark floor but the heels lifting, knees driving straight, arms flung overhead. Both sneakers are planted at the very bottom of the frame, razor-sharp and the most saturated objects in the image. His body above rises and disperses into the darkness.

**MOTION TECHNIQUE:** A single long exposure with no flash pinning the subject — the body renders as a smooth continuous smear, not as distinct frozen copies. His torso, arms and head stretch vertically upward into soft overlapping veils of mint fabric that elongate, thin and fade toward the top of the frame, with no hard edges anywhere above the ankles. The sneakers alone hold absolute zero blur, reading as solid and still beneath a body that is coming apart upward.

**WARDROBE:** An oversized hooded tracksuit — zip hoodie and matching loose trousers — in a soft pale mint green with a brushed cotton finish, fabric lifting with the drive. The mint must read as a pale cool seafoam green — NOT white, NOT teal, NOT lime, NOT grey. Short white athletic socks. No visible brand logos on the clothing.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** A pure black studio void with a tight pool of light on the dark floor at his feet, falling away to complete black above. A single cool white key from camera front-low picking out the shoes brightest and the rising figure progressively dimmer. The mint glows against the darkness. The shoe colour and the mint are the only saturation in the frame. No coloured gels.

**CAMERA:** Medium format digital back, 35mm equivalent lens, f/8, low angle near floor level looking up the length of the rising body. 4:5 vertical, generous empty black across the top of the frame. No wide-angle distortion, no exaggerated foreshortening — the shoes read in correct natural proportion to the body.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin where visible — visible pores, fine natural texture, a sheen of exertion sweat on the face and neck, natural skin tone variation, visible tendon and vein detail on the raised hands. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no distinct strobe-frozen duplicate figures, no multi-pop ghost copies, no hard-edged repeats, no chrome, no steel, no reflective floor, no mirrored reflection, no coloured gels, no teal, no cyan, no red, no white background, no warped or extra fingers or toes, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment, no motion blur on the sneakers.
