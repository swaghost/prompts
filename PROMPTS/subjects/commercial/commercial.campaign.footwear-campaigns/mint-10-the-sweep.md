**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / BLACK VOID**

## Concept

Wide deep lunge in pure black void. Male dancer in low lunge, front knee bent with shoe planted flat and sharp in foreground, rear leg extended long behind with shoe also sharp on floor. Arms sweeping horizontally across body. Single long exposure with no flash - arms and torso drag wide horizontal mint veils, no hard edges. Both sneakers zero blur, only fixed points.

**Style:** Black void, continuous motion smear, seafoam mint  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 50mm lens, knee height  
**Technique:** Single long exposure, no flash (continuous smear, no hard edges)

**Sneaker product reference** - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

## PROMPT

Use the attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe.

**SHOT:** Professional athletic footwear campaign photograph. A lean young male dancer in a wide deep lunge in a black void, front knee bent low with that sneaker planted flat and razor-sharp in the foreground, rear leg extended long behind him with its shoe also sharp on the dark floor. Both arms sweep horizontally across his body in one direction, head following the movement. The two planted sneakers are the most saturated and defined objects in the frame.

**MOTION TECHNIQUE:** A single long exposure with no flash pinning the subject — the body renders as a smooth continuous smear, not as distinct frozen copies. His arms and torso drag a wide horizontal band of soft mint veils across the frame, layered and semi-transparent, thinning at the outer edge, with no hard edges anywhere above the ankles. Both sneakers hold absolute zero blur, the only fixed points in a horizontally flowing image.

**WARDROBE:** An oversized hooded tracksuit — zip hoodie and matching loose trousers — in a soft pale mint green with a brushed cotton finish. The mint must read as a pale cool seafoam green — NOT white, NOT teal, NOT lime, NOT grey. Short white athletic socks. No visible brand logos on the clothing.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** A pure black studio void with a low wide pool of light across the dark floor beneath the lunge. A single cool white key from camera front raking low across both shoes, heavy falloff into complete black above and behind. The mint glows against the darkness. The shoe colour and the mint are the only saturation in the frame. No coloured gels.

**CAMERA:** Medium format digital back, 50mm equivalent lens, f/8, low angle at knee height, the lunge running across the full width of the frame. 4:5 vertical. No wide-angle distortion, no exaggerated foreshortening — the shoes read in correct natural proportion to the body.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin where visible — visible pores, fine natural texture, a sheen of exertion sweat on the face, neck and forearms, natural muscle definition through the extended rear leg, visible tendon and vein detail on the hands. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no distinct strobe-frozen duplicate figures, no multi-pop ghost copies, no hard-edged repeats, no chrome, no steel, no reflective floor, no mirrored reflection, no coloured gels, no teal, no cyan, no red, no white background, no warped or extra fingers or toes, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment, no motion blur on the sneakers.
