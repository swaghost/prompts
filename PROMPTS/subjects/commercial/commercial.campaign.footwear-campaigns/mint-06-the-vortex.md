# The Vortex — Mint Series

**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / BLACK VOID**

---

## Platform

**GPT Image 2** or **Seedream 5.0 Pro** (4:5 vertical)

---

## Concept

Spinning figure in pure black void. Male dancer torqued hard around vertical axis, body dissolving into continuous mint-colored veils above the ankles. Single long exposure with no flash - smooth continuous smear, NOT distinct frozen copies. Sneakers anchored to floor, zero blur.

**Style:** Black void, continuous motion smear, seafoam mint  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 50mm lens, eye level  
**Technique:** Single long exposure, no flash (continuous smear, no hard edges)

---

## Reference Requirements

**ONE REFERENCE REQUIRED:**

**Sneaker product reference** - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

## PROMPT

Use the attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe.

**SHOT:** Professional athletic footwear campaign photograph. A lean young male dancer spins hard on one heel in a black void, body torqued around its own axis, one arm whipping outward and the head turning against the direction of the movement. Both sneakers are planted flat on the dark floor and are the sharpest, most saturated objects in the frame. Everything above the ankles is caught mid-rotation.

**MOTION TECHNIQUE:** A single long exposure with no flash pinning the subject — the body renders as a smooth continuous smear, not as distinct frozen copies. His torso, arms and head dissolve into soft overlapping veils of fabric that wrap around the axis of the spin, semi-transparent and layered, with no hard edges anywhere above the ankles. The sneakers alone hold absolute zero blur and complete edge definition, anchoring the whole dissolving figure to the floor.

**WARDROBE:** An oversized hooded tracksuit — zip hoodie and matching loose trousers — in a soft pale mint green with a brushed cotton finish. The mint must read as a pale cool seafoam green — NOT white, NOT teal, NOT lime, NOT grey. Short white athletic socks. No visible brand logos on the clothing.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** A pure black studio void — no walls, no horizon, only a faint pool of light on the dark floor beneath his feet. A single cool white key light from camera front picking out the mint tracksuit and the shoes, with heavy falloff into complete black at the frame edges. The mint glows against the darkness. The shoe colour and the mint are the only saturation in the frame. No coloured gels.

**CAMERA:** Medium format digital back, 50mm equivalent lens, f/8, straight-on eye-level angle, full body in frame with headroom above and the floor visible below. 4:5 vertical. No wide-angle distortion, no exaggerated foreshortening — the shoes read in correct natural proportion to the body.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin where visible — visible pores, fine natural texture, a sheen of exertion sweat on the face and neck, natural skin tone variation, visible tendon and vein detail on the hands. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no distinct strobe-frozen duplicate figures, no multi-pop ghost copies, no hard-edged repeats, no chrome, no steel, no reflective floor, no mirrored reflection, no coloured gels, no teal, no cyan, no red, no white background, no warped or extra fingers, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment, no motion blur on the sneakers.
