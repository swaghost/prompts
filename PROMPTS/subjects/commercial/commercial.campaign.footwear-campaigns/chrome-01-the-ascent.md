# The Ascent — Chrome Series

**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / STEEL STUDIO**

---

## Platform

**GPT Image 2** or **Seedream 5.0 Pro** (4:5 vertical)

---

## Concept

Multi-stage jump progression with rear-curtain sync strobe. Three distinct frozen ghost copies climbing the frame - crouched at bottom, mid-drive in middle, airborne at top. Brushed steel backdrop with mirror floor. Female athlete in slate grey. Sneaker holds zero blur at all stages.

**Style:** Chrome studio, mirror reflections, ghost-trail structure  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 35mm lens, knee height  
**Technique:** Rear-curtain sync or multi-pop strobe (distinct frozen copies)

---

## Reference Requirements

**TWO REFERENCES REQUIRED:**

1. **FIRST attachment:** Pose reference image showing the exact composition - same vertical stack of three jump stages, same crouched bottom figure, same mid-drive middle figure, same tucked airborne top figure, same transparency falloff, same framing
2. **SECOND attachment:** Sneaker product reference - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

## PROMPT

Use the FIRST attached image as an exact reference for the pose, body position, limb placement, ghost-trail structure, composition and camera angle. Reproduce that composition exactly — the same vertical stack of three jump stages climbing the frame, the same crouched bottom figure, the same mid-drive middle figure, the same tucked airborne top figure, the same transparency falloff between stages, the same framing and crop. Do not invent a new pose.

Use the SECOND attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe. Every duplicate of the shoe across all three stages must match this reference exactly.

**CHANGE ONLY THE FOLLOWING:**

**MODEL:** A lean athletic woman in her late twenties, dark hair pulled back tight, replacing the male dancer in the pose reference. Same body positions, same stages, same energy.

**WARDROBE:** A fitted long-sleeve athletic top and high-waisted full-length leggings in a cool slate grey with a fine seamless knit texture, replacing the navy workwear. The slate must read as a cool mid-grey — NOT navy, NOT charcoal black, NOT brown, NOT silver. Short white athletic socks. No visible brand logos.

**SETTING:** A brushed stainless steel backdrop with fine horizontal grain replacing the black void, and a polished mirror-finish steel floor beneath. The planted sneakers on the bottom crouched figure cast clean inverted reflections in the metal directly below them.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** Cool neutral daylight-balanced key from camera front-left, strongest on the bottom stage and falling away up the stack, with broad soft highlights streaking across the brushed metal and a hard specular kick along the floor. The palette is entirely neutral — steel greys, cool whites, and the colour of the shoe. No coloured gels, no teal, no cyan.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin with visible pores, fine natural texture, faint peach fuzz on the forearms and shins, a sheen of exertion sweat on the collarbone, temples and calves, natural muscle definition through the loaded legs, visible tendon and vein detail on the hands. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no black void background, no navy clothing, no male model, no coloured gel lighting, no teal, no cyan, no red, no warm tones, no distorted or mismatched reflection, no mismatched shoes between stages, no warped or extra fingers or toes, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment or crew reflections in the metal, no motion blur on the bottom figure's sneakers.
