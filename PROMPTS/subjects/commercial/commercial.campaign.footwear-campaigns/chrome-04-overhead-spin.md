## Concept

True overhead top-down view. Figure lying on back with one leg raised showing outsole to camera, radial sweep of ghosted legs spinning around the body. Mirror-finish steel floor. Female athlete in slate grey. Raised sneaker outsole holds zero blur.

**Style:** Chrome studio, overhead perspective, radial spin  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 50mm lens, top-down  
**Technique:** Rear-curtain sync or multi-pop strobe (distinct frozen copies)

## PROMPT

Use the FIRST attached image as an exact reference for the pose, body position, limb placement, ghost-trail structure, composition and camera angle. Reproduce that composition exactly — the same true top-down overhead view, the same figure lying on their back with arms thrown wide, the same raised leg with the outsole facing the lens, the same radial sweep of ghosted legs around the body, the same circular pool of light and the same crop. Do not invent a new pose.

Use the SECOND attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread pattern, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe. Every ghosted duplicate of the shoe in the radial sweep must match this reference exactly.

**CHANGE ONLY THE FOLLOWING:**

**MODEL:** A lean athletic woman in her late twenties, dark hair pulled back tight, replacing the male dancer in the pose reference. Same position on the floor, same limb placement, same upward gaze.

**WARDROBE:** A fitted long-sleeve athletic top and high-waisted full-length leggings in a cool slate grey with a fine seamless knit texture, replacing the navy workwear. The slate must read as a cool mid-grey — NOT navy, NOT charcoal black, NOT brown, NOT silver. Tall white ribbed athletic socks. No visible brand logos.

**SETTING:** A polished mirror-finish steel floor replacing the dark studio floor, filling the entire frame, with faint reflections of the sweeping limbs catching in the metal.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** A single soft elliptical pool of cool neutral light centred on her body, falling off toward darker brushed steel at the frame edges. The palette is entirely neutral — steel greys, cool whites, and the colour of the shoe. No coloured gels, no teal, no cyan.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin with visible pores, fine natural texture, faint peach fuzz on the forearms and shins, a sheen of exertion sweat across the forehead, neck and forearms, natural tendon and vein detail on the outstretched hands, subtle natural skin tone variation. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no black void background, no navy clothing, no male model, no coloured gel lighting, no teal, no cyan, no red, no warm tones, no distorted or mismatched reflection, no mismatched shoes between duplicates, no flattened or smeared tread on the sharp shoes, no warped or extra fingers or toes, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment or crew reflections in the metal, no motion blur on the sharp sneakers.
