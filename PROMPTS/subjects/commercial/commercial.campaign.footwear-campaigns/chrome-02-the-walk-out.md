# The Walk-out — Chrome Series

**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / STEEL STUDIO**

---

## Platform

**GPT Image 2** or **Seedream 5.0 Pro** (4:5 vertical)

---

## Concept

Mid-stride walk directly toward camera with receding ghost figures. Three stages - sharp leading figure walking forward with two semi-transparent ghosts trailing behind. Brushed steel backdrop with mirror floor. Female athlete in slate grey. Leading sneaker holds zero blur.

**Style:** Chrome studio, mirror reflections, walk progression  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 85mm lens, chest height  
**Technique:** Rear-curtain sync or multi-pop strobe (distinct frozen copies)

---

## Reference Requirements

**TWO REFERENCES REQUIRED:**

1. **FIRST attachment:** Pose reference image - same mid-stride walk straight toward camera, same leading foot placement, same two receding ghost figures behind, same low chest-height framing and crop
2. **SECOND attachment:** Sneaker product reference - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

---

## PROMPT

Use the FIRST attached image as an exact reference for the pose, body position, limb placement, ghost-trail structure, composition and camera angle. Reproduce that composition exactly — the same mid-stride walk straight toward camera, the same leading foot placement, the same two receding ghost figures behind, the same low chest-height framing and crop. Do not invent a new pose.

Use the SECOND attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe. Every duplicate of the shoe in the receding ghosts must match this reference exactly.

**CHANGE ONLY THE FOLLOWING:**

**MODEL:** A lean athletic woman in her late twenties, dark hair pulled back tight, replacing the male dancer in the pose reference. Same stride, same posture, same eye contact down the lens.

**WARDROBE:** A fitted long-sleeve athletic top and high-waisted full-length leggings in a cool slate grey with a fine seamless knit texture, replacing the navy workwear. The slate must read as a cool mid-grey — NOT navy, NOT charcoal black, NOT brown, NOT silver. Short white athletic socks. No visible brand logos.

**SETTING:** A brushed stainless steel backdrop with fine horizontal grain replacing the black void, and a polished mirror-finish steel floor beneath. The leading sneaker casts a clean inverted reflection in the metal directly below it, and the receding ghost figures are faintly mirrored too.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** Cool neutral daylight-balanced key from camera front, clean and even on her face and the shoes, with the receding ghosts falling into softer steel-grey shadow behind. The palette is entirely neutral — steel greys, cool whites, and the colour of the shoe. No coloured gels, no teal, no cyan.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin with visible pores, fine natural texture, faint peach fuzz on the forearms, a faint residual sheen of sweat on the forehead and neck, natural skin tone variation across the face, visible tendon and vein detail on the hands. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no black void background, no navy clothing, no male model, no coloured gel lighting, no teal, no cyan, no red, no warm tones, no distorted or mismatched reflection, no mismatched left and right shoes, no warped or extra fingers, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment or crew reflections in the metal, no motion blur on the sneakers.
