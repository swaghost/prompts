# The Kick — Chrome Series

**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / STEEL STUDIO**

---

## Platform

**GPT Image 2** or **Seedream 5.0 Pro** (4:5 vertical)

---

## Concept

High kick with sweeping curved light trail. One leg extended fully up and out, torso twisted, sweeping arc of pale silver-white light behind the kicking leg. Brushed steel backdrop with mirror floor. Female athlete in slate grey. Kicking sneaker holds zero blur.

**Style:** Chrome studio, dynamic kick, light trail arc  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 50mm lens, slightly low  
**Technique:** Rear-curtain sync or multi-pop strobe (distinct frozen copies)

---

## Reference Requirements

**TWO REFERENCES REQUIRED:**

1. **FIRST attachment:** Pose reference image - same high kick with one leg extended fully up and out to camera-left, same torso twist, same counterbalancing arm across chest, same sweeping curved light trail arcing behind kicking leg, same framing and crop
2. **SECOND attachment:** Sneaker product reference - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

---

## PROMPT

Use the FIRST attached image as an exact reference for the pose, body position, limb placement, light-trail structure, composition and camera angle. Reproduce that composition exactly — the same high kick with one leg extended fully up and out to camera-left, the same torso twist, the same counterbalancing arm across the chest, the same sweeping curved light trail arcing behind the kicking leg, the same framing and crop. Do not invent a new pose.

Use the SECOND attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe.

**CHANGE ONLY THE FOLLOWING:**

**MODEL:** A lean athletic woman in her late twenties, dark hair pulled back tight, replacing the male dancer in the pose reference. Same kick, same twist, same intensity in the face.

**WARDROBE:** A fitted long-sleeve athletic top and high-waisted full-length leggings in a cool slate grey with a fine seamless knit texture, fabric flaring with the kick, replacing the navy workwear. The slate must read as a cool mid-grey — NOT navy, NOT charcoal black, NOT brown, NOT silver. Tall white ribbed athletic socks. No visible brand logos.

**SETTING:** A brushed stainless steel backdrop with fine horizontal grain replacing the black void, and a polished mirror-finish steel floor beneath. The planted supporting foot casts a faint inverted reflection in the metal.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** Cool neutral daylight-balanced key from camera front-right hitting the extended shoe and her face. The sweeping arc behind the kicking leg glows pale silver-white against the steel rather than teal. The palette is entirely neutral — steel greys, cool whites, and the colour of the shoe. No coloured gels, no teal, no cyan.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin with visible pores, fine natural texture, faint peach fuzz on the forearms, a visible sheen of exertion sweat on the forehead, temples and neck, natural muscle definition through the extended leg, visible tendon and vein detail in the hands. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no black void background, no navy clothing, no male model, no coloured gel lighting, no teal, no cyan, no red, no warm tones, no distorted or mismatched reflection, no warped or extra fingers or toes, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment or crew reflections in the metal, no motion blur on the kicking sneaker.
