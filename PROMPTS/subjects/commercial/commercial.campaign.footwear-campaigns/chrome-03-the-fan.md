# The Fan — Chrome Series

**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / STEEL STUDIO**

---

## Platform

**GPT Image 2** or **Seedream 5.0 Pro** (4:5 vertical)

---

## Concept

Symmetrical fan of ghosted limbs radiating from central standing figure. Sharp central figure with ghost arms and legs fanning to both sides, shoes visible at outer points. Brushed steel backdrop with mirror floor. Female athlete in slate grey. Central sneakers hold zero blur.

**Style:** Chrome studio, radial symmetry, ghost fan structure  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 65mm lens, eye level  
**Technique:** Rear-curtain sync or multi-pop strobe (distinct frozen copies)

---

## Reference Requirements

**TWO REFERENCES REQUIRED:**

1. **FIRST attachment:** Pose reference image - same sharp central standing figure, same symmetrical fan of ghosted arms and legs radiating to both sides, same number and placement of ghost limbs, same shoes at outer points, same straight-on framing
2. **SECOND attachment:** Sneaker product reference - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

## PROMPT

Use the FIRST attached image as an exact reference for the pose, body position, limb placement, ghost-trail structure, composition and camera angle. Reproduce that composition exactly — the same sharp central standing figure, the same symmetrical fan of ghosted arms and legs radiating to both sides, the same number and placement of ghost limbs, the same shoes at the outer points, the same straight-on framing and crop. Do not invent a new pose.

Use the SECOND attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe. Every ghosted duplicate of the shoe in the fan must match this reference exactly.

**CHANGE ONLY THE FOLLOWING:**

**MODEL:** A lean athletic woman in her late twenties, dark hair pulled back tight, replacing the male dancer in the pose reference. Same stance, same symmetry, same stillness at the centre.

**WARDROBE:** A fitted long-sleeve athletic top and high-waisted full-length leggings in a cool slate grey with a fine seamless knit texture, replacing the navy workwear. The slate must read as a cool mid-grey — NOT navy, NOT charcoal black, NOT brown, NOT silver. Tall white ribbed athletic socks. No visible brand logos.

**SETTING:** A brushed stainless steel backdrop with fine horizontal grain replacing the black void, and a polished mirror-finish steel floor beneath. The two planted central sneakers cast clean inverted reflections in the metal directly below them.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** Cool neutral daylight-balanced key from camera front picking out her face and the sharp central shoes, with pale steel-toned edge light defining each ghosted limb against the brushed metal. The palette is entirely neutral — steel greys, cool whites, and the colour of the shoe. No coloured gels, no teal, no cyan.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin with visible pores, fine natural texture, faint peach fuzz on the forearms, a faint sheen of sweat across the forehead and neck, natural tendon and vein detail across the hands and forearms, subtle natural skin tone variation. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no black void background, no navy clothing, no male model, no coloured gel lighting, no teal, no cyan, no red, no warm tones, no distorted or mismatched reflection, no mismatched shoes between duplicates, no asymmetry in the fan, no warped or extra fingers, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment or crew reflections in the metal, no motion blur on the central figure's shoes.
