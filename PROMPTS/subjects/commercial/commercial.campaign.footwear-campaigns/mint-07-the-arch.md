# The Arch — Mint Series

**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / BLACK VOID**

---

## Platform

**GPT Image 2** or **Seedream 5.0 Pro** (4:5 vertical)

---

## Concept

Backward arch in pure black void. Male dancer arched deeply backward, spine curved, head thrown back out of light. Single long exposure with no flash - chest, arms and head cascade into soft mint veils, no hard edges. Feet planted wide, both sneakers zero blur taking full weight.

**Style:** Black void, continuous motion smear, seafoam mint  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 50mm lens, hip height looking up  
**Technique:** Single long exposure, no flash (continuous smear, no hard edges)

---

## Reference Requirements

**ONE REFERENCE REQUIRED:**

**Sneaker product reference** - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

## PROMPT

Use the attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe.

**SHOT:** Professional athletic footwear campaign photograph. A lean young male dancer arched backward in a black void, spine curved deeply, head thrown back and out of the light, both arms sweeping down and behind him. His feet are planted wide and flat on the dark floor, both sneakers razor-sharp and the most saturated objects in the frame, taking the entire weight of the collapsing pose above them.

**MOTION TECHNIQUE:** A single long exposure with no flash pinning the subject — the body renders as a smooth continuous smear, not as distinct frozen copies. His chest, arms and thrown-back head cascade into long soft veils of mint fabric that fall away behind him and thin into the black, with no hard edges anywhere above the ankles. The sneakers alone hold absolute zero blur and complete edge definition.

**WARDROBE:** An oversized hooded tracksuit — zip hoodie and matching loose trousers — in a soft pale mint green with a brushed cotton finish. The mint must read as a pale cool seafoam green — NOT white, NOT teal, NOT lime, NOT grey. Short white athletic socks. No visible brand logos on the clothing.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** A pure black studio void with a faint pool of light on the dark floor beneath his feet. A single cool white key from camera front-left raking down the arched body, heavy falloff into complete black. The mint glows against the darkness. The shoe colour and the mint are the only saturation in the frame. No coloured gels.

**CAMERA:** Medium format digital back, 50mm equivalent lens, f/8, low angle at hip height looking slightly up at the arch. 4:5 vertical, full body in frame. No wide-angle distortion, no exaggerated foreshortening — the shoes read in correct natural proportion to the body.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin where visible — visible pores, fine natural texture, a sheen of exertion sweat on the throat and forearms, natural skin tone variation, pronounced tendon and vein detail on the sweeping hands. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no distinct strobe-frozen duplicate figures, no multi-pop ghost copies, no hard-edged repeats, no chrome, no steel, no reflective floor, no mirrored reflection, no coloured gels, no teal, no cyan, no red, no white background, no warped or extra fingers, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment, no motion blur on the sneakers.
