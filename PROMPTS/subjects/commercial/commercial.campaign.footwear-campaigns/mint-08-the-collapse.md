**LONG-EXPOSURE FOOTWEAR CAMPAIGN / BLACK VOID**

**GPT Image 2** or **Seedream 5.0 Pro** (4:5 vertical)

## Concept

Floor slide in pure black void. Male dancer dropping into slide, one hip low and skidding, leading leg thrown forward toward camera with outsole tread visible. Single long exposure with no flash - torso and fabric stretch into horizontal veils. Leading sneaker razor-sharp, largest object in frame.

**Style:** Black void, continuous motion smear, seafoam mint  
**Format:** 4:5 vertical, 50mm lens, floor level  
**Technique:** Single long exposure, no flash (continuous smear, no hard edges)

---

## Reference Requirements

**ONE REFERENCE REQUIRED:**

**Sneaker product reference** - identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread pattern, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish

## PROMPT

Use the attached image as an exact product reference for the sneaker. The shoe must be reproduced with total fidelity — identical silhouette, upper paneling, midsole profile, outsole tread pattern, lacing structure, colorway, logo placement, stitching and material finish. Do not redesign, restyle or recolour the shoe.

**SHOT:** Professional athletic footwear campaign photograph. A lean young male dancer dropping into a floor slide in a black void, one hip low and skidding, one hand braced on the dark ground behind him, the leading leg thrown forward toward the camera so that sneaker sits closest to the lens with part of the outsole tread visible, razor-sharp. That shoe is the largest and most defined object in the frame. His upper body trails behind the movement.

**MOTION TECHNIQUE:** A single long exposure with no flash pinning the subject — the body renders as a smooth continuous smear, not as distinct frozen copies. His torso, trailing arm and the fabric of the tracksuit stretch backward into long horizontal veils that thin and fade into the black behind him, with no hard edges anywhere above the ankles. The leading sneaker holds absolute zero blur, every lug and groove of the tread crisply defined.

**WARDROBE:** An oversized hooded tracksuit — zip hoodie and matching loose trousers — in a soft pale mint green with a brushed cotton finish, fabric dragging along the slide. The mint must read as a pale cool seafoam green — NOT white, NOT teal, NOT lime, NOT grey. Short white athletic socks. No visible brand logos on the clothing.

**LIGHTING & COLOR:** A pure black studio void with a soft pool of light on the dark floor along the line of the slide. A single cool white key from camera front-left raking across the leading shoe, heavy falloff into complete black. The mint glows against the darkness. The shoe colour and the mint are the only saturation in the frame. No coloured gels.

**CAMERA:** Medium format digital back, 50mm equivalent lens, f/8, low angle close to floor level and level with the leading foot. 4:5 vertical. No wide-angle distortion, no exaggerated foreshortening — the shoe reads in correct natural proportion to the body.

**SKIN REALISM:** Photographic skin where visible — visible pores, fine natural texture, a sheen of exertion sweat on the forehead and forearm, natural skin tone variation, pronounced tendon and vein detail across the braced hand and wrist. Absolutely no plastic smoothing, no airbrushing, no waxy or CGI-rendered skin, no beauty-filter softening.

**NEGATIVE:** no distinct strobe-frozen duplicate figures, no multi-pop ghost copies, no hard-edged repeats, no chrome, no steel, no reflective floor, no mirrored reflection, no coloured gels, no teal, no cyan, no red, no white background, no flattened or smeared tread pattern, no warped or extra fingers or toes, no distorted shoe geometry, no melted outsole, no text, no watermarks, no brand logos other than those on the reference shoe, no visible studio equipment, no motion blur on the sneaker.
