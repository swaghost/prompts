## Tips for Custom Logos

1. **Describe the logo clearly** in both prompts (colors, shapes, details)
2. **Specify fabric type and color** (cotton, jersey, canvas, etc.)
3. **Mention completion percentage** in first frame (usually 30-40%)
4. **Keep everything identical** between frames except embroidery completion
5. **Use macro photography terms** (shallow depth of field, thread texture, fabric weave)
6. **Emphasize realism** (photorealistic, ultra realistic, industrial quality)
7. **Describe thread colors** precisely
8. **Include lighting details** (warm studio lighting, cinematic lighting)

---

## Common Issues to Avoid

❌ **Logo deformation or morphing**  
❌ **Duplicated stitches or extra threads**  
❌ **Flickering or unstable footage**  
❌ **Camera movement or angle changes**  
❌ **Distorted fabric texture**  
❌ **Abrupt motion or unnatural speed**  
❌ **Different lighting between frames**  
❌ **Inconsistent fabric color or texture**
