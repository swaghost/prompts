# Lacoste Sneaker - Eight-Part Commercial Campaign

## Campaign Overview

An eight-part commercial campaign built around the exact Lacoste chunky sneaker from the provided reference images. The visual system combines black, charcoal grey, burnt orange, and white with luxury sportswear art direction across brand identity, product, advertising, lifestyle, digital commerce, packaging, and model-campaign touchpoints.

## Campaign Product Lock

Use the exact sneaker from the provided reference images without changing its shape, colors, materials, sole design, branding, proportions, or detailing. Preserve the black upper, burnt orange accents, grey overlays, oversized white midsole, orange outsole, mesh and synthetic materials, and Lacoste crocodile branding across every asset. Maintain perfect product consistency across all designs.

## Shared Campaign Direction

- **Product:** Lacoste chunky sneaker from reference images
- **Category:** Premium sportswear / footwear
- **Primary palette:** Black, charcoal grey, burnt orange, white
- **Aesthetic:** Luxury sportswear, editorial fashion, premium ecommerce
- **Recommended quality:** 4K to 8K, photorealistic product and material detail
- **Visual references:** Lacoste, Nike, Adidas Originals, Puma, Apple-style product presentation

## Production Order

1. Establish the identity board and campaign palette.
2. Generate the signature product showcase to lock the hero product.
3. Build the advertisement poster and campaign message.
4. Generate the handheld and model lifestyle assets.
5. Translate the product system into mobile UI and website layouts.
6. Finish with packaging and retail touchpoints.

## Consistency Checklist

- [ ] Use the same reference sneaker in all eight prompts.
- [ ] Preserve black upper, grey overlays, burnt orange accents, white oversized midsole, and orange outsole.
- [ ] Keep the Lacoste crocodile branding consistent in placement, shape, and color.
- [ ] Keep typography and accent colors within the shared campaign system.
- [ ] Keep the sneaker clearly identifiable in product, interface, packaging, and model scenes.
- [ ] Review hands, product geometry, logos, sole proportions, and material transitions before publishing.
- [ ] Replace any generated placeholder copy or UI data with approved campaign content.
