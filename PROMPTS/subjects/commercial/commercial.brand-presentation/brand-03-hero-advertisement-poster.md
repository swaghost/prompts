# Brand Presentation 03: Hero Advertisement Poster

## Overview

**Purpose**: Bold advertisement poster with dynamic motion and inspiring messaging  
**Platform**: Any AI image generator  
**Style**: High-fashion commercial campaign, cinematic, magazine cover quality

Create powerful advertisement poster with floating product, dynamic effects, and brand messaging.

---

## When to Use

- ✓ Print advertisements (magazines, billboards)
- ✓ Social media campaign posts
- ✓ In-store display posters
- ✓ Product launch announcements
- ✓ Brand awareness campaigns
- ✓ Digital advertising banners

---

## Complete Prompt

```
Design a bold hero advertisement poster for the exact [PRODUCT NAME] shown in
the reference images. The [product] should appear floating in the center with
dynamic lighting, motion streaks, [color] energy effects, and [color] geometric shapes.
Use a luxury [product category] campaign aesthetic with a premium white background
fading into [dark color]. Include large modern typography reading "[PRIMARY HEADLINE]"
with smaller copy "[SECONDARY TAGLINE]." Leave space for the [BRAND] logo at the top.
High-fashion commercial campaign inspired by [COMPARABLE BRANDS]. Ultra-realistic,
cinematic lighting, magazine cover quality, 8K.
```

### Example (Lacoste Sneaker):

```
Design a bold hero advertisement poster for the exact Lacoste sneaker shown in
the reference images. The sneaker should appear floating in the center with
dynamic lighting, motion streaks, orange energy effects, and black geometric shapes.
Use a luxury sportswear campaign aesthetic with a premium white background
fading into dark grey. Include large modern typography reading "MOVE WITHOUT LIMITS"
with smaller copy "Engineered for Everyday Motion." Leave space for the Lacoste
logo at the top. High-fashion commercial campaign inspired by Nike, Puma, and
Lacoste. Ultra-realistic, cinematic lighting, magazine cover quality, 8K.
```

## Key Elements Breakdown

### Product Presentation

- **Position**: Floating in center (hero placement)
- **Effects**: Motion streaks, energy effects
- **Lighting**: Dynamic cinematic lighting
- **Graphics**: Geometric shapes supporting product

### Background Design

- **Top**: Premium white (clean space for logo)
- **Bottom**: Fades into dark color (drama and contrast)
- **Gradient**: Smooth professional transition
- **Effect**: High-fashion campaign aesthetic

### Typography & Messaging

- **Primary Headline**: Large, bold, modern font (2-4 words)
- **Secondary Tagline**: Smaller supporting copy (benefit-focused)
- **Logo Space**: Reserved at top for brand identity
- **Hierarchy**: Clear visual priority

### Visual Style

- **Aesthetic**: High-fashion commercial
- **Quality**: Magazine cover, ultra-realistic
- **Lighting**: Cinematic, dramatic
- **Resolution**: 8K for print quality

## Customization Guide

### For Your Product

Replace these elements:

- `[PRODUCT NAME]`: Specific product
- `[product]`: Product type
- `[color]`: Primary accent colors for effects
- `[product category]`: Product category/industry
- `[dark color]`: Bottom fade color (grey, black, navy)
- `[BRAND]`: Your brand name
- `[PRIMARY HEADLINE]`: 2-4 word inspiring headline
- `[SECONDARY TAGLINE]`: Benefit-focused supporting line
- `[COMPARABLE BRANDS]`: Similar tier brand references

## Pro Tips

1. **Floating Product**: Creates premium suspended magic
2. **Motion Streaks**: Shows product in action, adds energy
3. **Energy Effects**: Use product accent colors for cohesion
4. **Geometric Shapes**: Modern, dynamic visual support
5. **White-to-Dark Fade**: Classic premium campaign background
6. **Bold Typography**: 2-4 words maximum for headline impact
7. **Logo Space**: Always reserve clean area for branding
8. **Comparable Brands**: Reference aspirational competitors

## Troubleshooting

### Typography Overwhelming Product

**Problem**: Headline too large or competing with product  
**Solution**:

- Specify "product remains clear focal point"
- Add "balanced composition with typography supporting product"
- Reduce headline to 2-3 words maximum

### Background Too Busy

**Problem**: Effects and shapes distracting  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize "premium white background fading smoothly"
- Add "minimal" before geometric shapes
- Reduce to "subtle motion effects"

### Product Not Central Focus

**Problem**: Effects or typography dominating  
**Solution**:

- Add "product floating prominently in center as main focus"
- Specify "all elements supporting and directing eye to product"

### Wrong Campaign Aesthetic

**Problem**: Doesn't look high-fashion or premium  
**Solution**:

- Adjust comparable brand references to match desired tier
- Add "haute couture," "luxury campaign," "editorial quality"
- Specify "magazine cover quality" and "ultra-realistic"
