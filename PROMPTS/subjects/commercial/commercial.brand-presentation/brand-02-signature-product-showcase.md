## Troubleshooting

### Pedestal Not Floating

**Problem**: Product sitting on surface, not floating  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize "floating circular pedestal" and "suspended above ground"
- Add "levitating" or "hovering" to description
- Specify "shadow beneath showing height"

### Background Too Busy

**Problem**: Gradient or graphics overwhelming product  
**Solution**:

- Add "subtle" and "minimal" before gradient/graphics
- Specify "product is clear focal point"
- Reduce to "soft gradient" or "minimal geometric accents"

### Lighting Too Flat

**Problem**: Not dramatic enough lighting  
**Solution**:

- Specify "dramatic studio lighting with multiple light sources"
- Add "rim lighting," "key light," "fill light" specifically
- Mention "commercial photography lighting setup"

### Product Details Lost

**Problem**: Material details not visible  
**Solution**:

- List specific materials and features to highlight
- Add "macro detail," "texture clarity," "material definition"
- Specify "sharp focus on all product elements"
