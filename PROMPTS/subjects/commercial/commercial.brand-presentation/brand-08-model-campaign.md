# Brand Presentation 08: Model Campaign

## Overview

**Purpose**: Premium fashion campaign featuring model wearing/using product  
**Platform**: Any AI image generator  
**Style**: Editorial fashion photography, luxury campaign, Vogue/GQ quality

Create high-end lifestyle fashion campaign with model showcasing product in aspirational environment.

---

## When to Use

- ✓ Fashion advertising campaigns
- ✓ Lifestyle brand storytelling
- ✓ Social media hero content
- ✓ Magazine advertisements
- ✓ Billboard campaigns
- ✓ Aspirational brand positioning

---

## Complete Prompt

```
Create a premium fashion campaign featuring a stylish [DEMOGRAPHIC] model [wearing/using]
the exact [PRODUCT NAME] from the reference images. The model wears modern [clothing style]
with subtle [accent color] accents that complement the [product]. Pose
confidently while [action/movement] in a contemporary [environment type] with luxury
[setting details]. Emphasize the [product] through dynamic angles and natural movement.
Editorial fashion photography, luxury [product category] campaign, cinematic lighting, shallow
depth of field, realistic textures, Vogue and GQ style, high-end commercial
advertisement, ultra-realistic, 8K.
```

### Example (Lacoste Sneaker):

```
Create a premium fashion campaign featuring a stylish Asian male model wearing
the exact Lacoste sneaker from the reference images. The model wears modern black
streetwear with subtle burnt orange accents that complement the shoe. Pose
confidently while walking in a contemporary urban environment with luxury
architecture. Emphasize the sneakers through dynamic angles and natural movement.
Editorial fashion photography, luxury sportswear campaign, cinematic lighting, shallow
depth of field, realistic textures, Vogue and GQ style, high-end commercial
advertisement, ultra-realistic, 8K.
```

## Key Elements Breakdown

### Model Specifications

- **Demographic**: Specific ethnicity, age range, gender
- **Style**: Fashion-forward, aspirational, on-brand
- **Expression**: Confident, natural, authentic
- **Pose**: Dynamic, natural movement (not static)
- **Action**: Walking, jumping, sitting, standing, etc.

### Wardrobe Coordination

- **Style**: Matches brand aesthetic (streetwear, luxury, athletic)
- **Colors**: Complements product colors
- **Fit**: Modern, well-tailored, fashion-forward
- **Accents**: Subtle product color accents in outfit
- **Focus**: Product remains hero despite full outfit

### Environment Setting

- **Type**: Urban, natural, studio, architectural
- **Quality**: Luxury, contemporary, aspirational
- **Details**: Specific setting elements (architecture, nature, etc.)
- **Lighting**: Natural or cinematic artificial
- **Mood**: Matches brand positioning

### Photography Style

- **Quality**: Editorial fashion photography
- **Lighting**: Cinematic, dramatic yet natural
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow (subject sharp, background soft)
- **Textures**: Realistic fabric, skin, materials
- **Standard**: Vogue, GQ, high-end magazine quality

### Product Emphasis

- **Visibility**: Product clearly visible and prominent
- **Angles**: Dynamic angles showcasing product
- **Movement**: Natural movement involving product
- **Context**: Product integrated naturally in lifestyle

## Pro Tips

1. **Dynamic Pose**: Walking or movement beats static standing
2. **Product Emphasis**: Angles and lighting highlight product
3. **Wardrobe Coordination**: Outfit complements, doesn't compete
4. **Natural Movement**: Authentic, not overly posed
5. **Cinematic Lighting**: Dramatic yet natural quality
6. **Shallow DoF**: Subject sharp, background beautifully blurred
7. **Aspirational Setting**: Luxury or contemporary environment
8. **Editorial Quality**: Vogue/GQ magazine standard

## Troubleshooting

### Product Not Visible Enough

**Problem**: Product gets lost in full outfit/scene  
**Solution**:

- Add "Emphasize the [product] through dynamic angles"
- Specify "[product] clearly visible and prominent in frame"
- Adjust pose: "Positioned to showcase [product] prominently"

### Model Looks Too Posed/Unnatural

**Problem**: Pose appears staged or stiff  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize "natural movement" and "authentic poses"
- Change action to dynamic: "walking," "mid-step," "natural movement"
- Add "candid fashion photography" style

### Wardrobe Competing with Product

**Problem**: Outfit too busy or distracting from product  
**Solution**:

- Specify "minimal outfit" or "simple wardrobe"
- Add "outfit complements product without competing"
- Emphasize "subtle accents that complement the [product]"

### Background Too Busy

**Problem**: Setting overwhelming or distracting  
**Solution**:

- Add "shallow depth of field" to blur background
- Specify "softly blurred [setting]" or "out-of-focus background"
- Simplify setting description

### Wrong Fashion Aesthetic

**Problem**: Doesn't match brand tier or style  
**Solution**:

- Adjust comparable references (Vogue, GQ, or specific magazines)
- Specify brand tier: "luxury," "premium," "contemporary"
- Define style: "streetwear," "haute couture," "athletic"
