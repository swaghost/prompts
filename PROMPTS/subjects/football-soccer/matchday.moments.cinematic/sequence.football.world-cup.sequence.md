## PROMPT 2: Underground Tunnel Ape Walk with Chaos

**Platform:** Apetelligence (Seedance)  
**Duration:** 3:4, 15-second continuous live-action sequence  
**Style:** Real 35mm photochemical footage aesthetic

### Complete Prompt

```
THIS WAS MADE ON SEEDANCE IN APETELLIGENCE

3:4, 15-second continuous live-action sequence. One uninterrupted backward tracking shot, no cuts.

IMAGE REFERENCES: Image 1 = exact ape face, fur, anatomy and proportions. EVERY visible character must be an ape derived from Image 1. Zero humans anywhere, including blurred, partial or silhouetted figures.

LEAD APE: exact Image 1 identity. Black oversized technical bomber with full-length sleeves to wrists, black fitted tee, dark loose cargos, black leather boots. No exposed forearm fur. Calm, serious, unreadable. Walks at one steady pace, never looks back or reacts.

LOCATION: Long underground bicycle tunnel — curved concrete ceiling, dense graffiti, rows of parked bikes on both sides, narrow central path, overhead fluorescent strips, damp floor and puddles. 5–6 background apes only, all visibly ape-faced and furred like Image 1, wearing dark streetwear/workwear.

CONTINUITY: Lead face, fur, body and outfit stay identical. Every extra remains an ape. No species, outfit, anatomy or logo drift.

[SHOT 1 — 0s to 3s] Chest-height medium-full camera tracks backward directly in front of lead at his walking speed. He walks calmly down the center. Two apes cycle slowly behind him; others stand near bikes or adjust chains. Logo graffiti is visible. Everything feels alive but controlled.

[SHOT 2 — 3s to 6s] One cyclist clips a parked bicycle, starting a domino chain along one side. Bikes tip into each other, metal rattles, wheels spin, handlebars clash. Cyclists steer away and background apes react. Lead keeps walking unchanged.

[SHOT 3 — 6s to 9s] The bike domino continues. One loose bicycle rolls diagonally across the tunnel behind lead, wobbling naturally. One ape jumps aside, another catches a falling bike, another backs into the wall. Lead remains centered and unaffected.

[SHOT 4 — 9s to 12s] A wall pipe vents a strong burst of white steam across the tunnel. Overhead fluorescents begin flickering sequentially from the far end toward camera. Fallen bikes settle, wheels keep spinning, apes move through the steam. Lead walks calmly onward.

[SHOT 5 — 12s to 15s] Lead stops beneath one bright fluorescent. Behind him, lights shut off one after another toward camera, progressively swallowing the tunnel in darkness. He slowly raises his eyes and looks directly into lens. Hold under the final light while steam drifts, the logo graffiti remains faintly visible and one bicycle wheel spins in darkness.

VISUAL STYLE: Arriflex BL4, Technovision 2x anamorphic, 24mm equivalent. Strong one-point perspective, mild barrel distortion, oval bokeh, horizontal fluorescent flare, mild edge softness, visible lens breathing. Fuji F-500T/F-400T tungsten stock — tight grain, cyan-green shadows, dirty red/blue graffiti accents, deep blacks. Hard fluorescent toplight, almost no front fill, sharp brow/cheek shadows. Real 35mm photochemical footage, natural motion blur, grounded physics, realistic steam and metal movement. NOT digital or CGI-clean.

AUDIO: fluorescent buzz, tunnel ambience, bicycle chains, metal impacts, rolling wheels, pedaling, footsteps, low ape murmurs, steam burst. No music, no dialogue.

NEGATIVE: NO humans. NO face/fur/body/outfit/logo drift. NO exposed forearms. NO cartoon physics, CGI fur, AI-clean edges, cuts, resets, teleports, abrupt zooms, explosions or fire.
```
