## BALL PHYSICS & CHARACTERISTICS

### Knuckleball Technique:

**Definition:**
- **No-spin kick** (ball travels with minimal or zero rotation)
- Creates unpredictable, erratic flight
- Ball wobbles and dips suddenly
- Difficult for goalkeepers (trajectory uncertainty)

**Visual Characteristics:**
- **Ball not spinning** (surface patterns wobble, not rotate smoothly)
- **Irregular wobble** (slight side-to-side/up-down deviation)
- **Fast initial speed** (struck hard)
- **Sudden dip** (aerodynamic instability causes sharp drop)
- Appears to float briefly at peak

**Striking Technique:**
- Contact with **center of ball** (not top or side)
- **Flat instep contact** (laces area of foot)
- **Minimal follow-through** (abbreviated swing after contact)
- **Stiff ankle** (locks ball's rotation)
- **Left foot** for this player (southpaw)

**CRITICAL AVOIDANCE:**
- **NO spinning/curling ball** (must be knuckleball, no rotation)
- Not a curve/banana kick (different technique)
- Not topspin or backspin (must be no-spin)
