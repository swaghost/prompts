# Portrait Sports Particle Render Aesthetic

## Description of Resulting Image

A high-contrast monochrome image featuring subject reconstructed entirely from thousands of tiny glowing white dots and voxel-like particles on pure black background. The composition uses point-cloud dispersion, volumetric particles, halftone density gradients, and scattered noise trails to define the silhouette with sparse orange/red accent particles, creating futuristic AI architecture visualization mixed with experimental generative art aesthetic in vertical poster layout.

## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
Transform the uploaded image into high-contrast monochrome particle-render aesthetic. Subject reconstructed entirely from thousands of tiny glowing white dots and voxel-like particles on pure black background. Preserve the original subject composition and pose, but simplify details into clean geometric forms.

**Setting/Environment:**
Pure black background only with white particle clusters forming the subject. Cinematic negative space with ultra minimal atmosphere. No realistic environments or props.

**Lighting:**
Soft bloom glow on dense particle areas with cinematic lighting emphasizing volumetric nature of particles. Procedural dots with volumetric dispersion creating depth through particle density.

**Composition:**
Clean composition with vertical poster layout. Abstract but recognizable silhouette centered in frame. Subtle floating debris and dissolving particles around edges adding dynamism.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Futuristic AI architecture visualization mixed with experimental generative art. Style should feel like brutalist futuristic design aesthetic with clean composition. Sparse orange/red accent particles (very minimal) provide color accents against black and white. No realistic textures, no colors besides black, white, and tiny orange accents. High contrast generative AI art style.

**Technical Specifications:**
Rendering style: octane render, point cloud simulation, particle system render, volumetric dispersion, procedural dots, ultra detailed, abstract minimalism. Slight voxel/point-cloud rendering effect. 4:5 ratio.

**Typography Treatment:**
Small thin mono-space typography placed minimally on left and right sides, similar to futuristic design posters, white text only, subtle and secondary to the artwork.

**Negative Specifications:**
Avoid realistic shading, painterly textures, colorful backgrounds, low contrast, photorealism, messy composition, extra objects, blur, noisy artifacts, low resolution.
