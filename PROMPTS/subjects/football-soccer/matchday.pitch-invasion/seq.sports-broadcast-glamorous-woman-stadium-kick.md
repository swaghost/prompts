## Video Prompt

Ultra-realistic sports broadcast video, cinematic night match atmosphere. A glamorous woman sits in a packed football stadium surrounded by fans in jerseys and scarves. She is mid-drinking the beer cup in her other hand, while a half-eaten French fry on another hand. Suddenly the stadium PA booms her name "Skai" — she freezes, eyes wide, everybody looks at her cheering. She carefully sets the beer cup and eats the French fry, wipes her hand with the tissue paper, and throws it. She stands, slides past the row of fans, and walks purposefully down the stadium steps toward the pitch. She steps onto the field — the crowd murmurs with anticipation. The football players on the ground toss the ball towards her. She lines up, takes two measured steps, and sends a powerful, clean kick straight through the goal posts. The stadium erupts.
