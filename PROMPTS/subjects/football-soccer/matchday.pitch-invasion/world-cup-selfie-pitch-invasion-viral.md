## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Looks too professional/polished like studio photo

**Solution:**

- Strengthen smartphone realism: "authentic smartphone camera artifacts, slight blur, imperfect focus, natural grain"
- Add imperfections: "camera shake, motion blur, imperfect framing, slightly off-center"
- Emphasize spontaneity: "unposed, spontaneous, caught in moment, NOT professional photography"
- Add negative: "no studio lighting, no professional sports photography, no perfect composition"

### Problem: Faces don't match reference images

**Solution:**

- For Midjourney: Use --cref parameter with uploaded reference images
- For DALL-E: Upload both references first, then explicitly reference "first image" and "second image"
- Add: "preserve exact facial features from reference, maintain identity accuracy, recognizable as same person"
- Use face-focused descriptions: "exact face shape, nose, eyes, mouth, bone structure from reference"

### Problem: Scene looks too static/posed

**Solution:**

- Add motion: "motion blur, camera shake, fan off-balance, security pulling, dynamic movement"
- Emphasize chaos: "chaotic moment, urgent intervention, spontaneous action, frozen mid-movement"
- Include: "security guard in motion, another guard running with blur streaks, active scene"

### Problem: Lighting too perfect/cinematic

**Solution:**

- Specify authentic smartphone lighting: "natural stadium lighting, mixed light sources, authentic exposure, not perfectly lit"
- Add: "realistic smartphone sensor handling of light, some areas overexposed or underexposed"
- Include negative: "no cinematic color grading, no studio lighting setup, no perfect exposure"

### Problem: Composition too centered/professional

**Solution:**

- Emphasize imperfection: "imperfect framing, not centered, slightly cut off at edges, asymmetric composition"
- Add: "natural selfie angle, typical smartphone selfie composition, unplanned framing"
- Specify: "fan and player may be off-center, edges of frame show typical selfie imperfections"

### Problem: Lacks viral/authentic social media feel

**Solution:**

- Add social media aesthetic language: "viral TikTok moment, Instagram Reels quality, shareable content aesthetic"
- Emphasize authenticity: "real smartphone selfie quality, authentic viral sports moment, documentary realism"
- Include: "the kind of image that spreads on social media, genuine unfiltered moment"
