# Touchline Perspective - Match Day Image Prompts

## Overview

Create compelling touchline and sideline photography showcasing managers, coaching staff, and the technical area perspective during crucial match moments. Features tactical focus, leadership visualization, and dramatic sideline atmosphere with documentary sports photography style.

**Part of:** [Match Day Tunnel Collection](PROMPTS.TUNNEL.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Manager Spotlight** - Coach leadership emphasis
- **Tactical Storytelling** - Strategic visualization
- **Sideline Drama** - Emotional coaching moments
- **Documentary Content** - Behind-the-game perspective
- **Social Media Content** - Instagram and Twitter coach features
- **Broadcast Graphics** - TV sideline coverage
- **Leadership Features** - Manager profiles and analysis
- **Match Atmosphere** - Stadium environment from touchline
- **Coaching Education** - Tactical instruction visualization
- **Premium Sports Photography** - High-quality sideline imagery

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Manager on touchline at kickoff, tactical focus expression, filled stadium behind with national color supporters, sideline perspective, documentary sports photography, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick manager shot needed
- Touchline perspective emphasis
- Stadium atmosphere from sideline
- Documentary-style photography

**Customization:**

- Specify expression (tactical focus, animated, calm, intense)
- Adjust stadium fill (filling, packed, one end visible)
- Add specific details (tactical board, gesturing, suit or tracksuit)
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for touchline scenes
- Manager and stadium context
- Broadcast standard
- Versatile documentary
- Professional sports photography

**4:3 Traditional:**

- Documentary classic feel
- More intimate framing
- Portrait-oriented scenes
- Film-like aesthetic
- Photojournalistic style

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Single manager portraits
- Close-up emotional moments
- Mobile social content
- Instagram/Facebook Stories

**1:1 Square:**

- Manager portrait focus
- Balanced composition
- Social media posts
- Instagram feed versatility

**Recommendation:** 16:9 for most touchline scenes, 4:3 for documentary feel, 9:16 for portraits

---

### Lighting & Time of Day

**Golden Hour (Best for Portraits):**

- Warm flattering light
- Beautiful manager portraits
- Romantic atmospheric
- Pre-match timing ideal
- Premium aesthetic

**Floodlights (Night Matches):**

- Bright stadium illumination
- Modern night match feel
- Clean sharp lighting
- High-energy atmosphere
- Contemporary aesthetic

**Overcast (Documentary Realism):**

- Even consistent light
- Authentic atmosphere
- Professional documentation
- No harsh shadows
- Reliable quality

**Mixed Stadium Light:**

- Floodlights and natural
- Transitional atmosphere
- Blue hour timing
- Dramatic mood
- Beautiful color palette

**Recommendation:** Golden hour for pre-match portraits, floodlights for match-action touchline

---

### Camera Angles & Perspectives

**Eye Level (Standard):**

- Human perspective
- Relatable viewpoint
- Professional standard
- Balanced composition
- Documentary authenticity

**Low Angle (Heroic):**

- Looking up at manager
- Powerful imposing feel
- Stadium looming behind
- Dramatic perspective
- Epic scale

**Side Profile:**

- Manager watching pitch
- Profile composition
- Observational angle
- Documentary style
- Strategic thinking visual

**Slightly Elevated:**

- Looking down at touchline
- Technical area visible
- Context emphasis
- Tactical overview
- Professional broadcast angle

**Tight Close-Up:**

- Face and expression focus
- Emotional intensity
- Portrait quality
- Intimate connection
- Psychological depth

**Recommendation:** Eye level for documentary authenticity, low angle for dramatic impact

---

### Depth of Field Options

**Shallow (Portrait Emphasis):**

- Manager sharp, background blurred
- Stadium soft bokeh
- Professional portrait aesthetic
- Subject isolation
- Premium quality

**Medium (Balanced):**

- Manager in focus, stadium visible
- Balanced depth
- Documentary standard
- Context maintained
- Versatile approach

**Deep (Full Context):**

- Manager and stadium sharp
- Maximum information
- Wide documentary
- Environmental emphasis
- Comprehensive view

**Recommendation:** Shallow for pre-match portraits, medium for documentary match moments

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 16:9       (Touchline scenes - primary)
--ar 4:3        (Documentary traditional feel)
--ar 9:16       (Vertical manager portraits)

--stylize 500-700   (Photorealistic sports documentary)
--stylize 700-900   (More cinematic, dramatic)

--quality 2     (High quality for detail and expression)

--style raw     (Photorealistic sports photography)
```

**Example Kickoff Manager:**

```
Manager on touchline at kickoff, tactical focus expression, filled stadium behind with national color supporters, sideline perspective, documentary sports photography --ar 16:9 --stylize 600 --quality 2 --style raw
```

**Example Animated Coaching:**

```
Manager gesturing passionately from touchline, arms raised shouting tactical instructions, assistants beside, packed stadium background, dynamic sideline action photography --ar 16:9 --stylize 650 --quality 2 --style raw
```
