# Personal Focus & Preparation - Match Day Image Prompts

## Overview

Create intimate personal preparation photography showcasing individual player moments, psychological readiness, equipment preparation, and pre-match mental focus. Features close-up detail shots, emotional portraits, and authentic behind-the-moments athlete preparation with cinematic sports documentary style.

```
Goalkeeper gloves adjustment close-up, intense pre-match expression, national kit visible, stadium crowd beginning to fill in background, psychological preparation moment, cinematic sports photography, shallow depth of field.
```

**When to Use:**

**Customization:**

- Adjust expression (intense, focused, calm, determined)

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**4:5 Portrait (Primary):**

- Best for close-up details
- Instagram feed ideal
- Vertical emphasis
- Product photography style
- Social media optimized

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Full vertical close-ups
- Instagram/Facebook Stories
- Mobile-first content
- Immersive detail shots

**1:1 Square:**

- Detail photography
- Balanced composition
- Instagram feed versatile
- Equipment showcases

**16:9 Horizontal:**

- Some context visible
- Wider detail shots
- Broadcast-friendly
- Traditional sports photography

**Recommendation:** 4:5 portrait for equipment details, 9:16 for social Stories

---

### Depth of Field (Critical)

**Shallow (f/1.4-f/2.8) - Recommended:**

- Subject in sharp focus
- Background beautifully blurred
- Professional aesthetic
- Equipment detail emphasis
- Premium photography look

**Very Shallow (f/1.4):**

- Maximum background blur
- Artistic bokeh
- Subject isolation
- Cinematic quality
- Premium portrait feel

**Medium (f/4-f/5.6):**

- More context visible
- Balanced depth
- Some background detail
- Documentary approach

**Recommendation:** Very shallow (f/1.4-f/2.0) for maximum professional quality and subject isolation

---

### Lighting for Close-Ups

**Natural Soft Light:**

- Window light in dressing room
- Soft flattering illumination
- Authentic atmosphere
- Warm documentary feel

**Dramatic Side Light:**

- Light from one side
- Strong shadows and highlights
- Cinematic mood
- Artistic aesthetic

**Golden Hour Glow:**

- Warm sunset light
- Beautiful skin tones
- Romantic cinematic
- Pitch-side moments

**Stadium Floodlights:**

- Bright clean illumination
- Modern match feel
- Sharp detail
- Professional quality

**Soft Overcast:**

- Even consistent light
- No harsh shadows
- Professional documentation
- Reliable quality

**Recommendation:** Natural soft light or dramatic side light for dressing room, golden hour for pitch moments

---

### Camera Angles for Detail

**Close-Up (Primary):**

- Equipment fills frame
- Maximum detail emphasis
- Product photography style
- Intimate perspective

**Extreme Close-Up (Macro):**

- Texture and detail focus
- Boot studs, grass blades
- Artistic abstraction
- Premium aesthetic

**Over-Shoulder:**

- Player's perspective
- Immersive viewpoint
- First-person feeling
- Relatable angle

**Straight-On Detail:**

- Direct view of subject
- Clean composition
- Product photography
- Professional standard

**Recommendation:** Close-up with shallow depth of field for most preparation details

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final personal preparation image:

- [ ] Equipment or detail clearly in focus and sharp
- [ ] Background appropriately blurred (shallow depth of field)
- [ ] National team colors visible and identifiable
- [ ] Expression or mood appropriate and visible
- [ ] Lighting flattering and cinematic
- [ ] Composition balanced and engaging
- [ ] Photorealistic quality, professional standard
- [ ] Equipment detail realistic (gloves, boots, tape authentic)
- [ ] Hands or body parts anatomically correct
- [ ] Context established (dressing room, tunnel, pitch)
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
- [ ] No distracting background elements
