## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: Tunnel Lighting Too Flat

**Solutions:**

- Emphasize "high contrast" and "dramatic shadows"
- Add "dark tunnel with bright pitch light at end"
- Request "low key lighting" explicitly
- Specify "deep shadows along walls"
- Add "cinematic film noir atmosphere"

---

### Issue: Not Enough Cinematic Feel

**Solutions:**

- Add "cinematic sports documentary" multiple times
- Request "film-like quality" or "premium cinema aesthetic"
- Specify "wide-angle lens" for tunnel shots
- Add "dramatic lighting" emphasis
- Increase --stylize value (700-900) in Midjourney

---

### Issue: Team Colors Not Clear

**Solutions:**

- Specify "national team colors clearly visible"
- Add "jerseys prominent in frame"
- Request "team color identification clear"
- For tunnel: "kit colors visible despite shadows"
- Specify "contrasting colors" for opposing teams

---

### Issue: Too Bright/Not Enough Drama

**Solutions:**

- Add "low key lighting" or "moody atmosphere"
- Request "dramatic shadows" and "high contrast"
- Specify "tunnel darkness" for tunnel scenes
- Add "dressing room low light" for briefings
- Avoid "bright" or "well-lit" terminology

---

### Issue: Perspective Not Dramatic Enough

**Solutions:**

- Add "wide-angle lens" or "ultra-wide perspective"
- Request "exaggerated perspective" and "vanishing point"
- Specify "deep tunnel depth" for tunnel scenes
- Add "architectural perspective emphasis"
- Use 21:9 aspect ratio for maximum width

---

### Issue: Doesn't Look Like Real Tunnel/Stadium

**Solutions:**

- Add "photorealistic" and "professional sports photography"
- Specify "authentic stadium tunnel" or "real dressing room"
- Request "documentary realism" and "observational photography"
- Add architectural details: "concrete walls," "stadium architecture"
- Include context: "stadium visible," "crowd in background"

---

### Issue: Emotions Look Fake

**Solutions:**

- Emphasize "authentic emotion" and "genuine expression"
- Specify exact emotion: "pre-match tension," "tactical focus," "determined confidence"
- Add "photojournalistic style" for authenticity
- Request "candid moment" and "unposed"
- Avoid "smiling" unless celebrating

## AI Tool-Specific Tips

### Midjourney Optimization

**Stylize Values:**

- 500-600: Clean photorealistic sports documentary
- 600-700: Balanced realism and cinematic atmosphere
- 700-900: Maximum dramatic cinematic quality
- Avoid >900: Can become too artistic/unrealistic

**Quality Parameter:**

- Always use `--quality 2` for sports photography
- Critical for detail in faces and equipment
- Essential for tunnel texture and atmosphere

**Style Raw:**

- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic documentary
- Reduces Midjourney's artistic enhancement
- More authentic sports photography look

**Aspect Ratios:**

- 21:9: Maximum cinematic tunnel depth
- 16:9: Versatile standard for most scenes
- 4:3: Documentary traditional feel
- 9:16: Vertical portraits and Stories
- 1:1: Social media square posts

## Quick Reference Summary

### Essential Elements for Any Tunnel/Pre-Match Image

1. **Cinematic Sports Documentary Style** - Always specify
2. **Dramatic Lighting** - High contrast or golden hour
3. **National Team Colors Visible** - Clear identification
4. **Appropriate Atmosphere** - Tension, focus, or celebration
5. **Wide-Angle Perspective** - For tunnel and team scenes
6. **Photorealistic Quality** - Professional sports photography
7. **Authentic Behavior** - Documentary not staged

### Most Versatile Prompting Formula

```
[Setting] [action/moment], [team colors visible],
[lighting description], cinematic sports documentary,
[wide-angle if applicable], [aspect ratio]
```

**Example Tunnel:**

```
Both teams in tunnel facing pitch light at end,
national kit colors alternating, dark tunnel with bright destination,
cinematic sports documentary, wide angle --ar 21:9
```

**Example Manager:**

```
Manager on touchline gesturing tactically,
filled stadium behind with national colors,
golden hour light, documentary sports photography --ar 16:9
```
