# Pre-Match Rituals & Ceremony - Match Day Image Prompts

## Overview

Create powerful pre-match ritual and ceremony photography showcasing warm-ups, national anthems, coin tosses, and traditional match-day protocols. Features patriotic moments, referee ceremonies, and the final preparations before kickoff with cinematic sports documentary style.

**Part of:** [Match Day Tunnel Collection](PROMPTS.TUNNEL.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Pre-Match Programming** - Build anticipation content
- **Patriotic Moments** - National pride emphasis
- **Broadcast Graphics** - TV ceremony coverage
- **Documentary Content** - Match-day tradition storytelling
- **Social Media Build-Up** - Instagram and Twitter anticipation
- **Match Programs** - Printed and digital publications
- **Historical Documentation** - Capture traditional ceremonies
- **Tournament Marketing** - Show scale and atmosphere
- **Educational Content** - Explain match protocols
- **Premium Sports Photography** - High-quality ritual moments

## Base Prompt Template

```
Pre-match warm-up on pitch, players in national colors stretching and passing, early evening light, stadium filling with supporters, calm before storm atmosphere, cinematic sports documentary, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick pre-match scene needed
- Standard warm-up shot
- Stadium atmosphere building
- Documentary-style photography

**Customization:**

- Specify lighting (golden hour, evening, floodlights)
- Adjust stadium fill (empty, filling, packed)
- Add specific details (exercises, drills, activities)
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for warm-ups and team lines
- Stadium context inclusion
- Broadcast standard
- Versatile composition

**21:9 Ultra-Wide Cinematic:**

- Anthem lineups full team
- Stadium spectacle emphasis
- Epic scale feeling
- Premium cinematic quality

**4:3 Traditional:**

- Documentary feel
- Classic sports photography
- More intimate framing
- Portrait orientation option

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Single player anthem moments
- Close-up emotional shots
- Mobile-first social content

**1:1 Square:**

- Social media posts
- Balanced compositions
- Instagram feed versatility

**Recommendation:** 21:9 for anthem lineups, 16:9 for warm-ups and versatility

---

### Lighting & Time of Day

**Golden Hour (Early Evening):**

- Warm beautiful light
- Flattering player tones
- Romantic atmospheric glow
- Pre-match timing ideal
- Premium aesthetic

**Blue Hour (Twilight):**

- Deep blue sky backdrop
- Stadium lights beginning
- Dramatic romantic mood
- Transitional atmosphere
- Beautiful color palette

**Floodlights (Night Matches):**

- Bright stadium illumination
- Modern night match feel
- Clean sharp lighting
- High-energy atmosphere
- Contemporary aesthetic

**Overcast (Soft Light):**

- Even consistent illumination
- Good for all activities
- Professional documentation
- No harsh shadows
- Reliable quality

**Recommendation:** Golden hour for warm-ups, floodlights for night anthem ceremonies

---

### Camera Angles & Perspectives

**Wide Establishing Shot:**

- Full pitch and stadium
- Shows scale and atmosphere
- Team and crowd together
- Epic cinematic scope

**Mid-Range Team View:**

- Players as main subjects
- Stadium visible but secondary
- Balanced composition
- Versatile framing

**Close-Up Portrait:**

- Individual player emotion
- Anthem personal moments
- Facial expressions focus
- Intimate connection

**Aerial Overhead:**

- Huddle formations
- Flag displays
- Pattern emphasis
- Unique perspective

**Ground Level Low Angle:**

- Heroic player perspective
- Sky and stadium behind
- Powerful imposing feel
- Dynamic angle

**Recommendation:** Wide for ceremonies, close-up for anthem emotion, aerial for huddles

---

### Depth of Field Options

**Shallow (Portrait Emphasis):**

- Player in focus, background blurred
- Emotional close-ups
- Individual player features
- Professional portrait aesthetic

**Medium (Balanced):**

- Team in focus, stadium soft
- Balanced depth
- Versatile approach
- Documentary standard

**Deep (Full Context):**

- Everything in focus
- Stadium and team sharp
- Maximum information
- Documentary wide shots

**Recommendation:** Shallow for anthem close-ups, deep for wide ceremony shots

- [ ] Composition balanced and compelling
