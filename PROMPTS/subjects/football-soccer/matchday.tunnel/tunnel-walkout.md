# Tunnel & Walkout - Match Day Image Prompts

## Overview

Create cinematic tunnel and player walkout photography showcasing pre-match tension, the journey from darkness to pitch light, and iconic moments as teams emerge. Features dramatic lighting, wide-angle compositions, and emotional sports documentary style.

**Part of:** [Match Day Tunnel Collection](PROMPTS.TUNNEL.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Pre-Match Content** - Build anticipation before kickoff
- **Social Media Drama** - Instagram and Twitter cinematic moments
- **Broadcast Graphics** - TV show intros and transitions
- **Documentary Content** - Behind-the-scenes storytelling
- **Match Day Programs** - Printed materials and digital publications
- **Player Features** - Individual player spotlight content
- **Tournament Marketing** - Epic pre-match atmosphere
- **Historical Moments** - Capture iconic walkout scenes
- **Team Psychology** - Show mental preparation journey
- **Sports Photography Portfolio** - Premium cinematic sports imagery

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Teams in tunnel facing pitch light at far end, national kit colors visible, pre-match tension atmosphere, cinematic sports documentary, wide angle, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick tunnel shot needed
- Standard pre-match scene
- Dramatic lighting emphasis
- Documentary-style photography

**Customization:**

- Specify lighting (dramatic shadows, pitch light)
- Adjust perspective (wide angle, player POV)
- Add specific details (jerseys, numbers, expressions)
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Enhanced Prompt Variations

### Classic Tunnel Lineup

**Prompt:**

```
Both teams lined up in the tunnel facing the pitch light at the far end, national kit colors alternating in the line, pre-match tension, cinematic sports documentary, wide angle.
```

**Best For:**

- Iconic pre-match moment
- Team unity and rivalry contrast
- Dramatic lighting showcase
- Wide establishing shots
- Tournament atmosphere emphasis

**Key Features:**

- Both teams visible in frame
- Tunnel darkness to pitch light
- Alternating team colors
- Pre-match tension mood
- Wide-angle perspective
- Cinematic documentary style

**Visual Elements:**

- **Tunnel Architecture:** Concrete walls, low ceiling, confined space leading forward
- **Pitch Light:** Bright rectangular light at tunnel end, dramatic destination
- **Both Teams:** Players from opposing sides lined up, waiting together
- **Alternating Colors:** National kit colors interspersed in line
- **Pre-Match Tension:** Body language shows focus, nervous energy, anticipation
- **Dramatic Shadows:** Dark tunnel contrasts with bright pitch light ahead
- **Wide Angle:** Exaggerated perspective, depth toward light source

**Lighting Variations:**

**High Contrast (Dark Tunnel):**

```
Very dark tunnel, only pitch light illuminating scene, deep shadows, dramatic silhouettes
```

**Partially Lit Tunnel:**

```
Tunnel lights on along walls, balanced exposure, faces visible, moderate contrast
```

**Backlit Silhouettes:**

```
Players completely silhouetted against bright pitch light, dramatic outlines only
```

**Side Lighting Accents:**

```
Strip lights along tunnel sides, players partially illuminated, cinematic mood lighting
```

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for tunnel depth and width
- Cinematic standard
- Broadcast and documentary ideal
- Shows tunnel architecture
- Captures team lines

**21:9 Ultra-Wide Cinematic:**

- Maximum tunnel depth emphasis
- Epic scale feeling
- Premium cinematic content
- Exaggerated perspective
- Film-like aesthetic

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Single player walkout
- Tunnel height emphasis
- Mobile-first social content
- Instagram/Facebook Stories

**1:1 Square:**

- Social media posts
- Balanced tunnel composition
- Instagram feed versatility

**Recommendation:** 21:9 for maximum cinematic impact, 16:9 for versatility

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 21:9       (Ultra-wide cinematic tunnel depth - premium)
--ar 16:9       (Standard cinematic horizontal - versatile)
--ar 9:16       (Vertical single player walkout)

--stylize 500-700   (Cinematic sports documentary aesthetic)
--stylize 700-900   (More dramatic, film-like quality)

--quality 2     (High quality for detail and texture)

--style raw     (Photorealistic cinematic sports photography)
```

**Example Classic Tunnel:**

```
Both teams lined up in tunnel facing pitch light at far end, national kit colors alternating, pre-match tension, cinematic sports documentary, wide angle --ar 21:9 --stylize 600 --quality 2 --style raw
```

**Example Single Player:**

```
Single player walking from tunnel toward pitch light alone, number on back visible, stadium roar implied, emotional cinematic sports moment, wide angle --ar 16:9 --stylize 650 --quality 2 --style raw
```

### DALL-E Recommendations

- Specify "cinematic sports documentary" or "cinematic sports photography"
- Request "wide-angle perspective" or "wide-angle lens"
- Mention "dramatic lighting" and "pitch light at tunnel end"
- Request "high contrast" or "dramatic shadows"
- Specify "teams in national kit colors" for color clarity

---

### Stable Diffusion Tips

- Use "cinematic sports photography," "wide angle," "dramatic lighting"
- Lighting: "tunnel darkness," "bright pitch light," "high contrast shadows"
- For realism: "photorealistic, professional sports photography, cinematic quality"
- Negative prompts: "no flat lighting, no even exposure, dramatic contrast required"
- Control with CFG (7-10 for photorealistic sports cinematography)
- Use cinematic photography LoRA for film-like quality
- Wide-angle lens LoRA for perspective distortion

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final tunnel/walkout image:

- [ ] Tunnel structure clearly visible (walls, ceiling, architecture)
- [ ] Pitch light visible at tunnel end (bright destination)
- [ ] Players in national kit colors identifiable
- [ ] Dramatic lighting contrast present (dark to light)
- [ ] Wide-angle perspective appropriate and effective
- [ ] Pre-match tension or emotion palpable
- [ ] Photorealistic quality, cinematic standard
- [ ] Sharp focus on players or deliberate artistic blur
- [ ] Tunnel depth and perspective compelling
- [ ] Stadium or crowd implied/visible where appropriate
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
- [ ] Composition balanced and dramatic
