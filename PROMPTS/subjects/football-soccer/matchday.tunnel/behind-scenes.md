# Behind the Scenes - Match Day Image Prompts

## Overview

Create intimate behind-the-scenes photography showcasing dressing room moments, team briefings, tactical preparations, and private pre-match team environments. Features low dramatic lighting, photojournalistic style, and exclusive access to the inner sanctum of match-day preparation.

**Part of:** [Match Day Tunnel Collection](PROMPTS.TUNNEL.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Exclusive Content** - Behind-the-scenes privileged access
- **Documentary Storytelling** - Authentic team preparation
- **Team Culture Showcase** - Unity and bonding emphasis
- **Tactical Education** - Strategy and planning visualization
- **Coach Spotlight** - Manager leadership moments
- **Team Building Content** - Collective preparation emphasis
- **Premium Photography** - Exclusive intimate imagery
- **Historical Documentation** - Important match preparation
- **Social Media Exclusivity** - Instagram and Twitter insider access
- **Broadcast Features** - TV documentary segments

## Base Prompt Template

```
Dressing room final pre-match briefing, backs of players in national colors facing tactics board, low dramatic lighting, intense team atmosphere, photojournalistic style, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick dressing room shot needed
- Team briefing emphasis
- Behind-scenes atmosphere
- Photojournalistic documentary style

**Customization:**

- Specify lighting (low dramatic, natural window, fluorescent)
- Adjust perspective (from behind, from front, side angle)
- Add specific details (tactics board, coach gesturing, team huddle)
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for dressing room scenes
- Team briefings and huddles
- Wide room context
- Documentary standard
- Broadcast friendly

**21:9 Ultra-Wide Cinematic:**

- Wide dressing room shots
- Emphasize team scale
- Cinematic premium feel
- Epic atmosphere

**4:3 Traditional:**

- Documentary classic feel
- Intimate closer framing
- Photojournalistic aesthetic
- Film-like quality

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Single player moments
- Locker introspection
- Mobile social content
- Vertical perspective

**1:1 Square:**

- Balanced compositions
- Social media posts
- Instagram feed versatility

**Recommendation:** 16:9 for team briefings, 4:3 for documentary feel, 9:16 for individual moments

---

### Lighting & Atmosphere

**Low Key Dramatic (Recommended):**

- Dark shadows, limited light
- Cinematic moody atmosphere
- High contrast
- Premium dramatic feel
- Film noir aesthetic

**Side Lighting:**

- Light from one side
- Strong shadow definition
- Sculptural dramatic
- Cinematic quality
- Artistic atmosphere

**Natural Window Light:**

- Soft from windows
- Authentic dressing room
- Documentary realism
- Moderate contrast
- Warm atmosphere

**Fluorescent Overhead:**

- Institutional lighting
- Authentic realism
- Flat but real
- Documentary harsh truth
- Utilitarian feel

**Single Source Spotlight:**

- One light on coach or board
- Maximum drama
- High contrast
- Theatrical atmosphere
- Premium cinematic

**Recommendation:** Low key dramatic side lighting for maximum cinematic impact

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 16:9       (Dressing room briefings - primary)
--ar 4:3        (Documentary traditional feel)
--ar 9:16       (Vertical individual moments)

--stylize 500-700   (Photorealistic documentary style)
--stylize 700-900   (More cinematic, dramatic atmosphere)

--quality 2     (High quality for detail and mood)

--style raw     (Photorealistic photojournalistic style)
```

**Example Briefing:**


## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final behind-the-scenes image:

- [ ] Dressing room environment clearly identifiable
- [ ] National team colors visible on players
- [ ] Lighting appropriately dramatic and moody
- [ ] Activity or moment clearly identifiable (briefing, huddle, speech)
- [ ] Photojournalistic documentary quality
- [ ] Authentic atmosphere not staged
- [ ] Composition balanced and engaging
- [ ] Perspective appropriate for scene
- [ ] Emotional tone appropriate and palpable
- [ ] Details realistic (tactics board, lockers, equipment)
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
- [ ] Exclusive behind-scenes feeling achieved
