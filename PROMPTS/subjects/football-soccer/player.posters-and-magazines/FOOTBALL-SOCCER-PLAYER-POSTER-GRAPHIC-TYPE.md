# FOOTBALL/SOCCER PLAYER POSTER - GRAPHIC TYPE-PLAY

**Prompt Type:** Image Generation - Graphic Poster Design  
**Subject:** Football/Soccer Player (Reference: Neymar Jr.)  
**Style:** Photographic subject integrated into flat graphic poster design  
**Format:** Vertical 3:4 movie-poster frame  
**Platforms:** Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion XL, Flux Pro (with reference image support)

---

## CONCEPT

Graphic poster design merging photographic realism with flat typography. Subject appears as true photograph woven through massive typographic elements, creating dimensional interplay between flat graphic design and realistic human presence.

**Key Visual Elements:**

- Massive slab-serif typography dominating center
- Subject physically integrated (in front of/behind letterforms)
- Flat graphic background (Brazil Green)
- Photorealistic subject with poster-grade drama
- Minimal text: title, tagline, greeked credit block

---

## COMPLETE PROMPT

```
Unmistakably the same person from the attached reference photo (Neymar JR) — carry person's exact features across without altering them: the same hairline, the same hair texture and natural fall, the same brow shape and set, the same eye shape and spacing, the same nose line and bridge, the same lip shape, the same jawline and cheekbone structure, the same skin tone. The reference photo's own lighting, background and clothing are entirely discarded and replaced by everything described here. THE FACE IS THE POSTER'S PRODUCT: person's likeness is the single most important element — rendered large, sharp and exact, holding perfect identity under poster-grade drama; no stylisation, no averaging, no drift; anyone who knows the person recognises the person instantly.

THE POSTER: the graphic type-play. A flat [Brazil Green] field holding one colossal word — the invented title "PRINCE" in massive slab-serif black capitals filling the poster's middle third — and person woven through it: leaning back easily against the letter P's upright as if it were a wall, one ankle crossed, one elbow hooked over the letter's top serif, fully photographic against the flat graphic — the interleave (body in front of some strokes, behind others) selling the depth. Keep outfit from reference image; person face large, amused, exact. One committed expression — the smirk of a person leaning on own myth: eyes to the lens, one brow raised.

Light: one clean soft key from upper-left models her honestly with a crisp small shadow onto the letterform she leans on — the only shadow in the flat graphic world. Color story: [Brazil Green], slab black, gold.

Beneath the giant word, small tracked capitals: the invented tagline "Story of a Talent". Camera feel: 50mm flat-on graphic framing, the type and the person one machine.

Grooming committed: thick hair styled with real volume and a polished finish. Person head sized naturally against her shoulders — head and body in true natural proportion, exactly as a real camera would capture person. The person is a true photograph inside the designed poster, not an illustration or 3D render, carrying subtle fine film grain and true skin texture with visible pores. At the poster's base runs a classic thin CREDIT BLOCK rendered as pure unreadable greeked texture — tight rows of tiny compressed marks that suggest names without containing a single legible letter. Vertical 3:4 movie-poster frame. Besides the title, the single tagline and the greeked credit texture, no other text — no real names, no logos, no brands anywhere. no logos, no brands.
```

## KEY COMPONENTS BREAKDOWN

### Face/Identity Lock

- **Priority:** Face is the poster's product — most important element
- **Accuracy:** Exact features from reference photo (hairline, brows, eyes, nose, lips, jaw, cheekbone, skin tone)
- **Recognition:** Instantly recognizable to anyone who knows the person
- **No drift:** No stylization, no averaging, no alteration
- **Only replace:** Lighting, background, and clothing from reference

### Typography Integration

- **Main Title:** "PRINCE" in massive slab-serif black capitals
- **Placement:** Middle third of poster
- **Size:** Colossal — dominates composition
- **Physical Integration:** Subject woven through letterforms
  - Leaning against letter P's upright like a wall
  - One ankle crossed, one elbow hooked over P's top serif
  - Body in front of some strokes, behind others (dimensional interplay)

### Expression & Pose

- **Expression:** "Smirk of a person leaning on own myth"
- **Eye contact:** Direct to lens
- **Detail:** One brow raised
- **Mood:** Amused, confident, relaxed
- **Physicality:** Leaning back easily, casual crossed ankle

### Lighting

- **Key light:** Clean soft key from upper-left
- **Quality:** Models subject honestly
- **Shadow:** Crisp small shadow cast onto letterform (only shadow in flat graphic)
- **Consistency:** Photographic lighting on subject, flat graphics everywhere else

### Color Palette

- **Background:** Brazil Green (flat field)
- **Typography:** Slab black
- **Accents:** Gold
- **Overall:** Bold, iconic, minimal

### Additional Text Elements

- **Tagline:** "Story of a Talent" in small tracked capitals beneath giant word
- **Credit Block:** Thin block at poster base
  - Pure unreadable greeked texture
  - Tight rows of tiny compressed marks
  - Suggests names without legible letters
  - Classic movie-poster treatment

### Technical Quality

- **Realism:** True photograph, NOT illustration or 3D render
- **Texture:** Subtle fine film grain, true skin texture with visible pores
- **Proportions:** Natural head-to-shoulder ratio (exactly as real camera captures)
- **Grooming:** Thick hair with real volume and polished finish
- **Framing:** 50mm flat-on graphic framing
- **Format:** Vertical 3:4 movie-poster frame

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Hierarchy

1. **Face** — Most important (product)
2. **Typography** — Dominates composition
3. **Integration** — Dimensional interplay
4. **Color** — Bold, minimal palette
5. **Details** — Credit block, tagline (supporting)

### Dimensional Layering

- **Flat graphic world:** Background color, typography, credit block
- **Photographic element:** Subject only
- **Integration point:** Shadow from subject onto letterform bridges both worlds
- **Depth cue:** Subject in front of/behind different letter strokes

### Authenticity Balance

- **Photographic realism:** Face, body, texture, grain
- **Graphic design:** Typography, color field, credit greeking
- **Hybrid:** Both coexist without either compromising the other

## CONSTRAINTS & NEGATIVES

### Critical Negatives

```
illustration, 3D render, digital painting, cartoon, anime, stylized face, altered features, wrong person, face drift, averaged features, smoothed skin, beauty filter, plastic skin, waxy face, incorrect proportions, oversized head, undersized head, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, missing fingers, legible text in credit block, real names, visible logos, brand names, company logos, sponsor logos, excessive text, multiple taglines, cluttered composition, muddy colors, gradient backgrounds, textured backgrounds, photographic background, multiple light sources, hard shadows everywhere, rim lighting, dramatic backlighting, lens flares, motion blur, tilted frame, portrait orientation mistakes
```

### Must Maintain

- Exact face recognition from reference
- Natural head-to-body proportions
- Photographic realism on subject only
- Flat graphic design elements
- Clean integration between photo and graphics
- Unreadable greeked credit block texture
- Minimal text (title, tagline, credit texture only)

## USAGE NOTES

### Reference Photo Requirements

- **Quality:** High resolution, clear facial features
- **Angle:** Front or three-quarter view preferred
- **Lighting:** Well-lit face (original lighting will be replaced)
- **Expression:** Any (will be replaced with specified expression)
- **Clothing:** Any (can be kept or replaced)

### Best Practices

1. **Generate face reference first** if using multi-image platforms
2. **Emphasize identity lock** in prompt to prevent drift
3. **Test with different letters** if name doesn't start with P (adjust pose description)
4. **Maintain 3:4 aspect ratio** for classic poster proportions
5. **Iterate on integration** if subject appears fully in front or fully behind letters

### Common Issues & Fixes

**Issue: Face doesn't match reference**

- Strengthen identity lock language
- Use platform-specific face-matching tools (IP-Adapter, etc.)
- Add negative: "face drift, averaged features, wrong person"

**Issue: Subject appears illustrated, not photographic**

- Emphasize: "true photograph, NOT illustration"
- Add: "subtle film grain, skin texture with visible pores"
- Negative: "illustration, 3D render, digital painting, stylized"

**Issue: Credit block is legible**

- Emphasize: "pure unreadable greeked texture"
- Add: "suggest names without containing a single legible letter"
- Negative: "legible text, readable names"

**Issue: Head proportions wrong**

- Emphasize: "head sized naturally against shoulders, true natural proportion"
- Add: "exactly as a real camera would capture"
- Negative: "oversized head, undersized head, distorted proportions"

**Issue: Integration unclear (subject fully in front or behind)**

- Specify: "body in front of some strokes, behind others"
- Describe: "leaning against letter [X]'s upright, elbow hooked over top serif"
- Add: "dimensional interplay, woven through letterforms"
