## Base Prompt

_Use this first before any style-specific prompt_

```
Analyze my uploaded reference image carefully.

Preserve the athlete exactly as shown.

Keep the same identity, jersey, facial features, hairstyle, body proportions,
pose, action, camera angle, framing, environment, stadium, crowd, ball, goalposts,
field markings, composition and perspective.

Do not redesign the scene.

Only upgrade the cinematic lighting, atmosphere, weather effects, stadium
ambience, color grading, depth, realism and professional sports photography quality.

Everything must remain ultra realistic and physically accurate.
```

### What This Does

- Locks down the original athlete's identity and appearance
- Preserves composition, pose, and action
- Allows only lighting, atmosphere, and quality enhancements
- Prevents AI from redesigning or altering the scene

## Troubleshooting

### AI Changes Athlete's Face/Identity

**Problem**: Generated image doesn't look like original person  
**Solution**:

- Re-emphasize in base prompt: "Preserve the athlete exactly as shown. Keep the same identity, facial features"
- Add: "Do not alter the person's face, identity, or appearance"

### Composition Gets Altered

**Problem**: Pose, angle, or framing changes  
**Solution**:

- Use base prompt first
- Add to style prompt: "Do not alter composition, pose, camera angle, or framing"
- Emphasize: "Keep original positioning unchanged"

### Too Dark or Over-Lit

**Problem**: Lighting too dramatic or unnatural  
**Solution**:

- Adjust prompt: "Balanced cinematic lighting" instead of "dramatic"
- Add: "Natural stadium lighting, realistic broadcast quality"
- Use Sports Portrait prompt for softer lighting

### Looks CGI or Fake

**Problem**: Result appears artificial or over-processed  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Ultra realistic," "photorealistic," "authentic broadcast photography"
- Add: "Real photograph quality, no CGI, no artificial effects"
- Use Universal Refinement Prompt for realism boost

### Rain/Weather Too Intense

**Problem**: Rain effects overwhelming or unrealistic  
**Solution**:

- Change "heavy rainfall" to "subtle rain mist" or "light rain"
- Add: "Realistic weather effects, not overdone"

### Background/Stadium Wrong

**Problem**: Stadium or setting doesn't look right  
**Solution**:

- Specify in prompt: "Realistic football stadium background"
- Add: "Authentic stadium architecture, real crowd atmosphere"
- For Training to Match: Be more specific about stadium type
