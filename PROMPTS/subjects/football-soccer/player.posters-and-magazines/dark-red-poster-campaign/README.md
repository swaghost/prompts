# Dark Red Athletic Poster & Stadium Campaign

Four football-inspired athletic poster prompts built around identity-consistent portrait references, crimson stadium lighting, black sportswear, gritty smoke, embers, and distressed motivational typography.

## Shared Identity Anchor

Use a clear front-facing reference photo and preserve the exact facial structure, eye shape, nose contours, skin texture, and identity of the person. Do not alter their face. Place the person into the chosen high-intensity athletic campaign poster.

## Visual Language

- **Palette:** Crimson red (#E50914 / #C8102E), pitch black, smoke grey, and restrained white or warm key light
- **Lighting:** Harsh red edge rim or kicker light with low-key directional key light
- **Setting:** Night football stadium, atmospheric haze, gritty graphic studio backdrops
- **Wardrobe:** Black compression gear, zip-up training tops, and black football jerseys with subtle red details
- **Typography:** Ultra-condensed distressed sans-serif lettering behind the subject
- **Output:** Portrait-oriented poster composition; 4:5 is recommended for the alternative setup

## Production Notes

- For Midjourney or Flux.1: `--ar 4:5 --style raw --cref [IMAGE_URL] --cw 15 --iw 2.0`
- Suggested display fonts: Impact, Bebas Neue, Druk Wide Heavy, Teko, or Anton
- Place background typography behind the subject and apply a grunge or concrete texture mask
- Deep blood red typography: #C8102E; foreground serif copy: #A00E17
