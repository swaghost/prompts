# Master Guide - Dark Red Athletic Poster and Stadium Campaign

## Overview

A football-inspired sports poster series for identity-consistent AI portraits. The campaign uses a high-contrast crimson-red palette against pitch black, gritty smoke, floating embers, and textured volumetric backlighting. The subject wears black athletic clothing while oversized distressed typography turns each image into a motivational poster.

## Workflow

1. Upload a clear, front-facing reference photo of the face or model.
2. Add the shared identity anchor from the campaign README.
3. Choose one of the four scene prompts in this folder.
4. For Midjourney or Flux.1, use the alternative setup below.
5. Add or refine poster typography in Canva or Photoshop when exact lettering is important.

## Midjourney / Flux.1 Alternative Setup

```text
[IMAGE_URL] Dramatic high-intensity athletic poster of [SUBJECT], wearing black sportswear, fiery crimson red volumetric smoke and stadium backlighting, intense red rim light, sweat texture, distressed red typography in background, commercial sports campaign, 85mm f/1.8 --ar 4:5 --style raw --cref [IMAGE_URL] --cw 15 --iw 2.0
```

- `--cref [URL]` locks facial identity from the uploaded photo.
- `--cw 15` preserves facial features while allowing athletic styling and red lighting.
- `--style raw` preserves sweat and gritty textures.

## Typography Layout

- Use Impact, Bebas Neue, Druk Wide Heavy, Teko, or Anton for background slogans.
- Place the typography behind the cut-out athlete and scale it from the shoulders toward the top edge.
- Use deep blood red (#C8102E) with Multiply or Overlay and a grunge/concrete texture mask.
- For small foreground copy, use a high-contrast serif such as Playfair Display or Editorial New in deep crimson red (#A00E17).

## Shared Negative Direction

Avoid altered identity, duplicate people, bad anatomy, deformed hands, plastic skin, excessive smoothing, flat lighting, unreadable or misspelled typography, weak contrast, washed-out reds, cartoon or CGI rendering, logos, watermarks, and unrelated background clutter.
