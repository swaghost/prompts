## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Jersey looks cropped, tied, or fashion-styled

**Solution:**

- Emphasize authentic fit: "full authentic jersey, proper length to mid-thigh, realistic fan clothing fit, NOT cropped, NOT tied, NOT fashion oversized"
- Add: "jersey as worn by real fans at matches, authentic supporter clothing"

### Problem: Reference face not preserved accurately

**Solution:**

- For Midjourney: Strengthen --cref weight, use --cref with higher influence
- For DALL-E: "preserve exact facial identity 100% from uploaded reference, same face shape, eyes, nose, all features"
- Add: "do NOT beautify, do NOT alter features, maintain authentic real person identity"

### Problem: Famous player unrecognizable

**Solution:**

- Be specific: "instantly recognizable as [PLAYER NAME], accurate facial features, distinctive hairstyle"
- Add reference: "[PLAYER] as he appears in 2024-2026, current appearance"

### Problem: Background too busy or stripes too bright

**Solution:**

- Simplify: "clean minimalist background, stripes subtle soft glow NOT overpowering, black background dominant"
- Control opacity: "faded flag watermark 15-25% opacity barely visible, subtle texture only"

### Problem: Wrong team colors or flag colors

**Solution:**

- Be explicit: "Argentina = sky blue-white stripes, flag stripes sky blue/white/sky blue" (specify exact colors for chosen team)
- Verify: Double-check official national team colors before generation

### Problem: Composition not symmetrical or balanced

**Solution:**

- Strengthen composition: "centered vertically and horizontally, balanced symmetrical composition, both subjects equal height in frame"
- Add: "perfect symmetry, professional alignment, clean minimalist layout"
