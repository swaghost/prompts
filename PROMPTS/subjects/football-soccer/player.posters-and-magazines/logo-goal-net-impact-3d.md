# 3D Logo Goal-Net Impact

## Description of Resulting Image

A hyper-realistic sports hero image where an uploaded logo is transformed into a premium 3D object and captured at the exact moment it slams into a professional football goal net. The logo keeps its exact brand identity while showing realistic net tension, compression, and contact physics.

## Image Prompt

### Subject and Prerequisites

Upload the logo and preserve its exact identity.

Transform the uploaded logo into a realistic high-end 3D object that has just been kicked at extreme speed into a professional football goal net. Preserve the uploaded logo's exact shape, colors, proportions, typography, symbols, and brand identity. Convert it into premium glossy 3D geometry with beveled edges, realistic thickness, reflective material, subtle scratches, and physical weight.

Capture the exact moment of impact from inside the goal. The football net must be in the foreground and physically wrapped over the logo. The logo is partially behind the net from the camera point of view. Net ropes visibly cross over the front surface, stretch tightly across it, bend around contours, and press into edges. Show deep concave net deformation, realistic rope tension, tight knots, bent fibers, compression marks, shadows, and contact points. Some ropes may be slightly frayed or beginning to tear from force, but the net must remain intact.

Photorealistic sports-commercial quality, premium materials, clean brand readability, no text overlays, no watermark. Aspect ratio 9:16.
