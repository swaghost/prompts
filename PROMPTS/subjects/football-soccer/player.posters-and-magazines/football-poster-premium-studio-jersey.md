## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Face doesn't match reference image

**Solution:**

- Strengthen identity preservation language: "preserve EXACT facial identity, same bone structure, maintain all distinctive features"
- For Midjourney: Ensure --cref parameter is used correctly with reference URL
- For DALL-E: Upload reference first, explicitly state "maintain 100% facial identity"
- Add negative: "no altered face, no different person, no beautified features"

### Problem: Jersey looks too tight or athletic-fit instead of oversized

**Solution:**

- Emphasize sizing: "oversized modern fit, relaxed contemporary streetwear style, shoulder seams dropped"
- Add: "not tight athletic fit, loose comfortable modern jersey, trendy oversized aesthetic"

### Problem: Background too busy or design elements cluttered

**Solution:**

- Simplify: "clean minimal background, subtle design elements, NOT cluttered, premium minimal aesthetic"
- Specify: "thin frame lines only, small technical labels in corners, faint pitch markings barely visible"
- Add negative: "no busy background, no cluttered composition, no messy layout"

### Problem: Lighting too flat or too dramatic

**Solution:**

- Balance lighting description: "soft commercial lighting, NOT flat, NOT overly dramatic, balanced professional"
- Add: "slightly directional light creating natural shadows, dimensional depth, product photography standard"

### Problem: Looks too edited or artificial

**Solution:**

- Emphasize natural realism: "natural skin texture with visible pores, realistic jersey fabric, authentic material rendering"
- Add: "photorealistic, NOT over-processed, NOT fake AI render, professional photography realism"
- Include negative: "no plastic skin, no over-airbrushed, no unrealistic clarity"

### Problem: Design elements too prominent

**Solution:**

- Reduce prominence: "subtle editorial elements, minimal typography, faint pitch markings, enhance without cluttering"
- Specify: "design elements support composition, don't compete with subject, clean visual hierarchy"
