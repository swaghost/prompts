# Logo Goal - 3D Brand Impact in Football Net

## Description

Transform any uploaded logo into a realistic high-end 3D object that has been kicked at extreme speed into a professional football goal net. Preserves exact logo shape, colors, proportions, typography, and brand identity while converting to premium glossy 3D geometry with beveled edges, realistic thickness, reflective materials, and physical weight. Critical feature: football net in foreground physically wrapped over and around the logo, creating authentic impact moment with net ropes crossing over logo surface, stretching tightly, bending around contours, and pressing into edges. Deep concave net pocket from impact, realistic rope tension, compression marks, shadows, and contact points. Dramatic lighting from stadium environment with motion blur, flying grass particles, and explosive goal-scoring energy. Perfect for brand activations, sports marketing campaigns, team logo showcases, and high-impact visual storytelling.

## Image Prompt

**Logo Preservation (Critical):**
Transform the uploaded logo into premium 3D object while preserving EXACT logo design: same shape, proportions, colors (match precisely), typography (fonts, text, letter-spacing), symbols, icons, brand elements, visual identity. Logo should be instantly recognizable as the original brand - do NOT alter design, do NOT change colors, do NOT modify proportions. Only conversion to 3D geometry and material properties, keeping all visual brand identity intact.

**3D Logo Characteristics:**
Logo converted to high-end glossy 3D geometry. Physical thickness: 20-40cm implied depth (substantial but not cartoonish). Beveled edges creating professional product-like appearance. Surface material: premium glossy reflective finish (plastic, metal, or coated material aesthetic) showing subtle environmental reflections, light catching edges, professional render quality. Surface has subtle realistic imperfections: minor scratches, slight wear marks, light scuff marks suggesting real physical object that has been kicked with force. Color saturation rich and accurate to brand. Overall look: premium brand-quality 3D logo object with physical presence and weight.

**Net Interaction - CRITICAL DETAIL:**
Football goal net is in the FOREGROUND and physically wrapped over and around the logo. The logo is partially behind the net from camera's point of view. Net ropes must visibly cross over the front surface of the logo, stretch tightly across it, bend around the logo's contours, and press into the logo edges.

Specific net behavior: Net forms deep concave pocket around logo from impact force - net is pushed backward into goal creating dramatic curved depression with logo at deepest point. Net ropes stretch tight across logo surface (viewer sees ropes crossing over logo face from foreground to background). Net bends and wraps around logo edges and beveled contours. Where net contacts logo surface: visible compression, rope fibers pressing into logo, tension points, shadows cast by net ropes onto logo surface, contact marks.

Net knots pulled tight from tension. Net fibers bending realistically under stress. Some rope sections stretched thinner from force. Possible slight fraying on few fibers (net nearly tearing but intact) suggesting extreme impact force. Net color: white or light gray traditional goal net. Net should look authentic - real rope fiber texture, realistic knot details, natural wear, professional football goal netting.

CRITICAL: Logo must look trapped INSIDE net, not floating in front of it, not pasted on top. Net is physical object in foreground interacting with logo behind/within it.

**Moment Captured:**
The exact instant after logo has impacted goal net from inside at extreme speed. Logo has just struck net from behind (coming from pitch side) and net is wrapped around it from force. Motion frozen at peak impact moment - net compressed deeply, logo settled into net pocket, but still sense of explosive energy. Some motion blur on logo edges suggesting extreme velocity. Flying grass particles, dirt, and dust clouds still in air around impact zone (particles launched from force of kick). Dramatic frozen action moment.

**Stadium Environment:**
Professional football pitch visible behind/through net. Green grass (realistic texture, individual blades visible near camera, softer in distance), white goal line markings, pitch extending into background. Stadium stands visible in far background (soft focus) with crowd or empty seats. Stadium floodlights or natural stadium lighting creating dramatic atmosphere. Sky visible (evening golden light, overcast dramatic clouds, or night sky with stadium lights) adding cinematic quality. Goal post structure (white metal posts) partially visible at sides of frame. Premium professional stadium environment.

**Lighting:**
Dramatic cinematic sports lighting. Strong directional light from above and/or side (stadium floodlights or natural light) creating bold highlights on glossy logo surface and casting defined shadows. Reflections on logo: environmental reflections showing stadium, sky, grass (adds realism). Shadows from net ropes cast onto logo surface creating dimensional depth. Light catching net fibers from behind creating slight backlit glow on some rope sections. Atmospheric volumetric lighting with visible light rays or haze (subtle). High-contrast dramatic lighting enhancing impact energy. Professional commercial photography lighting quality.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Logo looks flat or just overlaid on net

**Solution:**

- Emphasize 3D conversion: "premium glossy 3D geometry with realistic thickness 30cm, beveled edges, physical depth"
- Strengthen net interaction: "net physically wrapped OVER logo, ropes crossing over logo surface, logo behind net"
- Add negative: "no flat 2D logo, no logo pasted on top, no logo floating in front"

### Problem: Net not interacting realistically with logo

**Solution:**

- Be extremely explicit: "net in FOREGROUND wrapping over logo, net ropes visible crossing over front of logo surface, logo partially behind net"
- Add physics detail: "net stretched tight, bending around logo contours, pressing into edges, deep concave pocket"
- Emphasize contact: "compression marks where net touches logo, shadows from net ropes on logo, tension points visible"

### Problem: Logo colors or design changed

**Solution:**

- Strengthen preservation: "maintain EXACT logo colors, shapes, proportions, typography from uploaded image"
- Add: "logo instantly recognizable as original brand, do NOT alter design or change colors"
- For platforms: Upload high-quality clean logo file with transparent background

### Problem: Net looks destroyed or unrealistic

**Solution:**

- Balance damage: "some rope fibers slightly frayed from force, but net remains intact, NOT completely destroyed"
- Add: "realistic goal net, authentic rope fiber texture, natural wear, professional football netting"

### Problem: Lighting too flat or not dramatic enough

**Solution:**

- Strengthen drama: "dramatic cinematic sports lighting, strong directional from above and side, bold shadows and highlights"
- Add: "volumetric atmospheric lighting, light catching net fibers, reflections on glossy logo surface"

### Problem: Composition cluttered or logo not focal point

**Solution:**

- Simplify: "clean composition, logo as clear focal point, balanced negative space, professional commercial framing"
- Add: "logo branding remains visible despite action chaos, space for text overlay if needed"
