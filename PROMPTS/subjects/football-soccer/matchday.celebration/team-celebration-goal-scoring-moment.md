## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Celebration looks staged or unnatural

**Solution:**

- Emphasize authenticity: "genuine spontaneous celebration, NOT staged, NOT posed, natural mid-action moment"
- Add: "captured documentary style, real emotion, authentic football celebration"
- Strengthen emotion: "intense expression, open mouth shouting, eyes wide with joy, genuine adrenaline"

### Problem: Reference face not preserved or altered

**Solution:**

- For Midjourney: Use --cref with higher weight
- For DALL-E: "preserve exact facial identity 100%, same face shape, eyes, all features, do NOT beautify"
- Add: "maintain authentic real person identity, natural appearance"

### Problem: Teammates look identical or unrealistic

**Solution:**

- Emphasize variety: "teammates in varied poses, different heights, natural diversity, authentic spontaneous reactions"
- Add: "NOT identical poses, NOT clones, believable different players"

### Problem: No motion or energy in photo

**Solution:**

- Strengthen action: "frozen mid-motion, drops of sweat in air, grass particles flying, motion blur on backgrounds"
- Add: "dynamic frozen energy, viewer should feel the movement and intensity"

### Problem: Lighting too flat

**Solution:**

- Boost drama: "dramatic stadium floodlights, strong directional lighting, high contrast, bold highlights and shadows"
- Add: "backlit sweat droplets catching light, professional sports photography lighting"

### Problem: Background too sharp or distracting

**Solution:**

- Control DOF: "shallow depth of field f/1.4, subject razor-sharp, teammates soft focus, crowd bokeh blur"
- Add: "85mm f/1.4 lens characteristic, selective focus, professional portrait DOF"
