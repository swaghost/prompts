## Troubleshooting

### Celebration Emotion Lost

**Problem**: Enhancement makes pose stiff or less emotional  
**Solution**:

- Strongly emphasize: "Preserve the original celebration pose exactly"
- Add: "Maintain authentic celebration expression and body language"
- Use Base Prompt to lock down pose

### Rain Overwhelms Scene

**Problem**: Dramatic rain becomes too heavy  
**Solution**:

- Change "dramatic rain particles" to "subtle rain particles"
- Add: "Light atmospheric rain, not heavy downpour"

### Lens Bloom Too Strong

**Problem**: Subtle lens bloom becomes hazy glow  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Very subtle lens bloom effect"
- Add: "Minimal glow, maintaining sharp detail"

### Colors Too Saturated

**Problem**: Premium grading becomes oversaturated  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Balanced color saturation, premium but natural"
- Change "warm golden" to "warm stadium lights"

### Lost Facial Expression

**Problem**: Face detail diminishes in enhancement  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Powerful facial highlights" and "ultra detailed realism"
- Add: "Sharp focus on celebration expression"
