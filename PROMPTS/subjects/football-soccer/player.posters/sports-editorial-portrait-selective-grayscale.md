# Sports Editorial Portrait – Selective Grayscale with Team Colors

## Description

A hyper-realistic vertical sports editorial portrait style featuring athletes in explosive celebratory moments with dramatic selective color treatment. The subject, body, and environment are rendered in highly detailed grayscale with crisp charcoal-like tonal texture, strong contrast, and bright studio highlights, while only the team jersey, shorts accents, and a single oversized curved athletic swoosh remain in authentic club colors. Features identity-accurate facial structure preservation, powerful athletic poses with defined musculature, clean centered poster composition against seamless pure-white studio backgrounds, and premium campaign-quality lighting. Emphasizes raw emotional intensity through open-mouth roars, clenched fists, jersey-gripping gestures, and explosive body language. Creates striking visual impact through grayscale-to-color contrast, generous negative space, and editorial polish suitable for sports marketing campaigns, athlete portfolio covers, magazine features, and premium promotional materials. Maintains authentic kit details including visible club crests and shirt numbers without invented text or promotional overlays.

## Usage

Perfect for professional athlete portfolio covers, sports marketing campaign visuals, magazine editorial features, poster and print merchandise, social media hero graphics, athlete brand partnership materials, club promotional campaigns, match-day program covers, stadium display graphics, sports memorabilia designs, athlete testimonial headers, recruitment and signing announcements, player-of-the-match graphics, season highlights covers, sports documentary key art, athlete biography covers, premium trading card designs, sports agency portfolio showcases, club website hero images, mobile app splash screens, jersey launch campaign visuals, athlete achievement celebrations, hall of fame presentations, sports exhibition graphics, motivational poster series, gym and training facility décor, sports journalism feature images, and any premium sports visual requiring dramatic emotional impact with sophisticated selective color treatment.

## Prerequisites

- **AI Image Platform**: Midjourney v6.1/v7 (excellent for photorealism + selective color), DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion XL with sports/portrait models, Leonardo AI PhotoReal
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 vertical (native sports portrait), 4:5 vertical (social media), 9:16 (mobile/stories)
- **Style Mode**: Hyper-realistic sports photography with selective grayscale treatment
- **Reference Image**: Identity reference photo of athlete (facial structure, proportions, hairstyle)
- **Team Information**: Authentic club colors, crest design, kit details
- **Resolution**: High resolution minimum 2K vertical for print quality and detail visibility

## Technical Specifications

- **Portrait Orientation**: Vertical 3:4 ratio (standard editorial)
- **Framing**: Waist-up to upper-thigh composition
- **Background**: Seamless pure-white studio (no texture, gradient, or environment)
- **Color Treatment**: Selective grayscale (body/skin/scene) + full color (jersey/shorts/swoosh only)
- **Grayscale Style**: Charcoal-like tonal texture, high contrast, bright studio highlights
- **Pose**: Explosive celebration (roar, fist clench, jersey grip, arms raised)
- **Expression**: Powerful intensity (open mouth roar, fierce determination)
- **Lighting**: Premium studio campaign lighting (bright, high-key, professional)
- **Anatomy**: Sharply defined muscles (neck, arms, torso), realistic proportions
- **Composition**: Clean centered poster layout with generous negative space
- **Authenticity**: Preserve real club crest, shirt number, kit design (no invented text)

- Include in prompt: "Use uploaded reference image as identity reference"
- Specify: "Preserve recognizable facial structure, proportions, hairstyle and expression of subject"

```
Use the uploaded reference image as [FOOTBALLER NAME]'s identity reference. Preserve the recognizable facial structure, bone structure, facial proportions, hairstyle, hairline, and natural expression of the subject. Maintain identity likeness while showing celebration intensity.
```

**Without Reference Image:**

- Use well-known athlete name: "Cristiano Ronaldo," "Lionel Messi," "Kylian Mbappé"

- Bone structure (jawline, cheekbones, brow ridge)

- Maintain recognizable identity while showing intense celebration

- Natural emotion that fits athlete's personality
- Preserve characteristic expression style if known

## Selective Color Treatment Specifications

### Grayscale Rendering (Body, Skin, Face, Environment)

**Elements in Grayscale:**

- Player's face and facial features
- All visible skin (arms, neck, torso, legs, hands)
- Hair and eyebrows
- Background (pure white, but any shadows or elements in grayscale)
- Atmospheric elements
- Sweat, skin texture, and body details

**Grayscale Quality:**

- **Charcoal-like tonal texture**: Rich textural quality resembling charcoal or graphite drawing
- **Strong contrast**: Deep blacks, bright whites, full tonal range
- **Bright studio highlights**: Specular highlights on skin, muscle definition emphasis
- **Defined shadows**: Clear shadow definition showing anatomy and form
- **Crisp detail**: Sharp photographic detail in monochrome
- **Premium finish**: High-end editorial retouching quality

**Tonal Range:**

- Deep blacks in shadows (not pure black—retain detail)
- Bright whites in highlights (studio lighting creating luminosity)
- Full middle gray range showing skin tone variation
- Contrast creates drama and dimension
- Highlight muscle definition through light and shadow

### Color Rendering (Jersey, Shorts, Swoosh Only)

**Elements in Full Color:**

- **Authentic team jersey**: Shirt fabric, sleeves, collar, entire top
- **Shorts visible accents**: Waistband, stripes, any visible shorts details
- **Single curved athletic swoosh**: Large oversized graphic element behind player

**Color Quality:**

- Vibrant saturated club colors (not muted or desaturated)
- Accurate color representation of team identity
- Realistic fabric color with appropriate shading and highlights
- Color creates focal point and visual impact against grayscale
- Clean color separation (no color bleeding into grayscale areas)

## Authentic Kit Details Specifications

### Club Crest

**Placement & Rendering:**

- Positioned on left chest (player's right side from viewer perspective)
- Accurate club badge design with correct colors, shape, and elements
- Realistic embroidered or printed appearance on jersey fabric
- Appropriate size (standard badge proportion to jersey)
- Sharp detail showing crest elements

**Authenticity:**

- Use actual club crest design (iconic recognizable elements)
- Correct colors within crest even if jersey is simplified
- Do not invent or modify crest design
- If uncertain, simplify to general shield shape with club colors

### Shirt Number

**Placement & Rendering:**

- Centered on chest or upper torso (depending on kit style)
- Appropriate size relative to jersey (not oversized or tiny)
- Accurate typography style for club's numbering system
- Correct color (often white, black, or contrasting club color)
- Realistic printed or heat-pressed appearance on fabric

**Number Selection:**

- Use athlete's actual number if known
- Use iconic numbers (7, 10, 9, 11) if generic
- Include in prompt: "shirt number [NUMBER]"

### Sponsor Logos & Kit Details (Optional)

**If Including:**

- Chest sponsor logo (main jersey sponsor)
- Sleeve sponsors (if applicable)
- Manufacturer logo (Nike, Adidas, Puma, etc.)
- All logos accurate to actual kit design

**If Simplifying:**

- Omit sponsor logos for cleaner aesthetic
- Focus on crest and number only
- Prompt: "Minimal kit details, show crest and number only, no sponsor logos"

**Important:**

- Do not invent fake sponsor names or text
- If unsure of sponsor, omit entirely
- Keep kit authentic to club identity

## Studio Background & Composition

### Background Specifications

**Pure White Studio:**

- Seamless white backdrop (cyclorama wall style)
- No texture, no gradient, no variation
- Clean professional studio environment
- Pure white (#FFFFFF or near-white)
- No shadows on background (high-key lighting eliminates background shadows)

**What to Avoid:**

- Stadium or field environments
- Textured backgrounds (concrete, paper, fabric)
- Colored backgrounds
- Gradient backgrounds
- Environmental elements (clouds, abstract shapes beyond single swoosh)

### Composition Layout

**Centered Poster Style:**

- Subject centered in frame (vertical axis)
- Balanced left-right composition
- Vertical format emphasis
- Professional poster aesthetic

**Negative Space:**

- Generous white space above head (breathing room)
- Space on sides allowing figure to breathe
- Clean uncluttered layout
- Premium editorial spacing

**Framing:**

- Waist-up to upper-thigh (not full body, not tight crop)
- Head not cut off at top
- Slight space above head
- Bottom of frame at mid-thigh or upper leg

**Rule of Thirds (Optional):**

- Face positioned at upper third intersection
- Eyes at upper horizontal third line
- Dynamic placement while maintaining centered feel

## Lighting Specifications

### Premium Campaign Lighting

**Studio Setup:**

- High-key bright lighting (professional sports campaign standard)
- Multiple light sources creating even illumination
- Strong key light creating highlights and definition
- Fill lights reducing harsh shadows
- Rim or hair light separating subject from background

**Characteristics:**

- Bright professional appearance
- Clean commercial lighting quality
- Highlights emphasize muscle definition
- Shadows define anatomy without going too dark
- Editorial retouching quality

### Light Direction & Quality

**Key Light:**

- Primary light source from front-above angle
- Creates highlights on face, shoulders, arms
- Defines facial structure and muscle tone
- Bright intensity for high-key look

**Fill Light:**

- Reduces shadow depth
- Maintains detail in shadow areas
- Softer than key light
- Balances overall exposure

**Rim/Hair Light (Optional):**

- Backlight creating edge highlight
- Separates subject from white background
- Adds dimension and depth
- Creates luminous edge on shoulders or hair

### Highlight & Shadow Quality

**Highlights:**

- Bright specular highlights on skin
- Show sweat or skin sheen (optional)
- Create dimension and form
- Enhance muscle definition

**Shadows:**

- Defined but not harsh
- Retain detail (not pure black)
- Show anatomy (neck muscles, arm definition)
- Natural shadow falloff

## Pro Tips

### Achieving Perfect Selective Color Separation

**Clarity in Prompt:**

- Use "ONLY" and "exclusively" when specifying what should be in color
- Enumerate grayscale elements: "Player's skin, face, arms, hair, ALL in grayscale"
- Enumerate color elements: "ONLY jersey, shorts accents, and swoosh in [color]"
- Include both positive (what to do) and negative (what not to do) instructions

**Prompt Language Example:**

```
CRITICAL COLOR TREATMENT: Render player's skin, face, body, arms, legs, hair in highly detailed grayscale with charcoal texture. Keep ONLY the authentic jersey and curved swoosh in vibrant [club color]. Do not color the face or skin.
```

**Multiple Generations:**

- May require 3-5 attempts to get perfect color separation
- Some platforms handle selective color better than others
- Midjourney and DALL-E 3 generally most reliable

**Post-Processing Option:**

- Generate full-color portrait
- Convert to grayscale in Photoshop
- Mask and restore color only on jersey/shorts/swoosh
- Provides absolute control over separation

### Identity Preservation Mastery

**With Reference Image:**

- Upload clearest possible facial reference
- Include: "Preserve recognizable facial structure and proportions of reference subject"
- Mention specific distinctive features: "maintain strong jawline," "preserve short textured hair"
- Balance likeness with expression: "identity accurate but showing intense celebration"

**Without Reference (Generic):**

- Use well-known player names AI has training data on
- Or provide detailed description: "25-year-old male athlete, strong jaw, short dark hair, athletic build"
- Focus on expression and emotion as primary if identity is generic

**Common Issue: Losing Likeness in Intense Expression:**

- **Solution**: Remind AI to preserve features: "Maintain recognizable facial features despite open-mouth roar"

### Realistic Hands Strategy

**Hands Are Challenging for AI:**

- Explicitly request: "Natural hands with correct finger count, realistic grip"
- Describe hand pose: "Hands gripping jersey fabric, fingers wrapped naturally, five fingers per hand visible"
- Strong negative prompt: "no extra fingers, no deformed hands, no unrealistic hand anatomy"

**Pose Choices to Help:**

- Fists clenched (easier than open hands—less visible fingers)
- Hands gripping fabric (partially obscured fingers reduce error)
- Avoid complex hand gestures with all fingers fully visible

**If Hands Fail:**

- Crop composition higher (chest-up) reducing hand visibility
- Use inpainting to regenerate hands only
- Manual retouching in post-processing

### Grayscale Texture Quality

**Charcoal-Like Tonal Texture:**

- Include "charcoal texture," "graphite quality," "rich tonal range"
- Request "crisp detailed grayscale, not flat gray"
- Mention "strong contrast, deep blacks, bright whites"
- Include "editorial retouching quality monochrome"

**Avoiding Flat Gray:**

- Specify "high contrast"
- Request "bright studio highlights on skin"
- Include "defined shadows showing muscle structure"
- Mention "dimensional monochrome, not flat"

### Platform-Specific Strategies

**For Midjourney Selective Color:**

- Use `--style raw` (reduces artistic interpretation, better color control)
- Lower `--s` value (150-250) for more literal interpretation
- Emphasize "ONLY" and "exclusively" in color specifications
- May need `--no colored skin, colored face` as parameter

**For DALL-E 3:**

- DALL-E excels at following complex instructions
- Provide extremely detailed color treatment description
- Use enumerated lists: "1. Skin in grayscale, 2. Jersey in red, 3. Background white"
- Include "selective color treatment, professional sports photography"

**For Stable Diffusion:**

- Use ControlNet with color mask for guaranteed separation
- Or generate full color, convert to grayscale, restore color on jersey/swoosh manually
- Sports photography LoRA models can help with realism
- Realistic Vision model recommended

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Problem: Entire Image in Color (No Grayscale)**

- **Solution**: Stronger language: "Player's body and face MUST be grayscale, NO colored skin," increase emphasis in prompt

**Problem: Entire Image in Grayscale (No Color)**

- **Solution**: Emphasize color: "Jersey in VIBRANT [color], NOT grayscale, full color fabric"

**Problem: Partial Color on Skin/Face**

- **Solution**: Add negative prompt: "no colored skin, no colored face, no colored arms," regenerate

**Problem: Hands Look Unnatural**

- **Solution**: Simplify hand pose (fists), request "realistic five-finger hands," crop higher, or fix in post

**Problem: Expression Too Subtle**

- **Solution**: Emphasize: "WIDE OPEN mouth, loud powerful roar, intense fierce expression, mouth agape"

**Problem: Muscles Not Defined**

- **Solution**: Request "sharply defined muscles," "studio highlights showing muscle definition," "athletic physique with visible muscle separation"

**Problem: Background Not Pure White**

- **Solution**: Specify "seamless pure white studio background, #FFFFFF, no texture, no gradient," negative: "no colored background"

**Problem: Kit Details Inaccurate**

- **Solution**: Provide reference image of actual kit, specify "authentic [club] crest design, accurate shirt number [#]," or simplify to just colors and crest shape
