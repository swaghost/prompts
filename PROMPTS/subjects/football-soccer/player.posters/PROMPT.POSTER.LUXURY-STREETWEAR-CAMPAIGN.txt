## FACE IDENTITY PRESERVATION (CRITICAL)

The player's face MUST be generated from the user's uploaded reference photo and preserve with maximum realism:

* Exact facial structure
* Eye shape and spacing
* Nose anatomy
* Jawline and cheekbones
* Lips and facial proportions
* Skin tone and texture
* Hairline and hairstyle
* Beard details (if present)
* Overall identity recognition

### CRITICAL FACE RULES

* The face MUST remain identical to the uploaded reference
* Do NOT redesign or beautify the face
* Do NOT stylize facial anatomy
* Do NOT alter ethnicity
* Do NOT change facial proportions
* The final result should look exactly like the same real person professionally photographed for a luxury fashion campaign
