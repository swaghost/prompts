## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**9:16 Vertical (Primary):**

- Best for portrait photography
- Instagram/Facebook Stories ideal
- Mobile-first social content
- Emphasizes vertical face framing

**4:5 Portrait (Instagram):**

- Instagram feed portrait format
- Slightly wider than Stories
- Good balance for portraits
- Social media optimized

**1:1 Square:**

- Classic portrait square
- Instagram feed versatile
- Balanced composition
- Universal platform compatibility

**16:9 Horizontal:**

- Environmental context included
- Side-by-side dual portraits
- Landscape portrait style
- More background visible

**Recommendation:** 9:16 vertical for close-up portraits, 4:5 for social media posts

### Portrait Composition Guidelines

**Tight Headshot:**

- Face fills entire frame
- Maximum emotional impact
- Face paint details visible
- Intimate connection

**Classic Portrait (Chest Up):**

- Head and shoulders visible
- Shows jersey/colors
- Balanced standard portrait
- Versatile framing

**Environmental Portrait:**

- Subject in context
- Stadium/crowd visible but blurred
- Story beyond just face
- Documentary context

**Side Profile:**

- Face from side angle
- Artistic aesthetic
- Face paint design visible
- Different perspective

**Direct Eye Contact:**

- Subject looking at camera
- Confident engagement
- Strong connection with viewer
- Powerful presence

**Looking Away:**

- Watching match
- Authentic candid moment
- Documentary observational
- Natural behavior

**Recommendation:** Tight headshot for maximum emotional impact, chest-up for versatility

---

### Depth of Field for Portraits

**Shallow (f/1.4-f/2.8):**

- Face in sharp focus
- Background beautifully blurred
- Subject isolated
- Professional portrait aesthetic
- Best for close-ups

**Medium (f/4-f/5.6):**

- Face and immediate context sharp
- Background still soft
- Balanced depth
- Versatile approach

**Deep (f/8+):**

- More environment in focus
- Context visible and sharp
- Documentary wide scene
- Less isolation

**Recommendation:** Shallow depth of field (f/1.8-f/2.8) for portrait isolation and professional look

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final fan portrait image:

- [ ] Face in sharp focus, good detail visible
- [ ] Face paint clearly visible and realistic
- [ ] National colors identifiable
- [ ] Expression authentic and emotional
- [ ] Background appropriately blurred (if close-up)
- [ ] Lighting flattering and appropriate
- [ ] Photorealistic quality, not illustrated
- [ ] No fake or digitally-added face paint
- [ ] Skin texture realistic, not over-smoothed
- [ ] Eyes sharp and in focus
- [ ] Composition strong and engaging
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
