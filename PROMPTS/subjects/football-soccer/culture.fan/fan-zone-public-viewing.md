# Fan Zone & Public Viewing - Fan Culture Image Prompts

## Overview

Create vibrant fan zone and public viewing photography showcasing massive crowds, outdoor screens, carnival atmosphere, and collective watching experiences. Features photorealistic crowd photography, festival energy, and documentary-style supporter celebrations.

**Part of:** [Fan Culture Collection](PROMPTS.CULTURE.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Fan Zone Marketing** - Promote official public viewing areas
- **Social Media Content** - Instagram and Twitter crowd atmosphere
- **Tournament Promotion** - Show fan engagement and energy
- **Documentary Content** - Behind-the-scenes supporter stories
- **Broadcast B-Roll** - TV show backgrounds and crowd shots
- **City Marketing** - Highlight urban celebration spaces
- **Sponsor Activations** - Fan zone brand integration
- **Tourism Content** - Attract visitors to host cities
- **News & Media** - Tournament impact and fan culture
- **Memory/Recap Content** - Capture collective experience

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Fan zone crowd scene, supporters in national colors watching outdoor screen, flags waving, tournament atmosphere, golden afternoon light, photorealistic crowd photography, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick fan zone shot needed
- Standard public viewing scene
- Crowd atmosphere content
- Documentary-style photography

**Customization:**

- Specify crowd size (hundreds, thousands)
- Adjust time of day and lighting
- Add specific fan elements (flags, scarves, face paint)
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for crowd scenes and fan zones
- Wide landscapes showing scale
- Broadcast and documentary standard
- Desktop and TV viewing

**21:9 Cinematic:**

- Ultra-wide crowd shots
- Epic scale emphasis
- Cinematic establishing shots
- Premium content

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Close crowd sections
- Mobile-first social content
- Instagram/Facebook Stories
- Vertical perspective shots

**1:1 Square:**

- Instagram feed posts
- Balanced crowd compositions
- Social media versatility

**Recommendation:** 16:9 for most fan zone photography, 21:9 for epic scale

---

### Photography Style & Lighting

**Golden Hour (Best for Outdoor):**

- Warm, flattering crowd lighting
- Long shadows add depth
- Romantic festival atmosphere
- 1 hour before sunset ideal

**Blue Hour (Night Viewing):**

- Deep blue sky backdrop
- Screen illumination on faces
- City lights glowing
- Magical twilight atmosphere

**Overcast (Even Documentation):**

- Soft, even lighting
- Good for consistent coverage
- No harsh shadows
- Professional documentation

**Night (Electric Energy):**

- Screen as primary light source
- City lights and artificial glow
- High contrast drama
- Electric excitement emphasis

**Recommendation:** Golden hour for outdoor daytime, blue hour for night events

---

### Crowd Photography Composition

**Wide Establishing Shot:**

- Full fan zone visible
- Shows scale and atmosphere
- Cinematic scope
- Environmental context

**Mid-Range Crowd Section:**

- 50-200 people visible
- Faces distinguishable
- Group energy clear
- Balanced detail and scale

**Close Crowd Detail:**

- 10-30 people visible
- Individual expressions
- Intimate moments
- Emotional connection

**Aerial/Elevated Perspective:**

- Bird's eye or elevated view
- Full scope of gathering
- Pattern of colors and flags
- Impressive scale emphasis

**Recommendation:** Mix wide shots (scale) with mid-range (atmosphere) and close details (emotion)

---

### Screen Integration

**Screen as Light Source:**

- Screen illuminating crowd faces
- Night viewing emphasis
- Dramatic lighting effect
- Collective watching moment

**Screen in Background:**

- Crowd in foreground
- Screen visible but secondary
- Shows context of viewing
- Balanced composition

**Screen Visible but Small:**

- Focus on crowd reaction
- Screen establishes context only
- People-first storytelling
- Reaction over content

**No Screen Visible:**

- Pure crowd energy
- Celebration or reaction focus
- Documentary human moment
- Implied viewing context

**Recommendation:** Screen visible but crowd as primary subject

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 16:9       (Fan zones and crowd scenes - primary)
--ar 21:9       (Ultra-wide cinematic crowd shots)
--ar 9:16       (Vertical Stories, close crowd sections)

--stylize 400-600   (Photorealistic documentary crowd photography)
--stylize 600-800   (More atmospheric, festival energy)

--quality 2     (High quality for crowd faces and detail)

--style raw     (Photorealistic, documentary photography)
```

**Example Outdoor Daytime:**

```
Fan zone crowd scene, hundreds of supporters in national colors watching giant outdoor screen, flags waving overhead, carnival tournament atmosphere, golden afternoon light, photorealistic crowd photography --ar 16:9 --stylize 500 --quality 2 --style raw
```

**Example Night Blue Hour:**

```
Urban plaza fan zone at night, giant LED screen illuminating crowd faces, city lights surrounding, blue hour twilight sky, thousands gathered, electric atmosphere, photorealistic night photography --ar 16:9 --stylize 550 --quality 2 --style raw
```

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final fan zone image:

- [ ] Crowd scale appropriate (hundreds/thousands as specified)
- [ ] Supporters in national colors visible throughout
- [ ] Flags, scarves, or team identification clear
- [ ] Outdoor screen or viewing setup visible/implied
- [ ] Lighting appropriate (golden hour, blue hour, night)
- [ ] Atmosphere palpable (carnival, electric, festival energy)
- [ ] Photorealistic quality, documentary standard
- [ ] Faces and expressions visible (where appropriate)
- [ ] No staged or artificial-looking crowd
- [ ] Location setting clear and authentic
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
- [ ] Collective experience and energy evident
