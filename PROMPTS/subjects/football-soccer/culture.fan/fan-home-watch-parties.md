# Home Watch Parties - Fan Culture Image Prompts

## Overview

Create intimate, authentic home viewing party photography showcasing friends and family watching matches together. Features living room settings, genuine emotions, documentary-style authenticity, and relatable supporter experiences in domestic environments.

**Part of:** [Fan Culture Collection](PROMPTS.CULTURE.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Relatable Fan Content** - Showcase everyday supporter experiences
- **Social Media Posts** - Instagram and Twitter authentic moments
- **Broadcasting Promos** - "Watch with us" campaign imagery
- **Streaming Service Marketing** - Home viewing emphasis
- **Documentary Content** - Real fan stories and emotions
- **Brand Advertising** - TVs, snacks, beverages in natural settings
- **Fan Engagement** - Encourage home viewing parties
- **Community Storytelling** - Friends and family bonding
- **Authentic Moments** - Genuine reactions and emotions
- **Lifestyle Content** - Football integrated into daily life

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Living room watch party, friends in national colors on sofa watching match on TV, authentic expressions and emotions, documentary photography style, natural indoor lighting, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick home viewing shot needed
- Standard watch party scene
- Relatable domestic content
- Documentary-style photography

**Customization:**

- Specify group size (couple, small group, family)
- Adjust emotions (tense, celebrating, anxious)
- Add specific details (snacks, decorations, flags)
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for living room scenes
- Wide enough for groups
- TV viewing context fits naturally
- Standard home photography

**4:3 Classic:**

- Traditional home photography feel
- Intimate documentary aesthetic
- Nostalgic warmth
- Film-like quality

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Close intimate moments
- Mobile-first social content
- Instagram/Facebook Stories
- Vertical couple or small group shots

**1:1 Square:**

- Instagram feed posts
- Balanced compositions
- Social media versatility

**Recommendation:** 16:9 for most home viewing scenes, 4:3 for intimate documentary feel

---

### Photography Style & Lighting

**Natural Indoor Light:**

- Window light as main source
- Soft, authentic, warm
- Golden hour window light ideal
- Real home atmosphere

**Mixed Light (TV + Window):**

- TV screen illuminating faces
- Window providing fill light
- Balanced natural and artificial
- Evening viewing realism

**TV Glow Only (Night):**

- Dark room, TV as only light
- Faces illuminated by screen
- Intimate night viewing
- Dramatic but authentic

**Afternoon Natural:**

- Daytime match viewing
- Bright natural window light
- Warm, comfortable, casual
- Family-friendly atmosphere

**Recommendation:** Natural window light with TV glow for balanced authenticity

---

### Composition for Home Scenes

**Wide Room Shot:**

- Full living room visible
- Environmental context clear
- Shows domestic setting
- Establishes space and atmosphere

**Mid-Range Group:**

- Sofa and TV both in frame
- People as main subjects
- Balanced composition
- Most versatile framing

**Close Emotional Moment:**

- Tight on faces and reactions
- Emotion primary focus
- Intimate connection
- Documentary close-up style

**Over-Shoulder TV View:**

- From behind group looking at TV
- TV screen content visible
- Immersive viewer perspective
- Shared watching experience

**Recommendation:** Mid-range capturing both people and TV, balanced composition

---

### Group Size Variations

**Solo Viewing:**

- One person alone on sofa
- Intimate, relatable, modern life
- Quiet focused watching
- Personal connection to match

**Couple (2 people):**

- Intimate romantic or friendship
- Cozy atmosphere
- Close connection emphasis
- Lifestyle feel

**Small Group (3-5 people):**

- Friends on one sofa
- Comfortable not crowded
- Individual faces visible
- Most common watch party size

**Family Group (5-10 people):**

- Multiple generations possible
- Living room full but not packed
- Warm family atmosphere
- Traditional gathering

**Packed Party (10+ people):**

- Standing room, floor seats
- Party atmosphere
- High energy
- Special match importance

**Recommendation:** Small group (3-5) for most relatable and authentic feel

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 16:9       (Living room scenes - primary)
--ar 4:3        (Intimate documentary feel)
--ar 9:16       (Vertical Stories, close moments)

--stylize 400-600   (Photorealistic documentary home photography)
--stylize 600-800   (More atmospheric, warm emotional tone)

--quality 2     (High quality for facial expressions and detail)

--style raw     (Photorealistic, authentic documentary)
```

**Example Tense Moment:**

```
Living room watch party, four friends in national colors on sofa, match on TV, hands over faces in tense penalty moment, authentic documentary photography --ar 16:9 --stylize 500 --quality 2 --style raw
```

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final home watch party image:

- [ ] Setting clearly domestic/home environment
- [ ] TV or screen visible/implied in composition
- [ ] Supporters in national colors or team gear
- [ ] Emotions genuine and authentic (not posed)
- [ ] Lighting natural and appropriate for time/setting
- [ ] Group size appropriate and believable
- [ ] Composition balanced and engaging
- [ ] Photorealistic quality, documentary standard
- [ ] No overly staged or artificial moments
- [ ] Relatable and authentic atmosphere
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
- [ ] Facial expressions clear and emotional
