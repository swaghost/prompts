## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: Face Paint Looks Digital/Fake

**Solutions:**

- Add "authentic face paint, not digital overlay"
- Specify "physically painted on face"
- Request "imperfect application"
- Add "smudged" or "worn" descriptors
- Use "body paint" terminology

---

### Issue: Crowd Looks Staged or Sparse

**Solutions:**

- Specify exact numbers: "hundreds of supporters"
- Add "packed gathering, densely crowded"
- Request "authentic crowd photography"
- Add "no empty space visible"
- Use "overflowing" or "sprawling" descriptors

---

### Issue: Emotions Look Posed

**Solutions:**

- Emphasize "candid," "unposed," "genuine"
- Specify exact emotional moment: "mid-celebration," "frozen anticipation"
- Add "documentary photography" multiple times
- Request "photojournalism style"
- Avoid "everyone looking at camera"

---

### Issue: Indoor Lighting Unrealistic

**Solutions:**

- Specify light sources: "window light and TV glow"
- Add "natural indoor lighting"
- Request "mixed lighting, authentic"
- Avoid "perfect studio lighting"
- Add "realistic home atmosphere"

---

### Issue: Scale Not Impressive Enough

**Solutions:**

- Add "aerial view" or "elevated perspective"
- Specify "thousands visible"
- Request "bird's eye view"
- Add "overwhelming scale"
- Use ultra-wide aspect ratios (21:9)

---

### Issue: Too Perfect/Clean

**Solutions:**

- Add "documentary authenticity"
- Request "realistic imperfections"
- Specify "worn," "messy," "chaotic"
- Add "lived-in," "authentic chaos"
- Embrace motion blur and clutter

## AI Tool-Specific Tips

### Midjourney Optimization

**Stylize Values:**

- 400-500: Clean photorealistic documentary
- 500-600: Balanced realism and atmosphere
- 600-800: More atmospheric, emotional mood
- Avoid >800: Can become too artistic/unrealistic

**Quality Parameter:**

- Always use `--quality 2` for people/faces
- Critical for crowd detail
- Essential for portrait photography

**Style Raw:**

- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic documentary
- Reduces Midjourney's default artistic enhancement
- More authentic photographic look

**Aspect Ratios:**

- 16:9: Most fan zones, groups, streets
- 9:16: Portraits, vertical Stories
- 21:9: Epic wide crowd shots
- 1:1: Social media square posts

---

## Quick Reference Summary

### Essential Elements for Any Fan Culture Image

1. **National Colors Visible** - Jerseys, flags, face paint
2. **Authentic Emotion** - Genuine, unposed, documentary style
3. **Appropriate Lighting** - Golden hour, natural, or night atmosphere
4. **Clear Context** - Setting identifiable (fan zone, home, street, etc.)
5. **Photorealistic Quality** - Documentary photography standard
6. **Scale Appropriate** - Crowd size matches scene type
7. **Cultural Respect** - Authentic representation, no stereotypes

### Most Versatile Prompting Formula

```
[Setting] [crowd/group size] [action/emotion],
national colors throughout, [specific details],
[lighting description], documentary photography,
photorealistic, [aspect ratio]
```

**Example:**

```
Street goal celebration, dozens of supporters pouring from bars,
arms raised in joy, national flags waving, strangers hugging,
golden hour urban light, documentary photography,
photorealistic --ar 16:9
```

## Final Recommendations

### For Best Results

1. **Use Documentary Language** - "authentic," "candid," "unposed," "genuine"
2. **Specify Emotions Precisely** - Don't just say "happy," say "explosive joy" or "overwhelming relief"
3. **Describe Lighting Sources** - "golden hour," "TV glow," "stadium floodlights"
4. **Embrace Imperfection** - Smudged paint, motion blur, messy celebrations = authenticity
5. **Show Scale** - Specify crowd numbers, use aerial views for massive gatherings
6. **Natural Behavior** - People watching, celebrating, reacting - not posing
7. **Appropriate Aspect Ratio** - 16:9 for groups, 9:16 for portraits, 21:9 for epic wide shots

### Common Success Patterns

**Fan Zone Success:**

- Wide shots showing scale
- Golden hour lighting
- "Hundreds/thousands" specified
- Flags and screens visible
- Documentary crowd photography

**Home Viewing Success:**

- Natural window light
- Small specific group size (3-5 people)
- TV visible in frame
- Genuine emotions (tension, joy)
- Documentary home photography

**Portrait Success:**

- Shallow depth of field
- Face fills frame
- Authentic face paint
- Specific emotion
- Golden hour or dramatic light

**Celebration Success:**

- Mid-action capture
- Arms raised, jumping, hugging
- Golden hour street photography
- Spontaneous chaos
- Documentary candid style
