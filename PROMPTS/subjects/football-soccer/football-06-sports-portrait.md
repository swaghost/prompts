## Troubleshooting

### Skin Too Smooth (Looks Fake)

**Problem**: Premium skin rendering becomes over-smoothed  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Premium but realistic skin texture, not over-smoothed"
- Change "premium skin rendering" to "refined natural skin rendering"
- Emphasize: "Realistic broadcast photography"

### Background Too Sharp or Distracting

**Problem**: Stadium background not blurred enough  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Shallow depth of field with heavily blurred background"
- Add: "Background softly out of focus, subject sharp"

### Lighting Too Soft or Too Harsh

**Problem**: Not balanced properly  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Balanced portrait lighting, flattering but dimensional"
- Adjust: "Soft but defined side lighting with beautiful rim light"

### Lost Original Identity

**Problem**: Face altered too much in enhancement  
**Solution**:

- Use Base Prompt more strongly
- Emphasize: "Keep the original framing and identity exactly"
- Add: "Preserve facial features precisely"

### Colors Too Muted or Too Saturated

**Problem**: Color grading not balanced  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Balanced color saturation, premium but natural"
- Emphasize: "Clean blue and orange tones, not overdone"
