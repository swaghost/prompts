## Troubleshooting

### Player Looks Too Dark (Silhouette Only)

**Problem**: Rim lighting creates complete silhouette, losing detail  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Rim lighting outlining player while keeping jersey details visible"
- Emphasize: "Detailed jersey texture" (already in prompt)
- Add: "Balanced exposure showing player clearly with dramatic outline"

### Smoke Too Heavy

**Problem**: Subtle smoke becomes thick fog  
**Solution**:

- Change "subtle smoke and haze" to "very minimal atmospheric smoke"
- Add: "Clear visibility of player and jersey"

### Not Feeling Like "Entrance"

**Problem**: Looks like regular match photo  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Pre-match entrance moment, before kickoff begins"
- Add: "Anticipatory atmosphere, calm before the match"
- Strengthen: "Official football trailer quality"

### Sky Not Blue Enough or Wrong Time

**Problem**: Sky doesn't look like cinematic evening  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Cinematic blue evening sky, golden hour transitioning to night"
- Add: "Magic hour lighting, twilight atmosphere"

### Field Reflections Not Showing

**Problem**: No beautiful field reflections appearing  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Wet polished field surface with light reflections"
- Emphasize: "Beautiful stadium light reflections on the grass"
