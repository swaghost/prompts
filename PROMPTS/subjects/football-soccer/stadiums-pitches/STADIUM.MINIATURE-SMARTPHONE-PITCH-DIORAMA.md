# Miniature Smartphone Football Pitch Diorama

## Description

A photorealistic macro image of a smartphone resting on a wooden table, with its glass screen transformed into a physically integrated miniature football pitch. Tiny goals and players create convincing diorama scale while fingerprints, smudges, and fine scratches preserve the realism of the phone surface.

## Usage

Use for conceptual football artwork, social-media visuals, editorial sports imagery, technology-and-sport campaigns, match promotions, and miniature-world compositions.

## Subject

A smartphone containing one miniature grass football pitch with two goals and tiny players in red and white kits.

## Image Prompt

```text
Macro photograph of a smartphone positioned on a wooden table at a dramatic oblique angle. The phone's screen contains a detailed miniature diorama of a lush grass football pitch, physically integrated beneath and within the glass surface, featuring two miniature goals and tiny football players in red and white kits actively positioned across the field.

Preserve convincing miniature scale: individually visible grass blades, crisp white pitch markings, delicate goal frames and netting, tiny proportionate players, believable spacing, and subtle contact shadows. The pitch must conform precisely to the flat rectangular screen area without spilling beyond the phone or appearing as a separate floating object.

Authentic smudges, fingerprints, faint dust, and light hairline scratches mark the phone's glass surface. Retain realistic edge reflections, screen-glass thickness, metal or dark phone frame, speaker and camera details, and natural contact between the phone and tabletop.

Use soft natural daylight from one side with physically accurate reflections and gentle falloff. Use very shallow depth of field, focusing sharply on the miniature pitch and nearest players while the wooden table, far phone edges, and distant diorama details transition into smooth natural blur.

Photorealistic macro product photography, realistic wood grain, tactile glass imperfections, premium editorial sports concept, physically believable materials, precise miniature craftsmanship, restrained color grading, cinematic composition.

Completely free of text and UI elements. The phone screen displays no icons, buttons, status bar, clock, notifications, app interface, score graphics, advertising, logos, or written marks.
```

## Composition

- Smartphone placed diagonally at a dramatic oblique angle
- Screen and miniature pitch remain the clear focal point
- Entire pitch rectangle reads within the phone display
- Wooden table provides a warm, tactile surrounding surface
- Focus plane crosses the nearest miniature players and pitch texture

## Technical Specifications

- **Camera:** Macro photography
- **Angle:** Oblique three-quarter view looking down at the phone
- **Focus:** Very shallow depth of field centered on the screen diorama
- **Lighting:** Soft natural side daylight
- **Materials:** Real glass, metal or dark phone frame, grass, painted markings, goal netting, and wood
- **Finish:** Photorealistic editorial product image with subtle optical imperfections
