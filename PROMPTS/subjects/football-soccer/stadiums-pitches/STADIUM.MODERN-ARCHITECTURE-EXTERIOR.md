## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
Contemporary football stadium exterior featuring bold modern architectural design. The structure showcases striking steel framework, extensive glass facades, geometric patterns, and innovative engineering. Materials include polished steel beams, glass curtain walls, concrete structural elements, and possibly colored panels or mesh screening. The design emphasizes clean lines, dramatic curves or angles, and impressive scale. Signage and branding are minimal, keeping focus on architectural form. The stadium may feature cantilever roof sections, exposed structural elements, or distinctive facade patterns that make it architecturally noteworthy.

**Setting/Environment:**
Urban or suburban setting on a clear day with vibrant blue sky providing a clean backdrop. The stadium sits on a prepared site with landscaped surroundings, access roads, and public spaces visible. Minimal distractions in the background - the composition emphasizes the stadium architecture against sky. Some contextual elements like trees, paving, or adjacent buildings may appear but remain secondary. No crowds or heavy activity - focus remains on the structure itself. Weather conditions are ideal - clear visibility, good natural light, no atmospheric interference.

**Lighting:**
Bright natural daylight provides even illumination across the entire stadium exterior. The sun position (ideally mid-morning or mid-afternoon) creates enough shadow to reveal depth and structural detail while maintaining overall bright exposure. The lighting reveals material textures - steel finish, glass reflectivity, concrete surfaces - while maintaining clean aesthetic. Blue sky provides natural fill light preventing harsh shadows. The quality of light emphasizes the modernity and precision of the architecture.

**Composition:**
Architectural composition presenting the stadium from a flattering angle - typically three-quarter view showcasing both facade and side profile. The structure fills most of the frame while maintaining context and scale. Foreground may include landscaping or public space providing depth. The background is predominantly clean blue sky. Vertical lines are corrected (using tilt-shift lens or post-processing) maintaining architectural accuracy. Rule of thirds or centered composition depending on structural design. Negative space in sky balances solid mass of stadium.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Editorial architectural photography style typical of design magazines and professional portfolios. The mood is clean, impressive, and aspirational - presenting the stadium as modern engineering achievement and civic pride. Color palette dominated by structural materials (steel grays, glass reflections, concrete tones) against vibrant blue sky. Professional, polished aesthetic emphasizing precision, scale, and contemporary design excellence. Photorealistic rendering with attention to material accuracy and architectural detail.

**Technical Specifications:**
Professional architectural photography setup with tilt-shift or perspective-corrected wide-angle lens (24-45mm), aperture f/8-f/11 for optimal sharpness, ISO 100-200 for maximum detail and color accuracy. Tripod-mounted for stability and precision. High-resolution rendering emphasizing material textures, structural details, and clean lines. Minimal post-processing beyond exposure balancing, perspective correction, and subtle enhancement. Color grading maintains accurate material colors while optimizing blue sky saturation. Photorealistic quality matching professional architectural documentation and editorial publications.
