## Troubleshooting

### Stadium Looks Fake or CGI

**Problem**: Added stadium appears artificial  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Everything must remain photorealistic"
- Add: "Realistic stadium architecture, authentic crowd, natural integration"
- Strengthen: "Premium broadcast photography quality"

### Player Action Changed

**Problem**: Pose or movement altered during transformation  
**Solution**:

- Use Base Prompt more strongly
- Emphasize: "Preserving the player's exact position, pose and movement"
- Add: "Do not alter player's action or body position at all"

### Crowd Too Visible or Distracting

**Problem**: Added crowd draws attention from player  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Realistic crowd depth with natural blur, not distracting"
- Emphasize: "Focus remains on player with crowd as background context"

### Lost Training Ground Completely

**Problem**: Some training elements should stay (like specific goal or equipment)  
**Solution**:

- Specify: "Replace background with stadium while keeping [specific element]"
- Adjust prompt for what should be preserved vs transformed

### Lighting Too Dark or Too Bright

**Problem**: Stadium floodlights not balanced  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Bright but balanced stadium floodlights"
- Specify: "Well-lit professional broadcast lighting"
