**Setting/Environment:**
Urban night setting with the stadium positioned as the primary focal point. Behind the structure, a city skyline creates depth with illuminated buildings of varying heights. The stadium sits on a large forecourt area with concrete paving that has been recently wetted (from rain or cleaning), creating mirror-like reflections. Minimal pedestrian activity with focus on architecture. Clear night sky or subtle cloud cover above.

Sophisticated architectural lighting design illuminates the stadium exterior through uplighting, downlighting, and integrated facade lighting. City lights provide ambient background illumination. Foreground lighting reflects beautifully on wet concrete surfaces, doubling the visual impact.

**Composition:**
Wide-angle composition positioning the stadium prominently in the frame with city skyline providing background context. The wet forecourt occupies the lower third, creating reflection opportunities. Vertical elements of the stadium architecture lead the eye upward. Careful balance between stadium (primary subject), skyline (context), and reflections (visual interest). Rule of thirds or centered symmetrical composition depending on architectural design.
