## Troubleshooting

### Too Dark or Can't See Details

**Problem**: Night conversion makes image too dark  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Well-lit subject with balanced exposure"
- Emphasize: "Premium broadcast lighting" (already in prompt)
- Reduce "night" emphasis, increase "blue-hour" (twilight still has light)

### Sky Looks Fake or Too Blue

**Problem**: Blue-hour sky appears artificial  
**Solution**:

- Change "cinematic blue-hour sky" to "natural evening sky with subtle blue tones"
- Add: "Realistic twilight atmosphere"

### Orange Highlights Too Strong

**Problem**: Warm tones overwhelming  
**Solution**:

- Change "warm orange highlights" to "subtle warm stadium highlights"
- Add: "Balanced color grading"

### Lost Original Photo Quality

**Problem**: Transformation too heavy, loses original  
**Solution**:

- Re-emphasize Base Prompt: "Keep every part of original composition"
- Add: "Preserve original athlete appearance and pose exactly"

### Rain Mist Too Visible

**Problem**: Subtle rain looks like heavy rain  
**Solution**:

- Already says "subtle rain mist" but emphasize: "Very light atmospheric mist, barely visible"
