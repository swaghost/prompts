# Cultural Street Scenes - Host Country Culture Image Prompts

## Overview

Create authentic cultural street photography showcasing local football traditions, street art, grassroots football culture, and community connections. Features vibrant colors, documentary authenticity, and cultural storytelling through visual narratives.

**Part of:** [Host Country Culture Collection](PROMPT.Host-Country-Culture.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Cultural Storytelling** - Showcase host country football heritage
- **Documentary Content** - Behind-the-scenes local football culture
- **Social Media Posts** - Instagram and Twitter cultural content
- **Tourism Marketing** - Authentic local experiences
- **Community Engagement** - Grassroots football stories
- **Street Art Campaigns** - Urban art and tournament integration
- **Youth Football Programs** - Inspire participation and inclusion
- **Cultural Heritage Content** - Traditional football connections
- **Neighborhood Stories** - Local community football passion
- **Mural and Public Art** - Tournament-themed street art

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Street football scene in local neighborhood, vibrant cultural backdrop, children playing, authentic documentary photography, warm afternoon light, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick cultural street scene needed
- Grassroots football content
- Documentary-style photography
- Community focus

**Customization:**

- Specify exact cultural setting (Latin American, African, Asian, etc.)
- Adjust time of day and lighting
- Add specific cultural elements (murals, architecture, etc.)
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for street scenes and cultural contexts
- Wide environmental storytelling
- Documentary photography standard
- Desktop and broadcast viewing

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Tall murals and building art
- Mobile-first social content
- Instagram/Facebook Stories
- Portrait-oriented street scenes

**1:1 Square:**

- Instagram feed posts
- Balanced street photography
- Social media versatility

**4:3 Classic Documentary:**

- Traditional photojournalism ratio
- Documentary film aesthetic
- Authentic journalism feel

**Recommendation:** 16:9 for most street scenes, 9:16 for tall murals

### Composition for Street Scenes

**Environmental Context:**

- Include surrounding buildings, streets
- Show neighborhood character
- Establish cultural setting
- Context tells story

**Human Scale:**

- People as main subjects
- Action and emotion visible
- Environmental context frames them
- Balanced scale

**Leading Lines:**

- Streets, alleys lead eye to subject
- Architectural lines guide composition
- Depth created through perspective

**Layered Depth:**

- Foreground: Close action or detail
- Midground: Main subject/game
- Background: Cultural context (murals, buildings)

**Recommendation:** Include environmental context for cultural storytelling

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 16:9       (Street scenes and cultural contexts - primary)
--ar 9:16       (Tall murals and vertical streets)
--ar 4:3        (Classic documentary feel)

--stylize 250-500   (Photorealistic documentary style)
--stylize 500-750   (More vibrant, artistic colors)

--quality 2     (High quality for cultural detail)

--style raw     (Photorealistic, documentary photography)
```

**Example Street Football:**

```
Mexico City street football scene, vibrant painted mural backdrop, children playing in colorful alley, warm Mexican cultural palette, afternoon golden light, authentic documentary photography --ar 16:9 --stylize 400 --quality 2 --style raw
```

**Example Mural:**

