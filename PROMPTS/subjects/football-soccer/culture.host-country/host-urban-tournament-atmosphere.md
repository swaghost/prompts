# Urban Tournament Atmosphere - Host Country Culture Image Prompts

## Overview

Create vibrant urban celebration and tournament atmosphere imagery showcasing city streets, public viewing areas, and fan celebrations. Features photorealistic cultural fusion, festival energy, and documentary-style photography of supporters transforming host cities.

**Part of:** [Host Country Culture Collection](PROMPT.Host-Country-Culture.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Fan Engagement Content** - Supporter celebration photography
- **Social Media Posts** - Instagram and Twitter atmosphere content
- **Tournament Marketing** - Show festival atmosphere and energy
- **City Promotion** - Highlight host city character and culture
- **Broadcast B-Roll** - TV show backgrounds and transitions
- **Documentary Content** - Behind-the-scenes fan stories
- **Public Viewing Campaigns** - Promote fan zones and viewing parties
- **Tourism Marketing** - Attract visitors to host cities
- **News & Media** - Tournament impact on urban spaces
- **Memory/Recap Content** - Capture tournament atmosphere legacy

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Urban tournament atmosphere, city streets filled with supporters in team colors, festival energy, golden hour light, photorealistic documentary photography, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick fan atmosphere shot needed
- Standard urban celebration scene
- Documentary-style content
- Social media posts

**Customization:**

- Specify exact city and landmarks
- Adjust time of day and lighting
- Add specific fan elements (flags, face paint, etc.)
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for urban scenes and crowds
- Wide cityscapes and celebrations
- Broadcast and website use
- Desktop viewing optimized

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Tall buildings and vertical streets
- Mobile-first social content
- Instagram/Facebook Stories
- Portrait-oriented scenes

**1:1 Square:**

- Instagram feed posts
- Balanced crowd compositions
- Social media versatility

**21:9 Cinematic:**

- Ultra-wide street scenes
- Epic crowd cinematography
- Premium content

**Recommendation:** 16:9 for most urban atmosphere, 9:16 for mobile Stories

---

### Photography Style & Approach

**Photorealistic Documentary:**

- Authentic, candid moments
- Photojournalism quality
- Real people, genuine emotions
- Unposed, natural interactions

**Golden Hour Warmth:**

- Warm, glowing light
- Romantic and nostalgic
- Flattering for people and cities
- Most popular time

**Blue Hour Drama:**

- Twilight deep blue sky
- City lights glowing
- Magical transition time
- Urban scenes especially effective

**Street Photography:**

- Candid, unposed
- Capture authentic moments
- Documentary storytelling
- Human-focused narrative

**Recommendation:** Photorealistic documentary style with golden hour warmth

---

### Crowd Photography Tips

**Show Diversity:**

- Multiple nationalities visible
- Different ages and backgrounds
- International unity theme
- Cultural fusion emphasis

**Capture Emotion:**

- Joy, excitement, anticipation
- Genuine expressions
- Celebration moments
- Shared experience

**Include Context:**

- City landmarks visible
- Local architecture
- Regional characteristics
- Place-specific identity

**Balance Scale:**

- Some wide shots showing crowds
- Some closer shots showing individuals
- Multiple perspectives
- Varied framing

**Recommendation:** Mix wide crowd shots with closer emotional moments

---

### Lighting for Urban Scenes

**Golden Hour (Best):**

- Warm, flattering light on people
- Long shadows add depth
- Romantic city glow
- Sunset or sunrise

**Blue Hour (Dramatic):**

- City lights visible
- Deep blue sky
- Magical twilight
- Evening atmosphere

**Overcast (Even):**

- Soft, even lighting
- No harsh shadows
- Good for close-ups
- Consistent color

**Night (Electric):**

- City lights, neon, artificial glow
- High contrast
- Urban energy
- Party atmosphere

**Recommendation:** Golden hour for warmth and emotion, blue hour for drama

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 16:9       (Urban scenes and celebrations - primary)
--ar 9:16       (Vertical city shots for Stories)
--ar 1:1        (Square social posts)

--stylize 250-500   (Photorealistic documentary style)
--stylize 500-750   (More cinematic, artistic atmosphere)

--quality 2     (High quality for detail in crowds)

--style raw     (Photorealistic, documentary photography)
```

**Example NYC:**

```
New York City skyline at sunset merged with tournament atmosphere, Times Square screens showing match scores, national supporters filling streets in colors, photorealistic cultural fusion --ar 16:9 --stylize 400 --quality 2 --style raw
```

**Example Beach Fan Zone:**

```
Miami tournament fan zone, beach setting at golden hour, supporters in national colors around outdoor screens, palm trees and ocean behind, festival atmosphere --ar 16:9 --stylize 450 --quality 2 --style raw
```

### DALL-E Recommendations

- Specify "photorealistic" or "documentary photography"
- Request "golden hour" or specific lighting
- Mention exact city: "Times Square NYC," "Copacabana Beach Rio"
- Request "diverse crowd" for international representation
- Specify "candid" or "authentic moments" for documentary feel

---

### Stable Diffusion Tips

- Use "street photography," "documentary," "photojournalism"
- Lighting: "golden hour light," "sunset glow," "warm evening"
- For crowds: "diverse crowd, multiple nationalities, supporters"
- Negative prompts: "no posed, no staged, authentic, candid"
- Control with CFG (7-10 for photorealism)
- Use photorealism LoRA for documentary quality

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final urban atmosphere image:

- [ ] City or location clearly recognizable
- [ ] Supporters and crowds visible and authentic
- [ ] Lighting appropriate (golden hour, blue hour, etc.)
- [ ] Composition balanced and engaging
- [ ] Emotions visible - joy, excitement, celebration
- [ ] Photorealistic quality, documentary standard
- [ ] Cultural fusion or diversity represented
- [ ] No staged or artificial-looking poses
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
- [ ] Regional characteristics authentic
- [ ] Tournament elements visible (flags, colors, screens)
- [ ] Atmosphere and energy palpable
