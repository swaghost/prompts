# Training Ground Atmosphere - Host Country Culture Image Prompts

## Overview

Create atmospheric training ground and practice facility photography showcasing national teams adapting to local conditions, weather, and environments. Features documentary sports photography, environmental storytelling, and authentic behind-the-scenes training moments.

**Part of:** [Host Country Culture Collection](PROMPT.Host-Country-Culture.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Behind-the-Scenes Content** - Training camp documentation
- **Social Media Posts** - Instagram and Twitter practice content
- **Documentary Photography** - Team preparation stories
- **Sports Journalism** - Media day and practice coverage
- **Weather/Climate Stories** - Teams adapting to conditions
- **Environmental Portraits** - Athletes in local settings
- **Broadcast B-Roll** - TV show backgrounds and features
- **Team Media** - Official training camp content
- **Atmosphere Building** - Pre-tournament anticipation
- **Cultural Adaptation** - Teams experiencing host country

## Base Prompt Template

```
National team training session, practice facility with local environmental conditions, players in training kit, atmospheric sports documentary photography, natural lighting, 16:9 horizontal.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick training ground shot needed
- Standard practice documentation
- Team preparation content
- Documentary-style photography

**Customization:**

- Specify exact weather/climate conditions
- Adjust time of day and lighting
- Add regional environmental elements
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

**Prompt:**

```

**Key Features:**

- Winter weather conditions

- Visible cold (breath vapor)

- Atmospheric documentary style

**Visual Elements:**

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for training ground and environmental shots
- Wide landscapes and facilities
- Broadcast and documentary standard
- Desktop and TV viewing

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Player portraits with environment
- Mobile-first social content
- Instagram/Facebook Stories
- Vertical action shots

**1:1 Square:**

- Instagram feed posts
- Balanced compositions
- Social media versatility

**4:3 Classic Documentary:**

- Traditional sports photography ratio
- Authentic journalism feel
- Documentary film aesthetic

**Recommendation:** 16:9 for most training ground documentation

---

### Photography Style & Approach

**Sports Documentary:**

- Authentic, candid moments
- Photojournalism standards
- Behind-the-scenes access
- Real training captured, not posed

**Atmospheric Conditions:**

- Weather and environment emphasized
- Conditions visible and prominent
- Environmental storytelling
- Nature as co-subject

**Natural Lighting:**

- Available light preferred
- No artificial flash or studio lighting
- Weather-dependent lighting
- Authentic conditions

**Action Photography:**

- Players in motion
- Training drills captured
- Athletic moments
- Dynamic composition

**Recommendation:** Sports documentary with atmospheric conditions emphasized

---

### Lighting for Training Grounds

**Overcast (Common):**

- Even, soft lighting
- Good for consistent documentation
- No harsh shadows
- Weather atmosphere visible

**Golden Hour:**

- Warm, beautiful light
- Flattering for players
- Romantic atmosphere
- Morning or evening sessions

**Harsh Midday (Desert/Tropics):**

- Overhead sun
- Intense shadows
- Heat and challenge visible
- Extreme condition emphasis

**Stadium Artificial:**

- Even, consistent lighting
- Professional quality
- Indoor or night sessions
- Broadcast-ready illumination

**Recommendation:** Natural light appropriate to conditions being documented

---

### Composition for Training Grounds

**Environmental Context:**

- Include surrounding landscape, weather
- Show local conditions and environment
- Establish training location character
- Context tells adaptation story

**Player Focus with Backdrop:**

- Players as main subject
- Environment frames and supports
- Balanced subject and setting
- Both athlete and place important

**Wide Establishing Shots:**

- Full facility visible
- Environmental conditions clear
- Cinematic scope
- Documentary establishment

**Close Action Details:**

- Player faces, effort visible
- Equipment details (frost, sweat, etc.)
- Intimate athletic moments
- Human scale and emotion

**Recommendation:** Mix wide environmental shots with closer player moments

```
--ar 16:9       (Training ground documentation - primary)

--ar 9:16       (Vertical player portraits)

--stylize 250-500   (Photorealistic sports documentary)

--style raw     (Photorealistic, sports photography)

### Stable Diffusion Tips

- Use "sports photography," "training ground," "documentary"

- Negative prompts: "no posed, no studio, authentic conditions, candid"

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final training ground image:

- [ ] Environmental conditions clearly visible and authentic
- [ ] Players appear natural, in training mode (not posed)
- [ ] Weather/climate appropriate to story
- [ ] Lighting fits conditions (overcast for winter, harsh for desert, etc.)
- [ ] Training kit and equipment authentic
- [ ] Composition includes environmental context
- [ ] Photorealistic quality, sports documentary standard
- [ ] Athletic action or training visible
- [ ] No staged or artificial-looking moments
- [ ] Regional characteristics authentic
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
- [ ] Atmosphere and mood palpable
