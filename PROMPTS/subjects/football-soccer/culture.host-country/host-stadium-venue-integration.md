# Stadium Venue Integration - Host Country Culture Image Prompts

## Overview

Create stunning stadium and venue photography that integrates iconic local landmarks, natural backdrops, and regional characteristics with tournament infrastructure. Features architectural photography, dramatic golden hour lighting, and photorealistic compositions showcasing host locations.

**Part of:** [Host Country Culture Collection](PROMPT.Host-Country-Culture.txt)

- **Tournament Marketing Materials** - Venue showcase photography
- **Host City Promotion** - Highlight local landmarks with stadiums
- **Social Media Content** - Instagram and Twitter venue posts
- **Broadcast Graphics** - TV show intros and location establishment shots
- **Website Hero Images** - Tournament and venue websites
- **Print Materials** - Programs, posters, and promotional items
- **Documentary Content** - Behind-the-scenes venue storytelling
- **Travel & Tourism** - Promote host cities to visitors

- **Pre-Match Content** - Venue introduction graphics

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**16:9 Horizontal (Primary):**

- Best for stadium and venue photography
- Wide landscapes and architecture
- TV, broadcast, and website hero images
- Desktop and large screen viewing

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Tall stadium shots
- Vertical architecture emphasis
- Mobile-first social content
- Instagram/Facebook Stories

**1:1 Square:**

- Instagram feed posts
- Balanced compositions
- Stadium with equal sky and foreground

**21:9 Cinematic:**

- Ultra-wide cinematic shots
- Dramatic landscape emphasis
- Premium content
- Film-quality compositions

**Recommendation:** 16:9 for most venue photography, 21:9 for cinematic showcase

---

### Photography Style & Lighting

**Golden Hour (Primary):**

- 1 hour before sunset or after sunrise
- Warm, diffused, dramatic light
- Long shadows, depth and dimension
- Most flattering for architecture

**Blue Hour:**

- Twilight after sunset/before sunrise
- Deep blue sky, city lights glowing
- Magical transition lighting
- Urban venues especially effective

**Midday (Avoid Generally):**

- Harsh overhead sun
- Flat lighting, minimal shadows
- Only for bright, energetic looks
- Better for interior shots

**Overcast/Soft:**

- Even, diffused lighting
- Good for consistent color
- Reduces harsh shadows
- Professional documentation style

**Recommendation:** Golden hour for maximum impact, blue hour for urban night scenes

---

### Camera Angles & Perspectives

**Wide Angle Architectural:**

- 16-35mm equivalent
- Captures full stadium and backdrop
- Dramatic perspective
- Showcase scale and context

**Aerial/Drone:**

- Bird's-eye perspective
- Infrastructure and layout visible
- Unique viewpoint
- Cinematic establishing shots

**Eye-Level Approach:**

- Human perspective
- Supporter viewpoint
- Approachable and relatable
- Documentary style

**Low Angle Looking Up:**

- Stadium looks imposing, grand
- Dramatic sky emphasis
- Heroic architectural presence
- Monument-like quality

**Recommendation:** Wide angle architectural for most, aerial for special showcase

---

### Composition Rules

**Rule of Thirds:**

- Stadium on one third
- Landmark/backdrop on another third
- Sky or foreground on remaining third
- Balanced visual weight

**Foreground-Midground-Background:**

- Foreground: Approach elements (trees, flags, people)
- Midground: Stadium architecture
- Background: Landmark (mountains, skyline, ocean)
- Creates depth and layers

**Leading Lines:**

- Roads, boulevards, or paths leading to stadium
- Guide viewer's eye to venue
- Creates dynamic composition
- Emphasizes journey/approach

**Symmetry:**

- Centered stadium with balanced backdrop
- Formal, monumental feeling
- Works well for iconic venues
- Clean, organized aesthetic

**Recommendation:** Use foreground-midground-background for depth and storytelling

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 16:9       (Stadium and venue photography - primary)
--ar 21:9       (Cinematic ultra-wide landscapes)
--ar 9:16       (Vertical stadium shots for Stories)

--stylize 250-500   (Realistic architectural photography)
--stylize 500-750   (Cinematic drama, more artistic)

--quality 2     (High quality for architectural detail)

--style raw     (Photorealistic, documentary style)
```

**Example Coastal:**

```
Los Angeles coastal stadium at golden hour, Pacific Ocean visible beyond stands in distance, palm trees lining approach road, California light quality, architectural photography --ar 16:9 --stylize 400 --quality 2 --style raw
```

**Example Mountain:**

```
Vancouver mountain backdrop stadium, snow-capped peaks beyond venue, tournament and Canadian flags, stunning natural backdrop, wide architectural photography --ar 16:9 --stylize 350 --quality 2 --style raw
```

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting final venue integration image:

- [ ] Stadium architecture clearly visible and prominent
- [ ] Landmark or backdrop well-integrated, not overpowering
- [ ] Lighting appropriate (golden hour, blue hour, etc.)
- [ ] Composition balanced with foreground-midground-background
- [ ] Regional characteristics authentic and recognizable
- [ ] Tournament elements visible (flags, branding, etc.)
- [ ] Photorealistic quality, professional photography standard
- [ ] No architectural distortion or warping
- [ ] Aspect ratio appropriate for intended use
- [ ] Sky and weather appropriate for mood/message
- [ ] Scale and perspective realistic
- [ ] Colors accurate to location and time of day
