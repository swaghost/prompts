## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Formation looks like solid object instead of individual drone lights

**Solution:**

- Emphasize individual lights: "thousands of tiny individual LED drone lights, each visible as distinct point, dense dotted contours"
- Add: "NOT solid object, NOT hologram, made from separate drones, light-point geometry"
- Strengthen: "small gaps between drone positions visible, pixelated light-dot effect"

### Problem: Footballer unrecognizable or too abstract

**Solution:**

- Add detail: "recognizable face shape, distinctive hairstyle outline, facial features suggested by light density"
- Specify: "dense enough light points to create recognizable silhouette, [FOOTBALLER] instantly identifiable"
- Reference: "use sufficient drone density - 5,000-10,000 drones for detailed formation"

### Problem: Wrong national team colors or crowd support

**Solution:**

- Explicitly state: "Automatically infer [FOOTBALLER]'s correct national team: [TEAM NAME], jersey colors [COLORS], flag colors [COLORS]"
- Be specific: "Messi = Argentina sky blue-white, Ronaldo = Portugal red-green, Mbappé = France blue-white-red"

### Problem: Trophy shape wrong or unrecognizable

**Solution:**

- Detail trophy: "accurate FIFA World Cup trophy silhouette: wide base, narrow stem, dual handles, globe on top"
- Add: "distinctive World Cup trophy shape recognizable even from drone lights, golden highlights on key features"

### Problem: Daytime or wrong lighting

**Solution:**

- Emphasize night: "night scene, black night sky backdrop, pure darkness behind drones, stadium floodlights below only"
- Add negative: "no daytime, no daylight, no blue sky, dark night atmosphere only"

### Problem: Stadium looks empty or fake

**Solution:**

- Strengthen crowd: "packed World Cup stadium, 60,000-100,000 capacity completely filled, every seat taken"
- Add authenticity: "realistic stadium architecture, modern World Cup-standard venue, authentic tournament atmosphere"
