How to Customize Any Prompt

Every prompt in this guide is a starting point. Five variables change the output most significantly:

1. Country colors — swap in your nation's palette

    Argentina: "sky blue and white vertical stripes"
    Brazil: "yellow and green"
    Germany: "black white and red"
    Spain: "deep red and yellow"
    Morocco: "red and green"
    Japan: "deep blue and red"

2. Tournament stage — changes the emotional weight

    "group stage" → open, early tournament energy
    "quarter final" → pressure building
    "semi final" → near the summit
    "final" → maximum stakes

3. Platform format — add at the end of any prompt

    "9:16 vertical, Instagram Story format"
    "1:1 square, Instagram feed"
    "16:9, YouTube thumbnail"

4. Mood — one word shifts the entire atmosphere

    "euphoric", "tense", "melancholic", "triumphant", "exhausted"

5. Model — use the model table above to match style to output
