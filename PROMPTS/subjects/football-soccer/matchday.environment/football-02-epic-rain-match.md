## Troubleshooting

### Rain Too Heavy, Can't See Subject

**Problem**: Rain obscures athlete  
**Solution**:

- Change "heavy cinematic rainfall" to "moderate cinematic rainfall"
- Add: "Clear visibility of subject, rain in mid-ground and background"
- Emphasize: "Stadium floodlights illuminating subject clearly"

### Jersey Not Looking Wet Enough

**Problem**: Fabric doesn't show wetness  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Soaking wet jersey fabric clinging tightly to body"
- Add: "Visible water saturation in fabric"

### Puddles Too Prominent

**Problem**: Ground water overwhelming composition  
**Solution**:

- Change "puddles on the pitch" to "scattered puddles"
- Add: "Subtle water accumulation"

### Rain Looks Fake or Like Overlay

**Problem**: Rain appears added digitally, not natural  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Realistic rainfall integrated into scene"
- Add: "Authentic weather conditions, not post-processing effect"
- Strengthen: "Volumetric lighting through rain"

### Too Dark Overall

**Problem**: Heavy weather makes scene too dark  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Well-lit by bright stadium floodlights"
- Emphasize: "Floodlights illuminating every raindrop" (already there)
- Add: "Balanced exposure showing subject clearly"
