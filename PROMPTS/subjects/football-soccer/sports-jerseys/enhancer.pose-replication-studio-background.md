# Jersey Pose Replication with Studio Background

**AI IMAGE GENERATION / PLAYER PORTRAIT**

---

## Platform

**Gemini** (Google)

---

## Concept

Replicate an exact pose from a mockup reference image while preserving the player's original identity, jersey, and all clothing details. Only the background changes to a studio gradient matching the jersey's dominant color.

**Style:** Studio portrait with gradient background  
**Format:** Mid-thigh up, three-quarter angle  
**Use Case:** Player portraits with consistent pose across different jerseys

---

## Reference Requirements

**TWO REFERENCES REQUIRED:**

1. **Mockup reference:** The exact pose template showing body position, head angle, hand placement
2. **Player photo:** Original player photo with face, jersey, and outfit to preserve

---

## PROMPT

Using the reference mockup image as the exact pose template, recreate the identical body pose, head position, head tilt/angle, shoulder rotation, torso angle, and camera framing with the player from the second reference photo — matching every detail of the mockup's posture with no deviation: right hand raised gripping the shirt collar near the neck, left hand tucked into the pocket, head turned and tilted at the same angle as the mockup, shoulders and torso rotated the same way, same three-quarter eye-level camera shot from mid-thigh up.

Keep the player's face, facial features, expression, skin tone, and hairstyle completely unchanged and identical to the original photo — only reposition the head to match the mockup's head angle while preserving the player's exact identity and features. Keep the player's jersey, shorts, colors, logos, sponsor text, crest, and all clothing details completely unchanged and identical to the original photo — do not alter, remove, or replace any part of the outfit.

Change only the background to a smooth studio gradient in tones inspired by the dominant color of the player's jersey, with soft studio lighting and rim light on the shoulders, matching the lighting style of the mockup reference.

Do not change body, do not change the pose in any way including head angle, do not alter clothing design facial identity — replicate the mockup's exact pose (body AND head) and adjust only the background color.

---

## Key Elements

**Pose Details:**

- Right hand gripping shirt collar near neck
- Left hand tucked into pocket
- Head turned and tilted (match mockup angle)
- Three-quarter stance
- Mid-thigh up framing

**Preserve Exactly:**

- Player's facial identity and features
- Jersey design, colors, logos
- Sponsor text and crest
- Shorts and all clothing details
- Original expression and hairstyle

**Change Only:**

- Background to studio gradient
- Gradient color matches jersey dominant color
- Soft studio lighting with rim light
- Professional lighting on shoulders
