## PROMPT

Transform this shirt photo into a professional hanging product shot. Keep the shirt design, colors, logos, text, patterns, and every detail 100% unchanged — do not alter, redesign, or modify the garment in any way.

**Scene setup:**

- Hang the shirt naturally on a thin wire clothesline using two simple metal clothespins/clips, one on each shoulder area of the collar
- The shirt should hang freely with slight natural fabric drape, wrinkles, and gravity-realistic folds, as if just washed and slightly damp (subtle water droplet texture optional on fabric)
- Background: a rough textured concrete/plaster wall in neutral grey-beige tones, with visible cracks, stains, and organic weathering for an authentic urban aesthetic
- Lighting: soft natural daylight coming from the upper left, creating a gentle diagonal shadow/light streak across the lower right portion of the wall and shirt, with mild directional shadows falling behind the shirt onto the wall
- Camera: straight-on, eye-level product photography angle, shirt centered in frame, medium-close crop showing full garment from collar to hem
- Style: high-end sportswear/streetwear catalog photography, sharp focus, high resolution, crisp fabric texture detail, realistic photographic quality (not illustration or render)
- Do not add any new logos, text, or graphics — preserve the original shirt exactly as provided
- Ultra-high quality, 4K detail, professional commercial photography look
