**Hyper-Realistic Football Jersey Product Photography** — Complete 8-angle photography system for creating premium sportswear product images using reference jersey uploads and AI image generation.

**Output Format:** Vertical 4:5 (1080×1350px) Instagram-ready product photography

## How This System Works

### The Core Concept

**Composition Reference + Your Jersey = Professional Product Photography**

**Step 1: The Composition Reference**

- There's a base composition showing a burgundy jersey (appears to be Trabzonspor) on a matte-black mannequin in a brutalist concrete corridor
- This provides the lighting, environment, mannequin, and camera angles

**Step 2: Upload Your Jersey**

- You upload a photo/design of ANY jersey you want to photograph
- Can be any team, any colors, any design, any logos

**Step 3: AI Replacement**

- AI completely removes the burgundy reference jersey
- AI recreates YOUR jersey design in the same professional setting
- Maintains exact colors, logos, sponsors, names, numbers, fabric details
- Preserves the mannequin, lighting, environment, and camera angle

**Result:** Professional product photography of YOUR jersey design in 8 different angles

## The 8 Camera Angles

### Overview of All Angles

1. **Back View — Full Frontal** — Complete rear view showing back number/name
2. **Front View — Three-Quarter** — Slight 15-20° rotation showing chest and sleeves
3. **Front Torso — Cinematic Close-Up** — Tight chest/collar focus with dramatic framing
4. **Top Angle — Overhead Front** — High angle (35-45°) showing shoulder width and shape
5. **Logo Detail — Macro** — Extreme close-up of chest logo/crest with fabric texture
6. **Wide Angle — Environmental** — Full mannequin in corridor showing depth and context
7. **Back Name/Number — Close-Up** — Tight rear focus on player name and number
8. **Top Wide Angle — Overhead Full** — High angle showing entire mannequin and stand

```
CRITICAL: The jersey base color must be EXACTLY [specific color description]
as shown in the reference image. Do not alter, lighten, darken or shift the
color in any way.
```

**Be more specific about colors:**
Instead of "blue," say "deep navy blue (Pantone 282C)" or "electric bright blue"

**Reference specific brands:**
"The exact red of Manchester United home kit" or "Liverpool FC red"

**Regenerate:**
Sometimes AI needs a second attempt to capture colors accurately

---

### Issue 2: Logos or Sponsors Distorted/Wrong

**Problem:** Text is warped, logos are incorrect, sponsors missing or wrong placement.

**Solutions:**

**Emphasize preservation:**
Add to prompt:

```
The [specific logo/sponsor] must be reproduced EXACTLY as it appears in the
reference image with perfect clarity, no distortion, correct spelling, and
accurate positioning.
```

**Describe logo details explicitly:**

```
The chest logo is a circular crest approximately 10cm wide, positioned center
chest 15cm below collar. It features [description]. Reproduce every detail
with photographic accuracy.
```

**Use higher resolution reference:**
Provide a clearer, higher-res reference image of the jersey

**Try different AI platform:**
Some platforms (Nano Banana Pro, MidJourney) handle fine details better

---

### Issue 3: Fabric Looks Unrealistic or Plastic

**Problem:** Jersey material looks fake, overly smooth, plastic, or synthetic.

**Solutions:**

**Emphasize realistic textile:**
Add to prompt:

```
The fabric must show realistic cotton/polyester blend texture with visible
knit pattern, natural microstructure, subtle imperfections and authentic
sportswear material properties. No plastic sheen or artificial smoothness.
```

**Request specific fabric type:**
"Dri-FIT performance fabric with visible mesh texture" or "Classic cotton jersey with soft matte finish"

**Reduce sharpening:**
If image is over-sharpened: "Natural fabric rendering without excessive sharpening or clarity enhancement"

**Add fabric detail reference:**
Include a close-up reference photo of actual jersey fabric texture

---

### Issue 4: Background/Environment Wrong

**Problem:** AI doesn't maintain brutalist corridor or changes environment.

**Solutions:**

**Reemphasize environment:**

```
IMPORTANT: Preserve the EXACT brutalist concrete corridor environment from
the composition reference. Raw concrete texture, repeating columns, linear
perspective, no changes to the architectural setting.
```

**Lock the environment:**
At start of prompt, add:

```
DO NOT change the environment. The background must remain the brutalist
concrete corridor exactly as shown.
```

**Separate instructions:**
Generate jersey first, then composite into environment if needed

---

### Issue 5: Mannequin Changed or Human Model Appears

**Problem:** AI changes mannequin color/style or adds a human model.

**Solutions:**

**Restate mannequin requirements:**

```
The mannequin MUST remain a matte-black headless torso form. Do NOT use a
human model, do NOT add a head, do NOT change the mannequin color or style.
```

**Emphasize at beginning and end:**
State mannequin requirements both at start and end of prompt for reinforcement

**Use negative prompt (if platform supports):**
"--no human model, no person, no head, no face, no skin"

---

### Issue 6: Wrong Camera Angle or Perspective

**Problem:** AI generates different angle than requested.

**Solutions:**

**Use more specific angle description:**

Instead of: "from above"
Use: "from directly overhead at 90 degrees looking straight down"

Instead of: "three-quarter"
Use: "rotated 15-20 degrees from front-facing, showing both chest and side panel"

**Include perspective keywords:**

- "bird's eye view" for top-down
- "eye level" for straight-on
- "low angle looking up" for ground perspective
- "high angle looking down 35-45 degrees"

**Reference similar images:**
"Similar camera angle to professional product photography catalogs"

---

### Issue 7: Details Missing from Jersey

**Problem:** Small details like secondary logos, patches, or badges missing.

**Solutions:**

**Create detail list:**

```
The jersey includes these elements that MUST be present:
- [Main crest] at center chest
- [Manufacturer logo] at right chest
- [Sponsor] across chest
- [Secondary sponsor] on sleeve
- [League patch] on right sleeve
- [Badge] on left sleeve
Ensure ALL elements are included with correct placement.
```

**Use annotation:**
If possible, annotate reference image with arrows pointing to critical details

**Generate detail shots:**
Use Angle 5 (Macro) to capture small details in close-up

---

### Issue 8: Composition Too Tight or Too Wide

**Problem:** Framing cuts off parts of jersey or shows too much empty space.

**Solutions:**

**Specify framing boundaries:**

For close-ups:
"Frame from [starting point] to [ending point]. The entire [specific area] must be visible."

For wide shots:
"Leave [amount] of space around the mannequin while keeping jersey as main focal point"

**Use percentage framing:**
"The jersey should occupy approximately 60-70% of the frame"

**Reference rule of thirds:**
"Position mannequin according to rule of thirds with jersey centered in composition"

## Platform-Specific Guidance

### Nano Banana Pro (Recommended)

**Why it's best:**

- Exceptional photorealism
- Excellent fabric texture rendering
- Strong with product photography
- Accurate logo and text reproduction

**Tips:**

- Upload reference as primary image
- Use prompt exactly as written
- No need for negative prompts
- Generate at highest quality setting

**Settings:**

- Quality: Maximum
- Style: Photorealistic
- Aspect: 4:5 (or custom 1080×1350)

---

### MidJourney

**Strengths:**

- Beautiful cinematic lighting
- Excellent environmental consistency
- Strong architectural rendering

**Tips:**

- Use `--cref [URL]` for reference jersey
- Add `--style raw` for photorealism
- Use `--ar 4:5` for format
- Try `--stylize 300-500` for balance

**Example command structure:**

```
[Full prompt text] --cref [jersey reference URL] --ar 4:5 --style raw --stylize 400 --q 2
```

---

### Flux

**Strengths:**

- Good consistency across generations
- Fast generation speed
- Reliable detail preservation

**Tips:**

- Attach reference image first
- Paste full prompt
- Use "Realism" or "Photography" style preset
- Generate at 1080×1350 resolution

---

### GPT Image / DALL-E (ChatGPT)

**Strengths:**

- Accessible (ChatGPT Plus)
- Easy to use
- Good for quick mockups

**Limitations:**

- Less photorealistic than others
- May struggle with fine text/logos
- More stylized appearance

**Tips:**

- Upload reference jersey image to chat
- Paste prompt with extra emphasis on "photorealistic"
- Request: "Generate in highest quality photorealistic mode"
- May require multiple regenerations

## Notes & Best Practices

**Reference Image Quality Matters:**

- Higher resolution reference = better results
- Clear visibility of all details essential
- Multiple views (front + back) ideal for comprehensive generation

**Platform Selection:**

- **Nano Banana Pro** recommended for best photorealism
- **MidJourney** great for artistic quality
- **Flux** good for speed and consistency
- **ChatGPT** accessible but less precise

**Consistency is Key:**

- Use same platform for entire series
- Keep prompts identical except jersey reference
- Generate all angles for one jersey before moving to next

**Iteration Expected:**

- First generation may not be perfect
- Refine with specific feedback
- 2-3 iterations typical for ideal result

**Legal Considerations:**

- Respect trademark/copyright of team logos
- For commercial use, ensure proper licensing
- AI-generated images may have usage restrictions depending on platform

**Storage & Organization:**

- Name files systematically: `[Team]-[Kit]-[Angle].png`
- Example: `ManUtd-Home-Angle2-Front3Quarter.png`
- Keep reference images with generated results
- Document which platform/settings used
