## PROMPT

A professional, high-resolution portrait photograph of the football player, positioned from the hips up (three-quarter view). Their body is slightly angled, perhaps by about 15-20 degrees, to their right (the viewer's left). Crucially, their head is turned back to look directly into the camera with a composed expression. The key detail is that their arms are firmly crossed over their chest, with both biceps clearly visible and prominent due to the flexed muscles. The right arm is typically positioned on top of the left, but the core requirement is the crossed-arm pose itself. The player is standing straight and centered. All elements must be set against a perfectly seamless, solid white background with clean, sharp studio lighting, casting subtle shadows to define form.
