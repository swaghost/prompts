## PROMPT

Using the attached photo as the reference for the person's identity, recreate this exact same individual in a new studio photograph, replicating the following pose with precise accuracy:

Body angled slightly to a three-quarter stance, chest turned toward the camera.

Right hand pointing with the index finger toward the crest/logo on the left side of the chest.

Left hand raised near the shoulder, gently pulling/holding the collar of the shirt outward.

Head turned toward the camera with a warm, relaxed smile, direct eye contact with the lens.

Same shoulder height, arm bend angles, and hand positioning as the reference pose.

**Critical requirements:**

- Preserve the person's exact facial features, face shape, skin tone, hairstyle, facial hair, and body proportions with zero alteration — this must remain unmistakably the same individual.
- Keep the person's own clothing/outfit from the uploaded photo unchanged (do not replace it with a different jersey, uniform, or design) — only replicate the pose and lighting/background setup.
- Replace the background with a clean, seamless pure white studio backdrop (#FFFFFF).
- Maintain professional studio lighting: soft, even key lighting with subtle natural shadow beneath the body/feet, matching the polished look of professional athlete portrait photography — not flat, shadowless lighting.
- Output in ultra-high resolution, sharp focus, photorealistic quality, with realistic skin texture, fabric texture, and natural color accuracy — no plastic/AI-smoothed look, no distortion, no artifacts.
- Final image aspect ratio must be exactly 4:5 (portrait orientation), with the full body/pose properly framed and centered within this ratio.

The final image should look like an authentic high-end sports/portrait studio photoshoot.
