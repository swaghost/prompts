# Jersey Design Transfer to Mockup Pose

**AI IMAGE GENERATION / JERSEY MOCKUP**

---

## Platform

**Gemini** (Google)

---

## Concept

Take a jersey design from one image and place it onto the exact pose, shape, and drape of a mockup template. Preserve all jersey details (logo, crest, patterns, colors, text) with zero distortion while matching the pose, folds, and camera angle of the mockup.

**Style:** Product mockup with jersey design transfer  
**Format:** Match mockup framing and perspective  
**Use Case:** Jersey design visualization on consistent poses

---

## Reference Requirements

**TWO REFERENCES REQUIRED:**

1. **Image 1 (Mockup):** The pose template with exact shape, drape, folds, camera angle, and background
2. **Image 2 (Jersey):** The jersey design to transfer with all details to preserve

---

## PROMPT

Take the jersey design from the second image (image 2) and place it onto the exact pose, shape, and drape of the mockup in the first image (image 1). Preserve all fine details of the jersey from image 2 exactly as they are — logo, crest, patterns, colors, collar design, sleeve stripes, text, and print — with NO changes, NO distortion, NO removal of any detail. Match the pose, folds, wrinkles, camera angle, and perspective from image 1 (the mockup). Use the same background as image 1 — same sky color, same lighting.
