# Low Tracking Shot Over Bleachers

## Description

A dynamic, low-altitude drone photograph taken from just above the first row of bleacher seats, simulating a tracking shot moving parallel to the main football pitch. The perspective is highly compressed by a long telephoto lens, making the individual, detailed aluminum seat planks and railings of the bleachers dominant in the foreground. The frame looks across the pitch, making the field lines and the distant center field crest appear compressed and close together. The overall field looks very vast due to the compression effect. A few small stadium light poles are visible at the very edges of the frame. The focus is on the foreground bleacher texture and the compressed field lines. All watermarks and numerical labels have been completely removed.

## Best Practices

### Camera Positioning Strategy

- **Altitude Precision**: Just above bleacher top row for intimate perspective
- **Tracking Alignment**: Parallel to pitch sideline for consistent compression
- **Angle Optimization**: Shallow 10-20° downward angle across field
- **Lateral Position**: Center or slight offset along bleacher length
- **Stabilization**: Eliminate all movement for sharpness

### Telephoto Lens Selection

- **Focal Length**: 200-400mm equivalent for extreme compression
- **Aperture**: f/8-f/11 for deep depth of field
- **Image Stabilization**: Critical for handheld or drone mounting
- **Compression Effect**: Longer focal length increases flattening
- **Cropping Alternative**: Shoot wider and crop if compression insufficient

### Timing and Conditions

- **Empty Facility**: Schedule when bleachers are vacant
- **Field Condition**: Freshly lined and maintained pitch
- **Weather**: Clear conditions for maximum sharpness across distance
- **Time of Day**: Late afternoon for dramatic side lighting
- **Seasonal**: Optimal grass condition for vibrant green color

### Technical Camera Settings

- **High ISO Management**: Balance ISO for shutter speed needs
- **Fast Shutter**: 1/500s minimum to freeze drone and prevent blur
- **Deep DOF**: Small aperture (f/8-f/11) for sharpness throughout
- **Manual Focus**: Lock focus between foreground and middle distance
- **RAW Capture**: Maximum dynamic range and post-processing flexibility

## Technical Notes

### Extreme Telephoto Compression

- **Focal Length Impact**: Longer lenses create more dramatic compression
- **Distance Distortion**: Near and far objects appear similar in size
- **Depth Collapse**: Three-dimensional space appears flat
- **Detail Magnification**: Distant elements magnified and visible
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Haze and clarity more pronounced at distance

### Drone Flight Challenges

- **Low Altitude Safety**: Careful navigation near bleacher structure
- **Wind Factors**: Low altitude subject to structure-induced turbulence
- **Clearance**: Maintain safe distance from bleacher structure
- **Gimbal Limits**: Shallow angle may approach gimbal mechanical limits
- **Battery Efficiency**: Hovering at low altitude may reduce flight time

### Depth of Field Management

- **Foreground-Background Balance**: Deep DOF needed for both layers
- **Critical Aperture**: f/8-f/11 optimal for lens sharpness
- **Hyperfocal Technique**: Focus 1/3 into scene for maximum sharpness
- **Diffraction Limit**: Avoid f/16+ to prevent softness
- **Sensor Size**: Larger sensors may require smaller apertures

### Focus and Sharpness

- **Manual Focus Lock**: Prevent autofocus hunting across layers
- **Focus Point Selection**: Target between foreground and middle ground
- **Depth Preview**: Use depth-of-field preview if available
- **Image Review**: Check foreground and background sharpness on-site
- **Focus Stacking**: Optional technique for extreme sharpness
