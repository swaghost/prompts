# Cinematic Filmic Stadium Logo Timelapse

## Description

A cinematic timelapse rendered in Seedance 2.0 with photoreal filmic qualities. Shot from a sweeping dolly-style angle in the stadium stands of a European football arena, capturing a grounds crew worker using a riding lawnmower to carve the team logo into the pitch. Features golden-hour lighting, long shadows, rich contrast, and premium film-grade color grading with smooth exposure transitions as the logo emerges.

This prompt generates premium cinematic footage that elevates stadium grounds maintenance into visual art.
