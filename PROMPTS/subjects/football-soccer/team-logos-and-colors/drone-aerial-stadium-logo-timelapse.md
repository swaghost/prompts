## Best Practices

### Reference Logo Preparation

- **Symmetry**: Logos with bilateral symmetry work best for top-down view
- **Contrast**: Clear, simple designs read well from altitude
- **Size Consideration**: Scale logo appropriately for pitch dimensions
- **Orientation**: Consider how logo reads from overhead perspective
- **Complexity**: Moderate detail level optimal for aerial visibility

### Drone Flight Planning

- **Altitude Selection**: High enough for full view, low enough for detail
- **Weather Conditions**: Stable, clear conditions with minimal wind
- **Sun Position**: Overhead sun minimizes shadows on pitch
- **Flight Time**: Battery life sufficient for full sequence capture
- **Backup Plan**: Multiple batteries or alternate drone ready

### Timelapse Technical Settings

- **Frame Interval**: Appropriate capture rate for smooth acceleration
- **Consistent Framing**: Maintain exact position throughout sequence
- **Exposure Lock**: Prevent flickering from changing light
- **Focus Lock**: Keep pitch in sharp focus throughout
- **Color Consistency**: Match color temperature across all frames

### Safety and Regulations

- **Flight Permissions**: Secure airspace authorization for stadium
- **Operator Certification**: Qualified drone pilot
- **Stadium Coordination**: Work with grounds crew and management
- **No-Fly Zones**: Confirm location not restricted airspace
- **Insurance**: Appropriate coverage for commercial drone operation
