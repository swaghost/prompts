# Drone Aerial Full-Day Stadium Preparation

## Description

Aerial drone timelapse rendered in Seedance 2.0 with ultra-photoreal, hyper-detailed quality capturing an entire day at a European football stadium from overhead perspective. Documents the sun rising, sprinklers activating and watering the pitch, grounds crew carving the team logo with a riding lawnmower, field technician painting the logo by hand with spray rigs and stencils, sun setting, stadium lights igniting, and full crowd filling the arena. Features smooth drone stabilization, HDR sunlight transitions, crisp aerial detail, and cinematic overhead composition.

## Overview

This prompt generates stunning aerial timelapse footage documenting the complete transformation of a football stadium from empty dawn to capacity crowd from a bird's-eye perspective.
