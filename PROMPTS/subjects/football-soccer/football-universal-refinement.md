## Troubleshooting

### Over-Processing or Looking Fake

**Problem**: Refinement makes image too processed  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Subtle refinement, maintaining photorealistic quality"
- Reduce: Skip refinement if initial result was already realistic
- Balance: Use refinement selectively, not always needed

### Lost Some Original Quality

**Problem**: Refinement altered something that was good  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Do not alter identity, pose, jersey, composition or action"
- Consider: Go back to previous generation if this happens

### Details Too Sharp or Over-Enhanced

**Problem**: Texture enhancement looks unnatural  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Natural texture enhancement, not oversharpened"
- Specify: "Realistic detail increase, not artificial"

### Colors Shifted

**Problem**: Color grading changed from desired look  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Maintain existing color palette while refining quality"
- Specify: "Enhance without changing color balance"
