# Stadium Tailgate Party - Casual Smartphone Montage

## Overview

Super casual real smartphone home video footage capturing a lively tailgate party in a stadium parking lot before a football game. Natural mobile phone camera aesthetic with smooth natural motion, rapid-fire montage with constant quick jump cuts every 1-2 seconds—like scrolling through phone memories. Unpolished, authentic phone recording of friends grilling, cheering, and hanging out near cars.

**Style:** Pure raw home video feel, no cinematic polish or heavy effects.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**

- Over-stabilize (kills authentic phone feel)
- Add cinematic color grading
- Use slow-motion or speed ramps
- Apply transitions (fades/dissolves)
- Make cuts too slow (needs to feel like rapid scroll)
- Over-polish the footage
- Add VFX or effects
- Make lighting too perfect
- Use tripod-stable shots

✅ **Do:**

- Keep natural handheld shake
- Maintain raw color (phone camera look)
- Use only jump cuts
- Cut every 1-2 seconds (rapid energy)
- Preserve authentic casual vibe
- Show real interactions
- Keep main character visually consistent
- Use natural outdoor lighting
