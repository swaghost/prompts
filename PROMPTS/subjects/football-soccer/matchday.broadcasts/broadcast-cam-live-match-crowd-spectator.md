## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Jersey looks too fashion-styled or tight

**Solution:**

- Emphasize authentic fan clothing: "normal full jersey, real supporter wear, NOT tight, NOT cropped, NOT fashion-styled, loose comfortable fit"
- Add negative: "no tight jersey, no cropped showing stomach, no tied knot, no glossy fashion fabric"

### Problem: Crowd looks too perfect or staged

**Solution:**

- Strengthen crowd realism: "mixed believable crowd, different ages, various outfits, imperfect poses, unstaged natural crowd"
- Add: "slightly soft background faces, partially cropped people, authentic diverse crowd, NOT uniform"
- Include negative: "no perfect staged crowd, no all same age, no uniform posing"

### Problem: Looks like professional portrait instead of broadcast

**Solution:**

- Emphasize broadcast aesthetic: "authentic live TV broadcast screenshot, distant telephoto camera, NOT professional portrait photography"
- Add: "broadcast camera sensor characteristics, natural grain, live television quality, documentary realism"
- Include negative: "not fashion photo, not studio image, not clean smartphone photo, not AI render"

### Problem: Face doesn't match reference

**Solution:**

- For Midjourney: Use --cref parameter correctly
- For DALL-E: Upload reference first, explicitly state "preserve exact identity from uploaded image"
- Strengthen: "maintain face shape, facial proportions, natural features accurately, recognizable as same person"

### Problem: Lighting too perfect or artificial

**Solution:**

- Add authenticity: "natural stadium lighting, broadcast camera color balance, NOT studio lighting, authentic ambient light"
- Specify: "realistic skin tones under stadium lighting, professional broadcast exposure but not artificially enhanced"

### Problem: Composition too close or selfie-like

**Solution:**

- Emphasize distance: "shot from distance with telephoto lens, broadcast camera in stands, NOT close-up, NOT selfie"
- Add: "subject visible within crowd context, distant perspective, camera positioned across stadium section"
