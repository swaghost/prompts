## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Subject looks posed or aware of camera

**Solution:**

- Emphasize candid nature: "completely unaware of camera, absorbed in match, NO eye contact, natural spectator focus"
- Strengthen body language: "body turned 45 degrees toward pitch, head following same direction, looking at pitch NOT camera"
- Add negative: "no looking at camera, no posed expression, no awareness of being filmed"

### Problem: Doesn't look like broadcast television quality

**Solution:**

- Add broadcast-specific language: "professional broadcast television camera, telephoto lens compression, authentic TV broadcast quality"
- Specify: "HD broadcast sensor, television color science, spectator cutaway typical of live sports broadcast"
- Include negative: "not professional portrait photography, not fashion shoot, not studio lighting"

### Problem: Face doesn't match reference image

**Solution:**

- For Midjourney: Use --cref parameter with uploaded reference
- For DALL-E: Upload reference first, explicitly state "preserve exact face from uploaded image"
- Add: "maintain exact facial features, recognizable as same person, accurate identity preservation"

### Problem: Wrong body angle (facing camera)

**Solution:**

- Be explicit: "body turned 45 degrees toward pitch, NOT facing camera straight-on, shoulders angled toward right"
- Add: "head follows body direction, looking toward right side of frame at pitch"
- Specify: "three-quarter turn away from camera toward pitch action"

### Problem: VIP suite environment not visible enough

**Solution:**

- Add environment detail: "premium VIP suite interior visible, sophisticated finishes, glass railing prominent, hospitality setting clear"
- Emphasize: "subject positioned behind clear glass railing, VIP environment context important"

### Problem: Expression too dramatic or not authentic

**Solution:**

- Refine emotional description: "subtle tense hopeful expression, not overly dramatic, natural spectator emotion"
- Add: "eyebrows slightly tightened (not raised dramatically), lips slightly parted (not wide open)"
- Specify: "authentic fan concern, genuine absorbed concentration, documentary realism"
