# Countdown & Schedule Graphics - Matchday Image Prompts

## Overview

Create urgency-driven countdown graphics and clear schedule displays for tournament matches. Features time-based designs, multiple match listings, and kickoff announcements to keep fans informed and engaged.

**Part of:** [Matchday Graphics Collection](PROMPT.MATCHDAY.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Match Countdown Posts** - Build anticipation for kickoff
- **Daily Schedule Graphics** - Multi-match day schedules
- **Kickoff Tonight Announcements** - Same-day match reminders
- **Social Media Stories** - Instagram/Facebook countdown Stories
- **Mobile Notifications** - Push notification graphics
- **Website Countdowns** - Homepage countdown displays
- **Digital Signage** - Stadium and venue countdowns
- **Email Reminders** - Match day reminder emails
- **Tournament Schedule Posts** - Full day or round schedules
- **TV Broadcast Teasers** - Pre-match countdown graphics

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Match countdown graphic, digital clock display showing hours to kickoff, team colors, bold typography, 9:16 Story format, clean and urgent.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick countdown needed
- Standard match reminder
- Social media Stories
- Time-constrained projects

**Customization:**

- Adjust time display format (hours, days, minutes)
- Change color scheme to match teams
- Modify aspect ratio for platform
- Add specific match details

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**9:16 Vertical (Stories):**

- Best for countdown urgency
- Mobile-first viewing
- Full-screen Story takeover
- Maximum impact on mobile

**1:1 Square (Feed Posts):**

- Universal platform format
- Tonight announcements
- Schedule graphics
- Desktop and mobile friendly

**16:9 Horizontal (Broadcast/Website):**

- Multi-day schedules
- Landscape viewing
- Desktop hero images
- TV/streaming graphics

**Recommendation:** 9:16 for countdowns, 1:1 for tonight/schedule graphics

---

### Typography for Urgency

**Countdown Numbers:**

- Font Weight: Black (900) or ultra-bold
- Size: Very large, dominating frame
- Style: Digital/LED style or bold sans-serif
- Color: High contrast, usually white on color
- Treatment: Clean and readable

**"TONIGHT" / "HOURS" Text:**

- Font Weight: Bold to Black (700-900)
- Size: Large but secondary to numbers
- Style: All caps, impactful
- Color: Gold, white, or accent color
- Treatment: Often with glow or outline

**Match Details:**

- Font Weight: Regular to Medium (400-500)
- Size: Small, supporting information
- Content: Team names, kickoff time, group
- Readable but not competing with primary message

**Recommendation:** Maximum contrast for countdown numbers, clear hierarchy for all text

---

### Color Psychology

**Urgency Colors:**

- Red: Emergency, last-minute, immediate action
- Orange: Warning, attention, upcoming soon
- Yellow/Gold: Premium, tonight, special event
- Electric Blue: Energy, modern, exciting

**Team Colors:**

- Use team/national flag colors as background or accents
- Gradient blends for neutral countdown
- Maintain readability of white text over colors

**Neutral/Information:**

- White background for schedule clarity
- Black/dark grey for minimal countdowns
- Gold accents for premium tournament feel

**Recommendation:** Red for final hour, team colors for standard countdown, white for schedule graphics

### Time Display Formats

**Digital Clock Style:**

```
12:30 or 05:45 with colon separator, LED or digital font
```

**Large Numeral with Label:**

```
24 (large) + "HOURS UNTIL KICKOFF" (small below)
```

**Segmented Display:**

```
02 : 15 : 30 (DAYS : HOURS : MINUTES) in boxes or segments
```

**Text-Based:**

```
"IN 5 HOURS" or "TONIGHT AT 21:00" text emphasis
```

**Recommendation:** Match format to urgency level and time until kickoff

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 9:16       (Stories, countdown urgency - primary for countdowns)
--ar 1:1        (Tonight announcements, schedule - universal)
--ar 16:9       (Multi-day schedule, broadcast)

--stylize 250-500   (clean sports graphic aesthetic)
--stylize 100-250   (minimal, information-focused)

--quality 2     (high quality professional output)

--style raw     (more literal, less artistic)
```

**Example:**

```
Match countdown social media graphic, large digital clock showing 5 hours to kickoff, team color gradient, electric blue accent, bold white typography, 9:16 Story, clean urgent design --ar 9:16 --stylize 400 --quality 2
```
