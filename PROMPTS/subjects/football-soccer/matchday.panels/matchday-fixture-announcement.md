# Fixture Announcement Graphics - Matchday Image Prompts

## Overview

Create bold, dramatic fixture announcement graphics for tournament matches and knockout rounds. Features split compositions with team colors, typography-focused designs, and stadium atmosphere to build anticipation for upcoming matches.

**Part of:** [Matchday Graphics Collection](PROMPT.MATCHDAY.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Tournament Fixture Announcements** - Group stage match schedules
- **Knockout Round Reveals** - Quarter-finals, semi-finals, finals
- **Social Media Promotion** - Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook posts
- **Broadcast Graphics** - TV and streaming match previews
- **Stadium Display** - Digital screens and jumbotrons
- **Mobile App Notifications** - Push notification graphics
- **Website Hero Images** - Match preview page headers
- **Email Marketing** - Match day promotional emails

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Tournament fixture announcement poster, bold typography, team colors split composition, stadium atmosphere background, 'MATCHDAY' header, professional sports broadcast graphic quality, 9:16 vertical.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick fixture announcement needed
- Standard group stage matches
- General tournament promotion
- Time-constrained projects

- Adjust format ratio (9:16, 1:1, 16:9)
- Change header text (MATCHDAY, KICKOFF, etc.)
- Specify tournament stage

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**9:16 Vertical (Instagram/Facebook Stories):**

- Best for mobile-first viewing
- Full-screen Story experience
- Maximum engagement on Stories
- Portrait orientation dominant

**1:1 Square (Instagram Feed, Twitter):**

- Universal social media format
- Works on all platforms
- Balanced composition
- Feed-optimized

**16:9 Horizontal (YouTube, Broadcast):**

- TV and streaming graphics
- Landscape viewing
- Desktop/TV optimized
- Broadcast standard

**4:5 Vertical (Instagram Feed Portrait):**

- Tall Instagram feed posts
- More screen real estate than square
- Mobile feed optimized

**Recommendation:** 9:16 for Stories, 1:1 for feed posts, 16:9 for broadcast

---

### Typography Hierarchy

**Primary Text (Team Names):**

- Font Weight: Bold to Black (700-900)
- Style: Condensed or compressed sans-serif
- Size: Very large, dominant
- Color: High contrast white or team colors
- Treatment: All caps, tight tracking

**Secondary Text (Stage/Round):**

- Font Weight: Bold (600-700)
- Size: Medium-large
- Placement: Top header or prominent position
- Style: Display type, impactful

**Tertiary Text (Details):**

- Font Weight: Regular to Medium (400-500)
- Size: Small to medium
- Content: Date, time, venue, group
- Style: Supporting information, readable

**Recommendation:** Maximum 3 typography levels, clear hierarchy, high contrast

---

### Color Strategy

**Split Composition:**

- Divide canvas vertically (50/50 or 60/40)
- Team 1 colors left side
- Team 2 colors right side
- Sharp division or subtle gradient blend
- Maintain contrast for text readability

**Flag Color Palettes:**

- Use official national flag colors
- Primary color as dominant
- Secondary/accent colors as gradients or accents
- Avoid muddy color mixing
- Ensure legibility of white text over colors

**Dark Background Approach:**

- Dark navy, charcoal, or black base
- Team colors as accent elements (borders, glows, highlights)
- White typography for maximum contrast
- Stadium atmosphere integrated into darkness

**Recommendation:** Split composition for standard fixtures, dark background for premium/knockout matches

---

### Background Elements

**Stadium Atmosphere:**

- Crowd silhouettes (recognizable but not distracting)
- Floodlights and light beams
- Architectural stadium elements
- Dark, moody, atmospheric
- Adds context without competing with typography

**Abstract/Minimal:**

- Solid colors or gradients only
- Geometric shapes
- No photographic elements
- Modern, clean aesthetic
- Typography-focused

**Photographic:**

- Actual stadium exterior or interior
- Iconic venue recognition
- Dramatic lighting conditions
- Atmospheric effects (fog, light rays)
- Cinematic production value

**Recommendation:** Stadium atmosphere for most fixtures, photographic for premium matches, abstract for minimal designs

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 9:16       (Instagram/Facebook Stories - primary recommendation)
--ar 1:1        (Instagram feed, Twitter, universal)
--ar 16:9       (YouTube, broadcast, landscape)
--ar 4:5        (Instagram feed portrait)

--stylize 250-500   (balanced sports graphic aesthetic)
--stylize 750-1000  (more artistic, dynamic energy)

--quality 2     (high quality for professional output)

--style raw     (more literal, less artistic interpretation)
```

**Example:**

```
Tournament group stage fixture announcement poster, bold condensed white typography, Spain vs Germany flag colors split composition, dark stadium atmosphere, MATCHDAY header, professional sports broadcast quality --ar 9:16 --stylize 500 --quality 2
```
