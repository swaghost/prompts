# Squad Lineup Graphics - Matchday Image Prompts

## Overview

Create clear, tactical squad lineup graphics displaying starting eleven formations, player positions, and team selections. Features pitch diagrams, tactical formations, and professional team announcement designs.

**Part of:** [Matchday Graphics Collection](PROMPT.MATCHDAY.txt)

---

## Use Cases

- **Starting Eleven Announcements** - Pre-match lineup reveals
- **Formation Displays** - Tactical setup visualization
- **Team Selection Graphics** - Coach's chosen squad
- **Social Media Posts** - Instagram and Twitter lineup announcements
- **Broadcast Graphics** - TV and streaming pre-match analysis
- **Stadium Displays** - Jumbotron team lineup shows
- **Mobile App Content** - In-app lineup graphics
- **Match Programs** - Printed lineup displays
- **Tactical Analysis** - Formation breakdown content
- **Fan Engagement** - Predicted lineup polls and discussions

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Squad lineup graphic, numbered player positions on pitch diagram, team colors background, modern typography, starting eleven announcement, Instagram Story format.
```

**When to Use:**

- Quick lineup announcement
- Standard pre-match reveal
- Social media Stories
- Time-constrained projects

**Customization:**

- Specify formation (4-4-2, 4-3-3, etc.)
- Adjust to team colors
- Add player names or numbers
- Modify aspect ratio for platform

## Enhanced Prompt Variations

### Top-Down Formation Display

**Prompt:**

```
Pre-match squad lineup graphic, eleven numbered player positions marked on a top-down pitch diagram, team colors as background, clean modern sans-serif typography, starting eleven announcement design, Instagram Story format.
```

**Best For:**

- Standard lineup announcements
- Clear formation visualization
- Social media Stories (Instagram, Facebook)
- Mobile-first viewing
- Pre-match content

**Key Features:**

- Top-down pitch view (aerial perspective)
- Eleven player position markers
- Numbered positions (1-11)
- Team color background
- Clean modern typography
- Vertical Story format (9:16)

**Visual Elements:**

- **Pitch Diagram:** Top-down football/soccer pitch, simplified markings
- **Player Positions:** Circles, shields, or player silhouettes at tactical positions
- **Numbers:** Player jersey numbers in positions
- **Names:** Player surnames or full names (optional)
- **Formation Label:** "4-3-3" or formation shown prominently
- **Team Colors:** Background in team colors or gradient
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif, readable at small sizes

**Formation Options:**

**4-4-2 (Traditional):**

```
Two strikers, four midfielders, four defenders, goalkeeper - balanced classic formation
```

**4-3-3 (Attacking):**

```
Three forwards, three midfielders, four defenders - modern attacking setup
```

**3-5-2:**

```
Three center backs, five midfielders including wingbacks, two strikers - flexible formation
```

**4-2-3-1:**

```
Single striker, attacking midfielder, two defensive mids, modern popular formation
```

**5-3-2 / 3-4-3:**

```
Defensive or attacking variations, five at back or three forwards
```

## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio Guidelines

**9:16 Vertical (Instagram/Facebook Stories):**

- Best for single team lineup announcements
- Mobile-first viewing
- Full-screen Story experience
- Maximum engagement

**1:1 Square (Instagram Feed, Twitter):**

- Universal social media format
- Works across platforms
- Square pitch diagram fits well
- Feed-optimized

**16:9 Horizontal (YouTube, Broadcast):**

- Tactical analysis graphics
- TV and streaming
- Desktop/TV viewing
- Landscape orientation

**4:5 Vertical (Instagram Feed Portrait):**

- Tall Instagram feed posts
- More screen space than square
- Mobile feed optimized

**Recommendation:** 9:16 for Stories, 1:1 for feed posts, 16:9 for analysis

---

### Pitch Diagram Styles

**Realistic Pitch:**

- Grass texture and markings
- Photorealistic appearance
- Traditional look
- Detailed pitch lines

**Minimalist Pitch:**

- Simple line drawing
- White lines on team color
- Clean diagram aesthetic
- Focus on formation, not pitch

**Abstract Pitch:**

- Geometric or grid representation
- Modern design approach
- Stylized pitch concept
- Contemporary aesthetic

**No Pitch:**

- Pure formation without pitch context
- Text or icon-based layout
- Maximum minimalism
- Information-first design

**Recommendation:** Minimalist or abstract for modern brands, realistic for traditional

---

### Player Position Markers

**Circles with Numbers:**

- Simple clean circles
- Jersey numbers inside
- Color-coded by position line
- Most common approach

**Player Silhouettes:**

- Simplified player figure shapes
- Dynamic pose or standing
- More visual interest
- Recognizable as players

**Jersey Icons:**

- Miniature jersey shapes
- Team colors and numbers
- Kit representation
- Brand identity connection

**Photo Portraits:**

- Actual player headshots
- Circular frames
- Premium presentation
- Player recognition emphasis

**Recommendation:** Circles for clean/minimal, photos for premium

---

### Typography for Lineups

**Player Names:**

- Font Weight: Medium to Bold (500-700)
- Size: Medium, readable on mobile
- Style: Sans-serif, all caps common
- Format: Surname only or full name

**Numbers:**

- Font Weight: Bold to Black (700-900)
- Size: Large, prominent
- Style: Uniform width (tabular figures)
- Placement: With name or in position marker

**Formation Label:**

- Font Weight: Bold (700)
- Size: Medium-large
- Display: "4-3-3" or similar
- Placement: Header or prominent corner

**Headers:**

- Font Weight: Black (900)
- Style: All caps, condensed acceptable
- Content: "STARTING XI," "LINEUP," position names
- High contrast color

**Recommendation:** Sans-serif throughout, bold for emphasis, high contrast

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Recommendations

```
--ar 9:16       (Stories, lineup announcements - primary)
--ar 1:1        (Square social posts, universal)
--ar 16:9       (Tactical analysis, broadcast)

--stylize 250-500   (professional sports graphic aesthetic)
--stylize 100-250   (clean, minimal, information-focused)

--quality 2     (high quality for clarity)

--style raw     (more literal, less artistic)
```

**Example:**

```
Pre-match squad lineup graphic, eleven numbered positions on top-down pitch diagram, team colors background, clean modern typography, starting XI announcement, 9:16 Story --ar 9:16 --stylize 400 --quality 2
```
