# Universal Player × Jersey Launch Campaign - Auto Club Adaptation

## Description

Premium professional football jersey launch campaign featuring a full-body player wearing the uploaded jersey, surrounded by four oversized presentations of the same jersey creating a dimensional product frame. Uses dual reference system: uploaded PLAYER reference for 100% facial identity preservation and uploaded JERSEY reference for exact kit fidelity. Automatically adapts all club-specific elements (colors, patterns, typography, background) to the uploaded jersey while maintaining universal composition architecture. Features bold typography in left-center region, clean studio background with subtle club-pattern texture, and high-key commercial lighting. Creates official global kit launch campaign aesthetic comparable to Nike, Adidas, and Puma product releases. Works universally with ANY football player, ANY club, ANY national team, and ANY jersey. Vertical 4:5 format perfect for premium sportswear editorial and social media campaigns.

## Usage

Perfect for official football kit launches, club jersey announcements, player signing reveals, home/away/third kit campaigns, season jersey launches, national team kit reveals, manufacturer product launches (Nike/Adidas/Puma/etc.), sports fashion editorials, premium fan content, personalized player jersey posters, team sponsor activations, social media jersey campaigns, Instagram kit launch posts, football lifestyle branding, sportswear product photography, club merchandise marketing, player ambassador content, jersey pre-order campaigns, kit unveiling graphics, collector edition launches, limited release jerseys, goalkeeper kit campaigns, anniversary special editions, tournament jersey reveals, and professional football marketing materials.

## Actor or Subject

**Identity Preservation (Critical - PRIORITY 01):**

Subject must remain exactly the same person from PLAYER reference image. Preserve with maximum fidelity:

- Facial identity and proportions
- Face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, ears
- Skin tone and ethnicity
- Hairstyle, hair texture, hair color
- Facial hair
- Visible tattoos
- Body proportions and height impression
- Age appearance
- All recognizable physical characteristics

The generated athlete must clearly remain the SAME PERSON. Do NOT redesign face, do NOT beautify excessively, do NOT create generic football player, do NOT mix facial characteristics with another athlete, do NOT alter recognizable tattoos, do NOT change ethnicity or hairstyle unnecessarily.

**Subject Position & Pose:**

ONE full-body football player prominently in CENTER-RIGHT portion of composition. Visible approximately from head to shoes. Player's head around upper-middle region, feet approach bottom edge. Athlete occupies 45-55% of total visual dominance.

Relaxed premium fashion-editorial posture with:

- Body facing mostly toward camera with subtle natural rotation
- One arm raised naturally toward shoulder, collar or upper jersey area (subtly presenting garment)
- Opposite hand resting casually in trouser pocket or relaxed near hip
- Relaxed shoulders and confident natural expression
- Direct or slightly relaxed eye contact with camera

This is PRODUCT PRESENTATION campaign, not matchday action. NO aggressive football action, NO running, NO kicking, NO ball, NO celebration pose.

**Subject Styling:**

Player wears the EXACT uploaded jersey paired with minimal neutral fashion styling:

- Black or dark neutral relaxed trousers
- Clean contemporary silhouette with subtle fabric texture
- Premium casual sportswear appearance
- Clean sneakers compatible with club color palette

Jersey remains the visual hero. Do not introduce competing fashion graphics.

## Typography Composition

**Placement & Hierarchy:**

Typography in LEFT-CENTER region with generous negative space.

Bold contemporary condensed sans-serif sports-fashion typeface. All uppercase.

Hierarchy structure:

```
[CLUB / TEAM NAME]

[HOME / AWAY / THIRD]
JERSEY

[SEASON]
```

Example layout only:

```

AWAY
JERSEY

```

**Typography Scale:**

- **CLUB NAME:** Small-to-medium bold uppercase, positioned immediately above headline
- **MAIN KIT TYPE:** Extremely large, heavy condensed sans-serif, uppercase, tightly spaced, strong rectangular visual mass. "AWAY" / "HOME" / "THIRD" occupies one line, "JERSEY" on following line
- **SEASON:** Medium-large bold typography below headline, increased tracking between numbers (e.g., 2 0 2 6 / 2 7)

Typography feels integrated into composition, not floating independently.

**Automatic Typography Color:**

Derived from uploaded jersey using:

- Darkest club color, OR
- Secondary jersey color, OR
- Desaturated darker version of primary color

Maintains strong readability against pale background. No unrelated colors introduced.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CLUB NAME:

Small-to-medium bold uppercase text.

Position immediately above the main headline.

MAIN KIT TYPE:

Extremely large.
Heavy condensed sans-serif.
Uppercase.
Tightly spaced.
Strong rectangular visual mass.

"AWAY" / "HOME" / "THIRD" should occupy one line.

"JERSEY" should occupy the following line.

SEASON:

Medium-large bold typography below the headline.

Use increased tracking between numbers.

Example:

2 0 2 6 / 2 7

Typography should feel integrated into the composition rather than floating independently.

AUTOMATIC TYPOGRAPHY COLOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Derive typography color from the uploaded jersey.

Use primarily:

• darkest club color
OR
• secondary jersey color
OR
• a desaturated darker version of the primary color

Maintain strong readability against the pale background.

Do NOT introduce unrelated colors.

Large soft key light from upper front-left.

Soft frontal fill.

Gentle rim separation around the player's shoulders and silhouette.

Bright environment.

Controlled highlights.

Very soft natural shadows.

No harsh cinematic darkness.

No dramatic stadium lighting.

No neon.

No colored smoke.

No excessive glow.

Allow subtle warm or cool environmental reflections derived from the club palette.

• micro fabric weave
• breathable performance textile
• stitched seams
• ribbed collar

COMPOSITIONAL BALANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain the visual balance:

LEFT SIDE:
large typography + oversized garment framing

CENTER-RIGHT:
full-body player

TOP CORNERS:
cropped suspended jerseys

BOTTOM CORNERS:
extreme foreground jersey close-ups

CENTER:
clean breathing space connecting typography and athlete

The composition must feel intentionally asymmetrical but perfectly balanced.

Do not center everything.

Do not convert the layout into a generic sports poster.

• player's face
• main jersey torso
