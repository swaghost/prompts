# Universal Football Kit Launch Poster - Auto Detection

## Overview

Premium vertical football kit launch poster system with automatic club and kit detection. Shows only the jersey floating naturally with no player, mannequin, or hanger. Background built exclusively from enlarged fragments of the same jersey. Commercial product presentation with luxury sports editorial lighting and automatic typography generation.

## Core Principle: REFERENCE IMAGE IS ABSOLUTE AUTHORITY

**The uploaded jersey is the ONLY reference.**

Everything in the reference overrides every written instruction.

### Automatic Preservation

The system automatically identifies and faithfully preserves:

**Jersey Details:**

- Club or national team identity
- Manufacturer (Nike, Adidas, Puma, etc.)
- Crest/badge
- Sponsors (chest, sleeve, back)
- Official badges (competition, champions, etc.)
- Colors (exact shades and gradients)
- Fabric pattern and texture
- Stitching details
- Collar style and design
- Sleeve trim and cuffs
- Shoulder details and panels
- Proportions and fit
- Silhouette and cut

**Never:**

- Replace any element
- Redesign any component
- Simplify details
- Invent any visible detail
- Change manufacturer
- Alter sponsor placement
- Modify colors or patterns
