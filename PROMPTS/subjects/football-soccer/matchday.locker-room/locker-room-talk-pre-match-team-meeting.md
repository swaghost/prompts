## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Looks staged or posed like team photo

**Solution:**

- Emphasize candid: "authentic documentary moment, NOT staged, NOT posed, fly-on-the-wall perspective, players unaware of camera"
- Add: "natural varied postures, NO one looking at camera, genuine pre-match focus"

### Problem: Reference face or Ronaldo face not accurate

**Solution:**

- Strengthen identity preservation: "preserve exact facial identity 100% for reference subject, Ronaldo instantly recognizable accurate features"
- For Midjourney: Use --cref with higher weight
- For multi-face consistency: May need to generate with focus on one face, then use image-to-image for second face accuracy

### Problem: Locker room too clean or fake-looking

**Solution:**

- Add authenticity: "authentic lived-in sports facility, scattered equipment, realistic details, NOT sterile showroom, natural wear"
- Details: "jerseys hanging, boots on floor, water bottles, tape rolls, towels, authentic preparation chaos"

### Problem: Lighting too perfect or studio-like

**Solution:**

- Realistic lighting: "natural overhead fluorescent lights, realistic uneven illumination, some shadows, documentary lighting NOT studio-perfect"
- Add: "institutional sports facility lights, natural light falloff, gritty realistic atmosphere"

### Problem: Too many players or composition cluttered

**Solution:**

- Simplify: "4-6 teammates total visible (NOT crowd), clear focus on reference subject and Ronaldo, others background context"
- Composition: "balanced documentary framing, intentional subject placement, natural depth"

### Problem: Wrong team colors or branding

**Solution:**

- Be explicit: "Portugal national team = red jerseys with green accents, Portugal team crest, FPF branding"
- Verify official team colors and crests before generation
