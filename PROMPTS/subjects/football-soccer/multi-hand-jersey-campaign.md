# Multi-Hand Jersey Campaign — Collective Team Identity

## Prompt Type

Sports campaign photography · Product visualization · Team identity · Overhead composition

## Overview

Premium cinematic sports campaign image featuring a dramatic top-down overhead composition where a large group of supporters forms a dense circular crowd around a single jersey held in the center by multiple hands. Creates the feeling of collective unveiling, celebration, and strong team identity.

## FULL PROMPT

Create a premium cinematic sports campaign image using the **uploaded jersey/shirt image as the absolute visual reference for the uniform**.

### MAIN CONCEPT

Create a dramatic **top-down overhead composition** where a large group of supporters, athletes or team members forms a dense circular crowd around a single jersey positioned exactly in the center of the image.

The central jersey is being physically held and presented by multiple hands coming from every direction, creating the feeling of a collective unveiling, celebration and strong team identity.

### CENTRAL HERO JERSEY

Place one complete jersey in the exact center of the composition.

Camera perspective:

**perfect 90-degree overhead top-down view**

The jersey should be displayed predominantly from the front unless the uploaded reference clearly requires another orientation.

Keep the jersey large and visually dominant.

Approximate composition:

- jersey occupies around 45–60% of the frame height
- centered vertically and horizontally
- entire garment visible
- realistic proportions
- no unnatural stretching
- no cropped collar
- no cropped sleeves
- no cropped hem

Multiple hands from the surrounding crowd should naturally hold:

- shoulder areas
- sleeves
- side seams
- lower hem

The hands must create natural fabric tension while still allowing the jersey design to remain clearly visible.

The central garment should feel slightly suspended above the people beneath it.

---

**EVERY visible person surrounding the central jersey must wear the exact same uniform design as the central jersey.**

This is mandatory.

The AI must automatically analyze the uploaded jersey and apply that same jersey design to every visible person in the surrounding crowd.

Match exactly:

- jersey colors
- collar
- sleeves
- cuffs
- patterns
- stripes
- logos
- crest

red basketball jersey → crowd wears the same red basketball jersey.

white baseball jersey → crowd wears the same white baseball jersey.

black esports jersey → crowd wears the same black esports jersey.

The uniform system must automatically adapt to whatever garment is uploaded.

There must be **no unrelated casual clothing** visible.

Avoid:

- random black shirts
- hoodies

- generic clothing

Any visible torso must communicate the **same team identity as the hero jersey**.

Adapt how the surrounding people wear it naturally according to the sport.

Examples:

Football / soccer:<br>standard football jersey fit.

Basketball:<br>sleeveless basketball jersey proportions.

Baseball:<br>button-front baseball jersey construction when applicable.

American football:<br>larger shoulder-area proportions appropriate to the uniform.

Hockey:<br>looser hockey jersey silhouette.

Rugby:<br>rugby shirt proportions and collar construction.

Esports:<br>modern performance esports jersey fit.

The garment type must remain authentic to the uploaded reference.

### CROWD COMPOSITION

Create approximately:

**12–18 visible people**

distributed tightly around the central jersey.

Only portions of many people should be visible because the camera is directly above them.

Show a natural mixture of:

- heads
- shoulders
- arms
- hands
- partial torsos

The crowd should form a dense organic ring.

People should naturally lean toward the center.

Many hands reach toward or physically support the hero jersey.

Some people may be partially hidden by others.

Avoid symmetrical cloning.

Every person should feel unique.

Use diverse:

- skin tones
- hairstyles
- ages within young-adult/adult range
- hand positions
- body positions

The image should feel like a real collective moment rather than duplicated AI characters.

### HANDS — HIGH PRIORITY

Hands are a major visual element.

- five fingers
- realistic joints
- believable grip
- natural finger pressure
- realistic contact with fabric
- correct perspective

Avoid:

- extra fingers
- fused fingers
- duplicated hands
- floating hands
- disconnected arms
- malformed anatomy

The hands holding the jersey must visibly affect the fabric through subtle folds and tension.

- realistic contact shadows
- subtle fabric sheen

The jersey must remain the clearest and most readable object.
