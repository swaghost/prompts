## Troubleshooting

### Looks Too Dark or Dramatic

**Problem**: Lighting too heavy, losing commercial clean look  
**Solution**:

- Add: "Balanced exposure, clean commercial lighting"
- Change "dramatic side light" to "elegant side light"
- Emphasize: "Clean commercial color grading"

### Lost the "Nike" Feel

**Problem**: Doesn't look like brand campaign  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize: "Luxury advertising aesthetics, Nike-style campaign"
- Add: "Aspirational, powerful, clean brand aesthetic"
- Reference: "High-end sports brand commercial quality"

### Fog Too Heavy

**Problem**: Subtle fog became thick haze  
**Solution**:

- Change "subtle fog" to "very minimal atmospheric depth"
- Add: "Clean visibility, not hazy"

### Muscle Definition Overdone

**Problem**: Looks overly sculpted or artificial  
**Solution**:

- Change "muscular definition" to "natural athletic build definition"
- Add: "Realistic, not exaggerated physique"

### Contrast Too Harsh

**Problem**: Shadows too dark, highlights too bright  
**Solution**:

- Change "premium contrast" to "balanced premium contrast"
- Add: "Rich shadows with detail preserved"
