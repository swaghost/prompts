# GIANT HAALAND

## Scene Description

Create an epic hyper-realistic cinematic scene of Erling Haaland transformed into a colossal giant towering above a completely sold-out football stadium.

## Character Details

Preserve his recognizable face, blonde slicked-back hair, muscular physique, and Norway national team home jersey with realistic fabric folds, authentic red, navy, and white colors, and highly detailed textures.

## Crowd & Environment

Surround him with tens of thousands of passionate Norway supporters waving Norwegian flags, wearing Norway national team jerseys, scarves, and face paint, while football players appear tiny beneath his feet.

## Visual Emphasis

Emphasize an incredible sense of scale, realistic proportions, detailed skin textures...
