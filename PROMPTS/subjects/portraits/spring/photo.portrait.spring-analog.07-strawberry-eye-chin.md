## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 intimate editorial portrait of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, naturally thick dark-brown eyebrows, a small straight nose and softly smiling muted rose lips. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw with softly curved ends, airy bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Preserve visible pores, fine skin texture, faint freckles, subtle cheek warmth and slight facial asymmetry.

She wears medium rounded gold hoop earrings, a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse with a broad pointed collar, an antique gold ring with a deep-red oval stone and a slim brown leather wristwatch. No trench coat. She gently presses one ripe strawberry against her closed eyelid while holding another strawberry close to her chin or lower cheek with the other hand. Her expression is serene and pleased, eyes closed, lips softly curved into a private smile.

Place her beneath a large tree in a peaceful park. Frame tightly from the upper chest to just above the headband. The background should dissolve into soft warm green-yellow lawn, olive tree shadow and a few blurred trunks. The strawberries should remain vivid but still natural, not oversaturated candy red.

Photograph with a Canon EOS R5 Mark II and 85mm f/1.2 lens from close eye level. Focus on the visible skin texture, lips, fingers and the strawberry near the cheek, allowing the far strawberry and background to soften gently.

Use soft late-afternoon sunlight filtered by tree branches. The light should be warm honey-gold but softened by shade, creating gentle amber highlights on the cheek, nose bridge and fingers. Greens must remain faded olive-yellow, skin peach-beige, blouse dusty salmon and shadows warm brown. Add low digital clarity, creamy highlight rolloff, subtle film halation and fine 35mm grain.

## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Composition

- Fair neutral-warm skin, softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, thick dark-brown brows
- Short dark-chocolate bob with airy bangs, wide cream headband
- Tight framing: upper chest to above headband
- One strawberry pressed gently against closed eyelid
- Another strawberry held close to chin/lower cheek
- Serene pleased expression, eyes closed, soft private smile

### Lighting & Color

- Soft late-afternoon sunlight filtered by tree branches
- Warm honey-gold softened by shade
- Gentle amber highlights on cheek, nose bridge, fingers
- Faded olive-yellow greens, peach-beige skin, dusty salmon blouse
- Warm brown shadows
- Strawberries naturally vivid (not oversaturated)

### Technical & Quality

- Canon EOS R5 Mark II, 85mm f/1.2, close eye level
- Focus on skin texture, lips, fingers, strawberry near cheek
- Background softens gently
- Low digital clarity, creamy highlight rolloff
- Subtle film halation, fine 35mm grain

### Restrictions

Avoid: oversaturated candy red strawberries, fresh bright green, harsh light, high digital clarity, beauty filters, open eyes, posed expression
