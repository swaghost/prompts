## Troubleshooting

### ⚠️ AI Filter Warning

**Potential Trigger Words**:

- "cropped tank top"
- "low-rise jeans"

**Alternative Phrasing** (if filtered):

- Replace with "fitted white tank top" and "relaxed blue jeans"

### Common Issues

**Static poses instead of walking**:

- Emphasize "dynamic, candid walking pose" and "relaxed stride"
- Add "mid-step" or "in motion" to prompt

**Car too prominent**:

- Ensure focus stays on "sharp focus on the walking subjects"
- Car should be contextual element, not main subject

**Too polished/studio look**:

- Reinforce "genuine lifestyle photograph" and "street-fashion snapshot"
- Add more emphasis on "subtle film grain" and "natural motion blur"

**Background too sharp**:

- Emphasize "natural motion blur on the background" with f/2.8
