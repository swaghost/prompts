**Grease looks fake or too clean**:

- Specify "glossy grease/oil smudges" and "black grease on cheeks and arms"

**Tools too generic or blurry**:

**Poses too stiff or staged**:

- Emphasize "candid photograph," "interacting naturally," "warm, intimate, playful"

**Background car too prominent**:

- Keep focus on "couple's faces and tool case"
- Car should be "soft background falloff"
