## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Angle too low or straight-on**:

- Emphasize "high-angle shot looking down at the subject"
- Specify "shot from above"

**Too posed or stiff**:

- Reinforce "relaxed pose" and "standing in a relaxed pose"
- Mention "natural, authentic posture"

**Car details not visible**:

- Specify "showcasing the taillights" and "circular red taillights and dual chrome exhaust tips"
- Position subject "next to the rear corner of the car"

**Lighting too dramatic**:

- Emphasize "even, soft overhead daylight" and "natural shadows"
- Avoid harsh or directional lighting language

**Too clean/CGI looking**:

- Add emphasis on "textured concrete pavement" and "subtle grain"
- Specify "real-photograph look"
