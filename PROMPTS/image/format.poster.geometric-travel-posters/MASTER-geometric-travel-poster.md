# Master Geometric Travel Poster Template

## Description of Resulting Image

A sophisticated vertical landmark-led geometric art poster for any city. The real destination remains instantly recognizable while restrained circles, arches, stepped blocks, and flat color fields organize the surrounding architecture into a collectible museum-shop print.

## Usage

Perfect for travel art prints, gallery walls, tourism campaigns, hotel interiors, editorial city guides, and destination branding. Ideal for creating a coherent multi-city series with one shared visual system. This prompt excels at balancing recognizable architecture with contemporary geometric simplification.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or another text-to-image model.

## Actor or Subject

The recognizable landmarks and architectural identity of [CITY NAME].

## Prerequisites

None - generates from description. Fill in the city, landmark-shape, supporting-elements, and palette slots.

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** City-specific natural daylight or seasonal light

**Photographic Effects:** None; illustrated museum-print treatment

**Atmospheric Effects:** Simplified haze and layered color depth

**Post-Processing:** Gouache, paper fiber, dry-brush, colored-pencil, or silkscreen texture

**Style Filters:** Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, fine-art travel poster

## Included Camera Frames

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Illustrated composition, not photographic
- **Framing & Composition:** Vertical poster, quiet upper 40-50% sky, cityscape below
- **Camera Angle:** Landmark-appropriate architectural viewpoint
- **Depth of Field:** Flat graphic layers with depth suggested by scale and color
- **Aspect Ratio:** 2:3 or 3:4 portrait
- **Shot Distance:** Wide destination view

## Image Prompt

```text
REUSABLE TEMPLATE - DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - [CITY NAME]

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring [CITY NAME]. Use a 2:3 or 3:4 high-resolution editorial poster format with generous negative space and a stable vertical composition.

CORE CONCEPT: LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. First clearly depict the iconic landmarks that make [CITY NAME] instantly recognizable, then apply geometric simplification on top of that recognizable foundation. Recognizable landmark first, artistic simplification second. The viewer should immediately think: "This is [CITY NAME]."

LANDMARK PRIORITY: Primary landmarks, the protagonists: [ONE OR TWO LANDMARKS DESCRIBED BY DISTINCTIVE SHAPE]. Secondary elements arranged around them: [THREE TO FIVE SUPPORTING ELEMENTS - rooftops, river, bridges, hills, trees, street pattern]. Keep the main landmarks at approximately 70% recognizable real-world form and simplify only unnecessary detail.

COMPOSITION: Keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. At the upper center, place only [CITY NAME] in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif typography with wide letter spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower portion, with the main landmark leading the eye.

COLOR PALETTE: [NAME THE LIGHT OR SEASON, THEN SIX TO EIGHT SPECIFIC MUTED COLORS]. Use a limited sophisticated 6-10 color palette with a museum-print or gouache feel and no oversaturation.

TEXTURE: Add subtle paper fiber, dry brush, gouache, colored-pencil, or silkscreen texture. Allow pigment irregularity and slight handmade variation. Do not use aged paper, sepia, yellowed backgrounds, or smooth digital-vector surfaces.

STYLE: Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural illustration, fine-art museum-shop print.

BALANCE: Geometry organizes but never dominates. Preserve the landmark silhouette, proportions, and defining architectural characteristics. At thumbnail size, [CITY NAME] must be immediately recognizable.

CONSTRAINTS: Exact spelling "[CITY NAME]". No other text beyond that single destination name. No slogans, subtitles, over-abstraction, giant circles dominating, photorealism, cartoon styling, 3D rendering, neon, gold, vintage filters, watermark, or logos.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3 or 3:4
- **Hierarchy:** 70% recognizable landmark and architecture, 30% geometric abstraction
- **Typography:** Destination name only, small uppercase at top
- **Palette:** Six to ten muted city-specific colors
- **Finish:** Contemporary gouache, museum print, subtle handmade texture

## Quality Keywords

Landmark-led, recognizable city silhouette, geometric abstraction, contemporary travel poster, Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometry, gouache texture, museum-shop print, quiet negative space, refined color blocking.

## Negative Prompt

No unrecognizable landmarks, generic skyline, giant circles dominating, excessive Bauhaus abstraction, photorealism, cartoon, 3D render, neon colors, gold decoration, vintage brown filter, sepia, yellowed paper, complex typography, slogans, subtitles, watermark, logos, clutter.
