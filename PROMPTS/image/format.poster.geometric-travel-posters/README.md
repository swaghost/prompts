# Geometric Travel Posters

A landmark-led fine-art city poster system for GPT Image 2 and Picsart Flow. The collection uses a strict **70/30 balance**: 70% recognizable real landmark and architecture, 30% restrained geometric abstraction and color blocking.

## One-Second Test

At thumbnail size, the viewer should immediately recognize the city. If the geometry becomes the first thing seen, reduce it and restore landmark detail.

## Shared Rules

- Vertical 2:3 or 3:4 poster
- Destination name only, small and refined at the top
- Landmark silhouette and proportions remain dominant
- Six to ten muted colors tied to the city's real light
- Subtle gouache, paper-fiber, colored-pencil, or silkscreen texture
- No photorealism, 3D rendering, neon, gold decoration, vintage brown filters, or extra copy
