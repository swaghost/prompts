# Geometric Travel Poster - London

## Description of Resulting Image

An overcast London travel poster led by Elizabeth Tower and Tower Bridge, with St Paul's dome, the Thames, brick terraces, and muted British color fields.

## Usage

Perfect for London travel art, museum and hotel decor, tourism campaigns, editorial city guides, and collectible prints. Ideal for a recognizable architectural composition with cool overcast light and restrained warm accents.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - LONDON

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring London. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict Elizabeth Tower with its clock face and Tower Bridge with its twin Gothic towers. Preserve their silhouettes and defining architectural details.

Arrange the dome of St Paul's Cathedral, the Thames, brick terraces with chimney pots, a red double-decker bus, and plane trees around the landmarks. Geometry may simplify the river, rooftops, streets and trees but must not dominate the architecture.

Keep the upper 40-50% as quiet overcast sky. Place only LONDON at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide letter spacing. Build the cityscape below.

Draw from overcast London light: soft grey-blue, warm Portland stone, muted brick red, deep green, pale cream, dusty slate. Use subtle handmade gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean museum-shop print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "LONDON" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
