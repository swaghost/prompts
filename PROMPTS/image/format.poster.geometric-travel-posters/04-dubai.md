# Geometric Travel Poster - Dubai

## Description of Resulting Image

A warm desert-haze Dubai travel poster led by the tapering Burj Khalifa and sail-shaped Burj Al Arab, surrounded by glass towers, palms, gulf water, and restrained color fields.

## Usage

Perfect for Dubai tourism prints, luxury hotel interiors, architectural campaigns, editorial city guides, and collectible wall art. Ideal for combining iconic skyscraper silhouettes with desert and coastal atmosphere.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - DUBAI

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Dubai. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the tapering spire of the Burj Khalifa and the sail-shaped Burj Al Arab. Preserve their distinctive silhouettes and let them dominate the composition.

Arrange a cluster of glass towers, flat desert horizon, palm trees, calm Gulf water, and a low dune line around them. Use geometric blocks and arches to organize the supporting landscape, never to replace the landmarks.

Keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or pale heat haze. Place only DUBAI at the upper center in small uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing. Build the cityscape below with restrained geometry.

Draw from desert heat haze: warm sand, pale gold, soft apricot, mineral blue, dusty turquoise, off-white. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary fine-art print, not vintage or sepia. Exact spelling "DUBAI" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
