# Geometric Travel Poster - Venice

## Description of Resulting Image

A soft-lagoon Venice poster led by St Mark's brick campanile and the basilica domes, with canals, gondolas, rose plaster, mooring poles, and restrained geometric layers.

## Usage

Perfect for Venice travel prints, hotel and restaurant decor, tourism campaigns, editorial city guides, and collectible fine-art posters. Ideal for making the campanile and domes readable while simplifying water and surrounding facades.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

## Actor or Subject

St Mark's campanile and basilica domes.

## Prerequisites

None - generates from description.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - VENICE

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Venice. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the tall brick campanile of St Mark's and the domes of the basilica beside it. Preserve their height, shape and recognizable relationship.

Arrange a canal with gondolas, ochre and rose plaster facades with peeling texture, pointed arched windows, striped mooring poles, and low bridges around the landmarks. Use geometric forms only to organize water, facades, and negative space.

Keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only VENICE at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing. Build the canal city below.

Draw from soft lagoon light: faded rose, warm ochre, muted terracotta, pale lagoon green, dusty blue, cream white. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "VENICE" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3 or 3:4
- **Palette:** Rose, ochre, terracotta, lagoon green, dusty blue, cream
- **Balance:** Campanile and basilica domes remain dominant
- **Typography:** VENICE only
