# Geometric Travel Poster - Tokyo

## Description of Resulting Image

A pale spring-morning Tokyo travel poster led by Tokyo Tower's red-and-white lattice and the tapering Skytree, with shrine, low-rise city, Fuji, blossoms, and restrained geometric organization.

## Usage

Perfect for Tokyo travel prints, cultural campaigns, hotel decor, editorial city art, and collectible modern posters. Ideal for preserving the two tower silhouettes while simplifying dense urban context.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Tokyo Tower and Tokyo Skytree.

## Prerequisites

None - generates from description.

**Style Filters:** Scandinavian editorial, mid-century geometric, museum-shop print

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - TOKYO

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Tokyo. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the red-and-white lattice of Tokyo Tower and the tall tapering Tokyo Skytree. Preserve their true recognizable silhouettes.

Arrange a vermilion shrine gate, dense low-rise blocks, Mount Fuji faint on the horizon, cherry-blossom branches, and elevated train lines around the towers. Simplify supporting elements with restrained circles, rectangles, arches and stepped color blocks.

Reserve the upper 40-50% for quiet spring sky. Place only TOKYO at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif typography with wide spacing. Cityscape and landmarks occupy the lower portion.

Draw from a pale spring morning: soft blossom pink, muted vermilion, pale sky blue, warm concrete grey, deep indigo, off-white. Use subtle paper fiber, gouache, dry-brush, colored-pencil and silkscreen texture, with no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "TOKYO" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3 or 3:4
- **Palette:** Blossom pink, vermilion, sky blue, concrete grey, indigo, off-white
- **Balance:** Tokyo Tower and Skytree remain dominant and recognizable
- **Typography:** TOKYO only
