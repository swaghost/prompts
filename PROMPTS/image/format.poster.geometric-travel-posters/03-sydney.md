# Geometric Travel Poster - Sydney

## Description of Resulting Image

A bright southern-daylight Sydney poster led by the Opera House's overlapping white shells and the steel arch of the Harbour Bridge, with harbour water and restrained geometric layers.

## Usage

Perfect for Sydney travel prints, coastal tourism, hotel decor, editorial city art, and collectible destination posters. Ideal for keeping the Opera House and bridge unmistakable while simplifying boats, foliage, and the skyline.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

Sydney Opera House and Harbour Bridge.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - SYDNEY

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Sydney. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the Opera House with its overlapping white shell forms and the great steel arch of the Harbour Bridge. Keep both silhouettes and proportions unmistakable.

Arrange harbour water, small sailing boats, the downtown tower cluster, eucalyptus foliage, and a low headland around the primary landmarks. Geometry may simplify water, boats, foliage and distant buildings but must never overpower the Opera House or bridge.

Keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky. Place only SYDNEY at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Use restrained circles, arches, rectangles and layered color blocks in the lower cityscape.

Draw from bright southern daylight: clear sky blue, deep harbour blue, chalk white, soft sand, muted eucalyptus green, faint coral. Add subtle paper fiber, gouache, dry-brush, colored-pencil and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum-shop print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "SYDNEY" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
