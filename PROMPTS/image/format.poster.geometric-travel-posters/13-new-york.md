# Geometric Travel Poster - New York

## Description of Resulting Image

A crisp-autumn New York travel poster led by the Empire State Building and Chrysler Building crown, with Brooklyn Bridge, stepped skyscraper blocks, rooftop water tanks, and river context.

Perfect for New York travel art, hotel and office decor, tourism campaigns, editorial city guides, and collectible urban prints. Ideal for preserving the two iconic skyline profiles while simplifying dense Manhattan context.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - NEW YORK

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring New York. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the Empire State Building and the stepped metal crown of the Chrysler Building. Preserve their height, proportions and unmistakable skyline profiles.

Arrange the cables and stone towers of the Brooklyn Bridge, dense stepped skyscraper blocks, wooden rooftop water tanks, and the river around the landmarks. Simplify only supporting architecture with restrained rectangles, steps, arches and color fields.

Reserve the upper 40-50% for quiet crisp sky. Place only NEW YORK at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing.

Draw from a crisp autumn morning: cool slate blue, warm limestone, muted brick red, steel grey, pale cream, soft amber. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "NEW YORK" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
