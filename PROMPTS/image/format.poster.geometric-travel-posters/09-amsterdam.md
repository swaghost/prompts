# Geometric Travel Poster - Amsterdam

## Description of Resulting Image

A soft northern-daylight Amsterdam poster led by a row of narrow gabled canal houses, with arched bridges, houseboats, elm trees, and bicycles arranged through quiet geometry.

Perfect for Amsterdam travel art, hotel decor, tourism campaigns, editorial city guides, and collectible prints. Ideal for making the canal-house silhouette carry the destination identity.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - AMSTERDAM

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Amsterdam. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict a row of tall narrow canal houses with stepped and bell-shaped gables. Preserve their varied narrow proportions and recognizable canal-front rhythm.

Arrange arched canal bridges, slightly leaning facades, moored houseboats, elm trees along the water, and bicycles against a railing around them. Simplify only supporting elements with restrained arches, rectangles and layered color fields.

Reserve the upper 40-50% for quiet northern sky. Place only AMSTERDAM at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing. Build the canal city below.

Draw from soft northern daylight: muted brick red, deep canal green, warm cream, slate blue, dusty olive, pale grey. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "AMSTERDAM" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
