# Geometric Travel Poster - Seoul

## Description of Resulting Image

A clear-autumn-light Seoul poster led by N Seoul Tower on Namsan and the tiled roofs of Gyeongbokgung Palace, with Han River bridges, towers, pines, and restrained geometric layering.

## Usage

Perfect for Seoul travel prints, cultural tourism campaigns, hotel decor, editorial city art, and collectible destination posters. Ideal for combining a modern tower with historic palace roofs in one recognizable composition.

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

N Seoul Tower and Gyeongbokgung Palace.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - SEOUL

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Seoul. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict N Seoul Tower on the wooded slope of Namsan mountain and the curved tiled roofs of Gyeongbokgung Palace. Keep both the modern tower and palace roofline unmistakable.

Arrange the Han River with long low bridges, glass towers, granite ridgeline, pine trees, and palace courtyard walls around the landmarks. Use geometry to simplify supporting city layers while preserving the tower and palace architecture.

Reserve the upper 40-50% for quiet clear autumn sky. Place only SEOUL at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing.

Draw from clear autumn light: pale sky blue, muted teal, warm granite grey, soft cream, deep pine green, dusty coral. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "SEOUL" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
