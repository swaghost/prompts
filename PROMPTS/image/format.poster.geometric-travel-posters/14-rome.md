# Geometric Travel Poster - Rome

## Description of Resulting Image

A warm Roman-stone travel poster led by the Colosseum's tiered arches and the dome of St Peter's Basilica, with umbrella pines, terracotta facades, columns, and the Tiber arranged in restrained geometry.

## Usage

Perfect for Rome travel prints, cultural campaigns, hotel and restaurant decor, editorial city guides, and collectible fine-art posters. Ideal for making ancient architecture remain clear while simplifying the surrounding historic city.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Colosseum and St Peter's dome.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - ROME

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Rome. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the tiered arches of the Colosseum and the dome of St Peter's Basilica. Preserve their ancient silhouettes and make them visually dominant.

Arrange umbrella pines with flat spreading canopies, ochre and terracotta facades, ancient columns, the Tiber river, and low stone bridges around the landmarks. Use restrained arches, rectangles and layered fields to organize supporting elements without reducing the landmarks to primitives.

Keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky. Place only ROME at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing. Build the historic cityscape in the lower portion.

Draw from golden Roman stone: warm ochre, faded terracotta, dusty sienna, pale cream, deep pine green, soft sky blue. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "ROME" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
