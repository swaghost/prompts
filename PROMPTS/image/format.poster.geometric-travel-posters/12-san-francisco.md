# Geometric Travel Poster - San Francisco

## Description of Resulting Image

A fog-and-late-light San Francisco poster led by the Golden Gate Bridge and Transamerica Pyramid, with steep hills, Victorian houses, cable tracks, and layered bay haze.

## Usage

Perfect for San Francisco travel art, tourism campaigns, hotel decor, editorial city guides, and collectible architectural prints. Ideal for balancing the bridge's red towers with cool fog and urban hills.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Golden Gate Bridge and Transamerica Pyramid.

## Prerequisites

None - generates from description.

**Lighting Effects:** Late light filtered through fog

**Atmospheric Effects:** Low bay fog and layered haze

**Post-Processing:** Gouache, dry-brush, paper fiber and silkscreen texture

**Style Filters:** Landmark-led museum-shop travel print

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - SAN FRANCISCO

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring San Francisco. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the Golden Gate Bridge with its tall red towers and the Transamerica Pyramid. Preserve the bridge silhouette and pyramid profile as the visual protagonists.

Arrange steep hills, rows of narrow painted Victorian houses, low fog rolling across the bay, cable-car tracks running downhill, and cypress trees around them. Simplify only supporting elements with restrained arches, rectangles and layered color fields.

Keep the upper 40-50% as quiet foggy sky. Place only SAN FRANCISCO at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing.

Draw from fog and late light: soft grey-white, muted vermilion, pale mint, dusty rose, cool bay blue, warm cream. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "SAN FRANCISCO" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
