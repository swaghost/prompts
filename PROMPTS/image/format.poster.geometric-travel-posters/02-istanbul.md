# Geometric Travel Poster - Istanbul

## Description of Resulting Image

A landmark-led Istanbul travel poster featuring Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque above the Bosphorus, with hazy mineral colors and restrained geometric simplification.

## Usage

Perfect for Istanbul travel prints, cultural tourism campaigns, hotel and restaurant decor, and editorial destination art. Ideal for balancing domes and minarets with water, ferry, and hillside context.

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - ISTANBUL

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Istanbul with LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict Hagia Sophia with its broad central dome and the Blue Mosque with its six slender minarets. Preserve their real silhouettes and proportions; simplify only the surrounding city.

Arrange the Bosphorus strait, a passenger ferry, dark cypress trees, terraced rooftops descending to the water, and distant domed silhouettes around the landmarks. Keep the domes and minarets visually dominant and immediately recognizable.

Reserve the upper 40-50% for quiet hazy sky. Place only ISTANBUL at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing. Use restrained geometric arches, semicircles, stepped blocks and color fields to organize the strait, hills and rooftops.

Draw from a hazy Bosphorus afternoon: mineral blue, dusty teal, warm sandstone, muted coral, deep ultramarine, pale cream. Add subtle handmade gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum print, not aged or sepia. Exact spelling "ISTANBUL" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3 or 3:4
- **Palette:** Mineral blue, dusty teal, sandstone, coral, ultramarine, cream
- **Balance:** Hagia Sophia and Blue Mosque remain 70% recognizable
- **Typography:** ISTANBUL only
