# Geometric Travel Poster - Paris

## Description of Resulting Image

A refined vertical Paris travel poster led by the Eiffel Tower and Sacre-Coeur, with cool Parisian daylight, Haussmann rooftops, and restrained geometric color fields.

## Usage

Perfect for Paris travel art, hotel decor, editorial city guides, tourism campaigns, and collectible museum-shop prints. Ideal for a recognizable landmark composition that uses geometry only to organize the surrounding city.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Eiffel Tower and Sacre-Coeur basilica on its hill.

None - generates from description.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - PARIS

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Paris. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%: first depict the Eiffel Tower with its unmistakable open iron lattice silhouette and Sacre-Coeur basilica with its white domes on the hill, then simplify only the surrounding city.

Arrange Haussmann rooftops in grey zinc with mansard slopes and chimney stacks, the Seine crossed by low stone arch bridges, plane trees along the embankment, and wrought-iron balconies around the landmarks. Keep the Eiffel Tower and Sacre-Coeur visually dominant and recognizable at thumbnail size.

Reserve the upper 40-50% for quiet cool sky. At the upper center place only PARIS in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape below using restrained circles, arches, rectangles, stepped rooftops and layered color blocks.

Draw from cool Parisian daylight: pale sky blue, soft grey-lilac, zinc grey, warm cream stone, muted rose, faded olive. Use subtle paper fiber, gouache, dry-brush, colored-pencil and silkscreen texture, never smooth vector polish. Contemporary museum-shop print, Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art. Exact spelling "PARIS" and no other text. No slogan, subtitle, photorealism, 3D, neon, gold, vintage brown filter, watermark, or logo.
```
