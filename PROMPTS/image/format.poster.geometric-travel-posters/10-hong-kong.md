# Geometric Travel Poster - Hong Kong

## Description of Resulting Image

A humid-harbour Hong Kong poster led by dense harbour skyscrapers and green Victoria Peak, with ferry, junk boat, water, and layered ridgelines in muted geometry.

## Usage

Perfect for Hong Kong travel art, tourism campaigns, hotel interiors, editorial city guides, and collectible architectural prints. Ideal for preserving the dense vertical city-versus-mountain relationship.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, or Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Harbour-front skyscrapers and Victoria Peak.

## Prerequisites

None - generates from description.

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** Humid harbour daylight

**Photographic Effects:** None; fine-art illustration

**Atmospheric Effects:** Misty blue-grey harbour haze

**Post-Processing:** Gouache, dry-brush, paper fiber and silkscreen texture

**Style Filters:** Landmark-led contemporary travel poster

## Included Camera Frames

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Illustrated wide harbour skyline
- **Framing & Composition:** Vertical poster with open sky
- **Camera Angle:** Wide view across harbour toward peak
- **Depth of Field:** Layered graphic haze
- **Aspect Ratio:** 2:3 or 3:4 portrait
- **Shot Distance:** Wide destination view

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - HONG KONG

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Hong Kong. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the dense wall of harbour-front skyscrapers and Victoria Peak rising green behind them. Preserve the vertical city-and-mountain relationship.

Arrange the green hillside, a Star Ferry crossing the harbour, a junk boat with red battened sails, harbour water, and layered ridgelines around the landmarks. Simplify supporting towers and water with restrained blocks, stepped shapes and layered fields, never replacing the main skyline or peak.

Keep the upper 40-50% as quiet humid sky. Place only HONG KONG at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing. Build the harbour city below.

Draw from humid harbour haze: misty blue-grey, deep jade green, muted teal, warm cream, soft coral, pale silver. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "HONG KONG" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3 or 3:4
- **Palette:** Blue-grey, jade, teal, cream, coral, silver
- **Balance:** Harbour skyline and Victoria Peak remain dominant
- **Typography:** HONG KONG only
