# Geometric Travel Poster - Barcelona

## Description of Resulting Image

A Mediterranean Barcelona poster led by the sculpted spires of Sagrada Familia and the undulating tiled facade of Casa Batllo, with sea, palms, terracotta roofs, and muted geometric fields.

## Usage

Perfect for Barcelona travel prints, cultural tourism, architectural campaigns, hotel decor, and editorial city art. Ideal for preserving Gaudi's distinctive silhouettes while simplifying the surrounding city.

## Image Prompt

```text
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - BARCELONA

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster featuring Barcelona. Maintain LANDMARK 70% + GEOMETRIC ABSTRACTION 30%. Clearly depict the tapering sculpted spires of Sagrada Familia and the undulating tiled facade of Casa Batllo. Keep both forms specific, dominant and instantly recognizable.

Arrange the Mediterranean sea, palm trees, terracotta rooftops, Montjuic hill, and a grid of block corners cut at an angle around the landmarks. Simplify only these supporting elements with arches, circles, rectangles and layered fields.

Reserve the upper 40-50% for spacious Mediterranean sky. Place only BARCELONA at the upper center in small refined uppercase thin sans-serif type with wide spacing. Build the city below.

Draw from Mediterranean warmth: terracotta, muted coral, warm cream, sea blue, pale mint, soft ochre. Add subtle gouache, dry-brush, paper-fiber and silkscreen texture. Clean contemporary museum print, no aged paper or sepia. Exact spelling "BARCELONA" and no other text. No slogan, photorealism, cartoon, 3D, neon, gold, watermark, or logo.
```
