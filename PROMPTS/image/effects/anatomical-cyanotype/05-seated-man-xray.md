# Seated Man Anatomical Cyanotype

Moody monochromatic cyanotype of a man sitting cross-legged on textured asphalt, three-quarter view, head turned slightly away, dark curly hair, sunglasses, simple T-shirt and rugged jeans. A semitransparent X-ray overlay aligns the skull, spine, rib cage, arms, and clasped hands with his casual seated posture. Soft side light, dark soft-focus background, natural arm and neck texture, curly hair detail, vintage large-format camera look, watercolor paper grain, natural vignetting, deep indigo and steel blue, 3:4.

Negative: bright modern colors, digital rendering, smooth plastic textures, extra limbs, clean digital border.
