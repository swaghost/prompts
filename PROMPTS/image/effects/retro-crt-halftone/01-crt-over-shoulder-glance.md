# CRT Halftone Over-Shoulder Portrait

Retro-cyberpunk photograph of a young woman displayed on a curved vintage 1990s CRT monitor, looking over her bare shoulder with a direct backward gaze. Render the face entirely as a fine hot-pink LED-like halftone dot grid; vary dot density and brightness to preserve anatomically correct eyes, nose, lips and 3D shading. Long dark hair with sparse glowing-dot wisps, thin chain necklace, dark curved glass bezel in complete darkness. Macro lens, scanlines, moire, chromatic aberration, screen dust, film grain, phosphor bloom, 9:16.

Negative: flat vector, clean digital art, modern LCD, unrelated colors, text, watermark, CGI.
