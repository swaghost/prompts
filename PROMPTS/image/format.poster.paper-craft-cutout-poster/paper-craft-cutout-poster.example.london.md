# London Papercraft Letter Diorama Poster

## Positive Prompt

```text
A high-detail 3D papercraft diorama poster featuring clean, sharp, perfectly formed cutout letters spelling "LON" on a warm cream paper background with a subtle heavy-stock paper-grain texture. Straight-on eye-level composition, lit by soft directional studio light from the top-left, casting soft drop shadows along the inner cut edges. Aspect ratio 3:4.

Atmospheric sky and textures: The internal backdrop behind the buildings features a soft paper-textured gradient sky transitioning from warm, muted dusk pink/coral at the horizon to delicate pale blue at the top. Apply rich physical paper textures throughout: embossed stone grain on bridge masonry and building facades, fibrous textured cardstock on trees and foliage, visible paper grain on riverbank walls, and finely layered papercraft textures on the horse figurine.

Orthographic facades and spatial layering: All buildings face straight forward in flat, upright frontal elevation, avoiding rotational angles and keystoning. Create depth through progressive scale and layering rather than skewed building geometry: foreground structures are larger, while background buildings are smaller and recessed on flat parallel planes. The River Thames meanders diagonally in an S curve through the scene with layered, ripple-textured slate-blue paper waves.

Internal diorama scene, London landmarks:
- "L": Big Ben and Westminster facades, straight-on and elevated above a paper stone bridge and river boat, set against the soft dusk-gradient sky.
- "O": St. Paul's Cathedral and the London Eye in flat-facing background layers, with Tower Bridge on the foreground water plane.
- "N": Upright, flat-facing Georgian townhouse facades arranged along a textured rising diagonal embankment, with a red double-decker bus.

Foreground elements and text: At the bottom right, a 3D origami-style paper draft horse with dark leather harness details and visible paper-grain craftsmanship stands in profile facing left. Clean dark-gray sans-serif typography at the bottom left reads "London" in bold, with "United Kingdom" underneath.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
flat white sky, monochrome sky, untextured surfaces, smooth plastic texture, CGI gloss, tilted buildings, rotated facades, wide-angle distortion, keystoning, angled building planes, misshapen letters, warped typography, extra letters, text wrapping, curved letter frames, overly deep diorama, harsh black shadows, oversaturated colors, neon hues, floating objects, cluttered background, low resolution, blur, watermark
```

## Key Controls

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical poster
- **Camera:** Straight-on eye-level, orthographic-style facades
- **Lighting:** Soft top-left studio direction; soft cut-edge shadows
- **Depth:** Layered paper planes and scale progression, never tilted architecture
- **Material:** Heavy-stock cream paper, cardstock fiber, embossed masonry, layered waves
