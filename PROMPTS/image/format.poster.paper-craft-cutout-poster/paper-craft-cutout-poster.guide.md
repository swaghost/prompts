# **Papercraft Cityscape & Horse Diorama Style Guide**

### **Overall Concept**

The style is a sophisticated, 3D papercraft diorama poster. A multi-layered scene, often of a famous city and its landmarks, is viewed through large, clean, die-cut typography in the upper-middle of the frame. This creates a powerful foreground/background contrast, where the paper itself acts as a frame for the intricate, miniature world within. The entire composition is built from finely textured, cut-and-folded paper models. A central, prominent, paper-modeled horse is positioned on the solid paper foreground. Text at the bottom left identifies the location.

### **1. Structure and Dimensions**

- **Composition:** Vertical portrait poster (e.g., 9:16).
- **Upper-Middle Zone:** Dominated by 3-4 large, die-cut uppercase letters spelling a key part of the city's name or its initials (e.g., "LON", "SYD", "HKG").
- **Lower-Middle Zone:** Features a prominent, central, standing paper-modeled horse.
- **Bottom Left Zone:** Clear typography in two lines. Top line: City Name. Bottom line: Country/Region.

### **2. Die-Cut Typography (Main Feature)**

- **Letter Formation:** Large, geometric, block uppercase letters. Letters are exceptionally clean and precisely cut.
- **Letters Used:** 3-4 letters are standard. They can be consecutive (SYD), or key letters from the name (HKG for Hong Kong), or a short name (ROM, PAR, RAK, IST, MAD, DEL).
- **Depth & Shadow:** The inner edges of the cutouts are deep and cast soft, but distinct, inner shadows. This is crucial for the "window" effect and defining the depth of the diorama within.
- **Cut Precision:** Absolutely sharp, like die-cut paper, with no fraying. This creates a powerful frame.

### **3. Intricate Diorama Scene (Within the Cutouts)**

- **Model Style:** Fine paper models of architectural landmarks, vehicles (trains, ferries, buses), landscapes (mountains, trees, water), and small, proportional figures (people, animals).
- **Architectural Elements:** Specific and highly accurate miniatures of iconic landmarks from the city. They should be arranged to fit perfectly within the letters.
- **Landscape Elements:** Paper water features (often blue/turquoise) with cut-ripple effects; paper trees (green) with varied textures; paper hills/mountains (rock-colored, like Nuku Hiva).
- **Composition within the Cutouts:** The scene should flow cohesively across all letters, creating a continuous miniature world. Background elements are further back, foreground elements closer, all within the diorama's depth. Soft clouds or distant views can form the deepest layer.
- **Specific Examples:** "LON" must have Big Ben and Tower Bridge. "SYD" must have the Opera House and Harbour Bridge. "HKG" must have the skyline and Star Ferry.

### **4. Foreground: Paper-Modeled Horse (Central Feature)**

- **Placement:** Placed on the main foreground paper surface, below the die-cut letters. It should be large and detailed.
- **Style:** A highly detailed, geometric, folded-paper sculpture of a standing or walking horse (often a draft horse type). The facets of the folds should be clearly visible.
- **Saddle & Tack:** Crucial detailed element. Must feature a detailed paper-modeled saddle and bridle. The saddle often has complex, intricate, miniature embossed or painted patterns and colors.
- **Pattern Examples:** Traditional woven designs, floral motifs, or symbolic patterns (e.g., the gold floral pattern for Marrakech, the blue/gold pattern for Istanbul, the red medallion for Hong Kong).
- **Horse Colors:** The horse itself is usually light, such as cream or light gray.

### **5. Colors and Textures**

- **Primary Foreground Paper:** A solid, warm cream or very light beige paper surface. It should have a subtle, visible paper grain texture.
- **Interior Scene Colors:** Saturated but natural paper colors for specific elements (blue water, green trees, red bus, tan stone). The background is often a soft, muted sky gradient (like dusk pink to blue).
- **Tack Colors:** Richer, accented colors for the saddle and bridle (dark reds, deep blues, forest greens) to contrast with the horse.
- **Paper Textures:** Visible paper grain throughout the entire composition, especially on large surfaces like the horse and main poster frame. Edges should show the clean cut-paper effect.

### **6. Bottom Text (Identification)**

- **Location:** Bottom left of the poster.
- **Structure:** Two-line layout.
- **Line 1 (City Name):** Clean, modern, sans-serif font (like Helvetica or Inter). Bold weight. Large size. Crisp, dark charcoal gray or black color. (e.g., "**London**").
- **Line 2 (Country/Region):** Same font family, same color. Regular weight. Smaller size (approximately 60-70% of Line 1). Directly below Line 1. (e.g., "United Kingdom").
- **Alignment:** Strict left alignment.

### **7. Visual Angle, Lighting, and Perspective**

- **Angle:** A direct, straight-on, eye-level camera perspective. Not tilted.
- **Perspective:** Shallow depth of field for the interior diorama. The cutout letters are in sharp focus, and the miniature world within them has a strong sense of depth.
- **Lighting:** Soft, directional, studio light from the upper-left. This light casts the critical, soft, internal shadows of the die-cut edges and highlights the paper folds on the horse and buildings.

### **8. Elements to Avoid**

- **Flat Surfaces:** Avoid anything looking flat; everything must have a sense of paper thickness or depth.
- **Smooth Surfaces:** Avoid smooth, plastic-like textures. All paper must have a subtle, tactile grain.
- **Complex Backgrounds:** The background of the poster itself (outside the cutouts) must be simple, solid paper.
- **Extra Letters/Text:** The cutout typography must only be 3-4 letters. Avoid text wrapping.

### **Example Template Construction (e.g., Chicago)**

1. **Framing:** Use a 9:16 warm cream paper background.
2. **Cutout:** Place large, bold, geometric die-cut letters "CHI" in the upper-middle, making deep, shadow-casting edges.
3. **Diorama:** Inside "CHI," create a paper miniature scene of Chicago: the Willis Tower, the Cloud Gate sculpture, a section of the L-train, and Lake Michigan with paper waves.
4. **Foreground:** Place a detailed, geometric paper-modeled draft horse on the foreground surface. Dress it in a saddle with a intricate patterned gold and blue design (inspired by early 20th-century Chicago styles).
5. **Text:** In the bottom left, place "**Chicago**" (large, bold, charcoal sans-serif) with "United States" (smaller, regular sans-serif) below it.
6. **Details:** Add a few small, cut-paper leaves or elements around the upper edges (like the eucalyptus branch in Sydney or leaves in Hong Kong). Ensure lighting is from the top-left to create the key shadows.
