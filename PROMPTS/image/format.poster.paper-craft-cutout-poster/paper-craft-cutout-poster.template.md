How to Use the Template (Example: Tokyo)

A highly detailed 3D layered paper-cutout composition depicting a vibrant Tokyo.

The foreground is set on a solid cream-colored paper base. Standing on this base is a precise, geometric paper-sculpture of a Shiba Inu dog looking to the right. The animal is cream-colored and features a stylized saddle colored red and gold with small brass buckles.

Dominating the center are large, bold, die-cut uppercase letters spelling TKY. These letters are cut directly through the cream foreground paper, revealing a deeply recessionary miniature world inside.

The inside of the text cutouts reveals a sprawling, multi-layered miniature paper cityscape. It includes iconic landmarks such as Tokyo Tower, the Shibuya Crossing buildings, the Senso-ji Temple gate, and Mount Fuji in the background. The layered elements feature small traditional wooden houses, a paper bullet train on elevated tracks, stylized cherry blossom trees, and layered paper waves for Tokyo Bay.

The furthest background layer, visible at the very top of the cutouts, features a soft, vertical gradient sky transitioning from warm dusk pink to pale twilight blue.

The entire composition emphasizes a tactile paper craft aesthetic. Lighting is soft, directional studio light coming from the top-left, casting realistic, soft drop shadows along all inner die-cut edges and giving the paper layers tangible depth. Every surface shows subtle paper grain and clean cut edges.
