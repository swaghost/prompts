# Double-Exposure Travel Poster Editorial Portraits

## Description

A master workflow for producing premium travel-editorial portraits in which a recognizable subject blends seamlessly into an iconic destination. The visual system combines sharp portrait identity, landscape-filled clothing and torso silhouettes, Swiss editorial typography, geographic metadata, geometric accents, and restrained travel-lookbook color palettes.

## Usage

Use this guide for destination campaigns, travel posters, editorial portraits, social media artwork, tourism branding, and personalized travel keepsakes. Choose one destination prompt, prepend the consistency anchor, and finish the layout in an image editor when exact typography is required.

## Prerequisites

- A clear, front-facing reference portrait
- ChatGPT with image generation, DALL-E 3, Midjourney, Flux.1, or a comparable reference-image model
- Optional: Canva or Photoshop for precise poster typography and graphic elements

## Visual Style Breakdown

### VFX and Technique

Use a double-exposure or in-camera silhouette blend. The subject's chest, torso, and clothing seamlessly blend into an iconic city landscape, lake panorama, or architectural skyline, while the face, hair, and neck remain in sharp focus.

### Graphic Layout

- **Editorial side typography:** Massive vertical destination titles such as `DUBAI`, `TURKEY`, `PARIS`, or `SWITZERLAND`
- **Metadata accents:** GPS coordinates, subtle color swatches, horizontal divider bars, dot grids, and country labels
- **Picture-in-picture:** A small rounded-corner reference card at the bottom right
- **Geometric shapes:** Muted terracotta, pine green, or slate-blue semicircles and horizontal crossbars behind the silhouette

### Palette and Tone

Warm desert beige, terracotta rust, deep pine green, and slate gray against clean cream or sand backgrounds such as `#F4EFEA`.

### Styling and Wardrobe

Neutral oversized linen button-ups, ribbed tanks, cream V-neck knit sweaters, dark oversized hoodies, gold jewelry, and round wire sunglasses.

## Step 1: Upload the Reference Photo

1. Open a new chat in ChatGPT with image generation.
2. Upload a clear, front-facing reference photo of the subject.
3. Paste the master consistency anchor followed by one destination prompt from Step 2.

### Master Consistency Anchor

```text
Maintain the exact facial structure, bone features, skin texture, hair type, and identity of the person in the uploaded reference photo. Do not alter their face. Render a double-exposure travel poster portrait combining the subject with the following destination:
```

## Step 3: Midjourney and Flux.1 Setup

Use this alternative format when generating in Midjourney V6 or Flux.1:

```text
[IMAGE_URL] A double exposure editorial portrait of [SUBJECT] combined with [DESTINATION: e.g., Dubai skyline / Swiss lake village / Paris Eiffel Tower], face in sharp focus, torso blending into cityscape, warm golden hour lighting, clean graphic travel poster aesthetic, shot on 85mm f/1.8 --ar 4:5 --style raw --cref [IMAGE_URL] --cw 15 --iw 2.0
```

- `--cref [IMAGE_URL]`: Locks facial likeness from the reference image.
- `--cw 15`: Retains the face while allowing clothing to merge with the city landscape.
- `--style raw`: Keeps highlights organic and textures sharp without artificial AI smudging.
- `--ar 4:5`: Creates a vertical editorial poster composition.
- `--iw 2.0`: Gives the uploaded reference image stronger influence.

## Step 4: Poster Typography and Graphic Elements

### Vertical Title Header

- Use a bold, clean sans-serif such as Bebas Neue, Anton, Montserrat ExtraBold, or Futura Bold.
- Rotate the title 90 degrees or set the letters vertically down the left margin.
- Use destination text such as `DUBAI`, `PARIS`, `TURKEY`, or `SWITZERLAND`.
- Set the title in deep terracotta rust (`#C35A2B`) or pine green (`#2C4A3E`).

### Metadata and Grid Accents

- Add GPS coordinates in small monospace text in a top or bottom corner, such as `25.2048° N, 55.2708° E`.
- Add a `4 x 6` dot-matrix icon.
- Use thin `1px` vertical and horizontal divider rules.
- Include subtle color swatches and a restrained country label.

### Picture-in-Picture Reference Card

- Create a small rounded-corner rectangle at the bottom-right corner.
- Use a `20px` corner radius and `2px` white border.
- Insert the original reference portrait inside the card.
- Add a small `REFERENCE` label below it.

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 4:5 poster
- **Portrait focus:** Face, hair, and neck remain sharp and recognizable
- **Blend region:** Chest, torso, and clothing transition into the destination
- **Lens character:** 50mm to 85mm portrait rendering
- **Texture:** Fine 35mm film grain with organic highlights
- **Layout:** Swiss editorial travel lookbook with disciplined negative space
- **Background:** Cream or sand, approximately `#F4EFEA`

## Negative Constraints

- No altered facial structure, identity, skin texture, or hair type
- No landscape covering or obscuring the eyes and defining facial features
- No hard cutout edges between portrait and destination
- No disconnected collage pieces or arbitrary scenic overlays
- No oversaturated palette, neon accents, or glossy synthetic finish
- No cluttered typography, excessive metadata, illegible coordinates, or competing titles
- No malformed landmarks, duplicate architecture, warped sunglasses, or distorted jewelry
- No unrelated destination elements, logos, watermarks, or extra text
