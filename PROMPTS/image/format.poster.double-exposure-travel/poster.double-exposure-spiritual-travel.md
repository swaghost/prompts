# Spiritual Travel Double-Exposure Poster

## Description

A premium minimalist travel poster that preserves an uploaded subject's identity in a clean side-profile silhouette while revealing a layered spiritual journey through a chosen destination inside the silhouette. The composition combines iconic landmarks, sacred architecture, local landscapes, cultural details, and documentary-grade atmosphere in a photorealistic vertical poster.

## Usage

Use for luxury tourism campaigns, spiritual-travel storytelling, destination editorials, personalized travel artwork, documentary key art, and social-media posters. Supply a clear face reference, replace `[LOCATION]` and `[LANDMARKS]`, and keep the selected landmarks geographically and culturally consistent with the destination.

## Required Inputs

- **Face reference:** One clear uploaded portrait with a readable side profile or enough facial detail to reconstruct one accurately.
- **`[LOCATION]`:** The destination, region, pilgrimage route, or sacred landscape.
- **`[LANDMARKS]`:** A concise list of real, recognizable landmarks belonging to the selected location.

## Composition Controls

- Keep the side-profile silhouette immediately readable against clean negative space.
- Preserve the face, hairline, head shape, and recognizable profile throughout the blend.
- Place scenic layers inside the silhouette with believable foreground, middle-ground, and background depth.
- Keep iconic landmarks recognizable, correctly proportioned, and geographically coherent.
- Blend architecture and landscape organically without hard collage edges.
- Use pilgrims, wildlife, and sacred details only when authentic to `[LOCATION]`.
- Keep the modern wardrobe subordinate to the face and internal destination story.

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 4:5 poster
- **Rendering:** Photorealistic, ultra-realistic double exposure
- **Lighting:** Golden hour with soft volumetric fog and restrained spiritual glow
- **Detail:** 8K-level texture and environmental detail
- **Palette:** Subtle colors derived from the selected destination
- **Layout:** Minimalist luxury tourism campaign with no typography

## Negative Constraints

- No altered identity, facial proportions, hairstyle, skin tone, or recognizable features
- No scenery obscuring the defining facial profile
- No hard cutout edges, disconnected collage fragments, or flat overlays
- No invented, duplicated, malformed, or geographically incorrect landmarks
- No culturally unrelated temples, clothing, sacred symbols, wildlife, or landscape elements
- No impossible scale, conflicting perspective, or physically inconsistent reflections
- No excessive glow, dense fog, oversaturation, or synthetic CGI texture
- No text, captions, logos, signatures, borders, or watermarks
