# Double Exposure Poster

**Character:** Conceptual, artistic, cinematic  
**Best for:** Movies, music artwork, portrait campaigns, conceptual design, social media

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a powerful artistic double exposure poster featuring [MAIN SUBJECT]. Blend the silhouette or portrait naturally with [SECONDARY SCENE OR ENVIRONMENT]. Combine both visuals seamlessly using layered transparency, detailed textures, cinematic lighting, and emotional visual storytelling. Create depth inside the subject while maintaining a clear and recognizable silhouette. Use dramatic contrast, atmospheric elements, and a sophisticated fine-art composition. Professional double exposure photography style, conceptual poster artwork, cinematic and emotional, highly detailed, vertical 4:5 format.
```

## Pro Tip

Pair subjects that create a clear story, such as `human silhouette + forest` or `portrait + city skyline`.

## Negative Prompt

```text
unclear silhouette, harsh cutout edges, unrelated overlays, muddy transparency, duplicate face, obscured identity, flat collage, excessive layers, low contrast, cartoon, watermark, logo
```
