# Paper Cutout Poster

**Character:** Layered, soft, handcrafted  
**Best for:** Children's content, travel, education, creative campaigns, nature visuals

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a beautiful layered paper-cutout poster featuring [SUBJECT]. Build the scene using multiple layers of carefully cut paper with visible depth, soft shadows, dimensional shapes, handcrafted textures, and smooth curved forms. Use colorful paper layers and a balanced composition to create a charming handmade aesthetic. Add subtle lighting between the layers to enhance depth and realism. The artwork should look like an intricate premium paper craft illustration. Highly detailed paper art, layered diorama effect, soft shadows, handcrafted design, vertical 4:5 poster format.
```

## Pro Tip

Choose subjects with naturally readable layers, such as mountains, forests, cities, underwater scenes, or landscapes.

## Negative Prompt

```text
flat vector art, single paper layer, plastic CGI, photorealistic landscape, jagged accidental cuts, weak depth, hard digital shadows, clutter, torn damaged paper, watermark, logo
```
