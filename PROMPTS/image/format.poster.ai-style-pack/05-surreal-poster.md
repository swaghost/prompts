# Surreal Poster

**Character:** Creative, artistic, experimental  
**Best for:** Concept art, campaigns, music artwork, editorial storytelling

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a visually striking surreal poster featuring [SUBJECT]. Build an imaginative dreamlike environment with impossible landscapes, floating objects, unusual scale, unexpected combinations, and artistic visual storytelling. Create a scene that feels mysterious, creative, and slightly unreal while maintaining a visually balanced composition. Use cinematic lighting, atmospheric depth, soft shadows, and carefully composed details. Blend reality with imagination in a sophisticated artistic way. High-end surreal digital artwork, conceptual poster design, highly detailed, visually powerful, vertical 4:5 composition.
```

## Pro Tip

Use an unexpected but legible combination, such as a giant floating clock above an ocean of clouds.

## Negative Prompt

```text
random disconnected objects, incoherent scale, clutter, weak focal point, generic fantasy, cheap collage, flat lighting, muddy atmosphere, cartoon, low detail, watermark, logo
```
