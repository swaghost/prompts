# Cinematic Movie Poster

**Character:** Cinematic, dramatic, narrative  
**Best for:** Film concepts, gaming artwork, character posters, campaigns, music visuals

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a dramatic cinematic movie poster featuring [CHARACTER OR SUBJECT]. Build an epic storytelling composition with a strong focal point, dramatic environment, atmospheric depth, cinematic lighting, realistic textures, and emotional visual storytelling. Use dramatic perspective, subtle fog, volumetric light, powerful shadows, and a carefully layered background. Create the feeling of a high-budget movie campaign with intense visual impact. Professional film poster aesthetic, cinematic color grading, detailed environment, dramatic composition, highly realistic and visually immersive, vertical 4:5 poster format.
```

## Pro Tip

State the intended emotion explicitly, such as hopeful, dark, mysterious, epic, emotional, or intense.

## Negative Prompt

```text
weak focal point, generic character pose, flat lighting, empty storytelling, random background layers, excessive fog, muddy shadows, cheap action montage, cartoon, low detail, text artifacts, watermark, logo
```
