# Minimalist Poster

**Character:** Clean, simple, powerful  
**Best for:** Branding, fashion campaigns, motivational posters, modern advertisements, premium social media

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a premium minimalist poster featuring [SUBJECT]. Use a clean and modern composition with generous negative space, strong visual hierarchy, bold geometric shapes, and a refined editorial layout. Use a limited color palette of black, white, and one strong accent color. Keep the main subject visually powerful and uncluttered. Include dramatic scale, balanced composition, subtle shadows, and a sophisticated modern design aesthetic. High-end graphic design, premium poster composition, sharp details, clean background, professional art direction. Vertical 4:5 poster composition.
```

## Pro Tip

Do not add too many elements. Minimalist impact depends on simplicity and spacing.

## Negative Prompt

```text
clutter, excessive objects, busy background, weak hierarchy, multiple accent colors, ornamental decoration, random typography, low contrast, muddy composition, watermark, logo
```
