# Retro Vintage Poster

**Character:** Classic, nostalgic, old-school  
**Best for:** Music, cafes, fashion campaigns, events, travel, creative projects

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a nostalgic retro vintage poster featuring [SUBJECT]. Use classic print textures, faded colors, subtle paper grain, halftone effects, aged ink details, and vintage-inspired illustration. Create an authentic old-school advertising aesthetic with retro composition and nostalgic visual storytelling. Use warm muted colors, imperfect printing textures, classic shapes, and expressive vintage artwork. The design should feel like a collectible poster from a past era while remaining visually attractive and professionally composed. High-detail retro graphic design, vintage print aesthetic, vertical 4:5 poster format.
```

## Pro Tip

Specify a decade such as `1970s retro`, `1980s pop culture`, or `1990s vintage magazine`.
