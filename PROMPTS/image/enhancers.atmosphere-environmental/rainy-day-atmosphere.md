# Rainy Day Atmosphere - Weather Transformation

## Description

Transform any scene into atmospheric rainy conditions with grey overcast sky, soft diffused light, wet surfaces with realistic reflections, visible rain outside windows or in environment, puddles on ground with subtle reflections, moody photorealistic atmosphere, and natural lighting. This prompt converts existing architectural, interior, or urban scenes while preserving exact structure and composition, adding cinematic rain effects, dramatic weather mood, and exceptional realism—ideal for emotional storytelling, seasonal variations, and atmospheric architectural visualization.

## Effect Overview

**Transformation:** Clear/sunny scene → Rainy day scene  
**Weather:** Active rainfall, wet conditions  
**Sky:** Grey overcast, diffused light  
**Atmosphere:** Moody, contemplative, cinematic  
**Surfaces:** Wet with realistic reflections  
**Purpose:** Emotional depth, realism, seasonal variation
