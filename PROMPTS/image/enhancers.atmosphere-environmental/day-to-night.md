# Day to Night - Atmospheric Transformation

## Description

Transform any daytime architectural or interior scene into a moody atmospheric nighttime shot with bright moon as the primary light source and soft rim lighting outlining objects. This prompt converts existing scenes while preserving exact architecture and composition, adding cinematic nocturnal ambiance with dramatic lighting, deep shadows, artificial light sources activated (windows, street lights), and photorealistic night atmosphere—ideal for adding emotion, contrast, and realism to architectural visualization and real estate marketing.

## Effect Overview

**Transformation:** Daytime scene → Nighttime scene  
**Primary Light Source:** Bright moon (window not visible in frame)  
**Lighting Style:** Soft rim light outlining objects  
**Atmosphere:** Moody, cinematic, dramatic  
**Purpose:** Emotional impact, contrast, nighttime visualization
