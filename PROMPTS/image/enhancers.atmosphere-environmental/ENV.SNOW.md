# Winter/Snow Scene Transformation

## Description

Transform any architectural or outdoor image into a winter scene while preserving the exact original architecture and composition. This environmental effect adds authentic snow coverage on vegetation and surfaces, creates a cold crisp atmosphere, applies soft natural winter lighting, and maintains photorealistic quality—enhancing seasonal mood without altering the underlying structure or design.

## Effect Characteristics

**Primary Elements:**

- **Snow coverage** - White snow on vegetation, roofs, surfaces
- **Cold atmosphere** - Crisp, clean winter air quality
- **Soft lighting** - Gentle diffused natural light typical of winter
- **Photorealistic appearance** - Natural, believable winter effect
- **Seasonal vegetation** - Bare trees or evergreens with snow
- **Temperature impression** - Visual cues suggesting cold conditions

**Visual Impact:**

- Peaceful, serene mood
- Clean white palette with architectural contrast
- Soft shadows and gentle light
- Atmospheric depth with winter haze
- Seasonal transformation
- Cozy, contemplative aesthetic

## Technical Specifications

### Snow Coverage

**Distribution:**

- Roofs, ledges, horizontal surfaces heavily covered
- Tree branches and evergreen boughs laden with snow
- Ground completely covered with even snow blanket
- Vertical surfaces mostly clear (gravity effect)
- Snow accumulation in corners and protected areas
- Natural variation in depth and coverage

**Snow Texture:**

- Fresh powdery appearance
- Soft, undisturbed surface
- Natural mounding and drifting
- Pristine white or slight blue shadow tint
- Realistic snow structure and detail
- Appropriate for recent snowfall

**Accumulation Patterns:**

- Heavy on flat horizontal surfaces
- Moderate on sloped roofs (some sliding)
- Light dusting on vertical walls
- Protected areas (overhangs) show contrast
- Wind-drift patterns where appropriate
- Natural snow physics

### Atmosphere & Lighting

**Sky Conditions:**

- Overcast gray-white clouds (typical snow day)
- OR clear pale blue sky (post-snowfall)
- OR twilight with soft color gradient
- Soft diffused natural light
- No harsh shadows
- Gentle atmospheric haze

**Light Quality:**

- Soft, even illumination
- Reduced contrast from overcast
- Cool color temperature (blue-white tones)
- Gentle shadows with soft edges
- Snow reflects light upward (fill light effect)
- Ethereal, calm lighting mood

**Color Palette:**

- Dominant whites and cool grays
- Architecture provides color accent
- Cool blue tones in shadows
- Muted, desaturated colors overall
- Clean, crisp appearance
- Winter color harmony

### Vegetation Treatment

**Deciduous Trees:**

- Bare branches with snow accumulation
- Delicate snow on thin branches
- Dark bark contrast against white
- Winter silhouette visible
- Realistic branch loading

**Evergreens:**

- Snow weighing down boughs
- Green foliage visible through snow
- Heavy snow clumps on dense areas
- Natural conifer winter appearance
- Bent branches from snow weight

**Shrubs & Ground Cover:**

- Completely or partially covered
- Mounded snow forms
- Some structure visible beneath
- Natural snow-plant interaction
- Protected areas show more detail

### Architectural Elements

**Preservation:**

- Exact original structure maintained
- All design details preserved
- Proportions and scale unchanged
- Windows, doors, features intact
- Only seasonal snow addition

**Snow on Architecture:**

- Rooflines with snow accumulation
- Window sills with snow ledges
- Architectural details highlighted by snow contrast
- Gutters and ledges showing natural accumulation
- Building materials visible where vertical

## Best Practices

**For Optimal Results:**

1. **Preserve Architecture Completely**
   - Never alter building structure or design
   - Maintain all architectural details exactly
   - Keep original proportions and perspective
   - Only add snow as environmental overlay
   - Respect design intent fully

2. **Realistic Snow Physics**
   - Snow accumulates on horizontal surfaces
   - Vertical surfaces remain mostly clear
   - Natural drifting and mounding
   - Protected areas show less coverage
   - Gravity affects snow placement

3. **Natural Snow Appearance**
   - Fresh, powdery texture
   - Slight blue tint in shadows (reflected sky)
   - Pure white in direct/ambient light
   - No artificial or exaggerated effects
   - Believable recent snowfall

4. **Consistent Winter Lighting**
   - Soft, diffused light quality
   - Cool color temperature throughout
   - Gentle shadows with soft edges
   - Snow reflects light upward
   - Coherent light source and direction

5. **Vegetation Accuracy**
   - Deciduous trees bare in winter
   - Snow realistically on branches
   - Evergreens with weighted boughs
   - Natural plant-snow interaction
   - Seasonal appropriate appearance

6. **Atmospheric Coherence**
   - Cold, crisp air quality suggested
   - Appropriate sky conditions
   - Consistent weather impression
   - Winter color harmony
   - Photorealistic seasonal authenticity

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

- Altering original architecture or structure
- Unrealistic snow placement (on vertical surfaces)
- Too-white, artificial-looking snow
- Inconsistent lighting (warm light with winter scene)
- Green, lush vegetation in winter scene
- Over-saturated colors in winter palette
- Missing snow on obvious horizontal surfaces
- Harsh shadows inappropriate for overcast conditions
- Cartoon-like or exaggerated snow effects
- Loss of architectural detail or photorealism

## Technical Considerations

### Snow Material Properties

**Surface Characteristics:**

- Matte, non-reflective surface (fresh snow)
- Slight sparkle in sunlight (ice crystals)
- Soft texture with subtle variations
- Natural mounding and sculpting
- Shadow color: cool blue tones

**Accumulation Logic:**

- Heavier on exposed flat areas
- Lighter on angled or protected surfaces
- Drifts form against obstacles
- Wind patterns affect distribution
- Edge details where snow meets surfaces

### Color Temperature

**Snow White Balance:**

- Pure white in neutral light
- Cool blue-white overall
- Blue tones in shadowed areas
- Slight warm tint in sunrise/sunset
- Maintains photographic realism

**Scene Color Adjustment:**

- Overall cooler color temperature
- Desaturated colors for winter mood
- Architecture colors slightly muted
- Natural winter color harmony
- Authentic seasonal palette

### Depth & Atmosphere

**Atmospheric Haze:**

- Slight fog or mist possible
- Reduced distant clarity
- Depth perception through layers
- Snow in air if actively falling
- Soft atmospheric perspective

**Focus & Detail:**

- Foreground snow texture detailed
- Mid-ground architecture sharp
- Background slightly softer
- Photographic depth of field
- Professional quality throughout
