# Rain Scene Transformation

## Description

Transform any architectural or outdoor image into a realistic rain scene while preserving the exact original architecture and composition. This environmental effect adds authentic rain precipitation, wet surfaces, puddle reflections, cloudy overcast sky, and atmospheric moisture—creating a photorealistic rainy atmosphere that enhances mood and drama without altering the underlying structure or design.

## Effect Characteristics

**Primary Elements:**

- **Rain precipitation** - Visible rain streaks and droplets falling
- **Wet surfaces** - Reflective wet pavement, floors, walls
- **Puddle reflections** - Water accumulation with mirror-like reflections
- **Cloudy sky** - Overcast gray sky with rain clouds
- **Atmospheric moisture** - Hazy, humid air quality
- **Photorealistic appearance** - Natural, believable rain effect

**Visual Impact:**

- Dramatic mood enhancement
- Reflective surfaces doubling visual elements
- Softened lighting from overcast conditions
- Atmospheric depth and haze
- Enhanced texture contrast (wet vs dry)
- Cinematic rain aesthetic

## Technical Specifications

### Rain Precipitation

- **Density:** Light drizzle to heavy downpour (adjustable)
- **Direction:** Vertical fall or wind-driven diagonal
- **Visibility:** Streaks, droplets, and spray visible in air
- **Motion blur:** Slight blur on falling rain for realism
- **Depth layers:** Rain at multiple distances for depth

### Wet Surface Treatment

- **Reflection intensity:** High reflectivity on wet pavements
- **Surface pooling:** Water accumulation in natural low points
- **Wetness variation:** Some areas wetter than others
- **Material response:** Different materials show wet differently
- **Darkening effect:** Wet surfaces appear darker

### Puddle Formation

- **Location:** Natural depressions and flat areas
- **Reflection quality:** Mirror-like reflections of architecture and sky
- **Ripples:** Concentric ripples from raindrops hitting water
- **Depth variation:** Shallow to moderate puddle depths
- **Edge blending:** Natural transition between wet and puddled areas

### Sky & Atmosphere

- **Cloud cover:** Dense overcast gray clouds
- **Sky tone:** Dark gray to light gray gradient
- **Visibility:** Reduced atmospheric clarity
- **Haze/fog:** Slight moisture haze in distance
- **Light quality:** Soft, diffused lighting from cloud cover

### Lighting Adjustments

- **Overall tone:** Cooler color temperature
- **Contrast:** Slightly reduced from bright day
- **Highlights:** Softened, diffused light
- **Shadows:** Less defined, softer edges
- **Wetness shine:** Specular highlights on wet surfaces

## Application Requirements

## Best Practices

**For Optimal Results:**

1. **Preserve Architecture Integrity**
   - Never alter building structure or proportions
   - Maintain original design details exactly
   - Keep composition and framing identical
   - Respect architectural intent completely
   - Only add environmental effects

2. **Realistic Rain Behavior**
   - Rain falls naturally downward (or with wind)
   - Wetness appears on all exposed surfaces
   - Protected areas (overhangs) stay drier
   - Puddles form in logical depressions
   - Reflections follow physics accurately

3. **Surface Wetness Consistency**
   - All horizontal surfaces show wetness
   - Vertical surfaces have rain streaks/dampness
   - Material-appropriate wet appearance (concrete darker, glass more reflective)
   - Consistent wetness level throughout scene
   - Natural variation in water accumulation

4. **Lighting Cohesion**
   - Lighting matches overcast conditions
   - Soft, diffused light quality
   - Reduced contrast appropriate for clouds
   - Cool color temperature for rain
   - Specular highlights on wet surfaces only

5. **Atmospheric Depth**
   - Slight haze for distant elements
   - Moisture in air visible
   - Depth of field maintained
   - Rain visible at multiple distances
   - Cohesive atmosphere throughout

6. **Photorealistic Quality**
   - Natural, not exaggerated effects
   - Believable rain physics
   - Accurate wet surface appearance
   - Realistic cloud formation
   - Professional photography quality

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

- Altering original architecture or structure
- Unrealistic rain angle or behavior
- Inconsistent wetness (some surfaces dry when others wet)
- Over-saturated or artificial-looking puddles
- Wrong lighting for overcast conditions
- Missing reflections in wet surfaces
- Puddles in impossible locations (slopes, vertical surfaces)
- Sky that doesn't match rain intensity
- Loss of photorealistic quality
- Exaggerated or cartoon-like rain effects

## Technical Considerations

### Image Quality Maintenance

- Preserve original resolution
- Maintain detail sharpness where appropriate
- Controlled depth of field
- Professional photography standards
- No degradation of original quality

### Reflection Physics

- Accurate mirror reflections in puddles
- Distorted reflections with ripples
- Partial reflections on wet non-mirror surfaces
- Reflection brightness matches light conditions
- Perspective-correct reflections

### Rain Rendering Techniques

- Motion blur on rain streaks for realism
- Multiple depth layers of rain
- Varying raindrop sizes and densities
- Natural randomness in rain distribution
- Splash effects where rain hits surfaces

### Wet Material Behavior

- **Concrete/Asphalt:** Darker, more reflective
- **Glass:** Streaks, droplets, increased reflection
- **Metal:** High reflectivity when wet
- **Wood:** Darkened, saturated appearance
- **Stone:** Deeper colors, reflective sheen
- **Painted surfaces:** Glossy when wet

## Intensity Scale Reference

**Scale 1-10:**

1. **Barely Damp** - Recently rained, mostly dried, few wet spots
2. **Light Mist** - Fine drizzle, subtle wetness, no puddles
3. **Gentle Drizzle** - Visible light rain, wet surfaces, tiny puddles
4. **Light Rain** - Clear rain presence, reflective surfaces, small puddles
5. **Moderate Rain** - Standard rain scene, good puddles, visible rain (DEFAULT)
6. **Steady Rain** - Persistent rain, larger puddles, strong reflections
7. **Heavy Rain** - Intense precipitation, extensive puddling, dramatic
8. **Downpour** - Very heavy rain, large water accumulation, reduced visibility
9. **Storm** - Extreme rain, potential flooding, dark dramatic sky
10. **Deluge** - Torrential conditions, severe weather, maximum drama
