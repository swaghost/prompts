## Description

The composition features lots of free space above the head, emphasizing the sky and the perspective of an ultra-wide angle. ## Best Practices

### Camera Positioning Technique

- **Knee Level**: Position phone at or below knee height
- **Upward Angle**: Point camera upward toward face and sky
- **Arm Extension**: Extend arm forward and down naturally
- **Stability**: Use timer or burst mode to ensure sharpness
- **Framing Check**: Preview to ensure enough sky space above

### Golden Hour Timing

- **Sunset Window**: 1 hour before sunset
- **Sunrise Window**: 1 hour after sunrise
- **Weather Watch**: Cloudy days create best dramatic skies
- **Light Direction**: Position so light breaks through clouds behind/beside you
- **Quick Work**: Light changes rapidly during golden hour

### Hair and Wind Management

- **Natural Wind**: Allow natural breeze to move hair
- **Artificial Wind**: Use fan if needed for movement
- **Timing**: Capture during wind gust for dramatic effect
- **Multiple Takes**: Wind is unpredictable, shoot many frames
- **Embrace Imperfection**: Windswept hair adds authenticity

### Makeup for Low-Angle Shots

- **Under-Eye Attention**: Low angle emphasizes under-eye area
- **Highlighter Placement**: Inner corners to open eyes
- **Lash Emphasis**: Dramatic lashes read well from below
- **Jawline Contour**: Low angle can flatten jawline, contour if needed
- **Natural Skin**: Keep base light as texture more visible

### Compositional Guidelines

- **Sky Space**: Leave 60-70% of frame for sky
- **Subject Lower**: Place face in bottom third or half
- **Horizon Line**: Keep horizon straight (use grid)
- **Cloud Interest**: Time shoot when clouds are dramatic
- **Negative Space**: Embrace empty space as design element

## Technical Notes

### Ultra-Low Angle Challenges

- **Stability**: Difficult to hold phone steady at knee level
- **Framing**: Hard to see screen while holding low
- **Use Timer**: Set 3-10 second timer for stability
- **Multiple Attempts**: Angle requires practice to perfect
- **Ground Support**: Can rest phone on low surface if available

### iPhone Wide-Angle Perspective

- **Field of View**: Wide angle captures more sky
- **Slight Distortion**: Edges may show perspective distortion
- **Subject Position**: Keep face near center to minimize distortion
- **Sky Emphasis**: Wide angle perfect for environmental context
- **Natural Look**: Maintains authentic smartphone aesthetic

### Sky Photography Technical

- **Exposure for Sky**: Slight underexposure prevents blown highlights
- **HDR Balance**: Soft HDR preserves sky detail without flat look
- **Dynamic Range**: RAW capture maximizes sky and subject detail
- **Cloud Timing**: Shoot when clouds are interesting, not flat overcast
- **Sun Position**: Best when sun partially behind clouds for drama

### Wind and Hair Movement

- **Shutter Speed**: Fast enough to freeze hair strands (1/500s+)
- **Burst Mode**: Capture multiple frames, choose best
- **Wind Timing**: Anticipate wind gusts for dramatic movement
- **Hair Care**: Wind can tangle hair, be prepared for touch-ups
- **Embrace Chaos**: Imperfect windblown hair looks authentic
