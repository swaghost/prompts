A clean fashion-editorial portrait shot at eye level with the subject re-photographed from a straight-on angle, featuring symmetrical framing, soft even studio lighting, neutral seamless background, and sharp focus on the eyes with an 85mm lens creating a professional editorial look.

## Image Prompt

Using the attached photo as reference, keep the exact same person – face, identity, hair, makeup and outfit. Re-shoot her from a straight eye-level angle, camera at her eye height, lens perfectly level.

**Subject:**
[Person from reference photo] maintaining exact same identity, facial features, hairstyle, makeup application, and complete outfit. Same person preserved perfectly.

**Camera Position & Angle:**
Eye-level straight-on angle. Camera positioned at subject's eye height. Lens perfectly horizontal with no upward or downward tilt. Straight perspective with subject looking directly at camera or slightly off-axis.

**Setting/Environment:**
Neutral seamless background. Clean studio environment with no visual clutter or distractions. Solid color backdrop optimized for fashion-editorial presentation. Professional studio setting.

**Lighting:**
Soft even studio light. Neutral balanced illumination with no harsh shadows or dramatic contrast. Three-point lighting creating dimensional form without harshness. Clean, professional lighting setup. Soft diffused light wrapping around subject.

**Composition:**
Symmetrical framing with subject centered or following rule of thirds. Head and shoulders composition. Balanced negative space. Clean, uncluttered frame focusing attention on subject. Professional editorial framing.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Clean fashion-editorial look. Professional commercial photography aesthetic. Magazine-quality portrait treatment. Polished, refined style with attention to detail. Contemporary fashion photography standard.

**Technical Specifications:**
85mm lens for flattering perspective. Sharp focus on the eyes as primary focal point. Professional portrait rendering. High-resolution capture. Clean edges and precise focus control.

**Color Palette:**
Neutral, accurate color reproduction. Professional color grading for fashion-editorial work. Skin tones rendered naturally. Colors balanced for commercial reproduction.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Professional, clean, editorial. Fashion-forward presentation. Confident and polished. Contemporary commercial aesthetic. Sophisticated and refined.
