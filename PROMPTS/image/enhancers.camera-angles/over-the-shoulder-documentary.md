# Portrait - Over-The-Shoulder Documentary

## Description of Resulting Image

An over-the-shoulder shot from behind with the camera looking past her shoulder toward what she sees, capturing a candid storytelling moment in her workspace with natural light, shallow depth of field, and documentary feel with photorealistic rendering.

## Usage

Perfect for documentary-style editorial features, workplace storytelling, behind-the-scenes content, and narrative-driven campaigns. Ideal for brands showcasing process and authenticity, editorial features about professionals, lifestyle magazines, and storytelling content. This prompt excels at creating intimate, voyeuristic perspectives that invite viewers into the subject's world and activities.

Single person photographed from behind over shoulder, maintaining exact same identity and outfit from reference photo, engaged in activity with camera perspective suggesting viewer looking past them toward their focus.

## Image Prompt

Using the attached photo as reference, keep the exact same person and outfit. Re-shoot as an over-the-shoulder shot from behind her, camera looking past her shoulder toward what she sees.

**Subject:**
[Person from reference photo] maintaining exact same identity and outfit. Subject positioned with back or side to camera. Camera positioned behind and to side, looking over shoulder. Subject engaged in activity - working, reading, creating, or observing something in workspace. Natural, candid positioning. Same person captured in authentic moment.

**Camera Position & Angle:**
Over-the-shoulder shot from behind. Camera behind subject looking past her shoulder toward what she sees and engages with. Positioned to create voyeuristic, documentary perspective. Viewer sees through photographer's eyes looking over subject's shoulder. Shoulder, back of head, or profile visible in foreground with subject's focus area beyond.

**Setting/Environment:**
Candid storytelling moment in workspace. Natural environment where subject works or engages in activity. Workspace props visible - desk, tools, materials, computer, books, creative supplies, or activity-specific items. Authentic setting revealing subject's world and process. Documentary-appropriate environment.

**Composition:**
Over-the-shoulder framing creating depth and layers. Subject's shoulder or back in foreground (may be slightly out of focus). Subject's activity or focus area in middle ground or background. Composition draws viewer into what subject sees and does. Documentary storytelling through perspective.
