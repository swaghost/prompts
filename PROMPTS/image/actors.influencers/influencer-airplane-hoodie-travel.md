# Influencer Prompt: Airplane Travel Selfie

## Demographics

- **Gender**: Female
- **Ethnicity**: Young East Asian Woman
- **Age**: Early twenties (approximately 20-24)
- **Style**: Intimate introspective urban luxury, modern travel aesthetic

## Description

An extreme close-up low-angle selfie of a young East Asian woman inside an airplane cabin. Features chunky navy blue hoodie, silver headphones, and elegant manicured nails. Captures intimate introspective mood with quiet hint of urban luxury during in-transit moment.

## Complete Prompt

An extreme close-up, low-angle selfie captures a young East Asian woman in her early twenties, featuring a flawless complexion and striking model-like features with full eyebrows, long curled lashes, defined cheekbones, and glossy lips. She wears a chunky, rib-knit navy blue hoodie zipped up to the neck, enveloping her face and framing her sharp jawline, with her hand brought close to her lips, displaying elegantly manicured nails and multiple silver rings set with gemstones, one standing out with a winged or feather motif. Large, cushioned silver headphones rest over her ears beneath the hood, suggesting she's in transit. The background reveals the interior of an airplane, with a pale gray wall, overhead storage panel, and curved leather seat visible behind her, giving a distinctly modern, enclosed atmosphere. The lighting is diffused and clinical, typical of in-cabin lighting, generating an even, natural color rendering of her skin and wardrobe. The rich navy blue and metallic silver provide a cool, subdued color palette, with accents of gloss from her lips and rings. The focus is shallow, with the sharpest details on the subject's face, rings, and eyelash extensions, using a smartphone camera indicated by the angle and depth distortion. The overall mood is intimate and introspective, with a quiet hint of urban luxury.

## Pro Tips

- Navy blue hoodie should have realistic rib-knit texture and chunky appearance
- Headphones should appear cushioned and comfortable, positioned naturally under hood
- Manicured nails should be elegant but not overly stylized
- Silver rings with gemstones should catch subtle light, especially winged/feather motif
- Airplane interior should be recognizable but slightly out of focus
- In-cabin lighting should appear diffused and clinical, not warm or romantic
- Glossy lips should have natural shine, not excessive gloss
- Shallow focus should keep face, rings, and lashes sharpest
- Low-angle selfie perspective should feel authentic to smartphone usage
- Overall mood should balance intimacy with subtle luxury elements
