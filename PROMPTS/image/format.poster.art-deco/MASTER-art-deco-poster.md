# Master Art Deco Poster Template

## Description

A refined vertical Art Deco poster built from symmetrical geometry, stepped architectural forms, fan motifs, jewel-tone color blocking, and restrained metallic accents.

## Usage

Use for travel, culture, fashion, hospitality, entertainment, or product posters. Replace the bracketed fields with one clear subject and one exact headline.

## Prerequisites

- A defined subject or destination
- One exact headline and optional short subheading
- Optional reference image for subject identity

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a premium vertical Art Deco poster for [SUBJECT OR DESTINATION]. Use a 2:3 or 4:5 portrait format with a disciplined symmetrical composition and a clear central focal point.

Depict [PRIMARY SUBJECT] with elegant simplified forms and recognizable details. Surround it with stepped frames, radiating sunburst lines, stylized fans, vertical shafts, chevrons, and carefully balanced geometric borders. Build depth through layered shapes, scale, and contrast rather than photorealistic clutter.

Use a limited palette of deep black, warm ivory, [JEWEL TONE 1], [JEWEL TONE 2], and restrained antique gold accents. Add subtle screen-print grain, paper texture, and slight ink variation while keeping edges crisp and the composition luxurious.

Place the exact headline "[HEADLINE]" in a refined geometric display typeface. Add only "[OPTIONAL SUBHEADING]" if supplied. Keep all typography centered, correctly spelled, legible, and integrated into the geometry without covering the subject.

The final image should feel glamorous, architectural, theatrical, collectible, and authentically Art Deco, inspired by 1920s-1930s poster design but finished with contemporary clarity.

No extra words, logos, watermark, modern branding, photorealistic background clutter, cartoon styling, neon colors, excessive gradients, or random ornaments.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3 or 4:5
- **Composition:** Symmetrical, central focal point, stepped geometric framing
- **Palette:** Black, ivory, two jewel tones, restrained antique gold
- **Finish:** Fine screen-print grain on premium matte paper
- **Typography:** Exact headline only, geometric display lettering

## Negative Prompt

```text
asymmetry without purpose, generic vintage poster, clutter, photorealistic background, neon, excessive gradients, muddy colors, illegible text, misspelled words, extra text, logos, watermark, cheap 3D render, cartoon, low detail
```
