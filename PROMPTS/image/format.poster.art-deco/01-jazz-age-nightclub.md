# Art Deco Jazz Age Nightclub Poster

## Description

A glamorous Art Deco nightlife poster for an exclusive jazz club, centered on a poised saxophone player beneath a dramatic fan-shaped architectural proscenium.

## Usage

Use for jazz venues, cocktail lounges, music festivals, hotel bars, and period-inspired event campaigns.

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a premium vertical Art Deco event poster for an elegant Jazz Age nightclub. Center a poised saxophone player in a tuxedo, rendered as a sophisticated simplified illustration with a confident silhouette and subtle gold highlights. Frame the figure with a monumental stepped arch, layered fan shapes, radiating sunburst lines, slim vertical columns, and mirrored geometric borders.

Use deep midnight black, warm ivory, emerald green, burgundy, and restrained antique gold. Keep the geometry crisp and symmetrical, with theatrical spotlight contrast and a subtle screen-print grain on matte paper. Place the exact headline "MIDNIGHT JAZZ" at the top and the smaller exact subheading "THE BLUE HOUR CLUB" at the bottom. No other text.

The mood is glamorous, intimate, rhythmic, and unmistakably 1920s-1930s Art Deco. Make the musician and saxophone immediately readable at thumbnail size. No photorealistic crowd, neon signage, modern logos, watermark, extra words, or illegible lettering.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3
- **Subject:** Central saxophone player
- **Geometry:** Stepped arch, fan motifs, sunburst rays, symmetry
- **Palette:** Midnight black, ivory, emerald, burgundy, antique gold
- **Finish:** Crisp lithographic illustration with subtle paper grain
