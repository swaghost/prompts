# Art Deco Cinema Palace Poster

## Description

A theatrical Art Deco poster for a grand cinema palace, with a luminous marquee, stepped facade, and dramatic rays creating a strong evening landmark composition.

## Usage

Use for film festivals, cinema openings, theater programs, cultural events, hospitality branding, and architectural wall art.

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a premium vertical Art Deco poster for a grand cinema palace at night. Show the building frontally and symmetrically, with a monumental stepped facade, tall vertical windows, a luminous geometric marquee, fan-shaped ornament, and a central entrance glowing warmly. Use a dark sky and a restrained pattern of radiating rays to make the architecture feel iconic and theatrical.

Use charcoal black, warm ivory, deep ruby red, teal, and restrained antique gold. Render the building as a sophisticated flat architectural illustration with crisp edges, controlled light, subtle lithographic texture, and a premium matte-paper finish. Place the exact headline "THE GRAND SCREEN" above the facade and the exact small line "CINEMA PALACE" below it. No other text.

The result should feel elegant, cinematic, symmetrical, and unmistakably Art Deco, with the architecture readable at thumbnail size. No modern signage, film stills, logos, extra words, photorealistic clutter, watermark, or illegible typography.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3
- **Subject:** Symmetrical cinema palace facade
- **Lighting:** Warm marquee glow against a dark evening sky
- **Palette:** Charcoal, ivory, ruby, teal, antique gold
- **Finish:** Architectural lithographic poster with fine paper grain
