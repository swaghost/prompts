# Art Deco Fashion Portrait Poster

## Description

A striking Art Deco fashion poster centered on an elegant figure in a sculptural evening look, surrounded by fan geometry and jewel-toned architectural framing.

## Usage

Use for fashion editorials, couture campaigns, beauty launches, fragrance advertising, and luxury lifestyle artwork.

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a premium vertical Art Deco fashion poster featuring a poised woman in a sculptural black evening gown with a sharply defined geometric silhouette, long gloves, and a jeweled fan held at her side. Preserve a graceful, elongated pose and refined facial features without making the image photorealistic. Frame her with a symmetrical stepped portal, layered fan shapes, sunburst rays, and thin metallic geometric lines.

Use black, ivory, sapphire blue, blush coral, and restrained antique gold. The illustration should have elegant flat color blocking, controlled highlights, crisp contour work, and subtle screen-print grain on matte paper. Place the exact headline "THE NEW SILHOUETTE" at the top and the exact small line "ART DECO EDITION" at the bottom. No other text.

Make the composition feel confident, glamorous, architectural, and editorial. Keep the figure as the unmistakable hero. No recognizable brand, logo, watermark, extra words, modern clothing details, distorted hands, or illegible lettering.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 4:5
- **Subject:** Central elongated fashion portrait
- **Geometry:** Stepped portal, fan shapes, sunburst rays
- **Palette:** Black, ivory, sapphire, coral, antique gold
- **Finish:** Luxury fashion illustration with fine print grain

## Negative Prompt

```text
photorealistic fashion photo, modern streetwear, brand logo, watermark, extra text, misspelled text, distorted anatomy, distorted hands, clutter, neon, muddy colors, excessive gradients, cartoon, low detail
```
