# Art Deco Ocean Liner Travel Poster

## Description

A luxurious 1930s-inspired travel poster featuring a monumental ocean liner cutting through geometric waves beneath a radiating sunburst sky.

## Usage

Use for travel campaigns, cruise brands, destination marketing, maritime wall art, and nostalgic luxury hospitality concepts.

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a premium vertical Art Deco travel poster advertising an elegant ocean voyage. Show a monumental white ocean liner in three-quarter view moving through stylized geometric waves, its streamlined hull and tiered decks clearly recognizable. Place the ship against a large radiating sunburst and frame the scene with stepped borders, fan motifs, symmetrical columns, and clean angular clouds.

Use navy blue, warm ivory, seafoam green, coral red, and restrained antique gold. Render the scene as a bold period-inspired lithographic illustration with flat color fields, crisp silhouettes, subtle ink misregistration, and fine paper grain. Place the exact headline "SAIL INTO TOMORROW" at the top and the exact destination line "THE RIVIERA" at the bottom. No other text.

The poster should feel optimistic, streamlined, glamorous, and collectible, evoking 1930s ocean-liner travel without copying any existing advertisement. No modern cruise branding, logos, photorealism, excessive detail, extra words, watermark, or illegible typography.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 2:3
- **Subject:** Monumental streamlined ocean liner
- **Composition:** Low heroic viewpoint, sunburst behind ship, geometric waves below
- **Palette:** Navy, ivory, seafoam, coral, antique gold
- **Finish:** Vintage lithographic texture with restrained print imperfections

## Negative Prompt

```text
generic boat, modern cruise ship branding, photorealistic ocean, cluttered port, neon, extra text, misspelled text, logos, watermark, weak silhouette, excessive gradients, cartoon, low resolution
```
