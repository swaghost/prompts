# Logo Rendered as Natural Leaf Material - Organic 3D Sculpture

## Description of Resulting Image

A 3D-rendered logo maintaining its original shape and proportions but transformed to appear as if sculpted entirely from soft organic leaf material. The rendering features realistic leaf veins, surface depth, natural matte gloss, and high-contrast studio lighting that enhances shadows and surface details in Cinema 4D style.

## Usage

Use this prompt when you want to create an eco-friendly, organic brand representation that connects your logo with nature and sustainability. Perfect for environmental organizations, organic food brands, natural products, green technology companies, or any business wanting to emphasize their connection to nature. The hyper-realistic 3D leaf texture creates memorable visual impact while maintaining brand recognition.
