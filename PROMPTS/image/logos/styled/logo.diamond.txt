# 3D Crystal Holographic Logo with Rainbow Reflections

## Description of Resulting Image

A three-dimensional logo crafted from flawless faceted crystal or holographic glass, positioned at center on a deep black backdrop. The crystal form scatters light into vivid rainbow colors (purple, blue, pink, yellow, green) with sharp highlights and colorful reflections creating a bright, futuristic appearance combining transparent and mirror-like surfaces.

## Usage

Use this prompt when you want to create a premium, futuristic logo representation with high-tech aesthetic appeal. Perfect for tech brands, luxury products, or modern digital services that want to convey innovation, precision, and premium quality. The holographic crystal effect creates eye-catching social media content and memorable brand presentations.
