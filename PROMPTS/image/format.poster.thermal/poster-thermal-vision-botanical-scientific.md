## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
An exotic flowering plant specimen presented as thermal imaging data, with petals, leaves, stems, and pollen rendered in accurate thermal color gradients ranging from deep purples and blues (cool zones) through oranges and reds (warm zones) to bright yellows and whites (hot zones). The botanical structure maintains scientific accuracy with visible stamens, pistils, petal venation, and cellular detail translated into heat signature visualization.

**Setting/Environment:**
A pristine scientific poster layout with the thermal flower image centered within a modular grid system. The composition includes imaging scales along the edges, calibration bars showing temperature ranges (measured in Celsius/Kelvin), coordinate markers (X-Y axis references), timestamp data, imaging parameters, and research annotations in clean typographic layouts surrounding the specimen.

**Lighting:**
Thermal visualization lighting simulating infrared camera capture, with color-coded temperature mapping creating natural gradients across the botanical structure. Different plant tissues showing varying thermal signatures - petals displaying cooler tones, actively photosynthesizing areas showing warmer signatures, and reproductive structures exhibiting heat concentration patterns consistent with scientific thermal imaging.

**Composition:**
Centered botanical specimen occupying the primary focal area, surrounded by a modular Swiss-inspired grid system containing calibration data, measurement scales, coordinate references, and scientific annotations. Typography uses IBM Plex Sans for primary labels and IBM Plex Mono for technical data and coordinates. The layout maintains generous white space with technical elements organized in systematic margins and corners. Imaging parameters displayed in corner blocks with precise alignment.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Speculative scientific design aesthetic blending authentic thermal imaging visualization with contemporary graphic design. The style references real infrared botanical research photography while elevating it to museum-quality poster art. Color palette restricted to scientific thermal gradients (violet, indigo, blue, cyan, green, yellow, orange, red, white) mapped to temperature ranges. The overall mood balances technical precision with artistic presentation.

**Technical Specifications:**
High-resolution thermal imaging simulation (simulated 640×480+ infrared sensor output upscaled for poster printing), accurate thermal color mapping following standard scientific palettes (Iron, Rainbow HC, or Arctic colormaps), IBM Plex Sans Bold for headlines and labels, IBM Plex Mono Regular for all numerical data and coordinates, subtle scan noise overlay (2-5% opacity), fine paper grain texture simulating matte scientific print paper, calibration artifacts and timestamp data positioned systematically, vector-sharp typography with pixel-perfect alignment, museum-quality poster printing simulation with authentic infrared research visualization characteristics.
