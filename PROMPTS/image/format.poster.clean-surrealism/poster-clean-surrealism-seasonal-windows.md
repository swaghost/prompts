## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
A minimalist contemporary room with clean white or warm gray walls, hardwood flooring, and four distinct windows positioned around the space. Each window opens to a completely different season: one reveals spring cherry blossoms and fresh green meadows, another shows bright summer sunshine with blue skies and full foliage, a third frames autumn golden leaves and harvest light, while the fourth displays winter snow-covered landscapes and bare trees. The room itself remains neutral and photorealistic with simple furniture - perhaps a single chair, small table, or minimal decor element - allowing the impossible seasonal windows to command attention.

**Setting/Environment:**
An isolated architectural interior space existing in impossible temporal simultaneity. The room features clean contemporary architecture with pristine walls, natural wood or polished concrete flooring, and generous empty space. Each window maintains perfect photorealistic quality showing authentic seasonal landscapes - spring flowers in bloom, summer verdant growth, autumn colorful foliage, winter frost and snow - all coexisting within the same continuous moment. The spatial logic is impossible yet rendered with such conviction that it feels believable and contemplative rather than jarring.

**Lighting:**
Soft natural daylight entering from each seasonal window, creating an impossible yet harmonious blend of light qualities. Spring window casts gentle cool-warm balanced light, summer window provides bright warm golden rays, autumn window contributes warm amber-orange illumination, and winter window offers cool blue-gray tones. These seasonal lights overlap softly on the floor and walls without harsh contrast, creating a dreamlike yet naturalistic illumination that feels both impossible and peaceful. No artificial lighting - purely natural daylight from paradoxical sources.

**Composition:**
Centered architectural interior view with symmetrical or balanced window placement. The room occupies the middle ground with windows positioned to create visual rhythm and spatial intrigue. Typography positioned in generous white space - either at the bottom third or integrated cleanly into margins. Headline uses Helvetica Neue Bold in oversized scale, while supporting information appears in Helvetica Neue Light with precise spacing and alignment. The layout maintains museum-quality negative space with restrained typographic intervention allowing the surreal image to breathe.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Refined contemporary surrealism inspired by architectural photography, conceptual art, and impossible spaces. The aesthetic references René Magritte's believable impossibilities and contemporary architectural visualization while maintaining photorealistic materials and lighting. Muted natural color palette dominated by architectural neutrals (whites, grays, natural wood tones) with seasonal colors providing controlled accents through windows. The overall mood is contemplative, quiet, and elegantly mysterious rather than dramatically surreal.

Photorealistic 3D rendering or composite photography with seamless integration, high-resolution output suitable for large-format poster printing (minimum 300 DPI), Helvetica Neue Bold for primary headline, Helvetica Neue Light for all supporting text with Swiss-inspired typographic precision, subtle photographic grain overlay (5-8% opacity), matte museum-quality paper simulation, restrained contemporary color grading with accurate seasonal light qualities, clean vector-sharp typography with perfect baseline alignment, generous white space preservation, and refined editorial production values suitable for contemporary gallery exhibition.
