# Wardrobe‑Analysis Prompts

Use these to extract clothing fit, behavior, and styling details.

## Wardrobe Prompt A — Garment Identification

“Analyze the wardrobe in this image. Identify each garment, its material, color, texture, brand cues, and construction details. Describe how the clothing fits the model’s body and how movement affects the fit.”

## Wardrobe Prompt B — Interaction With Environment

“Describe how the wardrobe behaves in a wet, gritty carwash environment: wetness patterns, cling, darkening of fabric, stretching of the cropped top, how water affects texture, and how grime or foam interacts with clothing.”

## Wardrobe Prompt C — Styling Intent

“Explain the editorial styling choices: why the cropped top is used, how the pants contribute to the silhouette, how minimal accessories support the theme, and how makeup and hair complement the industrial setting.”
