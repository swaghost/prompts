# 🌎 **Environment Realism Analysis Prompts**

Here are **environment‑realism analysis prompts**—the exact kind of prompts you’d use to break down the physical space, textures, materials, and environmental behavior in a gritty carwash editorial. These are structured, production‑grade analysis prompts designed to extract every environmental detail from an image.

They’re purely analytical and do not generate or edit images.

## **Prompt A — Full Environment Scan**

“Analyze the entire environment in this image. Identify the location type, structural materials, textures, signs of wear, grime, rust, moisture, and any industrial elements. Describe how the space is used, how water interacts with surfaces, and how the environment contributes to the realism of the scene.”

---

## **Prompt B — Surface & Material Behavior**

“Break down the physical surfaces in the environment: concrete, metal, tile, painted surfaces, hoses, drains, grates, and carwash equipment. Describe how each material behaves when wet, how light reflects off them, how grime accumulates, and how water runoff forms patterns or puddles.”

---

## **Prompt C — Water Physics & Carwash Dynamics**

“Analyze the water behavior in the scene: spray patterns, droplets, mist, foam, suds, runoff, reflections, and wetness gradients. Describe how water interacts with the car, the ground, the model, and the equipment. Note any realistic physics such as splash direction, pressure, or pooling.”

---

## **Prompt D — Industrial Atmosphere & Environmental Mood**

“Describe the environmental mood created by the carwash setting. Identify elements such as humidity, ambient mist, reflections on wet pavement, industrial lighting, exposed pipes, metal beams, and utilitarian architecture. Explain how these contribute to a gritty, realistic atmosphere.”

---

## **Prompt E — Environmental Interaction With the Subject**

“Explain how the environment interacts with the model and the car: wet clothing, water droplets on skin, reflections on the car body, foam on surfaces, grime transfer, and how the model’s actions (spraying, leaning, writing on glass) affect the realism of the space.”

---

## **Prompt F — Environmental Storytelling**

“Analyze how the environment contributes to narrative. Identify cues such as equipment placement, water residue, rust streaks, puddles, hoses, signage, and structural wear. Explain what these details suggest about the location’s age, usage, and authenticity.”

---

## **Prompt G — Spatial Composition & Depth**

“Break down the spatial layout: foreground elements, mid‑ground industrial structures, background vehicles, ceiling beams, wall textures, and floor patterns. Describe how depth, perspective, and environmental clutter contribute to realism and editorial composition.”

---

## **Prompt H — Environmental Sound & Sensory Cues (Advanced)**

“Identify implied sensory details: sound of water pressure, echo from concrete walls, hum of fluorescent lights, dripping water, wet‑rubber smell, metallic reflections, and tactile sensations. Explain how these sensory cues enhance environmental realism.”
