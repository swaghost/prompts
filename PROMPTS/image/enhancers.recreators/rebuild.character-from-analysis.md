# ✅ **Character Reconstruction Prompt — From Analysis to Recreation**

## **Description**

This is a **production-grade character reconstruction prompt** designed to take a detailed realism analysis (like the output from `analysis.character-realism.md`) and convert it into a **comprehensive character description** suitable for:

- **AI image generation** (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, etc.)
- **3D character modeling** and digital sculpting
- **Casting descriptions** and actor selection
- **Character design briefs** for artists and designers
- **Continuity documentation** for film and TV production
- **VFX reference** for digital character creation
- **Performance direction** and actor coaching

This prompt synthesizes analyzed details into a cohesive, actionable character specification that maintains fidelity to the original subject while being optimized for recreation.

---

## **Prompt:**

"Using the detailed character analysis provided, create a comprehensive character reconstruction specification. Synthesize all analyzed elements into a cohesive, actionable character description optimized for accurate recreation. Structure the output as follows:

---

### **CHARACTER RECONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATION**

#### **1. CORE PHYSICAL IDENTITY**

**Body Type & Structure:**

- Overall physique classification (ectomorph/mesomorph/endomorph)
- Height indicators and body proportions
- Shoulder, chest/bust, waist, hip measurements and shapes
- Posture and alignment characteristics
- Bone structure prominence
- Muscle definition and athletic markers
- Body fat distribution patterns

**Distinctive Physical Markers:**

- Unique identifying features
- Asymmetries and natural imperfections
- Tattoos (location, style, size, color, detail)
- Scars (type, location, size, appearance)
- Birthmarks and moles (placement, prominence)
- Piercings (type, location, jewelry style)

---

#### **2. FACIAL ARCHITECTURE**

**Face Shape & Structure:**

- Face shape classification
- Facial proportions and symmetry notes
- Bone structure (cheekbones, jawline, chin, forehead)

**Eyes:**

- Eye color, shape, size, and spacing
- Eyelid type and depth
- Contact lenses (if present)
- Eyebrow shape, thickness, arch, grooming
- Eyelash characteristics
- Eye expression default state
- Gaze characteristics

**Nose:**

- Nose shape, size, bridge width
- Nostril shape and tip definition

**Mouth & Teeth:**

- Lip shape, fullness, symmetry, natural color
- Mouth corners default position
- Teeth visibility, color, alignment
- Dental work (if visible)
- Default mouth expression

**Ears:**

- Size, shape, positioning, protrusion
- Lobe type

**Facial Hair (if applicable):**

- Style, length, thickness, neatness
- Color and growth patterns

---

#### **3. SKIN CHARACTERISTICS**

**Skin Tone & Texture:**

- Skin tone (specific description)
- Undertone classification
- Skin texture (smooth, textured, pores visible)
- Ethnicity markers in skin

**Skin Condition:**

- Imperfections (freckles, moles, scars, discoloration)
- Oiliness vs. dryness patterns
- Environmental effects visible
- Sun damage or tan patterns
- Natural sheen vs. matte areas

**Age Indicators:**

- Wrinkles and fine lines (location, depth)
- Skin elasticity and firmness
- Age spots or sun damage
- Overall skin quality and vitality

---

#### **4. HAIR SPECIFICATIONS**

**Head Hair:**

- Hair style name and description
- Length, volume, thickness
- Hair color (including highlights, lowlights, roots)
- Texture (straight, wavy, curly, coily)
- Hairline shape and any recession
- Styling products visible
- Neatness vs. natural disarray
- Part location and style

**Body Hair:**

- Visibility and density on arms, legs, chest, back
- Grooming level
- Color and thickness

---

#### **5. HANDS, ARMS, LEGS & FEET**

**Arms:**

- Length and proportions
- Muscle definition
- Skin condition and characteristics
- Veins visibility

**Hands:**

- Hand size and shape
- Finger length and shape
- Nail condition (length, shape, grooming, polish)
- Occupation markers (calluses, ink stains)
- Dominant hand indicators

**Legs:**

- Length and proportions
- Muscle definition (thighs, calves)
- Stance and weight distribution

**Feet (if visible):**

- Foot shape and size
- Condition and grooming

---

#### **6. WARDROBE SPECIFICATION**

**Top/Shirt:**

- Exact garment type (shirt, blouse, t-shirt, dress, etc.)
- Neckline style and fit
- Color (specific hue)
- Fabric type and texture
- Fit characteristics (tight, loose, tailored)
- Condition (new, worn, wrinkled)
- Brand or style indicators

**Bottom/Pants:**

- Garment type (pants, jeans, skirt, shorts, etc.)
- Color and fabric
- Fit at waist, hips, thighs
- Rise height (low, mid, high)
- Condition and wear patterns
- Length and hemline

**Layering:**

- Outerwear (jackets, coats, cardigans)
- Visible underlayers
- Formal wear or uniforms (complete description)
- Suits or specialized clothing details

**Footwear:**

- Shoe type (sneakers, boots, heels, sandals, etc.)
- Color, style, condition
- Heel height (if applicable)
- Wear patterns and condition

**Undergarments (if visible):**

- Bra straps, waistbands, visible elements
- Hosiery type and condition

---

#### **7. ACCESSORIES & ADORNMENTS**

**Jewelry:**

- Rings (fingers, style)
- Necklaces and chains (style, length)
- Bracelets (type, wrist)
- Earrings (style, size)
- Watches (style, wrist placement)

**Headwear:**

- Hat, cap, or head covering type
- Fit, position, style
- Items stored in/on headwear

**Eyewear:**

- Glasses or sunglasses (frame style, color, fit)
- Lens type and tint
- Contact lenses details

**Face Coverings (if applicable):**

- Mask type and style
- Fit and material
- Breathing equipment details

**Other Accessories:**

- Belts (style, buckle)
- Scarves (draping style)
- Ties (type, knot, condition)
- Audio equipment (headphones, earpieces)
- Communication devices

---

#### **8. BAGS & CARRIED ITEMS**

**Bags:**

- Bag type (purse, backpack, briefcase, etc.)
- How carried (shoulder, hand, back)
- Condition and style
- Brand indicators

**Props & Objects:**

- Items held or touched
- Documents, phones, tablets
- Writing implements and placement
- Food or beverages
- Smoking items (if applicable)
- Personal items

---

#### **9. MAKEUP & GROOMING**

**Makeup (if applicable):**

- Foundation coverage and finish
- Eye makeup style and colors
- Lip color and finish
- Highlighter and contouring
- Overall makeup intensity (natural, editorial, stage)
- Wear patterns

**Grooming Details:**

- Hair styling freshness
- Nail care level
- Facial hair maintenance
- Overall hygiene presentation
- Self-care investment level

---

#### **10. BODY LANGUAGE & EXPRESSION**

**Stance & Posture:**

- Overall body position (standing, sitting, leaning, etc.)
- Weight distribution
- Body openness (open, closed, neutral)
- Muscle tension patterns

**Gesture & Movement:**

- Hand positioning and gestures
- Arm placement
- Movement quality (fluid, stiff, natural)

**Facial Expression:**

- Primary emotion conveyed
- Expression intensity
- Eye expression (intensity, focus, emotion)
- Mouth expression
- Brow and forehead tension
- Overall facial energy

**Performance Mode:**

- Natural vs. performative quality
- Camera awareness level
- Confidence and energy level

---

#### **11. INTERPERSONAL DYNAMICS** (if multiple subjects)

**Proximity & Positioning:**

- Distance between subjects
- Body orientation toward others
- Physical contact type and location

**Social Dynamics:**

- Hierarchy indicators
- Emotional connections visible
- Eye contact patterns
- Mirroring or complementary positioning

---

#### **12. ENVIRONMENTAL CONTEXT**

**Lighting on Subject:**

- Primary light direction (front, side, back, top)
- Light quality (hard, soft, diffused)
- Color temperature (warm, cool, neutral)
- Shadow placement on face and body
- Highlight areas
- Catchlights in eyes

**Environmental Interaction:**

- Water, dirt, or environmental elements on subject
- Temperature effects (sweat, goosebumps, flushing)
- Wind effects on hair and clothing
- Reflections of subject
- Props interaction

**Spatial Context:**

- Distance from camera (close-up, medium, full-body)
- Camera angle (eye-level, looking up, looking down)
- Foreground/midground/background placement
- Relationship to surroundings

---

#### **13. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS**

**Image Quality:**

- Focus areas (what's sharp, what's soft)
- Depth of field
- Motion blur (if present)
- Lighting setup implications

**Color Grading:**

- Color temperature and tone
- Saturation level
- Contrast characteristics
- Any visible post-processing

---

#### **14. CHARACTER ESSENCE**

**Personality Projection:**

- Overall energy and vibe
- Personality traits suggested
- Confidence level
- Approachability indicators

**Social & Cultural Markers:**

- Social/economic class indicators
- Professional or lifestyle markers
- Cultural or ethnic identity markers
- Religious or belief markers (if present)
- Subculture affiliations

**Character Story:**

- Age indicators and life experience
- Occupation or skill indicators
- Life circumstances suggested
- Backstory elements implied
- Authenticity vs. constructed persona

---

#### **15. RECREATION INSTRUCTIONS**

**For AI Image Generation:**
[Provide a structured prompt optimized for AI image generators, combining all elements in proper syntax with emphasis on most important identifying features]

**For 3D Modeling:**
[Provide specific measurements, proportions, and structural details needed for digital sculpting]

**For Casting/Actor Selection:**
[Summarize key physical and performance characteristics needed in an actor match]

**For Artist Brief:**
[Provide clear visual description prioritizing most distinctive and important features]

**Continuity Notes:**
[List critical elements that must remain consistent across recreations]

---

#### **16. PRIORITY FEATURES HIERARCHY**

**Must-Have (Essential for recognition):**
[List 5-10 most distinctive features that define this character]

**Should-Have (Important for accuracy):**
[List 10-15 important features that add significant authenticity]

**Nice-to-Have (Refinement details):**
[List subtle details that enhance realism but aren't critical]

---

### **RECONSTRUCTION COMPLETENESS CHECK**

- [ ] All physical characteristics specified
- [ ] Wardrobe completely documented
- [ ] Accessories and props detailed
- [ ] Expression and body language captured
- [ ] Environmental context described
- [ ] Lighting conditions noted
- [ ] Character essence articulated
- [ ] Recreation instructions provided for intended use case
- [ ] Priority features ranked

---

**Output should be:**

- Comprehensive yet organized
- Specific rather than vague
- Actionable for the intended recreation method
- Faithful to the original analysis
- Optimized for accurate reproduction
- Structured for easy reference

Prioritize clarity, completeness, and practical usability for character recreation."

---

## **Usage Notes**

**Input:** Paste the detailed analysis output from the character realism checklist.

**Output:** A structured character reconstruction specification ready for:

- AI image generation prompts
- Character design briefs
- Casting descriptions
- 3D modeling references
- Continuity documentation
- VFX recreation

**Customization:** Adjust sections 15-16 based on your specific recreation needs (AI art, casting, 3D modeling, etc.)
