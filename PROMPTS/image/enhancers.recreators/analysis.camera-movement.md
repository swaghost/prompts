# 🎥 1. Camera‑Movement Analysis Prompt (tracking, tilt, pan, editorial motion)

## Prompt:

“Analyze the camera movement in this image or sequence. Identify whether the camera is static or moving, and describe any implied motion such as tracking, panning, tilting, or handheld drift. Explain how movement direction (horizontal, vertical, diagonal) affects the subject’s presence, energy, and editorial tone. Describe how motion interacts with water spray, reflections, industrial geometry, and the model’s pose. Note whether the movement creates dynamism, tension, intimacy, or dominance. Break down how lens choice and perspective amplify or soften the sense of motion.”
