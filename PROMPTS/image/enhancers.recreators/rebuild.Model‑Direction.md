🧍‍♀️ Model‑Direction Manual Creation Prompt
Prompt:  
“Create a detailed model‑direction manual for this photoshoot. Focus on emotional guidance, physical coaching, and editorial performance. Break down facial expression control: gaze intensity, micro‑expressions, jaw tension, lip behavior, and how to convey confidence, seduction, aggression, or playfulness. Provide pose direction: weight distribution, hip placement, shoulder angles, arm extension, hand tension, and how to interact with props such as hoses, car hoods, poles, and platforms. Include movement cues for dynamic shots: how to spray water, how to lean, how to stretch upward, how to recline inside a car, and how to write on a windshield with expressive intention. Add guidance for maintaining continuity in emotion, posture, and energy across the entire shoot. Include notes on how wardrobe behaves (wetness, cling, stretch) and how the model should adjust poses to highlight clothing, environment, and editorial mood. The manual should function as a complete guide for directing the model throughout the shoot.”
