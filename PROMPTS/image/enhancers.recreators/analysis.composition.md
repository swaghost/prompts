# 🧩 Composition‑Only Prompt (geometry, symmetry, negative space)

## Prompt:

“Analyze the composition of this image. Identify geometric structures such as leading lines, diagonals, verticals, horizontals, and curves created by the environment (hoses, beams, car edges, shadows). Describe symmetry or intentional asymmetry, and how the subject is positioned within the frame (centered, rule of thirds, edge‑weighted). Break down the use of negative space, foreground elements, background depth, and environmental clutter. Explain how composition guides the viewer’s eye, enhances editorial storytelling, and interacts with lighting, water, and industrial textures.”
