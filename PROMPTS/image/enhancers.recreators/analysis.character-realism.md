# ✅ **Model / Character / Actor / Subject Realism Checklist — Analysis Prompt**

## ** Description **

Here's a **clean, professional, production‑grade analysis prompt** specifically designed to create a **comprehensive Model/Character/Actor/Subject Realism Checklist**.  
This prompt is structured to extract _every_ physical, anatomical, cosmetic, wardrobe, performance, lighting, environmental, health, grooming, temporal, interpersonal, technical, and character detail needed to reproduce a subject with high fidelity — **without crossing into anything sexual or suggestive**.

This is the exact kind of prompt you'd use to analyze realism in editorial photography, film production, character design, performance capture, casting, VFX reference, continuity management, forensic analysis, or AI dataset creation. It covers physical appearance, body language, expression, context, lighting, health, grooming, props, interpersonal dynamics, technical factors, and character presence in exhaustive detail.

## **Prompt:**

"Create a complete realism‑analysis checklist for the subject in this image. Break down every physical, anatomical, cosmetic, wardrobe, performance, interpersonal, technical, and character detail with precision. Include:

**Skin:**

- Skin tone, undertone, texture, pores, natural sheen, matte areas
- Pore visibility: enlarged, fine, clogged, visible in T-zone
- Skin texture zones: smooth areas, rough patches, texture variation
- Imperfections: redness, blotchiness, discoloration, scars, moles, freckles
- Sweat: presence, placement, sheen, sweat stains on clothing
- Oiliness vs. dryness: T-zone shine, dry patches, combination skin
- Environmental effects on skin (humidity, water spray, cold, heat)
- Sun damage patterns: tan lines, sun spots, uneven pigmentation
- Ethnicity markers: melanin distribution, natural undertones

**Face & Eyes:**

- Eye color, clarity, redness, tiredness, bags, swelling
- Eye shape, size, spacing, depth, eyelid type
- Contact lenses: presence, color (natural or cosmetic), visibility, lens edge, movement
- Vision correction indicators: glasses indentations on nose, squinting patterns
- Pupil size and dilation
- Sclera visibility and condition: bloodshot, yellowing, clarity
- Gaze direction, focus point, eye contact, emotional intensity
- Eyebrow shape, thickness, grooming, color, arch
- Eyelashes: length, density, curl, mascara behavior
- Facial hair: beard style, length, thickness, neatness, color, growth patterns
- Micro‑expressions: tension, relaxation, emotional cues

**Facial Structure & Features:**

- Face shape: oval, round, square, heart, diamond, oblong
- Nose: shape, size, bridge width, nostril shape, tip definition
- Mouth & Lips: shape, fullness, symmetry, natural color, texture, dryness/moisture
- Teeth: visibility, color, alignment, gaps (if visible in expression)
- Tongue visibility (if mouth open)
- Gum visibility and color (if mouth open)
- Dental work: fillings, crowns, braces, retainers (if visible)
- Items in mouth: pen/pencil, cigarette, cigar, pipe, toothpick, straw, food
- How items are held in mouth: between teeth, corner of mouth, center, casual vs. intentional
- Lip lines, chapping, or product (lipstick, balm)
- Mouth corners: upturned, downturned, neutral
- Philtrum depth and definition
- Jawline: definition, width, angle, symmetry
- Chin: shape, prominence, dimples, cleft
- Cheekbones: prominence, height, definition
- Forehead: size, shape, lines, wrinkles
- Ears: size, shape, positioning, protrusion, lobe type
- Overall facial symmetry and proportions

**Natural Asymmetries & Imperfections:**

- Facial asymmetries: eye size differences, eyebrow height, nostril variations
- Body asymmetries: shoulder height, arm length, posture compensations
- Dominant side indicators: hand preference, leg stance, facial expression bias
- Natural quirks: crooked smile, head tilt habit, favored eye
- Imperfect elements that add realism: uneven skin, irregular features
- How asymmetries contribute to unique character appearance

**Hair (Head):**

- Hair style, length, part, volume, thickness
- Hair color, highlights, lowlights, root growth
- Texture: straight, wavy, curly, coily
- Neatness vs. disarray, environmental effects (wetness, humidity, wind)
- Hair products visible (gel sheen, product buildup)
- Hairline shape and recession patterns

**Body Type & Physique:**

- Overall body type: ectomorph, mesomorph, endomorph
- Height indicators and body proportions
- Shoulder width and structure: broad, narrow, sloped, square
- Chest/torso: shape, size, posture, muscle definition
- Bust/chest shape (if applicable): full, small, average, asymmetrical, natural shape vs. augmented
- Bust positioning: high, low, wide-set, close-set, projection
- Waist definition and shape
- Hip width and structure
- Hip shape: narrow, wide, rounded, square, heart-shaped
- Hip-to-waist ratio and body curve definition
- Thigh gap or thigh contact
- Glute shape and prominence (if visible)
- Muscle tone and definition: arms, legs, core, back
- Body fat distribution
- Athletic/activity-specific body markers: runner's legs, swimmer's shoulders, climber's forearms
- Bone structure visibility: collarbones, ribs, hip bones, wrist bones
- Veins visibility on hands, arms, legs, neck
- Posture: upright, slouched, relaxed, tense, asymmetrical
- Back visibility (if visible): upper back shape, lower back curvature, spine alignment
- Posterior view details (if visible): back of head, shoulder blades, glute shape

**Hands & Arms:**

- Arm length and proportions
- Muscle definition in biceps, triceps, forearms
- Arm hair (see Body Hair section)
- Hand size and shape
- Finger length and shape
- Knuckle prominence
- Nail condition: length, shape, cleanliness, polish, wear
- Hand positioning and gestures
- Palm visibility and lines
- Skin condition: dryness, moisture, calluses, scars
- Occupation indicators: calluses, ink stains, tool marks, typing wear
- Veins and bone structure visibility
- Rings, bracelets, watches (see Accessories)
- Dominant hand indicators: size difference, wear patterns

**Legs & Feet:**

- Leg length and proportions
- Thigh shape and muscle definition
- Calf definition and shape
- Knee structure and appearance
- Ankle definition
- Leg hair (see Body Hair section)
- Foot shape and size (if visible)
- Toe visibility and condition
- Toenail polish (if visible)
- Stance and weight distribution

**Body Hair:**

- Arm hair, leg hair, armpit hair: visibility, density, grooming
- Natural variation and environmental interaction (wetness, lighting)
- Chest hair, back hair (if visible)
- Body hair color and thickness

**Neck & Throat:**

- Neck length and thickness
- Muscle definition (sternocleidomastoid, trapezius visibility)
- Throat structure and Adam's apple prominence
- Skin texture and lines
- Veins visibility
- Jewelry or accessories (see Accessories)

**Age Indicators:**

- Fine lines and wrinkles: forehead, crow's feet, smile lines, nasolabial folds
- Skin elasticity and firmness
- Age spots, sun damage, hyperpigmentation
- Hand appearance and aging signs
- Neck lines and sagging
- Hair graying, thinning, or hair loss
- Overall skin quality and vitality

**Distinctive Marks:**

- Tattoos: location, size, color, style, clarity, aging/fading
- Scars: type, location, size, color, texture, healing stage
- Birthmarks: location, size, color, shape
- Moles and beauty marks: placement, size, prominence
- Piercings: location, type, jewelry style (see Accessories)
- Unique features: dimples, clefts, asymmetries
- Medical indicators: bandages, braces, medical devices, IV marks
- Recent injuries: bruises, cuts, swelling, healing stages
- Surgical scars or medical intervention marks

**Makeup & Cosmetics (if applicable):**

- Foundation: coverage, finish, color match, creasing
- Concealer: placement, blending, coverage
- Powder: finish, flashback, settling
- Blush/contour: placement, blending, intensity
- Eye makeup: eyeshadow colors, eyeliner style, mascara application
- Lip color: lipstick, gloss, stain, natural
- Highlighter: placement, intensity, shimmer
- Makeup wear throughout the day: fading, smudging, transfer
- Natural vs. editorial vs. stage makeup intensity

**Wardrobe:**

- Shirt/top: length, style, color, fit, stretch, cling, wear and tear
- Neckline type: crew, V-neck, scoop, boat, off-shoulder, turtleneck, cowl, halter
- Neckline fit on bust: gaping, pulling, sitting flat, revealing, modest, appropriate fit
- Bust fit: tight, loose, supportive, compressing, enhancing, natural drape
- Fabric strain at bust: pulling, stress lines, button gaps, seam stress
- Top tightness/looseness: across chest, at waist, at shoulders, sleeve tightness
- Fabric type: cotton, silk, denim, polyester, leather, knit, woven
- Fabric quality: cheap, mid-range, luxury, worn, new
- Fabric texture: smooth, rough, wrinkled, crisp, soft
- Layering: visible layers, undershirt, jacket, vest, outerwear
- Outerwear specifics: jackets, coats, blazers, cardigans, hoodies, windbreakers
- Formal wear: suits (jacket and pants/skirt match), tuxedos, evening gowns, cocktail dresses
- Suit details: lapel style, button count, fit, tailoring quality, formality level
- Dress suits: business suits, three-piece suits, double-breasted vs. single-breasted
- Swimwear: swimsuits, bikinis, one-pieces, swim trunks, board shorts, rash guards
- Swimwear fit: coverage, support, tightness, style, condition (dry/wet)
- Specialized clothing: uniforms, costumes, athletic wear, protective gear, workwear
- Uniform types: medical scrubs, military uniforms, police uniforms, service industry, flight attendant, security
- Uniform details: rank insignia, badges, name tags, patches, pressed vs. worn, authenticity
- Pants/shorts/skirt: length, style, color, fit, wrinkles, stains, wear
- Waist fit: tight, loose, digging in, gaping, proper fit
- Hip fit: snug, loose, straining, smooth, pulling, stretching
- Thigh fit: tight, loose, restrictive, comfortable, fabric stress
- Rise: low-rise, mid-rise, high-rise, and how it sits on waist/hips
- Bottom tightness/looseness: at waist, hips, thighs, calves, ankles
- Crotch fit: proper length, too short, too long, riding up
- Skirt drape: how it falls over hips, clings to body, swings freely
- Undergarments: visibility of bra straps, waistbands, undershirt hems, tank tops showing
- Undergarment fit indicators: proper fit, ill-fitting, adjustments needed
- Socks/stockings: color, thickness, visibility, condition, height (ankle, crew, knee-high, thigh-high)
- Hosiery details: sheer, opaque, patterned, runs or snags
- Footwear: type, color, wear, environmental interaction, sole condition
- Footwear specifics: shoes, boots, sandals, heels, sneakers, dress shoes, athletic shoes
- Footwear details: lacing, buckles, straps, heel height, toe style, condition of soles
- Brand indicators: visible logos, tags, designer markers
- Clothing age and wear: fading, pilling, stretching, holes, repairs
- Overall fit quality: tailored, off-the-rack, too small, too large, size appropriate
- Fit issues by body area: tight at bust/loose at waist, tight at hips/loose at waist, etc.
- Seasonal appropriateness: weather-appropriate or not
- Style coherence: matching, clashing, intentional vs. unintentional
- Cleanliness: pristine, lived-in, dirty, stained, fresh
- Clothing dynamics: how fabric drapes, clings, stretches with body movement
- Wrinkle patterns: natural creasing at joints, seated wrinkles, compression marks
- Hemlines and how clothing sits on body at rest vs. in motion
- Undergarment lines or visible panty lines (VPL)
- Clothing adjustments needed: riding up, falling down, gaping, bunching
- Fabric tension points: where clothing is stretched tight by body shape
- Excess fabric: where clothing is baggy or has extra material

**Accessories:**

- Jewelry: rings, necklaces, bracelets, earrings
- Piercing jewelry: studs, hoops, gauges
- Watches: style, wear, reflection, wrist placement
- Belts: style, wear, buckle details
- Headwear: hats, caps, beanies, helmets, turbans, hijabs, headbands, visors, crowns, tiaras
- Headwear details: fit, wear, positioning, tilt, material, condition, cultural significance
- Items stored in/on headwear: pens, pencils, tools, cards, tucked behind brim or band
- Hoods: up or down, fit, coverage, drawstrings, attached to garment
- Hair accessories: clips, pins, bands, barrettes, scrunchies, combs
- Eyewear: glasses, sunglasses, reading glasses, safety glasses, goggles
- Eyewear details: frame style, material, color, lens type, lens tint, reflections, fit on nose and ears
- Prescription indicators: lens thickness, magnification visible
- Face coverings: masks (medical, surgical, N95, cloth, fashion), face shields, respirators
- Mask details: fit, material, ear loops vs. ties, nose wire, coverage, fogging on glasses
- Medical breathing equipment: oxygen masks, nasal cannulas, CPAP masks, ventilator attachments
- Breathing equipment details: tubing visibility, fit, fogging, oxygen flow indicators
- Costume/theatrical masks: full face, half face, character masks, animal masks, cultural masks
- Mask effects on expression: what's visible, what's obscured, eyes-only communication
- Blindfolds: presence, material, tightness, coverage, purpose (medical, costume, performance)
- Neckwear: necklaces, chains, pendants, chokers, collars, lanyards
- Scarves and wraps: material, pattern, draping style, knot type
- Ties and bowties: style, knot type (Windsor, half-Windsor, four-in-hand), tightness, straightness, pattern
- Tie condition: loosened, tight, askew, pristine, wrinkled, length appropriate
- Tie accessories: tie clips, tie bars, tie pins, placement and style
- Audio equipment: earpieces, earbuds, in-ear monitors (IEMs), headsets, headphones (over-ear, on-ear)
- Microphones: handheld, lavalier/lapel, boom, headset mics, visibility, professional vs. consumer
- Communication devices: walkie-talkies, radio earpieces, Bluetooth devices
- Material, color, wear, reflections, environmental interaction
- How accessories interact with movement and light
- Cultural or religious significance of accessories

**Bags & Carriers:**

- Handheld bags: purses, clutches, briefcases, tote bags, shopping bags
- Shoulder bags: messenger bags, crossbody bags, shoulder purses, satchels
- Backpacks: casual, hiking, school, tactical, size, wear
- Luggage: suitcases, duffel bags, garment bags, carry-ons, roller bags
- Wallets: visibility (in hand, pocket bulge, chain), style, wear
- Bag/carrier condition: new, worn, damaged, luxury, budget
- How carried: in hand, over shoulder, across body, on back, dragged
- Bag interaction: casual hold, protective grip, struggling with weight, easy carry
- Bag contents visibility: overstuffed, empty, organized, messy
- Straps: wear, adjustment, digging into shoulder, comfort level
- Brand indicators on bags: logos, designer markers, status symbols

**Props & Object Interaction:**

- Objects held or touched by subject
- Documents and cards: ID cards, credit cards, passports, licenses, tickets, papers
- Paper items: documents, letters, notes, forms, contracts, scripts, sheet music
- Document handling: how held, visibility of information, condition, authenticity indicators
- Clipboards: presence, how held, papers attached, professional context
- Folders and binders: organization, condition, how carried or held
- Books and magazines: open or closed, page visibility, condition, subject matter
- Technology: phones, tablets, laptops, cameras, headphones, smartwatches
- Writing implements: pens, pencils, markers, styluses
- Writing implement placement: in hand, behind ear, in hat, in pocket, clipped to clothing
- Food items: being eaten, held, on plate, utensils used, bite marks, portion size
- Food handling: natural eating, posed, messy vs. neat, stage of consumption
- Eating utensils: forks, knives, spoons, chopsticks, grip style, proper vs. improper use
- Beverages: drinks held, sipped, cups/glasses/bottles, condensation, fill level, straw presence
- Beverage containers: coffee cups, water bottles, wine glasses, beer bottles, mugs, thermoses
- Consumption indicators: lipstick marks on cups, liquid level, ice melting, steam rising
- Personal items: keys, pens, books, coffee cups, water bottles
- Smoking items (if applicable): cigarettes, cigars, pipes, vape devices, lighters, ashtrays
- Smoking indicators: smoke presence, ash, burn stage, hand positioning, mouth placement
- How subject grips or handles objects: natural, awkward, expert, tentative
- Relationship to props: familiar, unfamiliar, professional use, casual use
- Object placement in hands: secure grip, loose hold, pointing, presenting
- Props revealing occupation or skill: tools, instruments, equipment
- Habitual object interaction: pen twirling, phone checking, hair touching
- Natural vs. posed interaction with objects
- Weight distribution showing object heaviness or lightness
- Wear patterns on frequently used objects
- Muscle engagement appropriate to object handling

**Body Language & Performance:**

- Overall stance: confident, shy, aggressive, relaxed, defensive
- Weight distribution: balanced, shifted, leaning
- Body openness vs. closed positioning
- Gesture style: expansive, contained, natural, theatrical
- Muscle tension: relaxed, tense, specific areas of tension
- Movement quality: fluid, stiff, graceful, awkward, energetic, lethargic
- Spatial relationships: proximity to objects, people, camera
- Eye contact and gaze behavior: direct, averted, focused, wandering
- Breathing visibility: calm, heavy, controlled
- Movement blur: sharp focus, motion blur, frozen action, dynamic movement
- Speech indicators: mouth open/closed, mid-word, speaking, silent
- Lip position: if speaking, what sound shape is being formed

**Interpersonal Dynamics (if multiple subjects):**

- Proximity to other people: intimate, personal, social, public distance
- Body orientation: facing toward, turned away, angled, parallel
- Touch or physical contact: type, location, pressure, comfort level
- Mirroring or complementary body language between subjects
- Social hierarchy indicators: who's leading, following, dominant, submissive
- Emotional connection visible: intimate, professional, familial, stranger
- Eye contact patterns between subjects
- Synchrony or discord in movement and positioning
- Power dynamics revealed through stance and positioning
- Comfort level with proximity and interaction

**Expression & Emotion:**

- Primary emotion being conveyed
- Micro‑expressions and secondary emotions
- Expression intensity: subtle, moderate, exaggerated
- Authenticity vs. performative quality
- Eye expression: intensity, focus, emotion
- Mouth expression: relaxed, tense, smiling, frowning, neutral
- Forehead and brow tension
- Overall facial tension or relaxation
- Congruence between face, body, and context
- Energy level: high, medium, low, exhausted, energized

**Environmental Interaction:**

- Water on skin, clothing, hair
- Foam, grime, dust, dirt, mud
- Temperature effects (goosebumps, flushed skin, dryness, sweating)
- Wind effects: hair movement, clothing flutter, skin exposure
- Shadows cast by subject on environment
- Reflections of subject in mirrors, water, glass, surfaces
- Subject interaction with props or objects
- Environmental elements on subject: leaves, snow, rain, sand
- Light catching on subject surfaces: highlights, rim light, backlighting
- Subject casting or blocking light in the scene

**Overall Realism:**

- How all physical details combine to create authenticity
- Consistency across all body parts and features
- How environment affects subject appearance
- Lighting interaction with skin, hair, clothing, and accessories
- Natural vs. artificial appearance indicators
- Age consistency across all features
- Proportional accuracy and anatomical correctness
- Performance authenticity and naturalness
- Continuity notes for maintaining realism across multiple shots
- Overall cohesion: do all elements work together believably?
- Context appropriateness: does the subject fit the environment and scenario?

**Lighting on Subject:**

- Primary light source direction and intensity
- Shadow placement on face and body: under eyes, nose, chin, neck
- Highlight placement: cheekbones, forehead, nose, shoulders
- Light quality: hard, soft, diffused, direct
- Color temperature of light: warm, cool, neutral
- Rim lighting or edge lighting presence
- Catchlights in eyes: size, shape, position
- How light reveals or conceals texture and detail
- Skin translucency in backlit areas (ears, fingers, thin fabric)
- Subsurface scattering effects on skin

**Physical Condition & Health:**

- Overall health appearance: robust, frail, average
- Energy level and vitality: energetic, fatigued, lethargic
- Fitness level: athletic, average, sedentary indicators
- Hydration level: dry lips, skin, well-hydrated
- Recent activity signs: exertion, perspiration, flush
- Sleep indicators: alert, tired, exhausted, well-rested
- Posture health: good alignment, poor posture, compensations
- Breathing pattern: normal, labored, controlled
- Color in face: healthy color, pale, flushed, ashen
- Pain or discomfort indicators: grimacing, guarding, tension patterns
- Illness signs: pallor, feverish appearance, weakness
- Physical limitations visible: mobility aids, movement restrictions
- Overall wellness impression

**Grooming & Care Details:**

- Hair grooming: freshly styled, maintained, neglected
- Nail care: manicured, natural, neglected, bitten, polish condition
- Facial hair maintenance: freshly shaved, stubble age, intentional style
- Skincare indicators: moisturized, dry, product use visible
- Hygiene indicators: clean, fresh, lived-in, unkempt
- Fragrance indicators (if described): cologne/perfume application points
- Dental hygiene: teeth cleanliness, oral care level
- Overall self-care investment level

**Temporal & Context Indicators:**

- Time of day suggested: morning freshness, midday, evening tiredness
- Activity recency: just woke up, post-workout, end of workday
- Seasonal indicators: tan lines, sun exposure, cold weather effects
- Recent event indicators: post-meal, post-activity, preparing for event
- Wear patterns through the day: makeup fading, clothing creasing
- Freshness level: just dressed, been wearing all day

**Scale & Spatial Context:**

- Height relative to environment and objects
- Body proportions relative to surroundings
- Distance from camera: close-up, medium, far
- Depth perception: foreground, midground, background placement
- Size comparison to other people or objects in frame
- Perspective distortion effects on body proportions
- Camera angle effects: looking up (heroic), looking down (diminished), eye-level
- Lens distortion: wide-angle stretch, telephoto compression

**Voice & Audio Indicators (if subject is speaking/performing):**

- Mouth position indicating sound production
- Vocal effort visible: relaxed speech, shouting, whispering, singing
- Throat tension indicating volume or vocal strain
- Breath support visible in posture and torso engagement
- Articulation patterns: clear enunciation, mumbling, accent indicators
- Emotional tone suggested by facial and body engagement
- Performance mode: conversational, presentational, theatrical, intimate

**Narrative & Contextual Elements:**

- Story being told through subject's appearance and positioning
- Moment in time: before action, during action, after action, at rest
- Implied action: what subject just did or is about to do
- Relationship to viewer: confrontational, inviting, ignoring, performing for
- Emotional narrative arc suggested
- Scene context: candid, posed, performance, documentary, staged
- Subject awareness of camera: conscious, unconscious, performing, natural
- Authenticity of moment: genuine, constructed, rehearsed, spontaneous

**Technical & Image Quality Factors:**

- Image sharpness and focus areas: tack sharp, soft focus, bokeh
- Motion blur indicators: subject movement, camera shake, intentional blur
- Depth of field: what's in focus, what's out of focus
- Image grain or noise: digital noise, film grain, clean capture
- Color grading effects: warm grade, cool grade, desaturated, high contrast
- Post-processing indicators: over-sharpening, smoothing, filters applied
- Compression artifacts or image degradation
- HDR or exposure effects on appearance
- How technical factors affect perceived realism of subject

**Character Essence & Presence:**

- Overall character energy and vibe
- Personality indicators from appearance and body language
- Social/economic class indicators
- Professional/lifestyle indicators
- Occupation-specific physical markers: muscle development, posture, wear patterns
- Cultural or regional appearance markers
- Ethnicity and heritage indicators: facial features, hair texture, skin tone patterns
- Religious or belief system markers: specific accessories, head coverings, grooming styles
- Subculture or community affiliations: fashion choices, tattoo styles, piercings
- Character backstory suggested by physical details
- Life experience indicators: stress marks, sun exposure, manual labor signs
- Level of grooming and self‑care
- Authenticity vs. constructed persona
- Confidence level and self-assurance
- Approachability and openness indicators

The checklist should be exhaustive, structured, and suitable for high‑end editorial, cinematic character design, performance capture, casting analysis, character continuity, VFX reference, or photorealistic reproduction."
