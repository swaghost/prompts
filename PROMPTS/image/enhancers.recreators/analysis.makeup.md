# 💄 Makeup Analysis Prompt

## The Prompt

“Analyze the makeup in this image. Identify the overall makeup style, including skin finish (matte, dewy, glossy), contouring, highlight placement, blush tone, and foundation behavior under the lighting. Describe eye makeup in detail: brow shape, brow grooming, eyeshadow color and blending, eyeliner thickness and direction, mascara intensity, and any water‑interaction effects (smudging, shine, reflections). Break down lip makeup: color, finish (matte, satin, gloss), edge definition, and how expression affects lip shape. Explain how the makeup supports the editorial theme, interacts with moisture or water spray, and contributes to the model’s emotional tone.”
