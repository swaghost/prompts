# 📘 **Character Continuity Bible — Creation Prompt**

## Description:

Here’s a **clean, production‑grade analysis prompt** specifically designed to create a **Character Continuity Bible** — the document used in film, TV, editorial photography, and character‑driven storytelling to ensure a subject remains visually and behaviorally consistent across every scene, shot, or appearance.

This prompt is structured to capture **physical traits, wardrobe continuity, emotional consistency, environmental interaction, and narrative logic** — everything needed to maintain realism and coherence.

## **Prompt:**

“Create a complete Character Continuity Bible for the subject in this image or project. Document every detail required to maintain perfect continuity across all scenes, shots, or appearances. Include:

**Physical Appearance:**

- Skin tone, undertone, texture, imperfections, sheen, sweat, redness, freckles, moles, scars
- Facial structure, jawline, cheekbones, nose shape, lip shape
- Eye color, clarity, redness, tiredness, bags, swelling
- Eyebrows: shape, thickness, grooming, color
- Eyelashes: length, density, curl
- Facial hair: beard style, length, thickness, neatness, color
- Body hair: arm, leg, armpit — density, visibility, grooming
- Head hair: style, length, part, volume, thickness, color, highlights, neatness, environmental effects

**Makeup Continuity:**

- Foundation tone, finish, coverage
- Contour placement, highlight intensity
- Eyeshadow colors, eyeliner style, mascara behavior
- Lip color, finish, edge definition
- Environmental interaction (water, sweat, humidity, lighting)

**Wardrobe Continuity:**

- Shirt/top: style, color, fit, length, stretch, wear and tear
- Pants/shorts/skirt: style, color, fit, wrinkles, stains, wear
- Socks/stockings: color, thickness, visibility
- Footwear: type, color, condition
- Accessories: jewelry, piercings, watches, belts, hair accessories
- Prop interaction: hoses, tools, vehicles, objects

**Behavioral Continuity:**

- Signature poses, gestures, posture habits
- Emotional baseline (confidence, intensity, seduction, calmness, aggression)
- Micro‑expressions: brow tension, gaze intensity, lip behavior
- Movement style: how the subject walks, leans, lifts, interacts with environment

**Environmental Interaction:**

- How water affects skin, hair, clothing
- Foam, grime, dust, reflections on body or wardrobe
- Temperature effects (flush, goosebumps, dryness)

**Scene‑to‑Scene Consistency:**

- What must remain identical (hair part, accessories, stains, wetness level)
- What may vary (pose, expression, angle)
- Continuity notes for multi‑day shoots

**Narrative Continuity:**

- Character motivation, emotional arc, tone consistency
- How physical and wardrobe details support the narrative
- How environment influences character presentation

The continuity bible should be exhaustive, structured, and suitable for film, editorial photography, or character‑driven storytelling.”
