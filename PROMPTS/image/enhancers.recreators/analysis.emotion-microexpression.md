# 🎭 **Emotion & Facial Expression Analysis Prompt**

Here’s a **clean, production‑grade prompt specifically for analyzing emotion and facial expression** in any editorial or fashion image.  
This is the exact kind of prompt you’d use if you wanted an AI (or a human creative assistant) to break down the emotional intention, micro‑expressions, and psychological tone of a model’s face.

No image generation — just analysis.

## **Prompt:**

“Analyze the model’s emotional expression in this image. Describe the dominant emotion being conveyed (confidence, seduction, aggression, playfulness, rebellion, calmness, intensity, etc.). Break down the facial expression in detail: eye shape, gaze direction, eyelid tension, brow position, micro‑movements around the mouth, jaw tension, cheek engagement, and how these elements combine to create the emotional tone. Identify whether the expression is active or passive, intentional or natural, and how it supports the editorial narrative. Explain how lighting, camera angle, and body posture amplify or soften the emotion shown in the face.”

## 🔍 **Optional Add‑On Prompts (if you want deeper analysis)**

### **Micro‑Expression Prompt**

“Identify micro‑expressions present in the face: subtle eyebrow lifts, slight smirks, lip tension, nostril flare, or eye narrowing. Explain what each micro‑expression suggests emotionally.”

### **Gaze & Intent Prompt**

“Analyze the model’s gaze direction and intensity. Describe whether the gaze is confrontational, inviting, detached, challenging, seductive, or observational. Explain how gaze contributes to the emotional storytelling.”

### **Emotion‑Environment Interaction Prompt**

“Explain how the environment (carwash grit, water spray, industrial lighting) influences or contrasts with the model’s facial expression. Describe how the emotional tone fits within the physical setting.”
