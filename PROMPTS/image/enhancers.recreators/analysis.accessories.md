# 📿 Accessories Analysis Prompt

## The Prompt

“Analyze all accessories in this image. Identify visible items such as jewelry, hair accessories, belts, watches, or props integrated into styling. Describe their materials, textures, colors, and how they interact with the lighting and environment. Explain how accessories contribute to the editorial theme, whether they add contrast, minimalism, industrial grit, or fashion emphasis. Note how movement, water, or pose affects the visibility or behavior of accessories. If accessories are intentionally absent, explain how that choice supports the styling direction.”
