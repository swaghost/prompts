# Lighting Analysis

## Lighting Prompt A — Environment & Source Identification

“Analyze the lighting in this image. Identify every light source, including natural light, artificial fixtures, reflections, and ambient bounce. Describe the direction, intensity, color temperature, softness/hardness, and how the environment (wet ground, metal surfaces, concrete walls) affects the lighting.”

## Lighting Prompt B — Subject Interaction

“Explain how the lighting interacts with the model: highlights, shadows, contour definition, reflections on skin, wet clothing behavior, and how the light shapes mood or emotion. Note any rim lights, specular highlights, or fill created by reflective surfaces.”

## Lighting Prompt C — Cinematic Behavior

“Break down the cinematic qualities of the lighting: contrast ratio, dynamic range, how water droplets or foam catch light, how industrial fluorescents mix with daylight, and how the lighting contributes to the editorial tone.”
