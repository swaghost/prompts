# 🎥 Camera Angles & Framing Analysis Prompt

# The Prompt

“Analyze the camera angle and framing used in this image. Identify the camera height (low, eye‑level, high), angle (straight‑on, three‑quarter, profile, overhead, under‑angle), and distance (close‑up, medium, full‑body, wide environmental shot). Describe how framing includes or excludes environmental elements, how the subject is positioned within the frame (centered, rule of thirds, diagonal composition), and how lines from the environment (hoses, beams, car edges) guide the viewer’s eye. Explain how the chosen angle affects mood, dominance, intimacy, or dynamism. Note how depth of field, lens behavior, and perspective contribute to the editorial impact.”
