# 3x3 Scene Grid - Character/Product Consistency

## Description

Create a 3×3 grid of 9 photorealistic images based on an attached scene. Maintain the same character/product and location without changes: face, proportions, clothing, environment, lighting, and time of day - everything must match the reference. You need 9 distinct "moments" within this same scene: different actions/micro-narratives, as well as varied shots and compositions.

## Usage

Perfect for commercial photography, product campaigns, lifestyle storytelling, character studies, and creating cohesive visual narratives. Ideal for social media carousels, lookbooks, campaign pitches, storyboards, and demonstrating product versatility within a consistent scene.

## Prerequisites

- Reference scene image or detailed description
- Character or product to feature
- Location/environment details
- Lighting conditions and time of day
- Desired mood and atmosphere
- Shot variety requirements

## Image Prompt

Create a 3×3 grid of 9 photorealistic images based on this scene: [ATTACH REFERENCE IMAGE OR DESCRIBE SCENE]

**Consistency Requirements (Must Match Across All 9 Images):**

- **Character/Product:** [SPECIFY - exact same face, proportions, clothing]
- **Location:** [SPECIFY - identical environment, setting, background]
- **Lighting:** [SPECIFY - same lighting conditions, direction, quality]
- **Time of Day:** [SPECIFY - consistent time, same ambient light]
- **Weather/Atmosphere:** [SPECIFY - matching atmospheric conditions]
- **Color Grading:** Consistent across all frames
- **Photography Style:** Unified aesthetic throughout

**9 Distinct Moments to Create:**

For each frame, devise a bold angle, an expressive gesture or pose, and an emotional hook (do not describe them in words - come up with them yourself). Pay maximum attention to textures, materials, lighting, and atmosphere.

**Shot Composition Mix (Arrange randomly in grid - do not group by type):**

- **1 Wide Shot** - Establishing context, full environment visible
- **2 Medium Shots** - Mid-range framing showing subject and surroundings
- **1 Long Shot** - Full body or product in larger context
- **1 Extreme Close-Up** - Intimate detail, texture focus
- **1 Shot Focusing on Character** - Character-centric moment
- **2 Shots Focusing on Product** - Product as hero in scene
- **1 "Atmosphere" Shot** - Scene without character or product, pure mood

**For Each Frame:**

- Bold, distinct camera angle
- Expressive gesture or pose
- Emotional hook or micro-narrative moment
- Different action within the scene
- Varied composition and framing
- Maximum attention to: textures, materials, lighting, atmosphere

**Grid Arrangement:** 3×3 layout, mix up shot types throughout (do not group by type), each frame should feel like a distinct moment while maintaining perfect consistency in core elements.

**Technical Quality:**

- Photorealistic rendering
- Sharp focus appropriate to shot type
- Real materials and textures
- Natural shadows and lighting
- Professional photography aesthetic
- Consistent color grading

Generate all 9 images with seamless consistency in character/product, location, lighting, and time of day, while creating diverse moments, angles, and emotional beats throughout the grid.

## Negative Prompt

Inconsistent character appearance, changing clothing, different lighting, varying time of day, mismatched environments, poor continuity, artificial looking, low quality, blurry details, inconsistent color grading, repetitive compositions, same angle repeated, generic poses, lack of variety, grouped by shot type, unrealistic materials

## Output Specifications

- **Format:** 3×3 grid of 9 images
- **Consistency:** Perfect match in core elements
- **Variety:** 9 distinct moments and compositions
- **Quality:** Photorealistic, professional photography
- **Arrangement:** Mixed shot types throughout grid
- **Style:** Cohesive visual narrative

## Shot Type Guidelines

**Wide Shot:**

- Full environment context
- Subject/product in larger scene
- Establishing spatial relationships
- Atmosphere and setting emphasis

**Medium Shots (2):**

- Balance of subject and environment
- Mid-range interaction
- Storytelling moments
- Connection between subject and space

**Long Shot:**

- Full body or complete product
- More environment than close-up
- Movement and gesture visible
- Contextual framing

**Extreme Close-Up:**

- Intimate detail focus
- Texture and material emphasis
- Emotional intensity
- Product detail or character expression

**Character-Focused Shot:**

- Character as primary subject
- Expressive moment
- Personality and emotion
- Product may be secondary or absent

**Product-Focused Shots (2):**

- Product as hero element
- Feature highlighting
- Usage demonstration
- Design and detail showcase

**Atmosphere Shot:**

- Scene without main subject
- Environmental mood
- Lighting and texture
- Context and setting establishment

## Micro-Narrative Techniques

**Actions & Moments:**

- Candid, natural movements
- Transitional gestures
- Interaction with environment
- Product usage or display
- Contemplative pauses
- Dynamic motion
- Quiet stillness
- Emotional beats

**Expressive Elements:**

- Body language variety
- Facial expressions (if character)
- Hand gestures and positioning
- Product placement and handling
- Spatial relationships
- Eye direction and gaze
- Posture and stance

**Emotional Hooks:**

- Create feeling in each frame
- Distinct mood per moment
- Narrative progression implied
- Viewer engagement
- Story suggestions
- Personality revelation

## Consistency Checklist

**Must Match Exactly:**

- ✓ Character face and features
- ✓ Character proportions and build
- ✓ Clothing and accessories
- ✓ Product appearance and details
- ✓ Environment and location
- ✓ Lighting direction and quality
- ✓ Time of day indicators
- ✓ Weather conditions
- ✓ Color palette and grading
- ✓ Atmospheric haze or clarity
- ✓ Shadows and highlights
- ✓ Background elements

**Should Vary:**

- ✓ Camera angle and distance
- ✓ Framing and composition
- ✓ Character pose and gesture
- ✓ Product positioning
- ✓ Action or moment
- ✓ Focal point and emphasis
- ✓ Depth of field (when appropriate)
- ✓ Narrative beat
