# COCKTAIL BAR

## Lighting & Atmosphere
Use direct harsh on-camera flash with very little soft lighting, creating a dark nighttime atmosphere with wet streets reflecting warm neon signs, cafés, bars, and street lamps.

## Camera Style
Capture the scene in the style of an early-2000s CCD digital camera with heavy grain, visible high ISO noise, a slight green tint, weak dynamic range, subtle motion blur, soft bloom around bright lights, dirty lens flare, tiny chromatic aberration, and an optional timestamp for an authentic vintage feel.

## Color Grade & Styling
Apply a warm color grade with orange street lights, green shadows, slightly yellow highlights, low-contrast blacks, muted skin tones, and classic digital color science. Style the subject in a fitted white tee, black leather jackets....
