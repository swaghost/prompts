# Diagonal Slash Cinematic Lighting Enhancement

## Description of Resulting Image

A dramatically relit portrait featuring low-key cinematic lighting with a distinctive narrow diagonal band of hard light cutting across the face at eye level. The light enters from the upper left and descends toward the lower right, creating a striking slash of illumination across eyes, nose bridge, and cheekbone while the rest of the face falls into deep shadow. A bright rim light traces the hair outline, separating the silhouette from a deep blue gradient background with crushed blacks and muted highlights.

## Usage

This enhancer is perfect for creating dramatic editorial portraits, cinematic character studies, or moody artistic portraits with high visual impact. Use when transforming standard portraits into theatrical, emotionally intense images with strong chiaroscuro aesthetics. Apply this technique when you need to add dramatic lighting interest to flat-lit images or when creating portfolio pieces that demand attention through bold lighting design.

## Actor or Subject

Any portrait subject with clearly defined facial features. Works best with forward-facing or slightly angled portraits where the face is the primary focus and can receive the dramatic diagonal lighting treatment.

## Image Prompt

### Subject

Use the first image as the base image. Preserve the exact facial structure, features, proportions, expression, age, pose, camera distance, framing, hairstyle, and clothing with zero alterations. Do not reshape or reinterpret the subject in any way.

### Setting

Replace the background with a deep blue gradient, brighter toward the lower frame and darkening toward the upper corners, with a subtle atmospheric glow behind the head reinforcing the rim light.

### Lighting

Completely relight the scene using dramatic low-key cinematic lighting. The face falls mostly into deep shadow, with one defining element: a narrow diagonal band of hard light cutting across the face at the eye level, entering from the upper left and descending toward the lower right, illuminating the left brow, both eyes, the bridge of the nose, and the top of the right cheekbone, like a slash of light across an otherwise darkened face. The edges of this light band are defined but naturally feathered, as if cast through a narrow opening, with realistic falloff. Everything outside the band—forehead, lower cheeks, mouth, jaw, neck, and torso—sinks into near-black shadow while retaining subtle detail in the darks. Add a hard light source positioned high and behind the subject creating a bright rim of light tracing the outline of the hair, catching individual backlit strands and separating the silhouette from the background with a warm glowing halo that conveys depth.

### Composition

Eyes remain sharp and luminous inside the lit band, acting as the absolute focal point. The diagonal light slash creates strong geometric interest and draws the viewer's eye across the face.
