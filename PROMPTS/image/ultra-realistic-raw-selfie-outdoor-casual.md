## Best Practices

### iPhone RAW Photography

- **Shoot RAW**: Enable ProRAW format in iPhone camera settings
- **HDR Smart**: Use Smart HDR for balanced dynamic range
- **Manual Blur**: Apply Portrait mode or manual blur in post
- **No Filters**: Avoid Instagram filters for natural look
- **Natural Light**: Rely on available evening light only

### Selfie Technique

- **Arm Position**: Hold phone at arm's length naturally
- **Angle**: Slightly above eye level and to side for flattering angle
- **Stability**: Keep hand steady or use timer for sharpness
- **Focus**: Tap to focus on face before shooting
- **Framing**: Check background in frame before capturing

### Golden Hour Timing

- **Time Window**: Shoot 1-2 hours before sunset
- **Light Quality**: Soft, warm, golden light
- **Direction**: Position to catch warm sunlight on face
- **Shadows**: Utilize natural shadow fall for depth
- **Consistency**: Work quickly as light changes

### Styling Authenticity

- **Casual Wear**: Choose comfortable, authentic casual outfits
- **Natural Fit**: Clothing should fit naturally, not styled
- **Lived-In Look**: Distressed denim should look naturally worn
- **Minimal Accessories**: Keep accessories minimal and functional
- **Hair Natural**: Allow hair to fall naturally with minimal styling
