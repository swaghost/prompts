# Miniature Figure Trapped in Overturned Glass - Surreal Confinement

## Description of Resulting Image

A hyper-realistic surreal macro photograph depicting a miniature human figure trapped inside an overturned clear drinking glass. The confined subject appears crouched with one hand pressed desperately against the curved inner wall, creating a powerful sense of entrapment. Shot with shallow depth of field producing intense bokeh, the image features warm cinematic lighting with complex refractive distortions, specular highlights, and ray-traced caustics casting subtle light patterns on the polished wooden table surface.

## Usage

Use this prompt when creating conceptual photography that explores themes of confinement, isolation, or psychological constraint through surreal miniaturization.
