# Identity-Locked 8K Image Restoration

## Purpose

Upscale and restore a supplied image while preserving its original identity, composition, visual character, and content.

## Prerequisites

- Original image supplied as the primary visual reference
- Image upscaling or restoration model

## Prompt

```text
Use the provided original image as the primary source and upscale and restore it from low resolution to ultra-high-resolution 8K quality.

Preserve the original image exactly. Maintain the same subject, exact facial identity, facial proportions, expression, pose, body proportions, hairstyle, hairline, facial hair, skin tone, clothing, accessories, composition, camera angle, framing, background, lighting, shadows, colors, and overall visual appearance.

Enable strict facial consistency and identity lock. Preserve the exact facial structure and recognizable characteristics from the source image. Do not redesign, beautify, reshape, age, de-age, or reinterpret the face.

Enhance only resolution, sharpness, clarity, texture fidelity, and fine detail. Recover realistic facial details, natural skin pores, eyelashes, eyebrows, individual hair strands, beard strands, fabric fibers, clothing textures, edges, environmental textures, and other fine details while remaining completely faithful to the source.

Remove low-resolution artifacts, pixelation, compression artifacts, JPEG noise, color banding, unwanted grain, softness, blurry edges, aliasing, and digital artifacts. Carefully reconstruct missing micro-details naturally without inventing or changing important features.

Produce ultra-clean 8K-quality detail, high dynamic range, excellent tonal separation, accurate colors, crisp natural edges, realistic textures, professional photographic clarity, and refined micro-contrast.

Keep skin natural and photorealistic. Do not create waxy, plastic, overly smooth, artificially sharpened, or AI-looking skin.

Do not change the composition or content of the original image. This is a high-fidelity restoration and upscale only, not a redesign or re-generation.

Final result: pristine ultra-high-resolution 8K restoration, exceptionally detailed and photorealistic, while looking unmistakably like the exact original image at dramatically higher resolution.
```

## Negative Constraints

- No identity drift, face replacement, or facial feature redesign
- No changes to pose, clothing, accessories, scene content, camera angle, crop, or composition
- No invented objects, text, patterns, details, or environmental elements
- No beauty filter, smoothing, plastic skin, halos, oversharpening, or painterly texture
- No changes to lighting direction, color palette, shadows, or visual mood
