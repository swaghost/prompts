# Brand Identity Board Builder

## Purpose

Create a complete visual identity board for a premium brand, including the logo direction, color system, typography, graphic language, packaging, photography, and supporting brand assets.

## Prompt

```
Create a complete visual identity for "[BRAND NAME]", a premium [BRAND CATEGORY] inspired by [CORE INSPIRATION]. Develop the logo direction, [COLOR PALETTE DIRECTION] color palette, typography, graphic elements, packaging style, photography direction, patterns, and supporting brand assets. Keep the identity [BRAND TRAITS], with a cohesive system that works across packaging, physical spaces, website, and social media. Present it as a polished professional brand identity board with clear visual hierarchy, refined spacing, realistic material details, and a premium editorial art-direction quality.
```

## Example: Morrow Coffee Co.

```
Create a complete visual identity for "Morrow Coffee Co.", a premium specialty coffee brand inspired by slow mornings and warm sunlight. Develop the logo direction, earthy color palette, typography, graphic elements, packaging style, photography direction, patterns, and supporting brand assets. Keep the identity warm, minimal, contemporary, and highly recognizable, with a cohesive system that works across packaging, cafes, and social media. Present it as a polished professional brand identity board.
```

## Reference and Output Rules

- Use uploaded brand references as the visual source of truth when provided.
- Show logo direction, primary and secondary marks, palette swatches, type hierarchy, patterns, packaging applications, photography examples, and material cues.
- Keep the board organized as a clean editorial layout with deliberate whitespace and consistent alignment.
- Any displayed brand name or sample copy must be spelled exactly and remain legible.
- Generate a coherent system rather than unrelated mood-board images.

## Technical Specifications

16:9 presentation board or 4:5 social format, high resolution, premium editorial design, realistic print and packaging materials, crisp typography, natural color, professional brand presentation.

## Negative Prompt

No random logos, misspelled brand names, illegible typography, unrelated color palettes, generic stock imagery, cluttered layout, inconsistent packaging, competing brand identities, low-resolution assets, fake 3D text, or unfinished presentation-board elements.
