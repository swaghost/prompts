# Professional Remaster and Enhancement Builder

## Purpose

Improve the quality and clarity of an existing image while preserving its exact subject, identity, background, composition, and visible details.

## Prompt

```
Create an ultra-premium 16K professional remaster and enhancement of the provided image. Preserve the image exactly as provided while dramatically improving quality, realism, sharpness, and clarity. Enhance only existing visible details; do not invent, generate, reconstruct, or add facial features, hair, beard strands, clothing details, objects, or textures that are not present in the source. Maintain the exact face, hairstyle, beard density, expression, skin tone, facial proportions, lighting, colors, clothing, and composition. Improve only through faithful super-resolution, natural sharpening, artifact removal, realistic noise reduction, texture refinement, detail recovery, and physically believable dynamic range. Preserve the original background exactly; do not replace, redesign, extend, or reimagine it. Apply subtle improvements to clarity, textures, gradients, edges, lighting fidelity, and resolution while keeping the image visually identical in structure.
```

## Reference and Output Rules

- Treat the uploaded image as immutable source material.
- Recover detail only where evidence exists in the source.
- Preserve identity and anatomy without beautification or age changes.
- Keep the same crop, camera perspective, pose, expression, background, lighting direction, and color relationships.
- Use restrained professional camera processing rather than a stylized reinterpretation.

## Technical Specifications

16K output, faithful super-resolution, natural sharpening, realistic noise reduction, texture refinement, clean highlights, rich shadows, natural micro-contrast, crystal-clear focus, luxury commercial photography finish, medium-format fidelity.

## Negative Prompt

No background replacement or redesign, new elements, added objects, thicker beard, extra facial hair, invented skin texture, generated pores, AI hallucinations, facial modification, identity change, age change, altered composition, oversharpening, plastic skin, CGI, illustration, synthetic detail, or unrealistic textures.
