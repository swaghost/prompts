# Branded Product Mockup Builder

## Purpose

Apply an existing brand artwork or identity system to a realistic product mockup while preserving the original artwork precisely.

## Prompt

```
Use the provided brand artwork as the primary visual reference and apply it accurately to a premium [PRODUCT] mockup. Preserve the artwork's colors, typography, proportions, logo placement, layout, and key details. Create a realistic lifestyle scene with [SETTING AND MATERIALS], believable perspective, physically accurate lighting, realistic shadows, tactile surfaces, and subtle natural imperfections. Make the final image look like a professionally photographed brand campaign, not a digitally composited mockup. The product must remain the clear focal point and the brand identity must be immediately recognizable.
```

## Customization Fields

- **[PRODUCT]**: coffee bag, carton, bottle, cup, box, apparel, or other branded object
- **[SETTING AND MATERIALS]**: stone countertop, warm cafe table, linen, wood, ceramic, glass, or paper
- **[CAMPAIGN MOOD]**: calm, tactile, energetic, refined, playful, or premium

## Reference and Output Rules

- Use the supplied artwork as the only source for brand design details.
- Do not redraw, reinterpret, recolor, or simplify the logo or artwork.
- Keep the product proportions and packaging construction believable.
- Show realistic print, embossing, ink, foil, paper, glass, fabric, or label texture as appropriate.
- Use a natural camera perspective and physically plausible reflections and shadows.

## Technical Specifications

Commercial product photography, high resolution, realistic materials, natural depth of field, accurate perspective, premium lifestyle campaign finish, 4:5 or 16:9 composition as required.

## Negative Prompt

No altered logo, misspelled text, changed colors, warped typography, incorrect proportions, floating product, impossible reflections, excessive CGI gloss, pasted-on artwork, flat digital mockup, generic background, watermark, or unrelated branding.
