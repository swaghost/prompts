# Cinematic Realistic Facial Expressions - 8 Emotion Prompts

## Description

Eight cinematic emotion prompts for creating natural facial expressions, micro-expressions, body language, and believable emotional performances in AI-generated portraits. The set covers joy, surprise, sadness, fear, anger, shyness, embarrassment, and curiosity.

## Usage

Use these prompts for AI influencers, character design, acting references, emotion studies, social-media portraits, cinematic photography, animation references, and character-consistency workflows. Append one emotion block to an identity-locked portrait prompt, or use it as the expression and performance section of a larger scene prompt.

## Application Rules

- Preserve the subject's identity and defining facial anatomy.
- Treat the expression as coordinated facial-muscle action, not a sticker-like facial change.
- Keep eye direction, eyelids, brows, cheeks, lips, jaw, neck, shoulders, breathing, hands, and posture emotionally coherent.
- Use restrained micro-expressions and natural asymmetry.
- Preserve pores, folds, smile lines, moisture, blush, and tension appropriate to the emotion.
- Keep gestures anatomically plausible and hands naturally posed.
- Do not mix contradictory emotions unless the scene explicitly requires emotional conflict.
- Match emotional intensity to the framing and narrative context.

## Negative Prompt

```text
different identity, altered facial geometry, changed age, changed ethnicity, changed hairstyle, changed wardrobe, plastic skin, waxy skin, beauty-filter smoothing, frozen face, expressionless eyes, dead eyes, mismatched eye direction, contradictory expression, exaggerated cartoon emotion, theatrical grimace, forced smile, impossible eyebrow position, distorted mouth, malformed teeth, duplicated features, asymmetrical eye error, crossed eyes, deformed hands, extra fingers, fused fingers, broken wrists, impossible posture, unrelated gesture, stiff body language, anime, illustration, low resolution, blur, text, logo, watermark
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The intended emotion is readable without a caption.
- [ ] Facial muscles, gaze, breathing, posture, and gestures communicate the same emotion.
- [ ] Micro-expressions remain realistic rather than exaggerated.
- [ ] The subject's identity, age, facial proportions, and skin texture are preserved.
- [ ] Hands and body language are anatomically natural.
- [ ] Lighting, framing, wardrobe, and background remain consistent when using a reference image.
- [ ] No contradictory emotion, frozen eyes, plastic skin, or facial distortion appears.
