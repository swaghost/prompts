**Step 1: Take Your Photos**

- Take 2-3 selfies in natural daylight
- Minimal or no makeup
- Clear, well-lit, no filters
- Front-facing, side profile, and optional full-body

**Step 2: Open ChatGPT**

**Step 3: Upload Photos**

- Attach all 2-4 photos to the chat

**Step 4: Paste the Prompt**

- Copy the complete prompt above
- Paste into ChatGPT
- Send

**Step 5: Generate Pages Incrementally** (IMPORTANT)

The prompt asks for 8 different pages/images, which can sometimes overwhelm the AI.

**Option A: One page at a time**

- "Please create PAGE 1 and PAGE 2"

- "I'd like you to create PAGE 1, PAGE 3, PAGE 5, and PAGE 8"
- Choose only the pages most relevant to you

**Step 6: Review and Refine**

- Review each generated page
- Request adjustments if needed: "Can you make the makeup in PAGE 3 more natural?" or "Can you show me with a different hair color in PAGE 5?"

**Step 7: Save and Share**

- Download each page as high-resolution image
- Post as Instagram carousel if desired

## Incremental Generation Strategy

### Why Generate Pages Incrementally

**The Challenge:**

- The prompt asks for 8 separate high-resolution images (1080×1350px each)
- This can overwhelm ChatGPT's processing capacity
- May result in incomplete pages, lower quality, or errors

**The Solution:**
Generate pages in smaller batches or one at a time

### Three Recommended Approaches

**Approach 1: One Page at a Time** (Safest, Highest Quality)

```
"Please create PAGE 1 first — my Personal Colour Analysis"
```

Wait for completion, review, then:

```
"Great! Now please create PAGE 2 — Colour Draping"
```

Continue through all 8 pages.

**Pros:**

- Highest quality per page
- AI can focus full attention on each
- Easier to review and request edits
- Less likely to error or crash

**Cons:**

- Takes longer (8 separate requests)
- Requires more back-and-forth

---

**Approach 2: Two Pages at a Time** (Balanced)

```
"Please create PAGE 1 and PAGE 2 — my Personal Colour Analysis and Colour Draping"
```

Wait for completion, review, then:

```
"Great! Now create PAGE 3 and PAGE 4 — my Makeup Analysis and Placement Guide"
```

Continue in pairs.

**Pros:**

- Good balance of quality and efficiency
- Faster than one-at-a-time
- Still manageable for AI
- Thematically paired pages often flow well

**Cons:**

- Still requires 4 requests
- Slightly more chance of quality variation

---

**Approach 3: Select Specific Pages** (Customized)

```
"I'd like you to create these specific pages:
- PAGE 1 — Personal Colour Analysis
- PAGE 3 — Makeup Analysis
- PAGE 5 — Hair Analysis
- PAGE 8 — My Best Look"
```

**Pros:**

- Fastest option
- Focus on pages most relevant to you
- Skip pages you don't need
- Can always request others later

**Cons:**

- May miss valuable insights from skipped pages
- Less comprehensive consultation

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue 1: AI Substitutes Another Face

**Problem:** Generated pages show someone else's face instead of yours, or use stock model photos.

**Solutions:**

- **Reiterate identity lock:** "Please use ONLY my uploaded photographs. Every page must show MY actual face. Do not substitute another person."
- **Reference specific page:** "In PAGE 5, you used a different person's face. Please regenerate using my uploaded photos only."
- **Strengthen instruction:** "The person in the generated images doesn't look like me. Please recreate using my exact facial features from my photos."
- **Start over if needed:** If AI continues to substitute faces, start a new chat and paste prompt again, emphasizing identity lock section

---

### Issue 2: Face is Beautified or Altered

**Problem:** Your facial features are enhanced, smoothed, or altered (bigger eyes, slimmer nose, different face shape).

**Solutions:**

- **Emphasize preservation:** "Please maintain my EXACT facial features. Do not beautify, enhance, enlarge eyes, slim nose, or alter my face structure."
- **Specific correction:** "My nose looks different in this image. Please keep my exact nose shape from the photos."
- **Reference natural features:** "Please keep my natural freckles, skin texture, and facial proportions exactly as they appear in my photos."
- **Reject beautification:** "This looks too polished/filtered. Please show my natural features without smoothing or enhancement."

---

### Issue 3: Design Looks Like Generic AI Infographic

**Problem:** Pages have a rigid, templated, obviously-AI aesthetic instead of luxury editorial magazine quality.

**Solutions:**

- **Request redesign:** "This looks too much like an AI infographic. Please redesign with luxury editorial magazine aesthetic — think Vogue Beauty or House of Colour."
- **Specify design elements:** "Please use warm ivory background, dark olive headings, elegant serif typography, and generous white space. No clip art or rigid layouts."
- **Reference luxury brands:** "The design should feel like a Dior or Chanel beauty consultation, not a generic template."
- **Request specific page style:** "PAGE 3 needs to look more editorial and luxurious. Can you redesign with more sophisticated layout and styling?"

---

### Issue 4: Recommendations Feel Generic

**Problem:** Advice seems like standard suggestions not personalized to your specific features.

**Solutions:**

- **Request specificity:** "These recommendations feel generic. Please analyze my ACTUAL features more carefully and personalize every suggestion to me specifically."
- **Ask why:** "Can you explain WHY each of these colors/styles flatters MY specific features? I need personalized reasoning."
- **Reference your features:** "You haven't mentioned my [eye color/face shape/skin tone]. Please analyze these features and tailor recommendations accordingly."
- **Compare and contrast:** "Show me why this color works for me versus why another color doesn't, specific to my coloring."

---

### Issue 5: Image Quality Too Low

**Problem:** Generated pages are low resolution, blurry, or not suitable for saving/posting.

**Solutions:**

- **Specify clarity:** "The images are too low quality. Please regenerate with crisp, clear, high-resolution graphics."

### Issue 6: Wrong Color Season or Analysis

**Problem:** Color analysis doesn't seem accurate to your actual coloring or you disagree with assessment.

**Solutions:**

- **Provide more context:** "I've been professionally analyzed as [Spring/Summer/Autumn/Winter]. Can you reassess with this in mind?"
- **Question specific elements:** "I don't think I'm [X season]. My skin has [warm/cool] undertones and [high/low] contrast. Can you reconsider?"
- **Request explanation:** "Can you explain in detail why you determined I'm [X season]? Walk me through your analysis."
- **Upload better photos:** "Let me upload additional photos in better natural lighting for more accurate color analysis."
- **Try different subcategories:** "I think I might be [Bright Spring vs. Warm Spring]. Can you compare these subcategories for me?"

**Q: Do I need ChatGPT Plus?**

**Q: What if I'm between seasons?**
A: Most people are! The AI can identify you as a subcategory (e.g., "Bright Spring" or "Soft Autumn") which accounts for being between two main seasons.

**Q: Will this work for all ethnicities and skin tones?**

**Q: Can I share my results online?**
A: Yes, the pages are designed as Instagram-ready content. Many people share as carousel posts or Stories.

A: While in-person draping with physical fabrics is gold standard, AI analysis can be surprisingly accurate with good photos. Many find it matches their professional analysis. Best used as strong guidance, not absolute truth.

**Q: What if I disagree with the recommendations?**
A: Trust your instincts! This is a guide, not law. If something doesn't feel right, you can ask AI to reconsider or adapt recommendations. Your personal preference matters.

**Q: Can I get recommendations for someone else?**
A: Yes, just use their photos instead of yours. Make sure you have their permission to analyze and share their images.

**Q: How long does the whole process take?**
