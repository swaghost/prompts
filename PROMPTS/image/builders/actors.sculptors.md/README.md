## Material Categories

### Traditional Materials

- **Clay:** Wet, malleable, with fingerprints and tool marks
- **White Marble (Carrara):** Pristine stone with natural veins, chisel work
- **Oak Wood:** Solid hardwood with visible grain, chisel marks
- **White Plaster:** Fine texture, chips, powder residue

### Metal & Industrial Materials

- **Bronze (Fresh Cast):** Dark reflective surface, heat discoloration
- **Bronze (Aged Patina):** Deep brown/green oxidation, polished highlights
- **Matte Black Resin:** Smooth synthetic, sanding marks
- **Raw Concrete:** Brutalist aesthetic, exposed rebar, air bubbles
