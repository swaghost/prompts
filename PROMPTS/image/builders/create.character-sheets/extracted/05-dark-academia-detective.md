# Character Sheet Prompt 05 - Dark Academia Trench and Detective Sleuth

## Use

Create a cinematic character reference sheet for a mystery protagonist from one uploaded reference photo, preserving the brooding identity and wardrobe across all views.

## Master Consistency Anchor

```text
Maintain the exact facial identity, bone structure, eye shape, skin tone, and hair texture of the person in the uploaded reference photo across every panel of the character sheet. Do not alter their face. Preserve the same age, ethnicity, body proportions, hair, outfit, accessories, and shoes unless the selected style prompt explicitly specifies a new wardrobe. Render a comprehensive high-resolution character design sheet using the requested layout, with every panel clearly showing the same person.
```

## Image Prompt

```text
Maintain the exact facial structure and brooding expression of the person in the uploaded reference photo across all views. Create a cinematic character reference sheet for a mystery protagonist on an aged grey-paper background.

The top section shows a three-way full-body turnaround: FRONT, SIDE PROFILE, and BACK. Dress the subject in a tailored charcoal wool overcoat over a black knitted turtleneck sweater, straight-leg wool trousers, and leather boots.

The left column contains three expression headshots: NEUTRAL, THOUGHTFUL, and DETERMINED. The centre-bottom section displays four dynamic poses: hands in coat pockets while walking forward, leaning forward on a stool, pulling coat lapels together, and walking in motion. Add circular callouts for wool overcoat texture, turtleneck knit, and vintage leather watch.

Use cinematic noir production-asset styling, restrained typography, atmospheric but readable lighting, realistic wool and leather textures, and identity consistency throughout. Use a modular 4:5 vertical layout with complete figures and feet visible.
```

## Workflow and Parameters

Upload one clear front-facing reference photo, paste the anchor, then paste the image prompt. For Midjourney or Flux.1 append: `[IMAGE_URL] dark academia detective character reference sheet, front side back turnaround, noir expressions, movement poses, wool knit leather callouts, 8K --ar 4:5 --style raw --cref [IMAGE_URL] --cw 20 --iw 2.0`.

Add exact labels and character metadata in post-production. Keep the grey-paper background, restrained typography, charcoal coat, black knitwear, boots, and watch consistent.

## Production Checklist

- Front, side profile, and back views are present.
- Neutral, thoughtful, and determined headshots are present.
- Four walking, stool, lapel, and motion poses are present.
- Wool, knit, and leather-watch callouts are present.
- Face, brooding expression, hair, proportions, and wardrobe remain consistent.
- Noir layout and labels remain readable.

## Negative Prompt

```text
Altered identity, inconsistent brooding expression, changing coat, missing turnaround view, malformed boots, extra fingers, duplicate limbs, cropped feet, unreadable labels, excessive props, panel overlap, cartoon style, plastic skin, logos, watermark, low resolution, compositing artifacts.
```
