# Character Sheet Prompt 04 - Evening Gown and Luxury Gala Lookbook

## Use

Create a luxury fashion-design character turnaround sheet from one uploaded reference photo, documenting the gala wardrobe, expressions, poses, and materials.

## Master Consistency Anchor

```text
Maintain the exact facial identity, bone structure, eye shape, skin tone, and hair texture of the person in the uploaded reference photo across every panel of the character sheet. Do not alter their face. Preserve the same age, ethnicity, body proportions, hair, outfit, accessories, and shoes unless the selected style prompt explicitly specifies a new wardrobe. Render a comprehensive high-resolution character design sheet using the requested layout, with every panel clearly showing the same person.
```

## Image Prompt

```text
Maintain the exact facial identity, hair texture, and body proportions of the person in the uploaded reference photo across every panel. Create a luxury fashion-design character turnaround sheet on a soft warm-beige backdrop.

The top section shows a full-body turnaround in FRONT VIEW, 90-DEGREE SIDE VIEW, and BACK VIEW. Dress the subject in a glamorous floor-length jet-black satin-silk evening gown with spaghetti straps, a high leg slit, and open back, paired with black stiletto heels.

The upper-right section shows four facial expressions: NEUTRAL EDITORIAL, WARM HAPPY SMILE, PENSIVE WITH FINGER ON LIP, and PLAYFUL. The lower row shows dynamic poses holding a champagne glass, walking elegantly, and sitting gracefully on a black chair with legs crossed. Bottom swatches show black satin fabric, hazel-eye detail, and a skin-tone palette.

Use a high-end lookbook layout, refined studio lighting, realistic satin and skin texture, and consistent identity in every panel. Use a modular 4:5 vertical sheet with complete figures and feet visible.
```

## Workflow and Parameters

Upload one clear front-facing reference photo, paste the anchor, then paste the image prompt. For Midjourney or Flux.1 append: `[IMAGE_URL] luxury gala fashion character sheet, front side back turnaround, editorial expressions, elegant poses, satin and skin swatches, 8K --ar 4:5 --style raw --cref [IMAGE_URL] --cw 20 --iw 2.0`.

Add exact labels, metadata, and lookbook typography in post-production. Keep the gown construction, slit, open back, heels, and skin-tone palette consistent across all panels.

## Production Checklist

- Front, side, and back gown views are present.
- Four gala expressions are present.
- Champagne, walking, and seated poses are present.
- Satin, eye, and skin-tone swatches are present.
- Face, hair, proportions, gown, and heels remain consistent.
- Complete figures, labels, glass, and chair are readable.

## Negative Prompt

```text
Altered identity, inconsistent hair, changing gown construction, missing open back or slit, malformed heels, extra fingers, duplicate limbs, cropped feet, broken champagne glass, unreadable labels, extra jewelry, overlapping panels, plastic skin, logos, watermark, low resolution, compositing artifacts.
```
