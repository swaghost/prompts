# Character Sheet Prompt 03 - Pro Footballer and Athletic Kit

## Use

Create a commercial sports character sheet and athlete reference board from one uploaded reference photo. Lock the athlete's face and athletic build across every panel.

## Master Consistency Anchor

```text
Maintain the exact facial identity, bone structure, eye shape, skin tone, and hair texture of the person in the uploaded reference photo across every panel of the character sheet. Do not alter their face. Preserve the same age, ethnicity, body proportions, hair, outfit, accessories, and shoes unless the selected style prompt explicitly specifies a new wardrobe. Render a comprehensive high-resolution character design sheet using the requested layout, with every panel clearly showing the same person.
```

## Image Prompt

```text
Maintain the exact facial features and athletic build of the person in the uploaded reference photo across all panels. Create a commercial sports character sheet and athlete reference board on an off-white studio background.

The top section shows a full-body three-angle turnaround: FRONT, SIDE, and BACK. The subject wears a premium blue international football jersey with number 07 and national badge, white athletic shorts, team socks, and speed cleats.

The middle-right section displays four facial-expression portraits: FOCUSED, HAPPY CELEBRATION, DETERMINED YELL, and CALM. The bottom centre shows three dynamic action poses: sprinting with a soccer ball, sliding celebration on knees, and kicking a strike. The left section shows circular fabric callouts for jersey knit, mesh ventilation panels, and cleat spikes.

Use high-contrast commercial sports photography with hyper-detailed sweat and fabric textures. Keep the same face, build, kit, and identity throughout. Use a modular 4:5 vertical layout with full-body figures and feet visible.
```

## Workflow and Parameters

Upload one clear front-facing reference photo, paste the anchor, then paste the image prompt. For Midjourney or Flux.1 append: `[IMAGE_URL] commercial footballer character sheet, front side back turnaround, athletic expressions, action poses, jersey and cleat material callouts, 8K --ar 4:5 --style raw --cref [IMAGE_URL] --cw 20 --iw 2.0`.

Add exact kit labels and metadata in post-production. Keep number 07, national badge, pose names, and panel labels clean and intentional.

## Production Checklist

- Front, side, and back kit views are present.
- Four specified sports expressions are present.
- Sprint, knee-slide, and kicking poses are present.
- Jersey knit, mesh, and cleat-spike callouts are present.
- Face, athletic build, jersey, number, and identity stay consistent.
- Ball, cleats, full-body figures, and labels are readable.

## Negative Prompt

```text
Altered identity, inconsistent athletic build, changing kit, wrong jersey number, missing national badge, malformed football, extra players, duplicate limbs, distorted cleats, cropped feet, unreadable labels, logos beyond requested kit details, watermark, low resolution, compositing artifacts.
```
