# Character Sheet Prompt 06 - Modern Business Chic and Wide-Leg Trousers

## Use

Create a contemporary corporate editorial character sheet from one uploaded reference photo, documenting professional wardrobe, expressions, poses, and fabric details.

## Master Consistency Anchor

```text
Maintain the exact facial identity, bone structure, eye shape, skin tone, and hair texture of the person in the uploaded reference photo across every panel of the character sheet. Do not alter their face. Preserve the same age, ethnicity, body proportions, hair, outfit, accessories, and shoes unless the selected style prompt explicitly specifies a new wardrobe. Render a comprehensive high-resolution character design sheet using the requested layout, with every panel clearly showing the same person.
```

## Image Prompt

```text
Maintain the exact facial features and wavy hair of the person in the uploaded reference photo. Create a contemporary corporate editorial character sheet on a minimal cream background.

The top section shows a full-body turnaround in FRONT, PROFILE, and BACK views. Dress the subject in a cream silk button-down blouse with rolled sleeves, high-waisted wide-leg black trousers, a gold belt buckle, and pointed black heels.

The middle row features four headshot expressions: SOFT SMILE, JOYFUL LAUGH, FOCUSED, and CALM. The bottom row showcases five full-body poses: hands in pockets, holding a coffee cup, carrying a leather tote bag, holding a digital tablet, and sitting casually on a stool. Add fabric swatches for ribbed silk and matte cotton.

Use clean graphic presentation, contemporary corporate-editorial styling, realistic fabric texture, and consistent identity across all panels. Use a modular 4:5 vertical layout with complete figures and feet visible.
```

## Workflow and Parameters

Upload one clear front-facing reference photo, paste the anchor, then paste the image prompt. For Midjourney or Flux.1 append: `[IMAGE_URL] modern business chic character sheet, front profile back turnaround, corporate expressions, professional poses, silk and cotton fabric swatches, 8K --ar 4:5 --style raw --cref [IMAGE_URL] --cw 20 --iw 2.0`.

Add exact labels and metadata in post-production. Keep FRONT, PROFILE, and BACK labels, the cream and black wardrobe, accessories, and fabric chips aligned and legible.

## Production Checklist

- Front, profile, and back views are present.
- Four specified corporate expressions are present.
- Five professional poses are present.
- Ribbed silk and matte cotton swatches are present.
- Face, wavy hair, proportions, wardrobe, and identity remain consistent.
- Tote, tablet, cup, stool, complete figures, and labels are readable.

## Negative Prompt

```text
Altered identity, inconsistent wavy hair, changing wardrobe, missing turnaround view, malformed tablet or tote, extra fingers, duplicate limbs, cropped feet, unreadable labels, excessive props, overlapping panels, plastic fabric, beauty filter, logos, watermark, low resolution, compositing artifacts.
```
