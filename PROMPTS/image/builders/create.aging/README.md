# Age Progression Portrait Collection

Ultra-photorealistic professional portrait prompts featuring the same person at different ages using vertical split-screen composition. Emphasizes strict identity preservation, realistic biological aging, and professional photographic finish.

## Collection Overview

1 age progression prompt featuring vertical split-screen layout with present-day and elderly versions of the same subject.

### Prompt List

1. **[Vertical Split Screen 2026-2080](01-vertical-split-screen-2026-2080.md)**
   - Top half: Current age (2026)
   - Bottom half: 90 years old (2080)
   - Dark green sweatshirt consistent across both halves
   - Warm beige/terracotta matte wall background
   - Natural window light with defined shadow
   - 4:5 vertical aspect ratio

## Key Features

### Identity Preservation

- **Absolute identity lock:** Same person, unmistakably recognizable
- **Eye color lock:** Exact same iris hue, pattern, saturation (no aging alteration)
- **Facial structure:** Preserved bone structure, proportions, distinctive features
- **Natural asymmetries:** All unique characteristics maintained
- **Expression continuity:** Same recognizable expression/smile

### Realistic 90-Year-Old Aging

- Dense network of deep forehead wrinkles
- Pronounced crow's feet and tear troughs
- Deep nasolabial folds and marionette lines
- Loss of cheek volume, sunken temples
- Realistic jowls, loose neck skin
- Thin, slightly translucent aged skin
- Age spots, uneven pigmentation
- Grey-white eyebrows and hair
- Considerably more wrinkles than typical 70-year-old
- Healthy, dignified appearance (not skeletal or sickly)

### Composition Specifications

- **Layout:** Vertical split screen (top/bottom)
- **Top half:** Person at current age, labeled "2026"
- **Bottom half:** Same person at 90 years old, labeled "2080"
- **Dividing line:** Clean, subtle horizontal line at midpoint
- **Framing:** Close intimate crop, both complete faces fully visible
- **Orientation:** Both face forward, direct camera gaze
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 vertical

### Outfit Requirements

- **Garment:** Identical dark green sweatshirt in both halves
- **Color:** Bright, vivid, saturated (not muted or desaturated)
- **Style:** Crewneck or hoodie collar
- **Texture:** Realistic knit/fleece with soft fuzzy fabric
- **Consistency:** Same hue, fit, drape across both ages
- **Accessories:** All visible items preserved (piercings, jewelry, etc.)

### Background & Lighting

- **Wall:** Smooth matte beige with subtle terracotta undertone
- **Finish:** Clean, uniform, no objects or decorations
- **Window light:** Strong natural sunlight from unseen window
- **Shadow:** Clearly defined window-frame shadow with geometric edges
- **Direction:** Strong directional light from one side
- **Consistency:** Exact same lighting pattern in both halves
- **Quality:** Warm sunlit highlights, defined natural shadows

### Professional Photography Standards

- High-end editorial portrait photography
- Crisp focus on both sets of eyes
- Natural skin texture with visible pores
- Smooth highlight roll-off
- Subtle analog film color response
- Fine organic film grain
- Rich refined contrast
- Medium-format portrait quality
- 85mm portrait lens aesthetic
- Shallow depth of field

## Text Overlay

- **Top label:** "2026" - bold clean sans-serif black text
- **Bottom label:** "2080" - same style and size
- **Placement:** Near top of respective halves, above/beside head
- **Background position:** On wall, not overlapping face or shadow

## Critical Constraints

### Must Include

- Exactly two versions of same individual
- Top = 2026 (current), Bottom = 2080 (90 years old)
- Elderly version clearly reads as 90 with realistic wrinkles
- Exact same eye color in both halves (no clouding/fading)
- Both look directly into camera
- Both complete faces fully visible
- Identical dark green sweatshirt in both halves
- Same pose, expression, lighting, setting
- Warm beige/terracotta wall with window shadow
- Year labels clearly visible

### Must NOT Include

- Off-white/neutral studio background
- Artificial studio lighting
- Facial fusion or identity drift
- Generic elderly face
- Beauty filter or softened elderly skin
- Cloudy/discolored eyes in aged version
- Missing accessories
- Plastic/prosthetic-looking skin
- Watermark or border
- Wrong aspect ratio

## Biological Aging Accuracy

The 90-year-old version must show:

- **Considerably more aging** than typical 70-75 year old
- **Realistic wrinkle depth** following facial anatomy
- **Natural direction and variation** in wrinkle patterns
- **Healthy dignity** preserved (not horror-like)
- **No beautification** or minimization of age

All aging must be biologically accurate while maintaining recognizability and preserving the exact eye color and core identity from the reference.
