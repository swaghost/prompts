## Design Specifications

### Layer 1: Monumental Portrait

- **Position:** Upper half extending toward upper-right
- **Style:** Refined three-quarter profile looking image-right
- **Treatment:** Monochrome grayscale
- **Fade:** Lower neck and shoulders fade softly into background

### Layer 2: Jersey Number

- **Size:** Gigantic, perfectly legible
- **Color:** White
- **Typography:** Bold condensed Swiss-style
- **Position:** Vertically through central area
- **Overlap:** Partially overlapping portrait while remaining readable
- **Critical:** Exactly one number, every digit correct

### Layer 3: Full-Body Player

- **Size:** Smaller, naturally colored
- **Position:** Lower center foreground
- **Action:** Actively controlling or dribbling ball
- **Posture:** Leaning forward, believable athletic stance
- **Visibility:** Entire body, both feet, football, all kit details
- **Depth:** Must overlap oversized number for layered effect

## Technical Requirements

### Format

- **Aspect ratio:** 3:4 (vertical/portrait)
- **Delivery:** Full-bleed native exact
- **No alterations:** No crop, resize, padding, stretching, or letterboxing

### Critical Restrictions

**No inclusion of:**

- Headline, caption, club name, player name
- Extra typography beyond jersey number
- Duplicate number or incorrect digit
- Random letters
- Border or frame
- Collage panel
- Watermark or creator signature
- Social-media interface
- Extra person or extra football
- Duplicated limbs
- Malformed hands or feet
- Cropped footwear
- Distorted face
- Incorrect kit or generic replacement uniform
- Floating body parts

### Mandatory Elements

- Exactly one grayscale portrait
- Exactly one full-body depiction
- Same player in both representations
- One correctly displayed jersey number
- Complete visibility of all body parts and equipment

## String Phone Passport Photos Specifications (Prompt 02)

### Composition

- **Master object:** Two passport-sized photo prints held side-by-side
- **Holding:** Person's thumb and index finger visible
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:4 (vertical/portrait)

### Photo Background

- Solid blue background (Indonesian marriage book passport photo standard)
- Same background color for both photos

### Subjects

**Left Photo - Male Subject:**

- Face and hair 100% matching reference image
- Talking into white paper cup
- Cheerful laughing expression
- Outfit from reference image

**Right Photo - Female Subject:**

- Face and hair 100% matching reference image
- Holding white paper cup to ear, listening
- Funny expression with wide smile and slightly stuck-out tongue
- Outfit from reference image

### Interactive Prop

- White string connecting both paper cups
- String emerges from under male's cup (left photo)
- String connects to female's cup (right photo)
- Creates "string phone" illusion between the two photos

### Identity Requirements

- 100% facial similarity to reference images
- Hair style and color exactly matching references
- Clothing from reference images
- Natural, playful expressions
