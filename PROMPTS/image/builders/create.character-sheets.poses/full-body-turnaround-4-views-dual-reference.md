# Full-Body Turnaround Sheet - 4 Views with Dual Reference

## Description

Use a full-body image as the base reference and a bust portrait as the identity reference. Generate a full-body turnaround sheet with exactly four views in this order: Front (0°), Front 3/4 Left (45°), Left Profile (90°), Back (180°). Preserve the face from the first image and the outfit, body, and hair from the second image exactly. No extra views or duplicated angles.

## Usage

Perfect for character design, 3D modeling reference, animation turnarounds, game character development, and costume design documentation. Ideal when you have separate reference images for facial identity and body/outfit details that need to be combined into one comprehensive turnaround sheet.

## Prerequisites

- Full-body reference image (for outfit, body proportions, hair, pose)
- Bust portrait reference image (for facial identity)
- Clear view of both references
- Desired consistency between the two sources
- White background preferred

**[ATTACH FULL-BODY REFERENCE IMAGE]**
**[ATTACH BUST PORTRAIT REFERENCE IMAGE]**

Use the full-body image as the base reference and the bust portrait as the identity reference.

**Dual Reference Requirements:**

- **Face:** Preserve the face from the BUST PORTRAIT exactly
  - Facial features, structure, expressions
  - Skin tone and complexion
  - Eye color, shape, and detail
  - All facial proportions and characteristics

- **Body, Outfit, Hair:** Preserve from the FULL-BODY IMAGE exactly
  - Body proportions and build
  - Complete outfit and clothing details
  - Hairstyle, color, length, styling
  - Accessories and shoes
  - Overall appearance and styling

**Four-View Turnaround Sheet:**

Generate a full-body turnaround sheet with exactly four views in this order:

**View 1: Front (0°)**

- Straight-on frontal view
- Face directly facing camera (from bust portrait reference)
- Body, outfit, and hair from full-body reference
- Head to toe visible
- Arms in natural, relaxed position
- Neutral standing pose

**View 2: Front 3/4 Left (45°)**

- Three-quarter turn to the left
- Body rotated 45 degrees from front
- Face still clearly visible at angle
- Shows front-side of outfit and body
- Head to toe visible
- Maintains same pose and stance

**View 3: Left Profile (90°)**

- Full left side profile
- Body turned 90 degrees (complete side view)
- Profile of face visible
- Shows left side of outfit completely
- Head to toe visible
- Side silhouette clear

**View 4: Back (180°)**

- Full rear view
- Back of head and body
- Shows complete back of outfit
- Head to toe visible
- Rear view of hair and clothing
- Back posture matching front

**Critical Requirements:**

- **Exactly four views only** - no extra views or duplicated angles
- **No additional angles** - only Front, Front 3/4 Left, Left Profile, Back
- **Precise order maintained** - 0°, 45°, 90°, 180°
- **Consistent pose** across all views (same stance, arm position, weight distribution)
- **Face from bust portrait** in all views where visible
- **Body/outfit/hair from full-body image** in all views

**Technical Specifications:**

- **Background:** White, seamless studio background
- **Quality:** Photorealistic, 8K resolution
- **Lighting:** Even studio lighting, soft and consistent across all views
- **Focus:** Sharp detail throughout
- **Framing:** Full body head-to-toe in each panel
- **Camera Height:** Eye level, consistent across all views
- **Style:** Professional character sheet aesthetic

**Layout:**
Arrange the four views in a single horizontal row or clean grid format suitable for a turnaround reference sheet.

Generate the complete turnaround sheet with the face from the bust portrait perfectly integrated onto the body, outfit, and hair from the full-body reference, showing all four specified angles with perfect consistency.

## Negative Prompt

Extra views, duplicated angles, more than four views, inconsistent face, face not matching bust portrait, body not matching full-body reference, different outfit, altered hair, mismatched proportions, changing pose between views, inconsistent lighting, busy background, text overlays, low quality, blurry, unrealistic rendering, different person, merged references incorrectly

- **Views:** Exactly 4 views only
- **Angles:** Front (0°), Front 3/4 Left (45°), Left Profile (90°), Back (180°)
- **Order:** Precisely as specified above
- **Background:** White, seamless

- **Consistency:** Perfect match across all views

## View Angle Specifications

**Front (0°):**

- Camera directly in front of subject
- Bilateral symmetry visible
- Full frontal presentation
- Face completely visible

**Front 3/4 Left (45°):**

- Subject rotated 45 degrees to their left (camera's right)
- Three-quarter perspective
- Shows front and left side simultaneously
- Face visible at angle, more of left side visible

**Left Profile (90°):**

- Subject rotated 90 degrees
- Pure side view from the left
- Profile silhouette
- Only left side of body and face visible

**Back (180°):**

- Subject rotated 180 degrees from front
- Complete rear view
- Back of head and body
- No face visible (except back of head)

## Reference Integration Guidelines

**Facial Transfer:**

- Extract facial features from bust portrait
- Maintain exact facial identity
- Preserve skin tone and complexion
- Keep facial proportions accurate
- Transfer facial expression (if any)
- Ensure natural integration with body

**Body/Outfit/Hair Preservation:**

- Use exact outfit from full-body reference
- Maintain body proportions and build
- Preserve hairstyle exactly
- Keep all accessories and details
- Match clothing fit and draping
- Maintain shoe and bottom details

**Seamless Integration:**

- Natural connection between face and body
- Consistent skin tone throughout
- Proper scale and proportion matching
- Realistic neck and shoulder connection
- Cohesive overall appearance
- No visible seams or mismatches

## Pose Consistency

**Maintain Across All Views:**

- Same standing stance
- Identical arm positions
- Consistent weight distribution
- Matching foot placement
- Same posture and bearing
- Natural, relaxed positioning

**Camera Consistency:**

- Same camera height (eye level)
- Same distance from subject
- Same lens focal length
- Consistent framing
- Even lighting in all views
