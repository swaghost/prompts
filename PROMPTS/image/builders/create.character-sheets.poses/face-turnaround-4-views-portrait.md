# Face Turnaround Sheet - 4 Views Portrait

## Description

Use an uploaded photo as the identity reference only. Generate a professional 4-face turnaround sheet on a seamless white background with Front, 3/4 Left, Left portrait, Rear. Keep identical facial identity, hairstyle, skin texture, and proportions in every image. Upper chest crop, 85mm portrait, soft studio lighting, photorealistic, ultra realistic, 8K.

## Usage

Perfect for character design, 3D facial modeling, animation head turnarounds, portrait studies, and character development. Ideal for facial modeling reference, hair design documentation, portrait angle studies, and character consistency guides focusing on head and upper chest only.

## Prerequisites

- Reference photo of person's face
- Clear view of facial features and hair
- Identity to be maintained across all views
- Portrait framing preference (upper chest crop)
- White background desired

## View Angle Specifications

**Front (0°):**

- Straight-on frontal view
- Face directly toward camera
- Bilateral symmetry
- Both eyes visible equally
- Full face visible
- Neutral, straight-ahead gaze

**3/4 Left (45°):**

- Three-quarter turn left
- Face rotated 45 degrees to subject's left (camera's right)
- Front and left side both visible
- More of left side showing
- Right side partially visible
- Face still mostly toward camera

**Left Portrait (90°):**

- Pure left side profile
- Face rotated 90 degrees
- Only left side of face visible
- Profile silhouette
- Eye, nose, mouth in profile line
- Left ear visible
- Right side not visible

**Rear (180°):**

- Back view of head
- Full 180-degree turn
- Back of head only
- No face visible from this angle
- Back of hair prominently shown
- Neck and upper back visible
