# Time-of-Day & Environmental Lighting Prompts

**Platform:** GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, any image generation tool  
**Use Case:** Architectural photography, real estate, exterior shots, environmental mood  
**Purpose:** Quick lighting modifiers for time-of-day and weather conditions

## What Are Time-of-Day Lighting Prompts?

These are concise lighting modifiers designed to be appended to architectural, real estate, or exterior scene prompts to control the time of day, weather conditions, and atmospheric mood.

Unlike the photographic relighting prompts (which transform portraits), these prompts set environmental lighting for exteriors, buildings, landscapes, and outdoor scenes.

## How to Use These Prompts

**Basic usage:** Append to the end of your main scene description

```
[Your main prompt: building, landscape, exterior scene...]

+ [Time-of-day lighting prompt from this collection]
```

**Example:**

```
Modern glass office building with steel frame and reflective facade, surrounded by manicured landscaping

+ Warm golden hour lighting with long cinematic shadows, soft highlights, glowing reflections, and rich natural colors.
```

## When to Use Time-of-Day Prompts

- **Architectural visualization** — Show buildings at optimal lighting conditions
- **Real estate marketing** — Make properties look inviting and premium
- **Exterior product shots** — Cars, furniture, outdoor equipment in natural light
- **Environmental storytelling** — Set mood through time and weather
- **Before/after variations** — Same scene at different times creates variety

## Combining with Other Prompts

**Works well with:**

- Architectural style descriptions (modern, Victorian, industrial, etc.)
- Material callouts (glass, steel, wood, stone, concrete)
- Landscape elements (trees, water, mountains, urban)
- Camera specifications (wide angle, aerial, eye-level, etc.)

**Example combination:**

```
Scandinavian minimalist cabin with large floor-to-ceiling windows, natural pine wood exterior, surrounded by tall pine trees and a small lake

+ Dense morning fog, soft diffused light filtering through towering pine trees, atmospheric mist, reduced visibility, subtle light rays, dreamy cinematic mood, realistic moisture in the air
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Text modifiers to append to prompts
- **Length:** 1-2 sentences per lighting type
- **Complexity:** Minimal — designed for easy copy-paste
- **Platform compatibility:** Works across GPT Image 2, Picsart Flow, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, etc.

## Usage Tips

✅ **Do:**

- Append to end of your main prompt
- Use one lighting type per generation (don't combine multiple)
- Adjust specifics if needed (e.g., change "towering pine trees" to match your scene)
- Test variations (golden hour vs. blue hour on same building)

❌ **Don't:**

- Combine conflicting times (golden hour + night doesn't work)
- Use on interior-only scenes (these are for exteriors/environmental lighting)
- Expect portrait relighting (use the photographic relighting collection for faces)
- Overthink it — these are meant to be quick modifiers

## Real Estate & Architectural Photography Context

**Why lighting matters in real estate:**

- **Golden hour** = premium listing photos, highest perceived value
- **Blue hour** = luxury properties, elegant evening appeal
- **Morning sunlight** = welcoming residential, family-friendly
- **Midday sun** = commercial transparency, bright and open

**Professional real estate photographers shoot:**

- Exteriors at golden hour (mandatory for high-end listings)
- Interiors during day with window light
- Twilight/blue hour shots for luxury properties
- Avoid midday (harsh shadows) unless commercial/modern glass building

These prompts replicate that professional timing.

## Advanced: Creating Variation Sets

For portfolio or client presentation, generate same scene with multiple lighting conditions:

1. **Morning Sunlight** — Fresh optimistic daytime
2. **Golden Hour** — Warm premium hero shot
3. **Blue Hour** — Elegant evening ambiance
4. **Night Illumination** — Inviting nighttime view

This gives clients options and shows property at different times, increasing appeal.
