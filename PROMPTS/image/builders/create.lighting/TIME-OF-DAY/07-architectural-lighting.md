# 07 — Architectural Lighting

**Time:** Any (stylized/studio-like approach)  
**Mood:** Premium, material-focused, design showcase  
**Best For:** Product photography, design portfolios, material emphasis, premium showcases

## Lighting Prompt

```
Sophisticated architectural lighting emphasizing textures, materials, geometry, and premium exterior finishes.
```

## What This Does

Simulates controlled studio-like or editorial lighting designed specifically to emphasize materials, textures, and architectural geometry. Not tied to specific time of day — this is about showcasing premium design elements.

## Key Characteristics

- **Light direction:** Carefully controlled to emphasize form
- **Shadow quality:** Sculpted to show texture and dimension
- **Color temperature:** Neutral to slightly warm (material accuracy)
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, controlled, editorial quality
- **Mood:** Premium, design-focused, material showcase

## When to Use Architectural Lighting

✅ **Use architectural lighting when:**

- Product/material showcase (stone, wood, metal, concrete)
- Design portfolio or editorial feature
- Emphasizing premium finishes and craftsmanship
- Clean modern aesthetic without weather/time constraints
- Texture and geometry are the story

❌ **Avoid architectural lighting when:**

- Natural realistic environment needed
- Lifestyle context important (this is editorial)
- Warm inviting residential feel desired
- Time-of-day storytelling matters

## Usage Example

**Base prompt:**

```
Contemporary concrete and glass pavilion with textured board-formed concrete walls, floor-to-ceiling bronze-framed glass panels, blackened steel entry door with oversized pulls, polished concrete floor
```

**With architectural lighting:**

```
Contemporary concrete and glass pavilion with textured board-formed concrete walls, floor-to-ceiling bronze-framed glass panels, blackened steel entry door with oversized pulls, polished concrete floor

Sophisticated architectural lighting emphasizing textures, materials, geometry, and premium exterior finishes.
```

**Result:** Board-formed concrete texture clearly visible with raking light showing every mark, bronze frames catching controlled highlights, steel door showing brushed texture, overall mood is premium material showcase.

## Variations to Try

- **Texture emphasis:** "Architectural lighting with strong raking light emphasizing surface textures, material grain, and tactile qualities"
- **Geometry emphasis:** "Clean architectural lighting highlighting geometric forms, clean lines, shadow play, and dimensional depth"
- **Material richness:** "Premium architectural lighting showcasing rich materials, natural finishes, craftsmanship details, and surface quality"

## Editorial & Portfolio Context

Architectural lighting differs from time-of-day:

- **Time-of-day:** Shows building in natural environment/context
- **Architectural lighting:** Shows building as designed object/sculpture

**This approach is for:**

- Architect portfolios (showcasing design)
- Material supplier catalogs (showing product)
- Design magazines (editorial features)
- Awards submissions (emphasizing design quality)
- Product photography (doors, windows, cladding, etc.)

**Lighting strategy:**

- Raking light reveals texture
- Edge lighting shows form
- Controlled reflections on metal/glass
- Shadow sculpts dimension
- Clean neutral = material color accuracy

Think of this as "studio product photography for buildings" — controlled, perfect, editorial.
