# 01 — Morning Sunlight

**Time:** Dawn to early morning (6am-9am)  
**Mood:** Fresh, peaceful, optimistic, crisp  
**Best For:** Residential properties, lifestyle scenes, welcoming atmospheres

## Lighting Prompt

```
Fresh morning sunlight with gentle rays, soft shadows, crisp air, and peaceful atmosphere.
```

## What This Does

Simulates early morning light when the sun is low but risen, creating soft directional illumination with gentle shadows. The light has a clean, crisp quality without the warmth of golden hour, suggesting freshness and new beginnings.

## Key Characteristics

- **Light direction:** Low angle, gentle and directional
- **Shadow quality:** Soft but defined, not harsh
- **Color temperature:** Neutral to slightly cool, clean and fresh
- **Atmosphere:** Clear, crisp air quality
- **Mood:** Peaceful, optimistic, inviting

## When to Use Morning Sunlight

✅ **Use morning sunlight when:**

- Residential real estate (homes feel welcoming)
- Family-oriented properties or lifestyle scenes
- Emphasizing freshness and cleanliness
- Natural outdoor settings (gardens, parks, patios)
- Optimistic and approachable brand messaging

❌ **Avoid morning sunlight when:**

- Dramatic or moody aesthetic needed (use moody lighting)
- Luxury/premium positioning (use golden hour or blue hour)
- Maximum brightness required (use midday sun)
- Nighttime scenes obviously

## Usage Example

**Base prompt:**

```
Two-story craftsman home with white painted wood siding, large front porch with rocking chairs, surrounded by mature oak trees and flowering hydrangea bushes, well-maintained green lawn
```

**With morning sunlight:**

```
Two-story craftsman home with white painted wood siding, large front porch with rocking chairs, surrounded by mature oak trees and flowering hydrangea bushes, well-maintained green lawn

Fresh morning sunlight with gentle rays, soft shadows, crisp air, and peaceful atmosphere.
```

**Result:** Home appears welcoming, fresh, and family-friendly with soft natural light emphasizing the porch and garden.

## Variations to Try

- **Earlier dawn:** "Soft dawn light with pink-orange sky, very gentle shadows, quiet peaceful morning"
- **Later morning:** "Bright mid-morning sunlight, clear blue sky, slightly stronger shadows, energetic fresh atmosphere"
- **Morning with dew:** "Fresh morning sunlight with dew on grass catching light, soft mist in low areas, crisp clean air"

## Real Estate Context

Morning sunlight is a safe choice for residential listings because:

- Not as dramatic as golden hour (more broadly appealing)
- Shows property in natural daylight (buyers trust it)
- Suggests peaceful neighborhood (quiet morning = desirable)
- Flattering for gardens and landscaping (fresh and green)

Professional real estate photographers often shoot mornings for:

- East-facing facades (morning light hits front)
- Gardens and outdoor spaces (fresh dewy look)
- Residential neighborhoods (peaceful morning vibe)
