# 03 — Golden Hour

**Time:** One hour before sunset (varies by season/location)  
**Mood:** Warm, cinematic, premium, romantic  
**Best For:** Luxury real estate, hero shots, high-end marketing, aspirational imagery

## Lighting Prompt

```
Warm golden hour lighting with long cinematic shadows, soft highlights, glowing reflections, and rich natural colors.
```

## What This Does

Simulates the "magic hour" when the sun is low on the horizon, creating warm golden light that wraps around subjects with soft directional quality. This is considered the premium lighting condition in photography and cinematography.

## Key Characteristics

- **Light direction:** Low angle, side/back lighting
- **Shadow quality:** Long, soft, dramatic
- **Color temperature:** Warm golden orange
- **Atmosphere:** Glowing, luminous, rich
- **Mood:** Premium, cinematic, romantic, aspirational

## When to Use Golden Hour

✅ **Use golden hour when:**

- Luxury real estate hero shots (highest perceived value)
- Premium product photography (cars, outdoor furniture)
- Aspirational lifestyle imagery
- Romantic or cinematic storytelling
- Emphasizing warmth and natural beauty

❌ **Avoid golden hour when:**

- Clinical/medical/institutional settings (too warm)
- Maximum clarity needed (soft light reduces definition)
- Cool modern aesthetic preferred (conflicts with warmth)
- Daytime activity scenes (implies evening)

## Usage Example

**Base prompt:**

```
Mediterranean villa with white stucco walls, terracotta tile roof, infinity pool overlooking the ocean, palm trees and bougainvillea, outdoor lounge furniture
```

**With golden hour:**

```
Mediterranean villa with white stucco walls, terracotta tile roof, infinity pool overlooking the ocean, palm trees and bougainvillea, outdoor lounge furniture

Warm golden hour lighting with long cinematic shadows, soft highlights, glowing reflections, and rich natural colors.
```

**Result:** Villa appears luxurious and aspirational with warm golden light making the white stucco glow, rich colors in vegetation, and romantic atmosphere.

## Variations to Try

- **Earlier golden hour:** "Late afternoon golden light, warm but not yet orange, gentle shadows, soft glow"
- **Peak golden hour:** "Intense golden hour glow, rich amber light, dramatic long shadows, glowing highlights"
- **Golden hour backlight:** "Golden hour backlight creating warm rim light and lens flare, subject silhouetted with glowing edges"

## Professional Photography Context

Golden hour is **mandatory** for high-end real estate because:

- Creates warmth that buyers associate with "home"
- Long shadows add depth and dimension
- Glowing quality suggests premium/luxury
- Universally flattering (works on most architectural styles)
- Highest perceived property value

**Professional timing:**

- Shoot 1 hour before sunset (exact time varies by season)
- Prep and position during hour before, shoot in 20-30 minute window
- West-facing facades get direct warm light
- East-facing facades get warm reflected fill
- Windows glow from interior lights mixing with exterior warmth

This is why luxury real estate listings almost always show golden hour exteriors.
