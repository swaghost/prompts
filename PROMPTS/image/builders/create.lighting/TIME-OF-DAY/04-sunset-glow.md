# 04 — Sunset Glow

**Time:** During sunset (sun touching/below horizon)  
**Mood:** Dramatic, warm, romantic, bold sky  
**Best For:** Lifestyle imagery, romantic scenes, dramatic backdrops, silhouettes

## Lighting Prompt

```
Warm sunset light reflecting across glass surfaces with soft orange tones and dramatic sky colors.
```

## What This Does

Simulates the moment of sunset itself when the sun is at or just below the horizon, creating intense warm orange-pink light with dramatic colorful skies. More saturated and theatrical than golden hour.

## Key Characteristics

- **Light direction:** Horizontal at horizon level
- **Shadow quality:** Minimal (sun too low for strong shadows)
- **Color temperature:** Warm orange to pink-orange
- **Atmosphere:** Dramatic colorful sky, intense glow
- **Mood:** Romantic, dramatic, bold, ending-of-day

## When to Use Sunset Glow

✅ **Use sunset glow when:**

- Dramatic sky is important to composition
- Lifestyle/aspirational imagery with people
- Romantic or emotional storytelling
- Silhouettes against colorful sky
- Reflective surfaces (glass, water) that catch sunset colors

❌ **Avoid sunset glow when:**

- Architectural detail must be clearly visible
- Professional/corporate aesthetic (too romantic)
- Maximum clarity needed (too low light)
- Cool color palette desired

## Usage Example

**Base prompt:**

```
Modern lakeside cabin with large picture windows, wooden deck extending over water, Adirondack chairs facing the lake, surrounded by pine trees
```

**With sunset glow:**

```
Modern lakeside cabin with large picture windows, wooden deck extending over water, Adirondack chairs facing the lake, surrounded by pine trees

Warm sunset light reflecting across glass surfaces with soft orange tones and dramatic sky colors.
```

**Result:** Cabin windows glow with warm orange reflections, dramatic pink-orange sky above the lake, romantic end-of-day atmosphere suggesting relaxation and retreat.

## Variations to Try

- **Intense sunset:** "Brilliant orange-pink sunset with vivid sky colors, dramatic clouds lit from below, intense warm glow"
- **Subtle sunset:** "Gentle sunset glow, soft peach and lavender sky, warm light fading, peaceful dusk atmosphere"
- **Tropical sunset:** "Vibrant tropical sunset, rich orange and magenta sky, palm trees silhouetted, warm humid atmosphere"

## Photography Context

Sunset differs from golden hour:

- **Golden hour:** Sun still above horizon, provides usable directional light
- **Sunset:** Sun at/below horizon, light too low for modeling, sky becomes the feature

**Best for:**

- Lifestyle shots with people (romantic couples, families)
- Silhouettes (figures against colorful sky)
- Reflective architecture (glass catching sunset colors)
- Emotional storytelling (endings, transitions, romance)

**Challenges:**

- Low light = less architectural detail visible
- Color can overpower (too much orange/pink)
- Brief window (10-15 minutes at actual sunset)

Use sunset when **sky and mood** matter more than architectural clarity.
