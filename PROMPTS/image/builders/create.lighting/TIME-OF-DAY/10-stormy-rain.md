# 10 — Stormy Rain

**Time:** Daytime storm or heavy rain conditions  
**Mood:** Dramatic, moody, weather intensity, architectural resilience  
**Best For:** Dramatic storytelling, showing weather resistance, atmospheric intensity, cinematic scenes

## Lighting Prompt

```
Heavy rain under dark storm clouds, dramatic overcast lighting, realistic rainfall, wet glass and stone surfaces, reflective concrete, rain puddles, soft atmospheric mist, rich reflections, cinematic architectural photography.
```

## What This Does

Simulates active rainstorm with heavy rain, dark storm clouds, wet reflective surfaces, and dramatic overcast lighting. Creates intensity and drama while showing architecture in challenging weather conditions.

## Key Characteristics

- **Light direction:** Diffused overcast from dark storm clouds
- **Shadow quality:** Minimal (heavy overcast eliminates shadows)
- **Color temperature:** Cool neutral to slightly blue-gray
- **Atmosphere:** Dramatic, heavy, intense, wet surfaces everywhere
- **Mood:** Powerful, dramatic, moody, weather intensity

## When to Use Stormy Rain

✅ **Use stormy rain when:**

- Dramatic cinematic storytelling
- Showing architectural weather resistance
- Creating intensity and drama
- Reflective surfaces are feature (glass, polished stone)
- Moody atmospheric scenes
- Suggesting resilience, strength, endurance

❌ **Avoid stormy rain when:**

- Bright inviting mood needed
- Cheerful/optimistic storytelling
- Traditional residential real estate (buyers want sunshine)
- Maximum clarity required (rain obscures)

## Usage Example

**Base prompt:**

```
Modern glass and steel office building with polished granite plaza, geometric water features, sculptural landscaping with ornamental grasses
```

**With stormy rain:**

```
Modern glass and steel office building with polished granite plaza, geometric water features, sculptural landscaping with ornamental grasses

Heavy rain under dark storm clouds, dramatic overcast lighting, realistic rainfall, wet glass and stone surfaces, reflective concrete, rain puddles, soft atmospheric mist, rich reflections, cinematic architectural photography.
```

**Result:** Glass facade reflects stormy sky and rain, polished granite plaza is slick with water showing reflections, water features blend with rain, ornamental grasses bent by wind and rain, puddles reflect building, overall mood is dramatic and powerful.

## Variations to Try

- **Light rain:** "Light steady rain, overcast diffused light, wet surfaces with subtle reflections, gentle rainfall, soft atmospheric mood"
- **Torrential rain:** "Torrential downpour, dark storm clouds, heavy rainfall reducing visibility, dramatic weather intensity, powerful atmospheric conditions"
- **Rain with breaks:** "Rain with breaks in storm clouds, dramatic light breaking through, wet reflective surfaces, atmospheric depth, cinematic weather drama"

## Cinematic Weather Context

Storm/rain creates **drama and intensity**:

- Wet surfaces = rich reflections and visual interest
- Dark clouds = dramatic moody atmosphere
- Active rain = movement and life
- Weather intensity = resilience, strength
- Reduced visibility = mystery, focus on form

**Best for:**

- Commercial/institutional architecture (strength, permanence)
- Dramatic editorial features
- Cinematic storytelling
- Modern buildings with reflective materials
- Properties where weather resistance is feature

**Storytelling messages:**

- Strength and resilience
- Drama and intensity
- Architectural quality in all conditions
- Modern sophistication (wet reflections)
- Power and boldness

**Visual benefits:**

- Wet surfaces create reflections (doubles visual interest)
- Dark sky provides dramatic backdrop
- Rain adds motion and life
- Atmospheric mist adds depth
- Moody lighting emphasizes form

**Avoid for:**

- Residential listings (buyers want sunshine)
- Properties needing bright welcoming feel
- Situations requiring maximum clarity

Use stormy rain when **drama, intensity, and atmospheric power** are desired over brightness and traditional appeal.
