# Performance Board

## Purpose

Direct facial expression, gaze, body language, gesture, and emotional intensity without losing identity or wardrobe continuity.

## Required Input

- Uploaded subject or Identity Lock Board
- `[SUBJECT NAME]`
- `[CORE OUTFIT]`
- `[SELECTED EMOTION]` for the intensity scale

## Master Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity reference.

Create a premium cinematic PERFORMANCE BOARD in a true 4:5 portrait composition. Keep [SUBJECT NAME]'s exact face, apparent age, hair, body proportions, [CORE OUTFIT], accessories, and color palette perfectly consistent. Change only expression, gaze, posture, gesture, and emotional intensity.

DESIGN SYSTEM:
Matte charcoal-black performance dossier, thin metallic-gold grid lines, crisp off-white condensed labels, cinematic portraits, subtle texture, large readable panels, full-bleed 4:5 layout, no external mockup frame.

HERO FRAME:
A powerful medium portrait labeled PERFORMANCE RANGE.

PANEL 01 - CORE EXPRESSIONS:
Neutral, subtle smile, joy, anger, fear, and sadness. Use natural facial anatomy and preserve identity at every emotional extreme.

PANEL 02 - MICRO EXPRESSIONS:
Suspicion, controlled tension, quiet confidence, restrained grief, surprise, and determination. Keep each change subtle, specific, and believable.

PANEL 03 - BODY LANGUAGE:
Open stance, guarded stance, authority, vulnerability, readiness, and exhaustion. Show full or three-quarter body while preserving the exact outfit.

PANEL 04 - GAZE DIRECTION:
Directly at camera, off-camera left, off-camera right, downward reflection, and upward resolve. Change eye and head direction without altering facial identity.

PANEL 05 - INTENSITY SCALE:
Show [SELECTED EMOTION] at 20%, 50%, 80%, and 100% intensity. Increase facial amplitude, posture, and energy gradually while preserving stable identity and anatomy.

FOOTER:
CHANGE THE PERFORMANCE - KEEP THE CHARACTER CONSISTENT.

QUALITY RULES:
Realistic facial muscles, natural eyes and teeth, coherent pose transitions, consistent lighting family, stable identity, no theatrical exaggeration unless requested.

AVOID:
Identity drift, hairstyle changes, costume changes, random props, cartoon distortion, open-mouth artifacts, duplicated expressions, extra fingers, malformed hands, labels over faces, logos, watermarks, misspellings, cropped text, borders, or letterboxing.
```

## Builder Controls

- Use observable facial and body actions rather than emotion labels alone.
- Keep one lighting family across the board.
- Scale intensity gradually; do not jump from neutral to caricature.
- Pair with an emotion-to-prompt reference when exact expression control is needed.

## Output Check

- [ ] Six core expressions are distinct.
- [ ] Six micro-expressions remain subtle.
- [ ] Six body-language states preserve wardrobe.
- [ ] Five gaze directions are readable.
- [ ] Four intensity levels progress naturally.
- [ ] Face and body remain the same person throughout.
