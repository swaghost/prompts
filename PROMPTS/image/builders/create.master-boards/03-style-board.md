# Style Board

## Purpose

Explore radically different art directions while preserving the same underlying subject identity.

## Required Input

- Uploaded subject or Identity Lock Board
- `[SUBJECT NAME]`
- Optional custom replacement for any of the six style directions

## Master Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity anchor.

Create a premium cinematic STYLE BOARD in a true 4:5 portrait composition. Preserve [SUBJECT NAME]'s facial anatomy, apparent age, skin tone, eye shape, hairstyle, body proportions, and recognizable features in every panel. Reinterpret only wardrobe styling, material language, color grading, lighting, and environmental art direction.

DESIGN SYSTEM:
Black and charcoal editorial board, thin antique-gold dividers, elegant off-white condensed typography, restrained texture, high-end fashion and film-development aesthetic, full-bleed 4:5 composition.

HERO FRAME:
Definitive neutral portrait labeled ORIGINAL IDENTITY.

Create six large and clearly distinct style directions:

01 MINIMAL LUXURY:
Tailored monochrome clothing, clean architecture, restrained materials, soft controlled light, quiet premium atmosphere.

02 CINEMATIC NOIR:
Sculpted shadow, wet streets, black tailoring, silver highlights, low-key practical light, morally tense environment.

03 FUTURE TECH:
Precision materials, subtle luminous details, advanced interior, coherent near-future design, refined technology rather than generic neon sci-fi.

04 VINTAGE EDITORIAL:
Period-inspired styling, tactile film grain, muted palette, authentic materials, era-coherent grooming and production design.

05 HIGH FASHION:
Conceptual silhouette, couture materials, deliberate studio lighting, editorial pose, elevated but physically plausible garment construction.

06 DARK FANTASY:
Crafted armor or ceremonial fabric, ancient atmospheric environment, tactile patina, mythic light, grounded material realism.

STYLE DNA STRIP:
For each direction, show labeled cues for silhouette, palette, texture, lighting, and typography.

FOOTER:
CHANGE THE STYLE - NEVER LOSE THE IDENTITY.

QUALITY RULES:
Same face in all six panels. Every direction is clearly distinct and professionally art-directed. Clothing fits naturally. Materials are tactile and plausible.

AVOID:
Different people, age changes, facial redesign, uncontrolled gender changes, random costume mixing, cheap cosplay, inconsistent anatomy, copied brand marks, logos, watermarks, extra text, misspellings, cropped labels, empty padding, borders, or letterboxing.
```

## Builder Controls

- Change style, not identity.
- Keep each style internally coherent across wardrobe, location, palette, light, and typography.
- Replace one direction at a time when iterating.
- Avoid mixing incompatible period or genre cues inside one panel.

## Output Check

- [ ] Original identity hero is present.
- [ ] Six style directions are visually distinct.
- [ ] Every panel retains the same face and age.
- [ ] Style DNA includes silhouette, palette, texture, light, and type.
- [ ] Clothing and materials remain physically believable.
