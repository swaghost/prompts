# Master Board Prompt Pack

Six production-board builders that turn one uploaded subject reference into a controlled visual-development system.

## Contents

1. [Identity Lock Board](01-identity-lock-board.md) - Establish definitive identity, anatomy, wardrobe, and material references.
2. [Performance Board](02-performance-board.md) - Explore expressions, gaze, posture, gestures, and emotional intensity.
3. [Style Board](03-style-board.md) - Test six distinct art directions while preserving the subject.
4. [Cinematography Board](04-cinematography-board.md) - Compare shot sizes, camera angles, lenses, movement frames, and lighting.
5. [World Board](05-world-board.md) - Build coherent locations, environmental states, and world-design rules.
6. [Campaign Board](06-campaign-board.md) - Translate one identity and message across coordinated content formats.

## Universal Inputs

- `[SUBJECT NAME]` - Person or fictional character represented by the uploaded image
- `[CORE OUTFIT]` - Wardrobe that remains fixed unless a board intentionally explores clothing
- `[WORLD THEME]` - Location universe, historical period, or genre language
- `[CAMPAIGN MESSAGE]` - Core phrase, promise, or product message

## Workflow

1. Upload the highest-quality reference available without cropping defining identity features.
2. Replace all bracketed variables.
3. Submit one complete prompt without splitting its consistency or negative instructions.
4. Generate the Identity Lock Board first.
5. Use its clearest front and three-quarter views as additional references for the other boards.
6. Iterate one variable at a time: outfit, lens, lighting, world, emotion, or format.

## Shared Quality Rules

- True 4:5 portrait canvas, not a board shown inside a larger mockup
- Full-bleed layout with safe margins and no outer bars or letterboxing
- Same face, age, hair, body proportions, and defining marks in every relevant panel
- Readable labels with correct spelling
- One intended variable changes at a time
- Coherent anatomy, hands, eyes, clothing construction, and accessories
- Unified palette, typography, texture, lighting family, and art direction
- No unrequested logos, watermarks, duplicate panels, or extra text
