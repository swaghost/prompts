# Campaign Board

## Purpose

Turn one reference image into a coordinated launch system for feed posts, carousel assets, Reels, Stories, product collaborations, and thumbnails.

## Required Input

- Uploaded subject or Identity Lock Board
- `[SUBJECT NAME]`
- `[CORE OUTFIT]`
- `[CAMPAIGN MESSAGE]`
- `[AUDIENCE]`, `[CAMPAIGN STYLE]`, and channel headlines
- User-supplied or fictional `[PRODUCT]` and branding

## Master Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity reference.

Create a premium CAMPAIGN BOARD in a true 4:5 portrait composition. Build one complete visual campaign around [SUBJECT NAME] and [CAMPAIGN MESSAGE]. Preserve the exact same face, hair, body proportions, [CORE OUTFIT], signature colors, and art direction across every asset. Adapt composition to each format without changing identity.

DESIGN SYSTEM:
Matte black and charcoal campaign board, thin metallic-gold grid lines, crisp off-white condensed typography, one accent color sampled from the reference image, premium cinematic advertising, sharp readable layouts, full-bleed 4:5 board.

PANEL 01 - HERO POSTER:
Dramatic key visual with clear subject hierarchy and the campaign message.

PANEL 02 - FEED CAROUSEL:
Polished 4:5 editorial asset with headline [FEED HEADLINE]. Keep the subject and campaign mark readable at feed size.

PANEL 03 - REEL COVER:
Dynamic vertical composition with headline [REEL TITLE], readable at thumbnail size.

PANEL 04 - STORY AD:
Clean 9:16-inspired vertical layout shown inside the board, with headline [STORY CTA] and safe space for platform interface overlays.

PANEL 05 - PRODUCT COLLAB:
[SUBJECT NAME] naturally presents or uses [PRODUCT]. Use only a fictional or user-supplied brand. Preserve product geometry, logo placement, and scale.

PANEL 06 - VIDEO THUMBNAIL:
Expressive close-up, strong contrast, immediate subject recognition, and headline [THUMBNAIL HOOK]. Copy remains readable at small size.

CONSISTENCY STRIP:
SAME FACE / SAME OUTFIT / SAME COLORS / NEW FORMAT

CAMPAIGN BLOCK:
CHANNELS: FEED / REELS / STORIES
AUDIENCE: [AUDIENCE]
STYLE: [CAMPAIGN STYLE]
MESSAGE: [CAMPAIGN MESSAGE]

FOOTER:
ONE CHARACTER - A COMPLETE CONTENT CAMPAIGN.

AVOID:
Identity drift, random wardrobe changes, conflicting palettes, unreadable thumbnail copy, fake real-world logos, unauthorized brand marks, product deformation, extra fingers, duplicated layouts, inconsistent messages, watermarks, misspellings, cropped text, borders, padding, or letterboxing.
```

## Builder Controls

- Lock the campaign message before generating channel variants.
- Use one hierarchy, palette, type family, and identity treatment across all assets.
- Adapt crop and text scale to the destination format rather than stretching one layout.
- Use fictional or supplied brands only.

## Output Check

- [ ] Six campaign assets are present.
- [ ] Subject identity and outfit remain consistent.
- [ ] Campaign message and art direction remain unified.
- [ ] Headlines remain readable at intended display sizes.
- [ ] Product geometry and branding remain accurate.
- [ ] Story and Reel crops preserve safe areas.
