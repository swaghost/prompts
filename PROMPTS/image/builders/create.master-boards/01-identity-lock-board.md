# Identity Lock Board

## Purpose

Build the definitive identity reference before changing pose, performance, camera, style, world, or campaign direction.

## Required Input

- One sharp reference image with unobstructed facial features
- `[SUBJECT NAME]`
- `[CORE OUTFIT]`
- Accurate eye, hair, skin, accessory, and distinctive-feature descriptions

## Master Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the primary and exact identity reference.

Create a premium cinematic IDENTITY LOCK BOARD in a true 4:5 portrait composition. Preserve [SUBJECT NAME]'s exact facial structure, apparent age, skin tone, eye shape and spacing, nose, lips, jawline, ears, hairline, hairstyle, body proportions, distinctive marks, and overall recognizability. Do not beautify, redesign, merge, or reinterpret the identity.

DESIGN SYSTEM:
Matte black and charcoal production-board background, thin metallic-gold dividers, crisp off-white condensed typography, subtle film grain, high contrast, clean safe margins, full-bleed 4:5 canvas, no outer frame.

TOP HERO AREA:
One large definitive hero portrait plus a compact identity data block:
SUBJECT: [SUBJECT NAME]
IDENTITY: LOCKED
CORE OUTFIT: [CORE OUTFIT]
HAIR: [HAIR DESCRIPTION]
EYES: [EYE DESCRIPTION]
DISTINCTIVE FEATURES: [DISTINCTIVE FEATURES]

PANEL 01 - FACE VIEWS:
Front, three-quarter left, profile left, three-quarter right, and profile right. Use identical neutral lighting, grooming, and expression.

PANEL 02 - FULL-BODY VIEWS:
Front, three-quarter, side, and back. Preserve height, build, posture baseline, clothing construction, accessories, and footwear.

PANEL 03 - FACIAL DETAILS:
Sharp close crops of eyes, nose, mouth, hairline, natural skin texture, and distinctive marks.

PANEL 04 - CONSISTENCY CHECK:
Four new portraits under soft daylight, warm interior light, cool night light, and hard rim light. Only lighting may change. Identity, hairstyle, wardrobe, and anatomy remain identical.

PANEL 05 - COLOR AND MATERIAL:
Accurate labeled swatches for skin, hair, eyes, outfit colors, accessories, and principal material textures.

FOOTER:
LOCK THE IDENTITY - CHANGE EVERYTHING ELSE.

QUALITY RULES:
One person only. Same identity in every panel. Realistic anatomy. Sharp facial detail. Natural skin texture. Readable labels. Production-ready layout.

AVOID:
Different faces, age shifts, altered ethnicity, hairstyle changes, beauty-filter skin, facial redesign, asymmetrical eyes, distorted hands, wardrobe drift, duplicated panels, logos, watermarks, extra text, misspellings, cropped labels, padding, borders, or letterboxing.
```

## Builder Controls

- Keep expression neutral in the identity views.
- Do not combine this board with style exploration.
- Use fixed wardrobe and grooming as structural identity anchors.
- Regenerate this board first whenever another board shows identity drift.

## Output Check

- [ ] Five face views are present.
- [ ] Four full-body views are present.
- [ ] Facial-detail crops are sharp and anatomically consistent.
- [ ] Four lighting checks change only illumination.
- [ ] Color and material swatches match the reference.
- [ ] The board fills a true 4:5 canvas.
