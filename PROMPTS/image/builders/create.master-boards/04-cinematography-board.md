# Cinematography Board

## Purpose

Test shot size, camera height, angle, focal length, movement framing, depth of field, and lighting while identity and wardrobe remain locked.

## Required Input

- Uploaded subject or Identity Lock Board
- `[SUBJECT NAME]`
- `[CORE OUTFIT]`
- `[CAMERA MOOD]`

## Master Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity and wardrobe reference.

Create a premium cinematic CINEMATOGRAPHY BOARD in a true 4:5 portrait composition. Preserve [SUBJECT NAME]'s exact face, hair, body proportions, [CORE OUTFIT], accessories, colors, and distinctive details across every frame. Change only camera placement, focal length, shot size, framing, depth of field, movement implication, and lighting.

DESIGN SYSTEM:
Elite film-production dossier, matte black and charcoal texture, thin metallic-gold grid, crisp off-white condensed typography, restrained accent color sampled from the uploaded image, generous safe margins, full-bleed 4:5 canvas.

PANEL 01 - SHOT SIZES:
Establishing shot, full shot, medium shot, and close-up. Maintain consistent scene geography and subject identity.

PANEL 02 - CAMERA ANGLES:
Low angle, eye level, high angle, and overhead. Make camera height visually obvious without changing the underlying subject identity.

PANEL 03 - LENS LANGUAGE:
18mm wide, 35mm natural, 50mm cinematic, and 85mm portrait. Show authentic perspective, facial rendering, field of view, and background-compression differences. Prevent unrequested facial deformation.

PANEL 04 - CAMERA MOVEMENT FRAMES:
Locked-off frame, handheld-energy frame, slow push-in frame, and orbit frame. Render each as a convincing still from the same scene with directional motion implied through composition rather than blur.

PANEL 05 - LIGHTING SETUPS:
Soft daylight, warm interior, cool night, hard rim, and silhouette. Change illumination only; preserve identity, outfit, scene geography, and camera logic.

TECHNICAL BLOCK:
CAMERA: FULL-FRAME CINEMA
IDENTITY: LOCKED
LENSES: 18MM / 35MM / 50MM / 85MM
MOOD: [CAMERA MOOD]

FOOTER:
CHANGE THE CAMERA - KEEP THE CHARACTER CONSISTENT.

AVOID:
Altered face, inaccurate lens rendering, lens labels that do not match the image, costume drift, impossible perspective, inconsistent geography, excessive subject blur, random poses, logos, watermarks, misspellings, cropped text, tiny panels, borders, or letterboxing.
```

## Builder Controls

- Keep one scene and subject mark across every panel.
- Make lens differences optical, not cosmetic.
- Do not imply camera movement solely with destructive blur.
- Use this board to choose camera grammar before creating a shot board.

## Output Check

- [ ] Four shot sizes are clearly different.
- [ ] Four camera heights/angles are credible.
- [ ] Four lenses show distinct perspective and compression.
- [ ] Four movement frames share one scene.
- [ ] Five lighting setups preserve identity and wardrobe.
- [ ] Technical labels match their images.
