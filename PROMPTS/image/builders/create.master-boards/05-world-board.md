# World Board

## Purpose

Build a coherent narrative universe around one uploaded subject instead of generating unrelated locations and environmental styles.

## Required Input

- Uploaded subject or Identity Lock Board
- `[SUBJECT NAME]`
- `[CORE OUTFIT]`
- `[WORLD THEME]`
- `[GENRE]`, `[ERA]`, `[ATMOSPHERE]`, and `[ARCHITECTURAL LANGUAGE]`

## Master Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity reference.

Create a premium cinematic WORLD BOARD in a true 4:5 portrait composition. Place [SUBJECT NAME] inside one coherent [WORLD THEME] universe. Preserve the same face, apparent age, hair, body proportions, [CORE OUTFIT], accessories, and identity in every scene. The world may change state; the person must not.

DESIGN SYSTEM:
Black and charcoal world-building dossier, metallic-gold dividers, off-white condensed typography, cinematic environment art, unified color grade, large atmospheric imagery, full-bleed 4:5 layout.

PANEL 01 - HERO ENVIRONMENT:
One wide, high-impact image establishing the subject, architecture, terrain, scale, weather, and central visual motif.

PANEL 02 - CORE LOCATIONS:
[HOME BASE], [SOCIAL HUB], [HOSTILE ZONE], and [ANCIENT OR FUTURE LANDMARK]. Every location belongs to the same culture, era, geography, material system, and design language.

PANEL 03 - WORLD STATES:
Sunrise, rainstorm, moonlit night, and extreme season. Keep geography, architecture, and landmark placement consistent while changing time and weather.

PANEL 04 - STORY ZONES:
Safe haven, discovery zone, danger zone, and final threshold. Show the same subject at believable scale and maintain directional geography.

PANEL 05 - COLOR AND MATERIAL LANGUAGE:
Six labeled palette swatches plus four close-ups of [MATERIAL 1], [MATERIAL 2], [MATERIAL 3], and [ATMOSPHERIC ELEMENT].

WORLD RULES BLOCK:
GENRE: [GENRE]
ERA: [ERA]
ATMOSPHERE: [ATMOSPHERE]
ARCHITECTURE: [ARCHITECTURAL LANGUAGE]
IDENTITY: LOCKED

FOOTER:
BUILD THE WORLD - KEEP THE VISUAL LANGUAGE CONSISTENT.

AVOID:
Unrelated locations, mixed eras without intent, modern intrusions, random color grades, identity drift, outfit changes, changing geography, scale errors, conflicting architecture, logos, watermarks, extra text, misspellings, cropped labels, outer frames, or letterboxing.
```

## Builder Controls

- Define geography and cultural logic before adding spectacle.
- Reuse materials, motifs, architecture, weather logic, and color relationships.
- Keep location state changes separate from location redesign.
- Generate a separate Location Board when exact spatial continuity is required.

## Output Check

- [ ] One hero environment establishes the world.
- [ ] Four locations share one design language.
- [ ] Four world states preserve geography.
- [ ] Four story zones remain spatially and culturally coherent.
- [ ] Material close-ups match the wide locations.
- [ ] Subject identity and outfit remain stable.
