## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL:**
Use the uploaded image as a reference. Keep the model and her clothing consistent. Change only the emotion.

**Subject:**
The exact same model from the reference image maintaining identical appearance: same face, same hair style and color, same clothing, same body proportions, same pose, same everything EXCEPT facial expression.

**Facial Expression - Fear:**
Eyes opened wide with visible tension showing whites of eyes more prominently. Eyebrows raised high creating pronounced forehead wrinkles (horizontal lines across forehead). Lips parted with strain. Teeth slightly clenched. Jaw tight with visible muscle engagement. Facial muscles visibly engaged throughout showing alertness and tension. Overall expression conveying alarm and sudden fear. Not screaming terror - a controlled fearful reaction showing shock and fright.

**Setting/Environment:**
Match the exact background and environment from the reference image. Maintain all environmental elements, lighting conditions, and atmospheric qualities exactly as they appear in the reference.

**Lighting:**
Maintain the exact same lighting setup as the reference image. Same light direction, intensity, color temperature, and quality. Lighting naturally revealing the tension and alarm in the expression.

**Composition:**
Identical composition to reference image. Same framing, same camera angle, same subject positioning, same background arrangement. Only facial expression changes.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Match the exact photographic style of the reference image. Maintain same color grading, same contrast levels, same overall aesthetic approach. Professional portrait quality with authentic emotion capture.

**Technical Specifications:**
Match technical quality of reference image. Maintain same resolution, sharpness, color accuracy, and professional finishing. Sharp focus on face, especially wide eyes and raised brows. Natural skin texture preservation including tension wrinkles.

**Color Palette:**
Maintain exact color palette from reference image. Same clothing colors, same background colors, same skin tones, same overall color harmony. Natural appearance without artificial pallor.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Sudden fear, alarm, shock, fright, startled response, defensive alertness, anxious tension, surprised fear, nervous apprehension.
