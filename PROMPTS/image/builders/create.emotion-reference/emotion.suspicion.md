## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL:**
Use the uploaded image as the only reference. Keep this model and her clothing consistent. Change only emotions.

**Subject:**
The exact same model from the reference image maintaining identical appearance: same face, same hair style and color, same clothing, same body proportions, same pose, same everything EXCEPT facial expression.

**Facial Expression - Suspicion:**
She displays a suspicious expression. One eyebrow slightly raised higher than the other creating asymmetric brow position. Brows subtly drawn together creating slight tension. A narrowed questioning gaze directed at the camera showing doubt and evaluation. Lips gently pressed together with slight tension. A faint vertical crease forms between the brows from subtle frowning. Subtle tightening in the cheeks and jawline showing concentration. Her head is slightly tilted forward adding to the investigative quality. Overall expression conveying skepticism and doubt - she's not sure she believes what she's seeing or hearing.

Match the exact background and environment from the reference image. Maintain all environmental elements, lighting conditions, and atmospheric qualities exactly as they appear in the reference.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
