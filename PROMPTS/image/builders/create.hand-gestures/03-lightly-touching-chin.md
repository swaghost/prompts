# Lightly Touching the Chin

## Description

An image-to-image pose adjustment that creates a thoughtful, lightly playful chin-touch gesture without obscuring or reshaping the face. It coordinates two or three fingertips, thumb position, wrist angle, head tilt, and minimal contact pressure while preserving identity and scene fidelity.

## Best For

Thoughtful, curious, introspective, analytical, editorial, and lightly playful portraits.

## Actor or Subject

One person whose face, jawline, and at least one hand and wrist are clearly visible. Close, medium, and three-quarter portraits work best when there is enough space beside the chin for a relaxed hand.

## Prerequisites

- One uploaded identity and wardrobe reference image
- Clear jawline, mouth, and lower-face detail
- One visible hand with enough framing to retain its wrist
- An unobstructed path between the hand and chin

## Preservation Contract

- Preserve the exact face, facial proportions, expression family, apparent age, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, wardrobe, accessories, background, lighting, color treatment, camera angle, and aspect ratio.
- Change only the active hand and minimal head, wrist, elbow, and passive-arm positioning required by the gesture.
- Do not reshape the jaw, cover defining features, redesign the scene, or change the photographic style.

## Anatomy Requirements

- One anatomically correct active hand with five distinct fingers
- Two or three fingertips make light contact near the lower jaw
- Thumb rests naturally beneath or beside the chin without pinching
- Wrist remains softly aligned with the forearm
- Contact creates no facial deformation or unnatural skin indentation

## Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity and wardrobe reference. Preserve the same face, apparent age, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, outfit, accessories, lighting, setting, and color treatment.

Create a thoughtful natural pose with one hand lightly touching the chin. Rest two or three fingertips gently near the lower jawline without covering the lips, nose, or defining facial features. Keep the thumb relaxed beneath or beside the chin rather than pinching it. Use a soft wrist angle, naturally curved fingers, a slight head tilt, and minimal contact pressure. The other arm rests comfortably across the torso or by the side.

Photorealistic portrait, believable body language, subtle curious expression, realistic hand anatomy, natural jaw contact, visible fingertips and nail beds, comfortable posture.

Avoid hand covering the mouth, fingers fused to the face, distorted jaw, extra fingers, missing fingers, claw grip, hard pinching, broken wrist, floating fingertips, changed face shape, identity drift, or wardrobe changes.
```

## Gesture Lock

- Fingertips rest lightly near the jawline
- Lips and nose remain unobstructed
- Thumb does not distort the chin
- Contact pressure stays minimal
- Head tilt supports the thoughtful gesture

## Camera and Framing Guidance

- Prefer close, medium, or three-quarter framing with the active wrist visible.
- Keep lips, nose, eyes, and defining facial contours unobstructed.
- Preserve source camera position, focal-length character, crop, depth of field, and perspective.

## Technical Notes

- **Method:** Reference-guided image editing or image-to-image generation
- **Output:** Match source dimensions and aspect ratio
- **Priority:** Facial identity first, hand-to-jaw anatomy second, emotional nuance third
- **Realism:** Visible fingertips and nail beds, subtle contact shadow, natural skin texture, and no jaw deformation

## How to Use

- Apply this as the only new gesture and specify the active hand when direction matters.
- Use close, medium, or three-quarter framing that retains the active wrist and elbow.
- Keep contact to two or three relaxed fingertips near the lower jaw, with minimal pressure.
- Place the hand near but never over the lips, nose, eyes, or other defining features.
- Compare the result against the source for identity, face shape, wardrobe, background, lighting, and camera continuity.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The same recognizable person, face shape, outfit, setting, and lighting are preserved.
- [ ] Two or three fingertips touch the lower jaw lightly and plausibly.
- [ ] The thumb, wrist, elbow, and remaining fingers form one coherent hand.
- [ ] The chin and jaw show no pinching, reshaping, or excessive indentation.
- [ ] Lips, nose, eyes, and defining features remain visible.
- [ ] No extra, missing, fused, floating, or face-clipping fingers appear.

## Negative Prompt

```text
hand covering mouth, hand covering nose, fingers fused to face, distorted jaw, pinched chin, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, duplicated hand, claw grip, hard pressure, broken wrist, reversed hand, floating fingertips, cropped wrist, changed face shape, identity drift, wardrobe drift, background change, lighting change, plastic skin
```
