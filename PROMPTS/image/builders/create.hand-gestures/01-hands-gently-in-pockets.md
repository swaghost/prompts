# Hands Gently in Pockets

## Description

An image-to-image pose adjustment that places both hands naturally in the subject's front pockets while preserving identity, wardrobe, setting, lighting, and overall photographic treatment. The result should feel candid and relaxed, with believable thumb visibility, pocket tension, elbow position, and weight distribution.

## Best For

Casual fashion, street style, everyday influencer portraits, travel images, and relaxed lifestyle photography.

## Actor or Subject

One person wearing trousers, jeans, shorts, or another garment with two usable front pockets. Medium, three-quarter, or full-body source images work best because both wrists, elbows, hips, and pocket openings remain visible.

## Prerequisites

- One uploaded identity and wardrobe reference image
- Both front pockets visible or physically inferable
- Enough frame space to retain the wrists, elbows, and natural hip line
- Clothing with sufficient structure to show realistic pocket folds and tension

## Preservation Contract

- Preserve the exact face, apparent age, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, wardrobe, accessories, background, lighting family, color treatment, camera angle, and aspect ratio.
- Change only the hand placement and the minimal shoulder, elbow, hip, and weight-shift adjustments needed to support it.
- Do not redesign the outfit, pockets, pose concept, scene, or photographic style.

## Anatomy Requirements

- Two anatomically correct hands with five fingers per hand
- Natural thumb opposition and finger-length variation
- Plausible wrist-to-forearm alignment
- Fingers hidden only where naturally occluded by pocket fabric
- Realistic pocket opening, fabric compression, folds, and grip pressure

## Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity and wardrobe reference. Preserve the same face, age, hairstyle, body proportions, outfit, accessories, lighting, setting, and color treatment.

Create a natural relaxed pose with both hands resting casually inside the front pockets. Keep both thumbs slightly visible outside the pocket openings. The remaining fingers sit naturally inside the pockets without creating unexplained lumps or clipping through the fabric. Relax the shoulders, soften the elbows, and shift the body weight subtly onto one leg. Keep the wrists aligned with the forearms and let the clothing react naturally around the pocket openings with believable fabric tension and folds.

Photorealistic candid lifestyle photography, effortless body language, realistic anatomy, natural proportions, visible garment construction, relaxed expression, medium or full-body framing.

Avoid extra fingers, visible fingers passing through pocket fabric, missing thumbs, fused hands, duplicated limbs, stiff shoulders, symmetrical mannequin posture, distorted pockets, stretched clothing, identity drift, or wardrobe changes.
```

## Gesture Lock

- Both hands inside front pockets
- Both thumbs slightly visible
- Elbows relaxed and angled naturally
- Weight shifted subtly to one leg
- Pocket fabric reacts to the inserted hands

## Camera and Framing Guidance

- Prefer medium, three-quarter, or full-body framing.
- Keep both pocket openings and elbows readable.
- Preserve the source camera position, lens character, crop, depth of field, and perspective.
- Avoid crops that remove the wrists or make the gesture ambiguous.

## Technical Notes

- **Method:** Reference-guided image editing or image-to-image generation
- **Output:** Match the source image dimensions and aspect ratio
- **Priority:** Identity and wardrobe fidelity first, hand anatomy second, natural posture third
- **Realism:** Photographic skin, fabric, seams, pocket construction, and contact shadows

## How to Use

- Apply this as the only new gesture in the edit.
- Use medium, three-quarter, or full-body framing so both wrists, elbows, hips, and pocket openings remain readable.
- Specify front pockets and retain the visible-thumb requirement; do not substitute back pockets or fully hidden hands.
- Keep the fingers relaxed inside the pockets and support the gesture with only a subtle weight shift.
- Compare the result against the source for identity, wardrobe, pocket construction, background, lighting, and camera continuity.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The same recognizable person, outfit, accessories, setting, and lighting are preserved.
- [ ] Both hands belong to the subject and enter the two front pockets naturally.
- [ ] Two thumbs remain visible and anatomically connected to the hidden hands.
- [ ] Pocket openings, fabric folds, and pressure respond realistically.
- [ ] Wrists, elbows, hips, and weight distribution form a relaxed pose.
- [ ] No extra, missing, fused, floating, or fabric-clipping fingers appear.

## Negative Prompt

```text
extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, duplicated hands, extra hands, malformed palms, broken wrists, reversed hands, impossible thumb placement, rubber fingers, identical finger lengths, stiff claw pose, floating hand, visible fingers passing through pocket fabric, unexplained pocket bulges, hand clipping through body or clothing, distorted pockets, stretched clothing, cropped wrists, stiff shoulders, symmetrical mannequin posture, identity drift, wardrobe drift, background change, lighting change
```
