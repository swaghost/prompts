## Shared Identity And Anatomy Lock

Use the uploaded image as the exact identity and wardrobe reference. Preserve the same face, apparent age, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, clothing, accessories, background, lighting family, and color treatment. Change only the requested hand gesture and the small posture adjustments required to make it natural.

Hands must have two anatomically correct palms, five distinct fingers per visible hand, natural finger-length variation, plausible thumb opposition, realistic knuckle folds, wrist alignment, nail beds, cuticles, and believable grip pressure. Keep fingers separated where physically expected. Hide only fingers that are naturally occluded by pockets, hair, sleeves, arms, or perspective.

## General Negative Prompt

`extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, duplicated hands, extra hands, malformed palms, broken wrists, reversed hands, impossible thumb placement, rubber fingers, identical finger lengths, stiff claw pose, floating hand, hand clipping through body or clothing, fingers merging into hair, excessive grip pressure, plastic skin, beauty-filter hands, cropped wrists, identity drift, wardrobe drift`

## Usage

- Start with one gesture only.
- Describe which hand performs the action when direction matters.
- Keep fingers relaxed rather than perfectly spread or rigid.
- Add a subtle weight shift or shoulder adjustment to support the gesture.
- Use medium, three-quarter, or full-body framing so wrists and elbows remain visible.
- For close portraits, keep the gesturing hand near but not covering defining facial features.
