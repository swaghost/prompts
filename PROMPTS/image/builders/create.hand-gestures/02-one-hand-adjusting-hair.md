# One Hand Adjusting Hair

## Description

An image-to-image pose adjustment that creates a believable candid moment of one hand moving a few loose strands away from the face. It preserves the source identity and hairstyle while coordinating fingertip contact, hair separation, wrist angle, head turn, and shoulder response.

## Best For

Candid portraits, beauty content, street style, fashion editorials, and natural transitional moments.

## Actor or Subject

One person with visible hair near the face and at least one unobstructed hand, wrist, and elbow. Medium or close portrait references work best when the hairline, outer hair layer, and defining facial features are clear.

## Prerequisites

- One uploaded identity, hairstyle, and wardrobe reference image
- Clear visibility of the face, hairline, and outer hair strands
- Enough frame space for one bent arm and hand near the head
- A source pose that permits subtle head and shoulder movement

## Preservation Contract

- Preserve the exact face, apparent age, skin tone, hairstyle, hair length, body proportions, wardrobe, accessories, background, lighting family, color treatment, camera angle, and aspect ratio.
- Change only one hand, its supporting arm, and the smallest natural head, hair, and shoulder response.
- Do not restyle the hair, obscure identity, redesign the scene, or alter the photographic treatment.

## Anatomy Requirements

- One anatomically correct visible hand with five distinct fingers
- Natural finger spacing, nail beds, knuckles, thumb placement, and wrist alignment
- Fingertips contact only the outer hair layer
- Hair separates around fingers without merging, clipping, or flattening
- Contact pressure remains light and does not deform the scalp or face

## Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity and wardrobe reference. Preserve the same face, age, hairstyle, hair length, body proportions, outfit, accessories, lighting, setting, and color treatment.

Create a natural candid pose in which one hand gently moves only a few loose strands of hair away from the face. Keep the wrist softly bent, fingers relaxed with realistic spacing, and fingertips lightly contacting the outer hair layer without digging into the scalp or covering the eyes. The other arm rests naturally by the side. Add a subtle head turn toward the adjusting hand and a small shoulder response so the movement feels spontaneous rather than posed.

Photorealistic candid photography, authentic transitional moment, natural hair movement, realistic hand anatomy, believable proportions, separated hair strands around the fingertips, understated expression.

Avoid fingers merging into hair, hand passing through the head, extra fingers, missing fingers, stiff spread fingers, flattened hair, changed hairstyle, hand covering the face, broken wrist, floating hand, identity drift, or wardrobe changes.
```

## Gesture Lock

- One hand touches only the outer strands
- Fingers remain visible and naturally spaced
- Eyes and defining facial features remain unobstructed
- Other hand stays relaxed by the side
- Hair displacement remains subtle

## Camera and Framing Guidance

- Prefer close, medium, or three-quarter portrait framing that retains the active wrist and elbow.
- Keep the eyes, nose, mouth, jawline, and hairline unobstructed.
- Preserve the source camera position, crop, focal-length character, depth of field, and perspective.

## Technical Notes

- **Method:** Reference-guided image editing or image-to-image generation
- **Output:** Match the source dimensions and aspect ratio
- **Priority:** Identity and hairstyle fidelity first, hand anatomy second, candid motion third
- **Realism:** Individual hair strands, natural fingertip occlusion, skin contact, and source-matched shadows

## How to Use

- Apply this as the only new gesture in the edit and specify left or right hand when continuity matters.
- Use close, medium, or three-quarter framing that keeps the active wrist and elbow visible.
- Limit contact to a few outer strands rather than asking for a full hairstyle change.
- Keep the hand below or beside the eye line so defining facial features remain visible.
- Compare the result against the source for identity, hairstyle, wardrobe, background, lighting, and camera continuity.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The same recognizable person, hairstyle, outfit, setting, and lighting are preserved.
- [ ] Exactly one hand adjusts only a few outer hair strands.
- [ ] Five plausible fingers, thumb placement, wrist, and elbow remain readable.
- [ ] Hair separates naturally around the fingertips without merging or clipping.
- [ ] Eyes and defining facial features remain unobstructed.
- [ ] The passive arm rests naturally and no extra or duplicated hand appears.

## Negative Prompt

```text
extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, duplicated hands, malformed palm, broken wrist, reversed hand, impossible thumb placement, stiff spread fingers, fingers merging into hair, hand passing through head, fingertips embedded in scalp, flattened hair, changed hairstyle, changed hair length, hand covering eyes or defining facial features, floating hand, cropped wrist, identity drift, wardrobe drift, background change, lighting change
```
