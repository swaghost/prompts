# One Hand on the Waist

## Description

An image-to-image pose adjustment that places one hand lightly on the waist or upper hip for understated confidence. It preserves identity and styling while coordinating palm contact, finger direction, elbow angle, torso asymmetry, garment compression, and balanced weight distribution.

## Best For

Confident lifestyle portraits, fashion, fitness, brand imagery, creator profiles, and product photography.

## Actor or Subject

One person shown from at least the waist upward, with one hand, wrist, elbow, and the side-waist contour visible. Three-quarter and full-body source images provide the clearest posture context.

## Prerequisites

- One uploaded identity and wardrobe reference image
- Clear waist or upper-hip contour
- Enough frame space for one bent elbow and the passive arm
- Clothing detail sufficient to reproduce natural contact folds

## Preservation Contract

- Preserve the exact face, apparent age, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, wardrobe, accessories, background, lighting, color treatment, camera angle, and aspect ratio.
- Change only one hand and arm plus minimal torso, shoulder, hip, and weight-shift adjustments.
- Do not exaggerate body curves, redesign clothing, or turn the pose into a rigid pageant stance.

## Anatomy Requirements

- One anatomically correct active hand with five distinct fingers
- Palm follows the side-waist or upper-hip surface
- Fingers curve naturally along the body contour rather than splaying
- Wrist and elbow form a comfortable continuous line
- Garment or skin contact creates subtle, localized compression only

## Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity and wardrobe reference. Preserve the same face, age, hairstyle, body proportions, outfit, accessories, lighting, setting, and color treatment.

Create a confident but natural pose with one hand resting lightly on the waist. Keep the palm in gentle contact with the side waist or upper hip, fingers relaxed and following the body contour rather than splayed rigidly. Use a soft elbow angle and slight asymmetry through the torso. Let the other arm hang naturally by the side. Maintain balanced posture with a subtle body curve and realistic garment compression beneath the resting hand.

Photorealistic lifestyle photography, effortless confidence, realistic anatomy, natural finger spacing, believable wrist and elbow alignment, relaxed expression, three-quarter or full-body framing.

Avoid stiff pageant pose, claw fingers, extreme elbow flare, hand clipping through the waist, distorted hip, extra fingers, missing fingers, duplicated arms, unnatural spinal curve, identity drift, or wardrobe changes.
```

## Gesture Lock

- Palm rests lightly on waist or upper hip
- Fingers follow the body contour
- Elbow bends softly rather than sharply
- Other arm hangs naturally
- Torso curve remains subtle and anatomically plausible

## Camera and Framing Guidance

- Prefer medium, three-quarter, or full-body framing.
- Keep the active hand, wrist, elbow, waist contact, and passive arm visible.
- Preserve source camera position, focal-length character, crop, depth of field, and perspective.

## Technical Notes

- **Method:** Reference-guided image editing or image-to-image generation
- **Output:** Match source dimensions and aspect ratio
- **Priority:** Identity and body proportions first, hand anatomy second, confident asymmetry third
- **Realism:** Natural finger spacing, soft elbow, realistic garment pressure, and source-matched contact shadows

## How to Use

- Apply this as the only new gesture and specify left or right hand when pose direction matters.
- Use medium, three-quarter, or full-body framing so the hand, wrist, elbow, waist, and passive arm remain visible.
- Keep fingers relaxed along the body contour and avoid a rigid pageant-style elbow flare.
- Support the gesture with only a subtle torso asymmetry or weight shift; preserve the subject's real proportions.
- Compare the result against the source for identity, body shape, wardrobe, background, lighting, and camera continuity.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The same recognizable person, body proportions, outfit, setting, and lighting are preserved.
- [ ] Exactly one palm rests lightly on the side waist or upper hip.
- [ ] Five fingers follow the body contour with a plausible thumb and wrist.
- [ ] The elbow bend and torso asymmetry look relaxed rather than rigid.
- [ ] Garment pressure and contact shadow remain subtle and localized.
- [ ] No extra, missing, fused, floating, or body-clipping fingers appear.

## Negative Prompt

```text
stiff pageant pose, claw fingers, rigid splayed fingers, extreme elbow flare, hand clipping through waist, distorted hip, exaggerated body curve, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, duplicated hands, duplicated arms, broken wrist, reversed hand, floating contact, excessive fabric compression, cropped wrist, identity drift, wardrobe drift, background change, lighting change
```
