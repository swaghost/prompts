# Casual Conversational Hand Gesture

## Description

An image-to-image pose adjustment that raises one hand near the torso in a natural explanatory gesture. It preserves identity and scene fidelity while coordinating a partially open palm, relaxed finger spacing, neutral wrist, comfortable elbow bend, subtle torso movement, and engaged expression.

## Best For

Talking-to-camera images, educational posts, explainers, social reels, presenter portraits, and conversational creator content.

## Actor or Subject

One person facing or addressing the camera with at least one arm and hand available for gesturing. Medium and three-quarter portraits are preferred because the active hand, wrist, elbow, torso, and face remain visible together.

## Prerequisites

- One uploaded identity and wardrobe reference image
- Clear face and upper-torso visibility
- Enough negative space near the torso for one raised hand
- One unobstructed wrist and elbow

## Preservation Contract

- Preserve the exact face, apparent age, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, wardrobe, accessories, background, lighting, color treatment, camera angle, and aspect ratio.
- Change only one hand and arm plus minimal expression, torso, and passive-arm adjustments needed to support conversational delivery.
- Do not introduce pointing, a stop-sign palm, a new prop, or a different presentation style unless explicitly requested.

## Anatomy Requirements

- One anatomically correct active hand with five distinct fingers
- Palm remains partially open and angled inward or upward, not flat to camera
- Fingers form a soft natural curve with plausible spacing
- Thumb separates naturally and wrist remains neutral
- Hand scale, elbow bend, and forearm length remain proportional to the body

## Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity and wardrobe reference. Preserve the same face, age, hairstyle, body proportions, outfit, accessories, lighting, setting, and color treatment.

Create a natural conversational pose with one hand raised gently near the torso as if casually explaining one point. Keep the palm partially open and angled slightly upward or inward, fingers relaxed with natural spacing and a soft curve, thumb separated plausibly, wrist neutral, and elbow comfortably bent. The other hand rests by the side or near the waist. Add subtle torso movement and an engaged but unforced expression.

Photorealistic talking-to-camera portrait, authentic expressive body language, realistic hand anatomy, believable proportions, conversational energy, medium or three-quarter framing.

Avoid rigid stop-sign palm, pointing unless requested, perfectly spread fingers, claw pose, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, broken wrist, floating arm, oversized hand, gesture covering the face, identity drift, or wardrobe changes.
```

## Gesture Lock

- One hand raised near the torso
- Palm partially open, not fully frontal
- Fingers remain softly curved and separated
- Elbow stays bent and close to a conversational range
- Other hand remains passive

## Camera and Framing Guidance

- Prefer medium or three-quarter framing.
- Keep the active hand, wrist, elbow, torso, and complete face visible.
- Place the hand below the chin and away from defining facial features.
- Preserve source camera position, crop, focal-length character, depth of field, and perspective.

## Technical Notes

- **Method:** Reference-guided image editing or image-to-image generation
- **Output:** Match source dimensions and aspect ratio
- **Priority:** Identity and expression fidelity first, hand anatomy second, conversational energy third
- **Realism:** Correct hand scale, natural finger separation, subtle gesture motion, source-matched shadows, and relaxed muscle tension

## How to Use

- Apply this as the only new gesture and specify the active hand when direction matters.
- Use medium or three-quarter framing so the hand, wrist, elbow, torso, and complete face remain visible.
- Keep the palm partially open and angled inward or upward; do not flatten it toward the camera like a stop sign.
- Place the hand below the chin and away from defining facial features, with a subtle supporting torso shift.
- Compare the result against the source for identity, expression family, wardrobe, background, lighting, and camera continuity.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The same recognizable person, outfit, setting, lighting, and expression family are preserved.
- [ ] Exactly one hand gestures naturally near the torso while the other remains passive.
- [ ] Five fingers, thumb, palm, wrist, forearm, and elbow form plausible anatomy and scale.
- [ ] The palm angle and finger curve read as conversational rather than pointing or stopping.
- [ ] The gesture stays below the chin and does not cover the face.
- [ ] No extra, missing, fused, floating, oversized, or cropped hand anatomy appears.

## Negative Prompt

```text
rigid stop-sign palm, pointing finger unless requested, perfectly spread fingers, claw pose, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, duplicated hand, broken wrist, reversed hand, floating arm, oversized hand, undersized hand, gesture covering face, hand above eyes, stiff elbow, cropped wrist, identity drift, wardrobe drift, background change, lighting change, unrelated prop
```
