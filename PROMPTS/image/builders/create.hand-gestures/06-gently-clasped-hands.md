# Gently Clasped Hands

## Description

An image-to-image pose adjustment that brings both hands together in a loose, conversational clasp at the lower torso. It preserves identity and scene fidelity while maintaining correct hand ownership, visible thumbs, relaxed wrist alignment, natural finger overlap, and soft shoulders.

## Best For

Friendly, modest, conversational, professional, welcoming, and calm standing portraits.

## Actor or Subject

One standing person with both arms and hands visible or physically inferable. Medium, three-quarter, and full-body references work best because they retain the lower torso, elbows, and complete clasp.

## Prerequisites

- One uploaded identity and wardrobe reference image
- Both hands available near the lower torso
- Enough frame space to retain wrists and elbows
- A source pose that permits a subtle weight shift without reframing

## Preservation Contract

- Preserve the exact face, apparent age, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, wardrobe, accessories, background, lighting, color treatment, camera angle, and aspect ratio.
- Change only both hands and forearms plus minimal elbow, shoulder, and weight-shift adjustments.
- Do not convert the gesture into prayer hands, a ceremonial clasp, or rigid symmetry.

## Anatomy Requirements

- Two distinct anatomically correct hands with five fingers each
- Clear ownership of every finger and thumb
- Fingers overlap loosely without impossible interlocking
- Palms touch lightly while wrists remain neutral
- Occlusion follows real hand depth and does not merge the two palms

## Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded image as the exact identity and wardrobe reference. Preserve the same face, age, hairstyle, body proportions, outfit, accessories, lighting, setting, and color treatment.

Create a natural standing pose with both hands loosely clasped in front of the lower torso. Let the fingers overlap gently without interlocking tightly. Keep both wrists relaxed, palms lightly touching, thumbs resting naturally, shoulders soft, elbows close but not pinned to the body, and body weight shifted subtly to one leg. The clasp must feel temporary and conversational rather than fixed or ceremonial.

Photorealistic portrait, comfortable approachable posture, realistic hand anatomy, visible natural finger overlaps, believable proportions, soft body language.

Avoid tightly interlocked fingers, prayer pose, rigid symmetry, merged hands, extra fingers, missing fingers, duplicated thumbs, impossible overlaps, broken wrists, floating palms, excessive tension, identity drift, or wardrobe changes.
```

## Gesture Lock

- Hands meet in front of lower torso
- Fingers overlap loosely rather than interlock tightly
- Thumbs remain distinct
- Wrists stay neutral and relaxed
- Shoulders and elbows remain soft

## Camera and Framing Guidance

- Prefer medium, three-quarter, or full-body framing.
- Keep the clasp, both wrists, and both elbows inside the frame.
- Preserve source camera position, crop, focal-length character, depth of field, and perspective.
- Avoid angles that collapse both hands into one silhouette.

## Technical Notes

- **Method:** Reference-guided image editing or image-to-image generation
- **Output:** Match source dimensions and aspect ratio
- **Priority:** Identity fidelity first, two-hand anatomy and ownership second, welcoming posture third
- **Realism:** Distinct thumbs, natural overlap, soft contact shadows, nail beds, knuckles, and skin texture

## How to Use

- Apply this as the only new gesture in the edit.
- Use medium, three-quarter, or full-body framing so both hands, wrists, elbows, and the lower torso remain visible.
- Ask for a loose temporary clasp with overlapping fingers, not tightly interlocked or prayer-position hands.
- Keep shoulders soft and add only a subtle weight shift to prevent rigid symmetry.
- Compare the result against the source for identity, wardrobe, background, lighting, and camera continuity.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The same recognizable person, outfit, setting, and lighting are preserved.
- [ ] Two distinct hands meet naturally in front of the lower torso.
- [ ] Each hand has five fingers with clear ownership and two distinct thumbs.
- [ ] Fingers overlap loosely without merging or impossible interlocking.
- [ ] Wrists, elbows, shoulders, and weight distribution remain relaxed.
- [ ] No extra, missing, fused, duplicated, floating, or cropped hand anatomy appears.

## Negative Prompt

```text
tightly interlocked fingers, prayer pose, rigid symmetry, merged hands, fused palms, extra fingers, missing fingers, duplicated fingers, duplicated thumbs, duplicated hands, impossible overlap, broken wrists, reversed hands, floating palms, excessive tension, cropped wrists, ambiguous hand ownership, identity drift, wardrobe drift, background change, lighting change
```
