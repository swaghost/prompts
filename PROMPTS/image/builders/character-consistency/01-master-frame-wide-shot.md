## MASTER FRAME — PROMPT (WIDE)

Photorealistic documentary editorial photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens. This is the master frame of a series — it defines the location, light and colour grade for every later image.

Image 1: character reference sheet — use it ONLY for her exact face, hair, jewellery and wardrobe. Ignore its white studio background and its lighting.

**Scene:** a muddy street on the edge of a medieval northern town, late afternoon under a flat overcast sky. She stands in the open in front of a timber-framed tavern with a steep slate roof, its lower storey rough grey stone, its upper storey dark tarred beams and pale daub, small thick-glass windows glowing dull amber from within. A heavy iron bracket juts from the wall holding a blank carved wooden board with no writing on it. The street is deep churned mud with standing water in the wheel ruts, straw trodden into it, a stack of split firewood against the tavern wall, a wooden handcart tipped on its side, a rope-tied bundle of hides. Behind and beyond, the town builds into depth: more timber roofs, a stone chimney trailing smoke, a curtain wall of grey stone, and a square keep tower rising against the sky in the far distance. Two hooded townspeople in wool and leather pass in the middle distance, small and out of focus, not looking at her.

**Subject:** a 24-year-old woman, the woman from image 1, standing in the mud in the middle of the street, full body, feet apart and planted. She carries a heavy full wooden pail in one hand — that arm hangs dead straight, the shoulder visibly dragged down and the tendons standing in her forearm, the water tipping toward the lip. Her other arm hangs loose. Her weight is on the leg opposite the pail. She looks off to one side, out of the frame, unbothered and unposed, her face working and neutral, as if she has stopped for a second on her way somewhere.

**Camera — WIDE SHOT:** from further back and slightly elevated, so she occupies a smaller portion of the frame and the full location — the tavern, the churned street, the town roofs and the keep beyond — is visible around her, extending to its far edges. Keep her sharp and readable at that distance.

**Wardrobe as in image 1** — lavender crushed velour low-rise shorts with white piping, matching cropped velour zip hoodie worn open over a white cotton bralette top, silver chain choker, small hoops, beaded bracelets, thick white socks pulled to mid-calf, chunky white leather trainers.

**Wear state:** everything carries the street. Grey mud sprayed up the backs of her calves and across the white socks, the trainers caked brown to the laces, dark wet mud spatter on the pale velour, the nap of the velour matted and darkened where it is wet, dirt under her fingernails, a dry smear across one thigh, damp hair stuck at her temple. The clothes are real worn fabric, not costume.

**Light and grade:** flat soft overcast daylight from a high broad sky, no direct sun, gentle contrast, cool blue-grey shadows against warm wet brown mud and the dull amber of the windows. Desaturated, slightly cold, natural film-like colour. Real skin texture, visible pores and freckles, no retouching.

**Constraints:** no text, no letters, no writing, no signage, no numbers, no watermark, no logos, no captions, no fantasy creatures, no armour, no weapons, no snow, no direct sunlight, no golden hour, no fog, no airbrushed or plastic skin, no beauty lighting, no smiling, no posing for camera, no other people close to her or interacting with her, no modern objects other than her clothing.
