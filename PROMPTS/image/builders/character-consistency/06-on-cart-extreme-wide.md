# Scene 6: On the Cart — Extreme Wide Shot

**Small in the landscape, road to the keep**

---

## SCENE — PROMPT (EXTREME WIDE)

Photorealistic documentary editorial photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens. New scene in an ongoing series — the same woman on the same day.

Use image 1 as the reference for her exact face, hair and wardrobe. Use image 2 as the reference for the world, the light and the colour grade.

**Character lock:** the woman from image 1 and image 2, unchanged — same face, same hair, same clothes, same mud on her shoes and socks.

**Scene lock:** the same medieval northern country as image 2 — rough grey stone, dark weathered timber, steep slate roofs, deep churned mud, a grey stone curtain wall and a square crenellated keep on the high ground.

**Grade lock:** heavy flat overcast light, no direct sun, no bright sky, desaturated slightly cold naturalistic film colour, identical exposure and white balance to image 2.

**Scene:** the open bed of a wooden farm cart rolling along a rutted mud road toward the town. The bed is heaped with loose hay and the day's load — willow baskets of apples and pears, turnips and onions spilled across the straw, a sack of grain, a stack of empty crates roped to the side board. At the front of the cart, seen from behind, the driver sits hunched on the bench in a brown wool cloak with the reins in his hands, and beyond him two heavy draught horses in worn leather harness pull at a walk, their backs filling the upper part of the frame. Hedgerows and bare fields run past on both sides, the mud road ahead, the town wall and the keep grey in the distance.

**Pose lock:** she sits in the hay with her back against the side board of the cart and her knees drawn up in front of her, arms folded loosely over her knees, an apple in one hand. She is looking forward past the driver at the road ahead.

**Camera — EXTREME WIDE SHOT:** the camera far back and raised off the road, so the whole cart, the driver and the draught horses read small within the full landscape — hedgerows, bare fields and the mud road running out to the town wall and the keep on the horizon dominating the frame — while she stays lit and readable in the hay.

Photographed as a real documentary photograph, cold flat daylight, natural colour, no retouching.

**Constraints:** no text, no letters, no signage, no numbers, no watermark, no logos, no fantasy creatures, no armour, no weapons, no direct sunlight, no blue sky, no airbrushed skin, no beauty lighting, no posing for camera, no other people in the cart bed, no modern objects other than her clothing.
