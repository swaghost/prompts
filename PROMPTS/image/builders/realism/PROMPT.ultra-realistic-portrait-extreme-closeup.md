## Key Requirements

✅ **Maximum skin realism** — visible pores, texture, freckles, peach fuzz  
✅ **Perfect hair detail** — strand-by-strand, glossy, flyaways  
✅ **No smoothing or airbrushing** — texture fully intact  
✅ **Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting** — single hard key light, deep shadows  
✅ **Natural imperfections** — fine lines, tonal variation, natural notch in brow  
✅ **Makeup preserves texture** — freckles visible through makeup  
✅ **Catchlight in eyes** — real moisture and light reflection  
✅ **Shallow depth of field** — 85mm portrait lens

⚠️ **Avoid**:

- Beauty retouching or smoothing
- Even lighting or soft shadows
- Airbrush effects
- Perfect symmetry
- Synthetic hair rendering
- Flat lighting
