# Realism for Commercial & UGC Photography

**Platform:** AI Image Generation (Midjourney, DALL-E, Ideogram, or similar)  
**Style:** Ultra-realistic commercial photography, product campaigns, UGC aesthetics  
**Focus:** Achieving photographic realism through imperfection, detail, and authentic lighting  
**Applications:** Product campaigns, brand content, AI influencers, lifestyle photography

---

## Concept

Achieving true photographic realism in AI-generated commercial and user-generated content (UGC) requires embracing imperfection rather than perfection. Most AI images fail because they optimize for beauty and polish instead of reality. This guide provides the formula for creating ultra-realistic product photography and influencer content that feels genuinely photographed rather than artificially generated.

**Perfect for:** Product advertising, beauty campaigns, lifestyle photography, AI influencer content, social media marketing, brand campaigns, editorial photography, UGC-style content

---

## The Realism Formula

### 1. Destroy Perfection

**Add These Imperfections:**

- Visible pores
- Peach fuzz (fine facial hair)
- Flyaway hairs
- Subtle skin discoloration
- Minor blemishes
- Smile lines
- Slight asymmetry
- Realistic fingernails
- Tiny freckles
- Texture around eyes

**Avoid:**

- Flawless skin
- Perfectly symmetrical faces
- Airbrushed makeup
- Perfectly styled hair

### 2. Use Phone Camera Logic

The best AI influencer images feel like someone quickly grabbed their phone.

**Add:**

- Slightly awkward framing
- Cropped limbs
- Imperfect composition
- Accidental foreground objects
- Depth of field from smartphone lenses
- Natural camera angle

**Avoid:**

- Fashion-magazine composition
- Centered subjects
- Perfect studio positioning

### 3. Shoot Tiny Details

Most people only look at the face. Realistic creators zoom into:

- Hands
- Wrists
- Fingernails
- Shoes
- Shoulders
- Skin texture
- Jewellery
- Clothing fabric
- Drinks
- Table surfaces

**Why this matters:** These are the areas AI usually struggles with, so when they're done correctly, viewers assume the whole image is real.

### 4. Add Physical Evidence of Being Human

**Examples:**

- Condensation on a glass
- Wrinkles in clothing
- Slightly dirty shoes
- Skin creases
- Sun marks
- Uneven tan
- Hair caught in wind
- Veins
- Tiny scars
- Stretched fabric

**Impact:** These details massively increase perceived realism.

### 5. Natural Lighting Wins

**Use:**

- Bright daylight
- Cloudy daylight
- Golden hour
- Window light
- Harsh midday sun

**Avoid:**

- Beauty lighting
- Cinematic rim lights
- Colored lights
- Dramatic studio setups

**Reality:** Most real influencers are photographed with terrible lighting.

### 6. Add Environment Imperfections

**Instead of:**

```
luxury golf club terrace
```

**Use:**

```
luxury golf club terrace, slightly worn wicker chair, water spots on glass table, small scuffs on shoes, uneven shadows from afternoon sun
```

**Principle:** Reality lives in the imperfections.

### 7. The Influencer Realism Stack

**Universal Realism Prompt Formula:**

```
ultra-realistic iPhone photo, candid off-guard moment, natural daylight, realistic skin texture, visible pores, peach fuzz, flyaway hairs, authentic imperfections, shallow depth of field, slight framing imperfections, smartphone camera processing, natural shadows, realistic fabric texture, lifestyle photography, not retouched, social media candid aesthetic
```

**Key Principles:**

- Position product extremely close to camera
- Subject further in background
- Wide-angle lens (20-24mm)
- Low camera position for drama
- Maintain sharp focus on product
- Allow background to soften slightly

**Example Application:**

```
The bottle is dramatically oversized by forced perspective and dominates the upper-left half of the frame, positioned extremely close to the camera with the woman's face and body further behind it.
```

## Pro Tips

### Realism Over Beauty

**Critical Mindset:** Most AI influencers fail because creators accidentally optimize for beauty instead of reality.

**Solution:** Embrace imperfection as the path to believability.

### The "Destroy Perfection" Checklist

Before finalizing any prompt, ensure you've added:

- ✓ Visible pores
- ✓ Peach fuzz
- ✓ Flyaway hairs
- ✓ Slight asymmetry
- ✓ Minor blemishes or marks
- ✓ Realistic fingernail detail
- ✓ Natural skin discoloration
- ✓ Texture around eyes
- ✓ Imperfect hair styling
- ✓ Clothing wrinkles

### Avoid These AI Tells

**Common Mistakes:**

- Flawless airbrushed skin
- Perfectly symmetrical faces
- Overly centered composition
- Perfect studio lighting
- No environmental imperfections
- Unrealistic hand anatomy
- Pure white eyeballs (should be slightly off-white)
- CGI-perfect reflections
- No fingerprints or skin texture
- Perfectly styled hair

### Camera Setting Authenticity

**Include Technical Details:**

```
Shot on a full-frame camera with a 20mm wide-angle lens, f/7.1, 1/1000 sec, ISO 100
```

**Why:** Specific camera settings ground the image in photographic reality.

### Product Branding Control

**Always Specify:**

```
completely blank, without a logo, label or readable writing
no readable text, logo or branding
Leave the central packaging area clean and unbranded
```

**Reason:** Prevents AI from generating fake or garbled text.

### Physical Evidence Matters

**Small details that prove humanity:**

- Condensation on glass
- Wrinkled fabric
- Slightly dirty shoes
- Sun marks and uneven tan
- Hair caught in wind
- Visible veins on hands
- Tiny scars
- Stretched fabric under tension

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Skin looks too perfect/airbrushed

- **Solution:** Add "visible pores, fine facial hairs, natural asymmetry, slight skin discoloration, tiny freckles, realistic skin texture, not retouched"

**Issue:** Hands look malformed

- **Solution:** Specify "realistic finger proportions, visible knuckle folds, subtle veins, nail-bed detail, realistic grip pressure, anatomically accurate"

## Quick Reference Card

**CORE PRINCIPLE:** Realism lives in imperfection

**THE 7 REALISM RULES:**

1. **Destroy Perfection** – Add pores, peach fuzz, flyaways, asymmetry
2. **Phone Camera Logic** – Awkward framing, imperfect composition
3. **Shoot Tiny Details** – Hands, nails, fabric, surfaces
4. **Physical Evidence** – Condensation, wrinkles, scuffs, veins
5. **Natural Lighting** – Daylight, golden hour, window light (not studio drama)
6. **Environment Imperfections** – Worn surfaces, water spots, uneven shadows
7. **Influencer Stack** – Ultra-realistic iPhone photo, candid moment, authentic imperfections

**TECHNICAL ESSENTIALS:**

- **Lenses:** 20mm (forced perspective), 50-55mm (standard), 85mm (macro)
- **Aperture:** f/4–f/11 (depending on depth needs)
- **Shutter:** 1/250–1/1600 sec
- **ISO:** 100-200 (clean)
- **Format:** 4:5 vertical (social optimized)

**MUST-INCLUDE DETAILS:**

- Visible pores and skin texture
- Fine facial hair (peach fuzz)
- Flyaway hair strands
- Realistic hand anatomy (knuckles, veins, fingerprints)
- Natural nail detail
- Slight asymmetry
- Imperfect composition
- Natural lighting irregularity
- Environmental imperfections
- Product wear/condensation

**ALWAYS AVOID:**

- Overlay text, logos, readable branding
- Flawless airbrushed skin
- Perfect symmetry
- Malformed fingers/extra fingers
- Pure white eyeballs
- CGI-perfect reflections
- Dramatic studio lighting (for UGC)
- Perfectly centered composition
- Warped product geometry

**LIGHTING APPROACHES:**

- **Outdoor:** Bright midday sun, golden hour, natural daylight
- **Studio:** Diffused softbox, hard flash, bottom lighting
- **Mixed:** Cool cyan + warm red, flash + ambient
