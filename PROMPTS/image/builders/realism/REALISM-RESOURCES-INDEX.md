## Core Philosophy

**Universal Principle:** "Most AI content fails because creators accidentally optimize for beauty and perfection instead of reality. Embrace imperfection as the path to believability."

**The 7 Realism Rules** (applies to ALL AI image generation):

1. **Destroy Perfection** - Add visible pores, peach fuzz, flyaway hairs, asymmetry, minor blemishes
2. **Use Phone Camera Logic** - Embrace awkward framing, cropped limbs, imperfect composition
3. **Shoot Tiny Details** - Focus on AI's weak spots (hands, nails, fabric, surfaces)
4. **Add Physical Evidence of Being Human** - Condensation, wrinkles, scuffs, environmental wear
5. **Natural Lighting Wins** - Daylight, golden hour, window light (NOT studio perfection)
6. **Environment Imperfections** - Reality lives in the imperfections (worn surfaces, water spots)
7. **Influencer Realism Stack** - Universal authenticity prompt formula

## Universal Realism Stack

**Copy-paste this into any prompt for instant realism boost:**

```
ultra-realistic iPhone photo, candid off-guard moment, natural daylight, realistic skin texture, visible pores, peach fuzz, flyaway hairs, authentic imperfections, shallow depth of field, slight framing imperfections, smartphone camera processing, natural shadows, realistic fabric texture, lifestyle photography, not retouched, social media candid aesthetic
```

**For product photography, add:**

```
realistic [material] texture, visible imperfections, microscopic detail, natural wear, physically accurate shadows, realistic refraction, authentic lighting irregularity
```

**Always avoid:**

```
Avoid: flawless skin, perfectly symmetrical faces, centered composition, studio perfection, artificial CGI skin, no imperfections, beauty filters, airbrushing, dramatic studio lighting, perfect hair styling, pure white eyeballs
```

## Technical Specifications Reference

### Camera Settings by Context

**Product Photography:**

- 20mm wide-angle (forced perspective)
- 50-55mm standard (beauty shots)
- 85mm macro (detail shots)
- f/4–f/11 aperture
- ISO 100-200
- 1/250–1/1600 sec shutter

**UGC/Influencer:**

- iPhone 24mm equivalent
- Smartphone depth of field
- Natural camera angles
- f/1.8–f/2.8 (shallow depth)
- Imperfect framing

**Progressive Zoom:**

- 100-150mm macro equivalent
- f/2.8–f/11 (depending on zoom level)
- Precise focus control
- Zero identity drift

### Lighting by Context

**Product/Commercial:**

- Bright midday sun (outdoor)
- Golden hour (late afternoon)
- Diffused frontal softbox (studio)
- Hard directional flash (dramatic)
- Bottom lighting (translucent products)

**UGC/Influencer:**

- Natural daylight
- Window light
- Cloudy daylight
- Harsh midday sun
- NO studio setups

**Progressive Zoom:**

- Consistent lighting throughout
- Same color temperature
- Natural ambient light
- Soft diffused illumination
