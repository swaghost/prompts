### Enhanced Nose Macro Specifications

When creating extreme nose macro shots (Image 3 or standalone nose portraits), include these advanced anatomical details for medical-grade documentary realism:

#### Nose Surface Micro-Detail

**Pore Structure & Sebum:**

- Visible pores of varying sizes: smaller on bridge (0.1mm), larger on sidewall and tip (0.2-0.3mm)
- Individual pore structure with slight shadows within each pore
- Sebaceous filaments naturally emerging from enlarged pores (realistic, not excessive)
- Tiny blackheads scattered naturally around nose tip (organic placement, not uniform)
- Natural oiliness concentrating on bridge and tip, creating subtle shine
- Realistic pore size variation based on nose zone

**Micro-Texture & Biology:**

- Individual sweat pores visible in extreme macro
- Subsurface scattering creating natural translucency where skin is thin

- Natural skin ridges and furrows (dermatoglyphics)

**Pigmentation & Color:**

- Very slight redness around nostril edge (subtle, realistic)

- Natural skin tone unevenness (not uniform)
- Faint freckles or beauty marks if character-appropriate
- Realistic color variation from bridge to tip

## Universal Authenticity Constraints

### Zero Identity Drift

- Same look, same facial structure throughout
- Lock anatomical features: nose, lips, eyes, brows
- Preserve natural asymmetries and unique characteristics
- Never symmetrize, neutralize, or "perfect" features

### No Distortion

- No skin warping, morphing, stretching, or liquefying
- No fabricated textures or hallucinatory details
- No overlapping, smearing, or pixel mixing
- Maintain structural integrity during zoom operations

### Preserve Micro-Details

- Visible pores of varying sizes
- Fine vellus hair (peach fuzz)
- Natural freckles, pigmentation variations
- Realistic skin micro-textures and surface characteristics
- Individual hair strands with natural variation

### Authentic Depth of Field

- Shallow macro depth of field at close magnifications
- Natural bokeh and focus falloff based on lens physics
- Sharpest focus on primary subject
- Gentle blur transition (not digital/artificial blur)

### Lighting Consistency

- Retain exact same ambient lighting throughout
- Same color temperature across all zoom levels
- Consistent highlights, shadows, and exposure
- No studio relighting, no HDR, no cinematic grading

### No Beauty Filters

- No skin smoothing or airbrushing
- No plastic-looking skin or CGI feel
- No over-sharpening or artificial enhancement
- Maintain raw, biological texture quality

### Hair Accuracy

- Only soft, fine, nearly invisible light-colored vellus hair

- Individual hair strands with natural direction and variation

## Usage Tips

### For Best Results

1. **Generate in sequence**: Start with Image 1, then 2, 3, etc.
2. **Reference previous images**: Use previous generation as reference for next
3. **Multiple attempts**: Generate 2-3 versions per zoom level, select best
4. **Save originals**: Keep all generations for reference
5. **Document settings**: Note which attempts worked best

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Identity drift**: Subject looks different at different zoom levels
- **Over-beautification**: AI adds smoothing or filters
- **Lighting changes**: Color temperature shifts between images
- **Anatomical inconsistency**: Nose shape, lip contours change
- **Fabricated details**: AI invents textures not in base image

### Platform-Specific Notes

**GPT Image 2 (Deevid AI)**:

- Excellent for photorealism
- Strong identity consistency
- Good macro detail rendering
- May need 2-3 attempts per prompt

**DALL-E 3**:

- Very good photorealism
- May require shorter prompts
- Tends toward slight beautification (compensate in prompt)

**Midjourney**:

- Excellent stylization
- May require parameter tuning (--stylize 100-250)
- Use --style raw for photorealism
- Break longer prompts into sections

**Stable Diffusion**:

- Highly controllable with right models
- Use realistic checkpoint models
- May require negative prompts
- ControlNet helpful for consistency
