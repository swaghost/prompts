# Facial Expression Sheet - 8 Portraits 4x2 Grid (Front View Only)

## Description

Use an uploaded image as the identity reference only. Reconstruct the person into a standardized head-and-shoulders studio portrait regardless of the input framing. Crop from just below the collarbones to slightly above the head, with the head filling about 70-75% of the frame. Never generate a full-body, half-body, waist-up, upper-body, or bust shot.

Create one professional facial expression sheet with exactly eight portraits arranged in a clean 4×2 grid on a pure white seamless background. Keep the same person perfectly consistent in every portrait, preserving identical facial identity, hairstyle, bangs, hair length, skin tone, facial proportions, neck, and clothing visible at the neckline.

Keep identical framing, camera distance, lighting, color grading, and use a straight-on front view (0°). Only the facial expression may change.

## Usage

Perfect for character design emotion charts, animation facial reference, game character emotion sets, acting portfolios, emoji design reference, and visual communication guides. Ideal for demonstrating emotional range while maintaining perfect identity and framing consistency.

## Expression Guidelines

**Neutral:**

- Baseline expression
- Natural resting face
- No emotion
- Relaxed facial muscles
- Mouth closed, eyes naturally open

**Soft Smile:**

- Subtle, gentle warmth
- Approachable and friendly
- Not exaggerated
- Natural pleasantness
- Lips closed or barely parted

**Big Smile (Teeth Visible):**

- Genuine happiness
- Full engagement
- Teeth clearly shown
- Eyes participate in smile (Duchenne smile)
- Broad, open smile

**Laughing:**

- Active, dynamic expression
- Genuine laughter moment
- Mouth open wider
- Joy radiating from face
- Eyes crinkled, very animated

**Surprised:**

- Sudden reaction
- Wide eyes essential
- Raised eyebrows high
- Mouth open showing shock
- Alert, startled look

**Angry:**

- Clear displeasure
- Furrowed brow critical
- Tense face
- Intense eye contact
- Lowered brows, tightened jaw

**Sad:**

- Visible melancholy
- Downturned mouth
- Eyes show sadness
- Quiet sorrow
- Possibly glistening eyes

**Winking:**

- One eye closed deliberately
- Other eye fully open
- Playful or knowing
- Slight smile enhances effect
- Confident, friendly gesture
