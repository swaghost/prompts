# Power Board

## Description

A cinematic production-reference board that defines one character's power system through core abilities, activation stages, intensity levels, environmental impacts, statistics, and an energy palette. It keeps identity and costume locked while making power behavior repeatable across future images and sequences.

## Use It When

Best for visualizing a character's power system, intensity, and combat effects.

## Prerequisites

- One clear full-body or three-quarter character reference image
- Visible costume, equipment, signature traits, and a readable elemental or energy language
- Optional verified power names or mechanics; otherwise use descriptive functional labels and avoid invented canon

## Workflow

1. Upload the character reference image as the single source of truth.
2. Paste the complete master prompt without removing its power taxonomy or panel structure.
3. Generate natively in exact 4:5 portrait format, ideally 1080 x 1350 px or higher.
4. Verify identity, costume, power color, source, scale, and behavior across every demonstration.
5. Regenerate rather than crop if the board is 9:16, square, landscape, or extra tall.

## Continuity Rules

- Preserve the same face, age, body proportions, costume, equipment, and signature traits across all panels.
- Keep one coherent power source and visual grammar; intensity may increase, but the effect must not become a different ability.
- Use believable light interaction, environmental response, particles, shadows, and contact effects.
- Keep titles, labels, statistics, panels, and captions inside the 4:5 safe area.
- Use readable English and avoid unsupported lore; use `UNKNOWN` where necessary.
- Treat the board as a production reference system rather than a decorative effects collage.

## Master Prompt

```
Create a single high-resolution, densely packed character power reference sheet titled "POWER BOARD" using the attached character image as the single source of truth for identity, appearance, body proportions, costume, equipment, and visual language.

Generate in EXACT 4:5 vertical Instagram carousel aspect ratio. Design the entire layout natively for 4:5, not cropped from a taller poster. Preserve the exact same character identity and outfit throughout every panel.

Use a dark near-black cinematic background, thin yellow/gold technical borders, subtle character-specific energy accents, premium videogame ability-menu aesthetics, production-reference UI, faint grain, and dramatic lighting. Include one large hero image of the character demonstrating their signature power.

METADATA: SUBJECT · POWER CLASS · POWER SOURCE · COMBAT STYLE · RANGE · CONTROL · INTENSITY · SIGNATURE POWER.

PANEL 01 — CORE POWERS: Show 6 separate cinematic demonstrations: PRIMARY POWER · SECONDARY POWER · DEFENSIVE POWER · MOVEMENT POWER · SPECIAL POWER · ULTIMATE POWER.

PANEL 02 — POWER ACTIVATION: Four sequential stages: DORMANT · CHARGE · ACTIVATION · RELEASE.

PANEL 03 — POWER INTENSITY: Four progressively stronger versions: 25% · 50% · 75% · 100%.

PANEL 04 — IMPACT STUDIES: Show four cinematic examples of powers interacting with the environment: energy impact, defensive interaction, movement effect, and maximum-force impact.

PANEL 05 — POWER STATISTICS: Premium HUD-style bars for POWER · SPEED · RANGE · CONTROL · DEFENSE · STAMINA · DESTRUCTIVE POTENTIAL.

PANEL 06 — ENERGY / COLOR PALETTE: 6 HEX-coded swatches derived from the character and power effects.

Add the bottom caption: "Use this power board as a visual reference for consistent depiction of the character's powers across all generations." Bottom-right tags: STYLE · Power System · Realistic · Cinematic.

High-end cinematic realism, readable English labels, consistent character, realistic VFX integration, controlled visual density, 8K appearance.
```

## Negative Prompt

```
9:16, square, cropped poster, inconsistent character, random powers, unreadable HUD, excessive VFX, invented equipment, clutter, cartoon, low detail, watermark, logo, or clipped safe-area content.
```

## Quick Correction Line

```text
Keep all existing content, character identity, costume, and power system. Rebuild the entire composition natively in EXACT 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Do not crop a 9:16 poster. Reflow every panel, title, label, HUD bar, effect study, and caption inside the 4:5 safe area.
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Output is native 4:5 portrait with all content inside the safe area.
- [ ] Character identity, costume, equipment, and proportions remain consistent.
- [ ] Six core powers are distinct but belong to one coherent system.
- [ ] Dormant, charge, activation, release, and intensity states progress logically.
- [ ] Environmental impacts use believable scale, light, shadow, and material interaction.
- [ ] The signature-power hero image and title read first.
- [ ] HUD labels, statistics, and HEX-coded swatches are legible.
- [ ] No random ability, invented equipment, excessive VFX, watermark, or clipped panel appears.

Make the four activation stages and four intensity levels progress more clearly without changing power type.

Increase HUD and typography readability while preserving every required panel and statistic.
