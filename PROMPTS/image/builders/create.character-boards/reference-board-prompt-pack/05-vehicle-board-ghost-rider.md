# Vehicle Board - Ghost Rider

## Description

A cinematic vehicle-concept board that derives nine distinct machines from one character's visual language while keeping the character identity, infernal material system, palette, and production-board design consistent. Every vehicle is shown completely and reads as a unique engineered silhouette.

## Use It When

Use this board for vehicle ideation, franchise-style transport systems, game and film development, mechanical silhouette exploration, campaign art, and character-to-product design translation.

## Prerequisites

- One clear character reference showing identity, costume, signature materials, and effects
- Readable palette and motifs that can translate into mechanical design
- A model capable of nine complete, distinct vehicles in one grid
- Native 4:5 output; do not crop from square, landscape, or 9:16

## Workflow

1. Upload one reference as the identity and design-language source of truth.
2. Paste the full prompt without deleting vehicle labels, framing, or consistency rules.
3. Generate natively at exact 4:5, preferably 3000 x 3750 px or higher.
4. Verify that each vehicle is complete, mechanically legible, and distinct in silhouette.
5. Regenerate if vehicles are cropped, duplicated, merged, or disconnected from the character's visual language.

## Continuity Rules

- Preserve the flaming skull, glowing eyes, black leather, infernal orange fire, dark cinematic tone, and skull-inspired mechanical language.
- Keep blackened metal, scorched surfaces, orange flame, smoke, and reflection behavior coherent across all vehicles.
- Change vehicle category, proportions, wheelbase, body architecture, and silhouette according to each label.
- Show every vehicle fully inside its panel in a consistent cinematic three-quarter presentation.
- Keep the character and hero image separate from the vehicle studies; do not turn panels into combat scenes.

## Platform and Format

**Platform:** GPT Image 2  
**Format:** Exact 4:5 vertical Instagram carousel, 3000 x 3750 px preferred

## Prompt

```text
Create a premium vertical 4:5 VEHICLE BOARD featuring Ghost Rider. Use the uploaded image as the identity reference. Preserve his flaming skull, glowing eyes, black leather biker clothing, infernal orange flames and dark cinematic appearance. Do not copy the original composition.

LAYOUT: Exact Instagram carousel ratio 4:5. The board fills 96-98% of the canvas. No white margins, side bars or excessive padding. Use only a thin solid black perimeter frame, matte-black and charcoal production-board styling, distressed golden-yellow borders and typography, subtle film grain, a compact hero header showing Ghost Rider beside a flaming motorcycle, and the exact title "VEHICLE BOARD".

CTA: In the extreme upper-left, render COMMENT in white above FACE in golden yellow. No panel numbering.

PANEL GRID: Create a precise 3x3 grid showing: HELLCYCLE / INFERNO CHOPPER / DEMON MUSCLE / HELLFIRE ROADSTER / PHANTOM CRUISER / INFERNO TRUCK / HELLRIDE ATV / BLAZING BUGGY / DOOM RACER.

CONSISTENCY: Show every vehicle completely inside its panel in a cinematic three-quarter view. Use black metal, scorched textures, skull-inspired mechanical details, glowing orange fire, burning wheels, smoke and reflections. Every silhouette must be distinct.

STYLE: Photorealistic cinematic vehicle concept art, extremely detailed, deep-black environments, orange hellfire and distressed gold production graphics.

TEXT: Render only COMMENT, FACE, VEHICLE BOARD and the nine vehicle labels.

AVOID: No other characters, combat scene, blood, gore, cropped vehicles, panel numbers, logos, watermark, additional text or misspellings.
```

## Output Checklist

- [ ] Nine complete, distinct vehicle silhouettes in three-quarter view
- [ ] Flaming skull, glowing eyes, black leather, orange flame, and cinematic darkness remain consistent
- [ ] No cropped vehicles, combat scene, blood, gore, or unapproved text
- [ ] Board uses the required matte-black, charcoal, distressed-gold system

## Negative Prompt

```text
9:16, square, landscape, cropped board, white margins, fewer than nine vehicles, extra panels, duplicate vehicle, repeated silhouette, cropped vehicle, missing wheels, malformed chassis, impossible wheel placement, merged vehicle parts, weak mechanical logic, identity drift, missing flaming skull, changed flame color, generic clean vehicle, unrelated color palette, combat scene, weapons attack, blood, gore, other characters, panel numbers, extra text, misspelled labels, logo, watermark
```

## Quick Correction Line

```text
Keep the exact same character identity, infernal black-metal and orange-fire design language, nine vehicle labels, board design, and approved text. Rebuild natively in exact 4:5 portrait format with one hero header and a complete 3x3 vehicle grid. Show every vehicle fully inside its panel in a distinct cinematic three-quarter view.
```
