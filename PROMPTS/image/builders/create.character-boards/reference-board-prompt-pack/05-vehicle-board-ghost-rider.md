# Vehicle Board - Ghost Rider

## Platform and Format

**Platform:** GPT Image 2  
**Format:** Exact 4:5 vertical Instagram carousel, 3000 x 3750 px preferred

## Prompt

```text
Create a premium vertical 4:5 VEHICLE BOARD featuring Ghost Rider. Use the uploaded image as the identity reference. Preserve his flaming skull, glowing eyes, black leather biker clothing, infernal orange flames and dark cinematic appearance. Do not copy the original composition.

LAYOUT: Exact Instagram carousel ratio 4:5. The board fills 96-98% of the canvas. No white margins, side bars or excessive padding. Use only a thin solid black perimeter frame, matte-black and charcoal production-board styling, distressed golden-yellow borders and typography, subtle film grain, a compact hero header showing Ghost Rider beside a flaming motorcycle, and the exact title "VEHICLE BOARD".

CTA: In the extreme upper-left, render COMMENT in white above FACE in golden yellow. No panel numbering.

PANEL GRID: Create a precise 3x3 grid showing: HELLCYCLE / INFERNO CHOPPER / DEMON MUSCLE / HELLFIRE ROADSTER / PHANTOM CRUISER / INFERNO TRUCK / HELLRIDE ATV / BLAZING BUGGY / DOOM RACER.

CONSISTENCY: Show every vehicle completely inside its panel in a cinematic three-quarter view. Use black metal, scorched textures, skull-inspired mechanical details, glowing orange fire, burning wheels, smoke and reflections. Every silhouette must be distinct.

STYLE: Photorealistic cinematic vehicle concept art, extremely detailed, deep-black environments, orange hellfire and distressed gold production graphics.

TEXT: Render only COMMENT, FACE, VEHICLE BOARD and the nine vehicle labels.

AVOID: No other characters, combat scene, blood, gore, cropped vehicles, panel numbers, logos, watermark, additional text or misspellings.
```
