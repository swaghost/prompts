## Technical Specifications (All Prompts)

- **Format:** RAW capture
- **Style:** Documentary photography, uncompromising realism
- **Focus:** Razor-sharp, no blur, no diffusion
- **Lighting:** Warm golden natural sunlight (daylight to afternoon)
- **Texture:** Visible pores, wrinkles, age spots, micro-imperfections

## Key Features

- **Extreme aging details:** Leathery skin, sun damage, broken capillaries
- **Natural imperfections:** Age spots, cracked skin, yellowed nails, sparse hair
- **Authentic moisture/residue:** Eye discharge, denture moisture, natural oils
- **Hard lighting:** Micro-shadows emphasize every wrinkle and fold
- **No beautification:** Zero smoothing, no retouching, uncomfortable realism
- **Documentary intent:** Raw, honest, intimate capture of old age

## Subject Details

- Deeply weathered skin with heavy sun damage
- Uneven pigmentation and age spots
- Prominent veins and bony structure
- Thick irregular nails
- Sparse white or translucent hair
- Tired, non-glossy eyes
- Sagging, creased skin

## Lighting Style

All prompts emphasize warm, golden natural sunlight (late morning to afternoon) creating:

- Hard micro-shadows that carve texture
- No diffusion or softness
- Realistic contrast without cinematic grading
- Yellow-warm tones maintaining documentary authenticity

## Usage Notes

Designed for ultra-realistic elderly portrait generation emphasizing the unfiltered reality of extreme old age. Not suitable for beautified or commercial use. Focus on documentary honesty and raw human detail.
