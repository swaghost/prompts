# Severe Cold Injury of the Ear

## Classification

- **Body area:** One external ear, including rim, lobe, and base
- **Severity/stage:** Severe visible frostbite damage
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW documentary close-up

## Description of Resulting Image

A close view of an elderly man's ear with pale yellow-white areas along the rim and lobe, deep purplish-red discoloration near the base, dry cracked stiffness, sparse hairs carrying fine ice crystals, and leathery uneven texture.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts severe frostbite-related damage to an external ear.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Pale yellow-white areas along the ear rim and lobe
- Deep purplish-red discoloration toward the base
- Dry, cracked, stiff, leathery-looking skin
- Fine ice crystals attached to sparse hairs

These visual indicators reproduce the prompt and should not be treated as a diagnosis.

## Technical Direction

Frame one external ear tightly in RAW documentary style, keeping the helix, lobe, base, cracks, color transitions, hairs, and ice critically sharp. Use flat, unforgiving cold winter daylight and a neutral-cold grade with no diffusion or artistic effect.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW documentary close-up photograph of the ear of an elderly man exposed to extreme cold. The ear shows severe frostbite damage with pale yellow-white numb areas along the rim and lobe, mixed with deep purplish-red discoloration closer to the base. Skin is dry, cracked, and stiff, with fine ice crystals clinging to sparse hairs. Texture is leathery and uneven. Lighting is cold natural winter daylight, flat and unforgiving, revealing every crack and color shift.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Depict exactly one anatomically complete external ear with coherent helix, antihelix, concha, tragus, and lobe. Preserve natural attachment to the side of the head. Make cracks, discoloration, sparse hair, and ice conform to the ear's folds without duplicated cartilage or detached tissue.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, smooth cosmetic skin, CGI, illustration, extra or duplicated ears, malformed cartilage, fused folds, missing lobe, detached ice, fantasy frost, irrelevant gore, soft focus, cinematic glow, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
