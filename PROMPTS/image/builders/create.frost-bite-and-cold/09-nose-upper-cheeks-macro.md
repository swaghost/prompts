# Frostbite Changes Across the Nose and Upper Cheeks

## Classification

- **Body area:** Nose and both upper cheeks
- **Severity/stage:** Pronounced visible frostbite-related discoloration and surface change
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW macro

## Description of Resulting Image

A sharply resolved macro view of a man's nose and upper cheeks, contrasting waxy pale yellow-white areas with deep red and purple zones across rigid, dry, cracked skin carrying ice particles in pores and facial hair.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts pronounced frostbite-related facial discoloration and cracked skin.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Stark waxy pale yellow-white areas
- Adjacent deep red and purple inflamed-looking zones
- Rigid, dry, cracked-looking skin texture
- Ice particles attached to pores and facial hair

These are visual features specified by the source prompt, not diagnostic claims.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW macro composition centered on the nose and upper cheeks. Hold absolute sharpness across color transitions, pores, fine facial hair, cracks, and ice particles. Use cold natural daylight, realistic texture, a neutral-cold grade, and no stylization.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW macro of a man's nose and upper cheeks affected by frostbite. Skin shows stark contrast: waxy pale yellow-white areas with reduced blood flow next to deep red and purple inflamed zones. Texture looks rigid, dry, and cracked. Ice particles cling to pores and facial hair. Lighting is cold natural daylight, no stylization, absolute sharpness.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Maintain one anatomically correct nose with two nostrils and continuous, naturally proportioned upper cheeks. Keep pores and facial hair at credible scale. Discoloration, cracks, and ice must conform to curved facial planes without mirrored repetition, detached patches, or crystals floating above the skin.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, contour makeup, pore removal, skin smoothing, CGI, illustration, duplicated nose or nostrils, distorted cheeks, repeated texture stamps, fantasy ice, irrelevant wounds or gore, soft focus, warm beauty grade, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
