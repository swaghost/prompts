# Late-Stage Cold Injury of the Fingers

## Classification

- **Body area:** Fingers, fingertips, nails, and surrounding hand skin
- **Severity/stage:** Late-stage visible cold injury
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW clinical documentary extreme close-up

## Description of Resulting Image

An extreme close view of frostbitten fingers showing darkened blackened fingertips beside rigid pale zones, rough cracked matte skin with reduced elasticity, opaque brittle nails, and snow granules attached to the surface.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts late-stage cold injury with blackened fingertip areas.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Darkened blackened areas at the fingertips
- Adjacent rigid pale zones
- Rough, cracked, matte skin with loss of elasticity
- Opaque, brittle-looking nails
- Snow granules clinging to the surface

These are requested visual features and should not be interpreted as a diagnosis or prognosis.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW extreme close-up of the fingers and fingertips in clinical documentary style. Keep nail surfaces, cracks, matte texture, color boundaries, and snow granules critically sharp. Use cold daylight, neutral color, controlled highlights, and no stylization.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW extreme close-up of frostbitten fingers in late-stage cold injury. Fingertips show darkened blackened areas alongside rigid pale zones. Skin texture is rough, cracked, and matte, with loss of elasticity. Nails opaque and brittle. Snow granules cling to the surface. Cold daylight, clinical documentary realism.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

When a complete hand is visible, show exactly five anatomically connected fingers with correct joint sequence and one nail per digit. In tighter framing, keep every visible finger structurally coherent. Make discoloration continuous with tissue and place snow on upward-facing skin and creases without detached fragments.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, manicure polish, smooth plastic skin, CGI, illustration, extra or missing fingers, fused digits, duplicated joints or nails, detached fingertips, exposed anatomy, irrelevant gore, fantasy ice, blur, dramatic color grade, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
