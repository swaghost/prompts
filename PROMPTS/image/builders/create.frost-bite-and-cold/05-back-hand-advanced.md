# Advanced Frostbite on the Back of a Hand

## Classification

- **Body area:** Back of one hand and fingertips
- **Severity/stage:** Advanced visible frostbite
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW close-up

## Description of Resulting Image

A close view of the back of a man's hand dominated by pale yellow-white skin, with darkened patches near the fingertips, faint underlying veins, rigid surface texture, and snow granules caught in wrinkles.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts advanced frostbite and darkened tissue.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Predominantly pale yellow-white surface areas
- Darkened necrotic-looking patches near the fingertips
- Faint veins beneath rigid-looking skin
- Snow granules retained along skin wrinkles

These requested visual features do not independently establish a diagnosis or tissue viability.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW close-up of the dorsal hand from wrist through fingertips. Keep skin texture, subtle veins, wrinkles, snow granules, and tonal transitions sharply resolved. Use cold daylight, restrained contrast, neutral clinical color, and zero stylization.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW close-up of the back of a man's hand with advanced frostbite. Pale yellow-white areas dominate the surface, interrupted by darkened necrotic patches near fingertips. Veins faint beneath rigid skin. Snow granules trapped along wrinkles. Cold daylight, zero stylization.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Show one anatomically coherent hand with exactly five digits, correctly joined knuckles, natural tendon and vein paths, and believable nail placement where visible. Keep all tissue continuous and make discoloration and snow conform to the hand's contours and wrinkles.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, manicure styling, smooth plastic skin, CGI, illustration, extra or missing fingers, fused digits, duplicated nails, broken joint geometry, exposed anatomy, irrelevant gore, decorative ice, warm glamour light, blur, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
