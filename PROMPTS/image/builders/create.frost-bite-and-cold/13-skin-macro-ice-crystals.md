# Frostbitten Skin and Ice Crystals in Extreme Macro

## Classification

- **Body area:** Unspecified human skin surface, pores, and fissures
- **Severity/stage:** Severe visible cold injury with darkening and surface ice
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW extreme macro

## Description of Resulting Image

An extreme macro study of rigid pale yellow-white frostbitten skin transitioning into darkened regions, with visible cracks, stiffness, reduced elasticity, and ice set within pores and fissures under cold natural light.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts severe frostbite-related skin changes at extreme macro detail.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Rigid pale yellow-white areas transitioning into darkened regions
- Visible cracks and stiff, low-elasticity texture
- Ice present within pores and fissures
- Sharply defined boundaries between surface features

These are visual characteristics from the prompt, not diagnostic evidence.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW extreme macro with zero blur across the principal pores, fissures, color transitions, and ice crystals. Light with cold natural light, preserve realistic microtexture and restrained neutral color, and avoid any artistic or cosmetic stylization.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW extreme macro of frostbitten skin covered in ice crystals. Skin shows rigid pale yellow-white frostbite areas transitioning into darkened regions from severe cold injury. Cracks, stiffness, and loss of elasticity clearly visible. Ice embedded in pores and fissures. Cold natural light, zero blur, zero stylization.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Keep the surface recognizably human skin at a coherent macro scale, with plausible pores, fissures, and texture transitions. Ice must occupy surface recesses with believable depth, contact, translucency, and reflection. Preserve physical continuity between pale and darkened skin regions without tile-like repetition.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, pore removal, cosmetic smoothing, CGI, illustration, reptile or mineral texture, oversized crystals, floating ice, repeated texture tiles, exposed anatomy, irrelevant gore, depth blur, warm glamour grade, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
