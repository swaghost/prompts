# Cold-Exposed Wrists

## Classification

- **Body area:** Both wrists, superficial veins, skin folds, and fine hair
- **Severity/stage:** Extreme cold exposure with advanced visible skin and vascular changes
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW documentary close-up

## Description of Resulting Image

A close documentary study of an elderly man's wrists showing translucent, frost-damaged skin, darkened rigid-looking veins, cracking, dryness, pale-yellow-to-purple discoloration, and fine ice crystals on hair and folds.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts pronounced frost-related skin and vascular changes.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Darkened, rigid-looking veins visible beneath translucent skin
- Cracked, dry surface texture
- Patchy discoloration ranging from pale yellow to purple
- Fine ice crystals clinging to wrist hair and skin folds

These are depicted visual signs only and do not establish a diagnosis.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW documentary close-up that clearly includes both wrists. Hold sharp focus across veins, cracks, hairs, folds, and ice crystals. Light with cold natural daylight, no diffusion, and use a restrained neutral-cold grade that preserves realistic skin translucency and texture.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW close-up documentary photograph of the wrists of an elderly man exposed to extreme cold. Veins appear darkened and rigid beneath translucent, frost-damaged skin. The surface shows cracking, dryness, and patchy discoloration from pale yellow to purple. Fine ice crystals cling to hair and skin folds. Lighting is cold natural daylight, revealing vascular damage without softness.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Show exactly two anatomically coherent wrists with natural radius-ulna contours, tendons, superficial veins, skin folds, and hair direction. Keep vein paths beneath the skin and ensure cracks, discoloration, and ice follow the curved wrist surfaces with plausible contact and scale.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, skin smoothing, CGI, illustration, extra wrists or hands, fused limbs, duplicated veins, exposed or floating vessels, impossible joint contours, decorative ice, irrelevant gore, soft focus, warm glamour light, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
