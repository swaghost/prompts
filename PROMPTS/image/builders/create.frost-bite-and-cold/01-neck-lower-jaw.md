# Cold-Exposed Neck and Lower Jaw

## Classification

- **Body area:** Neck, lower jaw, beard, and Adam's apple
- **Severity/stage:** Visible cold exposure with frostbite patches
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW documentary close-up

## Description of Resulting Image

A tightly framed view of an elderly man's lower face and neck, emphasizing deeply wrinkled, stiff, unevenly discolored skin, frozen sparse beard hairs, and a tense-looking Adam's apple under flat winter light.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts visible frostbite-related skin changes and distressing cold injury.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Deep wrinkles with stiff-looking skin
- Uneven discoloration and frostbite patches along the jawline
- Sparse beard hairs frozen together
- Tense, rigid appearance around the Adam's apple

These are visual features requested by the prompt, not a diagnostic conclusion.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW documentary close-up centered on the lower jaw and anterior neck. Keep the jawline, beard hairs, skin texture, and Adam's apple in sharp focus. Use flat, cold winter daylight, restrained contrast, natural texture, and a neutral clinical-documentary grade without softness or stylization.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW documentary close-up photograph of the neck and lower jaw of an elderly man suffering from cold exposure. Skin is deeply wrinkled, stiff, and unevenly discolored, with frostbite patches along the jawline. Sparse beard hairs are frozen together. Adam's apple appears tense and rigid. Lighting is cold winter daylight, flat and realistic.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Maintain a coherent human lower jaw, chin, throat, and one anatomically centered Adam's apple. Preserve natural continuity between facial and neck skin, plausible beard growth, and physically consistent frost attachment to exposed hairs. Keep discoloration and stiffness integrated with the skin rather than layered on top.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, glamour retouching, smooth plastic skin, CGI, illustration, fantasy ice, duplicated facial structures, distorted jaw, misplaced or duplicated Adam's apple, floating beard hairs, irrelevant wounds or gore, dramatic colored lighting, blur, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
