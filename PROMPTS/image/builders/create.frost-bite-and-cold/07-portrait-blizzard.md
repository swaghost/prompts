# Cold-Exposure Portrait in a Blizzard

## Classification

- **Body area:** Face from upper chest to forehead, including cheeks, nose, brows, lashes, and lips
- **Severity/stage:** Severe visible cold injury during extreme exposure
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW environmental documentary portrait

## Description of Resulting Image

An eye-level portrait of an adult man standing in a blizzard, framed from upper chest to forehead. Pale yellow-white facial patches contrast with deep red areas; stiff matte skin, micro-cracks, snow-coated brows and lashes, and visible breath convey the exposure.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-weather training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts severe facial cold injury in an active blizzard setting.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Pale yellow-white patches on the cheeks and nose
- Surrounding deep red, inflamed-looking areas
- Stiff, matte, dehydrated-looking skin with micro-cracks
- Snow crystals and ice on eyebrows and eyelashes
- Breath condensation near the lips

These are visual depictions requested by the prompt and are not a diagnosis.

## Technical Direction

Frame from upper chest to forehead at eye level in RAW documentary style. Keep the entire face critically sharp while retaining a readable snowy blizzard environment. Use harsh cold daylight without diffusion, realistic snow and breath texture, and a restrained cold grade without cinematic stylization.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW close-up portrait of an adult man exposed to extreme cold, standing in a snowy environment during a blizzard. Framed from upper chest to forehead, camera at eye level. Facial skin shows severe cold injury: pale yellow-white patches on cheeks and nose where circulation is reduced, surrounding areas deep red and inflamed. Skin appears stiff, matte, dehydrated, with micro-cracks and rough texture. Eyebrows and eyelashes coated with real snow crystals and ice. Breath condensation visible near lips. Harsh cold daylight, no diffusion. Entire face critically sharp, documentary realism.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Preserve one coherent adult face with two aligned eyes, one nose, one mouth, two natural brows, and anatomically consistent upper chest and neck. Snow and ice must follow hair and facial contours; breath must originate at the mouth. Keep discoloration bilateral only where naturally plausible and integrated with continuous skin.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, heroic glamour, skin smoothing, CGI, illustration, extra facial features, asymmetrical duplicated eyes, distorted nose or mouth, floating snow, frozen breath detached from lips, irrelevant wounds or gore, fantasy storm effects, shallow-focus face, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
