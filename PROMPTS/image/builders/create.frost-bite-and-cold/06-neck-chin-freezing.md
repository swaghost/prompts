# Freezing Exposure of the Neck and Chin

## Classification

- **Body area:** Chin, stubble, and neck
- **Severity/stage:** Pronounced visible cold injury in freezing conditions
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW documentary close-up

## Description of Resulting Image

A close study of a man's chin and neck showing uneven pallor, waxy yellow-white patches, darker purple zones, crepey cracked stiffness, and fine ice dust attached to stubble.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts pronounced frostbite-related discoloration and damaged skin texture.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Uneven pallor with waxy yellow-white patches
- Darker purple zones
- Crepey, cracked, immobile-looking texture
- Ice dust resting on stubble hairs

These are visual characteristics specified by the prompt, not diagnostic proof.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW close-up spanning the chin and anterior neck. Keep the color boundaries, skin cracks, crepey texture, and ice-dusted stubble crisp. Use cold reflected daylight, realistic surface detail, a restrained neutral-cold grade, and documentary rather than cinematic treatment.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW close-up of a man's neck and chin in freezing conditions. Skin shows uneven pallor with waxy yellow-white patches and darker purple zones. Texture crepey, cracked, immobile. Ice dust on stubble hairs. Cold reflected daylight, documentary realism.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Maintain a coherent chin, jaw underside, throat, and neck with natural skin folds and one continuous surface. Preserve plausible stubble direction and density. Ice dust must attach to hairs and nearby skin consistently, while cracks and discoloration follow facial and neck contours.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, grooming polish, skin smoothing, CGI, illustration, duplicated chin, distorted jaw or throat, floating stubble, fantasy ice, unrelated wounds or gore, glossy skin, warm beauty lighting, blur, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
