# Frost-Exposed Bare Feet in Snow

## Classification

- **Body area:** Both bare feet, toes, interdigital spaces, and toenails
- **Severity/stage:** Extreme cold exposure with pronounced visible frostbite-related changes
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW environmental close-up

## Description of Resulting Image

A close view of two bare feet in snow, with pale waxy yellow and darkened areas across stiff, cracked, swollen-looking toes, ice crystals between the toes, and dulled opaque toenails in harsh cold daylight.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts pronounced cold injury to bare feet and toes.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Pale waxy yellow patches mixed with darkened areas on the toes
- Stiff, cracked, swollen-looking skin
- Ice crystals visible between toes
- Dulled, opaque toenails

These visible characteristics reflect the prompt and are not a medical diagnosis.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW close-up that clearly shows both bare feet in direct contact with snow. Keep toes, nails, cracks, color changes, and interdigital ice crystals sharply resolved. Use harsh cold daylight without diffusion, realistic snow texture, and a restrained clinical-documentary grade.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW close-up of frost-exposed bare feet in snow. Toes show pale waxy yellow patches mixed with darkened areas from extreme cold exposure. Skin appears stiff, cracked, and swollen. Ice crystals visible between toes. Toenails dulled and opaque. Harsh cold daylight, no diffusion, extreme realism.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Show exactly two anatomically coherent feet with five toes per foot and one correctly placed nail per toe. Preserve natural toe spacing, arches, joints, and contact with the snow. Keep ice physically seated between toes and on nearby surfaces without fused digits or impossible penetration.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, pedicure styling, smooth plastic skin, CGI, illustration, extra or missing toes, fused digits, duplicated or misplaced nails, reversed feet, floating feet or ice, irrelevant wounds or gore, fantasy snow, blur, warm beauty lighting, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
