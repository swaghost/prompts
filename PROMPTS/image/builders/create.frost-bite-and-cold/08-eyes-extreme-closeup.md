# Frost-Exposed Eyes in Extreme Close-Up

## Classification

- **Body area:** Both eyes, eyelids, eyelashes, eyebrows, and surrounding skin
- **Severity/stage:** Pronounced visible cold exposure with frostbite-related facial changes
- **Visual mode:** Ultra-photorealistic RAW forensic extreme close-up

## Description of Resulting Image

An unforgiving close view of a man's eyes with ice-clumped lashes, tiny icicles at lash tips, swollen reddened lids, pale stiff surrounding skin with yellowish patches, irritated sclera, fatigued matte eyes, and crystals in the brows.

## Usage

For medical education, survival documentary work, and cold-exposure training or visual reference. Graphic-content warning: depicts distressing eye-area inflammation and frost-related skin changes.

## Clinical Visual Indicators

- Eyelashes clumped with ice and snow, with tiny icicles at the tips
- Swollen, reddened eyelids
- Pale, stiff surrounding skin with yellowish patches
- Irritated-looking sclera and matte, fatigued eyes
- Ice crystals held in eyebrow hairs

These visible features are representational and do not establish a medical diagnosis.

## Technical Direction

Use a RAW extreme close-up that includes both eyes, lids, lashes, brows, and adjacent skin. Maintain zero blur across the key eye structures and ice detail. Use flat, unforgiving cold blue daylight, forensic texture, controlled highlights, and no beauty or cinematic grade.

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-photorealistic RAW extreme close-up of a frost-exposed man's eyes. Eyelashes clumped with ice and snow, tiny icicles forming at the tips. Eyelids swollen and reddened, surrounding skin pale and stiff with yellowish frostbite patches. Sclera irritated, eyes matte and fatigued. Ice crystals visible in eyebrow hairs. Cold blue daylight, flat and unforgiving. Zero blur, forensic realism.
```

## Realism and Anatomy Lock

Show exactly two anatomically aligned eyes with coherent irises, pupils, sclera, upper and lower lids, lash lines, and two eyebrows. Keep ice attached to individual hairs at plausible scale, with no crystals crossing through the eyeballs. Preserve natural lid swelling and continuous surrounding skin.

## Negative Prompt

No beautification, cosmetics, glossy fashion eyes, CGI, illustration, extra eyes or pupils, mismatched gaze, malformed lids, duplicated brows or lashes, ice piercing the eye, fantasy icicles, irrelevant gore, blur, dramatic catchlights, logos, captions, text, or watermark.

## Safety and Interpretation Note

This is a fictional image prompt for educational and documentary reference, not diagnosis or treatment guidance. Real frostbite is a medical emergency requiring prompt professional care. Do not present a generated image as clinical evidence.
