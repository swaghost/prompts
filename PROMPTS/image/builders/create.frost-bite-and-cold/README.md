## Medical/Clinical Details

### Frostbite Stages Depicted

- **Superficial frostbite:** Pale yellow-white numb areas, waxy appearance
- **Deep frostbite:** Purple-red discoloration, darkened necrotic patches
- **Severe cold injury:** Blackened tissue, rigid skin, complete loss of circulation

### Color Indicators

- **Pale yellow-white:** Reduced blood flow, frozen tissue
- **Deep red/inflamed:** Early cold injury, inflammation
- **Purple zones:** Vascular damage, compromised circulation
- **Darkened/black areas:** Necrosis, late-stage tissue death

## Technical Specifications (All Prompts)

- **Format:** RAW documentary capture
- **Focus:** Critically sharp, zero blur, forensic detail
- **Lighting:** Cold natural daylight (winter, blue-toned, flat)
- **Style:** Clinical documentary realism, no artistic effects
