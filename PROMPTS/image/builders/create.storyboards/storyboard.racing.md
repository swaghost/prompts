# Vintage Racing Storyboard Collection

## Description

A comprehensive collection of 11 cinematic vintage racing prompts capturing the drama, speed, and authenticity of period motorsport. Each prompt includes precise camera specifications (ARRI Alexa cameras), lens details, color grading guidance, and technical execution notes for authentic film-based racing cinematography.

## Style

Vintage motorsport documentary realism with fine film grain, warm period-accurate color grading, and practical effects. No CGI overlays or stylized effects. Authentic racing atmosphere reminiscent of 1960s-1970s motorsport films.

## Technical Foundation

**Camera Systems:**

- ARRI Alexa 35
- ARRI Alexa Mini
- ARRI Alexa Mini LF
- ARRI Alexa 65

**Color Grading:**

- Warm restrained grades
- Period-accurate desaturation
- High-contrast warm tones
- Subdued color palettes
- Natural contrast

**Film Aesthetic:**

- Fine film grain throughout
- Authentic vintage look
- Practical lighting and effects
- No digital enhancement or CGI
- Documentary realism approach

## Usage Guidelines

**Storyboard Sequencing:**
These 11 prompts can be used sequentially to create a complete racing narrative arc:

1. Pre-race tension (Prompt 1)
2. Racing action begins (Prompts 2, 3, 6, 8)
3. Mechanical details and atmosphere (Prompts 4, 5, 7)
4. Incident occurs (Prompt 9)
5. Aftermath and rescue (Prompts 10, 11)

**Technical Consistency:**

- Maintain fine film grain across all shots
- Use warm color grades for period authenticity
- Employ practical effects only (no CGI)
- Shoot on ARRI Alexa camera systems
- Use vintage lenses where specified (Leica Summilux)
- Preserve natural lighting and atmospheric haze

**Cinematic Approach:**

- Documentary realism style
- Period-accurate 1960s-1970s motorsport aesthetic
- Authentic racing atmosphere
- Visceral, practical action
- Human moments and tension
- Mechanical beauty and detail
