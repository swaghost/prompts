## Concept

Extreme close-up ultra-photorealistic portrait shot like raw high-resolution iPhone photograph in bright natural daylight. Face fills almost entire vertical frame with direct eye contact, relaxed neutral expression. Priority is unfiltered skin realism: abundant natural freckles, visible pores, uneven texture, subtle pigmentation, tiny blemishes, mild redness, fine lines, peach fuzz, individual facial hairs. Natural variation across every area of face with absolutely no skin smoothing or beauty retouching.

**Quality:** Genuine photographic with micro-detail revealing pores, freckles, individual eyelashes and hair strands

## Full Prompt

Create an extreme close-up, ultra-photorealistic portrait of a young adult woman with fair warm-toned skin, long straight light-blonde hair, hazel-green eyes, light natural eyebrows, soft pink lips, and delicate facial features. Frame the face straight-on and centered, filling almost the entire vertical image. Direct eye contact with the camera, relaxed neutral expression, lips naturally closed. Hair falls loosely along both sides of the face. Small gold hoop earrings and delicate layered gold necklaces are subtly visible.

Skin realism is the priority: abundant natural freckles across the nose, cheeks and forehead, visible pores, uneven texture, subtle pigmentation, tiny blemishes, mild redness, fine lines, peach fuzz and individual facial hairs. Keep natural variation across every area of the face. Absolutely no skin smoothing or beauty retouching.

The eyes should have intricate iris fibers, natural color variation, visible moisture, subtle veins in the whites, irregular individual eyelashes and realistic reflections. Eyebrows contain individually visible hairs. Lips show fine vertical lines, slight dryness, natural color variation and subtle asymmetry.

Hair should show individual blonde strands, darker natural roots, baby hairs around the hairline, flyaways and realistic variation in thickness and color.

Shot like a raw high-resolution iPhone photograph in bright natural daylight, with warm sunlight falling slightly from one side. Realistic highlights and natural shine across the forehead, nose and cheeks. Warm neutral white balance, natural exposure, authentic dynamic range. Face remains highly detailed and sharp.

The result should feel unfiltered, imperfect and genuinely photographic, with enough micro-detail that zooming into the image reveals pores, freckles, peach fuzz, individual eyelashes and hair strands.

No beauty filter, airbrushing, waxy skin, plastic texture, flawless complexion, artificial freckles, excessive symmetry, glamour retouching, CGI look or overly cinematic lighting. No text or graphics.

## Pro Tips

**Freckle Authenticity:**
Use "abundant natural freckles" not just "freckles" — prevents artificial-looking uniform patterns.

**Pore Distribution:**
"Visible pores" + "natural variation across every area" ensures realistic distribution, not uniform texture overlay.

**Hair Realism:**
"Individual strands" + "darker natural roots" + "baby hairs" + "flyaways" = authentic hair rendering vs. painted-looking hair.

**Eye Detail Hierarchy:**
Iris fibers → color variation → moisture → veins → irregular lashes → reflections. Each element prevents AI from simplifying eyes into flat colored circles.

**Lighting Direction:**
"Warm sunlight falling slightly from one side" creates natural dimensionality without dramatic studio lighting feel.

**Expression Naturalness:**
"Relaxed neutral expression" prevents forced smile or overly posed look that breaks authentic iPhone selfie aesthetic.

**Negative Prompt Power:**
The negative prompt list is as important as the positive description — explicitly stating what NOT to do guides platform away from default beautification.
