# Uploaded Face — Ultra Realistic Cinematic Portrait

## Overview

Generate ultra-realistic 8K cinematic portraits using an uploaded reference face with exact facial feature preservation. No AI face generation or alteration—the uploaded face appears exactly as provided while you customize everything else: pose, styling, outfit, background, lighting, and camera settings. Perfect for personal branding, character consistency across content, portfolio work, and creating professional portraits with your exact likeness in any scenario.

## Core Concept

### Face Upload Technique

**How It Works:**

- Upload reference photo of person's face
- AI preserves exact facial features, skin tone, structure
- Customize: age range, body, pose, outfit, setting, lighting
- Face remains identical across all generated variations
- Consistent character for series, campaigns, portfolios

**Best For:**

- Personal branding and professional headshots
- Character consistency in storytelling/content series
- Testing outfits and styles on yourself before shoots
- Creating diverse portfolio without multiple photoshoots
- Brand ambassadors in various scenarios
- Age progression/regression while keeping face identical

---

### What Gets Preserved from Upload

**Exact Preservation:**

- Facial structure (bone structure, face shape)
- Eye color, shape, and spacing
- Nose shape and size
- Mouth shape and lips
- Skin tone and complexion
- Distinguishing features (moles, freckles, scars)
- Facial proportions

**What You Can Change:**

- Hair (color, length, style, texture)
- Age range (within reason)
- Expression and gaze direction
- Makeup and grooming
- Body type and build
- Pose and positioning
- Outfit and styling
- Background and setting
- Lighting and atmosphere

## Technical Specifications

### Face Upload Requirements

**Photo Quality:**

- High resolution: 1500px+ shortest dimension
- Sharp focus on face (not blurred)
- Good lighting (even, not too dark/bright)
- Face clearly visible (no sunglasses, heavy shadows)
- Neutral expression preferred (easier to adapt)
- Direct or three-quarter angle works best
- Avoid heavy filters or edits

**What Works Best:**

- Professional headshot
- Passport-style photo
- Clear selfie in good lighting
- Studio portrait photo
- Recent photo (if matching current age)

**What Doesn't Work Well:**

- Blurry phone photos
- Face partially obscured (hand, hair, sunglasses)
- Extreme angles (upward, downward)
- Very low resolution
- Heavy Instagram filters
- Group photos (faces too small)

## Best Practices

### ✅ Do:

1. **Upload high-quality face reference** — Sharp, well-lit, clear facial features
2. **Use recent photos if matching age** — Facial features change over time
3. **Specify "use uploaded face exactly, no face change"** — Critical instruction
4. **Describe everything except face** — Hair, outfit, pose, background fully detailed
5. **Test with simple prompts first** — Verify face preservation before complex scenes
6. **Save successful face references** — Reuse same upload for series consistency
7. **Specify age range appropriately** — Guides body proportions and context
8. **Request photorealistic quality** — "8K, ultra detailed, HDR, photorealistic"

---

### ❌ Don't:

1. **Use blurry face uploads** — Won't preserve features accurately
2. **Upload heavily filtered photos** — AI may struggle with artificial enhancements
3. **Expect perfect match every time** — May need multiple generations
4. **Forget "no face change" instruction** — AI might alter features without it
5. **Use group photos as reference** — Face too small, context confusing
6. **Upload photos with accessories covering face** — Sunglasses, masks reduce accuracy
7. **Skip age/ethnicity descriptors** — Helps AI with appropriate body proportions
8. **Overlook lighting direction** — Face shadows should match scene lighting

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Face doesn't match upload accurately

**Solution:**

- Ensure uploaded photo is high resolution and sharp
- Use photo with even lighting on face (no heavy shadows)
- Add stronger instruction: "use uploaded face EXACTLY, preserve all facial features, no face alteration or generation"
- Try different face reference photo (more neutral expression, direct angle)
- Specify ethnicity and age to guide AI
- Generate multiple variations and select best match

---

### Issue: Face looks artificially placed on body

**Solution:**

- Ensure lighting direction matches between face and body
- Request "natural integration, consistent skin tone from face to body"
- Add "seamless transition between face and neck, natural shadows"
- Specify "realistic connection, no visible seams"
- Use softer lighting (harsh light shows mismatches more)

---

### Issue: Face style doesn't match scene aesthetic

**Solution:**

- Request "skin texture and detail matching scene quality"
- Specify "face rendering style consistent with background photorealism"
- Add "cohesive image quality across face and environment"
- Match face photo style to desired output (candid upload for candid result)

---

### Issue: Age or body doesn't match face

**Solution:**

- Clearly specify age range: "age 25-30" or "teenager age 16"
- Describe body type if important: "athletic build" or "petite frame"
- Request "age-appropriate proportions and styling"
- Ensure outfit and styling match age (don't put teen in business suit)

---

### Issue: Inconsistent results across series

**Solution:**

- Use exact same uploaded face photo for entire series
- Keep core prompt structure identical, changing only variables
- Save working prompts and modify slightly for variations
- Generate multiple options and select those with best face match
- Consider creating face reference sheet showing acceptable variations
