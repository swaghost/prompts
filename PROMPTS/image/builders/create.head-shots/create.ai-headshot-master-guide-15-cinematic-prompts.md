# AI Headshot Master Guide: 15 Cinematic Prompts

## Description

A collection of 15 reference-image headshot prompts spanning professional profiles, cinematic environmental portraits, and stylized editorial lighting. Every prompt uses an uploaded selfie or portrait as the identity source and preserves the person's recognizable facial structure and natural features.

## Usage

Use these prompts for LinkedIn profiles, founder pages, company biographies, creator branding, editorial portraits, music artwork, social profiles, and cinematic campaigns. Upload one clear selfie or portrait, select a numbered style, paste its prompt, generate several variations, and crop the strongest result for the destination platform.

## Requirements

- ChatGPT or another image model that supports an uploaded reference image
- One clear, well-lit selfie or portrait with visible facial features
- One prompt from this guide
- Optional preferred aspect ratio when the prompt does not specify one

## Universal Identity Lock

Apply this requirement to every prompt in the guide:

```text
Use the uploaded photo as the identity reference. Preserve the person's exact recognizable identity, facial structure, facial proportions, ethnicity, gender presentation, age, eye shape, nose, mouth, skin tone, and distinguishing natural features. Do not beautify, reshape, de-age, or replace the face. Keep realistic skin texture, pores, and natural imperfections. The result must look photographed, not AI-generated.
```

# Part 1 - Professional Headshots

## 01 - Founder Headshot

**Best for:** LinkedIn, founder profiles, personal branding, and websites.

```text
Transform the uploaded photo into a professional, hyper-realistic headshot. Keep the person's face, identity, facial structure, natural features, age, expression, and overall appearance accurate to the uploaded image.

Style the final image like a high-end personal-brand, LinkedIn, or founder headshot: tight portrait framing from upper chest to just above the head, subject centered and facing the camera, natural relaxed expression with a slight confident smile, clean polished skin while keeping realistic texture, pores, and natural imperfections, sharp focus on the eyes, soft flattering lighting from the front-left, subtle catchlights, realistic depth of field, smooth blurred background with warm indoor tones, 85mm portrait-lens look at a wide aperture, natural color grading with warm highlights and soft contrast, and a clean modern premium editorial feel.

Clothing: simple, modern, professional dark minimal top such as a black sweater.

Background: softly blurred modern office, studio, or upscale interior in neutral dark and warm tones, no logos or text.

Preserve the person's real identity and facial proportions. Do not make it look AI-generated. No plastic skin and no changes to ethnicity, gender, age, facial structure, or eye shape. The output should look like a real professional headshot.
```

## 02 - Corporate Headshot

**Best for:** Corporate profiles and professional avatars.

Create a polished, credible business-profile image with sharp eyes, balanced light, natural color, clean framing, and no text or logos. The result must look like a genuine professional photograph, not an AI-generated face.

## 03 - Executive Portrait

**Best for:** Executives, founders, consultants, and professional branding.

```text
Create a professional, high-resolution profile photo while maintaining the exact facial structure, identity, and key features of the person in the input image.

Frame the subject from the chest up with ample headroom and negative space above the head, ensuring the top of the head is not cropped. The person looks directly at the camera with a confident, authoritative expression, and the body is positioned at a slight three-quarter angle to the camera.

Style the subject for a professional photo-studio shoot, wearing a premium navy business suit with a crisp white dress shirt and understated tie. Use a solid #141414 neutral studio background.

Shoot from a subtly elevated angle with bright, airy, soft diffused studio lighting that gently illuminates the face and creates a subtle catchlight in the eyes, conveying authority and leadership. Use an 85mm f/1.8 lens look with shallow depth of field, exquisite focus on the eyes, and beautiful soft bokeh.

Show crisp detail in the suit fabric, individual strands of hair, and natural realistic skin texture. The atmosphere exudes confidence, professionalism, and executive presence. Use clean and bright cinematic color grading with subtle warmth and balanced tones for a polished contemporary feel.
```

## 06 - Black-and-White Close-Up

**Best for:** Fashion editorials, album artwork, and dramatic profile images.

```text

Use a deep black shadow background so only the face is visible and ultra-sharp. High contrast, realistic skin texture, crisp eyes, dramatic falloff, portrait-oriented 4:3 composition, shallow processing-depth effect. No beauty retouching, plastic skin, facial alteration, text, or watermark.
```

## 07 - Cinematic Metro Portrait

**Best for:** Urban campaigns and cinematic social profiles.

```text

Everyone else is motion-blurred with natural trailing effects while I remain in sharp focus with a serious expression, wearing a long trench coat. Cool blue tones dominate the scene with a 35mm film aesthetic. Ambient lighting comes from train signs and station fixtures. Preserve realistic anatomy, movement, skin texture, and environmental reflections. Portrait-oriented 4:3 composition. No facial alteration, text, logos, duplicated people, or artificial-looking motion.

## 08 - Night Car Portrait

**Best for:** Fashion, lifestyle, automotive, and cinematic portraits.

```text
Use my uploaded selfie as the exact identity reference. Create a moody black-and-white night portrait of me sitting inside a modern black car with the door open. Preserve my actual skin tone, hair, facial features, age, and gender presentation from the reference; do not invent or replace identity details. My expression is serious and intense. I wear a black puffer jacket and light-gray sweatpants.

The overall mood is cinematic, stylish, and introspective, with realistic automotive surfaces, natural reflections, deep blacks, controlled highlights, and authentic skin texture. No glasses unless present in the source image. No text, watermark, facial reshaping, or distorted car geometry.

## 09 - Editorial Business Portrait

**Best for:** Magazine covers, fashion editorials, press images, and premium personal branding.

```text

I am wearing a tailored business suit. Use dramatic studio-quality lighting with strong contrast and shadows that sculpt the features. Use a clean, blurred studio-style background with soft gradients or subtle texture to keep the focus on the subject.

Frame the portrait vertically in 9:16 format with a centered composition, ultra-realistic skin texture, and dimensional depth. No digital makeup or facial alterations. The result should look timeless, editorial, and emotionally powerful, suitable for a gallery or fashion magazine.
```

# How to Use the Prompts

1. Take or select a clear selfie.
2. Open ChatGPT or another reference-image-capable generator.
3. Upload the selfie.
4. Choose the visual style you want.
5. Paste the corresponding prompt.
6. Generate multiple versions.
7. Pick the strongest result.
8. Edit or crop it for the platform you need.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The generated face remains recognizably identical to the uploaded subject.

- [ ] Skin retains pores, texture, and natural imperfections.
- [ ] Eyes are sharp and catchlights follow the stated lighting.
