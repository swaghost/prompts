# Hair Lookbook Builder Prompts

Two comprehensive prompts for creating 12-panel hairstyle lookbooks that maintain perfect facial consistency while showcasing different hairstyles on the same person.

---

## Available Prompts

1. **[Women's Hairstyle Lookbook](women-hairstyle-lookbook-12-panel.md)** - 12 different women's hairstyles from pixie cuts to Hollywood waves
2. **[Men's Hairstyle Lookbook](men-hairstyle-lookbook-12-panel.md)** - 12 different men's hairstyles from buzz cuts to long flowing hair

---

## Key Features

- **Perfect Identity Consistency:** Same face, same person across all 12 panels
- **Hair Only Changes:** Only hairstyle shape, length, and style vary - color stays identical
- **Studio Setup:** Plain light grey background, professional portrait lighting
- **Accessories Preserved:** All jewelry, glasses, clothing remain identical
- **Numbered Panels:** 3×4 grid clearly numbered 1-12
- **Photorealistic:** High-detail, natural skin texture, visible pores

---

## Usage

1. Upload your reference image
2. Copy the full prompt (women's or men's version)
3. Paste into your AI image generator
4. The result will be a 4:5 aspect ratio grid with 12 hairstyle variations

**Note:** These prompts emphasize NO sunglasses, NO environmental backgrounds, and strict preservation of facial features and accessories.
