# Muscle Definition Enhancement

## Description of Resulting Image

A photorealistic body-enhancement edit that makes the subject appear stronger, more muscular, and more defined while preserving the original identity, pose, camera angle, lighting, background, clothing, and overall composition. The result should look like a believable photographic enhancement rather than a reshaped or regenerated person.

## Usage

Use this enhancer for fitness portraits, athletic progress visuals, personal-brand imagery, training content, and body-composition concept studies. Apply it when the subject's pose and scene already work and only a restrained, anatomically plausible increase in muscle definition and lower apparent body-fat level is needed.

## Actor or Subject

The person in the uploaded reference image. The source image is the authority for identity, face, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions, pose, clothing, background, and camera composition.

## Prerequisites

- High-quality source image with enough of the body visible to support the requested edit
- Clear facial features and natural skin texture
- Pose and camera angle that do not require reconstruction
- Reference image must be used as the identity and composition lock

## Included Effects

### Body Enhancement

- Larger, more visible, and more defined muscles
- Stronger overall athletic appearance
- Slightly narrower waist only where anatomically plausible
- Lower apparent body-fat level without changing identity or natural proportions
- Realistic muscle separation, shadows, and skin response

### Preservation

- Exact face, facial features, identity, apparent age, and skin tone
- Exact pose, gesture, movement position, and camera angle
- Exact clothing, background, environment, lighting direction, and composition
- Natural skin texture and realistic fabric interaction

### Style Filters

- Photorealistic enhancement
- Natural, believable fitness progress aesthetic
- No stylized body sculpting or artificial beauty retouching
