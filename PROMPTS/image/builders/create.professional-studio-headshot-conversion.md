# Professional Studio Headshot Conversion

## Description of Resulting Image

A clean professional studio headshot with tight head and shoulders framing, centered composition, neutral confident expression with eyes directed toward camera. Professional talent headshot aesthetic suitable for casting agencies and magazine profiles.

## Usage

Use this prompt to convert casual photos into professional studio headshots while preserving the subject's exact clothing, hairstyle, and accessories. Ideal for actors, models, and professionals needing consistent headshot aesthetics. Perfect for transforming informal portraits into industry-standard talent photos.

## Actor or Subject
