# Influencer Prompt: Yacht Lifestyle Luxury Portrait

## Demographics

- **Gender**: Female
- **Ethnicity**: European (Caucasian) Brunette
- **Age**: 19-21 years old
- **Style**: Relaxed luxury boat-day mood, modern smartphone vacation aesthetic

## Description

A casual close-range yacht lifestyle photograph of a young European brunette woman reclining on a boat deck during bright daytime. Features glittery red triangle bikini, maritime setting with ocean water and coastal hills. Captures chic glamorous sun-drenched vacation mood with polished social-media aesthetic.

## Complete Prompt

A casual, close-range yacht lifestyle photograph features a 19 to 21-year-old European brunette woman with soft, delicate, clearly feminine facial features and no masculine traits, reclining on the deck of a boat during bright daytime. She has smooth fair-to-light sun-kissed skin with a glossy summer glow, full lips, gentle cheekbones, a refined nose, well-groomed eyebrows, balanced facial proportions, and a naturally feminine, model-like appearance. Her long dark brown hair is loose, slightly messy, and naturally tousled, falling over her shoulder with a relaxed beachy texture.

She is wearing a glittery red triangle bikini with pale blue trim, fitted over a noticeably full bust / large breasts, paired with matching bikini bottoms tied at the hips. She accessorizes with delicate gold necklaces and subtle jewelry. Her pose is relaxed and confident, lying sideways on the bright white yacht deck with her body angled toward the camera, looking off to the side with a calm, sunlit expression.

The setting includes a white yacht deck, a red maritime flag with a union jack detail, bright blue ocean water, distant boats, a large white yacht in the background, and soft green coastal hills under a pale sky. The lighting is strong direct natural daylight, creating bright highlights on her skin and swimwear, mild overexposure on the white deck, and a slightly washed, summery smartphone-photo look. The overall color palette combines white yacht surfaces, deep ocean blue, red swimwear and flag tones, pale blue bikini accents, warm fair skin tones, gold jewelry, and soft coastal greens. The image should feel like a modern smartphone vacation photo with realistic proportions, natural skin texture, crisp detail, slight wide-angle perspective, and a relaxed luxury boat-day mood.
