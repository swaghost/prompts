# Influencer Prompt: Male Gym Fitness Selfie

## Demographics

- **Gender**: Male
- **Ethnicity**: Young Caucasian Man
- **Age**: Mid to late 20s (approximately 25-29)
- **Style**: Focus, determination, contemporary health-conscious lifestyle

## Description

A close-up selfie photograph captured at eye level of a young Caucasian man in a gym environment. Features strikingly symmetrical model-like facial features, large glossy black over-ear headphones, and tight-fitting black athletic t-shirt. Gym equipment visible in blurred background. Captures focus, determination, and contemporary fitness lifestyle aesthetic.

## Complete Prompt

A close-up selfie photograph, captured at eye level, shows a young Caucasian man in his mid to late 20s with strikingly symmetrical, model-like facial features. He has dark wavy hair, a strong jawline, and is clean-shaven. He is wearing large, glossy black over-ear headphones and a tight-fitting black athletic t-shirt which accentuates his muscular build. His skin has a light sheen of perspiration, indicating recent physical exertion. The depth of field is shallow, with the man in crisp focus while the gym environment in the background—corrugated metal ceiling, visible fluorescent tube lights, cinder block walls, and partial view of gym equipment—appears tastefully blurred. The color palette consists mainly of cool whites, muted grays, and deep blacks. The lighting is a combination of even ambient natural light from windows and overhead fluorescent fixtures, with soft highlights and diffuse shadows enhancing the contours of his face. The photo shows minimal grain and reveals the smooth digital sharpness typical of a high-quality smartphone selfie. The overall mood conveys focus, determination, and a contemporary, health-conscious lifestyle.

## Pro Tips

- Light sheen of perspiration should indicate recent exertion, not excessive sweat
- Large glossy black headphones should have realistic shine and cushioning
- Tight-fitting black t-shirt should accentuate muscular build naturally
- Dark wavy hair should have natural texture and movement
- Strong jawline should be emphasized by lighting and angle
- Gym equipment in background should be recognizable but blurred
- Corrugated metal ceiling and fluorescent lights establish gym setting authentically
- Cinder block walls add industrial gym aesthetic
- Combination of natural window light and fluorescent overhead creates even lighting
- Shallow depth of field keeps focus on subject while establishing context
- Minimal grain maintains professional quality
- Clean-shaven face should appear naturally smooth, not artificially perfect
- Symmetrical facial features should be highlighted by straight-on eye-level angle
- Overall mood should convey determination and focus without appearing overly intense
