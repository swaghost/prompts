# Influencer Prompt: Car Interior Casual Fashion

## Demographics

- **Gender**: Female
- **Ethnicity**: Mixed/Medium Skin Tone
- **Age**: Young woman (approximately 20-25)
- **Style**: Spontaneous, bold, fashion-forward, urban casual

## Description

A slightly low angle candid medium shot of a young woman with medium skin tone inside a car, featuring bold streetwear fashion with glossy colorblock windbreaker. Indoor parking garage setting with modern urban aesthetic. Captures spontaneous, confident energy with fashion-forward styling.

## Complete Prompt

A slightly low angle, candid medium shot features a young woman with medium skin tone and straight dark hair in an indoor parking garage, positioned inside the front passenger seat of a car. She faces diagonally away from the camera, glancing back over her shoulder with a strong, model-like gaze highlighted by glowing skin and delicate makeup. She wears a glossy, slightly oversized black and white colorblock windbreaker with a high sheen, with the word "FINEST" in large blue and white letters visible on her back and the edge of another word "USE" on her left sleeve. Her mid-rise dark blue denim jeans have a tailored fit and feature a zipper detail on the back, partially revealing the waistband of pink underwear beneath. The car interior is sleek and modern, with a leather seat and segmented design, while the partially open passenger door and window frame her. The garage background is utilitarian, featuring exposed concrete, pipes, and a white parked car outside the window adding to the atmosphere. The image is shot with direct on-camera flash, creating hard shadows and pronounced specular highlights across her skin, jacket, and car surfaces, emphasizing smooth and shiny textures. The color palette centers on blacks, grays, muted blues, and hints of white and red. Captured digitally with high clarity, prominent noise reduction, and shadow detail, the aesthetic is spontaneous, bold, and fashion-forward.

## Pro Tips

- Direct flash should create hard shadows and specular highlights (authentic smartphone aesthetic)
- Windbreaker material must have realistic high sheen/glossy finish
- "FINEST" text should be clearly visible and properly positioned
- Over-the-shoulder gaze should feel natural and confident, not forced
- Car interior should appear modern and sleek without overwhelming subject
- Parking garage setting should feel utilitarian and authentic
- Balance between fashion styling and spontaneous candid energy
- Skin should have natural glow from flash, not artificial smoothing
- Denim fit should be tailored and modern, emphasizing silhouette
