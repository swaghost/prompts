# Influencer Prompt: Poolside Lifestyle Portrait

## Demographics

- **Gender**: Female
- **Ethnicity**: European (Caucasian) Brunette
- **Age**: 19-21 years old
- **Style**: Chic glamorous vacation, polished social-media aesthetic

## Description

A casual close-range lifestyle photograph of a young European brunette woman posed outdoors by a swimming pool during bright daytime. Features luxurious outdoor atmosphere with turquoise pool, black triangle bikini, and gold-framed sunglasses. Sophisticated yet relaxed vacation mood with polished influencer aesthetic.

## Complete Prompt

A casual, close-range lifestyle photograph features a 19 to 21-year-old European brunette woman with soft, clearly feminine facial features and no masculine traits, posed outdoors by a swimming pool during bright daytime. She has smooth, lightly tanned skin with a radiant sunlit glow, full lips, refined cheekbones, well-groomed eyebrows, and a naturally elegant, model-like appearance. Her medium to dark brown hair appears slightly damp and is loosely parted, framing her face in a relaxed, natural way. She wears slim black oval sunglasses with gold frames, positioned low on her nose so her eyes remain partially visible, enhancing her confident, sultry expression. She is resting her face lightly against one hand in a relaxed pose, looking directly toward the camera. She is wearing a black triangle bikini top with thin straps, fitted over a noticeably full bust / large breasts, revealing her shoulders, collarbones, and upper chest, along with a delicate gold necklace featuring a round medallion pendant. The setting includes bright turquoise pool water, lush green trees, and a vivid clear blue sky in the background, creating a luxurious and summery outdoor atmosphere. The lighting is strong direct natural sunlight, producing crisp shadows, glossy highlights on her skin, and sharp detail throughout the image. The overall color palette combines warm skin tones, black swimwear and sunglasses, bright aqua pool tones, deep green foliage, and the vivid blue sky, creating a chic, glamorous, sun-drenched vacation mood with a polished social-media aesthetic.

## Pro Tips

- Sunglasses should sit low on nose to show eyes (creates engagement)
- Ensure skin has natural sunlit glow, not artificial enhancement
- Pool water should be bright turquoise, not dull or murky
- Balance crisp shadows with glossy highlights for dimension
- Hair should appear naturally damp from pool, not wet or dripping
- Maintain elegant model-like appearance while keeping casual, relaxed pose
- Gold accessories should complement warm skin tones
