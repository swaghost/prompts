# Man and Dog Anatomical Cyanotype

Vintage cyanotype double-exposure portrait of a young Black man with rounded afro sitting on a brick wall with a large mixed-breed dog between his knees. He cradles the dog's head and smiles warmly. Semitransparent X-ray overlays reveal the aligned human skull, hands, spine, ribs and limbs alongside the dog's skull, vertebrae, ribs and front legs. Overcast outdoor light, denim jacket over collared shirt, textured brick and foliage, paper grain, edge staining, Prussian blue, navy, steel blue, cream highlights. Heartwarming antique alternative photography, 3:4.

Negative: digital camera look, color photography, plastic skin, clean digital border, modern digital illustration, anatomy errors.
