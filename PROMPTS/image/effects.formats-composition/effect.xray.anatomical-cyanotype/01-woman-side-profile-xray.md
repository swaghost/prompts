# Woman Side Profile Anatomical Cyanotype

Vintage cyanotype photograph of a slender woman in sharp side profile, head tilted slightly upward, calm introspective expression, dark hair in a messy bun with loose wisps. A semitransparent X-ray double exposure aligns the skull, cervical spine, collarbone, and rib cage with her face, neck, and torso. Dark sleeveless top, deep Prussian-blue background, hand-coated watercolor paper, chemical vignetting, stains, paper grain, soft haloing, historical large-format feel. Monochrome indigo, cobalt, navy, steel blue, off-white highlights, no digital sharpness, 3:4.

Negative: bright colors, modern clothing, digital art, cartoon, rendering artifacts, extra limbs, plastic skin.
