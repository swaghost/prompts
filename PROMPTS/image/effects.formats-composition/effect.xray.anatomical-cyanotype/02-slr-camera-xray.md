# Woman with SLR Anatomical Cyanotype

Authentic hand-made cyanotype of a woman holding a vintage 35mm SLR camera to her eye, medium close-up three-quarter profile. Semitransparent X-ray overlay reveals the skull, cervical spine, shoulders, arms, and hands gripping the camera, precisely aligned with her windblown hair and textured cable-knit sweater. Soft outdoor trees and grass in cyanotype wash, diffused daylight, paper tooth, natural edge vignetting, analog grain, Prussian blue and off-white highlights. Retro meta-photographic alternative process, 3:4.

Negative: modern clothing, digital camera, neon colors, smooth plastic skin, 3D rendering, extra fingers.
