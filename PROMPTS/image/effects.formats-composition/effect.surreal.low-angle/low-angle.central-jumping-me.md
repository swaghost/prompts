# Mid-Air Jump Fisheye - Circular Courtyard Frame

## Description of Resulting Image

An ultra-realistic DSLR editorial photograph capturing the subject mid-air while jumping directly over a camera placed on the ground, using an extreme ultra-wide 8mm fisheye lens pointed straight upward. The exaggerated perspective makes hands and shoes appear larger than the face while maintaining realistic proportions. The background features a circular courtyard surrounded by old European-style brick buildings forming a ring that curves inward to create a circular architectural tunnel framing the bright deep blue sky.

## Usage

Use this prompt when creating dynamic action photography with extreme fisheye perspectives for social media, travel content, or creative portfolio pieces. This effect works exceptionally well for architectural photography combined with human subjects, creating immersive circular compositions that showcase both athletic energy and environmental grandeur. The circular building frame adds unique visual interest and architectural context.
