## Image Prompt

### Subject and Prerequisites

A striking typographic portrait of a young woman with a thoughtful, three-quarters gaze, completely rendered using a text-mosaic and word-cloud effect. Her skin, hair, and clothing are entirely formed by varied typographic fonts, headlines, and individual words of different sizes, orientations, and weights (such as "ENERGY", "BILLS", "MONEY", "SCHOOL", "SUCCESS"). Bold, prominent serif and sans-serif typography defines the heavy contours of her neck, shoulder, and jawline, while finer delicate cursive and micro-text fill in subtle highlights and midtones of her facial features.

Her lips are a distinct vibrant red, also composed of textured typographic text. The edges of her hair and torso gently dissolve into a chaotic fragmented spray of letters and ink splatters. Set the entire subject against a clean stark white background layered with faint horizontal traces of vintage handwritten script and blurred newspaper print, creating a layered multimedia collage effect.

High contrast, sharp detail, artistic graphic design style. Aspect ratio 9:16.
