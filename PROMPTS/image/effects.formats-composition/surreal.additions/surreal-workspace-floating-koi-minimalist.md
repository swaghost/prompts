## Prompt Description

An ultra-realistic surreal scene combining minimalist modern workspace with impossible floating koi fish. A person sits calmly at a simple black table working on a laptop in a pristine white room with polished concrete floor, while multiple large, photorealistic koi and goldfish float naturally through the air as if swimming in invisible water. The koi are mixed orange, white, and orange-white varieties at different depths and distances—some close to camera for foreground depth, others near ceiling and walls. Soft natural window light creates gentle shadows and a calm, surreal atmosphere. Perfect for conceptual advertising, meditation/mindfulness content, creative portfolio work, surreal editorial features, or brand campaigns emphasizing calm focus and creative flow.

**Key Elements**: Surreal magical realism, floating koi fish in air, identity preservation, minimalist white room, photorealistic cinematic composition, wide-angle view, clean aesthetic

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Koi Fish Look Cartoonish or Illustrated

**Solution**: Add "ultra-photorealistic koi fish, realistic fish scales, wet shimmering appearance, documentary photography quality" and avoid terms like "artistic" or "stylized"

### Issue: Person Looks Composited or Fake

**Solution**: Emphasize "naturally integrated into scene, cohesive lighting across person and environment, matching shadows and highlights"

### Issue: Fish Positioned Incorrectly (All Same Depth)

**Solution**: Explicitly describe depth layers: "some fish close to camera in foreground, some at person's depth in mid-ground, some near ceiling/walls in background"

### Issue: Room Looks Cluttered or Wrong Style

**Solution**: Specify "minimalist, clean, empty room with only black table, laptop, and mug visible, white walls, concrete floor, no other objects"

### Issue: Video Animates Person or Environment

**Solution**: In Seedance prompt, use stronger language: "The person MUST remain completely frozen like a photograph. Do not animate face, eyes, hands, body, or any clothing. Only fish move."

### Issue: Fish Motion Looks Unnatural in Video

**Solution**: Describe motion more specifically: "Slow, smooth, graceful gliding like swimming underwater but in air, gentle fin movements, curved flight paths, no jerky or fast motion"

### Issue: Lighting Looks Wrong or Too Dark

**Solution**: Specify "soft natural window light, bright clean aesthetic, gentle shadows, high-key lighting, not dark or moody"
