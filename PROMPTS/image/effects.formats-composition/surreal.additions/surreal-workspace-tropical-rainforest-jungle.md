## Prompt Description

An ultra-realistic surreal scene placing a professional workspace impossibly inside a lush tropical rainforest. The uploaded person sits at a simple wooden table working on a dark laptop, wearing a plain white t-shirt and beige pants, but the "room" is actually a dense jungle environment. The scene is framed as a wide cinematic shot from slightly farther distance, showing more of the surrounding rainforest. Dense green foliage, towering trees, tangled vines, ferns, and warm golden sun rays filter through the canopy with soft volumetric lighting. Several vibrant scarlet macaws fly naturally through the jungle—one bird passes closer across the foreground, while others circle between the trees. A small monkey climbs a tree branch on the left side. Additional jungle creatures like toucans, squirrels, or iguanas are subtly placed in the background among the trees and foliage. Orange heliconia flowers and bright pink tropical flowers bloom in the foreground around the table. A few leaves drift gently through the air, creating a peaceful, immersive, ultra-photorealistic atmosphere with natural depth of field and realistic colors. Perfect for environmental campaigns, adventure brand marketing, eco-tourism content, creative agency portfolios, or conceptual editorial features about nature connection and remote work freedom.

**Key Elements**: Surreal jungle workspace, identity preservation, vibrant scarlet macaws in flight, climbing monkey, tropical flowers, dappled sunlight, photorealistic wildlife, cinematic wide composition, peaceful immersive atmosphere
