# Giant Reaching Down - Extreme Worm's Eye Perspective

## Description of Resulting Image

A cinematic DSLR photograph captured from an extreme worm's-eye view with the camera placed on the ground pointing upward. The subject leans over the lens as if picking it up, with both arms stretching toward the camera from opposite directions creating dramatic forced perspective with oversized hands slightly out of focus. The face stays centered between the hands with a soft, natural, relaxed expression and gentle eye contact, creating an immersive first-person viewpoint.

## Usage

Use this prompt when creating dramatic perspective photography with forced perspective effects for social media, creative portraits, or experimental photography projects. This effect delivers powerful visual impact through extreme camera angles and works exceptionally well for content that needs to stand out with unique perspectives. The reaching hands create an immersive, interactive quality that draws viewers into the image.
