# Fisheye Street Perspective - Stepping Over Camera

## Description of Resulting Image

An ultra-realistic DSLR fisheye street photograph captured from an extreme worm's-eye view using an 8mm circular fisheye lens. The camera is placed directly on the ground with one foot stepping over the lens, the sneaker sole filling nearly half the frame in sharp detail. The person's hand reaches toward the lens creating dramatic perspective distortion, set against the iconic Piazza del Duomo in Milan with the Gothic cathedral and historic European buildings curving around the fisheye frame.

## Usage

Use this prompt when creating dramatic urban street photography with extreme fisheye distortion for travel content, social media posts, or creative portfolio pieces. This effect works exceptionally well for landmark documentation with unique perspectives, creating immersive wide-angle compositions that showcase both the subject and architectural surroundings. The stepping-over composition adds dynamic energy and scale to destination photography.
