# Calendar Breakthrough: July to August 2026 Transition

Create a hyper-realistic 3D cinematic scene of a woman (use the uploaded photo as reference) dramatically breaking through a giant futuristic wall calendar.

## Core Concept

The calendar is divided between July 2026 and August 2026, with the person aging younger and emerging into August.

Torn paper flies in all directions, symbolizing leaving the old month behind and entering a new beginning.

## Narrative Concept

This scene captures the powerful metaphor of breaking through time barriers, leaving the past behind, and emerging into a brighter future filled with new opportunities. The person literally breaks through the calendar wall, tearing away the old month and stepping into a glowing, premium-designed future month that represents fresh starts, new goals, and limitless possibilities.

The portal and futuristic elements emphasize that this isn't just a calendar change—it's a transformation, a leap forward, a breakthrough moment where the person takes control of their time and destiny.
