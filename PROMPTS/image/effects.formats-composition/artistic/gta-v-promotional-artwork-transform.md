## Image Prompt

### Subject and Prerequisites

Using the uploaded image as the only reference, preserve the exact person's identity, facial features, hairstyle, expression, body proportions, outfit, accessories, pose, camera angle, perspective, framing, lighting, and background composition.

Transform the entire photograph into the iconic Grand Theft Auto V promotional artwork style, as seen in Rockstar Games official loading screens and marketing illustrations.

Keep the composition identical while converting every element into polished premium digital illustration with:
- clean confident black linework
- smooth cel shading
- subtle painterly gradients
- rich cinematic color grading

The subject must remain instantly recognizable, with slightly stylized yet realistic facial proportions, expressive eyes, clean facial contours, and simplified hair rendered in smooth illustrated shapes.

Recreate the environment in the same GTA V aesthetic, including architecture, vehicles, streets, palm trees, water, buildings, and city details while preserving original perspective and layout.

Apply warm cinematic lighting, realistic reflections, soft ambient occlusion, balanced contrast, and vibrant but natural color.

Final output should look like an official GTA V promotional loading-screen illustration: highly polished, premium quality, bold, cinematic, and professional, while remaining faithful to the original subject and scene.
