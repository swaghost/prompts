# Eye Surveillance - Hyper Close-up with Targeting Reticle

## Description of Resulting Image

A hyper-close-up of a human eye with the pupil reflecting a flickering screen of code or scrolling data. Superimposed over the eye is a red, minimalist targeting reticle (crosshairs). The overall image is dark and desaturated, with the red reticle as the only color element, creating a powerful cyber-surveillance or technological dystopia aesthetic.

## Usage

Perfect for creating cybersecurity visuals, surveillance-themed artwork, tech thriller graphics, or dystopian conceptual photography. Ideal for book covers, album art, tech company presentations, privacy awareness campaigns, hacker culture content, and social media posts exploring themes of digital monitoring. This prompt excels at creating tension between human vulnerability and technological surveillance.

## Actor or Subject

A human eye in extreme close-up, with the pupil acting as a reflective surface showing code or scrolling data. The eye serves as both subject and screen, merging human and digital elements.

## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
A hyper-close-up of a human eye. The pupil reflects a flickering screen of code or scrolling data. Superimposed over the eye is a red, minimalist targeting reticle (crosshairs).

**Setting/Environment:**
Dark, minimal environment. Focus entirely on the eye. The pupil acts as a screen showing digital code or data scrolling. Red targeting crosshairs overlay the eye surface.

**Lighting:**
Dark, moody low-key lighting. Minimal illumination on the eye. Screen glow reflected in pupil. Dramatic shadows. Subtle rim light defining iris edge. Overall dark desaturated atmosphere.

**Composition:**
Extreme close-up macro of eye filling the frame. Iris texture highly detailed and sharp. Pupil reflecting code/data screen. Red minimalist targeting reticle (crosshairs) precisely superimposed over eye. Centered composition with eye as dominant element.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Cyberpunk surveillance aesthetic. Tech thriller visual style. Dystopian technological mood. Dark and desaturated color palette with red reticle as only color. Cold, clinical, surveillance-focused atmosphere. Digital monitoring theme.

**Technical Specifications:**
Macro photography, extreme close-up lens, sharp focus on iris texture, high-resolution detail capture. Code/data reflection composited into pupil. Red targeting reticle overlay. Heavy desaturation processing except for red element.

**Color Palette:**
Heavily desaturated overall image. Grayscale to near-monochrome on eye. Dark blacks and muted grays. Red targeting reticle as the ONLY color element. Screen reflection showing blue-tinted code data. High contrast dark-to-light ratio.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Surveillance and monitoring. Technological control. Dystopian future. Privacy invasion. Digital tracking. Cold and clinical. Tension and vulnerability. Cyber-threat atmosphere. Orwellian watching. Hacker culture aesthetic.

**Quality Keywords:**
Hyper-detailed, macro photography, 8K resolution, ultra-sharp iris texture, photorealistic, professional photography, high contrast, cinematic quality, precise overlay, tech thriller grade

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Blurry, soft focus, low quality, colorful (except red reticle), multiple colors, bright lighting, cheerful mood, low contrast, unrealistic eye anatomy, distorted pupil, cartoonish reticle, heavy-handed effects, over-processed, artificial eye, dilated pupil without reflection, poor macro detail, extra reticles, cluttered overlay
