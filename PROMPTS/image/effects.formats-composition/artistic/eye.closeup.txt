## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
Human eye in extreme close-up showing detailed iris texture and pupil. Pupil reflects flickering screen of code or scrolling data - green or white digital text flowing like Matrix-style display. Eye surface shows natural moisture and fine detail. Realistic human eye anatomy with technical digital elements in reflection.

**Setting/Environment:**
Dark minimalist environment with almost no visible context beyond the eye itself. Clinical dystopian atmosphere. No background distractions - pure black or very dark tones surrounding the eye. Focus entirely on eye and overlay elements.

**Lighting:**
Dark low-key lighting creating dramatic mood. Subtle catchlight in eye maintaining natural appearance while showing code reflection in pupil. Minimal ambient light - most of frame in shadow. Eye surface lit just enough to show iris detail and pupil reflection clearly.

**Composition:**
Eye centered and filling frame in extreme close-up. Red minimalist targeting reticle (crosshairs) superimposed over eye - clean geometric lines creating targeting/tracking interface element. Pupil positioned at reticle center. Code reflection visible within pupil area. Dark desaturated surroundings creating stark contrast with red reticle.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Hyper-close-up macro photography with dystopian tech surveillance aesthetic. Overall image heavily desaturated - almost black and white - with red targeting reticle as only color element. Code reflection in pupil adds cyberpunk digital layer. Minimalist composition emphasizing surveillance and monitoring themes. Dark tense atmosphere suggesting tracking, targeting, or digital control. Clinical precision combined with dystopian messaging.

**Technical Specifications:**
Macro lens, extreme close-up distance, desaturated color grading (except red), code/data reflection layer in pupil, red targeting reticle graphic overlay, dark low-key lighting, high contrast processing, 4K-8K resolution for iris detail, professional macro photography standards
