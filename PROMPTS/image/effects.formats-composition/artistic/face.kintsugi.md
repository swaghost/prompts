## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
A figure made of cracked kintsugi porcelain (white ceramic with golden seam repairs running through cracks), hands bound by shimmering ethereal ribbons, a gag of pure silence covering their mouth.

**Setting/Environment:**
Dark, claustrophobic environment. Deep shadows surrounding the figure. Minimal background detail to emphasize isolation and constraint. Mystical ethereal atmosphere created by the shimmering ribbons.

**Lighting:**
Dramatic tenebrism with extreme contrast between light and shadow. Strong directional light highlighting the porcelain figure and golden kintsugi seams. Deep, rich shadows creating claustrophobic atmosphere. Golden glow emanating from the repair seams. Spotlight effect focusing on the bound figure.

**Composition:**
Central figure made of cracked porcelain with visible golden kintsugi repairs. Hands bound together by ethereal, shimmering ribbons (not rope or physical material, but magical-looking translucent ribbons). Gag of silence covering mouth - representing inability to speak. Figure positioned against dark background. Negative space emphasizing isolation.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Surrealist masterpiece. Symbolic imagery exploring constraint, fragility, beauty in brokenness, and silenced voice. Tenebrism painting technique applied to 3D render. Fine art gallery quality. Philosophical and emotional depth. Kintsugi aesthetic representing healing and imperfection made beautiful.

Hyper-detailed octane render. 8K resolution. Photorealistic porcelain texture with visible cracks and golden seam repairs. Ethereal ribbon shader with shimmer and translucency. Dramatic lighting setup. High-contrast rendering. Museum-quality output.
