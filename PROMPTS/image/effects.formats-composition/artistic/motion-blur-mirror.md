# Motion Blur Mirror - Double Exposure Ghost Effect

## Description of Resulting Image

A cinematic studio portrait featuring a 3/4 view against a dark seamless background. The subject wears a black blazer and white t-shirt, captured with an 85mm lens at f/2.0. The image combines one sharp exposure with one semi-transparent ghost exposure slightly displaced to the right, creating an organic motion trail effect. Flash freeze base combines with long exposure simulation, featuring a warm amber light leak crossing the face horizontally, golden gel flare, and subtle bloom, all rendered with rich blacks, tungsten color grade, and slight film grain.
