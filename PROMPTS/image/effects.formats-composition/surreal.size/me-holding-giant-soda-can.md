# Man Struggling with Giant Soda Can - Surreal Scale Action

## Description of Resulting Image

A hyper-realistic surreal action photograph capturing a man struggling to hold a giant oversized blue soda can with a classic Pepsi logo. Soda splashes dramatically from the top as the man drinks through a white straw, while a massive off-screen hand grabs the back of his white T-shirt, pulling him backward. The chaotic scene features soda splattering across the floor and clothes, realistic ice cubes scattered around, captured with high-speed photography against a clean white background.
