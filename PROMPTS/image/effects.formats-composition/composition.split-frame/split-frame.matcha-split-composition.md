# Matcha Split Frame Composition - Same Moment Divided

## Description of Resulting Image

A creative split-frame photograph showing one continuous moment horizontally divided into two connected halves. The same person appears twice in one image with one iced matcha drink - the lower half raises the drink while the upper half drinks from it through the straw. The cup and straw remain perfectly aligned across the split, creating a surreal yet cohesive composition in a café setting.

## Usage

Use this prompt for creative beverage photography perfect for café marketing, matcha brand content, or Instagram/TikTok posts. This effect showcases both the drink presentation and consumption simultaneously, creating playful visual storytelling. Ideal for café content, beverage blogging, or trendy social media posts featuring specialty drinks.
