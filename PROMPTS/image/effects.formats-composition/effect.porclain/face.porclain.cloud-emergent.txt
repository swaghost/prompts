## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
Human face facing forward in frontal view. Left side of face dissolving into shimmering light particles, glowing rays, reflections, and bright ethereal energy - appearing to break apart into brightness. Right side remains realistic with softly shaded calm facial features showing natural detail and peaceful expression. Face appears to be breaking apart into floating fragments transitioning from form to light.

**Setting/Environment:**
Clean white background creating minimalist canvas. No additional environment elements. Pure white negative space emphasizing the transformation of the face. Elegant and simple backdrop allowing subject to be sole focus.

**Lighting:**
Left side radiates with intense shimmering light, glowing particles, and bright rays creating ethereal illumination. Right side lit with soft diffused light creating gentle realistic shading. High contrast between the bright dissolving light side and the softly shaded realistic side. Light appears to emanate from within the transformation itself.

**Composition:**
Face centered in frame with symmetrical division between realistic and dissolving sides. High contrast split creates visual impact. Minimalist composition with elegant simplicity. Floating light particles and fragments extend into white space. Balanced emotional weight between form and dissolution.

High contrast between physical form and ethereal light creating duality metaphor. Clean execution with focus on transformation symbolism. Calm features suggesting peace within change.
