## Image Prompt

### Subject and Prerequisites

Use the uploaded image as the only identity source. Preserve exact facial features, face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, skin tone, hairstyle or hijab style, body proportions, and natural expression with very high fidelity.

Create a playful cozy Pinterest-style multi-compartment collage where each compartment shows a different daily-life pose while keeping the same recognizable identity:
- using a smartphone
- holding flowers
- drinking coffee or tea
- wearing headphones
- holding a frying pan
- looking into a mirror
- making a peace sign
- smiling, pouting, laughing, or curious expressions
- holding cute lifestyle accessories

Wardrobe adaptation:
- if female: cute cozy outfits, oversized sweaters, hoodies, cardigans, soft pastel clothing
- if male: cozy neutral street-casual styling
- if hijab is present: preserve hijab naturally with matching cozy styling
- if no hijab: preserve original hairstyle naturally

Scene details:
- soft beige cardboard-box environment
- handwritten doodles and small cute notes on walls
- cozy aesthetic mood
- clean balanced composition
- realistic room depth

Photography style:
Canon EOS R5 with 50mm lens look, soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field, ultra-detailed skin texture, realistic fabric texture, warm polished Pinterest lifestyle grade.

Vertical composition, 3:4 aspect ratio.
No text overlays, no watermark, no distorted anatomy, no extra people, no AI artifacts.
