## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Oversized number looks like cheap overlay or disconnected

**Solution:**

- Emphasize integration: "number integrated into background creating architectural depth, seamless part of photographic space"
- Add: "transparent semi-transparent allowing player to overlap, NOT cheap overlay, premium editorial integration"
- Lighting cohesion: "number should interact with lighting, subtle shadows or highlights matching scene"

### Problem: Reference face not preserved or beautified

**Solution:**

- Strengthen identity: "preserve exact facial identity 100%, same face shape, eyes, features, do NOT beautify"
- For Midjourney: Use --cref with higher weight
- Add: "maintain authentic real person, natural appearance"

### Problem: Composition too cluttered with geometric elements

**Solution:**

- Simplify: "minimal geometric elements, 3-7 lines total, 2-4 shapes maximum, clean minimalist restraint"
- Add: "every element has purpose, negative space intentional, NOT busy or cluttered"

### Problem: Pose looks stiff or unnatural

**Solution:**

- Relax pose: "casual relaxed sitting, comfortable position, natural ease, confident but not rigid"
- Add: "slight natural asymmetry, subtle head tilt optional, approachable professional athlete"

### Problem: Colors wrong or inconsistent with team

**Solution:**

- Be explicit: "[TEAM] = [SPECIFIC COLORS] - jersey [COLOR], number [COLOR], geometric elements [COLORS]"
- Verify official colors before generation
- Add: "accurate official national team colors throughout"

### Problem: Background too boring or lighting too flat

**Solution:**

- Enhance lighting: "professional editorial lighting, subtle highlights and shadows, natural dimension"
- Background: "premium editorial backdrop, intentional color choice, sophisticated minimalist"
