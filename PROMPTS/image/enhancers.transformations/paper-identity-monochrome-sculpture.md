### Main Prompt

Transform the uploaded reference photo into a Paper Identity portrait while preserving the subject's identity with extremely high facial accuracy. The face, hairstyle, facial hair, expression, eye shape, nose, lips, jawline, age, ethnicity, skin proportions, clothing silhouette, pose, body proportions, and overall identity must remain instantly recognizable to the uploaded reference. The final artwork should look like the same person transformed into an extraordinary handcrafted paper sculpture rather than a different individual.

Reconstruct the entire portrait as if it has been physically handcrafted by a world-class paper sculptor using hundreds of individually cut, folded, sculpted, layered, and assembled premium paper pieces. Every visible element of the artwork must be made entirely from paper, creating the illusion of a real handcrafted sculpture photographed in a professional studio rather than a digital illustration.

The artistic style should resemble an ultra-premium paper sculpture with extraordinary craftsmanship. Every surface—including the face, forehead, cheeks, nose, lips, eyelids, eyebrows, eyelashes, ears, neck, clothing, accessories, and every visible detail—must be recreated using carefully layered paper with realistic thickness, stacked construction, folded edges, precision-cut shapes, subtle paper fibers, matte cardstock texture, delicate paper grain, handcrafted depth, and realistic dimensionality. Nothing should appear painted, digitally illustrated, plastic, metallic, glossy, or computer-generated. The final result should convincingly resemble a physical paper artwork built by hand.

The subject's facial identity is the highest priority. Preserve the exact facial proportions, eye spacing, eyelid shape, eyebrow thickness, nose structure, lips, ears, facial contours, smile, expression, wrinkles, skin folds, facial hair, and every defining characteristic that makes the individual instantly recognizable. Although the face should be reconstructed from layered paper geometry, the likeness must remain exceptionally accurate.

Hair should be transformed into an intricate paper sculpture using individually cut paper strips, curled paper ribbons, folded paper layers, stacked paper locks, sculpted paper volume, precision-cut strands, and overlapping handcrafted paper pieces. The hairstyle, volume, direction, and texture must faithfully match the original reference while appearing entirely constructed from paper.

The clothing should also be rebuilt entirely from folded and layered paper while preserving the original outfit and silhouette. Every collar, seam, zipper, pocket, wrinkle, fold, button, texture, stitch, and structural detail should become carefully engineered paper elements that enhance realism while maintaining the exact design of the original clothing.

If hands are visible, recreate them entirely from layered paper geometry while maintaining realistic anatomy, finger proportions, hand positioning, and natural gestures. Every finger joint, fold, and contour should appear carefully assembled from individually cut pieces of paper with convincing dimensional depth.

Use premium museum-quality matte cardstock as the primary material throughout the artwork. Visible paper fibers, clean cut edges, layered construction, stacked paper thickness, folded surfaces, origami-inspired engineering, handcrafted assembly, and luxury paper craftsmanship should define every element of the image. The finished artwork should feel like an expensive gallery installation rather than a digital effect.

The color palette should remain completely monochromatic, using only paper whites, soft ivory, light gray, medium gray, charcoal gray, deep graphite, and soft black tones. Avoid saturated colors entirely. The beauty of the artwork should come from paper depth, lighting, shadows, and layered construction rather than color.

Illuminate the sculpture with professional studio lighting that enhances the dimensional quality of the paper layers. Use soft cinematic lighting with natural highlights, realistic shadow separation between stacked paper pieces, subtle depth, and refined contrast. The lighting should emphasize craftsmanship and realism without introducing artificial colored lighting or glowing effects.

Place the sculpture against a pure solid black background with no textures, gradients, scenery, objects, environmental elements, or distractions. The black background should maximize the contrast between the handcrafted paper sculpture and its surroundings, creating a premium editorial presentation.

Compose the portrait in a vertical 4:5 aspect ratio with the subject framed from the chest up or waist up depending on the uploaded reference. The composition should feel balanced, luxurious, and editorial, allowing the intricate paper craftsmanship and facial identity to dominate the frame while maintaining clean negative space and a strong visual presence.

The final image should be ultra-high resolution with extraordinary craftsmanship visible in every layer of paper. Every cut edge, folded surface, stacked section, sculpted contour, paper fiber, shadow, and highlight should reinforce the illusion that the portrait is a real handcrafted paper sculpture photographed in a professional studio. The artwork should feel timeless, luxurious, museum-worthy, and instantly recognizable as part of the Paper Identity series.

Do not include any text, typography, logos, watermarks, signatures, borders, symbols, neon effects, glowing outlines, CGI elements, painted textures, vector illustration styles, anime aesthetics, cartoon rendering, glossy materials, metallic surfaces, plastic textures, background objects, or additional accessories that are not present in the original reference. Maintain complete identity accuracy while transforming the subject into a physically handcrafted paper masterpiece.
