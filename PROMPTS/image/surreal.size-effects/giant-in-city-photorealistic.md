## Image Prompt

### Subject and Prerequisites

Using Reference Image 1 as the identity reference, preserve the exact facial features, facial proportions, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions, age, expression, and overall identity with complete accuracy. The person must remain exactly recognizable. Use Reference Image 2 as the scene blueprint. Create an ultra-photorealistic scene of the same person transformed into a colossal giant, approximately 3x–5x the height of surrounding buildings, standing tall or in a modern city environment. The giant should tower over surrounding buildings, hands/arms, and spectators creating an immersive but believable scale. The feet should reach the ground, with citizens standing directly at ground level looking upward or beneath them, slight dust, and natural compression where the weight meets the ground. Avoid exaggerated destruction, overblown scale, or disaster effects. The environment should feel alive but realistically calm—moving cars on the streets, intersections, apartment buildings, cafes, palm trees (if appropriate), moving traffic, pedestrians, and realistic urban details. Cars should move at realistic speeds, with proper distances and light diffusion maintaining correct perspective. The environment should feel busy and lived-in. The pose should feel effortless and fashion-inspired, as if an influential celebrity stopped in the middle of the city. Natural body language, relaxed shoulders, elegant posture, one leg slightly bent or extended, subtle hand movement, confident stance. No rigid stiffness. Typical street items: sunglasses, a handbag, an ice cream, or a phone. The pose should feel candid rather than cinematic or overly dramatic. Style the person in contemporary high-fashion outerwear, t-shirt, sneakers, jewelry, caps, or bags. The camera angle should feel natural, as if photographing from a nearby rooftop, balcony, or upper-floor building using an iPhone or DSLR camera angle should naturally emphasize the scale without exaggerated limbs or oversized hands. The clothing and accessories must remain fully visible inside the frame: clean version high-fashion outerwear with crisp shadows, accurate sun direction, natural reflections, and balanced exposure. The hands should look natural, complete with realistic shadows and proportions. The lighting should feel like outdoor photography in soft daylight. The final image must have the look of a recent iPhone photo: ultra-realistic skin texture, sharp clothing details, believable scale, neutral unedited colors, natural lens perspective, minimal depth of field, and crisp shadows. No CGI appearance, no fantasy rendering, no AI glow, no plastic skin, no exaggerated colors, no excessive HDR, no cinematic grading, no background blur, and no artificial depth of field. Everything should appear authentic with sharp realistic detail, clean colors, clean edges, and properly generated.
