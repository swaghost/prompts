# Monumental Scale

## Image Prompt

```
Epic cinematic sci-fi interior inside a vast, dark futuristic hall. A colossal elliptical observation window fills almost the entire wall, revealing an enormous gas giant so close that its cream, gold and gray storms extend beyond the frame. A tiny solitary figure stands before the window as a scale reference, facing the planet. Low-angle extreme wide shot, deep interior shadows, subtle warm floor reflections. Extreme human-to-celestial scale contrast, monumental framing and realistic astronomical imagery, 70mm IMAX texture, fine film grain, 16:9.
```

## Technical Specifications

16:9 landscape, low-angle extreme wide shot, 70mm IMAX film texture, fine grain, deep shadows, realistic astronomical detail, cinematic photorealism.

## Negative Prompt

No cartoon rendering, fantasy spacecraft clutter, extra figures, text, logos, flat lighting, inaccurate planetary scale, oversaturated colours, distorted architecture, low detail, or artificial-looking gas-giant storms.
