# Colossal Ruins

## Image Prompt

```
Epic sci-fi landscape across a barren alien plain. A colossal ancient humanoid stone head lies tilted and partially buried in the ground, heavily weathered, cracked and eroded like the remains of a lost civilization. A tiny solitary human stands far away, creating an extreme scale contrast. Low-angle extreme wide shot with large negative space, scattered rocks and layered volumetric dust fading toward the horizon. Desaturated monochrome palette, hard directional light, archaeological realism, 70mm film grain, high-contrast cinematic photography, 16:9.
```

## Technical Specifications

16:9 landscape, low-angle extreme wide shot, hard directional light, desaturated monochrome palette, layered volumetric dust, archaeological realism, 70mm film grain.

## Negative Prompt

No intact statue, multiple people, alien creatures, futuristic vehicles, bright colours, fantasy ornament, cartoon style, smooth new stone, excessive fog, text, logos, or distorted human scale.
