# Volumetric Light

## Image Prompt

```
Epic sci-fi scene in a vast black void. A solitary astronaut floats at the absolute center, surrounded symmetrically by colossal humanoid stone figures emerging from darkness. A single narrow beam of white volumetric light descends from above and illuminates the astronaut, with fine particles suspended inside the beam. Perfect central symmetry, deep negative space, monumental scale, cold silver-gray palette and high contrast. Solemn, mysterious hard sci-fi atmosphere, realistic spacesuit materials, 70mm IMAX film grain, 16:9.
```

## Technical Specifications

16:9 landscape, perfect central symmetry, white volumetric light beam, suspended particles, deep negative space, cold silver-gray palette, realistic spacesuit materials, 70mm IMAX film grain.

## Negative Prompt

No extra astronauts, asymmetrical composition, colourful neon, visible spacecraft, fantasy monsters, glowing eyes, soft cheerful mood, text, logos, cartoon figures, low-detail stone, or light beams that fail to illuminate the central subject.
