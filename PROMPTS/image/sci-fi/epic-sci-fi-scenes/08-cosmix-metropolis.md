# Cosmix Metropolis

## Image Prompt

```
Epic cinematic sci-fi metropolis viewed from an elevated observation corridor. A sweeping curved roof and glass facade create strong architectural framing and leading lines toward an immense skyline fading through teal atmospheric haze. A tiny solitary figure walks along the corridor. Above the city, fine warm-golden particles drift like cosmic dust, glowing through the fog and reflecting on the wet floor. Extreme wide shot, tiny human scale, layered atmospheric depth and sweeping architecture, teal-charcoal palette, warm-gold highlights, 70mm film grain, mysterious and monumental, 16:9.
```

## Technical Specifications

16:9 landscape, elevated observation-corridor viewpoint, extreme wide shot, teal-charcoal palette, warm-gold particles, wet-floor reflections, layered atmospheric depth, 70mm film grain.

## Negative Prompt

No crowded corridor, multiple figures, readable signage, bright daylight, flat background, excessive glitter, fantasy creatures, generic skyline, distorted glass, text, logos, or oversaturated colour.
