# Futuristic Isolation

## Image Prompt

```
Epic cinematic sci-fi metropolis at night, with towering skyscrapers disappearing through layers of blue-gray fog and creating immense atmospheric depth. A massive dark building frames the right side, where a tiny solitary figure sits on the edge of a cantilevered balcony, facing the city with both legs hanging over the abyss. A distant river and tiny aircraft fade into the haze. Extreme wide high-altitude shot, strong architectural framing and restrained cold-blue versus warm-red contrast, realistic atmospheric perspective, 70mm film grain, quiet and monumental, 16:9.
```

## Technical Specifications

16:9 landscape, extreme wide high-altitude shot, blue-gray fog, cold-blue and warm-red contrast, strong architectural framing, atmospheric perspective, 70mm film grain.

## Negative Prompt

No crowds, visible facial detail, action pose, bright cyberpunk neon overload, flying cars dominating the frame, unstable balcony, extra figures, text, logos, flat fog, or distorted skyscrapers.
