## Key Elements Breakdown

### Essential Packaging Components

1. **Product Box**: Main packaging container
2. **Product**: Inside box or displayed separately
3. **Tissue Paper**: Premium wrapping (branded or colored)
4. **Shopping Bag**: Branded retail bag
5. **Thank-You Card**: Branded card with message
6. **Product Tags**: Hang tags with branding/info
7. **Stickers**: Brand stickers or seals
8. **Accessories**: Additional branded items

### Box Design Elements

- **Material**: Matte, glossy, textured, kraft, rigid
- **Color**: Primary box color (often black, white, or brand color)
- **Accents**: Secondary color details (stripes, patterns)
- **Logo**: Embossed, debossed, foil stamped, printed
- **Finish**: Matte, glossy, soft-touch, textured

### Presentation Layout

- **Background**: Clean white
- **Lighting**: Soft studio lighting
- **Arrangement**: Neat, organized, all elements visible
- **Style**: High-end presentation board
- **Quality**: Photorealistic rendering

## Packaging Components Detail

### Product Box

- **Type**: Shoe box, rigid box, flip-top, drawer-style
- **Size**: Proportional to product
- **Material**: Cardboard, rigid board, wood, etc.
- **Finish**: Matte, glossy, soft-touch, textured
- **Logo**: Embossed, debossed, foil stamp, screen print
- **Colors**: Primary box color + accents

### Tissue Paper

- **Type**: Branded tissue, colored, plain white
- **Logo**: Printed brand pattern or solid color
- **Quality**: Premium weight, soft texture
- **Purpose**: Protect product, add luxury layer

### Shopping Bag

- **Type**: Paper bag, cloth tote, plastic bag
- **Material**: Kraft paper, coated paper, canvas, cotton
- **Handles**: Rope, ribbon, die-cut, attached
- **Branding**: Logo printed on front/side
- **Size**: Fits product box comfortably

### Thank-You Card

- **Size**: Business card to postcard size
- **Material**: Premium cardstock
- **Design**: Brand logo + thank-you message
- **Finish**: Matte, glossy, textured, letterpress

### Product Tags

- **Type**: Hang tags attached to product
- **Content**: Brand name, product info, care instructions
- **Material**: Cardstock, wood, metal, leather
- **Attachment**: String, cord, ribbon

### Stickers/Extras

- **Stickers**: Brand logo stickers (round, die-cut)
- **Dust bag**: Fabric bag for product (luxury items)
- **Ribbon**: Tied around box (luxury packaging)
- **Seal**: Branded seal closing tissue or box

## Troubleshooting

### Packaging Looks Cheap

**Problem**: Doesn't appear premium or luxury  
**Solution**:

- Specify premium materials: "Rigid box," "soft-touch finish," "embossed logo"
- Add "luxury," "high-end," "premium materials"
- Reference high-tier brands: "Apple," "Hermès," "Louis Vuitton"

### Too Many Elements (Cluttered)

**Problem**: Presentation board too busy  
**Solution**:

- Focus on essentials: Box, bag, card, product
- Emphasize "clean presentation layout" and "neat arrangement"
- Add "minimal" and "organized"

### Colors Don't Coordinate

**Problem**: Box colors clash with product  
**Solution**:

- Specify "color palette matching product colors"
- Add "cohesive brand color system"
- List specific colors to use from product

### Not Photorealistic Enough

**Problem**: Rendering looks illustrated or fake  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize "photorealistic product visualization"
- Add "ultra-realistic materials," "authentic textures"
- Specify "soft studio lighting" for realism
