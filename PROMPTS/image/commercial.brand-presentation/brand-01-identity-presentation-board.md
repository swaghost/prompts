# Brand Presentation 01: Brand Identity Presentation Board

## Overview

**Purpose**: Create comprehensive brand identity mood board showcasing product and brand values  
**Platform**: Any AI image generator  
**Style**: Premium editorial, luxury sportswear, mood board presentation

Create sophisticated brand identity presentation board with clean editorial layout, color palettes, typography, and brand positioning.

---

## When to Use

- ✓ Pitch decks and presentations
- ✓ Brand identity development
- ✓ Marketing strategy documents
- ✓ Design inspiration boards
- ✓ Campaign planning materials
- ✓ Stakeholder presentations

---

## Complete Prompt

```
Create a premium brand identity presentation board featuring the exact [PRODUCT NAME]
from the provided reference images. [DESCRIBE PRODUCT DETAILS: colors, materials,
key features, branding elements]. Design a luxury [PRODUCT CATEGORY] mood board with a clean
editorial layout. Include a sophisticated color palette inspired by the [product]
([list main colors]), premium typography, geometric graphic elements, material swatches,
close-up texture details, brand values, logo placement, design inspiration, and minimalist
composition. Use a white background with subtle [accent color] accents and high-end fashion
presentation aesthetics similar to [COMPARABLE BRANDS]. Ultra realistic, modern, premium,
magazine-quality, 4K.
```

### Example (Lacoste Sneaker):

```
Create a premium brand identity presentation board featuring the exact Lacoste
chunky sneaker from the provided reference images. The sneaker has a black upper
with burnt orange accents, a white oversized midsole, grey overlays, and Lacoste
crocodile branding. Design a luxury sportswear mood board with a clean
editorial layout. Include a sophisticated color palette inspired by the shoe (black,
charcoal grey, burnt orange, white), premium typography, geometric graphic
elements, material swatches, close-up texture details, brand values, logo placement,
design inspiration, and minimalist composition. Use a white background with subtle
orange accents and high-end fashion presentation aesthetics similar to Nike,
Adidas Originals, and Lacoste campaigns. Ultra realistic, modern, premium,
magazine-quality, 4K.
```

## Key Elements Breakdown

### Essential Components

1. **Product**: Exact product from reference images (center focus)
2. **Color Palette**: Sophisticated palette inspired by product colors
3. **Typography**: Premium font selections and hierarchy
4. **Geometric Graphics**: Modern graphic elements supporting design
5. **Material Swatches**: Close-up texture and material details
6. **Brand Values**: Core brand messaging and positioning
7. **Logo Placement**: Brand identity integration
8. **Design Inspiration**: Visual references and mood
9. **Layout**: Minimalist, clean editorial composition

### Visual Structure

- **Background**: White (clean, premium)
- **Accent Colors**: Subtle use of product colors
- **Organization**: Modular grid layout
- **Balance**: Professional spacing and hierarchy
- **Quality**: Magazine-quality, 4K resolution

---

## Customization Guide

### For Your Product

Replace these elements:

- `[PRODUCT NAME]`: Your specific product
- `[DESCRIBE PRODUCT DETAILS]`: Colors, materials, features, branding
- `[PRODUCT CATEGORY]`: Sportswear, fashion, tech, luxury goods, etc.
- `[list main colors]`: Extract color palette from product
- `[accent color]`: Primary accent from product
- `[COMPARABLE BRANDS]`: Nike, Apple, Gucci, etc. (similar tier)

### Brand Positioning

Match aesthetic to brand tier:

- **Mass Market**: Clean, approachable, vibrant
- **Premium**: Sophisticated, modern, quality focus
- **Luxury**: Minimal, exclusive, refined elegance
- **Streetwear**: Bold, dynamic, contemporary edge

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Product Detail Specificity**: Describe exact colors, materials, branding elements
2. **Color Palette Extraction**: Pull colors directly from product for cohesion
3. **Comparable Brands**: Reference brands at same tier for aesthetic matching
4. **White Background**: Premium standard for luxury presentations
5. **Modular Layout**: Grid-based organization shows professionalism
6. **Material Swatches**: Close-ups add tactile dimension
7. **Brand Values**: Include core messaging (innovation, heritage, sustainability)

## Consistency Instruction

**Always add this at the end:**

```
Use the exact [product] from the provided reference images without changing its
shape, colors, materials, [key elements], branding, proportions, or detailing. Maintain
perfect product consistency across all designs.
```

## Troubleshooting

### Product Doesn't Match Reference

**Problem**: AI changes product details  
**Solution**:

- Add consistency instruction at end
- Be more specific about product details in prompt
- Reference specific design elements (logo position, color blocking, materials)

### Layout Too Cluttered

**Problem**: Too many elements competing  
**Solution**:

- Emphasize "minimalist composition" and "clean editorial layout"
- Specify "professional spacing" and "modular grid"
- Add "white space" and "breathing room"

### Wrong Brand Tier Aesthetic

**Problem**: Doesn't look premium/luxury enough  
**Solution**:

- Adjust comparable brands to match desired tier
- Add "ultra-premium," "haute couture," or "mass-market" as needed
- Specify materials: "matte finish," "embossed," "glossy premium"

### Colors Don't Match Product

**Problem**: Accent colors don't align with product  
**Solution**:

- Explicitly list product color palette in prompt
- Add "matching product colorway exactly"
- Specify hex codes if needed
