# Woven Rattan - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

A woven thing is full of holes, so light goes through it. Say the background shows through the gaps and it stops being a texture painted onto something solid. This prompt creates authentic basketwork structure.

## Key Characteristics

- Hand-woven rattan cane construction
- Thick cane frame, fine split cane weave
- Warp and weft pattern
- Not solid - light passes through gaps
- Background visible through weave
- Broken lattice shadow
- Pale honey to amber coloring
- Scorched bends, wrapped bindings

## Image Edit Prompt

Transform the subject of this photograph into a hand-woven rattan form. It was built the way basketwork is built: lengths of thick cane steamed and bent into a structural frame, then finer split cane woven over and under across it in a continuous warp and weft that follows the surface of the shape and changes direction wherever the form turns. Because it is woven it is not solid, and that is the whole point: light passes through the gaps between the canes, the background shows faintly through the thinner areas, and the form throws a broken lattice shadow rather than a filled one. The cane is real and used: pale honey where it is fresh, darkened amber where hands have worn it, faintly scorched at the tight bends where it was heated to make the turn, with cut ends bound in tight wrapped binding and a few splinters and frayed strands lifting away from the weave. Fine detail cannot survive a weave this coarse, so small features are simplified into broad woven volumes. Warm daylight. Keep the subject, pose, framing and composition exactly as they are.

## Technical Details

**Material Properties:**

- Thick structural cane frame
- Fine split cane weave

- Natural honey to amber color

**Construction Evidence:**

- Woven over/under pattern
- Direction changes with form
- Cut ends with wrapped binding
- Splinters and frayed strands
- Scorched marks at tight bends
- Wear darkening (hands)

**Optical Properties:**

- Light passes through gaps
- Background visible through weave
- Broken lattice shadow (not solid)
- Translucency in thinner areas

**Constraints:**

- Coarse weave simplifies detail
- Small features become broad volumes
- Structure follows basketwork logic

**Lighting:**

- Warm daylight
- Shows weave structure and gaps
