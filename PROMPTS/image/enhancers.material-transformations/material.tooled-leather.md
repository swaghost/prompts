# Tooled Leather - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

Leather has no lines, only cuts and the shadows they throw. Once tone can only come from depth, the whole image has to be redrawn in relief. This prompt creates authentic hand-tooled vegetable-tanned leather.

## Key Characteristics

- Vegetable-tanned leather panel
- Cased damp and cut with swivel knife
- Beveled relief (shapes lift up)
- Background stamped down with matting tool
- Tone from depth and dye only
- Dark oil-brown in cuts and hollows
- Warm tan on raised surfaces
- Burnished edges
- Waxed saddle stitch

## Image Edit Prompt

Transform this photograph into a panel of hand-tooled vegetable-tanned leather. The image was cased damp and cut into the hide with a swivel knife, so every outline is a fine incised valley rather than a drawn line, then beveled down one side of each cut so the shapes lift into shallow relief with a soft shaded edge. Background areas are stamped down with a matting tool into a dense field of small repeated impressions, pushing the main forms forward. Tone comes only from depth and dye: the panel is antiqued so dark oil-brown pigment has settled into every cut and every stamped hollow, while the raised surfaces stay warm tan, buffed slightly glossy from burnishing. The hide is genuinely used: natural range marks and open pores in the grain, darkened patina where it has been handled most, edges beveled and burnished to a hard round, and a line of heavy waxed saddle stitch running down one side. Keep the subject's shape, framing and composition exactly as they are.

## Usage Notes

- Use with **IMAGE EDIT** mode (Nano Banana Pro / Gemini 3 Pro Image)
- Platform: HIGGSFIELD.AI or FAL.AI
- **Never delete the last line** - it locks your composition
- Key principle: **Leather has no lines** - only cuts with shadows = tone from depth

## Technical Details

**Material Properties:**

- Vegetable-tanned leather
- Cased damp for tooling
- Warm tan base color
- Dark oil-brown antiquing

**Tooling Evidence:**

- Swivel knife cuts (incised valleys)
- Beveled relief (shapes lift)
- Matting tool stamps (background)
- Soft shaded edges
- Dense repeated impressions

**Tone Method:**

- Tone only from depth and dye
- Dark pigment in cuts/hollows
- Warm tan on raised surfaces
- No drawn lines

**Surface Treatment:**

- Edges beveled and burnished
- Raised surfaces buffed glossy
- Natural grain visible
- Range marks and open pores
- Darkened patina (handling)

**Construction:**

- Heavy waxed saddle stitch
- Hard round edges
- Genuine used leather character
