# Needle-Felted Wool - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

Felt has no hard edges because the fibres migrate as you stab them. Ask for softness at the colour boundaries, not just a woolly texture, and you get authentic felted construction.

## Key Characteristics

- Needle-felted carded wool
- Soft lumpy masses with fuzzy halo
- Stray fibres glowing in light
- Needle pocks in dense areas
- Compacted hard where worked most
- Woolly and open where less worked
- Color patches with soft migrated boundaries
- Felted nubs for small details
- Thin wool strands for fine lines

## Technical Details

**Material Properties:**

- Loose carded fleece
- Matted and tangled by needle stabbing
- Soft, slightly lumpy masses
- Faint fuzzy halo of stray fibres

**Felting Evidence:**

- Needle pocks (dimples in dense areas)
- Compacted hard (heavily worked areas)
- Woolly and open (less worked areas)
- No smooth or precise surfaces
- Stray fibres glowing in light

**Color Properties:**

- Dyed fleece in patches
- Blended only by carding
- Soft migrated boundaries (never clean edges)
- Limited to available fleece colors

**Detail Constraints:**

- Small features = tiny felted nubs
- Fine lines = thin wool strands pressed down
- Details simplified to technique limits
- No woven cloth or actual fur texture

**Lighting:**

- Soft diffused daylight
- Plain background
- Halo of stray fibres catches light
