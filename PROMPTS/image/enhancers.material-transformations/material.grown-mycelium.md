# Grown Mycelium - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

Grown things swell, cast things shrink, carved things cut. Naming which one you want decides every edge in the picture. This prompt creates authentic fungal growth with organic swelling.

## Key Characteristics

- Grown from mycelium on hemp substrate
- Neither cast nor carved - grown in mould
- Dense pale cream-white monolith
- Soft matte fungal mat skin
- Faintly velvety texture
- Swollen organic shape
- Hemp fibre visible beneath surface
- Pinning bumps and shrinkage cracks

## Image Edit Prompt

Transform the subject of this photograph into an object grown from mycelium. It was neither cast nor carved: a mould was packed with loose hemp substrate, inoculated, and the fungus was left in the dark to thread through it until the entire volume knitted solid, then heat-killed to stop it. The result is a dense pale cream-white monolith with the soft matte skin of a fungal mat, faintly velvety, the shape slightly swollen and organic wherever growth pushed against the mould wall. Its origin shows through: chopped hemp fibre and substrate flecks suspended just under the surface, skin that is smooth and tight where it met the mould and rougher and more open where it grew free, small pinning bumps, and hairline shrinkage cracks where it dried unevenly. Detail is soft and generalised everywhere, as though the form were breathed rather than cut. Neutral daylight, plain ground. Keep the subject, pose, framing and composition exactly as they are.

## Technical Details

**Material Properties:**

- Mycelium-knitted substrate
- Pale cream-white color
- Dense monolithic structure
- Soft matte fungal skin
- Faintly velvety texture

**Growth Evidence:**

- Hemp substrate visible beneath surface
- Substrate flecks suspended inside
- Smooth/tight where met mould
- Rough/open where grew free
- Small pinning bumps
- Hairline shrinkage cracks (uneven drying)

**Form Characteristics:**

- Slightly swollen and organic
- Pushed against mould wall
- Detail soft and generalized
- Form appears "breathed" not cut
- No hard carved edges

**Manufacturing Method:**

- Mould packed with hemp substrate
- Inoculated with fungus
- Left in dark to thread through
- Volume knitted solid
- Heat-killed to stop growth

**Lighting:**

- Neutral daylight
- Plain ground
