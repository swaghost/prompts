# Folded Origami - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

One sheet, no cuts, no glue. That single constraint is what forces the model to simplify instead of decorate. This prompt creates authentic origami with geometric folding logic.

## Key Characteristics

- Single uncut sheet of paper
- No curves - only flat facets and creases
- Mountain folds catch light on ridges
- Valley folds hold shadow
- Stacked paper thickness at joints
- Raw edges of original square visible
- Different color on reverse
- Ghost creases from reversed folds
- Stress tears at over-worked corners

## Image Edit Prompt

Transform the subject of this photograph into a figure folded from a single uncut sheet of paper. Nothing is curved, cut or glued: every form is a flat facet meeting its neighbours along a crisp crease, so round shapes become faceted approximations and fine detail collapses into a handful of decisive planes. Mountain folds catch light along their ridges while valley folds hold hard shadow. Layers of paper stack visibly thicker at the joints, and the raw edges of the original square appear somewhere on the finished form. The paper is real: heavy textured kami with visible fibre, a different colour on its reverse that shows wherever a flap turns over, faint ghost creases from folds that were made and reversed, and a small stress tear at one over-worked corner. It sits on a plain surface throwing a soft shadow, photographed close with shallow depth of field. Keep the subject, pose, framing and composition exactly as they are.

## Technical Details

**Material Properties:**

- Heavy textured kami paper
- Visible fibre texture
- Different color on reverse
- Raw edges from original square

**Folding Evidence:**

- Crisp creases (mountain and valley)
- Flat facets meeting at creases
- Stacked thickness at joints
- Ghost creases (reversed folds)
- Stress tear at over-worked corner
- Reverse color showing on flaps

**Geometric Constraints:**

- No curves (only planes)
- No cuts or glue
- Round shapes become faceted
- Fine detail simplified to decisive planes
- Forms must be achievable through folding

**Lighting & Shadow:**

- Mountain folds catch light on ridges
- Valley folds hold hard shadow
- Soft shadow on surface
- Close photography with shallow depth of field

**Environment:**

- Plain surface
- Close framing
