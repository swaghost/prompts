# Carved Limewood - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

Wood has a direction and stone does not. Once you name the grain, the splits and the tool facets all have to agree with it. This prompt creates authentic limewood carving in the Riemenschneider Gothic tradition.

## Key Characteristics

- Carved from single seasoned block
- Long drying split following grain
- Visible growth rings
- Gouge facets (not sanded smooth)
- Fine chisel chatter in tight areas
- Warm honey brown wood
- Dark wax/soot in recesses
- Pale bare timber on high points
- Old worm holes, remnant polychrome

## Image Edit Prompt

Transform the subject of this photograph into a limewood carving in the Riemenschneider tradition. It was cut from a single seasoned block, so one long drying split runs up through the form following the grain, and growth rings surface wherever the carver cut across them. Nothing is sanded smooth: the entire surface is made of gouge facets, each cut catching light as its own small plane, with fine chisel chatter left in the tight areas the tool could barely enter. Detail is simplified into the language of the gouge, drapery reduced to deep confident scoops and ridges. The wood is warm honey brown, darkened almost black with old wax and candle soot in the recesses, rubbed back to pale bare timber on the high points. Old worm holes run through it, and the original polychrome survives only as flecks of blue and gold trapped in the deepest cuts. Warm side light. Keep the subject, pose, framing and composition exactly as they are.

## Technical Details

**Material Properties:**

- Seasoned limewood
- Single carved block
- Visible grain and growth rings
- Warm honey brown base color

**Carving Evidence:**

- Gouge facets (not sanded)
- Each cut as distinct plane
- Chisel chatter in tight areas
- Detail simplified to gouge language
- Deep scoops and ridges
- Drapery as confident tool marks

**Age & Patina:**

- Drying split following grain
- Old wax darkening (almost black in recesses)
- Candle soot accumulation
- Rubbed pale timber on high points
- Old worm holes
- Remnant polychrome (blue/gold flecks in deep cuts)

**Constraints:**

- Form follows wood grain
- Details limited to gouge capability
- Splits follow grain direction

**Lighting:**

- Warm side light
- Emphasizes faceted surface
- Shows depth and tool marks
