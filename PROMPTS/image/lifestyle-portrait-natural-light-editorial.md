## Best Practices

### Reference Image Usage

- **High Quality**: Provide high-resolution reference image
- **Clear Features**: Ensure all facial features clearly visible
- **Multiple Angles**: Include additional angles if available
- **Lighting Reference**: Note reference image lighting if relevant
- **Identity Lock**: Emphasize 100% facial fidelity in prompt

### Natural Light Setup

- **Golden Hour**: Shoot during golden hour for warmth
- **Shade Plus Sun**: Position in open shade with reflected sunlight
- **Directional Light**: Angle subject to catch side lighting
- **Avoid Harsh Sun**: Don't shoot in direct overhead midday sun
- **Reflectors**: Use reflectors to fill shadows if needed

### Portrait Composition

- **Eye Level**: Camera at or slightly above eye level
- **Space**: Leave appropriate space around subject
- **Rule of Thirds**: Consider composition guidelines
- **Connection**: Ensure subject engages with camera
- **Background**: Choose clean, complementary backgrounds

### Makeup and Styling

- **Natural Enhancement**: Enhance features without heavy makeup
- **Dewy Skin**: Achieve luminous skin with moisturizer and highlighter
- **Subtle Color**: Use soft pink tones for cheeks and lips
- **Lash Volume**: Emphasize lashes for eye definition
- **Hair Polish**: Ensure hair is styled but natural-looking

### Technical Camera Settings

- **Aperture**: f/1.8-f/2.8 for shallow depth of field
- **Focus**: Lock focus on eyes
- **ISO**: Low ISO for clean skin texture detail
- **Shutter**: Fast enough to freeze any movement
- **White Balance**: Slightly warm for flattering skin tones

## Technical Notes

### Reference Image Facial Preservation

- **AI Challenge**: Maintaining exact facial identity is difficult for AI
- **Strong Emphasis**: Explicitly state "100% from reference image"
- **Feature Specification**: List specific features to preserve (nose, mouth, lips, etc.)
- **No Invention**: Clearly state "do not invent face identity"
- **Multiple Attempts**: May require several generations to achieve accuracy
- **Verification**: Always compare output to reference for fidelity

### Natural Light Photography

- **Time of Day**: Golden hour (1 hour after sunrise, 1 hour before sunset)
- **Soft Light**: Open shade with reflected sunlight ideal
- **Direction**: Side lighting creates dimension and interest
- **Avoid Overhead**: Midday overhead sun creates unflattering shadows
- **Reflectors**: Bounce light to fill shadows if needed
- **Exposure**: Slight overexposure can enhance skin glow

### Shallow Depth of Field

- **Large Aperture**: f/1.4-f/2.8 for background blur
- **Subject Distance**: Closer subject, more blur
- **Background Distance**: Further background, more blur
- **Lens Length**: Longer focal length (85mm, 135mm) increases blur
- **Focus Point**: Eyes should be sharpest point

### Skin Texture Detail Balance

- **Detail Level**: Show realistic skin texture without emphasizing flaws
- **Retouching**: Minimal retouching for natural look
- **Texture vs. Smoothness**: Balance detail with flattering softness
- **Lighting**: Soft light reduces harsh texture appearance
- **Post-Processing**: Subtle frequency separation if needed
