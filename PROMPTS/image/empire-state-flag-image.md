## Complete Prompt (For Higgsfield AI or Similar Platforms)

I want to recreate this image for my own content. Please keep the overall composition and style the same, but make the following changes:

**Replace the text on the black flag with:** "LEARN AI. POST SMARTER. GROW FASTER"

**Add a small "Osama.AI" at the bottom of the flag** so it looks like our own branded content.

**Make the black flag look completely realistic,** with natural folds and movement as if it's waving in the wind.

**Ensure the text is seamlessly integrated into the fabric,** following the folds and perspective of the flag so it looks like it was actually printed on it.

**Maintain a premium, cinematic, and highly realistic look** with natural lighting and high-quality details throughout the image.

## Customization Instructions

### Required Reference Image

Upload your reference image showing:

- Empire State Building or similar tall building with antenna mast
- People positioned on top
- Flag waving in urban setting
- Desired composition and overall aesthetic

### Custom Branding

**Flag Text (Main):** Replace "LEARN AI. POST SMARTER. GROW FASTER" with your own message

- Keep text concise (3-5 words per line recommended)
- Use all caps for impact
- Ensure text is legible when flag waves

**Bottom Branding:** Replace "Osama.AI" with your brand name

- Small but readable
- Positioned at flag bottom
- Should look like printed branding

### Flag Specifications

**Color:** Black flag (or customize to your brand color)
**Text Color:** White or high-contrast color for readability
**Fabric Behavior:** Natural wind movement, realistic folds, proper physics
**Text Integration:** Text must follow fabric contours, not appear flat or overlaid
**Size:** Large enough to be prominent in composition

### Realism Requirements

**Flag Realism:**

- Natural fabric folds and ripples
- Realistic wind movement patterns
- Proper weight and physics of fabric
- No flat or artificial appearance

**Text Realism:**

- Text warps with fabric folds
- Follows perspective of flag surface
- Appears printed/sewn onto fabric
- Not a flat overlay or separate element

**Environmental Realism:**

- Natural lighting conditions
- Atmospheric perspective
- Realistic shadows on flag fabric
- Proper integration with background

- Specify "realistic fabric physics"
- Emphasize text integration with flag surface
