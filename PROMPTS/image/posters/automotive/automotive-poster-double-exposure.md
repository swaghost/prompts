## Prompt Description

A cinematic double-exposure automotive collector poster combining stylized editorial portrait with car-themed environmental elements. The technique blends scenic roads, landscapes, atmospheric textures naturally within the portrait silhouette while featuring the vehicle in photorealistic detail at the bottom. Designed as premium automotive magazine cover or collector poster with luxury editorial layout, elegant typography, and specification panel.

## Double Exposure Technique Explained

### What Is Double Exposure?

A photographic technique where two images are superimposed to create a single artistic composition. In this prompt:

**Layer 1 (Base):** Stylized portrait silhouette serves as the container
**Layer 2 (Fill):** Automotive-themed scenery, roads, landscapes fill the silhouette
**Layer 3 (Anchor):** Photorealistic car placed at bottom as focal point

### Why It Works for Automotive Posters

- **Storytelling:** Portrait + car + environment tells complete story
- **Visual Interest:** Complex layered composition draws attention
- **Emotional Connection:** Person's passion merged with their dream car
- **Premium Aesthetic:** Sophisticated technique elevates perceived value
- **Magazine Quality:** Editorial style resonates with automotive publications

### Key Technical Elements

- **Seamless Blending:** Environmental elements naturally fade within portrait
- **Tonal Balance:** Lights and darks create depth without losing portrait features
- **Edge Treatment:** Soft transitions maintain artistic cohesion
- **Color Harmony:** Environment colors complement car and portrait tones
