## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
An imagined botanical garden scene illustrated with simplified flat shapes and clean geometry. The composition includes oversized tropical leaves (monstera, palm fronds, broad ferns), simplified flowering plants with geometric petal arrangements, winding pathways rendered as curved lines or ribbons, benches depicted as simple rectangular forms, small birds as minimalist silhouettes, and geometric landscaping elements like circular flowerbeds or rectangular planting areas. Each element is rendered in a flat vector-like illustration style with clean edges and simplified forms, avoiding excessive detail in favor of graphic clarity.

**Setting/Environment:**
A public botanical garden viewed from above or in flat elevation, showing the organized yet organic arrangement of plantings, pathways, and garden infrastructure. The garden layout might include curved paths intersecting at gentle angles, circular or geometric planting beds, simplified greenhouse structures, benches positioned along paths, and layered vegetation creating depth through overlapping shapes. The entire scene maintains a cheerful, accessible, public-space feeling appropriate for community cultural event promotion.

**Lighting:**
Bright, flat, contemporary illustration lighting without dramatic shadows or highlights. The colors are vibrant and cheerful, suggesting daylight but rendered in graphic style rather than naturalistic lighting. The lighting is implied through color choice rather than shading - the four risograph ink colors (forest green, fluorescent pink, sunflower yellow, and deep blue) create brightness and atmosphere through their combinations and overlaps rather than through tonal modeling.

**Composition:**
The botanical garden illustration occupies the majority of the poster area, with typography integrated into the design rather than separated. The layout follows a modular Swiss-inspired grid system with the headline in Helvetica Bold positioned at the top or bottom, and supporting information in Helvetica Regular placed in aligned blocks. Small registration crosses (+) appear in corners or margins. The composition balances organic botanical forms with geometric grid discipline. The four soy ink colors layer throughout: forest green for primary foliage and leaves, fluorescent pink for flowers and accent elements, sunflower yellow for paths and highlights, and deep blue for shadows, backgrounds, or architectural elements. Where these colors overlap, natural risograph color mixing occurs - green+yellow creating brighter chartreuse, pink+blue making purple, yellow+pink producing orange, blue+green creating cyan-teal variations. These overlaps are intentional and celebrated as part of the aesthetic.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Contemporary risograph print aesthetic celebrating the unique characteristics of this printing method - a Japanese duplicator technology using soy-based inks that became popular in independent poster and zine culture. The style references grassroots cultural event promotion, eco-conscious design movements, and affordable community arts marketing. The aesthetic embraces "imperfections" as authentic print character: slight misregistration between the four color layers (each color plate shifted 0.5-2mm from perfect alignment), visible halftone dot screens creating texture, roller marks showing ink application direction, inconsistent ink coverage creating darker and lighter patches within single color areas, and ink transparency allowing colors to mix optically where they overlap. The mood is vibrant, accessible, community-oriented, and contemporary while maintaining systematic Swiss-inspired typography.

**Technical Specifications:**
Authentic risograph printing simulation using exactly four soy-based ink colors - forest green (Pantone 349 or similar), fluorescent pink (Pantone Pink C or Rhodamine Red equivalent), sunflower yellow (Pantone 109 or 116), and deep blue (Pantone 294 or 280), each applied as separate layer with natural transparency allowing color mixing, slight misregistration between layers (0.5-2mm random shifts per layer), visible halftone dot screen patterns (approximately 60-90 LPI line screen creating visible texture under close inspection), roller marks showing horizontal or vertical ink application direction, inconsistent ink coverage within single color areas (10-20% opacity variation), small registration crosses (+) in corners (approximately 5-8mm), warm uncoated recycled paper stock (cream or warm white tone, approximately 5200-5500K), visible paper grain and fiber texture, Helvetica Bold for primary headline, Helvetica Regular for all supporting information with modular grid alignment, flush left text typical of Swiss poster design, and overall finish celebrating authentic risograph characteristics as aesthetic features rather than printing flaws.
