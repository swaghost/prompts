# Vintage Retro-Futurist Aviation Poster Portrait

## Concept Title

**Retro-Futurist Aviation Poster with Identity Preservation**

## Reference Image Instructions

Upload your reference image and then copy the prompt below.

## Core Prompt Template

Vintage retro-futurist aviation poster portrait of [HUMAN], shown from the chest up in a heroic three-quarter pose, wearing [CLOTHING] and a stylized winged flight helmet with goggles, bold graphic insignia and retro mechanical details, with [HAIR] and a poised [MOOD] expression. Richly painted facial features, large expressive eyes, soft blush, defined lips, glossy hair, and polished poster realism. Add a scenic [BACKGROUND] behind the figure, keeping the same poster aesthetic with painted environmental depth instead of a flat backdrop. Distressed aged-paper surface, faded ink, worn scratches, weathered border, vintage print texture, bold poster typography reading "[TITLE]" near the bottom, controlled [PALETTE] palette, dramatic nostalgic composition, aspect ratio 4:5.

## Customizable Variables

### [HUMAN]

- Subject name or description
- Example: "the pilot", "a confident aviator", "a female flight commander"

### [CLOTHING]

- Jacket or uniform style
- Examples: "a brown leather bomber jacket", "a military flight uniform", "vintage aviator gear", "a dark blue flight suit"

### [HAIR]

- Hair color and style
- Examples: "flowing blonde hair", "short dark hair", "red curls", "slicked-back brown hair"

### [MOOD]

- Expression or emotional tone
- Examples: "confident", "determined", "adventurous", "heroic", "calm", "bold"

### [BACKGROUND]

- Scene or environment
- Examples: "cloudy sky with vintage aircraft", "sunset horizon with distant mountains", "dawn sky with city skyline", "stormy clouds and lightning", "peaceful blue sky with seagulls"

### [TITLE]

- Poster text/headline
- Examples: "SKY COMMANDER", "WINGS OF DESTINY", "FLIGHT PIONEER", "AERIAL LEGEND", "AVIATION HERO"

### [PALETTE]

- Color scheme
- Examples: "warm sepia and gold", "cool blue and cream", "red and navy", "sunset orange and purple", "military green and tan"

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Reference Image:** Use clear, well-lit face reference for best identity preservation
2. **Clothing Choice:** Match clothing style to era (1920s-1950s aviation aesthetic)
3. **Title Text:** Keep titles short and punchy (2-4 words maximum)
4. **Color Palette:** Use period-appropriate muted/faded colors
5. **Background:** Choose backgrounds that complement pose and don't compete with subject
6. **Mood:** Select expressions that convey heroism and confidence
