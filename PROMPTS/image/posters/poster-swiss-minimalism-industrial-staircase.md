## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
A monochrome photograph of an industrial staircase captured from an extreme perspective angle - either dramatically looking up from below or vertiginously down from above. The staircase features clean geometric forms: metal or concrete steps, straight railings, angular supports, and repetitive structural patterns. The photograph is aggressively cropped to transform the recognizable architectural element into an abstract composition of lines, planes, shadows, and geometric shapes. Light rakes across the structure creating stark contrasts between brilliant whites and deep blacks, with mid-tones adding depth and dimension.

**Setting/Environment:**
Industrial or institutional interior space implied but abstracted through cropping. The environment provides architectural context without dominating - clean walls, minimal details, pure geometric forms. The background might show additional architectural elements reduced to simple shapes, or fade to pure white or black depending on composition needs. The space feels modernist, functional, and stripped of ornament.

**Lighting:**
Dramatic high-contrast lighting emphasizing geometric forms and creating graphic shapes through shadow play. Natural light from a skylight or large windows rakes across the staircase at an angle, creating sharp shadow lines, brilliant highlights on metal railings or concrete surfaces, and deep black shadows in recesses. The lighting enhances the abstract quality by reducing the three-dimensional architecture to a two-dimensional graphic composition of black, white, and gray tones.

**Composition:**
The photograph is positioned within a rigid twelve-column modular grid system governing the entire poster layout. The image might occupy 8-10 columns width with generous margins, or bleed to edges on some sides while maintaining grid alignment. Oversized typography in Helvetica Neue Bold interacts with the photograph through careful cropping and placement rather than overlapping - the headline might cut across the image area, creating tension between text and photograph. Supporting information appears flush left in Helvetica Neue Light with perfectly aligned baselines, positioned in generous white space at bottom or side margins. Signal red appears sparingly as a single accent - perhaps highlighting one critical piece of information or as a small geometric element. The overall layout maintains asymmetric balance with approximately 40-60% image and 40-60% white space.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Pure Swiss International Style / International Typographic Style aesthetic directly referencing classic modernist exhibition posters from 1950s-1970s Switzerland. The design embraces objective photography, mathematical grid systems, sans-serif typography, asymmetric layouts, and limited color palettes. The mood is intellectual, refined, and uncompromising - celebrating functional beauty and rejecting decoration. Color palette restricted to black, white, warm gray (approximately Pantone Warm Gray 3-5), and signal red (Pantone Red 032 or similar) used minimally as accent.

**Technical Specifications:**
High-resolution monochrome photography (300 DPI minimum for large-format printing), rigorous twelve-column modular grid with mathematical precision, Helvetica Neue Bold in oversized scale (headline potentially 72-144pt) with tight letter-spacing (-20 to -40 tracking), Helvetica Neue Light for supporting information (9-12pt) with generous line spacing (140-160% leading), flush left text alignment with ragged right maintaining optical balance, signal red as Pantone spot color or RGB equivalent, subtle analog grain texture simulating silver gelatin print or high-quality offset lithography (3-5% opacity), premium matte uncoated paper simulation, crisp edge definition, perfect baseline grid alignment with all text locked to 12pt baseline increment, and restrained editorial production maintaining Swiss design principles throughout.
