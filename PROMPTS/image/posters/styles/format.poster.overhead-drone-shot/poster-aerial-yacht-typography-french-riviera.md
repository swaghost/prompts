# Poster - Aerial Yacht Typography - French Riviera Marina

## Description of Resulting Image

A hyper-realistic aerial drone photograph of a large coastal marina on the French Riviera captured from directly overhead. Crystal-clear turquoise water fills the frame with a terracotta rooftop town and lush green hills visible around the edges. In the center of the water, hundreds of yachts and boats are densely packed together in a precise formation where every single hull forms part of three lines of massive blocky text spelled out entirely from boats—creating typography where every pixel of every letter is an actual yacht or boat viewed from directly above.

The text reads "DAY IN MY LIFE" arranged in three centered lines. Every part of every letter is composed of yacht and boat hulls packed tightly side by side and end to end—no empty space inside any letter. The letters are solid and filled entirely with boats. Letters feature bold, blocky, sans-serif proportions—large, centered, and clearly legible from the overhead perspective.

Yachts vary slightly in size and shape, with white hulls and some featuring dark or blue accents, creating natural texture within the letterforms the way individual blueberries create texture in a blueberry-font reference image. A few stray boats are scattered loosely around the outside edges of the letters, slightly outside the main formation—same as scattered berries in a reference font composition.

The surrounding context features open turquoise water with the terracotta rooftop town, marina docks, and green hills visible around the frame edges, establishing the authentic French Riviera coastal setting.
