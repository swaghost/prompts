# Vintage Travel Journal Illustration Poster

## Concept

Transform a reference photograph into a refined hand-drawn travel-journal illustration while preserving the original scene. The result should feel like an elegant European travel diary, archival postcard, and luxury travel sketchbook rather than a cartoon or generic poster.

## Prompt

```
Transform this exact reference photo into a refined hand-drawn travel journal illustration while keeping the same composition, perspective, objects, proportions, and main colours as the original photograph.

Style: elegant vintage travel sketch, delicate fine ink linework combined with coloured pencil and soft watercolour, slightly imperfect hand-drawn lines, subtle cross-hatching, and light shading.

Draw it on warm ivory / cream textured sketchbook paper, with plenty of negative space around the illustration. Make it look like an illustration made by a sophisticated traveller in a luxury travel journal - artistic, understated, and editorial rather than cartoonish.

Keep the colours slightly muted and natural, with a softly faded, sun-washed quality. Do not add new objects or change the scene.

Underneath the illustration, add small understated typewriter-style travel notes, similar to an old field journal:

"[LOCATION] N. 021
[3-4 short descriptive words separated by *]
2026"

The final result should feel like a vintage European travel diary, archival postcard, and luxury travel sketchbook, with realistic hand-drawn details and visible paper texture.

No cartoon style, no thick outlines, no digital vector look, no photorealism, no exaggerated colours, no childish drawing.
```

## Reference Preservation

- Use the uploaded photograph as the exact source for composition, perspective, objects, proportions, scene structure, and main colours.
- Change only the rendering medium from photography to refined hand-drawn travel illustration.
- Do not introduce, remove, relocate, or redesign objects.
- Preserve recognizable architectural, landscape, and object details from the source.

## Typography Note

Use small, restrained typewriter-style text beneath the illustration. Replace `[LOCATION]` with the destination and `[3-4 short descriptive words separated by *]` with three or four concise observations. Keep the note legible but secondary to the artwork.

## Technical Specifications

Warm ivory or cream textured sketchbook paper, visible paper grain, fine ink linework, coloured pencil, soft watercolour wash, subtle cross-hatching, light shading, muted natural colours, faded sun-washed finish, generous negative space, editorial travel-journal composition.

## Negative Prompt

No new objects, changed scene, altered perspective, changed proportions, thick outlines, cartoon style, childish drawing, comic-book rendering, digital vector artwork, photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, oversaturated colours, heavy typography, large captions, illegible notes, modern UI elements, watermark, or cluttered composition.
