## Image Prompt

### Subject and Prerequisites

A conceptual, artistic, and surreal poster with a contemporary editorial aesthetic.

A woman approximately 40 years old sits on the floor in a relaxed yet reflective pose. She wears a slightly wrinkled white shirt with rolled-up sleeves. The lighting is dramatic and cinematic, with soft side light emphasizing the textures of her skin, clothing, and background while creating deep shadows and an introspective atmosphere.

In place of her head, the woman has a realistic three-dimensional Rubik's Cube. Each visible face of the cube is composed of a photograph of a woman's face (use the supplied photo without altering her features). The face must be fragmented across the cube's tiles with anatomical coherence. The seated character's cube must convey an expression of despair: raised eyebrows, an anguished gaze, facial tension, and a slightly open mouth.

Behind her, a second woman stands while holding the first woman's cube at chest height with both hands. The second woman also has a Rubik's Cube-shaped head featuring the same fragmented photograph, but her expression conveys intense concentration and mental focus.

Both cubes must retain a realistic three-dimensional structure, correct perspective, defined edges, consistent shadows, and visible separation between pieces.

Background: giant white text reading "POLLY Cube," occupying much of the background in a modern minimalist style.
Small, elegant side text: "We all have puzzles to solve."
Subtle text at the bottom: "UNSOLVED CAN'T SOLVE UNSOLVED."

Laser-engrave the "ADM" logo into the wooden floor (use the attached logo if provided).

Final style: artistically motivational, fine grain, highly detailed, moderate contrast, predominantly cool tones with subtle warm accents, 8K resolution, centered composition, vertical 4:5 aspect ratio.
