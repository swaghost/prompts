## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
A mountain landscape scene - featuring peaks, valleys, sky, clouds, and possibly foreground elements like trees or rocks - entirely reconstructed using ASCII characters and terminal symbols. The mountain silhouettes are formed from characters like /\, ^, _, #, and M, while sky gradients use periods, colons, equals signs, and spaces to create tonal variation. Clouds might appear as clusters of ~ or ≈ characters, trees as combinations of | and _ or Y shapes, and distant elements as lighter characters like . and ` to suggest atmospheric perspective. The ASCII art maintains recognizable landscape composition while celebrating the limitations and aesthetic qualities of pure typographic construction.

**Setting/Environment:**
A technical poster layout where the ASCII landscape occupies a central rectangular area, surrounded by computational interface elements. Coordinate grids appear along edges with numerical markers (0x00, 0x10, 0x20...), hexadecimal color values are annotated in corners, code fragments or system diagnostics appear in margins (perhaps pseudo-code describing the landscape generation algorithm), scan lines subtly overlay the composition, and interface windows or terminal borders frame sections of the design. The overall environment simulates a hybrid between a vintage terminal screen, a modern computational art piece, and a technical blueprint.

**Lighting:**
The ASCII landscape suggests natural lighting through character density - dense characters (#, @, M, W) represent dark mountain slopes and shadows, while sparse characters (., :, -, ') indicate bright sky areas and highlights. The tonal range is achieved purely through typographic weight and spacing. Additionally, a subtle CRT glow effect bathes the entire composition in a faint electric blue luminescence, with gentle bloom around bright ASCII areas and slight chromatic aberration at high-contrast edges, evoking vintage computer terminal screens.

**Composition:**
The ASCII mountain landscape is centered or positioned in the upper two-thirds of the poster, contained within a terminal-style rectangular frame. Around this central image, a coordinate grid system provides X and Y axis references with hexadecimal numbering. In margins and corners, technical annotations appear in IBM Plex Mono: system diagnostics (RENDER_MODE: ASCII | RESOLUTION: 80x24 | CHARSET: EXTENDED), hexadecimal color values (0x0000FF, 0xFFFFFF), code snippets, and interface labels. The primary headline appears in IBM Plex Mono Bold, while all technical information uses regular weight. Generous white space frames the entire composition, preventing clutter despite the dense information architecture. The layout follows a subtle modular grid with systematic alignment.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Computational art aesthetic blending 1970s-1980s ASCII art traditions, vintage terminal interfaces, contemporary digital art, and Swiss International Style graphic design. The style references early computer graphics, ASCII art communities, retro computing culture, and modern tech conference visual identities. Color palette restricted to black for characters and technical information, electric blue (#0080FF or similar) for highlights and glow effects, and white for background, creating a clean three-color technical aesthetic. The mood is nostalgic yet contemporary, celebrating the intersection of early digital culture and modernist design principles.
