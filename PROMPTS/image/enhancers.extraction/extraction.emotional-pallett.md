# 🎭 **Prompt: Extract the Emotional Palette from an Image or Video**

**Prompt:**  
“Analyze the emotional palette conveyed by this image/video. Break down the emotional tone into distinct components:

- **Primary emotion** (the strongest emotional signal)
- **Secondary emotions** (supporting or contrasting feelings)
- **Micro‑emotions** (subtle cues in facial expression, body language, environment, or color)

Describe how composition, lighting, color, subject posture, facial expression, and environmental context contribute to the emotional tone. Identify emotional contrasts (e.g., serene but tense, joyful with underlying melancholy). Provide a short list of emotional descriptors (e.g., ‘intimate’, ‘chaotic’, ‘lonely’, ‘hopeful’, ‘gritty’, ‘playful’) and explain what visual elements create each one. If motion is present (video), describe how pacing, movement, and transitions affect the emotional palette.”
