# Isometric Realistic City

## Description

A clean, symmetrical isometric miniature of a chosen city or location. The entire urban scene remains visible within the canvas while realistic materials, global illumination, soft shadows, iconic landmarks, and current weather create a convincing architectural maquette.

## Usage

Replace `[CITY/LOCATION]` with the destination. The supplied prompt includes a Texas heritage direction; retain it for Texas locations or replace `Texas's rich cultural heritage` with the selected location's cultural heritage when adapting the prompt elsewhere. If the image model cannot retrieve live weather, append the desired weather conditions explicitly before generating.

## Image Prompt

```text
Present a clear, perfectly centered 45° top-down isometric realistic scene of [CITY/LOCATION], composed symmetrically so the entire miniature city remains fully visible and evenly framed within the canvas without edge cropping, featuring its most iconic landmarks and architectural elements, using highly realistic textures, physically accurate materials, and lifelike global illumination with natural soft shadows, integrating the current weather conditions directly into the environment through atmospheric interaction rather than overlays, set against a clean pure white background to isolate the scene while subtly reflecting Texas's rich cultural heritage through architectural cues and environmental details, ensuring ultra-high detail, hyper-realistic rendering, maximum sharpness, and the highest possible resolution quality, with a strict 4:5 aspect ratio. No crop.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **View:** Perfectly centered 45-degree top-down isometric
- **Framing:** Entire miniature city visible with even margins and no edge cropping
- **Composition:** Symmetrical and clearly organized
- **Subject:** Recognizable city landmarks and defining architectural elements
- **Materials:** Physically accurate, highly realistic surface textures
- **Lighting:** Lifelike global illumination with natural soft shadows
- **Weather:** Physically integrated into surfaces, light, air, and atmosphere
- **Background:** Clean pure white studio background
- **Cultural Detail:** Subtle Texas heritage expressed through authentic architecture and environmental cues
- **Output:** Ultra-high detail, hyper-realistic rendering, maximum sharpness, highest available resolution
- **Aspect Ratio:** Strict vertical 4:5

## Negative Prompt

```text
cropped city, landmarks cut off by frame, off-center composition, tilted horizon, low camera angle, street-level view, extreme satellite view, distorted isometric perspective, unrecognizable landmarks, invented architecture, inconsistent scale, flat materials, plastic toy appearance, low-poly geometry, cartoon, illustration, harsh artificial shadows, weather icons, weather overlays, text overlays, cluttered background, sky, horizon, border, watermark, logo
```
