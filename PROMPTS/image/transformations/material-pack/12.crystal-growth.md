# Crystal Growth - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

Crystals grow outward from edges and points, not evenly over a surface. Tell the model where growth had room and the silhouette does the work. This prompt creates authentic mineral crystal encrustation.

## Key Characteristics

- Mineral crystals grown from supersaturated solution
- Dense fine druzy on flat surfaces
- Long prismatic crystals from edges/points
- No uncovered original surface
- Semi-transparent crystals
- Light bounces inside clusters
- Clear tips, cloudy base
- Amethyst and citrine banding
- Furred and spiky silhouette

## Image Edit Prompt

Transform the subject of this photograph into a form encrusted in grown mineral crystals. The original object was suspended in a supersaturated solution and left alone, so crystals nucleated on it and grew outward: a dense fine druzy sugar across the broad flat surfaces, and long well-formed prismatic crystals reaching out from every corner, edge and point where growth had room to run. No original surface survives uncovered anywhere: skin, hair and clothing all disappear beneath the crystal, so the whole thing reads as a mineral specimen rather than a person wearing crystals. The growth follows the underlying shape but thickens and blurs it, so the silhouette turns furred and spiky and fine detail vanishes completely beneath it. Each crystal is a real solid with flat faces and a sharp termination, semi-transparent so light enters, bounces around inside the cluster and lights it from within, throwing pale colour into the shadows. The growth changes as it goes: clear at the tips, cloudy white in the crowded base, with faint amethyst and citrine banding where the solution shifted. Hard raking light so the terminations flare. Keep the subject's shape, framing and composition exactly as they are.

## Technical Details

**Material Properties:**

- Mineral crystals (quartz-family)
- Semi-transparent
- Flat faces and sharp terminations
- Color varies with growth stages

**Growth Pattern:**

- Dense fine druzy (flat surfaces)
- Long prismatic crystals (edges/corners/points)
- Growth outward where space available
- Follows underlying shape
- Thickens and blurs form

**Coverage:**

- No original surface visible
- Complete encrustation
- Silhouette becomes furred and spiky
- Fine detail vanishes

**Optical Properties:**

- Light enters crystals
- Bounces inside cluster
- Lights from within
- Pale color in shadows
- Terminations flare in hard light

**Color Variation:**

- Clear at tips
- Cloudy white in crowded base
- Faint amethyst banding
- Faint citrine banding
- Shifts where solution changed

**Lighting:**

- Hard raking light
- Shows crystal terminations
- Creates internal glow
