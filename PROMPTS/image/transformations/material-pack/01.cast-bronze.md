# Cast Bronze - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

Cast metal is soft where carved stone is sharp. When you say the object was modelled in wax first, the model stops trying to chisel it and instead creates forms with the characteristic softness and foundry marks of lost-wax bronze casting.

## Key Characteristics

- Hollow shell of poured bronze
- Wax-modelled softness (not stone-carved sharpness)
- Parting lines where mould halves met
- Ground-flat sprue/riser stubs
- Scattered porosity pinholes
- Decades-old patina: deep brown-black in recesses, green verdigris in hollows, warm gold-brown on high points

- Porosity pinholes
- Foundry finishing marks

**Surface Finish:**

- Deep brown-black (recesses)
- Green verdigris (sheltered areas)

**Lighting:**
