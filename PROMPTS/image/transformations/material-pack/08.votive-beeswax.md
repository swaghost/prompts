# Votive Beeswax - Material Transformation

**Source:** The Material Pack by Waviboy | [waviboy.com/material-pack](https://www.waviboy.com/material-pack)

## Material Description

Wax lets light travel about an inch inside before it stops, and that is the whole difference between wax and plastic. Say how deep the light gets and you create authentic translucent wax with internal glow.

## Key Characteristics

- Cast beeswax votive candle
- Burnt for hours (melted surface)
- Dense translucent honey-yellow
- Light travels 1" deep into wax
- Softened features (heat-affected)
- Long ridged tears down sides
- Pooled disc at base
- Blackened wick at crown
- Head intact and readable

## Technical Details

**Material Properties:**

- Dense beeswax
- Honey-yellow color
- Translucent (light travels ~1" deep)
- Opaque/creamy where thick
- Faint dusty bloom on untouched surfaces

**Heat/Melting Evidence:**

- Surface softened and rounded
- Long ridged tears down sides
- Pooled disc at base
- Blackened wick at crown
- Small shallow melt pool (crown only)
- Features softened but readable
- Head fully intact

**Mould Evidence:**

- Fingerprints (cooler parts)
- Mould seam visible
- Shape held faithfully
- Stance/silhouette preserved

**Optical Properties:**

- Glows warmly where thin
- Light penetrates 1" before stopping
- Translucent to opaque gradient

**Environment:**

- Deep oxblood red background
- Fully saturated, no horizon
- Solid color field
- Warm front lighting
- Honey wax glows against red
- Soft light pool on ground
