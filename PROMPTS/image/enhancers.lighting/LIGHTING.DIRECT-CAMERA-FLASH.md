# Direct Camera Flash

## Purpose

Apply the candid, direct-flash appearance of a point-and-shoot or disposable camera photograph at night, while preserving every subject and scene detail from the supplied image.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Attached source image

## Image Enhancer Prompt

```text
Keep everything in the attached image exactly as is: person, face, features, skin tone, hair, outfit, pose, jewelry, and background. Do not change anything about the person or the scene.

Apply a direct camera flash effect to the entire image: the signature look of a point-and-shoot or disposable camera firing a built-in flash at night.

CRITICAL: Do not alter the face, features, skin tone, hair, outfit, or any part of the person's appearance.

The flash must feel physically real: a single direct frontal light burst from the camera position, not a studio light and not a ring-light effect. The subject is hit with a flat, direct, slightly harsh burst of white light from the front. Skin appears bright and slightly overexposed, with a warm luminous glow on forward-facing surfaces: cheekbones, shoulders, chest, arms, and fabric highlights. Eyes have a small natural catchlight or subtle flash reflection, alive and bright.

The background drops dramatically darker than the subject: the flash illuminates only what is close to the camera, while everything behind falls into deep shadow or near-black. Keep a slight red-orange warmth on skin from the flash mixed with the existing ambient light, never a pure-white flash. Add a subtle color boost on the subject so skin tones become slightly more saturated and fabric colors pop against the dark background.

Add faint digital noise and slight softness in the darker background areas, creating the look of a compact camera at high ISO during a night shot. A thin natural shadow falls behind the subject from the direct flash, visible where the subject meets the ground or nearby surfaces.

Style: Must look like a real photo taken with a compact camera or disposable camera flash at night: candid and slightly imperfect, warm and bright on the subject, not professionally lit, not studio lit, not filtered, authentic flash-photography aesthetic.
```

## Negative Constraints

- No changes to identity, facial features, skin tone, hair, outfit, jewelry, pose, crop, background content, or composition
- No studio-light, ring-light, beauty-light, softbox, or directional cinematic-light effect
- No uniform background exposure; preserve the near-camera flash falloff into deep shadow
- No plastic skin, excessive smoothing, over-sharpening, artificial lens flare, or fake light leaks
- No washed-out pure-white flash; preserve the slight warm red-orange skin response and existing ambient-light influence
