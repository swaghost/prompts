# Posture Correction Enhancement

## Description of Resulting Image

A digitally enhanced portrait featuring subtle body posture improvements with shoulders positioned back and down, chest slightly elevated, and head naturally aligned over the shoulders for an aligned spine. The resulting image maintains natural, confident, and relaxed body language while preserving facial identity and all other photographic elements, with realistic fabric drape adjusted to the corrected posture.

## Usage

This enhancer is valuable for professional portraits where confident body language is essential but the original pose appears slouched or misaligned. Use when preparing corporate headshots, professional profiles, or editorial content where posture communicates competence and confidence. Apply this technique when you need to improve body language presentation without reshooting, particularly for images where the facial expression and lighting are perfect but posture needs refinement.

## Actor or Subject

Any portrait subject showing visible shoulders, upper body, and neck area. Works best with medium shots or closer that show enough body context to demonstrate improved posture.

## Prerequisites

- Image showing shoulders, neck, and upper torso

- Understanding of natural, confident posture principles
