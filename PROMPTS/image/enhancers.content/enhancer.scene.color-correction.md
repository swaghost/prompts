# Color Correction

## Purpose

Calibrate and correct image colors to achieve natural, balanced, and professional color accuracy by adjusting colors, contrast, brightness, and skin tones for realistic and visually pleasing results.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Any image with color issues including off-balance white balance, incorrect color casts, unnatural skin tones, poor exposure, flat colors, or images requiring professional color grading for consistency and visual appeal.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A professionally color-corrected image with accurate white balance, natural and balanced color palette, properly exposed highlights and shadows, realistic and flattering skin tones, corrected color casts, appropriate contrast and saturation, and overall professional color grading suitable for publication or professional use.

## Usage

Perfect for portrait photography correction, professional photo editing, product photography consistency, and marketing material standardization. Ideal for photographers, retouchers, content creators, and brands requiring color accuracy. This prompt excels at achieving natural, balanced color correction for professional-quality imagery.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Source image requiring color correction

## Prompt

Calibrate the colors, contrast, brightness, and skin tones in this image for a natural, balanced look. Correct white balance and remove any color casts. Ensure skin tones appear realistic and flattering. Balance exposure across highlights and shadows. Adjust saturation and contrast for professional color grading. The result should have accurate, natural colors suitable for professional publication.
