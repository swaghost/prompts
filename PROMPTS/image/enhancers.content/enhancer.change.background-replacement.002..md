# Change Background

## Purpose

Replace the background of an image seamlessly while preserving the subject perfectly, enabling context changes, setting updates, or creative background transformations without affecting the main subject.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Any image where the background needs replacement, including portrait photos requiring professional settings, product shots needing clean backgrounds, creative images requiring fantasy or alternate environments, or any photo where the original background is distracting or unsuitable.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A professionally composited image with the original subject perfectly preserved and a new background seamlessly integrated, maintaining proper lighting consistency between subject and background, realistic edge detail and natural integration, appropriate depth and perspective matching, and photorealistic final result where the background change appears natural and intentional.

## Usage

Perfect for professional portrait photography, e-commerce product imagery, creative photo manipulation, and marketing visuals. Ideal for photographers, content creators, product marketers, and creative professionals. This prompt excels at seamless background replacement while maintaining subject integrity and achieving photorealistic integration.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Source image with subject to preserve
- Description of desired new background

## Prompt

Replace the background of this image seamlessly without changing the subject material. Preserve the subject perfectly with clean edge detail and natural integration. Match lighting and color temperature between the subject and new background. Ensure proper depth, perspective, and shadows for realistic composition. The new background should be: [DESCRIBE NEW BACKGROUND]. The result should look naturally photographed in the new setting.
