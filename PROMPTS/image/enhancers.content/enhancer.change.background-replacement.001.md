# Background Replacement Enhancement

## Description of Resulting Image

A digitally enhanced portrait where the original background has been seamlessly replaced with a new environment while maintaining perfect subject preservation. The subject's facial features, identity, pose, clothing, hairstyle, and lighting characteristics remain completely intact with proper depth of field matching and environmental lighting integration.

## Usage

This enhancer is ideal for portrait photography that requires environmental context changes without reshooting. Use when the subject is perfectly captured but the background needs improvement for storytelling, aesthetic, or commercial purposes. Apply this technique when you need to transport a person to a different location while maintaining photographic authenticity and lighting consistency.

## Actor or Subject

Any portrait subject with clearly defined edges and separation from the background. Works best with medium to full-length portraits where the subject is the primary focus.

## Prerequisites

- High-quality source image with clear subject-background separation
- Subject with defined edges, hair, and clothing boundaries
- Original image with consistent lighting and shadows
- Desired background reference or description
- Understanding of depth of field and perspective matching requirements
