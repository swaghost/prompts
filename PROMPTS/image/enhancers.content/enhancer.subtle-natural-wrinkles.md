# Enhancer - Subtle Natural Wrinkles

## Description of Resulting Image

Uses the reference image as-is while keeping the model's age perception the same. Introduces subtle fine lines around the eyes and mouth that appear only under light and expression, remaining shallow, irregular, and softly integrated into the skin. Prevents deep creases and aging effects while preserving skin elasticity and editorial realism without a retouched look.

## Usage

Perfect for adding natural age-appropriate detail to portrait photography, editorial beauty content, character realism, and authentic lifestyle photography. Ideal for mature beauty campaigns, editorial magazines, authentic portrait work, and social media content emphasizing natural aging without artificial smoothing. This prompt excels at adding believable expression lines while maintaining age consistency and preventing premature aging or deep wrinkle effects.
