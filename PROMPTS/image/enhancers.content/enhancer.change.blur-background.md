# Blur Background

## Purpose

Add soft, cinematic blur (bokeh) behind the subject while keeping the subject perfectly sharp, creating professional depth-of-field effects that separate subjects from backgrounds and direct viewer attention.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Any image where background blur would enhance subject isolation including portraits, product photos, pet photography, or any photo where the subject should be emphasized by blurring distracting backgrounds.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A professionally enhanced image with smooth, natural background blur (bokeh) that increases with distance from subject, perfectly sharp subject with preserved detail and sharpness, realistic depth-of-field gradient transitioning smoothly from sharp to blurred, appropriate blur intensity creating separation without over-processing, and cinematic quality result mimicking large-aperture lens photography.

## Usage

Perfect for portrait enhancement, product photography, subject isolation, and professional depth-of-field effects. Ideal for photographers, content creators, product marketers, and portrait artists. This prompt excels at creating natural, cinematic background blur that mimics high-end lens bokeh.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Source image

## Prompt

Add a soft, cinematic blur to the background while keeping the subject perfectly sharp. Apply natural depth-of-field effect with blur intensity increasing with distance from the subject. Preserve all detail and sharpness in the subject. Create smooth, gradual transition from sharp to blurred areas. Ensure the blur looks natural and mimics the bokeh quality of a large-aperture lens. The result should have professional depth-of-field separation directing focus to the subject.
