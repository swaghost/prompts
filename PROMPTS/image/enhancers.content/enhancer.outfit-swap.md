# Outfit Swap Enhancement

## Description of Resulting Image

A digitally enhanced portrait where the subject's clothing has been replaced with an outfit from a reference image while maintaining perfect identity preservation, pose accuracy, and photographic authenticity. The transferred outfit displays realistic fabric folds, proper draping, accurate texture, and natural shadows that integrate seamlessly with the original scene's lighting conditions.

## Usage

This enhancer is essential for fashion visualization, virtual try-on scenarios, and outfit planning without physical wardrobe changes. Use when exploring styling options for photoshoots, creating fashion lookbooks with consistent models, or testing garment combinations across different body types. Apply this technique when you need to visualize how specific clothing items will photograph on a particular person while maintaining lighting and environmental consistency.

## Actor or Subject

Any full-body or partial portrait subject with clearly visible clothing area. Works best with poses that show the garment's full design and with subjects who have similar body proportions to the reference outfit image.

## Prerequisites

- High-quality source portrait with clear subject definition
- Reference image showing the desired outfit clearly
- Consistent lighting that reveals fabric texture and volume
- Similar body proportions between source and reference for accurate fit
- Clear understanding of how fabric should drape on the specific pose

## Image Prompt

### Subject

Dress the person in the first image with the clothing from the second image while keeping the same face, identity, pose, body proportions, hairstyle, lighting, background, and photo style. The person's facial features, body shape, and all identifying characteristics must remain absolutely unchanged.

### Setting

Maintain the exact same background and environmental elements from the original image without any alterations.

### Lighting

Preserve original lighting direction, intensity, and quality. The transferred outfit must cast natural shadows and receive highlights consistent with the existing light sources. Fabric should reflect light realistically based on its material properties (matte cotton, shiny silk, textured denim, etc.).

### Composition

Keep the original framing, camera angle, pose, and compositional structure intact. The transferred outfit should follow the body's contours and pose naturally.

### Style

Photorealistic clothing rendering with accurate fabric behavior. Preserve the outfit's design, colors, texture, and fit accurately, with realistic fabric folds, draping, and shadows matching the original scene.

### Technical Specs

Ensure the transferred outfit maintains accurate design details, proper fit to body proportions, realistic fabric physics, and natural integration with the pose. Preserve outfit colors and patterns precisely while adapting to the scene's lighting conditions. Maintain original image resolution and quality.

### Color Palette

Transferred outfit colors should adapt to the original image's lighting conditions and color grading while maintaining the garment's true colors. Ensure color temperature consistency and proper fabric color interaction with ambient light.

### Mood

Preserve the original mood and atmospheric qualities of the image. The new outfit should integrate naturally without disrupting the overall emotional tone or style narrative.
