# Professional Headshot

## Purpose

Transform casual photos into professional corporate headshots with appropriate styling, background, lighting, and polish suitable for business profiles, LinkedIn, company websites, and professional contexts.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Casual portrait photos that need professional transformation including personal photos for LinkedIn, informal portraits requiring corporate polish, or any portrait photo that needs to meet professional business headshot standards.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A professional business headshot with appropriate corporate styling and polish, clean professional background (neutral or office setting), optimized studio-quality lighting flattering the subject, professional retouching maintaining natural appearance, proper framing and composition for business use, and overall presentation meeting corporate headshot standards for LinkedIn, websites, and business materials.

## Usage

Perfect for LinkedIn profiles, company websites, business cards, email signatures, and professional directories. Ideal for professionals, job seekers, executives, and anyone requiring corporate headshots. This prompt excels at transforming casual photos into professional business headshots meeting corporate standards.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Source portrait photo

## Prompt

Turn this casual photo into a professional corporate headshot. Apply appropriate business styling and professional polish. Replace or clean up the background to a neutral, professional setting. Optimize lighting to be flattering and studio-quality. Apply professional retouching while maintaining natural appearance - enhance without over-processing. Ensure proper framing and composition for business use. The result should meet professional corporate headshot standards suitable for LinkedIn, company websites, and business materials.
