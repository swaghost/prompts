# Change Time of Day

## Purpose

Shift the scene lighting and atmosphere to different times of day, transforming images from day to night, morning to sunset, or any time transformation while maintaining photographic realism and adjusting all elements accordingly.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Any outdoor or windowed indoor scene where time of day transformation is desired including daytime photos requiring golden hour warmth, midday images needing sunset drama, or any scene where time-of-day mood change would enhance the image.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A convincingly transformed image reflecting the new time of day with appropriate lighting direction, color temperature, and intensity, sky and environmental lighting adjusted to match the time, shadows repositioned and recalculated, color palette shifted for atmospheric consistency, and photorealistic result appearing naturally photographed at the specified time of day.

## Usage

Perfect for creative photography, real estate golden hour enhancement, architectural visualization, and mood transformation. Ideal for photographers, real estate marketers, architects, and content creators. This prompt excels at photorealistic time-of-day transformations that completely change scene atmosphere.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Source image
- Desired time of day

## Prompt

Transform this scene to appear photographed at [TIME OF DAY: sunrise, sunset, golden hour, blue hour, midday, night, etc.]. Adjust lighting direction, color temperature, and intensity to match this time. Change the sky and environmental lighting appropriately. Reposition shadows based on sun position for the specified time. Shift the overall color palette for atmospheric consistency. Ensure all changes are photorealistic and cohesive. The result should look naturally photographed at this time of day with appropriate mood and atmosphere.
