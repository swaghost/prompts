# Enhancer - Realistic Skin Texture Detail

## Description of Resulting Image

Preserves the original image composition exactly while adding realistic skin texture with visible pores, micro bumps, and slight oiliness in the T-zone. Highlights break naturally across the skin surface creating an alive and imperfect appearance that avoids matte or airbrushed aesthetics. Prevents blur, skin polishing, and texture repetition while maintaining photorealistic quality.

## Usage

Perfect for enhancing portrait photography, beauty editorials, fashion campaigns, and professional headshots requiring authentic skin rendering. Ideal for commercial photography, editorial magazines, advertising content, and social media posts emphasizing natural human skin texture over artificial perfection. This prompt excels at adding micro-level skin detail while maintaining natural imperfection and preventing AI-generated smoothness or repetitive texturing.
