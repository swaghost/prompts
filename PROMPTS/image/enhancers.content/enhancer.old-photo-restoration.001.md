# Restore Old Photo

## Purpose

Repair and restore old, damaged, or faded photographs by removing scratches, tears, and damage, enhancing faded areas, and preserving original quality while returning the image to its intended appearance.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Vintage, old, or damaged photographs including scratched photos, faded images, torn pictures, water-damaged photos, or black and white images requiring restoration to preserve family memories or historical content.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A professionally restored photograph with all scratches, tears, and physical damage repaired and removed, faded areas enhanced and contrast restored, missing sections intelligently reconstructed, original tonal range and detail recovered, cleaned and sharpened while maintaining period-appropriate quality, and overall preserved appearance honoring the original photograph's character and historical authenticity.

## Usage

Perfect for family photo restoration, historical preservation, genealogy projects, and vintage photo enhancement. Ideal for families, historians, archivists, and collectors. This prompt excels at professional photo restoration that repairs damage while preserving historical integrity and original character.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Old or damaged photo requiring restoration

## Prompt

Restore this old photo by repairing all scratches, fading, tears, and damage. Remove physical damage marks and stains. Enhance faded areas and restore proper contrast and tonal range. Reconstruct any missing sections naturally based on surrounding context. Clean and sharpen the image while maintaining period-appropriate grain and quality. Keep the original character and historical authenticity. The result should preserve the photograph's integrity while returning it to its intended appearance free from damage and deterioration.
