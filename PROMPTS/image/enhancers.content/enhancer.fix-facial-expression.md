# Fix Facial Expression

## Purpose

Adjust facial expressions subtly and naturally, enabling minor corrections like adding a smile, opening closed eyes, or adjusting expression to be more flattering or appropriate while maintaining the person's natural appearance.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Portrait photos where facial expression needs adjustment including photos with closed eyes, slight frowns needing smile enhancement, awkward expressions requiring refinement, or any portrait where subtle expression adjustment would improve the final result.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A naturally enhanced portrait with adjusted facial expression that appears authentic and unmanipulated, preserved facial features and identity, smooth and realistic expression modification, appropriate muscle movements and skin texture for the new expression, and overall result that looks naturally captured rather than artificially altered.

## Usage

Perfect for portrait enhancement, family photo improvement, professional headshot refinement, and wedding photography. Ideal for portrait photographers, retouchers, and anyone needing subtle expression adjustments. This prompt excels at natural, subtle facial expression modifications that enhance portraits while maintaining authenticity.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Portrait photo
- Description of desired expression adjustment

## Prompt

Adjust the facial expression in this portrait subtly and naturally to: [DESCRIBE CHANGE: e.g., add a gentle smile, open the eyes, look more relaxed, appear more confident, etc.]. Ensure the adjustment appears completely natural and authentic. Preserve the person's identity and facial features. Apply appropriate muscle movements and skin texture for the new expression. Avoid artificial or over-processed appearance. The result should look naturally photographed with this expression, not digitally altered.
