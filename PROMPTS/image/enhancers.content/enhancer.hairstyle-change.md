# Hairstyle Change Enhancement

## Description of Resulting Image

A digitally enhanced portrait where the subject's hairstyle has been completely transformed while preserving facial identity, expression, and all other photographic elements. The new hairstyle features realistic texture, natural volume, accurate hairline placement, and properly integrated shadows that match the original lighting conditions.

## Usage

This enhancer is essential for exploring different hairstyle options without requiring actual cuts, colors, or styling. Use when testing hairstyle concepts for personal styling decisions, editorial shoots, or character design development. Apply this technique when you need to visualize how different hair lengths, styles, or textures would look on a specific person while maintaining complete photographic authenticity.

## Actor or Subject

Any portrait subject with clearly visible facial features and head shape. Works best with portraits that show the complete head area and hairline for accurate style integration.

## Prerequisites

- High-quality source portrait with clear facial features and head boundaries
- Visible hairline and scalp area for natural hair integration
- Consistent lighting that reveals head shape and volume
- Clear specification of desired hairstyle (length, texture, style, etc.)
- Understanding of how hair interacts with lighting in the original scene

## Included Effects

### Lighting

- Preserved directional lighting from original image
- Natural hair shadow integration
- Realistic hair shine and highlight patterns based on original light sources

### Photographic

- Natural hair texture rendering (straight, wavy, curly, coily)
- Realistic volume and density simulation
- Accurate hairline and scalp integration
- Natural strand separation and flyaways

### Atmospheric

- Hair movement and environmental interaction matching original scene conditions
- Ambient light reflection on hair surface

### Post-Processing

- Seamless blending at hairline and edges
- Natural shadow casting on face, neck, and shoulders
- Depth integration with background elements

### Style Filters

- None (preserves original photographic style and processing)

## Image Prompt

### Subject

Change the person's hairstyle to [DESIRED HAIRSTYLE] while keeping the same face, identity, expression, pose, clothing, lighting, background, and photo style. The person's facial features, head shape, skin texture, and all identifying characteristics must remain absolutely unchanged.

### Setting

Maintain the exact same background and environmental elements from the original image without any alterations.

### Lighting

Preserve original lighting direction, intensity, and quality. The new hairstyle must cast natural shadows and receive highlights consistent with the existing light sources. Hair should reflect light realistically based on texture and color.

### Composition

Keep the original framing, camera angle, pose, and compositional structure intact. The new hairstyle should integrate naturally within the existing spatial boundaries.

### Style

Photorealistic hair rendering with natural texture, volume, and movement. The hairstyle should look authentically captured in-camera with realistic hairline integration and proper depth relationships.

### Technical Specs

Make the hair look natural with realistic texture, volume, hairline, and shadows matching the original image. Ensure proper strand separation, natural density, and appropriate shine levels for the hair type. Maintain original image resolution and quality.

### Color Palette

Hair color should harmonize with the original image's color grading and lighting conditions. Maintain consistent color temperature and tonal relationships with skin tones and environmental colors.

### Mood

Preserve the original mood and atmospheric qualities of the image. The new hairstyle should complement rather than conflict with the overall emotional tone.

### Quality Keywords

Natural hair texture, realistic volume, accurate hairline, seamless integration, proper shadows, authentic shine, preserved identity, photographic authenticity, professional retouching.

### Negative Prompt

Avoid changing facial features, head shape, skin texture, or creating fake-looking hair. No unnatural hair placement, no floating hair strands, no inconsistent shadows, no plastic or synthetic hair appearance, no distorted hairline, no loss of facial detail, no altered identity.
