# Enhancer - Natural Freckles

## Description of Resulting Image

Uses the provided reference image as the base while adding naturally occurring freckles across the cheeks and nose bridge only. Freckles vary in size, opacity, and distribution with some faint and barely visible under the skin, others slightly more pronounced. They blend into the skin naturally without sitting on top, maintaining realistic skin translucency, visible pores, and natural color variation without stylization, makeup changes, or smoothing.
