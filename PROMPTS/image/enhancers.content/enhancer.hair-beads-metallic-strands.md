# Enhancer - Hair Beads and Metallic Strands

## Description of Resulting Image

Adds a few delicate hair beads and thin metallic hair strands woven naturally into the front sections of the hair. Includes small silver, glass, and colorful beads with slight imperfections and random placement, as if hand-attached. Beads remain subtle and blend seamlessly with the hairstyle while preserving identity, hairstyle structure, lighting, and composition exactly.

## Usage

Perfect for enhancing bohemian portraits, festival fashion photography, editorial beauty content, and creative hairstyle documentation. Ideal for fashion magazines, lifestyle blogs, music festival content, and social media posts showcasing artisanal hair accessories and natural styling.
