# Outfit Color Change Enhancement

## Description of Resulting Image

A digitally enhanced portrait where the clothing color has been precisely changed from the current shade to a target color while maintaining 100% preservation of all non-clothing elements. The transformation accurately maintains fabric texture properties (denim, silk, cotton), light interaction with tonal range, highlights, shadows, and realistic wrinkle and drape patterns.

## Usage

This enhancer is essential for fashion visualization, product photography variants, and styling exploration without requiring multiple garment versions. Use when testing color options for clothing lines, creating product catalog variations, or exploring how different colors photograph on specific fabrics and body types. Apply this technique when you need consistent product presentation across color ranges while maintaining identical lighting, pose, and model characteristics.

## Actor or Subject

Any portrait subject wearing clearly visible clothing items. Works best with solid-colored or simple-pattern garments where fabric edges and boundaries are well-defined.

## Prerequisites

- High-quality source image with clear clothing definition
- Precise identification of fabric edges and boundaries
- Clear specification of current and target colors
- Understanding of fabric material type for accurate texture preservation
- Consistent lighting that reveals fabric texture and dimension
