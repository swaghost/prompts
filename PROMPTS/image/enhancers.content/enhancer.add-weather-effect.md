# Add Weather Effect

## Purpose

Add realistic weather effects to images including rain, snow, fog, or light rays while keeping the subject and setting realistic, creating atmospheric enhancement that transforms mood and visual interest.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Any outdoor or appropriate indoor scene that would benefit from weather effects including landscapes, cityscapes, portraits, architectural photos, or any image where atmospheric weather would enhance mood and visual impact.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A realistically enhanced image with natural weather effects integrated seamlessly, appropriate atmospheric perspective and visibility changes, lighting and color adjustments matching weather conditions, subject and environment interaction with weather elements (e.g., wetness from rain, snow accumulation), and photorealistic result appearing naturally captured in these weather conditions.

## Usage

Perfect for creative photography, mood enhancement, atmospheric storytelling, and cinematic imagery. Ideal for photographers, content creators, filmmakers, and artists. This prompt excels at realistic weather effect integration that enhances atmosphere while maintaining photographic authenticity.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Source image
- Desired weather effect

## Prompt

Add realistic [WEATHER EFFECT: rain, snow, fog, mist, sun rays, storm, etc.] to this image while keeping the subject and setting realistic. Integrate weather effects naturally throughout the scene. Adjust lighting, color temperature, and atmospheric perspective to match the weather condition. Show appropriate interaction between the weather and environment (wet surfaces for rain, snow accumulation, reduced visibility for fog, etc.). Ensure all effects appear photorealistic and naturally captured. The result should look authentically photographed in these weather conditions with enhanced mood and atmosphere.
