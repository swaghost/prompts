# Relight Scene

## Purpose

Change the lighting direction, warmth, or ambience of photos, enabling creative lighting transformations that alter mood, time of day feel, or lighting quality without re-photographing the scene.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Any photograph where lighting adjustment is desired including portraits requiring different mood, product shots needing optimal lighting, architectural photography requiring ambience changes, or any image where lighting direction or quality needs modification.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A professionally relit image with new lighting direction, quality, or color temperature applied consistently throughout, appropriate shadows repositioned and recalculated, highlights and exposure adjusted for new lighting scenario, mood and atmosphere transformed according to new lighting design, and photorealistic result appearing naturally lit from the new light source.

## Usage

Perfect for creative photography, portrait mood adjustment, product optimization, and architectural ambience enhancement. Ideal for photographers, retouchers, creative directors, and content creators. This prompt excels at realistic lighting transformations that change mood and atmosphere while maintaining photographic authenticity.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Source image
- Description of desired lighting change

## Prompt

Change the lighting in this scene to: [DESCRIBE LIGHTING: e.g., warm golden hour from the right, soft studio lighting from above, dramatic side lighting, cool evening ambience, etc.]. Adjust the lighting direction, warmth, intensity, and overall ambience. Recalculate shadows and highlights based on the new light source position. Transform the mood and atmosphere accordingly. Ensure all lighting changes appear natural and photorealistic. The result should look naturally photographed under these new lighting conditions.
