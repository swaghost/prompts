# Add Realistic Shadows

## Purpose

Add natural, photorealistic shadows to images that accurately match the lighting direction, perspective, and depth, enhancing three-dimensionality and grounding subjects realistically in their environments.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Any image requiring shadow enhancement including composited subjects needing grounding, product photography requiring dimension, cut-out subjects needing integration, or any image where shadows would enhance realism and depth perception.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

An enhanced image with naturally rendered shadows that accurately reflect light source position and intensity, match perspective and surface geometry, exhibit appropriate softness or hardness based on light type, integrate seamlessly with existing lighting, and create convincing three-dimensional depth and spatial relationships.

## Usage

Perfect for product photography, composite images, e-commerce imagery, and professional photo manipulation. Ideal for photographers, product marketers, graphic designers, and retouchers. This prompt excels at creating photorealistic shadows that enhance depth and integrate subjects naturally into their environments.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Source image requiring shadow enhancement
- Description of lighting scenario (optional but helpful)

## Prompt

Add natural, realistic shadows to this image that match the lighting, perspective, and depth. Position shadows according to the light source direction [SPECIFY IF KNOWN: e.g., from upper left]. Ensure shadow softness/hardness matches the light quality (hard shadows for direct light, soft for diffused). Make shadows interact naturally with the surface and environment. The shadows should integrate seamlessly and enhance the three-dimensional appearance of the subject.
