# Upscale Resolution

## Purpose

Increase resolution and sharpness of low-quality or small images while maintaining natural appearance, enabling use of low-resolution source material for larger formats or higher-quality applications.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Low-resolution, small, or low-quality images that need to be enlarged or enhanced for print, web display at larger sizes, or any application requiring higher resolution than the source provides.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A high-resolution version of the source image with significantly increased pixel dimensions, enhanced sharpness and fine detail, no pixelation or blocky artifacts, natural appearance without over-sharpening or artificial enhancement, preserved original content and composition, and professional quality suitable for enlarged display, print, or high-resolution applications.

## Usage

Perfect for print preparation, social media high-res requirements, large-format display, and image recovery. Ideal for photographers, designers, marketers, and anyone needing to enlarge low-resolution images. This prompt excels at AI-powered upscaling that maintains natural appearance while dramatically increasing resolution.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Low-resolution source image
- Target resolution or usage context (optional)

## Prompt

Upscale this image to higher resolution [SPECIFY TARGET: e.g., 4K, 300 DPI for print, 2X original size] while maintaining natural appearance. Increase sharpness and enhance fine details. Remove pixelation and blocky artifacts. Preserve the original content without adding artificial elements. Avoid over-sharpening or unnatural enhancement. The upscaled image should maintain photographic authenticity and be suitable for [INTENDED USE: large display, print, web, etc.] at the higher resolution.
