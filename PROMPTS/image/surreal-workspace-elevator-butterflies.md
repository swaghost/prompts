## Prompt Description

An ultra-realistic surreal scene placing a person in a modern stainless-steel elevator impossibly filled with dozens of monarch butterflies at different depths. The uploaded person stands near the back elevator wall, appearing smaller in the frame with plenty of space between subject and camera. They wear a plain white t-shirt, navy trousers, and white sneakers, with one hand in pocket and the other slightly raised with a monarch butterfly resting on their finger. The uploaded person looks upward at the butterflies with calm wonder. The elevator is filled with realistic monarch butterflies at different depths—several large butterflies close to the camera for strong foreground depth, others flying around the subject at mid-ground, and more near the ceiling and elevator walls in background. Colorful flowers (orange, pink, yellow) are placed along the bottom edges and corners of the elevator floor. Butterflies fly gracefully with realistic smooth motion, and one butterfly gently lands on flowers, causing slight flower movement. Soft overhead ceiling light creates clean luxury aesthetic with brushed metal reflections, shallow depth of field, cinematic composition. Perfect for transformation campaigns, personal growth content, luxury brand storytelling, conceptual portfolio work, meditation apps, or creative agency branding emphasizing change and beauty.

**Key Elements**: Surreal modern elevator, monarch butterflies at multiple depths, identity preservation, colorful floor flowers, soft luxury lighting, photorealistic composition, clean aesthetic, peaceful magical atmosphere

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Butterflies Look Cartoonish or Fake

**Solution**: Add "photorealistic monarch butterflies, realistic wing texture with visible veins, accurate orange and black coloration, not illustrated or stylized"

### Issue: Elevator Looks Old or Dirty

**Solution**: Specify "pristine modern stainless-steel elevator, luxury aesthetic, spotless brushed metal, contemporary design"

### Issue: Composition Lacks Depth

**Solution**: Emphasize "shallow depth of field, several large butterflies very close to camera (soft bokeh), others in mid-ground sharp, background butterflies soft blur"

### Issue: Person Looks Composited or Unnatural

**Solution**: Add "naturally integrated into elevator, matching lighting on person and environment, cohesive shadows and highlights"

### Issue: Lighting Too Harsh or Dark

**Solution**: Request "soft overhead ceiling light, even diffused illumination, clean luxury lighting, not dramatic or moody"

### Issue: Video Animates Person or Elevator

**Solution**: Use stronger language: "Person MUST remain completely still, frozen like photograph. Only butterflies and flowers move. No camera movement, no person movement."

### Issue: Butterfly Motion Looks Unnatural in Video

**Solution**: Describe specifically: "Slow, graceful butterfly flight with realistic wing flapping, gentle floating quality, not fast or jerky, smooth aerodynamics"

### Issue: Wrong Butterfly Species or Colors

**Solution**: Specify "monarch butterfly (Danaus plexippus) specifically: orange wings with black veins and white spots, not generic butterfly"
