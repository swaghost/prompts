# Miniature City Diorama Portrait

## Description

Transform a chosen city or country into a tiny floating spherical world suspended in a vast deep minimal sky. The image should feel like an intricate handcrafted diorama brought to life, with the complete identity of the place wrapped naturally around the planet's curvature.

## Usage

Use this prompt for travel posters, destination campaigns, city identity artwork, tourism content, editorial covers, collectible wall art, and highly detailed social-media visuals. Upload a reference image first, then replace the city and tagline placeholders before generating.

## Prerequisites

- One optional reference image for visual direction or subject identity
- A selected `[CITY / COUNTRY]`
- A short cinematic tagline
- A model that can render detailed miniature environments and controlled typography

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a striking vertical 4:5 conceptual artwork featuring [City/Country] transformed into a tiny floating world, suspended in a vast deep minimal sky.

The entire identity of [CITY / COUNTRY] is beautifully wrapped around a small spherical planet, with its most recognizable landmarks, architecture, culture, landscapes, streets, food, transportation, nature, and everyday details naturally following the curvature of the miniature world.

Make the sphere feel physically believable and richly detailed, like an intricate handcrafted diorama brought to life. Tiny roads, buildings, vehicles, trains, people, trees, and cultural elements should feel naturally integrated rather than randomly placed.

Add one surreal, memorable signature element that represents the character of [CITY / COUNTRY] - something visually unexpected, elegant, and instantly shareable.

Use cinematic lighting, atmospheric depth, miniature-diorama realism, subtle surrealism, crisp details, realistic textures, sophisticated composition, and premium editorial poster aesthetics.

Keep the background clean, dark, and minimal so the floating world remains the unmistakable hero. Create a strong sense of scale by adding a tiny human figure observing the miniature world from outside.

The artwork should feel beautiful, mysterious, dreamy, cinematic, premium, and highly zoomable, with countless tiny details that reward closer inspection.

Minimal typography:

[CITY / COUNTRY]

[SHORT CINEMATIC TAGLINE]

No ordinary travel-poster layout, no flat collage, no generic landmark arrangement. Create an original visual concept that feels fresh, viral-worthy, artistic, and instantly recognizable.
```

## Typography Rules

- Display only `[CITY / COUNTRY]` and `[SHORT CINEMATIC TAGLINE]`.
- Keep the text minimal, crisp, correctly spelled, and subordinate to the floating world.
- Do not create a conventional flat travel-poster layout.
- Do not add unrelated labels, captions, logos, borders, or watermarks.

## Composition Requirements

- Vertical 4:5 format
- Small spherical planet centered or slightly offset in deep minimal sky
- Tiny external observer included for scale
- Landmarks and daily-life details follow the sphere's curvature
- One unexpected signature element gives the city a memorable visual identity
- Dark, clean background with no competing objects

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Vertical 4:5 conceptual poster
- **Style:** Handcrafted miniature diorama realism with subtle surrealism
- **Lighting:** Cinematic, atmospheric, physically coherent
- **Detail:** Crisp micro-details and realistic miniature materials
- **Quality:** Premium editorial artwork, highly zoomable

## Color Palette

Deep minimal sky with a city-specific palette derived from local architecture, landscape, culture, and signature element. Keep the sphere visually rich while maintaining clear separation from the dark background.

## Mood & Atmosphere

Beautiful, mysterious, dreamy, cinematic, premium, imaginative, instantly recognizable.

## Quality Keywords

Miniature city diorama, floating spherical planet, handcrafted model realism, atmospheric depth, cinematic lighting, realistic textures, tiny roads, landmarks, architecture, culture, transportation, tiny observer, subtle surrealism, premium editorial poster.

## Negative Prompt

```text
flat collage, ordinary travel poster, generic landmark arrangement, randomly placed buildings, disconnected elements, floating objects without physical integration, oversized observer, empty sphere, cluttered background, extra text, misspelled text, logos, watermark, borders, low detail, plastic toy look, cheap CGI, 3D render appearance, distorted perspective, inconsistent lighting, low resolution
```

## Workflow Notes

1. Choose one city or country and replace both placeholders.
2. Define one signature surreal element that is specific to the place.
3. Generate the image with minimal typography or add final text in post-production for maximum spelling accuracy.
4. Inspect the sphere at high zoom to verify landmark, road, vehicle, people, and material detail.
