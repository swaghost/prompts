# Coffee Beans Commercial - Dense Bean Coverage

## Description of Resulting Image

An overhead flat lay shot of a Café De Lucia coffee bottle lying horizontally, almost completely buried in a dense ocean of dark roasted arabica coffee beans that fill the entire frame edge to edge, dramatically lit with hard directional strobe creating deep shadows and rich contrast with subtle steam wisps rising from the label area.

## Usage

Use this prompt when creating premium coffee brand campaigns that emphasize the raw ingredient and artisan quality of the product. The dense bean coverage and dramatic lighting create a luxurious editorial feel perfect for specialty coffee brands targeting discerning consumers. Ideal for social media hero shots, e-commerce main images, or high-end coffee brand advertising materials.
