# Enhancer - Remove Photobomber

## Description

Remove the unwanted person or people photobombing this uploaded photo while keeping the main subject or subjects and the rest of the scene completely unchanged. Identify the photobomber—the person who is clearly not the intended subject.

## Usage

Perfect for group photos, event photography, tourist shots, and any image where an unwanted person appears in frame. Ideal for wedding photography, family portraits, travel photos, and professional events where background people detract from main subjects. This enhancer excels at cleanly removing unwanted people while seamlessly filling the area naturally.

## Enhancement Goal

Remove unwanted photobombers while preserving main subjects and scene exactly, with natural background fill where the photobomber was located.

## Enhancement Keywords

Remove photobomber, eliminate unwanted person, person removal, background person cleanup, unwanted people removal, preserve main subject, maintain scene, natural background fill, seamless removal, identify photobomber, clean composition, professional person removal, scene preservation, natural infill.

## Image Prompt

Remove the unwanted person or people photobombing this uploaded photo while keeping the main subject or subjects and the rest of the scene completely unchanged. Identify the photobomber—the person who is clearly not the intended subject.

**What to Remove:**

- Photobomber(s) - person(s) clearly not the intended subject
- Unwanted people in background
- People partially visible at frame edges
- Strangers disrupting group photos
- Background people distracting from main subjects
- Any person who shouldn't be in the shot

**What to Preserve:**

- Main subject(s) - the intended people in photo
- All other subjects who belong in photo
- Scene and environment completely unchanged
- Original composition and framing
- Background elements and details
- Lighting and shadows (adjusted naturally where person removed)
- All colors and atmosphere
- Foreground and background unchanged

**Identification Guidelines:**

- Photobomber is person clearly not the main subject
- Usually in background or edge of frame
- Often looking at camera or appearing unintentionally
- Not posed with main subjects
- Context makes it clear they don't belong
- Position or posing suggests they're not intended subject

**Enhancement Process:**

- Identify and isolate photobomber(s)
- Remove person cleanly from image
- Fill removed area with natural background
- Match surrounding textures and patterns
- Blend seamlessly with environment
- Maintain realistic lighting and perspective
- Preserve all intended subjects untouched

**Technical Requirements:**

- Seamless removal without obvious editing
- Natural background fill matching surroundings
- Proper perspective and depth maintained
- Realistic texture and pattern continuation
- No artifacts or obvious clone marks
- Natural lighting in filled areas
- Professional-quality cleanup
