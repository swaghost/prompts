An ultra-premium 3:4 futuristic technology advertising poster featuring the exact PlayStation 5 console and DualSense controller inside a monumental butter-white gallery environment with navy shadows and restrained electric-blue light.

Use as the hero opening image of a PlayStation 5 brand campaign, product launch, gaming editorial, or premium retail presentation. Ideal for a console-led composition where the product dominates and typography sits behind the architecture and object.
