An ultra-detailed 3:4 vertical macro design-study poster using circular and rectangular crops to show PS5 and DualSense materials, lighting, controls, and construction.

Use for technology magazine covers, design studies, product-detail campaigns, engineering storytelling, and launch presentations. Ideal for showing tactile surface quality and controller interaction points.
