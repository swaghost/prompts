# PlayStation 5 Poster 06 - Every Angle

## Description of Resulting Image

A 3:4 vertical product presentation poster showing the exact PS5 from front, three-quarter, and side views with a smaller DualSense frame below, connected by electric-blue design lines.

## Usage

Use for product catalogue pages, technology design presentations, retail displays, and social campaign carousels. Ideal for communicating form consistency and system identity across multiple views.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux Kontext, Higgsfield Soul, or Adobe Firefly.

## Actor or Subject

Uploaded PS5 console in three views and uploaded DualSense controller.

## Prerequisites

- Exact PS5 product reference
- Exact DualSense reference
- Authorized labels and brand assets

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** Clean studio light, cool blue accents

**Photographic Effects:** Realistic product views and shadows

**Atmospheric Effects:** Minimal architectural presentation

**Post-Processing:** Controlled cool grade

**Style Filters:** Luxury product catalogue, editorial system design

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Product photography camera
