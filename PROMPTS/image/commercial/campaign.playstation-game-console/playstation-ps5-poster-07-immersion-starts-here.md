# PlayStation 5 Poster 07 - Immersion Starts Here

## Description of Resulting Image

A dramatic 3:4 vertical gaming poster placing the exact PS5 in a monumental navy-and-graphite chamber with a circular opening, blue light lines, and a foreground DualSense.

## Usage

Use for gaming launches, console hero art, premium social advertising, and immersive technology campaigns. Ideal for a centered console composition with typography integrated behind the product.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux Kontext, Higgsfield Soul, or Adobe Firefly.

## Actor or Subject

Exact PS5 console and DualSense controller.

## Prerequisites

- PS5 reference image
- DualSense reference image
- Authorized typography and logo assets

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** Side and back rim light, electric-blue floor and wall lines

**Photographic Effects:** Cinematic product photography, controlled reflections

**Atmospheric Effects:** Deep chamber depth and restrained haze

**Post-Processing:** Cool high-contrast grade

**Style Filters:** High-end gaming campaign, architectural editorial

## Included Camera Frames

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Cinematic product camera
- **Framing & Composition:** Centered console, controller foreground
- **Camera Angle:** Eye-level hero view
- **Depth of Field:** Console and controller sharp, chamber layered
- **Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 vertical
- **Shot Distance:** Medium product hero

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a dramatic 3:4 vertical PlayStation 5 advertising poster using the uploaded PS5 and DualSense controller as exact product references. Preserve accurate product colors, geometry, panels, ports, blue LED, controller controls, and materials.

Place the PS5 at the centre of a monumental futuristic chamber with a giant circular opening behind it. Architecture is midnight navy and graphite with large ice-white curved surfaces. Thin electric-blue light lines travel through the floor and walls toward the console.

Illuminate the PS5 from behind and the side, creating sophisticated rim light around the white panels while preserving product accuracy. Place the DualSense slightly closer in the foreground. Add enormous condensed typography behind the console: "IMMERSION STARTS HERE." Partially hide the typography behind the product. Supporting text: "PLAY / FEEL / EXPERIENCE". Bottom: "PS5 / ARCHIVE 01". Upper-left: "STEAL PROMPTS". Upper-right: "@BRAINN.AI". High-end gaming campaign, 3:4 vertical.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Palette:** Navy, graphite, ice white, electric blue
- **Lighting:** Blue rim light with controlled shadows
- **Focus:** Console remains the hero

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

```text
wrong PS5, altered controller, excessive blue glow, typography covering product, floating objects, CGI, clutter, text errors, watermark, unrelated branding
```
