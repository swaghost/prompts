# PlayStation 5 Poster 09 - Power in Form

## Description of Resulting Image

A sculptural 3:4 vertical PlayStation 5 poster placing the exact console on a midnight-navy stone pedestal among white curved architectural forms inspired by its silhouette.

## Usage

Use for premium product campaigns, design editorials, architecture-led technology advertising, and hero imagery. Ideal for treating the console as a sculptural object while keeping the product itself unchanged.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux Kontext, Higgsfield Soul, or Adobe Firefly.

## Actor or Subject

Exact PS5 console and DualSense controller.

## Prerequisites

- PS5 product reference
- DualSense product reference
- Authorized campaign typography

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** Strong sunlight from high architectural opening, blue edge accents

**Photographic Effects:** Sculptural product photography, controlled highlights

**Atmospheric Effects:** Minimal architectural depth

**Post-Processing:** Sophisticated cool grade and subtle grain

**Style Filters:** Fashion-editorial technology, architectural minimalism

## Included Camera Frames

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Medium-format product photography aesthetic
- **Framing & Composition:** Close three-quarter console hero, controller low
- **Camera Angle:** Three-quarter architectural view
- **Depth of Field:** Products sharp, curved forms layered behind
- **Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 vertical
- **Shot Distance:** Close product hero
