# PlayStation 5 - 10-Poster Brand Campaign

## Description of Resulting Image

A ten-poster premium technology campaign for the PlayStation 5 console and DualSense controller. Each 3:4 vertical poster places the exact products inside a cool futuristic architectural world using ice white, graphite, midnight navy, and restrained electric blue, with editorial typography and precise product photography.

## Usage

Perfect for gaming-brand campaigns, product launches, console presentations, technology editorials, social media advertising, and premium retail graphics. Use the posters as a cohesive campaign series or generate individual assets for hero, system, design, lifestyle, macro, and product-catalogue applications. The product reference must remain authoritative across every poster.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux Kontext, Higgsfield Soul, Adobe Firefly, or another image model that supports uploaded product references.

## Actor or Subject

The uploaded PlayStation 5 console and DualSense controller, reproduced as the exact product reference.

## Prerequisites

- Clear uploaded PS5 console reference image
- Clear uploaded DualSense controller reference image
- Authorized use of PlayStation product imagery and trademarks
- Optional campaign style reference or brand-guideline reference

## Shared Product Lock

Use this block with every poster:

```text
Use the uploaded PlayStation 5 console and DualSense controller as exact product references. Preserve their precise proportions, white outer panels, black central section, curved side plates, PlayStation logo, disc-drive geometry, ports, blue LED lighting, and exact DualSense controller design. Do not redesign, restyle, simplify, re-letter, recolor, stretch, or reinterpret any physical product detail. Product reference images override text descriptions. Keep both products physically realistic, correctly scaled, and consistent across the entire campaign.
```

## Shared Campaign Style

- 3:4 vertical composition
- Ultra-premium technology advertising
- Futuristic gallery and architectural environments
- Ice white, cool grey, midnight navy, graphite, and electric blue
- Realistic product materials and controlled reflections
- Sophisticated cinematic shadows and restrained blue glow
- Condensed Swiss-inspired editorial typography when specified
- No invented product details or unrequested branding
