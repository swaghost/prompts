# PlayStation 5 Poster 03 - Built to Perform

## Description of Resulting Image

A cinematic 3:4 technology campaign poster placing the exact PS5 and DualSense inside a dramatic curved futuristic corridor with subtle blue speed streaks and strong directional light.

## Usage

Use for performance messaging, launch campaigns, gaming editorials, and premium technology advertising. Ideal for communicating speed and precision without sacrificing product sharpness or physical accuracy.

## Engines/Models

GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux Kontext, Higgsfield Soul, or Adobe Firefly.

## Actor or Subject

Uploaded PS5 console as dominant hero and DualSense controller in the lower foreground.

## Prerequisites

- Exact PS5 reference
- Exact DualSense reference
- Authorized campaign typography

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** Strong side key, blue architectural reflections

**Photographic Effects:** Cinematic product sharpness, controlled reflections

**Atmospheric Effects:** Light streaks and corridor depth

**Post-Processing:** Cool high-contrast grade, subtle grain

**Style Filters:** Premium gaming campaign, architectural editorial

## Included Camera Frames

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Medium-format cinematic product look
- **Framing & Composition:** Console dominant, controller forward and low
- **Camera Angle:** Close three-quarter hero angle
- **Depth of Field:** Product sharp, corridor receding
- **Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 vertical
- **Shot Distance:** Medium close-up product hero

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a cinematic 3:4 vertical PlayStation 5 campaign poster using the uploaded PS5 console and DualSense controller as exact product references. Do not redesign the products. Preserve all proportions, panels, black center, ports, blue LED, controller controls, and material finishes.

Place the PS5 vertically inside a dramatic futuristic architectural corridor surrounded by large curved walls and monumental geometric structures. Create a sense of speed through subtle electric-blue light streaks traveling through the architecture while the PS5 remains completely sharp and physically realistic. Place the DualSense slightly forward and lower.

Use strong directional light from one side, bright highlights on white PS5 panels, deep shadows around the black center, and restrained blue illumination reflecting onto nearby surfaces. Add huge condensed typography partially behind the product: "BUILT TO PERFORM." Make "PERFORM." electric blue. Supporting text: "FAST. PRECISE. IMMERSIVE." Metadata: "PS5 / PERFORMANCE / NEXT GEN". Upper-left: "STEAL PROMPTS". Upper-right: "@BRAINN.AI". Premium gaming campaign, 3:4 vertical.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Palette:** Ice white, graphite, navy, electric blue
- **Product priority:** Console remains tack sharp
- **Lighting:** Strong side key with restrained blue accents

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

```text
redesigned product, warped console, wrong controller, excessive glow, blurry PS5, distorted typography, clutter, CGI, extra objects, watermark, unrelated logos
```
