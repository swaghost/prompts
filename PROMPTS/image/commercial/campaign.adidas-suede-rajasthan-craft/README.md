## Shared Campaign Rules

- Use only authentic Adidas Suede silhouettes, preserving three-stripe branding, proportions, suede construction, stitching, sole, and material detail.
- Every product-focused slide uses a distinct suede colorway as specified in its prompt. Multi-shoe slides must show distinct colorways.
- Handwoven rugs are a storytelling element in every slide, but are not always the central subject.
- When artisans appear, show them naturally in off-white or cream kurta-pajama, authentic colorful Rajasthani pagri, and traditional scarf.
- Every poster is a 1200 x 1600 px, 3:4 vertical photorealistic premium fashion editorial.
- Include `STEAL PROMPTS` top-left and `@BRAINN.AI` top-right on every poster.
- Use authentic material, skin, wood, sandstone, suede, and wool textures. Avoid CGI, plastic materials, tourist cliches, and unrelated Adidas models.
- Use authorized brand assets and approvals before commercial publication.
