# The Journey - Craft Has a Destination

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a cinematic photorealistic 3:4 vertical fashion campaign poster. CANVAS: 1200 x 1600 px.

Set the scene in an authentic Rajasthan village environment. A traditional Rajasthani male artisan walks through a narrow sandstone street carrying a large rolled handwoven rug over one shoulder. He wears off-white kurta-pajama, colourful traditional Rajasthani pagri, and traditional scarf. He naturally wears an Adidas Suede sneaker in a dusty pink/mauve suede colourway. The sneaker must be visible through a natural walking pose. The rolled rug is important and visually prominent, but the image should primarily tell the story of movement and journey. Old sandstone walls, wooden doors, subtle architectural details and warm dusty atmosphere. Use long afternoon shadows. The artisan should look like a real craftsman returning from work, not a fashion model.

Typography: TOP LEFT: STEAL PROMPTS. TOP RIGHT: @BRAINN.AI. Large: CRAFT HAS A DESTINATION. Small: FROM THE LOOM TO THE STREET.

Photorealistic documentary fashion photography. No palace. No tourist cliches. No staged Bollywood styling.
```

## Specifications

- **Format:** 1200 x 1600 px, 3:4 vertical
- **Suede colorway:** Dusty pink / mauve
- **Focus:** Natural walking movement and the rolled rug
