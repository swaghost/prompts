# The Collision - Two Worlds, One Language

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a visually striking premium photorealistic 3:4 vertical fashion campaign poster. CANVAS: 1200 x 1600 px.

Create a seamless visual transition between traditional Rajasthan craftsmanship and contemporary Adidas streetwear.

LEFT SIDE: Authentic rug workshop with handloom, richly patterned handwoven rugs, yarn and sandstone.

RIGHT SIDE: Minimal contemporary urban concrete environment with modern architecture and streetwear atmosphere.

The transition between both environments happens through a flowing handwoven rug pattern that visually travels from the traditional workshop into the modern space. Place an Adidas Suede sneaker in a classic black suede colourway exactly where the two environments meet. The sneaker acts as the bridge. Use dramatic directional light. The rug remains important because it physically connects the two worlds, but the composition is not centred entirely on the rug.

Typography: TOP LEFT: STEAL PROMPTS. TOP RIGHT: @BRAINN.AI. Large: TWO WORLDS. ONE LANGUAGE. Small: CRAFT x STREET.

Premium global sports-fashion campaign aesthetic. Photorealistic. No CGI.
```

## Specifications

- **Format:** 1200 x 1600 px, 3:4 vertical
- **Suede colorway:** Classic black
- **Focus:** Rug pattern bridging the workshop and contemporary streetwear world
