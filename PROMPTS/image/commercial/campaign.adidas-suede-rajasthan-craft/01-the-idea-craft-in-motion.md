# The Idea - Craft in Motion

## Image Prompt

```text
Create a premium, photorealistic 3:4 vertical fashion editorial campaign poster for BRAINN.AI, presenting a conceptual Adidas Suede campaign inspired by the meeting of Rajasthan craftsmanship and contemporary sportswear.

CANVAS: 1200 x 1600 px, exact 3:4 vertical.

HERO PRODUCT: Use an authentic Adidas Suede sneaker in a rich cream/off-white suede colourway. The sneaker must be clearly recognizable as an Adidas Suede silhouette with authentic three-stripe branding. Preserve realistic proportions, suede construction, stitching, sole and branding. Do not redesign the sneaker into another Adidas model.

SCENE: Create an authentic Rajasthan rug-weaving workshop with aged sandstone walls, wooden handloom, yarn bundles and partially finished handwoven rugs. A traditional Rajasthani male artisan is seated naturally beside the loom, actively working on a richly patterned rug. He wears off-white kurta-pajama, colourful authentic Rajasthani pagri, and traditional scarf. The Adidas Suede sits naturally in the foreground beside the artisan, partially overlapping a beautifully woven rug. The rug should be visually important but should not occupy the entire composition. Warm late-afternoon Rajasthan sunlight enters through the workshop, producing realistic shadows, atmospheric dust and beautiful tactile highlights on the suede and wool fibres.

COMPOSITION: Artisan is the human focal point. Sneaker is the fashion focal point. Rug connects both worlds.

Typography: TOP LEFT: STEAL PROMPTS. TOP RIGHT: @BRAINN.AI. Large headline: CRAFT IN MOTION. Small supporting text: ADIDAS SUEDE x RAJASTHAN.

Sophisticated Vogue editorial composition, premium Indian craftsmanship photography, contemporary streetwear art direction. No CGI, no 3D render, no plastic materials, no palace, no tourist cliches, no floating sneaker, no excessive decoration.
```

## Specifications

- **Format:** 1200 x 1600 px, 3:4 vertical
- **Suede colorway:** Cream / off-white
- **Focus:** Artisan, sneaker, and rug connection
