Maintain the dynamic diagonal climbing composition, front shoe dominating lower-left foreground, body extending diagonally into frame, sky negative space upper-left for text overlay. No added text, logos, or watermarks beyond authentic packaging.

Shoot style: 28mm equivalent, ISO 200, f/5.6, natural outdoor grading, dynamic action capture, 8K resolution, ultra-sharp product and rock texture, premium campaign quality, photorealistic, no distortion or artifacts.
