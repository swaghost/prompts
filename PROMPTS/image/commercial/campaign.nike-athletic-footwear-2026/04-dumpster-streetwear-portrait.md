# Nike Athletic Footwear Campaign - Prompt 04: Dumpster Streetwear Portrait

## Image Prompt

Create an ultra-premium streetwear campaign photograph shot from a low upward angle against a clear bright blue sky, featuring a woman sitting casually on the edge of a weathered, rusted green industrial dumpster, legs dangling loosely off the side, hair blowing sideways in the wind, looking directly into the camera with a relaxed, confident expression. She wears a dark navy hooded jacket and loose grey wide-leg trousers, both feet wearing the uploaded shoes prominently in the foreground. The uploaded product must completely replace any reference shoes while preserving every authentic detail - suede or textile upper texture, side stripe branding, logo placement, gum outsole color, silhouette shape, and material finish exactly as shown.

Background: clear deep blue sky, weathered green metal dumpster with visible rust streaks and worn paint texture, and a soft blurred white structure in the lower-left corner.

Lighting: bright direct natural sunlight from above, crisp highlights on the shoes' suede texture and gum soles, warm sunlit tones on her jacket and trousers, strong natural contrast between sky and dumpster texture.

Maintain the same low-angle dangling-legs composition, both shoes positioned prominently in the foreground at different heights, model seated above, generous sky negative space filling the upper portion of the frame. No added text, logos, or watermarks beyond the authentic packaging.

Shoot style: 24mm equivalent, ISO 200, f/5.6, bright natural daylight color grading, crisp product and fabric detail, 8K resolution, ultra-sharp texture rendering, premium streetwear campaign photography, photorealistic, no distortion or compositing artifacts.
