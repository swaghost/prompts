# Nike Athletic Footwear Campaign - Prompt 03: Tucked Mid-Air Jump

## Image Prompt

Create an ultra-premium athletic campaign photograph shot from a low angle, featuring a woman captured mid-air in a dynamic tucked jump pose against a smooth, clean gradient blue sky. She wears an oversized white long-sleeve button-up shirt with sleeves flowing from the motion, black athletic shorts, tall grey-white crew socks, and the uploaded shoes on both feet, knees pulled up toward her chest, arms extended loosely with fingers relaxed, hair flying upward from the jump's momentum. Both shoes are prominently angled toward the camera in the lower portion of the frame, soles partially visible. The uploaded product must completely replace any reference shoes while preserving every authentic detail - mesh upper texture, dynamic graphic pattern or print on the upper, logo placement, midsole shape, outsole tread pattern, color scheme, and material finish exactly as shown.

Background: smooth, solid mid-blue sky gradient, clean and unobstructed, no clouds or props.

Lighting: bright even natural daylight from above, crisp highlights on the shirt fabric, socks, and shoes' textured upper, soft natural shadow beneath the tucked body for depth, high clarity throughout the frame.

Maintain the same dynamic tucked mid-air composition, body centered and slightly right, shoes angled toward the lower-center of the frame, generous clean negative space on the left for bold brand or tagline text overlay. No added text, logos, or watermarks beyond the authentic packaging.

Shoot style: 35mm equivalent, ISO 200, f/5.6, clean bright color grading, crisp motion capture, 8K resolution, ultra-sharp product and fabric detail, premium athletic campaign photography, photorealistic, no distortion or compositing artifacts.
