## Shared Product-Reference Rule

The uploaded product must completely replace any reference shoe while preserving every authentic detail visible in the upload: upper construction and texture, overlay panels, logo or Swoosh placement, branding details, midsole shape, outsole tread, cushioning elements, lacing or closure, color scheme, and material finish exactly as shown.

## Shared Creative Direction

- Premium athletic, streetwear, trail-running, and footwear-commercial photography
- Product-first composition with deliberate low angles, foreground scale, or controlled studio contrast
- Natural skin, fabric, shoe, and environmental texture
- 8K photorealistic output with no distortion or compositing artifacts
- Leave clean negative space for later brand or tagline layout where specified
- No added text, logos, or watermarks beyond authentic product packaging
