# Nike Athletic Footwear Campaign - Prompt 01: Airborne Low-Angle Jump

## Image Prompt

Create an ultra-premium athletic campaign photograph shot from a dramatic low angle looking upward, featuring the attached model mid-jump in an energetic airborne pose against a bright blue sky with soft scattered clouds and visible power lines crossing the frame for urban grit. She wears a textured grey athletic top, black athletic shorts, tall white crew socks, and the uploaded shoes, with one leg extended forward and one arm reaching out. Capture her at the peak of the jump so both shoes are prominently visible closest to the camera. The uploaded product must completely replace any reference shoes while preserving every authentic detail: upper mesh texture, Swoosh placement, midsole shape, translucent outsole color and gradient, lacing, heel structure, and material finish exactly as shown.

Background: bright clear daytime sky, soft natural clouds, thin diagonal power lines crossing the lower frame, and strong sunlight creating a sense of height and motion.

Lighting: bright direct overhead sunlight, crisp highlights on the mesh upper and translucent sole, natural rim light along the attached model's limbs and clothing, with strong dynamic shadow definition on the shoe soles closest to camera.

Maintain the extreme low-angle composition, with shoes filling the lower foreground through exaggerated perspective and the body extending upward. Leave generous sky negative space in the upper-left for brand logo or text overlay. No added text, logos, or watermarks.

Shoot style: 24mm equivalent, ISO 200, f/5.6, high-energy natural daylight color grading, crisp motion capture, 8K resolution, ultra-sharp product and fabric detail, premium athletic campaign photography, photorealistic, no distortion or compositing artifacts.
