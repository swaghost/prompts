# 3x3 Photorealistic Scene Moments Grid

## Prompt Description

Create a 3x3 grid of 9 photorealistic images capturing different moments within the same scene. Maintain complete consistency for character/product, location, environment, lighting, and time of day while showcasing 9 distinct moments through varied actions, micro-narratives, shots, compositions, angles, gestures, poses, and emotional hooks. Designed for dynamic storytelling with cinematic variety while preserving scene integrity.

---

## Complete Prompt

**Create a 3x3 grid of 9 photorealistic images based on the attached scene. Maintain the same character/product and location without changes: face, proportions, clothing, environment, lighting, and time of day - everything must match the reference.**

**You need 9 distinct "moments" within this same scene: different actions/micro-narratives, as well as varied shots and compositions. For each frame, devise a bold angle, a n expressive gesture or pose, and an emotional hook (do not describe them in words - come up with them yourself. Pay maximum attention to textures, materials, lighting, and atmosphere.**

**Arrange them in the grid (mix them up, do not group by type): - 1 wide shot, 2 medium shots, 1 long shot, 1 extreme close-up - 1 shot focusing on the character - 2 shots focusing on the product - 1 "atmosphere" shot (without the character or product)**

## Structured Breakdown

### OVERALL SPECIFICATIONS

**Format**: 3x3 grid layout  
**Total Frames**: 9 photorealistic images  
**Consistency Requirement**: Same scene, character, product throughout  
**Variation Requirement**: 9 distinct moments, angles, compositions

### CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS (MUST NOT CHANGE)

**Character Consistency**:

- Same face and facial features
- Identical proportions and body type
- Same clothing and outfit
- Same hairstyle and color
- Same accessories

**Location/Environment Consistency**:

- Same physical setting and location
- Same background elements
- Same environmental props
- Same time of day (lighting angle/color)

**Lighting Consistency**:

- Same light source direction
- Same lighting quality (soft/hard)
- Same time of day indicators
- Same color temperature

**Product Consistency** (if applicable):

- Same product design
- Same colors and materials
- Same logo and branding
- Same proportions

### VARIATION REQUIREMENTS (MUST CHANGE)

**Different Moments**:

- Distinct actions or micro-narratives
- Different gestures and poses
- Various emotional expressions
- Different interactions with product/environment

**Different Compositions**:

- Bold varied camera angles
- Different framing choices
- Varied perspectives (high, low, straight-on, overhead)
- Different focal points per frame

**Different Shot Types** (mixed throughout grid):

- 1 wide shot (establishes full scene context)
- 2 medium shots (character/product in context)
- 1 long shot (expansive environmental view)
- 1 extreme close-up (intimate detail)
- 1 shot focusing on character (person as hero)
- 2 shots focusing on product (product as hero)
- 1 atmosphere shot (no character or product visible)

**Emotional Hooks**:

- Create without describing in words
- Devise expressive gestures or poses
- Generate bold angles naturally
- Maximum attention to textures, materials, lighting, atmosphere

### GRID ARRANGEMENT STRATEGY

**Do NOT Group by Type**: Mix shot types throughout grid  
**Balanced Distribution**: Vary wide, medium, close-up across all panels  
**Visual Flow**: Create dynamic reading experience  
**Cohesive Yet Varied**: Unity through consistency, interest through variety

### TECHNICAL QUALITY STANDARDS

**Photorealistic Rendering**:

- Ultra-realistic textures (fabric, skin, materials)
- Accurate lighting physics
- Natural depth of field
- Realistic shadows and highlights

**Material Detail**:

- Fabric weave and texture
- Product materials (glass, metal, plastic, etc.)
- Skin texture and pores
- Environmental surfaces

**Lighting & Atmosphere**:

- Consistent but naturally varied per angle
- Volumetric effects if applicable
- Accurate color temperature
- Natural shadow behavior

## Shot Type Specifications

### 1 Wide Shot

**Purpose**: Establish full scene context  
**Framing**: Character/product in environment, showing surroundings  
**Distance**: Far enough to see complete setting  
**Use**: Sets spatial relationships and mood

### 2 Medium Shots

**Purpose**: Balance character/product with context  
**Framing**: Waist-up to knee-level, or product with partial environment  
**Distance**: Close enough for detail, far enough for context  
**Use**: Main storytelling shots, clear subject visibility

### 1 Long Shot

**Purpose**: Expansive environmental view  
**Framing**: Full body or product in vast space  
**Distance**: Character/product appears smaller in frame  
**Use**: Emphasizes scale, isolation, or grand setting

### 1 Extreme Close-Up

**Purpose**: Intimate detail and texture  
**Framing**: Very tight on face detail, hand, product element  
**Distance**: Fill frame with detail  
**Use**: Emotional intensity, material quality, micro-detail

### 1 Character-Focused Shot

**Purpose**: Character as hero of frame  
**Framing**: Character prominence, product secondary or absent  
**Distance**: Varies (portrait range)  
**Use**: Emotional connection, personality, human element

### 2 Product-Focused Shots

**Purpose**: Product as hero of frame  
**Framing**: Product prominence, character secondary or absent  
**Distance**: Varies (detail to styled shot)  
**Use**: Product showcase, brand visibility, commercial focus

### 1 Atmosphere Shot

**Purpose**: Mood without main subjects  
**Framing**: Environment, texture, lighting, no character or product  
**Distance**: Varies  
**Use**: Cinematic atmosphere, setting mood, visual break

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Character Face Changes Between Panels

**Solution**: Upload reference and specify "Use exact same face, identical facial features, same person in all 9 frames"

### Issue: Lighting Inconsistency Across Grid

**Solution**: Emphasize "Same time of day, same light direction, same lighting quality throughout all panels"

### Issue: Clothing or Outfit Changes

**Solution**: State "Same exact clothing, outfit, accessories in all frames without variation"

### Issue: Shot Types Grouped Instead of Mixed

**Solution**: Request "Mix shot types throughout grid randomly, do not group wide shots together or close-ups together"

### Issue: Environment Changes Between Frames

**Solution**: Specify "Same physical location, same background elements, same setting in all 9 frames"

### Issue: Not Enough Visual Variety

**Solution**: Emphasize "Bold different angles, varied gestures, distinct emotional moments, creative compositions"

---

## Workflow Tips

1. **Select Base Scene**: Choose compelling scene with good lighting and interesting environment
2. **Upload Reference Image**: Provide clear reference showing character, product, location, lighting
3. **Define Moment Types**: Brainstorm 9 different micro-narratives or actions
4. **Specify Shot Distribution**: List exact shot type requirements
5. **Generate Grid**: Create complete 3x3 grid in one generation
6. **Review Consistency**: Check face, clothing, location, lighting match across all panels
7. **Review Variety**: Ensure angles, moments, compositions feel distinct
8. **Refine if Needed**: Request adjustments to specific panels while maintaining consistency
