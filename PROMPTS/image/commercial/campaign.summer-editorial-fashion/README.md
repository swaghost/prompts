## Shared Settings

- **Format:** Vertical 4:5
- **Higgsfield Soul 2:** Style strength 80%; lock the seed after the first strong render
- **ChatGPT Images:** Append `photorealistic editorial, not illustrated`
- **Midjourney V7:** `--ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7 --q 2`
- **Finish:** Photorealistic editorial photography with realistic skin, anatomy, materials, water, and sunlight
