# 08 — OBJECT / PEDESTAL

Create a minimalist luxury product advertisement featuring the exact Prada sunglasses from the provided reference.

Place the sunglasses alone on a sculptural architectural pedestal made of warm ivory stone, photographed as a museum-quality luxury object. The overall image should feel quiet, expensive, timeless and highly intentional. Premium optical-product photography, realistic materials, soft shadows, subtle film grain, perfect symmetry with slight editorial tension.

No model, no extra accessories, no flowers, no unnecessary props.
