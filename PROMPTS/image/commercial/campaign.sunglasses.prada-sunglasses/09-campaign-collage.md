# 09 — CAMPAIGN COLLAGE

Create a sophisticated editorial collage poster for the exact Prada sunglasses from the provided reference.

Build a carefully balanced composition combining three visual layers: a large hero photograph of the sunglasses, a cropped macro detail of the frame and a smaller black-and-white fashion editorial-style image featuring the sunglasses.

Everything must feel like one cohesive luxury magazine spread rather than a random collage.

Use warm ivory paper as the dominant background, black typography, subtle burgundy/red accents, thin geometric rules and small catalog-style annotations.

Large typography reading "PRADA / SYMBOLE" should be partially cropped and integrated into the layout. Use generous negative space and an extremely precise editorial grid.

High-end Italian fashion magazine art direction, tactile paper texture, realistic photography, refined grain, sophisticated lighting and premium print-design quality.
