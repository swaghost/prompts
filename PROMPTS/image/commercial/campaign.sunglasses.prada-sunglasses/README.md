## Technical Specifications

### Aspect Ratio

- **Format:** 3:4 (vertical/portrait)
- **Platform:** Instagram poster composition
- **Delivery:** Full-bleed native exact, no crop/resize/padding/letterboxing

### Color Palette

- **Primary:** Warm ivory/off-white textured paper background
- **Secondary:** Black/charcoal typography
- **Accent:** Deep burgundy/red (used extremely sparingly)
- **Product:** Glossy black acetate frame with realistic reflections

### Product Requirements

- Exact Prada Symbole sunglasses from reference image
- Preserved frame shape, proportions, and geometry
- Glossy black acetate finish with controlled highlights
- Correct lens shape and Prada triangle detail
- No product redesign or alterations

### Visual Style

- High-end Italian luxury fashion aesthetic
- Premium European campaign art direction
- Sophisticated editorial typography (condensed fashion typefaces)
- Minimal Swiss-inspired grid layout
- Generous negative space
- Restrained composition with precise geometry

### Lighting & Photography

- Soft directional studio lighting
- Realistic contact shadows and reflections
- Subtle film grain and paper texture
- Museum-catalog quality product photography
- Cinematic composition with controlled highlights

### Typography Elements

- Oversized cropped editorial typography (partially visible)
- Small minimalist technical labels and catalog numbers
- Thin rules and registration marks
- Understated graphic coordinates
- Precise type hierarchy

## Key Design Features

### Premium Materials Focus

- Glossy black acetate with microscopic surface texture
- Polished reflections and controlled highlights

## Critical Restrictions

### Prohibited Elements

- No redesigned or distorted sunglasses
- No fake or altered Prada logos
- No unnecessary props or objects
- No excessive makeup or competing jewelry (in model shots)
- No random mechanical parts or futuristic styling
- No visual clutter or extra accessories
- No copyrighted Prada campaign reproductions
- No watermarks or creator signatures
- No social media interface elements
- No malformed products or floating parts

### Quality Standards

- Premium optical-product photography quality
- Vogue-level editorial photography (for model shots)
- Museum-quality object photography
- Luxury magazine art direction
- Realistic material rendering throughout

## Usage Notes

This collection provides a complete luxury fashion campaign framework, from hero product shots to editorial model imagery. Each prompt maintains strict product accuracy while exploring different visual storytelling approaches—technical documentation, material close-ups, heritage aesthetics, and contemporary fashion editorial.

Designed for premium brand campaigns requiring sophisticated Italian luxury aesthetics, architectural minimalism, and high-end commercial photography standards. All prompts emphasize restraint, precision, and timeless elegance over visual excess.
