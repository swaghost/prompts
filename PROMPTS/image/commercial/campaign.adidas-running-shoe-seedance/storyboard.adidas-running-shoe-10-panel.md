# Adidas Running Shoe - 10-Panel Product Storyboard

## Prerequisites

- Uploaded reference image of the black-and-white Adidas Cloudfoam running shoe
- ChatGPT Images or another reference-guided image model

## Storyboard Prompt

```text
Create a professional product-photography storyboard grid, 5 columns by 2 rows, 10 panels total. Label each panel with a number from 01-10 and a short title in the bottom-left corner like a film storyboard. Use black panel borders with thin green divider lines between rows, matching a cinematic ad-campaign layout.

PRODUCT: Use the black-and-white Adidas running shoe shown in the reference image, with its Cloudfoam sole, white three-stripe logo, and black mesh upper. Maintain exact product consistency across all 10 panels: same colorway, logo placement, proportions, mesh construction, heel, and sole design.

PANEL SEQUENCE:

01 - OPENING FRAME: Hands hold a black Adidas shoebox while lifting the lid, with dramatic side lighting.

02 - HERO PRODUCT REVEAL: Shoe inside tissue paper in the open box, close three-quarter angle.

03 - PURE PRODUCT HERO: Shoe on a white seamless background, clean studio lighting, side profile.

04 - TEXTURE DETAIL: Macro close-up of mesh fabric and stitching on the upper.

05 - LOGO AND LACE DETAIL: Close-up of the white three-stripe logo and laces in soft studio light.

06 - HEEL DETAIL: Close-up of the heel tab with Adidas branding and shallow depth of field.

07 - FIRST STEP IMPACT: Shoe striking a wet surface with the water splash frozen in motion.

08 - LIFESTYLE WEAR: Model's feet wearing the shoes while walking down stairs, cropped at the ankle.

09 - RUNNING MOTION: Side view of a model running on a city street, shoe razor sharp with slight background motion blur.

10 - FINAL HERO: Two shoes posed together on a reflective dark surface with dramatic rim lighting, full product beauty shot.

STYLE: High-end sneaker advertising photography, moody cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, consistent cool-neutral color grading with black backdrop accents, 16:9 aspect ratio inside every panel, ultra-realistic commercial photography quality.

Do not alter the shoe model, colorway, logo placement, sole geometry, proportions, or material construction. No duplicate shoes except in panel 10, no incorrect branding, no warped text, no deformed feet or hands, no inconsistent product between panels, no extra panels, no missing panels, no watermark.
```
