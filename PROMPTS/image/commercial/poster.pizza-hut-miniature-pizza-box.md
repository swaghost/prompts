# Pizza Hut - Miniature Pizza Box Commercial Poster

## Platform & Settings

**Format:** Vertical 4:5

**Style:** Premium surreal food advertising, photorealistic miniature-scale storytelling, sophisticated commercial campaign

**Lighting:** Cinematic studio lighting, warm highlights on pizza, realistic shadows

## Complete Prompt

```text
Create a premium surreal advertising poster for Pizza Hut, in a vertical 4:5 composition with a bold monochromatic deep Pizza Hut red background. The scene should look like a sophisticated commercial campaign with realistic miniature-scale storytelling.

Place a huge open red Pizza Hut pizza box in the center-lower portion of the image. The box should be angled slightly toward the camera, with the lid raised behind it. Clearly show authentic red packaging with the white Pizza Hut logo and subtle cardboard texture.

Inside the box, place a large freshly baked pepperoni pizza with a thick golden-brown crust, bubbling melted mozzarella, evenly distributed circular pepperoni slices, realistic cheese browning, and subtle steam. The pizza should look hot, fresh, glossy, and extremely appetizing.

Create a tiny realistic Pizza Hut employee standing beside the pizza box on the right. He should be dramatically small compared with the pizza, wearing a red Pizza Hut uniform shirt, dark pants, and a red cap. Have him operate or inspect a miniature industrial-looking pizza cutting/slicing mechanism positioned over the pizza, creating a whimsical oversized-world concept.

At the very top center, place the Pizza Hut logo in white with the red Hut-roof graphic.

At the bottom, add large clean white advertising typography:

"No One OutPizzas The Hut®"

Keep the composition minimal and highly controlled, with the pizza box and miniature employee as the main visual focus. Use realistic studio shadows beneath the box, subtle gradients in the red background, warm highlights on the pizza, crisp product photography, realistic cardboard fibers, melted cheese texture, detailed pepperoni, cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, premium food advertising aesthetic, photorealistic 4K UHD.
```
