# Universal Commercial Product Poster - Editorial E-Commerce Advertisement

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** ChatGPT/DALL-E (primary), MidJourney, Adobe Firefly

**Technical Specifications:**

- Aspect Ratio: 4:5 (vertical, ideal for Instagram, e-commerce)
- Lens: 85mm macro lens with slight low-angle perspective
- Style: Modern editorial e-commerce, premium commercial advertising
- Lighting: Soft three-point studio lighting
- Depth of Field: Ultra-shallow (product sharp, background softly blurred)

---

## Prompt Description

A universal prompt for creating premium, high-impact commercial product advertisements in modern editorial e-commerce style. Works with any product category by automatically adapting the lifestyle model, background environment, feature callouts, and visual styling to match the product's category, target audience, and branding. Creates magazine-quality compositions with hero product shot, professional model interaction, editorial typography, and relevant feature specifications.

---

## Complete Prompt

[Product Name]

Create a premium image, high-impact commercial infographic advertisement for [Product Name] in a modern editorial e-commerce style. Make the product the unmistakable hero of the composition, occupying 45–60% of the frame. Present it naturally according to its category (held, worn, displayed, installed, or in use), with cinematic perspective and shallow depth of field that keeps the product razor-sharp while softly blurring the surroundings. Include a professional lifestyle model that naturally matches the product's category, target audience, and branding. The model should interact with the product realistically and feel confident, approachable, and authentic. Adapt the model's age, clothing, styling, expression, and environment automatically to suit the product. Generate a premium background appropriate for the product category (studio, office, home, gym, luxury interior, automotive, outdoor, café, gaming setup, etc.). Add subtle floating versions of the same product, soft bokeh, light leaks, lens flares, reflections, and atmospheric depth to create a polished advertising look without distracting from the main product. Use a clean editorial layout with oversized semi-transparent background typography displaying the product category. Place [PRODUCT_NAME] prominently at the top with a short premium tagline. Automatically generate 4–6 relevant feature callouts with elegant icons based on the product category. Include only specifications that make sense for the product—never display irrelevant features. Use a premium color palette inspired by the product and its branding for perfect visual harmony. Maintain balanced spacing, refined typography, and a clean luxury aesthetic. Photography Style: Commercial product photography, 85mm macro lens, slight low-angle perspective, soft three-point studio lighting, ultra-shallow depth of field, premium reflections, hyper-realistic materials, crisp details, natural color grading. Design Style: Apple keynote quality, Nike campaign aesthetics, Samsung launch presentation, modern luxury editorial, premium e-commerce advertisement, minimal yet impactful, magazine-quality composition. Output Quality: Ultra-realistic, photorealistic, 8K, HDR, sharp focus, professional advertising, clean graphic design, visually striking, premium commercial artwork. in 4:5

## Structured Breakdown

### Product as Hero (45-60% of Frame)

**Presentation Modes (Automatic Category Adaptation):**

- **Held:** Smartphones, beverages, cosmetics, small gadgets
- **Worn:** Watches, shoes, jewelry, clothing, headphones
- **Displayed:** Laptops, home appliances, furniture, large electronics
- **Installed:** Automotive accessories, smart home devices
- **In Use:** Fitness equipment, gaming peripherals, kitchen tools

**Product Treatment:**

- Razor-sharp focus on product (hero element)
- Cinematic perspective with slight low-angle
- Ultra-shallow depth of field (f/1.4-f/2.8 equivalent)
- Premium reflections and hyper-realistic materials
- Natural positioning according to product category
- Unmistakable prominence in composition

### Professional Lifestyle Model

**Automatic Adaptation Based on Product:**

- **Age:** Matches target demographic (teen, young adult, professional, mature)
- **Clothing:** Suits product context (athletic wear for fitness, business attire for tech, casual for lifestyle)
- **Styling:** Hair, makeup, accessories appropriate to brand positioning
- **Expression:** Confident, approachable, authentic
- **Interaction:** Realistic product engagement (not forced or awkward)
- **Diversity:** Automatically varied to match target audience

**Model Behavior:**

- Natural confident posture
- Authentic product interaction
- Appropriate eye contact (camera or product)
- Professional yet approachable demeanor
- Believable real-world usage

### Atmospheric Depth Elements

- **Subtle floating product versions:** Same product duplicated with transparency
- **Soft bokeh:** Circular light orbs in background blur
- **Light leaks:** Cinematic lens flare effects
- **Lens flares:** Subtle optical effects from lighting
- **Reflections:** Product reflections on surfaces
- **Atmospheric particles:** Dust motes, light rays, subtle haze
- **Depth layers:** Foreground, midground, background separation

**Purpose:** Create polished advertising look without distracting from hero product

### Editorial Layout & Typography

**Background Typography:**

- Oversized semi-transparent text
- Displays product category (e.g., "SMARTPHONE", "ATHLETIC FOOTWEAR", "PREMIUM AUDIO")
- Integrated into composition without overpowering product

**Primary Text Elements:**

- **Product Name:** [PRODUCT_NAME] prominently at top
- **Tagline:** Short premium phrase (3-6 words)
- **Feature Callouts:** 4-6 relevant specifications with elegant icons

**Typography Style:**

- Clean sans-serif premium fonts
- Balanced spacing and hierarchy
- Minimal yet impactful

## Platform-Specific Tips

### ChatGPT/DALL-E (Primary Platform)

1. **Two input methods:**
   - Upload product image, then paste prompt
   - OR just type product name in prompt (no image needed)
2. **Replace [Product Name] and [PRODUCT_NAME]** with your specific product
3. **4:5 aspect ratio** is automatically applied
4. **Let AI choose everything** - model, background, features all auto-adapt
5. **Generate variations** - each generation offers unique model/styling
6. **Works with generic or branded products**
7. **No manual feature specification needed** - AI selects relevant specs

### MidJourney Adaptation

- Use `--ar 4:5` for vertical e-commerce format
- Add `--style raw` for photorealistic commercial quality
- Include `--stylize 700-900` for premium advertising polish
- Emphasize "commercial product photography with lifestyle model"
- Specify "editorial e-commerce advertisement style"
- Reference product image with URL or detailed description

### Adobe Firefly

- Select "Commercial Photography" preset
- Emphasize product hero prominence
- Request editorial layout with typography
- Specify premium e-commerce aesthetic

## Comparison: Universal Product Poster vs. Gravity-Style Poster

### Universal Product Poster (This Prompt)

- **Focus:** Product + lifestyle model in real-world context
- **Layout:** Editorial e-commerce with feature callouts
- **Model:** Professional lifestyle model interacting with product
- **Background:** Appropriate environment (office, gym, café, etc.)
- **Typography:** Product name, tagline, specification icons
- **Best For:** E-commerce, social media, general product marketing

### Gravity-Style Product Poster

- **Focus:** Product as gravitational center pulling elements
- **Layout:** Abstract artistic with vortex effect
- **Model:** No model, product-centric
- **Background:** Curved trajectories, particles, energy fields
- **Typography:** Minimal, product name only
- **Best For:** Eye-catching ads, artistic product showcases
