# McDonald's - Giant Burger Carton Commercial Poster

## Platform & Settings

**Format:** Vertical 4:5

**Style:** Surreal premium food advertising, photorealistic miniature photography, polished commercial art direction

**Lighting:** Cinematic soft lighting, realistic shadows, subtle studio gradient

## Complete Prompt

```text
Create a surreal premium advertising image for McDonald's, in a vertical 4:5 format with a deep dark forest-green background and subtle studio gradient. The concept should visually communicate the idea of an enormous burger through dramatically oversized packaging.

Place a gigantic dark-green McDonald's burger carton/package in the center. The packaging should be tall, rectangular, slightly curved, and designed with a bright yellow Golden Arches "M" logo prominently displayed on the front. The top of the package should be open, revealing an enormous realistic sesame-seed hamburger emerging from inside.

The burger should have a glossy golden sesame bun, fresh green lettuce, creamy sauce, melted yellow cheese, and a thick juicy beef patty. Make the burger look oversized, freshly prepared, and extremely appetizing, with realistic bun texture, sesame seeds, ingredient layers, and subtle highlights.

On the right side, add a tiny realistic McDonald's worker wearing a dark-green uniform and cap. The worker should appear miniature compared with the giant package. Have him use a large yellow measuring tape to measure the oversized packaging. The measuring tape should emerge from a small opening near the bottom-right side of the package, curve naturally across the floor, and contain clearly visible measurement markings.

Place another large yellow Golden Arches logo floating above the package near the top center.

At the bottom, add the classic white tagline:

"i'm lovin' it®"

Use highly realistic miniature photography, cinematic soft lighting, realistic shadows, subtle reflections, detailed packaging materials, convincing scale relationships, clean negative space, premium McDonald's-inspired yellow and green color palette, sharp product focus, shallow depth of field, polished advertising art direction, photorealistic 4K UHD.
```
