Create an exact 3:4, 1200 x 1600 px luxury jewellery product archive poster.

Use the uploaded necklace references exactly.

Create a spectacular emerald-and-ivory architectural archive room. The left third contains monumental ivory stone architecture with deep emerald-green recessed cavities. The right two-thirds contain a large black technical display board integrated into the architecture.

Show the exact necklace in multiple controlled views:

01 FRONT
02 THREE-QUARTER
03 SIDE / DEPTH
04 DETAIL

Arrange them like an elite jewellery design archive.

Add realistic technical measurements, dimension lines, crosshairs and construction annotations.

Headline:

PRODUCT ARCHIVE.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
No altered necklace design, no inconsistent views, no boring white background, no plain studio, no distorted necklace proportions, no unreadable labels, no extra text beyond requested information, no people, no jewellery box, no flowers, no CGI-looking jewellery, no watermark.
