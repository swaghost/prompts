## Description of Resulting Image

A dynamic 3:4 luxury jewelry poster where an enormous emerald-green sculptural ribbon sweeps around the exact necklace on a dark stone pedestal inside monumental ivory architecture.

## Usage

Perfect for motion-inspired product posters, fine jewelry campaigns, architectural luxury ads, emerald brand visuals, and energetic product layouts.

Ideal for creating an expensive, unexpected composition that implies movement without altering or touching the necklace.

## Image Prompt

Create a spectacular 3:4 vertical luxury jewellery poster, 1200 x 1600 px.

Use the exact uploaded necklace.

Create a huge architectural environment consisting of one enormous emerald-green sculptural ribbon sweeping dynamically through monumental ivory stone architecture.

The ribbon should curve around the necklace without touching or modifying it, creating a sense of motion.

The necklace sits on a dramatic dark stone pedestal at the center-right.

Background contains giant geometric shadows, curved structures, sunlit ivory surfaces and deep emerald cavities.

Headline:

IN MOTION.

Place typography vertically on the left.

Use cream typography with subtle emerald technical accents.

Add thin measurement lines and crosshairs.

The composition should feel energetic, expensive and unexpected, not static product photography.

Photorealistic luxury campaign, physically accurate materials, dramatic sunlight, realistic gemstone reflections.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
No altered necklace, no ribbon touching or modifying necklace, no static boring product shot, no generic room, no plain background, no people, no jewellery box, no flowers, no CGI-looking jewelry, no distorted typography, no watermark.
