# Man Portrait - Black Suit Chiaroscuro with Deep Black Background

## Prompt Type

Portrait Photography - Studio Chiaroscuro - High Contrast Minimalist

## Primary Platform

MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset, Stable Diffusion XL

## Core Concept

High-contrast portrait of a man in black suit against completely black background with soft chiaroscuro lighting, shallow depth of field, and minimalist color palette emphasizing photorealism and unaltered facial features.

## Detailed Prompt

```
A portrait of a man against a deep black background. The man is positioned in the center of the frame, looking into the camera at a 3/4 angle. The shot is medium-length, from the waist to the top of the head. The camera is positioned slightly below eye level, pointed upward at a slight angle. The gaze is directed toward the camera. His arms are crossed over his chest.

The man is wearing a black suit with a clean, fitted silhouette. The jacket has clean lines, the fabric is smooth, with a soft matte or subtle satin reflection. Under the jacket, his shirt is buttoned to the collar, without a tie. The overall appearance is neat, without unnecessary details.

The lighting is soft and directional. The main light is positioned from the front, slightly offset, creating a chiaroscuro pattern. The light emphasizes the contours of the face, the jawline, and cheekbones. The shadows are soft but readable, without sharp edges. The contrast is moderate, preserving detail in the highlights and shadows. Light enhances depth and volume, creating smooth transitions between illuminated and darkened areas.

The background is completely black, uniform, without texture or detail. It isolates the figure and enhances the contrast with the skin and clothing. The depth of field is shallow: the face and upper body are in full focus, the background is completely blurred.

The color palette is minimalist—black and warm skin tones. Photorealism is high. The face is unaltered.
```
