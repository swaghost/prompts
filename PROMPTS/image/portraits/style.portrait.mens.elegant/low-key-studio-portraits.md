## Common Technical Elements

### Consistent Identity Features

- Slim masculine face
- Sharp jawline
- Natural skin texture (no beautification)
- Realistic anatomy (no distortion)

### Wardrobe

- Black shirt
- Slightly unbuttoned collar
- Matte fabric (not shiny or glossy)

### Studio Setup

- Low-key studio photography
- Dark background
- Deep shadows
- High contrast
- Black or minimal background

### Lighting Technique

- Soft diffused key light with strong shadow falloff
- Cinematic lighting approach
- Directional light for definition
- Minimal fill light to maintain contrast
- Rim light for silhouette emphasis (Prompt 1)
- Top light for dramatic shadows (Prompt 5)

### Camera Specifications

- **Prompt 1:** Medium shot, 85mm lens
- **Prompt 2:** Slightly low angle, 50mm lens
- **Prompt 3:** 35mm lens (for motion)
- **Prompt 4:** 50mm lens, eye-level or slightly low angle
- **Prompt 5:** Ultra low angle, 24mm lens

### Style & Aesthetic

- Editorial fashion photography
- Ultra realistic rendering
- No distortion (except intentional in Prompt 5)
- Cinematic quality
- Professional campaign style
- Strong shadow falloff
- Clean editorial composition

## Usage Notes

### Best For:

- **Fashion editorials** — Masculine elegance and sophistication
- **Magazine spreads** — High-end men's fashion publications
- **Campaign photography** — Luxury menswear brands
- **Portfolio work** — Fashion photographers and models
- **Social media content** — Professional masculine aesthetic

### AI Generation Tips:

1. **Maintain identity consistency** — Use same reference photo across all 5 variations
2. **Emphasize "no beautification"** — Preserves masculine features authentically
3. **Low-key lighting is critical** — Deep shadows define editorial fashion look
4. **Matte fabric specification** — Prevents unrealistic shine on clothing
5. **Each prompt offers different mood** — Choose based on desired narrative

### Customization Options:

- **Change wardrobe color** — Navy, charcoal, burgundy (keep matte fabric)
- **Add accessories** — Watch, ring, necklace (keep minimal)
- **Adjust lighting intensity** — More/less shadow for different drama levels
- **Modify expressions** — Serious, slight smile, intense gaze
- **Background variations** — Textured wall, urban setting (keep low-key)
