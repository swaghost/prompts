# Look #03: Jungle Stream Stance - Full-Body Fashion Swimsuit Portrait

## Description

Ultra-realistic full-body luxury tropical fashion portrait in dense jungle rainforest, photographed from dramatic low angle looking upward creating tall elegant striking appearance. Subject standing barefoot on pale natural stones and rocks in middle of lush jungle with natural stream nearby. Wearing metallic olive-gold/bronze-green one-piece swimsuit with deep halter neckline, open front cut, gathered knot detail at center, and fitted silhouette matching reference image (@image2). Graceful elongated pose with both arms lifted above head holding or brushing palm leaf. Not looking directly at camera - gaze soft and slightly off to side or downward with calm natural expression. Bright tropical sunlight streaming through palm leaves creating sun flare, realistic leaf-shadow patterns, and warm golden patches across body. Part of the Green Summer portrait collection emphasizing rich green-toned styling, gold jewelry accents, and elevated candid luxury aesthetic.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Swimsuit design doesn't match @image2 reference

**Solution:** Ensure @image2 uploaded correctly, emphasize all specific design elements in prompt (halter neckline, knot detail, color), regenerate

### Problem: Camera angle not low enough or not looking up

**Solution:** Strongly emphasize "low angle looking upward," "so she appears tall and striking," "photographed from below," regenerate

### Problem: Subject looking at camera instead of away

**Solution:** Emphasize "NOT looking directly into camera," "gaze soft slightly off to side or downward," "calm natural effortless expression"

### Problem: Arms/hands position wrong

**Solution:** Specify "both arms lifted above head," "one forearm curving above head," "other hand holding or brushing palm leaf," may need regeneration for correct pose

### Problem: Lighting too flat or not tropical enough

**Solution:** Emphasize "real natural sunlight streaming through palm leaves," "bright highlights, sun flare," "leaf-shadow patterns," "warm golden patches," "high brightness"

### Problem: Swimsuit color wrong

**Solution:** Be very specific: "metallic olive-gold / bronze-green tone," include color codes if possible, emphasize "luxury metallic fabric with sheen"

### Problem: Body or proportions look unrealistic

**Solution:** Emphasize "realistic body proportions," "natural athletic physique," "true-to-life," negative prompt "unrealistic body, distorted anatomy"
