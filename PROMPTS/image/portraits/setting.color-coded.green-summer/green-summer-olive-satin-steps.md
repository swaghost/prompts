## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Identity doesn't match reference face

**Solution:** Use clearer face reference, increase character weight (--cw 75-100 in Midjourney), regenerate 2-3 times, ensure prompt emphasizes identity preservation

### Problem: Skin looks too perfect/plastic

**Solution:** Emphasize "visible pores, micro texture, natural tonal variation" in prompt, add "plastic skin, over-smoothed" to negative prompt, use --style raw in Midjourney

### Problem: Camera angle not overhead

**Solution:** Emphasize "camera angle from above looking down" and "elevated position at top of stairs angled downward," may need to regenerate

### Problem: Hands look distorted

**Solution:** Specify "5 fingers visible," "relaxed elegant fingers," "natural hand position," regenerate if needed

### Problem: Dress color wrong

**Solution:** Be specific: "rich olive green #556B2F," "botanical olive green," include both color name and mood descriptor

### Problem: Lighting too artificial

**Solution:** Remove studio lighting language, emphasize "natural summer daylight only," specify "warm sunlight reflecting off stone walls"
