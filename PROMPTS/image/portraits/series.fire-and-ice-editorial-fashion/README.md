## Shared Requirements

- **Model:** Seedream 5.0 Pro
- **Format:** 4:5
- **Reference:** Attach the same ice-blue gown reference image with every prompt.
- **Wardrobe:** Floor-length pale ice-blue silk chiffon gown, ruched sweetheart bodice, thin straps, enormous loose circle skirt, bare feet, long dark hair loose.
- **Skin:** Visible pores, fine vellus facial hair, natural unevenness, unretouched skin, no smoothing, no waxy sheen, accurate subsurface scattering.
- **Quality:** Photorealistic, natural film grain, high-fashion magazine quality.
