## CONCEPT

Photorealistic ultra-close portrait with dramatic chiaroscuro lighting. The entire image is rendered in high-contrast black and white except for the vivid green mirrored tint of retro-style round sunglasses, which remain in full color. Creates powerful, mysterious, rebellious atmosphere through selective color grading and extreme close cropping.

**Key Visual Elements:**

- Partial face view with tight intimate cropping
- One side in deep shadow, opposite side powerfully lit
- Black and white with selective green color on sunglasses only
- Retro round sunglasses with mirrored green lenses
- Sharp focus on eyes behind lenses and confident smirk
- Visible skin texture: pores, beard stubble, grain
- Dark blurred background isolating subject

## LIGHTING TECHNICAL DETAILS

### Chiaroscuro Setup

**Classic 1:3 Ratio (Recommended):**

- **Light side:** 33% of face fully illuminated
- **Shadow side:** 67% of face in deep shadow
- **Transition:** Sharp, defined shadow edge (not gradual)
- **Key light:** Single powerful directional source

**Light Position:**

- 45° to side of subject
- Slightly elevated above eye level
- Creates downward shadow edge across cheekbone
- Sculpts jawline with shadow underneath

**Shadow Quality:**

- **Deep:** True blacks, cinematic darkness
- **Defined:** Sharp edge between light and shadow
- **Purposeful:** Not accidental or muddy
- **Dramatic:** Creates mystery and intensity

### Alternative Lighting Styles

**Rembrandt Lighting:**

- Triangle of light on shadow side cheek
- Classic portrait technique
- Slightly softer than full chiaroscuro

**Split Lighting:**

- Exactly 50/50 light-to-shadow (1:1 ratio)
- Light from direct side
- Maximum drama and symmetry

**Broad Lighting:**

- Light illuminates side of face toward camera
- Shadow on far side
- More flattering, less dramatic

**Short Lighting:**

- Shadow on side of face toward camera
- Light on far side
- Slimming, most dramatic

## POST-PROCESSING GUIDE

If AI doesn't achieve perfect selective color, use this workflow:

### Photoshop Method

1. **Open image** in Photoshop
2. **Desaturate:** Image > Adjustments > Desaturate (or Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + U)
3. **Add Layer:** Create new layer from original colored version
4. **Mask:** Add black layer mask to color layer
5. **Paint:** Use white brush on mask to reveal color only on sunglasses
6. **Refine:** Adjust mask edge for precision
7. **Enhance:** Boost green saturation if needed (Hue/Saturation adjustment)
8. **Contrast:** Increase B&W contrast (Curves adjustment)

### Lightroom Method

1. **Import image** to Lightroom
2. **Increase contrast:** Push Contrast and Clarity sliders
3. **Convert to B&W:** Set Saturation to -100
4. **Brush tool:** Select Adjustment Brush
5. **Saturation:** Set brush Saturation to +100
6. **Paint:** Carefully paint over sunglasses only
7. **Refine:** Use Auto Mask for precision
8. **Boost green:** Adjust green luminance and saturation

### Mobile Apps

**Snapseed (Free):**

1. Open image
2. Tools > Selective (or use Brush)
3. Desaturate entire image
4. Use Selective tool on sunglasses only
5. Increase saturation to 100 on that area

**Lightroom Mobile:**

1. Same process as desktop Lightroom
2. Use Selective Edits > Brush
3. Paint saturation back onto sunglasses

## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

### Reference Photo Quality

- **Resolution:** High quality source image essential
- **Angle:** Three-quarter or profile works best
- **Lighting:** Can be any (will be replaced)
- **Expression:** Neutral or serious preferred (easier to modify)
- **Clarity:** Sharp, in-focus face required

### Generation Process

1. **Generate in color first** - Easier to control all elements
2. **Check composition** - Ensure tight crop and angle correct
3. **Verify lighting** - Chiaroscuro ratio should be clear
4. **Assess sunglasses** - Style and placement accurate
5. **Apply selective color** - Post-process if needed
6. **Boost contrast** - Final B&W should be dramatic

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Issue: Entire image is B&W including sunglasses**

- **Fix:** Post-process to restore color, or regenerate with stronger emphasis
- **Prompt fix:** "CRITICAL: sunglasses lenses are the ONLY element in vivid green color"

**Issue: Too much of image is in color**

- **Fix:** Post-process to remove unwanted color
- **Prompt fix:** "Everything except sunglasses completely desaturated black and white"

**Issue: Lighting too flat, no drama**

- **Fix:** Emphasize chiaroscuro ratio more strongly
- **Prompt fix:** "Extreme chiaroscuro with deep blacks on shadow side::2"

**Issue: Crop not tight enough**

- **Fix:** Crop in post or regenerate
- **Prompt fix:** "Extremely tight ultra-close crop showing only partial face"

**Issue: Skin too smooth**

- **Fix:** Regenerate with texture emphasis
- **Prompt fix:** "Visible pores, beard stubble, skin grain texture, NOT smoothed"
