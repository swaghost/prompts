# Colossal Woman Resting Across Machu Picchu

## Category

Surreal Scale Manipulation / Candid Documentary Composite / Cultural Landmark Photography

## Style

Hyper-realistic aerial documentary photograph, intimate giant-scale humor, misty Andean dawn

## Concept

A colossal woman lies across the full length of Machu Picchu as if taking five more minutes, with her body weight visibly integrated into the ancient site and the surrounding landscape.
