# Macro Miniature Portrait - Sitting on Real Person's Shoulder

## Category

Macro Photography / Miniature Portrait / Portrait Photography

## Style

Hyper-Realistic Portrait / Premium Editorial / Playful Conceptual

## Concept

Hyper-realistic portrait where tiny realistic miniature version of uploaded person is comfortably sitting on real person's shoulder, casually talking to them.

## Prompt

Use the uploaded photo as the exact identity reference. Create a hyper-realistic portrait where a tiny realistic version of the uploaded person is comfortably sitting on the real person's shoulder, casually talking to them. Preserve exact facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, skin tone, and natural facial expression.

**IDENTITY PRESERVATION:**

- Exact facial identity matching uploaded photo
- Exact hairstyle preserved
- Exact clothing preserved
- Exact skin tone preserved
- Natural facial expression preserved

**COMPOSITION:**

- Portrait orientation showing real person's head, shoulder, and upper torso
- Miniature person sitting comfortably on shoulder
- Miniature's body language casual and conversational
- Both real person and miniature visible in frame
- Natural interaction between them suggested
- Scale: miniature appears tiny compared to real person

**MINIATURE PERSON DETAILS:**

- Sitting posture natural and relaxed
- Legs dangling or crossed casually
- Body angled toward real person (conversational stance)
- Facial expression friendly, talking, or smiling
- Ultra-realistic skin texture at miniature scale
- Clothing details preserved despite small size

**REAL PERSON DETAILS:**

- Natural portrait pose
- Realistic skin textures
- Shoulder and neck anatomically accurate
- Clothing/fabric realistic
- Can show real person's face (partial or full) or focus on shoulder area

**INTERACTION:**

- Miniature appears to be in mid-conversation
- Natural social body language
- Comfortable, friendly relationship suggested
- Eye contact or turned toward each other

**LIGHTING & BACKGROUND:**

- Soft natural light or cinematic portrait lighting
- Shallow depth of field (both figures sharp, background soft)
- Realistic skin tones on both scales
- Background softly blurred
- Premium commercial quality lighting

**TECHNICAL DETAILS:**

- Hyper-realistic skin textures (both scales)
- Cinematic lighting with natural shadows
- Shallow depth of field appropriate for portrait
- Premium editorial photography quality
- Photorealistic, 8K

## Critical Avoidances

- ❌ Stylized or cartoonish miniature rendering

- ❌ Over-processed or artificial skin textures
- ❌ Forced or unnatural poses/expressions
- ❌ Awkward or unnatural interaction
