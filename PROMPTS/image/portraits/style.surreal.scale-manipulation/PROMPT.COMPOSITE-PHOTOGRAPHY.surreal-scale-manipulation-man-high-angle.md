# Surreal Scale-Manipulation Composite - Man from High Angle

## Category

Composite Photography / Surreal Scale Manipulation

## Style

Out-of-Bounds Scale / Hyper-Realistic Composite / Casual Streetwear

## Prompt

Surreal out-of-bounds scale-manipulation photograph, vertical 4:5, casual streetwear aesthetic, hyper-realistic composite, shot from a HIGH ANGLE looking down.

The torso of a man seen from above, wearing an oversized cropped brown-taupe washed cotton work jacket with a full-length zipper, a flap chest pocket and boxy sleeves, worn open over a white ribbed tank top, with wide-leg BLACK trousers filling the lower third of the frame and chunky black leather lug-sole loafers - only his torso, hands and legs are visible in foreshortening, his head out of frame.

A black belt with a large oval polished silver buckle, and a small carabiner with keys clipped to the belt loop hanging over the black trousers. He holds a smartphone in both open hands, fingers extended, palms relaxed as if presenting an offering large hands in the foreground wearing several chunky silver rings.

The man appears impossibly large in the urban environment, as if he is a giant. The foreshortening and high angle create a surreal out-of-bounds effect.
