# Hyper-Realistic Macro Miniature Portrait Transformations

## Overview

Transform uploaded portrait photos into hyper-realistic macro photography scenarios where a **tiny realistic miniature version** of the person interacts with real-world objects or body parts at extreme scale. All variations preserve exact facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, skin tone, and natural facial expression while creating playful, premium-quality macro compositions.

- **Cinematic lighting** (soft, natural, or dramatic as appropriate)

## Universal Application Notes

### Identity Preservation Workflow

1. **Reference uploaded photo carefully** for all facial features
2. **Maintain exact hairstyle** including color, texture, length, style
3. **Preserve clothing** including colors, patterns, fit, style
4. **Match skin tone precisely** including undertones and natural variations
5. **Replicate facial expression** naturally (not forced or artificial)

### Macro Photography Standards

- **100mm macro lens equivalent** for authentic macro perspective
- **Shallow depth of field** appropriate to focal length and subject distance
- **Ultra-realistic textures** at extreme magnification
- **Natural lighting behavior** (reflections, shadows, highlights)
- **Premium editorial quality** matching high-end commercial photography

### Scale & Proportion Guidelines

- **Miniature person typically 2-4 inches tall** in concept (scale varies by scenario)
- **Proportions remain human** (not stylized or cartoonish)
- **Physics and lighting behave realistically** at both scales
- **Interaction points physically believable** (sitting, standing, holding objects)

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- ❌ Stylized or cartoonish miniature rendering
- ❌ Incorrect proportions (oversized head, etc.)
- ❌ Identity drift (miniature doesn't match reference)
- ❌ Unrealistic lighting (mismatched between scales)
- ❌ Over-processed or artificial skin textures
- ❌ Forced or unnatural poses/expressions

### Quality Benchmarks

- ✅ Instant recognition: "That's definitely the same person, just tiny"
- ✅ Photorealistic believability: "This looks like a real macro photograph"
- ✅ Technical excellence: Sharp focus, proper depth of field, accurate lighting
- ✅ Creative impact: Surprising, playful, premium quality
- ✅ Commercial viability: Editorial/advertising photography standards
