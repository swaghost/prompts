# Cinematic Futuristic Portrait - Reflective Chrome Elements

## Category

Character Portrait / Editorial Photography

## Style

Cinematic Futuristic / High-Tech Luxury

## Prompt

Cinematic futuristic portrait of a young man, framed from mid-torso up, slightly right of center, facing three-quarters left and looking directly toward the camera with a calm, intense expression. Thick wavy dark hair, clean natural face, wearing a fitted black button-up shirt.

Behind him is a dramatic tunnel of hundreds of long reflective chrome/glass metallic strips and panels radiating outward from behind his head, creating a powerful futuristic burst perspective. Bright white linear lights and warm golden-orange reflections run between the reflective surfaces. Strong directional light passes through the gaps, casting sharp horizontal bands of white light and shadow across his forehead, eyes, cheeks, neck and black shirt.

Ultra-detailed reflections, glossy chrome, deep blacks, cinematic contrast, shallow depth of field, realistic skin texture, dramatic futuristic luxury aesthetic, photorealistic, vertical 4:5.

## Technical Specifications

**Aspect Ratio:** Vertical 4:5  
**Composition:** Mid-torso up, slightly right of center  
**Subject Position:** Three-quarters left, direct camera gaze  
**Depth of Field:** Shallow  
**Lighting:** Strong directional, horizontal bands, white and golden-orange
