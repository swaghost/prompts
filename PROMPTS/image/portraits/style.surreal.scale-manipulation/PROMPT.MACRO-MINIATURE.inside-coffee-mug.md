# Macro Miniature Portrait - Inside Coffee Mug

## Category

Macro Photography / Miniature Portrait / Lifestyle Product Photography

## Style

Extreme Macro Close-Up / Premium Editorial / Cozy Lifestyle

## Concept

Extreme macro close-up focused almost entirely on ceramic coffee mug. Mug dominates frame while tiny realistic miniature person is comfortably living inside with their legs hanging over edge, looking inside coffee.

## Prompt

Use the uploaded photo as the exact identity reference. Create an extreme macro close-up photograph focused almost entirely on a ceramic coffee mug. The mug dominates the frame while a tiny realistic miniature version of the uploaded person is comfortably living inside it, sitting on the edge with their legs hanging over the edge, looking inside the coffee. Preserve the exact facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, skin tone, and natural facial expression.

**IDENTITY PRESERVATION:**

- Exact facial identity matching uploaded photo
- Exact hairstyle preserved
- Exact clothing preserved
- Exact skin tone preserved
- Natural facial expression preserved

**COMPOSITION:**

- Ceramic coffee mug fills most of frame (extreme macro)
- Mug edge sharp and detailed in foreground
- Tiny miniature person sitting on mug rim
- Legs dangling naturally over edge into coffee
- Person looking down into coffee with relaxed posture
- Scale: person appears tiny compared to massive mug

**MUG & COFFEE DETAILS:**

- Realistic ceramic texture with subtle imperfections
- Coffee surface shows realistic reflections
- Gentle steam rising from hot coffee (optional)
- Coffee surface has natural meniscus near mug walls
- Realistic liquid behavior and surface tension

**BACKGROUND:**

- Softly blurred background
- Subtle hints of cozy cafe or kitchen environment
- Warm ambient lighting suggestions (bokeh optional)
- Background does not distract from main subject

**TECHNICAL DETAILS:**

- Extreme macro photography, 100mm macro lens
- Ultra-realistic skin tones and facial details on miniature
- Shallow depth of field (mug edge and person sharp, background soft)
- Cinematic lighting with warm tones
- Realistic ceramic textures and reflections
- Premium editorial product photography
- Photorealistic, masterpiece quality, 8K
