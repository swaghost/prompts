# Surreal Scale-Manipulation Composite - Woman Between Buildings

## Category

Composite Photography / Surreal Scale Manipulation

## Style

Monumental Scale / Hyper-Realistic Urban Composite

## Prompt

Surreal monumental scale-manipulation composite photograph, vertical 4:5, hyper-realistic with a casual urban aesthetic, captured from an extremely low street-level angle, looking almost straight upward between two buildings.

**Composition:** A concrete residential apartment tower enters from the bottom-right corner and rises diagonally toward the upper-right edge of the frame.

A much darker, slightly out-of-focus building occupies the upper-left corner. Between them, a triangular wedge of open sky creates the negative space where the subject appears, emphasizing a dramatic sense of confinement and towering verticality.

A colossal young woman using the face of the reference person, approximately 15–20 stories tall, is peeking out from behind the building with only her upper torso visible. Her expression is curious, playful, or slightly mischievous. The scale manipulation makes her appear as a giant in the urban environment.
