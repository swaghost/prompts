# Macro Miniature Portrait - Cleaning Eyeglasses Lens

## Category

Macro Photography / Miniature Portrait / Product Interaction

## Style

Hyper-Realistic Macro / Premium Product Photography / Playful Conceptual

## Concept

Tiny realistic miniature person standing on frame of real person's eyeglasses, cleaning one of the lenses with a miniature cloth.

## Prompt

Use the uploaded photo as the exact identity reference. Create a hyper-realistic macro photograph where a tiny realistic version of the uploaded person is standing on the frame of the real person's eyeglasses, cleaning one of the lenses with a miniature cloth. Preserve exact facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, skin tone, and natural facial expression.

**IDENTITY PRESERVATION:**

- Exact facial identity matching uploaded photo
- Exact hairstyle preserved
- Exact clothing preserved
- Exact skin tone preserved
- Natural facial expression preserved

**COMPOSITION:**

- Eyeglasses frame dominates foreground
- Miniature person standing on frame bridge or lens rim
- Tiny cleaning cloth in miniature person's hands
- Person actively wiping lens surface
- Scale: person tiny compared to massive glasses
- One lens being cleaned (shows streaks, shine, or cleaning motion)

**EYEGLASSES DETAILS:**

- Ultra-realistic reflections on glass lenses
- Realistic frame material (metal, plastic, acetate)
- Lens shows subtle cleaning marks or shine
- Realistic glass thickness and refraction
- Frame details sharp in macro focus

**MINIATURE PERSON DETAILS:**

- Natural working posture (leaning, reaching, wiping)
- Body language shows active cleaning motion
- Facial expression focused or concentrated
- Tiny cloth texture visible
- Realistic proportions and skin details

**BACKGROUND:**

- Softly blurred hints of environment
- Suggests desk, table, or personal space
- Warm natural lighting or soft studio light

**TECHNICAL DETAILS:**

- Ultra-realistic reflections on lenses
- Cinematic lighting with soft highlights
- Shallow depth of field (glasses and person sharp)
- Premium editorial photography quality
- Photorealistic, ultra detailed, 8K
