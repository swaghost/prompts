# Macro Miniature Portrait - First-Person POV Holding Collectible

## Category

Macro Photography / Miniature Portrait / First-Person POV

## Style

Hyper-Realistic Macro / Premium Collectible Photography / POV Perspective

## Concept

First-person POV photograph where realistic human hands hold a tiny collectible version of uploaded person. Miniature looking directly toward camera with realistic proportions and premium collectible quality.

## Prompt

Use the uploaded photo as the exact identity reference. Create a first-person POV photograph where realistic human hands are holding a tiny collectible version of the uploaded person. Preserve the exact facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, and facial expression. The miniature is looking directly toward the camera with realistic proportions and premium collectible quality.

**IDENTITY PRESERVATION (MINIATURE):**

- Exact facial identity matching uploaded photo
- Exact hairstyle preserved
- Exact clothing preserved precisely
- Exact skin tone preserved
- Natural facial expression preserved

**POV COMPOSITION:**

- First-person perspective: camera looking down slightly
- Realistic human hands cupping or holding miniature
- Hands in natural holding position
- Miniature figure centered in palm or between fingers
- Person's gaze directed toward camera (eye contact)

**MINIATURE FIGURE DETAILS:**

- Premium collectible figure quality
- Ultra-realistic skin textures at tiny scale
- Realistic hair texture and details
- Clothing materials accurately reproduced
- Natural standing pose or casual stance
- Proportions accurate to real human body

**HAND DETAILS:**

- Realistic skin texture on hands
- Natural hand position and finger placement
- Subtle shadows cast on palm
- Realistic lighting on skin

**LIGHTING & BACKGROUND:**

- Macro photography lighting
- Shallow depth of field (miniature sharp, background soft)
- Natural daylight or soft studio light
- Realistic skin tones on both hands and miniature
- Background suggests home or studio environment (blurred)

**TECHNICAL DETAILS:**

- Cinematic composition
- Photorealistic rendering
- Ultra detailed facial features on miniature
- Premium collectible photography aesthetic
- 8K quality
