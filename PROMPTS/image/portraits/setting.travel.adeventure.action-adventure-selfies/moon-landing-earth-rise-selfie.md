# Moon Landing Selfie - Earth Rising Background

## Prompt Type

Space Exploration Photography - Lunar Surface Selfie

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Ultra-photorealistic smartphone selfie standing on the Moon. Earth appears enormous above the horizon while the first rays of sunlight illuminate the gray lunar landscape. The young man wears a modern EVA spacesuit with his visor open enough to clearly reveal his face. The selfie captures realistic lunar dust, subtle reflections inside the visor, and breathtaking silence. The lighting is soft, crisp, and scientifically grounded.
