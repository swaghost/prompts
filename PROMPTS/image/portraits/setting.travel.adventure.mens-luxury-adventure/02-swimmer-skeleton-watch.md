# Swimmer - Skeleton Watch

## Camera and Lighting

**Shot:** Water level, golden hour

## Full Prompt

```text
A white male, late 20s, fair skin with a light tan, short dark brown hair soaked and pushed straight back, light stubble and a faint mustache. Lean swimmer's build.

Expression: mouth slightly open, eyes fixed steadily on the lens, brows level. Calm, appraising, completely still.

Pose: treading water with the shoulders just breaking the surface. Right hand raised out of the water and curled loosely under the chin with the knuckles against the jaw, the forearm angled across the frame so the watch sits at the centre of the composition. Left shoulder submerged.

Details: a black and clear-lensed dive mask pushed up onto the forehead with the strap around the back of the wet hair. A large skeletonised tonneau-cased sports watch on the right wrist - white ceramic case, exposed movement visible through the open dial, pale blue rubber strap, water beading on the crystal.

Setting: open sea in the late afternoon, small ripples catching warm light. Behind him, a large gunmetal grey sports yacht at anchor with a swept windscreen, an orange fender over the side and a figure standing on the foredeck. A dark treeline coastline at the far left. Pale blue sky with soft white cloud.

Shot angle: camera right at water level so the waterline cuts across the lower third of the frame, about a metre away, wide lens with slight barrel distortion, a couple of water droplets on the front element.

Lighting: warm low golden-hour sun from the side - soft warm highlights on the wet shoulders and forearm, gentle contrast, muted teal water against warm skin, no harsh shadow.

Sharp high-resolution capture, clean and noise-free, filmic warm grade, hyper-realistic wet skin and water texture. Photorealistic.
```
