## Image Prompt

Realistic editorial photograph of a young adult woman standing in a narrow, floor-to-ceiling bookstore aisle, leaning slightly against the shelf, both hands holding an open hardcover book at chest height, eyes cast down and fully absorbed in reading, completely unaware of the camera. She wears thin gold wire-frame reading glasses slipping slightly down her nose, an oversized pale cream cable-knit sweater with sleeves loosely rolled, and dark hair pulled back into a low ponytail with a few loose strands framing her face. Warm brass reading lamps mounted between the packed wooden shelves cast pools of amber light, with the aisle receding into soft shadow behind her and a wooden rolling ladder faintly visible in the background. Natural fair skin with visible fine pore texture, a faint warm flush across the cheeks and nose from the lamp light, small authentic under-eye texture and a faint blemish near the jaw, no airbrushing or digital smoothing. Shot on a Sony A7III equivalent, 50mm lens, f/1.4, shallow depth of field with the far shelves softly out of focus, warm tungsten color grade with rich shadow detail, quiet intimate documentary-editorial realism.

Suggested settings: Camera: 50mm f/1.4. Light: warm brass tungsten sconces, single source. Grade: warm shadow-rich tungsten. Mood: quiet, absorbed, unposed.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Avoid fake reading pose, eye contact with camera, plastic skin, airbrushing, warped glasses, unreadable book shape, empty shelves, harsh white light, modern sterile bookstore, extra fingers, CGI look, text, logos, and watermarks.
