## Available Styles

### 1. [Editorial Candid Portrait](editorial-candid-portrait-mens.md)

**Style:** Ultra-realistic candid portraits with natural spontaneous moments  
**Lighting:** Natural golden hour warm sunlight  
**Technical:** 85mm f/1.8, 3:4 vertical, shallow depth of field  
**Wardrobe:** Neutral tones, loose white shirts, casual blazers  
**Mood:** Authentic, natural, spontaneous, warm  
**Best For:** Lifestyle brands, authentic editorial, social media, personal branding

### 2. [Studio Fashion Creative Angles](studio-fashion-creative-angles-mens.md)

**Style:** Extreme wide-angle fisheye distortion, bold editorial angles  
**Lighting:** Hard studio flash, beauty dish, high contrast  
**Technical:** 14mm fisheye f/5.6-f/11, centered composition  
**Wardrobe:** Y2K aesthetic, vintage tech props (Razr phone), bold colors  
**Mood:** Edgy, experimental, retro-futuristic  
**Best For:** Fashion-forward brands, editorial statements, creative campaigns

### 3. [Cinematic Moody Side Profile](cinematic-moody-side-profile-mens.md)

**Style:** Dramatic studio lighting with sophisticated atmosphere  
**Lighting:** Soft moody studio key light, dramatic shadows  
**Technical:** 85mm/135mm f/1.8-2.8, side profile composition  
**Wardrobe:** Layered urban (hoodie + denim jacket), vintage sunglasses  
**Mood:** Sophisticated, mysterious, cinematic, moody  
**Best For:** Luxury menswear, GQ-style editorials, premium brands

### 4. [Analog Film Photography](analog-film-photography-mens.md)

**Style:** Authentic analog film aesthetic with grain and imperfections  
**Lighting:** Warm tungsten interior, natural mixed light  
**Technical:** Simulates disposable camera/Kodak Gold 200, heavy grain  
**Wardrobe:** Retro 1980s-90s sportswear, vintage jerseys  
**Mood:** Nostalgic, authentic, casual, documentary  
**Best For:** Vintage-inspired campaigns, nostalgic branding, retro aesthetics

### 5. [Street Style Fashion](street-style-fashion-mens.md)

**Style:** Contemporary street style in real urban environments  
**Lighting:** Natural daylight, overcast soft light, golden hour  
**Technical:** 35mm/50mm/85mm f/2-4, documentary + editorial approach  
**Wardrobe:** Sneaker culture, layered streetwear, contemporary casual  
**Mood:** Urban cool, confident, contemporary, accessible  
**Best For:** Streetwear brands, lifestyle content, fashion blogs, social media

### 6. [Luxury High-Fashion Editorial](luxury-high-fashion-editorial-mens.md)

**Style:** Premium menswear with sophisticated minimal aesthetic  
**Lighting:** Controlled studio or natural window light, flawless quality  
**Technical:** 85mm f/1.4, 50mm f/1.2, medium format preferred  
**Wardrobe:** Tailored suiting, cashmere, premium materials, impeccable fit  
**Mood:** Refined, elevated, sophisticated, timeless  
**Best For:** Luxury brands, high-fashion magazines, designer lookbooks

## Prompt Writing Tips

### Essential Elements to Include

1. **Technical Specifications:**
   - Camera/lens details
   - Aperture and depth of field
   - Resolution/quality descriptors

2. **Lighting Description:**
   - Light source and direction
   - Quality (soft, hard, moody)
   - Time of day or studio setup

3. **Subject Details:**
   - Wardrobe specifics
   - Pose and expression
   - Accessories and styling

4. **Environmental Context:**
   - Background and setting
   - Atmospheric elements
   - Contextual storytelling

5. **Style References:**
   - Photography style keywords
   - Color grading description
   - Reference aesthetic/mood

### Keywords for Better Results

**Quality/Resolution:**

- "ultra realistic", "8K", "ultra detailed", "DSLR quality"
- "high resolution", "photorealistic", "professional photography"

**Depth of Field:**

- "shallow depth of field", "f/1.8", "background blur", "bokeh"
- "selective focus", "subject separation"

**Lighting:**

- "soft lighting", "golden hour", "moody lighting", "natural light"
- "studio lighting", "dramatic shadows", "warm highlights"

**Texture:**

- "realistic skin texture", "fabric detail", "detailed", "textured"
- "visible grain" (for film), "sharp detail"

**Style:**

- "editorial photography", "fashion photography", "cinematic"
- "documentary style", "candid", "artistic"

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Too Generic:** "A man wearing clothes" - be specific  
✓ **Specific:** "A man wearing a grey hoodie under a black denim jacket"

❌ **Conflicting Styles:** Mixing analog film grain with ultra-sharp studio  
✓ **Coherent:** Choose one aesthetic approach

❌ **Unrealistic Combinations:** Fisheye lens with shallow DOF  
✓ **Technically Sound:** Fisheye = wide aperture (f/5.6-f/11)

❌ **Missing Key Elements:** No lighting description  
✓ **Complete:** Include lighting source, quality, and direction

❌ **Overcomplicating:** Too many conflicting instructions  
✓ **Focused:** Clear primary style with supporting details

## AI Tool Parameters

### Midjourney Examples

**Aspect Ratios:**

- `--ar 3:4` (vertical portrait - most common)
- `--ar 2:3` (classic portrait)
- `--ar 4:5` (Instagram portrait)
- `--ar 1:1` (square)

**Quality/Style:**

- `--raw` (more photographic, less artistic)
- `--style raw` (realistic rendering)
- `--stylize 250` (balanced)
- `--stylize 0-100` (very photographic)
- `--stylize 300-1000` (more artistic)

**Other:**

- `--hd` (higher detail mode)
- `--q 2` (higher quality rendering)
