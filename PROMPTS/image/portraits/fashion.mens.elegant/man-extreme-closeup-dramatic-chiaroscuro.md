# Man Portrait - Extreme Close-Up Dramatic Chiaroscuro

## Prompt Type

Portrait Photography - Extreme Close-Up - High Contrast Lighting

## Primary Platform

MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Extreme close-up portrait of a man's face with dramatic chiaroscuro lighting creating mysterious atmosphere through contrast of light and shadow.

## Detailed Prompt

```
Preserve identity 100%. Do not change facial features or proportions. Extreme close-up of a face.

Portrait of a man where the contrast of light and shadow creates a mysterious atmosphere. Surrounded by darkness. The face is illuminated by a narrow strip of light, highlighting cheekbones, a sharp nose, and expressive eyebrows. He looks confident, with a daring gaze. The lower part of the body is hidden; attention is focused on the face. Contrasting lighting, cinematic style with high quality and detail. 8K.
```

## Identity Preservation Settings

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--cref [reference_image_url]` for character reference
- Add `--cw 100` for maximum character weight
- Specify "Preserve identity 100%"

**Flux/Stable Diffusion:**

- Upload reference image with ControlNet
- Use "exact facial features and proportions"
- High CFG scale for prompt adherence

## Technical Notes

**Focus:**

- Attention directed to face only
- Sharp detail on illuminated features
- Soft or dark areas fall into shadow

**Contrast:**

- High contrast between light and dark

**Depth:**

- Shallow depth of field
- Face in sharp focus

---

**Key Philosophy**: Use extreme light/shadow contrast to create mystery and drama, focusing all attention on the illuminated facial features while letting darkness hide the rest. The narrow strip of light acts as a revealing element in an otherwise concealed portrait.
