# Man Portrait - Three-Quarter Monochrome Minimalist Vintage

## Prompt Type

Portrait Photography - Three-Quarter Pose - Black & White Editorial

## Primary Platform

MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset, Stable Diffusion XL

## Core Concept

Timeless monochrome fashion photography of a man in three-quarter pose with vintage-inspired elegance, dramatic side lighting, and clean minimalist background.

## Detailed Prompt

```
The man stands with a confident pose, his body turned slightly to three-quarters, his shoulders open. Both hands are in his trouser pockets, his elbows pointed back, creating a clear silhouette. His head is turned to the side, his gaze directed beyond the frame. The shot is medium-length, from the hips to the crown of the head, with the eye level. The composition is centered with plenty of white space around the figure.

The scene is shot in black and white with a distinct gradation of grays. The skin texture of the face is clearly visible thanks to the directional lighting, which highlights the cheekbones, nose, and jawline.

The man's clothing is classic and elegant. He wears a light-colored shirt with an open collar and a white T-shirt made of soft knit fabric underneath. He wears a straight-cut black jacket with a soft texture over it. The trousers are black, thick, waist-level, and have pronounced creases. Dark leather suspenders run across the shoulders and attach to the trousers, creating a vintage accent.

The background is minimalist and light, without pronounced texture. The lighting is directional studio lighting, positioned from the side and slightly above. The light creates dramatic shadows along the face, shoulders, and folds of clothing. The contrast is medium to high, highlighting the texture of the fabric and the shape of the figure.

Shot with an 85mm portrait lens, moderate depth of field, focusing on the face and upper body, and a gently blurred background. Highly detailed fabric, skin, and hair, editorial fashion photography, highly detailed, photorealistic.

Timeless monochrome fashion mood, black and white tonal palette, medium to high contrast lighting, dramatic side light, rich grayscale gradients, sharp fabric texture definition, clean minimalist background, classic editorial color grading, vintage-inspired elegance.
```
