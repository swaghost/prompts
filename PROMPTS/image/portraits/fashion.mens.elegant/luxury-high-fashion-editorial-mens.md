# Luxury High-Fashion Editorial - Men's Fashion

## Description

High-end luxury fashion editorial photography featuring premium menswear brands, sophisticated styling, and artistic composition. Emphasizes quality materials, impeccable tailoring, minimal aesthetic, and elevated editorial sophistication. Perfect for luxury brand campaigns, high-fashion magazines, designer lookbooks, and premium menswear marketing.

## Technical Specifications

**Camera Setup:**

- Camera: Medium format or full-frame professional (Phase One, Hasselblad, Sony, Canon)
- Lens: 85mm f/1.4, 50mm f/1.2, 35mm f/1.4 (premium glass)
- Aperture: f/2.8 - f/5.6 (controlled depth)
- ISO: 100-400 (maximum quality)
- Shutter Speed: 1/125 - 1/500 (studio or controlled natural light)

**Composition:**

- Precise intentional framing
- Minimalist sophisticated layouts
- Negative space utilization
- Architectural precision
- Vertical or square formats
- Magazine editorial standards

**Lighting:**

- Studio strobes or controlled natural light
- Soft even high-end commercial lighting
- Subtle dimensional modeling
- Premium quality light character
- Flawless technical execution
