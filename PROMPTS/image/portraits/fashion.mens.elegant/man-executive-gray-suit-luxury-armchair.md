# Man Executive Portrait - Gray Suit in Luxury Armchair

## Prompt Type

Portrait Photography - Seated Executive Style - Luxury Interior

## Primary Platform

MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset, Stable Diffusion XL

## Core Concept

Ultra-realistic 8K cinematic portrait of a young stylish man in tailored gray suit, seated in luxurious gray velvet armchair with refined wood-paneled interior and sophisticated lighting.

## Detailed Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic, hyper-detailed, 8K cinematic portrait of a young stylish man.

Outfit: Tailored dark gray suit with a black dress shirt underneath, polished matching shoes, and a metallic wristwatch. Fabrics show realistic texture and natural folds, blending modern elegance with subtle dominance and sophistication.

Pose: Seated in a luxurious gray velvet armchair, slightly reclined, legs apart. Left arm rests naturally on the armrest, right hand raised near the face in a calm, confident gesture. Expression conveys authority, composure, and leadership. Full-body or three-quarter view, centered and balanced composition.

Background: Refined wood-paneled room with warm cinematic lighting, soft key light from front-left highlighting facial contours and casting smooth shadows. Plush gray armchair, subtle reflections on wood, partial metallic/glass side table, and soft background blur create luxurious, executive ambiance.
```
