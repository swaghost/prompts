## Pro Tips

**Three-Quarter Profile:**
"Body turned away from camera while head turns slightly sideways toward the distant scenery" creates classic masculine travel portrait composition that feels candid rather than posed.

**Lens Compression:**
50–70mm equivalent with "slight compression making mountains appear grand and dramatic" is key to professional travel photography look vs. smartphone wide-angle aesthetic.

**Matte Black Fabric:**
Specify "matte technical fabric" not just "black jacket" — this prevents glossy/shiny rendering that looks less premium and expedition-appropriate.

**Individual Curls:**
"Thick messy curly dark-brown hair with individual strands clearly visible" ensures realistic hair rendering instead of painted/simplified texture.

**Atmospheric Depth:**
"Subtle atmospheric haze in distant mountains" plus "realistic atmospheric perspective" creates authentic depth instead of unnaturally sharp background.

**Clean Color Grading:**
"Clean neutral grading, no excessive orange tones" prevents overly warm Instagram-filter look that dates the image.
