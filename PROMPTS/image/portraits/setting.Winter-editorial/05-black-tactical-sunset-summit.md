## Pro Tips

**Mystery Through Concealment:**
"Most of the face is concealed by winter protection" creates mysterious explorer aesthetic. Balaclava + cap + sunglasses combination ensures anonymity while maintaining human form.

**Matte Black Specification:**
"Matte weather-resistant material" is critical — glossy or shiny black fabric looks less technical and expedition-appropriate. Emphasize "realistic wrinkles and subtle highlights" for texture.

**Hunched Natural Posture:**
"Slightly hunched natural posture, head lowered as if checking footing" creates authentic mountaineering moment rather than posed fashion shot. Adds narrative and realism.

**Sunset Rim Light:**
"Low sunset rim light grazing one side" creates dramatic edge lighting that defines the all-black subject against environment. Without this, subject can disappear into shadows.

**Warm-Cool Contrast:**
"Warm edge highlights contrast with cool mountain shadows" is key to cinematic quality. This color temperature contrast adds depth and drama.

**Atmospheric Depth:**
"Realistic atmospheric depth" with "natural atmospheric haze" ensures layered mountain ranges fade naturally into distance, not unnaturally sharp throughout.

**No Extra Limbs:**
Explicitly state "no extra limbs or distorted gear" — complex backpack configurations with multiple straps can sometimes confuse AI generation.
