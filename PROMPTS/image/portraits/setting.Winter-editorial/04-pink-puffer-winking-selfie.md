## Pro Tips

**Natural Wink and Kiss:**
"One eye closed in a natural wink, lips puckered gently as if blowing a kiss" creates playful expression without feeling forced. Emphasize "natural" and "gently" to avoid exaggerated cartoon look.

**Pastel Pink Specification:**
Use "pastel blush-pink" not just "pink" — this ensures soft feminine tone rather than hot pink or neon. "Realistic quilted panels" adds texture detail.

**Goggles Geometry:**
Critical: "proper goggles geometry" prevents warped or distorted rendering. Oversized futuristic goggles can be tricky for AI.

**Arm Perspective:**
"Arm visibly enlarged closer to lens because of authentic perspective" is key to smartphone selfie realism. Without this, image feels like professional camera.

**Glossy Highlights:**
"Realistic glossy highlights on fabric and goggles" ensures shiny puffer material reads correctly with natural light interaction, not artificial shine.

**Face Distortion Prevention:**
Explicitly state "no face distortion" — wide-angle selfies can sometimes generate subtle facial distortion that looks unnatural.

**Background Detail:**
"Mountains remain detailed" prevents over-blurring common in AI-generated selfies. Smartphone depth of field keeps background recognizable.
