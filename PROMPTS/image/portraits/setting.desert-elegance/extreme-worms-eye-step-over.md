# Desert Elegance — Prompt 07: Extreme Worm's-Eye Step Over Camera

## Campaign Context
Part of the Desert Elegance luxury fashion campaign. Dramatic ground-level perspective with model diversity, featuring an extreme worm's-eye angle that makes the heel the dominant foreground element.

## Full Prompt

Create a 3:4 vertical Soul 2.0 luxury footwear campaign photograph. Show a 24-year-old tall slim woman with deep warm brown skin, a sharp jawline, almond eyes and a sleek buzz-cut hairstyle. She wears a fitted butter-yellow sleeveless knit mini dress, small gold hoop earrings and black polished calfskin pointed-toe slingback heels with narrow straps and slim sculptural gold-metal heels.

Place her at a retro desert petrol station with a cream stucco canopy, a faded red fuel pump and a vivid cobalt-blue sky.

Shoot from an extreme worm's-eye angle on the ground as she steps over the camera, with one heel passing very close to the lens and dominating the foreground. Her body rises tall into the sky, one arm holding the side of her dress against the breeze, gaze looking down past the lens with calm confidence. A partial oxblood vintage convertible is visible in the far background.

Hard late-afternoon sunlight creates crisp shadows, rich contrast and warm highlights on the cream architecture. Use a Canon EOS R5 with a 24mm lens feel, dramatic perspective, realistic shoe scale, true leather texture, natural skin detail, subtle film grain and expensive editorial colour grading.

## Key Elements

- **Model:** 24-year-old, tall slim, deep warm brown skin, sharp jawline, almond eyes, sleek buzz-cut hairstyle
- **Outfit:** Fitted butter-yellow sleeveless knit mini dress
- **Accessories:** Small gold hoop earrings, narrow black oval sunglasses
- **Footwear:** Black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold-metal heels (heel dominates foreground)
- **Pose:** Stepping over camera, one arm holding dress against breeze, gaze down past lens
- **Expression:** Calm confidence
- **Setting:** Retro desert petrol station, cream stucco canopy, faded red fuel pump
- **Background:** Vivid cobalt-blue sky, partial oxblood vintage convertible (far background)

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Camera:** Canon EOS R5 with 24mm lens feel
- **Angle:** Extreme worm's-eye (on the ground), looking up as model steps over
- **Perspective:** Dramatic wide-angle distortion, heel very close to lens
- **Lighting:** Hard late-afternoon sunlight, crisp shadows, rich contrast, warm highlights
- **Style:** Expensive editorial colour grading, realistic shoe scale, true leather texture, natural skin detail, subtle film grain

## Diversity Note

This prompt features intentional model diversity with deep warm brown skin tone and sleek buzz-cut hairstyle, different from the primary campaign model.

## Dramatic Perspective Strategy

- Ground-level camera creates extreme drama
- Heel becomes hero element through proximity
- Wide-angle distortion elongates figure dramatically
- Creates powerful, confident visual impact
- Model literally towers over viewer
