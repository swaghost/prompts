# Desert Elegance — Prompt 08: Fisheye Interior Convertible Footwell

## Campaign Context
Part of the Desert Elegance luxury fashion campaign. Bold interior car shot with playful fisheye distortion from the footwell, creating an unexpected and luxurious perspective.

## Full Prompt

Create a 3:4 vertical Soul 2.0 luxury footwear campaign image. Show a 25-year-old slim woman with golden beige skin, full lips, straight nose and shoulder-length copper-brown hair in soft glossy waves. She wears a tomato-red sleeveless shift dress, narrow black cat-eye sunglasses and black polished calfskin pointed-toe slingback heels with narrow straps and sculptural gold-metal heels.

Place her inside an oxblood vintage convertible parked beside a long cream stucco wall with palm trees and saturated blue desert sky beyond.

Use a close fisheye-like 20mm lens feel from the passenger-side footwell, creating a bold distorted editorial perspective. Her legs stretch toward the lens so one heel becomes the foreground hero, while she leans back in the driver's seat with one hand on the steering wheel and the other resting behind her head. The cream leather interior wraps around the frame.

Bright late-afternoon sunlight pours into the car, creating strong highlights, glossy reflections and warm shadows. Keep the mood playful and expensive, with realistic anatomy, true leather grain, polished car surfaces, natural skin pores, subtle analogue film grain and rich luxury colour.

## Key Elements

- **Model:** 25-year-old, slim, golden beige skin, full lips, straight nose, shoulder-length copper-brown hair in soft glossy waves
- **Outfit:** Tomato-red sleeveless shift dress
- **Accessories:** Narrow black cat-eye sunglasses
- **Footwear:** Black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold-metal heels (hero in foreground)
- **Pose:** Leaning back in driver's seat, one hand on steering wheel, other behind head, legs stretching toward lens
- **Setting:** Inside oxblood vintage convertible, cream leather interior
- **Exterior Visible:** Long cream stucco wall, palm trees, saturated blue desert sky

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Lens:** Fisheye-like 20mm feel from passenger-side footwell
- **Perspective:** Bold distorted editorial perspective, interior wraps around frame
- **Lighting:** Bright late-afternoon sunlight pouring into car, strong highlights, glossy reflections, warm shadows
