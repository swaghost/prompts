# Desert Elegance — Prompt 05: Mid-Walk Under Petrol Station Canopy

## Campaign Context
Part of the Desert Elegance luxury fashion campaign. Dynamic movement shot capturing the model mid-walk under a desert petrol station canopy, emphasizing motion and lifestyle narrative.

## Full Prompt

Create a hyper-realistic Soul 2.0 luxury editorial campaign image in 3:4 vertical format. Show the same 26-year-old tall slim woman with light warm skin, chestnut-brown hair in a sleek low ponytail, and narrow black oval sunglasses walking under a minimalist desert petrol-station canopy beside a deep oxblood vintage convertible.

She wears a long soft dove-grey trench coat left open so one leg steps forward clearly, revealing the same black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold heel detail. Include a retro red petrol pump near the car. Behind the station structure, show a wide dry desert landscape with distant mountains and scrub.

Instead of a static standing pose, capture her mid-walk, one hand lightly holding the trench belt, the other hand near her side, chin forward, gaze slightly away from camera. Use a 35mm wide editorial lens feel, eye-level framing, spacious composition, hard late-afternoon sunlight, crisp shadows, fluorescent canopy fixtures above, premium colour grading, subtle grain, real textures, luxury magazine realism.

## Key Elements

- **Model:** 26-year-old, tall slim, light warm skin, chestnut-brown hair in sleek low ponytail
- **Outfit:** Long soft dove-grey trench coat (left open to show leg movement)
- **Accessories:** Narrow black oval sunglasses
- **Footwear:** Black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold heel detail (clearly visible)
- **Pose:** Mid-walk action, one hand holding trench belt, other hand near side, chin forward
- **Gaze:** Slightly away from camera
- **Setting:** Minimalist desert petrol-station canopy, deep oxblood vintage convertible beside model
- **Details:** Retro red petrol pump near car
- **Background:** Wide dry desert landscape, distant mountains, scrub
