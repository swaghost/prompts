# Desert Elegance — Prompt 01: Dramatic Lean Against Convertible

## Campaign Context
Part of the Desert Elegance luxury fashion campaign featuring consistent visual elements: oxblood red vintage convertible, saturated cobalt-blue sky, warm cream stucco walls, palm trees, and black slingback heels with sculptural gold heel detail.

## Full Prompt

Create a hyper-realistic editorial campaign image of the same 25-year-old tall slim female model with light warm skin and chestnut-brown hair in a sleek low ponytail, wearing black narrow cat-eye sunglasses and a fitted black sleeveless cutout midi dress with subtle textural ruffle detailing. She leans dramatically backward against the rear trunk of an oxblood red vintage convertible, arching her upper body with one hand braced behind her and one hand resting near her thigh, creating a long elegant line through the legs.

The same black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold metal heel detail must be clearly visible and beautifully proportioned. The setting is an open roadside area with a tall cream stucco wall running horizontally through the frame and a strip of cobalt-blue sky with palm trees above it. Keep the model slightly off-center with generous negative space around her.

Hard late-afternoon sun from upper left creates crisp shadows on the gravel and luminous highlights on the car paint. Use a 35mm editorial lens feel, slightly low angle, with luxurious cinematic realism, authentic skin texture, real fabric drape, true metal reflections, subtle film grain and a rich warm-vintage colour grade.

## Key Elements

- **Model:** 25-year-old, tall slim, light warm skin, chestnut-brown hair in sleek low ponytail
- **Outfit:** Black sleeveless cutout midi dress with textural ruffle details, black cat-eye sunglasses
- **Footwear:** Black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold metal heel detail (clearly visible)
- **Pose:** Dramatic backward lean against convertible trunk, arched upper body
- **Vehicle:** Oxblood red vintage convertible (rear trunk visible)
- **Setting:** Open roadside, tall cream stucco wall, palm trees, cobalt-blue sky
- **Composition:** Model slightly off-center with generous negative space

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** Standard editorial
- **Lens:** 35mm editorial feel, slightly low angle
- **Lighting:** Hard late-afternoon sun from upper left, crisp shadows, luminous highlights
- **Style:** Luxurious cinematic realism, authentic skin texture, real fabric drape
- **Details:** True metal reflections, subtle film grain, rich warm-vintage colour grade
