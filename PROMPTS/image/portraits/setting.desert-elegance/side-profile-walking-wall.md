# Desert Elegance — Prompt 06: Side Profile Walking Along Wall

## Campaign Context
Part of the Desert Elegance luxury fashion campaign. Classic side profile walking shot with compressed telephoto perspective, emphasizing graphic shadow and elegant silhouette.

## Full Prompt

Create a hyper-realistic Soul 2.0 luxury fashion campaign image in 3:4 vertical format. Show the same 26-year-old tall slim female model in side profile walking along a tall warm cream stucco wall with palm fronds rising above it and a saturated cobalt-blue sky beyond.

She wears a fitted butter-cream mock-neck top with soft draping, a high-waisted rust-brown midi skirt with a front slit, narrow black oval sunglasses, and the same black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold heel detail. One heel is clearly visible mid-step as she walks with calm confidence.

A sliver of oxblood convertible bodywork enters the lower foreground to tie the campaign world together. Her shadow falls long and graphic across the wall. Use a 70mm compressed editorial lens feel, off-center framing, clean negative space, warm hard sunlight, subtle film grain, realistic skin and fabric texture, refined cinematic colour.

## Key Elements

- **Model:** 26-year-old, tall slim, light warm skin, chestnut-brown hair in sleek low ponytail
- **Outfit:** Fitted butter-cream mock-neck top (soft draping), high-waisted rust-brown midi skirt with front slit
- **Accessories:** Narrow black oval sunglasses
- **Footwear:** Black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold heel detail (clearly visible mid-step)
- **Pose:** Side profile walking, calm confidence
- **Setting:** Tall warm cream stucco wall, palm fronds above, saturated cobalt-blue sky
- **Foreground Detail:** Sliver of oxblood convertible bodywork
- **Shadow:** Long and graphic across the wall

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Lens:** 70mm compressed editorial feel
- **Composition:** Off-center framing, clean negative space
- **Lighting:** Warm hard sunlight creating long graphic shadow
- **Style:** Refined cinematic color, realistic skin and fabric texture, subtle film grain

## Color Palette

- Butter-cream mock-neck top
- Rust-brown midi skirt
- Warm cream stucco wall
- Saturated cobalt-blue sky
- Deep oxblood red convertible (sliver)
- Black accessories with gold accents

## Compositional Strategy

- Side profile emphasizes elegant walking silhouette
- Compressed telephoto flattens perspective
- Long graphic shadow creates bold graphic element
- Off-center placement creates sophisticated negative space
- Convertible sliver ties to campaign visual language
