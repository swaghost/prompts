# Fashion Editorial Storyboard 3x3 Grid

## Description

A women's fashion editorial storyboard in black and white, organized in a 3x3 square grid with nine distinct, playful, and stylized poses. Uses an attached reference image as absolute visual guide to create a cohesive editorial composition featuring the same woman across all nine frames in different poses. Features studio editorial photography aesthetic with chiaroscuro lighting, minimalist background, and fashion magazine quality. The composition maintains total consistency of face, body, hair, and figure across all frames while varying poses and expressions.

## Overview

This prompt generates a professional fashion editorial contact sheet or storyboard grid showing multiple poses of the same model in a cohesive black and white aesthetic. Perfect for fashion portfolios, editorial presentations, lookbooks, and social media fashion content. The 3x3 grid format mimics traditional contact sheets while providing modern editorial sophistication.

**Primary Style:** Black and white fashion studio editorial photography
**Format:** 3x3 square grid with nine frames
**Subject:** Single female model with reference image consistency
**Quality:** Studio editorial with 4K Ultra HD quality

## The Prompt

```
OBJECTIVE: Use my attached image as an absolute visual reference to create a women's fashion editorial storyboard in black and white, organized in a 3x3 grid with nine distinct, playful, and stylized poses.

FORMAT
Aspect ratio: square grid.
Grid:
- Lines: 3
- Columns: 3
- Total frames: 9
- Spacing: fine black dividers between frames
- Margins: minimal external border, clean and uniform

REFERENCE STYLE
Type of image: leaf from a fashion studio editorial.
Color: black and white.
Background: smooth and minimalist studio background, without decorative elements or distractions.
Lighting: chiaroscuro alternating between whole body, three-quarters and half body.
Camera: editorial fixed framing, alternating between whole body, three-quarters and half body.

DETAILS OF THE PERSON
Person: feminine.
Identity: preserve high fidelity of height, facial structure, eye format, nose, mouth, eyebrows, apparent age, skin tone and identity of the woman in the reference image.
Hair: maintain exactly the same cut, length, volume, texture, color, parting and movement seen in the attached photograph.
Appearance: feminine, elegant, extremely beautiful, natural and sophisticated, without modifying real features, without excessive rejuvenation and without plastic surgery effect.
Clothing:
- feminine rock band themed regatta;
- vintage-inspired oversized t-shirt;
- casual shorts jeans;
- accessories: discreet with rock editorial aesthetic.
Vibe: playful, seductive, expressive, safe, modern and sophisticated.

POSES PER FRAME
1. Crossed arms, relaxed and symmetrically expressive posture.
2. Light and relaxed pose, soft body, soft natural gaze.
3. Serious, with pushed-out chest joints, looking up at shoulder.
4. Side profile, soft side body rotation, fingers relaxed and natural smile and gaze.
5. From back, one hand on hips or hair, looking over shoulder, fingers relaxed and relaxed smile.
6. Hands adjusting waist or back of shorts, confident posture and striking expression.
7. Turning back, relaxed side-view pose, naturally leaning and sincere gaze at camera.
8. Serious look, relaxed pose, arms naturally relaxed and sincere gaze at camera.
9. Standing, straight, with light smile, relaxed hands and casual elegant posture.

VISUAL STYLE
Tone: monochrome high contrast, with deep blacks, clean whites and wide range of gray tones.
Texture: light film grain and refined, creating an analogical editorial sensation.
Aesthetic: fashion magazine editorial leaf, minimalist composition, elegant, modern and without distractions.

MODEL DIRECTION: each frame should present a different pose, maintaining total facial, body, hair and figure consistency between all images.

TO AVOID:
- face changes;
- age changes;
- different hair from reference;
- deformed hands;
- extra fingers;
- duplications;
- incorrect anatomy;
- exaggerated expressions;
- excessive accessories;
- random texts;
- deformed logos;
- identity differences between frames.

OUTPUT FORMAT
Type: image generation prompt.
Final output: a single square grid in black and white, in 3x3 format, containing nine distinct editorial photographs of the same woman, with high fidelity, in a single composition that is a reference image.
Camera: Sony A7R V, lens 85 mm, studio editorial photography, precise focus, soft depth of field, realistic skin texture, 4K Ultra HD quality.
```

## Technical Notes

### Identity Consistency Challenges

- **AI Stability**: Some AI models struggle with face consistency across multiple images
- **Reference Strength**: Strong, clear reference image critical for consistency
- **Feature Locking**: Explicitly specify which features must remain identical
- **Verification**: Always check final output for consistency issues
- **Regeneration**: May require multiple attempts to achieve perfect consistency

### Hand Anatomy Accuracy

- **Common AI Issue**: Hands are challenging for many AI image generators
- **Specification**: Explicitly state correct hand anatomy in prompt
- **Avoidance List**: Include "deformed hands" and "extra fingers" in avoid list
- **Pose Selection**: Choose hand positions that are clear and anatomically simple
- **Verification**: Carefully check hand accuracy in all frames

- **Divider Lines**: Black lines separating frames must be clean and consistent
- **Frame Spacing**: Equal spacing between all frames

- **Aspect Ratio**: Square format (1:1) for Instagram and versatile use
