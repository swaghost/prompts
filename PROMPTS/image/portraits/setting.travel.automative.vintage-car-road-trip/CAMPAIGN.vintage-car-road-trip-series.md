## Universal Prompt Architecture

All scenes share the same core prompt structure:

### Core Sections

1. **Introduction:** RAW photograph, hyperrealistic, fashion editorial
2. **Model Specification:** European Caucasian only (with exclusions)
3. **SKIN — HIGHEST PRIORITY:** Visible pores, vellus hair, natural imperfections
4. **CAMERA:** Phase One/Hasselblad, 85mm f/2.8, ISO 200, Kodak Portra 400
5. **LIGHT:** Natural light only, organic shadows
6. **STYLE:** Peter Lindbergh + Slim Aarons, Vogue Italia quality
7. **SCENE:** [Variable — specific scene description]
8. **NEGATIVE:** No AI smoothing, no malformed anatomy
9. **KEYWORDS:** 8K RAW, analog film grain, real skin pores

### Variable Element

Only the **SCENE** section changes between the 5 prompts. All other elements remain constant for campaign cohesion.

## Technical Consistency

### Camera Equipment (All Scenes)

- **Body:** Phase One IQ4 150MP or Hasselblad H6D-400c
- **Lens:** 85mm, f/2.8
- **ISO:** 200
- **Film Stock:** Kodak Portra 400

### Skin Texture Priority (Highest)

- Visible open pores (nose, cheeks, forehead)
- Fine vellus facial hair catching natural light
- Natural skin unevenness and slight redness
- Lip fine line texture visible
- Subsurface light scattering on cheekbones
- Real under-eye shadows
- Zero AI smoothing, zero beauty filter, zero porcelain texture

### Lighting Approach (All Scenes)

- Natural light only
- Overcast soft diffused OR warm golden hour sunset
- No studio flash, no artificial light
- Soft organic shadows
- Natural catchlights in eyes

### Style References (All Scenes)

- **Peter Lindbergh** — Raw analog editorial aesthetic
- **Slim Aarons** — Aspirational lifestyle photography
- **Vogue Italia** — Fashion campaign quality
- **Old Hollywood glamour** with modern energy
