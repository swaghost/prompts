# Dramatic Beach Sunset with Fabric Silhouette

A dramatic, photorealistic sunset beach scene with warm golden light flooding the horizon. A person stands barefoot on the sand near the shoreline, holding a large patterned fabric that billows in the wind, creating a striking silhouette against the glowing sun. The ocean waves shimmer with reflected sunlight, forming bright highlights and soft gradients across the water. The sky is partly cloudy, with intense rays of sunlight breaking through and casting long shadows across the beach. The mood is cinematic, serene, and visually powerful, with crisp detail, lifelike textures, and rich golden tones.
