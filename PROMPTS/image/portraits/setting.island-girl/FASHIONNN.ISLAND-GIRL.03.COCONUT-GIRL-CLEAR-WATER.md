## Image Prompt

SCENE: Tropical island shoreline with impossibly clear turquoise water, shallow crystal-clear water showing the rocky/sandy bottom, vibrant blue-green colour. Deeper blue ocean beyond stretching to the horizon. Green mountainous island or headland visible in the far background right. Bright blue sky with a few white clouds. Very vibrant, saturated tropical colours, Koh Larn or Con Dao Island energy.

MODEL: Adult East Asian/Korean woman with light warm skin and youthful features. Dark auburn-brown hair with natural curl and volume, worn up in a loose high bun or half-up style with lots of natural curl texture falling around it. Straight-across blunt fringe/bangs. Pale grey-blue eyes. Serious, slightly pouty expression, cool girl stare at camera, lips slightly parted. Standing in very shallow water at the shore's edge.

OUTFIT: Pale lemon-yellow halter top, deep V-neck with ruched/gathered fabric creating a draped effect, ties behind neck. Matching lemon-yellow mini skirt with fluffy/feathered fringe hem trim, ostrich feather or tulle fringe at the bottom edge giving a playful textured hem. Bare midriff. Delicate gold coin pendant necklace on thin chain. Small gold hoop earrings. Silver or white resin cuff bracelet on one wrist. Several thin silver rings.

PROPS: Fresh young green coconut held in left hand, natural coconut with husk still on, a thin bamboo or paper straw inserted at the top.

LIGHTING: Bright tropical midday sun, strong overhead light, slight harsh shadows. Vibrant saturated colour from the clear water and sky. Water reflection bounce creating light on legs.

CAMERA: Canon R6, 35mm, f/4.0. Maximum colour saturation on the turquoise water. Bright and punchy. Real tropical island photography.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Avoid underage appearance, cloudy dull water, fake coconut, distorted hands, plastic skin, over-smoothed face, CGI water, incorrect outfit color, extra people, text, logos, and watermarks.
