## Image Prompt

SCENE: Tropical beach at the most dramatic moment of sunset. Sky is intense deep crimson-orange-red, heavy with dark dramatic clouds lit from behind in scarlet, burnt orange and deep purple. Ocean water reflects the fiery sky in copper and dark orange tones. A silhouetted rocky outcrop with a wind-bent tree visible on the right shoreline. Wet sand at the water's edge. Extremely cinematic, moody, almost otherworldly sunset atmosphere.

MODEL: Adult Southeast Asian/Thai woman with warm medium-tan golden skin and luminous glow from sunset light. Dark hair loosely pulled back into a low messy bun, wispy strands framing the face, slightly dishevelled from sea breeze. Striking defined features, high cheekbones. Direct gaze at camera, calm, composed, slightly mysterious expression. Standing at water's edge on wet sand, one hand loosely in the fabric at hip level.

OUTFIT: Floor-length pale lemon-yellow chiffon halter gown, high mock-neck halter style, backless. Very sheer and light semi-transparent chiffon catching breeze. Ruffle tier detail at hem, layered chiffon ruffles at bottom. Bare shoulders and back. Large ornate silver sculptural cuff bracelet on right wrist, chunky statement piece. No other jewellery.

SKIN & MAKEUP: Warm golden tan skin bathed in fiery red-orange sunset light, deeply warm and luminous. Natural glowing makeup, nude-bronze lip, defined lashes, highlight catching sunset.

LIGHTING: Intense dramatic sunset backlighting from behind model, fiery red-orange rim light on hair, shoulders and sheer dress edges. Front of face lit by warm ambient sky reflection. Yellow dress takes on warm orange tones.

CAMERA: Hasselblad, 85mm, f/2.0. Maximum drama on sunset, push reds and oranges. Slight underexposure on subject to make sky more dramatic. Kodak Portra 800 pushed film look.
