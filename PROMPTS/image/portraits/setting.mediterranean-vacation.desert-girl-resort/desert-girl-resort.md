A series of hyper-realistic lifestyle images featuring a young female influencer at a luxury desert resort during golden hour. iPhone-style photography with authentic imperfections and natural lighting.

## 1. Main Establishing Shot

A highly photorealistic lifestyle image of a young female influencer walking through a luxury desert resort pathway during golden hour. She has long, slightly wavy dark blonde hair with lighter sun-kissed highlights, softly glowing natural skin, subtle makeup, and a relaxed, elegant expression as she looks over her shoulder away from the camera. She is wearing a loose, earthy beige-brown linen co-ord set with long sleeves and a relaxed fit, styled in a soft minimalist luxury way. Over her shoulder she carries a large woven straw tote bag.

The setting is an upscale desert resort with warm beige plaster buildings, palm trees, dry landscaping, ornamental grasses, succulents, and a pale irregular stone walkway leading through the scene. The sunlight is soft, warm, and low, creating natural highlights on her face and hair and long subtle shadows across the path.

Composition is vertical 9:16, medium-full body framing, influencer positioned slightly left of centre, with plenty of environment visible around her to establish the location. The scene should feel like a candid luxury travel photo taken on an iPhone, with authentic skin texture, natural light falloff, realistic materials, and a calm premium holiday atmosphere. The overall look should be elegant, natural, believable, and not overly polished or studio-lit.

**Negative prompt:** Overly perfect skin, studio lighting, plastic AI beauty, glossy retouching, unrealistic hair, fashion-editorial pose, overly cinematic grading, fake-looking resort, stiff body language, extra fingers, distorted bag straps, unrealistic text placement, cluttered frame.
