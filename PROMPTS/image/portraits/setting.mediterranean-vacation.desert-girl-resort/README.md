## Style Guidelines

**Photography Aesthetic:**

- Authentic iPhone-style photography
- Natural imperfections preserved
- Realistic skin texture with pores, freckles, subtle imperfections
- Natural light falloff
- Shallow depth of field where appropriate
- Candid luxury travel photo feel
- Not overly polished or studio-lit

**Quality Standards:**

- Hyper-realistic/photorealistic
- Ultra-detailed textures (skin, fabric, straw)
- Natural materials rendered authentically
- Calm premium holiday atmosphere
- Elegant, natural, believable
