# Mountaineering Outdoor Editorial - Six Image Prompt Set

## Description

A six-image nature, outdoor, and adventure editorial set for Higgsfield AI. The prompts combine athletic fashion, realistic alpine environments, intentional portrait direction, natural light, and high-detail photorealism in a consistent 3:4 portrait format.

## Usage

Use this set for mountaineering and outdoor apparel campaigns, alpine tourism content, adventure lifestyle editorials, hiking and trail-running brands, and social-media portrait series. Each prompt is standalone, but the six scenes can be generated as a cohesive campaign by preserving the 3:4 format, natural editorial photography, and realistic outdoor atmosphere.

## Platform & Settings

- **Primary platform:** Higgsfield AI
- **Alternatives:** Kling or Runway for video animation; Midjourney v6 or Flux.1 Pro for static images
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:4 portrait / vertical
- **Quality:** Hyperrealistic editorial photography, 4K, sharp focus
- **Variants:** Generate multiple variations per prompt and keep the strongest natural pose

## Series Guidance

- Preserve 3:4 portrait framing across all six images.
- Use Higgsfield AI as the primary generation platform.
- Keep natural outdoor light, realistic skin, fabric texture, and sharp subject detail.
- Use the camera direction in each prompt as a hard compositional constraint.
- Vary the scenes through weather and time of day: misty golden hour, blue-hour dawn, colorful afternoon, summer lake daylight, storm light, and warm river sunset.
- For animation, append `slow cinematic camera push-in` or `subtle wind movement` while preserving the original composition.
