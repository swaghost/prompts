# Desert Elegance — Prompt 04: Isolated Roadside Canopy Step

## Campaign Context
Part of the Desert Elegance luxury fashion campaign. Product-focused shot emphasizing quiet luxury and isolation, with shoes small but clearly the focus within a wider environmental context.

## Full Prompt

Create a hyper-realistic Soul 2.0 luxury footwear campaign image in 3:4 vertical format. Show a single black pointed-toe slingback heel with smooth polished calfskin and a sculptural gold-detailed heel placed on a cream concrete step under a minimalist roadside canopy. A second matching heel sits slightly behind it, softer in focus, so the pair remains part of the story.

The lower edge of the step is painted a strong warm oxblood red. Behind the step, show a desert landscape with distant pale mountains, dry shrubs, utility poles, and a pale blue sky with soft clouds. The composition should feel isolated, quiet, and expensive, with the shoes small but clearly the focus inside a wider world.

Hard afternoon sunlight creates a long clean shadow of the heel across the cream surface. Use a 70mm lens feel, real environmental texture, true scale, subtle grain, premium location-photography realism.

## Key Elements

- **Product:** Black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold detail (pair, one sharp, one soft focus)
- **Setting:** Cream concrete step under minimalist roadside canopy
- **Design Detail:** Lower edge of step painted strong warm oxblood red
- **Background:** Desert landscape, distant pale mountains, dry shrubs, utility poles, pale blue sky with soft clouds
- **Mood:** Isolated, quiet, expensive
- **Composition:** Shoes small but clearly focused within wider environmental context

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Lens:** 70mm feel
- **Lighting:** Hard afternoon sunlight creating long clean shadow across cream surface
- **Style:** Premium location-photography realism, real environmental texture, true scale
- **Details:** Subtle grain, authentic setting

## Color Palette

- Cream concrete
- Strong warm oxblood red accent
- Pale blue sky with soft clouds
- Deep black leather
- Polished gold heel detail
- Desert earth tones

## Composition Strategy

- Wide environmental shot with small product focal point
- Conveys luxury through isolation and negative space
- Authentic location rather than studio setting
- True scale creates quiet sophistication
