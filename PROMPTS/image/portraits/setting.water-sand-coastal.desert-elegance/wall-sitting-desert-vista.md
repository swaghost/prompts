# Desert Elegance — Prompt 03: Wall Sitting with Desert Vista

## Campaign Context
Part of the Desert Elegance luxury fashion campaign featuring casual elevated styling with butter silk shirt and chocolate shorts, emphasizing spacious composition and generous negative space.

## Full Prompt

Create a 3:4 vertical Soul 2.0 luxury fashion campaign photograph. Feature a 26-year-old tall slender woman with warm olive skin, angular oval face, defined cheekbones, soft full lips and chestnut-brown hair tied into a sleek low ponytail. She wears narrow black oval sunglasses, an oversized pale-butter silk shirt with rolled sleeves, tailored high-waisted chocolate-brown shorts and black polished calfskin pointed-toe slingback pumps with slim sculptural gold-metal heels.

She sits casually on top of a tall ivory stucco wall, one leg hanging downward and the other bent loosely, hands supporting her behind her. A deep oxblood 1960s convertible occupies the lower foreground, while uneven palm trees rise against a saturated cobalt-blue sky. Keep the woman small and slightly off-centre with generous negative space.

Shoot straight-on from a low camera height using a Leica SL3 and 50mm lens. Hard afternoon sunlight, crisp shadows, warm skin, cream architecture, deep red car, realistic fabric folds, natural pores, subtle film grain and polished editorial colour.

## Key Elements

- **Model:** 26-year-old, tall slender, warm olive skin, angular oval face, chestnut-brown hair in low ponytail
- **Outfit:** Oversized pale-butter silk shirt (rolled sleeves), high-waisted chocolate-brown shorts
- **Accessories:** Narrow black oval sunglasses
- **Footwear:** Black pointed-toe slingback heels with sculptural gold-metal heels
- **Pose:** Casual sitting on top of tall ivory stucco wall, one leg hanging, one bent
- **Foreground:** Deep oxblood 1960s convertible
- **Background:** Uneven palm trees, saturated cobalt-blue sky
- **Composition:** Model small and slightly off-centre, generous negative space

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Camera:** Leica SL3 with 50mm lens
- **Angle:** Straight-on from low camera height
- **Lighting:** Hard afternoon sunlight, crisp shadows
- **Style:** Polished editorial color, realistic fabric folds, natural pores, subtle film grain
