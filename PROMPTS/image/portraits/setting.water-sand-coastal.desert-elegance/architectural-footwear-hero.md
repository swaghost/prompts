# Desert Elegance — Prompt 02: Architectural Footwear Hero Shot

## Campaign Context
Part of the Desert Elegance luxury fashion campaign. Product-only hero shot featuring the campaign's signature black slingback heels with gold detail in a desert modernist architectural setting.

## Full Prompt

Create a 3:4 vertical hyper-realistic luxury footwear campaign photograph set inside a desert modernist arts complex. The hero product is a pair of identical black polished calfskin pointed-toe slingback pumps with a low-cut vamp, narrow back strap and slim curved architectural heels containing polished gold metal inserts, with no visible branding.

Photograph the heels positioned on the inner lower ledge of a large circular opening cut through a warm ivory stucco wall. One shoe stands upright in profile while the second lies slightly behind it at a diagonal, creating an elegant asymmetrical silhouette. Through the circular opening, reveal an uninterrupted saturated cyan-cobalt desert sky and the soft blurred outline of distant pale mountains. A thin branch from a dry desert plant crosses one edge of the opening, introducing an organic foreground detail.

Use a low camera position approximately 40 centimetres above the ground, looking slightly upward through the circular architecture. Frame the shoes in the lower-right quadrant rather than perfectly centred, leaving strong blue negative space inside the opening. Use the visual compression and clean geometry of a Hasselblad X2D with an XCD 65mm lens, focus precisely on the leather edge and gold heel insert, moderate depth of field.

Hard late-afternoon sunlight enters from upper left, producing a crisp curved shadow around the circular opening and a narrow highlight along the black leather. Preserve dense cyan blues, sun-warmed ivory, deep neutral black and restrained gold highlights. Real leather grain, fine stitching, microscopic dust on the stucco ledge, natural shadow density, soft analogue highlight roll-off, subtle medium-format film grain and premium magazine realism.

No model, no car, no petrol station, no readable text, no logo, no wall-top shoe arrangement, no artificial product rendering, no duplicate or malformed footwear.
