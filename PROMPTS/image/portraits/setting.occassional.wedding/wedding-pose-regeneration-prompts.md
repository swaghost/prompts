## Overview

### What This Guide Covers

This comprehensive system teaches you to **regenerate wedding photographs with different poses** while preserving all other elements—location, lighting, clothing, facial features, background, camera angle, and atmosphere. Perfect for creating multiple pose variations from a single successful setup without reshooting.

### Core Concept

- **Change**: Couple's pose, body positions, interactions, gaze direction
- **Preserve**: Everything else—location, lighting, clothing, faces, background, camera, atmosphere
- **Purpose**: Create diverse pose portfolio from single setup
- **Quality**: Editorial wedding photography, cinematic, romantic aesthetic
- **Applications**: Wedding portfolios, client galleries, pose variety, storytelling sequences

### Why This Works

✅ **Efficient Portfolio Building** - Multiple poses from one setup  
✅ **Client Variety** - Give clients diverse pose options without reshooting  
✅ **Creative Exploration** - Test different poses virtually before actual shoot  
✅ **Consistent Quality** - Same lighting and location across all variations  
✅ **Cost Effective** - No need for multiple setups or location changes  
✅ **Storytelling Sequences** - Create narrative flow with pose progression  
✅ **Perfect Preservation** - Faces, clothing, environment remain identical

## Core Regeneration Principles

### What MUST Be Preserved (Never Changes)

**Subject Preservation:**

- ✅ **Facial features** - Exact face structure, identity, appearance
- ✅ **Clothing** - Every detail (dress, suit, tie, shoes, accessories)
- ✅ **Hair** - Style, color, length, positioning
- ✅ **Body proportions** - Height, build, physical characteristics
- ✅ **Skin tones** - Exact coloring and complexion

**Environment Preservation:**

- ✅ **Location** - Exact same setting and background
- ✅ **Background elements** - Trees, foliage, architecture, sky
- ✅ **Path/ground** - Surface material, texture, color
- ✅ **Landscape** - All environmental elements
- ✅ **Props** - Bouquet (unless specified to change), other items

**Technical Preservation:**

- ✅ **Lighting** - Direction, quality, time of day, shadows
- ✅ **Camera angle** - Perspective, height, distance
- ✅ **Camera settings** - Focal length, depth of field
- ✅ **Color grading** - Overall tone, warmth, color treatment
- ✅ **Atmosphere** - Mood, romantic feel, cinematic quality

### What CAN Change (Pose Variations)

**Body Positioning:**

- ❌ Standing vs. walking
- ❌ Facing camera vs. facing away
- ❌ Side-by-side vs. one behind other
- ❌ Distance between subjects
- ❌ Body angles and orientations

**Interactions:**

- ❌ Holding hands vs. embracing
- ❌ Close together vs. separated
- ❌ Touching vs. not touching
- ❌ Type of embrace (behind, side, front)

**Gaze & Expression:**

- ❌ Looking at camera vs. at each other
- ❌ Looking away vs. downward
- ❌ Facial expressions (while maintaining identity)
- ❌ Head positioning and angles

**Hand & Arm Positions:**

- ❌ Arms around each other vs. at sides
- ❌ Holding hands vs. separate
- ❌ Bouquet position and hand holding it
- ❌ Hands clasped vs. loose

## 30 Pro Tips

### Preservation Tips (1-10)

**1. Emphasize Identity Preservation Multiple Times**
State "preserve all original facial features, identity, proportions exactly" multiple times for critical elements.

**2. Describe Original Subjects in Detail**
The more detail about original appearance (hair, clothing, features), the better AI preserves them.

**3. Use "Without Any Alterations" Language**
Add explicit "without any alterations" after preservation statements for emphasis.

**4. List Specific Clothing Details**
Name every element: suit color, boutonniere, shirt style, shoes—specificity aids preservation.

**5. Describe Background in Full Detail Every Time**
Even though it should stay same, full description helps AI maintain consistency.

**6. Specify Lighting Characteristics Thoroughly**
"Overcast sky, even illumination, no harsh shadows" helps preserve exact light quality.

**7. Reference Exact Material Colors**
"Dark gray fine gravel", "deep red roses", "cream/beige suit"—specific colors maintain palette.

**8. State Camera Settings Each Time**
"35mm prime lens, slightly elevated, looking faintly downward" maintains perspective.

**9. Always Include "Everything Else Identical"**
End with explicit "everything else identical" as final preservation reminder.

**10. Use High-Quality Source Images**
Better original = better preservation—start with sharp, well-lit, professional photos.

---

### Pose Variation Tips (11-20)

**11. Change One Element at a Time**
Vary pose OR expression OR gaze, not everything at once—maintains control.

**12. Test Similar Poses Sequentially**
If generating embrace pose, try multiple embrace variations in sequence for comparison.

**13. Specify Exact Body Positioning**
"Man behind woman, arms wrapped from behind, cheek against her head"—be very specific.

**14. Describe Hand Positions Explicitly**
"Hands gently clasped", "arms around waist", "holding bouquet with both hands"—detail matters.

**15. Direct Gaze Clearly**
"Both looking at camera", "woman at camera, man at woman"—eliminate ambiguity.

**16. Specify Bouquet Positioning Every Time**
Bouquet is key prop—always state where it is and how it's held in each pose.

**17. Define Crop/Framing for Each Pose**
"Full body", "three-quarter", "upper body"—different poses suit different crops.

**18. Include Emotional Direction**
"Calm expression", "soft smile", "playful look"—guides facial expression while preserving features.

**19. Use Movement Language Carefully**
"Walking", "paused", "standing", "turning"—action verbs set pose dynamics clearly.

**20. Build Pose Sequences Logically**
Walking toward → paused facing camera → embrace → walking away = storytelling flow.

---

### Technical & Workflow Tips (21-30)

**21. Generate 3-5 Variations Per Pose**
AI varies slightly each time—generate multiple, select best preservation result.

**22. Check Facial Preservation First**
Before evaluating pose success, verify faces maintained identity—non-negotiable.

**23. Verify Clothing Details**
Check suit fit, dress style, boutonniere, all clothing matches original exactly.

**24. Examine Background Consistency**
Zoom in on foliage—leaves should look same, not randomly regenerated.

**25. Compare Lighting Quality**
Side-by-side with original—light direction, shadows, exposure should match.

**26. Save All Successful Variations**
Build library of successful pose variations from single setup—portfolio gold.

**27. Use Consistent Platform**
Stick with one AI platform per project for consistency in style and quality.

**28. Test on Lower-Stakes Images First**
Practice pose regeneration on test shoots before applying to critical client work.

**29. Combine with Light Manual Editing**
Minor Photoshop touch-ups acceptable if preservation is 95% there with small fixable issues.

**30. Document Successful Prompts**
Save prompts that worked well—build personal template library for future use.
