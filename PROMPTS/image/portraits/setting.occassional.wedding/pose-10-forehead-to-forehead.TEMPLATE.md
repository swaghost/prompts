## Prompt Description

An intensely intimate romantic wedding portrait where both subjects stand facing each other with foreheads touching gently. This creates a deeply personal private moment emphasizing emotional connection and tenderness. Eyes are typically closed or gazing downward, creating introspective romantic intimacy perfect for emotional storytelling and showcasing vulnerability and connection.

## Photographer Notes

When creating this pose:

- Ensure gentle forehead contact, not pressing hard
- Eyes closed creates more intimacy than open
- Hands should be gentle and tender, not gripping
- Allow subjects to breathe and be present
- This requires trust and comfort between couple
- Best captured in upper body or three-quarter crop
- Focus should be sharp on faces touching
