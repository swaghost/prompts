### Issue 1: Face Doesn't Match Source Image

**Problem:** Generated portrait doesn't preserve identity from @image1 reference.

**Solutions:**

- Strengthen identity lock language: "ABSOLUTE IDENTITY LOCK — face must match @image1 exactly"

- Specify: "subject must remain instantly recognizable, no face morphing allowed"
- Use consistent seed numbers if platform supports it
- Regenerate with stronger emphasis on identity preservation

### Issue 2: Lighting Too Flat or Not Dramatic Enough

**Problem:** Chiaroscuro effect weak, lighting looks too even or soft.

**Solutions:**

- Strengthen contrast language: "VERY high contrast, deep TRUE blacks, bright sculpted highlights"
- Emphasize: "single HARD directional key light, NO fill light, NO reflectors"
- Add: "shadow side falling COMPLETELY into black darkness, no detail visible in shadow"
- Specify: "sharp shadow edges, not soft or gradual transitions"
- Reference specific style: "Rembrandt lighting, Renaissance chiaroscuro painting style"

### Issue 3: Skin Looks Too Smooth or Artificial

**Problem:** Skin appears airbrushed, plastic, or overly perfect.

**Solutions:**

- Add negative prompts: "no smoothing, no airbrushing, no beauty filters, no plastic skin, no waxy appearance"
- Layer texture descriptors: "pronounced visible pores, rich fine texture, natural skin variation"
- Emphasize imperfections: "soft peach fuzz visible, light scattered freckles, fine lines, natural redness"
- Add: "texture fully intact through makeup, pores clearly defined, looks like real human skin"
- Specify: "maximum realism, authentic skin surface with all natural characteristics"

### Issue 4: Hair Looks Too Perfect or CGI

**Problem:** Hair appears artificial, too smooth, or digitally rendered.

**Solutions:**

### Issue 5: Eyes Look Dead or Artificial

**Problem:** Eyes lack depth, appear flat, or don't have lifelike quality.

**Solutions:**

- Add: "clear light green iris with high contrast against black hair and tan skin"

### Issue 6: Makeup Overpowers Natural Features
