# Prompt 06: Overhead on the Green Sofa, Racing Leathers

**OVERHEAD SELFIE · BRIGHT DAYLIGHT**

---

A young East Asian woman, early-to-mid 20s, fair skin, a sharp jaw-length black bob with a centre parting and bright turquoise-blue streaks running through the under-layer, visible where the hair splays out. Small black oval sunglasses with dark lenses.

Expression: lips together and pushed forward in a slight pout, chin level, head tipped back against the sofa, gaze up into the lens through the dark lenses. Cool, flat, deliberately unimpressed.

Pose: lying back along a sofa, shot from directly above by her own outstretched arm - the arm visible running up through the lower foreground of the frame, hand out of shot, a small dark tattoo on the inner forearm. Legs stretched out along the seat, one knee slightly bent.

Outfit: a vintage motocross racing leather jacket in panels of milk-brown, black, cream and white - round embroidered sponsor patches on the chest and sleeve, a bold black chest panel with large white block lettering, white leather racing stripes running down the arms, a white star on one sleeve and a leaping-figure logo on the black lower sleeve, zip open. A plain white tee beneath. Matching brown and black leather racing trousers with white side stripes.

Setting: a designer living room seen from a steep overhead angle. She lies on a chartreuse-olive velvet sofa, the deep cushions and rolled arm filling the right of the frame. Behind and below: a dark walnut grid bookshelf packed with art and photography books, a small white figurine, a wall clock, a dark carved bust and a red and black sculptural object. A patterned Persian rug on the floor, cream walls, tan cabinetry at the edge of frame.

Shot angle: arm's-length selfie held directly above her face looking straight down, the room tilted and receding diagonally behind her head.

Lighting: bright soft natural daylight from a window off frame - even clean light across the face and leather, gentle sheen on the velvet, warm bounce from the timber shelving, no hard shadow.

Sharp phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic leather and velvet texture. Photorealistic.
