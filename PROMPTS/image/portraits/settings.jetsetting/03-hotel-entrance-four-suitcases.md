# Prompt 03: Hotel Entrance, Four Suitcases

**OVERCAST DAYLIGHT · FULL FIGURE**

---

A young woman, mid-20s, warm tanned olive skin, bare-faced with a natural sheen, long dark brown hair falling past the shoulders in loose undone waves, slightly wind-messed. Slim build with lean arms. Full natural brows, dark eyes, bare lips.

Expression: looking straight into the lens, mouth slightly open, brows level and relaxed. Blank and unbothered, caught mid-wait.

Pose: standing perched back against a suitcase with legs apart and knees slightly bent, weight resting on the case behind her. Both hands together at waist height holding a phone.

Details: a striped canvas crossbody bag hanging across the body at hip level. Several slim rings. A grey hoodie draped over the suitcase behind her. White low-profile sneakers.

Outfit: a light blue ringer t-shirt with navy trim at the collar and sleeve cuffs, printed across the chest with blue script text and a small yellow cartoon chick graphic. Grey wide-leg sweatpants with a white drawstring tied at the waist, hems breaking over the shoes.

Luggage: four pieces arranged around her. Left: a large coral-pink ribbed hard-shell suitcase standing upright with a black leather camera-style handbag sitting on top. Directly behind her: a large silver ribbed hard-shell suitcase, its handle extended, that she is leaning against. Right: a smaller silver ribbed suitcase with its handle up, a blue, pink and cream tweed plaid tote bag balanced on top with snack packets poking out.

Setting: the entrance of an upscale hotel. A tall dark timber portal frame with glass revolving doors directly behind her, the warm lobby visible through the glass with sofas, table lamps, a white orchid arrangement and a slatted timber ceiling. Polished stone flooring inside, glass walls to the left reflecting trees and parked cars, cobblestone paving and a granite kerb in the foreground.

Shot angle: camera at chest height a few metres back, straight on, full figure and all four cases in frame, doorway symmetrical behind her.

Lighting: soft flat overcast daylight - even and shadowless on the face, cool neutral outdoors against the warm glowing lobby behind. Clean and balanced.

Sharp phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic fabric and skin texture. Photorealistic.
