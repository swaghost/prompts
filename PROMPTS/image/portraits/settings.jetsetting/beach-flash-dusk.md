## Complete Prompt

A white male, mid-20s, deeply tanned skin with a warm sunburn flush across the shoulders and chest, bright blue eyes, dark brown hair pushed up under a cap. Clean-shaven, defined muscular build with broad chest and shoulders.

Expression: looking straight down the lens, lips together in a flat cocky half-smile, eyes wide and lit by the flash. Confident, playful, mid-pose.

Pose: standing square to camera, shirtless, right arm raised close to his face throwing an extended index-and-pinky rock sign, the index finger passing just in front of his nose and partly covering it.

Headwear: vintage cream rope-front trucker cap, curved brim, gold braid cord across the front panel, an embroidered oval patch in red, yellow and green reading "Wish Me Luck 777 READY MIX".

Jewellery: white puka shell necklace sitting high on the collarbone. Beneath it a fine gold chain with a round gold coin pendant and a small blue bead. Small gold hoop earring. A stack of pale yellow-green beaded bracelets on the raised wrist with a blue and white patterned bandana tied beside them.

Tattoos: a large detailed butterfly on the forearm, fine botanical leaf and branch work down the arm, a small crescent moon, small hand-written script lines reading "THAT'S ALL" near the elbow and a short line of small text high on the shoulder.

Setting: a tropical beach at dusk. Tall palm trees leaning in from the upper left, their fronds silhouetted. Behind him a wide sky of grey-blue cloud breaking to warm orange and yellow at the horizon, calm water reflecting it, a scatter of small warm lights along the far shoreline, pale sand to the right. Blurred figures far behind in shadow.

Shot angle: camera slightly below his eye level a couple of steps away, upper body filling the frame, raised hand large in the foreground.

Lighting: hard direct on-camera flash - bright hot highlights on the chest, shoulders, raised hand and cap, sharp falloff into the darkening dusk behind, the sky and water retained at ambient exposure. Deep contrast between the lit subject and the twilight background.

35mm film compact snapshot with a small red digital date stamp printed vertically in the left margin. Photorealistic.
