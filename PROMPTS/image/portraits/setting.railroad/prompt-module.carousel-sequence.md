# Prompt Module - Railroad Fashion Carousel Sequence

## Purpose

Use these ten prompts as a coherent Instagram carousel or editorial image sequence. Keep the same subject, outfit, location, time of day, colour grade, and identity across every slide.

## Slide 1 - Establishing Shot

```
wide environmental shot showing the full desert railroad setup, with the subject visible but small, the chair centred on the tracks, rails converging into the horizon, and the distant train establishing scale and delayed danger
```

## Slide 2 - Hero Shot

```
the main extreme ground-level low-angle fashion portrait, full outfit visible, subject seated between the rails, converging tracks leading toward the face, maximum cinematic impact and environmental tension
```

## Slide 3 - Detail Close-Up

```
tight detail shot of the gloved hands holding lipstick and mirror, pearl necklace, fabric texture, hat brim, and natural skin detail, with the desert and rails reduced to atmospheric blur
```

## Slide 4 - Alternate Angle

```
profile side view or over-the-shoulder perspective of the same seated subject, showing the chair, tracks, desert depth, and distant train from a new but continuous camera position
```

## Slide 5 - Action Moment

```
the subject captured mid-action while applying lipstick, adjusting the hat, rising from the chair, or looking toward the distant train, with subtle wind movement in clothing and hair
```

## Slide 6 - Outfit Detail

```
fashion-focused crop emphasizing the sculptural sleeve, skirt slit, opera glove, pearl choker, dress construction, and tactile matte-black fabric under directional desert light
```

## Slide 7 - Environmental Detail

```
close-up of railroad steel, dusty wooden sleepers, cracked desert earth, chair shadow, heat haze, or the distant train, grounding the editorial story in its physical location
```

## Slide 8 - Face Portrait

```
head-and-shoulders portrait focused on the subject's expression, direct gaze, natural skin texture, makeup detail, and restrained cinematic lighting, with the desert softly blurred behind
```

## Slide 9 - Cinematic Wide

```
cinematic wide shot showing the subject within the vast desert, dramatic sky, converging railroad lines, distant train, and atmospheric isolation, preserving the same outfit and colour grade
```

## Slide 10 - Conceptual Closer

```
final conceptual image with the subject standing and walking away, or the chair left empty facing the horizon, creating a symbolic conclusion about departure, danger, freedom, or time
```

## Continuity Lock

```
Keep the same subject identity, facial features, hairstyle, outfit, accessories, location, weather, time of day, colour grade, film grain, and environmental object positions across every slide. Use the same seed or reference image where supported. Change only the explicitly requested camera framing, pose, or action.
```

## Base Negative Prompt

```
CGI, 3D render, illustration, anime, stylized, beauty filter, airbrushed skin, plastic face, AI skin smoothing, crushed blacks, over-sharpening halos, excessive HDR, fake bokeh, bloom, studio lighting, eye-level camera when low angle is specified, distorted anatomy, missing fingers, warped hands, floating chair, incorrect rails, oversized or too-close train, duplicated objects, logos, text, watermark, oversaturated colours, low resolution, compression artifacts
```
