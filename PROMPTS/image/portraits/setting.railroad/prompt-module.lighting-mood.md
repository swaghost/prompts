# Prompt Module - Lighting, Colour, and Mood

## Base Late-Afternoon Light

```
late-afternoon desert sun, hard but motivated and softened by airborne dust, warm directional side light creating crisp micro-highlights and deep shadow on the subject, clothing, pearls, rails, and ground. Restrained cinematic teal-and-amber balance, natural desert tones, deep blacks preserved with visible texture, heat haze, suspended dust particles, and subtle natural film grain.
```

## Golden Hour

```
golden-hour light with the sun low on the horizon, intense amber directional light, extremely long shadows, three-quarter backlight creating a rim around the silhouette, dust particles glowing in the backlight, reduced contrast, and romantic dreamy atmosphere
```

## Harsh Midday

```
harsh midday sun directly overhead, hard shadows beneath the hat, nose, chin, and body, bleached highlights on the desert, extreme contrast, dry clear air, unforgiving documentary realism, and neutral-to-cool colour temperature
```

## Overcast Soft Light

```
overcast sky creating a giant softbox, even diffused illumination, soft gentle shadows, muted desert colours, cool colour temperature, restrained editorial catalogue quality, and emphasis on garment texture
```

## Sunrise Pastels

```
early morning sunrise with a pastel sky shifting from peach and pink near the horizon through lavender to cool blue overhead, long shadows, fresh morning atmosphere, delicate mist, and hopeful beginning-of-journey mood
```

## Blue Hour

```
blue-hour twilight after sunset, deep blue ambient illumination, mysterious transitional atmosphere, practical warm light from a lantern or vehicle headlight, stars beginning to appear, and a slightly underexposed but detailed silhouette
```

## Storm Drama

```
dark storm clouds with breaks that allow shafts of sunlight to pierce through, wet ground reflecting the light, high contrast, dynamic natural illumination, wind-blown dust or rain, and a tense atmosphere shaped by nature's power
```

## Night Artificial Light

```
nighttime scene lit by practical vintage headlights or a warm handheld lantern, cool moonlight providing ambient fill, long concentrated shadows, warm tungsten versus cool moonlight contrast, deep blacks, and cinematic noir chiaroscuro
```

## Mood Directions

### High-Fashion Cinematic Power

```
high-fashion cinematic power and tension - dominance, elegance, calm defiance, danger held at a distance, ultra-detailed photorealism, true-to-life proportions, documentary realism
```

### Romantic Melancholy

```
romantic melancholy and nostalgic longing - wistful beauty, graceful sadness, elegant solitude, bittersweet isolation, muted colours, gentle haze, and vulnerable strength
```

### Rebellious Adventure

```
rebellious adventure and free-spirited defiance - confident rebellion, playful danger, joyful risk-taking, untamed freedom, dynamic composition, wind-blown elements, and rule-breaking cool
```

### Dystopian Survival

```
dystopian survival and post-apocalyptic endurance - gritty determination, weathered resilience, harsh reality, worn beauty, desaturated colour, increased grain, and functional toughness
```

### Surreal Dream State

```
surreal dream state and magical realism - ethereal beauty, liminal ambiguity, time standing still, subtle reality bending, unusual colour grading, and fashion as art performance
```

### Serene Meditation

```
serene meditation and zen minimalism - peaceful acceptance, mindful presence, calm centeredness, minimal elements, balanced composition, gentle light, and negative space as an active element
```

### Noir Mystery

```
film noir mystery and femme-fatale intrigue - dangerous beauty, secretive allure, shadow play, classic glamour meeting desert grit, high-contrast chiaroscuro, and mysterious restraint
```
