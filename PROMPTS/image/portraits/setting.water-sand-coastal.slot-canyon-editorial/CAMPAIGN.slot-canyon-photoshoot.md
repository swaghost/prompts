# Slot Canyon Photoshoot - Image Prompt Pack

## Campaign Overview

Six descriptive AI image prompts recreating the pose, lighting, wardrobe, and mood of a slot-canyon fashion and travel editorial. The collection moves from dynamic sandstone portraits and dramatic shafts of light to a molten-gold beauty close-up and quiet, contemplative frames.

## Shared Direction

- Natural sandstone slot canyons with swirling, layered rock textures
- Warm amber, orange, honey, rust, terracotta, and golden-bronze tones
- Editorial travel-fashion and fine-art portrait photography
- Strong single-source sunlight, chiaroscuro, dust particles, and atmospheric depth
- Medium-format film and cinematic realism with authentic skin, fabric, and rock texture
