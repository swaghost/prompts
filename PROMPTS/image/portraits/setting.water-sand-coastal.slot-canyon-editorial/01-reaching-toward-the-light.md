# Slot Canyon Photoshoot - Image 1: Reaching Toward the Light

## Full Prompt

A young woman with tousled dark wavy hair and sun-tanned skin, wearing a sage-olive strapless bandeau bikini top and black bikini bottoms, stands barefoot inside a narrow sandstone slot canyon. She is captured in profile in a dynamic pose: one leg bent with her hand resting on the knee, the other arm raised overhead with fingertips reaching up toward an opening in the rock, chin tilted up, gazing toward the light. The canyon's swirling orange and cream sandstone walls curve dramatically around her. Warm golden-hour light spills down from above, creating strong chiaroscuro shadow across her body and glowing highlights on the rock. Cinematic editorial travel-fashion photography, shot on medium format film, warm amber color grade, shallow depth with soft rock bokeh in the foreground.
