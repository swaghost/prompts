# Slot Canyon Photoshoot - Image 5: Seated in the Light Shaft

## Full Prompt

A woman with long dark hair, wearing a black bikini, is seated on sandy ground viewed from behind, deep inside a tall, narrow slot canyon. She is silhouetted against a bright, sharply defined vertical beam of light cutting down through a crack in the rock high above. Rich rust-orange and terracotta sandstone walls curve dramatically overhead and to either side, with small rocks and driftwood scattered on the pale sandy floor in the foreground. Moody, atmospheric lighting with a single strong light source, minimalist composition, fine-art landscape-portrait travel photography with a contemplative, quiet mood.
