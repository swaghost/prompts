# Slot Canyon Photoshoot - Image 3: Beam of Light, Hugging Knees

## Full Prompt

A woman with long dark hair, dressed in loose flowing mustard-yellow linen trousers and a matching top, sits cross-legged on a raised sandstone ledge inside a narrow slot canyon. She hugs her knees to her chest, arms wrapped around her legs, head tilted back with eyes closed, face lifted into a single dramatic vertical shaft of sunlight cutting down through the dark crevice above her. The deep red-orange sandstone walls curve away into shadow on either side. Single-light-source cinematic photography, serene and meditative mood, warm saturated tones, fine-art travel portrait composition with strong negative space.

## Key Elements

- **Wardrobe:** Loose flowing mustard-yellow linen trousers and matching top
- **Pose:** Cross-legged, knees hugged to chest, head lifted into the light
- **Setting:** Raised sandstone ledge inside a narrow dark crevice
- **Lighting:** One sharply defined vertical shaft of sunlight
- **Style:** Serene fine-art travel portrait, warm saturated tones
