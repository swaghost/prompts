## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL IDENTITY PRESERVATION:**
Upload your reference image and use it as the primary identity anchor. Create an ultra-high-resolution 4×4 black-and-white photobooth contact sheet featuring the same adult woman from the reference image. Preserve her identity with strict consistency, including facial features, hairstyle, face shape, skin texture, and proportions across all 16 panels.

**Subject:**
Same adult woman from reference maintained consistently across all 16 frames. Dress her in plain black T-shirt with no logos or graphics.

**Setting/Environment:**
Plain neutral gray background with classic photobooth aesthetic. No props, no dramatic backgrounds. Direct front photobooth flash with slightly hard but flattering lighting and soft short shadows behind the subject. Avoid cinematic side lighting or dramatic backgrounds.

**Lighting:**
Direct front photobooth flash creating authentic booth lighting. Slightly hard but flattering quality with balanced frontal illumination.

**Composition:**
Capture each panel as tight head-and-shoulders portrait using 50mm lens equivalent, keeping eyes perfectly sharp in every frame. Arrange the images in 4×4 square grid with thin gutters and high consistency between panels.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Pure black and white with medium-high contrast, subtle analog photobooth grain, crisp sharpness, and clean edges. Classic photobooth aesthetic throughout.

**Technical Specifications:**
Ultra-high-resolution with 50mm lens. Eyes perfectly sharp in every frame. Render in pure black and white with medium-high contrast. Prioritize realistic hands and facial anatomy, ensuring no extra or missing fingers, no warped faces, uneven eyes, melted mouths, plastic skin, text, watermarks, or logos.

Avoid color, soft focus, studio lighting with side shadows, heavy makeup, fashion styling, CGI skin, unrealistic smoothing, duplicate subjects, AI face-melt, misshapen hands, inconsistent identity across panels, visible grid lines bleeding into portraits, over-processed contrast, or watermarks.
