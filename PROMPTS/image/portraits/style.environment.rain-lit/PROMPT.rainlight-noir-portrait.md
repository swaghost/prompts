# 🌧️ Rainlight Noir Portrait Prompt

## Overview

Cinematic close-up portrait featuring dramatic rain, night lighting, and gritty noir atmosphere with identity preservation.

## Instructions

Upload your reference image and then use the prompt below.

---

## PROMPT

```
Cinematic close-up portrait of a person standing in heavy rain at night, dark coat, wet hair falling across the forehead, skin glistening with raindrops. Dramatic moody lighting, warm orange streetlight bokeh in the background, shallow depth of field, intense contemplative expression, gritty noir atmosphere, ultra-realistic, high contrast, detailed skin texture, cinematic color grading, shot on 85mm lens, f/1.8, photorealistic, dark urban aesthetic, film still look, preserve the subject's original facial features and likeness from the reference image.
```

## Key Elements

### Visual Style

- **Atmosphere**: Gritty noir with heavy rain
- **Lighting**: Dramatic moody lighting with warm orange streetlight bokeh
- **Aesthetic**: Dark urban, film still look

### Subject Details

- **Wardrobe**: Dark coat
- **Hair**: Wet, falling across forehead
- **Skin**: Glistening with raindrops, detailed texture
- **Expression**: Intense, contemplative

### Technical Specifications

- **Shot Type**: Cinematic close-up portrait
- **Lens**: 85mm, f/1.8
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow (bokeh background)
- **Contrast**: High contrast
- **Grading**: Cinematic color grading
- **Quality**: Ultra-realistic, photorealistic

### Identity Preservation

- Preserves subject's original facial features and likeness from reference image
- Maintains unique characteristics while adding rain/noir elements

## Use Cases

- Film noir character portraits
- Atmospheric editorial photography
- Cinematic character studies
- Dramatic rain scene portraits

## Tips

- Upload a clear reference image with visible facial features
- Works best with straight-on or slight angle portraits
- The warm orange streetlight creates a classic noir color palette
- Rain and wet elements add dramatic texture and atmosphere
- High contrast enhances the moody, cinematic feel
