# Luxury Resort Editorial - Infinity Pool Path White Haute Couture

## Prompt Type

Fashion Editorial Photography - Luxury Resort Lifestyle

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Ultra-realistic luxury editorial fashion photography at a 5-star beachfront resort, capturing an elegant woman walking slowly along a wide natural stone path between tall palm trees and transparent glass lifeguard stations, leading to an infinity pool and ocean. Sophisticated resort haute couture in pristine white with refined accessories and professional editorial makeup.

## Detailed Prompt

```
Create an ultra-realistic luxury editorial photograph set at a 5-star beachfront resort. I am walking slowly along a wide path of clear natural stone between tall palm trees and transparent glass lifeguard structures that lead to an infinity pool and the ocean. With one hand, I gently shield my eyes from the strong sun while holding a large designer beach bag with the other. My pose is natural, relaxed, and confident, as if I were being photographed for a luxury travel magazine cover.

I am wearing an elegant white haute couture resort dress with fitted bodice, wide skirt, and high-quality woven straps that accentuate my silhouette. The dress has a sophisticated air, perfect for an escapade at a luxury resort. My hair is perfectly smooth, pulled into a low elegant chignon.

Accessories: fine designer sunglasses, fine gold hoop earrings, a delicate bracelet, minimalist rings, and a large designer beach bag in cream color with contrasting stripes.

Makeup: sophisticated editorial fashion shine with perfectly radiant skin, natural nude lips, soft bronze contour, and a fresh natural tan.

Setting: modern 5-star beachfront resort, tall palm trees, infinity pool, panoramic view of calm ocean, transparent glass lifeguard structures, minimalist Mediterranean luxury architecture, and a bright clear sky.

Lighting is intense natural sunlight, a professional luxury photoshoot ensuring the skin appears perfectly smooth and radiant, white fabric maintains its rich texture, and the space is filled with soft shadows from the palm trees, creating cinematic depth.
```
