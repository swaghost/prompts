# Luxury Editorial - French Riviera Rocky Coast White Ensemble

## Prompt Type

Fashion Editorial Photography - Mediterranean Coastal Luxury

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Ultra-realistic luxury editorial fashion photography on the picturesque rocky coast of the French Riviera, at the foot of an old stone fortress. Walking along a stone walkway with the Mediterranean crystalline sea, soft maritime breeze, and traditional summer elegance in an all-white snow-like outfit with elegant straw hat.

## Detailed Prompt

```
Create an ultra-realistic luxury editorial photograph on the picturesque rocky coast of the French Riviera, at the foot of an old stone fortress. I am walking along a stone walkway beside the Mediterranean crystalline sea. I hold a small black handbag with a gold chain. I have a slight natural smile, a relaxed and confident pose, and my gaze is directed at the camera.

I am wearing a luxurious all-white snow outfit: a simple fitted bodysuit with wide straps and a long knitted skirt with a slit in the thigh and a draped marcação fold model envelope. The textured high-quality fabric beautifully accentuates the silhouette and appears to come straight from a haute couture resort collection. On my head, a large wide-brim white straw hat with long pearl droplet pins that cascade over my face.

Hair is perfectly pulled into a low chignon.

Accessories: a soft white leather handbag, gold minimalist bracelets, fine rings, elegant sandals with thin straps, and sophisticated sunglasses.

Makeup: sophisticated editorial fashion shine with perfectly radiant skin, a soft bronze tan, natural makeup on eyes, and nude lips.

Setting: the rocky coast of the French Riviera, an old fortress perched on a cliff, the blue Mediterranean with its crystalline waters, white cliffs, lush Mediterranean greenery, and dramatic clouds against a brilliant blue sky.

Lighting is natural midday sunlight, a professional luxury fashion photoshoot creating perfectly smooth and radiant skin, a beautiful texture in white fabrics, and a three-dimensional rich cinematic landscape.
```
