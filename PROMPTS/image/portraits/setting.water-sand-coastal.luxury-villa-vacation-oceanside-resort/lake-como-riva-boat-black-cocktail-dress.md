```
Create an ultra-realistic luxury editorial photograph on Lake Como, Italy. I am seated on the stern of a classic varnished wooden Riva boat with my legs crossed elegantly. I hold a small black leather bag with a gold chain. I have a slight natural smile, a relaxed and confident pose, and my gaze is directed at the camera.

I am wearing an impeccable short black dress with an evasé (flared) shoulder cut and visible shoulders. A large wide-brim straw hat with black ribbon, fine black frame sunglasses, large silver hoop earrings, a delicate ring, and minimalist manicure. My long dark hair is perfectly smooth and sleek.

Behind me, a luxurious villa on the shores of Lake Como, cypress trees, impeccable gardens, green mountains, emerald waters, and a picturesque Italian landscape. A sunny luminous day, soft reflections in the water, rich natural colors.

Color palette: black, caramel, emerald green, turquoise, navy blue, terracotta, wood.
```
