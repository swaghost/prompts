# Luxury Beach Editorial - Tropical Island Geometric Swimsuit

## Prompt Type

Fashion Editorial Photography - Tropical Beach Luxury Resort

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Ultra-realistic comfortable luxury fashion photography on an isolated tropical beach with white sand and turquoise water. Reclining at the beach edge in a relaxed elegant pose, one hand in sand, other gracefully touching a large wide-brim straw sun hat that softly frames the face.

## Detailed Prompt

```
Create an ultra-realistic comfortable luxury fashion photograph on an isolated tropical beach with white sand and turquoise water. I am reclining at the beach edge in a relaxed and elegant pose, with one hand resting on the sand and the other gracefully touching an enormous wide-brim straw sun hat that creates a sophisticated effect and beautifully frames my face.

I am wearing a luxurious one-piece swimsuit with a geometric pattern in cream and chocolate tones, decorated with a deep V-neckline and a decorative gold ring at the waist. My hair is long, natural, slightly damp, and falls loosely over my shoulders and onto the sand. She wears minimalist gold jewelry: a thin bracelet, several elegant rings, and small earrings.

Behind me, a turquoise-blue ocean with gentle waves, a white sand beach, and lush tropical green hills. The brilliant midday sun creates beautiful shadows on my hat and highlights its natural shine. The atmosphere is that of a private resort.

Core color palette: sand, milky tones, caramel, chocolate, gold, turquoise, emerald green.
```

## Identity Preservation Settings
