# Wild Bubble - Riviera Summer Editorial

## Description of Resulting Image

A playful resort fashion portrait of a brunette in a hot-pink textured swimsuit blowing soap bubbles from a white sun lounger beneath a golden overwater-bungalow sunset.

## Usage

Perfect for playful resort campaigns, swimwear editorials, family-friendly summer lifestyle content, and overwater-villa advertising. Ideal when a whimsical prop adds movement without losing a premium fashion finish.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney v6.1, Flux Kontext, GPT Image, or another photorealistic model.

## Actor or Subject

Young brunette woman with blue eyes, long wavy brown hair, oval tortoiseshell sunglasses, hot-pink textured one-piece with yellow trim and gold chest lettering, holding a yellow bubble wand.

- Optional model reference
- Optional overwater-bungalow resort reference

## Subject
