# Dotted Shore - Riviera Summer Editorial

## Description of Resulting Image

A polished coastal-cliff fashion portrait of an olive-skinned woman in a coordinated black-and-white polka-dot bikini and headscarf with rose-tinted sunglasses and layered gold jewelry.

## Usage

Perfect for island resort campaigns, coastal tourism, swimwear lookbooks, and editorial jewelry styling. Ideal for a strong standing pose with expansive sea and sandy-beach context.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney v6.1, Flux Kontext, GPT Image, or another photorealistic model.

## Actor or Subject

Tall olive-skinned woman with long straight black hair, polka-dot bikini and matching headscarf, rose-tinted sunglasses, gold necklaces, hoops, watch, and bracelets.

## Image Prompt

## Subject

A tall olive-skinned woman with long straight black hair wears a white-and-black polka-dot bikini set with a matching polka-dot headscarf. She wears small round rose-tinted sunglasses, stacked gold necklaces, gold hoop earrings, a gold watch, and bracelets. She stands with both arms raised above her head.

## Setting/Environment

Rocky coastal cliffs with a bright turquoise Caribbean sea and sandy beach behind her.

## Lighting

Warm noon sunlight illuminates the jewelry, polka-dot pattern, skin, and cliff textures with natural crisp shadows.

## Composition

Use the raised arms and headscarf to create a strong upper silhouette; keep the coast and sea legible behind her.

## Style & Aesthetic

Photorealistic luxury swimwear and jewelry editorial.

## Technical Specifications

85mm lens, 3:4 portrait, high-resolution fashion photography.

Confident, coastal, polished, sunlit, expansive.

Photorealistic, editorial swimwear, realistic jewelry, bright sea, sharp subject, high detail.
