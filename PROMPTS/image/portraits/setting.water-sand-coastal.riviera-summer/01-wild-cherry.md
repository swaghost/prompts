A candid luxury beach-club fashion portrait of a sun-kissed woman in a sheer white swimsuit and cherry-print headscarf laughing while lifting fresh red cherries above her head.

Perfect for summer swimwear campaigns, beach-club lifestyle content, resort advertising, and playful food-and-fashion editorials. Ideal for social media portraits and luxury hospitality campaigns with a warm candid mood.

## Image Prompt

## Subject

A sun-kissed woman with long wet dark hair wears a sheer white one-piece swimsuit, a white headscarf printed with red cherries, gold bangles, and a gold watch. She laughs with her mouth open while holding a bunch of fresh red cherries above her head as if about to eat them.

## Setting/Environment

She is seated on a white daybed at a luxury beach club. A crowded summer party fills the background, softly blurred and secondary.

## Lighting

Warm golden afternoon sunlight creates luminous highlights on wet hair, skin, jewelry, cherries, and white fabric.

## Composition

Capture an unposed laugh with the raised cherries creating a strong upper-frame gesture and the daybed grounding the lower frame.

## Style & Aesthetic

Photorealistic editorial fashion photography with candid summer luxury energy.

## Technical Specifications

85mm lens, realistic skin and wet-hair texture, shallow depth of field, high resolution.

## Color Palette

White, cherry red, warm skin bronze, gold, muted beach-club neutrals.

Joyful, candid, playful, sun-warmed, luxurious.

Photorealistic, luxury editorial, natural skin texture, golden light, sharp subject, high detail.
