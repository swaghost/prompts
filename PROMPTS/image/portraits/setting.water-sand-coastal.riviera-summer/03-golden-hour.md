# Golden Hour - Riviera Summer Editorial

## Description of Resulting Image

A glamorous poolside resort portrait of an olive-skinned woman in a hot-pink one-shoulder swimsuit and black cat-eye sunglasses looking back over her shoulder with a lemon drink in hand.

## Usage

Perfect for resort campaigns, swimwear launches, pool-club advertising, and luxury summer social content. Ideal for a strong color-forward portrait with jewelry, sunglasses, and turquoise water as the main anchors.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney v6.1, Flux Kontext, GPT Image, or another photorealistic image model.

## Actor or Subject

Glamorous olive-skinned woman with dark hair slicked back, hot-pink one-shoulder swimsuit, black cat-eye sunglasses, gold earrings and rings, holding a clear wine glass with a lemon slice.

## Prerequisites

- Optional model reference
- Optional resort-pool reference

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** Bright warm sun with natural pool reflections

**Photographic Effects:** 85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of field

**Atmospheric Effects:** Turquoise pool, softly blurred resort setting

**Post-Processing:** Saturated but realistic summer color grade

**Style Filters:** Glamorous editorial swimwear photography

## Included Camera Frames

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Professional camera, 85mm lens
- **Framing & Composition:** Upper-body pool portrait with over-shoulder pose
- **Camera Angle:** Three-quarter rear angle toward camera
- **Depth of Field:** Subject sharp, pool and resort softly blurred
- **Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 portrait
- **Shot Distance:** Medium close-up

## Image Prompt

## Subject

A glamorous woman with olive skin and dark hair slicked back stands in a luxury resort infinity pool. She wears a hot-pink one-shoulder swimsuit, oversized black cat-eye sunglasses with a gold logo, gold hoop earrings, and stacked gold rings. She holds a clear wine glass with a lemon slice and looks back over her shoulder toward camera.

## Setting/Environment

Turquoise pool water and a bright resort environment fall softly out of focus behind her.

## Lighting

Bright natural sun creates clean highlights on wet skin, jewelry, sunglasses, glass, and pool water.

## Composition

Use the over-shoulder turn to show the back and shoulder line while keeping the face, sunglasses, drink, and swimsuit prominent.

## Style & Aesthetic

Photorealistic glamorous swimwear editorial with polished resort energy.

## Technical Specifications

85mm lens, shallow depth of field, 3:4 portrait, realistic wet skin and glass.

## Color Palette

Hot pink, turquoise, black, gold, warm olive skin.

## Mood & Atmosphere

Glamorous, confident, sunlit, playful, aspirational.

## Quality Keywords

Photorealistic, luxury resort, editorial swimwear, sharp face, realistic water, high detail.

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

Flat pose, missing glass, missing sunglasses, wrong swimsuit color, distorted jewelry, plastic skin, clutter, text, watermark, CGI.
