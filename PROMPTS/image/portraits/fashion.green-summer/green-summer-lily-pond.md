# Look #08: Lily Pond - Full-Body Water Immersion Fashion Portrait

## Description

Ultra-atmospheric full-body fashion portrait capturing subject sitting elegantly waist-deep in natural lily pond or marsh water, creating striking water-immersion aesthetic. Wearing stunning pleated sage-green gown with distinctive gathered feather waist detail matching reference image (@image2), with dress floating dramatically around body in water creating beautiful fabric spread and movement. Surrounded by lily pads, water reeds, aquatic plants, and natural marsh vegetation creating lush immersive wetland environment. Moody atmospheric lighting - soft overcast or late afternoon quality with low contrast and cinematic feel. Expression calm serene contemplative gazing softly toward camera or slightly away. Part of the Green Summer portrait collection emphasizing rich green-toned styling and elevated luxury aesthetic, this look is most dramatic water-based fashion editorial requiring unique environment and striking visual composition.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Subject standing instead of sitting in water

**Solution:** Emphasize "sitting waist-deep in water," "positioned sitting," "water level reaching waist or mid-torso," negative prompt "standing in water"

### Problem: Dress not floating or looks dry

**Solution:** Specify "dress floating dramatically around body in water," "spread across water surface," "fabric movement in water," negative prompt "dry dress"

### Problem: Gown design doesn't match @image2 reference

**Solution:** Ensure @image2 uploaded correctly, emphasize all specific design elements (pleated texture, feather waist detail), regenerate

### Problem: Water looks fake or like swimming pool

**Solution:** Specify "natural lily pond or marsh water," "slightly murky marsh water realistic," "NOT crystal clear pool," negative prompt "artificial glow, fake water, crystal clear pool water"

### Problem: Lily pads or marsh vegetation not present

**Solution:** Emphasize "lily pads scattered across water surface," "water reeds," "aquatic plants," "lush marsh vegetation," "immersive wetland"

### Problem: Lighting too bright or commercial

**Solution:** Emphasize "moody atmospheric natural light," "soft overcast," "low contrast cinematic," "NOT too bright or harsh," negative prompt "harsh HDR, studio lighting"

### Problem: Feather waist detail missing or wrong

**Solution:** Be very specific: "distinctive gathered feather detail at waist," "key design element," "prominent," emphasize this is critical feature from @image2

### Problem: Skin too smooth despite moody atmosphere

**Solution:** Emphasize "visible pores even in moody lighting," "natural tonal variation," "micro texture maintained," negative prompt "plastic skin, over-smoothed"
