# Look #09: Sunlit Bandeau - 3/4 Close Sun-Drenched Beauty Portrait

## Description

Ultra-glamorous sun-drenched tropical beauty portrait captured in intimate 3/4 close framing from shoulders to head. Subject wearing olive-green bandeau-style top with distinctive gold ring center detail creating elegant minimal swimwear or fashion aesthetic. Dramatic expressive pose with both hands lifted into hair, head tilted up and back toward sunlight creating confident radiant energy. Eyes open gazing upward or closed basking in warmth with joyful serene expression - face bathed in golden sunlight. Bright tropical sunlight cutting dramatically through dense jungle foliage creates strong sun rays, warm golden patches across face and shoulders, and dynamic leaf-shadow patterns. Part of the Green Summer portrait collection emphasizing rich green-toned styling and elevated luxury aesthetic, this look feels most radiant, sun-worshipping, and glamorously confident with strong editorial beauty energy.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Hands not in hair or arms down

**Solution:** Emphasize "both hands lifted into hair," "fingers running through or grasping hair," "hands creating movement," may need regeneration for correct pose

### Problem: Head not tilted back or not toward sunlight

**Solution:** Specify "head tilted up and back toward sunlight," "face bathed in golden sunlight," "sun-worshipping energy," emphasize dramatic pose

### Problem: Hands look distorted

**Solution:** Specify "natural hand positions," "fingers visible," "5 fingers each hand," negative prompt "distorted hands, extra fingers," may need regeneration

### Problem: Sunlight not dramatic enough

**Solution:** Emphasize "bright tropical sunlight cutting intensely through jungle," "strong visible sun rays," "warm golden patches," "high brightness," "powerful sun-drenched"

### Problem: Leaf shadows not present or too subtle

**Solution:** Specify "dynamic leaf-shadow patterns across face neck shoulders," "dramatic," include in lighting section prominently

### Problem: Bandeau wrong style or color

**Solution:** Be specific: "olive-green bandeau-style top," "gold ring or O-ring at center front," "distinctive detail," emphasize minimal clean design

### Problem: Expression not joyful or radiant enough

**Solution:** Emphasize "joyful serene blissful expression," "confident radiant energy," "basking in warmth," "sun-worshipping"

### Problem: Skin too smooth despite dramatic lighting

**Solution:** Emphasize "visible pores," "natural tonal variation," "micro texture," "sun-kissed radiant glow NOT artificial," negative prompt "plastic skin, over-smoothed, artificial glow"
