# LinkedIn Profile Photo - Warm Approachable Smile Headshot

## Prompt Type

LinkedIn Portrait Photography - Friendly Professional Headshot

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Warm, approachable LinkedIn headshot with genuine smile, designed to create instant connection and trust while maintaining professional credibility through clean composition and quality lighting.

## Detailed Prompt

```
A corporate professional headshot featuring a warm, genuine smile that reaches the eyes, creating an instantly approachable and trustworthy presence. Professional attire (blazer or business shirt), clean neutral background (soft white, light grey, or subtle blue-grey), soft diffused studio lighting that creates gentle shadows and highlights the warmth of the smile. Shot from shoulders up, direct eye contact with camera, natural relaxed posture. The smile is authentic, not forced—showing personality while remaining professional. Ultra-sharp 8K detail, natural skin texture, perfect LinkedIn headshot aesthetic, photorealistic professional portrait.
```

## Best Practices for Authentic Smile

**Do:**

- Think of something genuinely pleasant
- Focus on warmth in the eyes
- Relax your jaw and face
- Take multiple shots to find natural moment
- Practice smiling to yourself before shoot

**Don't:**

- Force a "cheese" smile
- Smile with only your mouth
- Tense your face
- Over-smile (looks unnatural)
- Forget to engage your eyes
