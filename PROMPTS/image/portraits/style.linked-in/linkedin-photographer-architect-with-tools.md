# LinkedIn Profile Photo - Photographer/Architect with Camera/Blueprints

## Prompt Type

LinkedIn Portrait Photography - Professional with Tools/Props

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Professional LinkedIn headshot of a photographer, architect, or creative professional holding their camera or blueprints, shot in warm daylight with clean studio setting to convey expertise and approachability.

## Detailed Prompt

```
A photographer/architect, well-dressed in a clean, professional shirt or blazer, gently holding their camera or rolled-up blueprints at chest level, facing the camera directly with a warm, confident smile. The shot captures the shoulders and head, with the tool/prop slightly visible to add context without dominating. Clean, neutral background (soft white or light gray), warm natural daylight from the side creating soft shadows. Professional LinkedIn-appropriate composition. Ultra-sharp 8K detail, natural skin texture, approachable yet authoritative expression, photorealistic studio portrait.
```
