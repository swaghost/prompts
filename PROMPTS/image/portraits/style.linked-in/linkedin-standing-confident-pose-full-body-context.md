# LinkedIn Profile Photo - Natural Standing Confident Pose Full Body Context

## Prompt Type

LinkedIn Portrait Photography - Standing Full/Three-Quarter Body Shot

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

LinkedIn professional portrait featuring standing pose showing three-quarter to full body, conveying confidence and approachability through natural stance and body language while maintaining professional headshot quality on the face.

## Detailed Prompt

```
A professional standing portrait showing three-quarter to full body in professional business attire (suit, blazer, or professional dress), standing in a natural, confident pose with good posture. Arms can be at sides, one hand in pocket (relaxed confidence), or arms crossed (authority). Clean modern office environment or simple neutral background, slightly blurred to keep focus on the professional. Natural or professional lighting creates dimension and depth. The professional faces the camera with confident, engaging expression. Shot from slightly below or at eye level to convey presence and authority. Face remains the primary focus while body language adds context. Ultra-sharp detail on face and upper body, natural professional aesthetic, modern corporate portrait style.
```

## Identity Preservation Settings

**ChatGPT/DALL-E:**

- Upload reference image for facial features
- Specify "exact person in professional standing pose"
- Maintain body proportions naturally

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--cref [reference_url]` for character consistency
- Add `--cw 80-100` for identity preservation
- Use `--style raw` for professional photorealistic look
- Consider `--ar 3:4` or `--ar 2:3` for standing portrait
