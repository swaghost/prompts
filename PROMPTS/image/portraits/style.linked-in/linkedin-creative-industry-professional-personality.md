# LinkedIn Profile Photo - Creative Industry Professional with Personality

## Prompt Type

LinkedIn Portrait Photography - Creative Professional Branding

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

LinkedIn headshot for creative industry professionals (designers, artists, marketers, content creators) that balances professionalism with personality through relaxed styling, authentic expression, and subtle creative elements.

## Detailed Prompt

```
A creative professional headshot showing personality and authenticity, dressed in smart casual attire (button-down shirt, casual blazer, or modern top—no tie), with a natural, genuine smile or slight smirk that conveys confidence and creativity. The background can include subtle creative elements (soft bokeh, urban texture, or clean minimal space with interest). Lighting is natural with a creative edge—window light creating soft shadows, or moody studio lighting with character. Shot from shoulders up, relaxed confident posture, direct engaging eye contact. Hair and styling show individuality while remaining professional. Ultra-sharp detail, natural authentic skin texture, modern creative professional aesthetic, photorealistic with artistic sensibility.
```

## Identity Preservation Settings

**ChatGPT/DALL-E:**

- Upload reference image for facial features
- Specify "authentic personality and creative expression"
- Allow natural styling and individuality

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--cref [reference_url]` for character consistency
- Add `--cw 80-100` for identity with creative flexibility
- Use `--style raw` for authentic photorealistic look

## Technical Specifications

**Camera & Lens:**

- 50mm f/1.4 or 85mm f/1.8 lens
- Shoulders to head framing
- Shallow depth of field (f/1.8-f/2.8)
- Eye level or slightly above

**Lighting:**

- Natural window light with character
- Or moody studio lighting
- Creative shadows and highlights
- Not flat/corporate—has personality

**Resolution:**

- Ultra-sharp detail
- Natural skin texture
- Authentic character
- Professional but artistic quality

**Composition:**

- Shoulders up framing
- Can be slightly off-center (dynamic)
- Relaxed, natural positioning
- Creative but professional framing
