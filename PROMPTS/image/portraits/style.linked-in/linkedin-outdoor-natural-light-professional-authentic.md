# LinkedIn Profile Photo - Outdoor Natural Light Professional Authentic

## Prompt Type

LinkedIn Portrait Photography - Outdoor Natural Environment

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

LinkedIn professional portrait shot outdoors with natural daylight, blending professional credibility with approachable authenticity through environmental context, golden hour lighting, and relaxed-yet-polished styling.

## Detailed Prompt

```
A professional outdoor headshot taken in natural daylight, featuring a well-dressed professional in business casual attire (blazer, professional shirt, or smart casual) with soft natural environment in the background (urban architecture, greenery, or modern outdoor setting—all softly blurred). Shot during golden hour or soft overcast light for flattering natural illumination. The professional faces the camera with a warm, genuine smile that feels authentic and approachable. Shoulders up framing with shallow depth of field keeping face in sharp focus. Natural wind-touched hair, authentic expression, warm natural skin tones. The outdoor setting adds personality and authenticity while maintaining professional quality. Ultra-sharp 8K detail on face, photorealistic natural portrait, modern authentic LinkedIn aesthetic.
```

## Identity Preservation Settings

**ChatGPT/DALL-E:**

- Upload reference image for facial features
- Specify "natural outdoor setting with authentic expression"
- Maintain realistic environmental context

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--cref [reference_url]` for character consistency
- Add `--cw 100` for identity preservation
- Use `--style raw` for authentic photorealistic outdoor look

## Technical Specifications

**Camera & Lens:**

- 85mm f/1.4 or 50mm f/1.8 lens
- Shoulders to head framing
- Shallow depth of field (f/1.8-f/2.8)
- Eye level or slightly below

**Lighting:**

- Natural daylight (golden hour preferred)
- Or soft overcast conditions
- No artificial lighting
- Authentic natural quality

**Resolution:**

- Ultra-sharp 8K detail on face
- Natural skin texture
- Soft background blur
- Professional outdoor portrait quality

**Composition:**

- Shoulders up framing
- Centered or rule-of-thirds
- Environmental context visible but blurred
- Professional yet natural

## Lighting Conditions

**Golden Hour (Preferred):**

- **Time**: 1 hour after sunrise or before sunset
- **Quality**: Warm, soft, directional
- **Effect**: Flattering glow, warm skin tones
- **Mood**: Approachable, authentic, positive

**Soft Overcast:**

- **Time**: Cloudy day, even light
- **Quality**: Diffused, flattering, no harsh shadows
- **Effect**: Natural, true colors
- **Mood**: Professional, authentic, real

**Open Shade:**

- **Location**: Under building overhang, tree shade
- **Quality**: Even, soft, natural
- **Effect**: Balanced, flattering
- **Mood**: Professional outdoor authenticity

**Avoid:**

- ❌ Harsh midday sun (unflattering shadows)
- ❌ Direct overhead light (raccoon eyes)
- ❌ Extreme backlight without fill
- ❌ Dappled light through leaves (spotty lighting)
