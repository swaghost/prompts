# LinkedIn Profile Photo - Studio Clean Background Professional Classic

## Prompt Type

LinkedIn Portrait Photography - Classic Studio Professional Headshot

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Timeless, classic LinkedIn professional headshot shot in clean studio environment with professional lighting, neutral background, and polished appearance—the gold standard for corporate profiles, executive roles, and traditional industries.

## Detailed Prompt

```
A professional corporate headshot in a clean studio setting, featuring a well-dressed professional in business attire (suit, blazer, or professional dress shirt) against a perfectly smooth neutral background (soft grey, light blue-grey, or white). Professional studio lighting with softbox illumination creates even, flattering light across the face with subtle dimensionality. The subject faces the camera directly with a confident, professional expression—either a slight smile or composed neutral look. Shot from shoulders up with perfect focus, clean composition, and polished professional aesthetic. Ultra-sharp 8K detail, natural skin texture, classic corporate headshot quality, photorealistic professional standard.
```

## Lighting Details

**Three-Point Studio Lighting:**

- **Key Light**: Main softbox from front-side
- **Fill Light**: Softens shadows from opposite side
- **Hair/Background Light**: Separates subject from background (optional)

**Key Light Position:**

- 30-45 degrees from camera
- Slightly above eye level
- Creates subtle dimensionality
- Flattering facial modeling

**Fill Light:**

- Opposite side from key
- 2:1 or 3:1 ratio with key
- Softens shadows
- Maintains detail

**Overall Quality:**

- Even, flattering illumination
- No harsh shadows
- Subtle dimensionality
- Professional corporate standard
- Neutral color temperature (5000K-5500K)
