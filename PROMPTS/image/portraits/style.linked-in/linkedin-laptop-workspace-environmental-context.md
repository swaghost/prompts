# LinkedIn Profile Photo - Laptop Workspace Visible Environmental Context

## Prompt Type

LinkedIn Portrait Photography - Environmental Professional Shot

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

LinkedIn professional portrait with laptop or workspace partially visible in frame, adding environmental context while keeping focus on the professional, conveying active work engagement and modern professional lifestyle.

## Detailed Prompt

```
A professional seated at a modern workspace, laptop or computer partially visible in the lower portion of the frame, looking toward the camera with an engaged, approachable smile. The shot captures from mid-torso up, with the workspace adding context without dominating the composition. Professional attire (business casual or smart casual), clean modern office or home office environment with soft natural window light and subtle background blur. The laptop screen shows subtle glow or work-related content (charts, documents, or blank). Focus remains on the professional's face while workspace elements add authenticity. Ultra-sharp 8K detail, natural skin texture, modern professional aesthetic, photorealistic LinkedIn-appropriate composition.
```

## Identity Preservation Settings

**ChatGPT/DALL-E:**

- Upload reference image for facial consistency
- Specify "exact person, natural professional appearance"
- Maintain authentic workspace context

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--cref [reference_url]` for character reference
- Add `--cw 80-100` for identity consistency

**Do:**

- Keep workspace clean and minimal

**Don't:**

- Show identifiable client work on screen
- Use harsh laptop screen glare
- Neglect facial lighting for environmental context
