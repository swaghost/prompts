# Raw iPhone Selfie Portrait - Natural Photorealism (Image + Video)

## Description

Ultra-realistic portrait prompts for generating authentic iPhone selfie photography with maximum skin texture fidelity and natural imperfections. This two-part system includes both a photorealistic still image prompt and a subtle motion video prompt featuring a young woman in an overhead selfie angle. Emphasizes raw, unfiltered iPhone front camera aesthetic with visible pores, natural skin texture, and zero post-processing.

## Usage

Use Part 1 for generating photorealistic still images with authentic iPhone selfie quality, and Part 2 for creating subtle, intimate video portraits with natural micro-movements and magnetic eye contact. Perfect for creating realistic social media content, authentic beauty photography, or natural portrait references.

## PART 2: VIDEO PROMPT - Static Macro Close-Up with Subtle Movement

### Video Specifications

**Duration**: 12 seconds

**Camera**: Static macro close-up (no camera movement)

**Focus**: Shallow depth of field on face

**Lighting**: Soft natural window light

### Video Timeline (0-12 seconds)

**00:00-12:00 (Continuous):**

- Woman holds deep, alluring gaze directly into lens
- One finger resting gently against her lips
- Natural blinking at normal intervals throughout
- Subtle micro-movements: slight breathing visible in shoulders, imperceptible head micro-sway

**~00:06 (Around 6 seconds):**

- Eyes drift slowly to one side WITHOUT turning head
- A soft, brief glance away
- Then return to direct eye contact with camera

**Expression Throughout:**

- Heavy-lidded
- Magnetic
- Quietly confident
- Unchanged throughout entire duration

**Restrictions:**

- NO speech
- NO camera movement (static frame)
- NO dramatic movements
- ONLY subtle natural micro-movements


### Video Movement Breakdown

**Natural Micro-Movements (Continuous):**

- Breathing: Visible in subtle shoulder rise/fall

- Blinking: Normal intervals (every 3-5 seconds)
- Finger: Resting gently against lips, minimal movement

**Eye Movement (Single Event at ~6 seconds):**

- Slow drift to one side
- Head remains stationary
- Brief glance away (1-2 seconds)
- Smooth return to direct camera eye contact
- No other eye movements

**Facial Expression:**

- Consistent throughout
- Heavy-lidded (slightly lowered eyelids)
- Magnetic quality (intense but not aggressive)
- Quietly confident (calm, self-assured)
- NO smiling, talking, or expression changes

### Video Technical Details

- **Camera Movement**: NONE (locked off, static frame)
- **Frame**: Macro close-up on face
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow (face in sharp focus, background soft blur)
- **Frame Rate**: 24fps or 30fps (natural cinematic)

## Pro Tips

**For Maximum Realism:**

- Start with high-quality reference images if using img2img
- Emphasize "raw iPhone front camera" repeatedly in prompts
- Request "visible pores" and "natural skin texture" explicitly
- Avoid any language that suggests perfection or smoothing
- Use "faint subtle blemishes" to ensure realistic skin

**For Video Motion:**

- Keep movements EXTREMELY subtle—less is more
- Natural blinking is key to realism (specify normal intervals)
- The 6-second eye drift adds interest without breaking stillness
- Static camera is crucial—any movement breaks the intimate mood
- Shallow depth of field isolates subject and creates intimacy

**For Lighting:**

- "Soft diffused window light" is more natural than "soft box" or "studio"
- Specify direction (from left, from right) for dimensional lighting
- Avoid terms like "rim light," "key light"—too technical/artificial
- Natural daylight variations (golden hour, overcast) feel most authentic
