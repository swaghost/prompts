# Raw iPhone Selfie on Grey Sofa - Natural Photorealism (Image + Video)

## Description

Ultra-realistic portrait prompts for generating authentic candid iPhone selfie photography with maximum skin texture fidelity on a grey sofa setting. This two-part system includes both a photorealistic still image prompt featuring a young woman lying on her back looking up at the camera, and an intimate video prompt with subtle hair-tucking gesture and magnetic eye contact. Emphasizes raw, unfiltered iPhone aesthetic with visible pores, natural oil sheen, and sensor noise.

## Usage

Use Part 1 for generating photorealistic still images with candid raw iPhone quality and authentic imperfections, and Part 2 for creating intimate video portraits with natural gestures and mesmerizing eye contact. Perfect for authentic lifestyle content, natural beauty photography, or realistic portrait references with casual home settings.

## PART 2: VIDEO PROMPT - Extreme Macro Close-Up with Hair Tuck Gesture

### Video Specifications

**Duration**: 12 seconds

**Camera**: Extreme macro close-up, face filling frame at 3/4 angle, static (no camera movement)

**Focus**: Shallow depth of field on face

**Lighting**: Soft natural window light

### Video Timeline (0-12 seconds)

**00:00-12:00 (Continuous):**

- Woman holds deep, alluring gaze toward the lens
- Natural blinking at normal intervals throughout
- Imperceptible head micro-sway (subtle natural movement)
- Expression unchanged: heavy-lidded, magnetic, quietly confident

**~00:04 (Around 4 seconds):**

- She slowly lifts one hand
- Tucks a strand of hair behind her ear
- Fingers trailing gently along her jaw as her hand drops
- Movement is slow, graceful, natural
- Eyes remain on camera throughout the gesture

**Expression Throughout:**

- Heavy-lidded
- Magnetic
- Quietly confident
- Unchanged throughout entire duration

**Restrictions:**

- NO speech
- NO camera movement (static frame)
- Face filling frame at 3/4 angle
- ONLY subtle natural micro-movements plus the single hair-tuck gesture

### AI Video Generation Prompt

```
Extreme macro close-up, face filling the frame at a 3/4 angle. The woman holds a deep, alluring gaze toward the lens. Over 12 seconds: natural blinking, imperceptible head micro-sway. At around 4 seconds she slowly lifts one hand and tucks a strand of hair behind her ear, fingers trailing gently along her jaw as her hand drops. Eyes remain on camera throughout. Expression unchanged: heavy-lidded, magnetic, quietly confident. No speech. No camera movement. Shallow depth of field. Soft natural window light.

Light-medium tan skin — visible pores, natural oil sheen, uneven texture, faint blemishes. White ribbed textured top. Thin delicate necklace.

Duration: 12 seconds
Camera: Extreme macro close-up at 3/4 angle, static
Movement: Natural micro-movements + single hair-tuck gesture at ~4 seconds
Expression: Heavy-lidded, magnetic, quietly confident
Lighting: Soft natural window light
```

### Video Movement Breakdown

**Continuous Natural Micro-Movements (0-12s):**

- Natural blinking at normal intervals (every 3-5 seconds)
- Imperceptible head micro-sway (subtle, not frozen)
- Slight breathing visible in subtle facial movements
- Eyes maintain focus on camera throughout

**Hair-Tuck Gesture (~4 seconds):**

- One hand slowly enters frame
- Gracefully lifts toward face
- Fingers gently tuck strand of hair behind ear
- Fingers trail softly along jaw line
- Hand drops back out of frame naturally
- Movement is slow, deliberate, feminine
- Takes approximately 2-3 seconds total
- Eyes never leave camera during gesture

**Facial Expression (Constant):**

- Heavy-lidded (slightly lowered eyelids, sensual)
- Magnetic quality (intense but not aggressive gaze)
- Quietly confident (calm, self-assured presence)
- NO smiling, talking, or expression changes
- Consistent throughout all 12 seconds

### Video Camera & Framing

- **Camera Position**: Static, locked off (no movement)
- **Framing**: Extreme macro close-up at 3/4 angle
- **Face Coverage**: Face fills majority of frame
- **Angle**: 3/4 profile (not straight-on, not full profile)
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow (face sharp, background soft blur)
- **Focus Point**: Eyes and facial features
- **Background**: Soft out-of-focus blur

### Video Technical Details

- **Camera Movement**: NONE (completely static frame)
- **Duration**: Exactly 12 seconds
- **Frame Rate**: 24fps or 30fps (natural cinematic)
- **Audio**: Optional soft ambient sound or silence
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical) or 1:1 (square) for social media

## Pro Tips

**For Maximum Candid Realism:**

- Request "candid raw iPhone selfie" explicitly in prompt
- Include "slight sensor noise and grain" for authentic phone camera quality
- Emphasize "no beauty filter, no smoothing, no retouching" repeatedly
- Specify "natural oil sheen" to avoid overly matte skin
- Use "uneven texture" to ensure realistic skin variation

**For Video Intimacy:**

- The hair-tuck gesture adds natural, feminine movement
- Timing the gesture at ~4 seconds creates mid-video interest
- "Fingers trailing along jaw" adds sensuality and grace
- Static camera creates intimate, focused connection
- Shallow depth of field isolates subject for emotional impact

**For Natural Expression:**

- "Heavy-lidded" creates alluring, sensual quality
- "Magnetic" gaze holds viewer attention
- "Quietly confident" suggests calm, self-assured presence
- Maintaining unchanged expression throughout creates mesmerizing quality

**For Lighting Authenticity:**

- Soft natural window light is key to avoiding artificial studio look
- Natural oil sheen on skin requires adequate but not harsh lighting
- Avoid terms like "studio lighting," "professional lighting"
- "Soft" and "natural" are the most important descriptors
