# Wide-Brim Hat Editorial

Six summer fashion prompts built around oversized natural straw hats, Mediterranean and tropical settings, and cinematic travel-editorial photography.

## Shared Direction

- Vertical portrait composition
- Fashion and resort-wear editorial photography
- Natural straw wide-brim hats as a recurring visual anchor
- Realistic fabric, skin, straw weave, landscape, and natural light
- Source: Wide-Brim Hat Editorial - Image Prompts
