# Wide-Brim Hat Editorial 03 - Terracotta Wall

**Scene:** Olive Green Top
**Format:** Vertical portrait
**Source:** Wide-Brim Hat Editorial - Image Prompts

## Description of Resulting Image

A minimalist high-contrast portrait of a dark-skinned woman in an olive fitted top and enormous straw hat against a dusty terracotta stucco wall, with the hat creating a bold graphic shadow.

## Image Prompt

```text
Minimalist editorial beauty photograph: a woman with dark skin stands against a flat dusty terracotta/pink stucco wall in harsh direct sunlight, casting a bold graphic shadow behind her. An enormous natural straw wide-brim hat completely shadows and hides her eyes, only her nose, lips, and jawline visible beneath the brim. She wears a sleeveless, fitted olive-green top with a high boat neckline. High-contrast midday sunlight, clean architectural composition, symmetrical framing, warm terracotta and olive color palette, sculptural and graphic mood, vertical portrait, fashion campaign still.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Framing:** Symmetrical vertical portrait
- **Light:** Harsh direct midday sunlight
- **Finish:** High contrast, clean architectural composition

## Color Palette

Dusty terracotta, pink stucco, olive green, natural straw, deep skin tones, graphic shadow.

## Mood & Atmosphere

Sculptural, graphic, still, powerful, minimalist.
