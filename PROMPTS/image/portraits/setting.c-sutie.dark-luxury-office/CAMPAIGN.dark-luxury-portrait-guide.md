# Dark Luxury Portrait Guide

**Style:** Old Money / Mafia Boss / Luxury Office  
**Theme:** Cinematic editorial fashion portraits with consistent character identity  
**Ratio:** 4:5 portrait (recommended for editorial composition)

---

## WORKFLOW

1. Upload your reference portrait
2. Use the Master Prompt
3. Add one Pose Prompt
4. Optionally add Wolf, Fox, or Cigar variation
5. Generate at 4:5 portrait ratio
6. If needed, regenerate while keeping same reference image to maintain facial consistency

---

## 1. CHARACTER GUIDELINES

**Keep these consistent across all variations:**

- Black tailored three-piece suit
- White dress shirt
- Black silk tie
- Slim fit
- Luxury wristwatch
- Clean styling
- Natural skin texture
- Wavy medium-length hair
- Sharp jawline
- Editorial fashion look

---

## 2. ENVIRONMENT

**Luxury Office Setting:**

Luxurious old-money private study, dark walnut wood panel walls, leather furniture, antique bookshelves, vintage paintings, brass table lamps, marble floor, whiskey table, fireplace, warm practical lighting, rich brown tones, expensive gentleman's library, timeless European mansion interior.

---

## 3. LIGHTING

**What makes these images work:**

Cinematic Rembrandt lighting, warm tungsten lamps, fireplace glow, soft shadows, low-key lighting, dramatic contrast, rich amber highlights, realistic global illumination, subtle film grain, premium editorial photography, moody luxury atmosphere.

---

## 4. CAMERA

**Nearly identical settings across all images:**

Medium shot, seated portrait, eye-level camera, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, f/1.8, ultra realistic skin texture, natural color grading, cinematic composition, luxury magazine photography.

---

## 5. MASTER PROMPT

Use this after uploading your reference image.

**Full Master Prompt:**

Use my uploaded image as the identity reference. Preserve the exact face, hairstyle, facial proportions and identity. Dress the subject in a perfectly tailored black three-piece suit with a crisp white shirt, black silk tie and luxury wristwatch. Seat them confidently inside an elegant old-money private study with dark walnut wood paneling, antique paintings, leather armchair, bookshelves, brass table lamps, fireplace and luxury vintage décor. Warm cinematic tungsten lighting mixed with fireplace glow creates deep shadows and rich amber highlights. Editorial luxury fashion photography, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, ultra realistic skin texture, expensive masculine aesthetic, timeless wealth, sophisticated atmosphere, highly detailed, photorealistic.

## TECHNICAL NOTES

- **Character Consistency:** Upload reference portrait first, system will preserve exact face and identity
- **Lighting Key:** Rembrandt lighting with warm tungsten + fireplace creates signature look
- **Depth:** Shallow f/1.8 separates subject from rich background details
- **Color Palette:** Rich browns, deep blacks, warm ambers, subtle grain
- **Composition:** Eye-level medium shot, seated, 4:5 portrait ratio
