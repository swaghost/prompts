## Prompt Description

A cinematic environmental fashion photograph capturing a full-body figure beneath a monumental historic stone archway. Camera positioned inside the deep shaded passage looking outward toward an old pale ochre residential building with aged cream plaster, faded shutters, narrow iron balconies. The arch walls and floor occupy most of the frame in deep warm shadow, naturally framing the bright exterior. ## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 cinematic environmental fashion photograph of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, naturally thick dark-brown brows, a small straight nose and muted rose lips. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw with softly curved ends, airy bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Add medium rounded gold hoop earrings and natural makeup with visible pores, slight cheek colour and subtle facial asymmetry.

She wears an oversized warm oat-beige trench coat falling to mid-calf, a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse with a broad pointed collar and narrow tied neckline, a high-waisted deep cocoa-brown midi skirt, sheer warm-beige tights and polished cognac-brown leather loafers. A compact vintage silver-and-black 35mm film camera hangs at her hip from a thin worn brown leather strap.

Place her beneath a monumental historic stone archway. The camera is inside the deep shaded passage looking outward toward an old pale ochre residential building with aged cream plaster, faded shutters, narrow iron balconies and soft sunlight falling across the courtyard. The arch walls and floor occupy most of the photograph in deep warm shadow, naturally framing the bright exterior.

She stands full-body precisely beneath the centre of the arch, feet close together, arms resting loosely at her sides and shoulders slightly lowered. Her head turns toward frame-left and chin lifts subtly, as though listening to something outside. She should appear calm, still and small within the architecture rather than posing heroically.

Photograph from chest height using a Hasselblad X2D 100C and an 80mm lens. Use strong symmetrical architecture while retaining slight real-world imperfections. Keep the woman clear and the exterior building moderately softened, with the arch edges fading into shadow.

The colour treatment must be warm, aged and cinematic. The passage shadows should be deep charcoal-brown with faint olive undertones, never cold blue or pure black. Exterior plaster should be faded warm cream and pale ochre, not white. Sunlit areas should carry muted honey-yellow illumination with soft highlight bloom. Her coat must remain creamy oat-beige, blouse dusty salmon, skirt deep cocoa and shoes cognac brown. Slightly underexpose the interior while protecting detail in the illuminated exterior. Add lifted film blacks, restrained saturation, low digital clarity, warm midtones, soft amber halation and fine organic 35mm grain.
