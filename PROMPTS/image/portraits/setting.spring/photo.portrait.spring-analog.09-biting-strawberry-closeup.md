# Spring Portrait - Biting Strawberry Close-Up

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, MidJourney (--style raw, --ar 3:4), Flux

**Technical Specifications:** Fujifilm GFX100 II, GF 110mm f/2 lens, eye level, vertical 3:4, close editorial beauty portrait, analog film aesthetic

---

## Prompt Description

A close editorial beauty portrait capturing playful moment of biting into ripe strawberry. Subject holds strawberry delicately between thumb and fingers, bites into it playfully with one eye narrowing slightly in mischievous half-wink expression. Strawberry natural deep red with visible seeds and green crown, not glossy artificial. Quiet park with faded yellow-olive grass and tree trunks softly blurred behind. Tight framing from upper chest to slightly above headband, strawberry/lips/eyes as main focus. Warm late-afternoon sunlight softened by surrounding trees, gently illuminated with honey-gold tones and soft warm-brown shadows.

## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 close editorial beauty portrait of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, naturally thick dark-brown brows, a small straight nose and softly defined muted rose lips. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw with airy bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Preserve visible pores, faint freckles, slight asymmetry, realistic lashes and individual hair strands.

She wears rounded gold hoop earrings, a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse, an antique gold ring with a deep-red oval gemstone and a slim brown leather wristwatch. No trench coat in this frame. She holds a ripe strawberry delicately between thumb and fingers and bites into it playfully, one eye narrowing slightly, with a mischievous half-wink expression. The strawberry should be natural deep red with visible seeds and green crown, not glossy artificial red.

Place her in a quiet park with faded yellow-olive grass and tree trunks softly blurred behind. Frame tightly from the upper chest to slightly above the headband, keeping the strawberry, lips and eyes as the main focus.

Photograph with a Fujifilm GFX100 II and GF 110mm f/2 lens from eye level. Focus sharply on the nearest eye, the strawberry surface and the fingers holding it, while the far side of the face and the background soften naturally.

Lighting must be warm late-afternoon sunlight, softened by the surrounding trees. The face should be gently illuminated with honey-gold tones and soft warm-brown shadows, never stark or high-contrast. Greens must be muted olive-yellow, blouse dusty salmon and skin peach-beige with soft rosy warmth. Use subtle highlight bloom, lowered micro-contrast, lifted shadows and fine 35mm grain for a nostalgic analog feeling.

## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Composition

- Fair neutral-warm skin, softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, thick dark-brown brows
- Short dark-chocolate bob with airy bangs, wide cream headband
- Tight framing: upper chest to above headband
- Holds ripe strawberry between thumb/fingers
- Bites into strawberry playfully
- One eye narrows slightly - mischievous half-wink
- Strawberry natural deep red with visible seeds/green crown

### Lighting & Color

- Warm late-afternoon sunlight softened by trees
- Gently illuminated, honey-gold tones
- Soft warm-brown shadows, never stark or high-contrast
- Muted olive-yellow greens
- Dusty salmon blouse
- Peach-beige skin with soft rosy warmth

### Technical & Quality

- Fujifilm GFX100 II, GF 110mm f/2, eye level
- Focus: nearest eye, strawberry surface, fingers holding it
- Far side of face and background soften naturally
- Subtle highlight bloom, lowered micro-contrast
- Lifted shadows, fine 35mm grain

### Restrictions

Avoid: glossy artificial strawberry, harsh stark light, high contrast, bright greens, over-sharpness, beauty filters, symmetrical expression
