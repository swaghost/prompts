## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 cinematic fashion portrait of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, naturally thick dark-brown brows, a small straight nose and a wide genuine smile with naturally imperfect teeth. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw, soft inward-curving ends, loose airy bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Preserve realistic pores, smile lines, slight asymmetry, natural under-eye texture and individual windblown hair strands.

She wears medium rounded gold hoop earrings, an oversized warm oat-beige trench coat with broad lapels and oversized sleeves, a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse with a wide pointed collar and narrow front ties, a deep cocoa-brown midi skirt and a slim brown leather wristwatch. She holds a vintage silver-and-black 35mm film camera in both hands at upper-waist height, with a thin worn cognac leather strap falling loosely between her wrists.

Place her directly in front of an old dark wrought-iron fence with tall spear-shaped uprights, shaded trees and dense foliage behind it. She has just lowered the camera and bursts into uncontrolled laughter, head tilted backward, chin raised, eyes closed and shoulders lifting naturally. One loose lock of hair falls across her cheek while the bob lifts slightly from movement. The camera remains clearly visible but secondary to her expression.

Frame from approximately the hips upward, camera at lower-chest height, subject slightly left of centre. Photograph with a Sony Alpha 1 II and an 85mm f/1.4 lens from chest height. Keep her face and hands sharp while the fence and trees compress into soft dark vertical shapes.

Lighting must feel like the original warm cinematic campaign rather than clean daylight. Use late-afternoon sunlight entering narrowly from frame-right, creating a soft amber highlight on the right side of her face, coat sleeve and hair, while the opposite side remains in deep warm brown shade. The iron fence should be charcoal-brown rather than pure black. Foliage must be subdued dark olive with small golden highlights, not vivid green. The beige coat should remain creamy oat, and the gingham should be muted dusty salmon rather than bright orange. Use warm lifted shadows, restrained saturation, gentle highlight bloom, subtle softness, fine film grain and a slightly underexposed analog negative-film finish.
