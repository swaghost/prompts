## Prompt Description

An intimate editorial beauty portrait capturing a petite young woman in her early 20s with a warm backlit composition in a mature park setting during late afternoon golden hour. The subject features fair neutral-warm skin, softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, thick naturally shaped dark-brown eyebrows, and a short dark-chocolate bob with airy curtain-like bangs and cream fabric headband. ## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 intimate editorial beauty portrait of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, thick naturally shaped dark-brown eyebrows, a small straight nose and softly defined muted rose-nude lips. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw with gently flipped ends, airy curtain-like bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Add medium sculptural gold hoop earrings, finely separated lashes, soft peach-beige eyeshadow and natural skin showing pores, fine facial hairs, subtle freckles, faint under-eye texture and slight asymmetry.

She wears a warm oat-beige trench coat over a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse. Only the trench lapels, gingham collar and upper shoulders should appear. Her right hand rises gently beneath her chin, with two fingertips touching the jawline while the remaining fingers extend naturally toward the camera. She looks diagonally upward toward frame-left with a small dreamy smile, eyes appearing thoughtful and curious rather than posing directly for a beauty advertisement.

Place her outdoors in a mature park. The background should become a soft abstract field of faded yellow-olive grass, dark warm tree trunks and glowing ochre foliage. Frame extremely close from the upper chest to just above the headband, keeping her face central but slightly angled. Leave a little soft negative space in the direction of her gaze.

Photograph with a Fujifilm GFX100 II and GF 110mm f/2 lens from slightly below eye level. Focus precisely on both eyes and the fingertips beneath her chin, with creamy natural falloff through the hair and background.

Use low golden sunlight positioned behind and slightly above her, producing a fine honey-coloured rim around the headband, bangs and bob. The sunlight should glow through individual flyaway hairs without creating white clipped edges. Illuminate the front of her face with soft reflected warm light from the grass. Keep the skin warm peach-beige with subtle rosy cheeks, never bright orange or heavily red. Background greens must be muted olive-yellow, not fresh green. Apply warm caramel midtones, soft brown shadows, creamy highlights, slightly lifted blacks, reduced contrast, low micro-sharpness, subtle lens halation and delicate analog film grain.
