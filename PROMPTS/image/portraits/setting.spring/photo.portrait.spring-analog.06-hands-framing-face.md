## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 intimate editorial beauty portrait of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, naturally thick dark-brown eyebrows, a small straight nose and softly defined muted rose-nude lips. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw with softly flipped ends, airy bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Preserve visible pores, soft cheek texture, tiny beauty marks, natural lashes, faint under-eye detail and slight asymmetry.

She wears rounded gold hoop earrings, a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse with a broad pointed collar, an antique gold ring with a deep-red oval stone and a slim brown leather wristwatch. No trench coat in this frame. She gently cups her face with both hands, one palm supporting the cheek and the other resting softly along the jawline and lower cheek. Her expression is calm, dreamy and introspective, with her eyes glancing off toward frame-left rather than at camera.

Place her outdoors in a quiet historic park. Reduce the background to a creamy wash of faded yellow-olive grass and soft dark tree shapes. Frame tightly from the upper chest to just above the headband, with her face dominant and hands creating a soft sculptural frame.

Photograph with a Fujifilm GFX100 II and GF 110mm f/2 lens from slightly above eye level. Focus on the eyes, lips and fingers closest to the cheek while letting the background melt softly.

Use warm late-afternoon sunlight filtered through trees. The light should be soft and honeyed, not bright or white. Keep skin tones warm peach-beige with subtle rosy cheeks, the blouse muted dusty salmon, and the background green pushed toward faded olive-yellow rather than fresh green. Shadows should stay warm brown and softly lifted. Finish with low digital sharpness, fine analog grain, delicate halation, creamy highlight rolloff and restrained saturation.

## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Composition

- Fair neutral-warm skin, softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, thick dark-brown brows
- Short dark-chocolate bob with airy bangs, wide cream headband
- Tight framing: upper chest to above headband
- Both hands cupping face gently - one palm on cheek, other along jaw
- Calm dreamy introspective expression, eyes glancing frame-left
- No trench coat - gingham blouse and accessories visible

### Lighting & Color

- Warm late-afternoon sunlight filtered through trees
- Soft honeyed quality, not bright or white
- Warm peach-beige skin with subtle rosy cheeks
- Muted dusty salmon gingham
- Faded olive-yellow background (not fresh green)
- Warm brown shadows, softly lifted

### Technical & Quality

- Fujifilm GFX100 II, GF 110mm f/2, slightly above eye level
- Focus on eyes, lips, fingers closest to cheek
- Background melts softly
- Low digital sharpness, fine analog grain
- Delicate halation, creamy highlight rolloff
- Restrained saturation

### Restrictions

Avoid: bright white light, fresh green background, high contrast, high sharpness, beauty filters, direct camera gaze, posed commercial look
