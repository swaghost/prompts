# Prompt 5: Sunbathing Monokini on Wet Shoreline

A warm vintage-toned lifestyle photograph of a tanned woman reclining on wet shoreline sand as gentle ocean foam washes around her. One hand shields her forehead from the warm sun. The image must feel like a genuine 1970s Kodachrome-inspired film photograph with soft natural film grain, realistic wet sand textures, and authentic sun glints.

---

## OVERALL FEEL

- Warm, sensual, relaxed vintage summer vibe, retro beach aesthetic
- Authentic analog film look with natural grain and soft warmth

## SUBJECT

- Tanned woman with long slender legs and athletic core
- Lying on her side/back on wet beach sand, face turned slightly towards the side, arm resting across eyes to shield from bright sun

## EMOTIONS / EXPRESSIONS

- Relaxed, sun-drenched, tranquil, unwinding after a swim

## REALISM-CRITICAL HUMAN DETAILS

- Natural skin texture, subtle sand grains sticking to wet thighs and hips, visible ribcage definition, small delicate minimalist line-art tattoo on inner wrist

## HAIR

- Long straight dark brown hair, wet and fan-spread across the wet sand behind her head, catching golden sun highlights

## POSE / FRAMING

- High-angle full-body 9:16 framing, subject stretched diagonally along wet sandy shore
- Legs bent relaxed at knees, hips angled towards camera, one arm raised shielding forehead, other arm resting near body

## WARDROBE & ACCESSORIES

- Black designer cut-out monokini one-piece swimwear with delicate criss-cross tie strings across the waist and ribcage

## BACKGROUND

- Glistening wet beach sand reflecting golden light like a mirror, gentle white ocean foam edge sweeping across the sand near her head

## LIGHTING

- Low golden-hour sunlight coming from top-right, creating long soft directional shadows on sand ripples and highlighting wet skin

## COLOR PALETTE

- Golden sand, warm cream sea foam, deep espresso swimwear, warm bronze tan tones, muted retro color grading

## CAMERA / PHOTOGRAPHIC FEEL

- Shot on Leica M10 with 35mm Summilux lens, slight warm film grain, organic shadow falloff, subtle lens bloom on bright foam reflections

## TEXTURE / MATERIAL REALISM

- **skin:** wet glossy sheen, authentic pores, goosebumps from cool water wash
- **hair:** individual wet strands fanned out over wet sand surface
- **wardrobe:** textured stretchy swim fabric with realistic tension wrinkles around waist ties
- **background:** wet reflective sand texture with tiny sea bubbles and foam clusters

## STYLE

- Retro luxury resort lifestyle photography

## HEX VALUES

["#221A16", "#44342A", "#6E5745", "#9B7D64", "#C6A789", "#E5D0B7", "#FAF3E8", "#B59375", "#85664F", "#584232", "#35261C", "#D6BBA0", "#8D6E54", "#150F0B", "#F0E4D5"]

## AVOID

- Over-smoothed skin, cartoon body proportions, digital noise, fake foam, floating body effect, extra toes/fingers, heavy HDR tone mapping, text, watermark

**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16
