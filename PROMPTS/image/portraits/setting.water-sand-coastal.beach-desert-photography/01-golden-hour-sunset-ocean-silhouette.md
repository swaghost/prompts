# Prompt 1: Golden Hour Sunset Ocean Silhouette

A high-fashion summer editorial photograph of a slim, athletic woman standing waist-deep in a calm ocean at golden hour. Captured facing into the setting sun, her body forms a graceful silhouette against the shimmering water. The image must feel like an authentic 35mm film photograph taken in-camera on location, featuring natural skin micro-texture, real water glistening on skin, and true optical lens flare without synthetic AI smoothing.

---

## OVERALL FEEL

- Warm, romantic, serene, cinematic golden hour warmth
- Authentic 35mm analog photograph look, zero CGI or airbrushed polish

## SUBJECT

- Woman in her mid-20s, slim athletic build, defined collarbones, golden tanned skin tone
- Head tilted gently upward in a clean profile view towards the sky, eyes closed in bliss

## EMOTIONS / EXPRESSIONS

- Peaceful, serene, euphoric, quiet confidence

## REALISM-CRITICAL HUMAN DETAILS

- Natural skin pores, fine water droplets clinging to golden tanned shoulders and chest
- Realistic muscle definition along torso and arms, natural shadow falloff under jawline

## HAIR

- Long dark brown hair, completely wet and slicked back over shoulders, individual damp strands adhering softly to back and neck

## POSE / FRAMING

- Vertical 9:16 portrait framing, waist-deep shot in calm sea water
- Both arms raised elegantly above her head, elbows gently bowed, fingertips lightly touching in an arched diamond gesture, back subtly arched

## WARDROBE & ACCESSORIES

- Classic black string triangle bikini top with subtle scalloped lace edges, tied tightly around ribs
- High-cut black bikini bottoms with dainty side tie strings hanging softly at hips

## BACKGROUND

- Flat, tranquil ocean stretching to a soft horizon line, glowing golden sun hanging low just above distant land outline
- Shimmering vertical streak of bright sun reflection rippling across the water surface towards the camera

## LIGHTING

- Intense warm golden hour back-lighting and side-lighting from low-angled sun
- Strong rim lighting tracing her jaw, neck, raised arms, and torso silhouette; warm fill light bouncing off water onto skin

## COLOR PALETTE

- Rich amber, deep burnished gold, warm bronze, deep chocolate brown shadows, honeyed highlights

## CAMERA / PHOTOGRAPHIC FEEL

- Shot on Leica M6 with 50mm f/1.4 lens look, subtle organic grain, shallow depth of field
- Authentic highlight blooming around the sun, gentle natural vignetting at frame edges

## TEXTURE / MATERIAL REALISM

- **skin:** visible pores, subtle goosebumps, glistening wet sheen, natural skin folds at ribcage
- **hair:** clumped wet strands, authentic shine from seawater
- **wardrobe:** matte wet swimwear fabric texture with visible stitching details along seams
- **background:** subtle ripple textures on ocean surface, natural atmospheric haze near horizon

## STYLE

- Luxury resort wear advertising editorial, high-fashion beach realism

## HEX VALUES

["#1C1008", "#3B210B", "#683C0F", "#9A5D18", "#BD7D1E", "#DB9E27", "#F4BF3A", "#FDE36A", "#7C4A15", "#4B2909", "#291605", "#E39023", "#A86214", "#54300B", "#140A02"]

## AVOID

- Plastic skin, porcelain texture, digital painting, CGI, extra fingers, malformed hands, harsh direct electronic flash, unnatural waist distortion, over-saturation, AI beauty face filter, text, watermark
