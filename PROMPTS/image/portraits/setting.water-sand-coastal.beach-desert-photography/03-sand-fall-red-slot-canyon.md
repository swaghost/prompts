# Prompt 3: Sand Fall in Red Slot Canyon

An artistic fashion photograph of a slim brunette woman standing inside a narrow red sandstone slot canyon while holding sand in her raised hands, letting it stream down around her like a glowing curtain. Diffused overhead light illuminates the suspended dust and falling sand. The final image must look like a genuine editorial photograph captured in a natural geological location with authentic stone textures and zero digital painting artifacts.

---

## OVERALL FEEL

- Ethereal, earthy, majestic, moody, sculptural fine-art aesthetic
- Real medium-format film photograph look with rich organic color depth

## SUBJECT

- Woman in her mid-20s, slim toned physique, deep bronze skin tone
- Head tilted back gracefully looking upward, throat and collarbones highlighted, serene facial features in subtle shadow

## EMOTIONS / EXPRESSIONS

- Reverence, tranquility, spiritual connection to nature

## REALISM-CRITICAL HUMAN DETAILS

- True skin texture with visible micro-pores, natural shadow gradation across abs and ribcage, realistic muscle tone in legs and arms

## HAIR

- Short-to-medium dark brown hair cut in a wet-look shoulder-length style, sleekly tucked behind ears with damp strands clinging around neck

## POSE / FRAMING

- Full-length vertical 9:16 portrait, subject positioned centrally in the canyon corridor, legs crossed gracefully at calves
- Both arms extended high up and out to the sides in a V-shape, hands cup-shaped as fine golden sand cascades down from her palms in twin sheer veils

## WARDROBE & ACCESSORIES

- Black delicate lace bralette bikini top with thin shoulder straps
- Matching black lace high-cut bikini bottoms featuring delicate gold ring hardware on hip straps

## BACKGROUND

- Swirling, wave-patterned sandstone canyon walls (Antelope Canyon style) in rich terracotta, rust orange, and deep mahogany brown shades, sandy canyon floor

## LIGHTING

- Soft, warm ambient daylight filtering down from the top slot opening, creating a natural softbox effect
- Light catches the streaming sand particles, making them glow warmly against the deep red shadowy canyon walls behind

## COLOR PALETTE

- Rich terracotta red, burnt orange, warm ochre sand, deep mahogany, stark black lace

## CAMERA / PHOTOGRAPHIC FEEL

- Shot on Fujifilm GFX 100 II with 63mm f/2.8 lens, high dynamic range preserving deep shadow detail in sandstone while keeping falling sand sharply detailed

## TEXTURE / MATERIAL REALISM

- **skin:** matte natural skin texture with realistic highlights on clavicle and sternum
- **hair:** damp glossy texture with subtle realistic separation
- **wardrobe:** detailed lace fabric weave with transparent pattern gaps and metal ring sheen
- **background:** rough layered sandstone grain, soft loose desert sand bed

## STYLE

- Fine-art editorial fashion photography, natural environment portrait

## HEX VALUES

["#1F0A06", "#44180F", "#782A1B", "#AC432B", "#CB5F3E", "#E3805B", "#EAA079", "#8D3421", "#5C1E13", "#B54D33", "#F2B895", "#320F08", "#170603", "#D86E49", "#6A2216"]

## AVOID

- CGI sand effect, digital aura around sand, plastic skin, airbrushed body, unnatural limb length, low-resolution stone texture, cartoon canyon, text, watermark

**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16
