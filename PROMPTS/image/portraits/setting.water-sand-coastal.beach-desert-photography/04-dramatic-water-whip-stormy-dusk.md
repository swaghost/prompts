# Prompt 4: Dramatic Water Whip at Stormy Sea Dusk

A dramatic low-key action photograph of a woman emerging waist-deep from dark ocean waters and vigorously whipping her hair backwards, launching a wide circular ring of frozen water droplets into the air around her head. The photograph must feel like an authentic high-speed editorial shot with crisp water physics, dramatic cloud atmosphere, and unretouched human skin texture.

---

## OVERALL FEEL

- Dramatic, powerful, moody, dark cinematic intensity
- Raw 35mm photography feel, high shutter speed freezing intricate water splash physics

## SUBJECT

- Athletic woman with sun-bronzed skin, defined shoulders, and strong neck tension
- Head thrown back in profile view, mouth open in a euphoric gasping breath as water flies

## EMOTIONS / EXPRESSIONS

- Exhilaration, raw power, freedom, primal energy

## REALISM-CRITICAL HUMAN DETAILS

- Water droplets glistening on skin, visible muscle strain in neck and shoulders, natural skin texture with goosebumps and authentic pores

## HAIR

- Long dark hair wetly clumped together, whipping back through the air in a dramatic circular fan shape, trailing droplets of ocean water

## POSE / FRAMING

- Medium shot, waist-deep in dark ocean, torso tilted backwards at a 45-degree angle
- Arms extended backwards under water level for leverage, chest pushed upward

## WARDROBE & ACCESSORIES

- Dark charcoal / black cross-strap bikini top with delicate lace detailing across the cups
- Minimalist dark cord bracelet on her wrist

## BACKGROUND

- Dark, moody ocean with small whitecap sea foam ripples under a heavy, overcast stormy dusk sky with deep charcoal cloud layers

## LIGHTING

- Low-key dramatic natural lighting; cool overcast sky light reflecting sharply off water droplets and wet skin
- High contrast highlights tracing her cheekbone, neck, chest, and the arc of flying water drops

## COLOR PALETTE

- Dark slate blue, deep navy, stormy grey, cool white highlights, warm bronze skin tones

## CAMERA / PHOTOGRAPHIC FEEL

- Shot on Canon EOS R5 with 85mm f/1.4 lens, shutter speed at 1/8000s, shallow depth of field fading background waves into soft blur

## TEXTURE / MATERIAL REALISM

- **skin:** realistic wet sheen, individual water beads resting on chest and shoulders
- **hair:** heavy wet strands suspended in motion with sharp focus on droplets
- **wardrobe:** wet fabric clinging to body, subtle lace texture weave
- **background:** turbulent ocean surface texture, soft volumetric storm clouds

## STYLE

- Dark cinematic editorial splash photography

## HEX VALUES

["#0B1218", "#17232B", "#2B3C48", "#475C6A", "#6D8392", "#9CB2C0", "#241A17", "#483631", "#78584F", "#A88377", "#D5B4A8", "#101920", "#384B58", "#080D12", "#CBD9E2"]

## AVOID

- Motion blur on water drops, fake CGI water ring, porcelain skin, smooth digital render, oversaturated sky, fake lighting glow, extra limbs, text, watermark

**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16
