## Shared Settings

- **Platform:** Midjourney V6.1
- **Ratio:** 3:4
- **Style:** Analog 35mm double-exposure film portraits
- **Film treatment:** Expired film, heavy grain, dust, scratches, subtle light leaks, muted faded colors, soft matte finish
- **Mood:** Dreamy and melancholic

## Style Formula

Combine one readable human silhouette or profile with one internal nature, water, road, or landscape exposure. Preserve the tactile qualities of vintage photo paper, analog imperfections, and restrained faded color. Keep the supplied Midjourney parameters at the end of each prompt.
