## Pro Tips

**Realistic Tongue and Teeth:**
Critical specification: "realistic tongue and teeth" prevents AI from generating overly perfect or artificial-looking mouth. Tongue should look natural, not flat or illustrated.

**Individual Fur Fibers:**
"Individual fur fibers" with "dimensional texture" ensures realistic fur rendering instead of painted/blurry texture that looks fake.

**Vertical Knitted Texture:**
"Clearly visible vertical knitted texture" with "realistic folds" creates authentic sweater appearance. Without this detail, knit can look like solid fabric.

**Hair Flyaways:**
Requesting "hair flyaways" adds realism and natural movement. Perfect smooth hair reads as artificial or overly styled.

**Snow Landscape Reflections:**
"Realistic reflection of surrounding snow landscape in the lenses" grounds the sunglasses in the environment and prevents flat rendering.

**No Beauty Filter:**
Explicitly state "no beauty-filter skin" and "authentic skin texture" — playful expressions need natural skin to feel genuine, not processed.

**Background Recognition:**
"Background also recognizable" prevents over-blurring that makes image feel artificial. Smartphone portraits typically have good depth of field.
