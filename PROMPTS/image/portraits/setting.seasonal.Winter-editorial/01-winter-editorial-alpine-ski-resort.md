# Winter Editorial Photoshoot — Alpine Ski Resort Series

**6 ULTRA-PHOTOREALISTIC WINTER LIFESTYLE PORTRAITS**

## Overview

Complete winter editorial photoshoot series featuring six distinct alpine ski resort lifestyle portraits. Each prompt generates ultra-photorealistic vertical compositions (4:5) capturing luxury European ski vacation aesthetic with authentic smartphone and professional photography styles. Subjects include selfies, travel portraits, fashion shots, and cinematic expedition imagery across various times of day and emotional tones.

**Format:** Vertical 4:5 composition  
**Quality:** Ultra-photorealistic 8K detail  
**Setting:** High-altitude alpine ski resorts and mountain summits  
**Style:** Luxury ski vacation editorial, authentic social media content

## Pro Tips

**Smartphone Selfie Realism:**

- Specify "arm visibly enlarged closer to lens" for authentic perspective
- Request "realistic edge distortion from extended arm"
- Emphasize "natural handheld selfie pose"
- Use 24–28mm equivalent lens specification

**Professional Portrait Compression:**

- Use 50–70mm equivalent for flattering compression
- Request "slight compression making mountains appear grand"
- Specify "moderate background compression for epic scale"

**Snow Exposure:**

- Critical: "Correct exposure with visible snow texture rather than blown-out whites"
- Request "textured snow" not flat white
- Specify "subtle ski tracks and uneven snow texture"

**Fabric Realism:**

- List specific details: "seams," "wrinkles," "reflections," "quilted panels"
- Specify finish: "matte technical fabric" vs. "glossy satin-like sheen"
- Request "realistic folds" and "natural light reflections"

**Winter Light Quality:**

- Emphasize "strong but believable snow bounce lighting"
- Request "natural hard-edged shadows" for bright sun
- Specify "warm edge highlights" for sunset
- Use "physically accurate sunlight" for realism

**Background Detail:**

- Don't let background blur too much on selfies
- Request "distant mountains remain detailed"
- Specify "background also recognizable"
- Use "highly detailed mountain formations"
