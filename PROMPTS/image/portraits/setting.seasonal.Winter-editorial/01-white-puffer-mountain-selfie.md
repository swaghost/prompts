## Pro Tips

**Smartphone Selfie Authenticity:**
The key to authentic smartphone selfie aesthetic is explicitly requesting "realistic edge distortion from extended arm" and "arm visibly enlarged closer to lens." This prevents the AI from generating a professional camera look.

**Snow Exposure:**
Critical specification: "Correct exposure with visible snow texture rather than blown-out whites." Without this, snow often renders as flat overexposed white with no detail.

**Background Sharpness:**
Request "distant mountains remain detailed" to prevent over-blurring that makes image feel artificial. Smartphone selfies typically have good depth of field.

**Natural Imperfections:**
Emphasize "visible natural skin pores and tiny imperfections" plus "no excessive beauty retouching" to avoid Instagram-filter look that feels dated.

**Lighting Balance:**
"Strong but believable snow bounce lighting" ensures face is well-lit from below without looking artificially lit or studio-processed.
