# Identity-Consistent Couple Photography - Sunset Coastal Road

## Category
Identity-Consistent Photography / Candid Documentary

## Style
Natural Documentary / Romantic Road Trip

## Context
Uses two uploaded portrait references as strict identity references for both people.

## Prompt

Use the two uploaded portrait references as strict identity references. Preserve the exact recognizable facial identity of both the woman and the man: their natural facial proportions, eye shape, skin tone, age and individual features. Do not merge their faces, beautify them excessively or turn them into generic fashion models.

A realistic candid color photograph of the same couple beside a black vintage convertible on a quiet coastal road during warm sunset. The woman wears a simple fitted black mini dress with long sleeves, opaque fabric and a modest rounded neckline, paired with black knee-high leather boots. The dress follows her natural feminine figure without exaggeration.

The man wears a relaxed white linen shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark straight trousers and black leather shoes. Photographed from a very low angle near the wet asphalt, the woman and man are just turning toward the man after stepping away from the car. One knee is slightly bent, her weight rests unevenly on one leg, one hand instinctively holds the hem of her dress against the wind, her long blonde hair is again the sea breeze, with several strands crossing her cheek. She looks at the man rather than at the camera, with a faint amused expression.

The man leaves against the car in a relaxed, imperfect posture, watching her with a subtle half-smile. His shirt is moving in the wind. Neither person is posing for a fashion campaign. The moment feels accidentally captured during a real road trip.

Natural orange sunset light mixed with cool blue shadows, realistic skin texture, visible pores, natural hands, subtle unevenness in clothing, slight imperfect framing, gentle film grain, restrained colors, documentary photography, Leica Q2, 28mm lens, vertical 4:5. No plastic skin, no CGI, no monochrome, no text.
