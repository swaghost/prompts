# Identity-Consistent Couple Photography - Walking Together

## Category
Identity-Consistent Photography / Candid Motion Documentary

## Style
Authentic Cinematic Romance / Natural Road Trip

## Context
Uses two uploaded portrait references as strict identity references for both people.

## Prompt

Use the uploaded portraits as strict identity references for the same woman and man. Preserve their exact recognizable appearance and natural body proportions.

A candid color photograph of the same couple walking beside a black vintage convertible on a wet coastal road at sunset. The woman wears a fitted black mini dress with long sleeves and knee-high black leather boots. The man wears a relaxed white linen shirt and dark trousers.

They walk toward the camera, while still to each other but neither looks directly into the lens. The woman turns her face toward him mid-step with a lively, teasing expression. The man looks down briefly and smiles as if reacting to something she just said. Their joined hands swing naturally between them. Their walking rhythm is slightly uneven and their bodies are caught in a real, transitional movement rather than a synchronized pose.

Her long blonde hair blows backward and occasionally covers one eye. The dress and shirt move naturally in the sea wind. The car remains slightly out of focus behind them. Small puddles on the road reflect warm orange sunset light.

Realistic handheld photography, subtle motion blur in their legs and clothing while their faces remain recognizable. Natural texture, authentic skin tones, subtle analog grain, handheld photography, slightly off-center composition, 50mm lens, Kodak Portra 400, horizontal 3:2. No glossy editorial styling, no perfect symmetry, no posed look, no CGI, no text.
