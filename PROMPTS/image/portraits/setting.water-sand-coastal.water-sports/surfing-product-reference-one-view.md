# Ocean Performance Product Reference - One View

## Description of Resulting Image

A clean single-view product reference image for an ocean sports campaign. The product is isolated, centered, fully visible, and rendered with accurate shape, materials, color, surface treatment, and branding for reliable reuse in image and video generation.

## Usage

Use this prompt before generating a multi-shot sports campaign when the product needs a single visual source of truth. Perfect for goggles, swimwear accessories, surf equipment, water-sports gear, and other products that must remain identical across action scenes. Ideal for Seedance 2.5, product boards, campaign development, and commercial image generation.

## Engines/Models

ChatGPT image generation, GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux, or another product-reference-capable image model.

## Actor or Subject

The exact uploaded ocean-sports product, shown alone.

## Prerequisites

- One clean, high-resolution product reference image
- Authorized brand and product assets
- Preferred output format, defaulting to 4:5 portrait

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** Soft neutral studio illumination, controlled highlights, gentle contact shadow

**Photographic Effects:** Product-photography sharpness, accurate material rendering, clean reflections

**Atmospheric Effects:** Minimal neutral studio atmosphere

**Post-Processing:** Natural color correction, high-resolution detail refinement

**Style Filters:** Premium commercial product photography, luxury sports campaign

## Included Camera Frames

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Professional product camera, 85mm equivalent
- **Framing & Composition:** One centered, fully visible product view with generous margins
- **Camera Angle:** Straight-on or the clearest angle shown in the reference
- **Depth of Field:** Product sharp throughout, clean unobtrusive background
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 portrait or the campaign's required format
- **Shot Distance:** Medium product shot

## Image Prompt

**Subject:** Use the uploaded product image as the exact reference. Reproduce the identical silhouette, proportions, materials, colorway, lens or surface treatment, hardware, logo placement, and every distinctive design detail. Do not redesign, restyle, recolor, simplify, or replace the product.

**Setting/Environment:** Clean neutral studio background with no props or competing objects.

**Lighting:** Soft, even, daylight-balanced commercial light with controlled specular highlights and a subtle natural contact shadow.

**Composition:** Centered, fully visible product with enough negative space for a reference image. Keep the clearest product-facing angle from the source image.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Photorealistic luxury sports product photography, premium campaign quality, accurate physical materials.

**Technical Specifications:** High resolution, sharp edges, realistic reflections, 4:5 portrait format unless otherwise specified.

**Color Palette:** Match the uploaded product exactly; do not invent accent colors.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Precise, clean, premium, campaign-ready.

**Quality Keywords:** Ultra-detailed, photorealistic, sharp product focus, accurate materials, commercial photography, high resolution.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Redesigned product, altered proportions, wrong colors, wrong materials, missing details, invented branding, distorted logo, extra products, hands, model, clutter, text overlay, watermark, illustration, CGI look, blur, low resolution.
