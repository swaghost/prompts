# Ocean Performance Product Campaign - 12-Shot Seedance Series

## Description

A 12-shot, 30-second luxury sports product campaign built around one athlete and one exact product reference. The sequence moves from surfing to jet ski action to underwater swimming, then closes on a clean product hero. Use the attached product image as the strict visual anchor in every shot.

## Platform and Format

- **Primary platform:** Seedance 2.5
- **Campaign length:** 30 seconds total
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical by default; adapt to 16:9 or 4:5 when needed
- **Look:** Photorealistic luxury sportswear advertising
- **Camera language:** ARRI Alexa-style cinematic capture, anamorphic wides, macro detail, drone and underwater perspectives
- **Motion:** Slow-motion water and spray with sharp subject and product detail
