# Ocean Performance Campaign - Shot 08 Jet Ski Standing Dominance

## Description of Resulting Image

A low-angle full-body hero image of the athlete standing upright on a stationary jet ski in open ocean, arms crossed and product visible against a burning golden sky.

## Usage

Use as a character-and-product dominance frame or campaign midpoint hero. Ideal for luxury sportswear, eyewear, marine recreation, and vertical social advertising.

**Lighting:** Burning golden-hour sky with deep amber, molten orange, and crimson streaks; dramatic warm rim light.

**Composition:** Low angle makes the athlete tower over the frame. Full body and jet ski remain visible.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Premium fashion campaign meets marine athletic power.
