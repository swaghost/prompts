# Ocean Performance Campaign - Shot 04 Aerial Overhead

## Description of Resulting Image

A direct overhead aerial image of the athlete paddling powerfully on a white surfboard through dark teal water, with foam, long hair trails, and golden reflections defining the composition.

## Usage

Use as an aerial scale and movement frame in an ocean sports campaign. Ideal for surfing brands, water-sports equipment, campaign storyboards, and cinematic social content.

## Engines/Models

Seedance 2.5, GPT Image, Flux, or another photorealistic image model.

## Actor or Subject

The same athlete in the exact referenced product and campaign wardrobe.

## Prerequisites

- Athlete reference
- Exact product reference
- Optional surfboard reference

## Image Prompt

**Subject:** Same athlete lying flat and paddling powerfully on a white surfboard. Long dark hair streams straight behind like a comet trail. The white one-piece, bright magenta zipper, and exact referenced product are clearly visible from above.

**Setting/Environment:** Dark churning deep-teal ocean water. Both arms are mid-stroke and white foam erupts around the board.

**Lighting:** Golden-hour light casts long graphic shadows across foam and ripples.

**Composition:** Direct bird's-eye view with the board and athlete as the central graphic shape; camera appears to descend slowly.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Raw athletic energy, premium drone cinematography, photorealistic water.

**Technical Specifications:** High resolution, stable overhead geometry, sharp product and board.

**Color Palette:** Deep teal, white, warm gold, amber.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Powerful, expansive, elemental.

**Quality Keywords:** Cinematic 8K, drone cinematography, ultra sharp, realistic water.
