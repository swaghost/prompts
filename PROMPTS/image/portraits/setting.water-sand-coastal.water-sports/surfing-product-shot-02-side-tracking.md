# Ocean Performance Campaign - Shot 02 Side Tracking

## Description of Resulting Image

A dynamic low-angle side frame of an athlete crouched low on a surfboard inside the barrel of a curling dark teal wave, with golden backlight and explosive spray.

## Usage

Use for the campaign's first action beat, especially when the product must remain visible during lateral movement. Ideal for surfing, sportswear, eyewear, and action-advertising storyboards.

Seedance 2.5, GPT Image, Flux, or another reference-capable photorealistic image model.

## Image Prompt

**Subject:** Same athlete, same face, hair, wardrobe, and exact referenced product. She crouches aggressively low on the surfboard, center of gravity dropped, arms cutting through the air for balance while riding inside the barrel of a massive curling wave.

**Setting/Environment:** Drenched hair flies sideways, skin is covered in ocean spray, and the white one-piece with bright magenta zipper remains unchanged. The dark teal wave curls overhead.

**Lighting:** Golden backlight explodes behind the silhouette and rims the body in molten gold.

**Composition:** Track laterally at water level, keeping the board, athlete, product, and wave barrel readable.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Fearless, ferocious luxury sports advertising with realistic water physics.

**Color Palette:** Dark teal, white, amber, molten gold, burnt orange.
