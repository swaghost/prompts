# Ocean Performance Campaign - Shot 01 Wide Establishing

## Description of Resulting Image

A wide cinematic ocean-sports campaign frame showing an athlete standing powerfully on a white surfboard as it cuts through a towering dark teal wave at golden hour.

## Usage

Use as the opening image of a surfing product campaign or as a keyframe for a Seedance 2.5 video shot. It establishes the athlete, product, board, wave scale, and warm luxury-campaign color language. Ideal for sportswear launches, outdoor advertising, social campaigns, and cinematic storyboards.

## Engines/Models

Seedance 2.5, GPT Image, Flux, Midjourney, or another photorealistic image model with product reference support.

## Actor or Subject

Athlete from the uploaded reference, wearing the exact referenced product and white athletic one-piece.

## Prerequisites

- Athlete reference image
- Exact product reference image
- Optional surfboard or wave reference

**Subject:** Use the athlete reference for the same recognizable person and the product reference for the exact product. The athlete stands powerfully upright on a white surfboard, knees bent in an athletic stance, arms locked at the sides, jaw set, fierce and dominant expression. **Composition:** Wide establishing frame with the athlete dominant but the wave scale unmistakable. Leave breathing room around the silhouette.
