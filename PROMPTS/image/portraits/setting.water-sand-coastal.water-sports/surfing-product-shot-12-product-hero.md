# Ocean Performance Campaign - Shot 12 Product Hero

## Description of Resulting Image

A macro product-only hero image placing the exact ocean-sports product on dark wet shoreline rocks against crashing waves, warm golden light, and dramatic bokeh.

## Usage

Use as the closing product image of the campaign or as a standalone luxury product advertisement. Ideal for product launches, e-commerce hero art, social advertising, and premium marine-brand presentations.

## Engines/Models

Seedance 2.5, GPT Image, Flux, Midjourney, or another product-reference-capable image model.

## Actor or Subject

The exact uploaded product. No model and no hands.

## Prerequisites

- Clean, high-resolution product reference
- Authorized brand logo reference if branding must be visible
- Optional shoreline reference

## Included Effects

**Lighting Effects:** Golden-hour highlights and warm reflections

**Photographic Effects:** Macro focus, product-first sharpness, dramatic bokeh

**Atmospheric Effects:** Salt foam, ocean mist, crashing waves, water droplets

**Post-Processing:** Premium warm commercial grade

**Style Filters:** Luxury product campaign, photorealistic marine editorial

## Included Camera Frames

- **Camera Type & Lens:** Macro lens, ARRI Alexa-style rendering
- **Framing & Composition:** Product dominant in foreground with shoreline context
- **Camera Angle:** Low three-quarter product angle
- **Depth of Field:** Product tack sharp, waves and rocks softly layered behind
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 vertical
- **Shot Distance:** Extreme close-up product shot

## Image Prompt

**Subject:** Place only the exact uploaded product with precision on dark wet ocean rocks at the shoreline. Preserve identical silhouette, proportions, materials, colors, surface treatment, and logo placement. No model and no hands.

**Setting/Environment:** Salt foam and ocean mist move softly in the background while dark waves crash against the rocks. The product rests securely and physically on the wet surface.

**Lighting:** Golden-hour light bathes the scene in warm amber and orange. Water droplets cling to the product and catch the light.

**Composition:** Product fills the primary foreground focus. Its hero surface faces camera and reflects the golden horizon and crashing waves. Leave enough background for dramatic bokeh without reducing product clarity.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Pure luxury product power, photorealistic commercial photography.

**Technical Specifications:** Macro lens, cinematic 8K, ultra sharp product focus, realistic reflections and water droplets.

**Color Palette:** Blush or product-specific color, dark wet rock, deep teal, white foam, amber, orange, gold.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Premium, elemental, powerful, final, aspirational.

**Quality Keywords:** Ultra sharp, photorealistic, macro detail, cinematic bokeh, luxury campaign, accurate product.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Model, hands, extra products, redesigned product, wrong colors, altered logo, product distortion, product out of focus, clutter, text overlay, watermark, illustration, CGI, low resolution.
