# Ocean Performance Campaign - Shot 03 Extreme Close-Up

## Description of Resulting Image

An extreme close-up campaign portrait of the athlete's wet face and product, with ocean droplets, airborne hair, reflective surface detail, and blazing golden rim light.

## Usage

Use when the product and athlete identity need a high-impact detail frame. Ideal for campaign cutaways, product-feature emphasis, social-media key art, and luxury editorial sports photography.

## Engines/Models

Seedance 2.5, GPT Image, Flux, Midjourney, or any model supporting reference images.

## Actor or Subject

The same athlete wearing the exact referenced product.

## Prerequisites

- High-resolution athlete reference
- Exact product reference

## Image Prompt

**Subject:** Extreme close-up of the same athlete's face and upper body. Wet dark hair is plastered across the cheeks and flies across the lens. Preserve the exact face, product, wardrobe, and product surface details from the references.

**Setting/Environment:** Ocean spray and water droplets cover the skin. The product surface reflects a burning golden ocean horizon and dramatic amber sky.

**Lighting:** Golden-hour rim light blazes from behind, creating bright warm edges and controlled highlights.

**Composition:** Lock focus on the eyes and product. Keep the expression raw, powerful, and fearless: eyes forward, jaw tight, lips slightly parted.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Luxury fashion editorial fused with high-performance ocean realism.

**Technical Specifications:** Macro lens, shallow depth of field, cinematic 8K detail.

**Color Palette:** Amber, orange, molten gold, dark teal, wet skin neutrals.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Absolute intensity, power, fearlessness.

**Quality Keywords:** Sharp face, sharp product, photorealistic droplets, cinematic, ultra detailed.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Face drift, wrong product, missing product, soft focus on product, motion blur on face, plastic skin, distorted eyes, extra features, text, watermark, CGI, low resolution.
