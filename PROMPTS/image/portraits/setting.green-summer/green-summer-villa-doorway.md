# Look #06: Villa Doorway - Full-Body Architectural Luxury Portrait

## Description

Ultra-elegant full-body luxury portrait set in sophisticated Mediterranean or tropical villa architecture, emphasizing timeless fashion and refined environment. Subject leaning casually against pristine white doorframe or column, body slightly turned creating graceful elongated silhouette. Wearing stunning asymmetric deep emerald-green or forest-green column dress with one-shoulder design, floor-length elegant drape, and visible leg through side slit. Natural confident expression gazing softly toward camera with calm sophisticated demeanor. Framed by lush palm tree leaves in foreground (natural frame), with glimpse of pristine turquoise pool water visible in background creating depth and luxury resort atmosphere. Bright natural daylight with soft shadows creates clean architectural aesthetic. Part of the Green Summer portrait collection emphasizing rich green-toned styling and elevated luxury aesthetic, this look feels most architectural, refined, and full-body fashion editorial.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Dress design not matching asymmetric one-shoulder style

**Solution:** Be very specific: "asymmetric one-shoulder design," "visible side slit with leg shown," "floor-length column," emphasize all design elements, regenerate if needed

### Problem: Not full-body or cropped awkwardly

**Solution:** Emphasize "full-body portrait," "head to feet visible," "floor-length dress shown completely," specify framing clearly

### Problem: Architecture not clean or refined enough

**Solution:** Specify "pristine white," "clean doorframe," "minimalist modern villa," "elegant columns," negative prompt "cluttered background, poor architecture"

### Problem: Palm framing not present or pool not visible

**Solution:** Emphasize "lush green palm leaves in foreground naturally framing," "glimpse of turquoise pool in background," "pool visible through doorway"

### Problem: Pose too stiff or not elegant

**Solution:** Specify "leaning casually but gracefully," "body slightly turned elongated silhouette," "natural confident effortlessly sophisticated," regenerate for better pose

### Problem: Lighting too harsh or uneven

**Solution:** Emphasize "bright natural daylight with soft shadows," "clean architectural lighting," "even illumination," "gentle exposure," negative prompt "harsh HDR, artificial glow"

### Problem: Dress color wrong

**Solution:** Be specific: "deep emerald-green or forest-green," provide color reference if possible, emphasize "luxurious fabric with subtle sheen"
