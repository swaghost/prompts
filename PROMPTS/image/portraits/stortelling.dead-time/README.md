# Dead Time - Prompts.soul Vol. 001

Eight candid documentary image prompts about the hours between where you were and where you are going.

## Shared Direction

- **Format:** 4:5 portrait
- **Style:** Candid documentary photography
- **Subject:** Woman in her mid-twenties with shoulder-length wavy dark brown hair, light freckles, and a bare unstyled face
- **Camera:** Available light only, no flash, 50mm, shallow depth of field with a soft out-of-focus near foreground
- **Grade:** Warm faded colour with muted greens and browns, gentle halation, fine 35mm film grain
- **Source:** Prompts.soul, Dead Time, Vol. 001
