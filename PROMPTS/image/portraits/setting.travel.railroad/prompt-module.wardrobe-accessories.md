# Prompt Module - Wardrobe and Accessories

## Base Black Evening Gown

```
elegant matte-black high-fashion evening gown with a sculptural couture silhouette. Strapless sweetheart neckline with a tightly structured corset-style bodice defining the torso. Dramatic oversized off-the-shoulder sculptural sleeves forming bold architectural volumes around the upper arms. Long weighted skirt with a high thigh slit revealing the leg naturally while seated. Long matte-black opera gloves extending above the elbows. A classic multi-row pearl choker necklace fitted close to the neck. A wide-brim black hat with a flat crown and rigid structure, worn slightly tilted, no embellishments.
```

## Red Haute Couture

```
dramatic floor-length haute couture gown in deep crimson red silk-satin with a high-shine finish. Asymmetrical one-shoulder design with a wide structural strap, fitted corseted bodice, voluminous skirt with cascading folds and train, long matching silk gloves, geometric gold necklace, and wide-brim crimson felt hat.
```

## White Bridal Avant-Garde

```
avant-garde white bridal ensemble in textured raw silk with matte finish. Structured geometric bralette-style top, high-waisted billowing palazzo pants, oversized white organza jacket with dramatic bell sleeves, white elbow-length leather gloves, minimalist silver throat cuff, and pristine white wide-brim panama hat.
```

## Metallic Futurism

```
futuristic metallic silver bodysuit in high-shine liquid-metal fabric with seamless construction, articulated brushed-aluminum shoulder pieces, panelled sleeves, high-neck collar, metallic thigh-high boots, silver mesh gloves, and chrome visor-style sunglasses.
```

## Earthy Bohemian Luxury

```
flowing bohemian-luxury ensemble in cognac-brown leather and cream silk. Soft leather crop top with fringe and geometric beadwork, high-waisted cream silk palazzo pants, oversized cream linen duster, cognac leather gloves, multi-strand turquoise and silver necklace, and wide-brim cognac leather cowboy hat.
```

## Neon Street-Couture

```
high-fashion streetwear in electric neon pink and black. Oversized neon pink quilted puffer jacket, black leather corset over a white graphic T-shirt, black cargo pants with neon straps, chunky neon sneakers, fingerless gloves, layered silver chains, and a backwards black cap.
```

## Sheer Romantic Drama

```
romantic sheer black organza ensemble with Victorian gothic influence. High-neck sheer blouse with ruffled collar and poet sleeves, black lace bralette, floor-length tulle skirt, silk ribbon corset belt, long lace gloves, antique silver locket, and black velvet wide-brim hat.
```

## Accessory Options

- Opera gloves for classic elegance.
- Short wrist gloves for contemporary minimalism.
- Fingerless gloves for an edgy practical look.
- Wide-brim flat-crown hat for monumental presence.
- Panama hat for summer luxury.
- Pearls for timeless refinement.
- Gold chains for contemporary wealth.
- Silver geometric jewellery for architectural minimalism.
- Turquoise and gemstones for earthy bohemian colour.
