# Prompt Module - Scenes and Props

## Railroad Tracks in the Desert

```
the subject is sitting on a simple, worn wooden chair placed directly on railroad tracks, centered between the rails, in the middle of a vast desert. Posture relaxed and poised. In one gloved hand the subject holds a lipstick, gently pressing it to the lips mid-application; in the other a small handheld mirror, actively applying lipstick while maintaining eye contact with the camera. The chair casts a sharp shadow across dusty wooden sleepers and steel rails. Behind the subject, perfectly aligned with the tracks, a freight train is far in the distance, small in scale, with faint headlights diffused by heat haze.
```

## Abandoned Gas Station

```
the subject is sitting on a weathered metal folding chair beside vintage gas pumps at an abandoned desert gas station. A faded single-story structure, broken windows, rusty sign, tumbleweeds, and cracked concrete surround the subject. The subject holds a cold glass bottle of soda while maintaining eye contact. An empty highway disappears into heat haze in the distance.
```

## Desert Salt Flats

```
the subject is sitting on a modern clear acrylic chair placed on perfectly flat white salt flats extending endlessly to the horizon. The subject holds an open vintage hardcover book, finger marking a page, and looks directly at the camera. Distant mountains appear as faint blue silhouettes through heat shimmer, with shallow mirror-like water reflecting the sky.
```

## Canyon Edge Lookout

```
the subject is sitting on a natural flat boulder at the edge of a dramatic desert canyon, legs dangling above the canyon floor. Red rock formations and layered geological strata fill the gorge below. The subject lowers binoculars from the eyes and looks directly at the camera. Wind-bent juniper trees cling to the canyon rim.
```

## Ghost Town Saloon Entrance

```
the subject is sitting on a wooden rocking chair on the covered porch of an abandoned Wild West saloon in a desert ghost town. Peeling paint, swinging saloon doors, a hitching post, a water trough, and Victorian details frame the scene. The subject holds a vintage glass oil lamp glowing warmly while an empty dirt street extends into the desert.
```

## Desert Art Installation

```
the subject is sitting on a sculptural metal chair in an open desert art installation beside large-scale abstract rusted-steel forms. The subject holds a small vintage film camera and adjusts its settings while maintaining eye contact. Footprints lead to the installation, and golden-hour light creates dramatic shadows through the geometric frames.
```

## Dry Lake Bed with Vintage Car

```
the subject is sitting on a vintage leather car seat placed on cracked dry lake bed beside a rusted 1950s pickup truck half-buried in desert sand. The truck is faded turquoise with missing windows and doors. The subject holds a vintage road map across the lap while a dust devil turns in the distance.
```

## Desert Observatory Ruins

```
the subject is sitting on a modern white cube pedestal amid ruins of an abandoned astronomical observatory. A collapsed domed building, old telescope equipment, and sand surround the subject. The subject holds a small vintage brass telescope pointed upward while looking at the camera. The desert is reclaiming the abandoned scientific structure.
```

## Seated Posture Options

- Relaxed with crossed legs.
- Straight posture with knees together.
- Leaning forward with elbows on knees.
- Sitting sideways with legs over the chair arm.
- Slouched back with head tilted.
- Perched on the edge of the seat, ready to stand.
