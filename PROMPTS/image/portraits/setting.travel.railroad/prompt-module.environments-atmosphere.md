# Prompt Module - Environments and Atmosphere

## Base Desert Environment

```
endless desert landscape with pale sand, scattered stones, dry shrubs, cracked earth, distant mesas and low mountains on the horizon. Dust and heat shimmer dance in the air, dry wind carrying fine dust.
```

## Mojave Desert

```
Mojave Desert landscape with Joshua trees, beige sand, dark volcanic rocks, rust-red soil, sparse creosote bushes, distant blue-purple mountains, intense clear sky, and minimal heat distortion near the ground.
```

## White Sands

```
White Sands desert with pure white gypsum dunes, wind-carved ridges, sparse yucca plants, distant dark mountains, intense reflected light, deep blue sky, footprints, and delicate windblown surface patterns.
```

## Sonoran Desert

```
Sonoran Desert landscape with saguaro cacti, rocky sand and gravel, palo verde trees, ocotillo plants, rugged stratified mountains, and warm golden-hour light across red-orange earth.
```

## Atacama Desert

```
Atacama Desert landscape with rust-red and copper-toned earth, no vegetation, angular rock formations, white salt crusts, distant snow-capped Andes, extremely clear dry air, and alien Mars-like terrain.
```

## Sahara Desert

```
Sahara landscape with massive orange-gold dunes, wind-sculpted ridges, a distant oasis or acacia, hazy Atlas Mountains, fine sand texture, heat shimmer, and a romantic desert-postcard atmosphere.
```

## Death Valley

```
Death Valley landscape with eroded golden, rust, purple and gray badlands, cracked salt flats, steep mountain walls, sparse palms, extreme heat shimmer, and harsh bleached midday light.
```

## Nevada Playa

```
Nevada desert playa with a perfectly flat alkaline surface, geometric cracked ground, distant mountains, rare shallow reflective water, open sky, and an infinite minimalist sense of space.
```

## Atmospheric Effects

### Golden-Hour Haze

```
late-afternoon golden light diffused through airborne dust particles, soft warm glow, reduced contrast, gentle shadows, romantic atmospheric quality
```

### Heat Shimmer

```
intense heat causing visible distortion waves rising from the ground, distant objects wavering, mirage effects on the horizon, and liquid-like air movement
```

### Dust Storm

```
massive wall of dust approaching in the background, orange-brown cloud towering into the sky, diffused sunlight, visibility decreasing toward the horizon, and approaching environmental tension
```

### Storm Clearing

```
dark storm clouds breaking apart, shafts of sunlight piercing through gaps, wet sand or mud reflecting the light, fresh washed atmosphere, and dramatic natural contrast
```
