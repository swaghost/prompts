# Prompt Module - Camera and Composition

## Extreme Ground-Level Low Angle

```
cinematic film-still aesthetic, extreme ground-level low angle - camera placed directly on the ground between the rails, almost touching gravel and steel, looking steeply upward. 35mm lens, perspective exaggerated by proximity. Converging rails powerfully lead into the seated figure. The camera is clearly below knee level, with the sky occupying a significant portion of the upper frame. Strong foreground, middleground, and background layering; subject tack-sharp, foreground rails slightly soft, distant train atmospheric.
```

## Eye-Level Straight-On

```
eye-level perspective at the subject's eye height, direct frontal view, 50mm lens, natural undistorted perspective, shallow f/1.8 depth of field, centered composition, subject tack-sharp, background softly blurred into atmospheric haze.
```

## High-Angle Bird's-Eye

```
elevated high-angle perspective from 15-20 feet above the subject, looking down at approximately 45 degrees, wide 24mm lens, expansive desert context, subject small within the landscape, tracks and shadows forming graphic geometry, contemplative environmental composition.
```

## Low Dutch Angle

```
extreme low-angle Dutch tilt with the camera near the ground and rotated 15-25 degrees, 35mm lens, diagonal horizon, disorienting dynamic composition, converging rails and vertical elements leaning dramatically, psychological tension and experimental fashion-film energy.
```

## Profile Side View

```
extreme ground-level camera positioned beside the tracks, capturing the subject in perfect profile, 50mm lens, undistorted silhouette, desert extending to the horizon, chair visible from the side, dramatic side lighting emphasizing contours.
```

## Over-the-Shoulder Reverse

```
camera positioned behind and to the side of the subject, capturing an over-the-shoulder view toward the approaching train and railroad tracks. 35mm lens, subject's back and profile edge slightly out of focus, distant train larger and more threatening, shared perspective and suspense.
```

## Close-Up Intimate Detail

```
tight close-up from shoulders to top of hat, eye-level camera, 85mm portrait lens, f/1.4 depth of field, eyes and face sharp, environmental context reduced to soft desert tones, emphasis on expression, skin texture, makeup, jewellery, gloves, and fabric detail.
```

## Composition Controls

- Use converging rails, roads, fences, walls, or shadows as leading lines.
- Keep the foreground textured, the subject in the middleground, and distant landscape atmospheric.
- Use moderate f/2.8-f/4 depth of field when context must remain recognizable.
- Use deep f/8-f/11 focus for documentary environmental storytelling.
- Keep distant trains, buildings, and vehicles small enough to preserve believable scale.
- Use negative space intentionally around the subject to emphasize isolation and independence.
