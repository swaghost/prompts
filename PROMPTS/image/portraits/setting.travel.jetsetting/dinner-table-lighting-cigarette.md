## Complete Prompt

A white male, late 30s, olive Mediterranean skin, thick dark wavy hair swept back and slightly unruly, full dark mustache, no beard. Weathered handsome face.

Expression: staring directly into the lens, eyes intense and faintly amused under lowered brows, cigarette held between the lips. Confident, knowing, entirely unbothered.

Outfit: plain cream cotton t-shirt, softly worn. Deep red bandana knotted loosely around the neck. Chunky gold signet ring on one finger, thin silver ring on another, black leather cord bracelet and a black-faced diver's watch on the wrist.

Action: both hands raised to his mouth cupping a brushed gold rectangular lighter, flame just catching the tip of the cigarette, a faint wisp of smoke rising.

Setting: seated at a dinner table in an old European dining room. Across the bottom of the frame in the foreground: a glass of white wine, a tumbler of water, a large empty white plate, a plate of sliced tomato and mozzarella with a green basil quenelle, a bottle of white wine to the right, scattered cutlery. Distressed white plaster wall with a moulded panel directly behind him.

Shot angle: chest-up, camera at seated eye level directly across the table. Telephoto compression - foreground tableware heavily out of focus, subject tack sharp, wall softly blurred behind.

Lighting: warm natural window light from the left, soft and directional, warm highlights across skin and hair, gentle shadow falling on the right side of the face. Muted Euro-summer film grade.

Photorealistic.
