# Prompt 07: Studio Floor, Ink Brushwork

**NEUTRAL DAYLIGHT · HIGH ANGLE**

---

A young woman, mid-20s, warm light-tan skin with visible freckles across the nose and cheeks, long curly light brown hair gathered into a messy high half-up bun with loose corkscrew curls falling around the face, neck and shoulders. Small gold hoop earrings. Bare face with a natural sheen.

Expression: looking down at the canvas in complete concentration, lips softly closed, brows level, eyes following the brush. Absorbed, working, unaware of the camera.

Pose: kneeling forward on the floor with her weight on her knees and shins, torso leaning in. Right arm extended down and forward, palm flat on the canvas supporting her. Left arm reaching across holding a long wooden-handled brush mid-stroke, wrist loose, the tip loaded with black ink and touching the canvas.

Details: a stack of small colourful beaded bracelets on the brush wrist. A gold signet ring. A fine gold chain necklace with a small oval pendant. Ink already laid down beneath the brush in broad wet black gestural strokes.

Outfit: an oversized vintage mustard-ochre sweatshirt with a dark green ribbed collar, cuffs and side panel, printed on the chest with a small black-and-white photographic bowling graphic and on the sleeve with a red bowling pin and red script lettering. Loose olive green trousers, fabric pooling at the knee.

Setting: an artist's studio. White painted brick walls and a smooth grey concrete floor. Directly behind her, a huge unstretched canvas pinned to the wall bearing one enormous solid black ink form with dragged brush edges. More large canvases laid flat across the floor with bold black brush marks. A wooden chair with a green seat behind her, a black cap and a bottle of water on the floor beside it, a canvas tote bag hanging on the wall, a printed poster lying at the right edge of frame.

Shot angle: camera at standing height a couple of metres away, looking down at roughly 30 degrees, framing her full kneeling figure with the ink-marked canvas filling the foreground and the big black painting behind.

Lighting: bright neutral daylight across the studio - even and cool with a soft warm cast on the sweatshirt, gentle shadow under the knees and the brush, crisp wet sheen on the fresh ink.

Sharp phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic hair and ink texture. Photorealistic.
