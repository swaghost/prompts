Prominent straight Roman nose, full dark well-groomed mustache, no beard, medium stubble across the jaw and cheek, full lips slightly parted.

On the wrist, a gold tonneau-cased dress watch - rounded rectangular case, cream dial with black Roman numerals and slim hands, worn loose on a thick black leather strap with a squared keeper.
