A white male, mid-20s, fair skin with a light natural warmth, medium-brown hair cut short and slightly tousled. Ordinary approachable features - not a model face - straight nose, alert intelligent eyes, light stubble. Slim build, well-groomed.

Expression: mid-slurp, cheeks slightly full, eyebrows raised, glancing sideways at the camera with a small caught-out grin. Completely casual, zero glamour.

Pose: sprawled sideways across a cream leather captain's chair, back against one armrest, legs slung over the other, bare feet crossed. Holding an instant noodle cup in one hand, chopsticks raised to his mouth in the other.

Outfit: oversized charcoal hoodie, sleeves pushed to the forearms. Black lounge shorts. Bare feet, white socks discarded on the seat beside him. Silver chain, silver ring, black-strap watch.

Details: a laptop open and glowing on the polished walnut side table, an untouched glass of champagne next to it, a folded cashmere blanket, headphones coiled beside the noodle cup lid.

Setting: the cabin of a private jet at night. Cream leather club seating facing each other, walnut veneer panelling, brushed gold fittings, soft recessed ceiling strips. Oval window beside him showing black sky and a distant scatter of orange city lights far below through cloud.

Shot angle: camera from the aisle at seated eye level, cabin receding behind him.

Lighting: warm low-level cabin lighting, soft golden pools on the leather and walnut, cool blue laptop glow on his hands and chin, deep calm shadow in the cabin depth. Rich, quiet, expensive.
