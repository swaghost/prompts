# Prompt 01: Hotel Lobby Mirror, Bouquet

**MIRROR SELFIE · WARM LOBBY AMBIENCE**

---

A young East Asian woman, early-to-mid 20s, fair porcelain skin with a soft dewy finish and fine natural texture, dark brown hair pulled back into a low gathered ponytail with a centre parting, the front sections left loose as fine wispy face-framing strands falling past the jaw. Slim build, narrow shoulders. Softly arched brows, dark brown eyes with a subtle lifted liner, glossy nude-rose lip. Small gold huggie hoops in both ears.

Expression: cool and neutral, lips slightly parted, eyes lowered toward the phone screen rather than up at the mirror. Detached, unbothered, caught mid-scroll.

Pose: mirror selfie. Right arm raised holding the phone at chin height, elbow tucked in close to the ribs, wrist turned so the screen faces the mirror, fingers curled around the case with the thumb along the edge. Left arm crossed low over the body cradling a large bouquet against the opposite hip. Hips pushed to one side, weight on one leg, torso angled a few degrees away from the glass.

Details: a smartphone in a clear hard case with a red and orange pressed-flower graphic across the back and a triple camera module visible. Long pointed almond nails in glossy true red. A large cluster diamond ring on the index finger of the phone hand. A slim silver chain bracelet on the left wrist. A fine silver waist chain sitting low across the bare midriff, catching the light.

Bouquet: a large loose hand-tied arrangement held stems-down - tall blue delphinium spires, purple lisianthus, deep purple anemones with black centres, lime-green chrysanthemum pompons, broad glossy green anthurium leaves and sprigs of eucalyptus, with a long bundle of bare cut green stems fanning down past her hip toward the floor.

Outfit: a bright orange wide-wale corduroy cropped cardigan with short sleeves and scalloped edging along the opening, worn fully open and unbuttoned. Beneath it a black ribbed knit bandeau top fastened at the centre front with two polished silver hardware buckles. Low-rise dark grey-blue washed baggy jeans with a white top button and a wide waistband sitting well below the navel, bare midriff and navel exposed, denim pooling loose over the hips.

Setting: a luxury hotel lobby, completely empty. A large frameless wall mirror fills the frame. Reflected behind her: a warm-lit seating lounge with unoccupied armchairs, a cream drum-shade table lamp glowing on a dark wood console, marble and timber columns, a patterned low-pile carpet in muted greys and golds, tall glass panels, and a dark wall-mounted screen at the far left edge.

Strictly one person in the entire image - no other people, no figures, no reflections of anyone else anywhere in the mirror or the background. The lobby is empty apart from her.

Shot angle: phone held at chin height, straight into the mirror, framing from mid-thigh up, subject positioned to the right of centre with the lounge opening out behind her on the left.

Lighting: warm low-level hotel ambient light - soft golden pools from the table lamps and recessed ceiling lights, gentle even fill across the face with no hard shadow, saturated orange corduroy reading warm against the cool grey denim, subtle specular glints on the silver hardware and the waist chain.

Sharp modern phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic skin, corduroy and petal texture. Photorealistic.
