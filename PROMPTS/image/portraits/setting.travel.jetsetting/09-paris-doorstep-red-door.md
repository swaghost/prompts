# Prompt 09: Paris Doorstep, Red Door

**SOFT OVERCAST DAYLIGHT · HIGH ANGLE**

---

A young woman, early 20s, fair skin with light freckles across the nose and cheeks, shoulder-length dark blonde hair with lighter face-framing pieces, worn loose with a soft wave. Sunglasses pushed up on top of the head. Blue-green eyes, glowy natural makeup with a soft nude lip.

Expression: closed-lip smile with dimples pressing into both cheeks, eyes cut off to her left away from the lens, chin resting on her hand. Playful, sweet, mid-thought.

Pose: crouched down on a stone doorstep with knees drawn up and heels flat, left elbow propped on the knee with the chin resting in the palm, right forearm laid across the shins holding a phone loosely.

Details: chunky gold hoop earrings. Several gold signet and coloured-stone rings across both hands, one nail painted gold. A gold bangle, a fine gold chain bracelet and a jewelled crystal bracelet stacked on the wrists. A small black leather shoulder bag with a buckle flap resting against her hip. Navy suede sneakers with pale blue stripes and white ankle socks.

Outfit: a white cotton strappy sundress with tiered ruffled panels, the skirt pooling on the step around her.

Setting: a Parisian doorway. A tall deep-red painted panelled double door directly behind her with an ornate egg-and-dart moulded top rail and a polished brass kickplate along the base. Cream limestone wall to the right with patches of peeling paint, a cream ceramic plant pot with a green spiky plant, a metal grate set into the wall, and a bicycle wheel entering the right edge of frame. Grey stone pavement in the foreground.

Shot angle: camera at chest height standing over her, angled down at roughly 30 degrees, framing her full crouched figure with the red door filling the background.

Lighting: soft flat overcast daylight - even and shadowless on the face, gentle sheen on the white cotton, rich saturated red on the door, cool neutral tone throughout.

Sharp phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic skin texture with visible freckles. Photorealistic.
