# Prompt 02: Cafe Banquette, Blue Bandana

**WARM INTERIOR · SEATED PORTRAIT**

---

A young woman, early-to-mid 20s, fair skin with a warm luminous glow and a natural flush on the cheeks, chin-length dark brown bob with soft loose waves, one side tucked behind the ear. Full groomed brows, hazel-brown eyes with long defined lashes and soft brown liner, glossy nude-pink lips.

Expression: warm closed-lip smile with the corners just lifted, head tilted toward her raised arm, eyes looking directly and softly into the lens. Relaxed, charming, at ease.

Pose: seated on a banquette, leaning her shoulder against a white square column. Right arm raised with the hand resting flat on the top of her own head, elbow up and out. Left arm relaxed down beside her.

Details: a light blue cotton bandana printed with a small repeating orange and brown floral geometric motif, tied over the crown of the head with the hair falling loose beneath it. A slim silver ring on the raised hand. A thin dark braided cord bracelet on the raised wrist. A cream woven crochet handbag with rope handles resting on the seat beside her.

Outfit: a fitted grey ribbed cotton tank top with a wide scoop neck. Light blue washed high-waisted straight jeans with a visible button.

Setting: a bright modern cafe and boutique interior. She sits on a banquette upholstered in a deep blue botanical toile print. Behind her: warm backlit display shelving holding jewellery, small objects and colourful boxes; a shop sign in slim serif lettering above; pink velvet armchairs and a low table; a navy and white quatrefoil-patterned chair to the right beside a wooden table; pale wood flooring; a white beamed ceiling with recessed downlights; large windows to the right.

Shot angle: camera at seated eye level a couple of steps away, slightly to her right, framing from the hips up with the column edge just behind her shoulder.

Lighting: warm interior downlight and shelf backlighting mixing with cool daylight from the windows - soft golden highlight along the cheekbone, shoulder and bandana, gentle even fill, no hard shadow.

Sharp phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic skin texture. Photorealistic.
