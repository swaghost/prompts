# Face Forward - The Horizon Waits

**Style:** Back shot, horizon view

```

Bright midday overhead sun creates strong highlights on the hair crown and thighs. Use a 28mm wide lens at f/5.6, straight-on composition from below eye level, natural light only, Kodak Portra 160 base, full architectural yacht and city context.
```
