# Dark Cinematic Luxury Sedan Night Portrait - Low-Angle Editorial

## Prompt Type

Automotive Editorial Photography - Night Portrait with Luxury Vehicle

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

A dark cinematic low-angle full-body editorial portrait of an adult man standing beside a luxury black sedan at night, camera positioned low to ground level. The subject's crisp white slightly unbuttoned shirt, black tailored trousers, and brown loafers stretch confidently while leather accents in his trouser pocket add sophistication. He rests in his trouser pocket while the other lightly touches the door handle, and his gaze is sharp, aggressive, and untouchable.
