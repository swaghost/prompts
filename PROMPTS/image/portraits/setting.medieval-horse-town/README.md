## Platform and Workflow

- **Image generation:** GPT Image 2
- **Workflow:** Picsart Flow image-to-image
- **Image 1:** Character reference only: face, hair, jewellery, wardrobe
- **Image 2:** Master frame: world, location, light, colour grade
- **Format:** Use the project's portrait format; preserve the source composition

## Two-Reference Method

1. Create a clean character reference sheet as Image 1.
2. Generate Scene 1 first. It becomes Image 2, the master world frame.
3. For every later scene, load both Image 1 and Image 2.
4. Keep the character lock, scene lock, grade lock, and pose lock in every prompt.
5. Change only the camera position, scene action, and physical pose required by that scene.

## Camera Vocabulary

- **Wide shot:** Back and slightly up; full location visible.
- **Close-up:** Tight on face and neck; background soft.
- **Three-quarter front:** 45 degrees off the front.
- **Over the shoulder:** Behind the character, looking into the scene.
- **Low angle:** Knee height, tilted upward.
- **Cowboy shot:** Knees to head.
- **Extreme wide:** Far back and raised; character small in landscape.
- **Dutch angle:** Horizon tilted approximately 20 degrees.
- **High angle:** Raised above, tilted down.

## Continuity Notes

- Keep the same 24-year-old woman, ash-blonde hair with darker roots, blue butterfly clip, grey-blue eyes, freckles, jewellery, lavender crushed-velour outfit, white bralette, socks, trainers, and mud state wherever specified.
- Keep the same medieval northern country, materials, weather, hour, flat overcast exposure, and cold naturalistic grade.
- The later scenes are not new worlds. They are alternate camera positions and poses inside the master frame's world.
