# Blue Dome - Salt & Linen

## Description of Resulting Image

A rear-view full-body Mediterranean portrait of a man walking whitewashed steps toward the sea, his pale blue linen shirt billowing above cream trousers and a blue-domed chapel.

## Usage

Perfect for Aegean resort campaigns, coastal menswear editorials, travel magazines, and cinematic lifestyle imagery. Ideal for open composition, movement, sea light, and architectural color contrast.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux, or comparable image models.
