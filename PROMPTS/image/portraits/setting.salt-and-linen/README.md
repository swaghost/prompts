# Salt & Linen - Mediterranean Menswear Editorial

Eight standalone summer menswear prompts from the Salt & Linen collection. The set uses hard Mediterranean sunlight, open linen shirts, warm sun-bleached colors, medium-format film texture, and relaxed editorial poses.

## Prompts

1. [The Doorway](01-the-doorway.md) - Limestone doorway and black painted door
2. [Palm Shadow](02-palm-shadow.md) - Graphic palm shadow on white stucco
3. [First Coffee](03-first-coffee.md) - Morning espresso on an iron balcony
4. [Terrace Table](04-terrace-table.md) - Shaded café terrace above the sea
5. [Blue Dome](05-blue-dome.md) - Whitewashed steps descending to the Aegean
6. [Half Light](06-half-light.md) - Split sunlight across a close portrait
7. [Shutter Lines](07-shutter-lines.md) - Slatted shutter shadows and silver chain
8. [Golden Hour](08-golden-hour.md) - Backlit shoreline portrait

## Shared Direction

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Style:** Mediterranean menswear, editorial fashion photography
- **Camera:** Medium-format film aesthetic
- **Light:** Natural Mediterranean sunlight, hard sun unless scene specifies otherwise
- **Grade:** Warm sun-bleached palette with soft organic grain
- **Source:** PROMPTS.SOUL, Salt & Linen, Vol. 001
