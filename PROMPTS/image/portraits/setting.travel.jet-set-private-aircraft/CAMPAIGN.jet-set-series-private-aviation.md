## Usage Guidelines

### For AI Image Generation
1. Copy complete prompt from individual scene file
2. Use Higgsfield Soul or Seedance 2.0
3. Maintain 85mm f/1.8 lens specifications
4. Preserve shallow depth of field
5. Keep slight film grain texture
6. Honor time-of-day lighting characteristics

### For Campaign Adaptation
- Scenes work as cohesive 7-image luxury lifestyle series
- Can be split by time of day (golden hour, midday, dusk, blue hour)
- Can be split by wardrobe (formal, corporate, casual, streetwear)
- Maintain private aviation theme across all adaptations

### For Social Media
- Vertical frames Instagram/Pinterest ready
- High-contrast midday shot performs well
- Blue hour and golden hour shots create cinematic impact
- Streetwear shot appeals to younger luxury demographic

## Production Notes

### Platform Optimization
Tested and optimized for **Higgsfield Soul** and **Seedance 2.0** — ensure platform compatibility

### AI Realism Philosophy
"No camera. No set. No crew." — All elements are AI-generated including:
- Aircraft (helicopters and private jets)
- Tarmac and hangar environments
- Dubai skyline and Burj Khalifa
- Models and supporting cast
- Lighting and atmospheric effects
- All wardrobe and accessories

### Critical Success Factors
1. Maintain cinematic realism (not illustration or cartoon)
2. Shallow depth of field for editorial feel
3. Time-of-day lighting accuracy (golden hour vs. blue hour)
4. Slight film grain for photographic authenticity
5. Clean backgrounds without clutter
6. Natural skin rendering (not plastic or AI-smooth)
