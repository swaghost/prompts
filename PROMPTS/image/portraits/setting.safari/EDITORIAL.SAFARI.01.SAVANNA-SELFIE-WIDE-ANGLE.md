## Image Prompt

A close, arm's-length selfie of a young adult woman with shoulder-length wavy brown hair, light hazel-green eyes, light freckled skin, and minimal makeup, photographed with a wide-angle front-facing phone camera held at arm's length so her outstretched arms and legs dominate the foreground with strong fisheye-like forced perspective. She wears an oversized cream/off-white button-up shirt with rolled cuffs, olive-green cargo trousers, a tan crossbody bag with a brass buckle strap across her chest, and cream-and-black lace-up boots. She kneels or crouches in a field of tall dry golden savanna grass with patches of green, low rolling hills and scattered flat-topped umbrella acacia trees on the horizon, under an overcast pale grey sky. Soft, even diffused daylight with no hard shadows. Neutral, slightly deadpan expression, direct eye contact with the lens. Muted, naturalistic color grade with warm khaki and cream tones; candid travel-selfie aesthetic; shot on a smartphone ultra-wide lens; slight barrel distortion; vertical 4:5 aspect ratio.

Suggested settings: Lens: ultra-wide/front-facing phone camera, approximately 18-20mm equivalent. Aspect: 3:4 vertical. Light: overcast daylight, soft/diffused. Style: candid selfie, travel/safari.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Avoid harsh sunlight, studio lighting, saturated colors, fantasy safari styling, extra limbs, distorted hands beyond natural wide-angle perspective, warped boots, duplicated trees, city elements, modern buildings, aggressive beauty retouching, CGI look, painterly style, text, logos, and watermarks.
