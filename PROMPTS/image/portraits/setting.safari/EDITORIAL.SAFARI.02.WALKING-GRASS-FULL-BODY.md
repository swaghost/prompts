## Image Prompt

A full-body fashion editorial photograph of a young adult woman with long, straight dark brown hair blown sideways by wind, mid-stride walking through a field of tall golden dry grass. She wears a cream long-sleeve button-up shirt open at the collar with a thin gold necklace visible, a brown leather belt, loose olive-green cargo trousers, a tan/beige structured crossbody bag with a front flap pocket and adjustable brown strap worn across her chest, and tan lace-up hiking boots. Her gaze is directed downward and to the side, calm and composed expression. In the background, a vintage open-top olive-green safari Land Rover is parked on a dirt track beside a solitary flat-crowned umbrella acacia tree on a gently rolling hill, beneath a hazy, overcast white-grey sky. Muted, desaturated earth-tone color grading: khaki, cream, olive, dusty brown, with a soft filmic contrast. Medium-telephoto compression, shallow-to-moderate depth of field keeping the model sharp against a slightly softer background. Vertical portrait orientation, high-end travel/fashion campaign style.

Suggested settings: Lens: medium telephoto, approximately 85mm equivalent, moderate compression. Aspect: 4:5 vertical. Light: overcast, flat diffused. Style: high-fashion safari editorial, film grain.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Avoid harsh direct sun, neon colors, city background, modern SUV replacement, distorted vehicle geometry, extra people, unnatural walking pose, warped hands, excessive blur, oversaturated greens, fantasy landscape, CGI look, text, logos, and watermarks.
