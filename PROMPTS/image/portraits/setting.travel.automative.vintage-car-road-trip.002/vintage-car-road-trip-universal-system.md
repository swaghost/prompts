## Generation Tips

### For Best Results

1. Use the complete universal master prompt
2. Swap only the [SCENE] section
3. Keep CFG low (3.5-5.0) for natural results
4. Enable maximum film grain
5. DO NOT upload reference images (text prompt only)
6. Use 3:4 for single subject, 4:5 for groups
7. Adjust warmth based on scene lighting

### Common Issues

- **Too smooth skin?** Lower CFG further, emphasize SKIN section
- **AI-looking?** Increase film grain, emphasize "non-AI appearance"
- **Wrong ethnicity?** Strengthen NEGATIVE prompt section
- **Digital look?** Emphasize "analog film," "Kodak Portra 400," "RAW photograph"
