# Prompt 08: Scooter Mirror, Lipstick

**HARD MIDDAY SUN · OLD-TOWN STREET**

---

A young woman, mid-20s, fair skin lightly sun-warmed, dark brown chin-length blunt bob tucked behind one ear, glossy and straight. Narrow black rectangular cat-eye sunglasses. A large pearl and silver statement earring in the visible ear.

Expression: lips parted and slightly pushed forward mid-application, chin lifted, eyes on her own reflection in the scooter mirror. Focused, entirely absorbed in the task.

Pose: leaning forward over the parked scooter, weight on the balls of her feet, torso bent in toward the handlebars. Right hand raised holding a lipstick bullet to her lower lip. Left forearm resting on the handlebar beside the basket.

Details: a dark berry-red lipstick in a slim black tube. A fine gold chain necklace with a tiny pendant. A woven straw basket bag with rope handles hooked over the scooter's front hook, sitting against the leg shield.

Outfit: a white ribbed cropped tank top, slightly sheer, hem sitting above the waist with the midriff exposed. A long brown chiffon maxi skirt printed with large pale blue polka dots, sitting low on the hips, fabric flowing to the ankles.

Setting: a narrow sunlit Mediterranean old-town street. A bright yellow vintage Italian scooter parked hard against a rough golden sandstone rubble wall on the right, its round chrome mirror on a stalk directly in front of her face, chrome headlight and horn casing, ribbed side vents and a small sticker on the leg shield. To the left, an old stone townhouse with a terracotta tiled roof, green louvred shutters, an arched wooden door with the number 16 beside it, and terracotta pots of plants mounted on the wall. Further down the street: a grey hatchback parked, more cars, a hillside village and green wooded hills, deep blue sky.

Shot angle: camera at chest height on the street a couple of steps back, framing her from mid-shin up with the scooter dominating the right of the frame.

Lighting: hard bright midday Mediterranean sun from the upper left - brilliant highlights on the yellow bodywork, chrome mirror and white tank, crisp defined shadows across the stone wall and the street, warm golden bounce off the sandstone. Saturated and high contrast.

Sharp phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic skin and paintwork detail. Photorealistic.
