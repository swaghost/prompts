## Complete Prompt

A white male, early-to-mid 20s, deeply tanned olive skin with a warm sun-flushed sheen across the nose, cheeks and shoulders, a few small dark moles scattered on the cheek and chest. Dark brown-black hair in tight coiled ringlets, wet from the sea, some curls springing loose and standing up, a few strands catching the sun and flaring gold. Sparse stubble across the upper lip and jaw with a distinct small dark soul patch and chin tuft below the lower lip. Full lips, wide jaw.

Expression: broad open smile aimed straight at the lens, upper teeth fully showing and a hint of the lower row, cheeks pushed up, eyes crinkled slightly behind the lenses. Warm, unguarded, mid-laugh.

Pose: extreme close arm's-length selfie. His left arm reaches up and over the top of his head, hand plunged into the wet curls and pushing them back, fingers spread and visible among the hair. The bare tanned forearm crosses the entire left side of the frame in the near foreground, slightly out of focus, beaded with water droplets and a dusting of sand. Shirtless - bare shoulders, collarbones and upper chest in frame.

Accessories: thick black square acetate sunglasses, chunky rims with small gold rivet studs at the outer corners of each lens, fitted with warm amber-orange tinted lenses through which his brown eyes are clearly visible. A single fine delicate silver chain around the neck, sitting loose on the collarbone. A large solid dark tattoo on the chest, cropped at the lower edge of the frame.

Setting: directly beneath a large raffia straw parasol. The open woven mesh canopy fills the top of the frame with visible weave holes and daylight glowing through it, thin wire spokes radiating outward, ragged straw fringe hanging loose along the edge, a smooth pale wooden centre pole on the right. Beyond it: vivid saturated blue sky in the upper left, a tall ochre and cream sandstone cliff with scrub vegetation on the left, and below it a blurred beach with an out-of-focus sunbather and a dark polka-dot towel.

Shot angle: camera held high and close, tilted down toward his face. Head and the raised arm dominate the frame, sky and cliff visible past his shoulder.

Lighting: hard high midday sun from the upper left - brilliant specular highlights on the wet curls, forehead, nose and shoulders, glossy catchlight on the lips, deep contrast against the shaded skin beneath the parasol, dappled light spilling through the straw weave onto his hair and forehead.

Sharp oversaturated phone-selfie capture, high contrast, natural skin texture with visible pores and sun sheen. Photorealistic.
