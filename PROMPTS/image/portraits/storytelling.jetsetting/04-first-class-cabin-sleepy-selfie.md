# Prompt 04: First Class Cabin, Sleepy Selfie

**WIDE-LENS SELFIE · WARM CABIN LIGHT**

---

A young East Asian woman, mid-20s, fair skin with a smooth matte finish, straight black hair falling either side of the face beneath a hood. Chunky black acetate cat-eye glasses with thick rims.

Expression: eyes fully closed behind the lenses, lips pushed into a soft sleepy pout, brows relaxed. Completely drained, mid-flight, unbothered.

Pose: reclined into the seat, head tilted slightly, left hand brought up to rest against her cheek with the index finger extended up along the temple. Right arm outstretched toward the camera holding the phone for the selfie, forearm large in the lower foreground.

Details: a large solitaire diamond ring on the raised hand. A stack of polished gold bangles on the outstretched wrist. Fine dark script lettering tattooed across the back of the raised hand and a small tattoo on the outstretched forearm.

Outfit: a lime-green hoodie with the hood pulled up over the head, worn under an oversized grey wool blazer with a notched lapel and buttoned cuffs. A yellow tee visible at the chest beneath the open hoodie zip. Black trousers.

Details on the table: a burl-wood side table holding a green leather top-handle bag with silver hardware, a fine silver chain draped over it and a small white plush cat charm with a red heart clipped to the handle; a brushed metal case; a sealed water bottle; a wrapped amenity item.

Setting: the cabin of a first-class aircraft suite. A wide cream-grey leather seat with a white headrest cover behind her. Ivory moulded cabin walls and overhead panels. An oval window to the left with a translucent golden-tan shade drawn, glowing warm. Another suite visible ahead down the aisle with a green bag and a white blanket.

Shot angle: outstretched-arm selfie from slightly above and to her right, wide phone lens, cabin curving away behind her.

Lighting: warm low cabin lighting with a strong golden glow from the shaded window on the left - soft warm falloff across the face and blazer, gentle shadow under the raised hand, calm and even. Wide-lens perspective stretch toward the frame edges.

Sharp phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic skin and leather texture. Photorealistic.
