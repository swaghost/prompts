# Prompt 10: Car Window, Iced Coffee

**SHOT FROM INSIDE THE CAR · BRIGHT DAYLIGHT**

---

A young woman, early-to-mid 20s, fair skin with a warm glow, dark blonde hair pulled back into a low ponytail with fine wispy strands loose around the face. Softly defined brows, glossy nude-pink lips, subtle bronzed makeup.

Expression: closed-lip smile with the cheeks lifted, chin slightly tucked, eyes looking straight into the lens over the top of the sunglasses. Warm, playful, caught mid-greeting.

Pose: leaning in through the open passenger window of a car, torso bent forward, left forearm resting along the door frame. Right hand raised to her brow, fingers pressing the sunglasses up against the forehead, palm turned out.

Details: narrow black oval sunglasses pushed up at the brow. Very long pointed nails in a glossy milky nude. A long fine gold chain necklace with a large ornate gold cross pendant hanging at the chest. A slim gold ring, small gold hoop earrings. In the left hand, a clear plastic iced coffee cup with a domed lid, black straw and pale caramel-coloured coffee, a small teal logo mark printed on the side.

Outfit: a leopard-print sheer mesh long-sleeve top worn off the shoulders, both shoulders bare. Light blue denim visible at the waist. A black leather jacket slung over the forearm resting on the door.

Setting: shot from inside a car looking out through the open passenger window. Grey fabric headliner and sun visor across the top of the frame, black window frame and door ledge in the foreground, the wing mirror at the far left. Outside: a cream stucco building wall with a small wall light, a neatly trimmed green shrub, pale concrete driveway and the roof of another parked car at the left edge.

Shot angle: camera from the driver's seat at seated eye level, framing her head, shoulders and forearm through the window opening, interior edges framing all four sides.

Lighting: bright soft daylight outside contrasting with the shaded car interior - even clean light on her face and the mesh top, cool shadow across the door frame and headliner in the foreground, gentle highlight on the cup and the gold pendant.

Sharp phone capture, clean and noise-free, hyper-realistic mesh and skin texture. Photorealistic.
