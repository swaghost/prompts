A white male, early 30s, olive Mediterranean skin with visible natural texture - faint forehead lines pulled into a frown, light pores, a small dark mole on the cheek. Photographed from below in a close arm's-length selfie.

Hair: black-brown, cut short and tight at the sides and back, left long and messy on top - thick, slightly greasy, blown upward and sideways into loose spiky strands by the wind, a few separated pieces standing clear against the sky.

Face: strong straight nose, sharp cheekbones, lean jaw. A thin dark mustache along the upper lip, a small pointed soul patch beneath the lower lip and a narrow chin strip of stubble running down to the jawline, with light stubble across the cheeks and neck. Brow visibly furrowed above the frames.

Expression: mouth closed and set around a lit cigarette held loosely at the centre of the lips, white paper and glowing orange tip clearly visible, angled slightly downward, a faint wisp of smoke. Head tipped a fraction back, chin lifted, looking straight into the lens. Unimpressed, hard, entirely unbothered.

Accessories: black acetate sunglasses in a squared Wayfarer shape, thick glossy rims, dark lenses filled with a mirrored reflection of the pale building facade and bright sky. A small gold hoop earring in each ear, catching light. Fine gold chain links just visible at the open collar.

Outfit: black leather biker jacket with a wide notched collar and thick lapels, soft creased grain, worn open, the collar standing away from the neck. Beneath it a deep burgundy-maroon ribbed knit top with a high standing collar and a quarter-zip opening left undone at the chest. A sliver of white printed tee visible in the gap at the bottom of the frame.

Setting: a residential street in Milan. A tall cream, pale-grey and blue-grey stucco apartment facade fills the whole background, running from the lower right up to the top left - ornate stone window surrounds, small wrought-iron balconies with slim railings, projecting cornices, a crenellated roofline against the sky. Some windows glow warm amber from within, others reflect cold blue daylight. A blank overexposed white sky in the top left corner and along the upper edge.

Shot angle: selfie held low and close, camera below chin level pointing steeply up, so the face is large and slightly foreshortened and the building towers and converges behind him. Strong tilt - the facade running hard diagonally across the frame. Head and upper chest fill the lower two-thirds.

Lighting: flat bright overcast daylight, soft and even on the face with no hard shadow, sky completely blown out. Cool grey-blue cast across the stonework, neutral warm skin tones, glossy highlights on the leather and the sunglass lenses.

Handheld phone selfie - soft motion blur through the building edges and the frame corners, mild wide-lens distortion, slight compression softness in the background, sharpest on the face. Photorealistic.
