## Complete Prompt

A white male, early 20s, light skin, dark brown curly hair pushed up under a backwards washed-denim baseball cap. Light stubble.

Expression: face scrunched playfully, one eye winked fully shut, the other open and looking into the lens, mouth open around a straw with teeth showing. Goofy, mid-joke, completely unbothered.

Pose: reclined deep into the back seat, both arms raised with hands resting on top of his own head, elbows out wide. Legs spread open, one knee lifted toward the front seat.

Outfit: white fitted short-sleeve tee with bold red block serif text across the chest in two stacked lines reading "WORLD'S BEST EX". Light grey washed straight-leg jeans. White and grey New Balance sneakers, one foot visible in the lower frame. Thin black cord bracelet on one wrist. Small beaded necklace at the throat, mostly hidden by the collar.

Prop: large Pepsi fountain cup - blue with the red-white-blue circle logo - clear straw, held near his mouth in one raised hand.

Setting: rear seat of a car at night. Grey fabric headliner filling the top of the frame, plastic grab handle, ceiling vent, dark grey upholstered front headrests intruding into both foreground corners. Through the windows: black night with blurred amber and white city lights.

Lighting: harsh direct on-camera flash. Hot blown highlights on forehead, knuckles and the cup, rapid falloff into deep black behind the seat, everything past the glass dropping to darkness. Hard specular sheen on the headliner fabric. Slight red-eye warmth.

Shot angle: camera from the front passenger area shooting back between the seats, slightly above his eyeline.

Candid phone-flash snapshot. Photorealistic.
