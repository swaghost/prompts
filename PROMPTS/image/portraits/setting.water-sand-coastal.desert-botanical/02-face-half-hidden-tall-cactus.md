# IMAGE 2 — Face Half Hidden Behind Tall Cactus

Hyper-realistic vertical botanical fashion portrait of the same adult woman standing outdoors beside an old sun-weathered cream plaster wall. She has a short tousled black-brown bob haircut with delicate bangs, hazel-green eyes, freckled natural skin, thin brows, understated lips and almost no makeup.

A huge vertical blue-green columnar cactus dominates the entire left half of the foreground, extremely close to the camera. Long golden-brown cactus spines project outward in crisp detail. The cactus completely hides approximately half of the woman's face, leaving only one eye, half of her nose, one side of her lips, cheek and dark bob haircut visible on the right side.

She wears a lightweight vintage ivory blouse with soft embroidered floral details, subtle wrinkles and a loose V neckline. Her shoulders remain relaxed and still. Expression is quiet, mysterious, slightly distant; her visible eye looks directly into the lens.

Strong low-angle golden sunlight from frame-left hits her exposed face, creating sculptural highlights on the cheekbone, nose, eye and collarbone while the cactus creates irregular shadows across her skin. Background consists of an old cream wall with faded stains, rough mortar, visible stones and strong geometric shadow shapes.

Close portrait crop from upper chest to head, 70–85mm lens, shallow-but-not-extreme depth of field. Keep both cactus needles and exposed eye sharply readable while background falls gently softer.

Extremely realistic pores, freckles, tiny facial hairs, slight skin oil and natural sun texture. Hair should contain flyaways and imperfect strands. Cactus must look botanically accurate, dusty, scarred and naturally aged.

Earthy cinematic color palette of sage green, dusty cream, warm brown and muted gold. Analog film texture, slight grain, gentle highlight roll-off, documentary editorial photography, Mediterranean botanical campaign aesthetic.

No glamour retouching, no perfect skin, no artificial bokeh, no overly polished fashion lighting, no CGI, no text, no watermark.
