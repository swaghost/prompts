# Prompt 2: Dynamic Sand Arch on Bright Beach

An energetic daylight editorial action photo of a sun-kissed woman kneeling on a sandy shore and throwing a sweeping arc of sand high into the air. High shutter speed captures individual airborne sand grains and dust suspended mid-flight. The image must feel like a genuine commercial campaign photograph with organic textures, realistic sun reflections, and authentic motion freeze.

---

## OVERALL FEEL

- Joyful, dynamic, sun-drenched, high-energy beach campaign
- Real action photograph aesthetic with crisp high-speed shutter realism

## SUBJECT

- Woman in her mid-20s, toned athletic build, sun-tanned skin with soft dusting of dry sand on arms and thighs
- Profile view looking upward into the sky, jawline defined, mouth slightly open in an ecstatic laugh

## EMOTIONS / EXPRESSIONS

- Pure joy, playful exhilaration, carefree summer spirit

## REALISM-CRITICAL HUMAN DETAILS

- Authentic skin pore texture, natural freckles, light muscle tone visible in core and legs, fine sand particles stuck to damp skin on shins and wrists

## HAIR

- Long wavy sun-lightened brown hair flowing down her back, catching bright sunlight with natural flyaway strands and textured volume

## POSE / FRAMING

- Full-body vertical shot, woman kneeling on golden sand in side profile
- Back arched backwards, arms swept overhead in a powerful upward arc flinging a huge spray of coarse sand that forms a dramatic curving arch over her body

## WARDROBE & ACCESSORIES

- Earthy taupe-brown minimalist string bikini top and high-cut cheek bikini bottoms
- Sleek silver metallic bangle bracelet on upper wrist; a white sunscreen bottle sits upright in the sand behind her

## BACKGROUND

- Soft beige sandy beach fading into clear turquoise-blue shallow sea water, bright high-noon summer sky with wispy clouds

## LIGHTING

- Bright direct overhead summer daylight, crisp directional sunlight casting short realistic shadows on sand

## COLOR PALETTE

- Warm sand tones, bright turquoise water, sun-bleached whites, warm taupe, bronze skin tones

## CAMERA / PHOTOGRAPHIC FEEL

- Shot on Hasselblad H6D-100c with 85mm lens, 1/4000s ultra-fast shutter speed freezing sand particles sharply
- Rich midtone detail, razor-sharp focus on the subject and suspended sand arc

## TEXTURE / MATERIAL REALISM

- **skin:** natural tan sheen, realistic sand grain texture clinging to skin
- **hair:** textured, wind-swept waves with individual lit strands
- **wardrobe:** subtle ribbed swim fabric texture, realistic fabric tension from pose
- **background:** gritty sand texture with small shell fragments, gentle water foam ripples

## STYLE

- Luxury sun care / swimwear commercial photography

## HEX VALUES

["#F5F0E6", "#E8DBCA", "#D3BBA0", "#AF9070", "#896749", "#5F4229", "#3A2616", "#C5B29B", "#8CA6A2", "#B8D4D0", "#E4EDEA", "#A68668", "#745437", "#442D1C", "#1E120B"]

## AVOID

- Blurry sand arc, cartoon effect, airbrushed skin, smoothed sand, CGI dust clouds, unnatural joint bending, extra limbs, stock photo look, oversaturated colors, text, watermark

**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16
