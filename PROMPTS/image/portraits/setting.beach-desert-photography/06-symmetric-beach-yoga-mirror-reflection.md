# Prompt 6: Symmetric Beach Yoga Mirror Reflection at Twilight

A high-fashion wellness editorial photograph of two athletic women performing a perfectly synchronized shoulder-stand yoga pose head-to-head on a wet sandy beach at twilight. Their raised legs meet at the feet, forming a geometric triangle against a pastel pink and blue sky, with a flawless mirror reflection on the wet sand floor creating a complete symmetrical diamond composition. The photograph must feel like a genuine analog film photo captured in-camera, with realistic skin textures, authentic water reflections, and natural optical depth.

---

- Serene, symmetrical, artistic, peaceful, minimalist wellness aesthetic
- Authentic medium-format film look, soft organic grain, zero digital rendering or CGI polish

- Two women in their mid-to-late 20s, slim athletic builds, defined leg and core musculature
- Positioned lying on their shoulders on the wet sand floor, heads aligned close together in opposite directions

## EMOTIONS / EXPRESSIONS

## REALISM-CRITICAL HUMAN DETAILS

## HAIR

- Dark brunette shoulder-length hair lying flat and spread neatly on the damp sand surface beneath their heads

## POSE / FRAMING

- Full-length vertical 3:4 / 9:16 framing, perfectly centered symmetrical composition

- A flawless, mirror-like reflection in the thin film of tide water on the sand completes a symmetrical geometric diamond motif
