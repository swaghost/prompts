## Lighting Technique: Split Lighting

### What is Split Lighting?

Split lighting divides the face directly down the middle, with one side illuminated and the other in shadow. This creates a dramatic, mysterious, and intense portrait effect.

**Characteristics:**

- Face divided vertically in half
- One side bright, one side dark
- Strong contrast and drama
- Emphasizes facial structure
- Creates mood and intensity

**Rembrandt Influence:**

- Named after Dutch painter Rembrandt van Rijn
- Low-key lighting with high contrast
- Small triangle of light on shadow side cheek (Rembrandt triangle)
- Moody, dramatic atmosphere
- Classic portrait painting lighting

### Execution Details

**Key Light Position:**

- 90° to the side of subject
- At or slightly above eye level
- Creates sharp division down center of face

**Light Quality:**

- Warm golden tungsten (2700-3200K)
- Hard light for defined shadow edge
- Single source for clean split

**Shadow Side:**

- Fading into deep black
- No fill light (pure low-key lighting)
- Maintains drama and contrast
