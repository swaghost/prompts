## Shared Guidance

- Use the attached reference image as the identity reference when identity preservation is required.
- Preserve natural skin texture, realistic hair and fabric behaviour, believable water interaction, and accurate camera perspective.
- Treat brand names, logos, and printed graphics as optional only when the reference or project has the right to use them; otherwise replace them with original equivalents.
- Keep each prompt's camera, lighting, wardrobe, environment, and mood together when recreating its specific visual.
