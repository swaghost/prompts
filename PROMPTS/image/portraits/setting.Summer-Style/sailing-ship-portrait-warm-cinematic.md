# Sailing Ship Portrait - Warm Cinematic Style

Create a realistic vertical portrait in 9:16 format, in a warm cinematic style with a woman from the provided identity reference on a moving sailing ship. The composition is the most important requirement: perspective from a low angle from the deck, the subject lies diagonally in the lower half with his legs extending beyond the lower left edge, the body is thrown back, supported by a boom, a large white sail stretched behind her as a monumental fabric background, and the stormy dark ocean space visible below the horizontal boat rail.

## Lighting Design

The lighting design should be the warm sun just before the golden hour, strong enough to cut through the body, but still natural, with shining ivory reflections on the sail, soft gray sail shadows and the dark shimmering texture of the sea. The strongest visual contrast should be shown in the texture of the person in the lower right corner against the background of a bright white sail and pale clothes. She has very tanned skin, perfectly highlighted with a shimmer.

## Posture and Expression

Posture and expression: numb immobility from sun intoxication, head thrown back, eyes closed, lips relaxed, one elbow lightly touching the waistline and shirt fabric, one elbow slightly bent at the railing, shoulders free. The pose should feel like a spontaneous fashion moment, not like a staged emotional swimsuit shot.

## Clothes

- Oversized-a pale blue shirt, very wide and soft, blowing in the wind, hanging from the shoulders and forearms
- Bare feet, without brand tags
- Without large jewelry
- Hair should have realistic stray strands, soft, shiny, blowing in the wind

## Filter and Camera View

Realistic editorial style of 35mm film, warm, grainy noise, creamy highlights, deep desaturated blue tones, slight softness of the lens, faint gall on the sunlit canvas, real skin texture. No text, no watermark, no unnecessary people.
