# Ultra-Realistic Glamour Portrait Template

## Overview

This template creates hyper-realistic, editorial-quality glamour portraits that achieve international fashion magazine standards (Vogue, Dior, Harper's Bazaar, Saint Laurent). The system ensures the subject is immediately recognizable while maintaining absolute photographic realism with zero AI/CGI appearance.

**Key Philosophy:** Use reference image ONLY for identity (face, proportions, features). Everything else (pose, lighting, setting, styling) is completely original and professionally directed.

---

## Core Technical Specifications

### Camera & Format

```
Format: Vertical 4:5 ratio
Camera: Canon R5 with RF 85mm f/1.2L lens
Quality: Editorial-grade (Vogue Beauty, Dior, Saint Laurent, Harper's Bazaar standards)
Resolution: International magazine cover quality
File Type: RAW equivalent realism
```

### Identity Preservation (From Reference Photo)

**Preserve with Absolute Fidelity:**

- ✅ Facial structure and bone structure
- ✅ Eye shape, color, and characteristics
- ✅ Nose shape and proportions
- ✅ Mouth shape and lip characteristics
- ✅ Facial proportions and ratios
- ✅ Skin tone, undertones, and natural coloring
- ✅ Hair color, texture, and natural characteristics
- ✅ Apparent age and maturity

**Do NOT Copy from Reference:**

- ❌ Pose or body position
- ❌ Clothing or styling
- ❌ Lighting setup
- ❌ Background or setting
- ❌ Camera angle from selfie

**Result Standard:**
The subject must be immediately recognizable as the exact same person from the reference photo, without any appearance of AI, CGI, or cartoon rendering.

## Usage Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare Reference Image

Upload a clear, well-lit selfie or portrait that shows:

- Full face clearly visible
- Good lighting showing skin tone accurately
- Natural expression
- Hair visible and in focus
- Frontal or near-frontal angle preferred

### Step 2: Choose Template Options

Go through each section and select:

- Composition style (A, B, or C)
- Expression type
- Hair styling
- Outfit option
- Makeup style
- Lighting setup
- Background/scenario
- Compositional feeling

### Step 3: Customize Details

Fill in specific customizations:

- Exact clothing descriptions
- Specific jewelry details
- Precise color specifications
- Any unique elements

### Step 4: Assemble Final Prompt

Combine:

1. Base Prompt (Section 1)
2. Your chosen options from Sections 2-13
3. Add any final custom details

### Step 5: Generate & Refine

- Generate initial image
- Assess realism and identity preservation
- Refine specific elements if needed
- Iterate until achieving magazine-quality result

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Issue:** Subject doesn't look like reference

- **Solution:** Emphasize identity preservation more strongly at start of prompt
- Ensure reference photo is clear, well-lit, frontal angle
- Try generating multiple times for better match

**Issue:** Too much AI/CGI appearance

- **Solution:** Strengthen "ultra skin" section with more realism emphasis
- Add multiple "zero AI appearance" statements
- Reference specific photography equipment and standards
- Include "physically accurate" and "optical depth of field"

**Issue:** Anatomical deformations (extra fingers, odd proportions)

- **Solution:** Explicitly state "zero anatomical deformations"
- Add detailed proportions section
- Use "anatomically perfect" for specific body parts
- Generate multiple times, select best result

**Issue:** Lighting too flat or unrealistic

- **Solution:** Be more specific about lighting setup
- Reference cinematographic or editorial lighting by name
- Describe shadow quality and catchlights explicitly
- Mention specific softbox/light modifier types

**Issue:** Background too busy or distracting

- **Solution:** Emphasize "minimalist" and "no objects"
- Request specific backdrop type (seamless, gradient, infinity)
- Add "discreetly blurred background" or "shallow depth of field"
