# Stained Glass Portrait Style

## Overview

Transform portrait photographs into luminous handcrafted stained glass artworks with Art Nouveau elegance—preserving the subject's recognizable identity while replacing every element with jewel-like glass panels, ornamental frames, and Gothic rose-window backgrounds.

**Style:** Premium editorial stained-glass identity portrait  
**Recommended Canvas:** 4:5 portrait orientation  
**Aesthetic:** Luminous handcrafted glass with dark lead came, botanical Gothic backgrounds, Art Nouveau ornamental frames

---

## Core Prompt

```
Transform the supplied 4:5 portrait canvas into a premium editorial stained-glass identity portrait. Preserve the supplied person's recognizable face, natural eyes, hairstyle, hair color, skin tone, age and expression. Replace every part of the canvas—including any blurred background—with luminous handcrafted stained glass: face, hair, clothing, radiant halo, botanical Gothic rose-window background and Art Nouveau ornamental frame, elegant dark lead came, jewel-like cobalt, turquoise, amber, ruby, emerald and violet glass, warm transmitted light. Centered chest-up symmetrical composition filling the canvas edge to edge. Attractive anatomically correct face; no text, logo, watermark, extra person, cracks or scars across facial features. Preserve the input image's native 4:5 canvas shape exactly.
```

## Comparison with Other Glass Styles

### vs. Divided Glass Effect

**Stained Glass Portrait:**

- Complete artistic transformation
- Subject becomes made of glass
- Ornamental frames and halos
- Religious/sacred art inspiration
- Preserves identity while transforming medium

**Divided Glass:**

- Fractured/shattered appearance
- Subject behind or within broken glass
- Typically no frame elements
- Modern conceptual aesthetic
- Less about transformation, more about distortion/fragmentation

---

### vs. Double Exposure

**Stained Glass Portrait:**

- Single cohesive glass artwork
- Material transformation (person → glass)
- Structured lead came boundaries
- Traditional craft aesthetic

**Double Exposure:**

- Two images blended/overlaid
- Transparency and overlap
- Surreal dreamlike quality
- Photography-based technique

---

### vs. Thermal Imaging

**Stained Glass Portrait:**

- Artistic glass medium
- Warm transmitted light
- Jewel tone color palette
- Handcrafted appearance

**Thermal:**

- Heat signature visualization
- False color temperature mapping
- Scientific/technical aesthetic
- Digital sensor simulation

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Face not recognizable after transformation

**Solution:**

- Emphasize in prompt: "preserve the supplied person's recognizable face"
- Add: "maintain clear facial features and identity"
- Use higher quality input image
- Ensure original photo has clear visible features

---

### Problem: Too abstract, loses portrait quality

**Solution:**

- Emphasize: "attractive anatomically correct face"
- Add: "photorealistic facial features rendered in glass"
- Specify: "natural eyes, clear expression"
- Balance artistic transformation with identity preservation

---

### Problem: Background doesn't look like rose window

**Solution:**

- Specify: "Gothic cathedral rose window background with circular geometric patterns"
- Add: "intricate botanical tracery in the style of Notre-Dame rose windows"
- Request: "radiating geometric design with floral motifs"

---

### Problem: Glass doesn't look luminous or backlit

**Solution:**

- Emphasize: "warm transmitted light glowing through glass"
- Add: "backlit stained glass with radiant luminosity"
- Specify: "light source behind glass creating glowing effect"
- Request: "translucent jewel-like glass with inner glow"

---

### Problem: Lead came looks too thin or absent

**Solution:**

- Emphasize: "elegant dark lead came defining all glass panels"
- Add: "thick black leading outlining each glass piece"
- Specify: "traditional stained glass construction with visible joints"

---

### Problem: Colors too muted or dull

**Solution:**

- Emphasize: "jewel-like" and list all color names
- Add: "deeply saturated vibrant glass colors"
- Specify: "rich cobalt blue, glowing ruby red, luminous emerald green"
- Request: "maximum color saturation and brilliance"

---

### Problem: Canvas aspect ratio changes

**Solution:**

- Explicitly state: "Preserve the input image's native 4:5 canvas shape exactly"
- Add: "do not crop, extend, or alter aspect ratio"
- Verify input image is actually 4:5 before processing

---

### Problem: Unwanted text or watermarks appear

**Solution:**

- Prompt already prohibits: "no text, logo, watermark"
- Add if needed: "absolutely no text elements of any kind"
- Specify: "pure visual artwork with no typography"

## Pro Tips

### For Best Results

1. **Start with quality input:**
   - Well-lit portrait
   - Clear facial features
   - Simple or neutral background
   - Centered composition
   - 4:5 aspect ratio if possible

2. **Emphasize identity preservation:**
   - Repeat subject description if needed
   - Mention specific features (glasses, beard, hairstyle)
   - Request recognizable likeness

3. **Control glass complexity:**
   - More glass panels = more detailed
   - Larger panels = cleaner, bolder look
   - Lead came thickness affects style

4. **Balance transformation and recognition:**
   - Too abstract = loss of identity
   - Too realistic = doesn't look like glass
   - Find sweet spot in prompt emphasis

5. **Consider color harmony:**
   - Warm tones for inviting feel
   - Cool tones for serene atmosphere
   - Balanced palette for traditional look
