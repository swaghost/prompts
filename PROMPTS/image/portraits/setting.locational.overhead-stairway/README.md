## Shared Direction

- **Framing:** Full body head to toe
- **Camera:** Overhead or elevated directly-above perspective, wide flat lens
- **Finish:** Film grain, realistic texture, luxury editorial photography
- **Default exclusions:** No text, no logo, no watermark
- **Visual focus:** Architecture, wardrobe silhouette, graphic floor or stair geometry, and directional light
