## Core Concept

Ultra-realistic CCTV/security camera still frame in a building hallway featuring ceiling corner mount view with high angle 60-70° top-down perspective and wide 2.8-3.6mm fisheye barrel distortion with edge warping. Subject centered under camera looking up directly into lens with cool unbothered expression, one hand adjusting waistband, other holding bag strap or hanging relaxed. Outfit: oversized graphic print T-shirt (generic print, no readable text/logos), wide baggy jeans, Adidas Campus sneakers (recognizable silhouette), chunky chain necklace. Face is sharpest point.
