**Problem:** Chiaroscuro effect weak, lighting looks too even or soft.

**Solutions:**

- Strengthen contrast language: "VERY high contrast, deep TRUE blacks, bright sculpted highlights"
- Emphasize: "single HARD directional key light, NO fill light, NO reflectors"

- Reference specific style: "Rembrandt lighting, Renaissance chiaroscuro painting style, dramatic masculine lighting"

**Problem:** Skin appears airbrushed, has shine/glow, or looks too perfect.

**Solutions:**
