## Core Concept

A top-down overhead aerial portrait of an adult man seated in the driver's seat of an open-top vintage British Racing Green Shelby Cobra convertible, photographed from directly above. The man wears an unbuttoned white shirt and dark blue jeans, one hand resting on the steering wheel, the other on his thigh. His face is tilted upward toward the camera with a confident, relaxed smile. The open cockpit, tan leather racing seats, chrome gauges, and sweeping lines of the car's body create bold graphic shapes filling the frame.
