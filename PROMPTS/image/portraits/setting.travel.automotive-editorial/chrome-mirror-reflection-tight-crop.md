# Chrome Side Mirror Reflection - Straight-On Tight Crop Portrait

## Prompt Type

Automotive Editorial Photography - Chrome Mirror Reflection Close-Up Portrait

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

A straight-on, tight-crop editorial portrait of an adult man's face and upper chest reflected in the polished chrome side mirror of a vintage sports car. The man wears sunglasses and an unbuttoned white shirt, and his expression is calm, confident, and effortlessly stylish. The chrome mirror dominates the frame in sharp focus while the background vehicle body and coastal road are softly out of focus.

## Detailed Prompt

```
Use the uploaded reference image strictly for facial identity only. Preserve only the facial identity with exceptional consistency. Generate an entirely original hairstyle, clothing, accessories, and styling appropriate for a vintage sports car chrome mirror editorial.

A straight-on, tight-crop editorial portrait of the same adult man's face and upper chest reflected in the polished chrome side mirror of a vintage sports car. The man wears sunglasses and an unbuttoned white shirt, and his expression is calm, confident, and effortlessly stylish. The chrome mirror dominates the frame in sharp focus while the background vehicle body and coastal road are softly out of focus. The mirror's curved surface subtly distorts the reflection, adding a cinematic, editorial quality. Golden-hour light warms the man's face and shirt, while the chrome reflects hints of British Racing Green paint and distant coastal scenery. The composition is bold, graphic, and fashion-forward, perfect for high-end automotive lifestyle campaigns.
```
