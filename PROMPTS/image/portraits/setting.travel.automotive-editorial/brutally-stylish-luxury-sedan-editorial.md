# Brutally Stylish High-Fashion Luxury Sedan Editorial

## Prompt Type

Automotive Editorial Photography - High-Fashion Night Portrait

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

A brutally stylish high-fashion editorial portrait of an adult man standing beside a luxury black sedan at night, photographed from an extreme oblique angle. The man is visible almost under the car, with the wheel and lower body dominating the foreground. He wears an all-white slightly unbuttoned shirt, black tailored trousers, and one foot with heel rests near the car door while his body leans toward above the camera with quiet, unflappable confidence.
