The ocean behind the subject should be softly out of focus with a natural horizon line and calm water extending into the distance, free of dramatic waves.

Capture the image as if photographed on a professional full-frame mirrorless camera using an 85mm lens at f/2.8, ISO 100, and 1/1000 second, producing ultra-high dynamic range, natural optical depth, true lens rendering, realistic color science, authentic skin tones, physically accurate reflections, and no CGI appearance.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: Face Doesn't Match Reference

**Solutions:**

- Strengthen identity lock language
- List more specific facial features to preserve
- Add "NO face alteration" multiple times
- Reduce beautification language entirely
- Emphasize "instantly recognizable"

### Issue: Water Looks Unrealistic

**Solutions:**

- Add more physics-based descriptions
- Specify "subtle" and "delicate" ripples
- Reduce drama (no big waves, splashes)
- Emphasize "physically accurate" behavior
- Mention refraction and optical distortion

### Issue: Skin Too Perfect/Smooth

**Solutions:**

- List more imperfection types
- Emphasize "NO beauty filters" explicitly
- Add texture descriptors (pores, peach fuzz, slight redness)
- Use "authentic" and "realistic" repeatedly
- Mention what to avoid (airbrushing, smoothing)

### Issue: Lighting Too Artificial

**Solutions:**

- Emphasize "natural sunlight, NOT studio"
- Describe light direction more specifically
- Mention time of day and quality
- Reference "wide dynamic range"
- Avoid "perfect" lighting language

### Issue: Background Too Sharp/Distracting

**Solutions:**

- Specify "softly out of focus"
- Mention f/2.8 aperture creating shallow depth of field
- Describe background as "blurred" or "bokeh"
- Emphasize focus on subject's face
- Reduce background detail descriptions

### Issue: Too Much CGI/AI Appearance

**Solutions:**

- Add "NO CGI appearance" explicitly
- Use photography-specific technical terms
- Reference "genuine in-camera" results
- Mention specific camera model/sensor characteristics
- Emphasize "indistinguishable from real photograph"
