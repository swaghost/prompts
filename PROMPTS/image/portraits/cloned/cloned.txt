# Cloned - Surreal Identity Replication

## Description of Resulting Image

A surreal, ultra-cinematic high-angle aerial photograph showing the same person standing in the center of a wide empty street while multiple identical copies walk in different directions, creating a striking "NPC crowd" effect with dreamlike atmosphere and motion blur.

## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL IDENTITY PRESERVATION:**
Upload your reference image and use it as the primary identity anchor. Create a surreal, ultra-cinematic photograph of clones featuring the SAME PERSON from the submitted reference image. CRITICAL FACE LOCK: Maintain EXACTLY THE SAME FACE as the submitted reference image. Preserve identical facial structure, jawline, hairstyle, beard style, skin tone, eyebrows, eye shape, nose shape, lips, hair texture, forehead proportions, and overall identity. Do NOT alter ethnicity or facial features. Keep the face highly realistic and consistent across all clones. Use the submitted image as the primary identity reference. The face must be an exact match to the real person in the reference photo.

**Subject:**
High-angle aerial shot showing the SAME PERSON from the submitted reference image standing in the center of a wide, empty street or path. The central character is perfectly sharp, striking a confident pose with hands in pockets, wearing a loose, vibrant {argument name="clothing color" default="WHITE"} hoodie, baggy black trousers, and white sneakers. Multiple copies of HIM walk in different directions, all wearing the same outfit and sharing the same face, creating a repetitive "NPC crowd" effect.

**Lighting:**

**Composition:**
High-angle aerial perspective with the central subject perfectly sharp in the center. Surrounding clones positioned at various depths with motion blur suggesting movement in different directions. Dynamic composition featuring compressed perspective and tilt-shift lens effect.
