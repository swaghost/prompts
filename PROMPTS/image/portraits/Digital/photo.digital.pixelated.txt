## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL:**
Transform the uploaded image into a high-contrast monochrome particle-render aesthetic.

**Subject:**
Subject reconstructed entirely from thousands of tiny glowing white dots and voxel-like particles on a pure black background. Use point-cloud dispersion, volumetric particles, halftone density gradients, and scattered noise trails to define the silhouette. Preserve the original subject composition and pose, but simplify details into clean geometric forms.

**Setting/Environment:**
Pure black background only. Cinematic negative space creating ultra minimal atmosphere.

**Visual Characteristics:**
- Black background only
- White particle clusters forming the subject
- Subtle floating debris and dissolving particles around edges
- Sparse orange/red accent particles (very minimal)
- Depth created through particle density
- Abstract but recognizable silhouette
- No realistic textures
- No colors besides black, white, and tiny orange accents

**Typography Treatment:**
Small thin mono-space typography placed minimally on left and right sides, similar to futuristic design posters. White text only, subtle and secondary to the artwork.

**Composition:**
Vertical poster layout with clean composition emphasizing negative space and particle architecture.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Futuristic AI architecture visualization mixed with experimental generative art aesthetic. Brutalist futuristic design with clean abstract minimalism.

**Technical Specifications:**
Octane render, point cloud simulation, particle system render, volumetric dispersion, procedural dots, cinematic lighting, ultra detailed rendering.

**Color Palette:**
Black, white, and tiny orange/red accents only. High contrast monochrome with minimal color.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Futuristic, experimental, minimalist, architectural, abstract, cutting-edge, technological.

**Quality Keywords:**
Ultra minimal, high contrast, octane render, point cloud simulation, volumetric particles, ultra detailed, abstract minimalism, generative AI art style, experimental architecture visualization.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Realistic shading, painterly textures, colorful backgrounds, low contrast, photorealism, messy composition, extra objects, blur, noisy artifacts, low resolution.
