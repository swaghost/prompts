# Rising - Out of the Blue

**Style:** Editorial, ocean exit

```
Create a photorealistic editorial portrait on a private yacht or large boat deck with pool or open-ocean access. The subject emerges from crystal-clear turquoise-teal water while gripping silver chrome pool-ladder handles. Use an overhead bird's-eye angle so the entire background is vivid aquamarine water under bright direct sunlight.

Young woman in her early to mid twenties with long dark brown hair wet and naturally pulled back, olive-tanned skin, almond-shaped dark eyes looking slightly upward and sideways at camera, strong cheekbones, defined jawline, lean athletic build, and a knowing half-smile.

She wears a dark chocolate-brown or black bandeau bikini with subtle texture, matching high-waist bottoms, small gold hoops, a delicate gold chain necklace, a pink festival wristband on the right wrist, and soft pink nail polish. Water droplets cover her shoulders and chest; makeup is minimal, waterproof, and natural.

Bright overhead sun and vivid aqua reflection illuminate the underside of the face and torso. Chrome ladder shadows fall sharply across the white boat deck. Shoot from approximately 60 degrees above with a wide 28mm-equivalent lens, saturated jewel-toned water, rich warm skin, high contrast, and crisp editorial detail. Mood: she surfaces like a headline; everything else is just water. 4:5 portrait.
```
