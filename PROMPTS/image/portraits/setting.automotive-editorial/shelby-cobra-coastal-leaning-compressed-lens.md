## Core Concept

A dramatic compressed long-lens editorial portrait of an adult man leaning casually against the side of a vintage British Racing Green Shelby Cobra convertible on a winding coastal two-lane road. The man wears a casually unbuttoned white shirt and dark blue jeans, arms crossed loosely over his chest, his expression calm and approachable. Shot with a 200mm or 300mm lens, the background coastal cliffs, distant mountains, and surf are compressed into soft, layered bokeh.
