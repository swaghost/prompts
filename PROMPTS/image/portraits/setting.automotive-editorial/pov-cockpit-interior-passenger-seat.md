## Prompt Type

Automotive Editorial Photography - POV Interior Cockpit Portrait

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

A POV medium shot photographed from the passenger seat of a vintage British Racing Green Shelby Cobra convertible, showing an adult man in the driver's seat. The man wears an unbuttoned white shirt and dark blue jeans, one hand on the steering wheel, his face turned slightly toward the camera with a warm, confident, inviting smile. The open-air cockpit, tan leather seats, chrome gauges, and dashboard details fill the foreground, while the winding coastal road and distant ocean are visible through the windshield.
