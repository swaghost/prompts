# Artistic Three-Frame Smoke Portrait Series

## Description of Resulting Image

Cinematic triptych composition divided into three horizontal frames stacked vertically, showing the same person in different poses and moments against an open sky with dramatic clouds, creating a visual narrative with soft cinematic lighting.

## Usage

Perfect for artistic portrait series, editorial storytelling campaigns, and narrative-driven photography. Ideal for magazine layouts, social media carousel posts, gallery exhibitions, and creative portfolio pieces. This prompt excels at creating poetic visual narratives with multiple moments captured in a unified composition.

**Subject:**
Using the attached photo as a facial reference, preserve the person's facial identity accurately and consistently across all three frames. The same person appears in different poses and moments, interacting with the wind and space. Movement and expression increase from top to bottom, creating a visual narrative progression.

**Setting/Environment:**
Open sky environment with dramatic clouds serving as the background across all three horizontal frames. Natural outdoor setting with space for subject to move and interact with the wind. Atmospheric conditions creating visual interest and mood.

**Lighting:**
Soft cinematic light with film still quality. Natural lighting that feels authentic and unforced. Gentle illumination that enhances the poetic and emotional tone. Consistent lighting treatment across all three frames to maintain visual unity.

**Composition:**
Create a cinematic composition divided into three horizontal frames stacked vertically. Each frame shows the same person in a different pose and moment. Movement increases from top to bottom, creating a visual narrative flow. Balanced composition within each frame while maintaining overall triptych harmony.
