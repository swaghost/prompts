# CAMPAIGN: Bella Italia — European Summer Series

## Campaign Overview

**Title:** Bella Italia  
**Series:** European Summer Series  
**Platform:** Higgsfield AI  
**Year:** 2026  
**Creator:** LUXRY.AI  
**Format:** AI Travel Fashion Editorial  

**Campaign Theme:** Seven hyper-realistic AI frames capturing luxury European summer across Lake Como, the Riviera, Amalfi, Portofino, Venice, Italian villas and French chateaux. Pearl white to crimson red. Pearls to Kelly bags.

## Campaign Statistics

- **Total Prompts:** 7 unique location-based prompts

- **Film Stock:** Kodak Portra 400 (all 7)
- **Model Diversity:** Southeast Asian, Fair European, South Asian/Latin, Eastern European, Mixed-complexion

## Mood Statements

Each prompt concludes with a signature mood line:

1. **Como:** "The view is extraordinary. She's seen better."
2. **Riviera:** "The yacht is not hers. She's already over it."
3. **Amalfi:** "She's not going to the beach. She came from it."
4. **Portofino:** "This is exactly what she planned. Every detail."
5. **Venice:** "She got lost twice to find this exact bridge."
6. **Villa Garden:** "She arrived for dinner. She's not thinking about dinner."
7. **Chateau:** "The chateau is in the background. She's in the foreground. That's the correct order."

## Usage Guidelines

### For AI Image Generation
1. Use complete prompt text from individual files
2. Maintain Kodak Portra 400 color grading consistency
3. Preserve model diversity specifications
4. Keep location-specific architectural details accurate
5. Honor lighting conditions (mostly bright midday, one dusk)

### For Editorial Adaptation
- Campaign works as cohesive 7-image series
- Can be split into sub-series: Italian Coastal (4), Villas & Estates (2), Riviera (1)
- Color progression can be reordered for different narrative
- Mood statements are integral to campaign voice

### For Social Media
- Square crop may require reframing to preserve background landmarks
- Vertical 4:5 or 9:16 suitable for most compositions
- Caption with mood statement + location + color keywords

## Production Notes

### Platform
Generated for **Higgsfield AI** — ensure compatibility with platform's aspect ratio and resolution requirements.

### Rendering Priority
1. Architectural accuracy (chateau facades, canal details, villa gardens)
2. Natural lighting quality (Mediterranean sun, dusk ambient)
3. Wardrobe texture (pearl embellishment, gingham check, silk drape)
4. Background depth (distant mountains, marina yachts, formal gardens)

### Common Challenges
- Maintaining Mediterranean color saturation without oversaturation
- Preserving red dress vibrancy in bright midday sun
- Achieving natural skin texture in high-contrast lighting
- Rendering complex architectural details (balustrades, wrought iron)
