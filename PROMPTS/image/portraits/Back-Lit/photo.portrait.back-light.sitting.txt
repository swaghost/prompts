**Subject:**

**Setting/Environment:**
The subject is seated on a low bench or window ledge in front of a large bright rectangular window. The surrounding interior is dark and minimal, with the window acting as the main light source behind them. The bright background creates a soft glowing halo and dramatic silhouette effect.

**Lighting:**
Strong white backlight from the window, soft rim light on the hair, shoulders, arms, and knees, face in gentle shadow with realistic detail, natural skin texture, cinematic contrast, warm neutral color grading.

**Composition:**
Medium seated portrait framing, the subject is sitting in a relaxed, thoughtful pose, leaning slightly forward with elbows resting on the knees. Hands are loosely clasped between the legs, shoulders relaxed, head slightly turned to the side.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Cinematic lifestyle portrait, moody indoor atmosphere, premium editorial photography, realistic shadows, soft film grain, minimal composition.

**Technical Specifications:**
Slightly lower or eye-level angle, vertical 4:5 Instagram aspect ratio, shallow depth of field, realistic lens feel, professional photography quality.

**Color Palette:**

Looking away from camera with a calm, introspective expression. Body language should feel natural and contemplative.
