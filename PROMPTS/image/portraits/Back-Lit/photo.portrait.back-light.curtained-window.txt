# Soft Backlight Portrait – Curtained Window

## Description of Resulting Image

An ultra-realistic cinematic soft backlight portrait featuring a subject standing in front of sheer white curtains with strong daylight glowing from behind, creating dramatic rim lighting. The scene contrasts glowing semi-transparent linen-textured curtains against a dark minimal interior, with the subject in an introspective pose wearing premium neutral knitwear, creating an elegant lifestyle portrait with soft cinematic quality.

## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
Create an ultra-realistic cinematic soft backlight portrait using the uploaded reference person as the main subject. Replace the original subject completely with the uploaded reference person while preserving their exact facial features, hairstyle, hairline, hair texture, beard/stubble, skin tone, age, body proportions, and overall identity.

**Setting/Environment:**
The subject stands in front of sheer white curtains with strong daylight glowing from behind. The curtains should be softly illuminated, semi-transparent, and textured like linen. The surrounding interior is dark and minimal, creating contrast between the glowing curtains and shadowed room.

**Lighting:**
Diffused backlight through sheer curtains, soft rim light around hair, shoulders, arm, and side profile, gentle shadows across the face, warm neutral color grading, cinematic softness.

**Composition:**
Medium portrait framing, the subject stands slightly angled, one hand gently holding or touching the curtain, the other hand resting casually near the pocket.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Premium lifestyle portrait, soft cinematic light, elegant minimal interior, calm emotional mood, realistic fabric and skin texture.

**Technical Specifications:**
Vertical 4:5 Instagram aspect ratio, natural framing, shallow depth of field, realistic photography quality.

**Color Palette:**
Warm neutral tones, soft cream and beige, gentle shadows, glowing white curtain backlight, subtle color grading.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Calm, poetic, introspective, elegant, natural, lifestyle-oriented, peaceful, sophisticated, contemplative.

**Quality Keywords:**
Ultra-realistic, cinematic, photorealistic, professional photography, soft lighting, high resolution, premium quality, realistic texture, natural lighting.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Harsh lighting, over-exposed curtains, dark silhouette without detail, artificial lighting, cluttered background, logos on clothing, stiff unnatural pose, over-processed colors, low quality, unrealistic rendering.

**Outfit:**
Soft cream or beige knit sweater, matching light trousers, premium monochrome neutral styling, clean fabric texture, no logos.

**Pose:**
The subject looks down and away from camera with a calm, poetic, introspective expression. Body language should feel natural, elegant, and lifestyle-oriented.
