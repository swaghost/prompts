## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
Create an ultra-realistic cinematic studio backlight portrait using the uploaded reference person as the main subject. Replace the original person completely with the uploaded reference person while preserving exact facial features, hairstyle, hair texture, beard/stubble, skin tone, age, facial structure, body proportions, and identity.

**Setting/Environment:**
A dark studio space with a huge glowing rectangular softbox or white light panel directly behind the subject. The background should be almost black, with only the bright rectangle illuminating the scene and creating a strong silhouette effect.

**Lighting:**
Extreme white backlight from the large softbox, clean rim light around the hair, jawline, neck, shoulders, and hands. Face mostly in shadow but still readable, cinematic high contrast, warm skin tones, deep dark edges.

**Composition:**
Medium close-up portrait framing, the subject stands slightly angled toward the camera, head tilted upward and to the side, looking away with a calm, confident, mysterious expression. Hands are positioned near the chest, gently adjusting rings or clasped naturally.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Luxury minimal studio portrait, dramatic backlight, premium editorial mood, cinematic shadows, ultra-realistic skin and clothing texture.

**Technical Specifications:**
Vertical 4:5 Instagram aspect ratio, shallow depth of field, professional portrait lens, realistic photography quality.

**Color Palette:**
Deep blacks, bright white backlight, warm skin tones, cream/beige clothing, cinematic high contrast, minimal color palette.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Calm, confident, mysterious, dramatic, cinematic, luxurious, minimal, sophisticated, powerful, editorial.

**Quality Keywords:**
Ultra-realistic, cinematic, photorealistic, professional studio photography, dramatic backlight, high contrast, premium editorial quality, realistic texture, professional lighting.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Over-exposed face, complete black silhouette without facial detail, harsh artificial lighting, cluttered background, busy composition, visible logos, stiff unnatural pose, over-processed colors, low quality, unrealistic rendering.

**Outfit:**
Oversized cream or beige crewneck sweatshirt with premium soft fabric texture, ribbed cuffs and collar, minimal luxury casual styling. Add subtle rings if suitable.

**Pose:**
Looking away with calm, confident, mysterious expression. Body language should feel natural and editorial.
