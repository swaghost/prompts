# Executive Power Portraits - Index

## Overview

Ultra-realistic professional portrait photography capturing powerful commanding businessmen in high-end corporate environments with dramatic cinematic lighting and shallow depth of field. Five distinct portrait variations showcasing authority, confidence, sophistication, and executive presence through meticulously tailored suits, intentional body language, dramatic lighting, and premium settings.

---

### Lighting Techniques

**Dramatic Side Lighting:**

- Single strong light source from one side
- High contrast with deep shadows
- Facial modeling and structure revealed
- Minimal fill light preserving drama

**Rembrandt Lighting:**

- 45° key light angle (classic portrait setup)
- Triangular highlight under eye on shadow side
- Dimensional facial sculpting
- Subtle fill and rim lights

**Soft Directional Lighting:**

- Diffused key source (softbox, umbrella)
- Directional control for subtle sculpting
- Gentle flattering shadows
- Reveals texture without harshness

**Cinematic Key + Rim Setup:**

- 45° soft key light (main illumination)
- Subtle rim light (edge definition)
- Atmospheric mood creation
- Balanced controlled exposure

## 💡 Technical Success Factors

### Camera & Lens Selection

1. Professional camera bodies specified (credibility, quality)
2. Portrait telephoto lenses (flattering compression)
3. Shallow depth of field f/1.25-f/2 (subject isolation, bokeh)
4. Exceptional sharpness and resolution
5. Premium equipment matching intended quality

### Lighting Mastery

6. Dramatic directional lighting (sculpting, dimensional)
7. Rembrandt setup for classic portraits (triangular highlight)
8. Soft directional with rim (gentle sculpting, separation)
9. Deep shadows controlled (contrast without loss)
10. Cinematic lighting quality (film-level, intentional)

### Subject & Styling

11. Tailored suits impeccable (bespoke, perfect fit)
12. Power gestures intentional (commanding poses)
13. Sharp jawline and cheekbones (photogenic features)
14. Intense piercing gaze (engaging, memorable)
15. Meticulous grooming (professional, intentional)

### Composition Strategy

16. Low-angle authority shots (powerful perspective)
17. Eye-level confrontation (direct engagement)
18. Slightly off-center sophistication (editorial)
19. Symmetrical precision (controlled, formal)
20. Clean professional backgrounds

## ⚠️ Universal Critical Avoidances

**Technical Errors:**

- Blur on subject face
- Lens distortions
- Chromatic aberration
- Excessive overexposure
- Exaggerated contrast
- Digital artifacts

**Subject Errors:**

- Unprofessional appearance
- Ill-fitting suits
- Weak body language
- Awkward hand positions
- Poor grooming

**Lighting Errors:**

- Flat lighting
- Harsh unflattering shadows
- Poor skin tone rendering
- Mixed color temperatures

**Style Errors:**

- Overly edited HDR look
- Unrealistic CGI appearance
- Amateur quality
- Instagram filters
- Low resolution

## 📝 Platform-Specific Prompting Tips

### Midjourney

- Emphasize "ultra-realistic, hyper-detailed, photorealistic, 8K"
- Specify camera and lens explicitly
- Detail lighting setup comprehensively
- Include shallow DOF and bokeh mentions
- Use quality modifiers `--stylize 500 --quality 2`

### DALL-E 3

- Lead with subject and pose description
- Specify environment and setting clearly
- Detail lighting approach
- Emphasize realism and professional photography
- Mention specific camera equipment

### Stable Diffusion

- Use detailed technical terms (focal length, aperture)
- Specify lighting setup precisely
- Include negative embeddings for quality
- Add quality tags (masterpiece, best quality, ultra detailed)
- Use photorealism LoRA models
