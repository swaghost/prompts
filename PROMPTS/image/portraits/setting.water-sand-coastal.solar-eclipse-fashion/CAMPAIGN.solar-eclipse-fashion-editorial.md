## Shared Direction

- Desert dunes and silhouette-led compositions
- Warm amber, burnt orange, peach, and twilight gradients
- Editorial fashion, wellness, yoga, and dance photography
- Dramatic rim light with controlled highlights
- Medium-format film aesthetic, fine sand texture, and ultra-detailed 8K output
