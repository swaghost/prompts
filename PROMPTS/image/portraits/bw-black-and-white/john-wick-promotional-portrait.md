## Platform-Specific Tips

**ChatGPT with DALL-E:**

- Upload reference image first
- Specify "stylish assassin thriller atmosphere" or "John Wick-inspired"
- Emphasize "rain-soaked urban environment" for authentic setting
- Request "ultra-detailed skin texture" for premium quality
- Note "no text overlay" for clean image

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic action cinematography
- Add `--ar 2:3` or `--ar 4:5` for poster format
- Include "action movie poster" or "assassin thriller"
- Consider `--stylize 500-700` for dramatic cinematic quality
