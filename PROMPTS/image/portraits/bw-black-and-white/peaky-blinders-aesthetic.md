## Platform-Specific Tips

**ChatGPT with DALL-E:**

- Upload reference image first
- Specify "Peaky Blinders-inspired aesthetic" for period style
- Mention "wet cobblestone streets" and "atmospheric smoke" for authentic setting
- Note "no text overlay" for clean image

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic period photography
- Add `--ar 2:3` or `--ar 4:5` for portrait format
- Include "Peaky Blinders" or "1920s British gangster"
- Consider `--stylize 400-600` for cinematic period drama quality
