# ⛰️ IDENTITY PRESERVATION — MOUNTAIN-TOP CLOUD INVERSION TRANSFORMATION

## Concept

Transform a reference photo into an ultra-photorealistic mountain-top scene with dramatic cloud inversion while preserving the subject's exact facial identity. This prompt maintains all unique facial features while completely replacing the environment with epic natural scenery.

---

## Core Principle

**Use the uploaded image strictly as the identity reference only.**

Treat the uploaded image exclusively as an identity reference, never as a pose, lighting, composition, or facial orientation reference unless explicitly instructed.

Preserve the person's unique facial identity absolutely consistently.

---

## 📄 MASTER PROMPT

```
Use the uploaded image strictly as the identity reference only.

Treat the uploaded image exclusively as an identity reference, never as a pose, lighting, composition, or facial orientation reference unless explicitly instructed.

Preserve the person's unique facial identity absolutely consistently, including facial structure, hairstyle, hairline, forehead, eyebrows, eyes, nose, lips, ears, beard, skin tone, facial proportions, body proportions, height, natural physique, clothing, accessories, pose, framing, perspective, camera angle and composition.

Do NOT regenerate, redraw, beautify, age, de-age, reshape, replace or modify any part of the subject.

Only transform the atmosphere, weather, lighting and environment.

Maintain ultra-photorealism with physically accurate lighting, natural colors and realistic environmental interaction.

---

ENVIRONMENT TRANSFORMATION:

Replace the environment with a mountain-top cloud inversion.

Endless sea of clouds below, sunrise light, crisp mountain air, layered cloud formations, physically accurate perspective and natural color grading.

---

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:

Photographed on a full-frame cinema camera with shallow depth of field, cinematic film grain, realistic optical compression, and natural color grading.

The image must look exactly like a real outdoor portrait captured at sunrise on a mountain summit.

No CGI, fantasy, AI artifacts, waxy skin, or overprocessing.
```

## Key Parameters

### Identity Preservation (Locked Elements)

- **Facial structure**: Exact match to reference
- **Facial features**: Eyebrows, eyes, nose, lips, ears, beard, skin tone
- **Proportions**: Facial proportions, body proportions, height
- **Physical attributes**: Natural physique, hairstyle, hairline
- **Styling**: Clothing, accessories
- **Composition**: Pose, framing, perspective, camera angle

### Transformation Elements (Modified)

- **Environment**: Mountain-top setting
- **Atmosphere**: Cloud inversion (sea of clouds below)
- **Lighting**: Sunrise light, crisp mountain air
- **Weather**: Clear high-altitude conditions
- **Background**: Layered cloud formations

### Technical Quality

- **Camera**: Full-frame cinema camera
- **Depth of field**: Shallow
- **Film quality**: Cinematic grain, realistic optical compression
- **Color**: Natural sunrise color grading
- **Perspective**: Physically accurate

## Pro Tips

💡 **Maximum Identity Preservation**: This prompt is extremely conservative—it preserves pose, clothing, and composition. Use this when you want ONLY the background to change.

💡 **For More Freedom**: If you want to change pose or clothing too, remove those items from the preservation list in the first section.

💡 **Lighting Transitions**: The lighting will naturally adapt to the new environment (sunrise on mountain creates different light than the original photo).

💡 **Natural Integration**: The subject will be realistically lit and integrated into the new environment—expect appropriate rim lighting, atmospheric effects, etc.
