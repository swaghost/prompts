# Holiday Girl — Mediterranean Village Series

**Series Type:** Lifestyle Influencer Content  
**Setting:** Mediterranean coastal village  
**Time:** Golden hour  
**Style:** Hyper-realistic smartphone photography  
**Character:** Consistent female character  
**Format:** Vertical 4:5  
**Total Shots:** 5

---

## Series Overview

Hyper-realistic lifestyle photograph series featuring a consistent character in a Mediterranean coastal village setting during golden hour. Focus on ultra-photorealistic detail, authentic smartphone photography aesthetic, and strong character consistency across all shots.

---

## Character Consistency — CRITICAL

**Use EXACTLY the same character throughout all 5 shots:**

- Woman in her late twenties
- Shoulder-length sandy-blonde hair (slightly darker roots)
- Warm fair skin with light freckles
- Pale hazel-green eyes
- Natural minimal makeup
- Naturally shaped medium-brown eyebrows
- Soft pink lips

**Locked Outfit (maintain across all shots):**

- Sage-grey linen wrap dress with three-quarter sleeves
- Soft V-neckline, gathered waist
- Visible linen fibers, natural creases
- Relaxed, soft minimalist luxury styling

**Locked Accessories:**

- Several delicate thin gold rings
- Tiny gold necklace
- Long almond-shaped nails with translucent nude-blush polish
- Large structured ivory leather tote bag:
  - Warm ivory pebbled leather
  - Wide open top
  - Thick caramel-brown braided leather handles
  - Muted gold eyelets
  - Slightly lined beige interior

**Maintain strong character, outfit and accessory consistency while changing only shot composition and location within the scene.**

---

## Location Details

**Mediterranean Coastal Village:**

- Whitewashed walls and buildings
- Pale stone paving (irregular, pale-grey)
- Rustic wooden doors
- Small dark-framed windows
- Terracotta pots with succulents
- Flowering magenta bougainvillea
- Stone thresholds
- Simple black metal railings
- Narrow pedestrian lanes
- Old beige stone buildings
- Southern European/Italian aesthetic

**Lighting:** Warm low golden hour sunlight creating luminous highlights, soft directional shadows, and warm reflections on skin

## Style Guidelines

**Photography Aesthetic:**

- Ultra-photorealistic smartphone photography
- Modern smartphone rear camera (approximately 24-28mm equivalent)
- Natural computational HDR
- Realistic highlight roll-off
- Slight smartphone sharpening
- Subtle image compression
- Natural wide-angle distortion (where appropriate)
- Spontaneous, imperfect framing
- Candid travel photography feel
- Taken by a friend/travelling companion

**Texture Details to Emphasize:**

- Visible pores on skin
- Tiny freckles, subtle skin variations
- Fine facial hairs (peach fuzz)
- Individual flyaway hairs
- Natural skin sheen
- Delicate lip texture
- Realistic eye details with reflections
- Linen fabric fibers and creases
- Pebbled leather texture
- Braided leather detail with visible grain
- Natural material authenticity

---

## Quality Standards

- Hyper-realistic/ultra-photorealistic
- High-resolution lifestyle advertising photography
- Premium but believable travel imagery
- Authentic smartphone photography aesthetic
- No text, no logos, no visible brand names
- No excessive retouching
- Natural imperfections preserved
- Vertical 4:5 composition (all shots)
