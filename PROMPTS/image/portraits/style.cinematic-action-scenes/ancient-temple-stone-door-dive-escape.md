# Ancient Temple Stone Door Dive - Last Second Escape

## Prompt Type

Cinematic Action Photography - Jungle Temple Escape

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Ultra-photorealistic full-body cinematic action frame frozen at the exact instant the young man dives beneath the rapidly descending stone door of an ancient jungle temple moments before it seals forever. His body is stretched low to the ground, one arm extended forward clutching a weathered leather satchel while the opposite arm braces against the stone floor to propel himself through the narrowing gap. One boot has already cleared the doorway while the trailing leg remains inches from being trapped. The sense of urgency should feel overwhelming without appearing exaggerated.

The emotion should make the viewer feel the urgency of survival in a single frozen moment.

Position the camera just beyond the closing doorway using a 24mm Panavision anamorphic lens, looking back toward the collapsing temple. This perspective makes the viewer feel as though they have barely escaped and are witnessing the protagonist's final desperate dive from only a few feet away.

Capture with an ARRI Alexa 65, realistic volumetric dust, physically accurate stone debris, premium cinematic grain, subtle lens breathing, soft highlight bloom through jungle sunlight, authentic film halation, restrained motion blur on falling rocks only, rich blockbuster color grading combining lush jungle greens with warm sandstone tones, and ultra-photorealistic IMAX-quality rendering.
