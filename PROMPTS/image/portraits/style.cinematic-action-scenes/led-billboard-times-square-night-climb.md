# LED Billboard Times Square - Electric Urban Climb

## Prompt Type

Cinematic Action Photography - Times Square Night Climbing

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Ultra-photorealistic full-body cinematic action frame frozen at the electrifying moment the adventurer climbs across the massive LED billboard screen in Times Square at night. His hands grip the metal framework between glowing panels while his body presses against the structure, illuminated by thousands of vibrant colors cycling rapidly across the display. One foot finds purchase on a narrow support beam while the other searches for the next foothold. Behind him, the endless cascade of Times Square billboards creates an overwhelming sea of light and color.

The massive LED screen displays rapidly changing advertisements—vibrant reds, electric blues, neon greens, and brilliant whites cycling across his body and the surrounding structure. Reflections bounce off wet pavement from recent rain while steam rises dramatically from subway grates. The atmosphere captures the sensory overload of New York City at night.

Facial Performance: His expression communicates intense concentration illuminated by colorful light. His eyes focus upward on the next grip point, calculating the climb with precision. His eyebrows lower slightly from physical effort while colorful reflections dance across his pupils. His lips remain pressed together during exertion while his jaw clenches subtly. The emotion should convey adrenaline-fueled determination—someone pushing physical limits in one of the world's most iconic urban environments.

Position the camera using a 24mm Sigma anamorphic lens mounted on a stabilized drone, positioned just beyond the billboard at a slight angle, capturing both his climbing form against the glowing screen and the explosive Times Square environment beyond. Allow the LED colors to create dynamic lighting across the entire composition.

Capture with an ARRI Alexa 65, premium anamorphic optics, authentic LED screen simulation, realistic light bloom, vibrant color saturation, elegant lens flares, sophisticated HDR rendering, realistic urban night photography, practical LED color spill, and luxurious color grading emphasizing electric blues, vibrant reds, neon greens, warm taxi yellows, and deep urban shadows.
