## The magic is not beauty, it’s believability.

A realistic selfie lands when the lighting, framing, expression, and environment all feel a little imperfect in the right way. Keep it casual, human, and phone-camera true.

## Real over glossy

These work because they feel like someone actually grabbed a phone and took the shot, not like a studio image pretending to be casual.

## Messy details help

Suitcases, reflections, grocery aisles, rumpled bedding, and passing people all add proof that the image happened in a real moment.

## One face, many moods

The pack shows how the same identity can hold together across airport, rooftop, train, car, park, grocery, bedroom, and street scenarios.

## BEFORE GENERATION

- Keep Reference 1 as the only identity anchor in every selfie.
- Preserve natural skin texture, lens distortion, and believable phone-camera lighting.
- Make the environments feel lived-in and casual instead of overly polished.
- Let the realism come from imperfect framing, candid expression, and true-to-life backgrounds.

## NEGATIVES [GUARDRAILS]

The fastest way to break the illusion is to make a selfie too perfect. Leave room for realistic lighting, slight asymmetry, and everyday mess.

- No plastic skin, beauty-filter blur, or over-retouched facial features.
- No extra fingers, broken hands, warped phones, or bent background geometry.
- No fake text overlays, brand corruption, or unreadable signage dominating the frame.
- No studio-perfect symmetry, fake HDR glow, or cinematic grading that kills the selfie feel.
- No duplicate people, duplicate limbs, or floating accessories.
- No uncanny eye direction, inconsistent reflections, or impossible arm angles.
