# Under Construction 08 - Blueprint Hour

## Description of Resulting Image

A wide environmental fashion portrait of a woman studying a large architectural blueprint inside a monumental gutted heritage building lit by cold linear work lights and warm decay.

## Usage

Use for architectural fashion editorials, design-studio campaigns, and images about planning, restoration, or unfinished places. The small figure and expansive shell should communicate scale without losing the blueprint narrative.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0; also suitable for GPT Image, Midjourney, and Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Woman in a cream-white satin long-sleeve button-front shirt dress holding an unrolled architectural blueprint.

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

**Subject:** Moody dark fashion editorial with environmental scale. A woman in a cream-white satin long-sleeve button-front shirt dress stands at the centre of a vast two-storey gutted heritage-building interior, holding a large unrolled architectural blueprint open in both hands and studying it with focused quiet attention.

**Setting/Environment:** Ornate crumbling arched window openings rise high in the upper walls. A tall wooden ladder leans against the far wall. Loose electrical cables and construction debris scatter across the wide floor.

**Lighting:** Vertical LED work-light tubes and angled fluorescent bars create sharp cold-white linear streaks against a warm ambient decay glow.

**Composition:** Make the woman small within monumental architectural volume; blueprint remains readable as a shape, not as invented text.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Wide environmental fashion photography, dark heritage ruin, quiet concentration.

**Technical Specifications:** 3:4 portrait, wide lens, deep architectural perspective, realistic satin, ladder, cables, and debris.

**Color Palette:** Cream white, warm brown, concrete gray, cold white, muted amber.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Focused, monumental, contemplative, unresolved.

**Quality Keywords:** Photorealistic, architectural scale, cinematic, detailed ruin, editorial fashion, high resolution.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Modern finished interior, unreadable fake typography, blueprint covering face, clutter overload, distorted anatomy, extra people, text overlay, watermark, CGI.
