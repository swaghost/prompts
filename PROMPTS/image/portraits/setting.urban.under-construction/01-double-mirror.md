# Under Construction 01 - Double Mirror

## Description of Resulting Image

A full-body luxury fashion editorial portrait of a dark-haired woman in a cream silk wrap robe inside an abandoned concrete bunker, with a hand mirror obscuring her face and a large wall mirror returning her silhouette.

## Usage

Use this prompt for cinematic fashion editorials, character studies, and architectural mood imagery. It works especially well for luxury campaigns that contrast refined wardrobe and controlled posing with unfinished industrial spaces.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0; also suitable for GPT Image, Midjourney, or Flux with portrait reference support.

## Actor or Subject

Dark-haired woman in a cream silk floor-length wrap robe holding a small rectangular hand mirror to her face.

## Prerequisites

- Optional subject reference for identity and styling
- Optional bunker or mirror reference

**Lighting Effects:** One warm orange work lamp creating a pool of light

**Photographic Effects:** Cinematic 35mm film look, analog grain, restrained contrast

**Atmospheric Effects:** Dusty abandoned interior, scattered papers and torn polaroids

## Image Prompt

## Subject

Editorial fashion photograph with analog film grain and desaturated warm tones. A dark-haired woman in a cream silk floor-length wrap robe stands inside an abandoned concrete bunker room, holding a small rectangular hand mirror up to her face and completely obscuring her head. Preserve natural anatomy and realistic silk drape.

## Setting/Environment

A large wall mirror opposite reflects her full silhouette. An orange vintage work lamp sits on the concrete floor, scattered papers and torn polaroids litter the ground, industrial exposed pipes run along the right wall, and chipped white tiles are partially visible in the far background.

## Lighting

The work lamp casts a single warm pool of light through the otherwise dim bunker.

## Composition

Three-quarter side angle, full figure visible, hand mirror and reflected silhouette forming the central visual tension.

## Style & Aesthetic

Cinematic 35mm film, muted earth and bone tones, high-fashion editorial in architectural ruin.

## Technical Specifications

3:4 portrait, realistic fabric, natural proportions, subtle analog film grain.

## Color Palette

Concrete gray, bone white, cream silk, muted earth brown, warm orange.

## Mood & Atmosphere

Mysterious, controlled, quiet, dislocated.

## Quality Keywords

Photorealistic, cinematic, editorial fashion, 35mm film, full body, analog grain, high detail.

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

Visible face behind the hand mirror, duplicate body, distorted reflection, extra limbs, plastic skin, glossy CGI, bright modern room, cluttered styling, text, watermark, cartoon, low resolution.
