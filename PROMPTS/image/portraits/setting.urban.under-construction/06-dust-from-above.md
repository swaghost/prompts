# Under Construction 06 - Dust from Above

## Description of Resulting Image

A surreal full-body fashion editorial of a short-haired model standing beneath a jagged ceiling tear as white plaster dust and debris fall through a concentrated shaft of cold light.

## Usage

Use for dramatic fashion editorials, conceptual portraits, and images built around suspended physical tension. The prompt is designed for a still, surrendered pose with clearly visible airborne debris.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0; also suitable for GPT Image, Midjourney, and Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Short-haired model in a deep sapphire cobalt velvet turtleneck column gown, head tilted back and eyes closed.

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

**Subject:** Surreal cinematic fashion editorial with dramatic suspended tension. A short-haired model in a deep sapphire cobalt blue long-sleeve velvet turtleneck column gown stands with head tilted fully back, eyes closed, face upturned in surrender as white plaster dust and fine debris shower down from a large jagged ceiling tear above.

**Setting/Environment:** A black professional tripod studio floor lamp stands to her right. White painted walls and a bare concrete floor with scattered rubble surround the figure.

**Lighting:** A concentrated shaft of cold bright light beams through the ceiling break directly onto her figure, separating her from the darker room.

**Composition:** Center the model beneath the tear and let the falling dust form a vertical column through the frame.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Documentary fashion image, heavy analog grain, frozen physical tension.

**Technical Specifications:** 3:4 portrait, realistic velvet, plaster dust, debris, light shaft, high detail.

**Color Palette:** Sapphire cobalt, white plaster, concrete gray, black, cold blue-white.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Surrendered, suspended, surreal, solemn.

**Quality Keywords:** Photorealistic, cinematic, suspended dust, volumetric light, analog film, editorial fashion.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Dust covering the face, active collapse, smiling, missing ceiling tear, warm light, distorted hands, extra limbs, text, watermark, cartoon, CGI.
