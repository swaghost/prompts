# Under Construction 09 - City Wind

## Description of Resulting Image

A profile fashion editorial of a woman standing still in a raw brick top-floor room while translucent construction plastic billows around her like wind-caught wings and a grey city spreads beyond open windows.

## Usage

Use for quiet-power fashion campaigns, architectural portraits, and editorial images that contrast a composed subject with violent environmental movement. It is suited to atmospheric storyboards and magazine spreads.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0; adaptable to GPT Image, Midjourney, and Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Woman in a pale silver-grey liquid satin halter column gown, standing in profile and remaining completely still.

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

**Subject:** Editorial fashion photograph in overcast diffuse grey light with documentary stillness. A woman in a pale silver-grey liquid satin halter column gown stands in profile inside a raw exposed-brick top-floor room, facing a row of rustic wood-framed open window openings. She stands completely still and composed.

**Setting/Environment:** Multiple large sheets of thin translucent white construction plastic billow dramatically inward from the open windows, whipping and wrapping around her body like wind-caught wings. Through the windows, a wide grey overcast city skyline spreads below under a heavy atmospheric sky.

**Lighting:** Soft, cool, diffuse daylight fills the room and passes through the plastic, preserving its translucency.

**Composition:** Profile figure remains the still vertical anchor while plastic creates diagonal movement around her. Keep open windows and skyline readable.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Quiet composed power, muted cool blue-grey tones, restrained luxury editorial.

**Technical Specifications:** 3:4 portrait, realistic satin, brick, translucent plastic, wind movement, subtle film grain.

**Color Palette:** Silver gray, white, raw brick red-brown, cool blue-gray, concrete.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Still, windblown, quiet, powerful, melancholic.

**Quality Keywords:** Photorealistic, cinematic, documentary fashion, realistic translucent plastic, film grain, atmospheric skyline.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Subject movement, plastic obscuring entire body, finished room, sunny sky, distorted profile, extra limbs, text, watermark, cartoon, CGI, low resolution.
