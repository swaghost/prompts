# Under Construction 07 - Golden Plastic

## Description of Resulting Image

A back-facing cinematic fashion portrait of a pixie-cut model in a silver-white backless satin gown looking over a hazy city from a derelict top-floor shell wrapped in amber construction plastic.

## Usage

Use for atmospheric fashion campaigns, back-facing character portraits, and architectural images where translucent material and sunset light create the main movement. Ideal for quiet, nostalgic, melancholic editorials.

Higgsfield Soul 2.0; adaptable to GPT Image, Midjourney, and Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Close-cropped pixie-cut model, back to camera, wearing a backless silver-white liquid satin column gown with a draped bow.

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

**Subject:** Cinematic fashion editorial in warm late golden-hour light. A model with a close-cropped pixie cut stands with her back fully to camera inside a derelict unfinished top-floor building shell, wearing a backless silver-white liquid satin column gown with a soft draped bow at the lower back.

**Setting/Environment:** She faces an open rough brick archway window opening with a warm burnt-orange hazy city skyline spread below. Large translucent amber honey-gold construction plastic sheeting hangs in heavy organic folds from raw brick walls. The floor is dusty raw concrete.

**Lighting:** Warm sunlight catches and diffuses through the plastic, bathing the brick shell and satin in a honey-gold glow.

**Composition:** Back-facing figure centered toward the archway, with the plastic folds creating a layered translucent frame.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Atmospheric melancholy, warm film tones, nostalgic fashion feeling.

**Technical Specifications:** 3:4 portrait, realistic satin sheen, translucent plastic, brick texture, sunset haze.

**Color Palette:** Honey gold, amber, silver white, raw brick, burnt orange, concrete gray.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Melancholic, nostalgic, warm, solitary.

**Quality Keywords:** Photorealistic, cinematic, translucent material, golden hour, film grain, luxury editorial.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Front-facing pose, opaque plastic, finished building, blue daylight, distorted back, extra limbs, text, watermark, CGI, low resolution.
