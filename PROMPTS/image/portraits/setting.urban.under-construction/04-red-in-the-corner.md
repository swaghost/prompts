# Under Construction 04 - Red in the Corner

## Description of Resulting Image

An intimate cinematic fashion portrait of a woman in a crimson satin gown leaning into the corner of a raw-plastered room, lit like a small theatrical stage.

## Usage

Use for dramatic fashion campaigns, editorial portraits, and claustrophobic character studies. The composition works when the wardrobe color and wall geometry carry the visual tension.

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

**Subject:** Intimate cinematic fashion editorial with heavy film grain and warm enclosed theatrical light. A woman with dark hair slicked tight leans into the corner wall junction of a small raw-plastered room, arms folded across her chest, head tilted, gaze cast sideways off-camera. She wears a deep crimson red satin halter bias-cut floor-length gown.

**Setting/Environment:** Four bare globe wall sconces are mounted symmetrically on plaster walls. A long streak of dark wine-red paint drips down the lower right wall. White painter's plastic sheeting covers the dusty floor beneath her heels.

**Lighting:** The four globes create a stage-like warm glow with soft falloff into the raw plaster.

**Composition:** Enclosed claustrophobic corner composition, with the red gown and paint streak echoing each other.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Cinematic stillness, raw plaster, theatrical luxury, analog fashion photography.

**Technical Specifications:** 3:4 portrait, realistic satin, plaster texture, warm practical light, heavy analog grain.

**Color Palette:** Crimson red, dark wine, raw plaster white, dusty gray, warm amber.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Claustrophobic, sensual, controlled, theatrical.

**Quality Keywords:** Photorealistic, cinematic, satin detail, film grain, warm practical light, editorial fashion.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Bright studio, casual clothing, missing sconces, clean finished walls, extra limbs, warped hands, text, watermark, cartoon, CGI, low resolution.
