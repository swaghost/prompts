# Under Construction 02 - Scaffold Goddess

## Description of Resulting Image

A wide architectural fashion portrait of a woman in a sage silk halter gown standing on a high-rise concrete ledge wrapped in white construction mesh, with a hazy city far below.

## Usage

Use for high-fashion architectural editorials, luxury construction campaigns, and images where the human figure must be placed against monumental urban scale. The prompt emphasizes fabric movement, scaffolding, and atmospheric depth.

## Image Prompt

## Subject

High-fashion editorial photograph at dusk. A woman in a fluid sage green silk halter gown with a long trailing chiffon scarf stands on the exposed concrete ledge of a high-rise building wrapped in layers of white sheer construction mesh netting. One arm extends outward holding the trailing fabric mid-gesture.

## Setting/Environment

Dense grey-blue city skyline stretches far below in soft atmospheric haze. Warm amber construction-site lights bleed through the scaffolding metal beams behind her. A figure in all-black stands far left and another in red wide-leg trousers stands far right.

## Lighting

Muted dusk light with warm amber practical lights filtering through the metal structure.

## Composition

Architectural wide shot emphasizing the height, ledge, mesh, and small human scale.

## Style & Aesthetic

Luxury fashion editorial with restrained film grain and monumental construction geometry.

## Technical Specifications

3:4 portrait, wide lens, full figure, realistic chiffon movement and atmospheric perspective.

## Color Palette

Sage green, concrete gray, grey-blue, white mesh, amber, restrained red accent.

## Mood & Atmosphere

Elevated, exposed, godlike, precarious.

## Quality Keywords

Photorealistic, architectural scale, high fashion, cinematic dusk, film grain, detailed fabric.

## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

Safety rails, modern finished building, tiny unreadable figure, missing mesh, stiff scarf, distorted ledge, extra people, text, watermark, CGI, low detail.
