## Image Prompt

## Subject

Moody fashion editorial with 35mm analog film texture. A shaved-head model in a blush pink silk satin slip dress with ruffle lace trim along the bust stands barefoot amid broken concrete rubble inside a gutted interior. She holds a white porcelain teacup and saucer with absolute calm, gaze forward and unflinching into camera.

## Setting/Environment

Crumbling brick walls with dark empty window openings, shattered plaster debris across the floor, and a bare pendant glass bulb hanging by a chain. A large jagged ceiling tear exposes rough wooden beams above.

## Lighting

Soft grey light and dusty atmosphere fill the room; the pendant bulb remains a small architectural accent.

## Composition

Full figure centered in the ruin, with the ceiling tear and hanging bulb creating a vertical counterpoint.

## Style & Aesthetic

Cinematic, analog, quietly absurd luxury fashion in a destroyed interior.

## Technical Specifications

3:4 portrait, realistic rubble, silk texture, porcelain, and suspended dust.

## Color Palette
