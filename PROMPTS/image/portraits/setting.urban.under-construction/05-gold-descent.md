## Negative Prompt (What to Avoid)

**Subject:** Cinematic luxury fashion editorial in warm ambient indoor light. A dark-haired woman in a champagne-gold one-shoulder asymmetric bias-cut liquid satin gown descends a grand symmetrical marble staircase inside a faded decaying European palazzo. She wears chandelier drop crystal earrings, posture perfectly erect, gaze calm and direct into camera.

**Setting/Environment:** Ornate wooden banisters with hand-turned carved spindles frame both sides. Two warm overhead globe pendant lights glow from the dark ceiling. Chipped marble steps and flaking gilded paint show elegant decay.

**Lighting:** Warm pendant light pools across the staircase and catches the liquid satin and crystal earrings.

**Composition:** Shoot from below in full symmetrical composition; staircase lines frame the descending figure.

**Style & Aesthetic:** Old-world grandeur meeting elegant decay, cinematic luxury editorial.

**Technical Specifications:** 3:4 portrait, realistic marble wear, satin sheen, crystal reflections, subtle analog grain.

**Color Palette:** Champagne gold, worn marble cream, dark wood, faded gilding, warm amber.

**Mood & Atmosphere:** Regal, composed, decadent, timeless.

**Quality Keywords:** Photorealistic, symmetrical composition, luxury fashion, cinematic, detailed marble, satin, crystal.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):** Modern staircase, asymmetrical crop, casual pose, missing banisters, smooth new marble, distorted earrings, extra limbs, text, watermark, CGI.
