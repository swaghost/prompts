## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
Create an 8K ultra-realistic portrait using the reference image. I'm standing on the yellow subway platform of metro line, moving elegantly in front of the train that passes at high speed behind me, creating an intense contrast between the blurred movement and my firm posture. My expression is calm and composed, transmitting serenity amidst urban chaos. The image shows me from the knee upward, in a framing that reinforces the presence and the imposing nature of the portrait. I'm wearing an intense black overcoat, heavy and structured, with firm tailoring and elegant straight lines; dense and dark, absorbing the light in a soft way and without reflections, reinforcing the solid sensation. My body is slightly turned to the side, shoulders relaxed, creating an elegant and natural line, without rigidity. My head is turned toward the camera with a gaze of someone who wants to be captured by the lens. My hands are in my pockets, which adds naturalness and confidence to the pose.

My hair is loose, lightly tousled by the wind, distributed in a way that there is more volume on one side than the other, creating dynamism and movement that contrasts with the rigidity of the coat. The makeup is discreet and cinematographic, with matte finish and natural, highlighting real textures and fine details; eyes subtly highlighted with fine line and neutral shadows that create depth without exaggeration; lips with soft finish, without intense shine, maintaining balance with the lighting.

**Setting/Environment:**
The environment is marked by the arched ceiling covered with blue tiles and the superior lighting that creates a soft background of light and shadow with character, highlighting the volumetric and superb realism. No other people appear in the scene, just me, which reinforces the intensity of the portrait and the evidence of the train's movement as the principal element of contrast. The depth of field is carefully controlled, keeping my face and clothing in absolute focus, while the train dissolves into dynamic blur.

**Lighting:**
Natural and cinematographic lighting with soft and realistic contrast. Superior lighting from the arched ceiling creates volumetric and superb realism. Soft diffused quality enhancing natural textures without harsh shadows.

**Composition:**
Knee-upward framing reinforcing presence and imposing nature of the portrait. Subject positioned with train passing behind at high speed, emphasizing stillness versus motion contrast.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
