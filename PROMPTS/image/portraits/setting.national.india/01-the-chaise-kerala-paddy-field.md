## Complete Prompt

**CAMERA:** 35mm motion picture film, Kodak Vision3 250D, 50mm lens, handheld. Frame rolled eighteen degrees anticlockwise in a hard Dutch angle, taken low from just above the waterline and close in — the carved back raking across frame, the flooded field running away behind, horizon tilted. Shallow focus on her face and the velvet.

**ENVIRONMENT:** a flooded rice paddy in Kerala at midday, brutal overhead sun. Still sheet water holding a mirror of the white sky, broken by neat rows of young green shoots. Low mud bunds dividing the fields, dense coconut palms far behind.

**FOREGROUND:** bottom edge, close and out of focus — the water's surface itself, rice shoots breaking it in soft green strokes, the sun striking it into a hard white sheet of glare.

**MIDGROUND, SHARP:** a carved wooden chaise longue upholstered in deep emerald velvet standing in the flooded field, its scrolled back and carved feet sunk into mud with small brown clouds still hanging in the water around them. The velvet has wicked dark upward from the waterline in an uneven tide mark. Two parallel drag furrows run back through the mud toward the bund behind it.

Reclining along it, a fashion model on her side, one arm folded behind her head, ankles crossed, one bare foot trailing in the water and sending slow rings out across the mirror. She looks straight down the tilted lens with a flat unbothered gaze. A floor-length gown in pale champagne silk, its hem soaked and clinging where it meets the surface. Dark hair loose, fine gold at the wrist.

**HER, IN THIS HEAT:** sweat beaded along her hairline, upper lip and the hollow of her throat, one strand of hair stuck to her cheek, open pores across the nose, small natural moles, vellus hair blown out by the overhead sun, a faint sun flush high on the cheekbones, natural asymmetry. No retouching, no smoothing, no plastic finish.

**BACKGROUND:** three farmers working the rows behind — two bent double planting seedlings, one straightening with a hand pressed to the small of the back, all in hitched cotton and conical hats, calves submerged. Absorbed entirely in the row in front of them. None looks at the chaise.

**LIGHTING:** hard vertical midday sun, the only source — water sheeting blinding white, short black shadows pooled hard beneath the chaise and each bent figure, the velvet a deep dark mass against the glare, hot highlights along the carved wood and her silk.

**GRADE:** heavy cinematic film grade timed like a cinema print — crushed blacks, warm highlight roll-off carrying detail in the blown water, halation across the sheet glare, shadows lifted with a cool blue cast against warm sunlit skin. Saturated but earthy. Pronounced organic grain, gate weave, natural vignette.

**TEXTURE:** crushed velvet pile darkening with water, carved wood swelling at the joint, mud, young rice, wet cotton, silk with real weight. The planting continues down the row.
