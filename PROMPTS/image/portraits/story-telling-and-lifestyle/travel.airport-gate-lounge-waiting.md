# Airport Gate Lounge Waiting - Travel Editorial

## Description of Resulting Image

An editorial lifestyle photograph capturing the art of waiting beautifully at a modern airport gate lounge. A traveler sits relaxed in black airport seating with legs crossed, sipping iced coffee while looking at her phone with white headphones on, Rimowa cabin suitcase standing beside her. The scene features bright, clean terminal lighting, white columns, flight information screens, and an airy spacious atmosphere that transforms mundane airport waiting into an aspirational lifestyle moment.
