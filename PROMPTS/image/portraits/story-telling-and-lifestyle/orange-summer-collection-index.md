## System Features

### Identity Lock (@image1)

- **Strict Preservation**: Exact face, eye color, facial structure, skin tone
- **Zero Deviation**: Never replace, beautify, morph, or alter identity
- **Natural Appearance**: Visible pores, realistic skin texture
- **Adaptation**: Scene and styling adapt to reference person

### Orange Color Story

Every prompt creates cohesive orange environments:

- Multiple orange tones (tangerine, amber, burnt orange, coral, peach, copper)
- Orange outfit variations
- Orange props and accessories
- Orange/warm backgrounds
- Warm lighting creating orange reflections
- Orange-toned makeup

### Technical Approach

- **Lighting**: Flash + natural light combination for editorial quality
- **Camera**: 50mm or 85mm lens, f/2.8, shallow depth of field
- **Skin**: Realistic texture, visible pores, sun-kissed bronzing
- **Quality**: Photorealistic 2025 luxury editorial aesthetic

### Comprehensive Negative Prompts

All prompts include extensive negative prompts preventing:

- Identity changes, face morphing, beautification beyond recognition
- Plastic skin, over-smoothing, artificial texture
- Distorted hands, extra fingers, poor anatomy
- CGI look, cartoon effects, harsh HDR
- Cheap styling, wrong colors, messy backgrounds
