# Pull Over - Vintage Chrome, Denim & Desert Sun

## Campaign Overview

Eight editorial fashion prompts built around old paint, open doors, chrome, denim, desert light, and vintage Americana. The collection is designed as a cohesive reverse prompt deck for Higgsfield Soul 2.0, with a grainy film treatment and candid movement rather than overly polished styling.

## Platform & Settings

- **Model:** Higgsfield Soul 2.0
- **Ratio:** 3:4 portrait
- **Variants:** 4 per prompt
- **Negative:** no text, no watermark, no logo, no signature
- **Visual rule:** keep the film-grain and grade wording to preserve the era

## Production Notes

- Generate each prompt at four variants and select for the strongest natural gesture, vehicle detail, and film texture.
- The deck moves from desert gravel and roadside Americana through prairie dusk, coast-road brightness, workshop grit, and an evening city sprint.
- Swap the vehicle while retaining the same era, chrome treatment, denim styling, analog grain, and restrained editorial direction.
- Keep the models adult, naturally posed, and caught mid-motion rather than perfectly styled.
