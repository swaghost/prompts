# Fire and Ice — Editorial Fashion Series

**Series:** The Plate Book, Vol. 09  
**Total Plates:** 9  
**Model:** Seedream 5.0 Pro  
**Format:** 4:5  
**Style:** Hyper-realistic editorial fashion photography

---

## Series Overview

Fire and Ice is a 9-plate editorial fashion series featuring one model in one ice-blue silk chiffon gown across two extreme environments — arctic glaciers and desert dunes. The concept explores visual opposition: warm versus cold, fire tones versus ice tones, heat versus frost, while maintaining absolute wardrobe consistency across every frame.

The series alternates between glacier and desert settings, creating visual dialogue between paired environments. Two plates are explicitly matched conceptual pairs:

- **Plate 06 (Desert)** + **Plate 07 (Ice)**: Illusion versus reality — heat mirage false reflection vs. true water reflection
- **Plate 08 (Desert)** + **Plate 09 (Ice)**: Vastness in both worlds — tiny figure in endless landscape

---

## Wardrobe Consistency

All 9 plates use identical wardrobe:

- Floor-length pale ice-blue silk chiffon gown
- Ruched sweetheart bodice
- Thin straps
- Enormous circle skirt of loose flowing fabric
- Bare feet
- Long dark hair loose

---

## Texture Standard

All plates require hyper-realistic skin texture:

- Visible pores
- Fine vellus facial hair
- Natural unevenness in tone
- Unretouched with no airbrushing
- No smoothing filter
- No waxy or plastic sheen
- Accurate subsurface scattering

## Required Reference Image

Each prompt requires the same ice-blue gown reference image attached for identity and wardrobe consistency across the series.
