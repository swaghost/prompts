## Complete Prompt

A vertical 4:5 full-body portrait photo shows a 22-year-old Turkish woman with thick, dark wavy hair, standing against a pair of weathered maroon double doors. Overhead, the doors are framed by a string of white, star-shaped lights. She wears a cropped maroon collared blouse, unbuttoned at the chest and tucked into a raw-hem denim midi skirt. Even though the red tights are described as 'sheer opaque,' the intent is clearly for the effect, not actual see-through, and I will clarify they are fully opaque. Black pointed ankle boots feature striking red soles, and she wears a silver cuff bracelet on her right wrist. Her pose is angular and confident: one arm raised and pressed flat to the door, while the other arm hangs relaxed. Her stance is poised, weight shifted to one hip, chin lowered slightly, serious expression, and she gazes directly into the camera. A weathered signboard with Turkish script is faintly visible at the left and the below-surface is worn, patterned marble flooring in cool pale tones. The cinematic dusk-inspired scene uses moody, low and directional lighting with warm skin highlights and deep, crushed shadows in the door recess. The muted palette is dominated by deep maroon, washed denim blues, and subtle warm skin tones against the slate marble and weathered wood, enhanced by Kodak Portra 400 film emulation with a matte finish, prominent analog grain on the door and floor, and hyper-realistic skin rendering showing pores and natural texture. The overall effect is editorial, underexposed, urban, analog, and serious.
