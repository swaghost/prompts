## Signature Shoot

### Complete Prompt

Create a hyper-realistic black-and-white low-key fashion portrait of the uploaded person. Preserve facial identity with maximum accuracy, including face shape, hairstyle, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, skin texture, and natural expression.

Style: luxury editorial fashion photography, square crop, close-up upper-body portrait, head slightly tilted, body slightly angled, direct eye contact, calm and mysterious expression.

Lighting: dramatic high-contrast monochrome, bright white background, deep shadows covering most of the face, subtle rim light around the head and shoulders, only slight highlights on the cheekbone, nose bridge, lips, and facial outline. Deep blacks, clean whites, cinematic contrast, soft film grain.

Outfit: minimal oversized black crewneck sweatshirt, premium fashion aesthetic, blending naturally into the shadows.

Shot on an 85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of field, ultra-realistic skin and hair detail, professional magazine-quality fashion photography, HDR, photorealistic, dark editorial mood.

Negative prompt: cartoon, anime, CGI, illustration, low quality, blurry, beauty filter, overexposed face, distorted features, extra limbs, watermark, logo, readable text.

### Key Elements

- **Identity Preservation:** Maximum accuracy for face shape, hairstyle, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, skin texture, expression
- **Composition:** Square crop, close-up upper-body, head slightly tilted, body angled
- **Expression:** Direct eye contact, calm and mysterious
- **Lighting:** Dramatic high-contrast, bright white background, deep shadows, subtle rim light
- **Style:** Luxury editorial fashion, monochrome, deep blacks, clean whites
- **Outfit:** Oversized black crewneck sweatshirt
- **Technical:** 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, HDR, photorealistic

## Usage Guidelines

### How to Use These Prompts

1. **Upload Reference Image:** All prompts require uploading a reference image for identity preservation
2. **Choose Your Style:** Select the aesthetic that matches your creative vision
3. **Platform:** Optimized for AI image generators with reference image support
4. **Customization:** Adjust outfit details, expressions, or environmental elements as needed

### Common Elements Across All Prompts

- **Identity Preservation:** Maximum accuracy for facial features and expression
- **Monochrome:** Black-and-white or monochrome color grading
- **Dramatic Lighting:** High-contrast, cinematic, professional studio quality
- **Premium Quality:** Magazine-quality, ultra-realistic, photorealistic
- **Shallow Depth of Field:** Professional portrait lens aesthetic
- **No Text Overlay:** Clean images without watermarks or text (where specified)

### Best Practices

- Use high-quality reference images for best identity preservation
- Specify exact outfit details if different from prompt defaults
- Adjust lighting intensity by modifying "dramatic" to "subtle" or "extreme"
- Request specific expressions beyond those listed
- Combine elements from multiple prompts for unique variations
