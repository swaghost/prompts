# Intimate Hand-to-Face Portrait - Black & White Editorial

**Platform:** GPT Image 2 (or similar advanced image generation)  
**Style:** Photorealistic black-and-white close-up editorial portrait  
**Format:** Vertical composition  
**Technique:** Reference-based character replacement

---

## Concept

Classic black-and-white editorial portrait featuring an intimate hand-to-face pose with direct eye contact. The subject leans toward the camera with their face resting on one hand, creating a calm, confident, subtly contemplative mood. Perfect for magazine editorials, professional headshots, and character portraits with sophisticated, timeless appeal.

**Perfect for:** Magazine covers, editorial features, professional portfolio portraits, character studies, intimate close-ups, classic fashion photography, personal branding

---

## Complete Prompt Template

```
Photorealistic, hyperrealistic black-and-white close-up editorial portrait using the uploaded black-and-white reference image as the exact, non-negotiable template for vertical composition, camera position, subject scale, facial angle, hand placement, posture, direct eye contact, background structure, lighting direction/intensity, tonal distribution, contrast, depth of field, lens characteristics, focus placement, film grain, and classic magazine atmosphere.

Replace ONLY the original model with the person from the uploaded target character reference image, preserving their real, recognizable identity with maximum accuracy: natural skull/face shape, facial proportions, hairstyle/hairline/texture/volume, forehead, brows, eye shape/spacing and natural eye color, nose, lips, cheeks/cheekbones, jawline, chin, ears, skin tone and realistic skin texture, facial hair (if present), neck and body proportions. Do not blend any features from the original model.

Maintain the intimate front-facing pose: leaning slightly toward camera, side of face resting on one hand, palm under cheek and chin, fingers extended naturally along temple and outer eye area; calm, confident, subtly contemplative expression with a relaxed half-smile and intense eye contact. Hand anatomy must match the target identity while keeping the exact pose.

Wardrobe: soft long-sleeved knit, relaxed editorial silhouette, ribbed cuffs, medium-to-heavy texture, dark/medium grayscale. Tight head-and-upper-torso crop, face centered, hair upper-center. 85mm lens, f/2-f/2.8, very shallow DOF; sharp focus on both eyes and central facial plane, shoulders/background progressively soft.

Plain pale gray interior background with large bright empty space above and subtle vertical tonal transition on frame-right. Soft diffused frontal daylight from frame-left and slightly above eye level; gentle highlights, no hard shadows; smooth highlight roll-off, soft midtones, moderately deep blacks, restrained whites, low-to-medium contrast, fine analog grain, subtle lens softness. 8K resolution, ultra high definition, highly detailed, sharp focus, professional photography, masterful composition; no text, logos, accessories unless naturally present, no added objects, no dramatic relighting, no camera/gaze changes, no artificial skin smoothing.
```

## Pro Tips

### **Reference Image Selection**

- Choose a reference with the exact pose, lighting, and mood you want
- Ensure the reference has clear hand positioning
- Black-and-white references work best for consistency
- High-quality reference = high-quality output

### **Target Character Reference Quality**

- Use clear, well-lit photos of the target person
- Frontal or 3/4 view works best for facial accuracy
- High resolution ensures better identity preservation
- Multiple angles can be uploaded for better recognition

### **Identity Preservation**

- The prompt's "Do not blend any features from the original model" is critical
- "Maximum accuracy" and "real, recognizable identity" enforce fidelity
- Long list of anatomical features ensures complete preservation
- If identity isn't accurate, emphasize these instructions

### **Hand Positioning**

- "Hand anatomy must match the target identity" ensures natural proportions
- Hand should look natural and organic, not generic
- Fingers should appear relaxed, not stiff

- "Sharp focus on both eyes" ensures captivating engagement

- Direct gaze is critical to the intimate mood

### **Depth of Field Control**

- f/2-f/2.8 creates beautiful bokeh on shoulders/background
- "Very shallow DOF" isolates subject
- "Progressively soft" falloff looks natural
- Maintains editorial cinematic quality

### **Lighting Consistency**

- "Soft diffused" prevents harsh shadows
- "From frame-left and slightly above" creates natural modeling
- "Low-to-medium contrast" maintains editorial sophistication
- "No hard shadows" keeps mood gentle and intimate

### **Background Simplicity**

- "Plain pale gray" provides clean professional backdrop

### **Analog Film Aesthetic**

- "Fine analog grain" adds texture and authenticity

- "Classic magazine atmosphere" evokes timeless quality

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Generated face doesn't match target character

- **Solution:** Ensure target character reference is clear and high-resolution, emphasize "preserving their real, recognizable identity with maximum accuracy," verify "Do not blend any features from the original model" is in prompt

**Issue:** Hand looks unnatural or generic

- **Solution:** Ensure "Hand anatomy must match the target identity" is included, verify hand pose description is clear, consider uploading reference with visible hands

**Issue:** Pose doesn't match reference image

- **Solution:** Verify "uploaded black-and-white reference image as the exact, non-negotiable template" is in first line, ensure reference image clearly shows desired pose

**Issue:** Lighting is too harsh or too flat

- **Solution:** Adjust lighting description (see Lighting Variations), verify "soft diffused" and "gentle highlights, no hard shadows" are included

**Issue:** Background is distracting

- **Solution:** Emphasize "plain pale gray interior background," add "minimal, non-distracting" if needed, ensure very shallow DOF blurs background

**Issue:** Eyes aren't in sharp focus

- **Solution:** Verify "sharp focus on both eyes and central facial plane" is included, ensure this is emphasized in the technical specifications

**Issue:** Expression looks wrong

- **Solution:** Use Expression Variations to customize, be specific about desired mood, ensure expression description is detailed

**Issue:** Wardrobe doesn't match style

- **Solution:** Use Wardrobe Variations to customize, specify exact garment type and texture, add color/tone details
