## Pro Tips

### **Reference Image Selection**

- Choose reference with exact pose and lighting style you want
- Crossed-arms pose works best for casual confident portraits
- Hard lighting creates classic fashion magazine aesthetic
- Near-square format is versatile for magazine layouts

### **Target Character Quality**

- Clear, well-lit photos ensure better identity preservation
- Frontal or 3/4 view captures facial features accurately
- Photos showing teeth/smile help if laugh expression is desired
- High resolution = better anatomical accuracy

### **Authentic Laugh Expression**

- "Narrowed eyes" ensures authentic smile (Duchenne smile)
- "Spontaneous" prevents forced or fake-looking smile
- "Joyful energy" creates genuine emotional connection
- Visible teeth add to candid authenticity

### **Off-Camera Gaze**

- Creates candid "caught in the moment" feel
- More natural than posed eye contact
- Head turned toward frame-right with gaze looking away
- Suggests subject is laughing at something off-camera

### **Crossed-Arms Posture**

- "Relaxed" prevents defensive or closed-off appearance
- "Slight shoulder asymmetry" adds natural human element
- "Sleeve compression" shows fabric responding to pose
- Creates confident, approachable body language

### **Hard Lighting Quality**

- "Hard key light from high frame-right" = defined shadows
- Creates classic editorial contrast
- "Deep sweater blacks" = rich tonal depth
- "Controlled highlights" = professional studio quality
- "Visible midtones" = prevents overly high contrast

### **Wristwatch Detail**

- "Tasteful" and "photorealistic" ensure elegance
- Adds subtle personal detail
- Works well with crossed-arms pose (watch is visible)
- Can be removed entirely if minimal styling preferred

### **Moderate Depth of Field**

- f/4-f/5.6 keeps subject fully sharp
- Doesn't isolate subject as extremely as f/2
- Appropriate for full upper-body portrait
- Maintains professional studio aesthetic

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Face doesn't match target character

- **Solution:** Emphasize "preserving their immediately recognizable real identity with maximum anatomical accuracy," verify target reference is clear, ensure "do not borrow any facial features from the original model" is included

**Issue:** Laugh looks forced or unnatural

- **Solution:** Emphasize "spontaneous," "authentic," and "joyful energy," verify "narrowed eyes" is included (key to genuine smile), consider softer smile if broad laugh doesn't fit subject

**Issue:** Arms look stiff or defensive

- **Solution:** Ensure "relaxed folded arms" is included, add "natural comfortable stance," emphasize "slight shoulder asymmetry"

**Issue:** Gaze direction is wrong

- **Solution:** Specify exact gaze direction using Gaze Direction Variations, be explicit about "head turned toward frame-right, off-camera gaze"

**Issue:** Lighting is too harsh or too flat

- **Solution:** Use Lighting Variations to adjust, default hard light creates fashion editorial look, soft lighting for gentler aesthetic

**Issue:** Background is distracting

- **Solution:** Emphasize "subtle gradient," use Background Variations for simpler backdrop, ensure moderate DOF softens background

**Issue:** Wristwatch looks wrong or is in wrong position

- **Solution:** Specify "on the same wrist/position" as reference image, describe watch style if specific type desired, or remove entirely

**Issue:** Composition feels unbalanced

- **Solution:** Verify "subject slightly left of center with negative space to frame-right" is included, adjust using Crop & Framing Variations
