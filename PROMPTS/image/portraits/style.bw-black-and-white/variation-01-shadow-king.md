## Platform-Specific Tips

**ChatGPT with DALL-E:**

- Upload reference image first
- Emphasize "extreme low-key lighting" for maximum shadow coverage
- Request "piercing eyes barely visible" to maintain focal point
- Specify "strong rim light" for silhouette definition

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic lighting
- Add `--ar 2:3` or `--ar 4:5` for portrait orientation
- Consider adding "chiaroscuro lighting" for classical dramatic effect
- May benefit from `--stylize 400-600` for artistic interpretation
