**Platform:** GPT Image 2 (or similar advanced image generation)  
**Style:** Photorealistic black-and-white dramatic side profile portrait  
**Format:** Vertical framing  
**Technique:** Reference-based character replacement

## Concept

Cinematic black-and-white fashion editorial portrait featuring a three-quarter side profile with dramatic low-key lighting. The subject's hand is raised to face with a contemplative gesture, and an elegant wristwatch is prominently positioned in the composition. Hard directional lighting sculpts highlights and deep shadows for a sophisticated, introspective mood perfect for high-end fashion editorials and cinematic portraiture.

**Perfect for:** Fashion magazine editorials, luxury brand campaigns, dramatic character portraits, cinematic photography, men's style features, high-contrast artistic portraits, sophisticated professional imagery

- **Lens:** 85-105mm (compressed perspective)

- **Camera height:** Slightly below or level with face

- **Focus:** Sharp on illuminated eye area, nose bridge, hand, watch
- **Falloff:** Rear shoulder and background softly blurred

**Hard-to-Medium Key Light:**

- Direction: From high frame-left
- Angle: Angled downward onto subject
- Quality: Hard-to-medium hardness (defined shadows)
- Fill: Minimal (low-key aesthetic)

**Lighting Effect:**

- Sculpted highlights on illuminated side
- Deep shadows on frame-right side
- Dominant black tonal mass on shadow side

**Contrast:**

- High contrast
- Shadow density is deep (near black)
- Highlight intensity controlled (not blown out)
- Classic low-key cinematic mood

### **Background**

- Dark studio background

- Black to dark gray tones

### **Film & Texture**

- 8K resolution

## Pro Tips

### **Reference Image Selection**

- Choose reference with dramatic side lighting

- Hand gesture should be clear and well-composed
- Wristwatch should be visible in lighting
- Low-key mood requires dark background

### **Target Character Quality**

- Side profile or 3/4 view references work best
- Clear nose profile is critical for side portrait accuracy
- High resolution captures facial contours accurately
- Photos showing jawline/chin help with profile accuracy

### **Side Profile Accuracy**

- "Nose profile" emphasis ensures accurate profile line
- Three-quarter view shows more face than full profile
- Head inclination downward adds contemplative mood
- Camera slightly below face level adds subtle power

### **Hand Gesture Natural**

- Hand should appear relaxed, not stiff
- "Hand proportions" match target identity
- Hand position showcases wristwatch naturally
- Gesture adds contemplative narrative element

### **Wristwatch Prominence**

- "Lower-left central area" = prime visual position
- Positioned in lit area for visibility

- "Hard-to-medium key from high frame-left" = sculpted light
- "Minimal fill" preserves deep shadows

- f/2.8-f/4 creates beautiful bokeh

- "Deep shadows" without crushing blacks entirely
- "Controlled highlights" prevents blown-out areas
- "Matte monochrome finish" prevents overly glossy look
- "Fine analog grain" adds texture and character

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Profile doesn't match target character

- **Solution:** Emphasize "nose profile" specifically, ensure target reference shows clear side or 3/4 view, verify "maximum facial/anatomical accuracy" is included

**Issue:** Lighting is too flat or not dramatic enough

- **Solution:** Emphasize "hard key," "sculpted highlights and deep shadows," ensure "minimal fill" is included, verify "dominant black tonal mass" description

**Issue:** Wristwatch isn't visible or looks wrong

- **Solution:** Verify "wristwatch placement in the lower-left central area" and that it's in the illuminated zone, specify watch style using Wristwatch Variations

**Issue:** Expression doesn't look introspective

- **Solution:** Emphasize "eyes down," "subtle brow contraction," ensure "no smile" and "no direct eye contact" are in negative prompts

**Issue:** Background is too bright or distracting

- **Solution:** Emphasize "dark studio background," use Background Variations for pure black if needed, ensure shallow DOF blurs background

**Issue:** Hand looks unnatural

- **Solution:** Verify "hand proportions" matches target identity, ensure hand gesture description is clear, use Hand Gesture Variations for alternatives

**Issue:** Too much of face is in shadow

- **Solution:** Slightly reduce key light angle emphasis, or specify "more frontal three-quarter view" to expose more face to light

**Issue:** Shadows are too bright (not low-key enough)

- **Solution:** Emphasize "minimal fill," add "no brightened shadows" to negative prompts, specify "extreme low-key" if needed
