## Platform-Specific Tips

**ChatGPT with DALL-E:**

- Upload reference image first
- Specify "film noir" and "1940s detective aesthetic" for period accuracy
- Emphasize "Venetian blind lighting" for iconic noir visual
- Mention "cigarette smoke" and "rainy city-night" for atmosphere
- Note "no text overlay" for clean image

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic noir cinematography
- Add `--ar 2:3` or `--ar 4:5` for classic portrait format
- Include "film noir" or "1940s detective film"
- Consider `--stylize 400-600` for classic cinematic quality
- May benefit from adding "chiaroscuro lighting"
