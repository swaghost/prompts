## Platform-Specific Tips

**ChatGPT with DALL-E:**

- Upload clear reference image
- Specify "GQ magazine cover" for publication-specific aesthetic
- Request "ultra-sharp focus" for crisp details
- Can ask for variations with different poses or expressions

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic magazine photography
- Add `--ar 4:5` or `--ar 2:3` for typical magazine cover format
- Include "magazine cover photography" in prompt
- Consider `--stylize 300-500` for professional polished look
