# B&W Cinematic Portrait - Signature Shoot

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** ChatGPT with DALL-E, MidJourney, other AI generators with reference image support

**Technical Specifications:** Square crop, black-and-white monochrome, high-contrast dramatic lighting, 85mm portrait lens aesthetic, shallow depth of field

---

## Prompt Description

A hyper-realistic black-and-white low-key fashion portrait emphasizing dramatic high-contrast lighting with deep shadows covering most of the face. The subject wears a minimal oversized black crewneck sweatshirt against a bright white background, creating strong visual tension. Features luxury editorial fashion photography aesthetic with subtle rim lighting, soft film grain, and maximum facial identity preservation.

---

## Complete Prompt

Create a hyper-realistic black-and-white low-key fashion portrait of the uploaded person. Preserve facial identity with maximum accuracy, including face shape, hairstyle, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, skin texture, and natural expression.

Style: luxury editorial fashion photography, square crop, close-up upper-body portrait, head slightly tilted, body slightly angled, direct eye contact, calm and mysterious expression.

Lighting: dramatic high-contrast monochrome, bright white background, deep shadows covering most of the face, subtle rim light around the head and shoulders, only slight highlights on the cheekbone, nose bridge, lips, and facial outline. Deep blacks, clean whites, cinematic contrast, soft film grain.

Outfit: minimal oversized black crewneck sweatshirt, premium fashion aesthetic, blending naturally into the shadows.

Shot on an 85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of field, ultra-realistic skin and hair detail, professional magazine-quality fashion photography, HDR, photorealistic, dark editorial mood.

Negative prompt: cartoon, anime, CGI, illustration, low quality, blurry, beauty filter, overexposed face, distorted features, extra limbs, watermark, logo, readable text.

## Platform-Specific Tips

**ChatGPT with DALL-E:**

- Upload clear reference image first
- Paste complete prompt
- Request square aspect ratio explicitly
- Can request variations with different shadow intensity

**MidJourney:**

- Use uploaded image as reference with `--cref` parameter
- Add `--ar 1:1` for square format
- Use `--style raw` for photorealistic results
- Consider `--stylize 300-500` for balanced artistic interpretation
