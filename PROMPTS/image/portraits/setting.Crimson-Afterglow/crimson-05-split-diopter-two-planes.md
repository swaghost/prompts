# Crimson Afterglow 05: Split-Diopter Two Focal Planes

## Overview

An editorial mid-distance portrait using split-diopter lens technique to create two sharply-resolved focal planes. Weathered ticket stub and red-tinted sunglasses in extreme foreground-left; subject equally crisp in background-right. Hallmark vertical seam of soft bokeh transition visible between zones creating narrative link between figure and objects.

## Demographics

- **Subject**: 22-year-old Turkish woman
- **Style**: Modern, cinematic, contemplative narrative

## Complete Prompt

An editorial, mid-distance portrait uses a split-diopter lens technique to set two sharply-resolved focal planes in the same frame: a weathered ticket stub and red-tinted sunglasses with translucent red arms sit in the extreme foreground-left on a rough, cool-gray stone ledge, while in the background-right, a 22-year-old Turkish woman is rendered in equal crispness. Both objects and subject appear equally sharp, with the hallmark vertical seam of soft bokeh transition visible between global diopter-adjacent zones and the out-of-focus maroon cinema facade at rear. The woman stands mid-frame, three-quarter stance, weight balanced at the hip, arms loosely crossed, wearing a red satin blouse and denim culottes, with dark, shoulder-length wavy hair catching a light breeze. Her expression reads focused, non-smiling, gaze directed toward the ticket stub and sunglasses, evoking a narrative link between figure and object. Shot in diffuse, overcast daylight, the scene is lit evenly, with soft shadowing and no hard contrast between the two sharp focus zones. The color palette is warm and neutral, with reds echoed in the sunglasses' lenses and the woman's clothing. Captured digitally with Kodak Portra 400 film emulation, the image has a consistent, soft matte finish with moderate film grain in both sharp planes, naturally fading in the bokeh transition between. Subject is framed vertically with a 4:5 editorial crop and no visible text or logos, creating a modern, cinematic, and contemplative mood.
