## Image Prompt

SCENE: Exterior of an elegant colonial building with a flat wall painted vibrant teal/turquoise blue, smooth painted plaster finish. Wall broken by ornate white-painted arched doorway alcove, deeply recessed arched door frame with white plaster moulding surround and dark wooden door with iron vertical bar grille. Above the door, decorative white plaster relief details. On right side of teal wall, black ornate iron wall lantern bracket holding square glass lantern with elaborate curling ironwork arm. Dramatic strong side light creating a very sharp distinct shadow of both lantern and model projected onto the flat teal wall behind her. Paved concrete or stone pavement below.

MODEL: Adult mixed Latina/Mediterranean woman with warm medium-dark skin, naturally tanned, strong defined features. Dark hair worn back in a sleek tight bun or pulled back simply. Dark eyes, full lips with neutral expression, composed, almost stern, direct camera gaze. One hand on hip.

OUTFIT: Deep rust/terracotta vinyl or PVC crop top, short sleeve or cap sleeve, fitted, glossy finish, mock-neck zip detail. Light iridescent or metallic pale blue sequin wide-leg trousers, high waist, full length, catching light. Small tan leather crossbody bag or clutch at hip.

LIGHTING: Strong harsh afternoon sunlight from left or directly above, creating dramatic hard shadow of lantern and model silhouette projected sharply onto teal wall. Very graphic, high contrast.

CAMERA: iPhone or Canon R6, 35mm. Natural colours, slightly warm grade. Real street fashion photography.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Avoid underage appearance, soft shadows, wrong wall color, distorted lantern, plastic skin, extra fingers, dull trousers, CGI look, text, logos, and watermarks.
