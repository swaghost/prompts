## Image Prompt

SCENE: Urban street scene against a very large flat mustard/ochre yellow painted building wall, entire background this warm yellow plaster wall. Centered in the wall is a large beautiful arched window with semicircular fanlight top, grey stone architrave surround with elegant mullion grid pattern. Round grey vent above the window. Yellow industrial pipes running vertically on right side of wall. Concrete pavement below.

TWO VINTAGE BICYCLES parked leaning against wall, classic steel frame city bikes in silver/chrome, leather saddles; one has a green plastic milk crate attached to the front rack, another has a plastic bag wrapped package on rear rack.

MODEL: Adult mixed Mediterranean/South Asian woman with warm medium-dark tan skin, long straight very dark brown-black hair worn loose past chest. Dark eyes. Cool composed expression, slight pout, not smiling, looking slightly upward or to side. Standing between two bikes, slightly leaning against one.

OUTFIT: White fitted strapless mini dress, structured ruched corset bodice, flared bubble hem, button-front detail down center. White chunky platform sneakers, white tall crew socks pulled up. Black sunglasses in hand at side.

PROPS: Small clear plastic water bottle with label held in one hand, raised slightly as if about to drink.

LIGHTING: Bright midday summer sunlight from above, high contrast shadows on pavement. Yellow wall acts as warm reflector, bouncing warm light onto subject.

CAMERA: Canon R5, 35mm, f/4.0. Full body shot. Vivid warm colours. Real street photography.
