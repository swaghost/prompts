## Image Prompt

SCENE: Exterior of an old stone cottage or villa wall, rough-hewn pale grey granite stone blocks covering the entire background. A deep window alcove set into the thick stone wall, window frame and shutters painted bright forest green, brass hinge hardware visible on both open shutters. Interior behind is completely dark/black. Wide smooth pale grey stone sill large enough to sit on. Rough gravel or stone ground below. Sunlight hitting exterior stone wall and window frame from the right side, dappled afternoon sunlight with green leaf shadows visible on stone wall from a nearby tree.

MODEL: Adult mixed Mediterranean/Southern European woman with warm light-medium tan skin, long straight dark brown hair with subtle warm highlights, loose and natural past shoulders. Downcast eyes looking at book, profile or three-quarter view, not looking at camera. Serene, quiet, absorbed expression. Sitting sideways on the wide stone window sill with knees bent, legs pulled up, leaning against open shutter.

OUTFIT: White cotton puff-sleeve crop top, slightly sheer, relaxed, feminine. Short white lace or broderie anglaise midi skirt, semi-transparent lace overlay on nude/cream lining. Bare feet or simple sandals. Gold watch on wrist, thin red string bracelet.

PROPS: Open paperback book held open in both hands, actively reading. Clear stemless wine glass with white wine resting on the window sill ledge beside her.

LIGHTING: Warm afternoon Mediterranean sunlight from outside hitting stone wall. Slight dappled green leaf shadow on stone. Interior completely dark, strong contrast between bright exterior and dark interior.

CAMERA: Canon R6, 35mm, f/2.2. Warm natural colour grade. Real lifestyle travel photography.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Avoid underage appearance, looking at camera, plastic skin, distorted book, extra fingers, warped shutters, modern interior, harsh flash, CGI look, text, logos, and watermarks.
