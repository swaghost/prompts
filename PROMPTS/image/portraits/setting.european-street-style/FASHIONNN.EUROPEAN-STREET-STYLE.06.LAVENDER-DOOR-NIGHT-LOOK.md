Large double doors painted muted dusty lavender/periwinkle blue-grey with panelled detail, slightly worn, framed notice or sign mounted on door. OUTFIT: White ruched halter crop top, gathered fabric, deep V-neck halter, multiple button or tie details running down front centre. Low-rise leopard print wide-leg maxi trousers, classic animal print in tan/brown tones, full length. Wide white belt with silver studded detail at waist over trousers. Black leather or suede shoulder bag hanging from one shoulder. Silver stacking bracelets and rings. Large silver hoop earrings. Multiple silver chain necklaces layered. Open-toe black sandals or mules.

LIGHTING: Warm amber street lamp light from above and right, creating warm golden light on right side of face and body. Left side and door in cooler shadow. Night photography feel.
