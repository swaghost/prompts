## Image Prompt

**CRITICAL IDENTITY PRESERVATION:**
Transform the uploaded image into an authentic vintage black-and-white photobooth portrait while preserving the exact identity, facial features, hairstyle, body proportions, skin texture, clothing, accessories, and expression of the original person.

**Subject:**
Exact person from reference image with all features preserved. Pose naturally inspired by original image but subtly refined into classic photobooth composition with relaxed shoulders, elegant posture, and centered positioning. Expression remains authentic to reference with realistic skin showing visible pores and natural texture, never airbrushed or plastic.

**Setting/Environment:**
Softly pleated vintage curtains backdrop remaining softly out of focus with visible fabric folds and natural tonal variation, recreating authentic photobooth environment.

**Lighting:**
Flattering diffused frontal studio lighting with gentle contrast, smooth highlight roll-off, and subtle shadows that sculpt the face naturally without looking dramatic. Classic photobooth lighting creating timeless feel.

**Composition:**
Crop from approximately waist-up level with slight headroom, recreating the framing of a real analog photobooth camera. Subject centered in composition with classic photobooth positioning.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Timeless, elegant, candid, and naturally beautiful, as if the portrait was taken inside a real vintage photobooth in the 1950s-1970s. Rich monochrome with soft warm silver-gelatin tones rather than pure black and white.

**Technical Specifications:**
Recreate the look of an authentic mid-century photobooth print captured on vintage photographic paper. Add realistic analog characteristics including delicate film grain, tiny dust particles, subtle scratches, faint age marks, slightly softened edges, light chemical imperfections, gentle vignetting, minor fading, and a soft sepia tint from aged paper. Include extremely subtle vertical scan or print texture to mimic an old physical photograph, avoiding anything that feels like a digital filter.

Ultra-photorealistic with no AI artifacts, no modern editing style, no excessive sharpening, and no stylization beyond the authentic analog film aesthetic.

**Wardrobe:**
Maintain original outfit but render all fabrics with realistic monochrome texture, preserving the authentic appearance from reference image.
