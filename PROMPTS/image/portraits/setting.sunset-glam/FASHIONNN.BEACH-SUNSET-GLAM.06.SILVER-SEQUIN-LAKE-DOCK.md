## Image Prompt

SCENE: Wooden dock/pier extending over a calm inland lake at dusk. Dock surface is weathered dark brown horizontal wooden planks. Lake water behind is completely still and mirror-flat, reflecting the entire sky perfectly. Sky at dusk: soft gradient from pale lavender-lilac at top through peach to warm orange at horizon. Silhouetted pine tree line along far lake shore. Small silhouetted boat house or structure far left. No wind, perfect stillness. Northern wilderness feel.

MODEL: Adult East Asian woman with light warm skin tone and natural soft glow. Very long thick dark black hair, naturally wavy/curly with volume, worn loose cascading past chest. Small star-shaped crystal hair pin clipped at side near ear. Looking away from camera, profile or three-quarter view, head turned to right, gazing toward horizon. One arm raised with hand touching side of head lightly, elegant thoughtful gesture. Seated or semi-reclined on dock, dress pooling around her.

OUTFIT: Floor-length silver sequin halter gown, deep plunge V neckline halter neck tie. All-over iridescent silver sequin fabric with slight holographic rainbow shimmer when light catches it. Fitted through bodice, skirt spreading out on dock around seated position. Silver crystal mule heels with block heel. Crystal/rhinestone cuff bracelet on wrist. Silver star hair pin.

SKIN & MAKEUP: Soft luminous light skin, natural healthy glow. Soft pink flushed cheeks, glossy rose-nude lip, defined lashes and subtle liner. Delicate feminine makeup.

LIGHTING: Pure dusk ambient light, soft lavender-peach-orange glow from sky reflecting off still lake and onto subject. No harsh shadows. Sequins catch soft iridescent dusk light, creating soft all-over shimmer. No artificial light.

CAMERA: Canon EOS R5, 85mm, f/2.0. Dusk colour grade, preserve lavender and peach tones. Slight film grain. Kodak Portra 400 pushed.
