## Image Prompt

SCENE: Standing waist-deep in shallow tropical ocean water at vibrant fuchsia-magenta sunset. Sky is saturated deep pink-purple with dramatic layered clouds, sun dipping at horizon creating a glowing magenta orb. Ocean water reflects the pink sky, warm shallow water around legs, ripples visible. Completely open ocean behind, no land visible. Extreme colour saturation, an almost surreal pink world.

MODEL: Adult mixed complexion model with light-medium warm tan skin, luminous and dewy. Dark wet-look slicked-back hair with a single large white plumeria flower tucked at the left side. Soft direct gaze at camera, lips slightly parted, dreamy expression. Standing straight in water, arms relaxed at sides.

OUTFIT: Mini dress entirely constructed from hot pink/fuchsia ostrich feathers layered densely, fluffy, voluminous, textured. Beneath feathers, glimpses of gold/copper sequin base fabric. Thin pink spaghetti straps. Off-shoulder style with feathers covering chest and extending as wide arm wrap/cuff on both arms. Crystal/rhinestone bracelet on left wrist. Feathers moving slightly in breeze. Water level at mid-thigh, hem just touching water surface.

SKIN & MAKEUP: Warm dewy sun-kissed glow, natural healthy skin, slight perspiration sheen from heat. Rose-nude glossy lip, thick defined lashes, subtle bronze eyeshadow, rosy flushed cheeks matching pink environment.

LIGHTING: Extreme pink-magenta ambient light from saturated sunset sky, bathing entire scene in pink tones. Model's skin and feathers all tinted warm pink by environment. No artificial lighting, pure environmental colour.

CAMERA: Sony A7 IV, 85mm, f/1.8. Maximum sunset saturation, pushed colour grade to enhance magenta. Shot at water level for dramatic ocean perspective.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
Avoid underage appearance, land in background, dull sunset, distorted feathers, plastic skin, extra fingers, CGI water, muddy color, text, logos, and watermarks.
