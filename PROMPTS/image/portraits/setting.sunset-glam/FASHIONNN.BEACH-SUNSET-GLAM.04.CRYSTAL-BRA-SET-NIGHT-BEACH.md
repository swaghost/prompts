SCENE: Dark open beach at full night, pitch black sky above, no stars visible, no moon. Background is pure darkness fading to barely-lit dark sand at ground level. No ocean, no horizon details visible, complete darkness surrounding subject. Only ground sand faintly lit by a close practical light source from front. Highly dramatic noir editorial atmosphere.

MODEL: Adult mixed European woman with light-medium warm olive skin, natural tan. Long thick wavy dark brown hair, slightly tousled with natural volume. Both arms raised, hands behind head/at crown, elbows out wide, confident powerful pose. Direct straight-on gaze at camera. Lips slightly parted, no smile, fierce expression.

OUTFIT: Two-piece crystal embellished set, structured bra top fully encrusted with silver rhinestones, crystals and small dangling chain fringe at bottom edge. High-waist maxi skirt also fully encrusted with silver crystals, rhinestones and sequins throughout with crystal chain fringe trim at hip. Sheer mesh base fabric visible under crystal embellishments making skirt semi-translucent. Bare midriff between top and skirt. Small crystal drop earrings.

SKIN & MAKEUP: Warm golden tan, natural luminosity on shoulders and stomach. Strong makeup, deep rose-nude lip, heavy defined lash line with liner, smoky bronze eye.

LIGHTING: Single close frontal practical light source, warm low light hitting directly on subject only. Creates dramatic shadows on background sand. Crystal embellishments scatter and reflect light in all directions, creating sparkle halo effect. Deep shadows under chin and arms.

CAMERA: Canon 5D Mark IV, 50mm, f/1.4. Very low light, high ISO 3200 with intentional grain. Dark moody exposure. Real photography night shoot feel.

**Negative Prompt (What to Avoid):**
