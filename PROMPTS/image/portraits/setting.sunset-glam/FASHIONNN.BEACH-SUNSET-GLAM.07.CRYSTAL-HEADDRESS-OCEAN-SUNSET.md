Ocean water is dark copper-orange, rough with visible waves and texture. Rocky silhouette/cliff visible on far left. Intense, dramatic, almost mythological atmosphere.

MODEL: Adult East Asian woman with light-medium warm skin tone, structured high cheekbones, sharp strong features, very defined jaw. Eyes with upturned almond shape, heavy liner, bold eyes. Dark hair slicked back, barely visible under elaborate headdress. Extremely neutral, serious, almost imperious expression, no warmth, no smile, complete authority. Standing completely straight, arms loose at sides, hands resting gently at hip waistband. Direct camera stare.

OUTFIT: Elaborate jewelled headdress/headpiece, ornate silver base with cascading strands of crystals, pearls, diamantes and dangling chain droplets falling across forehead and sides of face like a crown veil. Matching large crystal chandelier earrings. Crystal-embellished bra top, geometric diamond/triangle pattern with silver seed beads, crystals and rhinestones on nude mesh base, thin spaghetti straps. Matching crystal-embellished mini skirt with heavily beaded fringe hem, cream/silver seed beads and crystals in geometric pattern, fringe dropping to mid-thigh. Thin gold chain bracelet on both wrists. Bare midriff.

SKIN & MAKEUP: Warm light-tan skin, very smooth, minimal texture visible. Dramatic editorial makeup, deep terracotta-orange lip matching sunset, heavy black cat-eye liner with strong wing, defined sculpted brow. Highlight on nose and cheekbones.

LIGHTING: Fiery sunset backlighting from behind creating strong warm rim light on shoulders, arms and sides of headdress. Crystal details catching orange light and reflecting it. Front of face lit by warm ambient sky bounce. Dramatic contrast.

CAMERA: Phase One IQ4, 80mm, f/2.8. Maximum sunset drama, push orange and amber tones. Sharp crystal details. Editorial fashion campaign quality.
