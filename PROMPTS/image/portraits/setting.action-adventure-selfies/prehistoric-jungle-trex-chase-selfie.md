# Prehistoric Jungle T-Rex Chase - Smartphone Selfie

## Prompt Type

Action Adventure Photography - Jurassic Survival Selfie

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Ultra-photorealistic smartphone selfie while running through a dense prehistoric jungle. A gigantic Tyrannosaurus Rex bursts through broken trees only a few meters behind him. Flying leaves, broken branches, mud splashes, cinematic dust, and sunlight piercing through rainforest canopy. His expression is absolute panic while still managing to hold the phone steady enough for a believable selfie.
