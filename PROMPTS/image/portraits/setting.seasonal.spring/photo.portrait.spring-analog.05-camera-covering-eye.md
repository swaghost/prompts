A cinematic editorial portrait capturing the moment of raising a vintage film camera to the face, covering one eye while the other looks directly past the camera toward viewer. ## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 cinematic editorial portrait of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, naturally thick dark-brown brows, a small straight nose and muted rose lips. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw with softly curved ends, airy separated bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Add medium rounded gold hoop earrings, a slim brown leather wristwatch and realistic skin texture with pores, faint under-eye texture, subtle freckles and slight facial asymmetry.

She wears an oversized warm oat-beige trench coat over a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse with a broad pointed collar, paired with a deep cocoa-brown high-waisted midi skirt. She lifts a realistic vintage silver-and-black 35mm film camera to her face so it covers one eye, while the other eye looks directly past the camera toward the viewer. One hand grips the camera body naturally and the other steadies it near the lens; the worn cognac-brown strap hangs loosely in a soft loop.

Place her in an old quiet square or courtyard with faded cream walls and pale warm stone architecture softly blurred behind her. Frame from the upper waist to slightly above the headband, keeping her centred but relaxed, with a little negative space around the camera silhouette.

Photograph with a Leica SL3 and 75mm portrait lens from eye level. Focus precisely on the visible eye, lashes, fingers and top edge of the camera, with the background gently softened.

Lighting must match the original campaign mood: low late-afternoon sun from behind and slightly upper-left, creating a soft honey rim around the headband, hair and trench shoulder. The front of the face should be filled with soft reflected warm light, not harsh direct sun. Keep the building tones faded cream and pale ochre, the trench creamy oat-beige, the gingham muted dusty salmon and the skin peach-beige with delicate warm blush. Greens, if present, should stay subdued olive. Apply gentle highlight bloom, lifted warm shadows, restrained saturation, soft filmic contrast and fine 35mm grain.
