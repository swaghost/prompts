## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 cinematic editorial portrait of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, naturally thick dark-brown brows, a small straight nose and softly defined muted rose lips. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw, airy bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Preserve visible pores, soft cheek texture, faint freckles, natural under-eye detail and slight facial asymmetry.

She wears medium rounded gold hoop earrings, an oversized warm oat-beige trench coat, a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse with a broad pointed collar, a deep cocoa-brown skirt, a slim brown leather wristwatch and an antique gold ring with a deep-red oval gemstone. She holds a vintage silver-and-black 35mm film camera close to her cheek with both hands, the worn brown strap hanging down softly. Her head tilts gently toward the camera, cheek lightly resting near the top plate, while her eyes look upward and away with a dreamy reflective expression.

Place her in an old intimate courtyard garden with muted olive shrubs, aged stone walls, faded plaster and narrow stone paving. Frame from the waist upward, slightly angled, with the subject near centre and soft foliage surrounding her.

Photograph with a Hasselblad X2D 100C and 80mm portrait lens from just below eye level. Focus on the eyes, camera body and hands, with the background softened but still retaining the historic texture of the courtyard.

Lighting should be soft and overcast-warm or lightly diffused late-afternoon light, closer to the original reference world than the bright generated result. Keep the courtyard tones earthy: olive-grey foliage, warm brown stone, faded cream plaster. Her coat should read creamy oat-beige, the blouse dusty salmon and the skin peach-beige with muted rosy cheeks. Avoid fresh greens and bright highlights. Apply gentle film softness, lifted warm shadows, subdued saturation, fine analog grain and light halation.
