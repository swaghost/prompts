# Spring Portrait - Seated on Grass with Strawberries

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, MidJourney (--style raw, --ar 3:4), Flux

**Technical Specifications:** Leica SL3, 50mm lens, slightly elevated angle, vertical 3:4, cinematic environmental portrait, analog film aesthetic

---

## Prompt Description

A cinematic environmental portrait of subject seated on sunlit grass in quiet old park beneath mature trees. Small wicker basket filled with strawberries nearby, few ripe strawberries scattered around legs. Subject leans back slightly on one arm, holds strawberry near lips with other hand, looking off toward frame-left with thoughtful distant expression as if caught in candid pause during picnic. One knee raised, skirt folds naturally over grass. Full seated body framing with breathing space around. Low warm afternoon sun mixed with tree shade, soft honey highlights on blouse/skin/strawberries while most grass stays subdued olive-green.

---

## Complete Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 cinematic environmental portrait of a petite young woman in her early 20s with fair neutral-warm skin, a softly rounded oval face, large blue-grey eyes, naturally thick dark-brown brows, a small straight nose and softly parted muted rose lips. She has a short dark-chocolate bob ending at the jaw, airy bangs and a wide cream fabric headband. Preserve real pores, slight asymmetry, natural body proportions and visible texture in the skin and hair.

She wears rounded gold hoop earrings, a muted salmon-and-cream gingham blouse with a broad pointed collar and softly gathered sleeves, a deep cocoa-brown midi skirt, sheer warm-beige tights, polished cognac-brown leather loafers, a slim brown leather wristwatch and an antique gold ring with a deep-red oval gemstone. A small wicker basket filled with strawberries sits on the grass nearby.

Place her seated on sunlit grass in a quiet old park beneath mature trees. A few ripe strawberries are scattered around her legs. She leans back slightly on one arm while holding a strawberry near her lips with the other hand, looking off toward frame-left with a thoughtful distant expression, as if caught in a candid pause during a picnic. One knee is raised and the skirt folds naturally over the grass.

Photograph from a slightly elevated angle with a Leica SL3 and 50mm lens, framing the full seated body with breathing space around her. Background should show faded green lawn, deep olive tree shade and a calm open park, softly rendered but still spatially real.

Lighting must follow the reference DNA: low warm afternoon sun mixed with tree shade, producing soft honey highlights on the blouse, skin and strawberries while most of the grass stays subdued olive-green. Keep the strawberries naturally rich red, the skirt deep cocoa, the blouse dusty salmon and the skin warm peach-beige. Avoid bright punchy greens or clean commercial light. Finish with warm lifted shadows, restrained saturation, soft halation, low-contrast filmic colour and fine analog grain.
