# Corporate Workwear - Feminine Structure

## Prompt Description

A full-length luxury editorial fashion portrait of a professional woman in a Feminine Structure outfit combining fitted knitwear with elegant skirt silhouette. Chocolate brown or warm-toned fitted knit sweater paired with camel column midi skirt. Nude slingback heels, structured taupe handbag, delicate gold jewelry. Hair styled in polished waves or elegant updo. The image should convey refined feminine professionalism with warm neutral sophistication - perfect for creative professional environments, client meetings, or elevated office days. Nordstrom Personal Styling aesthetic with warm tones and elegant proportions.

## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Styling

**Subject:** Professional woman with refined feminine style
**Hair:** Polished waves, elegant low bun, or sophisticated updo
**Makeup:** Warm natural tones, polished but soft
**Overall Mood:** Refined, feminine, sophisticated, elegant
**Aesthetic:** Warm luxury, feminine structure, elegant professionalism

### Clothing Details

**Sweater:**

- Color: Chocolate brown, warm taupe, or rich camel
- Style: Fitted knit sweater
- Fit: Close to body but not tight, emphasizes feminine silhouette
- Fabric: Fine quality knitwear with soft luxurious feel
- Neckline: Crew neck, soft V-neck, or elegant boat neck
- Sleeve: Full length or three-quarter

**Skirt:**

- Color: Camel, warm beige, or coordinating neutral
- Style: Column midi skirt
- Fit: Streamlined silhouette, not too tight
- Length: Midi - typically mid-calf
- Fabric: Quality knit, wool, or structured fabric with elegant drape
- Silhouette: Creates vertical elegant line, feminine without being overly fitted

**Shoes:**

- Style: Slingback heels or block heels
- Color: Nude, tan, or neutral matching skirt
- Height: Medium heel (2-3 inches)
- Quality: Elegant refined finish

### Accessories

**Handbag:** Structured leather handbag in taupe, camel, or cognac - medium professional size
**Jewelry:** Delicate gold - small hoops or studs, thin necklace, simple bracelet
**Belt:** Optional refined belt at natural waist if desired
**Styling:** Feminine and elegant without excess

### Setting & Environment

**Background:** Refined professional setting - elegant office, creative workspace, or warm minimal backdrop
**Lighting:** Natural warm daylight, soft flattering light
**Atmosphere:** Warm sophistication, elegant professionalism
**Color Palette:** Warm neutrals - chocolate, camel, taupe, nude with gold accents

### Technical Specifications

**Format:** Full-length portrait showing complete elegant silhouette
**Composition:** Emphasizes vertical column line and feminine proportions
**Focus:** Sharp detail on knitwear texture and skirt drape
**Style:** Editorial elegance, refined professional
**Quality:** High-resolution luxury fashion photography

### Restrictions

Avoid: Casual settings, cold color tones, overly tight fit, overstated jewelry, harsh lighting, busy backgrounds, trendy details that date the look
