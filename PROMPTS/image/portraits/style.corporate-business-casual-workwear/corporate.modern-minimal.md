# Corporate Workwear - Modern Minimal

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, MidJourney (--style raw), Flux (Photography preset)

**Technical Specifications:** Professional editorial photography, full-length portrait, minimal background, natural daylight, luxury fashion editorial aesthetic

---

## Prompt Description

A full-length luxury editorial fashion portrait of a professional woman in a Modern Minimal corporate outfit. Elevated ivory knit tank paired with wide-leg trousers in warm beige or taupe, cinched with refined leather belt. Neutral pointed-toe heels, structured leather tote, minimal gold jewelry. Hair in effortless natural styling. The image should embody quiet luxury minimalism - clean lines, monochromatic neutrals, refined simplicity. Timeless Nordstrom Personal Styling or Jenni Kayne aesthetic with generous white space and sophisticated neutral palette.

---

## Complete Prompt

A full-length professional editorial fashion photograph of a woman wearing an elevated ivory knit tank top paired with wide-leg trousers in warm taupe, cinched with a refined leather belt at the waist. She wears neutral pointed-toe heels and carries a structured leather tote bag. Minimal gold jewelry complements the look. Her hair is styled naturally with effortless texture. The setting features a minimal clean background with natural daylight. The image should convey modern minimal luxury styling reminiscent of Nordstrom Personal Styling, Jenni Kayne, or quiet luxury editorial - monochromatic neutral tones, clean lines, refined simplicity, generous white space. Polished yet relaxed, sophisticated minimalism.

## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Styling

**Subject:** Professional woman with modern sensibility
**Hair:** Natural effortless styling, relaxed texture
**Makeup:** Minimal, fresh, natural
**Overall Mood:** Elevated yet relaxed, modern, sophisticated minimal
**Aesthetic:** Quiet luxury, modern minimalism, refined simplicity

### Clothing Details

**Top:**

- Color: Ivory (soft white/cream)
- Style: Elevated knit tank
- Fit: Fitted but not tight, smooth drape
- Fabric: Fine quality knit with subtle luxury
- Neckline: Soft scoop or subtle V-neck

**Trousers:**

- Color: Warm taupe, beige, or camel
- Style: Wide-leg
- Fit: High-waisted, flowing wide leg, floor-grazing hem
- Fabric: Tailored but with soft drape
- Silhouette: Creates elongated elegant line

**Belt:**

- Style: Leather belt
- Color: Coordinating neutral (tan, cognac, or matching trousers)
- Width: Medium refined width
- Purpose: Defines waist, adds polish

**Shoes:**

- Style: Pointed-toe heels
- Color: Neutral (nude, taupe, beige, or matching trousers)
- Height: Medium heel (2-3 inches)
- Quality: Sleek leather finish

### Accessories

**Handbag:** Structured leather tote in neutral tone (taupe, cognac, ivory)
**Jewelry:** Minimal gold - simple hoops or studs, delicate chain necklace, thin bracelet
**Watch:** Optional simple elegant watch
**Styling:** No unnecessary embellishments - pure minimalism

### Setting & Environment

**Background:** Minimal clean backdrop - warm cream, soft white, or subtle architectural element
**Lighting:** Natural daylight, soft and even, no harsh shadows
**Atmosphere:** Generous white space, breathing room, quiet luxury
**Color Palette:** Monochromatic neutrals - ivory, taupe, beige, warm tones

### Technical Specifications

**Format:** Full-length portrait showing complete silhouette
**Composition:** Centered with emphasis on clean lines and vertical elegance
**Focus:** Sharp on fabric texture and proportions
**Style:** Editorial minimalism, not busy or overly styled
**Quality:** High-resolution magazine-quality

### Restrictions

Avoid: Busy patterns, bright colors, excessive jewelry, complicated styling, harsh lighting, cluttered backgrounds, trendy details, over-accessorizing
