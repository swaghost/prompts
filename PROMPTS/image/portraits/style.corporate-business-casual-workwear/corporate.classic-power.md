# Corporate Workwear - Classic Power

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, MidJourney (--style raw), Flux (Photography preset)

**Technical Specifications:** Professional editorial photography, full-length portrait, clean background or professional office setting, natural daylight, luxury fashion editorial aesthetic

---

## Prompt Description

A full-length luxury editorial fashion portrait of a professional woman in a Classic Power corporate outfit. Camel tailored blazer over ivory fitted knit tank, black straight-leg trousers, black pointed-toe heels. Structured black leather tote, minimal gold jewelry (hoops, delicate necklace, simple bracelet). Hair styled in loose waves with center part. The image should feel like a premium Nordstrom Personal Styling campaign or Vogue editorial - polished, confident, and instantly pulled together with warm neutral tones and elegant professional styling.

---

## Complete Prompt

A full-length professional editorial fashion photograph of a woman wearing a camel tailored blazer over an ivory fitted knit tank top, black straight-leg trousers, and black pointed-toe heels. She carries a structured black leather tote bag and wears minimal gold jewelry including small hoops, a delicate necklace, and a simple bracelet. Her hair is styled in loose waves with a center part. The setting is a clean professional environment with natural daylight. The image should have a luxury fashion editorial aesthetic reminiscent of Nordstrom Personal Styling, Banana Republic, or Vogue - warm neutral tones, generous white space, refined composition, quiet luxury styling. Polished, confident, and timeless.

## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Styling

**Subject:** Professional woman, confident presence
**Hair:** Loose waves with center part
**Makeup:** Natural, polished, professional
**Overall Mood:** Polished, tailored, powerful, instantly pulled together
**Aesthetic:** Luxury editorial, quiet luxury, modern classic

### Clothing Details

**Blazer:**

- Color: Camel (warm tan/beige)
- Style: Tailored, structured
- Fit: Hits just below hip for balanced proportions
- Quality: Premium fabric, clean lines

**Top:**

- Color: Ivory
- Style: Fitted knit tank
- Fit: Smooth, polished layer under blazer

**Trousers:**

- Color: Black
- Style: Straight-leg
- Fit: Mid-to-high rise, ankle-grazing hem
- Quality: Tailored, crisp

**Shoes:**

- Style: Pointed-toe heels
- Color: Black leather
- Height: Professional heel height (2-3 inches)

### Accessories

**Handbag:** Structured black leather tote (medium size for balanced proportions)
**Jewelry:** Minimal gold - small hoops, delicate necklace, simple bracelet
**Watch:** Optional elegant timepiece
**Sunglasses:** Optional classic black oversized sunglasses

### Setting & Environment

**Background:** Clean professional environment - office interior, neutral backdrop, or minimal urban setting
**Lighting:** Natural daylight, soft and even
**Atmosphere:** Luxury editorial with generous white space
**Color Palette:** Warm neutrals - camel, ivory, black with gold accents

### Technical Specifications

**Format:** Full-length portrait showing complete outfit
**Composition:** Centered or slightly off-center, showing head to shoes
**Focus:** Sharp detail on clothing texture and tailoring
**Style:** Professional editorial photography, not casual or lifestyle
**Quality:** High-resolution, magazine-quality imagery

### Restrictions

Avoid: Busy backgrounds, harsh lighting, casual poses, trendy styling, overstated jewelry, bright colors, patterns, distressed fabrics, informal settings

## Why This Outfit Works

Classic, confident, and instantly pulled together with minimal effort. The camel-ivory-black combination creates sophisticated contrast. Tailored blazer adds structure and authority. Straight-leg trousers elongate while maintaining professional polish. Minimal gold jewelry adds refinement without distraction. This is a timeless executive presence outfit that works for presentations, important meetings, and leadership moments.
