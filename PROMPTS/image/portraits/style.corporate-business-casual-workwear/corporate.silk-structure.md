# Corporate Workwear - Silk + Structure

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, MidJourney (--style raw), Flux (Photography preset)

**Technical Specifications:** Professional editorial photography, full-length portrait, office or minimal background, natural daylight, luxury professional aesthetic

---

## Prompt Description

A full-length luxury editorial fashion portrait of a professional woman in a Silk + Structure corporate outfit combining feminine elegance with tailored precision. Ivory silk blouse with soft drape paired with black straight-leg trousers. Black block heels, structured black handbag, gold jewelry. Hair styled naturally or in polished waves. The image should convey professional femininity and refined polish - perfect for presentations, executive meetings, or important client interactions. Nordstrom Personal Styling or Banana Republic editorial aesthetic with sophisticated neutral palette and clean executive presence.

---

## Complete Prompt

A full-length professional editorial fashion photograph of a woman wearing an ivory silk blouse with soft elegant drape paired with black straight-leg trousers. She wears black block heels and carries a structured black leather handbag. Gold jewelry including hoops and a delicate necklace completes the look. Her hair is styled in natural polished waves or a refined updo. The setting is a professional office environment or minimal clean background with natural daylight. The image should embody refined professional femininity reminiscent of Nordstrom Personal Styling or Vogue Business - ivory and black color contrast, silk and structure balance, polished elegance. Professional, feminine, and sophisticated.

## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Styling

**Subject:** Professional woman, executive presence
**Hair:** Polished natural waves, refined updo, or sleek styling
**Makeup:** Polished professional with subtle elegance
**Overall Mood:** Professional, feminine, polished, sophisticated
**Aesthetic:** Executive elegance, refined femininity, structured polish

### Clothing Details

**Blouse:**

- Color: Ivory (soft white/cream)
- Fabric: Silk - luxurious drape and subtle sheen
- Style: Classic button-front or elegant neckline
- Fit: Tailored but with feminine drape
- Details: Soft collar, refined buttons, quality construction
- Neckline: Soft scoop, square neck, open collar, or subtle V-neck

**Trousers:**

- Color: Black
- Style: Straight-leg, tailored
- Fit: Mid-to-high rise, ankle-grazing hem
- Fabric: Quality wool blend or structured cotton
- Silhouette: Clean lines, professional cut

**Shoes:**

- Style: Block heels
- Color: Black leather
- Height: Medium block heel (2-3 inches)
- Quality: Sleek professional finish
- Comfort: Stable block heel for all-day wear

### Accessories

**Handbag:** Structured black leather handbag - medium size, professional shape
**Jewelry:** Gold - hoops or studs, delicate necklace, simple bracelet or watch
**Belt:** Optional refined leather belt if desired
**Styling:** Polished and intentional without over-accessorizing

### Setting & Environment

**Background:** Professional office setting, conference room, minimal executive backdrop
**Lighting:** Natural daylight, soft even lighting emphasizing silk texture
**Atmosphere:** Executive polish, professional environment
**Color Palette:** Ivory and black with gold accents - classic professional contrast

### Technical Specifications

**Format:** Full-length portrait showing complete professional silhouette
**Composition:** Confident executive presence, centered or power stance
**Focus:** Sharp detail on silk fabric texture and tailored trousers
**Style:** Professional editorial, executive portrait quality
**Quality:** High-resolution, premium business photography

### Restrictions

Avoid: Casual settings, overly trendy styling, loud patterns, excessive jewelry, informal poses, harsh lighting, distracting backgrounds, wrinkled fabric

## Why This Outfit Works

Professional, feminine, and polished enough for presentations or executive meetings. The combination of silk (feminine) and structure (tailored) creates perfect balance for high-visibility professional moments. This outfit commands respect while maintaining elegance.
