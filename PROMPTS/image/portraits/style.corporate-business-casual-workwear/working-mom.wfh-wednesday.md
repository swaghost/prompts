# Working Mom Wardrobe - WFH Wednesday

## Platform & Settings

**Recommended AI Platform:** Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, MidJourney (--style raw), Flux (Photography preset)

**Technical Specifications:** Professional lifestyle photography, full-length or upper-body portrait, home office setting, natural window light, elevated comfortable aesthetic

**Time to Assemble:** ⏱ 6 Minutes

---

## Prompt Description

A professional lifestyle portrait of a working mother in a Work From Home Wednesday outfit combining comfort with camera-ready polish. Elevated matching knit lounge set in warm neutral tone (ivory, camel, oat, or soft grey). Minimal gold jewelry, hair clipped back naturally. The image should convey comfortable intentional styling - polished enough for Zoom meetings while remaining genuinely comfortable for full day at home. Natural home office setting with warm lighting, relaxed but professional presence. Perfect balance of comfort and credibility.

---

## Complete Prompt

A professional lifestyle photograph of a woman wearing an elevated matching knit lounge set in warm oat or camel tones - coordinating knit top and matching pants or joggers. She wears minimal gold jewelry including small studs and a delicate necklace. Her hair is pulled back naturally with a simple clip or loose low bun. She is in a comfortable home office setting with natural window light, perhaps working at a desk with laptop or sitting in comfortable chair. The image should embody elevated work-from-home style reminiscent of Jenni Kayne or Nordstrom at-home collections - intentionally comfortable, Zoom-ready polish, warm neutrals, soft natural light. Comfortable enough for all-day wear while looking intentional on camera.

## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Styling

**Subject:** Professional working mother, home-based work mode
**Hair:** Natural pulled-back styling - loose clip, low bun, simple ponytail
**Makeup:** Natural everyday look, camera-ready but minimal
**Overall Mood:** Comfortable, intentional, Zoom-ready, genuinely relaxed
**Time to Dress:** 6 minutes including quick hair clip
**Aesthetic:** Elevated comfort, intentional loungewear, WFH polish

### Clothing Details

**Lounge Set:**

- Color: Warm neutrals - oat, camel, ivory, soft grey, warm beige
- Style: Elevated matching knit set
- Top: Relaxed knit top - could be slightly oversized sweater or refined knit tee
- Bottom: Matching knit pants or elevated joggers - not athletic wear
- Fabric: Soft premium knit - cashmere blend, merino, or luxury cotton knit
- Quality: Looks intentional, not pajamas
- Comfort: All-day wearability, soft against skin

**Footwear:**

- Comfortable flats, cozy neutral slippers, or barefoot at home
- Quality: Elevated house shoes if visible on camera
- Reality: Actual comfort for home-based work

### Accessories

**Jewelry:** Minimal gold kept simple - small studs, delicate necklace
**Hair Accessories:** Simple claw clip, fabric scrunchie, or elastic tie
**Glasses:** Optional elegant reading glasses if applicable
**Coffee/Tea:** Lifestyle element - warm beverage nearby

### Outfit Formula Breakdown

**Pieces:**

- Knit matching set
- Gold jewelry
- Hair clip
- Comfortable flats or barefoot styling

**Why It Works:**
Comfortable enough for home while still looking intentional on camera. Matching sets create cohesive put-together appearance without thinking. Neutral colors are flattering on video calls. Soft knit fabric feels good all day. Can transition from Zoom meetings to household tasks seamlessly.

### Setting & Environment

**Background:** Home office with natural light - desk, comfortable seating, plants, books
**Lighting:** Soft natural window light, warm and flattering
**Atmosphere:** Comfortable productivity, intentional casual
**Props:** Laptop, notebook, coffee cup, cozy work-from-home elements
**Context:** Virtual meetings, focused work time, comfortable professionalism

### Time-Saving Strategy

- Matching set eliminates outfit decisions
- Pull from drawer, put on, done
- Hair pulled back in under 1 minute
- Jewelry already on from day before
- No shoes needed if staying home
- Comfortable enough to sleep in, polished enough for video
- Zero ironing or complicated styling

### Zoom-Ready Factors

- Neutral colors look good on camera
- Soft knit has flattering texture on video
- Neckline appropriate for video framing
- Hair pulled back looks neat and intentional
- Minimal jewelry doesn't distract
- Overall look is professional from waist up

### Restrictions

Avoid: Actual pajamas, sloppy fit, bright distracting colors, loud patterns, athletic wear, anything that reads as "didn't try," overly casual for credibility
