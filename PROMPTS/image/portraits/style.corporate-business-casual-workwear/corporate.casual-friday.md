## Prompt Description

A full-length luxury editorial fashion portrait of a professional woman in a polished Casual Friday outfit that maintains professionalism while introducing relaxed elements. Dark straight-leg denim paired with fine knit black tee, relaxed camel blazer layered over. White leather sneakers, black leather tote, minimal gold jewelry. Hair in natural effortless waves. The image should convey elevated casual professionalism - relaxed without sacrificing polish, perfect for casual Fridays, creative offices, or transitioning from work to after-work activities. Sophisticated denim styling with Nordstrom or Who What Wear aesthetic.

A full-length professional editorial fashion photograph of a woman wearing dark straight-leg denim jeans paired with a fine knit black tee and a relaxed camel blazer. Her hair is styled in natural effortless waves. The setting is a casual professional environment or urban backdrop with natural daylight. ## Structured Breakdown

### Identity & Styling

**Subject:** Professional woman with modern casual style
**Hair:** Natural effortless waves, relaxed texture
**Makeup:** Natural, minimal, fresh
**Overall Mood:** Relaxed without sacrificing professionalism, elevated casual
**Aesthetic:** Smart casual, modern professional, sophisticated comfort

### Clothing Details

**Blazer:**

- Color: Camel (warm tan/beige)
- Style: Relaxed blazer - softer structure than formal tailored
- Fit: Comfortable, slightly oversized or boyfriend fit
- Fabric: Softer wool blend, linen blend, or comfortable structured fabric
- Styling: Can be worn open and relaxed

**Tee:**

- Color: Black or white
- Style: Fine knit tee
- Fit: Fitted or slightly relaxed, high quality
- Fabric: Premium cotton or fine knit
- Neckline: Crew neck or subtle V-neck
- Quality: Elevated basics, not basic tee

**Jeans:**

- Color: Dark denim (dark indigo or black denim)
- Style: Straight-leg jeans
- Fit: Mid-to-high rise, straight through leg, ankle-length
- Wash: Dark clean wash, no distressing
- Quality: Premium denim with structure

**Shoes:**

- Style: White leather sneakers
- Quality: Premium clean leather, not athletic
- Brand aesthetic: Veja, Common Projects, or similar minimal luxury sneakers
- Condition: Pristine white, polished

### Accessories

**Handbag:** Black leather tote - structured professional style
**Jewelry:** Minimal gold - small hoops or studs, delicate necklace, simple bracelet
**Watch:** Optional casual-elegant timepiece
**Styling:** Intentionally minimal, elevated basics

### Setting & Environment

**Background:** Casual professional setting - modern office, coffee shop, urban exterior, or creative workspace
**Lighting:** Natural daylight, bright and fresh
**Atmosphere:** Comfortable confidence, modern professionalism
**Color Palette:** Dark denim, black, camel, white with gold accents

### Technical Specifications

**Format:** Full-length portrait showing complete casual-professional look
**Composition:** Relaxed confident stance, natural posture
**Focus:** Sharp on premium denim and sneakers, showing quality details
**Style:** Modern editorial casual, lifestyle professional
**Quality:** High-resolution, relatable yet polished

### Restrictions

Avoid: Overly distressed denim, athletic sneakers, sloppy fit, overly casual setting, wrinkled blazer, dirty sneakers, excessive jewelry, busy patterns
