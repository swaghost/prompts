## Core Concept

Ultra-realistic luxury editorial fashion photography on a Maldives white sand beach right after sunset (blue hour). Lying on soft white sand, leaning on arm with body positioned diagonally. ## Detailed Prompt

```
Create an ultra-realistic luxury editorial photograph on a white sand beach in the Maldives right after sunset (blue hour). I am lying on white soft sand, leaning lightly on my arm with my body positioned diagonally in the image. My gaze is directed straight at the camera, my expression calm, confident, and sophisticated, and my hair is beautifully and delicately combed, gently swaying with the ocean breeze.

I am wearing a luxurious long ocean turquoise dress, completely embroidered with thousands of Swarovski crystals and tiny sequins. The dress hugs my figure perfectly, shimmering beautifully under the flash, creating a liquid gem effect.

She holds a large golden designer handbag, sculpted with soft metallic waves. The jewelry includes a wide gold bracelet, delicate rings, and diamond earrings. Her makeup is editorial sophistication with radiant skin.

Background: white sand, palm tree silhouettes, luxurious beach villas in the distance, a calm ocean, and an intense sunset sky with tones ranging from gold to deep rose-purple and deep blue.

Lighting: editorial flash photography that intensely illuminates the face and dress, maintaining an atmospheric, deep, and cinematic background.

Color palette: turquoise, sea blue, gold, white, sand, rose-purple, and deep blue.
```
