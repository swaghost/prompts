# Luxury Editorial - Open Yacht Deck White Fluid Ensemble Freedom

## Prompt Type

Fashion Editorial Photography - Luxury Yacht Lifestyle

## Primary Platform

ChatGPT/DALL-E, MidJourney, Adobe Firefly, Flux Photography Preset

## Core Concept

Maintain my appearance 100% faithful to the photo sent: face, eye shape and color, eyebrows, nose, lips, cheekbone structure, skin tone and texture, and age. Do not alter my visual features nor mix with reference model's face. Use only my photo as reference for my appearance.

## Detailed Prompt

```
Create an ultra-realistic luxury fashion photograph on the open deck of a superyacht sailing on the open sea. The camera captures me full body from a slightly above angle. Behind me, the deep blue ocean with the white imposing wake of the yacht creates a sensation of movement and freedom.

I am standing at the varnished wood railing of the yacht with both hands in my hair which is loose in the wind. My face is slightly turned to the sun, my eyes semi-closed, my expression calm and confident. My gaze is sophisticated and elegant, my hair is beautifully combed, delicately swaying with the ocean breeze.

I am wearing an elegant impeccable white mesh fluid outfit: an asymmetric falling tomara-que-caia top with a long unique front scarf and a long high-waist cinched skirt with slight draping at the waist. The look valorizes my silhouette creating a luxurious minimalist visual. I wear stylish smoky transparent lens sunglasses and jewelry. Minimalist jewelry—a thin bracelet and earrings. No brand logos.

The deck has a natural teak wood finish, polished wood, and white metal details. The lighting is strong midday sunlight, rich sea colors, realistic water reflections, and natural shine on skin without artificial effect.
```

## Identity Preservation Settings

**ChatGPT/DALL-E:**

- Upload reference photo maintaining 100% facial similarity
- Do not alter face shape, eye shape and color, eyebrows, nose, lips, cheekbone structure
- Do not alter skin tone, texture, or age
- Do not mix with reference model's face features
- Use only my photo as appearance reference
- Specify "exact person on luxury yacht"

**MidJourney:**

- Use `--cref [reference_url]` for character consistency
- Add `--cw 100` for maximum identity preservation
- Use `--style raw` for ultra-photorealistic yacht photography
- Consider `--ar 2:3` or `--ar 4:5` for full-body vertical format
