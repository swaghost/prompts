## Structured Breakdown

### Setting & Environment

**Venue:** Grand opera house hall
**Key Elements:** Marble staircase, bronze statue, candlelight sources
**Time of Day:** Blue hour dusk (transition period after sunset)
**Lighting Zones:** Golden candlelight in immediate area, indigo shadow in distant halls
**Atmosphere:** Romantic cinematic

### Subject & Composition

**Subject:** Young Caucasian man, mid-20s, model-like features, sandy hair
**Pose:** Walking up stairs, caught mid-step
**Action:** In motion during twilight transition
**Framing:** Straight-on full-length capturing movement

### Lighting (Blue Hour + Candlelight)

**Natural Light:** Blue hour dusk - cool indigo tones
**Artificial Light:** Golden candlelight - warm amber tones
**Light Interaction:** Cool blue distant shadows contrasting warm golden immediate illumination
**Transition Quality:** Day-to-evening atmospheric shift
**Color Temperature Contrast:** Cool indigo vs. warm golden creating romantic depth
**Reach of Light:** Candlelight has limited reach, hall deepens to shadow beyond

### Clothing & Style

Black leather jacket, dark knit sweater, white dress shirt peeking out, black trousers, white sneakers

### Restrictions

Avoid: Bright daylight, full artificial lighting throughout, single color temperature, static pose, midday atmosphere, harsh transitions
