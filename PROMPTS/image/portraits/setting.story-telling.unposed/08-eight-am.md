# Unposed 08 - Eight AM

**Source:** BLUUU.AI, Unposed Vol. 001, Prompt 08

## Description of Resulting Image

Candid overhead phone portrait of a model lying on crisp hotel linen, caught between a warm diagonal blade of sun and cool early-morning shadow.

## Usage

Hotel and sleepwear campaigns, intimate morning editorials, natural beauty content, and quiet travel lifestyle photography.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney, Flux.

## Actor or Subject

American model in her early twenties with long dirty blonde hair fanned across the pillow, bare fresh skin, pale blue eyes, and soft pink lips.

## Prerequisites

None. Optional subject reference for identity consistency.

## Included Effects

Hard diagonal sunlight, cool shadow, fine facial hairs, visible skin texture, crisp linen, unedited phone-camera realism.

## Included Camera Frames

- Direct overhead portrait
- Side-lying pose across bed
- 3:4 vertical crop

## Image Prompt

```text
Candid overhead phone photo of an American model in her early twenties lying on her side across crisp white hotel bed linen in early morning light. Long dirty blonde hair fanned messily across the pillow, bare fresh skin with no makeup, clear pale blue eyes looking straight up into the lens, soft pink lips slightly parted, one arm folded under her head. She wears a white ribbed cotton tank top with thin straps, white duvet pushed down around her. A hard diagonal blade of golden sunlight cuts across her face and chest from a gap in the curtains, the rest of the frame in cool shadow. Extreme natural contrast between warm sun and blue shade, visible fine hairs and skin texture, unedited phone camera realism, vertical 3:4 crop.
```
