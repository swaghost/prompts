# Unposed 03 - Open Air

**Source:** BLUUU.AI, Unposed Vol. 001, Prompt 03

## Description of Resulting Image

Candid low-angle phone photograph of a model watching an outdoor movie at night, wrapped in a mohair scarf with popcorn and picnic blankets in the foreground.

## Usage

Outdoor cinema campaigns, casual fashion editorials, summer-night lifestyle content, and authentic social photography.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney, Flux.

## Actor or Subject

American model in her early twenties with long tousled light brown hair, dewy highlighted skin, honey-brown eyes, and glossy lips parted mid-gasp.

## Prerequisites

None. Optional subject reference for identity consistency.

## Included Effects

Cool projection light, warm string-light bokeh, night grain, handheld phone realism, soft foreground food detail.

## Included Camera Frames

- Low-angle seated portrait
- Knees pulled to chest
- 3:4 vertical crop

## Image Prompt

```text
Candid low-angle phone photo at an outdoor movie screening on a grass lawn at night. An American model in her early twenties with long tousled light brown hair, dewy highlighted skin, honey-brown eyes, glossy lips parted mid-gasp as she looks up and away at the glowing projection screen. She wears a soft butter yellow oversized hoodie with a blue and grey mohair blanket scarf draped over her shoulders, bare legs pulled up to her chest. One hand holds a single piece of popcorn near her mouth, chrome ring on her finger, almond French-tip nails. A large red popcorn bag rests in the foreground, striped picnic blankets and blurred silhouettes of other viewers behind. Cool screen light on her face mixed with a warm string-light bokeh line across the background, night grain, handheld phone realism, vertical 3:4 crop.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Camera:** Handheld low-angle phone
- **Lighting:** Cool screen light with warm string lights
- **Finish:** Night grain and candid realism

## Color Palette

Butter yellow, blue-grey, red, grass green, cool night tones, warm string-light amber.

## Mood & Atmosphere

Wonderstruck, social, cozy, spontaneous, nocturnal.

## Quality Keywords

Outdoor cinema, phone realism, mohair texture, popcorn, night grain, candid fashion.
