# Unposed 05 - Morning Pastry

**Source:** BLUUU.AI, Unposed Vol. 001, Prompt 05

## Description of Resulting Image

Candid bakery phone portrait of a model mid-bite with a flaky butter croissant, pinstripe blazer, messy ponytail, and deadpan sideways glance.

## Usage

Cafe and bakery campaigns, relaxed tailoring editorials, food-lifestyle photography, and authentic morning content.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney, Flux.

## Actor or Subject

French model in her early twenties with dirty blonde messy high ponytail, flyaways, freckled bare skin, pale green eyes, and soft mauve lip.

## Prerequisites

None. Optional subject reference for identity consistency.

## Included Effects

Flaky pastry texture, bakery depth, soft window daylight, shallow depth of field, natural grain, candid phone framing.

## Included Camera Frames

- Medium close-up at counter
- Pastry and both hands prominent
- 3:4 vertical crop

## Image Prompt

```text
Candid phone photo inside a small pastel bakery in the morning. A French model in her early twenties seated at a marble counter, dirty blonde hair scraped up into a messy high ponytail with loose flyaways, bare freckled skin, pale green eyes, soft mauve lip. She wears a charcoal pinstripe oversized tailored blazer layered over a white long-sleeve tee, chunky gold hoop earring, gold band ring. Both hands hold a half-eaten flaky butter croissant up to her mouth, mid-bite, eyes flicking sideways to the lens with a deadpan look. Glass display cases of viennoiserie behind her, checkerboard pastel tile wall, ceramic jugs on open shelving, a used cappuccino cup in the foreground. Soft daylight from a window at left, shallow depth of field, natural grain, vertical 3:4 crop.
```
