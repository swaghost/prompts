# Unposed 09 - Long Lunch

**Source:** BLUUU.AI, Unposed Vol. 001, Prompt 09

## Description of Resulting Image

Candid restaurant phone photograph of a laughing model raising a chilled white-wine flute at a bright window table, surrounded by food, stemware, and blurred diners.

## Usage

Restaurant campaigns, resort and travel editorials, summer fashion, hospitality marketing, and social lifestyle photography.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney, Flux.

## Actor or Subject

American model in her early twenties with long sun-lightened blonde balayage, dewy sunburnt-flushed skin, gold glitter eyeliner, and a laughing side-turned expression.

## Prerequisites

None. Optional subject reference for identity consistency.

## Included Effects

Bounced window light, soft film-like grain, glass highlights, restaurant depth, candid laughter, natural skin texture.

## Included Camera Frames

- Medium close-up at window table
- Glass and hand near chin
- 3:4 vertical crop

## Image Prompt

```text
Candid phone photo at a window table in a bright white-tablecloth restaurant during a long lunch. An American model in her early twenties with long sun-lightened blonde balayage hair, glowing dewy sunburnt-flushed skin, gold glitter eyeliner along the lash line, laughing with her head turned to the side toward someone off camera. She wears a white crepe wrap dress printed with small yellow and pink wildflowers, three-quarter cuffed sleeves, a tiny diamond stud and a long delicate gold chain drop earring. One hand raises a tall champagne flute of chilled white wine near her chin, soft pink manicure. Plates of tomato bruschetta and stemware on the table in the foreground, blurred diners and a round paper globe pendant lamp behind. Warm bounced daylight from a tall window at left, soft film-like grain, vertical 3:4 crop.
```

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Camera:** Candid phone photo

- **Finish:** Soft film-like grain and natural highlights
