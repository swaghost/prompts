# Unposed 02 - Rain Window

**Source:** BLUUU.AI, Unposed Vol. 001, Prompt 02

## Description of Resulting Image

Candid phone portrait of a French model curled into a leather booth beside a fogged rain-streaked cafe window, warming her face with a steaming cappuccino bowl.

## Usage

Rainy cafe editorials, knitwear campaigns, travel lifestyle content, and intimate low-light phone photography.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney, Flux.

## Actor or Subject

French model in her early twenties with deep auburn hair in a sleek low ponytail, flushed bare skin, pale green eyes, minimal makeup, and glossy natural lips.

## Prerequisites

None. Optional subject reference for identity consistency.

## Included Effects

Rain-streaked glass, window fog, steam, amber-versus-blue mixed light, grainy low-light phone capture, visible skin texture.

## Included Camera Frames

- Curled seated portrait
- Face and upper body with knees drawn up
- 3:4 vertical crop

## Image Prompt

```text
Candid phone photo of a French model in her early twenties curled into a leather booth seat beside a fogged, rain-streaked cafe window. Deep auburn hair slicked back into a low sleek ponytail, bare glowing skin with flushed pink cheeks and nose, pale green eyes, minimal makeup and glossy natural lip. She wears an oversized oatmeal cable-knit wool sweater with a charcoal scarf tucked at the neck, dark grey wide-leg denim, knees pulled up to her chest. Both hands cradle a wide white ceramic cappuccino bowl lifted just under her chin, steam rising, faint smile with a soft knowing look into the lens. Warm amber wall sconces glowing through the mist behind her, cold blue daylight outside, wooden window frames. Grainy low-light phone capture, natural highlights, real skin texture, vertical 3:4 crop.
```

- **Camera:** Grainy low-light phone capture
