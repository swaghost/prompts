# Unposed 06 - Stem Work

**Source:** BLUUU.AI, Unposed Vol. 001, Prompt 06

## Description of Resulting Image

Candid evening phone photograph inside a Parisian flower shop, showing a florist adjusting a blush rose in a glass cylinder among lush stems and warm shop light.

## Usage

Florist branding, lifestyle editorials, craft and maker campaigns, and natural work-in-progress portraits.

## Engines/Models

Higgsfield Soul 2.0, Midjourney, Flux.

## Actor or Subject

Latina model in her mid-twenties with dark hair in a low messy bun, face-framing tendrils, bronzed dewy skin, terracotta eyeshadow, glossy nude lip, and gold huggie earrings.

## Prerequisites

None. Optional subject reference for identity consistency.

## Included Effects

Glass reflections, flower texture, warm pendant light, ambient shop light, natural skin texture, candid profile framing.

## Included Camera Frames

- Profile medium portrait
- Hands and rose stem in working focus
- 3:4 vertical crop

## Image Prompt

```text
Candid phone photo inside a Parisian flower shop in the evening. A Latina model in her mid-twenties standing at a dark stone worktop, dark hair twisted into a low messy bun with soft face-framing tendrils, bronzed dewy skin, warm terracotta eyeshadow, glossy nude lip, looking down at her hands in profile. She wears a crisp white poplin oversized shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbow under a deep forest green canvas apron and matching green trousers, gold huggie earrings. One hand adjusts the tall stem of a blush pink rose in a large clear glass cylinder vase. Buckets of eucalyptus, white roses, carnations and baby's breath fill the background, black florist scissors on the counter, a single warm pendant lamp overhead. Soft ambient shop light, realistic skin texture, phone camera look, vertical 3:4 crop.
```

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** 3:4 vertical
- **Camera:** Candid phone photo, profile view
- **Lighting:** Soft ambient shop light and warm pendant
- **Finish:** Natural skin and glass/flower detail

## Color Palette

Forest green, crisp white, blush pink, eucalyptus, dark stone, warm amber.

## Mood & Atmosphere

Focused, tactile, gentle, handmade, quietly romantic.

## Quality Keywords

Flower shop realism, florist portrait, rose stem, glass vase, phone camera, natural skin texture.
