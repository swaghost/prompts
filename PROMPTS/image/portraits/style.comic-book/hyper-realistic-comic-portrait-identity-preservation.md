# Hyper-Realistic Comic Book Portrait with Identity Preservation

## Concept Title

**Realistic Comic-Book Character Portrait with Face Reference Preservation**

## Reference Image Instructions

Upload your reference image and then copy the prompt below.

## Core Identity Preservation Prompt

Use the uploaded image as the primary identity reference. Preserve the subject's exact recognizable facial identity, facial structure, apparent age, natural skin tone, eye shape and natural eye color, eyebrow shape, nose, lips, jawline, hairstyle characteristics, hair color, facial hair if present, and natural body proportions. Maintain maximum facial similarity to the uploaded subject. Do not beautify, de-age, reshape, replace, or significantly alter recognizable facial features.

## Style & Rendering

Create a hyper-detailed realistic comic-book color portrait combining believable human anatomy and facial realism with richly painted graphic-novel rendering. The finished image should look like premium modern comic-book cover art: realistic proportions, dimensional skin, expressive brushwork, bold yet controlled outlines, painterly texture, finely rendered clothing, dramatic highlights, deep shadows, and sophisticated cinematic color grading. Avoid flat cartoon rendering, cel shading, anime styling, exaggerated superhero anatomy, or overly clean digital 3D rendering.

## Composition & Perspective

Show the subject from a dramatic low-angle perspective, as though the camera is positioned near waist or hip level and looking upward. Frame the subject vertically from approximately the upper thighs to above the head, allowing the torso, face, arms, belt area, and accessories to dominate the composition. Give the figure a powerful, imposing presence while keeping realistic human proportions.

## Pose & Body Language

Position the subject standing naturally with the torso angled slightly away from the camera while the head turns toward or slightly past the viewer. Keep the shoulders relaxed but confident. One arm should hang naturally down toward the foreground, appearing slightly larger because of perspective without becoming anatomically distorted.

## Character Details – Facial Features

Give the subject a rugged, weathered appearance appropriate for a mature action-oriented graphic-novel character. Preserve realistic facial texture, natural wrinkles, pores, beard or stubble texture if present, and individual hair strands. Hair may appear slightly tousled and imperfect. If the uploaded subject has facial hair, preserve its natural shape, density, and color.

## Character Details – Accessories

Add dark, round or aviator-style sunglasses with realistic reflective lenses when appropriate to the desired character styling. Reflections should be subtle and integrated naturally into the environment rather than obscuring the facial structure.

Place a thick cigar naturally at one corner of the mouth, angled slightly outward. Render the cigar with detailed tobacco wrapping, a glowing ember, ash at the tip, and a thin trail of pale smoke drifting upward and curling naturally into the background.

## Character Details – Clothing

Dress the subject in a rugged dark charcoal, black, or desaturated blue-gray tactical-style jacket with rolled or pushed-up sleeves. Include realistic seams, pockets, zippers, snaps, shoulder details, patches, worn fabric, wrinkles, scuffs, and subtle weathering. Leave the jacket partially open over a muted light-colored shirt or undershirt. Keep the clothing grounded and functional rather than futuristic armor.

## Character Details – Equipment

Add a heavy utility belt with realistic pouches, buckles, holster components, worn leather details, metallic hardware, and practical equipment. A handgun may be positioned naturally at the side or held loosely downward beside the thigh, remaining secondary to the subject rather than becoming the main focal point. Keep all hands, fingers, grip, weapon proportions, and equipment mechanically believable.

If visible, add subtle tattoos or rugged skin details to the forearms while preserving the uploaded subject's natural anatomy. Do not cover important identifying facial features with tattoos or unnecessary markings.

## Environment & Background

Create an atmospheric near-future urban city environment behind the subject, viewed from the same low perspective. Include tall weathered buildings rising vertically, faint architectural details, hazy distant structures, large graphic signs or illuminated advertising panels, and occasional red signage. Keep background elements partially obscured by atmospheric haze so the subject remains dominant.

## Color Palette

Use a distinctive realistic comic-book color palette inspired by hand-painted graphic novels: muted turquoise and dusty teal skies, warm ochre and burnt-orange skin highlights, charcoal and blue-gray clothing, faded cream architecture, rusty brown details, and controlled crimson-red signage. Colors should feel sun-faded, slightly desaturated, weathered, and cinematic rather than bright or glossy.

## Lighting

Light the subject with strong warm directional sunlight, creating golden-orange highlights along the forehead, cheekbones, beard, arms, jacket edges, and hands. Balance this with cool teal ambient light in the shadows. Use dramatic chiaroscuro, strong dimensional modeling, subtle rim lighting, and naturally softened shadow transitions.

## Texture & Detail

Render the entire image with visible painterly comic-book texture: layered brush strokes, slightly rough ink-like edges, finely textured surfaces, controlled cross-hatching where appropriate, and subtle print-inspired grain. Maintain enough fine detail that the face, beard, clothing, skin, metal, leather, smoke, and surrounding architecture still feel convincingly realistic.

## Focal Points

Keep the face and upper torso as the strongest focal points. Use atmospheric perspective and softer detail toward the distant background. Preserve sharp facial features and recognizable identity despite the graphic-novel treatment.

## Negative Prompt

Avoid oversized heads, exaggerated muscles, distorted hands, extra fingers, malformed weapons, warped sunglasses, duplicate accessories, floating objects, plastic-looking skin, flat colors, overly saturated comic colors, cartoon proportions, anime features, superhero costumes, or excessive futuristic technology.

## Aspect Ratio

**4:5**

Vertical portrait composition, full frame height emphasis, cinematic poster layout.
