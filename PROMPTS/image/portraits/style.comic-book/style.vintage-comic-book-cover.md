# Vintage Comic Book Cover Style

**Platform:** ChatGPT Image Generation (GPT-Image)  
**Style:** Vintage Archie Comics / Romance Comics (1950s–1970s)  
**Format:** 4:5 aspect ratio  
**Input:** Portrait/reference photo  
**Output:** Fully designed comic book cover with identity-preserved illustrated character

---

## Overview

Transform any portrait into a premium vintage American comic book cover inspired by classic Archie Comics and romance comics from the 1950s–1970s. The person's facial identity remains recognizable while being converted into beautifully illustrated comic art with complete comic cover layout including logo, speech bubbles, badges, and vintage printing effects.

### Art Style

- Vintage Archie / Romance comic illustration
- Hand-inked line art
- Flat comic colors
- Halftone printing texture
- Heavy black outlines
- Slight painterly shading
- Printed paper grain
- Yellowed aged paper
- Fold marks
- Scratches
- Ink imperfections

### Color Palette

- Cream paper
- Warm orange
- Vintage red
- Navy blue
- Muted teal

- Dark brown outlines

### Layout Elements

- Large comic logo at top
- Issue information left column
- Comic code badge
- Speech bubbles
- Burst callouts
- Barcode
- Campus/diner/street background
- Character centered
- Small reference photo inset in one corner
