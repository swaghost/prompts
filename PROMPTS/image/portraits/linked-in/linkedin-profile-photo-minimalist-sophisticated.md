## Image Prompt

**Subject:**
I authorize the use of the reference image to create a minimalist and sophisticated professional portrait for LinkedIn, accurately preserving the real identity of the person, including facial structure, skin tone, hair, beard if any, eyes, nose, mouth, eyebrows, expression and apparent age. Image from the chest up with elegant framing, well-lit face, direct gaze at the camera and calm, firm and confident expression, conveying silent authority, competence and professional presence.

**Setting/Environment:**
Background completely clean and premium, in solid color or with extremely subtle texture. May be graphite gray, petroleum blue, warm beige, off-white or sophisticated neutral brown. Absolutely minimal environment focusing all attention on subject.

**Lighting:**
Refined studio lighting, soft and directional, with controlled contrast, light shadow on the side of the face, natural shine in the eyes and subtle separation between the person and the background. Ultra-realistic lighting creating dimension and depth.

**Composition:**
Portrait from chest up with elegant framing. Well-lit face as focal point. Direct gaze at camera creating connection. Minimalist composition with perfect balance and clean lines.

**Style & Aesthetic:**
Minimalist and sophisticated professional aesthetic. Silent authority and professional presence. Ultra-realistic photography with premium corporate finish. Clean, strong, and elegant visual language.

**Technical Specifications:**
Ultra-realistic photography with 85mm lens. Extremely precise focus on the eyes. Real skin texture preserved. Hair with natural strands visible. Fabric with true materiality rendered. Premium corporate portrait finish. High resolution capture.

**Color Palette:**
Completely clean premium background in solid color. Graphite gray, petroleum blue, warm beige, off-white, or sophisticated neutral brown. Professional discreet clothing in solid colors. Natural balanced skin tones. Sophisticated neutral palette.

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
Silent authority, competence, professional presence, calm, firm, confident, minimalist sophistication, clean elegance, premium quality, understated power, modern professionalism.

**Quality Keywords:**
