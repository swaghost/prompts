# Detective Scene - Complete Workflow

## Overview

Create a cinematic detective scene with a person standing in front of a wall covered with missing person photographs, newspaper clippings, maps, and a red string web connecting clues.

## Step 1: Record Source Video

1. Record a video of yourself looking at a wall
2. Take a screenshot from this video
3. This will be used as your base reference

## Step 2: Generate Detective Scene Image (Nano Banana Pro)

Upload your screenshot to Nano Banana Pro in Higgsfield and use the image generation prompt:

[See DETECTIVE-SCENE-IMAGE-GENERATION.md for the full prompt]

**Key Elements:**

- Preserves original face 100%
- ARRI Alexa cinematic look
- Shallow depth of field
- Wall with missing person photos, clippings, maps, and red string
- Small wall lamp with warm light
- Dark background with bookshelves
- Muted warm colors with film grain

## Step 3: Generate Final Video (Seedance 2.0)

Upload the generated image and original video to Seedance 2.0 and use the video generation prompt:

[See DETECTIVE-SCENE-VIDEO-GENERATION.md for the full prompt]

**Key Elements:**

- Matches visual target from image exactly
- Transfers motion from source video 1:1
- Preserves face, hair, and clothing
- Subtle environment movement
- 4K cinematic quality

## Tools Required

- **Nano Banana Pro** (Higgsfield): Image generation with face preservation
- **Seedance 2.0** (Higgsfield): 4K multimodal video generation
