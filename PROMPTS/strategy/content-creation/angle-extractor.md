# Prompt #1: Angle Extractor

## What It Does

Takes one insight and generates 10 completely different angles from it.

## Why It Works

One insight becomes 10 posts. Without brainstorming.

## Time Saved

2-3 hours of ideation.

## The Prompt

"I have this insight: [INSERT YOUR CORE INSIGHT]. Generate 10 completely different angles I could create content about. Each angle should target a different psychological hook: curiosity, contrast, controversy, aspiration, fear, transformation, validation, insider knowledge, myth-busting, trend-spotting. Make each one a one-sentence hook."

## Usage

Perfect for content creators, social media managers, and marketers who need to generate multiple pieces of content from a single core insight. Ideal for repurposing insights across platforms, maximizing content output, and targeting different audience segments with varied psychological triggers.

## Output Format

The prompt will generate 10 one-sentence hooks, each targeting a different psychological trigger:

1. **Curiosity** - Hook that makes audience want to learn more
2. **Contrast** - Hook highlighting unexpected differences
3. **Controversy** - Hook challenging common beliefs
4. **Aspiration** - Hook showing desired outcome
5. **Fear** - Hook addressing pain points or risks
6. **Transformation** - Hook showing before/after change
7. **Validation** - Hook confirming audience beliefs
8. **Insider Knowledge** - Hook revealing hidden information
9. **Myth-Busting** - Hook debunking misconceptions
10. **Trend-Spotting** - Hook identifying emerging patterns

## Benefits

- Eliminates writer's block
- Multiplies content output from single insights
- Systematically targets different audience psychology
- Creates content variety without repetition
- Saves hours of brainstorming time
- Provides structured approach to content ideation
