# Prompt #4: Example Generator

## What It Does

Fills your post with specific, credible examples (instead of generic advice).

## Why It Works

Generic advice gets scrolled past. Specific examples get saved.

## Time Saved

1-2 hours of research and example hunting.

## The Prompt

"I'm teaching about [CONCEPT]. Generate 5 real-world examples that prove this concept works. For each example: what company/person, what they did, what result they got, why it matters. Make examples from different industries. Format as: Company | Action | Result | Why it matters"

## Usage

Perfect for educational content, case study creation, proof-building content, and demonstrating concepts with real-world validation. Ideal for educators, consultants, coaches, and thought leaders who need credible examples to support their teaching. Essential for making abstract concepts tangible and believable.

## Output Format

The prompt will generate 5 diverse examples with four-part breakdown:

**For Each Example:**

1. **Company/Person** - Specific entity that used the concept
2. **Action** - What they specifically did
3. **Result** - Measurable outcome or achievement
4. **Why It Matters** - Relevance and key takeaway

**Example Structure:**

- Example 1: Tech Company
- Example 2: Consumer Brand
- Example 3: Individual/Creator
- Example 4: Service Business
- Example 5: Non-Profit or Different Sector

## Benefits

- Replaces generic advice with concrete proof
- Increases credibility instantly
- Makes content more engaging and memorable
- Provides social proof
- Demonstrates concept versatility across industries
- Saves hours of research time
- Creates shareable, reference-worthy content
- Builds authority through knowledge demonstration
- Increases saves and bookmarks

## Best Practices

- **Vary Industries** - Shows universal application
- **Use Recognizable Names** - When possible for credibility
- **Include Numbers** - Specific results more compelling
- **Recent Examples** - Demonstrates current relevance
- **Mix Scales** - Small wins + big transformations

## Application Areas

- LinkedIn posts and articles
- Newsletter content
- Course materials and lessons
- Presentation decks
- Blog posts and articles
- Social media educational content
- Case study compilations
- Proof sections in sales materials
