# Reverse Engineer the Storytelling Structure Behind a Viral Post

## Purpose

Deconstruct viral story-based posts to identify the classic story structure elements, inciting incident/tension points, turning point/resolution mechanics, lesson/takeaway delivery, and pacing techniques that kept readers moving forward without scrolling away.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Story-based social media posts, personal narratives, case studies, or anecdotal content that achieved strong retention, engagement, and viral spread through compelling narrative structure rather than just information delivery.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A narrative structure analysis identifying the Setup (who, what, ordinary world), the Inciting Incident or Tension Point creating immediate dramatic interest, the Struggle/Stakes section showing what made the outcome uncertain, the Turning Point or Resolution revealing how the story concluded, the Lesson/Takeaway and its delivery method, and identification of pacing techniques (short sentences, line breaks, cliffhangers) maintaining forward momentum. Generates a blank narrative template based on the identified structure with timestamps and structural notes for reuse.

## Usage

Perfect for narrative structure experts studying viral storytelling, content creators building story-based engagement, and social media strategists analyzing retention mechanics in personal narratives. Ideal for analyzing LinkedIn success stories, Instagram caption storytelling, Twitter/X thread narratives, Facebook storytelling posts, and any platform where personal or case study narratives drive engagement. This prompt excels at revealing the hidden structural choices separating compelling stories from meandering anecdotes while providing replicable frameworks for your own narrative content.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Full text of a story-based post that performed exceptionally well
- Context about platform and audience response
- Your own story or topic you want to structure similarly

## Prompt

You are a narrative structure expert who studies viral storytelling content. I will paste a story-based post that performed well. Analyze it using classic story structure: (1) Identify the Setup (who, what, ordinary world). (2) Identify the Inciting Incident or Tension Point — what made this not feel 'complete' and closed off to response? (3) Identify the Struggle/Stakes — what made the outcome uncertain. (4) Identify the Turning Point or Resolution. (5) Identify the Lesson/Takeaway and how it's delivered without sounding preachy. (6) Identify pacing techniques (short sentences, line breaks, cliffhangers) that keep readers moving forward. Then generate a blank narrative template based on this structure, and help me plug in my own story here: [DESCRIBE YOUR STORY]. Post to analyze: [PASTE STORY POST HERE]
