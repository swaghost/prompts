# Reverse Engineer Your Own Content Against a Viral Benchmark

## Purpose

Run comparative content audits between your underperforming content and a viral benchmark to identify hook strength gaps, structural clarity differences, emotional impact disparities, value delivery comparisons, and specific tactical rewrites that close performance gaps.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Two pieces of content on similar topics: one of your own that underperformed expectations, and one from another creator (viral benchmark) that significantly outperformed on the same or similar subject matter.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A side-by-side diagnostic comparing Hook Strength (scoring each on 1-10 scale with reasoning for each score), Structural Clarity (comparing how clearly each piece guides the reader from start to payoff), Emotional Impact (comparing what each piece makes the reader feel and why one lands harder), Value Delivery (comparing how tangible/specific/useful the takeaway is in each), and identification of the single biggest gap keeping your content's opening and structure to close that gap, with specific tactical rewrites. Explains why each rewrite should work better.

## Usage

Perfect for comparative content auditors running performance diagnostics, creators seeking systematic improvement frameworks, and strategists identifying specific performance gaps rather than general content advice. Ideal for comparing underperforming LinkedIn posts to viral benchmarks, analyzing Instagram caption effectiveness differences, diagnosing Twitter/X thread performance gaps, and any scenario where direct comparison reveals actionable improvement opportunities. This prompt excels at moving beyond vague advice to specific, tactical content improvements grounded in comparative analysis.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Your content that underperformed
- Viral benchmark content on similar topic
- Performance metrics for both (if available)

## Prompt

You are a comparative content auditor. I will paste two pieces of content on a similar topic: one of mine that underperformed, and one from another creator that significantly outperformed (my 'viral benchmark'). Run a side-by-side diagnostic: (1) Hook Strength — compare the opening lines and score each on a 1-10 scale with reasoning for each score. (2) Structural Clarity — compare how clearly each piece guides the reader from start to payoff. (3) Emotional Impact — compare what each piece makes the reader feel, and why one lands harder. (4) Value Delivery — compare how tangible, specific, and useful the takeaway is in each. (5) Identify the single biggest gap keeping my content's opening and structure to close that gap. Explain why each rewrite should work better. My content: [PASTE YOUR CONTENT HERE] Viral benchmark content: [PASTE THE VIRAL CONTENT HERE]
