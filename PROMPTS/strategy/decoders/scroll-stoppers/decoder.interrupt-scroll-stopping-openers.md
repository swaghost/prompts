# Reverse Engineer the Interrupt Behind Scroll-Stopping Openers

## Purpose

Deconstruct opening lines or visuals from top-performing posts to identify the pattern interrupt types, exact mechanisms creating the interrupts, speed of expectation violation, identification of violated expectations, and classification into reusable opener formulas.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Opening lines, first frames, or initial visuals from 5-8 top-performing posts that demonstrated exceptional scroll-stopping power, measured by immediate engagement or retention in the critical first moment of exposure.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A pattern-interrupt specialist analysis identifying What expectation the audience walks in with and exactly how this opener violates or subverts it, Classification of interrupt type (visual shock, statement reversal, unexpected juxtaposition, direct callout, incomplete information, etc.), How quickly the interrupt lands (word count or seconds), and generation of a pattern interrupt menu with 8 reusable opener formulas based on these patterns, applying the 3 strongest ones to specified topic with reasoning about why that specific interrupt type suits this audience/offer.

## Usage

Perfect for pattern-interrupt specialists studying scroll-stopping mechanics, content creators optimizing for immediate attention capture, and social media strategists analyzing first-impression performance. Ideal for analyzing Instagram Reel openers, TikTok hook frames, LinkedIn post first lines, Twitter/X opening tweets, and any content where the first moment determines survival. This prompt excels at systematizing the subtle expectation violations that separate scroll-past content from attention-grabbing content.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- 5-8 opening lines/visuals from top-performing posts in your niche
- Context about audience expectations and norms
- Your topic for applying interrupt patterns

## Prompt

You are a pattern-interrupt specialist who studies why certain openers stop scrolling instantly. I will paste 5-8 opening lines/visuals from top-performing posts in my niche. For each one: (1) Identify what expectation the audience walks in with, and exactly how this opener violates or subverts it. (2) Classify the interrupt type (visual shock, statement reversal, unexpected juxtaposition, direct callout, incomplete information, etc.). (3) Identify how quickly (word count or seconds) the interrupt lands, and why that speed matters. Then generate a 'pattern interrupt menu' of 8 reusable opener formulas based on these patterns, and apply the 3 strongest to my topic: [INSERT YOUR TOPIC]. Openers to analyze: [PASTE OPENERS HERE]
