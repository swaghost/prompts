# Identify Content Ideas and Whitespace Opportunities

## Purpose

Systematically analyze your niche's content landscape to identify topic clusters and saturation levels, emerging themes gaining momentum, contrarian angles underexplored by competitors, and whitespace opportunities where audience demand exists but content supply is limited.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your niche or industry, recent top-performing content from multiple creators (15-25 post titles or topics), trending conversations or questions in your community, and what your audience seems to care about or struggle with based on engagement patterns.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A content opportunity analysis identifying topic clusters showing what themes dominate current conversation with estimated saturation levels (oversaturated, moderately covered, undercovered), emerging themes gaining momentum that represent early-mover opportunities, contrarian angles where taking opposite stance from consensus could generate discussion, untapped topics where audience questions exist but comprehensive content doesn't, trend forecasts predicting what topics will matter in 3-6 months, and 15 specific content ideas organized by opportunity type (whitespace grab, contrarian play, emerging trend, saturation gap-fill) with hook suggestions for each.

## Usage

Perfect for content strategists building editorial calendars, creators seeking differentiation in crowded niches, thought leaders looking for contrarian positioning angles, and content marketers optimizing topic selection for engagement and authority-building. Ideal for planning quarterly content strategies, identifying podcast episode topics, developing video series, and any systematic content planning requiring market intelligence. This prompt excels at revealing both obvious gaps and subtle strategic opportunities others miss.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Your niche or industry context
- List of 15-25 recent top-performing post titles/topics from multiple creators
- Trending questions or conversations you've noticed
- What your audience cares about or struggles with

## Prompt

You are a content strategist analyzing competitive content landscapes. I will describe my niche and give you recent top-performing content from multiple creators. Analyze the content opportunity landscape: (1) Topic Clusters — identify the 5-7 dominant themes showing up repeatedly, and estimate saturation level for each (oversaturated, moderately covered, undercovered). (2) Emerging Themes — identify topics gaining momentum that represent early-mover opportunities before they become saturated. (3) Contrarian Angles — identify consensus views in the current content where taking an opposite or nuanced stance could generate discussion and differentiation. (4) Untapped Topics — identify questions your audience likely has or problems they face where comprehensive content doesn't exist yet (whitespace opportunities). (5) Trend Forecasts — based on emerging patterns, predict what topics will matter to this audience in 3-6 months that few are covering now. (6) Give me 15 specific content ideas organized by opportunity type (whitespace grab, contrarian play, emerging trend, saturation gap-fill), with a suggested hook for each. My niche and content landscape: [DESCRIBE YOUR NICHE, PASTE 15-25 POST TITLES/TOPICS, MENTION TRENDING QUESTIONS OR AUDIENCE STRUGGLES]
