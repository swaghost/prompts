# Diagnose Why Content Gets Likes But No Comments

## Purpose

Analyze posts receiving likes with minimal comments to identify whether the post asks anything requiring reader response, presence of debatable/incomplete/opinion-based elements inviting reaction, opportunities for readers to share their own experience, and specific comment hooks that could provoke reply without sounding forced or gimmicky.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Social media posts that generated passive engagement (likes, reactions) but failed to spark active conversation in comments, indicating the content resonated enough for acknowledgment but didn't create perceived openings for audience contribution.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A community engagement diagnostic examining whether the post feels 'complete' and closed off to response, presence of debatable elements or statements that could be challenged, whether the post creates space for readers to publicly agree/disagree/share their experience, and identification of 3 specific comment hooks that would provoke reply without feeling forced. Rewrites the caption or CTA using the strongest hook, speaking directly to what the audience is already thinking.

## Usage

Perfect for community engagement strategists analyzing conversation gaps, social media marketers seeking active participation, and content creators wanting to transform passive scrollers into vocal community members. Ideal for diagnosing LinkedIn posts with likes but no discussion, Instagram captions generating hearts without conversation, Twitter/X posts getting faves without replies, and Facebook content sparking acknowledgment without dialogue. This prompt excels at revealing the subtle differences between statement-based content and conversation-invitation content.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Full text of post that received likes but minimal comments
- Engagement metrics showing like-to-comment ratio
- Your desired topic or post for implementing fixes

## Prompt

You are a community engagement strategist. I will paste a post that received likes but very few comments. Analyze: (1) Does the post ask anything of the reader, implicitly or explicitly, or does it feel 'complete' and closed off to response? (2) Is there a debatable, incomplete, or opinion-based element the reader could react to, or is everything stated as settled fact? (3) Does the post make the reader feel safe enough to publicly agree, disagree, or share their own experience? (4) Identify 3 specific 'comment hooks' I could add — questions, controversial micro-claims, or open loops — that would provoke reply without feeling forced or gimmicky. Rewrite the caption/CTA using the strongest one. Post to analyze: [PASTE YOUR POST HERE]
