# Reverse Engineer Value Perception and Pricing Strategy

## Purpose

Analyze your pricing model and market positioning to identify the underlying value perception drivers, price elasticity assumptions you're making, bundle opportunities that could increase willingness to pay, and value gaps between what you're charging and what customers would actually pay for specific outcomes.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your current pricing structure, service offerings or product tiers, target customer segments, competitive pricing landscape, and any customer feedback or sales conversation insights about pricing reactions and value perception.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A pricing strategy analysis identifying what your current pricing signals about your positioning (premium, value, mid-market), price elasticity assumptions embedded in your current model (what you're assuming customers will or won't pay for), value perception drivers actually influencing willingness to pay (outcomes, speed, risk reduction, status, etc.), bundle opportunities where combining offerings could command premium pricing, value gaps where you're undercharging relative to delivered outcomes, and 3-5 specific pricing strategy recommendations with rationale for each (repackaging, unbundling, tiering, anchoring, value-based adjustments).

## Usage

Perfect for pricing strategists optimizing revenue models, business owners seeking to increase margins without losing customers, product managers designing tier structures, and sales leaders addressing pricing objections systematically. Ideal for service businesses, SaaS products, agencies, consultants, and any business where pricing is strategic positioning rather than cost-plus calculation. This prompt excels at revealing the psychological and strategic dimensions of pricing beyond simple number adjustments.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Your current pricing structure (tiers, packages, or pricing model)
- What you're selling and to whom
- Competitive pricing context (what others charge)
- Any customer feedback or sales insights about pricing reactions

## Prompt

You are a pricing strategist specializing in value-based pricing and willingness-to-pay psychology. I will describe my current pricing model, what I sell, and my market context. Analyze my pricing strategy: (1) What does my current pricing signal about my positioning in the market — premium, value, mid-market? Is this intentional or accidental? (2) What price elasticity assumptions am I making — what am I assuming customers will or won't pay for? Are these assumptions correct? (3) What are the actual value perception drivers influencing willingness to pay in my category — outcomes, speed, risk reduction, status, convenience, etc.? (4) Identify bundle opportunities where I could combine offerings, reframe value, or create premium tiers that would command higher prices. (5) Identify value gaps where I'm likely undercharging relative to the outcomes I deliver or the alternatives customers would choose. (6) Give me 3-5 specific pricing strategy recommendations — should I repackage, unbundle, add tiers, change anchoring, adjust positioning, or shift to value-based pricing? Include rationale for each. My pricing and business context: [DESCRIBE YOUR PRICING MODEL, WHAT YOU SELL, YOUR MARKET, COMPETITIVE PRICING, AND CUSTOMER REACTIONS]
