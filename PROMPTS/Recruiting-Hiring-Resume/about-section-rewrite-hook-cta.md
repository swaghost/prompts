# LinkedIn About Section Rewrite - Hook + CTA

## Prompt

"Rewrite my About section. Open with a hook that stops the scroll. Say what I do, who I help, and the results I deliver. End with a CTA. Human, not corporate. Here's mine: [paste]"

## Purpose

Transform your LinkedIn About section from corporate jargon into a compelling, human story that makes recruiters want to reach out. Focuses on the scroll-stopping hook and clear call-to-action.

## Use Case

- Making your profile more engaging
- Converting profile views into messages
- Differentiating from generic About sections
- Creating a personal brand narrative

## What You'll Get

- An attention-grabbing opening hook
- Clear value proposition (what you do + who you help + results)
- Human, conversational tone (not corporate-speak)
- Strong call-to-action to drive engagement

## Structure

1. **Hook** - Stops the scroll
2. **What I Do** - Clear role/expertise
3. **Who I Help** - Target audience/clients
4. **Results I Deliver** - Outcomes and impact
5. **CTA** - Next step for the reader

## Variables to Replace

- `[paste]` - Your current About section text
