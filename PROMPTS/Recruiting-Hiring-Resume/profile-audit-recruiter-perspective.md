# LinkedIn Profile Audit - Recruiter Perspective

## Prompt

"Here's my LinkedIn profile [Link]. Act as a recruiter hiring for [role]. Score my profile out of 10 and tell me the 3 things making you click away. Be honest."

## Purpose

Get brutally honest feedback on your LinkedIn profile from a recruiter's viewpoint. Identifies the top 3 issues that would cause a recruiter to skip your profile when searching for candidates.

## Use Case

- Before applying to jobs
- When updating your LinkedIn profile
- To understand what recruiters prioritize
- To identify blind spots in your professional presentation

## What You'll Get

- A numerical score (out of 10)
- The 3 main weaknesses that hurt your profile
- Honest, actionable feedback from a hiring perspective
- Insight into what makes recruiters "click away"
