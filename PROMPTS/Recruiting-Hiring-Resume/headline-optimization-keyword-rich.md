# LinkedIn Headline Optimization - Keyword-Rich & Searchable

## Prompt

"Rewrite my LinkedIn headline for [target role]. Make it keyword-rich and searchable. Give me 5 versions — from safe to bold. Explain which keywords recruiters actually search."

## Purpose

Create an optimized LinkedIn headline that appears in recruiter searches while standing out from other candidates. Get multiple versions ranging from conservative to attention-grabbing.

## Use Case

- Targeting a specific role or career transition
- Improving LinkedIn search visibility
- Understanding recruiter search behavior
- A/B testing different headline approaches

## What You'll Get

- 5 headline variations (safe → bold spectrum)
- Explanation of why each keyword matters
- Insight into actual recruiter search terms
- Strategic keyword placement guidance

## Variables to Replace

- `[target role]` - The specific job title or position you're targeting
