# PIZZA INFOGRAPHIC - EXPLODED VIEW

## Design Specifications

You are a senior museum-grade food infographic designer creating a STRICTLY CONSISTENT multi-image exploded-view Instagram series (4:5). The dish is PIZZA. All images must maintain IDENTICAL titles, labels, icons, lines, circles, layout, spacing, alignment grid, connector angles, margins, font style, font size, line thickness, circle size, and icon size across the entire series. Use one unified warm heritage tone (bronze / warm beige) for all text, connector lines, circles, and line-style icons. Thin elegant lines, clean circular points, no gradients, no shadows, no glow. Icon stroke weight must exactly match connector line thickness. Archival, museum-catalog premium aesthetic. Background: warm cinematic kitchen ambiance, soft depth of field, realistic lighting, natural textures. Ingredients must float vertically above the pizza in a physically believable exploded composition. Final pizza centered at bottom. Top Title Block (fixed, centered): Arabic dish title on top, English below, identical spacing and size across all images. Ingredient Labels (PIZZA ONLY): Auto-detect visible pizza ingredients only. No sauces, no unrelated toppings, no optional items unless clearly shown, no meat unless visible. Arabic names ONLY. One ingredient per label. Short, direct names. No adjectives. No duplication. If not clearly visible, do not label. Each ingredient must have: one small circular point near it, one thin straight connector line (no curves, no intersections), and one same-sized circular line-style icon beside the name (no fill, no text inside icon). Even left-right distribution, symmetrical balance, no overlap, consistent vertical stacking rhythm, safe margins. ABSOLUTE: Do not change layout, colors, or icon style. Do not add descriptions, emojis, hashtags, AI mentions, or analysis. Output only the final formatted on-image text. Aspect ratio 4:5.

## Animation Instructions

Static frame. All labels and layout visible. Subtle cinematic depth of field. Gentle camera push-in (very slow, premium). 0.8–2.5s Exploded ingredients (tomato slices, mozzarella slices, bell pepper rings, onion rings, black olives) gently descend in slow motion toward the pizza. Motion must feel physically believable. Soft gravity. No bouncing. No rotation exaggeration. Smooth vertical glide. 2.5–3.8s Each ingredient settles naturally into its exact position on the pizza surface. Mozzarella aligns softly. Tomato slices land gently. Bell peppers and onion rings settle without shifting labels. Black olives drop lightly into place. No camera cut. 3.8–5.0s Final assembled pizza. Micro steam shimmer. Subtle olive surface shine. Very gentle final push-in for premium finish. LABEL SYSTEM RULES (ABSOLUTE): Ingredient labels remain completely static throughout animation. Connector lines remain straight and unchanged. Circular points remain locked in position. Icons do not animate. No text transitions. No fade-ins. No glow effects. PLACEMENT LOGIC: Even distribution left and right. No overlap. Same spatial balance. All labels remain readable during motion. ABSOLUTE RESTRICTIONS: Do NOT change layout. Do NOT change color. Do NOT change icon style. Do NOT add ingredients. Do NOT remove ingredients. Do NOT add descriptions, emojis, or hashtags. Do NOT mention AI or analysis.
