# Critical Thinking Quick Reference

## The 10 Essential Questions

### 1. What do you mean by that?

→ **Clarify terms and definitions**

### 2. How do you know that's true?

→ **Demand evidence and justification**

### 3. What's the counter-argument?

→ **Consider opposing views**

### 4. What are you assuming here?

→ **Expose hidden premises**

### 5. What evidence supports this?

→ **Assess empirical grounding**

### 6. What are the implications?

→ **Trace consequences**

### 7. Why does this matter?

→ **Establish significance**

### 8. Is there another way to see this?

→ **Explore alternative perspectives**

### 9. What would change your mind?

→ **Test falsifiability**

### 10. How could this be improved?

→ **Generate constructive alternatives**

- Questions 2, 4, 5

**Before Concluding:**

- Questions 1, 3, 9

- Questions 6, 7, 10

- Question 8

- All 10

## Red Flag Detector

| Red Flag              | Question to Ask                  |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Vague terms           | Q1: What do you mean?            |
| Unsupported claim     | Q2: How do you know?             |
| One-sided view        | Q3: What's the counter-argument? |
| Hidden assumption     | Q4: What are you assuming?       |
| No evidence           | Q5: What evidence supports this? |
| Ignoring consequences | Q6: What are the implications?   |
| Irrelevant point      | Q7: Why does this matter?        |
| Narrow thinking       | Q8: Is there another way?        |
| Dogmatism             | Q9: What would change your mind? |
| Only criticism        | Q10: How could this be improved? |

## Power Combinations

**The Clarifier:** Q1 → Q4 → Q2
(Define → Expose → Verify)

**The Evaluator:** Q2 → Q5 → Q3
(Justify → Evidence → Opposition)

**The Strategist:** Q6 → Q7 → Q10
(Consequences → Significance → Improvement)

**The Humble Inquirer:** Q8 → Q9 → Q3
(Alternative views → Falsifiable → Counter-arguments)

## One-Minute Check

Before accepting any claim, ask yourself:

1. ✓ Do I understand the terms? (Q1)
2. ✓ Is there evidence? (Q2, Q5)
3. ✓ Have I heard the other side? (Q3)
4. ✓ What's assumed? (Q4)
5. ✓ What follows from this? (Q6)
6. ✓ Does it matter? (Q7)
7. ✓ Are there alternatives? (Q8)
8. ✓ Could I be wrong? (Q9)
9. ✓ Can it be improved? (Q10)
