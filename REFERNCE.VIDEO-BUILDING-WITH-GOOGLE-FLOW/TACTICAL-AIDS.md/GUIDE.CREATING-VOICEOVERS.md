To build a crisp, 30-second tactical voiceover script for an instructional clip (e.g., for _soccr.org_), structure your narration around a **4-stage coaching cadence**: **Setup $\rightarrow$ Problem $\rightarrow$ Trigger $\rightarrow$ Outcome**.

This structure aligns cleanly with the visual timeline of a telestrated video—leading into the freeze-frame overlays and accelerating through the dynamic resolution.

---

## The 30-Second Script Blueprint & Timeline

### Stage 1: The Context & Setup (0:00 – 0:06)

- **Visual:** Clip opens wide/overhead showing team formations in motion.
- **Script Objective:** Identify the structural setup and the key actor (e.g., team formation vs. defensive block).
- **Word Count Target:** ~15 words.

> _"When facing a rigid four-four-two mid-block, central space is completely choked off."_

---

### Stage 2: The Freeze-Frame / Telestrator Hook (0:06 – 0:14)

- **Visual:** Video pauses or drops to 10% slow-motion. Spotlight ring locks onto the playmaker; shaded highlight fills the half-space target area.
- **Script Objective:** Direct the viewer's eyes explicitly to the graphic highlights and explain the tactical mechanism.
- **Word Count Target:** ~20–25 words.

> _"Notice our central midfielder here—as the ball moves wide, he checks his shoulder and identifies this open half-space pocket behind their midfield line."_

---

### Stage 3: The Movement Trigger & Vector (0:14 – 0:22)

- **Visual:** Video resumes. Animated vector arrow draws along the pass path while the receiver makes a dismarking run.
- **Script Objective:** Highlight the timing of the action, the specific off-ball movement, or the passing execution.
- **Word Count Target:** ~20 words.

> _"The moment the defender steps up to press, he checks off his marker, turns on the half-turn, and slides a sharp ground pass into the seam."_

---

### Stage 4: Tactical Outcome & Coaching Point (0:22 – 0:30)

- **Visual:** Play speeds up to 100%. Ball is played through, breaking the line and leading to a shot or dangerous cross.
- **Script Objective:** Summarize the core takeaway or tactical rule for players to take into their next session.
- **Word Count Target:** ~15–20 words.

> _"By baiting the press and exploiting the half-space, you break the defensive block in just two touches. Scan early, move late."_

## 3 Audio & Recording Best Practices

1. **Pace for Clarity (130–140 WPM):** Conversational speech is around 150 words per minute, but tactical commentary requires slightly lower speed (~130 WPM) so viewers can absorb the visual overlay graphics simultaneously. Keep total script length strictly under **75 words** for a 30-second reel.
2. **Leave "Visual Breathing Room":** Do not speak during the exact half-second a major graphic pops on screen (like a spotlight ring scaling up). Let the graphic draw the viewer's eye for a split second, then explain it.
3. **Use Action-Oriented Verbs:** Replace passive voice (_"The ball is passed to the winger"_) with active tactical directives (_"He slices the through-ball into the channel"_). This adds natural urgency to match the visual tempo.
