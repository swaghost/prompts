# Templates for Set Piece Plays in Motion (Corners & Free Kicks)

Set pieces require a perspective that merges wide visibility (to see all players in the box) with depth of field, as multiple players will be moving in dense crowds. We utilize optical commands here to add dramatic focus to the dynamic action.

**Hierarchy used:** `[Style] -> [Set Piece Angle] -> [Dynamic Motion] -> [Focus Effect] -> [The "Play" Narrative]`

## Template 4: Corner Kick: Aerial Duel & Clearance

This captures the chaos of a crowded box, using a slight 'bullet-time' effect to emphasize the peak of the aerial battle.

```text
/cinematic /cornerangle /bullettime /rackfocus /rimlight A dynamic shot focused on a packed penalty area during a corner kick. The ball is crossed high and curls toward the 6-yard box. Five players leap simultaneously; the camera slows down in bullet-time at the peak of the jump, showing the moment of impact as a defender powerfully heads the ball away. Stadium lighting spotlights the spray of moisture. The depth of field shifts dynamically from the cross to the aerial duel.

```

## Template 5: Free Kick (Indirect): The Clever Overlap

Use this to generate the precise execution of a trick play rather than a direct shot on goal.

```text
/commercial /sideline /dollyin /selectivefocus /spotlight Shot from near the corner flag, tracking a clever indirect free kick routine. The primary kicker dummy runs over the ball. A second player lightly taps the ball, allowing a third runner, emerging from the wall, to receive it on an overlap and whip a dangerous low cross into the center of the box. High contrast lighting isolates the crucial players involved in the intricate choreography.

```
