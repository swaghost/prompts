# Close Up - Detail Focus Photography

## Description

Create a beautiful close-up shot showing one of the details of this image, using depth of field to blur background, adding bokeh effect, and showing specific details in sharp focus. This prompt transforms any scene into an intimate detail-focused composition with professional shallow depth of field, cinematic bokeh, and selective focus—ideal for highlighting architectural details, product features, interior design elements, material textures, and creating visually compelling storytelling through isolated details.

## Photography Overview

**Shot Type:** Close-up detail photography  
**Focus:** Single detail or element  
**Depth of Field:** Shallow (background blur)  
**Bokeh:** Soft out-of-focus background  
**Purpose:** Detail emphasis, visual storytelling, artistic composition  
**Application:** Architecture, interiors, products, materials, design elements

## Depth of Field Control

### Shallow Depth of Field

**Depth of Field Specifications:**

- Very shallow focus plane
- Subject in sharp focus
- Background progressively blurred
- Foreground may also blur
- Professional portrait-style DOF
- Cinematic quality

**Technical Approach:**

- Wide aperture (f/1.4 - f/2.8)
- Longer focal length (50-135mm)
- Close focusing distance
- Creates shallow plane of focus
- Professional photography technique
- Cinema lens aesthetic

**Focus Plane:**

- Critical detail in sharp focus
- Narrow depth of acceptable sharpness
- Precise focus point
- Professional focus technique
- Tack-sharp subject
- Blurred surroundings

### Background Blur (Defocus)

**Blur Characteristics:**

- Soft gradual blur
- Out-of-focus background
- Context suggested but not detailed
- Directs attention to subject
- Reduces visual clutter
- Professional photographic quality

**Blur Intensity:**

- Strong blur (very shallow DOF)
- Moderate blur (f/2.8-f/4)
- Slight blur (f/5.6)
- Typically strong for close-ups
- Cinematic quality
- Professional execution

**Background Treatment:**

- Recognizable but soft
- Colors and shapes present
- No distracting detail
- Complements subject
- Atmospheric context
- Professional aesthetic
