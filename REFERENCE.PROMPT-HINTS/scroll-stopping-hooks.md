# Scroll-Stopping Hooks

Attention-grabbing opening techniques for video content.

## Hook 1: Dramatic Close-Up Entry

Close-up subject, direct eye contact, sudden camera push-in, dramatic lighting, tension-filled opening frame

## Hook 2: Mid-Action Start

Unexpected motion start, subject already mid-action, fast cut energy, attention-grabbing first 2 seconds

## Hook 3: POV Immersion

POV style intro, viewer feels inside the scene, handheld motion, natural environment

## Hook 4: Bold Subject Focus

Minimal background, bold subject focus, high contrast lighting, strong visual entry

## Hook 5: Curiosity Gap

Start with confusion or curiosity, unclear situation that resolves in next frame
