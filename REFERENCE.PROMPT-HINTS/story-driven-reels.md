# Story-Driven Reels

Narrative structures for compelling short-form video content.

## Structure 1: Three-Part Sequence

3-part sequence: tension → development → payoff, each scene visually connected

## Structure 2: Transformation Narrative

Before vs after transformation, clear visual contrast, emotional shift

## Structure 3: Problem-Solution Arc

Problem → solution narrative, subject interaction with product or idea

## Structure 4: Micro-Story

Micro-story with beginning, middle, end in under 10 seconds

## Structure 5: Curiosity Build

Build curiosity through partial reveal, final frame completes the story
