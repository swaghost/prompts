# Cinematic Genres Reference

Use genre as a production and storytelling system, not as a mood word. Combine the genre with a specific subject, setting, era, director reference, camera movement, focus technique, and emotional objective.

## Genre Prompt Formula

`genre` + `dramatic situation` + `visual grammar` + `camera and lens` + `composition and blocking` + `lighting and palette` + `production design` + `editing rhythm` + `sound and score` + `performance direction` + `hard negatives`.

Films listed below are study references for craft analysis. Do not request a frame-for-frame recreation, copied character, dialogue, or exact living-director imitation.

## Primary Genre Reference

### Drama

- **Style:** Character-centered conflict, realistic behavior, emotional subtext, meaningful pauses, and social or familial pressure.
- **Cinematic language:** Eye-level coverage, restrained dolly or handheld movement, naturalistic lighting, lived-in production design, selective close-ups, and dialogue shaped by silence.
- **Emblematic films:** _Tokyo Story_, _A Separation_, _Manchester by the Sea_, _The Godfather_, _Moonlight_.
- **Prompt:** `Character-driven cinematic drama, naturalistic eye-level camera, restrained movement, lived-in location, soft motivated light, subtle facial performance, layered dialogue subtext, quiet emotional escalation, realistic sound, no melodramatic exaggeration.`

### Comedy

- **Style:** Timing, contrast, social discomfort, visual irony, escalation, repetition, and physical or verbal misalignment.
- **Cinematic language:** Clean readable staging, lateral movement, reaction shots, precise inserts, controlled wide frames, bright or neutral light, rhythmic cutting.
- **Emblematic films:** _Some Like It Hot_, _Playtime_, _The General_, _Groundhog Day_, _The Grand Budapest Hotel_.
- **Prompt:** `Visually precise cinematic comedy, readable ensemble blocking, carefully timed reaction shots, lateral tracking, expressive props, bright controlled palette, escalating physical behavior, dry performance, rhythmic editing, no broad caricature unless requested.`

### Romantic Comedy

- **Style:** Attraction, misunderstanding, banter, social friction, emotional vulnerability, and a movement from performance to sincerity.
- **Cinematic language:** Warm practical light, conversational two-shots, moving walk-and-talks, intimate close-ups, recurring locations, music-led transitions.
- **Emblematic films:** _When Harry Met Sally..._, _Roman Holiday_, _The Philadelphia Story_, _Before Sunrise_, _10 Things I Hate About You_.
- **Prompt:** `Warm romantic comedy, conversational two-shots, gentle handheld or smooth walking camera, golden practical light, charming urban locations, intelligent banter, restrained chemistry, escalating vulnerability, music-supported transitions.`

### Romance

- **Style:** Desire, longing, timing, intimacy, separation, memory, and emotional attention to gesture.
- **Cinematic language:** Soft shallow focus, lingering close-ups, reflective surfaces, slow push-ins, tactile hands and faces, color motifs, sparse or lyrical score.
- **Emblematic films:** _In the Mood for Love_, _Portrait of a Lady on Fire_, _Brief Encounter_, _The Umbrellas of Cherbourg_, _The English Patient_.
- **Prompt:** `Lyrical romantic cinema, intimate close framing, soft shallow focus, reflective surfaces, slow deliberate camera, tactile hands and restrained eye contact, recurring color motif, sparse yearning score, emotional distance made visible.`

### Melodrama

- **Style:** Heightened emotion, moral conflict, sacrifice, family revelation, symbolic objects, and controlled excess.
- **Cinematic language:** Expressive close-ups, dramatic backlight, saturated color, formal blocking, music cues, visual motifs, and decisive emotional cutting.
- **Emblematic films:** _All That Heaven Allows_, _Imitation of Life_, _The Bitter Tears of Petra von Kant_, _Magnificent Obsession_, _Far from Heaven_.
- **Prompt:** `Elegant cinematic melodrama, heightened but sincere performance, formal blocking, saturated emotional color, dramatic backlight, symbolic costume and objects, expressive close-ups, swelling but controlled score, no accidental camp.`

### Crime Film

- **Style:** Illicit systems, loyalty, betrayal, ambition, codes, surveillance, and consequences.
- **Cinematic language:** Night exteriors, practical sodium and neon, low angles, tracking through social spaces, inserts of money or objects, tense sound bridges.
- **Emblematic films:** _The Godfather_, _Heat_, _City of God_, _The French Connection_, _Le Samourai_.
- **Prompt:** `Cinematic crime drama, nocturnal practical lighting, sodium vapor and neon, controlled tracking through urban space, morally divided characters, tactile evidence and money inserts, tense sound design, restrained menace.`

### Film Noir

- **Style:** Fatalism, betrayal, moral ambiguity, obsession, urban isolation, and compromised narration.
- **Cinematic language:** Hard chiaroscuro, blinds and silhouettes, wet streets, low-key exposure, oblique angles, deep shadows, voice-over, sparse jazz.
- **Emblematic films:** _Double Indemnity_, _The Third Man_, _Out of the Past_, _Touch of Evil_, _The Maltese Falcon_.
- **Prompt:** `Classic film noir atmosphere, hard chiaroscuro, venetian-blind shadows, wet pavement, isolated figure, oblique composition, low-key exposure, smoky practical light, morally ambiguous performance, sparse jazz tension.`

### Neo-Noir

- **Style:** Noir fatalism translated into contemporary cities, technology, sexuality, surveillance, or corporate systems.
- **Cinematic language:** Neon night, reflective glass, cyan and amber contrast, shallow focus, wide urban perspective, digital screens, controlled slow movement.
- **Emblematic films:** _Chinatown_, _Blade Runner_, _L.A. Confidential_, _Drive_, _Memories of Murder_.
- **Prompt:** `Contemporary neo-noir, neon and reflective glass, cyan-amber night palette, slow controlled push, surveillance atmosphere, isolated protagonist, deep urban negative space, morally uncertain investigation.`

### Thriller

- **Style:** Suspense, information control, pursuit, danger, deception, and escalating uncertainty.
- **Cinematic language:** Subjective POV, focus pulls, constrained frames, surveillance angles, precise timing, silence before impact, layered ambient sound.
- **Emblematic films:** _Rear Window_, _The Conversation_, _Zodiac_, _The Silence of the Lambs_, _The Lives of Others_.
- **Prompt:** `Psychological cinematic thriller, controlled information reveal, subjective POV, constrained composition, slow focus pull, practical low-key lighting, distant surveillance perspective, restrained performance, silence before danger.`

### Horror

- **Style:** Fear, vulnerability, contamination, isolation, the uncanny, and the collapse of ordinary safety.
- **Cinematic language:** Negative space, slow reveals, darkness, motivated practical light, offscreen sound, focus drift, long pauses, sudden violent contrast.
- **Emblematic films:** _The Exorcist_, _The Shining_, _The Witch_, _Hereditary_, _The Texas Chain Saw Massacre_.
- **Prompt:** `Atmospheric cinematic horror, ordinary location made unsafe, deep negative space, slow motivated camera, practical light falling into darkness, restrained actor reaction, offscreen sound, focus drift, sudden controlled rupture, no cheap visual clutter.`

### Body Horror

- **Style:** Physical transformation, disease, technology, identity instability, disgust, and the body becoming unfamiliar.
- **Cinematic language:** Macro inserts, clinical framing, tactile surfaces, harsh fluorescent light, symmetrical medical spaces, invasive sound design.
- **Emblematic films:** _The Fly_, _Videodrome_, _Tetsuo: The Iron Man_, _Raw_, _Titane_.
- **Prompt:** `Clinical body-horror cinema, macro texture detail, sterile fluorescent light, controlled symmetrical framing, invasive practical sound, slow observation of physical transformation, psychological restraint, no gratuitous gore unless requested.`

### Psychological Horror

- **Style:** Unstable perception, trauma, obsession, unreliable reality, and fear generated by the mind.
- **Cinematic language:** Repetition, mirrors, subjective sound, ambiguous cuts, slow push-ins, visual doubles, shifting focus, and unstable continuity.
- **Emblematic films:** _Repulsion_, _Black Swan_, _Perfect Blue_, _The Babadook_, _Mulholland Drive_.
- **Prompt:** `Psychological horror, subjective camera, repeated visual motif, ambiguous continuity, slow push toward the face, distorted room tone, unstable focus, restrained performance gradually losing control, dream logic without random imagery.`

### Action

- **Style:** Physical objectives, pursuit, impact, sacrifice, skill, velocity, and clear spatial stakes.
- **Cinematic language:** Tracking, crane, vehicle and body-mounted cameras, readable geography, motivated motion blur, rhythmic cutting, impact-driven sound.
- **Emblematic films:** _Mad Max: Fury Road_, _Police Story_, _Seven Samurai_, _The Raid_, _Mission: Impossible - Fallout_.
- **Prompt:** `High-energy cinematic action, readable geography, motivated tracking camera, practical movement, clear physical objectives, controlled motion blur, escalating edit rhythm, tactile impacts, distinct character silhouettes, no incoherent camera shake.`

### Martial-Arts Film

- **Style:** Discipline, body geometry, rivalry, honor, rhythm, and movement as character expression.
- **Cinematic language:** Clear full-body framing, lateral tracking, locked master shots, selective slow motion, rhythmic cuts, textured locations.
- **Emblematic films:** _Enter the Dragon_, _Crouching Tiger Hidden Dragon_, _The 36th Chamber of Shaolin_, _Ip Man_, _The Grandmaster_.
- **Prompt:** `Cinematic martial-arts choreography, full-body readable framing, lateral tracking, clear spatial rhythm, expressive footwork and hand geometry, textured practical location, selective slow motion, percussive sound.`

### War Film

- **Style:** Collective survival, moral injury, chaos, duty, trauma, and the distance between command and body.
- **Cinematic language:** Handheld immersion, long takes, smoke, dust, desaturated palettes, tactical geography, radio and environmental sound.
- **Emblematic films:** _Come and See_, _Apocalypse Now_, _Saving Private Ryan_, _The Battle of Algiers_, _1917_.
- **Prompt:** `Immersive cinematic war drama, handheld proximity, tactical spatial clarity inside chaos, dust and smoke, desaturated natural light, radio chatter, breath and impact sound, exhausted restrained performances, no glamorization.`

### Western

- **Style:** Frontier myth, isolation, honor, violence, landscape, displacement, and community formation.
- **Cinematic language:** Monumental wides, horizon lines, dust, hard sun, long-lens standoffs, deliberate close-ups, sparse score.
- **Emblematic films:** _The Searchers_, _Once Upon a Time in the West_, _High Noon_, _Unforgiven_, _The Assassination of Jesse James by the Coward Robert Ford_.
- **Prompt:** `Cinematic western, monumental landscape, hard sun and dust, distant figures, patient standoff, extreme close-up on eyes and hands, restrained movement, sparse atmospheric score, moral ambiguity.`

### Historical Epic

- **Style:** Individual lives against political, religious, military, or cultural scale.
- **Cinematic language:** Large tableaux, crowd blocking, architectural environments, crane movement, period texture, orchestral scale.
- **Emblematic films:** _Lawrence of Arabia_, _Ran_, _The Leopard_, _The Last Emperor_, _Bajirao Mastani_.
- **Prompt:** `Historical epic cinema, monumental location, layered crowd tableau, period-accurate costume and architecture, controlled crane movement, expressive weather, orchestral scale, intimate human detail within historical forces.`

### Science Fiction

- **Style:** Speculative systems, technology, identity, future society, scale, and philosophical consequence.
- **Cinematic language:** Geometric production design, controlled camera, practical interfaces, cool light, atmospheric scale, precise sound design.
- **Emblematic films:** _2001: A Space Odyssey_, _Solaris_, _Blade Runner_, _Stalker_, _Arrival_.
- **Prompt:** `Thoughtful cinematic science fiction, coherent speculative technology, geometric architecture, controlled slow camera, cool industrial palette, atmospheric scale, precise mechanical sound, human wonder and philosophical tension.`

### Fantasy

- **Style:** Myth, transformation, wonder, moral choice, invented worlds, and the collision of ordinary life with the impossible.
- **Cinematic language:** Layered world-building, expressive weather, creature detail, practical texture, luminous backlight, sweeping movement.
- **Emblematic films:** _The Lord of the Rings_, _Pan's Labyrinth_, _The Green Knight_, _Princess Mononoke_, _The Princess Bride_.
- **Prompt:** `Cinematic fantasy, tactile invented world, layered production design, practical creature texture, luminous atmospheric light, sweeping but readable camera, mythic stakes grounded in intimate character choice.`

### Documentary / Observational Realism

- **Style:** Presence, contingency, social texture, ordinary behavior, and the ethics of looking.
- **Cinematic language:** Handheld or static observation, available light, real locations, ambient sound, long takes, minimal intervention.
- **Emblematic films:** _Salesman_, _Grey Gardens_, _The Gleaners and I_, _Hoop Dreams_, _The Act of Killing_.
- **Prompt:** `Observational documentary realism, real location, available light, patient handheld or locked camera, ambient room tone, unscripted-feeling behavior, social texture, no polished commercial staging.`

### Experimental / Surreal

- **Style:** Association, dream logic, visual metaphor, discontinuity, sensory disruption, and form as subject.
- **Cinematic language:** Unusual aspect relationships, non-linear editing, optical distortion, impossible transitions, symbolic production design, unpredictable sound.
- **Emblematic films:** _Un Chien Andalou_, _Meshes of the Afternoon_, _Eraserhead_, _The Color of Pomegranates_, _Holy Motors_.
- **Prompt:** `Experimental surreal cinema, coherent dream logic, symbolic objects, unexpected but motivated transitions, non-linear time, expressive color, altered sound perspective, uncanny performance, avoid random unrelated imagery.`

### Animation

- **Style:** Movement, design, rhythm, impossible physics, visual metaphor, and worlds unconstrained by live-action mechanics.
- **Cinematic language:** Graphic silhouettes, controlled camera paths, expressive backgrounds, match cuts, precise timing, stylized light and color.
- **Emblematic films:** _The Tale of the Princess Kaguya_, _Spirited Away_, _Akira_, _Fantastic Mr. Fox_, _Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse_.
- **Prompt:** `Cinematic animation, expressive graphic design, deliberate character motion, layered background movement, stylized lighting, precise rhythmic editing, visual metaphor, coherent world rules, no generic plastic CGI.`

## Usage Notes

- Start with one primary genre and add no more than two adjacent genre modifiers.
- Use the emblematic films as craft references: identify the camera, lighting, blocking, editing, sound, or production-design decision you want to borrow.
- Add a directorial profile only after the genre is clear; genre supplies the narrative contract, while a director reference supplies a more specific grammar.
- State what must remain original: character, setting, action, costume identity, dialogue, and story outcome.

## Hard Negatives

`No frame-for-frame recreation, no copied characters, no copied dialogue, no studio logos, no watermark, no incoherent genre mixing, no generic cinematic blur, no unmotivated camera movement, no accidental period mismatch.`
