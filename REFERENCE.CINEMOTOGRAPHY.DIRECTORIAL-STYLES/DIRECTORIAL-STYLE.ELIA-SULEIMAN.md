


## Nationality
Palestinian.

## Working Decades and Evolution by Decade
1980-2024; active across the 1980s through the 2020s.

# Directorial Style: Elia Suleiman

## Genre

Deadpan political comedy, observational satire, and poetic displacement film.

## Writing and Story Logic

Occupation, borders, bureaucracy, and absurdity emerge through repeated visual situations rather than explanation.

## Directorial Evolution

Personal observation expands into increasingly abstract national and diasporic tableaux.

## Visual Grammar

Stillness, symmetry, silence, repetition, and precise comic interruption.

## Camera and Lens

Locked wides, patient surveillance, composed lateral movement, and rare exact reframing.

## Composition and Blocking

Figures wait, cross, gather, and repeat actions inside politically charged geometry.

## Lighting and Color

Natural daylight, muted urban palette, clean contrast, and occasional surreal accent.

## Production Design

Walls, checkpoints, homes, streets, flags, signs, and bureaucratic objects.

## Editing

Deadpan duration, repetition, visual punchlines, and associative political montage.

## Sound and Scoring

Silence, footsteps, traffic, birds, announcements, and carefully placed music.

## Casting and Performance

Minimal gesture, blank observation, precise timing, and understated resistance.

## Collaborators and Collaborations

Cinematography, editing, design, performers, and sound work as one visual joke system.

## Notable Evidentiary Works and Scenes

_Divine Intervention_, _The Time That Remains_, and _It Must Be Heaven_.

## Themes

Displacement, borders, absurdity, occupation, identity, and quiet resistance.

## Usage and When to Reference

Use for political satire, border absurdity, displacement, tableaux, and silent visual comedy.

## Prompt Translation

`Suleiman-informed original political tableau: locked wide, checkpoint geometry, repeated gesture, traffic and birds, deadpan silence, quiet resistance.`

## Hard Negatives

Avoid political caricature, punchlines that target civilians, and symbolism without observed behavior.
