


## Nationality
Iranian.

## Working Decades and Evolution by Decade
1980-2024; active across the 1980s through the 2020s.

# Directorial Style: Marjane Satrapi

## Genre

Animated memoir, biographical drama, dark comedy, and literary adaptation.

## Writing and Story Logic

Personal memory intersects with political upheaval, family, exile, humor, and resilience.

## Directorial Evolution

Graphic autobiographical language expands into live action genre and biographical ensemble.

## Visual Grammar

Graphic silhouettes, expressive contrast, direct storytelling, and comedy beside trauma.

## Camera and Lens

Composed portraiture, bold animation framing, controlled movement, and visual metaphor.

## Composition and Blocking

Panels, thresholds, crowds, and family tableaux make history personally legible.

## Lighting and Color

Black and white contrast, graphic reds, theatrical practicals, and emotional palette shifts.

## Production Design

Posters, books, family rooms, streets, costumes, and politically charged objects.

## Editing

Chapter structure, memory ellipses, graphic match, and tonal transitions.

## Sound and Scoring

Voice, songs, public announcements, family noise, and evocative score.

## Casting and Performance

Direct emotional clarity, comic timing, family chemistry, and resilient vulnerability.

## Collaborators and Collaborations

Animators, illustrators, writers, editors, composers, and performers share narrative control.

## Notable Evidentiary Works and Scenes

_Persepolis_, _The Gang of Jotas_, and _The Voices_.

## Themes

Exile, politics, family, womanhood, memory, freedom, and resilience.

## Usage and When to Reference

Use for memoir, political coming-of-age, animated biography, exile, and dark family comedy.

## Prompt Translation

`Satrapi-informed original memoir: graphic contrast, family tableau, public announcement, dry humor beside political fear, chapter-like memory transition.`

## Hard Negatives

Avoid simplifying political history, decorative animation, and trauma used as a plot ornament.
