


## Nationality
French.

## Working Decades and Evolution by Decade
1980-2024; active across the 1980s through the 2020s.

# Directorial Style: Michel Gondry

## Genre

Surreal romance, music film, fantasy comedy, and memory drama.

## Writing and Story Logic

Love, memory, and imagination are material systems that can be built, broken, and rebuilt.

## Directorial Evolution

Handmade music-video tricks develop into feature-length emotional puzzles and intimate fantasy.

## Visual Grammar

Practical illusion, visible craft, elastic scale, and dream logic with emotional rules.

## Camera and Lens

Handheld intimacy, stop-motion-like movement, wide lenses, and playful optical transitions.

## Composition and Blocking

Rooms transform around performers; props, paper, miniatures, and bodies share the same logic.

## Lighting and Color

Tactile colored light, soft interiors, winter blues, and handmade contrast.

## Production Design

Cardboard, notebooks, miniatures, machines, studios, bedrooms, and visibly constructed objects.

## Editing

Match cuts, in-camera transitions, memory jumps, and music-led temporal play.

## Sound and Scoring

Whirrs, songs, handmade foley, room tone, and melodic memory cues.

## Casting and Performance

Tender awkwardness, physical play, emotional sincerity, and comic vulnerability.

## Collaborators and Collaborations

Musicians, effects artists, designers, animators, and editors extend the handmade world.

## Notable Evidentiary Works and Scenes

_Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind_, _The Science of Sleep_, and _Be Kind Rewind_.

## Themes

Memory, love, creativity, regret, childhood, and the limits of imagination.

## Usage and When to Reference

Use for handmade surrealism, romantic memory, dream transitions, and tactile fantasy.

## Prompt Translation

`Gondry-informed original memory scene: handmade miniature, soft colored light, elastic room, practical transition, song fragment, tender regret.`

## Hard Negatives

Avoid CGI polish, dream randomness, and whimsy without a clear emotional rule.
