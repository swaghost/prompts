# Directorial Style: Ava DuVernay

## Genre

Historical drama, social realism, biographical drama, and civic ensemble film.

## Writing and Story Logic

Individual experience connects to systems, testimony, memory, and collective action through accessible dramatic structure.

## Directorial Evolution

Independent intimacy grows into expansive historical and civic storytelling with multiple viewpoints.

## Visual Grammar

Human faces, purposeful symbolism, clear emotional geography, and institutions meeting bodies.

## Camera and Lens

Steady tracking, intimate portrait lenses, composed wides, and movement that follows agency.

## Composition and Blocking

Groups gather, march, testify, or stand isolated inside institutional architecture.

## Lighting and Color

Naturalistic period light, grounded skin tones, motivated contrast, and purposeful historical palette.

## Production Design

Archives, homes, schools, courthouses, streets, clothing, documents, and civic spaces carry evidence.

## Editing

Clear chronology, testimony, parallel perspectives, archival bridges, and measured emotional crescendos.

## Sound and Scoring

Voice, crowd, protest, archival texture, silence, and score connect private and public stakes.

## Casting and Performance

Specific, dignified performances with strong ensemble listening and moral clarity.

## Collaborators and Collaborations

Writers, researchers, historians, editors, composers, and community consultants support accountability.

## Notable Evidentiary Works and Scenes

_Selma_, _13th_, _When They See Us_, and _Origin_.

## Themes

Justice, race, memory, dignity, institutional power, testimony, and collective resistance.

## Usage and When to Reference

Use for historical drama, civic stories, courtroom testimony, protest, and systemic injustice.

## Prompt Translation

`DuVernay-informed original civic drama: human-scale testimony, institutional wide shot, grounded period light, purposeful tracking, crowd sound, collective agency.`

## Hard Negatives

Avoid false equivalence, inspirational simplification, and period texture without researched specificity.
