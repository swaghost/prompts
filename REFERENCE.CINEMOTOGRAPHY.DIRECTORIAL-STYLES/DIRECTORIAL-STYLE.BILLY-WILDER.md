# Directorial Style: Billy Wilder

## Genre

Use the director's established genres as a starting point, then specify the original scene's genre and dramatic objective.

## Writing and Story Logic

Wilder builds stories around sharp reversals, compromised decisions, social hypocrisy, and characters who understand the bargain they are making. Comedy and tragedy often share the same scene.

## Directorial Evolution

His early European and noir influences lead into efficient Hollywood classical storytelling, then toward darker satire, unreliable narration, and increasingly bitter examinations of fame, work, sex, and aging.
