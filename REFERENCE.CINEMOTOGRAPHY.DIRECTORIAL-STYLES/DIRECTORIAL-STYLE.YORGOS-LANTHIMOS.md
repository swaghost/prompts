


## Nationality
Greek.

## Working Decades and Evolution by Decade
1980-2024; active across the 1980s through the 2020s.

# Directorial Style: Yorgos Lanthimos

## Genre

Absurdist comedy, dystopian drama, psychological satire, and period grotesque.

## Writing and Story Logic

Social rules become literal systems that expose desire, obedience, hierarchy, and absurdity.

## Directorial Evolution

Sparse Greek absurdism expands into lavish period worlds and more emotionally open satire.

## Visual Grammar

Deadpan cruelty, formal distance, behavioral rules, and bodies made strange by context.

## Camera and Lens

Wide-angle distortion, locked frames, slow zooms, and detached tracking.

## Composition and Blocking

Symmetric rooms, formal groups, awkward distance, and ritualized movement.

## Lighting and Color

Cold daylight, sterile interiors, pale period palettes, and controlled black accents.

## Production Design

Rules made visible through uniforms, rooms, menus, architecture, and strange objects.

## Editing

Abrupt ellipses, deadpan pauses, tonal reversals, and exact reaction timing.

## Sound and Scoring

Flat dialogue, footsteps, institutional ambience, odd music, and sudden silence.

## Casting and Performance

Affectless delivery, rigid posture, social miscalculation, and flashes of animal feeling.

## Collaborators and Collaborations

Recurring performers, designers, cinematographers, writers, and composers sustain the rule system.

## Notable Evidentiary Works and Scenes

_Dogtooth_, _The Lobster_, _The Favourite_, and _Poor Things_.

## Themes

Obedience, desire, hierarchy, language, freedom, gender, and social performance.

## Usage and When to Reference

Use for absurdist social rules, institutional comedy, dystopia, and period grotesque.

## Prompt Translation

`Lanthimos-informed original social satire: wide detached frame, ritual blocking, pale interior, flat dialogue, odd score, desire resisting a rule system.`

## Hard Negatives

Avoid cruelty as a punchline, random awkwardness, and period whimsy without social logic.
