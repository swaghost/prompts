# Directorial Style: Terrence Malick

## Writing and Story Logic

Memory, spiritual questioning, nature, violence, innocence, and grace unfold through association rather than strict plot mechanics.

## Directorial Evolution

Early narrative realism develops into increasingly fragmentary, voice-over-driven, landscape-centered, and formally experimental cinema.

## Camera and Lens

Choose camera height, movement, focal length, depth of field, and coverage to serve the scene's emotional information.

Legacy overview evidence: Handheld drifting, wide-angle floating camera, low natural-light movement, unplanned-feeling reframing.

## Composition and Blocking

Use bodies, thresholds, screen direction, foreground layers, and group geometry to make power and attention legible.

Legacy overview evidence: Characters crossing fields, sky and foliage as active elements, off-center faces, intimate fragments.

## Lighting and Color

Motivate the source, contrast, palette, practicals, and weather through the scene's emotional and material conditions.

Legacy overview evidence: Golden hour, lens flare, backlight, natural haze, soft green and earth tones, luminous highlights.

## Production Design

Select locations, objects, costume relationships, and surfaces that reveal the story rather than decorate it.

## Editing
