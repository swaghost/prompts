# Directorial Style: Alfred Hitchcock

## Genre

## Writing and Story Logic

Suspense is built through information control: the audience may know more or less than the protagonist. Innocent social spaces conceal obsession, guilt, surveillance, or danger.

## Directorial Evolution

British thrillers establish the visual grammar; Hollywood work refines it through subjective camera, color, sound, elaborate set pieces, and increasingly psychological protagonists.

## Visual Grammar

## Camera and Lens

Choose camera height, movement, focal length, depth of field, and coverage to serve the scene's emotional information.

Legacy overview evidence: Subjective POV, slow dolly toward realization, constrained camera movement, voyeuristic observation.

## Composition and Blocking

Use bodies, thresholds, screen direction, foreground layers, and group geometry to make power and attention legible.

Legacy overview evidence: Frames within frames, isolated figures, visual motifs, foreground obstructions, audience knows more or less than the character.

## Lighting and Color

Legacy overview evidence: Clean studio control, high-contrast suspense lighting, expressive color accents, ominous shadows.

## Production Design
