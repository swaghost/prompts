# STARTUP FROM THE GROUND UP

## The Idea Autopsy

«Act as a startup investor who has seen 2000 pitches and funds fewer than 1% of them. Read my idea below and give me the verdict you would give in a partner meeting — not the polished feedback, the real one. Tell me exactly why you would pass, what would have to be true for you to lean in, and the one question my idea cannot currently answer that kills it. My idea: [describe].»

## The Customer Who Already Exists

«Act as a customer research specialist who finds the early adopters that make or break a startup. For my business idea below, tell me exactly who is already in pain right now, where they are congregating online today, what they are currently paying to solve this badly, and the single sentence that would make them stop scrolling and read my offer. My idea: [describe]. My assumed customer: [describe].»

## The Zero-Budget Launch Sequence

«Act as a bootstrapped founder who has launched three businesses with under $1000 each. Design my launch sequence for the next 30 days with zero paid advertising. My idea: [describe]. My budget: [amount].»

## The Competitive Blind Spot Map

«Act as a market intelligence analyst. Study the competitive landscape for my business idea and tell me what every competitor is getting wrong, which customer complaint keeps appearing in their reviews. Then tell me how I plant my flag in that gap from day one. My idea: [describe]. Known competitors: [list].»

## The First Revenue Roadmap

«Act as a revenue strategist who specializes in getting startups to their first $10K in the fastest honest way possible. Given my idea, my skills, and my constraints below, map the exact path to first revenue — the offer, the price, the channel, the pitch, and the follow-up. Give me a 30-day sequence I can start executing tomorrow morning. My idea: [describe]. My skills: [describe]. My constraints: [time, budget, network].»
