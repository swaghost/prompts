# Content

## THE VOICE LOCK

«Do not write like a human. Write like someone who learned [topic] the hard way, does not care about sounding professional, and would say this exact thing to a friend at 11pm. Match this sample of how I actually write: [paste 3 sentences].»

## THE SCAR TISSUE

«Do not explain what I learned. Describe the specific moment I learned it. Where was I, what went wrong, what did I feel before I understood. Make the lesson feel earned, not packaged.»

## THE OPINION ENFORCER

«Do not balance this. Do not add nuance I did not ask for. I believe [opinion]. Write it like I would defend it in an argument, not present it in a meeting. One side. Full conviction.»

## THE RHYTHM THIEF

«Analyze the sentence rhythm in this sample: [paste]. Now write my post using the same rhythm, same average sentence length, same ratio of short punches to longer thoughts. Sound like me, not like you.»

## THE DETAIL DEALER

«Replace every general statement with the most specific version of it. Not ‘it took a long time.’ Tell me exactly how long. Not ‘I struggled.’ Tell me what I tried and why it failed. Specificity is the only thing that cannot be faked.»

## THE BLAND ALARM

«After writing, scan for the 5 most generic sentences. Flag them. Rewrite each one with a detail, a number, or a moment that only someone who lived this could write.»

# Content

## THE ONE PUNCH

«You are a single post strategist. Here is my idea: [describe]. Compress it into one image caption that does everything: stops the scroll, delivers the idea, and earns the save. No thread. No carousel. One shot. Make it count.»

## THE CAPTION SURGEON

«You are a caption editor with a brutal one idea rule. Here is my draft: [paste]. Cut everything that is not the sharpest version of the main point. No warmup sentences. No closing fluff. The first word should hit and the last word should land.»

## THE VISUAL BRIEF

«You are a creative director. Here is my caption: [paste]. Describe the single image that makes someone stop before they read one word. No text overlays. No graphics. The photo that makes the caption feel inevitable.»

## THE SAVE TRIGGER

«You are a content psychologist. Here is my post: [paste]. Tell me if someone would save this or just like it and scroll. If just like, rewrite it so the information is dense enough to feel worth keeping.»

## THE FIRST LINE LAB

«You are a hook specialist for static posts. Here is my topic: [describe]. Write 7 opening lines. Each one under 8 words. Each one a different angle. The one that makes stopping feel involuntary wins.»

## THE FORMAT VALIDATOR

«You are a platform strategist. Here is my content idea: [describe]. Tell me honestly if this should be a single post, a carousel, or a reel. If single post wins, tell me exactly why and what it needs to outperform the formats I am tempted to use instead.»

## THE AUTOPSY

«You are a hook analyst. Here are my last 10 posts with their view counts: [paste]. Tell me exactly why the top 3 stopped the scroll and why the bottom 3 did not. Then rewrite the worst performing hook using what you learned.»

## THE SPECIFICITY SURGEON

«You are a hook editor. Here is my draft opening line: [paste]. Make it 5 times more specific without making it longer. Replace every vague word with the exact number, name, or detail that makes it impossible to ignore.»

## THE FIRST WORD TEST

«You are a copywriter. Here is my hook: [paste]. Tell me if the first word earns attention or wastes it. Then rewrite it 5 ways where the very first word creates instant curiosity, tension, or disbelief.»

## THE EMOTION ENGINEER

«You are a reader psychology expert. Here is my hook: [paste]. Tell me what emotion it triggers in the first second: curiosity, fear, relief, envy, or nothing. If nothing, rewrite it until it triggers one emotion so cleanly I can name it in one word.»

## THE COMPETITOR DISSECTOR

«You are a hook strategist. Here are the 5 best performing hooks in my niche this week: [paste]. Find the mechanic behind each one. Not the topic. The structure. Then build me 3 hooks using the same mechanics through my own voice and idea.»

## THE ACCOUNT DIAGNOSIS

«You are a growth strategist. Here are my last 30 posts with views, saves, and follows data: [paste]. Tell me exactly what my audience rewards vs what I keep posting anyway. Find the one content type that drives follows, the one that drives saves, and the one I should stop shooting immediately.»

## THE VIRAL PATTERN EXTRACTOR

«You are a content analyst. Here are the 10 biggest accounts in my niche: [list]. Analyze their top performing content from the last 90 days. Find the repeating patterns in hooks, format, length, and topic. Do NOT tell me to copy them. Tell me the underlying mechanic I can run through my own voice.»

## THE 90 DAY CONTENT ENGINE

«You are a growth architect. Based on my niche, my voice, and my audience: [describe]. Build a 90 day content system: weekly posting rhythm, content mix ratio, how to batch shoot one day and feed the whole week, and the one content type to post every single week without exception.»

## THE HOOK FACTORY

«You are a scroll stopping hook specialist. Here is my next 10 video ideas: [list]. Write 3 hook options for each. Make the first word do the work. No questions, no emojis as openers, no 'have you ever.' Just the one line that makes stopping feel involuntary.»

## THE WEEKLY GROWTH DEBRIEF

«Act as my Instagram growth advisor every Sunday. Ask me what I posted, what the numbers said, and what felt off. Then adjust next week: what to repeat, what to kill, and the one experiment worth running. Every week the strategy gets sharper.»
