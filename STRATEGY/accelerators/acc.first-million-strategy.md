# First-Million Strategy Accelerator

## Purpose

Use this accelerator to build a realistic, ethical, legal, and sustainable plan toward a first-million financial objective. The prompt combines perspectives associated with long-term value creation, rational capital allocation, systems thinking, risk management, behavioural finance, and practical execution. It must not imitate or claim to be any specific living person's mind, advice, or private views.

## Core Prompt

```
Act as a rigorous personal financial strategy advisor combining the useful public ideas associated with long-term value investing, mental-model thinking, systems thinking, behavioural finance, business building, risk management, and practical execution. Do not imitate any specific person's private voice or claim to be them. Give me an evidence-aware, ethical, legal, and sustainable plan; do not promise returns or recommend illegal, deceptive, or reckless actions.

Your mission is to create a detailed plan to increase my wealth as quickly as reasonably possible while protecting my downside and preserving long-term sustainability.

My current situation:

Age: [AGE]
Profession: [PROFESSION]
Monthly income: [INCOME]
Current assets: [ASSETS]
Debts: [DEBTS]
Time available: [HOURS PER DAY]
Capital available for investment: [AMOUNT]
Current skills: [SKILLS]
Financial objective: [OBJECTIVE]

Ask targeted clarifying questions before giving the plan. Continue until you are at least 95% confident that the plan is based on enough information, or clearly state what remains uncertain. Never invent missing numbers.

PHASE 1 - COMPLETE DIAGNOSIS
Analyze deeply:
- My financial situation
- My income-generating capacity
- My assets and liabilities
- My risk profile
- My strengths
- My financial bottlenecks
- My habits that enrich me
- My habits that impoverish me

Show the 20% of actions most likely to generate 80% of the results, and distinguish high-confidence findings from assumptions.

PHASE 2 - WEALTH MAP
Create a plan to:
- Generate immediate income
- Increase my main income
- Create extra sources of income
- Build assets
- Generate recurring income
- Create wealth

Sort each opportunity by ease, speed, investment required, profit potential, scalability, risk, and fit with my skills and constraints.

PHASE 3 - ACCUMULATION STRATEGY
Create a plan to reach these milestones:
- R$ 10,000
- R$ 50,000
- R$ 100,000
- R$ 500,000
- R$ 1,000,000

For each stage, explain:
- What to do
- What to avoid
- Where to invest time
- Where to invest money
- What skills to develop
- What measurable evidence shows I am ready for the next stage

Use scenario ranges and explicit assumptions. Separate active income, business equity, savings, investments, and debt reduction. Explain taxes, fees, liquidity, inflation, and downside risks where relevant.

PHASE 4 - MONEY-MAKING MACHINE
Create:
- 10 ways to make money using my current skills
- 10 ways to make money using Artificial Intelligence responsibly
- 10 ways to make money online
- 10 ways to make money creating digital assets
- 10 ways to make scalable money

For each idea, enter:
- Profit potential
- Time to first revenue
- Difficulty
- Scalability
- Startup cost
- Key risk
- Cheapest validation test

Prioritize ideas by evidence, fit, and execution feasibility rather than hype.

PHASE 5 - IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
Create:
- A 30-day plan
- A 90-day plan
- A 1-year plan
- A 3-year plan

Set specific targets for:
- Revenue
- Profit
- Emergency reserves
- Debt reduction
- Investments
- Asset ownership
- Skills and capabilities
- Business systems

For each horizon, define weekly actions, leading indicators, lagging indicators, review cadence, and what should be stopped if it is not working.

PHASE 6 - FATAL MISTAKES
List:
- The biggest financial mistakes I should avoid
- Common pitfalls of the middle class that may apply to my situation
- Bad investments and scams to avoid
- Invisible waste and financial leaks
- Limiting beliefs about money

For every item, distinguish a behavioural problem, an evidence-based financial risk, and a context-dependent claim. Do not use stereotypes as financial analysis.

PHASE 7 - MILLIONAIRE MINDSET
Explain:
- How wealthy people may think differently, without romanticizing wealth
- How to evaluate risk
- How to recognize opportunities
- How to make decisions under uncertainty
- How to build wealth through ownership, skills, systems, and patient compounding

Translate every mindset point into an observable behaviour, a decision rule, and a measurable practice.

Finally, give me:
- The single highest-leverage offer, career move, or asset strategy for my situation
- The single best channel for pursuing it
- The first action I should take this week
- The largest risk and how to limit it
- The cheapest test that could disprove the plan
- A concise 30-day scoreboard
```

## Clarifying Questions Contract

Before building the plan, ask only questions that could materially change the recommendation. Cover:

- Country, currency, tax jurisdiction, and legal constraints.
- Dependents, essential monthly expenses, and emergency reserves.
- Debt balances, interest rates, repayment terms, and delinquency risk.
- Income stability, available hours, and employment restrictions.
- Skills with demonstrated demand, portfolio, credentials, and sales ability.
- Current audience, distribution channels, business assets, and network.
- Risk tolerance, liquidity needs, time horizon, and ethical boundaries.
- Whether the objective means R$1,000,000 in net worth, liquid assets, annual revenue, business value, or another measure.

Do not treat the objective as clear until the unit, timeframe, and measurement method are defined.

## Required Output Structure

### 1. Executive Diagnosis

Provide a concise snapshot of the current position, the binding constraint, the strongest advantage, the key assumptions, and confidence level. Show what is known, estimated, and unknown.

### 2. Wealth Map Table

Use this table for every opportunity:

| Opportunity | Mechanism | Time to first result | Capital required | Upside | Downside | Scalability | Fit | Validation test |
| ----------- | --------- | -------------------: | ---------------: | -----: | -------: | ----------: | --: | --------------- |

Rank the opportunities by risk-adjusted fit, not gross upside alone.

### 3. Milestone Economics

For each milestone, show:

- Starting capital and expected contributions.
- Income or profit engine.
- Savings or reinvestment rate.
- Assumed return range, clearly labeled as an assumption rather than a promise.
- Time range to milestone.
- Main failure mode.
- Trigger for changing strategy.

Use conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios. Never present a projection as a guaranteed outcome.

### 4. Offer and Channel Decision

Choose one primary offer, career move, or asset-building strategy and one primary channel. Explain why focus is preferable to spreading effort across many ideas. Include the evidence needed to continue, pivot, or stop.

### 5. Implementation Plans

For 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, and 3 years include:

- Outcome target.
- Weekly actions.
- Time budget.
- Money budget.
- Leading indicators.
- Lagging indicators.
- Review date.
- Stop, continue, or change criteria.

### 6. Risk and Mistake Register

| Risk or mistake | Probability | Impact | Early warning | Prevention | Contingency |
| --------------- | ----------: | -----: | ------------- | ---------- | ----------- |

Include concentration risk, leverage, fraud, taxes, fees, liquidity, burnout, opportunity cost, and overconfidence where relevant.

### 7. Final Action Brief

End with:

- One primary direction.
- One channel.
- One action for the next 24 hours.
- One action for the next seven days.
- One metric to review weekly.
- One assumption to test first.

## Ethical and Financial Guardrails

- Do not promise wealth, investment returns, or a guaranteed path to R$1,000,000.
- Do not recommend fraud, tax evasion, insider trading, market manipulation, predatory lending, or deceptive marketing.
- Do not recommend regulated financial products without noting jurisdiction, fees, liquidity, risk, and the need for qualified local advice.
- Separate education and scenario planning from personalized regulated financial advice.
- Protect emergency reserves and essential living costs before recommending speculative investments.
- Treat debt interest, taxes, fees, inflation, and business failure as real variables.
- Do not assume that wealth reflects virtue or that financial struggle reflects personal failure.
- Do not use “the rich” as a monolith; identify which behaviour, market structure, or incentive is actually being discussed.
- Prefer reversible, low-cost experiments before large commitments.

## Follow-Up Prompt 1: Focused Income Goal

```
Based on everything you know about me, what is the fastest realistic way for me to reach my income goal of [GOAL]? Limit this to one offer and one channel only. Ask me targeted clarifying questions until you are at least 95% confident in the recommendation. State assumptions, the cheapest validation test, the first seven actions, the main risk, and the metric that decides whether I continue or pivot. Do not promise results or recommend illegal, deceptive, or reckless actions.
```

## Follow-Up Prompt 2: Compound the Opportunity

```
Once I unlock this opportunity, what is the best way to compound it into long-term wealth? Map the path from first revenue to durable assets, recurring income, ownership, systems, and optionality. Separate reinvestment into the business from personal savings and diversified investments. Include milestones, risks, liquidity needs, taxes and fees to investigate, decision rules, and what evidence would justify each next step. Do not assume high returns or guarantee an outcome.
```

## Follow-Up Prompt 3: Focused Execution Plan

```
Create a 30-day plan to maximize revenue from that opportunity while focusing on the one thing I should do for four hours every day. Define the single priority, the exact daily work block, weekly deliverables, outreach or distribution actions, learning limits, success metrics, stop conditions, and review ritual. Keep the plan ethical, legal, sustainable, and realistic. Identify which tasks to ignore so focus is protected.
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Objective is defined as net worth, liquid assets, revenue, profit, or business value.
- [ ] Currency, timeframe, tax jurisdiction, and constraints are known.
- [ ] Diagnosis separates facts, estimates, assumptions, and unknowns.
- [ ] Opportunities are ranked by risk-adjusted fit.
- [ ] One primary direction and one channel are selected.
- [ ] Milestones use scenarios rather than guaranteed projections.
- [ ] Emergency reserves, debt, taxes, fees, liquidity, and downside are addressed.
- [ ] Each plan includes measurable actions and stop or pivot criteria.
- [ ] The first action is small enough to execute immediately.
