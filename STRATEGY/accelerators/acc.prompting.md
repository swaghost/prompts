# Prompt Engineering and Context Management

## The Master Context Prompt

«You’re helping me build a permanent context block I paste at the top of every Claude Project. Here’s everything relevant about me: my work, role, audience, goals, tone, and the outputs I create most often: [describe]. Compress it into a 150-word block that gives Claude everything it needs to skip the setup and start working immediately.»

## The Tone Blueprint

«You’re a writing coach. Here’s a sample of my best work: [paste 2-3 pieces]. Extract my voice — sentence rhythm, vocabulary level, what I never say, how I open, how I close. Write a tone guide Claude can follow so everything it writes sounds like me, not like Claude.»

## The Role Card

«You’re helping me write a permanent role instruction for Claude. My work: [describe]. My goals: [describe]. My audience: [describe]. Write a single paragraph that tells Claude exactly who to be, how to think, and what to prioritize every time I open a new chat.»

## The Output Library

«You’re a prompt engineer. Here are the 5 outputs I ask Claude for most often: [list]. For each one, write a reusable prompt template with placeholders I can fill in under 60 seconds. Store them as a library I paste into my Project instructions once and use forever.»

## The Anti-Pattern List

«You’re a Claude optimizer. Here’s what I hate in AI responses: [describe — bullet lists, long intros, hedging, over-explaining, fake enthusiasm]. Write a permanent instruction block that kills every one of these patterns before they appear. One rule per bad habit. Brutal and specific.»
