# Faceless Content Builder Prompts - Instagram Strategy

## Purpose

A comprehensive prompt collection for building a profitable faceless Instagram account from scratch. These prompts are designed to be used sequentially, guiding you from niche selection through daily content creation and monetization strategy. Use with ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI assistant.

---

## Prompt Sequence Overview

1. **Niche Selection** - Find monetizable faceless niches
2. **Pain Point Mining** - Identify daily content opportunities
3. **Script Writing** - Create faceless reel scripts
4. **Hook Creation** - Generate scroll-stopping openings
5. **Resource Discovery** - Find free B-roll and stock footage
6. **Monetization Strategy** - Map revenue streams
7. **Content Calendar** - Plan 30 days of strategic posts

## 1️⃣ Prompt 1: Niche Selection & Monetization Ranking

### Purpose

Identify profitable faceless niches ranked by ease of monetization without showing your face.

### Prompt

```
List 10 faceless Instagram niches where people actually spend money. I do not want followers. I want buyers. Rank them by how easy it is to monetise without showing my face.
```

### What to Look For

- Niches with proven buyer intent (not just engagement)
- Markets where visual content (not personal brand) drives value
- Topics with existing product/service ecosystems
- Areas where expertise matters more than personality

### Example Output Niches

- Finance/budgeting tools
- Productivity systems
- Digital templates and resources
- Home organization methods
- AI tool tutorials
- Fitness programs (demo-focused)
- Recipe content
- Travel planning guides

### Next Step

Choose ONE niche from the ranked list to focus on completely before moving to other niches.

## 2️⃣ Prompt 2: Pain Point Mining for Daily Content

### Purpose

Extract 20 specific, actionable problems your target audience desperately wants solved. These become your content pillars.

### Prompt

```
I picked [niche]. Now give me 20 problems people in this niche are desperately trying to solve right now. Be specific. I want real pain points I can create content around every single day.
```

### How to Use Results

- Each pain point = 1-3 pieces of content
- Prioritize problems mentioned in comments/DMs of competitor accounts
- Look for problems with expensive solutions (high buyer intent)
- Mix "awareness stage" problems (broad reach) with "decision stage" problems (conversion)

### Content Mapping

- Problems 1-10: Broad awareness content (growth)
- Problems 11-20: Specific solution content (monetization)

### Example Pain Points (Productivity Niche)

- "I waste 2 hours deciding what to work on each morning"
- "My to-do list has 50+ items and I don't know where to start"
- "I keep switching between apps and lose my flow state"

## 3️⃣ Prompt 3: Faceless Reel Script Template

### Purpose

Generate complete 60-second reel scripts optimized for faceless accounts with text overlay guidance.

### Prompt

```
Write me a 60 second reel script about [topic] for a faceless Instagram account. Use a bold hook in the first 2 seconds. Add text overlay suggestions for each section. No face needed.
```

### Script Structure You'll Get

- **0-2 sec**: Bold hook (stops scroll)
- **3-10 sec**: Problem statement (creates relatability)
- **11-40 sec**: Solution steps/framework (delivers value)
- **41-55 sec**: Proof/example (builds credibility)
- **56-60 sec**: CTA (directs next action)

### Text Overlay Guidelines

- Large, bold text for hook (readable in thumbnail)
- 3-5 words max per screen
- Transition text with video cuts
- Use contrasting colors (white on dark, dark on light)

### Example Topic Fill-Ins

- "3 productivity apps that actually save time"
- "How I organize my week in 10 minutes"
- "The morning routine that doubled my output"

## 4️⃣ Prompt 4: Scroll-Stopping Hook Generator

### Purpose

Create curiosity-driven, bold hooks under 10 words that make viewers stop mid-scroll.

### Prompt

```
Give me 10 scroll stopping hooks for faceless reels in [niche]. Each one should create curiosity and make someone stop mid scroll. Keep them under 10 words. Bold and impossible to ignore.
```

### Hook Types You'll Get

- **Contrarian**: "Everyone's wrong about [topic]"
- **Numbered**: "5 [things] nobody talks about"
- **Transformation**: "I went from [bad] to [good] using [thing]"
- **Mistake**: "Stop doing [common practice]—here's why"
- **Secret**: "[Number] [niche] secrets pros don't share"

### Hook Testing Strategy

1. Generate 10 hooks
2. Test top 3 as Trial Reels (see Instagram Settings section)
3. Use winning hook format for next 5-7 reels
4. Refresh with new batch when performance drops

### What Makes a Hook Work

- Creates information gap (curiosity)
- Promises specific outcome
- Contradicts common belief
- Uses numbers (brain loves specificity)
- Speaks to pain or desire directly

## 5️⃣ Prompt 5: Free B-Roll & Stock Footage Resources

### Purpose

Discover professional-quality visual resources you can use commercially without spending money.

### Prompt

```
List 5 free websites where I can find b-roll footage, stock video and animations safe for commercial use. I need professional looking content without spending a dollar.
```

### What You'll Get

- Royalty-free video libraries (Pexels, Pixabay, etc.)
- Animation resources (Canva, LottieFiles)
- Sound effect libraries
- Music for commercial use
- License information for each

### Content Asset Strategy

1. Download 50-100 clips related to your niche
2. Organize by category/emotion
3. Create reels by combining clips with text overlays
4. Add trending audio
5. Batch-produce 7-14 reels in one session

### Pro Tips

- Search by emotion/mood, not just topic
- Download 4K when possible (future-proof)
- Keep a swipe file of competitor visual styles
- Mix stock footage with screen recordings (tools, apps)

## 6️⃣ Prompt 6: Monetization Strategy (No Product Required)

### Purpose

Identify 4 ways to generate revenue from faceless account without creating your own product.

### Prompt

```
Give me 4 ways to make money from a faceless Instagram account in [niche] without creating my own product. Include affiliate marketing, UGC, digital products with resell rights and brand deals. Tell me which one to start with first.
```

### Revenue Streams You'll Learn

1. **Affiliate Marketing**: Promote existing products, earn commission
2. **UGC (User Generated Content)**: Create content for brands, get paid per post
3. **Digital Products with Resell Rights**: Sell templates/tools you didn't create
4. **Brand Deals**: Sponsored posts/stories for relevant companies

### Which to Start With

- **Under 5K followers**: Affiliate marketing (no follower count required for most programs)
- **5K-20K followers**: UGC + Affiliate combo
- **20K+ followers**: Add brand deals
- **Established (6+ months)**: Layer in resell rights products

### Example Monetization Timeline

- Month 1-2: Focus on growth only
- Month 3: Add affiliate links in bio/stories
- Month 4-6: Pitch UGC to 10 brands
- Month 6+: Negotiate brand deals, add product sales

## 7️⃣ Prompt 7: 30-Day Content Calendar Builder

### Purpose

Generate a complete month of strategically sequenced content optimized for growth (weeks 1-2) and monetization (weeks 3-4).

### Prompt

```
Build me a 30 day content calendar for my faceless Instagram. Mix reels, carousels and stories. Optimise for growth the first 2 weeks and monetisation the last 2 weeks. Include the hook for every single post.
```

### Calendar Structure You'll Get

**Weeks 1-2 (Growth Phase)**:

- 70% Reels (algorithm favors)
- 20% Carousels (saves + shares)
- 10% Stories (engagement)
- Focus: Broad appeal, shareable value, relatable pain points
- Goal: Reach new audiences, gain followers

**Weeks 3-4 (Monetization Phase)**:

- 50% Reels (maintain reach)
- 30% Carousels (detailed value)
- 20% Stories (direct CTAs)
- Focus: Specific solutions, product mentions, case studies, testimonials
- Goal: Convert followers to customers

### Content Mix Per Week

- **Educational**: 40% (how-to, tutorials, frameworks)
- **Entertainment**: 30% (relatable, trending audio, memes)
- **Engagement**: 20% (questions, polls, this-or-that)
- **Promotional**: 10% (product mentions, CTAs, testimonials)

### Hook Examples Per Phase

**Growth Hooks (Weeks 1-2)**:

- "5 mistakes keeping you stuck in [pain point]"
- "I wish someone told me this about [topic]"
- "The [niche] method nobody talks about"

**Monetization Hooks (Weeks 3-4)**:

- "How [product] solved my [specific problem]"
- "Before I used [solution] vs After"
- "Why I'll never go back to [old method]"

## Content Format Optimization (Faceless Specific)

### Reels (Primary Growth Engine)

**Faceless Reel Best Practices**:

- **2-second hook rule**: Text overlay + visual change in first 2 seconds (no face = must grab attention faster)
- **Trending audio under 10K uses**: Sweet spot before saturation (search "trending" in audio library)
- **B-roll + text overlay combo**: Stock footage synchronized with on-screen text telling the story
- **Teleprompter for voiceover**: Record smooth voiceover using teleprompter apps (voice adds humanity to faceless)
- **Stack 3 hooks**: Opening visual + bold text + voiceover = triple retention
- **Translate captions**: Reach global audience (enable in settings, Instagram auto-translates)
- **Link related content**: "Part 2 coming tomorrow" drives profile visits

**When to Use**: Daily content for growth, trending topic reactions, quick tips, transformation stories

### Carousels (Save + Share Machine)

**Faceless Carousel Best Practices**:

- **First slide = pattern interrupt**: Bold statement or curiosity gap (no face = text must hook harder)
- **Step-by-step tutorials win**: Numbered breakdowns, checklists, frameworks get saved most
- **1080 x 1440 portrait format**: Optimal feed visibility on Instagram (test 1080 x 1350 if audience prefers)
- **Clean design > busy graphics**: Minimal text per slide, high contrast, one focal point
- **Final slide CTA**: "Save this for later", "Send to someone who needs this", "DM 'GUIDE' for free resource"
- **5 relevant hashtags maximum**: Hyper-niche only (2026 algorithm penalizes 6+)

**When to Use**: Educational deep-dives, lead magnets, tutorials, case studies, comparison posts

### Stories (Engagement + Community)

**Faceless Story Best Practices**:

- **Use polls/questions/quizzes**: Interactive stickers boost engagement even without face
- **Show your workspace/process**: Behind-the-scenes of content creation (screen recordings, editing clips)
- **70% value / 30% personal**: Share journey without showing face (memes, relatable quotes, progress updates)
- **Take 1 day off per week**: Prevents burnout and audience story fatigue
- **Promote new posts**: Reshare feed content to stories for double visibility

**When to Use**: Daily check-ins, Q&A sessions, promoting new reels/carousels, testing ideas before making reels

### Trial Reels (Risk-Free Testing)

**Faceless Trial Reel Best Practices**:

- **Test new niches**: Try adjacent topics without alienating current followers
- **Experiment with bold hooks**: Controversial takes, pattern interrupts, unusual angles
- **Test thumbnails/text overlays**: See which visual style stops scrolls best
- **Grow new audience segments**: Content for different demographics within niche
- **Post freely**: Doesn't affect main analytics or feed performance

**When to Use**: Validating new content directions, testing monetization angles, exploring niche expansion

### Single Posts (Community Building)

**Faceless Single Post Best Practices**:

- **Workspace/product photos**: Show what you use, where you create, results you've achieved
- **Quote graphics**: Inspirational or educational text-based images (clean design, brand colors)
- **Results screenshots**: Testimonials, analytics, transformation evidence (blur faces/names)
- **Caption hooks matter**: First 125 characters show in feed—make them curiosity-driven
- **5 hashtags matching niche**: Use exact keywords your audience searches
- **Repeat keyword 2-3 times in caption**: Helps Instagram semantic search surface your content

**When to Use**: Announcements, testimonials, milestone celebrations, weekly community posts

### Format Mix for Faceless Success

**Month 1 (Growth Focus)**:

- 65% Reels (discovery engine)
- 25% Carousels (shareable value)
- 10% Stories (engagement)
- 0% Single Posts (save for later)

**Month 2+ (Growth + Monetization)**:

- 50% Reels (maintain reach)
- 30% Carousels (detailed tutorials)
- 15% Stories (CTAs and community)
- 5% Single Posts (social proof, testimonials)

**Pro Tip**: Faceless accounts NEED volume in first 60 days. Batch-produce 7-14 reels in one session using templates, stock footage, and scripted voiceovers. Quality matters, but consistency matters more early on.

## Pro Tips for Maximum Results

### Prompt Customization

- Be specific about your niche when filling in [brackets]
- Add context: "I'm targeting [demographic]" for better results
- Include competitor examples: "Similar to @[account]"
- Specify tone: "Make it conversational/professional/edgy"

### Iteration Strategy

- Don't use AI output verbatim—edit for your voice
- Run same prompt 2-3 times, cherry-pick best ideas
- Combine outputs (e.g., Hook from batch 1 + Script from batch 2)
- Save winning prompts and modify slightly each month

### Content Production Workflow

1. **Monday**: Run prompts 3-4, get hooks + scripts for week
2. **Tuesday**: Download b-roll, organize assets
3. **Wednesday**: Batch-produce 7 reels (2-3 hours)
4. **Thursday**: Write captions with keywords
5. **Friday**: Schedule posts for next week
6. **Weekend**: Engage with comments, analyze performance

### Tracking What Works

- Keep spreadsheet: Hook | Format | Views | Saves | Conversions
- Double down on high-save content (algorithm boost)
- Repurpose top 20% of content monthly
- Test new formats on 20% of calendar only

## Notes

**Philosophy**: These prompts are designed to remove decision fatigue. Instead of staring at a blank screen wondering what to post, you have a systematic approach to content ideation, production, and monetization.

**Success Pattern**:

1. Choose ONE niche (Prompt 1)
2. Mine pain points (Prompt 2)
3. Create 30 days of content (Prompts 3-4-7)
4. Set up monetization (Prompt 6)
5. Analyze, optimize, repeat

**Timeline to Revenue**: Most accounts see first sales within 60-90 days following this system, assuming:

- Consistent posting (5-7x/week)
- Engagement with audience (reply to comments/DMs)
- Strategic monetization setup (affiliate links/products)
- Content optimization based on analytics

**Key Insight**: Faceless accounts work because they remove the personal brand bottleneck. You're not selling you—you're selling systems, solutions, and transformations. The content is the brand.
