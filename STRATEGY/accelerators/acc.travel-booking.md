# TRAVEL & BOOKING

## THE FLIGHT DECODER

«You are an expert flight hacker. I want to fly from [city] to [destination] around [dates] with a budget of [amount]. Give me 5 routing strategies the algorithms never show: which nearby airports to check, which days shift the price by hundreds, which layover combinations unlock cheaper fares, and which booking window to hit. Be ruthlessly specific.»

## THE PRICE DROP PREDICTOR

«You are an airfare analyst. Here is the route I am watching: [describe]. Tell me exactly when to buy based on this route’s historical pricing patterns, which days of the week prices drop, and whether waiting or booking now is the smarter move. Then tell me what to set alerts for and where.»

## THE HIDDEN CITY AUDIT

«You are a fare structure expert. Analyze this route: [describe]. Find every unconventional booking strategy that legally cuts the price: hidden city tickets, positioning flights, open jaw routings, and mistake fare windows. Explain the risk of each so I can decide what I am comfortable with.»

## THE ACCOMMODATION ARBITRAGE

«You are a travel cost strategist. I am visiting [destination] for [X] nights with a budget of [amount]. Find me the neighborhoods where I get the most value, what to search on each platform, what dates to shift if flexibility saves real money, and what I should never pay full price for in this city.»

## THE TOTAL COST REALITY CHECK

«You are a brutal travel budget advisor. Here is my trip as planned: [describe]. Find every cost I have not accounted for: baggage fees, tourist taxes, airport transfers, tipping culture, dynamic pricing traps, and the three things that always blow first time visitors to this destination. Give me the real number.»

## THE REBOOKING RADAR

«You are a flight monitoring strategist. I already booked [route] at [price]. Tell me exactly how to watch for price drops that trigger airline rebooking policies, which airlines on this route offer free changes, and what to say to customer service to get a credit if the price falls after I book.»
