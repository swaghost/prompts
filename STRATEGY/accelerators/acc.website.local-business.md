# Local Business Website Builder

## Prompt

```
Build a complete website for [BUSINESS TYPE] in [CITY] as one HTML file. The business goal is [PRIMARY GOAL, such as phone calls, quote requests, or bookings]. Write every word yourself in a voice their customers respond to; use no corporate filler. Include services, why choose us, an FAQ with the 5 questions this business actually gets asked, and a working contact form. Use original branding and make the page mobile-first, accessible, fast, and conversion-focused.
```

## Output Requirements

- Write specific local-business copy based on the supplied business and city.
- Include a clear primary action, service details, trust signals, five useful FAQs, and a contact form.
- Make phone, email, and location details easy to update.
- Explain what requires a backend or form service to function in production.
- Do not invent certifications, testimonials, guarantees, reviews, addresses, or claims that were not supplied.
