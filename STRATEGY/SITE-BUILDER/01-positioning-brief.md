# 1. The Positioning Brief

Decides what the site has to say before a single pixel gets designed.

Act as a positioning strategist. My business is [WHAT YOU DO] and my customer is [WHO]. Write a one page brief covering the single promise the site must land in five seconds, the three objections that stop this customer buying, the proof I need on the page to beat each one, and the one action the whole site should drive toward. Challenge my description if it is vague.
