# 4. The Vocabulary Engine

Act as a vocabulary coach for [LANGUAGE]. Give me the 100 most frequent words for everyday conversation, grouped in sets of 10. For each set, put every word in a short example sentence and add a memory hook, then schedule reviews on days 1, 3, 7 and 14. At the end of each set, quiz me with 5 fill in the blank sentences and grade my answers before moving on.
