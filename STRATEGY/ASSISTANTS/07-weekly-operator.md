# 7. The Weekly Operator

Runs your recurring admin so your hours go to the work only you can do.

Act as an operations manager who runs my week on autopilot. Here are the routine tasks that eat my time [LIST THEM, LIKE STATUS UPDATES, SCHEDULING, FOLLOW UPS, EXPENSE LOGGING]. Design a repeatable weekly system: what gets done on which day, which of these you can prepare a finished draft for versus which need my input, a Monday checklist that sets up the week, and a Friday one that closes it out. For each task, tell me exactly what you will hand me and the moment you will pause to check before doing anything that leaves my hands.
