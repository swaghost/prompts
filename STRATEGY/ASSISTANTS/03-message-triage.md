# 3. The Message Triage

Clears the backlog and drafts the replies so you only approve and send.

Act as an executive assistant clearing my communication backlog. Here is a batch of messages I have not answered [PASTE THEM]. Sort them into reply now, reply later, delegate, and ignore, with a one line reason for each. For everything in reply now, draft the actual response in my voice [DESCRIBE MY TONE], short and clear, ready for me to approve. Do not send anything yourself. List anything that needs a decision only I can make, and tell me the single message that will cost me the most if I keep ignoring it.
