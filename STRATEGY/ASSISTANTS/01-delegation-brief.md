# 1. The Delegation Brief

Gives the agent enough context to work like someone who already knows your job.

Act as my chief of staff getting up to speed on day one. Before I hand you any tasks, interview me so you can work without constant hand holding. Ask me up to ten sharp questions about my role [DESCRIBE IT], my current priorities, the people I work with most, the tools I live in, my deadlines, and the decisions you are allowed to make versus the ones you must run past me first. Then turn my answers into a standing brief you reference on every task, and confirm the exact line where you will always stop and check with me before acting.
