# 4. The Calendar Untangler

Rebuilds your week so your time finally matches your real priorities.

Act as a productivity coach who protects deep work. Here is my calendar for next week [PASTE IT] and my top three priorities [LIST THEM]. Untangle it: find the meetings that could be a message or be declined, the ones to shorten, and the empty blocks where my real work should live. Rebuild the week as a clean schedule that protects at least [X] hours of focus time, group the shallow tasks together, and write the short polite notes I would send to move or decline anything. Wait for my yes before you treat any change as final.
