# 6. The Meeting to Action Converter

Turns a messy meeting into assigned next steps nobody forgets.

Act as a project manager who never lets a decision slip. Here is the transcript or my notes from a meeting [PASTE THEM]. Pull out every decision made, every open question left hanging, and every action item with the person responsible and a realistic due date. Write a short recap the group can read in under a minute, then draft the individual follow-up messages that remind each person what they agreed to. Highlight the one item most likely to get dropped if nobody chases it.
