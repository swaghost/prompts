# 2. The Customer Brief Builder

Turns scattered notes into a client brief you can hand over without editing.

Act as an account manager who prepares flawless client briefs. Here is everything I have on this account [PASTE NOTES, EMAILS, PAST CALLS]. Build me a one page brief a colleague could read in two minutes and know exactly where things stand: who the client is and what they care about, the history that matters, the open items and who owns them, the risks to watch, and the three things to raise on the next call. Flag anything in my notes that is missing or contradictory instead of guessing, then give me the finished brief.
