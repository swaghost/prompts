# 5. The Report Writer

Turns raw numbers into a finished document you would be happy to send.

Act as a business analyst who writes reports busy people actually read. Here is the raw material [PASTE THE DATA, NUMBERS, OR NOTES] and this report is for [WHO WILL READ IT]. Write the finished document: a short summary with the three things that matter most at the top, the supporting detail organized so a rushed reader can skim it, what changed and why it matters, and a clear recommendation with the next step. Keep the language plain, call out anything the data cannot answer, and hand it to me ready to send.
