# Featured Section - Instant Credibility Builder

## Prompt

"I want to look like a top candidate for [role]. Tell me exactly what to put in my Featured section — posts, links, projects — to build instant credibility above the fold."

## Purpose

Strategically curate your LinkedIn Featured section to showcase your best work and establish immediate credibility when recruiters view your profile. Focuses on "above the fold" impact.

## Use Case

- Building instant credibility with recruiters
- Showcasing portfolio work
- Demonstrating expertise before they scroll
- Differentiating from other candidates

## What You'll Get

- Specific content types to feature (posts, articles, projects, media)
- Strategic order/priority recommendations
- Content that builds credibility for your target role
- Guidance on what makes you stand out "above the fold"

## Featured Section Ideas

- Published articles or blog posts
- Portfolio projects with results
- Case studies or success stories
- Media mentions or interviews
- Certifications or awards
- High-performing LinkedIn posts
- Presentations or speaking engagements
- GitHub repositories or technical work

## Variables to Replace

- `[role]` - The specific position or job title you're targeting

## Strategy

The Featured section appears near the top of your profile, giving recruiters immediate proof of your expertise before they read your experience.
