# Experience Bullets - Action Verb + Results Format

## Prompt

"Rewrite my experience bullets using: action verb + what I did + measurable result. If I have no numbers, ask me questions to find them. Every line should sound like a win."

## Purpose

Transform weak experience bullets into powerful achievement statements that demonstrate impact. Uses the proven formula: action verb + task + quantifiable result.

## Use Case

- Improving LinkedIn experience section
- Writing resume bullet points
- Quantifying accomplishments
- Making your work sound impressive

## What You'll Get

- Rewritten bullets with strong action verbs
- Measurable results for each accomplishment
- Questions to help you find metrics if you don't have them
- Achievement-focused language ("wins")

## Formula

**Action Verb** + **What I Did** + **Measurable Result**

Example:

- Before: "Responsible for social media"
- After: "Grew Instagram following by 340% in 6 months, driving 15K+ monthly website visits"

## Benefits

- Makes impact immediately visible
- Helps you pass ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)
- Shows results-oriented mindset
- Differentiates you from passive job descriptions
