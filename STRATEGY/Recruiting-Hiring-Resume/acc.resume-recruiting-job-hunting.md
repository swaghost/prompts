# RESUME & JOB-HUNTING

## The Rejection Decoder

«You’re a senior recruiter who screens 300 resumes daily. Here’s my resume: [paste]. Here’s the role I keep missing: [paste job description]. Don’t be kind. Tell me the exact reason I’m not getting called — weak positioning, ATS failure, wrong keywords, or poor formatting. Rank by damage. Start fixing immediately.»

## The Bullet Surgeon

«You’re a resume writer. Here’s my experience section: [paste]. Every bullet that describes a duty gets deleted and replaced with a result. If I have no numbers, tell me what to go find and exactly how to frame it. When you’re done, no bullet should say what I did — only what changed because I did it.»

## The 10-Second Auditor

«You’re a recruiter with 10 seconds per resume. Here’s mine: [paste]. Tell me what you know about me after 10 seconds, what’s still unclear, and what put me in the no pile. Then rewrite my header, title, and summary so the decision flips in my favor.»

## The Keyword Injector

«You’re an ATS specialist. My resume: [paste]. Job description: [paste]. Pull every keyword the system scores for. Show me what I’m missing and what I’ve buried. Rewrite my top 5 bullets to pass the scan without reading like a keyword list.»

## The Final Eye

«You’re a senior resume editor. Here’s my revised resume: [paste]. Last pass before it goes out. Kill every weak verb, vague claim, and inconsistent format. Raise the perceived seniority of every line. Output the final version — nothing left to fix.»

## The Hiring Manager Cold Read

«Act as a hiring manager who has already decided to pass on this resume before finishing the first section. Read my resume below and tell me exactly what made you stop caring — the weak opener, the buried achievements, the generic job titles, or the missing context. Be specific about the line where you mentally moved on. Resume: [paste]. Target role: [insert role].»

## The Career Story Reconstructor

«Act as an executive resume writer who specializes in making non-linear careers look intentional. Read my background below and rewrite my career narrative so every job, gap, and pivot looks like a deliberate step toward [target role]. Make the through-line obvious even to someone who reads it in 8 seconds. Background: [paste].»

## The Metric Excavator

«Act as a resume strategist who refuses to accept vague impact. Go through every bullet point in my resume and push me to find the real number behind it — revenue influenced, time saved, team size, growth percentage, cost reduced. For anything that truly cannot be quantified, rewrite it so the scale and consequence are still felt. Resume: [paste].»

## The Role Gap Analyst

«Act as a recruiter who specializes in [target role] and has filled this exact position 40 times. Compare my resume to what you actually look for and tell me what is present, what is missing, and what is there but framed wrong. Resume: [paste]. Job description: [paste].»

## The Seniority Signal Rewriter

«Act as an executive branding consultant. Rewrite my resume so every line signals the level I am targeting, not the level I am leaving. Target level: [insert]. Resume: [paste].»

## The One Page Forcing Function

«Act as an editor who has been told this resume must fit one page without losing a single meaningful achievement. Cut everything that is filler, repetition, or implied by the job title anyway. Resume: [paste].»

## THE RESUME TRANSLATOR 
“You are a hiring manager who has read 10,000 resumes. Here is mine and here is the job description: [paste both]. Tell me every place my resume speaks my language instead of theirs. Then rewrite my top 5 bullet points using the exact words, metrics, and outcomes this company is actually hiring for.”

## THE INTERVIEW INTELLIGENCE BRIEF 
“You are a research analyst. Here is the company I am interviewing with and the role: [describe]. Give me everything I need to walk in knowing: the real business problem this hire solves, the question they will definitely ask that most candidates answer badly, and the one thing I can say that proves I already think like someone in this role.”

## THE OUTREACH THAT GETS REPLIES 
“You are a cold outreach expert. I want to reach the hiring manager for this role directly, not apply through the portal: [describe role and company]. Write me a message under 60 words that leads with what I know about their business, not what I want from them. Make replying feel easier than ignoring.”

## THE OFFER NEGOTIATOR 
“You are a salary negotiation coach. I just received this offer: [describe]. Tell me exactly what to counter, what language to use, and what I am leaving on the table if I say yes today. Then write the exact message that opens the negotiation without risking the offer.”
