# Recruiter Search Terms - LinkedIn Visibility Check

## Prompt

"Pretend you're a recruiter searching LinkedIn for [role]. List the exact search terms you'd type. Then tell me if my profile would appear — and what's blocking me."

## Purpose

Reverse-engineer the recruiter's search process to understand exactly what terms they use and whether your profile would appear in their results. Identifies gaps in your keyword optimization.

## Use Case

- Understanding why you're not getting recruiter messages
- Optimizing for LinkedIn search algorithm
- Identifying missing keywords in your profile
- Improving search visibility for specific roles

## What You'll Get

- Exact search terms recruiters use for your target role
- Boolean search strings they might use
- Analysis of whether your profile would appear
- Specific blocking issues (missing keywords, wrong title, etc.)
- Recommendations for where to add keywords

## Variables to Replace

- `[role]` - The specific position or job title you're targeting

## Example Output

- Primary search terms
- Secondary/related keywords
- Boolean operators recruiters use
- Your current visibility status
- What's preventing you from appearing
- Strategic placement recommendations
