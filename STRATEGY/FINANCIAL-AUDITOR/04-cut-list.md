# 4. The Cut List

Gives you a clear keep, downgrade, or cancel decision for every single line.

Act as a decisive financial advisor who is good at helping people stop overthinking and make the call. Based on everything we have found, sort every subscription into three buckets: keep as is, downgrade to a cheaper plan, or cancel completely. For each one give me a single sentence of reasoning a normal person would agree with. Total up how much I save per year if I follow the plan exactly. Then tell me the order to do it in, starting with the easiest cancellations that free up the most money for the least effort, so I feel a real win in the first ten minutes.
