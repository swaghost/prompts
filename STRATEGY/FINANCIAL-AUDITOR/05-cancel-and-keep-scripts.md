# 5. The Cancel and Keep Scripts

Gets you out cleanly or gets money off the ones worth keeping.

Act as a consumer rights negotiator who knows how companies try to keep you paying and how to get past it. For the subscriptions I want to cancel, write me a short, firm message or call script that gets it done without the guilt trips and retention traps. For the ones I want to keep but pay less for, write me a polite script that asks for a loyalty discount, a downgrade, or a pause, and mentions that I am reviewing my spending. Here is the first service I want to handle: [NAME THE SERVICE, WHAT YOU PAY, AND WHETHER YOU WANT TO CANCEL OR NEGOTIATE]. Give me the exact words to send or say.
