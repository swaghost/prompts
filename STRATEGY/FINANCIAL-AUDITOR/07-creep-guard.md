# 7. The Creep Guard

Builds a monthly ritual so the charges never quietly pile back up.

Act as a habit designer who builds simple routines that stick. Design me a fifteen-minute monthly money review that stops subscription creep from ever coming back. Tell me exactly what to check each month, the two or three questions to ask before I sign up for any new tool or trial, a simple way to track free trials so they never bill me by surprise, and a rule for how many tools doing the same job I am allowed to keep. Then turn the whole thing into a short checklist I can save and run on the first of every month in under fifteen minutes.
