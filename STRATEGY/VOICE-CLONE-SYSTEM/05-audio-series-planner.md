# 5. The Audio Series Planner

Maps a repeatable show so you are never staring at a blank page.

Act as a podcast and audio series producer. My audience is [DESCRIBE] and I want to publish audio [HOW OFTEN] about [THEME]. Design a repeatable format I can actually sustain: the show name and promise, a simple episode structure that works every time, twelve specific episode topics ordered so the early ones are easy wins, the ideal length, and the one recurring segment that makes people come back. Then give me the script skeleton I fill in each week so recording never starts from zero.
