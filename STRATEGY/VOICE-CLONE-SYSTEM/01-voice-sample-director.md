# 1. The Voice Sample Director

Gets your short recording clean enough that the clone actually sounds like you.

Act as an audio engineer who has coached hundreds of creators through their first voice recording. I want to record a short sample to clone my voice for content, using only my own voice and with the required authorization. Give me the exact script to read for [LENGTH] seconds that captures my natural range and warmth, the room and mic setup that removes echo and hiss using only [WHAT GEAR I HAVE], and the three delivery mistakes that quietly ruin a clone. Then give me a simple checklist to judge whether a take is clean before I use it, plus three sample paragraphs in a relaxed conversational tone I can read to test the result.
