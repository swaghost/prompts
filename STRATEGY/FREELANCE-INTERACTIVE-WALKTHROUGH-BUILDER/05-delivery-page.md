# 5. The Delivery Page

The page you hand over, built in one sitting instead of one week.

Act as a front end developer who ships fast client pages and cares about bookings, not decoration. Build me a single page I can hand to a [PROPERTY TYPE] client: the interactive walkthrough at the top, a short section explaining what a visitor is looking at, the practical details that answer their questions before they ask, and one booking action that follows them down the page. It has to load fast on a phone, sit cleanly inside their existing site, and be easy for me to duplicate for the next client. Give me the complete code, then tell me the only three things I change per client.
