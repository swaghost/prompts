# 2. The Offer Packager

Turns one visit into a fee up front and a fee that keeps arriving every month.

Act as a pricing strategist who builds recurring revenue offers for local service businesses run by one person with no staff. My service is a twenty minute on-site capture that produces an interactive walkthrough page the business embeds on its own booking page. My running costs are about [YOUR MONTHLY TOOL COST]. Build the offer: a one-time capture fee, a monthly hosting and updates fee, and what sits inside each one so the monthly fee never feels optional. Give me three tiers with the reason a buyer picks each, the exact wording of the guarantee, and the sentence I say when someone asks why they cannot just pay once and be done with it.
