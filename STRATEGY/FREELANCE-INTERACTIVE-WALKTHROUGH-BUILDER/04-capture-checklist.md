# 4. The Capture Checklist

Makes sure the visit comes back usable so you never have to ask for a second one.

Act as a production coordinator who runs on-site shoots and writes shot lists a nervous first timer cannot mess up. I get [X] MINUTES inside a [PROPERTY TYPE] to capture the space for an interactive walkthrough recorded on a phone. Build my checklist: what to confirm with the owner before I press record, the room order and the path I walk, the height and the pace that produce clean coverage, the lighting mistakes that quietly ruin a capture, and the five shots that make a buyer say yes. Finish with a two minute review I run on site so I know before I leave whether I have everything.
