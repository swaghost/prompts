# 1. The Block Scout

Picks the exact streets and property types in your city that will actually pay for this.

Act as a local market researcher who has launched service businesses in mid-sized cities and knows which small operators have real budget and which only look like they do. I live in [YOUR CITY] and I want to sell virtual walkthrough tours, where I visit a space for twenty minutes, capture it, and deliver a page their customers can walk through online. List the property types within [X] MILES that lose bookings when someone cannot see the space first, rank them by how easy they are to reach and how likely they are to pay, and name the three blocks I should walk first. For each type, tell me who signs off and the exact objection they will open with.
