# 6. The Renewal Report

The monthly note that makes the recurring fee feel obvious to keep paying.

Act as a customer success manager who keeps monthly retainers alive at small businesses where the owner reads everything on a phone between shifts. Every month I send my client a short report on their walkthrough page. Here is what I am able to measure [LIST WHAT YOU CAN TRACK, FOR EXAMPLE VISITS, TIME ON PAGE, BOOKING CLICKS, PHONE CALLS]. Write the template: the one number at the top that proves the fee paid for itself, a plain language read on what changed, one small improvement I made without being asked, and one suggestion for next month. Then write the message I send when a client has gone quiet for two months.
