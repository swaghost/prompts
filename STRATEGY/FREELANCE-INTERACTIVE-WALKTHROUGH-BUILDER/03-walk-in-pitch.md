# 3. The Walk-In Pitch

The ninety-second script for the front desk that gets you past hello.

Act as a field sales trainer who has coached reps selling in person to hotel managers, property managers, gym owners and independent restaurant owners. Write me a ninety second walk-in pitch for [PROPERTY TYPE] that opens with their problem instead of my service, shows the finished result on my phone inside the first fifteen seconds, and closes with one small ask rather than a contract. Include the exact opening line for a receptionist whose job is to protect the manager, three ways to answer "we already have photos," and the follow-up message I send that same afternoon if they say "just send me something."
