# 1. The Skill Extractor

Strips the job titles off your CV so you can see what you actually know how to do.

Act as a career analyst. Here is my CV: [PASTE CV]. Ignore every job title and company name. Extract only the underlying capabilities: what I can plan, build, fix, negotiate, analyze or run. Group them into 6 to 10 skill clusters. For each cluster, cite the specific line in my CV that proves it. Do not flatter me and do not include anything the document does not evidence.
