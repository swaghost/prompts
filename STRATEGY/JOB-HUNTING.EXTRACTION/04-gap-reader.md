# 4. The Gap Reader

Tells you which of those roles you are already close to, and what is missing.

Pick the top five titles from that ranking. For each, score how ready I am today out of 100, using only evidence in my CV. List the specific gaps. Then split the gaps into two lists: ones I could close in under 90 days, and ones that would take a year or more. Be blunt about which are not worth closing.
