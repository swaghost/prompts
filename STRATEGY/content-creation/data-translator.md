# Prompt #5: Data Translator

## What It Does

Turns raw analytics into insights people actually care about.

## Why It Works

You don't just see numbers. You understand what they mean.

## Time Saved

1-2 hours of manual analytics review.

## The Prompt

"Here are my analytics: [PASTE YOUR METRICS]. Analyze this data and tell me: What's working? What's not? What's the surprising insight nobody would catch manually? What should I do differently next week? Format as: Working | Not Working | Surprising Insight | Action"

## Usage

Perfect for content creators, social media managers, marketers, and anyone analyzing performance data. Ideal for weekly reviews, monthly strategy sessions, campaign analysis, and data-driven decision making. Essential for extracting actionable insights from overwhelming analytics.

## Output Format

The prompt will analyze your metrics and provide four-part breakdown:

**1. Working**

- What's performing well
- Metrics showing positive trends
- Content types or strategies succeeding
- Strengths to double down on

**2. Not Working**

- Underperforming areas
- Declining metrics
- Strategies that aren't resonating
- What to stop or fix

**3. Surprising Insight**

- Non-obvious pattern in the data
- Counter-intuitive finding
- Hidden opportunity or risk
- What you'd likely miss manually

**4. Action**

- Specific steps for next week
- Tactical changes to implement
- What to test or experiment with
- Priority adjustments based on data

## Benefits

- Transforms data overwhelm into clarity
- Identifies non-obvious patterns
- Provides actionable next steps
- Saves hours of manual analysis
- Prevents analysis paralysis
- Surfaces hidden opportunities
- Guides strategic decisions
- Improves week-over-week performance
- Makes data accessible to non-analysts

## Metrics to Include

**Social Media:**

- Impressions, reach, engagement rate
- Follower growth, profile visits
- Saves, shares, comments
- Click-through rates

**Content Performance:**

- Views, read time, completion rate
- Top performing posts/topics
- Traffic sources
- Bounce rate, time on page

**Business Metrics:**

- Conversions, leads, sales
- Email list growth
- Revenue, ROI
- Customer acquisition cost

## Best Practices

- **Include Context** - Timeframe, goals, benchmarks
- **Raw Numbers** - Provide actual metrics, not just percentages
- **Multiple Metrics** - Give comprehensive data for better insights
- **Compare Periods** - Week-over-week or month-over-month data
- **Note Experiments** - Mention any tests or changes made

## Application Areas

- Weekly content performance reviews
- Monthly strategy planning sessions
- Campaign post-mortems
- A/B test analysis
- Social media audits
- Website analytics review
- Email marketing analysis
- Paid ads performance review
