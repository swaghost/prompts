# Prompt #2: Structure Architect

## What It Does

Builds the perfect carousel structure before you write a word.

## Why It Works

You start with architecture, not blank pages.

## Time Saved

1-2 hours of structural planning.

## The Prompt

"I'm creating a carousel about [TOPIC]. My hook is: [INSERT HOOK]. My goal is [ENGAGEMENT/SAVES/SHARES]. Build me a 10-slide structure that moves people from curiosity to action. Each slide should have: slide purpose, estimated word count, and what psychological shift happens. Format as: Slide 1: [Purpose] - [Shift]"

## Usage

Perfect for creating LinkedIn carousels, Instagram carousels, Twitter threads, and slide-based content. Ideal for educators, thought leaders, and content creators who need structured storytelling that drives specific engagement goals. Works for any topic requiring multi-slide narrative progression.

## Output Format

The prompt will generate a 10-slide structure with:

**For Each Slide:**

- **Slide Number**
- **Purpose** - What this slide accomplishes
- **Estimated Word Count** - Content length guidance
- **Psychological Shift** - Emotional/mental transition that occurs

**Typical Flow:**

- Slides 1-2: Hook and capture attention (curiosity)
- Slides 3-5: Build credibility and deliver value (trust → interest)
- Slides 6-8: Deepen engagement with insights (understanding → belief)
- Slides 9-10: Drive action and close (motivation → action)

## Benefits

- Eliminates structural guesswork
- Ensures psychological progression
- Provides clear content framework before writing
- Optimizes for specific engagement goals
- Creates natural narrative flow
- Prevents rambling or disorganized content
- Saves planning time
- Increases completion rates (structured = easier to finish)

## Goal Options

Customize based on desired outcome:

- **Engagement** - Comments, discussions, replies
- **Saves** - Bookmarks, reference material
- **Shares** - Viral potential, broad reach
