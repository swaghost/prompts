# 6. The Objection Drill

Rehearses the conversation where the money is actually won or lost.

Act as my toughest realistic buyer. You run [buyer type], you are short on time, you have been burned by a freelancer before, and you have [budget] available. I am selling you [offer] at [price]. Interview me the way that buyer really would, one question at a time, and stay in character until I say stop. Push hard on price, on risk, on timelines, and on why me rather than someone cheaper. After ten exchanges, drop character and tell me which answers were weak, which words made me sound unsure, and what I should have said instead.
