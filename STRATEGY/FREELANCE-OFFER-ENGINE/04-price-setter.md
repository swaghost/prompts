# 4. The Price Setter

Puts a defensible number on the offer, plus the sentence for saying it without flinching.

Act as a pricing consultant for independent service providers. My offer is [paste the offer from prompt three] and my buyer is [buyer]. Estimate what this outcome is worth to that buyer in money earned or hours saved, and show your working. Then give me three prices: a starting price to win the first two clients, a standard price, and the price I move to after five happy clients. Explain the reasoning behind each number. Finish by writing the exact sentence I say when someone asks what it costs, and the exact sentence I say when they push back on it.
