# 2. The Buyer Finder

Names the people who have the budget and the problem, instead of an audience.

Act as a market researcher who finds underserved buyers for small service businesses. My sellable skill is [skill from prompt one]. List ten specific types of buyer who feel this problem badly enough to pay someone about it this month. For each one give me the business type, roughly how big they are, the trigger event that makes them go looking for help, where they gather online, and what they currently do instead of hiring anyone. Rank them by how reachable they are for someone with no audience and no ad budget, then say which one you would start with and why.
