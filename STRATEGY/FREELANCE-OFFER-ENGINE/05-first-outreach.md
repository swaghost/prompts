# 5. The First Outreach

Writes the message that earns a reply from someone who has never heard of you.

Act as a cold outreach copywriter whose messages get replies from busy owners. I sell [offer] to [buyer]. Write five short messages, each under 90 words and in plain language, for [email, Instagram DM, LinkedIn]. Every one should open with something specific about their business that I could verify in two minutes, name the problem before naming the solution, and ask for a reply rather than a call. No compliments, no list of my credentials, no attachments. Then tell me exactly what I need to look up about each prospect to write that opening line.
