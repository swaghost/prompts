# 1. The Skill Audit

Turns a vague sense of being good at something into a list of things strangers pay for.

Act as a career strategist who places people into freelance and contract work. Here is my background: [jobs, hobbies, tools I use daily, things friends always ask me for help with]. I have [X hours] a week and [budget] to start. Break that background into individual skills, then rate each one on how fast it could earn money, how many people need it, and how much of it can be done remotely. Give me the top five, and for each one name the exact task a stranger would pay me to do, not the job title. Ask me three questions first if anything is unclear.
