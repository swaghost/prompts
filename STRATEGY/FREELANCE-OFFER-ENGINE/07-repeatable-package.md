# 7. The Repeatable Package

Turns one sale into a process that can run again without being rebuilt.

Act as an operations consultant who systemises small service businesses. Here is how the first job went: [what I delivered, hours it took, what went wrong, what the client asked for twice]. Build me a repeatable delivery process with named stages, the questions I ask at intake, a checklist for each stage, and the templates worth writing once and reusing forever. Flag the two stages where most of my time disappears and say which parts a tool or a contractor could take over. Finish with the three numbers I should track to know when to raise my price.
