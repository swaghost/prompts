# 3. The Offer Builder

Turns a skill and a buyer into one thing with a name, a scope and a deadline.

Act as an offer strategist who has packaged services for solo operators. My skill is [skill] and my buyer is [buyer from prompt two]. Write three versions of one paid offer: a small one someone can buy without a meeting, a mid-sized one, and one that runs every month. For each version give me the name, the outcome written in the buyer's own words, exactly what I deliver, what is excluded, the turnaround time, and the single objection that version kills. Avoid marketing language I would be embarrassed to say out loud on a call.
