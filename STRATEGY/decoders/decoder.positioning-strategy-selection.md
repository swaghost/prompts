# Identify Your Highest-Leverage Positioning Strategy

## Purpose

Systematically evaluate multiple positioning options based on expertise overlap with market demand, competitive saturation analysis, and category ownership opportunities to select the positioning strategy offering greatest competitive advantage with your specific strengths and constraints.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your background, expertise, and capabilities; the market or category you're considering positioning in; competitive landscape insights; and your goals (thought leadership, premium pricing, volume scale, category creation, etc.).

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A positioning strategy analysis identifying 3-5 viable positioning options (specialist in X vertical, pioneer of Y methodology, contrarian on Z conventional wisdom, etc.), expertise-demand overlap assessment showing where your capabilities meet market need, competitive saturation analysis revealing how crowded each positioning option is, category ownership opportunities showing which positions offer defensible differentiation, market gap identification highlighting uncontested positioning territory, and final recommendation ranking your top 3 positioning strategies with detailed strategic rationale for each including risks, requirements, timeline to establish authority, and first 90-day positioning actions.

## Usage

Perfect for positioning consultants advising on market strategy, founders determining how to position their company, consultants and coaches selecting their authority angle, thought leaders choosing their contrarian stance, and anyone facing multiple viable positioning paths. Ideal for career pivots, business repositioning, entering new markets, and strategic inflection points where positioning determines trajectory. This prompt excels at moving from intuition to data-driven positioning decisions with clear implementation paths.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your expertise, background, and capabilities
- Market or category you're considering
- What you know about the competitive landscape
- Your goals (authority, premium pricing, scale, etc.)

## Prompt

You are a positioning strategist helping me select my highest-leverage market position. I will describe my expertise, capabilities, the market I'm considering, and my goals. Analyze my positioning options: (1) Generate 3-5 viable positioning strategies I could pursue (e.g., specialist in X vertical, pioneer of Y methodology, contrarian on Z conventional wisdom, category creator for new space, premium tier in existing category, etc.). (2) Expertise-Demand Overlap — for each option, assess how well my capabilities align with genuine market demand. Where am I strong enough to be credible and different enough to be interesting? (3) Competitive Saturation — for each option, analyze how crowded that positioning is. How many others are competing for the same mental real estate? (4) Category Ownership Opportunities — which positioning options offer the clearest path to being the definitive voice or go-to authority? Where could I plausibly own the category in my target audience's mind? (5) Market Gaps — identify positioning territory where demand exists but no one has clearly claimed authority yet (whitespace positioning). (6) Rank my top 3 positioning strategies with detailed rationale for each, including: risks, what's required to execute, timeline to establish authority, and my first 90-day positioning actions. My expertise and positioning context: [DESCRIBE YOUR EXPERTISE, CAPABILITIES, MARKET CONTEXT, COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE, AND GOALS]
