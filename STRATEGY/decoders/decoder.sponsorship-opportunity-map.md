# Build a Sponsorship Opportunity Map

## Purpose

Create a prioritized database of potential sponsors ranked by strategic fit criteria including audience overlap, budget probability, partnership likelihood, and outreach timing to systematically approach sponsorship development rather than random cold outreach.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your platform, audience demographics and psychographics, content niche, audience size and engagement metrics, and what type of sponsorship or partnership you're seeking (content sponsors, event sponsors, product partnerships, affiliate relationships, etc.).

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A sponsorship opportunity map containing a prioritized sponsor database (20-30 potential companies) ranked by strategic criteria, audience overlap analysis showing which brands already target your audience successfully, budget probability assessment based on company size and marketing sophistication, partnership likelihood scoring based on past sponsorship history and brand alignment, outreach target priorities in A/B/C tiers, and 3-5 specific outreach angles for your top-tier prospects with pitch positioning and value proposition suggestions tailored to each sponsor type.

## Usage

Perfect for content creators monetizing audiences through sponsorships, event organizers seeking corporate partners, podcast hosts building sponsor pipelines, newsletter operators pursuing advertising revenue, and community builders exploring brand partnerships. Ideal for YouTubers, podcasters, newsletter writers, event producers, and any creator/platform where audience access has commercial value. This prompt excels at transforming wishful thinking about sponsors into systematic business development with clear prioritization and approach strategies.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your platform/content and audience
- Audience size and engagement metrics
- Your niche or industry focus
- What you're offering to sponsors (ad placements, content integration, event presence, etc.)

## Prompt

You are a sponsorship strategist who helps creators and platforms build partnership pipelines. I will describe my audience, platform, and what I'm offering to sponsors. Build me a sponsorship opportunity map: (1) Generate a prioritized sponsor database of 20-30 potential companies in these categories: direct competitors to brands my audience already uses, aspirational brands aligned with my audience's identity, service providers solving my audience's problems, and emerging brands seeking early adopter audiences. (2) Analyze audience overlap — which brands already successfully target my demographic and psychographic profile? (3) Assess budget probability — based on company size, marketing sophistication, and visible sponsorship activity, rank each by likelihood they have budget allocated for sponsorships like mine. (4) Score partnership likelihood — based on brand alignment, past sponsorship history, and strategic fit with my content, which are most likely to say yes? (5) Create A/B/C tier outreach targets based on combined scoring. (6) Give me 3-5 specific outreach angles for my top-tier prospects, including how to pitch my value proposition to each sponsor type. My platform and sponsorship context: [DESCRIBE YOUR AUDIENCE, PLATFORM, METRICS, NICHE, AND WHAT YOU'RE OFFERING TO SPONSORS]
