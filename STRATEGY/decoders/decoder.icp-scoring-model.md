# Build a Weighted ICP Scoring Model

## Purpose

Create a systematic prospect qualification framework by identifying and ranking the strongest buying signals that indicate ideal customer fit, enabling sales and marketing teams to prioritize outreach and resources toward highest-probability conversions.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your existing customer base, sales data, CRM records, and qualitative insights about customers who convert fastest, stay longest, and derive most value from your product or service.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A weighted ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) scorecard identifying the 15 strongest buying signals ranked by importance with assigned point values, a prospect grading system (A/B/C/D tiers) based on total scores, and a qualification framework that sales teams can apply consistently to prioritize pipeline. Includes both firmographic signals (company size, industry, tech stack) and behavioral signals (engagement patterns, pain point indicators, trigger events).

## Usage

Perfect for B2B sales strategists building systematic qualification processes, marketing teams optimizing lead scoring automation, and sales operations leaders seeking to improve pipeline quality over quantity. Ideal for SaaS companies, agencies, consulting firms, enterprise software providers, and any B2B business where not all leads are equal. This prompt excels at transforming subjective "gut feel" qualification into data-driven scoring systems that scale across teams.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Data or insights about your current best customers (who converts fast, stays long, pays well)
- List of common characteristics or patterns you've noticed among ideal customers
- Context about your product/service and what makes someone a good fit

## Prompt

You are a B2B sales strategist specializing in ICP development and lead scoring. Based on what I tell you about my business and best customers, build me a weighted ICP scoring model: (1) Identify the 15 strongest buying signals that indicate someone is likely to be an ideal customer — include both firmographic signals (company size, industry, tech stack, etc.) and behavioral signals (engagement patterns, pain point indicators, trigger events). (2) Rank them by importance and assign point values to each signal, with your strongest signals worth more points. (3) Create a prospect grading system (A/B/C/D tiers) based on total score ranges. (4) Give me a one-page scorecard I can hand to my sales team to qualify prospects consistently. Here's context about my business and ideal customers: [DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE, YOUR BEST CUSTOMERS, AND PATTERNS YOU'VE NOTICED]
