# Reverse Engineer Why a Competitor's Content Converts to Sales

## Purpose

Analyze competitor content-to-offer funnels to understand the core transformation being sold, how content seems to be pre-emptively addressing objections and positioning angle, identify their likely path from content engagement to conversion, and reverse-engineer content/offer alignment strategies applicable to your business.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Content from a competitor or brand whose content strategy demonstrably drives sales, including their content approach, apparent positioning, and the offers/products they're successfully converting audiences toward.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A conversion strategist analysis identifying What is the core transformation being sold (not stated outright) across their content, What content 'roles' are being used (awareness content, objection-handling content, trust-building content, direct-offer content) with rough ratio estimates, What is their positioning angle that makes their offer feel inevitable/congruent as next step, What is the likely path from 'sees a post' to 'buys' (step by step), and design of a 4-part content funnel you could build for your own offer using this same logic without copying their content. Describes what belief or identity shift each piece needs to create.

## Usage

Perfect for conversion strategists reverse-engineering content-to-offer funnels, business strategists analyzing successful competitor sales systems, and content creators seeking to align content strategy with revenue generation. Ideal for analyzing service business content strategies, digital product launch sequences, course creator content funnels, coaching business positioning, and any content-driven sales approach. This prompt excels at revealing the hidden strategic connection between seemingly educational content and systematic revenue generation.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of competitor's content approach
- Knowledge of what they're selling/their core offer
- Your own offer/product for strategy adaptation

## Prompt

You are a conversion strategist who reverse engineers content-to-offer funnels. I will describe a competitor's content approach along with what they appear to be selling. Analyze: (1) What is the core transformation being sold, and how is it hinted at (not stated outright) across their content? (2) What content 'roles' do you see — awareness content, objection-handling content, trust-building content, and direct-offer content — and roughly what ratio are they using? (3) What is their positioning angle — what belief or identity are they selling to their audience beyond the topic itself that makes their offer feel inevitable/congruent as a next step? (4) What is the likely path from 'sees a post' to 'buys,' step by step? (5) Design a 4-part content funnel I could build for my own offer, using this same logic without copying their content. My offer: [DESCRIBE YOUR OFFER]. Competitor content to analyze: [DESCRIBE THE COMPETITOR'S CONTENT AND OFFER]
