# Reverse Engineer the Retention Pattern of a Viral Video Script

## Purpose

Deconstruct video transcripts from high-retention videos to identify pattern interrupts, open loops, information tension points, re-hook timing, and pacing techniques that prevented scroll-away during critical first seconds and throughout playback.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Transcripts or scripts from short-form videos (Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts) that demonstrated exceptional watch-time retention, particularly videos where viewers watched to completion despite opportunities to scroll away.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A retention editing analysis breaking down the video second-by-second for the first 3 seconds (specific pattern interrupt or open loop preventing immediate scroll-away), every 5-10 second segments identifying new information/tension/curiosity gaps re-engaging viewers, identification of re-hooks (mid-video promises like "but here's the part nobody tells you") and their placement, the payoff structure showing when and how the opening promise is finally resolved and why the timing works, and generation of a blank retention script skeleton with timestamps and structural notes for reuse on specified topics.

## Usage

Perfect for retention editors specializing in short-form video, video content strategists optimizing watch-time, and creators seeking systematic retention patterns. Ideal for analyzing viral TikTok scripts, high-retention Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts with completion rates above average, and any short-form video content demonstrating exceptional audience retention. This prompt excels at revealing the micro-level retention mechanics that separate completed videos from scroll-aways while providing timestamped frameworks for systematic application.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Full video transcript from high-retention video
- Approximate video length and platform
- Topic you want to apply the retention pattern to

## Prompt

You are a retention editor for short-form video who has studied thousands of watch-time graphs. I will paste the transcript of a video that had strong retention. Break it down second-by-second in these terms: (1) The first 3 seconds — what specific pattern interrupt or open loop prevents an immediate scroll-away? (2) Every 5-10 second segment — identify what new information, tension, or curiosity gap re-engages the viewer at that point. (3) Identify any 're-hooks' (mid-video promises like "but here's the part nobody tells you") and where they're placed. (4) Identify the payoff structure — when and how the opening promise is finally resolved, and why that timing works. (5) Turn this into a blank retention script skeleton with timestamps and structural notes I can reuse for a video on this topic: [INSERT YOUR TOPIC]. Transcript to analyze: [PASTE VIDEO TRANSCRIPT HERE]
