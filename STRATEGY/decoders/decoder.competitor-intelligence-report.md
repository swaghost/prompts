# Build a Competitor Intelligence Report

## Purpose

Systematically analyze a competitor to identify their strengths and weaknesses, messaging patterns and emotional triggers, market positioning gaps you could exploit, and strategic insights about what they're doing well that you should consider and what they're missing that represents your opportunity.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

A specific competitor whose strategy, messaging, positioning, and market approach you want to deeply understand, including their website, marketing materials, content, sales process insights, customer reviews, and any other publicly available intelligence.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A comprehensive competitor intelligence report identifying documented strengths (what they do exceptionally well and why customers choose them), weaknesses and gaps (what they're missing, where customers complain, vulnerabilities in their approach), messaging patterns and emotional triggers (how they position, what psychology they activate, their narrative strategy), buying signals they likely prioritize, prospect grading criteria they probably use, market gaps their positioning creates, and strategic recommendations for how you should respond (what to borrow, what to counter, where to differentiate).

## Usage

Perfect for competitive intelligence analysts conducting strategic research, product marketers building battlecards, sales teams preparing for competitive deals, and business strategists seeking market positioning advantages. Ideal for entering established markets, defending against new entrants, repositioning against dominant players, and any competitive scenario requiring deep understanding of alternative choices. This prompt excels at transforming scattered competitive observations into systematic strategic intelligence.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Competitor name and basic information about their business
- Access to their website, marketing materials, or content
- Context about the market you're both competing in
- What you're trying to learn or decisions you need to make

## Prompt

You are a competitive intelligence analyst. I will describe a competitor and give you information about their business, messaging, and approach. Build me a comprehensive intelligence report: (1) Strengths — what do they do exceptionally well? Why do customers choose them? What advantages do they have? (2) Weaknesses & Gaps — what are they missing? Where do customers complain? What vulnerabilities exist in their approach? (3) Messaging Patterns & Emotional Triggers — how do they position their offer? What emotional triggers, psychological patterns, or narrative strategies do they use? What belief or identity do they sell beyond the product? (4) Buying Signals & Qualification — based on who they seem to target and win, what buying signals do they likely prioritize? How do they probably grade prospects? (5) Market Gaps — what positioning or market opportunities does their approach create for competitors? (6) Strategic Response — what should I borrow from their playbook? What should I counter or position against? Where should I differentiate? Competitor to analyze: [DESCRIBE COMPETITOR, PROVIDE THEIR MESSAGING/WEBSITE CONTENT, EXPLAIN MARKET CONTEXT]
