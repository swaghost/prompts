# Map a Competitor's Entire Content Strategy

## Purpose

Reverse-engineer a competitor or brand's complete content strategy by analyzing their recent posts to identify core content pillars, recurring formats, positioning angles, belief-shifting narratives, content funnel structure, and strategic framework that can be adapted without copying.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

A collection of 15-20 recent posts, titles, or topics from a creator or brand you want to analyze, ideally representing their consistent content output over weeks or months showing strategic patterns.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A comprehensive content strategy map identifying their 3-5 core content pillars with estimated percentage mix, recurring formats (listicles, before/after, personal stories, contrarian takes, tutorials), their positioning angle revealing what belief or identity they're selling to their audience beyond topic itself, their likely content funnel showing how each piece positions for trust/conversion, and a proposed content pillar framework you could build for your own brand that borrows the strategic logic without copying their content.

## Usage

Perfect for content strategy consultants analyzing competitive landscapes, brand strategists identifying market positioning gaps, and creators seeking to understand dominant players in their niche. Ideal for reverse-engineering Instagram content strategies, LinkedIn thought leadership approaches, YouTube channel structures, newsletter content frameworks, and Twitter/X content angles. This prompt excels at uncovering the hidden strategic architecture behind successful content programs while enabling you to build differentiated but equally systematic approaches.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- List of 15-20 recent post titles or topics from the competitor/brand
- Context about the niche, industry, or market they operate in
- Your own niche or brand context for strategy adaptation

## Prompt

You are a content strategy consultant. I will describe or paste a list of 15-20 recent posts/titles from a creator or brand I want to analyze. Based on this, reverse engineer their content strategy: (1) Identify their 3-5 core content pillars and estimate the % mix of each. (2) Identify recurring formats (listicles, before/after, personal stories, contrarian takes, tutorials). (3) Identify their positioning angle — what belief or identity are they selling to their audience beyond the topic itself? (4) Identify their likely content funnel — what believe are they selling to their audience that postures them for trust, where each piece fits (e.g., some content is pure value, some is angle-shifting, some is direct-offer content) — and roughly what ratio are they using? (5) Propose a content pillar framework I could build for my own brand that borrows their strategic logic without copying their content. Here is the list of posts: [PASTE POST TITLES/TOPICS HERE]
