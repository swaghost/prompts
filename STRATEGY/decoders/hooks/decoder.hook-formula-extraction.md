# Extract the Exact Hook Formula Top Posts Use

## Purpose

Systematically deconstruct high-performing hooks to identify the underlying formula, sentence structure, emotional triggers, and psychological patterns that stop scrollers and drive engagement.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Opening lines, headlines, or first sentences from top-performing social media posts, articles, emails, or any content where the hook demonstrated exceptional scroll-stopping power and engagement metrics.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A detailed analysis extracting the hook type classification (curiosity gap, bold claim, contrarian statement, relatable pain point, numbered promise, etc.), the underlying sentence structure as fill-in-the-blank template, identification of emotional trigger activators (fear, ego, curiosity, urgency, belonging), and synthesis into 10 new hook templates ready for reuse on any topic within your niche, each applying the same formula.

## Usage

Perfect for copywriting analysts deconstructing scroll-stopping hooks, content creators building systematic hook libraries, and social media marketers seeking proven attention-grabbing formulas. Ideal for analyzing Instagram captions, LinkedIn post openers, Twitter/X hooks, email subject lines, and blog headlines. This prompt excels at identifying the exact mechanical structure behind viral hooks while creating immediately actionable templates that preserve the psychological impact without copying content.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- 5-10 hooks from top-performing posts in your niche
- Context about where these hooks performed (platform, audience, content type)
- Evidence of performance (engagement rates, viral reach, or subjective assessment)

## Prompt

Act as a copywriting analyst specializing in scroll-stopping hooks. I will give you 5-10 hooks from top-performing posts in my niche. For each hook: (1) Identify the hook type (curiosity gap, bold claim, contrarian statement, relatable pain point, numbered promise, etc.). (2) Extract the underlying sentence structure as a fill-in-the-blank template. (3) Identify the emotional trigger it activates (fear, ego, curiosity, urgency, belonging). Then, synthesize all patterns into 10 new hook templates I can reuse for any topic in my niche, and apply 3 of them to this topic of mine: [INSERT YOUR TOPIC]. Hooks to analyze: [PASTE HOOKS HERE]
