# Identify the Transformation Beyond the Product

## Purpose

Uncover the underlying transformation, emotional outcome, identity shift, or desired state that customers are actually purchasing when they buy your product, enabling you to position and message around the real motivation rather than surface-level features.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Your product or service, what it does functionally, who buys it, customer testimonials or success stories, and any insights about why customers really chose you over alternatives or what changed in their life/business after purchase.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A transformation positioning analysis identifying the functional outcome (what the product literally does), the emotional outcome (how customers feel after achieving the functional outcome), the identity shift (who they become or how they see themselves differently), the desired state (the aspirational future state they're moving toward), and the core transformation being purchased (the fundamental change they're buying beyond the product itself). Includes messaging recommendations for how to position around transformation rather than features, and identification of which transformation dimension resonates most powerfully with your specific market.

## Usage

Perfect for product marketers seeking to transcend feature-based positioning, brand strategists uncovering deeper emotional drivers, copywriters crafting transformation-focused messaging, and founders trying to articulate why their product matters beyond what it does. Ideal for positioning pivots, messaging refreshes, premium repositioning, and any scenario where feature parity is high but emotional resonance creates differentiation. This prompt excels at revealing the psychological and aspirational dimensions that actually drive purchase decisions.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Description of your product/service and what it does
- Who your customers are
- Customer testimonials, success stories, or feedback
- Why people choose you over alternatives (if known)

## Prompt

You are a brand strategist specializing in transformation-based positioning. I will describe my product/service, my customers, and what I know about why they buy. Help me identify the transformation they're actually purchasing: (1) Functional Outcome — what does my product literally do or deliver? (2) Emotional Outcome — how do customers feel after they achieve that functional outcome? What emotional state changes? (3) Identity Shift — who do they become or how do they see themselves differently after using my product? What identity are they stepping into? (4) Desired State — what aspirational future state are they moving toward? What does life/business look like in their ideal scenario? (5) Core Transformation — synthesize all of this: what is the fundamental transformation being purchased beyond the product itself? (6) Give me messaging recommendations for how to position around this transformation rather than features, and tell me which transformation dimension (emotional outcome, identity shift, or desired state) likely resonates most powerfully with my market and why. My product and customer context: [DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT, YOUR CUSTOMERS, SUCCESS STORIES, AND WHY PEOPLE CHOOSE YOU]
