# Map Offer Positioning Landscape

## Purpose

Systematically cluster competitors by positioning angle to identify overcrowded narratives where competition is fierce, underutilized narratives representing differentiation opportunities, and whitespace positioning gaps where no one is playing yet.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

A list of 15-25 competitors, agencies, or brands in your market along with how they describe their services, their messaging angles, and what transformation or identity they're selling beyond the service itself.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A positioning market map clustering all competitors into 3-5 dominant positioning angles with percentage of market playing in each space, identification of overcrowded narratives where you'd be fighting for attention, underutilized narratives where fewer players exist but demand remains, and whitespace opportunities representing untapped positioning territory. Includes recommended positioning angles ranked by competitive advantage potential and strategic rationale for why each would or wouldn't work for your specific strengths.

## Usage

Perfect for positioning strategists analyzing competitive landscapes, agency founders seeking differentiation angles, and brand strategists identifying market gaps. Ideal for service businesses, creative agencies, consulting firms, SaaS companies, and any market where positioning creates competitive moats. This prompt excels at revealing the hidden clustering patterns in seemingly diverse competitive sets while surfacing strategic positioning opportunities others have missed.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- List of 15-25 competitors or similar businesses in your market
- How each describes their services/value proposition
- Context about your own capabilities and strengths

## Prompt

You are a positioning strategist analyzing competitive landscapes. I will give you a list of 15-25 competitors/agencies in my market and how they describe themselves. Cluster them by positioning angle: (1) Identify the 3-5 dominant positioning narratives in this market (e.g., "speed & execution," "strategic transformation," "industry specialization," "proprietary methodology," etc.) and estimate what % of the market plays in each space. (2) Identify overcrowded narratives where I'd be competing for attention against many similar players. (3) Identify underutilized narratives where fewer players exist but demand/opportunity remains. (4) Identify whitespace opportunities — positioning angles no one is claiming yet that could be defensible. (5) Recommend 3 positioning angles for my business ranked by competitive advantage potential, and explain the strategic rationale for each. Here's my competitive set and my capabilities: [LIST COMPETITORS AND THEIR POSITIONING + YOUR STRENGTHS]
