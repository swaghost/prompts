# Reverse Engineer Audience Psychology from the Comments Section

## Purpose

Extract audience insights from high-engagement post comments to identify recurring pain points, frustrations, or unmet needs, exact language and phrasing used by the audience, disagreements/misconceptions/debates revealing content gaps, emotional undertone, and turn findings into 10 content ideas speaking directly to what the audience is already saying.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Comment sections from high-engagement posts in your niche, particularly posts that sparked active discussion, debate, or personal story-sharing from multiple audience members revealing deeper motivations and pain points.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A market research analyst examination identifying Recurring pain points mentioned (extract these as verbatim phrases), Exact language and phrasing the audience uses to describe their problems (crucial for future hooks and headlines), Disagreements/misconceptions/debates happening in the thread revealing content gaps or myths worth addressing, The emotional undertone of the audience (skeptical, hopeful, overwhelmed, validated, defensive, curious), and turning this into 10 content ideas with specific suggested hooks that speak directly to what this audience is already saying.

## Usage

Perfect for market research analysts specializing in audience psychology, content strategists mining audience language for messaging, and creators seeking to build content directly addressing expressed needs. Ideal for analyzing Instagram comment sections, LinkedIn discussion threads, Twitter/X reply conversations, Facebook group discussions, and any platform where audience commentary reveals deeper psychology. This prompt excels at transforming comment-section insights into systematic content strategies grounded in actual audience language and concerns.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Batch of comments from a high-engagement post in your niche
- Context about the post topic and platform
- Your niche for generating targeted content ideas

## Prompt

You are a market research analyst specializing in audience psychology. I will paste a batch of comments from a high-engagement post in my niche. Analyze them to extract: (1) Recurring pain points, frustrations, or unmet needs mentioned directly or implied. (2) The exact language and phrasing the audience uses to describe their problems (for use in future hooks and headlines). (3) Any disagreements, misconceptions, or debates happening in the thread, which reveal content gaps or myths worth addressing. (4) The emotional undertone of the audience as a whole (skeptical, hopeful, overwhelmed, validated, defensive, curious). (5) Turn all of this into 10 content ideas, each with a suggested hook, that speak directly to what this audience is already saying. Comments to analyze: [PASTE COMMENTS HERE]
