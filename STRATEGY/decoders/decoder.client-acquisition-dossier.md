# Build a Client Acquisition Dossier

## Purpose

Create a comprehensive intelligence profile on a target client to identify their growth bottlenecks, probable KPIs and priorities, likely internal challenges and objections, and strategic outreach angles that demonstrate immediate value understanding.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

A specific target client or prospect you want to win, including their company, industry, current state, public information about their business, and any visible challenges or opportunities in their market position.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A complete acquisition dossier containing growth bottleneck diagnosis (what's likely limiting their growth right now), probable KPIs they're measured on and strategic priorities, anticipated internal challenges and organizational dynamics affecting decisions, predicted objections they'll raise in sales conversations, and 3-5 strategic outreach angles with specific Loom audit ideas or value-demonstration approaches. Includes competitive intelligence about who they might be considering and why you'd win against alternatives.

## Usage

Perfect for enterprise sales teams targeting high-value accounts, agencies pursuing dream clients, consultants building account-based strategies, and business development professionals seeking to stand out through research depth. Ideal for complex B2B sales, strategic partnerships, agency new business, and any scenario where winning one client requires deep understanding rather than volume outreach. This prompt excels at transforming public information into strategic sales intelligence that demonstrates you've done your homework.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- Target client/company name and basic information
- Their industry and market context
- What you know about their current situation or challenges
- What you're proposing to sell or help them with

## Prompt

You are a strategic account researcher building client acquisition dossiers for high-value targets. I will describe a target client I want to win. Build me a complete acquisition dossier: (1) Growth Bottleneck Diagnosis — based on their industry, size, and what I can see publicly, what is likely limiting their growth right now? (2) Probable KPIs & Priorities — what metrics are their decision-makers likely measured on, and what strategic priorities probably dominate their roadmap? (3) Internal Challenges — what organizational dynamics, resource constraints, or political factors might affect their buying decisions? (4) Objection Predictions — what concerns or pushback will they likely raise in sales conversations, and how should I preemptively address each? (5) Outreach Angles — give me 3-5 specific approaches for initial outreach, including Loom audit ideas or value-demonstration tactics that prove I understand their business. (6) Competitive Intelligence — who else might they be considering, and why would I win? Target client to research: [DESCRIBE THE TARGET CLIENT, THEIR INDUSTRY, WHAT YOU KNOW, AND WHAT YOU'RE SELLING]
