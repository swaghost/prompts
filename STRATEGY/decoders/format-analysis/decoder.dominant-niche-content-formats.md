# Reverse Engineer the Dominant Content Formats in Your Niche

## Purpose

Systematically identify and analyze the most successful content formats in a niche to understand which formats tend to work best, why each format satisfies specific needs, how often each appears, any underused formats representing whitespace opportunities, and generate 3 ready-to-use post outlines for each of top 5 recommended formats.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

A collection of 15-20 top-performing post titles or descriptions from multiple creators in your niche (not just one), ideally representing diverse content types to reveal format patterns across the competitive landscape.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A content format strategist analysis identifying the content being shared (listicles, before/after, case study, myth-take, tutorial, personal story, contrarian take, etc.), Why each format tends to work for this specific niche/audience (what need or curiosity it satisfies), Any formats that appear underused relative to how well similar formats perform elsewhere (potential whitespace opportunities), Rank of the top 5 formats prioritized based on ease of production and performance potential for someone with your resources, and generation of 3 ready-to-use post outlines on specified topic, one for each of top 3 recommended formats.

## Usage

Perfect for content format strategists analyzing niche-specific patterns, creators seeking systematic format libraries, and marketers identifying format opportunities in competitive landscapes. Ideal for analyzing Instagram format trends, LinkedIn content type performance, Twitter/X thread structures, YouTube video formats, and cross-platform content architecture. This prompt excels at revealing both dominant successful formats and underutilized whitespace opportunities while providing immediately actionable format templates.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- List of 15-20 top-performing post titles/descriptions from your niche (from multiple creators, not just one)
- Context about your niche and audience
- Your resources and constraints for content production

## Prompt

You are a content format strategist. I will list 15-20 top-performing post titles or descriptions from my niche (from multiple creators, not just one). Analyze them to: (1) Identify the content being shared (listicles, before/after, case study, myth-take, tutorial, personal story, contrarian take, etc.). (2) Why each format tends to work for this specific niche/audience — what need or curiosity it satisfies. (3) Any formats that appear underused relative to how well similar formats perform elsewhere — potential whitespace opportunities. (4) Rank the top 5 formats, prioritized based on ease of production and performance potential for someone with my resources: [DESCRIBE YOUR RESOURCES/CONSTRAINTS]. (5) Generate 3 ready-to-use post outlines, on this topic: [INSERT YOUR TOPIC]. Posts to analyze: [PASTE POST TITLES/DESCRIPTIONS HERE]
