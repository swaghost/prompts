# Reverse Engineer a Viral Writer's Voice and Style

## Purpose

Analyze a writer's distinctive voice to systematically deconstruct their stylistic patterns including sentence rhythm, word choice, tone construction, structural habits, and voice signature devices, enabling replication of the style's effectiveness without copying their content.

## Description of Target Content or Subject

Multiple posts (3-5) from a writer whose voice stands out and resonates strongly with audiences, demonstrating consistent stylistic choices that create recognizable personality and engagement patterns across their content.

## Discovery - Description of Resulting Output

A linguistic style analysis examining Sentence length and rhythm patterns (short punchy lines, long flowing ones, specific alternating patterns), Word choice analysis (formal vs. casual, simple vs. sophisticated, signature words or phrases they repeat), Tone construction (sarcastic, warm, blunt, authoritative) built through specific phrasing choices, Structural habits (one-line paragraphs, rhetorical questions, repetition as devices), and synthesis into a style guide for writing in similar voice on new topics. Summarizes into 5 'voice rules' that capture the essence, then applies them to specified topic.

## Usage

Perfect for linguistic style analysts deconstructing voice patterns, copywriters seeking to adopt effective stylistic frameworks, and content creators building distinctive personal voices. Ideal for analyzing LinkedIn thought leader voices, Twitter/X personality styles, newsletter writer tones, blog post authorial presence, and any written content where voice creates competitive advantage. This prompt excels at systematizing the seemingly intuitive elements of voice and style into reproducible writing frameworks.

## Prerequisites

**MANDATORY:**

- 3-5 posts from the writer whose voice you want to analyze
- Context about their niche and audience
- Your topic for applying the discovered voice patterns

## Prompt

You are a linguistic style analyst. I will paste 3-5 posts from a writer whose voice stands out. Analyze their style across these dimensions: (1) Sentence length and rhythm — do they favor short punchy lines, long flowing ones, or a specific alternating pattern? (2) Word choice — formal vs. casual, simple vs. sophisticated, any signature words or phrases they repeat? (3) Tone — sarcastic, warm, blunt, authoritative — and how is that tone constructed through specific phrasing choices? (4) Structural habits — do they use one-line paragraphs, rhetorical questions, or repetition as devices? (5) Summarize all of this into a 'style guide' I can hand to a writer (or use myself) to write in a similar voice on a new topic: [INSERT TOPIC]. Posts to analyze: [PASTE POSTS HERE]
