The process creates ARRI Alexa-quality crime thriller footage with authentic investigation materials, atmospheric lighting, and seamless motion transfer while preserving perfect identity consistency.

## Scene 1: Wall Investigation (Missing Person Evidence Board)

### Step 1: Record Your Source Video

Record a video of yourself **looking at a wall**, then take a screenshot from the video.

**Recording Tips:**

- Stand facing a plain wall
- Natural standing posture
- Look at wall (not camera)
- Simple movements - head turns, slight body shifts
- Good lighting on your face
- Clean background

### Step 2: Generate Styled Reference Image (Nano Banana Pro)

Go to [Nano Banana Pro in OpenArt](https://openart.ai/suite/create-image/nano-banana-2)

**Upload:** Your screenshot from Step 1

**Paste this prompt:**

```
The woman's face <<>> must remain 100% identical to the first image — exact same facial features, eyes, nose, lips, face shape, skin, expression. Do not modify, beautify, retouch or regenerate her face in any way. Preserve her identity pixel-perfect. Also keep unchanged: her hair, clothing, pose and position in frame.

Cinematic frame shot on ARRI Alexa: gentle organic image rendering, soft natural sharpness — not crisp, not oversharpened, filmic smooth roll-off in highlights and shadows. Shallow depth of field: the woman in soft natural focus, the wall behind her gently falling out of focus, creamy bokeh, real optical depth.

The wall behind her: photographs of missing people pinned directly onto the painted wall — portraits of different missing persons, alongside newspaper clippings, maps with marked locations, handwritten notes and a red string web connecting everything with push pins. Real tactile textures: paper fiber, creased corners, halftone print, fibrous red thread.

A small wall-mounted lamp above the photographs shines warm light down onto them, soft and contained, gentle falloff. Deep in the dark background the room continues: barely visible bookshelves with books, boxes and belongings in shadow, softly blurred, faint edges catching stray warm light; a desk lamp glows out of focus as a warm blurred point of light.

Balanced lighting: warm key on the wall and the woman, soft ambient shadows, no harsh contrast, no crushed blacks, no blown highlights. Muted true-to-life colors with gentle warmth, accurate skin tones, subtle fine film grain, cinematic night interior. Photorealistic, live-action movie still. No text overlays.
```

**Result:** You'll get a cinematic detective scene image with you standing in front of an evidence wall

### Step 3: Animate with Motion Transfer (Seedance 2.0)

Go to [Seedance 2.0 in OpenArt](https://openart.ai/suite/create-video/byte-plus-seedance-2)

**Upload:**

- Your Nano Banana generated image (from Step 2)

```
Soft organic image, not oversharpened, filmic highlight roll-off, balanced light, no harsh contrast, accurate skin tones, cinematic night interior, photorealistic live-action look. No text, no captions.
```

**Result:** Your movements transferred to the detective evidence wall scene with perfect identity preservation

## Key Technical Principles

### Identity Preservation

- **Pixel-Perfect Facial Lock:** Every feature must match original exactly
- **No Beauty Filters:** Preserve natural skin texture, imperfections, authentic appearance
- **Consistent Throughout:** Identity never drifts across animation frames
- **Expression Match:** Maintain original facial expression from source video

### Cinematic Quality

- **ARRI Alexa Aesthetic:** Gentle organic rendering, not oversharpened, filmic roll-off
- **Shallow Depth of Field:** Subject sharp, background gently defocused, creamy bokeh
- **Premium Color Grading:** Muted true-to-life colors, gentle warmth, accurate skin tones
- **Fine Film Grain:** Subtle analog character throughout

### Lighting Authenticity

- **Single Practical Source:** One warm tungsten lamp as dominant light
- **Natural Falloff:** Light gradually fades to deep shadows at edges
- **No Theatrical Lighting:** No studio lights, ceiling lights, or bright office lighting
- **Balanced Exposure:** Warm highlights, soft ambient shadows, no crushed blacks, no blown highlights
- **Nighttime Interior:** Dark cinematic atmosphere with intimate practical lighting

### Investigation Materials Realism

- **Tactile Physical Texture:** Folded paper, curled edges, paper fibers, handling marks
- **Authentic Wear:** Coffee stains, fingerprints, creases, worn corners, slight discoloration
- **Believable Arrangement:** Intentionally art-directed but not artificially perfect
- **Proper Orientation:** Documents readable from subject's perspective, not camera's
- **Realistic Depth:** Overlapping materials with natural stacking and shadows

### Motion Transfer Accuracy

- **100% One-to-One:** Identical timing, gestures, body language frame by frame
- **No Alterations:** Do not add, remove, speed up, slow down, or interpolate motion
- **Performance Integrity:** Reproduce complete performance from first to last frame
- **Natural Movement:** Breathing, weight shifts, subtle movements preserved exactly

### Environment Behavior

- **Static Investigation Materials:** Papers, photos, documents remain perfectly motionless
- **Constant Lighting:** Lamp glow stays consistent, no flickering
- **Still Air:** No paper movement, everything stationary except subject
- **Quiet Atmosphere:** Intimate, tense, obsessive investigative mood

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Identity Drifts During Animation

**Solution:** Strengthen identity preservation language in Seedance prompt. Emphasize "preserve her identity perfectly throughout the entire animation" and "do not change her face, hair or clothing from the reference image."

### Issue: Investigation Materials Move or Float

**Solution:** Add explicit instruction in Seedance prompt: "investigation papers remain completely motionless, photographs remain fixed, documents do not move, folders remain stationary."

### Issue: Lighting Flickers or Changes

**Solution:** Specify "warm practical lamp glow remains constant, no lighting flicker" and "lighting stays constant."

### Issue: Background Too Bright

**Solution:** In Nano Banana prompt, emphasize "nighttime interior," "dark cinematic atmosphere," "deep natural shadows," and specify that background should be "1.5 to 2 stops darker than subjects."

### Issue: Materials Look CGI or Artificial

**Solution:** Strengthen physical realism language: "authentic physical texture," "real tactile textures," "paper fiber," "creased corners," "handling marks," "physically real."

### Issue: Motion Doesn't Match Source Video

**Solution:** In Seedance prompt, emphasize "transfer the woman's movements 100% one-to-one with identical timing" and "reproduce the complete performance exactly from the first frame of the source video until the last frame."

### Issue: Camera Angle Changes

**Solution:** Specify "camera behavior matches the source video exactly, preserving identical framing, overhead perspective, camera movement, timing, lens behavior and composition."

## Pro Tips

1. **Record Clean Source Footage:** Simple movements, good lighting, steady camera make better results
2. **Screenshot Timing:** Choose frames with good composition and clear facial features
3. **Identity Reference Strength:** The stronger your original screenshot quality, the better identity preservation
4. **Lighting Consistency:** Ensure your source video lighting somewhat matches intended nighttime detective aesthetic
5. **Movement Subtlety:** Smaller, more contained movements transfer more reliably than large dramatic gestures
6. **Composition Planning:** Think about how your body position will look surrounded by investigation materials
7. **Iteration Strategy:** Generate multiple versions and select best identity/atmosphere match
8. **Material Orientation:** Remember documents should be readable from your perspective, not camera's
