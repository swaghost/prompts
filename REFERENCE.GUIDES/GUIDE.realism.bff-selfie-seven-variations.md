# Guide: BFF Selfie → Seven Different Image Variations

## Overview

This comprehensive guide demonstrates how to generate seven distinct ultra-realistic iPhone selfie variations of two women friends while maintaining perfect identity consistency across all scenarios. Each variation places the same two individuals in different environments, poses, and lighting conditions while preserving their exact facial features, proportions, and characteristics throughout.

## Image Model

**Nano Banana Pro** (recommended for identity preservation)

---

## Core Identity Preservation Requirements

**CRITICAL:** These requirements apply to ALL seven variations:

- **Exact Facial Identity Lock:** Preserve both women's facial structure, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, ears, skin tone, age, ethnicity completely
- **Hairstyle Consistency:** Maintain exact hairstyle, hairline, hair color, texture
- **Body Proportions:** Keep physique, body proportions, height identical
- **No Identity Drift:** Women must be instantly recognizable across all variations
- **Character Reference:** Use uploaded image as single source of truth for appearance

## Key Success Principles

1. **Identity is Sacred:** The uploaded reference image is the single source of truth. Preserve both women's complete identity across all variations without exception.

2. **Smartphone Realism:** Every variation should look like it was genuinely captured on an iPhone and uploaded directly to Instagram without editing (except Variation 4's intentional flash).

3. **Natural Imperfection:** Avoid perfect symmetry, flawless skin, or professional retouching. Include realistic skin texture, natural asymmetry, and authentic smartphone characteristics.

4. **Environmental Authenticity:** Each environment should feel real and lived-in, not CGI or artificially constructed. Use realistic lighting, proper physics, and believable atmospheric perspective.

5. **Consistent Physics:** Clothing drapes naturally, hair moves realistically, shadows fall correctly, reflections behave authentically, and depth of field matches smartphone camera behavior.

6. **Pose Variety:** While poses vary dramatically across variations, each should feel natural and spontaneous rather than choreographed or fashion-editorial.

7. **Technical Accuracy:** Match iPhone camera specifications exactly - lens equivalents, computational photography characteristics, Smart HDR behavior, natural color science.

## Application Tips

- **Start with Strong Reference:** Use a high-quality photo with clear facial features for both subjects

- **Lighting Matters:** Pay special attention to lighting specifications - they create authenticity
