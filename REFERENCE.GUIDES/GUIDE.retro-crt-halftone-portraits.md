# Retro CRT Halftone Portrait Guide

## Source

Extracted from the public Notion page `effect-3b5dbe23c4c38076b088f434d5b6767d`.

## Shared Direction

Create genuine photographs of vintage CRT displays, not flat digital vectors. Render the subject through a hot-pink/magenta LED-like halftone dot matrix with variable dot density, physical curved glass, scanlines, moire, chromatic aberration, dust, glare, phosphor bloom, and organic film grain.

## Shared Realism Rules

- Preserve anatomically correct facial contours, hands, accessories, and gestures within the dot grid.
- Keep the CRT bezel, glass curvature, screen reflections, and physical display behavior believable.
- Use deep black, dark plum, violet-black, neon magenta, hot pink, and glowing pink-white.
- Avoid flat vector art, modern LCD screens, clean digital gradients, text, watermarks, and CGI.
- Default aspect ratio: 9:16.

## Prompt Set

1. Over-the-shoulder woman with direct gaze on a curved CRT.
2. Woman with sunglasses and finger-to-lips "shh" gesture.
3. Duplicate of the shh composition for alternate generation.
4. Tilted close-up face with sunglasses and halftone phosphor grid.
5. Right-facing side profile with messy bun and glowing facial contour.
