## When to Use This Guide

### Use This Guide When:

- ✓ Launching a new product with comprehensive marketing
- ✓ Creating complete brand campaign across multiple platforms
- ✓ Developing brand visual identity for AI-generated content
- ✓ Planning multi-touchpoint customer journey (discovery to purchase)
- ✓ Rebranding or visual identity refresh
- ✓ Creating brand guidelines for marketing team

### Don't Use This Guide For:

- ✗ Single standalone marketing images
- ✗ Quick social media posts without campaign strategy

## The 8-Part Campaign System

### Complete Brand Touchpoint Journey

```
DISCOVERY → CONSIDERATION → PURCHASE → EXPERIENCE → ADVOCACY
    ↓            ↓            ↓           ↓            ↓
  1,3,8        2,3,4        5,6,2        7,4          4,8
```

### The 8 Campaign Components

#### 1. Brand Identity Presentation Board

- **Purpose**: Visual foundation - brand values, colors, typography
- **Use Case**: Internal alignment, pitch decks, brand guidelines
- **Customer Journey**: Brand discovery and understanding
- **Key Value**: Establishes visual system for all other assets

#### 2. Signature Product Showcase

- **Purpose**: Hero product photography for e-commerce
- **Use Case**: Product pages, announcements, e-commerce
- **Customer Journey**: Product consideration and desire
- **Key Value**: Iconic product representation

#### 3. Hero Advertisement Poster

- **Purpose**: Bold campaign creative with brand messaging
- **Use Case**: Paid ads, launch campaigns, awareness
- **Customer Journey**: Discovery and brand positioning
- **Key Value**: Makes brand statement, drives awareness

#### 4. Handheld Product Campaign

- **Purpose**: Lifestyle photography showing human connection
- **Use Case**: Organic social, relatable content, influencer-style
- **Customer Journey**: Consideration and social proof
- **Key Value**: Demonstrates scale, creates personal connection

#### 5. Mobile App UI Presentation

- **Purpose**: Mobile shopping experience and product page
- **Use Case**: App development, mobile commerce strategy
- **Customer Journey**: Purchase decision and transaction
- **Key Value**: Shows modern digital experience

#### 6. Website Design Showcase

- **Purpose**: E-commerce landing page and brand hub
- **Use Case**: Website design, online brand destination
- **Customer Journey**: Purchase and brand immersion
- **Key Value**: Complete online brand experience

#### 7. Packaging Design Presentation

- **Purpose**: Unboxing experience and brand touchpoint
- **Use Case**: Product packaging, unboxing content
- **Customer Journey**: Post-purchase experience
- **Key Value**: Physical brand interaction, shareable moment

#### 8. Model Campaign

- **Purpose**: Aspirational lifestyle fashion campaign
- **Use Case**: Hero content, lifestyle storytelling
- **Customer Journey**: Discovery, aspiration, advocacy
- **Key Value**: Aspirational brand positioning

## Brand Positioning Guide

Use this section to match your campaign aesthetic to your brand tier.

### LUXURY TIER (Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Rolex)

**What Matters to These Brands**:

- Heritage and craftsmanship above all
- Exclusivity and scarcity (never mass appeal)
- Uncompromising quality and materials
- Refined, sophisticated, never loud aesthetics
- Brand history and artisanal tradition
- Subtlety - logo visible but not dominant

**Visual Standards**:

- **Colors**: Muted, sophisticated palettes OR brand heritage colors
- **Typography**: Serif fonts, refined, elegant, timeless
- **Photography**: Magazine editorial quality, soft lighting
- **Composition**: Minimal, generous white space, refined
- **Materials**: Only premium materials referenced (silk, leather, gold)
- **Background**: Almost always white or very subtle

**Social Media Strategy**:

- Post quality over frequency (2-3x per week)
- Behind-the-scenes craftsmanship storytelling
- Heritage and brand history content
- Editorial photography, never casual
- Instagram and Pinterest primary platforms
- Avoid trends that compromise sophistication

**Messaging Tone**:

- Refined, never casual
- Storytelling over selling
- Heritage emphasis
- Craftsmanship focus
- Subtle, never boastful

**Comparable Brands for AI Prompts**:
"Hermès," "Louis Vuitton," "Gucci," "haute couture," "luxury fashion house"

---

### PREMIUM TIER (Nike, Apple, Adidas Originals, Lacoste, Lululemon)

**What Matters to These Brands**:

- Innovation and design excellence
- Performance and quality (demonstrable benefits)
- Strong brand identity and recognition
- Aspirational but achievable positioning
- Balance of style and function
- Modern, contemporary aesthetics

**Visual Standards**:

- **Colors**: Bold brand colors OR clean minimal with strong accents
- **Typography**: Sans-serif, modern, clean, confident
- **Photography**: Commercial quality, dynamic lighting
- **Composition**: Clean, modern, product prominent
- **Materials**: High-quality materials, innovation emphasis
- **Background**: White with gradient accents OR dynamic

**Social Media Strategy**:

- Consistent cadence (daily or near-daily)
- Mix of product showcases and lifestyle integration
- Influencer and expert partnerships
- User-generated content integration
- Multi-platform presence (Instagram, TikTok, Twitter)
- Trend integration with brand filter

**Messaging Tone**:

- Confident and bold
- Innovation-focused
- Benefit-driven
- Motivational and aspirational
- Action-oriented

**Comparable Brands for AI Prompts**:
"Nike," "Apple," "Adidas Originals," "Lacoste," "premium sportswear," "luxury tech"

### MASS MARKET TIER (H&M, Zara, Target, Uniqlo)

**What Matters to These Brands**:

- Accessibility and affordability
- Trend responsiveness and variety
- Value communication (price-quality balance)

## Maintaining Product Consistency

This is CRITICAL for campaign credibility and brand trust.

### The Consistency Problem

AI image generators may alter product details between different prompts, leading to:

- Different colors or color blocking
- Changed logo positions or sizes
- Altered proportions or shapes
- Modified materials or textures
- Missing design details

### The Consistency Solution

#### Always Add This Instruction

At the end of EVERY prompt for ALL campaign assets:

```
Use the exact [product] from the provided reference images without changing its
shape, colors, materials, [key elements], branding, proportions, or detailing. Maintain
perfect product consistency across all designs.
```

**Customize for your product**:

- `[product]`: shoe, watch, bag, phone, etc.
- `[key elements]`: sole design, strap, clasp, button layout, etc.

**Example for Sneaker**:

```
Use the exact shoe from the provided reference images without changing its
shape, colors, materials, sole design, branding, proportions, or detailing. Maintain
perfect product consistency across all designs.
```

#### Verification Checklist

After generating each asset, verify:

**Colors**:

- [ ] Primary color matches exactly
- [ ] Secondary/accent colors correct
- [ ] Color blocking pattern identical
- [ ] No AI-invented color variations

**Branding**:

- [ ] Logo in correct position
- [ ] Logo size proportional
- [ ] Logo style accurate (embossed, printed, etc.)
- [ ] All brand marks present

**Shape & Proportions**:

- [ ] Overall silhouette identical
- [ ] Proportions match (not stretched/compressed)
- [ ] Design details in correct positions
- [ ] No structural changes

**Materials & Textures**:

- [ ] Materials rendered accurately (leather, mesh, metal)
- [ ] Texture details present
- [ ] Finish correct (matte, glossy, textured)

**Design Details**:

- [ ] All design elements present
- [ ] Stitching, panels, overlays correct
- [ ] Functional elements accurate (laces, zippers, buttons)

## Campaign Cohesion Strategies

Cohesion creates brand recognition and professional polish.

### Visual Cohesion

#### Color Palette Consistency

1. **Extract 3-5 colors from product** (Use free tool or eyeball it)
   - Primary (dominant product color)
   - Secondary (accent color)
   - Background (usually white)
   - Typography (usually black/dark)
   - CTA/Highlight (product accent)

2. **Use consistently across ALL assets**
   - Backgrounds: Same color (usually white)
   - Typography: Same color (usually black/dark)
   - Accents: Same product accent colors
   - Gradients: Same color combinations

3. **Document your palette**
   ```
   Brand Palette:
   - Primary: #000000 (Black)
   - Secondary: #FFFFFF (White)
   - Accent: #D2691E (Burnt Orange)
   - Typography: #1A1A1A (Near Black)
   - Background: #FFFFFF (White)
   ```

#### Typography Consistency

1. **Choose 2-3 font styles**
   - Headlines: Bold, modern, prominent
   - Body: Clean, readable, contemporary
   - Accents: Special use (optional)

2. **Define hierarchy**
   - H1: Hero headlines (Hero Ad, Website)
   - H2: Section headings (Website, App)
   - Body: Product descriptions
   - CTA: Button text

3. **Maintain style across assets**
   - Same typeface family
   - Consistent weights (Bold for headlines, Regular for body)
   - Similar sizing hierarchy

#### Photography Style Consistency

1. **Lighting approach**
   - Dramatic studio lighting
   - Soft natural lighting
   - Cinematic lighting
   - _Choose one, use throughout_

2. **Composition style**
   - Minimal with white space
   - Dynamic with movement
   - Editorial magazine style
   - _Choose one, use throughout_

3. **Background treatment**
   - Clean white backgrounds
   - White-to-dark gradients
   - Lifestyle settings (blurred)
   - _Be consistent_

#### Graphic Elements

1. **Choose recurring motifs**
   - Geometric shapes (circles, rings, rectangles)
   - Dynamic lines or streaks
   - Motion graphics
   - _Use same elements across assets_

2. **Apply consistently**
   - Hero Advertisement: Motion streaks + geometric shapes
   - Signature Product: Geometric rings
   - Brand Identity: Graphic elements in layout
   - Website: Geometric accents in UI

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue 1: Product Looks Different Across Assets

**Symptoms**:

- Colors change between images
- Logo position shifts
- Proportions altered
- Design details missing or changed

**Solutions**:

1. ✓ Add consistency instruction to EVERY prompt
2. ✓ Use same reference images for all assets
3. ✓ Be very specific about product details in prompts
4. ✓ Verify each asset against original reference
5. ✓ Regenerate assets that don't match exactly

**Prevention**:

- Always describe product in detail (colors, materials, features)
- Always add consistency instruction at end of prompt
- List specific elements that must remain identical

---

### Issue 2: Campaign Doesn't Look Cohesive

**Symptoms**:

- Assets feel disconnected or random
- Different color palettes across assets
- Varying photography styles
- Inconsistent messaging

**Solutions**:

1. ✓ Extract color palette from product, document it, use everywhere
2. ✓ Choose one photography lighting style, specify in all prompts
3. ✓ Use same comparable brand references across assets
4. ✓ Maintain similar background treatments (white, gradients)
5. ✓ Repeat graphic elements (geometric shapes, motion lines)

**Prevention**:

- Create brand identity board FIRST (establishes visual system)
- Document color palette and typography before creating assets
- Use consistent prompt structure and references

---

### Issue 3: Wrong Brand Tier Aesthetic

**Symptoms**:

- Luxury brand looks too casual/cheap
- Mass market brand looks too expensive
- Aesthetic doesn't match positioning

**Solutions**:

1. ✓ Review brand tier framework (Luxury/Premium/Mass)
2. ✓ Adjust comparable brand references to match tier
3. ✓ Modify material language:
   - Luxury: "Matte finish," "embossed," "refined," "sophisticated"
   - Premium: "Bold," "modern," "premium," "quality"
   - Mass: "Bright," "approachable," "contemporary," "accessible"
4. ✓ Adjust photography style:
   - Luxury: Editorial, soft lighting, minimal
   - Premium: Commercial, dynamic lighting, clean
   - Mass: Lifestyle, bright lighting, relatable

**Prevention**:

- Define brand tier before starting
- Research comparable brands at same tier
- Review each asset: "Does this look [luxury/premium/mass]?"

---

### Issue 4: Social Media Performance vs Brand Standards

**Symptoms**:

- On-brand content gets low engagement
- High-performing content feels off-brand
- Struggle balancing authenticity with sophistication

**Solutions**:

1. ✓ Create platform-specific variations:
   - Feed: On-brand, polished, aspirational
   - Stories/Reels: Slightly more casual, authentic
   - TikTok: Trend-integrated but brand-filtered
2. ✓ Test and learn:
   - Track engagement by content type
   - Find sweet spot between brand and performance
   - Iterate based on data
3. ✓ Use hierarchy:
   - Feed: Brand standards maintained
   - Stories: 80% brand standards
   - Reels/TikTok: 70% brand standards (more trend flexibility)

**Prevention**:

- Accept that different platforms have different standards
- Maintain brand core, adapt execution
- Luxury brands: fewer platforms, higher standards maintained
- Mass brands: more platforms, more flexibility

---

### Issue 5: Product Not Prominent in Lifestyle Shots

**Symptoms**:

- Model campaign: outfit overwhelms product
- Handheld: product too small or unclear
- Packaging: product lost in arrangement

**Solutions**:

1. ✓ Add product emphasis to prompts:
   - "Emphasize the [product] through dynamic angles"
   - "[Product] clearly visible and prominent"
   - "Focus on [product] with [other elements] supporting"
2. ✓ Use shallow depth of field:
   - "Shallow depth of field with [product] in sharp focus"
   - "Background softly blurred, [product] sharp"
3. ✓ Adjust composition:
   - "[Product] takes up [X]% of frame"
   - "Close-up composition showing [product] details"

**Prevention**:

- Always specify product should be hero/prominent/emphasized
- Use dynamic angles that showcase product
- Consider composition in advance

### Issue 6: Assets Don't Adapt Well to Different Platforms

**Symptoms**:

- Cropping cuts off important elements
- Product too small on mobile
- Horizontal images don't work for vertical platforms

**Solutions**:

1. ✓ Create platform-specific versions from start:
   - Instagram Feed: 4:5 vertical
   - Stories/TikTok: 9:16 vertical
   - Website: 16:9 horizontal
   - Pinterest: 2:3 vertical
2. ✓ Adjust composition for format:
   - Vertical: Ensure product centered vertically
   - Horizontal: Product can be left/right positioned
3. ✓ Test safe zones:
   - Center-weight important elements
   - Avoid placing product at extreme edges

**Prevention**:

- Plan for multiple formats from beginning
- Create vertical versions of key assets
- Test mock-ups before finalizing
