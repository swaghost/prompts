## Shared Direction

Create genuine in-camera studio photographs, not CGI or digital painting. Use high-contrast halo backlighting, a light gray studio/cyclorama, a polished semi-reflective floor, realistic vehicle reflections, natural skin and fabric texture, slight photographic grain, and no HDR or over-sharpening. The visual language is premium streetwear lookbook plus automotive editorial.

## Shared Realism Rules

- No beauty smoothing, porcelain skin, or airbrushed perfection.
- Preserve natural skin, hair, stubble, hands, fabric, rubber, leather, denim, and floor textures.
- Use believable full-body composition and physically accurate car reflections.
- No visible logos unless explicitly required by the prompt.
- Avoid watermarks, text, cartoon rendering, surreal effects, extra limbs, and plastic skin.
- Default aspect ratio: 3:4.
