1. Open Soul and set model to Soul V2 (2.0)

## Step 2: The Three Prerequisites

### Before You Generate Anything

**You need these three elements in every prompt:**

#### 1. A Trained Soul

- Your character, trained once, reused on every prompt
- This is what keeps the face identical across all 20 shots
- Without it, every generation is a random face

#### 2. The World (Named Locations)

- **Real, named locations** — "Café de Flore" not "a café"
- "Avenue Montaigne, Paris" not "a street in Paris"
- "Faena Hotel, Miami" not "a luxury hotel"

**Why specificity matters:**

- Makes it read as a real photograph instead of a render
- Grounds the image in believable reality
- AI knows these places and their aesthetic

#### 3. One Hero Label

- One designer piece as the subject per shot
- Examples: Louis Vuitton bag, Hermès belt, Chanel sunglasses
- **One is luxury. Three is a fake account.**

**The rule:**

- Show one identifiable luxury item per image
- Don't pile on logos (reads as inauthentic)
- Let the item be the focal point

## Step 3: The 8-Block Prompt Formula

### The Anatomy Every Prompt Follows

Every luxury prompt in this system is built from **8 blocks in this exact order**:

| #     | Block                           | What It Does          | Example                                                                       |
| ----- | ------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | **Subject + body line**         | Locks the silhouette  | "Slim woman, 20s, athletic build"                                             |
| **2** | **Full outfit, one hero label** | The aspirational read | "White linen shirt, camel trousers, Louis Vuitton Speedy"                     |
| **3** | **Named location + periphery**  | Believability         | "Café de Flore terrace, Paris, marble table, espresso cup visible"            |
| **4** | **Light**                       | Mood + realism        | "Soft afternoon light through awning, warm shadows"                           |
| **5** | **Eye emotion**                 | The soul of the shot  | "Eyes direct to camera, confident but relaxed"                                |
| **6** | **Hand / action**               | Candid, not posed     | "One hand holding coffee, other adjusting sunglasses"                         |
| **7** | **Camera + angle**              | The format            | "35mm lens, eye-level, slight Dutch tilt"                                     |
| **8** | **Realism tags**                | Prevents AI look      | "Natural skin texture, film grain, no studio look, candid street photography" |

---

### Why This Order Matters

**Block flow creates believability:**

1. **Subject** → Establishes who
2. **Outfit** → Establishes what (the luxury piece)
3. **Location** → Establishes where (the world)
4. **Light** → Establishes when (time of day)
5. **Eye emotion** → Establishes feeling (connection)
6. **Hand/action** → Establishes motion (candid moment)
7. **Camera** → Establishes perspective (how we're seeing it)
8. **Realism tags** → Establishes medium (photograph, not render)

---

### The Non-Negotiable Realism Tags

**Block 8 is critical:**

```
natural skin texture, film grain, no studio look, candid photography,
visible pores, slight color noise, authentic moment, unposed,
shot on 35mm film
```

**Why these tags matter:**

- Ending on these stops the image from tipping into "obvious AI"
- Without them, the whole effect collapses
- They're the difference between "wait, is this AI?" and "obviously generated"
- Film grain + skin texture = believability

**What happens without realism tags:**

- Plastic-smooth skin
- Overly perfect lighting
- Studio-rendered look
- Immediate AI tell

## Step 5: Generation Settings

### Aspect Ratio Guide

**Choose based on platform and format:**

| Ratio    | Platform               | Use Case                                        |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **3:4**  | Instagram Feed         | Standard portrait post                          |
| **4:5**  | Instagram Feed         | Taller portrait (more real estate)              |
| **9:16** | Reels, Stories, TikTok | Vertical video format, any frame you'll animate |

**Ratio cheat-sheet:**

- 3:4 or 4:5 for the feed grid
- 9:16 for reels, stories, and any frame you'll animate into video

---

### Quality Settings

**Two-tier approach:**

1. **2K for drafts**
   - Fast generation
   - Review composition, face, hands
   - Decide which to keep

2. **4K for keepers**
   - Final quality for winners only
   - Upscale before editing
   - Send to Kling/Seedance if animating

**Why not generate everything at 4K:**

- Slower generation time
- Wastes resources on images you'll reject
- 2K sufficient for review

---

### Batch Generation Strategy

**Higgsfield concurrency limits:**

- **Maximum 8 jobs running simultaneously**
- Queue in waves of **7-8 prompts**
- Let them complete before sending next wave
- Don't overload (causes slowdown)

**Efficient workflow:**

1. Queue 7-8 prompts
2. Monitor completion
3. Review results (face, hands, composition)
4. Re-roll any with drift
5. Send next wave of 7-8

**For 20-prompt set:**

- Wave 1: Prompts 1-8
- Wave 2: Prompts 9-16
- Wave 3: Prompts 17-20 + any re-rolls

## Step 6: Review & Quality Control

### What to Check First

**Priority checklist:**

1. **Face consistency** — Does it match your trained Soul?
2. **Hands** — Correct number of fingers, natural position?
3. **Hero label** — Is the luxury item visible and recognizable?
4. **Location accuracy** — Does environment match prompt?
5. **Lighting** — Natural and believable?
6. **Composition** — Well-framed, not awkward crop?

---

### When to Re-Roll

**Re-generate if:**

- Face has drifted (doesn't match your Soul character)
- Hand deformities (extra fingers, unnatural pose)
- Hero label is unreadable or missing
- Lighting looks studio-artificial
- Composition is awkward or cropped incorrectly
- Overall image reads as "obviously AI"

**Don't re-roll for:**

- Minor background details
- Slight color variations
- Small imperfections (adds realism)
- Film grain or texture (intentional)

## Step 7: Batch & Scale Strategy

### The Core Principle

**Lock the character, swap the scene.**

- Keep the **same Soul** on every generation
- Change **only** the outfit, location, and light
- That's how you post in volume without face drift

**This enables:**

- 20+ posts with identical face
- Cohesive brand/character IP
- Scalable content production
- Consistent influencer presence

---

### The No-Text Rule

**Critical: Don't ask Soul for text.**

**Why:**

- Soul doesn't render legible in-frame typography
- Text attempts always look AI-generated
- Wastes generation trying to fix it

**The solution:**

- **Let the image sell the look**
- **Let the caption sell the message**
- Never chase text in the image
- All typography lives in the caption/overlay

**Examples:**

- ❌ Don't: "Generate text saying 'PARIS' on a cafe sign"
- ✅ Do: Generate the cafe, add "Paris ☕️" in the caption

---

### Volume Workflow

**Creating a month of content:**

1. **Train your Soul character once** (15-25 photos)
2. **Choose 20 prompts** from the 6 categories
3. **Generate in waves** (7-8 at a time)
4. **Review and re-roll** as needed
5. **Upscale keepers** to 4K
6. **Schedule posts** (1-2 per day for sustained presence)

**Time investment:**

- Character training: 30 minutes (one time)
- Prompt selection: 10 minutes
- Generation: 2-3 hours (for 20 prompts with review)
- **Result: Entire month of luxury content**

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Face doesn't match my trained Soul

**Causes:**

- Wrong character selected
- Insufficient training photos
- Low-quality reference photos
- Extreme prompt causing drift

**Solutions:**

- Verify correct Soul selected in dropdown
- Re-train with 20-25 varied, high-quality photos
- Include multiple angles and lighting conditions
- Simplify extreme pose/angle requests
- Re-generate 2-3 times (variation is normal)

---

### Problem: Hands look deformed or wrong

**Causes:**

- AI struggles with hand anatomy
- Complex hand interactions
- Partially visible hands

**Solutions:**

- Specify simple hand actions: "one hand holding coffee"
- Avoid "both hands holding small object"
- Try "hands resting casually" or "one hand in pocket"
- Re-generate multiple times
- Crop hands out in edit if necessary

---

### Problem: Image looks too AI/rendered

**Causes:**

- Missing realism tags (Block 8)
- Over-perfect lighting
- Plastic-smooth skin

**Solutions:**

- **Always include Block 8 realism tags:**
  - "Natural skin texture, film grain, no studio look"
  - "Visible pores, slight color noise"
  - "Candid photography, unposed"
- Specify "soft natural light" not "perfect studio lighting"
- Add "slight imperfections, authentic moment"

---

### Problem: Luxury item/logo not visible

**Causes:**

- Prompt not specific enough about item placement
- Camera angle hiding the item
- Item too small in frame

**Solutions:**

- Specify position: "Louis Vuitton bag prominently on table"
- Adjust camera: "medium shot showing bag clearly"
- Make item the focal point: "holding Hermès bag at chest level"
- Ensure lighting hits the item
- Re-generate if item is critical

---

### Problem: Location doesn't look like the named place

**Causes:**

- AI doesn't know obscure locations
- Generic description competing with specific name
- Insufficient location details

**Solutions:**

- Use famous, well-known locations (Café de Flore, Mykonos)
- Add location details: "marble table, red awning" for Paris cafe
- Don't contradict: "Café de Flore" + "modern minimalist" = conflict
- Research location visuals first
- Stick to iconic luxury destinations AI knows

---

### Problem: Colors look wrong or washed out

**Causes:**

- Conflicting color descriptions
- "Natural light" washing out color block shots
- Missing saturation keywords

**Solutions:**

- For color block shots: "saturated [color], vibrant, bold"
- Specify "rich color, high saturation, color dominance"
- Avoid "muted" or "soft" with color block category
- Check that lighting supports color (not overexposed)

## Best Practices Summary

### Character Training

1. **15-25 photos minimum** — More is better
2. **One face only** — No other people in frame
3. **Varied angles** — Front, profile, 3/4, looking up/down
4. **Good lighting** — Natural light, clearly visible features
5. **High resolution** — Sharp, clear photos

### Prompt Structure

1. **Follow 8-block order** — Don't skip or rearrange
2. **One hero label only** — One luxury piece per shot
3. **Named locations** — "Café de Flore" not "a cafe"
4. **Always include Block 8** — Realism tags are non-negotiable
5. **Paste unedited** — Don't modify the prompts

### Generation Strategy

1. **Queue 7-8 at a time** — Respect concurrency limits
2. **2K for drafts, 4K for keepers** — Don't waste resources
3. **Check face and hands first** — Most common failure points
4. **Re-roll drift immediately** — Don't settle
5. **Same Soul for entire batch** — Never change character mid-set

### Content Strategy

1. **Mix all 6 registers** — Variety prevents monotony
2. **Color block for engagement** — Highest share rate
3. **Quiet luxury for credibility** — Most believable
4. **Don't ask for text** — Caption handles words
5. **Animate selectively** — Color block and evening work best

### Scaling

1. **Lock character, swap scene** — Core principle
2. **Generate in waves** — 7-8 prompts per wave
3. **20 prompts = month of content** — 1-2 posts per day
4. **Consistent posting** — Builds character IP
5. **Track what performs** — Double down on top categories
