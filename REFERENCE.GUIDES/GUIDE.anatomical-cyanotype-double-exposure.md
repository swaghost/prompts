## Shared Direction

Create monochromatic Prussian-blue cyanotype photographs with semitransparent X-ray skeletal overlays aligned to the subject's anatomy. The result should resemble a physical historical print on hand-coated watercolor paper, with chemical stains, paper grain, edge variation, soft vignetting, analog grain, and no modern digital rendering.

## Shared Realism Rules

- Anatomical overlay must align precisely with the visible skull, spine, ribs, limbs, and hands.
- Preserve natural skin, fur, hair, fabric, paper, and environmental texture.
- Use indigo, navy, Prussian blue, steel blue, and off-white highlights.
- Avoid bright colors, plastic skin, extra limbs/fingers, cartoon rendering, clean digital borders, and CGI.
- Default aspect ratio: 3:4.

## Prompt Set

1. Woman in side profile with skull, cervical spine, collarbone, and ribs.
2. Woman photographing with a vintage SLR and visible skeletal overlay.
3. Baby macaque hugging a stuffed monkey with biological anatomy contrasted against plush form.
4. Young man and dog with both human and canine skeletons in harmony.
5. Seated man with aligned skull, spine, rib cage, arms, and clasped hands.
