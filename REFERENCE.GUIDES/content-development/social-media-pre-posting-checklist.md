# Social Media Pre-Posting Checklist

## The 6-Minute Check Nobody Talks About

**Most people lose reach before they ever hit share.**

This is the comprehensive pre-posting quality control checklist that separates high-performing content from algorithmic graveyard posts.

---

## ⚠️ Step 1: Check Account Status First

**Time: 30 seconds**  
**Priority: CRITICAL**

### How To Check

1. Go to Settings → Account Status
2. Scroll all the way down
3. Look for:
   - Content restrictions
   - Removed content
   - Recommendation issues
   - Violations or warnings

### Why This Matters

If anything is flagged, fix that before you feed the machine. Posting with active violations guarantees:

- Reduced reach
- Shadow ban effects
- Suppressed distribution
- Lower engagement rates

### Action Items

- [ ] Navigate to account status
- [ ] Review all sections completely
- [ ] Resolve any violations before posting
- [ ] Wait 24-48 hours after resolution before posting high-priority content

---

## 👤 Step 2: Take The Stranger Test

**Time: 2 minutes**  
**Priority: HIGH**

### The Test

Pretend you are a random nonfollower in your exact target audience.

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **Would this stop you mid-scroll?**
2. **Would it hold your attention?**
3. **Would the CTA make you take action?**

### Evaluation Criteria

**Stop Power (First 1-3 seconds)**

- Pattern interrupt present?
- Visual hook compelling?
- Text overlay draws eye?
- Thumbnail intriguing?

**Hold Power (Middle section)**

- Pacing keeps attention?
- Value delivered quickly?
- No unnecessary fluff?
- Payoff worth the wait?

**Action Power (CTA)**

- Clear next step?
- Specific instruction?
- Benefit obvious?
- Low-friction action?

### Decision Matrix

- **3 YES answers** → Ready to post
- **2 YES answers** → Strengthen weak element
- **1 or fewer YES** → Do not post yet, rework content

**If the answer is "maybe" on any question → Do not post yet.**

### Action Items

- [ ] View content as target audience member
- [ ] Score stop, hold, and action power
- [ ] Identify specific weak points
- [ ] Strengthen before posting

---

## 🔔 Step 3: Ping Your Warmest People

**Time: 1 minute**  
**Priority: MEDIUM-HIGH**

### The Strategy

Before you publish, like recent comments on your last 3-5 posts.

### What Happens

1. They get a notification
2. They come back to your profile
3. Your new post has a warmer first audience
4. Initial engagement velocity signals quality to algorithm

### Why This Works

The algorithm watches:

- How quickly engagement happens
- Who engages (warm vs cold audience)
- Engagement rate in first 30-60 minutes

Warm audience = better initial metrics = better distribution.

**Do this before the clock starts.**

### Action Items

- [ ] Open your last 3-5 posts
- [ ] Like 5-10 recent comments on each
- [ ] Engage with any questions in comments
- [ ] Post within 5-10 minutes of doing this

---

## 📍 Step 4: Run The Post Office Test

**Time: 1 minute**  
**Priority: CRITICAL**

### The Concept

The algorithm needs an address to deliver your content.

Without a clear niche keyword, the algorithm can't effectively route your content to the right audience.

### Where To Place Niche Keywords

**Put one clear niche keyword in:**

- On-screen text (static posts, carousels)
- Spoken audio if it is a Reel
- The first caption line

### Keyword Specificity

❌ **Too Generic**

- "Tips"
- "Marketing"
- "Workout"
- "Recipe"

✅ **Properly Addressed**

- "Content creator tips"
- "Small business marketing"
- "Fitness for beginners"
- "Quick dinner recipes"

### Examples By Platform

**Instagram Reel:**

- Say keyword in first 3 seconds of voiceover
- Include in on-screen text
- Mention in first line of caption

**Carousel:**

- Put keyword on slide 1
- Include in caption hook
- Add to alt text

**Static Post:**

- Keyword in graphic text
- First line of caption
- Alt text description

### The Result

**No address = weaker delivery to your target audience**

**Clear address = algorithm knows exactly where to send it**

### Action Items

- [ ] Identify your niche keyword
- [ ] Verify keyword is specific (not generic)
- [ ] Place keyword in text/audio/caption
- [ ] Confirm keyword appears in first 3 seconds/lines

---

## 🎵 Step 5: Add Audio To Everything

**Time: 30 seconds**  
**Priority: MEDIUM**

### The Rule

Yes, even carousels and single images.

If Instagram lets you add music, use it.

### Why Audio Matters

Audio engagement is a ranking factor:

- Listening time tracked
- Sound-on views weighted higher
- Music/audio adds discoverability layer
- Trending audio can boost reach

**Silent posts give away a signal you could have used.**

### Platform-Specific

**Instagram:**

- Reels: Audio required
- Carousels: Music can be added
- Single images: Music can be added
- Stories: Music/audio recommended

**TikTok:**

- Audio is primary discovery mechanism
- Trending audio = increased visibility
- Original audio can go viral

**LinkedIn:**

- No audio requirements
- Video voiceovers recommended

### Action Items

- [ ] Add music to carousels/static posts
- [ ] Use trending audio for Reels (when relevant)
- [ ] Include voiceover for video content
- [ ] Test audio-on vs audio-off performance

---

## 💾 Step 6: If It Is A Reel, Back It Up

**Time: 1 minute**  
**Priority: HIGH (for Reels only)**

### The Problem

Reels can:

- Glitch during upload
- Get suppressed for technical reasons
- Underperform due to bad timing
- Lose trending audio window

### The Solution

**Reel Insurance Protocol:**

1. **Save it as a draft**
2. **Duplicate it twice** (3 copies total)
3. **Post Trial Reel first**
4. **Feed Reel right after** (if trial performs well)

### Why Backups Matter

- Repost without recreating
- Test timing without losing content
- Recover from technical glitches
- Capitalize on trending moments
- A/B test captions/thumbnails

### Trial vs Feed Strategy

**Trial Reel:**

- Post first
- Monitor for 1-2 hours
- Check performance metrics
- Identify issues

**Feed Reel:**

- Post if trial succeeds
- Adjust based on trial data
- Better caption if needed
- Different hashtags if needed

### Action Items

- [ ] Save Reel as draft
- [ ] Create 2 duplicate copies
- [ ] Label: Trial, Feed, Backup
- [ ] Post trial first
- [ ] Monitor trial performance before posting feed version

### Before Every Post

**Account Health (30 sec)**

- [ ] No recommendation issues

- [ ] Passes stranger test

- [ ] Draft saved
- [ ] Duplicates created

**Total Time: 6 minutes**

## Platform-Specific Checklists

### Instagram

**Static Posts & Carousels:**

- [ ] Account status clean
- [ ] Stranger test passed
- [ ] Warm comments liked
- [ ] Niche keyword in caption + text
- [ ] Audio added to post
- [ ] Aspect ratio: 4:5 or 1:1

**Reels:**

- [ ] All standard checks above
- [ ] Keyword in voiceover + caption + text
- [ ] Trending/relevant audio
- [ ] 3 copies saved (trial, feed, backup)
- [ ] Aspect ratio: 9:16

### TikTok

- [ ] Stranger test (more critical - competitive feed)
- [ ] Niche keyword in text + spoken
- [ ] Trending audio (when relevant)
- [ ] Draft saved
- [ ] Aspect ratio: 9:16
- [ ] Hashtags: 3-5 relevant

### LinkedIn

- [ ] Professional stranger test
- [ ] Keyword in first 2 lines
- [ ] No engagement bait language
- [ ] Profile complete (professional credibility)
- [ ] Thoughtful take (not just sharing)

## Common Mistakes That Kill Reach

### 1. Posting With Account Violations

**Result**: Guaranteed suppression, wasted content

**Fix**: Always check account status first

### 2. Skipping The Stranger Test

**Result**: Echo chamber blindness, content only appeals to existing followers

**Fix**: Get objective feedback, pretend you're the target audience

### 3. Generic Keywords

**Result**: Algorithm can't route content effectively

**Fix**: Use specific niche keywords (e.g., "solopreneur marketing" not "marketing")

### 4. Silent Posts

**Result**: Missing engagement signals, lower watch time metrics

**Fix**: Add music/audio to everything that allows it

### 5. No Audience Warm-Up

**Result**: Cold launch, poor initial engagement velocity

**Fix**: Like comments 5-10 minutes before posting

### 6. Single Reel Copy

**Result**: No backup if it flops or glitches

**Fix**: Save draft + create 2 duplicates before posting

### 7. Weak CTAs

**Result**: Low conversion, unclear next step

**Fix**: Specific action with clear benefit (not "link in bio")

## Advanced Optimization Tactics

### Timing Your Posts

**After completing the checklist:**

- Post when your audience is most active
- Check analytics for peak engagement times
- Consider time zones for target audience
- Avoid posting during major news/events (unless relevant)

### Content Sequencing

**Post order matters:**

1. Like comments (warm audience)
2. Post Stories (prime followers)
3. Wait 5-10 minutes
4. Post main content (feed/Reel)
5. Engage immediately with first comments

### Hashtag Strategy

**After keyword optimization:**

- 3-5 targeted hashtags (Instagram)
- Mix of sizes (small, medium, large reach)
- Niche-specific (not generic)
- First line or first comment

### A/B Testing With Backups

**Using your Reel duplicates:**

- Trial Reel: Caption A, Time Slot 1
- Feed Reel: Caption B, Time Slot 2
- Compare performance
- Learn audience preferences

## When To Use This Checklist

### Every Post

- All high-priority content
- Campaign launches
- Promotional content
- Content you spent significant time creating

### Especially When

- Reach has been declining
- After resolving account violations
- Testing new content formats
- Reposting underperforming content
- Launching new campaigns
- Content for business/income goals

### Can Skip For

- Quick Stories (not main feed)
- Time-sensitive reactive content
- Community engagement posts
- Behind-the-scenes casual content
