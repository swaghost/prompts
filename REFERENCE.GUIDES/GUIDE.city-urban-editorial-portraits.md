## Shared Direction

Create genuine in-camera editorial photographs of a Mediterranean/Middle Eastern woman in urban architectural settings. Keep realism first and style second: natural pores, vellus hair, flyaways, fabric texture, believable lens behavior, subtle grain, natural shadows, and realistic concrete, glass, stone, and metal. Avoid beauty retouching, porcelain skin, plastic faces, CGI, excessive HDR, over-sharpening, text, logos, watermarks, and extra limbs.

## Shared Visual Language

- Editorial fashion, urban realism, architectural street photography, or macro human-detail study.
- Deep charcoal, black, concrete gray, warm gold, muted skin tones, and sky blue for the city scenes.
- Strong low angles, architectural scale, golden-hour rim light, Dutch angles, and realistic perspective distortion.
- Default aspect ratio: 3:4.

## Prompt Set

1. Extreme low-angle woman pulling a charcoal coat in front of a skyscraper.
2. Intimate golden-hour portrait holding a black coat collar by a stone column.
3. Worm's-eye view with a woman towering over the camera and skyscraper.
4. Dutch-angle waist-up fashion portrait against a modern building.
5. Extreme macro profile showing pores, vellus hair, eyelashes, and a cheek mole.
