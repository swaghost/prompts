# Create Ultra-Realistic AI Characters in Video

## Description

A comprehensive system for generating hyper-realistic AI-generated people in video with natural skin textures, realistic lighting, and subtle imperfections that make them feel real. This workflow creates a sequence of macro close-up images followed by cinematic video transitions that maintain character consistency while showcasing forensic-level detail.

## Usage

Perfect for creating ultra-realistic AI characters for video content, cinematic productions, beauty photography, and detailed character studies. Ideal for content creators, filmmakers, beauty brands, and digital artists who need photorealistic human subjects with authentic skin texture, natural imperfections, and cinematic camera movements. This system excels at creating forensic macro detail with natural photographic finish — visible pores, freckles, fine hair, and realistic eye/lip/skin texture without smoothing or plastic skin effects.

- Image reference of subject
- Macro photography aesthetic understanding

## Key Principles for Ultra-Realism

### Imperfection Stacking

Layer multiple natural imperfections for maximum realism:

- Visible fine pores (not uniform, irregular sizes)
- Natural scattered freckles in warm brown tones
- Soft peach-fuzz vellus hair catching light
- Subtle tonal mottling and color variation
- Fine lines, creases, and natural aging markers
- Faint moisture lines and natural sheen on high points
- Slight asymmetry in features and expression

### Hard Preservation Lock

When zooming or cropping existing images:

- Explicitly state "this is a zoom-and-enhance of the existing frame, NOT a new generation"
- Lock identical person, expression, head angle, lighting direction
- Match existing tones rather than prescribing new ones
- Prevent AI from regenerating a new face

### Macro Detail Requirements

For extreme close-ups, specify:

- Fine radial fibers in iris
- Crypts and furrows in eye structure
- Limbal ring at iris outer edge
- Glossy wet tear film
- Natural blood vessels in sclera
- Individual eyelash detail with slight clumping
- Fine vertical texture lines on lips
- Natural vermilion border (not sharp liner)
- Vellus hair catching rim light
- Pore detail and natural skin texture

### Lighting Consistency

Maintain across all frames:

- Specific color temperature (e.g., 3200K sunset)
- Direction of rim/backlight
- Warm ambient tone throughout
- Natural soft falloff
- Same atmospheric conditions
- Catchlight positioning in eyes

### Camera & Technical Specs

Specify for each shot:

- Lens type and focal length (e.g., 100mm macro, 85mm portrait)
- Aperture (e.g., f/2.8 for shallow DOF)
- Film stock simulation (e.g., Kodak Portra 400)
- Camera body (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro Max)
- Rendering style (photorealistic, candid UGC realism)

## Negative Prompts (Universal)

Always exclude:

- Smoothing, airbrushing, plastic skin
- AI morph, CGI, anime aesthetic
- Beauty filters, Instagram filters
- Distorted anatomy, wrong proportions
- Oversaturated, overexposed
- Fake teeth (veneer-white, flat)
- Heavy makeup, drawn-on liner
- Perfect skin, missing pores
- Uniform texture, synthetic look

## Technical Notes

- Use "MAXIMUM PRIORITY" prefix for critical details
- State "Match existing [feature] tone rather than prescribing new ones"
- "No smoothing, no airbrushing, absolutely no plastic skin" in every prompt
- Specify Fitzpatrick skin tone classification for consistency
- Include film grain and natural texture
- Maintain consistent character identity across all frames
- Use shallow depth of field (f/2.8) for macro shots
- Golden hour lighting (3200K) for warm, flattering tones
