# Ball-in-Face Viral Fan Moment (World Cup) Guide

**Source:** Google Doc — “Ball in Face Prompt”  
**Model Mentioned in Source:** Nano Banana Pro

---

## Overview

This guide creates a candid viral-style stadium photo captured at the exact split second a World Cup match ball hits a fan’s face. The look should be documentary and accidental, not cinematic ad art.

---

## Inputs

- 1 identity photo of the subject
- Country/team target for jersey customization (`[Country Name]`)

---

## Copy/Paste Prompt (Source Adapted)

```
Transform @IMAGE1 into a candid action portrait captured at the exact moment a FIFA World Cup match ball collides with the side of their face. Preserve the subject's identity, facial features, hairstyle, body proportions, skin texture, and overall appearance exactly. Dress the subject in the official modern national team jersey of [Country Name], worn naturally like a real football fan attending the match.

Expression should look naturally interrupted: eyes squeezed shut or blinking, mouth slightly open, cheeks compressed from impact, hair reacting naturally from motion. The expression must feel completely accidental, captured in the exact split second before the subject could react.

Composition: close-up portrait framing from chest upward. The football should dominate the foreground, positioned extremely close to the face at the exact moment of impact, occupying roughly one-third of the frame with realistic perspective and depth. The facial compression should come from timing and camera angle only—never from cartoon distortion.

Background: the subject is seated among tightly packed spectators in the stadium stands during a live FIFA World Cup match. The camera is positioned from the perspective of another fan sitting just a few rows away, as if someone nearby instinctively photographed the moment. Do not show the football pitch, players, goalposts, scoreboard, stadium roof, or wide panoramic arena. The background should consist only of nearby stadium seats filled with spectators, creating the authentic feeling of sitting inside the crowd.

The football has accidentally flown into the spectator section after a powerful kick during live play. The surrounding spectators react naturally to the unexpected moment. Some nearby fans are laughing, pointing, ducking, flinching, leaning away, raising their hands, covering their faces, or staring in shock at the subject. Every reaction should feel candid and spontaneous, like genuine human instinct—not posed or staged.

Keep every spectator softly blurred with shallow depth of field so the subject remains the clear focus while the crowd still communicates excitement, surprise, and energy.

Photography style: documentary sports photography mixed with accidental yearbook energy. Authentic, unstaged, imperfect, humorous, and captured in a split second. The image should feel like a real viral fan photo taken unexpectedly during a live World Cup match.

Lighting: natural outdoor daylight with realistic stadium ambient light, crisp skin texture, subtle facial shadows, accurate fabric detail, and physically believable motion.

Depth of field: extremely shallow. Keep only the subject and football tack sharp while the surrounding spectators dissolve into creamy bokeh.

Color grading: soft daylight tones, mild contrast, realistic skin colors, subtle nostalgic digital-camera rendering, authentic editorial sports photography.

The final image should feel like a genuine viral photograph captured by another spectator—not promotional artwork, cinematic concept art, AI-generated imagery, or a staged portrait. Prioritize believable physics, authentic expressions, documentary realism, and accidental humor above everything else.
```

---

## Quick Settings

- Prioritize identity lock
- Keep ball large in foreground (~1/3 frame)
- Exclude field/goal/wide-arena context
- Keep crowd blurred but expressive

---

## Common Mistakes

- Over-cinematic lighting
- Cartoon face deformation
- Showing pitch/players/scoreboard
- Staged-looking reactions
