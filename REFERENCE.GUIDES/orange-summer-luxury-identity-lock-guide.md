# Luxury Orange Summer AI Image Generation Guide

## Overview

Comprehensive system for creating luxury orange-themed summer lifestyle and fashion portraits using AI image generation with strict identity preservation. This collection features 14 photorealistic prompts covering beach clubs, Mediterranean resorts, sunset photography, editorial fashion, and lifestyle moments—all unified by a rich orange color story.

## Core System

### Platform & Settings

- **Primary Tool**: Nano Banana Pro (OpenArt.ai)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 (vertical portrait format)
- **Alternative**: Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion with appropriate settings

### Identity Lock System

This system uses **@image1** as the main identity reference with absolute preservation:

**What Gets Preserved:**

- Recognizable face and facial features
- Eye color and facial structure
- Skin tone and natural beauty features
- Body proportions and overall presence
- Natural texture and tonal variation

**Critical Rules:**

- ✅ Preserve exact identity throughout
- ✅ Adapt scene and styling to the reference person
- ✅ Keep face clearly recognizable
- ❌ NEVER replace, reinterpret, beautify, or alter identity
- ❌ NO face swapping, morphing, or identity drift
- ❌ NO plastic smoothing or over-retouching

### Additional References

- **@image2**: Optional outfit/styling reference (used in specific prompts for exact garment matching)
- **Purpose**: Ensures exact clothing design continuity while maintaining @image1 identity

## Prompt Structure

### Essential Components

1. **Identity Declaration**

```
Use @image1 as the main identity reference. Preserve the recognizable
identity, facial features, eye colour, facial structure, skin tone, natural
beauty details, and overall presence of the person in the reference image.
```

2. **Scene Description**

- Specific location (beach club, terrace, canyon, pool, water)
- Time of day and lighting conditions
- Atmosphere and mood
- Background elements and environment

3. **Styling Specifications**

- **Outfit**: Detailed garment description with fabric, cut, color tones
- **Accessories**: Jewelry, sunglasses, scarves, bags (all orange-coordinated)
- **Hair**: Elegant styling appropriate to scene while maintaining recognition
- **Nails**: Glossy orange gel nails, polished finish

4. **Makeup Details**

- **Eyes**: Orange/warm eyeshadow, defined lashes, diffused liner
- **Skin**: Bronzed, sun-kissed, realistic texture with visible pores
- **Cheeks**: Warm blush, sculpted contour, subtle highlighter
- **Lips**: Glossy, satin finish, natural lip lines, warm tones
- **Rule**: Polished and realistic—never plastic or over-smoothed

5. **Pose and Expression**

- Body language and positioning
- Hand placement and natural gestures
- Facial expression and gaze direction
- Level of candid vs. posed feeling

6. **Lighting Specifications**

- **Primary Source**: Natural sunlight, sunset, golden hour, flash
- **Secondary**: Reflections from water, walls, orange surfaces
- **Technique**: Often combines frontal flash + natural backlight
- **Quality**: Realistic shadows, natural highlights, believable reflections
- **Technical**: f/2.8, ISO 100-250, 1/250s typical settings

7. **Camera Simulation**

- **Lens**: 50mm or 85mm portrait lens
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow (f/2.8) for editorial feel
- **Quality**: DSLR realism, HD crisp detail, subtle grain
- **Composition**: Vertical 3:4 or 9:16 format

8. **Negative Prompt (Critical)**

```
changed identity, different face, unrealistic skin, plastic skin,
over-smoothed face, distorted hands, extra fingers, awkward pose, poor
anatomy, CGI look, cartoon effect, harsh HDR, artificial glow, blurry face,
text overlay, watermark, app UI
```

## Technical Specifications

### Skin Rendering

- **Texture**: Visible pores, natural tonal variation, realistic detail
- **No Plastic**: Never over-smooth or artificially perfect
- **Bronzing**: Sun-kissed, warm, luminous glow
- **Highlights**: Subtle, natural placement (cheekbones, collarbones)
- **Contour**: Soft sculpting, realistic shadow placement

### Hand & Anatomy Rules

- Natural hand movements and gestures
- Correct finger count and proportions
- Believable object interaction
- No distortion, morphing, or extra digits
- Realistic body contact and positioning

### Fabric & Material Detail

- Realistic textile texture (silk, linen, chiffon, organza)
- Natural draping and movement
- Accurate wet fabric when in water
- Visible weave, folds, and structure
- Jewelry with realistic shine and reflections

### Water & Environmental Realism

- Believable ripples and movement
- Realistic reflections and refraction
- Natural droplets on skin
- Accurate waterline and immersion depth
- Soft background blur for depth

## Lighting Techniques

### Flash + Natural Light Combination

**Primary Setup**: Bold frontal flash + warm natural backlight

**Benefits**:

- Crisp foreground clarity
- Defined shadows and dimension
- Realistic skin and fabric detail
- Professional editorial quality

**How to Prompt**:

```
Lighting should combine bold frontal flash for crisp foreground clarity and
defined shadows with warm [natural sunlight/sunset/golden hour] as the main
source, soft reflected light from [surface], and a gentle ambient [glow type]
wrapping the face, body, and [elements].
```

### Sunset & Golden Hour

**Characteristics**:

- Warm orange, amber, golden tones
- Long shadows and dramatic backlight
- Natural lens flare and sun starburst
- Reflections on water or surfaces
- Glowing highlights on skin and hair edges

### Mediterranean Daylight

**Characteristics**:

- Bright, warm, natural sunlight
- Realistic outdoor shadows
- Slight lens reflections
- Auto-exposure variation (shade to sun)
- High contrast, true-to-life colors

## Workflow

### Step 1: Prepare Reference

- **@image1**: Clear face selfie showing full face, natural lighting
- **Requirements**: Recognizable features, neutral expression, good quality
- **Optional @image2**: Specific outfit reference if exact garment match needed

### Step 2: Choose Scene

- Select from 14 orange summer scenarios
- Consider mood: candid vs. editorial, playful vs. sophisticated
- Match location to desired aesthetic

### Step 3: Customize Prompt

- Keep identity lock language intact
- Adjust styling details to suit reference person
- Modify hair description to match natural appearance
- Maintain orange color story throughout

### Step 4: Generate

- Use Nano Banana Pro with 3:4 aspect ratio
- Upload @image1 reference
- Paste complete prompt
- Generate and refine as needed

### Step 5: Quality Check

- ✅ Identity preserved and recognizable
- ✅ Skin texture realistic (no plastic smoothing)
- ✅ Hands and anatomy correct
- ✅ Orange color story cohesive
- ✅ Lighting natural and believable
- ✅ Styling polished and luxurious

## Pro Tips

### Identity Consistency

- Use same @image1 across all variations for series
- Reference specific features in prompt if drift occurs
- Emphasize "clearly recognizable as @image1" in prompt
- Include "zero deviation" language for strict preservation

### Orange Harmony

- Layer multiple orange tones (tangerine + amber + copper)
- Include orange in outfit, background, props, makeup, nails
- Use warm lighting to enhance orange reflections
- Create monochromatic orange worlds for strongest impact

### Realistic Luxury

- Specify "photorealistic 2025 luxury editorial"
- Reference real locations (Amalfi, Antelope Canyon, Saint-Tropez)
- Include tactile details (textile texture, jewelry shine, water droplets)
- Combine natural + flash lighting for editorial quality

### Avoiding Common Issues

- Always include comprehensive negative prompt
- Specify "visible pores" and "natural skin texture" for realism
- Mention "correct hand anatomy" and "accurate finger count"
- Request "believable" and "realistic" repeatedly
