# Precision Psychological Neo-Noir Directing Treatment

## Positioning

A directing treatment for controlled, psychologically tense, modern neo-noir work. The approach favors exact visual systems, restrained performances, architectural compositions, procedural detail, and unease created through timing, space, sound, and repetition rather than spectacle.

## Visual Grammar

- Use precise, symmetrical or deliberately rigid compositions with strong geometric lines.
- Keep the camera controlled: locked-off frames, measured lateral movement, slow pushes, and motivated tracking.
- Use wide negative space to make characters feel observed, isolated, or structurally contained.
- Favor cool desaturated neutrals, steel, concrete, fluorescent green, sodium amber, and carefully isolated accent colors.
- Build contrast through practical sources: window spill, fluorescent tubes, street lamps, monitors, and hard architectural shadows.
- Maintain clean edges, stable horizon lines, and consistent perspective. Every camera move should have a spatial reason.
- Let production design carry narrative information through repeated objects, grids, corridors, reflections, and institutional surfaces.

## Performance Direction

- Underplay dialogue and reaction; favor small eye movements, delayed responses, controlled breathing, and tension in the jaw or hands.
- Avoid broad emotional signaling when a micro-reaction can carry the beat.
- Give characters a private objective in every shot, even when they are apparently still.
- Use pauses, interruptions, and incomplete gestures to create psychological pressure.
- Direct extras as a system or pattern first, then introduce one behavior that breaks the pattern.

## Editing and Rhythm

- Establish a measured baseline before interrupting it.
- Prefer hard cuts motivated by action, information, or sound rather than decorative transitions.
- Use repetition with controlled variation: the same angle, object, route, or gesture should return with one meaningful change.
- Hold a frame slightly longer than expected when discomfort or observation is the point.
- Keep action legible and cause-and-effect precise; do not hide a story beat behind arbitrary cutting.
- Use inserts of hands, mechanisms, documents, reflections, and surfaces as evidence, not filler.

## Sound Direction

- Build the track from practical ambience: ventilation, fluorescent hum, distant traffic, footsteps, room tone, fabric, switches, and machinery.
- Use silence as an active pressure change.
- Let sound arrive slightly before or after an image when it creates anticipation or dislocation.
- Avoid constant score. When music is present, keep it restrained, textural, and rhythmically purposeful.
- Design transitions through sound bridges and recurring sonic motifs.

## Lighting and Color

- Motivate every source and preserve its direction across cuts.
- Separate cool ambient space from a warmer practical source or a single controlled accent.
- Protect shadow detail while allowing blacks to feel dense and intentional.
- Avoid glossy beauty light, uncontrolled bloom, and generic teal-orange grading.
- Keep skin natural and textural; the world may be stylized, but faces must remain human.

## Prompt Formula

```text
Controlled psychological neo-noir direction, precise architectural composition, restrained performance with micro-reactions, deliberate camera movement, motivated practical lighting, cool desaturated palette with one controlled accent color, procedural environmental detail, hard editorial cuts, purposeful silence, layered diegetic sound, measured tension, realistic skin and materials, no gratuitous spectacle, no arbitrary camera movement
```

## Avoid

- Unmotivated handheld movement
- Excessive slow motion or speed ramps
- Generic cinematic blue-orange grading
- Overwritten dialogue and theatrical reactions
- Random lens flares, fog, or particles
- Coverage that destroys spatial continuity
- Visual polish that removes texture, wear, or human imperfection

## Attribution Note

This guide translates recognizable high-level traits associated with precision psychological neo-noir cinema into reusable directing language. It is not intended to reproduce any specific filmmaker's exact style, shot, or film.
