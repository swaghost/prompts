# Macro Close Up - Extreme Detail Photography

## Description

Create an extreme macro close-up of a material surface from the scene, revealing fine texture and realistic imperfections with surrounding objects softly visible in the background. This prompt delivers professional macro photography with ultra-detailed material examination, photorealistic surface characteristics, shallow depth of field, and contextual background blur—ideal for material documentation, texture studies, quality demonstration, design detail emphasis, and creating compelling visual narratives through intimate surface exploration.

## Photography Overview

**Shot Type:** Extreme macro close-up  
**Focus:** Material surface texture  
**Detail Level:** Fine texture, imperfections visible  
**Depth of Field:** Very shallow (extreme blur)  
**Background:** Surrounding objects softly visible  
**Purpose:** Material documentation, texture study, design detail  
**Application:** Materials, surfaces, textures, quality showcase
