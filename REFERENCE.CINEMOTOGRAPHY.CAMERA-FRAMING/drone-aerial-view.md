# Drone Aerial View - High Elevation Perspective

## Description

Move the camera to a high drone viewpoint above the scene, revealing a large surrounding environment around the project. This prompt repositions the camera to an elevated aerial perspective, capturing birds-eye views, revealing site context, spatial relationships, and comprehensive environmental understanding—ideal for architectural projects, urban planning, landscape design, real estate marketing, and cinematic establishing shots requiring contextual overview and dramatic scale.

## Camera Movement Overview

**Camera Action:** Elevate to high drone position  
**Resulting View:** Aerial bird's-eye perspective  
**Height:** Drone elevation (varies by subject)  
**Purpose:** Environmental context, spatial overview, scale revelation  
**Application:** Architecture, urban scenes, landscapes, real estate

## Drone Photography Specifications

### Typical Drone Heights

**Height Variations:**

- Low aerial: 15-30m / 50-100ft (detail + context)
- Medium aerial: 30-75m / 100-250ft (balanced overview)
- High aerial: 75-120m / 250-400ft (wide context)
- Extreme aerial: 120m+ / 400ft+ (landscape scale)
- Subject-dependent appropriate height

**Angle Variations:**

- Top-down (90° looking down) - architectural
- Oblique high angle (45-70°) - cinematic
- Low oblique (30-45°) - contextual but closer
- Straight-down nadir - mapping, roofs
- Professional drone cinematography
