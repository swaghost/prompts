# OTS CLOSE (Over-The-Shoulder Close)

## Description

Hyperrealistic photograph. Camera positioned close behind one shoulder, tight over-the-shoulder framing with the subject's face filling the far plane. Foreground shoulder large and soft, subject sharp and near. Compressed intimate perspective, shallow depth of field. Photorealistic, natural lighting.

## Camera Position

- **Location:** Close behind one shoulder
- **Distance:** Very close to subject
- **Angle:** Over-the-shoulder looking at face

## Framing Characteristics

- **Foreground:** Shoulder large and soft (out of focus)
- **Subject:** Sharp and near, face filling far plane
- **Framing Type:** Tight, intimate over-the-shoulder composition
- **Depth of Field:** Shallow - foreground soft, subject sharp

## Technical Specifications

- **Perspective:** Compressed intimate perspective
- **Focus:** Subject's face in sharp focus
- **Lighting:** Photorealistic, natural lighting
- **Style:** Hyperrealistic photograph

## Visual Effect

- Creates intimate, personal feeling
- Provides context through foreground shoulder
- Emphasizes connection between foreground and subject
- Shallow DOF creates separation and focus on face
- Compressed perspective brings viewer close to action

## Common Applications

- Conversation scenes
- Intimate portraits
- Reaction shots
- Point-of-view adjacent framing
- Emotional close-ups with context
- Fashion and beauty with depth
- Relationship dynamics

## Technical Execution

- Large aperture for shallow depth of field (f/1.4-f/2.8)
- Longer focal length for compression (85mm-135mm)
- Focus on subject's face/eyes
- Position shoulder to frame one side
- Ensure sufficient bokeh on foreground shoulder
- Natural soft lighting for photorealism
