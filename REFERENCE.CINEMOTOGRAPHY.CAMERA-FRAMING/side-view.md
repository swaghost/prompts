# Side View - Lateral Perspective Camera Angle

## Description

Move the camera all the way to the right, showing objects and architecture from a right-side perspective. This prompt repositions the camera to capture a complete lateral view of the subject, revealing side elevations, profiles, and spatial relationships from a 90-degree shifted perspective—ideal for architectural documentation, product visualization, character studies, and comprehensive visual presentations requiring multiple angle coverage.

## Camera Movement Overview

**Camera Action:** Move camera to the right  
**Resulting View:** Right-side lateral perspective  
**Angle Shift:** 90 degrees from front/original view  
**Purpose:** Side elevation documentation, profile view, spatial understanding  
**Application:** Architecture, products, characters, interiors
