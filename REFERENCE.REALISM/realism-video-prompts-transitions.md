## Video Prompt 1: Portrait to Mouth Zoom

### Video Prompt

Use the provided start frame (@Image 1) and end frame (@Image 2) to generate a 4-second, ultra-realistic vertical 9:16 video. Create a continuous single shot with no cuts. The camera movement must feel smooth and keyframe-planned, like a controlled optical or digital zoom—no handheld feel, no shaking, and no camera jitter.

The video starts with the exact composition of Image 1: a young woman sitting on a beach mat. Over the duration of the video, execute a slow, fully continuous push-in/zoom, cutting directly from this full close-up portrait into the mouth area, zooming in toward the lips until the frame reaches the exact composition, proportion, and focus of Image 2. The zoom must never stop, stutter, or jump—it fades in via smooth keyframes and gently decelerates, ultimately coming to a clean, stable stop on the final composition of Image 2.

Throughout the entire movement, maintain the exact same lighting, color temperature, exposure, skin tone, and lighting atmosphere as Image 1, making it look like one continuous, real moment.

### Key Authenticity Constraints:

- **Zero Identity Drift**: Same look, same facial structure, and the exact same teeth and lip shapes at any scale.
- **No Distortion**: No skin warping, no morphing, no stretching, no liquefying, no fabricated textures, and no overlapping or smearing.
- **Preserve Details**: Precisely retain natural pores, freckles, fine vellus hair, and lip textures frame by frame.
- **Ultra-Low Motion Blur**: Keep details crisp and sharp during movement, with no trailing and no pixel mixing across the timeline.
- **No Beauty Filters**: No skin smoothing, no CGI feel, no plastic skin, and no over-sharpening.
- **Natural Physical Behavior**: Any micro-movements of the subject throughout must remain subtle and believable.
- **Precise Ending**: End exactly on the composition, focal plane, and macro framing of Image 2, with the teeth and lips in sharp focus.

## Universal Video Constraints

These principles apply to ALL video transitions:

### Camera Movement

- **Smooth keyframe-planned movement**: No handheld feel, no shaking, no jitter
- **Continuous motion**: Never stop, stutter, or jump mid-transition
- **Gentle deceleration**: Smooth ease-in/ease-out to final frame
- **Controlled zoom**: Optical or digital zoom behavior (not physical camera movement for most)

### Identity Consistency

- **Zero drift**: Same person, same features throughout entire transition
- **Lock anatomical details**: Preserve exact facial structure, proportions
- **Maintain expressions**: No unintended expression changes (except deliberate actions like speaking/blinking)
- **Same skin characteristics**: Pores, freckles, texture remain stable frame-by-frame

### Technical Realism

- **Lighting consistency**: Same ambient light, color temperature, highlights, shadows throughout
- **Exposure stability**: No brightness fluctuations or color shifts
- **Authentic depth of field**: Natural bokeh behavior as focus changes
- **No fabricated details**: No hallucinatory textures, no pixel mixing, no smearing

### Motion Blur Control

- **Ultra-low motion blur**: Keep micro-details crisp during movement
- **No trailing**: Edges remain sharp, no ghosting
- **Maintain texture**: Skin pores, hair strands, surface details visible throughout

### Natural Behavior

- **Realistic blinking**: Soft, natural eyelid movement when applicable
- **Subtle micro-movements**: Believable, minimal subject motion
- **Physical accuracy**: No rubbery feel, no distortion during actions
- **Natural physics**: Hair, fabric, jewelry move realistically

### Anti-Beautification

- **No skin smoothing**: Preserve authentic skin texture
- **No CGI feel**: Maintain photographic realism
- **No plastic skin**: Keep biological texture quality
- **No over-sharpening**: Avoid artificial enhancement artifacts

### Precision Ending

- **Exact match to end frame**: Stop precisely on target composition
- **Correct focal plane**: Match sharpness and depth of field of end frame
- **Stable final hold**: Clean, stable stop (no drift or wobble)

## Usage Tips

### Generation Best Practices

1. **Generate all images first**: Complete all 7 images before starting videos
2. **Use exact frame pairs**: Match start/end frames precisely to table above
3. **Copy prompts exactly**: Use full prompt text, don't abbreviate
4. **Generate multiple attempts**: Create 2-3 versions of each, select best
5. **Check for artifacts**: Review each video for distortion, morphing, or identity drift
6. **Download immediately**: Save all videos before leaving Deevid AI

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Identity Drift During Transition**:

- Problem: Subject's face changes mid-transition
- Solution: Regenerate, emphasizing "zero identity drift" in prompt
- Tip: Ensure start and end frames show same person

**Motion Blur or Distortion**:

- Problem: Warping, morphing, or blurry movement
- Solution: Emphasize "ultra-low motion blur, no distortion"
- Tip: Master V4.0 model handles this better than other models

**Lighting Changes**:

- Problem: Color temperature shifts during transition
- Solution: Add "maintain exact same lighting throughout"
- Tip: Check source images have consistent lighting

**Stuttering or Jumping Motion**:

- Problem: Movement isn't smooth
- Solution: Regenerate with emphasis on "smooth keyframes, gentle deceleration"
- Tip: May need to try 2-3 times

### Platform-Specific Tips

## Troubleshooting Guide

### Problem: Generation Fails or Errors

**Possible Causes**:

- Platform overload (high traffic)
- Account credit/subscription issue
- Image file format incompatibility
- Prompt too long or complex

**Solutions**:

1. Check account status and credits
2. Verify image files are JPG or PNG
3. Try during off-peak hours
4. Simplify prompt if necessary (keep key constraints)

### Problem: Video Quality Lower Than Expected

**Possible Causes**:

- 720P resolution setting
- Compression during generation
- Source images not high enough quality

**Solutions**:

1. Ensure source images are 1024x1024 or higher
2. Check export settings in Deevid AI
3. Post-process in CapCut if needed
4. Consider upscaling in post-production

### Problem: Transition Doesn't Match End Frame

**Possible Causes**:

- AI interpretation variation
- End frame not clearly specified
- Movement too complex

**Solutions**:

1. Emphasize "end exactly on composition of Image 2"
2. Regenerate 2-3 times
3. Use "Precise Ending" constraint more forcefully
4. Simplify movement if too complex
