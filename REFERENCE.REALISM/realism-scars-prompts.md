# Realism Scars Prompts

## Source Note

Extracted from the shared Canva design's visible "8k realism hack" card. The card's core instruction is to name the imperfections you want to see instead of relying on generic claims such as "ultra realistic" or "8k." The Canva link exposed this card's text but not a complete readable set of additional pages.

## Core Prompt

```text
Instead of saying "[SUBJECT], ultra realistic, 8k," name the imperfections you want to see. Perfect is the enemy of realism.
```

## Expanded Realism-Scars Prompt

```text
Create [SUBJECT] as a candid, believable photograph. Do not rely on generic "ultra realistic" or "8k" language. Specify and show the physical imperfections that make the subject feel lived-in: [VISIBLE SCAR OR MARK], [SKIN OR MATERIAL IRREGULARITY], [ASYMMETRY], [WEAR, WRINKLE, OR SCUFF], and [NATURAL LIGHTING OR ENVIRONMENTAL IMPERFECTION]. Keep these details subtle, physically plausible, and consistent with the subject's age, materials, setting, and camera distance. Avoid beauty retouching, plastic surfaces, perfect symmetry, sterile studio polish, CGI smoothness, and invented details that would not be visible at this scale.
```

## Human Subject Template

```text
Photograph [PERSON] in [SETTING] with natural [LIGHTING]. Include believable lived-in details: a healed [SCAR TYPE] in [LOCATION], visible pores, slight uneven skin tone, fine vellus hair, natural facial asymmetry, [WRINKLES/FRECKLES/BLEMISHES], and [CLOTHING WEAR]. Use an imperfect [PHONE CAMERA/CAMERA TYPE] viewpoint with realistic focus falloff and ordinary environmental texture. No beauty filter, skin smoothing, plastic skin, perfect symmetry, or generic "ultra realistic 8k" filler.
```

## Object, Product, or Environment Template

```text
Photograph [OBJECT/SPACE] in [SETTING] as a real, used thing rather than a pristine render. Show specific physical evidence: [SCRATCHES], [SCUFFS], [DENTS], [DUST OR WATER MARKS], [UNEVENTEN SURFACE/FINISH], [WORN EDGE], and [IMPERFECT REFLECTION OR SHADOW]. Use [LIGHTING] and a believable [CAMERA VIEW]. Keep wear proportional and plausible. Avoid sterile perfection, repeated texture patterns, floating objects, impossible reflections, showroom polish, CGI surfaces, and generic "8k photorealism" language.
```

## Detail Checklist

Before generating, replace generic realism adjectives with observable details from these categories:

- **Skin:** pores, freckles, peach fuzz, scars, healed marks, uneven tone, fine lines, tiny blemishes, goosebumps, sunscreen or sweat sheen
- **Hair:** flyaways, uneven strands, varied thickness, flattened sections, stray hairs, realistic roots
- **Hands and nails:** cuticles, slight dryness, nail ridges, asymmetry, small marks, natural creases
- **Clothing:** wrinkles, lint, stretched seams, uneven hems, worn cuffs, fabric pilling, small stains
- **Objects:** scratches, fingerprints, dents, scuffs, dust, water spots, tarnish, edge wear, inconsistent finish
- **Environment:** uneven shadows, glare, condensation, clutter, aged surfaces, crooked objects, dust in corners, imperfect reflections
- **Camera evidence:** awkward crop, slight exposure variation, ordinary phone processing, natural focus falloff, imperfect framing

## Negative Prompt

```text
No beauty filter, no airbrushed skin, no plastic or waxy surfaces, no perfect symmetry, no sterile showroom finish, no CGI smoothness, no repeated texture patterns, no impossible reflections, no warped anatomy, no invented scars or marks that are not specified, no excessive dirt, no melodramatic damage, no generic "ultra realistic 8k" filler.
```

## Usage Rule

Specific imperfections should support the scene, not become decoration. Choose details that would be visible at the requested camera distance and that make sense for the subject's age, material, history, and environment. Realism comes from coherent physical evidence, not from adding more adjectives or random damage.
